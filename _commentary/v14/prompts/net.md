# v13 step N — the surah's image network (English records only)

Every ayah of the surah has been read on its own: its findings (records `S:A F<n>`) follow, after the surah text, the
scan (every branch of every root in the surah or window, one line each) and the surah's channel review (an earlier
model's map of its image chains). You assemble the images that run through
the surah and how they interact. You write no prose for readers; later steps test the findings against the rest of the
Quran and write the commentaries from your network.

## What to do

- Assemble every image that runs across two or more ayat: its members (ayah, word, branch, role: source, conduit,
  guide, container, pressure, release, loss, reversal …) and the movement they draw together. Name mechanisms, not
  themes: an image is complementary roles in motion, not a list of words from one field.
- Mature the images: once an image forms, reread the scan through it and add the members it makes visible, even
  branches no ayah recorded (mark them `N-added`, with the activating word).
- Show how the images interact: one word can carry branches of two images; one figure can play a role in two images;
  say what each meeting shows.
- Say how the images bear on the surah's movement and purpose: several images often converge on its main theme from
  different sides.
- **Do not prune.** Every ayah finding is placed in an image, or listed under `unplaced` with the reason it stands
  alone. Nothing is rejected here; weak members stay members. No confidence, no ranking, no verdicts.
- For the ayah commentaries, give each image a disclosure plan that names **every ayah holding a member**, each with
  a role: `meet` (the reader first senses the image here), `touch` (this ayah's word adds one member, in a sentence),
  `develop` (it grows here), `assemble` (the whole image is shown here, with every member). Exactly one `assemble`.

## Your final message

A line `===== NETWORK =====`, then for each image:

`I<n> | name | members: S:A:W root Bnnn (role) [S:A F-ids or N-added]; … | movement: … | meets: I<m> at S:A:W — what the meeting shows; … | purpose: … | disclosure: S:A meet (word); S:A touch (word); S:A develop (word); S:A assemble (word)`

then one line `unplaced: S:A F<n> (why it stands alone); …` listing every finding no image absorbs (they go on to the
commentaries; nothing is dropped). English, compact.
