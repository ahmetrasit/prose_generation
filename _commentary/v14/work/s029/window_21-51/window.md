29:24|فَمَا كَانَ جَوَابَ قَوْمِهِۦٓ إِلَّآ أَن قَالُوا۟ ٱقْتُلُوهُ أَوْ حَرِّقُوهُ فَأَنجَىٰهُ ٱللَّهُ مِنَ ٱلنَّارِ ۚ إِنَّ فِى ذَٰلِكَ لَءَايَٰتٍۢ لِّقَوْمٍۢ يُؤْمِنُونَ
29:25|وَقَالَ إِنَّمَا ٱتَّخَذْتُم مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ أَوْثَٰنًۭا مَّوَدَّةَ بَيْنِكُمْ فِى ٱلْحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنْيَا ۖ ثُمَّ يَوْمَ ٱلْقِيَٰمَةِ يَكْفُرُ بَعْضُكُم بِبَعْضٍۢ وَيَلْعَنُ بَعْضُكُم بَعْضًۭا وَمَأْوَىٰكُمُ ٱلنَّارُ وَمَا لَكُم مِّن نَّٰصِرِينَ
29:26|۞ فَـَٔامَنَ لَهُۥ لُوطٌۭ ۘ وَقَالَ إِنِّى مُهَاجِرٌ إِلَىٰ رَبِّىٓ ۖ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلْعَزِيزُ ٱلْحَكِيمُ
29:27|وَوَهَبْنَا لَهُۥٓ إِسْحَٰقَ وَيَعْقُوبَ وَجَعَلْنَا فِى ذُرِّيَّتِهِ ٱلنُّبُوَّةَ وَٱلْكِتَٰبَ وَءَاتَيْنَٰهُ أَجْرَهُۥ فِى ٱلدُّنْيَا ۖ وَإِنَّهُۥ فِى ٱلْءَاخِرَةِ لَمِنَ ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ
29:28|وَلُوطًا إِذْ قَالَ لِقَوْمِهِۦٓ إِنَّكُمْ لَتَأْتُونَ ٱلْفَٰحِشَةَ مَا سَبَقَكُم بِهَا مِنْ أَحَدٍۢ مِّنَ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ
29:29|أَئِنَّكُمْ لَتَأْتُونَ ٱلرِّجَالَ وَتَقْطَعُونَ ٱلسَّبِيلَ وَتَأْتُونَ فِى نَادِيكُمُ ٱلْمُنكَرَ ۖ فَمَا كَانَ جَوَابَ قَوْمِهِۦٓ إِلَّآ أَن قَالُوا۟ ٱئْتِنَا بِعَذَابِ ٱللَّهِ إِن كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰدِقِينَ
29:30|قَالَ رَبِّ ٱنصُرْنِى عَلَى ٱلْقَوْمِ ٱلْمُفْسِدِينَ
29:31|وَلَمَّا جَآءَتْ رُسُلُنَآ إِبْرَٰهِيمَ بِٱلْبُشْرَىٰ قَالُوٓا۟ إِنَّا مُهْلِكُوٓا۟ أَهْلِ هَٰذِهِ ٱلْقَرْيَةِ ۖ إِنَّ أَهْلَهَا كَانُوا۟ ظَٰلِمِينَ
29:32|قَالَ إِنَّ فِيهَا لُوطًۭا ۚ قَالُوا۟ نَحْنُ أَعْلَمُ بِمَن فِيهَا ۖ لَنُنَجِّيَنَّهُۥ وَأَهْلَهُۥٓ إِلَّا ٱمْرَأَتَهُۥ كَانَتْ مِنَ ٱلْغَٰبِرِينَ
29:33|وَلَمَّآ أَن جَآءَتْ رُسُلُنَا لُوطًۭا سِىٓءَ بِهِمْ وَضَاقَ بِهِمْ ذَرْعًۭا وَقَالُوا۟ لَا تَخَفْ وَلَا تَحْزَنْ ۖ إِنَّا مُنَجُّوكَ وَأَهْلَكَ إِلَّا ٱمْرَأَتَكَ كَانَتْ مِنَ ٱلْغَٰبِرِينَ
29:34|إِنَّا مُنزِلُونَ عَلَىٰٓ أَهْلِ هَٰذِهِ ٱلْقَرْيَةِ رِجْزًۭا مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ بِمَا كَانُوا۟ يَفْسُقُونَ
29:35|وَلَقَد تَّرَكْنَا مِنْهَآ ءَايَةًۢ بَيِّنَةًۭ لِّقَوْمٍۢ يَعْقِلُونَ
29:36|وَإِلَىٰ مَدْيَنَ أَخَاهُمْ شُعَيْبًۭا فَقَالَ يَٰقَوْمِ ٱعْبُدُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ وَٱرْجُوا۟ ٱلْيَوْمَ ٱلْءَاخِرَ وَلَا تَعْثَوْا۟ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ مُفْسِدِينَ
29:37|فَكَذَّبُوهُ فَأَخَذَتْهُمُ ٱلرَّجْفَةُ فَأَصْبَحُوا۟ فِى دَارِهِمْ جَٰثِمِينَ
29:38|وَعَادًۭا وَثَمُودَا۟ وَقَد تَّبَيَّنَ لَكُم مِّن مَّسَٰكِنِهِمْ ۖ وَزَيَّنَ لَهُمُ ٱلشَّيْطَٰنُ أَعْمَٰلَهُمْ فَصَدَّهُمْ عَنِ ٱلسَّبِيلِ وَكَانُوا۟ مُسْتَبْصِرِينَ
29:39|وَقَٰرُونَ وَفِرْعَوْنَ وَهَٰمَٰنَ ۖ وَلَقَدْ جَآءَهُم مُّوسَىٰ بِٱلْبَيِّنَٰتِ فَٱسْتَكْبَرُوا۟ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ وَمَا كَانُوا۟ سَٰبِقِينَ
29:40|فَكُلًّا أَخَذْنَا بِذَنۢبِهِۦ ۖ فَمِنْهُم مَّنْ أَرْسَلْنَا عَلَيْهِ حَاصِبًۭا وَمِنْهُم مَّنْ أَخَذَتْهُ ٱلصَّيْحَةُ وَمِنْهُم مَّنْ خَسَفْنَا بِهِ ٱلْأَرْضَ وَمِنْهُم مَّنْ أَغْرَقْنَا ۚ وَمَا كَانَ ٱللَّهُ لِيَظْلِمَهُمْ وَلَٰكِن كَانُوٓا۟ أَنفُسَهُمْ يَظْلِمُونَ
29:41|مَثَلُ ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّخَذُوا۟ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ أَوْلِيَآءَ كَمَثَلِ ٱلْعَنكَبُوتِ ٱتَّخَذَتْ بَيْتًۭا ۖ وَإِنَّ أَوْهَنَ ٱلْبُيُوتِ لَبَيْتُ ٱلْعَنكَبُوتِ ۖ لَوْ كَانُوا۟ يَعْلَمُونَ
29:42|إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَعْلَمُ مَا يَدْعُونَ مِن دُونِهِۦ مِن شَىْءٍۢ ۚ وَهُوَ ٱلْعَزِيزُ ٱلْحَكِيمُ

## The surah's channel review (an earlier model's map of its image chains)

# channels.md — the surah's image chains as an earlier whole-surah review mapped them

### 4. P2 (29:14-27) Manufactured Allegiance and Its Reversal
- Semantic invariant: A cultic object becomes powerful through service, possession, affection, and alliance, then those same relations reverse into repudiation and expulsion.
- Surface relation: direct; 29:16-17 and 29:25 join worship, idols, possession, mutual affection, denial, cursing, and absent aid.
- Surprising reach: Idolatry appears less as a static object class than as a deliberately assembled social and property regime whose bonds later run backward.
#### Subchannel A. Idol, Owner, and Servile Hierarchy
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: An object is installed as a worshiped superior, people submit in service, and possession and sovereignty make the arrangement durable.
- Active motifs: a made object of worship `ء ل ه B001`; a worshiped idol or stone `و ث ن B001`; submissive ritual service `ع ب د B003`; reducing another to servile labor `ع ب د B004`; ownership and disposal of property `م ل ك B002`; sovereignty and rule `م ل ك B003`; an inferior substitute `د و ن B003`
- Ayah anchors: ء ل ه 29:16-17,19-20,22-25; و ث ن 29:17,25; ع ب د 29:16-17; م ل ك 29:17; د و ن 29:17,22,25.
- Synthesis: The idols are placed inside an ownership hierarchy: the worshiper behaves as a servant while attributing rule and disposal to an inferior object. The lexical scene exposes the social labor required to manufacture a "master."
#### Subchannel B. Affection as an Alliance Contract
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Shared attachment to a cultic object binds parties through proximity, patronage, and a connective social compact.
- Active motifs: affection and mutual love `و د د B001`; Wadd as a named idol `و د د B005`; loving and protective alliance `و ل ي B004`; legal clientage `و ل ي B005`; a connective relation between parties `ب ي ن B003`; taking and possessing an object `ء خ ذ B001`
- Ayah anchors: و د د 29:25; و ل ي 29:22; ب ي ن 29:18,25; ء خ ذ 29:14,25.
- Synthesis: "Affection among you" behaves like alliance infrastructure: proximity, protection, clientage, and possession gather around a shared object. Wadd's lexical identity as an idol sharpens the pun by making affection itself both bond and cultic name.
#### Subchannel C. Repudiation, Separation, and Expulsion
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: The alliance is disowned, its connective middle is cut, parties turn away, and each drives the other beyond protection.
- Active motifs: repudiation and disavowal `ك ف ر B005`; separation after connection `ب ي ن B001`; irrevocable severance `ب ي ن B012`; turning away and abandoning proximity `و ل ي B007`; expulsion under wrath `ل ع ن B001`; reciprocal imprecation `ل ع ن B004`; aid against an adversary `ن ص ر B001`
- Ayah anchors: ك ف ر 29:23,25; ب ي ن 29:18,25; و ل ي 29:22; ل ع ن 29:25; ن ص ر 29:22,25.
- Synthesis: The eschatological reversal dismantles every relation that sustained the idol compact. Affection becomes mutual disavowal, connection becomes irrevocable separation, patronage becomes withdrawal, and blessing is replaced by reciprocal expulsion.

### 5. P2 (29:14-27) The Message Made Visible
- Semantic invariant: A dispatched communication reaches its limit by becoming an intelligible, inspectable mark.
- Surface relation: direct; 29:14,18 name sending and clear delivery, while 29:15,19-20,23-24 repeatedly call for seeing signs and creation.
- Surprising reach: Communication is simultaneously courier-work, eloquent arrival, landmark, banner, and trained observation.
#### Subchannel A. Dispatch, Carriage, and Clear Delivery
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A sender releases a bearer, the bearer carries a consequential report, and expression reaches the intended recipient clearly.
- Active motifs: dispatch and release `ر س ل B001`; messenger and carried message `ر س ل B002`; delivery of a message `ب ل غ B002`; reaching an endpoint `ب ل غ B001`; eloquence that reaches its purpose `ب ل غ B004`; spoken clarification `ب ي ن B005`; a consequential report arriving `ن ب ء B002`
- Ayah anchors: ر س ل 29:14,18; ب ل غ 29:18; ب ي ن 29:18,25; ن ب ء 29:27.
- Synthesis: The messenger is a carrier whose task is completed when the communication reaches its limit. "Clear delivery" therefore combines transport and intelligibility: the report arrives and opens its meaning rather than merely occupying the recipient's hearing.
#### Subchannel B. Sign, Banner, and Deliberate Inspection
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A visible mark is raised or distinguished, observers direct eye and judgment toward it, and concealed structure becomes known.
- Active motifs: a manifest sign `ء ي ي B003`; a route-guiding landmark `ع ل م B002`; an identifying banner `ع ل م B002`; sensory and inward vision `ر ء ي B001`; a raised banner visible at distance `ر ء ي B011`; directed visual and reflective inspection `ن ظ ر B001`; emergence into clarity `ب ي ن B004`
- Ayah anchors: ء ي ي 29:15,23-24; ع ل م 29:15-16; ر ء ي 29:19; ن ظ ر 29:20; ب ي ن 29:18,25.
- Synthesis: The ark, fire-rescue, and recreated world function as raised evidence rather than inert spectacle. A sign is a landmark that solicits disciplined inspection until what was merely visible becomes inwardly known.

### 6. P2 (29:14-27) Origin, Measure, and Return
- Semantic invariant: Making proceeds through prior measure and first construction, then proves its power by turning the made thing back into renewed existence.
- Surface relation: direct; 29:17,19-20 join creating, beginning, redoing, and a later emergence, while 29:21 names reversal toward God.
- Surprising reach: Creation is rendered as leather-cutting, measurement, foundation work, assembly, and a reversible production cycle.
#### Subchannel B. Reversal, Return, and Renewed Emergence
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A completed form is turned, sent back toward its point of return, and made to emerge again.
- Active motifs: beginning paired with repetition `ب د ء B002`; return after departure `ع و د B001`; destination of return `ع و د B002`; turning a thing from one face to another `ق ل ب B004`; final destination or outcome `ق ل ب B005`; return to an earlier state `ر ج ع B001`; rising and coming forward `ن ش ء B008`
- Ayah anchors: ب د ء 29:19-20; ع و د 29:19; ق ل ب 29:21; ر ج ع 29:17; ن ش ء 29:20.
- Synthesis: Re-creation is not a second unrelated manufacture but a controlled reversal of an existing trajectory. The object is turned, reaches its return-point, and rises into a renewed emergence.

### 7. P2 (29:14-27) Water Governing Survival
- Semantic invariant: Water can encircle and destroy, support a crossing craft, preserve life on raised ground, or descend as apportioned nourishment.
- Surface relation: direct; 29:14-15 gives flood and ship, 29:15 and 29:24 give rescue, and 29:17 makes provision the object of seeking and gratitude.
- Surprising reach: Destruction and sustenance share one hydrological system of enclosure, flotation, elevation, rainfall, and living yield.
#### Subchannel B. Extraction to Flood-Safe Height
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Persons separate from danger through rapid movement and reach an elevated place beyond the flood-line.
- Active motifs: escape by separation `ن ج و B001`; rapid departure `ن ج و B002`; raised ground beyond flood reach `ن ج و B004`; companionship and close attendance `ص ح ب B001`; preservation through accompaniment `ص ح ب B002`; land as the lower supporting surface `ء ر ض B001`
- Ayah anchors: ن ج و 29:15,24; ص ح ب 29:15; ء ر ض 29:20,22.
- Synthesis: Rescue has a topography: separation from the destructive medium, accelerated movement, and arrival on a raised safe place. Noah's companions and Abraham's rescue are locally joined by extraction that preserves an accompanied group.
#### Subchannel C. Rain-Apportioned Life and Gratitude
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Rain descends as a measured allotment, revives land and animals, and makes a small input visibly productive.
- Active motifs: apportioned gift `ر ز ق B001`; nourishment entering the body `ر ز ق B002`; rain as provision `ر ز ق B003`; land vivified by rain and growth `ح ي ي B002`; gratitude that recognizes a benefactor `ش ك ر B001`; small feed or rain yielding visible increase `ش ك ر B002`; abundant milk after pasture `ش ك ر B003`
- Ayah anchors: ر ز ق 29:17; ح ي ي 29:25; ش ك ر 29:17.
- Synthesis: Provision is not only possessed food; it is rainfall entering a living production system. Gratitude reads the disproportion between a small received input and the greening, fattening, or milk-yield that follows.

### 8. P2 (29:14-27) Generative Continuity
- Semantic invariant: Continuity is produced by scattering and covering viable beginnings, then stabilizing the resulting line through gift, record, and durable return.
- Surface relation: indirect; 29:27 names gifted descendants, prophecy, book, and reward, while 29:17-20 supplies creating and emergence.

#### Subchannel A. Seed Scattering, Covering, and Mixed-Age Growth

- Active motifs: scattering grain and powder `ذ ر ر B002`; fine dispersed emergence `ذ ر ر B004`; covering seed with soil `ك ف ر B008`; growing and being raised among a people `ن ش ء B002`; young stock growing beside its source `و ث ن B004`; lasting and remaining in place `و ه ب B007`
- Ayah anchors: ذ ر ر 29:27; ك ف ر 29:23,25; ن ش ء 29:20; و ث ن 29:17,25; و ه ب 29:27.
- Synthesis: The descendant line is materialized as cultivation: minute beginnings are distributed, hidden under soil, and raised beside an older stock. Continuity depends on both dispersal and protective covering before it becomes visible growth.
#### Subchannel B. Gift Joined to Record and Vocation
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A gift is prepared and made durable, its elements are joined into a record, and a prophetic vocation and return are assigned to it.

- Ayah anchors: و ه ب 29:27; ك ت ب 29:27; ن ب ء 29:27; ء ج ر 29:27.
- Synthesis: The gifted line is not left unformed: it is prepared, joined, entered into a durable register, and entrusted with a reporting office. Reward is the returning benefit of that established continuity.

### 9. P3 (29:28-44) Routes and Forums Turned Hostile

### 11. P3 (29:28-44) Distinct Seizure Mechanics
- Semantic invariant: Each destroyed community is removed by a different physical operation that attacks its assumed stability.
- Surface relation: direct; 29:34,37,40 enumerates descending punishment, tremor, stone-bearing force, cry, sinking, and drowning.
- Surprising reach: The list becomes a mechanics of vibration, projectile wind, immersion, substrate failure, and acoustic shock, with distinct body states and outcomes.
#### Subchannel A. Tremor and Ground-Locked Bodies
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Severe oscillation passes through earth and body until persons lose upright motion and remain pressed to the ground.
- Active motifs: violent shaking across bodies, sea, trees, or thunder `ر ج ف B001`; repeated disturbance and rolling motion `ر ج ز B001`; bodily or terrestrial shudder `ء ر ض B008`; clinging to the ground `ج ث م B001`; the bodily mass left in place `ج ث م B003`; becoming fixed in a resulting state `ص ب ح B010`
- Ayah anchors: ر ج ف 29:37; ر ج ز 29:34; ء ر ض 29:36,39-40,44; ج ث م 29:37; ص ب ح 29:37.
- Synthesis: The tremor is a transfer of instability from ground to body. Its outcome is not generic death but loss of stance: bodily masses end adhered to the substrate in the state in which morning finds them.
#### Subchannel B. Stone-Bearing Wind and Descending Impact
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A force moving through the upper air lifts grit and stones, carries them, and delivers repeated impacts downward.
- Active motifs: loose stones and stony ground `ح ص ب B001`; pelting with stones `ح ص ب B002`; violent wind carrying grit or hail `ح ص ب B003`; upper cover, sky, and cloud `س م و B004`; causing an affliction to descend `ن ز ل B002`; a calamity that comes down upon a people `ن ز ل B006`; weather striking land and vegetation `ض ر ب B013`
- Ayah anchors: ح ص ب 29:40; س م و 29:34,44; ن ز ل 29:34; ض ر ب 29:43.
- Synthesis: The stone-bearing punishment has an aerial transport mechanism: wind entrains particles, the sky becomes a delivery field, and impacts descend onto exposed ground. It is therefore distinct from both immersion and substrate collapse.
#### Subchannel C. Immersion and Engulfing Water
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Water fills the available space, occupies the victim's field, and submerges until movement and life cease.
- Active motifs: sinking beneath engulfing water `غ ر ق B001`; land or eye filled with water `غ ر ق B003`; a medium occupying the whole field `غ ر ق B005`; a basin or depression retaining water `ء خ ذ B006`; the gathered body of water `ج ث م B007`; lower terrestrial support `ء ر ض B001`
- Ayah anchors: غ ر ق 29:40; ء خ ذ 29:37,40-41; ج ث م 29:37; ء ر ض 29:36,39-40,44.
- Synthesis: Drowning is modeled as progressive occupation: water gathers, fills, and takes over the victim's whole field. Unlike the tremor, which arrests bodies on the surface, immersion removes the breathable surface itself.
