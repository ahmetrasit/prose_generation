===== SEEDS =====

### Waymarks and the trodden road
- members: 1:2 ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ → ع ل م B003 (the worlds; each kind of creation is itself a sign of its maker) → the terrain that signals; 1:2 ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ → ع ل م B002 (distinguishing, guiding mark: banner, mountain seen from afar, road sign) → waymark; 1:1 بِسْمِ → س م و B002 (a figure rising into view from afar) → visibility of the mark; 1:6 ٱهْدِنَا → ه د ي B001 (showing the way gently) → the showing; 1:6 ٱهْدِنَا → ه د ي B002 (direction, course followed) → the heading taken; 1:6 ٱلصِّرَٰطَ / 1:7 صِرَٰطَ → ص ر ط B001 (road, especially the straight road) → the road; 1:5 نَعْبُدُ → ع ب د B005 (ṭarīq muʿabbad: a road levelled by much treading) → the road kept passable by those who walk it; 1:4 مَٰلِكِ → م ل ك B006 (the middle or main stretch of a road or valley) → the crown of the road; 1:6 ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ → ق و م B008 (straightness, evenness) → quality of the course; 1:7 أَنْعَمْتَ → ن ع م B001 (benefaction) → earlier travellers' well-being as proof of the road; 1:7 غَيْرِ → غ ي ر B005 (otherness, exclusion) → the boundary; 1:7 ٱلضَّآلِّينَ → ض ل ل B001 (swerving from the aim: «كل جائر عن القصد ضال») → the swerve; ض ل ل B003 (failing to find the place) → failure to arrive
- mechanism: The worlds raise marks that can be seen from afar. Guidance gently points along those marks, and worship is the treading that keeps the road beaten and level. The favoured who walked before show that it can be walked. Failure takes two forms: swerving (jawr ʿan al-qaṣd), or losing the destination altogether.
- containment: We praise the Lord of the worlds and ask to be guided on the straight path. The roots add that the worlds are the waymarks by which that path is read, and that the path stays open because those who serve walk it.
- perceptible: ʿālamīn in 1:2 and ṣirāṭ in 1:6 turn out to be one landscape. The praise at the opening is praise for a legible country, and the request at the centre asks for the reading of it. The plain muʿabbad sense also makes naʿbudu a treading that keeps the road usable, not only an inner act.
- staging: 16:15–16 «وأنهارا وسبلا لعلكم تهتدون وعلامات وبالنجم هم يهتدون» (paths, landmarks and stars as the means of hudā); 16:9 «وعلى الله قصد السبيل ومنها جائر» (the straight aim and the swerving road, in the very word of ض ل ل B001's definition); 6:71 «كالذي استهوته الشياطين في الأرض حيران له أصحاب يدعونه إلى الهدى ائتنا … وأمرنا لنسلم لرب العالمين» (the bewildered traveller, companions calling, the Lord of the worlds); 6:153 «وأن هذا صراطي مستقيما فاتبعوه ولا تتبعوا السبل»; 43:10 «وجعل لكم فيها سبلا لعلكم تهتدون».
- limits: 90:10 «وهديناه النجدين» and 76:3 «إنا هديناه السبيل إما شاكرا وإما كفورا» show that marks show both ways and do not compel. 37:23 «فاهدوهم إلى صراط الجحيم» shows that ه د ي and ص ر ط are not good in themselves; direction is what matters. 16:9 «ولو شاء لهداكم أجمعين» shows that legibility is not coercion.
- maturity: mature (definitional: ع ل م B002 «أثر يميز الشيء ويهدي إليه» → ه د ي; ض ل ل B001 «الضلال ضد الهدى»; ص ر ط B001 «الطريق المستقيم» → ق و م)

### The sea crossing
- members: 1:1 بِسْمِ → س م و B005 (name) → launch formula; 1:2 رَبِّ → ر ب ب B017 (rubbān, master of the sailors) → pilot; 1:5 نَعْبُدُ → ع ب د B005 (a ship coated with pitch or oil) → vessel made seaworthy; 1:2 ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ → ع ل م B005 (ʿaylam: sea, deep well; a root sense that the form ʿālamīn does not carry) → the deep; 1:1 ٱلرَّحِيمِ → ر ح م B001 (tender mercy) → the only shelter; 1:7 أَنْعَمْتَ → ن ع م B001 (benefaction) → the favour by which the ship runs; 1:6 ٱلصِّرَٰطَ → ص ر ط B002 (swallowing, vanishing in passage) → engulfment; 1:5 إِيَّاكَ … نَسْتَعِينُ → ع و ن B001 (aid) → the single appeal; 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ → د ي ن B001 (submissive devotion) → «مخلصين له الدين»; 1:7 ٱلضَّآلِّينَ → ض ل ل B002 (vanishing from sight) → what every other addressee becomes in the storm
- mechanism: A vessel is launched under the name and made fit. It is carried over the deep by favour and threatened with engulfment. In the storm every other name that is called on vanishes, and one Addressee remains: the rabb-pilot who is raḥīm.
- containment: "You alone we worship and of You alone we ask help" is a daily act of worship. It is also the cry the Quran records from people on a ship among the waves, when all else they call on vanishes (ḍalla). The Fātiḥa makes that cry the ordinary speech of dry land.
- perceptible: The exclusive iyyāka of 1:5 and the ḍāllīn of 1:7 are already paired in the Quran's sea scenes. Iyyāka is storm-sincerity made permanent, and one kind of ḍalāl is the vanishing of false addressees.
- staging: 11:41–44 «بسم الله مجراها ومرساها إن ربي لغفور رحيم … لا عاصم اليوم من أمر الله إلا من رحم … يا أرض ابلعي ماءك ويا سماء أقلعي» (the basmala, rabb, raḥīm, the swallowing earth and the sky in one scene); 17:66–67 «ربكم الذي يزجي لكم الفلك في البحر … إنه كان بكم رحيما وإذا مسكم الضر في البحر ضل من تدعون إلا إياه» (rabb, raḥīm, ḍalla, iyyāhu); 10:22 «دعوا الله مخلصين له الدين»; 31:31–32 «الفلك تجري في البحر بنعمت الله … دعوا الله مخلصين له الدين فلما نجاهم إلى البر فمنهم مقتصد».
- limits: 17:67 «فلما نجاكم إلى البر أعرضتم», 29:65 and 10:23 show that sincerity at sea lapses on land; only some keep the middle course (31:32 «فمنهم مقتصد»). ر ب ب B017, ع ب د B005 and ع ل م B005 are homonymous lexemes, heard in the root but not the words' sense here.
- maturity: mature

### The named term and the record
- members: 1:4 مَٰلِكِ → م ل ك B002 (ownership, right of disposal) → holder of claims; 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ → د ي ن B003 (debt, deferred payment) → the running claim; 1:1 بِسْمِ → س م و B005 (naming) → the named term (ajal musammā); 1:4 يَوْمِ → ي و م B001 (a bounded day) → date of maturity; 1:2 ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ → ع ل م B001 (knowing) → the record; 1:7 ٱلضَّآلِّينَ → ض ل ل B004 (forgetting) → the lapse the record guards against; 1:6 ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ → ق و م B010 (valuation, price) and ق و م B015 (coin of exact full weight) → the standard; 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ → د ي ن B002 (reckoning and recompense) → settlement; 1:7 أَنْعَمْتَ → ن ع م B001 (benefaction) → unearned credit; 1:7 ٱلضَّآلِّينَ → ض ل ل B003 (blood left unrequited) → the claim lost; 1:7 غَيْرِ → غ ي ر B002 (blood-money accepted in place of retaliation) → the substitution the Day refuses
- mechanism: An owner holds claims that run to a named term. A record is kept against human forgetting. On the day the claims are valued against an exact standard and settled. Benefaction advances what could not be earned, while some claims are lost and some substitutions are refused.
- containment: God is Owner and King of the Day of Judgment. The root's debt sense adds that this Day is also a maturity date: everything owed runs toward it under a Lord whose record, unlike ours, does not lapse.
- perceptible: Together, mālik, yawm and dīn form a creditor–term–settlement triad. Ḍāllīn gains the sense of "forgetters", those whose memory of the account has lapsed. Mustaqīm gains the sense of "full-weight", the measure the scales accept.
- staging: 2:282 «إذا تداينتم بدين إلى أجل مسمى فاكتبوه … أن تضل إحداهما فتذكر إحداهما الأخرى … ذلكم أقسط عند الله وأقوم للشهادة» (debt, the named term, ḍ-l-l as forgetting and aqwam in one ayah); 20:52 «علمها عند ربي في كتاب لا يضل ربي ولا ينسى»; 82:17–19 «وما أدراك ما يوم الدين … يوم لا تملك نفس لنفس شيئا والأمر يومئذ لله»; 83:1–6 (short measure, then «يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين»); 56:86 «فلولا إن كنتم غير مدينين»; 21:47 «ونضع الموازين القسط ليوم القيامة».
- limits: 6:12 «كتب على نفسه الرحمة ليجمعنكم إلى يوم القيامة» and 6:160 «فله عشر أمثالها» show that the ledger is not symmetric, because mercy exceeds it. 2:48 «ولا يؤخذ منها عدل» bounds غ ي ر B002: no substitute payment is taken. 6:164 shows that debts are not transferable.
- maturity: mature (definitional: د ي ن B002 «الدين الحساب ومنه مالك يوم الدين»)

### Soft and hard
- members: 1:1/1:3 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ → ر ح م B001 (riqqa: tenderness, softening of the heart) → source of softness; 1:7 أَنْعَمْتَ → ن ع م B002 (becoming soft; easy life) → the pliancy of the favoured; 1:6 ٱهْدِنَا → ه د ي B001 (guidance bi-luṭf, gently) → a guidance that acts on what is pliant; 1:6 ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ → ق و م B008 (straightness) → a shape that can still be re-formed; 1:7 ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ → غ ض ب B004 (hard rock) → hardness; غ ض ب B005 (thick-skinned: «غليظ الجلد») → insensibility; غ ض ب B008 (hide folded like a shield) → armour against address; غ ض ب B006 (swollen upper eyelid) → blocked sight; 1:6 ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ → ق و م B021 (eye intact but sight gone) → blindness with the organ whole; 1:7 ٱلضَّآلِّينَ → ض ل ل B001 → the resulting swerve
- mechanism: Mercy softens. Softness is the condition in which gentle guidance can bend a life straight. Wrath's root supplies the counter-texture: rock, thick hide and a shield-hide on which address does not register. It also supplies sight blocked by swelling, or lost while the eye stays whole.
- containment: Those on whom anger falls are distinguished from the favoured. The roots give the two sides a texture: the favoured are softened by the tender Merciful, while the others are hardened like rock and hide.
- perceptible: 1:1/1:3 and 1:7 are antonyms of texture, riqqa against ghilaẓ. The gentle mode of hudā in 1:6 explains why hardness, not ignorance alone, blocks it.
- staging: 3:159 «فبما رحمة من الله لنت لهم ولو كنت فظا غليظ القلب لانفضوا من حولك» (mercy yields softness; its opposite is ghalīẓ, the very word of غ ض ب B005's definition); 39:23 «تقشعر منه جلود الذين يخشون ربهم ثم تلين جلودهم وقلوبهم إلى ذكر الله ذلك هدى الله … ومن يضلل الله فما له من هاد» (softening skin is hudā); 2:74 «فهي كالحجارة أو أشد قسوة»; 57:16 «فطال عليهم الأمد فقست قلوبهم»; 22:46 «فإنها لا تعمى الأبصار ولكن تعمى القلوب».
- limits: 48:29 «أشداء على الكفار رحماء بينهم» and 9:73 «واغلظ عليهم» show that hardness is not blamed in itself. 20:81 «ومن يحلل عليه غضبي فقد هوى» shows that ghaḍab is God's real act and not only the texture of its objects. The rock and hide images belong to the root; the word says that anger falls upon them.
- maturity: mature

### The unrivalled name
- members: 1:1 بِسْمِ → س م و B001 (elevation) → height of the name; س م و B005 (name; equality of name or rank) → designation; س م و B007 (rivalry: «فلان لا يسامى») → no rival; 1:1 ٱللَّهِ → ء ل ه B002 (the proper name, in vocative and oath) → the name itself; 1:2 لِلَّهِ → ء ل ه B001 (worship, the worshipped) → object; 1:5 نَعْبُدُ → ع ب د B003 (devoted service) → devotion made exclusive by the fronted iyyāka; 1:7 غَيْرِ → غ ي ر B005 (otherness, exclusion) → boundary; غ ي ر B003 (replacing one thing with another) → the refused substitution; 1:7 ٱلضَّآلِّينَ → ض ل ل B002 (vanishing) → the fate of rival names
- mechanism: The name is raised, and its root itself says that none can rival it or be its namesake. Exclusive worship answers that uniqueness. The boundary of "other" refuses any substitute, and rival names vanish when tested.
- containment: The surah begins in God's name. The root adds that this name is lifted beyond rivalry, so that the exclusive iyyāka of 1:5 and the ghayr of 1:7 unfold what bismi already claims.
- perceptible: Bismi is not a neutral label. The root of ism contains height and "no namesake", so the basmala already holds the exclusivity that 1:5 states outright.
- staging: 19:65 «رب السماوات والأرض وما بينهما فاعبده واصطبر لعبادته هل تعلم له سميا» (rabb, samāwāt, ʿibāda, ʿilm and samiyy together); 87:1 «سبح اسم ربك الأعلى»; 7:180 «ولله الأسماء الحسنى فادعوه بها»; 17:67 «ضل من تدعون إلا إياه»; 6:24 «وضل عنهم ما كانوا يفترون».
- limits: 53:23 and 12:40 «إلا أسماء سميتموها» show that names can be empty, so the name's height is not in the sound. 2:31 shows that naming is also a human gift; the claim concerns this Named One, not naming as such.
- maturity: mature (definitional: ء ل ه B002 «اسم الله في القسم والنداء» → س م و)

### The three standings
- members: 1:6 ٱهْدِنَا → ه د ي B008 (a swaying walk, leaning on two supporters) → the unsteady walker; ه د ي B009 (a dull, weak man) → incapacity; 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ → ع و ن B001 (aid, backing) → the support leaned on; 1:5 نَعْبُدُ → ع ب د B007 (strength, durability) → endurance; 1:4 مَٰلِكِ → م ل ك B001 (holding together; restraining oneself from falling) → cohesion; 1:6 ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ → ق و م B002 (standing upright; the qawma of prayer) → the posture of service; 1:6 ٱهْدِنَا → ه د ي B010 (calm, measured progress, unlike a routed flight) → composure; 1:6 ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ → ق و م B008 (straightness) → upright walking; ق و م B016 (water freezing, a mount halting exhausted) → arrest; 1:5 نَعْبُدُ → ع ب د B011 (stranded when one's mount gives out) → breakdown; 1:6 ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ → ق و م B013 (the Rising, when creation stands for judgment) → the final standing; 1:2 رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ → ر ب ب B001 + ع ل م B003 → the One before whom all stand
- mechanism: A weak walker, supported between helpers, is raised to stand in service and to walk upright with composure, neither halting nor stranded. That standing rehearses the last one, when people rise before the Lord of the worlds.
- containment: We ask to be guided on the straight path. The root adds that istiqāma is an embodied standing, begun in prayer, sustained with aid on the road, and completed when people stand before the Lord of the worlds.
- perceptible: Nastaʿīn and mustaqīm become support and posture. The Day of 1:4 and the path of 1:6 are joined by one verb, qāma.
- staging: 67:22 «أفمن يمشي مكبا على وجهه أهدى أمن يمشي سويا على صراط مستقيم»; 83:4–6 «ليوم عظيم يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين»; 41:30 «قالوا ربنا الله ثم استقاموا»; 11:112 «فاستقم كما أمرت».
- limits: 2:20 «وإذا أظلم عليهم قاموا» shows qāma as stock-still arrest (ق و م B016), so standing without light is paralysis. 3:191 «قياما وقعودا وعلى جنوبهم» shows that service is not tied to posture.
- maturity: mature (definitional: ق و م B013 «يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين», mufradat)

### The flock, its owner and the stray
- members: 1:2 رَبِّ → ر ب ب B001 (owner-master) → owner; ر ب ب B002 (tending to maturity) → the tending; ر ب ب B014 (herd of wild cattle) → herd as unit (homonym); 1:6 ٱهْدِنَا → ه د ي B003 (hawādī: the lead animals, the neck, the foremost) → the lead going ahead; 1:4 مَٰلِكِ → م ل ك B008 (lead animal; queen bee; «ملك الدابة قوائمها وهاديها») → the directing part; 1:7 أَنْعَمْتَ → ن ع م B005 (anʿām, grazing livestock) → the herd; 1:5 نَعْبُدُ → ع ب د B005 (camel tarred and made docile) → the tended beast; 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ → ع و ن B006 (herd of wild asses) → the untended counter-herd; 1:7 ٱلضَّآلِّينَ → ض ل ل B005 (stray camel, «لا يعرف ربها») → lost to its owner
- mechanism: An owner tends a herd. A lead goes before it, and the tended beast follows. The loss is the stray whose owner is no longer known, which is loss of the rabb, not only of the road.
- containment: The surah opens with the Lord of the worlds and closes with those who go astray. The root adds that a ḍālla is precisely the beast whose rabb is unknown, so the surah's end names the loss of its beginning.
- perceptible: 1:2 and 1:7 are bookends: rabb and the stray that has lost its rabb. Ihdinā can be heard as "let the lead go before us".
- staging: 16:5–9 (the anʿām going out to pasture and coming home, then «وعلى الله قصد السبيل ومنها جائر ولو شاء لهداكم أجمعين»); 13:7 «ولكل قوم هاد»; 6:71 (the bewildered one, called back to hudā «لرب العالمين»); 25:44 «إن هم إلا كالأنعام بل هم أضل سبيلا».
- limits: 25:44 and 7:179 use the herd image for failure. The Fātiḥa's speakers are not a herd: they speak, choose and ask. The system names care and direction, not reduction.
- maturity: mature (definitional: م ل ك B008 → ه د ي)

### Womb and rearing
- members: 1:1/1:3 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ → ر ح م B003 (womb) → enclosure; ر ح م B002 (kinship) → the bond; ر ح م B004 (womb disorder; afterbirth not expelled) → complication; 1:2 رَبِّ → ر ب ب B009 (freshness of beginning; newly delivered ewe) → the newborn stage; ر ب ب B005 (fosterage, rabīb) → care beyond begetting; ر ب ب B002 (rearing stage by stage to completion) → maturation; 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ → ع و ن B005 (bodily balance; strength catching up with age) → maturity; 1:6 ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ → ق و م B011 (stature, upright build) → the standing adult; 1:2 ٱلْحَمْدُ → ح م د B004 (ḥamādā: the utmost praiseworthy limit) → completion
- mechanism: Enclosure forms, kinship binds, fosterage tends and rearing carries a being stage by stage until strength matches age and it stands at full stature. The praiseworthy end is its completion.
- containment: God is the Merciful Lord of the worlds. The roots add that His mercy has the shape of the womb, and His lordship has the shape of patient rearing that brings the dependent to full stature.
- perceptible: The pairing of raḥmān-raḥīm with rabb in 1:1–3 is one process, enclosure followed by upbringing, not two unrelated attributes.
- staging: 39:6 «يخلقكم في بطون أمهاتكم خلقا من بعد خلق في ظلمات ثلاث ذلكم الله ربكم له الملك»; 3:6 «يصوركم في الأرحام كيف يشاء»; 22:5 and 40:67 «ثم لتبلغوا أشدكم»; 4:1 «واتقوا الله الذي تساءلون به والأرحام»; hadith qudsi (Abū Dāwūd, Tirmidhī) «أنا الرحمن خلقت الرحم وشققت لها اسما من اسمي» (ism, raḥmān and raḥim together; hadith, not Quran).
- limits: 19:88–92 «وما ينبغي للرحمن أن يتخذ ولدا» shows that the womb image must never become filiation. 60:3 «لن تنفعكم أرحامكم … يوم القيامة» and 80:34–37 show that kinship dissolves on the Day of 1:4 while the Raḥmān's mercy does not.
- maturity: mature

### Rain as mercy
- members: 1:7 أَنْعَمْتَ → ن ع م B009 (naʿāmā: soft, moist south wind) → driver; 1:2 رَبِّ → ر ب ب B008 (layered, hanging cloud) → carrier; 1:1 بِسْمِ → س م و B004 (sky; cloud and rain are called samāʾ) → the giver above; ر ب ب B013 (gathered, abundant water) → store; 1:2 ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ → ع ل م B005 (sea, deep well) → reservoir; 1:4 مَٰلِكِ → م ل ك B007 (water as what keeps affairs and life going) → sustenance; 1:7 غَيْرِ → غ ي ر B001 (provisioning; rain watering people and land) → the effect; ر ب ب B012 (a green plant that does not wither in summer) → receiver; 1:1 بِسْمِ ~alt و س م B003 (first rain, which marks the earth with plants) → the mark (flagged analogy)
- mechanism: A soft wind drives layered cloud from the sky. Gathered water falls, sustains and repairs, and the earth shows the rain's mark in lasting green.
- containment: God is the Merciful. The Quran itself calls rain His raḥma, and the window's roots, heard at their edges, carry the whole cycle of wind, cloud, sky, water and green that renders that mercy visible.
- perceptible: Raḥma in 1:1/1:3 has a Quranic body, rain, and the "marks of mercy" are legible on the land. This joins the waymark system: the worlds are signs partly because mercy writes on them.
- staging: 7:57 «يرسل الرياح بشرا بين يدي رحمته حتى إذا أقلت سحابا ثقالا سقناه لبلد ميت»; 30:50 «فانظر إلى آثار رحمت الله كيف يحيي الأرض بعد موتها»; 42:28 «ينزل الغيث من بعد ما قنطوا وينشر رحمته وهو الولي الحميد» (rain, raḥma and ḥamīd together); 25:48.
- limits: 46:24 «هذا عارض ممطرنا بل هو ما استعجلتم به ريح فيها عذاب أليم» and 7:84 show that rain can be punishment. ر ح م has no rain branch; the Quran supplies it. Every member here is a homonymous lexeme, audible in the root but never the word's sense in al-Fātiḥa.
- maturity: mature (heard only as root resonance)

### Credit returned
- members: 1:2 ٱلْحَمْدُ → ح م د B005 (yataḥammadu: reproaching someone with one's favour, crediting oneself) → the rival claim; ح م د B001 (praise, including thanks for good done) → praise assigned; ح م د B006 («أحمد إليك الله»; «أشكر إليك أياديه ونعمه») → praise shared with the hearer; 1:7 أَنْعَمْتَ → ن ع م B001 (benefaction, whose agent is "You") → the true source; 1:6 ٱهْدِنَا → ه د ي B001 → guidance as the favour; 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ → ع و ن B001 → even the capacity, sought and not owned
- mechanism: The root of ḥamd contains its own corruption: claiming praise for one's favours. Assigning all praise to God cancels human claims to credit. Benefaction, guidance and even the capacity to serve are then located in the second person, "You".
- containment: All praise belongs to God. The root adds that praise can be stolen by self-crediting, so al-ḥamdu li-llāh returns every credit to its owner, and the anʿamta of 1:7 names the benefactor.
- perceptible: The definite article in al-ḥamd acts as a confiscation: no praise is left over for the speakers, which is why 1:5 asks for aid instead of claiming strength.
- staging: 49:17 «يمنون عليك أن أسلموا قل لا تمنوا علي إسلامكم بل الله يمن عليكم أن هداكم للإيمان»; 7:43 «الحمد لله الذي هدانا لهذا وما كنا لنهتدي لولا أن هدانا الله»; 3:188 «ويحبون أن يحمدوا بما لم يفعلوا»; 16:53 «وما بكم من نعمة فمن الله»; 28:78 «إنما أوتيته على علم عندي» (the claim, then its collapse).
- limits: 2:264 forbids human manna while 49:17 affirms God's, so the logic is asymmetric and not a general ban on acknowledgement. 14:7 «لئن شكرتم لأزيدنكم» shows that the human answer counts.
- maturity: mature (definitional: ح م د B006 → ن ع م)

### Conveyed to the sanctuary
- members: 1:1 بِسْمِ → س م و B005 (the name pronounced over the offering) → consecration; 1:7 أَنْعَمْتَ → ن ع م B005 (anʿām) → the offering's substance; 1:6 ٱهْدِنَا → ه د ي B005 (hady: animals and goods sent to the sanctuary) → conveyance to the House; ه د ي B006 (conducting the bride to her husband) → conveyance into belonging; ه د ي B007 (a protected refugee) → conveyance under protection; ه د ي B004 (a gift sent to one loved) → the conveyed as gift; 1:1 ٱللَّهِ → ء ل ه B001 (worship) → the aim of the rite; 1:6 ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ → ق و م B009 (mainstay, qiwām) → the House as the people's qiyām
- mechanism: What is named over is led along a road to a destination where it is received. The pattern is that of the hady to the House, the bride to her home and the refugee to safety. Ihdinā asks to be the thing conveyed.
- containment: We ask to be guided. The root adds that hady is also what is led to the Sanctuary under God's name, so the petitioners ask to be conducted, as offering, bride and protected guest are conducted, to where they belong.
- perceptible: The surah's opening name and its request for hudā are bound by the rite. The name is spoken over what is then led to God, so bismi and ihdinā are two moments of one conveyance.
- staging: 22:34–37 «ليذكروا اسم الله على ما رزقهم من بهيمة الأنعام فإلهكم إله واحد … فاذكروا اسم الله عليها صواف … لن ينال الله لحومها ولا دماؤها ولكن يناله التقوى منكم … لتكبروا الله على ما هداكم»; 5:97 «جعل الله الكعبة البيت الحرام قياما للناس والشهر الحرام والهدي والقلائد»; 9:6 «استجارك فأجره حتى يسمع كلام الله ثم أبلغه مأمنه».
- limits: 22:37 shows that what reaches God is taqwā, not the thing conveyed. 48:25 «والهدي معكوفا أن يبلغ محله» and 2:196 show that conveyance can be blocked short of its place.
- maturity: mature (definitional: ه د ي B005 «ما يهدى إلى الحرم من النعم» → ن ع م)

### The gathered many and the scattering
- members: 1:5 نَعْبُدُ … نَسْتَعِينُ (the speaking "we") → the body that speaks; 1:6 ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ → ق و م B001 (a people) → the community; 1:2 رَبِّ → ر ب ب B004 (ribbiyyūn: great throngs; tribes joined in one) → the many; ر ب ب B011 (rabāba: binding pact and pledge) → the bond; 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ → ع و ن B001 (mutual aid, taʿāwun) → cohesion; 1:5 نَعْبُدُ → ع ب د B010 (ʿabādīd: groups scattered in every direction) → dispersal; 1:7 أَنْعَمْتَ → ن ع م B008 (shālat naʿāmatuhum: a people scattering and losing its strength) → breakup; 1:7 ٱلضَّآلِّينَ → ض ل ل B001 → divergence
- mechanism: A many becomes one through a bond, mutual aid and a single road. Failure is not only straying but scattering into many ways, so that the collective itself dissolves.
- containment: We worship and ask help in the plural. The roots add that this "we" is a gathered many held by pact and mutual aid, and that the alternative to one straight road is dispersal as well as error.
- perceptible: The plural of 1:5–6 does work: the road is one because the people are one. Ḍalāl has a social face, fragmentation.
- staging: 3:103 «واعتصموا بحبل الله جميعا ولا تفرقوا واذكروا نعمت الله عليكم … فأصبحتم بنعمته إخوانا»; 6:153 «ولا تتبعوا السبل فتفرق بكم عن سبيله»; 6:159 «إن الذين فرقوا دينهم وكانوا شيعا»; 3:159 «لانفضوا من حولك»; 3:146 «وكأين من نبي قاتل معه ربيون كثير فما وهنوا»; 5:2 «وتعاونوا على البر والتقوى».
- limits: 5:2 «ولا تعاونوا على الإثم والعدوان» shows that cohesion is not good in itself. 11:118–119 shows that difference persists by divine will, so unity is asked for, not assumed.
- maturity: mature

### Vanishing in transit
- members: 1:6 ٱلصِّرَٰطَ → ص ر ط B002 (disappearing in passage; swallowing; the road named because «الذاهب فيه يغيب») → directed disappearance; 1:7 ٱلضَّآلِّينَ → ض ل ل B002 (vanishing, merging into earth; burial) → undirected disappearance; ض ل ل B003 (losing a thing, losing one's way) → losing track; ض ل ل B004 (forgetting) → loss from memory; 1:2 ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ → ع ل م B001 (knowing) → the knowledge that retains; 1:1 بِسْمِ → س م و B005 (name) → what makes the lost retrievable
- mechanism: Both the road and the straying involve passing out of sight. On the road the traveller vanishes forward into arrival. In ḍalāl the thing vanishes into ground or oblivion without direction. Knowledge and name keep what disappears recoverable.
- containment: The straight path is distinguished from straying. The roots add that both involve passing out of view: one completes a directed transit, the other sinks away unrecovered.
- perceptible: The last word of the surah answers the ṣirāṭ in a concealed way. Ṣirāṭ and ḍalāl share the root idea of ghayba, disappearance, and differ in whether it leads anywhere.
- staging: 32:10–11 «وقالوا أإذا ضللنا في الأرض أإنا لفي خلق جديد … قل يتوفاكم ملك الموت الذي وكل بكم ثم إلى ربكم ترجعون»; 20:52 «لا يضل ربي ولا ينسى»; 17:67 «ضل من تدعون إلا إياه»; 36:78–79 «قل يحييها الذي أنشأها أول مرة وهو بكل خلق عليم».
- limits: The swallowing etymology of ṣirāṭ is Ibn Fāris's; many authorities treat ṣirāṭ as a loanword (strata), so the ص ر ط member is a root claim, not settled. 6:24 shows that some vanishing (of false gods) is good.
- maturity: mature

### Days of God
- members: 1:4 يَوْمِ → ي و م B004 (ayyām Allāh: days remembered for His favours and punishments) → the remembered days; ي و م B003 (a momentous day or event) → the event; 1:7 أَنْعَمْتَ → ن ع م B001 → the days of favour; ن ع م B010 (increasing, carrying further) → the promised increase; 1:7 ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ → غ ض ب B001 (anger turning to retribution) → the days of retribution; 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ → د ي ن B002 → the final day that gathers them
- mechanism: History already contains God's days, some of favour and some of wrath. The Day of Judgment is their last, gathered instance, and 1:7's two outcomes name the kinds of day one may belong to.
- containment: God owns the Day of Judgment. The root adds that yawm is also the word for His past days of favour and anger, so 1:4 and 1:7 are one sequence.
- perceptible: The anʿamta / maghḍūb split of 1:7 is prefigured in 1:4's single word yawm. The one Day at the centre sums up the many days the surah's end sorts.
- staging: 14:5–7 «وذكرهم بأيام الله … اذكروا نعمة الله عليكم إذ أنجاكم من آل فرعون … لئن شكرتم لأزيدنكم ولئن كفرتم إن عذابي لشديد»; 45:14 «للذين لا يرجون أيام الله».
- limits: 1:4's yawm is singular and final. The historical days foreshadow it and do not replace it.
- maturity: mature (definitional: ي و م B004 «أيامه: نعمه» → ن ع م)

### Single-branch surprises
- 1:4 مَٰلِكِ → م ل ك B005 (milāk al-amr: that on which the whole affair rests, as the heart does for the body). Heard with 82:19 «والأمر يومئذ لله», the Owner of the Day is also the mainstay on which that Day's entire amr rests.
- 1:4 يَوْمِ → ي و م B002 (a span of any length). Yawm al-dīn is not twenty-four hours; compare 32:5 «في يوم كان مقداره ألف سنة» and 70:4.
- 1:5 نَعْبُدُ → ع ب د B008 (ʿabad: indignant zeal, wounded pride; tahdhīb: «عبد عليه أي غضب»). Some early philologists read 43:81 «فأنا أول العابدين», beside الرحمن, as "the first to disdain". Exclusive service carries a jealous refusal of rivals and stands as the servant's counterpart to the ghaḍab of 1:7. The minority reading is a limit; the majority reads "worshippers".
- 1:7 غَيْرِ → غ ي ر B004 (ghayra: protective jealousy over one's own). This is an identity branch, not the particle's sense. It echoes the hadith «لا أحد أغير من الله» (Bukhārī): the boundary drawn by ghayri guards those who are His.
- 1:2 ٱلْحَمْدُ → ح م د B002 (finding someone praiseworthy after trial). Al-ḥamd is a verdict reached through experience, not an opening compliment. This is kept for 1:2 alone because it overlaps with the ledger system.
- 1:7 ٱلضَّآلِّينَ → ض ل ل B005 (stray camel «لا يعرف ربها»). The surah's last word defines itself by the loss of 1:2's rabb. It is recorded separately because it is the most precise bookend in the window.

===== AYAT =====

- 1:1: Bismi Rakipsiz Ad sisteminin kapısıdır: س م و kökü aynı anda yüceliği (B001), adı (B005) ve "kimsenin boy ölçüşemediği" olmayı (B007) taşır. Aynı söz İşaretler ve Yol sisteminde uzaktan beliren yüksek işareti (B002), Deniz Geçişi'nde Nûh'un gemiye binerken söylediği "bismillâh"ı (11:41), Kurbanlık sisteminde kurbanın üzerine anılan adı, Adı Konmuş Vade sisteminde de "ecel-i müsemmâ"yı duyurur. er-Rahmân er-Rahîm ise Yumuşak ve Katı sisteminde rikkatin (ر ح م B001), Rahim ve Terbiye sisteminde döl yatağının (B003) ve akrabalığın (B002) kaynağıdır; Kur'an'ın rahmet dediği yağmurun kökte duyulan halkasını da açar.
- 1:2: el-Hamd, Övgünün İadesi sisteminde kökün içindeki bozulmayı (ح م د B005, iyiliği başa kakıp kendine pay çıkarma) geri alır: bütün övgü sahibine döner. Rabb aynı anda sürünün sahibi ve güdücüsü (Sürü sistemi), aşama aşama yetiştiren ve üvey çocuğu bile büyüten terbiyeci (Rahim ve Terbiye), kalabalıkları ahitle bağlayan (Toplanmış Çokluk) ve gemicilerin başıdır (Deniz Geçişi). el-Âlemîn ise İşaretler ve Yol sisteminde yaratıcısını gösteren ve uzaktan seçilen işaretler alanını (ع ل م B003, B002) verir; Üç Ayakta Duruş sisteminde de 83:6'daki "Rabbü'l-âlemîn" önünde kıyamın muhatabını hazırlar.
- 1:3: Rahmet ikilisinin din gününden hemen önce yinelenmesi, Adı Konmuş Vade sisteminde hesabı rahmetin çerçevelediğini gösterir (6:12: "kataba alâ nefsihi'r-rahme"). Aynı tekrar, Yumuşak ve Katı sisteminde yumuşaklığın kaynağını 1:7'deki katılığın karşısına yerleştirir. Rahim ve Terbiye sisteminde de kıyamet günü akrabalık bağları çözülürken (60:3) Rahmân'ın rahmetinin baki kaldığını sezdirir.
- 1:4: Mâlik, Adı Konmuş Vade sisteminde alacakların sahibidir (م ل ك B002). Sürü sisteminde önden giden öncü hayvanı (B008), İşaretler ve Yol sisteminde yolun orta ve ana kesimini (B006), Üç Ayakta Duruş sisteminde düşmekten alıkoyan tutarlılığı (B001) sezdirir; "milâk" anlamıyla da o günün bütün işinin (82:19: "ve'l-emru yevmeizin lillâh") dayandığı temeldir. Yevm hem vadenin dolduğu gün hem de nimet ve azap günlerinin (eyyâmullâh) toplandığı son gündür (Allah'ın Günleri sistemi). ed-Dîn ise borç (B003), hesap (B002) ve denizde "muhlisîne lehü'd-dîn" diye yakaranların içtenliği olarak Deniz Geçişi'ne de bağlanır.
- 1:5: İyyâke'nin öne alınması Rakipsiz Ad sisteminin sonucunu açıkça söyler; Deniz Geçişi sisteminde ise dalgalar arasında yalnız O'na yönelen yakarışı (17:67) karanın gündelik diline çevirir. Na'budu kökü dövülüp düzlenmiş yolu (ع ب د B005: İşaretler ve Yol), katranlanıp uysallaştırılmış deveyi (Sürü), kalafatlanmış gemiyi (Deniz Geçişi) ve sağlamlığı (B007: Üç Ayakta Duruş) duyurur. Nesteîn ise Üç Ayakta Duruş sisteminde sendeleyen yolcunun dayandığı desteği, Toplanmış Çokluk sisteminde teâvünü, Övgünün İadesi sisteminde kişinin kendine mal etmediği gücü verir.
- 1:6: İhdinâ, İşaretler ve Yol sisteminde işaretler boyunca incelikle yol göstermeyi (ه د ي B001, B002) dile getirir. Sürü sisteminde "öncüyü önümüze koy" (B003), Üç Ayakta Duruş sisteminde "sendeleyeni iki yanından tutup sakin adımla yürüt" (B008, B010) anlamını duyurur. Kurbanlık sisteminde ise Beyt'e götürülen hedy, eşine götürülen gelin ve güvenli yerine ulaştırılan sığınmacı gibi "bizi ait olduğumuz yere ulaştır" (B005–B007) diye işitilir. es-Sırât, Kayboluşta Geçiş sisteminde yolcusunu ileriye doğru gözden yitiren yoldur (ص ر ط B002). el-Müstakîm ise namazdaki kıyamdan kıyamet gününe uzanan ayakta duruşu (ق و م B002, B013), Adı Konmuş Vade sisteminde tam ayarlı ölçüyü (B010, B015), Toplanmış Çokluk sisteminde tek bir kavmi (B001) taşır.
- 1:7: En'amte, Övgünün İadesi sisteminde iyiliğin failini "Sen" diye adlandırır. Yumuşak ve Katı sisteminde nimet verilenlerin yumuşaklığını (ن ع م B002), Allah'ın Günleri sisteminde nimet günlerini ve şükre vaat edilen artışı (B010), Sürü sisteminde en'âmı, Toplanmış Çokluk sisteminde ise karşıt tabloyu, yani devekuşu gibi dağılıp gücünü yitiren topluluğu (B008) duyurur. Gayri, Rakipsiz Ad sisteminde başkasını dışarıda bırakan ve yerine başkası konmasını reddeden sınırdır (غ ي ر B005, B003); Adı Konmuş Vade sisteminde o gün kabul edilmeyen bedeli (B002) de hatırlatır. el-Mağdûb, kaya, kalın deri ve kalkanlaşmış post imgeleriyle (غ ض ب B004, B005, B008) rahmetin yumuşaklığına karşı duran katılığı taşır. ed-Dâllîn ise aynı anda amacından sapan (ض ل ل B001), sahibi bilinmeyen başıboş deve (B005, 1:2'deki Rabb'in yitirilişi), unutan (B004, 2:282) ve denizde çağrılan sahte ilahlar gibi toprağa karışıp gözden kaybolandır (B002; Deniz Geçişi ve Kayboluşta Geçiş sistemleri).
