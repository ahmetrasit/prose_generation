# The brief (v11 seed pass): latent image systems of a surah window (English record, Turkish notes)

Everything above this brief is evidence for one surah (or one window of a long surah), prepared by scripts and not
yet judged: the window's text; (a) every attested branch of every root of every word in it, from the classical
Arabic dictionaries; (b) where they exist, earlier readers' hypotheses (HFT) with their word-and-branch traces;
(c) passages elsewhere in the Quran whose surface carries several of these branch images in other words; (d)
branches whose own dictionary phrase uses another root of the window.

## What this pass is for

A reader who knows no Arabic hears only the primary sense of each word. A careful Arabic listener also hears the
other attested senses of the same roots, and when several of them, from different words and ayat, fit together
into one working picture, the surah says something through that picture that no single word says. Example (surah 103): the branches of ع ص ر — press, rain-cloud, husk, choking throat,
withholding, refuge, yield — are one idea, retention under compression; that makes خسر audible as leakage from
what is pressed, and the surah's close on صبر (holding fast) structurally necessary rather than a pious ending.
Your task is to find such systems in this window: **all of them the evidence supports**, including ones nobody has
proposed.

This applies al-Khūlī (a word through all its Quranic uses and its root's attested senses), al-Biqāʿī's *naẓm* (an
ayah through its neighbours and the surah's purpose) and the principle that the Quran explains the Quran. The
familiar interpretation is the anchor, not the measure; a supported surprise is the goal. Rigour comes from exact
evidence, not from caution.

## How to work

1. Read the whole branch table before judging anything. Coalition before judgement: a branch that looks weak alone
   can be the missing member of a system; once a system forms, re-read the weak members against it. Do not prune
   early.
2. A system is **complementary roles in motion** (source → carrier → gate → receiver → loss …), not a list of words
   from one field. Name its mechanism: what supplies, leads, obstructs, carries or loses what.
3. Test each system against the Quran: where does the Quran tell this scene **openly**, in plain words (section c
   lists candidates, most of them noise; your own knowledge of the Quran is welcome — quotations are checked)? Such
   a passage makes the latent picture checkable. Also name **limits**: ayat that bound or complicate the system.
4. Keep the primary reading intact: every system must be stateable in a sentence that contains the plain meaning of
   the ayat and adds to it (never "not X but Y").
5. Only identity branches are the word's own root; `~alt` branches belong to a cited alternative analysis, `~echo`
   roots only to sound — use them only as flagged analogies.
6. HFT hypotheses are proposals by earlier readers: adopt, correct, merge or reject them; do not copy them.
