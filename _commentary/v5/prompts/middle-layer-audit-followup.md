# Legacy V2 same-author middle-layer follow-up

This file is retained only for already prepared V2 prompt/ledger runs. New V3
runs use `middle-layer-review.md` with a fresh independent agent.

Before finalizing, perform a fresh audit of the same middle-layer task. Re-read
the complete filled prompt, the complete editorial source, and both current
outputs. Do not treat your earlier report, paragraph citations, validator
success, or the ledger's own assertions as proof of semantic completeness.

Re-inventory every source paragraph at atomic granularity. Do not cap a source
paragraph at one unit: separate every independent carrier, trigger, mechanism,
changed reading, concrete detail, qualification, boundary, and live
alternative. Recheck every overlap classification and synthesis cluster, then
locate one exact substantive prose landing for every unit. Confirm word by word
that negation, possibility, attribution, conditionality, agency, referent, and
scope remain unchanged.

Next, read the prose without relying on the ledger. Confirm that each paragraph
gives enough carrier, trigger, mechanism, consequence, distinguishing detail,
and boundary clues for a reader to decide whether to open its cited editorial
paragraphs. Confirm that synthesis is not source-paragraph mirroring, that
citations have not produced fragments or claim-ledger rhythm, that long prose
has useful navigation headings, that every Quran reference is explicit rather
than an interval, and that the Turkish is continuous and grammatically sound.

Recompute the structural metrics yourself rather than trusting the ledger. If
the source has at least ten prose paragraphs and `retained_word_ratio` exceeds
`0.80`, `output_to_source_paragraph_ratio` exceeds `0.75`, or fewer than `0.40`
of output prose paragraphs are multi-source, perform a mandatory adversarial
compression challenge even when both validators pass and every audit array is
empty. These values are diagnostic triggers only: they are not targets, hard
limits, or evidence that the prose must become shorter.

During that challenge, inspect every repeated setup, recap, defensive
qualification, and sequence of single-source paragraphs that develops the same
carrier, question, image, mechanism, or consequence. Privately write the best
continuous merged alternative instead of merely asserting that a merge would
be unsafe. Compare the alternative unit by unit with the current prose. Adopt
it only if every distinct unit retains an explicit substantive landing, every
mechanism, concrete detail, modality, qualification, boundary, and live
alternative remains visible, citations remain locally meaningful, and the
Turkish is easier to follow rather than merely shorter. Otherwise keep the
passages separate and ensure that each affected standalone cluster records the
specific semantic reason the attempted merge failed. Never delete,
generalize, make implicit, or overload a finding to cross a metric boundary;
an irreducible result may legitimately remain beyond every warning signal.

Repair only the same reader-prose and claim-ledger outputs. Do not modify the
source, prompt, validators, tests, or any other ayah. Rerun both validator
commands from the filled prompt until they report `ok`. Do not stop after
describing a defect: repair it, synchronize exact anchors and metrics, and then
report the final validator results, counts, structural metrics, and any concern
that remains unresolved.
