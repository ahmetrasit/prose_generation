## v12_reader_walks reader_a: activated_readings_md

1. **Purification maps readiness from front to support.** `و ج ه B001` supplies the bodily front, `ر ء س B004` the beginning of a matter, `ر ج ل B019` resolute standing, and `ك ع ب B001` raised joint-bone. Their ordered washing/wiping precedes standing for prayer. I infer that cleaning bodily orientation, agency, command-center, and support enacts readiness to enter the act.
2. **Earth substitutes by shared surface logic.** `ص ع د B004` is earth surface/dust and `م س ح B001` passes the hand over a surface and removes a trace. Water's absence does not cancel purification but changes its medium. I infer that ordered contact, not liquid alone, carries the cleansing sign; the ayah reads as preserving ritual structure under material scarcity.
3. **Completion is visible in small sufficiency.** `ش ك ر B002` means being sustained by little and showing its effect, beside `ت م م B001` full completion. The packet supplies reduced means during illness or travel; I infer that gratitude manifests when limited material still produces complete ritual response. The final purpose is therefore practical responsiveness, not only verbal thanks.

## v12_reader_walks_wide reader_a: activated_readings_md

1. **Purification preserves access by changing medium.** Fixed **ط ه ر / B003** (“ritual washing with water”) is expanded by **B004** (“purifying water or medium”): the ayah substitutes clean earth when water is unavailable yet retains wiping of face and hands. Fixed **ح ر ج / B001** (“dense gathering and entanglement”) and **B002** (“constriction and tightness”) state what the design refuses. I infer that medium-substitution prevents circumstances from tangling the route to prayer. Purity therefore reads as a resilient access mechanism rather than dependence on one material.
