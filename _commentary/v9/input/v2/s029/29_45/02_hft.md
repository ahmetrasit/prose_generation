## baseline_text_into_practice [baseline_model, strong]

- before: Recite scripture and perform prayer.
- after: Follow the revealed written instruction into a continuously maintained practice that carries the text into conduct.
- mechanism: Received, ordered discourse is followed into a maintained embodied practice: recitation supplies the lead and iqama keeps its ritual enactment standing.
- trace:

  - 29:45 w7 **وَأَقِمِ** (ق و م B004: sürekli gözetip yönetme / رعاية وحفظ وولاية) — Care, maintenance, and oversight make iqama an ongoing keeping-up of prayer.

## baseline_prayer_as_brake [baseline_model, strong]

- before: Prayer accompanies a prohibition of immorality.
- after: Prayer itself is presented as a maintained inhibitory practice that checks boundary-crossing and rejectable conduct.
- mechanism: Established worship functions as a repeated brake: its inhibiting action reaches both conduct that crosses bounds and conduct recognized as reprehensible.
- trace:

  - 29:45 w13 **ٱلْفَحْشَآءِ** (ف ح ش B002: hoş karşılanmayan ölçü aşımı / مجاوزة الحد والقدر) — Exceeding a limit supplies one class of behavior on which the brake acts.

## delta_social_ecology_of_restraint [context_delta, strong]

## delta_countering_beautified_obstruction [context_delta, medium]

- after: Prayer repeatedly retrains attention and action where even genuine insight can be overridden by beautified wrongdoing.

## delta_recitation_as_sufficient_event [context_delta, strong]

- before: Privately read the revealed Book, then establish prayer.
- after: Relay the Book in a public, ethically governed event whose sufficient mercy and reminder are taken up and maintained in prayer.
- mechanism: After the focus, engagement with scripture is placed in non-coercive, best-form dialogue; prior book-recitation is denied; then the descending Book recited upon hearers is declared sufficient mercy and reminder. "Recite" thus becomes a public transmission event through which the Book acts on a community, with prayer as its paired embodied uptake.

## delta_anti_reversion_practice [context_delta, medium]

- before: Prayer restrains wrongdoing whenever it is performed.
- after: Keeping prayer standing preserves Godward orientation across the dangerous transition from urgent need to comfortable safety.
- mechanism: Crisis produces purified devotion at sea, but safety is followed by renewed association; the surah closes with strenuous effort opening guided paths. Establishing prayer can therefore be read as an anti-reversion institution that carries remembered allegiance from emergency into ordinary safety.
- trace:
  - 29:65 w7 **مُخْلِصِينَ** (خ ل ص B004: özgüleme ve başkalarından ayrılma / اختصاص وانفراد عن الشرك) — Exclusive purification from association supplies the crisis-state orientation.
  - 29:65 w16 **يُشْرِكُونَ** (ش ر ك B002: Tanrı'ya ortak koşma / الشِّرك بالله) — Association with God supplies the relapse after rescue.
  - 29:69 w2 **جَٰهَدُوا۟** (ج ه د B001: gücün sonuna dek çaba gösterme ve güçlüğe katlanma / بذل الوسع تحت مشقة) — Exerting capacity under hardship supplies the sustained effort missing from temporary crisis sincerity.

  - 29:69 w5 **سُبُلَنَا** (س ب ل B001: yol ve bir amaca ulaştıran yol / طريق ممتد يسلك) — Traversable paths supply the durable courses opened by that guidance.
