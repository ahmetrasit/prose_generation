# HFT — precomputed activation hypotheses (whole-surah window)

Each record: changed reading, mechanism, and every trace step resolved to the actual word.
Note: HFT used an older root map; a trace step on a root the gateway now withholds is an echo, not identity.

## baseline_exclusive_service_and_support [baseline_model, strong]

- before: We worship you and we ask you for help as two coordinated statements.
- after: Our submission is directed only to you, and even the backing by which we sustain it is sought only from you.
- mechanism: Submissive service and requested backing form a two-part orientation: allegiance is restricted to the addressee, then the efficacy needed to continue that allegiance is restricted to the same addressee.
- trace:
  - 1:5 w2 **نَعْبُدُ** (ع ب د B003: boyun eğerek itaat ve tapınma / العبادة والطاعة الخاضعة) — Submissive worship supplies the pledged act and makes the first clause an allegiance claim.
  - 1:5 w4 **نَسْتَعِينُ** (ع و ن B001: yardım, destek ve dayanışma / الإعانة والمظاهرة) — Helping and backing supply the requested support and make the second clause the enabling side of that allegiance.

## baseline_dependent_agency [baseline_model, medium]

- before: The speakers abase themselves and then request an external intervention.
- after: The speakers accept non-sovereign status while asking for the capacity by which dependent service can genuinely act.
- mechanism: Owned status removes sovereign self-possession without removing action; seeking backing then supplies caught-up strength. The result is agency exercised from dependence, not passivity.
- trace:
  - 1:5 w2 **نَعْبُدُ** (ع ب د B001: özgür olmayan, sahip olunan kişi / الرق والملك) — Owned status supplies the speakers' non-sovereign position and frames their action as dependent service.
  - 1:5 w4 **نَسْتَعِينُ** (ع و ن B005: bedensel denge ve güç olgunluğu / استواء الخلقة وتلاحق القوة) — Strength that has caught up supplies operative capacity and makes aid the completion of dependent agency.

## baseline_honoring_service [baseline_model, medium]

- before: Worship is primarily the speakers' downward posture.
- after: Worship is also active magnification of the addressee, sustained by aid the speakers do not claim to originate.
- mechanism: Service is not exhausted by self-lowering: it raises and honors its object. Assistance prevents this magnifying service from being construed as a self-generated achievement.
- trace:
  - 1:5 w2 **نَعْبُدُ** (ع ب د B006: saygı gösterilip hizmet edilen kişi / التكريم والتعظيم) — Honoring and magnifying supply the upward-facing effect of service on the one served.
  - 1:5 w4 **نَسْتَعِينُ** (ع و ن B001: yardım, destek ve dayanışma / الإعانة والمظاهرة) — Backing supplies what lets the honoring action persist without becoming a claim of self-sufficiency.

## HFT reader overview

- remaining_uncertainties: The clause order supports both service-then-aid and aid-enables-service arrows; context strengthens the latter without canceling the former.
- remaining_uncertainties: Material branches such as smoothing, scattering, cash, and demolition are visibly packet-anchored but form-distant, so their proper downstream prominence remains uncertain.
- remaining_uncertainties: Owned dependence, merciful enclosure, covenant, and gift may be rival social models or simultaneous aspects of the same asymmetric relation.
- strongest_changes: The explicit road context activated the focus root's material passability branch, changing worship from a stationary act into practiced path-work sustained by aid.
- strongest_changes: Replacement, opposition, and wandering turned repeated إِيَّاكَ into a discipline that resists displaced allegiance and directional drift.
- strongest_changes: Naming, mercy, nurture, and reckoning made the addressee and the temporal shape of dependence more specific without eliminating competing ownership, gift, and covenant models.
- what_was_special: The focus yielded several coexisting agency models rather than one gloss: exclusive allegiance, dependent but real action, honoring service, formative support, path-making, maintenance, and collective re-coherence. Split mappings also preserved a watchful-eye reading of aid and an obstacle-breaking reading of guidance as contained exploratory activations.
