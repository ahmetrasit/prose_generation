## v12_reader_walks reader_a: activated_readings_md

1. **Exclusive address as redirecting worship and aid away from rivals.** Lexical evidence: ع ب د B003, worship and submissive obedience; ع و ن B001, help/backing; ء ل ه B001 in 1:1–1:2, worshipped object. Structural cue: إِيَّاكَ is repeated before both verbs. Assigned role: the doubled object sets a boundary around devotion and reliance. Abductive move: the packet supplies repeated exclusivity and branches for worship/help; I infer that the repetition blocks competing objects of worship or sources of backing. Reading change: the ayah reads as a double act of reallocation: service and dependency are assigned to one addressee.

2. **Worship as making the self/path passable.** Lexical evidence: ع ب د B005, physical subduing/smoothing of road; ص ر ط B001/B002 in 1:6, road and passage/swallowing; ق و م B008, straightness/uprightness. Structural cue: “we worship” is followed immediately by “guide us to the straight path.” Assigned role: worship supplies disciplined smoothing; guidance supplies direction; straightness supplies stable form. Abductive move: the packet supplies the sequence worship → request for path; I infer that worship prepares the worshipper to be traversed by guidance or to traverse the path. Reading change: نعبد is not only ritual service; it becomes bodily and practical path-conditioning.

3. **Seeking help as supported walking.** Lexical evidence: ع و ن B001, assistance/backing; ه د ي B008 in 1:6, walking while leaning on support; ه د ي B001, guidance; ق و م B009, support/subsistence. Structural cue: نَسْتَعِينُ precedes ٱهْدِنَا. Assigned role: help becomes the support that makes guided movement possible. Abductive move: the packet supplies help and later supported walking; I infer that the request for aid is concretized as being borne along the path. Reading change: “we ask for help” reads less like generic assistance and more like support for unstable walkers.

4. **Help as strength in a repeated conflict.** Lexical evidence: ع و ن B003, seasoned repeated war; غ ض ب B003 in 1:7, defiance/opposition; ض ل ل B001, deviation from aim. Structural cue: after the request for help, the path is defined against angered and astray patterns. Assigned role: استعانة supplies backing in a conflict where deviation and defiant opposition recur. Abductive move: the packet supplies war/repetition and later opposition/deviation; I infer a struggle-mechanism rather than a single moment of need. Reading change: the ayah reads as enlistment of divine backing for a continuous contest over direction.

## v12_reader_walks reader_b: activated_readings_md

1. **Exclusive service as the practical form of the invoked name.** Evidence: `ع ب د B003` worship/submissive obedience, activated by `ء ل ه B001` worshipped one and `ء ل ه B002` invocation in 1:0–1:2; reinforced by `د ي ن B001` obedience/submission in 1:4. Mechanism: the repeated fronted “إياك” narrows worship and help to the same addressee named earlier. The packet supplies invocation, worship, obedience, and repetition; I infer exclusivity as a relational narrowing. Abductive move: direct address is not a new topic but the activated consequence of naming Allah at the start. Reading change: the ayah becomes the moment where prior praise turns into pledged practice.

2. **Servitude that is backed, not abandoned.** Evidence: `ع ب د B001` owned/slave status and `ع ب د B004` being made slave-like, held together with `ع و ن B001` help/backing; activated by `م ل ك B002` ownership/disposal and `د ي ن B004` subjugation/possession in 1:4. Mechanism: the vocabulary could harden into ownership and subjugation, but the paired استعانة supplies backing from the same addressee. The packet supplies servitude, ownership, subjugation, and help; I infer that help transforms dependence from abandonment into supported service. Abductive move: if the servant immediately asks for aid, the relation is not naked domination but dependent empowerment. Reading change: worship reads as dependence that receives support, preserving both humility and agency.

3. **Road-smoothing before the request for the road.** Evidence: `ع ب د B005` physical subduing/smoothing, especially a passable road; activated by `ص ر ط B001` path and `ق و م B008` straightness in 1:6–1:7; paired with `ع و ن B001` assistance. Mechanism: before the explicit request for صراط, “نعبد” activates the making-passable branch: service prepares or smooths the way, while help provides the power to move on it. The packet supplies road-smoothing, path, straightness, and help; I infer preparation-before-navigation. Abductive move: one asks to be guided to a path after first submitting to the work that makes a path traversable. Reading change: the ayah is not only ritual allegiance; it becomes roadwork that precedes guidance.

4. **Worship as the undoing of prideful anger.** Evidence: `ع ب د B008` prideful aversion/anger, activated by `غ ض ب B001` anger in 1:7 and contrasted with `ع ب د B003` submissive obedience. Mechanism: the same root-field can hold offended pride and worshipful submission; later غضب shows the danger if pride hardens. The packet supplies pride/anger and divine-directed worship; I infer an inner reversal from self-assertive refusal to service. Abductive move: exclusive worship redirects the energy that otherwise becomes aversion and wrath. Reading change: “نعبد” becomes affective discipline, not only outward service.

## v12_reader_walks_wide reader_a: activated_readings_md

1. **Exclusive service coupled to enabling support.** **Branch evidence:** fixed `ع ب د` B003 is worship and submissive obedience; fixed `ع و ن` B001 is help, backing, and seeking assistance. **Structural cue:** `إياك` is fronted and repeated in two parallel clauses with the same plural actor, after lordship and dominion and before the guidance request. **Role and abductive move:** service supplies allegiance and aid supplies capacity; I infer from their coordination and order that divine backing enables the service it follows rather than functioning as an unrelated request. **Reading change:** the ayah states a single dependency structure: the community serves only the addressee and can sustain that service only through the addressee’s aid.

2. **Service as the material making-passable of a road.** **Branch evidence:** fixed `ع ب د` B005 is physical subduing and smoothing, explicitly including a trodden, made-passable road; 1:6 supplies `ص ر ط` B001, a road, and `ق و م` B008, straightness and evenness. **Structural cue:** `نعبد` is followed immediately by the request for the straight path. **Role and abductive move:** the fixed branch contributes the material analogy and the next ayah supplies its object; I infer that repeated obedient practice both disciplines the traveler and treads a traversable course. **Reading change:** without replacing worship’s ordinary force, `نعبد` also becomes path-work: service is how the straight route is bodily inhabited and kept passable.

3. **Aid as stabilization into upright movement.** **Branch evidence:** fixed `ع و ن` B005 is settled build and strength catching up into balance; 1:6 `ق و م` B002 and B008 supply upright standing and straight, even alignment. **Structural cue:** seeking aid immediately precedes `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`, and both verbs retain the collective `نـ`. **Role and abductive move:** the dormant aid branch supplies internal stabilization and the path adjective supplies the resulting alignment; I infer that backing works by bringing the travelers’ strength and formation into line with the course. **Reading change:** `نستعين` can ask not only for external rescue but for the capacity to stand and proceed without directional collapse.

## v12_reader_walks_wide reader_a: retrospective_surprises_md

The immediate arrival of `الصراط المستقيم` in 1:6 was the decisive surprise: it activated fixed `ع ب د` B005’s explicitly smoothed-road image and fixed `ع و ن` B005’s settled-strength image at once. 1:7’s contrasted path-groups then showed why those material readings matter: service makes a course inhabitable, while aid stabilizes travelers against the failed orientations named at the end.
