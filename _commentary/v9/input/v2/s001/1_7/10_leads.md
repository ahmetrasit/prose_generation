## v12_reader_walks reader_b: activated_readings_md

1. **The road is identified by marked recipients of favor.** Evidence: `ص ر ط B001` road/path, `ن ع م B001` bestowed favor and pleasant well-being, `ن ع م B003` praise as excellent, and `ع ل م B002` distinguishing mark/landmark from 1:2. Mechanism: the path is not defined abstractly but through “those” upon whom favor has landed; they function as markers that make the road recognizable. The packet supplies path, favor, praise, and sign/landmark branches; I infer that favored people become road-markers. Abductive move: if a path is hard to find, the visible trace of those already favored can identify it. Reading change: “صراط الذين أنعمت عليهم” reads as a marked social route, not merely a class of fortunate people.

2. **Negation splits the path away from retaliation and aimless deviation.** Evidence: `غ ي ر B005` otherness/exception/negation, `غ ض ب B001` intensified anger toward retaliation, and `ض ل ل B001` going astray from guidance/aim. Mechanism: “غير … ولا” creates two exclusions: the path is not the retaliation-field and not the deviation-field. The packet supplies negation, anger, and straying; I infer that the syntax functions as route-separation. Abductive move: guidance must name avoided trajectories as well as desired direction, because error can resemble movement. Reading change: the ayah becomes a boundary-drawing map: favor is read together with explicit separation from anger and loss.

3. **Repair and substitution away from damaged routes.** Evidence: `غ ي ر B003` altering/substituting one thing for another, `غ ي ر B001` repair through provision/watering/setting right, activated by `ر ب ب B002` repair/nurture in 1:2 and `ه د ي B001` guidance in 1:6. Mechanism: غير does more than exclude; it also activates replacement and repair, as if the requested path substitutes a rightly provisioned route for one ending in anger or loss. The packet supplies repair, substitution, nurture, and guidance; I infer a corrective route-change. Abductive move: to be guided is to have one route changed out for another, not only to receive a label saying “wrong.” Reading change: “غير المغضوب” reads as active redirection and repair, not bare negation.

4. **Herding: favored creatures are led; lost creatures become strays.** Evidence: `ن ع م B005` grazing livestock, especially camels; `ض ل ل B005` stray animal in a wasteland; `ه د ي B008` supported/swaying walking and heavy camels; `ر ب ب B014` herd or gathered wild cattle. Mechanism: the animal-material branches convert the social contrast into a pastoral mechanism: favor gathers and leads, while ضلال leaves an animal in a place of loss with no known owner. The packet supplies livestock, straying, supported walking, and herd gathering; I infer the shepherding relation. Abductive move: “upon them” can be read as care resting on a group so that they do not become ownerless strays. Reading change: the final ayah becomes a request to be gathered and led under care, not scattered into wasteland-loss.

5. **Anger as hardened material obstruction.** Evidence: `غ ض ب B004` hard compact rock, `غ ض ب B008` tough hide/folded leather like a shield, activated by `ص ر ط B003` cutting sword passing through and `ق و م B008` straightness. Mechanism: anger is not only affect; its branches materialize as hard compactness that blocks or armors against the path. The packet supplies hard rock/tough hide, cutting-through, and straightness; I infer obstruction. Abductive move: a straight path must cut through or avoid hardened states that retaliation produces. Reading change: “المغضوب عليهم” carries a material image of hardened blockage, not only a moral category.
