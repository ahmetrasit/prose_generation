# HFT — precomputed activation hypotheses (whole-surah window)

Each record: changed reading, mechanism, and every trace step resolved to the actual word.
Note: HFT used an older root map; a trace step on a root the gateway now withholds is an echo, not identity.

## base_embodied_route [baseline_model, strong]

- before: The verse names a road used by a favored class and excludes two bad classes.
- after: The verse makes a favored human trajectory the road's visible embodiment and distinguishes oppositional rupture from aimless deviation.
- mechanism: The verse does not identify a road by geometry alone. It makes bestowed well-being visible in a recipient group, so their lived trajectory becomes the road's positive continuity; opposition and deviation are rival ways of leaving that continuity.
- trace:
  - 1:7 w1 **صِرَٰطَ** (ص ر ط B001: yol, özellikle düz yol / الطريق المستقيم) — The straight-road image supplies continuous movement, while the following relative phrase relocates that continuity into an embodied human route.
  - 1:7 w3 **أَنْعَمْتَ** (ن ع م B001: iyi yaşam durumu ve başkasına ulaştırılan iyilik / حسن الحال والنعمة) — Bestowed favorable condition supplies the observable state by which the road-bearing group is identified.
  - 1:7 w5 **غَيْرِ** (غ ي ر B005: başka olma, dışta bırakma veya olumsuzlama / السوى والخلاف والاستثناء والنفي) — Otherness and exception turn the verse from a single route description into a contrast among trajectories.
  - 1:7 w6 **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** (غ ض ب B003: karşı koyup muhalefet etmek / المراغمة والمخالفة) — Defiance and opposition supply a relational mechanism for one trajectory's rupture rather than merely naming an emotion.
  - 1:7 w9 **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** (ض ل ل B001: doğru yoldan ve amaçtan sapma ya da başkasını saptırma / الضلال عن الهدى والقصد) — Deviation from aim supplies the second failure mode: motion continues but no longer holds the road's direction.

## base_successful_and_failed_disappearance [baseline_model, medium]

- before: Road and straying are simple opposites: presence on the route versus absence from it.
- after: Both right passage and straying can involve disappearance; the decisive difference is whether disappearance completes directed transit or erases recoverable direction.
- mechanism: Two kinds of disappearance coexist. A soft, capacious passage can carry a traveler through so completely that the traveler vanishes into successful transit, whereas straying can also make something vanish but without destination or recoverability. Compact hardness acts as the obstructing condition that separates the two.
- trace:
  - 1:7 w1 **صِرَٰطَ** (ص ر ط B002: geçişte gözden kaybolmak; özellikle yiyeceği yutmak / الغيبة في المرور والبلع) — Disappearance through swallowing supplies a successful passage in which being taken out of sight is evidence of transit.
  - 1:7 w3 **أَنْعَمْتَ** (ن ع م B002: yumuşamak, rahat yaşamak veya rahat yaşatmak / اللين والنعومة ورفاه العيش) — Softness and easeful living supply low resistance, making the favored passage traversable rather than abrasive.
  - 1:7 w6 **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** (غ ض ب B004: sert, yığılmış veya yuvarlak kaya / صلابة الصخرة وتماسكها) — Hard compact rock supplies the blocking material state opposed to softened passage.
  - 1:7 w9 **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** (ض ل ل B002: gizlenerek, karışıp eriyerek veya gömülerek gözden yitme / الغيبوبة والخفاء) — Hiddenness and disappearance supply a failed vanishing that lacks the road's directed transit.

## base_decisive_partition [baseline_model, exploratory]

- before: The final contrasts are descriptive labels attached to people.
- after: The verse can stage a decisive partition among favorable passage, retaliatory exposure, and an unresolved loss that never arrives for settlement.
- mechanism: The road can image a decisive cut that separates outcomes. The exception pivot opens an alternative to retaliatory settlement, while the final group represents what never reaches settlement because it has gone unaccounted for.
- trace:
  - 1:7 w1 **صِرَٰطَ** (ص ر ط B003: vuruşta kesip ilerleyen kılıç / السيف القاطع الماضي في الضربة) — A cutting implement passing cleanly through a stroke supplies the road as a decisive separator of outcomes.
  - 1:7 w5 **غَيْرِ** (غ ي ر B002: cana karşılık ceza yerine kabul edilen kan bedeli / الغَيْر في الدية) — Compensation offered instead of retaliation supplies a legal fork at the verse's exception pivot.
  - 1:7 w6 **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** (غ ض ب B001: şiddetli öfke ve öç alma yönelimi / اشتداد السخط وثورانه للانتقام) — Anger rising toward retaliation supplies the threatened punitive settlement on one side of the fork.
  - 1:7 w9 **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** (ض ل ل B003: bir şeyi yitirme veya yerini bulamama; özel olarak kanın karşılıksız kalması / فقدان الشيء) — A lost and unlocated thing supplies the unresolved outcome that falls outside both favorable passage and settled retaliation.

## base_agency_gradient [baseline_model, medium]

- before: All three groups are parallel moral categories.
- after: They encode different causal positions: actively furthered recipients, people bearing relational opposition, and agents continuing to deviate.
- mechanism: The three groups differ not only in outcome but in causal grammar. Favor actively carries recipients further; the angered group bears a condition produced in a relation of opposition; the strayers enact continuing deviation. The road is therefore a pattern of agency distribution.
- trace:
  - 1:7 w3 **أَنْعَمْتَ** (ن ع م B010: daha da artırmak veya ileri dereceye götürmek / زاد وأنعم في الفعل) — Adding and carrying an action further supplies active propulsion to the explicitly second-person benefaction.
  - 1:7 w6 **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** (غ ض ب B003: karşı koyup muhalefet etmek / المراغمة والمخالفة) — Mutual opposition supplies the relation whose result is borne by the passive angered group.
  - 1:7 w9 **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** (ض ل ل B001: doğru yoldan ve amaçtan sapma ya da başkasını saptırma / الضلال عن الهدى والقصد) — Deviation from aim supplies ongoing self-propelled divergence to the active-participial final group.
  - 1:7 w5 **غَيْرِ** (غ ي ر B003: biçimini değiştirme veya yerine başkasını koyma / تغيير الصورة أو إبدال الشيء بغيره) — Replacement and change of state make the exception pivot a switch among causal regimes rather than a flat list boundary.

## ctx_marked_route [context_delta, medium]

- before: The road is known chiefly by where it leads.
- after: The road is also known by legible effects borne by favored travelers, who function as its identifying marks.
- mechanism: The opening designation cue, including its non-dominant visible-mark mapping, turns the road into something recognized by signs. The favored group's condition can function as a trail mark rather than merely as a credential possessed before travel.
- trace:

  - 1:1 w1 **بِسْمِ** (س م و B001: tanıtıcı fiziksel iz koyma, iz ve araç / أثر وسم ظاهر يجعل الشيء معروفا) — A visible identifying mark supplies the concrete sign by which the favored trajectory can be followed.
  - 1:7 w1 **صِرَٰطَ** (ص ر ط B001: yol, özellikle düz yol / الطريق المستقيم) — The road supplies the spatial carrier on which identifying marks accumulate.
  - 1:7 w3 **أَنْعَمْتَ** (ن ع م B001: iyi yaşam durumu ve başkasına ulaştırılan iyilik / حسن الحال والنعمة) — Bestowed well-being supplies the visible human effect that can serve as a route marker.

## ctx_mercy_affiliation [context_delta, medium]

- before: Those favored are separate precedents whom a traveler should copy.
- after: Those favored can be a mercy-formed affiliation into which the requested road incorporates the traveler.
- mechanism: Worship relation, tenderness, and kinship recast the favored plural as an affiliation generated by mercy. The road becomes entry into and continuity within a relational household, not imitation of isolated exemplary individuals.
- trace:
  - 1:1 w2 **ٱللَّهِ** (ء ل ه B001: tapınma ve tapınılan varlık / التعبد والمعبود) — The worshipper-worshipped relation supplies the vertical bond around which the favored affiliation coheres.
  - 1:1 w3,4 **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** (ر ح م B001: acıma duygusuyla esirgeyip iyilik etme / الرَّحْمَة والرقة) — Tender mercy supplies the affective force that forms rather than merely rewards the group.
  - 1:1 w3,4 **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** (ر ح م B002: yakın soy bağı / الرَّحِم والقرابة) — Kinship supplies a lateral bond among recipients, making their shared road a relation of belonging.
  - 1:7 w3 **أَنْعَمْتَ** (ن ع م B001: iyi yaşam durumu ve başkasına ulaştırılan iyilik / حسن الحال والنعمة) — Benefaction given to another supplies the transfer that creates and sustains the affiliation.
  - 1:7 w1 **صِرَٰطَ** (ص ر ط B001: yol, özellikle düz yol / الطريق المستقيم) — The road supplies continuity through which the mercy-formed affiliation persists.

## ctx_cultivated_completion [context_delta, strong]

- before: Favor is a possession already held by the people who define the road.
- after: Favor is an ongoing curriculum of repair, nourishment, marked progress, and completion that produces the road-bearing people.
- mechanism: Praiseworthy end, repairful upbringing, nourished growth, and orienting marks combine into a developmental process. Favor no longer appears only as a prior gift qualifying certain people; it is the sustained cultivation that carries them further toward completion and leaves signs others can follow.
- trace:
  - 1:2 w1 **ٱلْحَمْدُ** (ح م د B004: övülesi işin varılabilecek en ileri sınırı / حماداك الغاية المحمودة) — The praiseworthy end supplies a telos toward which the road's cultivation is directed.
  - 1:2 w3 **رَبِّ** (ر ب ب B002: adım adım yetiştirip tamamlama / إصلاح وتربية وإتمام) — Repair, upbringing, and completion supply the staged process that produces favored travelers.
  - 1:2 w3 **رَبِّ** (ر ب ب B005: besleyip büyütmek ve yetişmek / تغذية ونشوء) — The non-dominant nourishment-and-growth image supplies the material rate of development within that process.
  - 1:2 w4 **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** (ع ل م B002: ayırt edici ve yol gösterici işaret / أثر يميز الشيء ويهدي إليه) — A distinguishing trace that guides supplies feedback marks by which developing travelers stay oriented.
  - 1:7 w3 **أَنْعَمْتَ** (ن ع م B010: daha da artırmak veya ileri dereceye götürmek / زاد وأنعم في الفعل) — Adding and carrying further supplies continuing advancement rather than a completed one-time grant.

## ctx_mercy_bounded_wrath [context_delta, medium]

- before: Favor and wrath are two symmetrical divine dispositions assigning opposite groups.
- after: Mercy is the persistent field of the route, while wrath is a bounded relational response marking resistance within that field.
- mechanism: The repeated tenderness cue gives mercy greater textual persistence than wrath. Wrath can therefore be read as a boundary response to opposition inside a mercy-dominant field, not as an equal and independent destination paired symmetrically with favor.
- trace:
  - 1:1 w3,4 **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** (ر ح م B001: acıma duygusuyla esirgeyip iyilik etme / الرَّحْمَة والرقة) — Tender mercy establishes the first affective field in which the later contrast will be heard.
  - 1:3 w1,2 **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** (ر ح م B001: acıma duygusuyla esirgeyip iyilik etme / الرَّحْمَة والرقة) — The repeated tenderness image supplies persistence and makes mercy the enclosing rather than incidental condition.
  - 1:7 w6 **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** (غ ض ب B003: karşı koyup muhalefet etmek / المراغمة والمخالفة) — Opposition supplies the relational breach to which bounded wrath responds.
  - 1:7 w3 **أَنْعَمْتَ** (ن ع م B001: iyi yaşam durumu ve başkasına ulaştırılan iyilik / حسن الحال والنعمة) — Transferred benefaction supplies mercy's positive operation in the focus rather than leaving mercy as context-only atmosphere.

## ctx_accountable_partition [context_delta, strong]

- before: The focus lists spiritually approved and disapproved populations.
- after: The focus can map trajectories by their status in an eventual settlement: advanced by favor, exposed to retaliatory claim, or still lost from account.
- mechanism: Disposition, bounded time, and reckoning turn the focus partition into an accountable process. The road cuts among outcomes that eventually become settleable: benefaction carries forward, opposition incurs a retaliatory claim, and straying remains a loss until located and accounted for.
- trace:
  - 1:4 w1 **مَٰلِكِ** (م ل ك B002: sahiplik ve tasarruf yetkisi / المِلْك والتصرف) — Ownership with power of disposition supplies authority to settle the divergent trajectories.

## ctx_enacted_road [context_delta, strong]

- before: The road exists complete before the traveler and needs only to be pointed out.
- after: The road is received but also enacted underfoot: humble practice levels it, communal walking preserves it, and aid keeps the enactment possible.
- mechanism: Humble service levels a way, walking places it underfoot, and requested assistance supplies the capacity to continue. The road is therefore partly enacted by repeated service rather than merely discovered as external geography.
- trace:
  - 1:5 w2 **نَعْبُدُ** (ع ب د B003: boyun eğerek itaat ve tapınma / العبادة والطاعة الخاضعة) — Humble worship supplies the repeated practice through which travelers submit their motion to the route.

## ctx_growth_with_measure [context_delta, medium]

## out_hydrological_way [surprising_valid_outlier, exploratory]

- after: Exploratorily, it can behave like a watercourse: favor replenishes and repairs along directed flow, hardness blocks it, and straying is flow that disappears without fructifying the marked terrain.

- containment: This is surprising because it coordinates branch-distant rain, spring, wind, swallowing, watering, and rock images across several roots. Downstream prose should present it only as a material analogy for directed provision versus blocked or lost flow, never as a lexical replacement for the verse.
