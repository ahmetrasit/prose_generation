Structural cue: the opening places “name” before any praise, judgment, worship, help, or guidance. Assigned role: the name is not a neutral label but an elevated designation under which the following speech is performed. Abductive move: the packet supplies the sequence name → praise → address/request; I supply the arrow that beginning “in the name” authorizes and channels the later acts. Reading change: the ayah reads as a formal entry into a governed speech-act, not only as a pious preface.

2. **Mercy as the covering atmosphere before lordship and judgment.** Lexical evidence: س م و B004, overhead sky/cover; ر ح م B001, compassion; ر ب ب B002/B008 in 1:2, nurturing and cloud imagery; د ي ن B002 in 1:4, reckoning. Structural cue: mercy is doubled before “Lord of the worlds” and before “Owner of the Day of dīn.” Assigned role: the two mercy-forms become the upper cover beneath which nurture and reckoning are later heard. Abductive move: the packet gives cover/cloud/nurture/reckoning as branch material; I infer that the opening sequence frames later authority through mercy rather than adding mercy after authority. Reading change: divine judgment and lordship are pre-activated as occurring inside a merciful canopy.

3. **Mercy as generative relation, not only affect.** Lexical evidence: ر ح م B002, kinship bond; ر ح م B003, womb; ر ب ب B005 in 1:2, fostered child/caretaker relation; ن ع م B001 in 1:7, bestowed favor. Structural cue: the same mercy root is repeated, then the packet later gives Lord/caretaker, worshipper, helper, and favored people. Assigned role: ر ح م supplies a relational matrix that can produce, bind, and maintain dependents. Abductive move: the packet supplies kinship/womb/caretaker/favor; I infer a social-biological mechanism of dependency and care. Reading change: “al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm” reads as the source of a family-like and womb-like order in which beings can be nurtured, guided, and favored.

## v12_reader_walks reader_b: activated_readings_md

1. **The opening name as a raised public marker of agency.** Evidence: `س م و B005` name as raised designation, with `س م و B008` good repute spread; `ء ل ه B002` fixed divine name in invocation; `ح م د B001/B003` later praise and praiseworthy naming in 1:2. Mechanism: the initial “name” is not only a label but the visible elevation of the agent under whom the later acts of praise, worship, help-seeking, and guidance will occur. The packet supplies name, divine invocation, and praise; I infer the directional arrow from naming to public orientation. Abductive move: if the speech begins by raising a name, later praise becomes the social confirmation of that raised designation. Reading change: the basmalah reads less as a detachable formula and more as the banner under which the whole passage is organized.

2. **Mercy as the enclosure that precedes both nurture and judgment.** Evidence: `ر ح م B001` mercy and tenderness, `ر ح م B002` kinship bond, `ر ح م B003` womb; activated by `ر ب ب B002` nurture/completion in 1:2 and by `م ل ك B003` plus `د ي ن B002` dominion and reckoning in 1:4. Mechanism: the double mercy surrounds the move from ربّ care to ملك/دين judgment, making the later legal-sovereign scene enter through an already merciful enclosure. The packet supplies mercy, nurture, dominion, and reckoning; I infer that mercy frames rather than cancels judgment. Abductive move: a sequence that names mercy before ownership of reckoning asks the reader to carry tenderness into the tribunal. Reading change: “الرحمن الرحيم” becomes a governing atmosphere for the whole surah, not only a pair of attributes.

3. **Invocation as relation before demand.** Evidence: `ء ل ه B001` worship and the worshipped one, `ء ل ه B002` invocation formula, `ع ب د B003` worship/submissive obedience in 1:5, `ع و ن B001` help/backing in 1:5. Mechanism: the opening invocation establishes the addressee before the “You alone” repetition appears; worship and help-seeking are later disclosed as the practical shape of the name invoked here. The packet supplies the divine name and later direct address; I infer that the name authorizes the later second-person turn. Abductive move: one invokes a name before asking because the relation must be recognized before dependence is voiced. Reading change: the basmalah becomes a relational threshold into petition, not merely a pious preface.

## v12_reader_walks_wide reader_a: activated_readings_md

1. **The name as a performative point of entry.** **Branch evidence:** the fixed `بِسْمِ` carries `س م و` B005, “name as raised designation,” while fixed `ٱللَّهِ` carries `ء ل ه` B002, the fixed divine name in invocation, and B001, the worshipped one. **Structural cue:** the prefixed `بـ` attaches the opening to the name; `الله` then receives praise in 1:2, and the twice-fronted `إِيَّاكَ` in 1:5 turns designation into exclusive direct address. **Role and abductive move:** the packet supplies naming, repetition, and the shift to address; I infer that beginning by the name places the ensuing praise, service, request for aid, and request for guidance under that named addressee. **Reading change:** the phrase is not merely identification but an enacted alignment that initiates the surah’s speech.

2. **Naming as the raising of mention.** **Branch evidence:** fixed `س م و` B001 includes figurative elevation and raised mention, and B008 gives good repute spreading; 1:2 `ح م د` B001 supplies praise and commendation. **Structural cue:** `اسم` comes first, then praise follows, and the two fixed mercy names recur exactly in 1:3. **Role and abductive move:** praise and recurrence are packet facts; I supply the arrow that they actualize the root’s elevation image by making the designation repeatedly heard and commended. **Reading change:** `بسم` begins a process of raising the named one’s mention, not just referring to him.

3. **Mercy as a formative enclosure.** **Branch evidence:** the two fixed `ر ح م` forms retain B001, tenderness and beneficence, but also activate B003, the womb as a protected place of growth. **Structural cue:** 1:2 `ر ب ب` B002 describes nurture and completion stage by stage, the mercy pair is repeated in 1:3, and 1:5–1:6 move to aid and guidance. **Role and abductive move:** the womb branch supplies the spatial model and later nurture, aid, and guidance supply the dependent’s development; I infer that protective mercy enables that development rather than merely accompanying it. **Reading change:** the doubled attributes can be heard as sustained, generative shelter within which the addressed dependents are formed and led.
