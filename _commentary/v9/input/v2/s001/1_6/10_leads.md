Structural cue: the imperative “guide us” asks for a road, not merely knowledge. Assigned role: هدى is the forward guide; صراط is the traversable field; مستقيم is the upright shape of movement. Abductive move: the packet supplies guide/front/road/straightness; I infer that guidance includes being led ahead. Reading change: the ayah reads as a request for conducted movement, not just instruction.

2. **The straight path as maintained support.** Lexical evidence: ق و م B004, care/guardianship/preservation; ق و م B009, support/mainstay/subsistence; م ل ك B005 in 1:4, mainstay; ر ب ب B002 in 1:2, nurturing/completion. Structural cue: مستقيم is built from ق و م and appears after Lordship, ownership, worship, and help. Assigned role: straightness is the maintained condition by which the path keeps standing. Abductive move: the packet supplies nurture, mainstay, support, and straightness; I infer that the path remains straight because it is upheld, not because it is merely geometrically direct. Reading change: “straight” reads as sustained uprightness and managed stability.

3. **The path as passage that can swallow the traveler.** Lexical evidence: ص ر ط B002, disappearing in passage and swallowing; ض ل ل B002 in 1:7, hiddenness/disappearance; ض ل ل B003, loss. Structural cue: the same صراط is repeated in 1:7 and then contrasted with lost/astray groups. Assigned role: صراط carries an ambivalent passage-image: movement can be safe traversal or disappearance. Abductive move: the packet supplies swallowing and later disappearance/loss; I infer that the prayer asks for a passage that does not erase or lose the traveler. Reading change: the road becomes perilous and absorbing, not merely a clear line.

4. **Guidance as gift/favor.** Lexical evidence: ه د ي B004, gracious gift sent to one close; ن ع م B001 in 1:7, bestowed favor; ر ح م B002/B003 in 1:1–1:3, kinship/womb relation. Structural cue: the next ayah identifies the desired path by those on whom “You bestowed favor.” Assigned role: guidance is a gracious transfer that places the receiver within a favored relation. Abductive move: the packet supplies gift, favor, and relational mercy; I infer that being guided is a received benefaction, not a self-generated achievement. Reading change: “Guide us” reads as “grant us the path as a favoring gift.”

5. **Guidance as composed, non-panicked bearing.** Lexical evidence: ه د ي B010, calm stillness and composed bearing; ع و ن B001 in 1:5, help/backing; غ ض ب B001 and ض ل ل B001 in 1:7, anger and deviation. Structural cue: the request for help precedes the request for guidance, and the following ayah names destabilizing alternatives. Assigned role: الهداية steadies the petitioner against panic, anger, and drift. Abductive move: the packet supplies calm bearing and later destabilizing states; I infer that guidance has an affective-postural dimension. Reading change: the ayah asks for a composed way of moving, not only the correct route.

## v12_reader_walks reader_b: activated_readings_md

1. **Gentle showing of the straight road.** Evidence: `ه د ي B001` gentle guidance toward way/truth, `ص ر ط B001` straight road, and `ق و م B008` straightness/uprightness/evenness. Mechanism: the three roots form the surface mechanism: an addressee is asked to show a road whose defining quality is uprightness. The packet supplies guidance, road, and straightness; I infer that guidance is not only information but movement toward an aligned course. Abductive move: a straight path must be both indicated and sustained, because the speaker cannot generate it by worship alone. Reading change: the ayah reads as a request for enabled orientation, not merely for knowledge of a route.

2. **A leading front, not only an abstract direction.** Evidence: `ه د ي B003` leading front part/guide, activated by `م ل ك B008` leading forepart in animals and by `ص ر ط B001` road; supported by `ق و م B003` resolve/rising to undertake a matter. Mechanism: guidance becomes embodied as something or someone going ahead; straightness is followed through a leading edge. The packet supplies leading-part branches and the road; I infer a caravan-like directionality. Abductive move: those who ask “guide us” need a front to follow, not only a map. Reading change: “اهدنا” becomes a request to be led from ahead into undertaking, not only to be instructed from a distance.

3. **The path as passage that can swallow, hide, or cut through.** Evidence: `ص ر ط B002` disappearing in passage/swallowing and `ص ر ط B003` cutting sword passing through, activated by `ض ل ل B002/B003` hiddenness/loss in 1:7 and `غ ض ب B004/B008` hard rock/tough hide. Mechanism: the requested path is not a neutral line; it is a passage through conditions where things disappear, are lost, or meet hard resistance. The packet supplies passage/swallowing, cutting-through, hidden loss, and hard compactness; I infer a dangerous transit mechanism. Abductive move: straightness matters because passage itself can consume or deflect the traveler. Reading change: الصراط المستقيم becomes a protected passage through risk, not only a morally correct road.

4. **Guidance as gift and offering.** Evidence: `ه د ي B004` gracious gift, `ه د ي B005` offering sent to sanctuary, activated by `ن ع م B001` bestowed favor in 1:7 and `ح م د B001` thanksgiving in 1:2. Mechanism: the request for guidance receives the gift-field of هدي: the path is something graciously sent or given, and the response to it is praise. The packet supplies gift/offering, favor, and thanksgiving; I infer a reciprocal devotional economy. Abductive move: what guides is not earned by the speaker’s service; it is bestowed, then answered by praise and worship. Reading change: “اهدنا” becomes request for a gift-bearing guidance, not a claim to entitlement.

## v12_reader_walks_wide reader_a: activated_readings_md

1. **Guidance as a bestowed gift.** **Branch evidence:** fixed `ه د ي` B004 is a gracious gift sent to one who is close, while 1:7 `ن ع م` B001 is bestowed favor; fixed `ص ر ط` B001 supplies the road. **Structural cue:** the petition is immediately expanded as the road of people upon whom the addressee has conferred favor. **Role and abductive move:** the gift branch supplies mode of transfer and the next ayah supplies benefaction and recipients; I infer that favor takes concrete form as access to the road. **Reading change:** `اهدنا` is not merely “give us directions” but “bestow the traversable way upon us.”

2. **Guidance as a supported procession.** **Branch evidence:** fixed `ه د ي` B003 is the leading front part or guide going ahead, and B008 is supported, swaying walking; fixed `ق و م` B008 supplies upright alignment. Preceding `ع و ن` B001 supplies requested support, and 1:7 supplies people already associated with the path. **Structural cue:** aid comes first, then guidance, then the human exemplars who are ahead on the road. **Role and abductive move:** the earlier recipients function as a leading front and aid steadies those who would otherwise sway; I infer both roles from the ordered sequence. **Reading change:** guidance becomes accompanied movement into an existing procession, not information delivered to an already stable traveler.

3. **Straightness as maintained and sustaining infrastructure.** **Branch evidence:** fixed `ق و م` B004 is care, guardianship, and preservation, and B009 is the support or mainstay by which something stands and life is sustained. Earlier `ر ب ب` B002 supplies nurture and administration, and `ع و ن` B001 supplies backing. **Structural cue:** nurture, aid, and the `مستقيم` path form a causal sequence. **Role and abductive move:** the fixed branches make straightness both an effect of maintenance and a source of support; I infer that the route remains upright because it is sustained and that it in turn sustains its travelers. **Reading change:** `المستقيم` describes a dependable life-bearing order, not only a geometrically unbent line.

4. **An exploratory contrast between contained passage and untracked disappearance.** **Branch evidence:** fixed `ص ر ط` B002 is disappearance through passage or swallowing; later `ض ل ل` B002 is hidden disappearance and B003 is loss of locatability. **Structural cue:** `صراط` is repeated at the start of 1:7 before the contrasting `الضالين`. **Role and abductive move:** both sides supply disappearance images, but I infer a directional distinction: the road contains and carries passage, whereas straying removes the traveler from a locatable course. **Reading change:** the straight path can absorb the traveler into disciplined transit, contrasted with being absorbed into trackless loss.
