## base_gentle_upright_alignment [baseline_model, strong]

- before: Tell us which road is correct.
- after: Gently enable us into and along a real course whose correctness consists in sustained uprightness and even measure.
- mechanism: Guidance initiates movement by gentle indication and enabling; the path supplies a traversable course, while its qualifier makes straightness an active condition of uprightness, evenness, and just proportion.
- trace:
  - 1:6 w1 **ٱهْدِنَا** (ه د ي B001: doğru yolu gösterme ve doğruya yönelme / دلالة بلطف إلى الطريق والحق) — Gentle indication toward a true way supplies the initiating guidance and enabling, not merely destination data.
  - 1:6 w2 **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ** (ص ر ط B001: yol, özellikle düz yol / الطريق المستقيم) — A straight traversable road supplies the concrete course into which the plural object asks to be led.
  - 1:6 w3 **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** (ق و م B008: düzgünlük, denge ve doğru yoldan sapmama / استقامة واعتدال واستواء) — Uprightness, evenness, and justness make the road's straightness a maintained norm of alignment.
