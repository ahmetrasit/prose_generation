## b1_praise_as_tested_verdict [baseline_model, strong]

- before: A general declaration that praise belongs to God.
- after: A considered verdict that the worshipped Lord proves praiseworthy across the full scope of lordship.
- mechanism: Hamd is both praise opposed to blame and the finding of something satisfactory after testing. Joined to divine lordship, it reads as an evaluative verdict grounded in the Lord's relation to all worlds, not as unexamined applause.
- trace:
  - 1:2 w1 **ٱلْحَمْدُ** (ح م د B001: yermenin karşıtı olan, iyilik için teşekkürü de kapsayan övgü / الحمد خلاف الذم) — Praise opposed to blame supplies the evaluative polarity of the declaration.
  - 1:2 w1 **ٱلْحَمْدُ** (ح م د B002: deneyip övülesi ya da uygun bulma / وجود الشيء محمودا) — Finding something satisfactory after testing makes the praise a considered verdict.
  - 1:2 w2 **لِلَّهِ** (ء ل ه B001: tapınma ve tapınılan varlık / التعبد والمعبود) — The worshipped one identifies the exclusive recipient of the evaluative response.
  - 1:2 w3 **رَبِّ** (ر ب ب B001: sahip olup yönetme / ربوبية وملك وسيادة) — Lordship, ownership, and authority provide the relation whose exercise is being judged praiseworthy.

## b4_credit_claim_reversal [baseline_model, exploratory]

- before: The clause directs positive speech toward God.
- after: The clause also interrupts human claims to own others' gratitude by returning the ground of every benefaction to the Lord.
- mechanism: A live Hamd branch describes claiming praise over others through benefaction. Assigning hamd to God reverses that social economy: the ultimate nurturer does not leave creatures able to convert derivative favors into absolute claims of credit over one another.
- trace:
  - 1:2 w1 **ٱلْحَمْدُ** (ح م د B005: iyiliğini başa kakıp kendine pay çıkarma / يتحمد بالمنة) — Claiming praise by favor exposes the coercive credit economy that the attribution can reverse.
  - 1:2 w2 **لِلَّهِ** (ء ل ه B001: tapınma ve tapınılan varlık / التعبد والمعبود) — Exclusive devotion relocates ultimate credit away from intermediate benefactors.
  - 1:2 w3 **رَبِّ** (ر ب ب B016: gereksinim, sıkı düğüm veya iyilik / رُبَى حاجة وعقدة ونعمة) — Need, binding, and benefaction place praise inside a concrete relation of dependence and favor.

## HFT reader overview

- remaining_uncertainties: The gestational model depends on a reader-supplied spatial arrow from womb to world-formation.
- remaining_uncertainties: The eye-of-care, breakage, rain-mark, and covenantal-bundle activations are live but mapping-distant and should remain explicitly exploratory.
- remaining_uncertainties: Alamin may support the mark and knowledge activations without making those branches a direct lexical gloss of worlds.
- strongest_changes: The tested-quality branch of Hamd becomes a whole-history verdict when activated by ownership, duration, and accounting.
- strongest_changes: The mark branch of alamin becomes a navigable sign-field when activated by guidance, road, straightness, and support.
- strongest_changes: Rabb's nurture becomes enacted dependence through worship and aid, with a live split-root image of watchful care.
- strongest_changes: The final contrasts revise praise into differential diagnosis rather than indiscriminate affirmation.
- what_was_special: The trace preserved simultaneous evaluative, developmental, semiotic, social-credit, gestational, forensic, navigational, and ecological readings. Non-dominant mapped roots were most productive when they sharpened an already anchored focus mechanism rather than replacing it.
