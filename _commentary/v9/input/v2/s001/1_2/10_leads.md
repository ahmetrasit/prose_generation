## v12_reader_walks reader_a: activated_readings_md

1. **Praise as an evaluative verdict after testing the order.** Lexical evidence: ح م د B001, praise opposite blame; ح م د B002, finding something satisfactory after testing; ع ل م B001, knowing as clarity. Structural cue: praise follows the named mercy and precedes the list of lordship, judgment, worship, and guidance. Assigned role: الحمد is both spoken praise and a judgment that the whole disclosed order proves praiseworthy. Abductive move: the packet gives praise/testing/clarity; I infer that the following ayat function as the evidentiary field that makes praise intelligible. Reading change: “all praise” reads less like a formula and more like the verdict produced by seeing the whole mechanism.

2. **Lordship as stage-by-stage cultivation of worlds.** Lexical evidence: ر ب ب B001, lordship/ownership/authority; ر ب ب B002, repair, nurture, completion; ع ل م B002, distinguishing signs and road markers. Structural cue: “Lord” is attached to “worlds,” while later 1:6 asks for a road and 1:7 identifies the path by people marked by favor. Assigned role: رب is the maintainer who forms worlds into legible, navigable fields. Abductive move: the packet supplies nurture and markers; I infer that the worlds are not merely objects owned but terrains cultivated into guidance-bearing order. Reading change: “Lord of the worlds” reads as “caretaker and completer of sign-marked realms.”

3. **The worlds as signs, not only domains.** Lexical evidence: ع ل م B002, a distinguishing mark that points to something; ه د ي B001 in 1:6, guidance to road/truth; ص ر ط B001 in 1:6–1:7, road. Structural cue: عَٰلَمِينَ sits immediately after رب and later the path request asks for orientation. Assigned role: worlds act as marked surfaces through which lordship becomes knowable and directional. Abductive move: the packet supplies “worlds” through the root علم and “path/guidance” later; I infer that visibility of marks enables movement. Reading change: the ayah’s cosmology becomes semiotic: the worlds are readable signs under رب, not inert scenery.

4. **Praise resists benefaction-credit rivalry.** Lexical evidence: ح م د B005, claiming praise by favor; ن ع م B001 in 1:7, bestowed favor; ء ل ه B001, worshipped object. Structural cue: 1:7 mentions favor bestowed by “You,” while 1:5 confines worship and help to “You.” Assigned role: الحمد prevents favor from becoming a human credit-claim. Abductive move: the packet supplies favor and exclusive address; I infer that all benefaction-credit is redirected to Allah. Reading change: praise is not only gratitude; it blocks rival ownership of benefaction.

## v12_reader_walks reader_b: activated_readings_md

1. **Praise as tested recognition, not only spontaneous acclaim.** Evidence: `ح م د B001` praise/thanksgiving, `ح م د B002` finding something praiseworthy after testing, `ن ع م B001` favor in 1:7, and `غ ض ب B001`/`ض ل ل B001` negative alternatives in 1:7. Mechanism: the later contrast between favored, angered, and lost paths makes الحمد sound like a verdict reached after discriminating between outcomes. The packet supplies praise, favor, anger, and straying; I infer the evaluative testing relation. Abductive move: praise becomes meaningful when the path has alternatives that could have ended in anger or loss. Reading change: “الحمد” reads as thankful recognition after moral-path testing, not only as opening doxology.

2. **Rabb as caretaker who brings many marked worlds to completion.** Evidence: `ر ب ب B002` repair, nurture, and completion; `ع ل م B002` distinguishing mark/landmark; `ه د ي B001` guidance toward way/truth in 1:6; `ق و م B009` support/mainstay in 1:6. Mechanism: “العالمين” is activated by the branch of signs and markers: worlds are readable, marked domains that require ربّ care, and guidance later asks to move through those marks rightly. The packet supplies Rabb, worlds/knowledge-sign branches, guidance, and straightness; I infer that nurture makes the marked order navigable. Abductive move: a ربّ of many worlds is not only owner of plurality but the one who maintains their intelligibility. Reading change: “رب العالمين” becomes a claim about sustaining a legible cosmos, not only possessing all beings.

3. **Lordship as authority, covenant, and administered dependence.** Evidence: `ر ب ب B001` lordship/ownership/authority, `ر ب ب B011` covenant or protection pact, `م ل ك B003` kingship/dominion and `د ي ن B001/B002` obedience/reckoning in 1:4, plus `ع ب د B003` worship in 1:5. Mechanism: later obedience, judgment, and worship activate the legal-political side of ربّ: praise is directed to one whose care is also binding authority. The packet supplies authority, covenant, obedience, and worship; I infer that praise acknowledges administered dependence. Abductive move: if the speaker will later declare exclusive service, the ربّ here must already be more than a nurturer; he is the binding governor of the relation. Reading change: the ayah preserves both care and rule, rather than disambiguating Rabb into either tenderness or sovereignty.

## v12_reader_walks_wide reader_a: activated_readings_md

1. **Praise as a grateful verdict grounded in benefit.** **Branch evidence:** fixed `ح م د` B001 includes both commendation and thanksgiving for benefaction; B002 adds finding someone or something satisfactory after testing. Later `ن ع م` B001 in 1:7 names bestowed favor, while 1:3 and 1:5 supply mercy and aid. **Structural cue:** praise precedes the petition and its eventual description of favored recipients, and the path’s alternatives provide an evaluative contrast. **Role and abductive move:** favor, mercy, and aid are packet-supplied grounds; I infer that the speaker treats their experienced or anticipated effects as the “test” by which the praised one is found praiseworthy. **Reading change:** `الحمد` becomes an evidence-bearing, grateful judgment, not undirected acclaim.

2. **Lordship as cultivation of legible worlds.** **Branch evidence:** fixed `ر ب ب` B002 is repair, nurture, administration, and completion stage by stage; fixed `ع ل م` B002 is a distinguishing mark, landmark, or sign that points to something. **Structural cue:** the two roots are joined in `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, while 1:6 asks for guidance on a road and 1:7 identifies that road through differentiated human outcomes. **Role and abductive move:** `ر ب ب` supplies cultivation and `ع ل م` supplies legibility; I infer that stagewise care makes domains navigable by preserving meaningful distinctions within them. **Reading change:** the phrase depicts ongoing cultivation of orientable, sign-bearing realms rather than static possession of an undifferentiated totality.

3. **`رَبّ` as a title that entails allegiance.** **Branch evidence:** fixed `ر ب ب` B001 includes ownership, mastery, and obeyed authority, and fixed `ء ل ه` B001 identifies the worshipped one. Later 1:4 adds `م ل ك` B003, dominion, and `د ي ن` B001, obedience, before 1:5 `ع ب د` B003 enacts submissive worship. **Structural cue:** the sequence moves from third-person title to sovereign title and then to the repeated exclusive object `إياك`. **Role and abductive move:** the titles and service are explicit; I infer that exclusive service is the practical consequence of the lordship claim. **Reading change:** `رب العالمين` is not only a description of scale but a claim on the speakers’ allegiance.

## v12_reader_walks_wide reader_a: retrospective_surprises_md

The unexpected activator was 1:7 `أنعمت`: it retrospectively gave fixed `الحمد` a concrete benefaction to answer and strengthened `ح م د` B001 over a merely generic praise reading. Meanwhile, 1:6–1:7 turned `ع ل م` B002 from a dormant sign image into a route mechanism—worlds under `رب` become legible by distinctions that let travelers recognize a course and its outcomes. 1:4–1:5 also confirmed that fixed lordship develops into allegiance, not scale alone.
