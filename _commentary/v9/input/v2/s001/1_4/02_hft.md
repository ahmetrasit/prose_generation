# HFT — precomputed activation hypotheses (whole-surah window)

Each record: changed reading, mechanism, and every trace step resolved to the actual word.
Note: HFT used an older root map; a trace step on a root the gateway now withholds is an echo, not identity.

## b_sovereign_reckoning_event [baseline_model, strong]

- before: The phrase merely assigns ownership of a future date.
- after: The phrase stages a sovereign public event in which authority becomes operative as reckoning and recompense.
- mechanism: Kingship becomes executable in a marked event: the severe-event branch of day concentrates sovereignty into an occasion when reckoning and recompense are carried out.
- trace:
  - 1:4 w1 **مَٰلِكِ** (م ل ك B003: hükümdarlık ve kamusal egemenlik / المُلك والسلطان) — Kingship and public dominion supply the governing authority exercised in the event.
  - 1:4 w2 **يَوْمِ** (ي و م B003: büyük olayın yaşandığı çetin gün veya olay / كائنة اليوم وشدته) — A severe event-day turns time from a calendar label into the occasion on which authority becomes manifest.
  - 1:4 w3 **ٱلدِّينِ** (د ي ن B002: yargılayıp hesap görerek karşılığını verme / الحساب والجزاء) — Reckoning and recompense specify what the sovereign event does.

## b_debt_maturity [baseline_model, medium]

- before: Dīn is only a religious label or a judicial verdict.
- after: The focus can depict the holder of every deferred liability and of the very interval in which it comes due.

- trace:
  - 1:4 w1 **مَٰلِكِ** (م ل ك B002: sahiplik ve tasarruf yetkisi / المِلْك والتصرف) — Ownership and disposal make the focus figure the holder of the claim and its settlement.

  - 1:4 w3 **ٱلدِّينِ** (د ي ن B003: borç alıp verme ve vadeli ödeme ilişkisi / الدين المالي) — Debt and credit make the final relation one of liability coming due.
