3. **Debt, favor, and repayment.** Evidence: `د ي ن B003` debt/credit, `ن ع م B001` bestowed favor in 1:7, `ح م د B001` thanksgiving in 1:2, and `ع و ن B001` help in 1:5. ## v12_reader_walks_wide reader_a: activated_readings_md

1. **Sovereignty over a decisive sorting event.** **Branch evidence:** fixed `م ل ك` B003 is kingship and dominion, fixed `ي و م` B003 is a major or severe event-day, and fixed `د ي ن` B002 is reckoning, judgment, and recompense. **Structural cue:** 1:7 later differentiates favored people, people under wrath, and people astray. **Role and abductive move:** dominion supplies control, the event-day supplies decisive occasion, and reckoning supplies allocation; I infer that the three differentiated outcomes are consequences made visible by that reckoning. **Reading change:** the phrase presents not a date on a calendar but a controlled event in which divergent courses become consequentially sorted.

2. **`الدين` as the horizon of enacted obedience.** **Branch evidence:** fixed `د ي ن` B001 includes obedience, submission, worship, and the way one follows; 1:5 `ع ب د` B003 is worship as submissive obedience. **Structural cue:** the sovereign title is followed immediately by `إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ`, with the object fronted for exclusivity. **Role and abductive move:** the fixed branch names the relation and the next ayah enacts it; I infer that the coming “day” is already allowed to govern present allegiance. **Reading change:** `يوم الدين` can coexist with reckoning as the horizon on which submission is disclosed, while 1:5 is its present rehearsal.

3. **An exploratory debt-settlement mechanism.** **Branch evidence:** fixed `د ي ن` B003 is debt and credit, and fixed `م ل ك` B002 is ownership and authorized disposal. Earlier mercy and nurture, plus later aid and 1:7 `ن ع م` B001 favor, supply received benefit. **Structural cue:** benefaction, ownership, service, and accounting occur in a tight sequence. **Role and abductive move:** debt supplies an obligation model and ownership supplies standing to settle it; I explicitly infer the causal arrow that received favor creates a nonfinancial obligation answered by service and finally accounted for. **Reading change:** alongside judgment and obedience, `الدين` can make the day a settlement of what dependent recipients owe in response to what they were given.
