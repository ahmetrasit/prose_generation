## v12_reader_walks reader_a: activated_readings_md

1. **Mercy repeated as bridge between cosmic lordship and legal reckoning.** Lexical evidence: ر ح م B001, mercy and beneficence; ر ب ب B001/B002 in 1:2, authority and nurture; د ي ن B002 in 1:4, reckoning/recompense. Structural cue: this ayah repeats 1:1’s mercy immediately after “Lord of the worlds” and immediately before “Owner of the Day of dīn.” Assigned role: mercy is the hinge that prevents lordship from reading as bare power and reckoning from reading as bare penalty. Abductive move: the packet supplies the adjacency; I infer that repetition performs a framing function across the transition. Reading change: the ayah reads as a structural brace in the surah’s sequence, not only as a restated attribute.

2. **Mercy as kinship-like continuity across the prayer.** Lexical evidence: ر ح م B002, kinship bond; ر ح م B003, womb; ع ب د B003 in 1:5, worship/submissive obedience; ه د ي B004 in 1:6, gracious gift; ن ع م B001 in 1:7, favor. Structural cue: the prayer moves from divine names to “we worship,” “guide us,” and “those You favored.” Assigned role: mercy supplies the relational continuity that lets command, help, guidance, and favor belong to one household of dependence. Abductive move: the packet supplies kinship/womb, worship, gift, and favor; I infer a dependency-system rather than isolated attributes. Reading change: the double mercy reads as the generative bond within which the worshipping “we” can speak.

3. **Mercy as softening force against later hardening.** Lexical evidence: ر ح م B001, tenderness; ن ع م B002 in 1:7, softness/easeful living; غ ض ب B004/B005 in 1:7, hard compact rock and thick intense redness. Structural cue: tenderness and mercy precede the negative images of anger and deviation. Assigned role: mercy supplies material softness before the packet later shows hardened wrath. Abductive move: the packet supplies tenderness/softness and hardness/anger; I infer a material contrast in the moral landscape. Reading change: the ayah reads as a softening condition that keeps the path from becoming only a hard juridical surface.

## v12_reader_walks reader_b: activated_readings_md

1. **Mercy as the hinge between nurture and reckoning.** Evidence: `ر ح م B001` mercy/tenderness, preceded by `ر ب ب B002` nurture/completion in 1:2 and followed by `م ل ك B003` kingship plus `د ي ن B002` reckoning in 1:4. Mechanism: the repeated mercy sits structurally between ربّ care and ملك يوم الدين, preventing either side from being read alone. The packet supplies the sequence; I infer the mediating function. Abductive move: an attribute repeated at the transition point is meant to govern both what came before and what comes after. Reading change: the ayah becomes a hinge that carries tenderness into sovereignty and sovereignty back into care.

2. **Mercy as kinship-making around the praying “we.”** Evidence: `ر ح م B002` kinship bond and `ر ح م B003` womb; activated by the first-person plural in 1:5–1:6 and by “الذين أنعمت عليهم” in 1:7; supported by `ر ب ب B005` fostered child/step-family relation. Mechanism: the later “we/us” and “those upon whom” form a social body; mercy supplies the bond by which speakers and favored predecessors can belong together. The packet supplies kinship, womb, foster-care, and collective pronouns; I infer the family-making relation. Abductive move: a group seeking one path needs more than instruction; it needs a bond that makes shared guidance possible. Reading change: the repeated divine mercy reads as incorporation into a protected relation, not only as compassion toward isolated individuals.

3. **Exploratory: mercy includes vulnerable after-birth repair.** Evidence: `ر ح م B004` womb ailment after birth, activated by `ر ب ب B009` animal just after birth/freshness and `ر ب ب B002` stage-by-stage nurture, with later `ه د ي B008` supported walking. Mechanism: this is a weaker but visible material analogy: the surah moves from birth-like invocation into supported walking on a path; mercy includes the painful vulnerability after emergence. The packet supplies postpartum ailment, freshness after birth, nurture, and supported walking; I infer the lifecycle arc. Abductive move: if guidance is requested after naming and praise, the speaker may be newly born into dependence rather than already stable. Reading change: mercy becomes restorative care for fragile transition, not only benevolent feeling.

## v12_reader_walks_wide reader_a: activated_readings_md

1. **Mercy as the governing medium between nurture and reckoning.** **Branch evidence:** both fixed words instantiate `ر ح م` B001, tenderness, compassion, and beneficence toward the recipient. **Structural cue:** the pair stands immediately after 1:2 `رَبِّ` and immediately before 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ`; the same pair has already appeared in 1:0. **Role and abductive move:** the packet supplies the placement and repetition; I infer that the intervening mercy pair qualifies how nurture, authority, and reckoning cohere. **Reading change:** mercy becomes the operating mode of rule rather than an isolated affective attribute set beside rule.

2. **The doubled mercy as protected formation and continuing support.** **Branch evidence:** fixed `ر ح م` B003 contributes the womb as a vessel of growth, while B001 contributes active beneficence. Before it, `ر ب ب` B002 supplies stagewise nurture; after it, `ع و ن` B001, `ه د ي` B001, and `ن ع م` B001 supply help, guidance, and bestowed favor. **Structural cue:** dependent-making actions extend on both sides of the repeated pair. **Role and abductive move:** the womb is the enclosure model and the surrounding acts are the developmental process; I infer that mercy both contains and sustains that process. **Reading change:** the two forms can carry complementary spatial and temporal force: encompassing protection and care that continues through the dependent’s movement.

3. **Mercy as a bond that forms a path-community.** **Branch evidence:** fixed `ر ح م` B002 is close kinship and the maintenance of kin ties. **Structural cue:** 1:5–1:6 speak in a collective first person, and 1:7 defines the requested road by relation to people already favored rather than by geometry alone. **Role and abductive move:** kinship supplies the social bond, while the collective petition and predecessor-defined path supply its structure; exploratorily, I infer that shared reception of mercy and favor creates affiliation across the two groups. **Reading change:** the pair can establish relational continuity between present petitioners and earlier path-bearers, not only kindness to separate individuals.

## v12_reader_walks_wide reader_a: retrospective_surprises_md

1:4 made the placement of the fixed pair newly significant: mercy does not follow judgment as repair but precedes it as the medium in which sovereignty is announced. More surprisingly, the collective grammar of 1:5–1:6 and the predecessor-defined road of 1:7 activated `ر ح م` B002 as affiliation across recipients; this corrected an initial tendency to treat the two mercy forms only as intensified near-synonyms.
