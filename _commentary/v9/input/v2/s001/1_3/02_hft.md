## b-mercy-double-register [baseline_model, strong]

- before: Two near-synonymous titles both say that God is merciful.
- after: The pair stages one mercy in two temporal registers: overflowing in scope and remaining available in action.
- mechanism: The pair does more than duplicate a quality: the first form can present mercy at full reach, while the second presents it as settled and repeatedly operative. The sequence moves from amplitude to continuance.
- trace:
  - 1:3 w1,2 **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** (ر ح م B001: acıma duygusuyla esirgeyip iyilik etme / الرَّحْمَة والرقة) — Tenderness and beneficent mercy supply the shared substance, while the doubled morphology distributes it across expansive reach and durable action.

## b-mercy-as-kinship [baseline_model, medium]

- before: Mercy is a favorable disposition toward an otherwise separate recipient.
- after: Mercy creates and maintains a near bond, making the recipient one whose connection is actively kept.
- mechanism: Repeated mercy can be heard relationally: not only an emotion directed at a remote recipient, but a bond that establishes nearness and carries an obligation not to sever connection.
- trace:
  - 1:3 w1,2 **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** (ر ح م B002: yakın soy bağı / الرَّحِم والقرابة) — The kinship bond supplies durable attachment, functioning as the relational architecture beneath the two mercy epithets.

## b-mercy-as-womb [baseline_model, exploratory]

- before: Mercy arrives as a discrete gift to an already formed recipient.
- after: Mercy is the enclosing and nourishing condition within which the recipient can become and endure.
- mechanism: The two forms can carry a spatial-material model: encompassing protection together with ongoing nourishment. Mercy is then an environment in which dependent life is formed, not merely an intervention from outside.
- trace:
  - 1:3 w1,2 **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** (ر ح م B003: döl yatağı / رَحِم الأنثى) — The womb supplies an enclosing, life-forming vessel, functioning as a material analogy for mercy's encompassing and sustaining aspects.

## d-mercy-frames-accounting [context_delta, strong]

- before: Mercy is leniency opposed to judgment.
- after: Mercy names the governing disposition of the one who owns and administers the full event of accountability.
- mechanism: Ownership and disposal, a bounded duration, and reckoning place the mercy pair directly before an administered temporal judgment. Mercy does not erase accounting; it becomes the prior frame and governing manner in which accounting is held.
