## HFT reader overview

- strongest_changes: Successive time turned static trade loss into compounding depletion, while the same context root preserved a coexisting pressure-and-extraction model.
- strongest_changes: Security, intentional work, and fitting repair recast the focus state as unsecured exposure, failed conversion of capacity, and relational or functional misfit.
- strongest_changes: Repeated reciprocal transmission, reality correspondence, and sustained undertaking produced a distributed mechanism for preserving correct measure across persons and time.
- what_was_special: The focus inventories already split the human into manifest, perceiving, familiar, and represented presence, and loss into broad deficit, failed trade, and short measure. Context did not collapse those possibilities; it activated temporal, material, affective, productive, relational, and networked readings that can coexist.
- remaining_uncertainties: The context's temporal pressure may cause loss, reveal it, or merely frame it; the packet does not settle that direction.
- remaining_uncertainties: The accounting and measurement mechanisms are strongly branch-backed analogies but are not forced by syntax alone.
- remaining_uncertainties: The network model depends on reading the repeated reciprocal construction as causal preservation infrastructure rather than as a list of parallel virtues.
- remaining_uncertainties: The pupil-image and bitter-extract models remain intentionally contained outliers because their branches are vivid but form-distant.
