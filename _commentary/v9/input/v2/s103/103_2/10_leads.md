Activated by `و ص ي` B003 twice, mutual exhortation, and `ص ل ح` B002, reconciliation between people. 3. **Loss as short measure, corrected by truth.**

Structural cue: the later phrase `بالحق` follows a repeated act of mutual enjoining, suggesting correction within exchange. The packet supplies the short-measure branch and truth/verification branches. I infer that advice "to truth" functions as recalibration of the deficient scale. Reading change: the human condition can be read as being inside a regime of under-measurement: one loses, and perhaps causes loss, until truth is made the measure.

## v12_reader_walks reader_a: turkish_prose_synthesis_md

Bu yüzden `خسر` sadece "zarar" değildir; insanın, özellikle karşılıklı doğrultma ve dayanışma olmadan, eksilen bir hesaba girmesidir. Ticaret ve ölçü dalları ayeti daha keskin yapar: insan, sermayesi eriyen yahut ölçüsü eksik tutulan bir alışveriş düzeninin içindedir. `بالحق` bu eksik ölçünün standardını, `بالصبر` ise paniğe kapılmadan ve birbirine kefil olarak dayanma kuvvetini getirir. Böylece ayet, yalnız bireysel bir mahkûmiyet değil, algılaması gereken hâli algılayamayan ve sosyal bağla onarılması gereken insanın teşhisidir.

   Lexical evidence: fixed `ء ن س B001` (visible human presence over against hidden/wild beings) and `خ س ر B001` (general deficiency), activated by `ع ص ر B001` (recurring time) and `ر ح م B002` (kinship bond). Assigned role: `الإنسان` names humanity as a visible social species, while `خسر` names the erosion that time reveals in that species. Abductive move: I supply that the plural exception retroactively marks the singular generic human as a collective condition, not merely an isolated individual. Reading change: the ayah becomes a diagnosis of the human creature as publicly exposed to depletion unless a bond-system interrupts it.

2. Loss as commercial and metrological deficit.
   Lexical evidence: fixed `خ س ر B002` (trade loss) and `خ س ر B003` (shorting measure/weight), activated by `ع م ل B004` (wage/pay for work), `ع م ل B005` (dealing or transaction), `ح ق ق B002` (due obligation), and `ح ق ق B003` (particular owned right). Structural cue: loss is followed by work, right, and reciprocal enjoining; the exception reads like a correction of accounts. Assigned role: `خسر` supplies the deficit ledger; work and right supply the means of making accounts answer to what is due. Abductive move: I infer that loss is not only suffered but produced by failed measure, failed profit, or unpaid due. Reading change: `لَفِي خُسْرٍ` reads as being inside an unbalanced economy of existence, where capital, measure, and rights are all at risk.

3. Loss as damaged perception and estranged companionship.
   Lexical evidence: fixed `ء ن س B002` (perceiving into awareness by sight, hearing, or sensation), `ء ن س B003` (comfort that removes estrangement), and `خ س ر B005` (loss, ruin, baseness, weakness), activated by `ء م ن B001` (settled security/trust), `و ص ي B003` (mutual exhortation), and `ص ب ر B003` (standing with someone). Structural cue: the next ayah does not only say “do good”; it creates a reciprocal speaking-and-standing structure. Assigned role: `الإنسان` can be the perceiving/companionable being; `خسر` is the collapse of that awareness and companionship into weakness. Abductive move: I supply the relational psychology: a creature made for awareness and familiar comfort is “in loss” when those capacities fail or turn lonely. Reading change: the loss becomes existential and social estrangement, not just moral failure or negative balance.

4. Loss as enclosure under pressure.
   Lexical evidence: fixed `خ س ر B001` (diminution) with the phrase `لَفِي` as spatial cue, activated by `ع ص ر B002` (pressing until liquid runs), `ع ص ر B006` (withholding/holding back), `ص ب ر B002` (compelled holding), and `ص ب ر B006` (inescapable trouble). Structural cue: the ayah places the human “in” loss after the oath by `العصر`, then 103:3 describes a way of holding together. Assigned role: `خسر` becomes the enclosing medium; `العصر` supplies pressure; `صبر` supplies counter-holding. Abductive move: I infer that the preposition makes loss a containing condition, while the surrounding roots specify whether enclosure destroys or disciplines. Reading change: the ayah reads as a human being trapped in compressive diminishment, awaiting a different form of containment in patience and mutual charge.

1. **Loss as a specified, remediable deficit.** **Lexical evidence:** خ س ر `B001` (general deficiency), sharpened by ص ل ح `B001` (soundness against corruption) and the opening ر ح م `B001` (beneficence). **Structural cue and role:** the emphatic `إن ... لفي` encloses the generic human in deficiency, but `إلا` immediately identifies a four-part exception; deficiency supplies the diagnosed lack, and the exception supplies its missing capacities. **Abductive move:** I infer from the logical complement that trust, sound action, truth-bearing reciprocity, and restraint are what the deficient condition lacks. **Reading change:** `خسر` reads less as an unexplained terminal ruin and more as a severe but diagnostically articulated shortfall disclosed under a mercy-frame.

3. **Loss as giving less than is due.** **Lexical evidence:** خ س ر `B003` (shorting measure and weight), activated by ح ق ق `B002` (due obligation), `B003` (an owned right), ص ل ح `B002` (reconciliation), and ع م ل `B005` (human transaction). **Structural cue and role:** 103:3 places `الحق` inside doubled reciprocal counsel after sound acts; short measure supplies the social fault, due right the standard, reconciliation the repair, and mutual counsel the checking mechanism. **Abductive move:** I infer that at least one cause of the universal deficit is systematic under-delivery to reality and to other people; the packet gives no explicit scale or debtor. **Reading change:** `في خسر` acquires an ethical and legal edge: the human inhabits a regime of short measure, not only a private state of disappointment.

4. **The perceiver needs another perceiver to expose the enclosure.** **Lexical evidence:** ء ن س `B002` (detecting by sight, hearing, or inward sensation) and, exploratorily, `B005` (the human image in another's pupil), activated by و ص ي `B003` (mutual exhortation) and ح ق ق `B005` (making truth stand). **Structural cue and role:** the one capable of awareness is nevertheless `في` loss, while the exception uses reciprocal speech; perception supplies latent capacity, another person's voice or gaze supplies external correction, and truth supplies what becomes visible. **Abductive move:** I infer that enclosure distorts self-assessment and that mutual counsel functions as a social sensor or mirror. **Reading change:** `الإنسان` reads as a perceiving being whose own deficit may become perceptible only through another human's truthful intervention.

5. **Loss includes estrangement from the human social capacity.** **Lexical evidence:** ء ن س `B003` (companionship that removes estrangement), activated by ص ل ح `B002` (removing interpersonal hostility), و ص ي `B001`/`B003` (joining through mutual charge), and ص ب ر `B003` (standing with others). **Structural cue and role:** the generic singular `الإنسان` is enclosed in loss, but the exception shifts to a plural whose defining verbs include two reciprocal forms; companionship supplies a dormant human capacity, and reciprocal repair makes it operative. **Abductive move:** I infer that isolated self-sufficiency is part of the loss and that social attachment helps reverse it. **Reading change:** the statement diagnoses not only depleted individuals but a failure of human togetherness, with escape taking a necessarily communal form.
