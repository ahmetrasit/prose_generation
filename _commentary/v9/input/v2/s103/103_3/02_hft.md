# HFT — precomputed activation hypotheses (whole-surah window)

Each record: changed reading, mechanism, and every trace step resolved to the actual word.

## baseline_reciprocal_maintenance_circuit [baseline_model, strong]

- before: The ayah lists four qualities possessed by the people excepted.
- after: The ayah describes a plural feedback system in which assent becomes repair and reciprocal truth-and-restraint talk keeps that repair viable.
- mechanism: Trusting assent becomes intentional sound-making work, while the two mutual charges feed truth and restraint back through the group. The exception is therefore generated by a self-maintaining circuit rather than by four detachable merits.
- trace:
  - 103:3 w3 **ءَامَنُوا۟** (ء م ن B002: dogru sayip kabul etme / تصديق يطمئن إليه القلب) — Trusting assent supplies the inward commitment that the rest of the circuit externalizes and maintains.
  - 103:3 w4 **وَعَمِلُوا۟** (ع م ل B001: bilerek yapılan iş veya eylem / الفعل المقصود والعمل) — Intentional doing converts assent into purposive intervention rather than leaving it as a state.
  - 103:3 w5 **ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ** (ص ل ح B001: iyi ve düzgün olma; düzeltme / الصلاح ضد الفساد والطلاح) — Soundness against corruption gives the work a reparative direction.
  - 103:3 w6,8 **وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟** (و ص ي B003: birbirine öğüt veya talimat iletmek / تبادل الوصية بين القوم) — Mutual exhortation makes maintenance distributed and recurrent across members.
  - 103:3 w7 **بِٱلْحَقِّ** (ح ق ق B001: gerçekliğe uygun, kesin doğruluk / ثبات مطابق للواقع ضد الباطل) — Firm correspondence to reality supplies the circuit's orienting constraint.
  - 103:3 w9 **بِٱلصَّبْرِ** (ص ب ر B001: kendini tutarak dayanma / حبس النفس عن الجزع) — Self-restraint prevents panic or impulse from breaking the circuit under strain.
