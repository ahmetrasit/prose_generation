## v12_reader_walks reader_a: activated_readings_md

1. **Time as the container that exposes loss.**  
   Lexical evidence: `ع ص ر` B001, "age or recurring time"; activated by `خ س ر` B001/B002 in 103:2 and by the fourfold exception in 103:3. Structural cue: the oath precedes a spatial verdict, "in loss," then an exception. Assigned role: `العصر` supplies the enveloping duration in which the human condition becomes measurable. The packet supplies: time → human → loss → exception. I supply the directional arrow: recurring time is the medium that lets deficiency show itself. Reading change: the ayah is not only "by time" as a neutral swear-word; it becomes an oath by the very temporal field in which human value is depleted unless a counter-pattern appears.

2. **The press that extracts the hidden yield.**  
   Lexical evidence: `ع ص ر` B002, "pressing until liquid runs," and B007, "giving, yield, and extracted gain"; activated by `خ س ر` B002, "trade loss," and by `ع م ل` B001/B004/B005 in 103:3, work, wage, and transaction. Structural cue: 103:1 is a compact noun, then 103:2 names loss, then 103:3 names practices. Assigned role: `العصر` becomes the press; human life is the material put under pressure; faith, work, truth, and patience are the extractable yield. The packet supplies the press-branch, the loss-branch, and the work/transaction branches. I infer that pressure reveals whether a yield or a deficit results. Reading change: the oath points to a compressive test, not merely duration; what comes after it is what runs out when existence is squeezed.

3. **Withholding and rescue under scarcity.**  
   Lexical evidence: `ع ص ر` B005, "refuge, rescue, and clinging," and B006, "withholding, holding back, and reclaiming"; activated by `ء م ن` B001, secure trust, `و ص ي` B003, mutual exhortation, and `ص ب ر` B001/B003, self-restraint and suretyship. Structural cue: 103:3 is introduced by `إلا`, making a marked exit from the condition in 103:2. Assigned role: `العصر` names the constricted situation in which rescue requires attachment and disciplined holding. The packet supplies the exception and the branches of clinging, security, reciprocal charge, and restraint. I infer that the exception functions as a refuge-route out of the constriction. Reading change: the ayah can be heard as swearing by the pressure-zone in which humans are held back or rescued by what they cling to together.

4. **A weather-and-growth cycle.**  
   Lexical evidence: `ع ص ر` B003, "rain-clouds squeezed into rain," and B010, "grain enclosed in its husks"; activated by `ص ب ر` B010, "white layered cloud," `و ص ي` B004, pasture suiting grazing animals, and `ص ل ح` B003, suitability or fitness. Structural cue: the last ayah supplies ecological branches clustered around cloud, pasture, fitness, and endurance after the oath-root already offers rain-bearing clouds. Assigned role: `العصر` is the season-like compression that brings rain and encloses grain until maturity. The packet supplies all the ecological images; I supply the arrow that rain/season enables fit growth. Reading change: the oath can open a latent agricultural reading: human loss is set against a natural process in which pressure, enclosure, and patient suitability are conditions of fruitfulness.

5. **The choking morsel and the small saving sip.**  
   Lexical evidence: `ع ص ر` B008, "sipping to get a choking morsel down"; activated by `ص ب ر` B001, restraint, and `و ص ي` B003, repeated mutual advising. Structural cue: the exception is not one grand act but a sequence of held practices, with `تواصوا` repeated. Assigned role: `العصر` becomes the acute constriction, the stuck point; the later virtues act like repeated small sips that allow passage. The packet supplies the choking/sipping branch and repeated mutual counsel. I infer that repetition turns the rescue into gradual relief rather than instant escape. Reading change: the ayah’s compactness can be read as the throat-tight moment before the surah releases a measured remedy.

Later 103:3 made the refuge branch of `ع ص ر` B005 more visible than it first appeared: `تواصوا` twice does not only advise, it creates a clinging network. `ص ب ر` B008, bitter extract, also echoed back onto `ع ص ر` B002: the pressure may produce not sweetness but medicine. Finally, `ح ق ق` B011, fitted socket, and `ص ب ر` B018, stopper or plug, retrospectively made `العصر` feel less like open flowing time and more like a constrained apparatus in which things are held, pressed, and made to fit.

## v12_reader_walks reader_a: turkish_prose_synthesis_md

`العصر` burada yalnız "zaman" diye düzleşmiyor. Sonraki ayetler onu, insanın eksildiği alanı açığa çıkaran süre olarak etkinleştiriyor: zaman, kaybın içinde bulunduğu kabı ve ölçüm sahasını veriyor. Aynı kök, sıkıp öz çıkarma dalıyla da çalışıyor; insan hayatı baskı altına konmuş malzeme gibi, iman, amel, hak ve sabır ise bu baskıdan çıkabilecek verim gibi okunuyor. Bunun yanında `العصر`, daralma içinde sığınılacak bağ anlamına da kayıyor: 103:3'teki karşılıklı tavsiye, kurtuluşun tekil değil tutunmalı ve ortak olduğunu geriye doğru gösteriyor. Daha keşifsel bir katmanda, yağmur bulutu, başak kılıfı, mera ve uygunluk imgeleri `العصر`i bir yetişme mevsimi gibi de okutturuyor. En şaşırtıcı yankı ise acı ilaç ve boğazdaki lokma imgesi: sure, daralmaya küçük ama tekrarlı bir tedaviyle cevap veriyor.

## v12_reader_walks reader_b: activated_readings_md

1. Time as the recurring witness of human attrition.
   Lexical evidence: fixed `ع ص ر B001` (age, recurring time, day/night, afternoon), activated by `ء ن س B001` (visible human presence) and `خ س ر B001` (general deficiency). Structural cue: the isolated oath-noun is followed by an emphatic universal clause about the human. Assigned role: `العصر` supplies the temporal field in which the human condition is observed. Abductive move: I supply the evidentiary arrow, that recurring time is not only the object sworn by but the witness that exposes deficiency. Reading change: the ayah reads less as “time is important” and more as “the repeated passage of time visibly proves the human’s diminishing condition.”

2. Time as a press that extracts either loss or yield.
   Lexical evidence: fixed `ع ص ر B002` (pressing until liquid runs) and `ع ص ر B007` (giving, yield, extracted gain), activated by `خ س ر B002` (trade loss), `ع م ل B004` (wage/pay for work), and `ع م ل B005` (transactions between people). Structural cue: the sequence moves from `العصر` to `خسر` and then to acts, works, and mutual obligations. Assigned role: `العصر` becomes the press; human life and work are the material pressed; loss or gain is the extracted result. Abductive move: I supply the economic-material conversion, that the later vocabulary of loss/work lets the pressure image become an accounting of yield. Reading change: the oath becomes a pressure-test: time squeezes human existence until its real profit, deficiency, or waste appears.

3. Time as constriction that also names the needed refuge.
   Lexical evidence: fixed `ع ص ر B005` (refuge, rescue, clinging) and `ع ص ر B006` (withholding/holding back), activated by `و ص ي B001` (joining one thing to another), `و ص ي B003` (mutual exhortation), and `ص ب ر B001` (self-restraint from panic). Structural cue: a terse oath is followed by being “in” loss, then by an exception formed through collective attachment. Assigned role: `العصر` supplies both the compressive danger and the lexical possibility of resort. Abductive move: I infer that the same pressure that exposes loss also forces the search for a clinging-place, and that the exception describes that place socially. Reading change: `العصر` no longer names neutral duration; it names a crisis-space in which rescue requires attachment, not escape from time.

4. A rain-cloud/irrigation reading of the oath.
   Lexical evidence: fixed `ع ص ر B003` (rain-clouds squeezed into rain), activated by basmalah `س م و B004` (overhead sky/cover, cloud, rain), `ر ح م B001` (beneficence), `ص ل ح B003` (suitability/fitness), and `ص ب ر B010` (white layered cloud). Structural cue: the opening has an overhead divine-merciful frame, and the closing lists practices that can make human ground receptive and sound. Assigned role: `العصر` is the rain-bearing compression, while faith/work/right counsel/patience are the cultivated receptivity that can receive yield rather than loss. Abductive move: I supply the ecological arrow, that mercy from above and pressure in the cloud can become beneficial only where the human field is made fit. Reading change: the oath can be heard as more than late time: it also evokes compressed cloud and rain, making the surah’s rescue-pattern agricultural and formative.

## v12_reader_walks reader_b: retrospective_surprises_md

The later ayat pushed `العصر` away from a single time-gloss. 103:2’s `خسر` activated the press/accounting readings (`ع ص ر B002`, `ع ص ر B007`) because loss looked like extracted deficit. 103:3’s repeated mutual `و ص ي` and final `صبر` then made `ع ص ر B005` unexpectedly central: time is not only what consumes, but the condition in which refuge and clinging become legible. The cloud/rain reading remained exploratory, but the basmalah’s `س م و B004` plus `ص ب ر B010` made it more than a loose image.

## v12_reader_walks_wide reader_a: activated_readings_md

1. **Recurring time as an accumulating ledger.** **Lexical evidence:** ع ص ر `B001` (age or recurring time). **Structural cue and role:** the sworn noun precedes the emphatic generalization of 103:2, while 103:3 answers with a coordinated sequence of intentional and repeatedly reciprocal acts; recurring time is the medium in which both depletion and repair accumulate. **Abductive move:** the packet supplies succession and repeated forms, while I infer that recurrence makes omissions and corrective acts compound. **Reading change:** `العصر` denotes not merely a date or late hour but the succession that continuously registers the human trajectory.

2. **A press that exposes yield or deficit.** **Lexical evidence:** ع ص ر `B002` (pressing until liquid runs) and `B007` (extracted yield or gain), activated by خ س ر `B002` (trade loss), ع م ل `B001` (intentional work), and ع م ل `B004` (wage for work). **Structural cue and role:** pressure is named first, commercial deficit next, and labor-like corrective outputs last; the press supplies the process, human action the input, and sound deeds and counsel the possible yield. **Abductive move:** the causal arrow “time-pressure extracts what a life actually produces” is inferred from this order and branch convergence. **Reading change:** the oath invokes an assay-like process: under pressure, the human either yields a return or is disclosed as consuming capital without profit.

3. **The sworn term contains its own refuge branch.** **Lexical evidence:** ع ص ر `B005` (refuge, rescue, and clinging), activated by ء م ن `B001` (security), و ص ي `B001` (joining), and ص ب ر `B003` (standing with or guaranteeing another). **Structural cue and role:** `إلا` opens an escape from the enclosing `في خسر`, and the exception is plural and reciprocal; the ع ص ر branch supplies the refuge-image, while trust, joining, and solidarity construct it. **Abductive move:** I infer that 103:3 instantiates the latent refuge in the noun sworn by; the packet does not explicitly equate the exception with `العصرة`. **Reading change:** `والعصر` can signal both the condition that presses humanity and the possibility of clinging rescue within that condition.

4. **Pressure can release life-giving rain.** **Lexical evidence:** ع ص ر `B003` (rain-clouds squeezed into rain), activated by س م و `B004` (overhead cloud and rain), ر ح م `B001` (beneficence), and ص ل ح `B001` (soundness and doing good). **Structural cue and role:** the opening supplies an overhead mercy-frame, the oath supplies compressed rain-bearing material, and the exception supplies beneficial human output. **Abductive move:** exploratorily, I treat rain released from pressure as a material analogue for sound action released from temporal pressure; only the images and sequence, not that analogy, are packet-supplied. **Reading change:** the oath need not image pressure as purely destructive: it can also name the condition under which withheld benefit is released.
