## HFT reader overview

- remaining_uncertainties: The antecedent and precise instrumental role of بِهِۦ remain unresolved; dust-enabled ingress is an abductive option, not a grammatical settlement.
- remaining_uncertainties: جَمْعًا can support a target host, a collected state, concentrated motion, or specialized lexical images; the packet does not force these into one syntactic reading.
- remaining_uncertainties: The direction from sparks, dust, desire, or later extraction back into the focus is reader-inferred even where the branch sequence is tight.
- remaining_uncertainties: Split-root and specialized-branch outliers are legitimate activations but carry substantial form and domain distance; they should remain explicitly contained.
- remaining_uncertainties: The physical, strategic, ethical, and epistemic models compete in places, but no packet evidence requires selecting one and deleting the others.
- strongest_changes: Neutral center-entry became an exertive boundary-overrun when running and transgression activated the distinction between perimeter and interior.
- strongest_changes: Latent fire and striking changed already-given motion into generated, released, and concentrated force; alternate branches changed the same clause into execution of a concealed plan.
- strongest_changes: Stirred dust plus adjacent repeated بِهِۦ activated a self-generated medium of ingress that can both conceal the movers and leave a witnessing trace.
- strongest_changes: The turn to heart-attachment, beneficial good, and binding intensity made the outer collective center coexist with an inner accumulative center occupied by desire.
- strongest_changes: Later uncovering, scattering, collection-until-result, kernel extraction, and knowledge of interiors reversed the focus topology and recast center-entry as an assay-like operation.
- what_was_special: The focus remained productively polyphonic: tactical penetration, concentrated discharge, strategic execution, environmental self-concealment, relational fracture, affective accumulation, reversible gather/scatter, and inner extraction all remained live. Accepted non-dominant mappings were especially productive: ء ث ر made dust a sign, ع و د made ingress recurrent, and ر ب و supported a contained ecological outlier. Specialized focus branches also exposed faultline, hydrological, projectile-container, botanical, and gestational structures without promoting them to direct translations.
