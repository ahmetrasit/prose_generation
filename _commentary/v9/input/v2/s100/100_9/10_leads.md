The operations are disassembly of a concealed aggregate followed by synthesis of its result. The packet supplies the two passive operations; I infer that they are consecutive stages of one audit. `بُعْثِرَ` thus reads as discriminating exposure through dispersal, not merely indiscriminate destruction.
