- strongest_changes: 100:5 gives the plume an inverse geometry: dust disperses outward while the agents penetrate inward into a gathered center.
- strongest_changes: 100:7 and 100:9 convert the non-dominant trace activation into witness and forensic guidance, allowing dust to conceal direct sight while revealing passage.

- strongest_changes: 100:11 makes concealment observer-relative: the plume can veil bodies without erasing their trace or blocking knowledge of inward reality.
