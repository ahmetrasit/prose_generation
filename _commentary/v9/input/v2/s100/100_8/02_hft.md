## HFT reader overview

- remaining_uncertainties: The construction permits live competition among intensity, firm attachment, forceful pursuit, and miserliness; the packet does not force one predicate function.
- remaining_uncertainties: خَيْرِ may remain general benefit, selected betterment, or transferable gift, and each scope changes the social mechanism.
- remaining_uncertainties: The kinetic context clearly energizes the focus, but assigning the gathered center as the object-domain of desire is an explicit reader inference.
- remaining_uncertainties: The spark, barren-seed, coaxing-lure, and non-dominant split-root readings are traceable but intentionally exploratory and should not be lexicalized as settled senses.
- strongest_changes: شَدِيدٌ changed from a scalar adjective into the firmness, exertion, and at times miserliness of an attachment mechanism.
- strongest_changes: The 100:6 gift relation changed love of khayr into an asymmetry: clinging to received benefit while cutting reciprocity with its nurturing source.
- strongest_changes: The adjacent شَهِيدٌ / شَدِيدٌ frames changed private attachment into a witnessing mark of the subject's chosen orientation.
- strongest_changes: The 100:9-11 exposure sequence changed حُبِّ into a buried heart-kernel that is extracted from the source of actions and made inwardly known.
- what_was_special: The most productive activations were not broad themes but reversals of function: movement became motive, gift became a severed relation, severity became evidence, and an inward heart-kernel became an excavated cause. Accepted split mappings also preserved live minority models of recurrence, monopolization, and surplus detached from nurture.
