## BSL-01-direct-presence [baseline_model, strong]

- before: An unspecified subject is called a witness concerning an unspecified matter.
- after: The subject is present to the matter indicated by ذَٰلِكَ and witnesses it from direct proximity.
- mechanism: The witness is present with the matter and sees it directly. Its force comes from participation or immediate observation, not from receiving a report.
- trace:
  - 100:7 w **?** (ش ه د B001: hazır bulunup görme / الحضور مع المشاهدة) — 

## BSL-02-knowledge-testimony [baseline_model, strong]

- before: The predicate may mean only that the subject has seen something.
- after: The subject bears articulate, knowledge-based testimony concerning that matter.
- mechanism: Knowledge is converted into a decisive declaration, acknowledgement, or proof-bearing judgment. The witness does not merely perceive; it makes what is known evidentially available.
- trace:
  - 100:7 w **?** (ش ه د B002: bilgiye dayalı tanıklık / البيان بعلم) — 

## BSL-03-visible-expression [baseline_model, medium]

- before: The subject deliberately reports what it knows.
- after: The subject's own expression can speak evidentially about the subject, even before deliberate confession.
- mechanism: An expression can testify about its source independently of a formal declaration. The subject's outward articulation is therefore capable of exposing the subject.
- trace:

## BSL-04-witnessing-sign [baseline_model, medium]

- after: The subject can be a standing evidentiary sign whose condition testifies without a separate speech act.
- mechanism: A visible condition functions indexically: it points beyond itself to the state that produced it. Witnesshood can thus reside in what the subject is or displays, not only in what the subject says.

## CTX-09-two-tier-knowing [context_delta, strong]

- before: Focus-only grammar leaves open whether the pronominal witness is the subject under discussion or an external knower.
- after: The context weights the human as self-witness and reserves explicitly named, inwardly comprehensive expertise for the Lord; the two readings coexist as nested rather than interchangeable.
- mechanism: The final ayah explicitly names the Lord as sovereign and attributes knowledge of the report and the matter's interior to Him. This differentiates two epistemic levels: the human is witness through self-presence, expression, or trace, while the Lord comprehends the inward reality of that witness. A divine subject for 100:7 remains grammatically imaginable in isolation but is contextually weakened rather than silently erased.

## HFT reader overview

- remaining_uncertainties: The exact antecedent of the pronoun and scope of ذَٰلِكَ are not fixed by the focus alone; context strongly weights the human and the 100:6 condition but does not make every alternative formally impossible.
- remaining_uncertainties: It remains open whether self-witness is conscious acknowledgement, involuntary embodied disclosure, an external forensic sign, or several of these at once.
- remaining_uncertainties: The present predicate may describe evidence already legible or evidence only later exposed by the events of 100:9-100:10.
- remaining_uncertainties: The counted-recurrence, sterile-spark, comb, and emergence readings are packet-anchored analogical activations, not proposed lexical replacements for شَهِيدٌ.
- strongest_changes: شَهِيدٌ moved from a conscious observer-speaker to self-generated evidence: panting, altered matter, residue, attachment, and conduct can all testify.
- strongest_changes: The immediate 100:6 relation revised the focus into self-indictment: a perceiving recipient's severance from nurture is itself evidence of ingratitude.
- strongest_changes: 100:9-100:10 added temporal latency and extraction: witness-status can persist while buried or inward, then become public through uncovering and consolidation.
- strongest_changes: 100:11 differentiated human-scale self-witness from the Lord's knowledge of both report and inward reality, preserving nested epistemic levels.
- what_was_special: The focus root's sign, tongue, comb, and emergence branches let one short predicate support several coexisting mechanisms: direct presence, articulate testimony, involuntary expression, forensic trace, inner archive, and material containment. The packet's non-dominant mappings added a live cumulative-pattern reading rather than noise, while the strongest contextual shift made the subject not merely a witness who speaks but a bearer whose body, habits, desire, surroundings, and interior can speak.
