## HFT reader overview

- remaining_uncertainties: The packet does not force a single referent for the witness in 100:7, so self-attestation and Rabb's expert testimony remain genuinely competitive.
- remaining_uncertainties: The arrows from opening motion to human severance, and from material exhumation to inward moral disclosure, are reader inferences rather than packet-stated equations.
- remaining_uncertainties: Agricultural and collection models are strongly cross-anchored but remain analogical; the packet does not specify that the human literally is soil, seed, residue, or an account.
- remaining_uncertainties: The lots, breath-swelling, and contagion models depend on remote or non-dominant branch activations and require explicit containment in any downstream rendering.
- strongest_changes: Kanūd changed from a static label into enacted and habituated severance: motion penetrates a gathered whole, while recurring benefit receives no relational return.
- strongest_changes: 100:8 changed ingratitude from absence of love into displaced attachment: love tightens around useful gift and can become avaricious while the giver-bond is cut.
- strongest_changes: 100:9-11 changed an outward moral judgment into an excavation of the inward source from which acts issue.
- strongest_changes: The barren-land branch expanded into a full nurture-seed-cultivation-yield mechanism rather than remaining a decorative metaphor.
- strongest_changes: Self-witness and divine expert witness remain coexisting readings; neither was collapsed into the other.
- strongest_changes: Gathering, scattering, and recollection changed cutting into fragmentation that is later totaled and made visible.
- what_was_special: The focus inventories already contain unusually productive tensions—social familiarity versus separation, nurture/growth versus barren soil, perception versus denial, and a split Rabb mapping. The ordered context then supplies motion, ignition, concealment, witness, gift-binding, burial, extraction, and repeated Rabb, allowing several causal models and a few branch-distant outliers to coexist without selecting one final interpretation.
