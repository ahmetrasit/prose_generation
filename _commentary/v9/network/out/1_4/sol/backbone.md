## 1. Surah-level arguments (HFT mechanisms) [M]

- **M1** b_sovereign_reckoning_event — The phrase stages a sovereign public event in which authority becomes operative as reckoning and recompense.
  - mechanism: Kingship becomes executable in a marked event: the severe-event branch of day concentrates sovereignty into an occasion when reckoning and recompense are carried out.
  - words: 1:4 مَٰلِكِ (م ل ك B003); 1:4 يَوْمِ (ي و م B003); 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ (د ي ن B002)
- **M2** b_debt_maturity — The focus can depict the holder of every deferred liability and of the very interval in which it comes due.
  - mechanism: Ownership, an open span of time, and deferred debt form a maturity mechanism: the owner controls both the liability and the interval at whose end it is settled.
  - words: 1:4 مَٰلِكِ (م ل ك B002); 1:4 يَوْمِ (ي و م B002); 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ (د ي ن B003)
- **M3** b_obedience_regime — It can also name sovereignty over every duration constituted by worship, allegiance, and followed law.
  - mechanism: Sovereignty extends across a duration organized by obedience, worship, and law; the phrase can name an enacted regime rather than only a terminal courtroom.
  - words: 1:4 مَٰلِكِ (م ل ك B003); 1:4 يَوْمِ (ي و م B002); 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ (د ي ن B001)
- **M4** b_mainstay_of_customary_order — Mālik can be the mainstay that keeps a temporal, customary, and civic order coherent.
  - mechanism: The owner is read systemically as what holds an order together; time is the field, and dīn is the repeated custom or organized authority sustained within it.
  - words: 1:4 مَٰلِكِ (م ل ك B001); 1:4 مَٰلِكِ (م ل ك B005); 1:4 يَوْمِ (ي و م B002); 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ (د ي ن B005); 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ (د ي ن B006)
- **M5** d_named_worshipped_jurisdiction — The named worshipped one is sovereign of an order already enacted whenever allegiance and worship are given.
  - mechanism: The preceding act of designation identifies the sovereign as the worshipped one; this brings the obedience sense of dīn forward and makes the focus a named jurisdiction of worship.
  - words: 1:4 مَٰلِكِ (م ل ك B003); 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ (د ي ن B001); 1:1 بِسْمِ (س م و B005); 1:1 ٱللَّهِ (ء ل ه B001)
- **M6** d_mercy_framed_account — The same exact reckoning is held within repeatedly asserted tenderness and relational nearness.
  - mechanism: Repeated tenderness and kinship surround the move into ownership and reckoning, recasting exact account as an exercise of relational authority rather than affectless possession.
  - words: 1:4 مَٰلِكِ (م ل ك B002); 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ (د ي ن B002); 1:1 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ (ر ح م B001); 1:3 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ (ر ح م B002)
- **M7** d_praiseworthy_valuation — It is also the terminal valuation in which praiseworthiness and blameworthiness become manifest.
  - mechanism: Praise versus blame and the image of a praised ultimate end turn reckoning into valuation: the event discloses what is worthy and carries the account toward an evaluative end.
  - words: 1:4 يَوْمِ (ي و م B003); 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ (د ي ن B002); 1:2 ٱلْحَمْدُ (ح م د B001); 1:2 ٱلْحَمْدُ (ح م د B004)
- **M8** d_cultivated_maturity — The sovereign is also the mainstay of the developmental interval through which the account itself is brought to maturity.
  - mechanism: Nurture, completion, and growth turn the debt model into a developmental process: the owner sustains the interval in which persons and liabilities mature before settlement.
  - words: 1:4 مَٰلِكِ (م ل ك B005); 1:4 يَوْمِ (ي و م B002); 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ (د ي ن B003); 1:2 رَبِّ (ر ب ب B002); 1:2 رَبِّ (ر ب ب B005)
- **M9** d_legible_disclosed_account — The owned event is itself a disclosure in which hidden identities, conditions, and values become distinguishably known.
  - mechanism: Visible marking and disclosure turn the event of reckoning into an epistemic operation: what was only possessed or recorded becomes recognizable, distinguishable, and known.
  - words: 1:4 مَٰلِكِ (م ل ك B002); 1:4 يَوْمِ (ي و م B003); 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ (د ي ن B002); 1:1 بِسْمِ (س م و B001); 1:2 ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ (ع ل م B001); 1:2 ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ (ع ل م B002)

  - words: 1:4 مَٰلِكِ (م ل ك B002); 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ (د ي ن B001); 1:5 نَعْبُدُ (ع ب د B003); 1:5 نَعْبُدُ (ع ب د B006)

  - mechanism: The creditor-like owner is also the source of assistance, creating a non-zero-sum account in which the one to whom liability is owed supplies help for meeting it.
  - words: 1:4 مَٰلِكِ (م ل ك B002); 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ (د ي ن B003); 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ (ع و ن B001)
- **M12** d_guiding_owner_of_approach — The owner can also be encountered as leader and controller of the main course by which subjects approach the account.

  - words: 1:4 مَٰلِكِ (م ل ك B006); 1:4 مَٰلِكِ (م ل ك B008); 1:4 يَوْمِ (ي و م B003); 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ (د ي ن B002); 1:6 ٱهْدِنَا (ه د ي B001); 1:6 ٱهْدِنَا (ه د ي B003); 1:6 ٱلصِّرَٰطَ (ص ر ط B001)
- **M13** d_rectifying_appraisal — Reckoning becomes a transparent act of appraisal against a stable standard and restoration to proportionate measure.

  - words: 1:4 مَٰلِكِ (م ل ك B005); 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ (د ي ن B002); 1:6 ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ (ق و م B008); 1:6 ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ (ق و م B010); 1:6 ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ (ق و م B015)

  - words: 1:4 مَٰلِكِ (م ل ك B003); 1:4 يَوْمِ (ي و م B003); 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ (د ي ن B002); 1:7 أَنْعَمْتَ (ن ع م B001); 1:7 غَيْرِ (غ ي ر B005); 1:7 ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ (غ ض ب B001); 1:7 ٱلضَّآلِّينَ (ض ل ل B003)

  - words: 1:4 مَٰلِكِ (م ل ك B003); 1:4 يَوْمِ (ي و م B003); 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ (د ي ن B005); 1:7 غَيْرِ (غ ي ر B003)
