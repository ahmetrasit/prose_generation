## 1. Surah-level arguments (HFT mechanisms) [M]

- **M1** b1_praise_as_tested_verdict — A considered verdict that the worshipped Lord proves praiseworthy across the full scope of lordship.
  - mechanism: Hamd is both praise opposed to blame and the finding of something satisfactory after testing. Joined to divine lordship, it reads as an evaluative verdict grounded in the Lord's relation to all worlds, not as unexamined applause.
  - words: 1:2 ٱلْحَمْدُ (ح م د B001); 1:2 ٱلْحَمْدُ (ح م د B002); 1:2 لِلَّهِ (ء ل ه B001); 1:2 رَبِّ (ر ب ب B001)
- **M2** b2_stagewise_world_cultivation — The Lord continually raises, repairs, differentiates, and completes many developing worlds.
  - mechanism: The dominant Rabb branch supplies repair, nurture, and completion, while the non-dominant mapped growth branch supplies increase and growing up. The worlds are therefore not merely possessed domains but developing orders brought toward completion stage by stage.
  - words: 1:2 رَبِّ (ر ب ب B002); 1:2 رَبِّ (ر ب ب B005); 1:2 ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ (ع ل م B002); 1:2 ٱلْحَمْدُ (ح م د B001)
- **M3** b3_worlds_as_legibility_field — Alamin also presents a field of differentiating signs through which caring lordship can be known and praised.
  - mechanism: Alamin activates both disclosure to a knower and a mark that points beyond itself. Rabbani knowledge formed by care links that legibility to lordly cultivation, so the worlds function as disclosed signs through which lordship becomes recognizable.
  - words: 1:2 ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ (ع ل م B001); 1:2 ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ (ع ل م B002); 1:2 رَبِّ (ر ب ب B003); 1:2 ٱلْحَمْدُ (ح م د B002)
- **M4** b4_credit_claim_reversal — The clause also interrupts human claims to own others' gratitude by returning the ground of every benefaction to the Lord.
  - mechanism: A live Hamd branch describes claiming praise over others through benefaction. Assigning hamd to God reverses that social economy: the ultimate nurturer does not leave creatures able to convert derivative favors into absolute claims of credit over one another.
  - words: 1:2 ٱلْحَمْدُ (ح م د B005); 1:2 لِلَّهِ (ء ل ه B001); 1:2 رَبِّ (ر ب ب B016)
- **M5** c1_named_and_marked_praise — Prior naming marks the addressee and makes the sign-bearing worlds readable enough for praise to become recognition and invocation.
  - mechanism: The preceding name is both an indication and, through a split mapping, a visible mark; the repeated divine name is also an invocation formula. Together they turn the focus declaration into recognition made possible by prior naming and marking.
  - words: 1:2 ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ (ع ل م B002); 1:2 ٱلْحَمْدُ (ح م د B002); 1:1 بِسْمِ (س م و B005); 1:1 بِسْمِ (س م و B001); 1:1 ٱللَّهِ (ء ل ه B002)
- **M6** c2_gestational_lordship — Rabb's maturation of the worlds can be heard as merciful, enclosing formation that protects difference while bringing dependents to completion.
  - mechanism: Mercy, kinship, and the womb recast Rabb's repair and growth as protected formation from within an enclosing relation. The plural worlds can coexist as differently developing dependents rather than merely governed territories.
  - words: 1:2 رَبِّ (ر ب ب B002); 1:2 رَبِّ (ر ب ب B005); 1:2 ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ (ع ل م B002); 1:3 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ (ر ح م B001); 1:3 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ (ر ح م B003)
- **M7** c3_praise_at_the_end_of_account — Praise anticipates the verdict of a complete history in which every outstanding claim is finally accounted for.
  - mechanism: Ownership, an extended temporal span, and accounting or recompense make the focus praise eschatological and forensic. Hamd as finding something satisfactory after testing becomes a verdict capable of looking across the whole process and its settlement.
  - words: 1:2 ٱلْحَمْدُ (ح م د B002); 1:2 رَبِّ (ر ب ب B001); 1:4 مَٰلِكِ (م ل ك B002); 1:4 يَوْمِ (ي و م B002); 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ (د ي ن B002); 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ (د ي ن B003)
- **M8** c4_praise_performed_as_dependence — The speakers perform that praise by entering exclusive service, asking for backing, and placing themselves under watchful care.
  - mechanism: Worship and sought aid convert nominal praise into enacted dependence. The non-dominant eye mapping adds watchful care, so Rabb's nurturance is not only assistance supplied on demand but attentive preservation of the worshippers.
  - words: 1:2 لِلَّهِ (ء ل ه B001); 1:2 رَبِّ (ر ب ب B002); 1:5 نَعْبُدُ (ع ب د B003); 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ (ع و ن B001); 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ (ع و ن B003)
- **M9** c5_worlds_as_navigable_signs — The worlds form a navigable sign-field whose aligned route is continually indicated, repaired, and upheld by their Lord.
  - mechanism: The focus world's distinguishing marks become landmarks on a gently indicated road. Straightness and structural support make Rabb's cultivation navigable: praise responds not only to a created field of signs but to a maintained route through it.
  - words: 1:2 ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ (ع ل م B002); 1:2 رَبِّ (ر ب ب B002); 1:6 ٱهْدِنَا (ه د ي B001); 1:6 ٱلصِّرَٰطَ (ص ر ط B001); 1:6 ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ (ق و م B008); 1:6 ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ (ق و م B009)
- **M10** c6_praise_as_differential_diagnosis — Praise is a discriminating judgment that recognizes successful nurture while refusing to confuse hardening or lost orientation with the same outcome.
  - mechanism: Benefaction, explicit exclusion, hardening, and hiddenness make Hamd's opposition to blame diagnostic. Nurture produces recognizable good condition; its negative contrasts appear as hardened or lost legibility, so praise discriminates among trajectories rather than flattening every condition into approval.
  - words: 1:2 ٱلْحَمْدُ (ح م د B001); 1:2 ٱلْحَمْدُ (ح م د B002); 1:2 ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ (ع ل م B002); 1:7 أَنْعَمْتَ (ن ع م B001); 1:7 غَيْرِ (غ ي ر B005); 1:7 ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ (غ ض ب B004); 1:7 ٱلضَّآلِّينَ (ض ل ل B002)
- **M11** o1_ecological_water_cycle — Exploratorily, praise rises from a world-system in which gathered water, layered cloud, rain, wind, and vegetative marks circulate as forms of continuous nurture.
