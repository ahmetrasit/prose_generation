## 1. Surah-level arguments (HFT mechanisms) [M]

- **M1** base_embodied_route — The verse makes a favored human trajectory the road's visible embodiment and distinguishes oppositional rupture from aimless deviation.
  - mechanism: The verse does not identify a road by geometry alone. It makes bestowed well-being visible in a recipient group, so their lived trajectory becomes the road's positive continuity; opposition and deviation are rival ways of leaving that continuity.
  - words: 1:7 صِرَٰطَ (ص ر ط B001); 1:7 أَنْعَمْتَ (ن ع م B001); 1:7 غَيْرِ (غ ي ر B005); 1:7 ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ (غ ض ب B003); 1:7 ٱلضَّآلِّينَ (ض ل ل B001)
  - touches hubs: L1
- **M2** base_successful_and_failed_disappearance — Both right passage and straying can involve disappearance; the decisive difference is whether disappearance completes directed transit or erases recoverable direction.
  - mechanism: Two kinds of disappearance coexist. A soft, capacious passage can carry a traveler through so completely that the traveler vanishes into successful transit, whereas straying can also make something vanish but without destination or recoverability. Compact hardness acts as the obstructing condition that separates the two.
  - words: 1:7 صِرَٰطَ (ص ر ط B002); 1:7 أَنْعَمْتَ (ن ع م B002); 1:7 ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ (غ ض ب B004); 1:7 ٱلضَّآلِّينَ (ض ل ل B002)
  - touches hubs: L1
- **M3** base_decisive_partition — The verse can stage a decisive partition among favorable passage, retaliatory exposure, and an unresolved loss that never arrives for settlement.
  - mechanism: The road can image a decisive cut that separates outcomes. The exception pivot opens an alternative to retaliatory settlement, while the final group represents what never reaches settlement because it has gone unaccounted for.
  - words: 1:7 صِرَٰطَ (ص ر ط B003); 1:7 غَيْرِ (غ ي ر B002); 1:7 ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ (غ ض ب B001); 1:7 ٱلضَّآلِّينَ (ض ل ل B003)
- **M4** base_agency_gradient — They encode different causal positions: actively furthered recipients, people bearing relational opposition, and agents continuing to deviate.
  - mechanism: The three groups differ not only in outcome but in causal grammar. Favor actively carries recipients further; the angered group bears a condition produced in a relation of opposition; the strayers enact continuing deviation. The road is therefore a pattern of agency distribution.
  - words: 1:7 أَنْعَمْتَ (ن ع م B010); 1:7 ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ (غ ض ب B003); 1:7 ٱلضَّآلِّينَ (ض ل ل B001); 1:7 غَيْرِ (غ ي ر B003)
