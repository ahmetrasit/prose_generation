## 1. Surah-level arguments (HFT mechanisms) [M]

- **M1** baseline_exclusive_service_and_support — Our submission is directed only to you, and even the backing by which we sustain it is sought only from you.
  - mechanism: Submissive service and requested backing form a two-part orientation: allegiance is restricted to the addressee, then the efficacy needed to continue that allegiance is restricted to the same addressee.
  - words: 1:5 نَعْبُدُ (ع ب د B003); 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ (ع و ن B001)
- **M2** baseline_dependent_agency — The speakers accept non-sovereign status while asking for the capacity by which dependent service can genuinely act.
  - mechanism: Owned status removes sovereign self-possession without removing action; seeking backing then supplies caught-up strength. The result is agency exercised from dependence, not passivity.
  - words: 1:5 نَعْبُدُ (ع ب د B001); 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ (ع و ن B005)
- **M3** baseline_honoring_service — Worship is also active magnification of the addressee, sustained by aid the speakers do not claim to originate.
  - mechanism: Service is not exhausted by self-lowering: it raises and honors its object. Assistance prevents this magnifying service from being construed as a self-generated achievement.
  - words: 1:5 نَعْبُدُ (ع ب د B006); 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ (ع و ن B001)
- **M4** delta_named_merciful_address — The doubled 'you' points back to an already named, worshipped, merciful addressee, so aid is requested within a disclosed relation rather than from an abstract power.
  - mechanism: Prior naming and identification resolve the repeated deictic object, while tenderness changes assistance from generic reinforcement into backing governed by the disclosed character of the one addressed.
  - words: 1:5 نَعْبُدُ (ع ب د B003); 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ (ع و ن B001); 1:1 بِسْمِ (س م و B005); 1:1 ٱللَّهِ (ء ل ه B001); 1:1 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ (ر ح م B001)
- **M5** delta_enclosed_dependency — The speakers act from within a dependence that also holds and forms them, so service emerges from sustained life rather than naked coercion.
  - mechanism: The womb branch recasts dependence as life held and formed within a sustaining enclosure. This cross-pressures the owned-status baseline without erasing its asymmetry.
  - words: 1:5 نَعْبُدُ (ع ب د B001); 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ (ع و ن B001); 1:3 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ (ر ح م B003)
- **M6** delta_agency_without_self_credit — The speakers own the act of service while refusing to own its ultimate credit or capacity.
  - mechanism: Prior praise relocates credit before the speakers announce their own verbs. The help clause then prevents 'we worship' from becoming self-congratulation.
  - words: 1:5 نَعْبُدُ (ع ب د B003); 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ (ع و ن B001); 1:2 ٱلْحَمْدُ (ح م د B001); 1:2 ٱلْحَمْدُ (ح م د B005)
- **M7** delta_formed_for_service — The servants are still being repaired, nourished, and brought into the strength by which their service becomes possible.
  - mechanism: Repair, completion, growth, and nourishment turn aid into a developmental process. Service is the activity of beings continually brought toward operative form, not a task performed after formation is complete.
  - words: 1:5 نَعْبُدُ (ع ب د B003); 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ (ع و ن B005); 1:2 رَبِّ (ر ب ب B002); 1:2 رَبِّ (ر ب ب B005)
- **M8** delta_covenantal_pair — The verse can be heard as a compact-shaped utterance: we commit exclusive service and seek the backing required to keep that commitment.
  - mechanism: A compact branch activates the two focus clauses as a relational pair: service is voiced as commitment, and backing is voiced as the provision without which that commitment cannot be kept.
  - words: 1:5 نَعْبُدُ (ع ب د B003); 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ (ع و ن B001); 1:2 رَبِّ (ر ب ب B011)
- **M9** delta_accountable_obedience — Worship is obedience carried under answerability toward a future settlement, and help is sought for the endurance that trajectory requires.
  - mechanism: Possession and disposal, a bounded severe event, and reckoning place present service under a future settlement. Assistance becomes what is needed for obedience to endure to that horizon.
  - words: 1:5 نَعْبُدُ (ع ب د B003); 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ (ع و ن B001); 1:4 مَٰلِكِ (م ل ك B002); 1:4 يَوْمِ (ي و م B003); 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ (د ي ن B002)
- **M10** delta_service_as_path_work — Worship is also collective route-making and traversal, with help sought to keep that practiced way passable and straight.
  - mechanism: The focus root's material passability branch meets explicit route, guidance, and straightness branches. Service becomes path-work: repeated practice makes a route traversable, while aid keeps the traversal directed and balanced.
  - words: 1:5 نَعْبُدُ (ع ب د B005); 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ (ع و ن B001); 1:6 ٱهْدِنَا (ه د ي B001); 1:6 ٱلصِّرَٰطَ (ص ر ط B001); 1:6 ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ (ق و م B008)
- **M11** delta_maintained_uprightness — We ask to be continuously upheld and gently corrected so that exclusive service remains standing.
  - mechanism: Guidance supplies direction and upright care supplies continuous preservation. The help clause therefore concerns maintenance of a standing practice, not only rescue at moments of incapacity.
  - words: 1:5 نَعْبُدُ (ع ب د B003); 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ (ع و ن B001); 1:6 ٱهْدِنَا (ه د ي B001); 1:6 ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ (ق و م B004)
- **M12** delta_help_as_gift_not_wage — Help and guidance are unearned gifts that make service possible, not compensation generated by service.
  - mechanism: Gift and benefaction branches interrupt a transactional inference from 'we worship' to 'therefore help us.' Aid is received as favor that enables service, not wages earned by it.
  - words: 1:5 نَعْبُدُ (ع ب د B003); 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ (ع و ن B001); 1:6 ٱهْدِنَا (ه د ي B004); 1:7 أَنْعَمْتَ (ن ع م B001)
- **M13** delta_servitude_softened_by_benefaction — The ownership register remains live, but service is more plausibly dependence accompanied and enabled by benefaction than forced reduction alone.
  - mechanism: Ease, softness, and good living weaken a coercive realization of the owned-status branch. Asymmetry survives, but the context resists reading service as mere forced reduction.
  - words: 1:5 نَعْبُدُ (ع ب د B001); 1:5 نَعْبُدُ (ع ب د B004); 1:7 أَنْعَمْتَ (ن ع م B002)
- **M14** delta_exclusivity_as_drift_resistance — The doubled object also acts as an anti-drift discipline: do not replace the object, reverse into opposition, or lose the intended aim.
  - mechanism: Replacement, active opposition, and loss of orientation turn repeated exclusivity into a stabilizing constraint. Help is sought not just to accomplish service but to keep its object from being substituted, resisted, or lost.
  - words: 1:5 نَعْبُدُ (ع ب د B003); 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ (ع و ن B001); 1:7 غَيْرِ (غ ي ر B003); 1:7 ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ (غ ض ب B003); 1:7 ٱلضَّآلِّينَ (ض ل ل B001)
- **M15** delta_collective_recoherence — The utterance helps make a community: potentially scattered speakers converge on one addressee and seek the backing that keeps their paths from separating.
  - mechanism: Community, scattering, and wandering activate the focus root's own dispersal branch. The plural 'we' is no longer presumed unified; repeating one object and seeking one backing performatively gather actors with divergent trajectories.
  - words: 1:5 نَعْبُدُ (ع ب د B010); 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ (ع و ن B001); 1:6 ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ (ق و م B001); 1:7 أَنْعَمْتَ (ن ع م B008); 1:7 ٱلضَّآلِّينَ (ض ل ل B001)
- **M16** outlier_watchful_eye_aid — Seeking help may also imagine placing the serving community under an attentive, preserving gaze.
  - mechanism: 
  - words: 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ (ع و ن B003); 1:5 نَعْبُدُ (ع ب د B006)
- **M17** outlier_present_provision_against_debt — The pair can momentarily image dependent servants asking for present provision to meet an obligation they cannot fund from themselves.
  - mechanism: 
  - words: 1:5 نَعْبُدُ (ع ب د B001); 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ (ع و ن B011); 1:4 مَٰلِكِ (م ل ك B002); 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ (د ي ن B003)
- **M18** outlier_obstacle_breaking_guidance — Aid may also be imagined as breaking obstructive structures and opening passage while service supplies the durability to continue.
  - mechanism: 
  - words: 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ (ع و ن B001); 1:5 نَعْبُدُ (ع ب د B007); 1:6 ٱهْدِنَا (ه د ي B001); 1:6 ٱلصِّرَٰطَ (ص ر ط B003)
- **M19** outlier_seasoned_conflict_backing — Under a contained conflict activation, it can sound like renewed allegiance and a request for backing amid an already recurring struggle.
  - mechanism: 
  - words: 1:5 نَعْبُدُ (ع ب د B003); 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ (ع و ن B003); 1:6 ٱلصِّرَٰطَ (ص ر ط B003); 1:7 ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ (غ ض ب B001)
