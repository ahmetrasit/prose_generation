## 1. Surah-level arguments (HFT mechanisms) [M]

- **M1** b-mercy-double-register — The pair stages one mercy in two temporal registers: overflowing in scope and remaining available in action.
  - mechanism: The pair does more than duplicate a quality: the first form can present mercy at full reach, while the second presents it as settled and repeatedly operative. The sequence moves from amplitude to continuance.
  - words: 1:3 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ (ر ح م B001)
- **M2** b-mercy-as-kinship — Mercy creates and maintains a near bond, making the recipient one whose connection is actively kept.
  - mechanism: Repeated mercy can be heard relationally: not only an emotion directed at a remote recipient, but a bond that establishes nearness and carries an obligation not to sever connection.
  - words: 1:3 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ (ر ح م B002)
- **M3** b-mercy-as-womb — Mercy is the enclosing and nourishing condition within which the recipient can become and endure.
  - mechanism: The two forms can carry a spatial-material model: encompassing protection together with ongoing nourishment. Mercy is then an environment in which dependent life is formed, not merely an intervention from outside.
  - words: 1:3 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ (ر ح م B003)
- **M4** d-name-discloses-mercy — The repeated pair is an invoked doorway into how the named deity becomes known and approached.
  - mechanism: The opening naming construction and its exact repetition of the pair turn the focus adjectives into an invoked disclosure of identity. Their recurrence makes mercy the threshold through which the named deity is approached.
  - words: 1:3 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ (ر ح م B001); 1:1 بِسْمِ (س م و B005); 1:1 ٱللَّهِ (ء ل ه B002); 1:1 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ (ر ح م B001)
- **M5** d-mercy-as-nurturing-rule — Mercy is cosmological gestation: a ruling practice of feeding, repairing, and bringing many worlds toward manifest completion.
  - mechanism: Praise, lordly repair and completion, the split image of feeding and growth, and the plurality of worlds expand the womb model into cosmic formation. Mercy becomes the governing labor by which dependent domains are nourished toward a praiseworthy state.
  - words: 1:3 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ (ر ح م B003); 1:2 ٱلْحَمْدُ (ح م د B002); 1:2 رَبِّ (ر ب ب B002); 1:2 رَبِّ (ر ب ب B005); 1:2 ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ (ع ل م B001)
- **M6** d-mercy-frames-accounting — Mercy names the governing disposition of the one who owns and administers the full event of accountability.
  - mechanism: Ownership and disposal, a bounded duration, and reckoning place the mercy pair directly before an administered temporal judgment. Mercy does not erase accounting; it becomes the prior frame and governing manner in which accounting is held.
  - words: 1:3 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ (ر ح م B001); 1:4 مَٰلِكِ (م ل ك B002); 1:4 يَوْمِ (ي و م B002); 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ (د ي ن B002)
- **M7** d-mercy-enables-dependent-service — Mercy establishes a relation in which the recipient can answer through service and expose dependence through a direct request for aid.
  - mechanism: Humble service and requested assistance convert kinship-like mercy into a reciprocal but asymmetric bond: maintained nearness elicits service, while dependence can openly ask the bond-holder for aid.
  - words: 1:3 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ (ر ح م B002); 1:5 نَعْبُدُ (ع ب د B003); 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ (ع و ن B001)
- **M8** d-mercy-guides-and-maintains — Mercy is navigational infrastructure: gentle direction plus the ongoing care that keeps a life aligned and moving.
  - mechanism: Gentle indication, a traversable straight route, and the paired images of guardianship and balanced uprightness turn mercy into continuous navigation. It both shows a direction and maintains the traveler and route in viable alignment.
  - words: 1:3 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ (ر ح م B001); 1:6 ٱهْدِنَا (ه د ي B001); 1:6 ٱلصِّرَٰطَ (ص ر ط B001); 1:6 ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ (ق و م B004); 1:6 ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ (ق و م B008)
- **M9** d-mercy-softens-against-loss — Mercy is what keeps a life soft enough to be guided and held enough not to harden, vanish, or lose its course.
  - mechanism: Blessed softness, grammatical exclusion, stony hardness, and loss of preservation define an outcome field around mercy. Mercy now appears as a keeping medium that preserves responsiveness and visibility against hardening, severance, and disappearance.
  - words: 1:3 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ (ر ح م B001); 1:7 أَنْعَمْتَ (ن ع م B002); 1:7 غَيْرِ (غ ي ر B005); 1:7 ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ (غ ض ب B004); 1:7 ٱلضَّآلِّينَ (ض ل ل B004)

- **M11** o-mercy-enters-the-ledger-as-kinship — Mercy establishes a kinship-like claim whose preservation or severance can matter when relations are accounted for.
  - mechanism: 
  - words: 1:3 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ (ر ح م B002); 1:4 ٱلدِّينِ (د ي ن B003)
- **M12** o-womb-pathway-delivery — Mercy may enclose, form, and then press life through a costly passage toward upright independent standing.
  - mechanism:
