## 1. Surah-level arguments (HFT mechanisms) [M]

- **M1** baseline_operative_designation — A portable operation that places the next act under the raised divine designation and makes mercy its governing manner.
  - mechanism: The opening does more than identify a speaker's deity: it places an otherwise unstated act under a raised divine designation and specifies mercy as the designation's operative manner. The missing verb lets the formula travel into whatever act follows.
  - words: 1:1 بِسْمِ (س م و B005); 1:1 ٱللَّهِ (ء ل ه B002); 1:1 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ (ر ح م B001)
  - touches hubs: L1
- **M2** baseline_legible_mercy_mark — Marking the act so that its divine affiliation becomes legible as mercy in the act's effects.
  - mechanism: The formula can function like an identifying impression placed upon an act. The mark is not merely ownership signage: discerning observation should be able to read mercy in the act's visible trace.
  - words: 1:1 بِسْمِ (س م و B001); 1:1 بِسْمِ (س م و B002); 1:1 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ (ر ح م B001)
  - touches hubs: L1
- **M3** baseline_generative_enclosure — A beginning inside a protected, kin-like, generative enclosure constituted by the divine name.
  - mechanism: The formula spatializes beginning as entry into a merciful enclosure. Cover, womb, and kinship yield a protected relational interior from which the unnamed act can emerge, so mercy is generative containment rather than an external ornament.
  - words: 1:1 بِسْمِ (س م و B004); 1:1 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ (ر ح م B003); 1:1 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ (ر ح م B002)
  - touches hubs: L1
- **M4** delta_1_2_cultivated_public_proof — The formula makes a testable claim: cultivation, repair, and praiseworthy completion should render that merciful affiliation publicly legible.
  - mechanism: The marked-name baseline becomes an evidential cycle: lordly cultivation repairs and completes what is under care; the resulting praiseworthy state is the distinguishing sign by which its source is recognized. The opening mark must therefore become readable in completed benefit.
  - words: 1:1 بِسْمِ (س م و B002); 1:1 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ (ر ح م B001); 1:2 ٱلْحَمْدُ (ح م د B002); 1:2 رَبِّ (ر ب ب B002); 1:2 ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ (ع ل م B002)

  - mechanism: Several material images lock into an ecological birth sequence: an upper cover bears cloud, first rain marks earth with growth, lordship nourishes emergence, and womb-mercy encloses formation. The invocation becomes an onset of cultivated life rather than only a verbal preface.
