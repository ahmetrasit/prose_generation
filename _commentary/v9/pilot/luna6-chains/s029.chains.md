# Sûrat al-ʿAnkabūt (Surah 29): dictionary-root image chains

## Scope and method

The dictionary is treated as a root-level catalog of latent semantic activations. A branch remains relevant when its root entry traces it, even if the branch describes a different form, phrase, or use of the root than the word in the āyah. Each link labels its branch as either `contextual word meaning` or `root-catalog activation`; multiple links in one āyah count once for the coverage threshold. The threshold is **10 distinct āyāt** (the smaller of ten and two thirds of 69).

Interpretation of “all three” and “exactly two”: domain reach is counted by the explicit domain labels under each chain. The report includes chains spanning three domains and chains spanning exactly two. Source pages: [Surah 29, āyah 1 dictionary](https://kelimeden-kelama.web.app/agent/29/1/dictionary) and linked root-entry pages shown at each link.
