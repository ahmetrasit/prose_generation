# Sûrat al-Fātiḥa (Surah 1): dictionary-root image chains

## Scope and method

The dictionary is treated as a root-level catalog of latent semantic activations. A branch is retained when the root entry traces it, even if the branch describes a different form or use of the root than the word in the verse. Each link says whether the branch is a contextual word meaning or a root-catalog activation. Duplicate branches in one verse do not increase the verse count. The threshold is **5 distinct āyāt** (the smaller of ten and two thirds of seven, rounded up).

Source pages: [Surah 1, āyah dictionary](https://kelimeden-kelama.web.app/agent/1/1/dictionary) and the linked root-entry pages shown at every link. The report is rebuilt from the opened dictionary data and preserves the full chain paths and evidence fields available in this run.
