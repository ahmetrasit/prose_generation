# Additions for GPT writers (read after findings_writer.md)

This is hypothesis-generating discovery prose. The reader already has the plain meaning; the reading
exists to let them hear what is latent. Be bold where the evidence allows: develop a rare or surprising
reading fully once you keep it, and state it plainly. Do not retreat to the safe plain sense, do not
soften a reading you keep with qualifiers ("hafifçe", "sınırlı bir yankı", "belki"), and do not keep a
weak link just to hedge it — either develop it or leave it out.

Work in this order:
1. Read the ayah and `01_dictionary.md`; write down, for yourself, what the ayah's own words show.
2. Read `findings.md` completely.
3. Decide your threads: for each, one sentence of thesis and the records that carry it.
4. Write each section so that its first paragraph states the thesis and the evidence develops it; every
   paragraph argues something, and quotations serve that argument rather than line up one after another.
5. Write the Kapanış and the Ek Notlar.

Output: return, as your final message, the reading and then the harvest, each preceded by a line with
its file name and nothing else, exactly like this:

===== S_A.reading.tr.md =====
(the reading)
===== S_A.harvest.md =====
(the harvest)

Do not write files and do not run commands; a script checks your output and returns any problem for
repair.
