### H01 — b_authorized_recitation [baseline_model, strong]
- before: Read or recite.
- after: Read or recite as sourced, authorized speech in the designation of your Creator-Lord.
- mechanism: Reading or recitation is not left as an autonomous vocal act: the prepositional phrase places it within the Creator-Lord's designation and authority.
- trace:
  - 96:1 w1 **ٱقْرَأْ** (ق ر ء B002: okumak, okutmak ve birlikte okumak / قراءة وتلاوة وإقراء) — Reading, reciting, and teaching recitation supply the commanded act and its transmissible voice.

  - 96:1 w3 **رَبِّكَ** (ر ب ب B001: sahip olup yönetme / ربوبية وملك وسيادة) — Lordship, ownership, and authority make the named relation one of authorization and answerability.

### H14 — o_hydrological_regulation [surprising_valid_outlier, exploratory]
