### H01 — base_embodied_route [baseline_model, strong]
- before: The verse names a road used by a favored class and excludes two bad classes.
- after: The verse makes a favored human trajectory the road's visible embodiment and distinguishes oppositional rupture from aimless deviation.
- mechanism: The verse does not identify a road by geometry alone. It makes bestowed well-being visible in a recipient group, so their lived trajectory becomes the road's positive continuity; opposition and deviation are rival ways of leaving that continuity.
- trace:
  - 1:7 w1 **صِرَٰطَ** (ص ر ط B001: yol, özellikle düz yol / الطريق المستقيم) — The straight-road image supplies continuous movement, while the following relative phrase relocates that continuity into an embodied human route.
  - 1:7 w3 **أَنْعَمْتَ** (ن ع م B001: iyi yaşam durumu ve başkasına ulaştırılan iyilik / حسن الحال والنعمة) — Bestowed favorable condition supplies the observable state by which the road-bearing group is identified.
  - 1:7 w5 **غَيْرِ** (غ ي ر B005: başka olma, dışta bırakma veya olumsuzlama / السوى والخلاف والاستثناء والنفي) — Otherness and exception turn the verse from a single route description into a contrast among trajectories.
  - 1:7 w6 **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** (غ ض ب B003: karşı koyup muhalefet etmek / المراغمة والمخالفة) — Defiance and opposition supply a relational mechanism for one trajectory's rupture rather than merely naming an emotion.
  - 1:7 w9 **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** (ض ل ل B001: doğru yoldan ve amaçtan sapma ya da başkasını saptırma / الضلال عن الهدى والقصد) — Deviation from aim supplies the second failure mode: motion continues but no longer holds the road's direction.

### H02 — base_successful_and_failed_disappearance [baseline_model, medium]
- before: Road and straying are simple opposites: presence on the route versus absence from it.
- after: Both right passage and straying can involve disappearance; the decisive difference is whether disappearance completes directed transit or erases recoverable direction.
- mechanism: Two kinds of disappearance coexist. A soft, capacious passage can carry a traveler through so completely that the traveler vanishes into successful transit, whereas straying can also make something vanish but without destination or recoverability. Compact hardness acts as the obstructing condition that separates the two.
- trace:
  - 1:7 w1 **صِرَٰطَ** (ص ر ط B002: geçişte gözden kaybolmak; özellikle yiyeceği yutmak / الغيبة في المرور والبلع) — Disappearance through swallowing supplies a successful passage in which being taken out of sight is evidence of transit.
  - 1:7 w3 **أَنْعَمْتَ** (ن ع م B002: yumuşamak, rahat yaşamak veya rahat yaşatmak / اللين والنعومة ورفاه العيش) — Softness and easeful living supply low resistance, making the favored passage traversable rather than abrasive.
  - 1:7 w6 **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** (غ ض ب B004: sert, yığılmış veya yuvarlak kaya / صلابة الصخرة وتماسكها) — Hard compact rock supplies the blocking material state opposed to softened passage.
  - 1:7 w9 **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** (ض ل ل B002: gizlenerek, karışıp eriyerek veya gömülerek gözden yitme / الغيبوبة والخفاء) — Hiddenness and disappearance supply a failed vanishing that lacks the road's directed transit.

### H04 — base_agency_gradient [baseline_model, medium]
- before: All three groups are parallel moral categories.
- after: They encode different causal positions: actively furthered recipients, people bearing relational opposition, and agents continuing to deviate.
- mechanism: The three groups differ not only in outcome but in causal grammar. Favor actively carries recipients further; the angered group bears a condition produced in a relation of opposition; the strayers enact continuing deviation. The road is therefore a pattern of agency distribution.
- trace:

  - 1:7 w9 **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** (ض ل ل B001: doğru yoldan ve amaçtan sapma ya da başkasını saptırma / الضلال عن الهدى والقصد) — Deviation from aim supplies ongoing self-propelled divergence to the active-participial final group.
  - 1:7 w5 **غَيْرِ** (غ ي ر B003: biçimini değiştirme veya yerine başkasını koyma / تغيير الصورة أو إبدال الشيء بغيره) — Replacement and change of state make the exception pivot a switch among causal regimes rather than a flat list boundary.
