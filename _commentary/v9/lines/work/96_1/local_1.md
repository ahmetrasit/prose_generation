### L05 word 5: خَلَقَ — lemma خَلَقَ, root خ ل ق, pos V

- analysis prose: {{ar:ٱلَّذِى}} ({{tr:alladhi}}) carries {{ar:رَبِّكَ}} ({{tr:rabbika}}) into the relative clause and supplies the recoverable subject for {{ar:خَلَقَ}} ({{tr:khalaqa}}).
