### S-hft-baseline_chosen_shelter (baseline_model)
- after: The ayah models patron-selection as self-built protection whose architecture cannot perform the sheltering work for which it was chosen.
- mechanism: Voluntary acquisition converts patrons into a built protection system. The spider's woven dwelling has the name and appearance of shelter but lacks the force needed to perform shelter, so deliberate construction and possession do not establish protective capacity.

### S-hft-baseline_relational_web (baseline_model)
