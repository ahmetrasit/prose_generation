### Surah
- 4:32 uses almost the same formula just before the focus: مَا فَضَّلَ ٱللَّهُ بِهِۦ بَعْضَكُمْ عَلَىٰ بَعْضٍ. It comes with a ban on coveting and separate earned shares for men and women. The faḍl ground in 4:34 therefore reuses wording just framed as an allocated difference that no one should covet. — core
- 4:5 calls wealth itself a support: أَمْوَٰلَكُمُ ٱلَّتِى جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمْ قِيَٰمًا. قيام shares the root of قَوَّٰمُونَ. The men's standing rests on wealth God made a support, which they let flow out. — core
- 4:135 uses the same intensive form in قَوَّٰمِينَ بِٱلْقِسْطِ … وَلَوْ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِكُمْ. Here عَلَى turns against the self, and justice becomes the measure of qiwāma. — core
- 4:127 وَأَن تَقُومُوا۟ لِلْيَتَٰمَىٰ بِٱلْقِسْطِ uses the q-w-m root for standing up for the weak with justice. — develop
- 4:128 uses the same خاف + نشوز construction with the positions reversed: a woman fears her husband's نُشُوزًا أَوْ إِعْرَاضًا. The answer there is settlement (وَٱلصُّلْحُ خَيْرٌ). Nushūz can come from either spouse. — core
- 4:35 follows at once: fear of شِقَاق, one arbiter from each family, إِصْلَٰحًا, يُوَفِّقِ ٱللَّهُ بَيْنَهُمَآ. The focus is not the last step. The ṣ-l-ḥ thread runs الصالحات → إصلاح → صلح. — core
- 4:90 has فَمَا جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمْ عَلَيْهِمْ سَبِيلًا, and 4:141 has لَن يَجْعَلَ … عَلَى ٱلْمُؤْمِنِينَ سَبِيلًا. The surah's formula of "no way against" appears once peace is offered. — core
- 4:16 فَإِن تَابَا وَأَصْلَحَا فَأَعْرِضُوا۟ عَنْهُمَآ has the same shape: a condition, then a halt to further action. — develop
- 4:15 has يَجْعَلَ ٱللَّهُ لَهُنَّ سَبِيلًا: a way made for women, set against the way sought against them in 4:34. — note
- 4:19 forbids أَن تَرِثُوا۟ ٱلنِّسَآءَ كَرْهًا and لَا تَعْضُلُوهُنَّ, and commands عَاشِرُوهُنَّ بِٱلْمَعْرُوفِ. The karh named here is the opposite of the ṭawʿ in أَطَعْنَكُمْ, and the verse limits leverage. — core
- 4:20 and 4:21 forbid taking back even a qinṭār; they give أَفْضَىٰ بَعْضُكُمْ إِلَىٰ بَعْضٍ and مِّيثَٰقًا غَلِيظًا. What was spent or given cannot be reclaimed, and baʿḍ appears here in intimacy rather than rank. — develop
- 4:4, 4:7 and 4:32 establish women's own property before the focus. مِنْ أَمْوَٰلِهِمْ marks the men's own assets. — develop
- 4:1 says men and women spread from one soul and its mate: a shared origin before any differentiation. — develop
- 4:58 إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ نِعِمَّا يَعِظُكُم بِهِۦٓ, 4:63 وَعِظْهُمْ وَقُل لَّهُمْ فِىٓ أَنفُسِهِمْ قَوْلًۢا بَلِيغًا, 4:66 مَا يُوعَظُونَ بِهِۦ: waʿẓ is God's own mode toward the addressees, and it is speech that reaches into the self. — develop
- 4:80 فَمَآ أَرْسَلْنَٰكَ عَلَيْهِمْ حَفِيظًا: guarding does not mean controlling others. — develop
- 4:3 وَإِنْ خِفْتُمْ أَلَّا تُقْسِطُوا۟: in this surah fear triggers measures that often restrict the one who fears. — develop
- 4:94 ضَرَبْتُمْ فِى سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ … تَبْتَغُونَ … فَتَبَيَّنُوا۟ and 4:101 ضَرَبْتُمْ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ … إِنْ خِفْتُمْ use ḍ-r-b for travel with fī, a different construction from the focus. — note
- 4:103 كَانَتْ عَلَى ٱلْمُؤْمِنِينَ كِتَٰبًا: عَلَى marks obligation, so "over" can mean burden. — note
- 4:129 forbids كُلَّ ٱلْمَيْلِ فَتَذَرُوهَا كَٱلْمُعَلَّقَةِ, and 4:130 foresees separation with God enriching each. These are further limits on male conduct. — develop
- 4:36 comes right after: لَا يُحِبُّ مَن كَانَ مُخْتَالًا فَخُورًا, a rebuke of pride next to the coda. — develop
- عَلِيًّا كَبِيرًا closes only this ayah in the surah. Other closings are عليما حكيما, غفورا رحيما, خبيرا and similar. — develop
- 4:2 حُوبًا كَبِيرًا uses كبير for a wrong involving wealth. — note
- 4:75 and 4:98 name ٱلْمُسْتَضْعَفِينَ مِنَ ٱلرِّجَالِ وَٱلنِّسَآءِ: vulnerability is not assigned by gender. — note
- 4:124 مِن ذَكَرٍ أَوْ أُنثَىٰ gives equal reward. — note
- 4:24 أَن تَبْتَغُوا۟ بِأَمْوَٰلِكُم: seeking with wealth in marriage is allowed, while seeking a way against wives is forbidden. — note
- 4:81 يَقُولُونَ طَاعَةٌ, obedience as a formula. Not used, because it could wrongly imply the women are insincere. — note

### Readings and grammar
- قَوَّام is an intensive faʿʿāl form: sustained, habitual upholding, not a single act. — core
- بِمَا فَضَّلَ: ما works as a maṣdar. بَعْضَهُمْ عَلَىٰ بَعْضٍ does not name its endpoints or its respect (not بتفضيلهم عليهن). The masculine plural can cover both sexes. — core
- بِمَآ أَنفَقُوا۟ مِنْ أَمْوَٰلِهِمْ: a past act of spending, from their own assets. — develop
- فَٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتُ: fāʾ of detailing, followed by a descriptive nominal sentence rather than an imperative. — core
- بِمَا حَفِظَ ٱللَّهُ: ما can be a maṣdar ("through God's guarding") or a relative ("what God guarded"). Abū Jaʿfar's reading بِمَا حَفِظَ ٱللَّهَ means by their guarding of God's due. — develop
- وَٱلَّٰتِى تَخَافُونَ: a relative clause with conditional force, so fear is the gate. — develop
- Three imperatives are joined by وَ. The conjunction alone does not impose order, but the order of mention runs speech → space → body and ends in a halt. — develop
- فِى ٱلْمَضَاجِعِ: the locative confines the separation to the beds. — core
- وَٱضْرِبُوهُنَّ: Form I, transitive, with a direct human object. It is not Form IV أضرب عن. — core
- فَإِنْ أَطَعْنَكُمْ: an uncertain إن, with a switch to second-person "you". — note
- فَلَا تَبْغُوا۟ عَلَيْهِنَّ سَبِيلًا: an indefinite noun under prohibition, meaning any way whatsoever. — core
- إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ: emphatic, with kāna for an enduring attribute. — note
- Every imperative and the one prohibition are addressed to men; the women are never addressed. — develop
- The sound chain عَلَى – عَلَىٰ – عَلَيْهِنَّ – عَلِيًّا is heard as a chain, not claimed as one root. — develop
