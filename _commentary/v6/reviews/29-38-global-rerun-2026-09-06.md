branch has no reviewed facet:

| `root_000074/B003` | `/branch_registry/3` | `SOURCE_IMAGE`: a visible sign |
| `root_000180/B002` | `/branch_registry/26` | `SOURCE_IMAGE`: leaving an effect to remain |
| `root_001240/B023` | Branch identity lookup | `SOURCE_IMAGE`: road robbery or blocking the way |

Each record contains a nonempty `review_facets` array, exact Arabic and English

facets. This is an erroneous judgment about available evidence, not packet loss.

The mistake affects `global:dwellings-field-trace`,
`global:social-obstruction-bound`, and `global:literal-insight-nonbinding`.
The first finding drops the trace and sign activations; the social finding drops
the road-blocking activation. A reader may reject an activation on semantic
grounds, but the stated absence is false. All final activations use focus
carriers; supplied context-carrier opportunities were not retained.

### 2. An inherited validation rule induces an unsupported grammatical reading

`discovery.py` requires an activated core facet somewhere in the discovery when
a branch's specialization or extension survives. The rule predates the fixes
and is also present in the V5 prompt. It confuses retaining knowledge of a
branch's core with claiming that the core meaning is active in this occurrence.

At 04:52:42 UTC, `finish` rejected the draft because `root_000848/B001` retained
its transitive specialization without its intransitive core. At 04:53:04 the
producing agent inserted `F001` into `global:literal-insight-nonbinding`, with
the carrier claim: "The focus verb also retains its core intransitive
turning-away sense."

The focus construction is transitive: the named adversary turns **them** away,
and the supplied verb morphology is third-person masculine singular. The
inserted explanation shifts to the people themselves turning away and supplies
no grammatical mechanism for that additional reading. The first pilot also
selected this intransitive facet through its generic core default. The rerun
trace additionally exposes the validator's direct pressure to reintroduce it
after the agent had selected the transitive facet. This is an inherited defect
missed by the earlier review, not a new checkpoint-code regression.

Explicit candidate facet obligations should still be accounted for. An extension
should not require an invented core activation when the occurrence does not
support it. Source definitions can remain available without being activated.

### 3. Missing lexical verification is used to discard available contextual meaning

Several narrower readings omit more than an unverified branch identity:

- The rival-route finding drops false burden transfer and 29:13 because the
  nominated lexical branches lack independent descriptors. The supplied Arabic
  of 29:12 explicitly offers to carry others' sins and denies that transfer;
  29:13 supplies the resulting burdens. That ordinary textual relation remains
  assessable independently of a specialized branch claim.
- The field-trace finding drops 29:19-20 with the unresolved inspection
  branches. The supplied 29:20 explicitly commands travel and looking. The
  proposed relation to the focus can be evaluated without claiming verified
  lexicon status for every cited root.
- A rejected 29:61 candidate is described as lacking context evidence, although
  it explicitly names 29:61 and the complete Arabic and morphology are in the
  packet. Its cosmic-direction reading still needs a semantic decision; missing
  candidate-specific anchors do not make the shared context unavailable.

Genuinely unresolved branch records have attributed HFT roles and explicit
qualification. Those roles are not verified lexical definitions, but that
limitation does not establish that every associated ordinary-text comparison is
unsupported. The existing prompt says source trust controls qualification;
this run does not apply that distinction consistently.

Two context exclusions also misdescribe their sources: 29:48 is called a
concealment episode, and 29:62 a correct-answer episode. Their supplied Arabic
concerns prior recitation/writing and provision, respectively. Rejecting or
narrowing a candidate requires accurate source facts too.

### 4. Post-compaction lookup truncation escaped recovery

At 04:44:20 UTC, the agent combined ten complete support lookups in one
`functions.exec` without increasing its outer output allowance. The returned
response explicitly warned of truncation at 12,449 original tokens. Subsequent
calls did not repeat that lookup set before synthesis.

Those supports had been read earlier during the complete batch pass, so this
does not show initial delivery loss. It shows a failed exact-evidence reload
after the fourth compaction. The same output-budget rule needs to apply to
targeted lookups as well as the scheduled reading pass.

## Discovery breadth and cost

All 16 findings have candidate origins. This alone is not a failure and should
not be remedied with a quota. The notes do recognize 41:17, 46:26, and 14:45,
but those comparisons do not reach the final findings or a specific final
disposition. The existing literal-insight finding could overlap their general
theme; the run still does not demonstrate a convincing final assessment of
these particularly relevant sources beyond its broad batch observations.

Cumulative usage through discovery completion was 34,096,115 input tokens,
including 32,910,336 cached input tokens, and 96,270 output tokens, including
27,747 reasoning tokens. These totals count repeated conversation context across
requests. They are neither the packet's token count nor a price estimate. The
separate lifecycle-completion turn is excluded.

The additional reading discipline cost time and did preserve analysis across
compactions. It did not, by itself, make final evidence selection dependable.
The next correction should address the core-activation rule, distinguish
`SOURCE_IMAGE` facets from truly unresolved citations, separate lexical
verification from ordinary contextual evidence, and require exact rereads for
availability-based exclusions and truncated lookups. Keep the current outputs
as evidence of this result; corrections belong in code and instructions before
another fresh run. Large-packet scale and downstream prose remain untested.
