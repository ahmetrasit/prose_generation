# Friction Report

- Main containment risk: the `ف ك ه` pleasant-talk branch could make the reader think the local noun stops being fruit. I kept the prose anchored in concrete fruit and rendered the speech echo only after the primary fruit-selection scene was established.
- Review friction: the cross-run publication rejects a stronger social-environment expansion. I used that as a boundary and kept the prose to a narrowed atmospheric echo from 56:25-26.
- Channel friction: the bundle includes first-pass channel material but no adjudicated ledger or maturity. I did not name a channel or make a surah-wide channel claim; I used the material only to connect this ayah’s own fruit/choice wording with the adjacent desire wording.
- Grammar friction: `مِّمَّا` can produce a result-like feel, but the bundle identifies the local construction as partitive-relative. I phrased the result shade as a taste inside the source/selection relation.
- Variant friction: the nominative pressure on `فَٰكِهَةٍۢ` could overstate a separate-clause reading. I used it only to show that the canonical genitive dependency is meaningful.
- Negation use: I used a few explicit negatives where they protect likely misconceptions: fresh-start vs continuation, passive receipt vs active selection, and social-talk echo vs replacing fruit. These negatives are bounded; the prose otherwise states what each form positively does.
- Source absence: v12 focus trace and staged focus-run reader responses are absent/retired in this workflow. I reported that in the evidence coverage note, not in the reader prose.
