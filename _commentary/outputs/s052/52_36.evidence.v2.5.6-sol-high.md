## Counter-Evidence And Limits

- HFT `B_FABRICATED_COSMOLOGY` is exploratory. The local verbal sense of `خَلَقُوا۟` is actual creation; fabrication enters only as branch pressure.
- HFT `D_RECIPROCAL_AUTHORSHIP_TEST` may be deliberate reciprocal transfer or a rhetorically linked series of independent challenges.
- HFT `D_EXCLUSIVE_CAUSAL_AGENCY` may retrospectively resolve cosmological agency, while 52:43 can also be read within worship alone.
- HFT `D_AGENCY_REVERSED_BY_PLOT` may diagnose bounded creaturely agency or remain a separate moral reversal.
- HFT `D_KNOWLEDGE_SETTLEMENT_GRADIENT` may distinguish `ي ق ن` from `ع ل م`, while rhetorical variation remains live.
- `O_HEAVENS_AS_MARKED_EVIDENCE` and `O_HEAVEN_AS_APERTURE` depend on non-dominant split-root mappings. They are rendered only as analogies, never lexical glosses.
- `O_CERTAINTY_AS_PROTECTED_INTERIOR` uses the remote nominal `root_001696/B002`. It is rendered only as an affective-spatial analogy for `root_001696/B001`.
- The death pressure is bounded by `root_001696/B001` lexical unit `lu_017`: certainty that a killing occurred, rather than “death” as the local meaning of `يُوقِنُونَ`.
