=== PROMPT FRICTION ===

Run cleanly from the supplied hermetic prompt. I read the instantiated prompt
and used no external file or network source. The four requested artifacts are
the only files written for this ayah; no editorial file was created.

The main tension was density. The bundle contains 42 `must_integrate` topics and
35 candidate topics, while the prompt also prohibits list-like aggregation and
requires proportionate continuous prose. I admitted all 35 candidates because
each was marked `used` or `narrowed` and supplied a distinct reader payoff. I
grouped them by shared grammatical, lexical, form, sound, or intertext mechanism
and gave every topic its own index line.

The upstream word-analysis/QAC alignment is inconsistent: 20 orthographic
word-analysis units are paired with 16 QAC words, and the upstream aligned refs
`18:74:17` through `18:74:20` dangle against `qac_morphemes`. The prompt supplies
20 resolved `word_morpheme_spans`, so I used those spans for morphology and kept
the upstream topic identities without renumbering them.

The prompt requires local surprise prose but also warns that anchored channel
review is first-pass, single-reader material with no adjudication or maturity.
I treated the two anchored nominations as bounded local resonances, did not use
their names as claims, and made no recurring-system or surah-wide assertion.
Overlapping focus models and nominations were grouped only when they shared a
mechanism; distinct before/after effects remain separate in the prose and index.

The default per-ayah `v12_reader_responses` family is explicitly retired and
absent. I used the supplied Hermetic Focus Trace, regular reader walk, wide
reader walk, and cross-run publication only for reconstructed context and
coverage. No stage response, confidence movement, or staged reveal was
invented. `butuncul_okuma_line` is null because the bundle reports no whole-
surah reading for this surah, and the pericope is marked `synthesized: false`.

Some root entries have no reviewed Turkish gloss, and root occurrence tables are
trimmed by design. I used the word-analysis prose, retained branch IDs and root
identities in evidence, and did not treat missing occurrence tables as missing
readings. The generated channel manifest lists an absent path-family file; it
was not needed and was not read.

The two exploratory outliers were difficult to place because their own
containment notes call them branch-distant. They were retained as explicitly
bounded lexical echoes: the patience reading remains an echo around ordered
life-for-life response, and the replacement-economy reading remains an echo
around later substitution. The local `بِغَيْرِ نَفْسٍ` sense stays the controlling
surface wording in prose.

The prose retains two explicit negative predicates: the later account is marked
as outside the actor's own command, and the visible scene is marked as lacking a
displayed life-counterpart for authority. These protect the live evidence gap
and authority misconception; they are in separate sentences and no sentence
stacks two negatives. Other negative formulations were rewritten as positive
predication. Raw roots, branch IDs, reader IDs, confidence labels, and source
layer names remain in apparatus rather than prose.

No further ambiguity required an invented production rule. The only local
grouping rule was to merge overlapping evidence nominations into one resonance
when their mechanism and reader payoff were the same, while preserving each
distinct resonance relation in the findings index.
