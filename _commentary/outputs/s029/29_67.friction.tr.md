- Prompt self-containment and output-shape guidance pull in different directions. The task says the input bundle is the only ayah-specific source and forbids unmanifested semantic lookup; the repository does not provide a separate schema for the evidence surface or findings-index prose. I used only the inlined bundle for semantic claims. Existing artifact conventions were used only to make phrase addresses and checker-compatible index lines, not to add evidence.
- Candidate handling has no numeric admission threshold. I used the following local rule to proceed: a candidate enters prose when the bundle gives it a distinct reader payoff that can be stated through an ayah word without displacing the primary reading. This admitted 31 of 35 candidates. The omitted candidates are 29:67:10:faith-danger-recurrence, 29:67:16:variant-direct-address, 29:67:20:surah-divine-source-thread, and 29:67:21:variant-direct-address. The first and third require a wider recurring-system claim; the two accepted person-shift variants repeat the canonical charge without changing its local objects.

- The reconstructed HFT has baseline models, context deltas, and surprising outliers but is not a strict staged reader response. The bundle separately marks v12_reader_responses as absent. I therefore used reader-language before/after consequences only, kept confidence and stage labels out of prose, and preserved live alternatives in the evidence surface.
- The boundary between a valid local cross-ayah resonance and a Layer 3/network claim is underspecified. I admitted six of seven context deltas only where the landing remained anchored in a word of 29:67. I omitted delta_security_detached_from_single_place because its portable-provider generalization exceeds this isolated ayah. No parent channel, recurring system, maturity, network, or surah thesis was written.
- The channel material is explicitly first-pass single-reader review with no adjudication or maturity. It was used only as corroboration for the local protected-center/periphery reading, alongside HFT and reader-walk evidence. The channel names and parent system were not carried into prose.
- The first وَ has a live circumstantial/coordination ambiguity, and ءَامِنًۭا has a second-object/adjectival ambiguity. The prose states the common reader payoff while evidence records both parses. This is an invented containment rule required because the prompt requires no disambiguation but still asks for fluent prose.
- Accepted direct-address variants for يَكْفُرُونَ remain apparatus evidence. The canonical third-person surface controls the reader text; the variants are not separate prose readings because their changed person does not produce a materially distinct local object or mechanism.
- Sound evidence is sparse and topic-specific. I retained only the supplied cadence and rough sound pressures. No phonetic symbolism was invented, and sound was never allowed to determine grammar or theology.

I preserved the supplied span and QAC identities and did not repair the mismatch by renumbering.
- The bundle supplies no whole-surah reading, no synthesized pericope reading, and no Layer 1 consideredNotPrimary artifact. I did not infer any omitted upstream exclusion from those absences. The generated channel manifest also names a missing semantic path-family file; it was not needed and was not read.
