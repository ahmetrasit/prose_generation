## Counter-evidence and boundaries

- The bond/full-discharge reading is inferred from completion and tied-end material; the HFT preserves the alternative that the earlier contracts form a narrower legal class.
- The embodied-grace reading may reach beyond the immediately preceding regimen; it is not restricted to that one favor.
- The prevented-harm reading may be a bounded historical incident rather than a general definition of favor.
- The just-witness reading may be an independent legal exhortation rather than a direct gloss on this ayah.
- The reciprocal-architecture reading does not establish equality between parties; the later loan language may remain exhortative metaphor.
- The breach reading preserves uncertainty about whether hardening, forgetting, word displacement, and betrayal form a strict causal sequence or coordinated consequences.
- The disclosure-and-route reading preserves the uncertainty that this ayah states knowledge of the interior but leaves its mode unstated.
- The threshold reading may be a thematic recurrence rather than a direct narrative explanation of this ayah.
- The documentary remembrance outlier is a rights-record resonance, not a claim that the ayah names an actual document.
- The affirmative-answer outlier is a word-level echo, not a translation of niʿma as "yes".
- The three channel review rows are first-pass single-reader nominations with no adjudication, no accept/reject decision, and no maturity claim. They are used only for local, writer-inferred images.
