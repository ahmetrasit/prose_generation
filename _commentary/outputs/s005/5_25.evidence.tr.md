## Phrase map

`P01`–`P16` address the prose movements in order; one movement can cover
adjacent paragraphs. `[bundle]` marks a claim directly traceable to the
instantiated bundle. `[inference]` marks the writer's connection among supplied
readings. QAC references identify morphemes; word-topic references identify the
exact obligation record.
