## Ambiguities and invented conventions

- The prompt requires `surprise:<id>` rows but does not define stable IDs for channel nominations, HFT readings, or reader-walk surprises. I used descriptive local IDs (`selective-repair`, `bounded-operations`, `displaced-words`, `forensic-repair`, `clarity-by-separation`, `protected-room`, `light-route`, and `paired-arrival`) and kept their source IDs in the evidence surface.
- The prompt requires evidence to be addressable per prose phrase but gives no prose-span schema. I used quoted phrase headings and local evidence sections rather than adding markers to the reader prose, because provenance markers are forbidden there.
- The prompt distinguishes source readings from writer synthesis but does not prescribe whether duplicated reader-walk and HFT findings should each become an index row. I retained each materially distinct supplied reading under its source ref and consolidated only identical mechanisms in shared prose landings.

- The bundle contains no separate acoustic artifact. The prose uses only the supplied repeated-arrival and cadence effects that are already attached to the word-analysis evidence; it makes no independent pronunciation or sound claim.
