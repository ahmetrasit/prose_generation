=== PROMPT FRICTION ===

- Output contract friction: the prompt requires prose, evidence surface, findings index, and friction, but the user explicitly allowed writing only three paths and did not provide a findings path. Following the existing S42 convention, I placed the findings index inside the evidence file after the coverage note.
- Source-shape friction: the instantiated prompt says the bundle is inlined and self-contained, but the JSON bundle is a very large single line and terminal output truncates it. I used the named `bundles/s042/42_20.ayah.json` file for structured reads of the same bundle evidence and did not read external quran-data files.
- Alignment friction: coverage reports inconsistent word-analysis/QAC word alignment because orthographic word counts and QAC word counts differ. I cited word-analysis topic IDs for readings and used `word_morpheme_spans`/QAC morphemes for surface anchoring where needed.
- Channel friction: channel review and generated outputs are present and three subchannels are anchored here, but the prompt warns that they are first-pass candidate evidence and not an adjudicated channel ledger. I used them only as local support for cultivation/account imagery and did not name a surah-wide channel.
- Later-context grounding limitation: HFT context deltas provide refs, roots, branch IDs, and role descriptions, but not full Arabic surfaces for every later-ayah word. I compressed later developments by reference and Turkish concept rather than inventing later Arabic spans.
- Coverage limitation: `v12_reader_responses` are absent by retired workflow design, and no whole-surah reading exists for S42. HFT, regular reader walk, plus/minus-5 reader walk, cross-run publication, root lexicon, inter-ayah rows, and pericope metadata are present.
- Style audit: explicit negation was required because the ayah itself ends with a negated possession clause and because the prose had to protect two live misconceptions: treating worldly receipt as unreal and treating cultivation/increase as an automatic mechanical payout. I kept negation concentrated in those places.
