## Counter-evidence and boundaries

- HFT does not force one ontology for `ٱلْحُطَمَةِ`: place, crushing agent, condition, crowding force, and processing apparatus remain live. The prose keeps these as contained additions to the destination reading.
- The reciprocal-pressure and inverse-aggregation readings are writer inferences from supplied sequences. Their live alternatives remain: earlier conduct may identify the person without mechanically mirroring the punishment, and wealth may identify the offender without determining the punishment’s operation.
- The binding-transfer reading may be too remote as a juridical image; its narrower surviving pressure is irreversible finality. The prose labels it an analogy and retains the passive disposal floor.
- The attritional, duration, and permanence readings coexist with a sudden-impact reading. Extended supports may describe spatial architecture rather than time; the prose keeps both directions.
- Closure may actively enable crushing pressure or may only prevent escape after an independent crushing act. Both causal orders are preserved.
- The operational, inward-access, destructive-exposure, and ignited-grinder readings are context-activated through 104:5-9. They do not replace the local grammar of 104:4.
- The vessel, emesis, reserve, crowded-structure, water-flow, withdrawal, and dependent images nominated by the first-pass channel review were merged into the HFT/word landings where mechanism and reader payoff were shared. The withdrawal pressure remains explicitly directional counter-pressure inside P12; the water and stone-store images remain bounded analogies in P24-P26. No channel nomination was promoted to a recurring system, maturity claim, or surah-wide name.
- The hydraulic outlier depends on the packet’s split mapping and remains branch-distant. The fuel outlier remains an “as if” feedstock analogy because the grammar gives a subject cast into the destination. The wealth-store outlier remains an ironic storage analogy.
- Cross-run publication supplies five strong coverage findings, but the grade is not used as truth rank or a filter. Its five findings are landed in P12, P14-P15, P22, and P21.
- The first-pass channel review is `first-pass-single-reader`; it has no adjudication, accept/reject decision, or per-ayah maturity. Its nominations are therefore evidence for local inference only.
