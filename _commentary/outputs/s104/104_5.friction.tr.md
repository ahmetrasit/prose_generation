The specification was executable without an invented semantic rule. The main production tension was between complete no-selection coverage and readable Turkish prose. I resolved it by grouping readings only when their mechanism and reader payoff were shared, while giving every admitted topic and resonance an addressable evidence landing. This preserves coexistence rather than ranking.

The prompt requires `surprise:<id>` rows but the bundle supplies model IDs and outlier IDs rather than canonical surprise IDs. I assigned descriptive local IDs (`epistemic-threshold`, `active-consuming-crusher`, `collector-as-feedstock`, `aggregation-reversal`, `attritional-depletion`, `social-pressure-return`, `targeted-inward-disclosure`, `sealed-pressure-architecture`, `rotary-vortex-grinder`, and `wealth-vault-inversion`) and recorded their source refs in the evidence surface. This is a naming rule invented for the artifact index, not a claim about upstream identity.

The word-analysis/QAC join is internally inconsistent: the analysis has five orthographic words, QAC has four morphological words, and upstream ref `104:5:5` is dangling. I followed the supplied `word_morpheme_spans` and preserved the upstream identity without renumbering. The prose therefore treats the five visible spans as the ayah’s reader words, while the evidence records the join defect.

`v12_reader_responses` is absent by explicit retirement of the default per-ayah focus workflow. I did not invent an isolated-stage response, confidence movement, or stage labels. The Hermetic Focus Trace is reconstructed rather than staged, so the prose renders its before/after changes as later understanding and does not call them stages.

The channel review is explicitly first-pass and single-reader, with no adjudication or maturity. The grooming nomination was omitted because it supplied no independent ayah-level payoff after grounding.
