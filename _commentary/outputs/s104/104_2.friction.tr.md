## Specification tensions
- The prompt requires every anchored candidate and materially distinct resonance while also requiring prose integration rather than a catalogue. I resolved this by admitting all 14 word-analysis candidates and 23 distinct HFT baseline/context/outlier resonances, sharing a paragraph only when the mechanism and reader payoff were shared, and giving each reading its own prose sentence or clause and index row.
- The prompt asks for a before/after reader experience, while the bundle marks v12_focus_trace_hermetic as reconstructed rather than staged. I used baseline_models, context_deltas, surprising_valid_outliers, and reader-walk retrospective surprises as instructed; I did not invent stage labels or an isolated response.
- The first-pass channel material nominates local mechanisms but explicitly has no adjudication, accept/reject result, or maturity. I carried only nominations with a distinct local changed-understanding payoff, recorded the other nominations as omissions for lack of payoff, and made no recurrence, maturity, or channel-status claim.
- The output contract requires one line per reading but permits shared prose landings. The index therefore keeps one line per topic and resonance while the evidence surface points several rows to a shared paragraph where the mechanism is genuinely shared.

## Bundle limitations and contradictions
- v12_reader_responses is absent by design in the default workflow. The prompt says to use it when present and to report its absence when both reader-response families are absent; the reconstructed focus trace and reader walks are present, so I used those and recorded the retired family as a coverage gap.

- The channel-generated manifest lists one missing expected optional file: paths/path_families/semantic_path_families.jsonl.gz. Channel-generated files were not read because the local HFT, reader walks, channel nominations, and branch inventories already supplied the needed local detail. No required core Layer 2 input was missing.

The spindle/whirlpool reading was retained only with the trace’s containment and exploratory status; the missing scoped root inventory was not filled from outside.

## Rendering choices
- The bundle specifies structured Arabic surface spans but does not impose a single Turkish transliteration standard. I used the Turkish-readable forms already present in word_analysis, including ellezî, cemeʿa, mâlen, and ʿaddedehû, and kept raw roots and branch IDs in apparatus only.
- “Prose landing” has no machine schema beyond reader-visible prose. For the density audit I counted P01-P20 as 20 addressable paragraph landings and separately counted each of the 23 resonance rows as an explicit reader-visible resonance landing.
- The three explicit negative predicates in the prose protect likely misconceptions or the primary floor: the clenched-palm image is not the surface verb, the spider branch is not the surface gloss, and the closing resonances do not replace the plain reading. Other containment was rendered with positive predication.
- No outside file, network source, model output, or vendor source was used beyond the hermetic prompt and its inlined bundle.
