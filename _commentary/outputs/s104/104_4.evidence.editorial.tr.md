## Counter-evidence and boundaries

- HFT does not force one ontology for `ٱلْحُطَمَةِ`: place, crushing agent, condition, crowding force, and processing apparatus remain live. The prose keeps these as contained additions to the destination reading.
- Reciprocal pressure and inverse aggregation are writer inferences from supplied sequences. Earlier conduct may identify the person without mechanically mirroring the punishment, and wealth may identify the offender without determining the punishment’s operation.
- Binding transfer may be too remote as a juridical image; its narrower surviving pressure is irreversible finality. The passive disposal floor remains primary.
- Attritional, duration, and permanence readings coexist with sudden impact. Extended supports may describe spatial architecture rather than time; both directions remain open.
- Closure may enable crushing pressure or may only prevent escape after an independent crushing act. Both causal orders are preserved.
- Operational disclosure, inward access, destructive exposure, and ignited-grinder readings are activated through 104:5-9; they do not replace 104:4’s local grammar.
- Vessel, emesis, reserve, crowded-structure, water-flow, withdrawal, dependent, stone-store, and hydraulic images from the first-pass channel review remain bounded within the landed mechanisms. No nomination was promoted to a recurring system, maturity claim, or surah-wide name.
- The hydraulic outlier depends on split mapping and remains branch-distant. The fuel outlier remains an “as if” feedstock analogy because the grammar gives a subject cast into the destination. The wealth-store outlier remains an ironic storage analogy.
- Cross-run publication supplies five strong coverage findings, but the grade is not used as truth rank or a filter. Its findings remain landed in E12, E14-E15, E21, and E22.
- The first-pass channel review is `first-pass-single-reader`; it has no adjudication, accept/reject decision, or per-ayah maturity. Its nominations therefore support local inference only.
