## Counter-Evidence And Boundaries

- The local canonical surface is active possession, while `WA:1:4:1:qiraat-authority-range` preserves owner, kingly sovereignty, and intensified authority together. The prose states the aligned surface first and lets the authority range remain nearby.
- The canonical genitive chain is retained even though `WA:1:4:2:qiraat-adverbial-contrast` records an alternative case direction toward an adverbial time setting. This alternative is rendered as a grammatical pressure, not selected as the ayah's parse.
- `HFT:summary.remaining_uncertainties` says obedience, reckoning, debt, custom, and civic branches of the final word coexist without a packet rule that selects one. The prose therefore gives each a contained landing and does not collapse them into a single definition.
- `HFT:d_named_worshipped_jurisdiction`, `HFT:d_mercy_framed_account`, `HFT:d_praiseworthy_valuation`, `HFT:d_cultivated_maturity`, `HFT:d_legible_disclosed_account`, `HFT:d_present_avowed_obedience`, `HFT:d_aided_liability`, `HFT:d_guiding_owner_of_approach`, `HFT:d_rectifying_appraisal`, `HFT:d_forked_recompense`, `HFT:d_customary_regime_change`, and `HFT:d_recovery_of_hidden_account` each carry a live alternative in their `reader_inference` field. Those alternatives remain in the evidence by preserving the readings as contained additions rather than treating the inferred causal arrows as grammar.
- The five outliers are retained because the bundle marks them `surprising_valid_outliers`; their own containment notes are carried into the prose. In particular, `o_advance_from_the_creditor` is bounded by its non-dominant split mapping, `o_rising_event` by its contextual resurrection status, and `o_decisive_passage`/`o_hardened_boundary` by their analogy status.
- Weak and contrary retrieval rows remain part of the apparatus: `IA:72:21` contrasts human power with the ownership claim, `IA:109:6` preserves a distinct-identity dīn reading, and `IA:109:2`, `IA:12:40`, and `IA:98:5` preserve the present-obedience horizon. The 13 `no value` rows were not rendered as findings because their own notes say they add no material contribution.
