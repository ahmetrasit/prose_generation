  from carrying a recurring surah architecture. The candidate

  retained in the evidence surface as an explicit omission rather than
  rendered as a surah-wide claim. Its locally usable completed-revelation

  while the task asks for before/after reader experience. I used its
  baseline_models, context_deltas, and surprising_valid_outliers as

  missing v12_reader_responses.
- The bundle contains a first-pass channel review and an external generated
  channel manifest, but channel_subchannels_anchored_here is empty and the
  review has no adjudication or maturity. No channel was named or rendered.
- The prose contract requires every surface word and every admitted finding,
  while the evidence warns that word-analysis identities do not align with
  QAC word numbering. The final two word-analysis records therefore use
  word_morpheme_spans to reach qac 17:39:17:1 and 17:39:18:1; no renumbering
  rule was invented.
- Some HFT context deltas include broader contextual sequences. I rendered
  their locally anchored mechanisms only: formative correction, accountable
  wisdom, bounded versus absolute authority, agency reversal, and the
  downward/outward consequence geometry. Their live alternatives remain in

  none is ranked as the true or strongest reading.
- The prose span syntax is specified, but no exact gloss punctuation or
  transliteration normalization is prescribed. I used the bundle's surface
  forms and Turkish-readable transliterations, with compact target-language
  glosses.
