=== PROMPT FRICTION ===

## Specification friction

- The bundle gives stable IDs for focus-trace baselines, context deltas and outliers, but it does not provide a canonical identifier scheme for `surprise:<id>` rows. The findings index therefore uses descriptive local IDs for the four baselines, eight deltas and three outliers. This naming rule was invented to satisfy the required addressable synthesis rows; the source model IDs remain in the evidence surface.
- The prompt requires `[supports-primary]` or `[shifts-primary]` for every surprise row, while the HFT packet supplies before/after readings without those relation labels. Relations were assigned from the contained reader-visible effect: a deepening of the plain act is `supports-primary`, and a changed temporal, referential or social frame is `shifts-primary`. This is a rendering rule, not a claim about source confidence or truth rank.
- The active Layer 2 instructions prohibit naming a recurring channel or maturity, while the anchored channel review supplies a parent channel and subchannel name. The local enclosure synthesis was retained through the ayah's own words and marked `[inference]`; no channel name, recurrence claim or maturity claim entered prose.
- The hermetic prompt says the bundle is complete and restricts external reads, while the rendering profile permits listed channel files when detail is necessary. The inlined channel nomination, HFT and reader-walk material supplied the needed local detail, so no external manifest file was read.
- The word-analysis prompt and the QAC layer use different word units. The bundle explicitly records 26 analysis words versus 23 QAC words, seven unresolved surface spans and dangling analysis refs `12:23:24-26`. I used direct QAC morpheme identities and kept `WA` topic identities separate; no positional repair was invented.

## Coverage friction

- Per-ayah staged reader responses are absent because the default workflow retired them. The available HFT record is reconstructed, so the prose uses baseline/context-delta/outlier reader experience and never reports staged labels or confidence movement.
- A whole-surah reading file is absent. No whole-surah claim was made.
- The expected uncompressed path-family file `quran-data/data/analysis/channels/network-v3/s012/paths/path_families/semantic_path_families.jsonl` is missing from the manifest coverage. It was unnecessary for this local first pass and its contents were not inferred.
- Reviewed Turkish glosses are absent for `root_001533`, dominant `root_001272` and `root_000967`. The prose stays within the bundle's word-analysis payoff, English gloss range, direct ayah form and supplied activation records.
- The channel review is first-pass single-reader material without adjudication, a second reader or maturity. It was used as nomination evidence only.
- No candidate topic was omitted. The 59 candidates were admitted because each supplied a distinct local form, semantic, sound, intertext or later-reader payoff. Sound observations were kept compact and attached to the word movement that carries them.
