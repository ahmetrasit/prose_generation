## Counter-Evidence And Boundaries

- `وَهَنَ` has non-bodily branches, including resolve, late-night hour, occupational and idiomatic uses (`root_001687:B002`, `B005-B008`). The local perfect verb plus bone subject establishes bodily structural weakness; prose uses only branch pressure that remains locally connected.
- `ٱلْعَظْمُ` belongs to a field that also includes greatness, calamity and pride (`root_001029:B001-B004`, `B006-B010`). The concrete noun is locally selected as bone (`B005`); greatness appears only as resonance.
- `ش ع ل` includes anger, flow, vessels and names (`root_000799:B003`, `B005-B007`). Head plus `شَيْبًا` establishes blazing whiteness (`B002`), with flame and spread (`B001`, `B004`) retained as metaphorical mechanism.
- `د ع و` includes claims, collapse and several lexicalized senses (`root_000478:B002-B008`). The vocative prayer setting and verbal noun select petitionary calling (`B001`).
- `شَقِيًّا` retains moral/evaluative wretchedness, but the governed prayer phrase narrows the local outcome to unsuccessful, unanswered deprivation. The prose keeps both layers.
- Inter-ayah rows labelled `no value` were retained in the bundle but not rendered. They are retrieval artifacts under `PRINCIPLES.md` section 9, not counter-readings requiring prose.
