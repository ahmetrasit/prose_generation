## Counter-evidence and boundaries

- `HFT:D01` retains the alternative that the proof ends one attachment without causing the later intra-group split. The prose says “gibi görünür” and keeps the direct after-time grammar.
- `HFT:D02` leaves open whether the proof means the messenger, recited contents, whole delivery complex, or a wider manifest proof. The prose uses the complex as a contextual image.
- `HFT:D03` says upright content may mark value without making an audience-causation claim. The prose therefore locates the focus on the holders’ response while preserving the content’s value.
- `HFT:D04` leaves open whether 98:5 states a norm without explaining the sociology of 98:4. The prose presents the demand as a later sharpening of the reader’s understanding.
- `HFT:D05` says the repeated giving-root may be ordinary wording. The prose calls the receive-to-give relation a reading produced by the recurrence, not a causal fact.
- `HFT:D06` leaves open whether 98:6-7 classifies outcomes without explaining the earlier split. The prose uses the later contrast as a possible extension of the response-sorting operation.
- `HFT:D07` leaves open whether the road-fork image is only analogy and whether later outcomes grammatically continue the split. The prose preserves the inferential wording.
- `v12_cross_run_publication.findings[5]` grades the pathological pressure/flow formulation as `reject`. That row is retained here as a boundary; `surprise:pressure-delivery` is restricted to HFT’s pressure/toppling analogy and is never presented as the lexical meaning of `جَاءَتْهُمُ`.
- The three HFT outliers are marked exploratory and contain their own warnings. They remain in prose because the bundle calls them surprising valid outliers, gives anchored traces, and the task requires materially distinct grounded outliers to be preserved when their reader payoff can be stated without replacing the primary.
- The 51 channel nominations are first-pass, single-reader review material with no adjudication or maturity. They were used only as candidate corroboration for local word relations; the prose names no channel, recurrence, maturity, or surah-wide system.
