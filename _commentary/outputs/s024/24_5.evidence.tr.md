## Counter-evidence and review boundaries

- The HFT legal-reentry model leaves the exact reach of the exception unresolved: it may concern only the `fāsiq` status, only divine liability, or a wider part of the preceding consequences. The prose states a conditional reentry path and does not choose among those scopes.
- The speech-circulation model infers counter-speech and relational remediation from the supplied network; its live alternative is generic good conduct with no duty to reverse the communication network.
- The route-break model infers a replacement practice from return, tracked steps, and purification; its live alternative is a general warning that does not specify the form of repair here.
- The collective-return and permission models are retained inside the shared relational-reintegration landing, not presented as separate surah architecture. Their alternatives remain: individual distribution without communal reintegration, and temporary permission rather than wrongdoing reentry.
- The two outliers are explicitly exploratory. The transparent-covering image is not a lexical identity, and the generative-kin image is not a literal kinship claim.
- The anchored channel review is first-pass, single-reader material with no adjudication or maturity decision. Channel names and recurring-system claims are absent from the prose.
