- d_speech_action_test records that the later obedience scenes may concern covenantal action rather than the focus’s epistemic warrant. d_accountable_delivery likewise says the messenger-and-burden comparison remains analogical. Both alternatives are preserved.

None is presented as the primary lexical meaning or as an established channel.
