# Friction — 1:5

- **Channel material and the boundary it sits on.** Both surprise readings developed here (`surprise:road-work`, `surprise:backed-service`) rest partly on branches — ع ب د `B005` (road smoothed by treading) and the ع ب د `B001`/`B006` (owned vs. honored) contrast — that also appear, verbatim, as members of first-pass channel-discovery subchannels anchored at 1:5 ("Orientation, Passage, and Breakdown > The Marked and Straight Route"; "Devotional Dependence and Rule > Sovereignty, Ownership, and Service"). `docs/CHANNELS.md` §3 reserves channel disclosure for the layer-2.5 pass and explicitly forbids the isolated layer-2 writer from naming a channel, asserting recurrence, or importing members from other ayahs. At the same time, `COMMENTARY_SPEC.md` and the task prompt require carrying the full field of activated readings, and the must_integrate topic `1:5:2:service-submission-range` already names "physical smoothing" in its own reason field — meaning this material is not optional discovery noise but is partly load-bearing for an obligatory topic. The two documents pull in opposite directions on the same evidence at the exact point where the discovery pipeline's own worked example (`docs/CHANNELS.md` §1, "The Fātiḥa path channel") describes this precise material. Resolution taken: both surprises are written strictly as this-ayah's-own-root-range color, entered only through نَعْبُدُ and نَسْتَعِينُ's own branches, with the forward reference to 1:6 limited to the single bundle-supplied word span (`ٱهْدِنَا`) and no description of 1:6/1:7's content, no channel name, and no members imported from other ayahs. This is a judgment call at a boundary the governing documents themselves flag as high-risk, not a mechanical application of a rule, and a reviewer may reasonably judge the line was drawn in a different place than intended.

- **No mechanical inclusion rule for `v12_focus_trace_hermetic`.** Word-level topics carry `commentary_obligation` (`must_integrate` / `candidate` / `ledger_only`), which is checkable. The focus-trace material (3 baseline models, 12 context deltas, 4 surprising valid outliers for this ayah) carries no equivalent tag — only `status` (new/revised/strengthened/weakened) and `confidence` (strong/medium/exploratory), which the task prompt itself says are not to be reported to the reader and are not stated to function as an inclusion gate. The instructions say to "render this as reading experience" and treat outliers as "not errors by default," but give no rule for how many of 12 deltas or 4 outliers must surface in continuous prose for a single ayah. Absent such a rule, 2 of 3 baseline models, 1 of 12 deltas, and 0 of 4 outliers were developed into full prose (the two surprise turns above); the rest were read, judged either redundant with material already carried, too exploratory to state as more than a possibility (all four outliers are rated `exploratory`), or dependent on lexical material from 1:4/1:7 not licensed by any word_analysis topic for 1:5. This is recorded transparently in the evidence surface's "Not admitted / not rendered" section rather than silently dropped, but the selection itself is a discretionary compression the instructions do not fully specify.

- **`aligned_qac_word_ref` dangling reference.** The bundle's own coverage block flags `word_analysis_qac_alignment.consistent: false` and names `1:5:5` as a dangling ref (word_analysis's 5-word orthographic count vs. QAC's 4-word morphological count, since وَ and the second إِيَّاكَ are one QAC word with two morphemes). This is an upstream data property, explicitly not auto-repaired by design (per PRINCIPLES §11's no-hidden-renumbering rule). It did not block commentary because `word_morpheme_spans` supplied a reliable alternate join, but it meant every word-level QAC citation in the evidence surface had to be double-checked against `word_morpheme_spans` rather than taken directly from `word_analysis.aligned_qac_word_ref`. Noted here since a future reviewer checking evidence rows against raw QAC by the naive ref would find an apparent mismatch at word index 4 that is not a citation error.

- **Gloss vs. positional meaning for إِيَّاكَ.** The task's structured-span format asks for `gloss` as "the target-language meaning" of the Arabic word. إِيَّاكَ's bare lexical content is simply the accusative second-person pronoun ("Seni" / "you"); the exclusivity ("yalnız Seni") is a positional/grammatical effect of fronting, established in prose immediately after the span is opened, not part of the pronoun's dictionary sense. The gloss chosen here folds the fronting-derived reading into the gloss itself ("yalnız Seni") rather than glossing the bare pronoun and explaining fronting separately, on the judgment that this is what the word contributes *as heard in this ayah* — but the instructions do not disambiguate whether `gloss` should be the word's isolated lexical meaning or its in-context contribution, and a stricter reading might require "Seni" alone with the exclusivity carried entirely by surrounding prose.

- **Candidate admission for `1:5:5:patience-help-recurrence`.** This candidate was read and not admitted (see evidence surface) on the judgment that its reader_payoff mostly states an absence ("patience itself is not named here") rather than a distinct positive reading, and the prompt is explicit that "surprise alone does not justify inclusion." The instructions give no sharper test than "reader_payoff... if you cannot make that payoff land, the topic is not yet written," which leaves the line between "thin but real payoff" and "not yet written" to the writer's judgment. A different writer could reasonably have admitted a one-sentence version of this candidate.

- **No `shifts-primary` surprise survived.** Both admitted surprises are `supports-primary`. Material that might have shifted the primary reading's frame (e.g., the focus-trace delta reading service under a future day-of-reckoning horizon, `delta_accountable_obedience`) was considered but requires importing م ل ك/يوم/دين material from 1:4 as full word spans not licensed by any word_analysis topic scoped to 1:5's own words; it was set aside rather than force-grounded.
