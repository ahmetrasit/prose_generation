# Friction — 1:7

- **`word_analysis`/QAC word-count mismatch.** The bundle's own coverage block
  flags `word_analysis_qac_alignment.consistent: false`: `word_analysis`
  segments the ayah into 10 orthographic critical words while `qac_morphemes`
  has 9 morphological words, leaving `aligned_qac_word_ref: "1:7:10"` as a
  dangling reference. The bundle explicitly supplies `word_morpheme_spans` as
  the reliable join and instructs against using `aligned_qac_word_ref`
  directly. This is documented as an upstream defect, not something for the
  writer to repair, so it produced no uncertainty in the output — but it is
  worth flagging that any downstream process joining on `aligned_qac_word_ref`
  for 1:7 will get a wrong answer for the final word.

- **Citing another ayah by number without its own span.** The instructions
  require that "a word from another ayah is not in front of the reader" and
  that a reading built on such a word must give it a full `{ar, tr, gloss}`
  span before leaning on it. Several strongly-evidenced bundle materials (the
  4:69 favored-class formula, the 2:61/2:90/3:112/5:60 wrath formula, several
  `inter_ayah_rows`, and most of `v12_focus_trace_hermetic`'s `context_deltas`
  drawing on 1:1–1:6) point at *other ayahs' words* without supplying those
  words' Arabic surface forms in this bundle — only ayah numbers and English
  paraphrase notes. The instructions do not fully resolve whether a bare
  ayah-number citation ("aynı kalıp Kur'an'ın başka bir yerinde de belirir,
  4:69") without any span counts as "leaning on" that word, or whether it is
  a permitted footnote-level gesture. I treated citation-only, no-argument-
  weight-bearing mentions as permitted (matching how the bundle's own
  `word_analysis` prose for word 3 already cites 4:69 the same way) and
  declined to build any reading's substance on the *content* of those
  unshown words. A stricter reading of the rule would drop even the bare
  ayah-number citations; a looser one would let me paraphrase what 1:4's
  "mâlik/yevmi'd-dîn" contributes (as several reader-walk items and one
  `context_delta` do) without ever showing its Arabic. I did not take the
  looser path.

- **Retrospective-surprise framing for a terminal ayah.** The instructions'
  guidance on retrospective surprises assumes a later ayah exists to supply
  one. 1:7 is the surah's last ayah, so both `v12_reader_walks_wide` and
  `v12_reader_walks.reader_a`'s `retrospective_surprises_md` report that no
  forward change is available — instead they describe 1:7 as reinterpreting
  *earlier* terms (mercy, lordship, reckoning, worship, guidance) backward.
  Rendering that backward reach in reader prose would require citing those
  earlier ayahs' specific words, which runs into the same span problem above.
  I left this out of the prose entirely rather than gesture at it vaguely;
  the instructions do not say whether a bounded, non-lexical gesture ("bu son
  âyet geride bıraktığı isteği somutlaştırır") is acceptable scope for this
  material or whether it edges toward the surah-thesis territory reserved for
  layer 3. I kept the one sentence I wrote in this vein deliberately thin and
  scoped to 1:6 only, since 1:6→1:7 is the one backward relation the bundle
  lets me ground without an unshown span (1:7's own word-analysis prose for
  `صِرَٰطَ` already frames it as continuing 1:6's request).

- **`channel_subchannels_anchored_here` as corroboration vs. as material.**
  The instructions permit using channel material "only when they help you
  notice a locally grounded resonance already supported by this ayah's word
  evidence," explicitly forbidding naming a surah-wide channel or asserting
  recurrence. I used two anchored subchannel entries ("Herd, Livestock, and
  the Stray Margin," "Engulfing and Penetrating Passage") purely as
  corroboration for readings I had already built from this ayah's own
  `root_lexicon` branches and reader-walk evidence — never as the source of
  the reading and never named as channels in the prose. Whether citing their
  motif labels even in the *evidence surface* (not the prose) counts as
  "importing members from other ayahs" is not fully specified; I judged the
  evidence surface is the right place for exactly this kind of apparatus
  (`PRINCIPLES.md` §12) and kept all channel/family/parent-channel names out
  of the prose itself.

- **Two-thirds of `v12_focus_trace_hermetic` set aside.** The trace supplied
  four baseline models, twelve `context_deltas`, and four `surprising_valid_
  outliers` — far more coherent material than the "no length limit" and
  "carry the full field" instructions could comfortably accommodate without
  either (a) leaning on unshown neighbor-ayah words (see above) or (b)
  admitting `exploratory`-confidence, branch-distant analogies whose own
  containment notes warn against treating them as more than material
  metaphor. I used one baseline model (folded into the "two disappearances"
  surprise) and none of the outliers or most context_deltas outright, listing
  what was set aside in the evidence coverage note per `PRINCIPLES.md` §7
  rather than silently dropping it. This is a judgment call about how far
  "full field" extends into D3/exploratory material at ayah level; the spec
  does not give a hard boundary between "candidate worth admitting" and
  "exploratory outlier correctly left for a later, better-grounded pass."

- **No style-audit profile was specified.** The prompt allows a
  "profile-specific style audit" section here when a prompt profile requests
  one; none was named in this run, so none is included.
