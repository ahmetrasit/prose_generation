# Friction — 1:6 (sonnet run)

- **External quran-data channel files not fetched.** `channel_generated_outputs` grants read access to 13 external network-v3 files (channel_candidates, candidate_graphs, semantic_path_families, etc.), several of them very large (path_families jsonl ~2 MB, candidate_graphs ~782 KB). The governing prompt says to read them "only when channel-family or path detail is necessary" and "do not browse the repository generally." The in-bundle `channel_subchannels_anchored_here` (32 first-pass subchannels already anchored at 1:6, with active motifs, synthesis, and ayah anchors given in full) plus `inter_ayah_rows` and `root_lexicon` already supplied enough locally-grounded candidate material to write the commentary. I judged the external files unnecessary and did not open them. This is a defensible reading of "necessary," not a certainty — a reviewer might want the path-family files consulted for the swallowing/passage or gift-return threads specifically, since those are exactly the kind of cross-ayah path material the external files index.

- **No topic_id for the "leading front" (ه د ي B003) and "sustaining support" (ق و م B004/B009) readings.** Both are well-evidenced — the first through `root_gloss_range`, the hermetic focus trace, both reader walks, and a **strong**-grade cross-run publication finding; the second through the root_lexicon branch glosses and another **strong**-grade cross-run finding — but neither has its own `word_analysis` topic_id the way `gift-bestowal-pressure` or `swallowing-passage-pressure` do. The instructions ("root_lexicon is full... full field means all distinct reader payoffs from the activated material") and the findings-index instruction ("`<ref>` is whatever the bundle calls the thing: a topic_id, a root:branch pair...") both anticipate this case, so I cited these two readings as `root:branch` refs marked `[inference]` rather than omitting them. This is the specification working as designed, but it is worth flagging that two of this ayah's best-corroborated secondary readings sit outside the topic-obligation bookkeeping entirely and would have been silently droppable by a stricter "must_integrate-only" reading of the task.

- **No stated disposition for a cross-run "reject" grade.** `v12_cross_run_publication` grades its five findings strong/strong/strong/weak/reject. PRINCIPLES.md §9 defines how to treat inter-ayah retrieval labels (`no value` = retrieval artifact, retain but do not render) but says nothing about cross-run finding grades, which are a different axis (review-adjudicated synthesis quality, not retrieval marginality). The `reject` finding here is the sword-cutting-passage sense of `ص ر ط` B003 (root_000858). I treated `reject` as excluding that reading from prose by analogy to `no value`, consistent with `word_analysis` itself never having selected B003 as a topic at any point. I could not find an explicit rule confirming this analogy is correct rather than merely convenient.

- **Almost all candidate topics were admitted (19 of 19).** The task warns against admitting candidates "by surprise or branch count" and asks for a payoff test per topic, not blanket admission. For this ayah every candidate topic did carry a distinct, checkable reader payoff on inspection, and the ayah is unusually dense for its length (three words, 34 word-level topics total, an 18-branch root family on the last word alone) — the length-is-a-consequence-not-a-target instruction and the S103-style precedent ("a three-word ayah with a dense lexical field can run long and that is correct") both point toward admission here. Still, the near-total admission rate is close enough to "carry everything" that a reviewer should specifically check each candidate against its stated payoff rather than trusting the admission count.

- **Hermetic focus trace `context_deltas` were used only lightly.** The trace's `context_deltas` and `surprising_valid_outliers` pull in roots from 1:1–1:5 (mercy, praise, lordship, ownership, day, recompense, service, aid) to show how later/surrounding context sharpens the 1:6 reading. PRINCIPLES.md §5 requires that "a resonance enters through a word the reader has already met, in a form they have already been given" — introducing six or seven other ayahs' roots with full spans to ground each context-delta model would have turned this into something closer to a surah-level synthesis, which layer 2 is explicitly forbidden from writing. I used the `baseline_models` (which stay entirely within 1:6's own three words) as the main before/after material and only referenced 1:5 (already grounded via the `boundary-from-declaration` topic) and 1:7 (forward-looking, already grounded via three separate forward-facing topics) directly. The richer context-delta material (nurture-as-nurture-of-the-course, accountable-calibration, habituated-service, coactive road-preparation) was set aside rather than rendered, since properly grounding it would have required introducing un-met words from 1:1–1:4. This is a defensible but not unique reading of the grounding rule at the layer-2/layer-3 boundary.

- **`medium`-grade inter_ayah_rows (65 of 143) were not individually rendered.** They were scanned in full for counter-evidence and contrastive language (`contrary`, `counter`, `oppos-`) rather than read one-by-one into the prose. PRINCIPLES.md §9 forbids filtering by the strong/medium/weak axis, but also frames the axis as ordering marginal contribution, not as a mandate to narrate every row. Given that the 48 `strong` rows already establish the recurring straight-path formula exhaustively and the scan of all 143 rows surfaced only two further contrary-evidence rows (7:16, already used; 14:28, weak grade, a people led from favor into ruin — set aside as duplicating the swallowing/passage tension already carried by 7:16), I judged no medium-grade row carried a distributed finding invisible from the strong/weak rows already used. A stricter reading of §9's "distributed findings" warning (illustrated there with the S103 weak-cluster case) would ask for closer individual review of the 65 medium rows.

- **No true gaps in the bundle.** `v12_reader_responses` (staged stage_00/01/02) is absent, but this is by design (retired from the default workflow per the bundle's own coverage note) and the hermetic focus trace served as its sanctioned replacement without any live instruction going unsatisfied. The single missing file (`semantic_path_families.jsonl.gz`) duplicates an uncompressed file already present. Neither is friction in the sense the task means it; both are recorded here only for completeness per PRINCIPLES.md §7 ("report gaps rather than filling them").
