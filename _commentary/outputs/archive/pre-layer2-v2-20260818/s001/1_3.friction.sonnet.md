# Friction report — 1:3

1. **Cross-ayah grounding vs. channel-material caution.** The grounding rule requires that "a word from another ayah... cite that ayah by reference and give its word its own full span before leaning on it." But the only place in this ayah's bundle carrying the literal Arabic surface text for 1:2, 1:4, 1:5, 1:6, and 1:7's words is `channel_subchannels_anchored_here.*.ayah_anchors` — first-pass, single-reader, unadjudicated channel-discovery material that the same prompt elsewhere insists is "candidate evidence, not your output" and must not be treated as established. I resolved this by using that field only for its literal, uncontested Qur'anic surface-text quotations (which are just the plain text of those ayahs, not a channel claim), while explicitly not naming, asserting the recurrence of, or importing any channel system from that source. The prompt does not say this is permitted; it also does not forbid it. Flagging the tension rather than silently picking a side.

2. **1:4's `يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` has no surface form anywhere in the bundle.** The Hermetic Focus Trace's `d-mercy-frames-accounting` delta cites the roots `ي و م` and `د ي ن` for 1:4, but no field anywhere in the bundle (not `channel_subchannels_anchored_here`, not `branch_inventories`, not `root_lexicon`) gives their literal Arabic surface text — only `مَٰلِكِ` is available as a surface form for that ayah. I therefore grounded the reckoning reading only through `مَٰلِكِ` and referred to "hesap" (reckoning/accounting) by gloss rather than by Arabic span, to avoid asserting a spelling not present in the bundle. This is a genuine gap, not a judgment call I'm fully confident in: the reading still leans in part on unsourced lexical content (the concept of "the Day of Reckoning") beyond what I could span.

3. **Cross-run "reject" grade vs. word_analysis topic status — no stated interaction rule.** `v12_cross_run_publication` grades a postpartum-care synthesis of the mercy root's B004 branch as `reject`. `word_analysis`'s own `1:3:1:root-image-pressure` topic (status `narrowed`/`candidate`) does not itself name B004 or exclude it — its `representative_source_ids` and reasoning discuss the root's branches generically. Nothing in `PRINCIPLES.md`, `COMMENTARY_SPEC.md`, or the task prompt states how a cross-run-publication `reject` grade should interact with an already-`candidate` word_analysis topic. I treated the `reject` grade as sufficient reason to keep B004 out of the prose entirely (leaving the root-pressure surprise built only on B002/B003), reading it as the kind of "rejected reading" that PRINCIPLES §7 says should be preserved as a production record rather than rendered — but this is my own interpretive bridge between two evidence tracks the spec never explicitly connects.

4. **How many `candidate` topics to admit is a real judgment call, not a rule.** The prompt says "read the candidate topics before deciding; surprise alone does not justify inclusion," but gives no test beyond "distinct reader payoff." Of 8 candidate topics on this ayah, I admitted all 8, because each survived that test on inspection (none simply restated another). For a two-word ayah this produces an unusually long commentary and a 21-line findings index (13 topic lines + 8 surprise lines, see point 6). This is very likely correct under "carry the full field" and "a dense ayah has a long index; that is correct," but I flag it because the density comes almost entirely from one extraordinarily rich Hermetic Focus Trace (3 baseline models + 6 context deltas + 4 outliers) attached to a two-word ayah, and a reviewer may reasonably ask whether all 8 candidates truly clear the payoff bar or whether some should have been folded together further.

5. **Selecting a subset of the Hermetic Focus Trace's 6 context deltas and 4 outliers.** The prompt asks for "before and after" to be rendered "as reading experience," but does not state whether every context delta and every outlier must appear. I rendered all 6 context deltas and 3 of the 4 outliers (all but `o-mercy-as-visible-mark`, a cross-domain reading built on a non-dominant split-root mapping that I judged too thin to explain to a reader with no Arabic without either overclaiming or spending a disproportionate amount of space on the split-mapping mechanism itself). This is recorded in the evidence surface under "Not rendered," but the decision to drop exactly one outlier and keep the rest is a judgment call the spec does not adjudicate.

6. **Surprise-line count.** Eight `surprise:` lines emerged from this ayah — more than the two worked examples in `docs/CHANNELS.md` (1:6, 1:7) show for a full path-channel disclosure. The prompt permits an unbounded number ("no length limit... never a reason to leave something out") and warns separately against manufacturing coherence; I believe each of the 8 lines is independently evidence-supported and distinct rather than padding, but a two-word ayah producing 8 secondary synthesis lines is a genuinely unusual density worth a reviewer's attention rather than something I resolved silently.

7. **`v12_reader_walks` and `v12_reader_walks_wide` were folded into the same before/after readings as the Hermetic Focus Trace rather than narrated separately.** The prompt treats staged v12 reader responses and the Hermetic Focus Trace as alternative signals ("If the bundle contains v12 reader responses... If the bundle contains v12_focus_trace_hermetic, use it as the cheaper replacement signal"), but this bundle has neither staged responses nor an absence of reader walks — it has reader walks (2 regular-window readers, 1 wide-window reader) *and* the Hermetic Focus Trace *and* the cross-run publication, all three corroborating largely the same handful of readings (hinge-to-reckoning, kinship/womb-as-bond, softening-against-hardening). The prompt does not say whether these three tracks should be narrated as separate movements or merged where they agree. I merged them (using the trace's own model text as the primary voice, since it comes with explicit before/after framing and containment notes) and used the reader walks and cross-run findings mainly as corroboration/exclusion signals in the evidence surface. This kept the prose from repeating the same claim three times under three labels, but it also means the reader-walk material's own distinctive phrasing (e.g. reader_a's "yolun sertleşmeye ve yitip gitmeye karşı yumuşatıcı bağı" framing) is not quoted directly in the prose, only used to confirm a reading already built from the trace.

8. **No style-audit request in this prompt profile.** The task document's negation-avoidance table and "not X but Y" containment table are project-wide style rules, not a profile-specific style-audit request, so nothing here is logged as a style audit; all points above are genuine ambiguity/judgment-call friction under the definition given ("ambiguous, contradictory, unsatisfiable, or silent").
