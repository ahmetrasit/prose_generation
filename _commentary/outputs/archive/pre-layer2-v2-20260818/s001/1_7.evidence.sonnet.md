- The four `surprising_valid_outliers` in `v12_focus_trace_hermetic`
  (`out_hydrological_way`, `out_gestational_passage`, `out_watchful_eye_states`,
  `out_fracture_and_standing`) were read and are not rendered. Each is
  `confidence: exploratory` and branch-distant (the packet's own containment
  notes for two of them explicitly warn against treating them as lexical
  replacement). Admitting them risked "manufactured coherence" beyond what
  this ayah's own words can carry at reader-facing grounding. Recorded here
  rather than silently dropped, per `PRINCIPLES.md` §7.
- Most `context_deltas` in the same trace (`ctx_mercy_affiliation`,
  `ctx_cultivated_completion`, `ctx_mercy_bounded_wrath`,
  `ctx_accountable_partition`, `ctx_enacted_road`, `ctx_guided_specification`,
  `ctx_path_as_transmitted_gift`, `ctx_communal_infrastructure`,
  `ctx_growth_with_measure`, `ctx_marked_route`) were read but not built into
  prose, because they lean substantially on specific words from 1:1–1:6 whose
  own Arabic spans are outside this bundle — rendering them would have meant
  asking the reader to hold an unintroduced word (`PRINCIPLES.md` §5.2). The
  one exception already integrated organically is the observation that 1:7
  repeats and specifies the road named in 1:6, which the word-analysis prose
  for `صِرَٰطَ` itself already states without leaning on any specific new
  Arabic word from 1:6.
- `v12_reader_walks.reader_a` (`activated_readings_md`, items 1–6) and
