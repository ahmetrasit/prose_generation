## Counter-evidence and composition decisions

- The primary reading is retained in every resonance sentence. No resonance is ranked, selected as the ayah's hidden meaning, or promoted to a surah-wide channel.
- The channel nomination is first-pass single-reader material with no accept/reject decision or maturity. It is used only for the two local prepared-place and bodily-movement syntheses; the parent channel name and its architecture are not disclosed in prose.
- The two HFT baseline models remain separate because prepared inhabitable center and bodily choreography have different reader payoffs. The housekeeping baseline is merged with the later `ctx_house_kept_standing` resonance because both enter through `al-qāʾimīn` and have the same custodial mechanism; this is a composition merge, not a source adjudication.
- The wide reader-walk surprise about routes, witnessing, feeding, and redistribution is merged into `surprise:equal-access-receiving-center`; it shares the receiving-center mechanism. The narrow reader-walk surprise about 22:77 is kept in the distinct inherited-office resonance because its payoff is continuity of commission and beneficent action.
- The filtered-container and snare items are admitted because the bundle explicitly marks them as surprising valid outliers and supplies containment rules. Both are qualified in prose; neither is used as a replacement translation.
