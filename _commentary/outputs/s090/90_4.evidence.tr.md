- The ordinary hardship reading is retained as the floor throughout. HFT `baseline_constitutive_hardship` supports a constitutive medium; HFT `baseline_measured_upright_formation` keeps uprightness alongside it; HFT `baseline_visceral_core` keeps the bodily-center reading alongside it. The packet explicitly says the locative construction still presses toward a containing condition, so uprightness is rendered as a coexistence rather than a replacement.
- HFT `delta_bounded_birth_apportionment` records a live alternative: the opening oath and city may authorize the assertion without specifying the mechanics of `kabad`. The prose therefore calls the city-to-body movement an inferred scale image.
- HFT `delta_hardship_released_between_people` preserves the alternative that the later acts demonstrate a response within unchanged hardship rather than relieving `kabad` itself. The prose leaves both the transfer of pressure and the response-within-pressure reading available.
- HFT `delta_opening_versus_sealing` preserves the alternative that release and final enclosure belong to separate domains. The prose presents an aperture polarity as a local inference, not a surah-wide system.
- HFT outliers `outlier_city_as_macro_body`, `outlier_gestational_pressure_and_mercy`, `outlier_compacted_middle_and_piled_wealth`, `outlier_guidance_shadowed_by_hard_descent`, and `outlier_bow_grip_and_launched_action` are explicitly marked in the evidence as analogical, qualified, or form-distant. None is used as a replacement translation.
- The first-pass channel review supplied 29 nominations but no adjudication, second reader, maturity, or channel ledger. Relevant local nominations were used as corroboration for formation, social presence, pupil-image, body-core, terrain, and release/closure; unrelated water, textile, fragrance, and similar nominations were not promoted to prose because they did not produce a grounded, distinct payoff for this ayah.
