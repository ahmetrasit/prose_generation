## Internal reconciliation

- The scope analysts own explicit evidentiary decisions inside their lanes. Do
  not reopen an accepted, narrowed, or rejected disposition because another
  reading seems more canonical, confident, vivid, concise, or elegant.
- Preserve every accepted, narrowed, and newly accepted finding. A format or
  accounting defect is not a reason to discard its semantic content. Internally
  partition the complete union of their finding refs so that none is omitted,
  repeated, or left outside the prose and evidence accounting.
- Audit every supplied candidate against its lane ledger. Inspect the packet
  when `coverage_complete` is false, a candidate or connection is absent, a
  finding identity is unclear, or a ledger leaves a grounded contact
  unaccounted for. V4 gives you authority to recover accounting from supplied
  evidence; it does not give you authority to invent evidence or replace an
  analyst's substantive judgment. Record anything that cannot be losslessly
  recovered in evidence and friction rather than silently making the set look
  complete.
- Inspect every rejection. Canonical absence, novelty, similarity, conflict,
  difficulty of explanation, source count, confidence, vividness, prose length,
  or a trust label alone is not a valid reason to lose a reading. When such a
  rejection or an omitted disposition can be recovered from a packet with a
  complete carrier, mechanism, changed reading, payoff, containment, and
  evidence trail, preserve it as an accounting recovery and identify that
  recovery in evidence and friction. Otherwise preserve the unresolved problem
  there without fabricating a verdict.
- Preserve narrowed-candidate exclusions exactly. Do not restore an excluded
  branch as if it survived, and do not erase its stated exclusion reason.
- Resolve a referral only when the receiving lane independently accepted or
  explicitly incorporated the same grounded finding. Preserve the referral's
  complete carrier, mechanism, payoff, containment, and evidence refs. If the
  receiving decision is absent, keep the referral unresolved in evidence and
  friction; do not promote or discard it silently.
- Consolidate only exact semantic duplicates: the same local anchor,
  mechanism, direction, and reader payoff. Preserve related findings when any
  of those differ.
- For an exact duplicate, preserve the complete union of member candidate,
  proposal, support, branch, connection, contact, and branch-facet evidence.
  Preserve one scope movement per member, including exact macro
  `local_before`/`context_after` and global
  `isolated_before`/`wider_after`/`wider_trigger` fields. Keeping a finding while
  genericizing its evidence or movement is not conservation.
- Keep countervailing findings alive without verdict or rank. Canonical status,
  source count, confidence, novelty, vividness, prose length, and ease of
  explanation never decide inclusion.
- Preserve every cited HFT limitation, live alternative, analogy or cross-root
  boundary, and every warning that an image is not a lexical gloss.
- A wider finding must return through a word, relation, or act in the focus
  ayah. Contextual pressure must not be presented as lexical meaning.
- For every HFT-backed finding, preserve source containment, live alternatives,
  analogy or cross-root limits, and any warning that an image is not a lexical
  gloss. Source qualification alone never establishes rejection, and anchor
  Arabic does not independently verify HFT-attributed morphology or roots.
- Availability alone is not activation. Accounting recovery is allowed only
  under the complete packet-grounding rule above.

## Internal prose preparation

Give micro findings their local linguistic footing. Enter macro findings
through the focus ayah, establish the isolated reading, and then show exactly
what the declared pericope changes. Render global findings as a wider discovery
that preserves its wider trigger and returns through an existing word, relation,
or act in the focus ayah. Preserve each finding's concrete image, exact
before/after movement, trigger, mechanism, containment, epistemic status, and
reader payoff. Contextual pressure must never be rewritten as lexical meaning.

Use the three scope ledgers as source work to recompose, never as sections to
concatenate. Organize the commentary by the ayah's acts, relations, images,
tensions, and reader payoffs. Findings from different scopes may share a
movement only when they genuinely perform the same work; every distinct
mechanism and payoff must remain recoverable.

The first-pass prose should reveal before it compresses. Do not replace a
concrete lexical or contextual image with a generic summary, and do not hide an
exploratory or noncanonical finding merely because it needs a short boundary.
Scope names, candidate identities, analysis instruments, and internal
coordinates stay out of reader prose.

Use the supplied focus-surface evidence for exact Arabic and transliteration
values, analytic gloss boundaries, and morphology. Render Arabic in the
canonical single-span syntax with a natural Turkish gloss. Never expose QAC or
analysis coordinates in prose.
