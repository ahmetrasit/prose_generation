## Records whose focus is 1:5

### baseline_exclusive_service_and_support [baseline model, strong]
- before: We worship you and we ask you for help as two coordinated statements.
- after: Our submission is directed only to you, and even the backing by which we sustain it is sought only from you.
- mechanism: Submissive service and requested backing form a two-part orientation: allegiance is restricted to the addressee, then the efficacy needed to continue that allegiance is restricted to the same addressee.
- trace:
  - 1:5 w2 نَعْبُدُ → ع ب د B003 (boyun eğerek itaat ve tapınma) — Submissive worship supplies the pledged act and makes the first clause an allegiance claim. [plain here: the same branch; usage group: The worshipper tells God, “You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help”]
  - 1:5 w4 نَسْتَعِينُ → ع و ن B001 (yardım, destek ve dayanışma) — Helping and backing supply the requested support and make the second clause the enabling side of that allegiance. [plain here: the same branch; usage group: Worshippers say they worship God and seek help from Him]

### baseline_dependent_agency [baseline model, medium]
- before: The speakers abase themselves and then request an external intervention.
- after: The speakers accept non-sovereign status while asking for the capacity by which dependent service can genuinely act.
- mechanism: Owned status removes sovereign self-possession without removing action; seeking backing then supplies caught-up strength. The result is agency exercised from dependence, not passivity.
- trace:
  - 1:5 w2 نَعْبُدُ → ع ب د B001 (özgür olmayan, sahip olunan kişi) — Owned status supplies the speakers' non-sovereign position and frames their action as dependent service. [plain here: B003; usage group: The worshipper tells God, “You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help”]
