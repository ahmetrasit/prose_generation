# S1 source claims requiring editorial review

The Sol augmentation preserves the source commentary. These findings concern existing prose, not repairs applied to it. They remain visible in the marked merged commentary until a separate source-editing decision is made.

1. **¶21 and ¶61 — 82:16.** The source uses this ayah for “no one can disappear from sight on that day.” In 82:14–16, “they” are the wicked and “from it” refers to the Fire. The universal claim is not established by this citation. Image 3 records a mixed cited/conflict verdict; image 12 records the neighboring-ayah conflict.
2. **¶21 — 83:3 and 83:6.** The source says crooked weighing and standing speak from the same root. The quoted weighing ayah uses k-y-l, w-z-n and kh-s-r; q-w-m appears in 83:6. Their thematic relationship survives, but the same-root assertion needs correction. Image 3 retained the valid weighing reference at ¶20 while flagging ¶21.
3. **¶42 — yawm and the Day of Recompense.** The source applies a sunrise-to-sunset lexical definition directly to Fatiha’s fourth ayah. Image 8 contrasts this with the Quran’s extended day measurements and judgment-day contexts. The image may retain daylight as a lexical scene, but should distinguish that scene from a claimed duration of the Day of Recompense.
4. **¶60 — going astray and destination.** The source says the straight path reaches a destination while dalal has none. Image 12 supplies passages where misguidance leads to punishment. Distinguish failure to reach the desired destination from having no destination at all.

These are four editorial issues, supported by multiple individual verdict lines. They are not four additional ayat to insert automatically. See each section’s verdicts.md and parent_review.json for the individual decisions and the corrections made to the new augmentation itself.
