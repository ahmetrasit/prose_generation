# Revision3 observations (provisional until all batches finish)

The first batch supports separating discovery breadth from verification. A shorter list is not itself better: the first image retains all 16 existing source citations and recovers several review cases, but still misses direct cases such as 36:66 and 20:108. Do not tune to a target row count or seed these evaluation cases into the current attempt.

The repeated remaining defect is ayah-local attribution. A plausible passage connection does not establish that every quoted word or described action occurs in that row's ayah. Examples: Luna section2 mixes 4:171 with 4:172 and imports Iblis's reply into 38:75; Terra section3 imports 65:9 into 65:8 and 79:38 into 79:37. Canonical text and immediate context should be checked before downstream prose is accepted.

Arabic string flags need careful classification. Roots, dictionary forms, attached particles, and ordinary/Uthmani spelling generate legitimate mismatches. Keep an exact-quotation check separate from root/lemma/source-section labels. English summaries still need verse-boundary verification even when the Arabic checker reports no issue.

Candidate grading still needs judgment. Broad religious unity, beneficence, and long resurrection continuations can produce defensible but less specific connections. A future experiment could require distinct source-image anchor and ayah-local fact fields, then evaluate whether that improves precision without losing secondary images. That is a proposal, not a change to this frozen run.

Raw lists and labels remain preserved. Review notes do not silently repair candidates or promote model agreement into validated confidence. No Opus call has run.
