# Surah 109: map of image chains

## Chains

### 1. The lowered servant and his master: owned, subdued, made tractable, and the refusal

The root ع ب د runs through 109:2–5 eight times. Its dictionary core is not abstract "worship". It is a person lowering himself before a master (تذلل): the owned slave, the act of making someone servile, and the camel tarred until it is tractable. The same root also names the reverse: a camel that refuses people, and the disdain (أنفة) of someone who will not be humbled. The surah's last word, دين, is defined by the same lowering ("جنس من الانقياد والذل"), and also as the owned, subdued servant (مدين). So the scene is a master, a servant lowered before him, and the question of which master. Each clause in 109:2–5 places the two parties before their objects of service. The speaker's "لَآ أَعْبُدُ" and "وَلَآ أَنَا۠ عَابِدٌۭ" are a servant refusing to be lowered before a false master. In 109:1 the addressees' name holds two senses drawn from this scene: the servant's bow (B014), and its reversal, an obedient servant driven to disobey his master (B007).

Members:
- 109:2–5 أَعْبُدُ / تَعْبُدُونَ / عَٰبِدُونَ / عَابِدٌ / عَبَدتُّمْ — ع ب د B003 — "العبودية إظهار التذلل والعبادة غاية التذلل" (mufradat); "تعبدت للرجل إذا تذللت له" (jamhara); "العبادة الطاعة والتعبد التنسك" (sihah) — the act itself: lowering oneself, at its utmost, before someone. Each clause asks: before whom?
- 109:2–5 same words — ع ب د B001 — "العبد خلاف الحر والجمع عبيد" (sihah); "العبد وهو المملوك" (maqayis) — the owned person: the worshipper as his master's property.
- 109:2–5 same words — ع ب د B002 — "العبد الإنسان حرا أو رقيقا هو عبد الله" (ayn); "عبد بالإيجاد وذلك ليس إلا لله" (mufradat) — every human is already God's servant by being brought into existence. The speaker's أَعْبُدُ keeps to that given ownership. The addressees' worship is directed to another owner.
- 109:2–5 same words — ع ب د B004 — "عبدت الرجل إذا ذللته وعبدت القوم اتخذتهم عبيدا" (jamhara); "استعبدت فلانا اتخذته عبدا" (maqayis;ayn) — making someone servile: the pressure the clauses resist.
- 109:2–5 same words — ع ب د B005 — "البعير المعبد المهنوء بالقطران المذلل" (maqayis;sihah); "المعبدة السفينة المقيرة" (sihah;tahdhib) — the camel smeared with tar and made tractable, the ship coated with pitch: the concrete object that has been "lowered" and prepared for use.
- 109:2–5 same words — ع ب د B011 — "بعير متعبد ومتأبد إذا امتنع على الناس صعوبة" (tahdhib) — the reversal inside the root: the camel that refuses people. The tractable beast and the refusing beast stand under one root, as the refusals in 109:2–5 stand under the word for submission.
- 109:2–5 same words — ع ب د B008 — "العبد الأنفة وعبدت فصمت أي أنفت فسكت" (jamhara); "العبد مثل الأنف والحمية" (maqayis); "العبد بالتحريك الغضب والأنف والاسم العبدة" (sihah) — disdain and indignation at being humbled: the opposite pole of تذلل, carried by the same root as the speaker's refusal.
- 109:6 دِينُكُمْ / دِينِ — د ي ن B001 — "أصل واحد إليه يرجع فروعه كلها وهو جنس من الانقياد والذل" (maqayis); "فالدين الطاعة" (maqayis;sihah) — dīn is being led and lowered. The closing word names the state that the worship clauses enacted.
- 109:6 دِينُكُمْ / دِينِ — د ي ن B004 — "العبد مدين كأنهما أذلهما العمل ويا دين قلبك أي أذل" (maqayis); "دانه دينا أي أذله واستعبده ودينته ملكته" (sihah); "المدين والمدينة العبد والأمة" (mufradat) — dīn as being subdued, enslaved, owned. "Your dīn" is also "your subjection": the master you are under.
- 109:1 ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ — ك ف ر B014 — "التكفير أن يخضع الإنسان لغيره يضع يده على صدره ويتطامن له" (sihah) — the subordinate's body lowered, hand on chest, before a superior (shared with chain 2).
- 109:1 ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ — ك ف ر B007 — "إذا ألجأت مطيعك إلى أن يعصيك فقد أكفرته" (ayn;tahdhib) — pushing one's own obedient servant into disobedience. This is the lowering relation reversed, and it is attested for the addressees' word.

The dictionary joins the two roots in one phrase: "العبد مدين كأنهما أذلهما العمل" (maqayis, under د ي ن), and "الدين لله طاعته والتعبد له" (tahdhib, under د ي ن). Both roots are also glossed with the same word: "العبادة الطاعة" (sihah, ع ب د) / "فالدين الطاعة" (sihah, د ي ن).

- 16:75 — God's parable: "عَبْدًۭا مَّمْلُوكًۭا لَّا يَقْدِرُ عَلَىٰ شَىْءٍۢ" — the owned slave (B001) as a figure.
- 12:39–40 — Yusuf to his two prison companions: "ءَأَرْبَابٌۭ مُّتَفَرِّقُونَ خَيْرٌ أَمِ ٱللَّهُ ٱلْوَٰحِدُ ٱلْقَهَّارُ ... مَا تَعْبُدُونَ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ ... ذَٰلِكَ ٱلدِّينُ ٱلْقَيِّمُ" — masters, service and dīn in one argument.
- 26:22 — Moses answering Pharaoh: "أَنْ عَبَّدتَّ بَنِىٓ إِسْرَٰٓءِيلَ" — ع ب د as enslaving (B004). (The exchange opens at 26:18, where Pharaoh reminds Moses of his upbringing; recalled from memory.)
- 23:47 — Pharaoh and his chiefs on Moses and Aaron: "وَقَوْمُهُمَا لَنَا عَٰبِدُونَ" — عابدون meaning "in servitude to us", the same form as 109:3/5 عَٰبِدُونَ.
- 39:64 — the Prophet is told to say to the idolaters: "قُلْ أَفَغَيْرَ ٱللَّهِ تَأْمُرُوٓنِّىٓ أَعْبُدُ أَيُّهَا ٱلْجَٰهِلُونَ" — the addressees pressing God's servant to serve another (the B007 scene), answered with قل.
- 39:11, 39:14–15 — the Prophet is told: "قُلِ ٱللَّهَ أَعْبُدُ مُخْلِصًۭا لَّهُۥ دِينِى فَٱعْبُدُوا۟ مَا شِئْتُم مِّن دُونِهِۦ" — أعبد and ديني paired, with the same allocation to each side as 109:6.
- 10:104 — the Prophet is told: "قُلْ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ إِن كُنتُمْ فِى شَكٍّۢ مِّن دِينِى فَلَآ أَعْبُدُ ٱلَّذِينَ تَعْبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَلَٰكِنْ أَعْبُدُ ٱللَّهَ" — the surah's frame (قل, vocative, لا أعبد ... تعبدون, دين) in one ayah.
- 43:81 — the Prophet is told: "قُلْ إِن كَانَ لِلرَّحْمَٰنِ وَلَدٌۭ فَأَنَا۠ أَوَّلُ ٱلْعَٰبِدِينَ". From memory: some philologists and commentators read ٱلْعَٰبِدِينَ here as "the first to disdain/reject" (the B008 sense, عَبِدَ = أَنِفَ).
- 48:26 — narrator on Hudaybiya: "إِذْ جَعَلَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ فِى قُلُوبِهِمُ ٱلْحَمِيَّةَ حَمِيَّةَ ٱلْجَٰهِلِيَّةِ" — the deniers' own pride (حمية, the word maqayis uses for B008).
- 89:29 — God addressing the soul at rest (scene opens 89:27): "فَٱدْخُلِى فِى عِبَٰدِى"; sihah glosses it "أي في حزبي" (B002).
- 1:4–5 — the servant's words in al-Fātiḥa: "مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ" — owner, dīn and service in sequence; tahdhib glosses "إياك نعبد إياك نطيع الطاعة التي نخضع معها".

### 2. The court of the served master: bow, crown, the ruler's effective word, the city of obedience

This chain stays at the level of ceremony and polity rather than ownership. Its parts are a superior honoured and served (المعبد), a subordinate's bow (التكفير), a king's crown (التكفير), a ruler whose word is carried out (القيل), and a city defined as the place where obedience to those in command is kept (المدينة, from دين). The objects in 109:2–5 (مَا تَعْبُدُونَ / مَآ أَعْبُدُ) are the occupants of the throne in this scene: the one served. The command قُلْ comes from the ruler whose word takes effect, and the speaker carries it out. 109:6 assigns each party its own dīn: two courts, each with its own law of obedience. The single verbal noun التكفير names both ends of the court, the subject's bow and the king's crown.

Members:
- 109:1 ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ — ك ف ر B014 — "التكفير إيماء الذمي برأسه لا يقال سجد له وإنما يقال كفر له" (ayn); "التكفير أن يخضع الإنسان لغيره يضع يده على صدره ويتطامن له" (sihah) — the subordinate's nod or bow to a superior; ayn separates it explicitly from prostration (سجد).
- 109:1 ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ — ك ف ر B015 — "التكفير تتويج الملك بتاج والتكفير ههنا التاج نفسه" (ayn) — crowning the king, and the crown itself.
- 109:2–5 مَا تَعْبُدُونَ / مَآ أَعْبُدُ / مَّا عَبَدتُّمْ — ع ب د B006 — "المعبد المكرم والمعظم كأنه يعبد" (jamhara); "المعبد أي معظما مخدوما" (tahdhib) — the one honoured, exalted and served: the object position (ما) in every clause.
- 109:1 قُلْ — ق و ل B004 — "القيل ملك من ملوك حمير دون الملك الأعظم والمرأة قيلة" (sihah); "كأنه الذي له قول أي ينفذ قوله" (sihah) — the ruler whose word is enforced, ranked beneath "the greatest king". The saying (قول) is the ruler's attribute; قُلْ is a word from the one whose word takes effect.
- 109:6 دِينُكُمْ / دِينِ — د ي ن B006 — "المدينة كأنها مفعلة سميت بذلك لأنها تقام فيها طاعة ذوي الأمر" (maqayis); "ومنه سمى المصر مدينة" (sihah) — the city named for the obedience kept in it: each دين a realm.
- 109:6 دِينُكُمْ / دِينِ — د ي ن B001 — "الدين كالملة اعتبارا بالطاعة والانقياد للشريعة" (mufradat) — the order a realm obeys.
- 109:2–5 — ع ب د B002 — "فادخلي في عبادي أي في حزبي" (sihah) — the master's retinue or party: those who stand in his court.

### 3. Brought out by the tongue, covered over

قُلْ and ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ, side by side in 109:1, carry one sense and its reversal. Mufradat defines قول as letters "brought out" by utterance, and names what is held in the soul before that bringing-out. كفر is covering: the night that hides people, the sea, the covering of truth, the covering of a received benefit. The surah is the bringing-out: a held position (in mufradat's B013 sense of يقول بـ, "holds the view") is spoken aloud by the tongue to those named for covering. 109:2–5 brings both sides' worship into the open, the speaker's and the addressees'. 109:6 states the speaker's own دين openly.

Members:
- 109:1 قُلْ — ق و ل B001 — "المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق" (mufradat); "القول من النطق" (maqayis) — speech as what is brought out into the open by utterance.
- 109:1 قُلْ — ق و ل B002 — "المقول اللسان" (maqayis;ayn;sihah) — the tongue, the organ that brings it out.
- 109:1 قُلْ — ق و ل B012 [fixed expression] — "المتصور في النفس قبل الإبراز باللفظ قول" (mufradat); "في نفسي قول لم أظهره" (mufradat) — the saying still held inside before disclosure; the imperative orders it out.
- 109:1 قُلْ — ق و ل B013 [fixed expression] — "للاعتقاد نحو فلان يقول بقول أبي حنيفة" (mufradat) — saying as holding a position. What is brought out is a creed, the دِينِ of 109:6.
- 109:1 ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ — ك ف ر B001 — "كل شيء غطى شيئا فقد كفره" (ayn;sihah;tahdhib); "كفرت الشيء أي سترته ورماد مكفور" (sihah); "كفرت الشمس النجوم" (mufradat) — covering, as wind-blown earth covers ashes or the sun hides the stars.
- 109:1 ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ — ك ف ر B002 — "الليل كافر لأنه ستر بظلمته" (tahdhib); "الكافر الليل المظلم والكافر البحر والنهر العظيم" (sihah); "وصف الليل بالكافر لستره الأشخاص" (mufradat) — the covering medium: night, sea, great river.
- 109:1 ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ — ك ف ر B003 — "الكفر ضد الإيمان سمى لأنه تغطية الحق" (maqayis) — the covering of truth.
- 109:1 ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ — ك ف ر B004 — "كفر النعمة وكفرانها سترها بترك أداء شكرها" (mufradat); "كفران النعمة جحودها وسترها" (maqayis) — covering a benefit received.
- 109:2–5 the clauses — ع ب د B003 — what is brought out: each side's worship stated plainly, including the addressees' own state (وَلَآ أَنتُمْ عَٰبِدُونَ).

### 4. The trodden road, the worn habit, the parting ways

ع ب د names a road made smooth by much walking (الطريق المعبد), and also roads that diverge and groups that go off in every direction (العباديد). دين names habit, "what one is used to" (العادة, الأمر الذي تعهده), and is glossed as milla. The scene: a path worn smooth by repetition, followed out of habit, and then two paths that part. 109:4's past tense, "مَا عَبَدتُّمْ", is the track the addressees have already worn. The repeated clauses go over the same ground again and again. 109:6 sends each party down its own road: "لَكُمْ دِينُكُمْ وَلِىَ دِينِ".

Members:
- 109:2–5 أَعْبُدُ / تَعْبُدُونَ / عَٰبِدُونَ / عَابِدٌ / عَبَدتُّمْ — ع ب د B005 — "الطريق المعبد وهو المسلوك المذلل" (maqayis); "طريق معبد أي مذلل" (jamhara;mufradat) — the road walked until it is smooth.
- 109:4 عَبَدتُّمْ — ع ب د B003 — (past tense in the text) — the worship already practised: a track already worn. (Reading the change of tense across 109:2–5 as present/future against past is from memory of the commentators.)
- 109:6 دِينُكُمْ / دِينِ — د ي ن B005 — "الدين بالكسر العادة والشأن" (sihah); "العادة يقال لها دين" (maqayis); "الحال والأمر الذي تعهده" (maqayis) — habit: the state one keeps returning to.
- 109:6 دِينُكُمْ / دِينِ — د ي ن B004/B005 — "يا دين قلبك" (maqayis, glossed there "أي أذل") — the dictionary lists the phrase under both branches. Its sense is disputed: subdued, habituated, forced, or an old ache of the heart. It is the heart's worn habit and its subjection in one phrase.
- 109:6 دِينُكُمْ / دِينِ — د ي ن B001 — "الدين كالملة اعتبارا بالطاعة والانقياد للشريعة" (mufradat) — the way one follows.
- 109:2–5 — ع ب د B010 — "العباديد والعبابيد الأطراف البعيدة والأشياء المتفرقة والطرق المختلفة" (tahdhib); "العباديد الفرق من الناس الذاهبون في كل وجه" (sihah) — roads that diverge, and parties going off in every direction: the parting of 109:6.

- 6:153 — God's charge: "وَأَنَّ هَٰذَا صِرَٰطِى مُسْتَقِيمًۭا فَٱتَّبِعُوهُ ۖ وَلَا تَتَّبِعُوا۟ ٱلسُّبُلَ فَتَفَرَّقَ بِكُمْ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦ" — paths that scatter (the العباديد image).
- 6:161 — the Prophet is told: "قُلْ إِنَّنِى هَدَىٰنِى رَبِّىٓ إِلَىٰ صِرَٰطٍۢ مُّسْتَقِيمٍۢ دِينًۭا قِيَمًۭا مِّلَّةَ إِبْرَٰهِيمَ" — road, dīn and milla equated.
- 36:60–61 — God to the children of Adam: "أَن لَّا تَعْبُدُوا۟ ٱلشَّيْطَٰنَ ... وَأَنِ ٱعْبُدُونِى ۚ هَٰذَا صِرَٰطٌۭ مُّسْتَقِيمٌۭ" — the two services as two roads.
- 42:13 — God's ordinance: "أَنْ أَقِيمُوا۟ ٱلدِّينَ وَلَا تَتَفَرَّقُوا۟ فِيهِ".

### 5. The terms offered and refused

From memory (Ibn Isḥāq, al-Ṭabarī): the occasion reported for the surah is a proposal by Qurashī leaders that the Prophet worship their gods for a year and they worship his God for a year. ق و ل names that kind of exchange of words over a matter (تقاولنا = تفاوضنا). دين names reciprocal dealing on deferred terms, taking or giving. The surah answers an alternation with alternating clauses: أنا ... أنتم ... أنا ... أنتم. Every slot of the proposed swap is refused, in present and past. The answer is no counter-offer. It is a reply that keeps its قُلْ in the recited text: the opposite of تقوّل, a saying made up and attributed. 109:6 closes the account. Each party keeps its own dīn, and nothing is exchanged.

Members:
- 109:1 قُلْ — ق و ل B009 [fixed expression] — "قاولته في أمره وتقاولنا أي تفاوضنا" (sihah) — trading words over an affair: the negotiation the reply answers.
- 109:1 قُلْ — ق و ل B005 [fixed expression] — "تقول عليه أي كذب عليه" (sihah); "تقول باطلا أي قال ما لم يكن" (ayn) — the invented saying: the reversal of a reply given as commanded.
- 109:2–5 أَعْبُدُ / تَعْبُدُونَ / عَٰبِدُونَ / عَابِدٌ / عَبَدتُّمْ — ع ب د B003 — the alternating subjects and tenses mirror a proposed swap of worship and refuse each slot of it.
- 109:6 دِينُكُمْ / دِينِ — د ي ن B003 — "الدين وداينت فلانا إذا عاملته دينا إما أخذا وإما إعطاء" (maqayis); "تداينوا تبايعوا بالدين" (sihah) — dealing with each other on deferred terms: one year now against one year later. Against that sense, "لَكُمْ دِينُكُمْ وَلِىَ دِينِ" leaves each side holding only its own.

Quran outside the surah:
- 68:8–9 — God to the Prophet: "فَلَا تُطِعِ ٱلْمُكَذِّبِينَ وَدُّوا۟ لَوْ تُدْهِنُ فَيُدْهِنُونَ" — a give-and-take compromise: you soften and they soften.
- 17:73–74 — God to the Prophet: "وَإِن كَادُوا۟ لَيَفْتِنُونَكَ عَنِ ٱلَّذِىٓ أَوْحَيْنَآ إِلَيْكَ لِتَفْتَرِىَ عَلَيْنَا غَيْرَهُۥ ... لَقَدْ كِدتَّ تَرْكَنُ إِلَيْهِمْ شَيْـًۭٔا قَلِيلًا" — pressure to alter what was given.
- 10:15 — the deniers, and the Prophet's commanded reply: "ٱئْتِ بِقُرْءَانٍ غَيْرِ هَٰذَآ أَوْ بَدِّلْهُ ۚ قُلْ مَا يَكُونُ لِىٓ أَنْ أُبَدِّلَهُۥ مِن تِلْقَآئِ نَفْسِىٓ".
- 69:44–46 — God on the Messenger: "وَلَوْ تَقَوَّلَ عَلَيْنَا بَعْضَ ٱلْأَقَاوِيلِ" — the same تقوّل as B005, denied of him (69:45–46 from memory).
- 39:64 — the Prophet is told: "قُلْ أَفَغَيْرَ ٱللَّهِ تَأْمُرُوٓنِّىٓ أَعْبُدُ أَيُّهَا ٱلْجَٰهِلُونَ" — the proposal treated as a command, refused with قل.
- 37:161–162 — said to the idolaters: "فَإِنَّكُمْ وَمَا تَعْبُدُونَ مَآ أَنتُمْ عَلَيْهِ بِفَٰتِنِينَ" — the worshippers and what they worship cannot lure anyone away.

### 6. Disavowal, and leaving each to his own dīn

كفر can itself mean disavowal (البراءة, التبري), and it can mean calling a man كافر. So the vocative in 109:1 does two things: it names them, and it sets the Prophet's disavowal against their kufr. Those named for denial are met with a denial of what they worship. 109:2–5 carries out that disavowal clause by clause. 109:6 carries out the act sihah records under د ي ن: "دينت الرجل تديينا إذا وكلته إلى دينه", entrusting a man to his own dīn. Through the sense of دين as recompense, each side's dīn is also what will be repaid to it.

Members:
- 109:1 ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ — ك ف ر B005 — "يكون الكفر أيضا بمعنى البراءة" (tahdhib); "قد يعبر عن التبري بالكفر" (mufradat) — disavowal: the act the surah performs, attested for the addressees' own name.
- 109:1 ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ — ك ف ر B006 — "أكفرت الرجل أي دعوته كافرا" (sihah); "أكفره إكفارا حكم بكفره" (mufradat) — calling someone كافر, ruling him so: what يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ does.
- 109:2–5 لَآ أَعْبُدُ / وَلَآ أَنتُمْ عَٰبِدُونَ / وَلَآ أَنَا۠ عَابِدٌۭ — ع ب د B003 — each negation disowns one service, the speaker's and theirs.
- 109:6 دِينُكُمْ / دِينِ — د ي ن B007 — "دينت الرجل تديينا إذا وكلته إلى دينه" (sihah); "دينت الرجل في القضاء وفيما بينه وبين الله أي صدقته" (tahdhib) — leaving a man to his own dīn, to what lies between him and God: the act of لَكُمْ دِينُكُمْ.
- 109:6 دِينُكُمْ / دِينِ — د ي ن B002 — "الدين الجزاء والمكافأة" (sihah); "يوم الدين أي يوم الحكم والحساب والجزاء" (maqayis); "غير مدينين أي غير مجزيين" (mufradat) — each party's dīn as its reckoning and recompense.

Quran outside the surah:

- 6:78 — Ibrahim after the setting of the sun (from memory): "يَٰقَوْمِ إِنِّى بَرِىٓءٌۭ مِّمَّا تُشْرِكُونَ" (scene opens 6:74/6:76).

### 7. Seed covered, yield returned

ٱلْكَٰفِرُ is also the farmer, because he covers the seed with earth, and كافور is the sheath that covers forming fruit. دين is recompense, what comes back. Read across the surah, those named in 109:1 by the sower's word receive in 109:6 "دِينُكُمْ", their return. The worship of 109:2–5 is the sowing between the two.

Members:
- 109:1 ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ — ك ف ر B008 — "الكافر الزارع لأنه يغطي البذر بالتراب" (sihah); "يقال للزارع كافر لأنه يغطى الحب بتراب الأرض" (maqayis); "الكفار الزراع" (mufradat) — the sower burying seed.
- 109:1 ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ — ك ف ر B010 — "الكافور اسم أكمام الثمرة التي تكفرها" (mufradat); "الكافور الطلع ووعاء طلع النخل" (sihah) — the sheath enclosing forming fruit.
- 109:2–5 أَعْبُدُ / تَعْبُدُونَ ... — ع ب د B003 — the practice carried on: what each side puts into the ground.
- 109:6 دِينُكُمْ / دِينِ — د ي ن B002 — "الدين الجزاء والمكافأة" (sihah) — what is returned.

Quran outside the surah:
- 57:20 — God's parable of this life: "كَمَثَلِ غَيْثٍ أَعْجَبَ ٱلْكُفَّارَ نَبَاتُهُۥ ثُمَّ يَهِيجُ فَتَرَىٰهُ مُصْفَرًّۭا ثُمَّ يَكُونُ حُطَٰمًۭا" — ٱلْكُفَّار read by commentators as the sowers (from memory). The crop that delights them withers.
- 48:29 — God's description of the Prophet's companions: "كَزَرْعٍ أَخْرَجَ شَطْـَٔهُۥ ... يُعْجِبُ ٱلزُّرَّاعَ لِيَغِيظَ بِهِمُ ٱلْكُفَّارَ" — sowers and kuffār set side by side.
- 42:20 — (from memory) "مَن كَانَ يُرِيدُ حَرْثَ ٱلْءَاخِرَةِ نَزِدْ لَهُۥ فِى حَرْثِهِۦ" — deeds as tillage and their return.

## Interactions

- Chains 1 and 2 — shared member ك ف ر B014 (the subordinate's bow). Chain 1's lowered servant is the same person who stands bowing in chain 2's court. Evidence: sihah "التكفير أن يخضع الإنسان لغيره".
- Chains 1 and 2 — shared object ع ب د (master/served one): chain 1 takes the owner of the slave, chain 2 the one honoured and served (B006 "معظما مخدوما"). Evidence: 12:39–40, where "أرباب" (masters), "تعبدون" and "الدين القيم" stand in one argument.
- Chains 1 and 6 — د ي ن in 56:86 is glossed both "غير مملوكين" (tahdhib, B004) and "غير مجزيين" (mufradat, B002): being owned and being repaid in one phrase.
- Chains 1 and 4 — "يا دين قلبك" is listed under abasement (B004, "أي أذل") and under habit (B005): subjection and the worn habit in one phrase. Within ع ب د, B005 holds both the trodden road and the tarred, tractable camel under "المذلل".
- Chains 1 and 5 — ك ف ر B007 (driving an obedient servant to disobey) is the pressure that 39:64 stages: "أَفَغَيْرَ ٱللَّهِ تَأْمُرُوٓنِّىٓ أَعْبُدُ".
- Chains 1 and 6 — ع ب د B008 (disdain) and ك ف ر B005 (disavowal) are both refusals. 43:81 (العابدين read as الآنفين) and 60:4 (كفرنا بكم) stage them with قل/قالوا and تعبدون.
- Chains 3 and 5 — قول brought out by the tongue (B001) against تقوّل (B005), and disclosure against the coating of ادهان (68:9): the reply is neither altered nor smoothed over.
- Chains 4 and 6 — the roads part (ع ب د B010) and each man is left to his own dīn (د ي ن B007). Evidence: 6:153 "فَتَفَرَّقَ بِكُمْ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦ" and 10:28 "فَزَيَّلْنَا بَيْنَهُمْ".
- Chains 4 and 5 — the proposal reported for the occasion is a swap of habits; 7:88 "لَتَعُودُنَّ فِى مِلَّتِنَا" stages a demand to return to the old track, refused.
- Chains 2 and 4 — د ي ن B001 "الدين كالملة ... للشريعة" is both a realm's law (12:76 "دِينِ ٱلْمَلِكِ") and a way followed (6:161 "دِينًۭا قِيَمًۭا مِّلَّةَ إِبْرَٰهِيمَ").
- Chains 6 and 7 — د ي ن B002 recompense is both the reckoning of the Day (1:4) and the harvest returned to the sower.
- Chains 3 and 6 — ك ف ر as covering (B001/B003) and as disavowal (B005): the addressees cover, and the speaker disavows openly. 18:29 stages speaking out ("وَقُلِ ٱلْحَقُّ") and leaving the choice to cover ("فَلْيَكْفُرْ") together.

## Ayat

### 109:1 قُلْ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ
- Chain 1 (lowered servant): ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ adds the servant's bow (B014) and its reversal, the obedient servant driven to disobey (B007). Whole scene: men lowered before masters, owned, subdued, made tractable like a tarred camel, with the root also naming the refusing camel and proud disdain; the speaker refuses lowering before a false master, and دين names the subjection each side is in.
- Chain 2 (court): قُلْ adds the ruler whose word is carried out (القيل); ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ adds the bow and the crown (both التكفير). Whole scene: a crowned, served master in his court, subjects bowing, his word enforced, a city where obedience to command is kept; two such realms are assigned in 109:6.
- Chain 3 (brought out vs covered): قُلْ is speech brought out by the tongue from what was held inside; ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ is covering, like night, sea, a hidden truth or a hidden benefit. Whole scene: a held creed spoken openly to those named for covering, both sides' worship brought into the light, the speaker's dīn declared.
- Chain 5 (terms refused): قُلْ is a reply to an exchange of words (تقاولنا), given as commanded, not invented (تقوّل). Whole scene: a proposed swap of worship answered clause by clause, each slot refused, the account closed with each keeping its own dīn.
- Chain 6 (disavowal): ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ both names them (أكفرت الرجل) and carries كفر as disavowal (البراءة). Whole scene: naming, disowning each service in turn, and entrusting each party to its own dīn and its recompense.
- Chain 7 (seed and yield): ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ adds the farmer covering seed and the sheath covering fruit. Whole scene: the sower named at the start, the practice in between, the return (دين) at the end.

### 109:2 لَآ أَعْبُدُ مَا تَعْبُدُونَ
- Chain 1: أَعْبُدُ / تَعْبُدُونَ — lowering oneself before a master, ownership, being made servile. The speaker refuses one master, and the root also holds the refusing camel and disdain. Whole scene: as at 109:1.
- Chain 2: مَا تَعْبُدُونَ — the one honoured and served (المعبد) on the addressees' side. Whole scene: two courts, each with its served master and its bowing subjects.
- Chain 3: the speaker's refusal stated aloud. Whole scene: a held creed brought out to those who cover.
- Chain 4: ع ب د as the trodden road; their worship is the well-worn track. Whole scene: a road worn smooth by habit, then two roads parting, each party on its own way.
- Chain 5: first slot of the alternation (I / you, present-future) refused. Whole scene: a proposed swap of worship refused clause by clause, the account closed.
- Chain 6: the first disavowal. Whole scene: naming, disowning, entrusting each to his own dīn.
- Chain 7: the practice between sowing and return. Whole scene: sower, practice, yield.

### 109:3 وَلَآ أَنتُمْ عَٰبِدُونَ مَآ أَعْبُدُ
- Chain 1: عَٰبِدُونَ — the addressees as not serving the speaker's master. 23:47 uses the same form, "لَنَا عَٰبِدُونَ", for people in servitude. Whole scene: as at 109:1.
- Chain 2: مَآ أَعْبُدُ — the speaker's served master, whose court they do not stand in. Whole scene: two courts.
- Chain 3: the speaker brings their state into the open as well. Whole scene: as at 109:1.
- Chain 4: they do not walk his road. Whole scene: worn road, parting ways.
- Chain 5: the second slot (you / mine) refused. Whole scene: swap refused.
- Chain 6: their non-service stated; the disavowal is two-sided. Whole scene: naming, disowning, entrusting.
- Chain 7: their practice is not his. Whole scene: sower, practice, yield.

### 109:4 وَلَآ أَنَا۠ عَابِدٌۭ مَّا عَبَدتُّمْ
- Chain 1: عَابِدٌۭ — the speaker denies himself the servant's status toward what they have served. Whole scene: as at 109:1.
- Chain 2: مَّا عَبَدتُّمْ — the master they have honoured until now. Whole scene: two courts.
- Chain 3: the refusal brought out again, now about the past. Whole scene: as at 109:1.
- Chain 4: عَبَدتُّمْ, past tense — the track already worn, the inherited habit (as in 21:53, 26:75–76, 43:22). Whole scene: worn road, parting ways.
- Chain 5: the past-tense slot (I / what you have worshipped) refused. Whole scene: swap refused.
- Chain 6: disavowal of their past worship. Whole scene: naming, disowning, entrusting.
- Chain 7: what they have already sown. Whole scene: sower, practice, yield.

### 109:5 وَلَآ أَنتُمْ عَٰبِدُونَ مَآ أَعْبُدُ
- Chain 1: the addressees again as not in service to his master. Whole scene: as at 109:1.
- Chain 2: مَآ أَعْبُدُ — the speaker's served master, whose court they do not enter. Whole scene: two courts.
- Chain 3: their state brought out once more. Whole scene: as at 109:1.
- Chain 4: the last pass over the same ground before the roads part. Whole scene: worn road, parting ways.
- Chain 5: the final slot refused, completing the alternation. Whole scene: swap refused.
- Chain 6: the disavowal completed on both sides. Whole scene: naming, disowning, entrusting.

### 109:6 لَكُمْ دِينُكُمْ وَلِىَ دِينِ
- Chain 1: دين — "جنس من الانقياد والذل", "العبد مدين", "دانه دينا أي أذله واستعبده". Each side's subjection, and the master it is under. Whole scene: as at 109:1.
- Chain 2: دين — the city of obedience (المدينة) and a realm's law (12:76 "دِينِ ٱلْمَلِكِ"). Two realms assigned. Whole scene: two courts, each with served master, bowing subjects and enforced word.
- Chain 3: دِينِ — the creed brought out (يقول بـ = holds the view). Whole scene: a held creed spoken openly to those who cover.
- Chain 4: دين — habit (العادة) and way (كالملة). Each party sent down its own road (العباديد). Whole scene: worn road, then two roads parting.
- Chain 5: دين — reciprocal deferred dealing (تداينوا) refused. Each side keeps its own and nothing is exchanged. Whole scene: swap refused, account closed.
- Chain 6: دين — "دينت الرجل تديينا إذا وكلته إلى دينه" (leaving each to his own dīn) and "الدين الجزاء" (recompense). Whole scene: naming, disowning, entrusting each to his dīn and its return.
- Chain 7: دين — "الدين الجزاء والمكافأة": the yield returned to the sower. Whole scene: sower, practice, yield.
