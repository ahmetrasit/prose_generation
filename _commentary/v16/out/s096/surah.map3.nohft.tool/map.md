## Chains

### 1. The womb: gathering, clinging, taking form
Blood gathers and the womb closes on it; the drop clings and the pregnancy holds; form becomes apparent; the pregnancy shows; birth comes near, or the womb casts out its load before the form is clear. 96:2 names the origin (عَلَق). The command اقرأ (96:1, 96:3) also carries the womb that gathers and holds. Words later in the surah carry the later stages: رأى (the pregnancy shows), الرجعى (casting before form), صلّى and اقترب (birth near). Heard this way, the surah runs from conception to delivery.
- 96:1/96:3 ٱقْرَأْ — ق ر ء root_001211 B003 — «القرء اجتماع الدم في الرحم»; «لم تضطم رحمها على الجنين»: blood gathering in the womb, the womb closing on the embryo.
- 96:1/96:3 ٱقْرَأْ — ق ر ء root_001210 B004 — «ما قرأت الناقة سلى قط وما قرأت ملقوحا قط» … «لم تحمل علقة أي دما ولا جنينا» (tahdhib): the dictionary joins قرأ and علقة in one phrase. To "قرأ" is to hold an ʿalaqa in the womb.
- 96:1/96:3 ٱقْرَأْ — ق ر ء root_001210 B003 — «القرء الحيض والقرء أيضا الطهر وهو من الأضداد»; «القرء وقت يكون للطهر مرة وللحيض مرة»: the cycle that times the womb.
- 96:1/96:3 ٱقْرَأْ — ق ر ء root_001210 B009 [fixed expression] — «استقرأ الجمل الناقة إذا تاركها لينظر ألقحت أم لا»: the male leaves her alone to see whether she has conceived.
- 96:2 عَلَقٍ — ع ل ق B003 — «العلق الدم الجامد والقطعة منه علقة»: the clot.
- 96:2 عَلَقٍ — ع ل ق B009 — «علقت المرأة حبلت»; «علقت وعقدت على الماء»: conception takes hold.
- 96:1–2 خَلَقَ — خ ل ق B003 — «مضغة مخلقة أي تامة الخلق»; «مخلقة قد بدا خلقها وغير مخلقة لم تصور»: the form appears.
- 96:1/3/8 رَبّ — ر ب ب B002 — «التربية، وهو إنشاء الشيء حالا فحالا إلى حد التمام»: bringing up by stages to completion.
- 96:7/9/11/13/14 رَءَاهُ، أَرَءَيْتَ، يَرَىٰ — ر ء ي B010 — «أرأت الناقة إذا أظهرت الحمل حتى يرى صدق حملها»: the pregnancy becomes visible.
- 96:8 ٱلرُّجْعَىٰٓ — ر ج ع B013 [fixed expression] — «إذا ألقت الناقة حملها قبل أن يستبين خلقه قيل قد رجعت» (tahdhib): the dictionary joins رجع and خلق in one phrase. This is the reversal: the load is cast before its form is clear.
- 96:10 صَلَّىٰٓ — ص ل و B005 — «أصلت الناقة فهي مصلية إذا وقع ولدها في صلاها وقرب نتاجها»; «كل أنثى إذا ولدت انفرج صلاها»: the young drops toward birth.
- 96:19 وَٱقْتَرِب — ق ر ب B012 — «أقربت المرأة إذا قرب ولادها»: delivery is near.
- 96:5 مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ — ع ل م B001 — «العلم نقيض الجهل»: the person born knowing nothing (see 16:78).

### 2. Gathering words: recitation, teaching, the pen, the reader who worships
Reading is the joining of letters and words into a whole, spoken aloud. Teaching awakens the mind to meanings. The pen fixes what is taught. Each ق ر ء step is built on the same act of gathering. The reader gathers things into knowledge and testifies to them. The reader is also a worshipper, so the surah's opening word reaches its servant who prays (96:10) and its final prostration (96:19). In recitation the voice is repeated and modulated.
- 96:1/96:3 ٱقْرَأْ — ق ر ء root_001210 B001 — «قرأت الشيء قرآنا جمعته وضممت بعضه إلى بعض»; «القراءة ضم الحروف والكلمات بعضها إلى بعض» (mufradat): gathering.
- 96:1/96:3 ٱقْرَأْ — ق ر ء root_001210 B002 — «قرأت القرآن لفظت به مجموعا»; «قرأت القرآن عن ظهر قلب أو نظرت فيه»; «أقرأت غيري أقرئه إقراء»: reciting, from memory or from the page, and making another recite.
- 96:1/96:3 ٱقْرَأْ — ق ر ء root_001210 B012 — «يقرون الأشياء حتى يجمعوها علما ثم يشهدون بها»; «الناس قواري الله تعالى في الأرض هم الشهود»: the dictionary joins ق ر ء and علم. The reader-witness.
- 96:1/96:3 ٱقْرَأْ — ق ر ء root_001210 B006 — «رجل قارئ عابد ناسك» (ayn): the dictionary joins ق ر ء with عابد (96:10 عَبْدًا).
- 96:4–5 عَلَّمَ — ع ل م B001 — «التعليم تنبيه النفس لتصور المعاني»; «أعلمته بكذا وعلمته تعليما»: teaching.
- 96:4 بِٱلْقَلَمِ — ق ل م B003 — «القلم الذي يكتب به»; «أقلامهم التي كانوا يكتبون بها التوراة»: the writing reed.
- 96:1/3/8 رَبّ — ر ب ب B003 — «الرباني… العالم المعلم الذي يغذو الناس بصغار العلوم»; «يرب العلم»: the dictionary joins ر ب ب and teaching. The Lord who teaches.
- 96:8 ٱلرُّجْعَىٰٓ — ر ج ع B007 — «الترجيع ترديد الصوت باللحن في القراءة» (mufradat): the dictionary joins رجع and القراءة. The reciting voice turns back on itself.
- 96:7 ٱسْتَغْنَىٰٓ — غ ن ي B003 — «الغناء الصوت ممدود والتطريب وتحزين القراءة وترقيقها» (tahdhib): the dictionary joins غناء and القراءة. The recitation is softened.
- 96:3 ٱلْأَكْرَمُ — ك ر م B001 — «اسم جامع لكل ما يحمد»: the generosity that teaches (82:6, 56:77 and 80:13–16 join كرم with the Qur'an and its pages).

### 3. The pared reed and the smoothed shaft
This is a craftsman's sequence on hard material: measure before cutting, pare the hard end, smooth the shaft. The reed pared again and again becomes the pen. A pared, smoothed shaft marked for drawing lots is also called قلم and is kept in a quiver-bag. خلق (96:1–2) supplies the measuring and the smoothing, قلم (96:4) the paring and the instrument, رب the container.
- 96:1–2 خَلَقَ — خ ل ق B001 — «خلقت الأديم إذا قدرته قبل القطع» (sihah); «الخلق أصله: التقدير المستقيم» (mufradat): measuring before cutting.
- 96:4 ٱلْقَلَمِ — ق ل م B001 — «أصل القلم القص من الشيء الصلب كالظفر وكعب الرمح والقصب» (mufradat); «قلمت الشيء بريته»: paring.
- 96:4 ٱلْقَلَمِ — ق ل م B003 — «سمي القلم قلما لأنه يقلم منه كما يقلم من الظفر»; «إنما سمي قلما لأنه قلم مرة بعد مرة»: the pen is named after the paring.
- 96:1–2 خَلَقَ — خ ل ق B008 — «السهم المصلح مخلق لأنه يصير أملس» (maqayis); «المخلق القدح إذا لين» (sihah); «خلقت الحبل والوتر… تخليقا إذا ملسته»: smoothing the shaft.
- 96:4 ٱلْقَلَمِ — ق ل م B004 — «القلم الزلم» (sihah); «الأقلام ها هنا القداح جعلوا عليها علامات على جهة القرعة» (tahdhib): the marked lot-shaft. خلق B008 (المخلق القدح) and قلم B004 (القدح … قلم) meet on the same object.
- 96:1/3/8 رَبّ — ر ب ب B010 — «الربابة شبيهة بالكنانة تجمع فيها سهام الميسر»: the bag that gathers the shafts.
Outside the surah:

### 4. Name, mark and sign
A thing is made known by something raised up or pressed into it: the name (from height, or from the brand), the distinguishing mark, the flag set up to be seen, the marked lot-shaft, writing lines inked over again until dark. The surah begins with the Name (بِٱسْمِ), teaches by the instrument that marks (ٱلْقَلَمِ), and ends on the forehead where the mark of prostration settles (chain 9).
- 96:1 بِٱسْمِ — س م و B005 — «أصل اسم سمو وهو من العلو لأنه تنويه ودلالة على المعنى»; «الاسم ما يعرف به ذات الشيء»: the name as raised token.
- 96:1 بِٱسْمِ — و س م B001 (the Kufan derivation the dictionary records for this word) — «ووسمت الشيء وسما: أثرت فيه بسمة»; «الميسم المكواة»: the name as brand.
- 96:4–5/14 عَلَّمَ، يَعْلَمْ — ع ل م B002 — «أصل صحيح واحد يدل على أثر بالشيء يتميز به عن غيره»; «العلم الراية»; «علم الثوب ورقمه في أطرافه»; «أعلم الفارس إذا كانت له علامة في الحرب»: mark, flag, the embroidered border.
- 96:7/9/11/13/14 رَءَاهُ… — ر ء ي B011 — «الراية العلامة المنصوبة للرؤية» (mufradat): the dictionary joins seeing and علامة.
- 96:4 ٱلْقَلَمِ — ق ل م B004 — «جعلوا عليها علامات»: marks put on the shafts.
- 96:8 ٱلرُّجْعَىٰٓ — ر ج ع B009 [fixed expression] — «ترجيع وشي النقش والوشم والكتابة خطوطها» (ayn); «أن يعاد عليه السواد مرة بعد أخرى» (tahdhib): writing and tattoo lines re-inked.
- 96:12 أَمَرَ — ء م ر B005 — «الأمارة العلامة، والأمار أمار الطريق معالمه»: the road-mark.
- 96:15 لَنَسْفَعًۢا — س ف ع B002 — «السفعة وهي السواد»; «سواد مشرب حمرة»: the dark mark (chain 9).
- 96:19 وَٱسْجُدْ — س ج د B003 — «المسجد بالفتح جبهة الرجل حيث يصيبه ندب السجود»: the mark on the forehead.

### 5. The eye, its image, and who sees whom
الإنسان also names the small figure seen in the dark of the pupil. The human sees himself (رَءَاهُ, same subject and object, 96:7). He is caught in the mirror of his own sufficiency. The surah keeps telling the listener to look (أَرَءَيْتَ ×3). It answers that God sees (يَرَىٰ, 96:14). It ends with the posture whose root also names the long, lowered, still gaze (ٱسْجُدْ). The seeing moves from self-regard, through the listener being made to look and being seen by God, to the eyes cast down.
- 96:2/5/6 ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ — ء ن س B005 — «إنسان العين المثال الذي يرى في السواد أي سواد العين» (sihah): the image in the pupil.
- 96:2/5/6 ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ — ء ن س B002 — «آنسته أبصرته وآنست الصوت سمعته وآنست منه رشدا علمته»: perceiving by sight.
- 96:7 رَّءَاهُ — ر ء ي B001 — «نظر وإبصار بعين أو بصيرة»: seeing with the eye or the mind.
- 96:7 رَّءَاهُ — ر ء ي B006/B012 — «المرآة ما يرى فيه صورة الأشياء»; «رأيت الرجل ترئية إذا أمسكت له المرآة لينظر فيها» (tahdhib): the mirror held up so he looks at himself.
- 96:7 رَّءَاهُ — ر ء ي B002 — «الرأي اعتقاد النفس»: seeing as judging.
- 96:9/11/13 أَرَءَيْتَ — ر ء ي B013 — «يجري أرأيت مجرى أخبرني وكل ذلك فيه معنى التنبيه»: the listener is alerted.
- 96:14 يَرَىٰ — ر ء ي B001: God's seeing.
- 96:14 يَعْلَم — ع ل م B001 — «إدراك الشيء بحقيقته»: grasping a thing as it really is.
- 96:7 رَّءَاهُ — ر ء ي B005 — «وراءى فلان يرائي… وهو أن يفعل شيئا ليراه الناس»; «يرآءون الناس إذا أبصرهم الناس صلوا» (tahdhib): the dictionary joins رياء and صلاة. Prayer done to be seen is the reversal of the servant who prays under God's seeing.
- 96:19 وَٱسْجُدْ — س ج د B005 — «الإسجاد إدامة النظر مع سكون»; «أصل السجود إدامة النظر في إطراق إلى الأرض» (jamhara): the long, lowered gaze.

### 6. Hanging on and needing nothing
The human is made from a thing that clings, hangs and holds on. علقة is also the bare morsel that keeps one alive. He is taught what he lacked. Then he sees himself as needing nothing (ٱسْتَغْنَىٰٓ). Sufficiency and need meet in single words: one form of رب means both "need" and "favour". The Most Generous is the one whose giving relieves need. Return (96:8) undoes the claim.
- 96:2 عَلَقٍ — ع ل ق B001 — «يناط الشيء بالشيء العالي»; «علق به إذا لزمه»; «علق بالشيء نشب به»: hanging from something above, clinging.

### 7. Water that overruns its bounds and the pool where it stops
Water rises past its measure and sweeps everything away. It then reaches the end of its course and settles in a pool. The surah's words give the process: يَطْغَىٰٓ (96:6) is the flood; الرجعى (96:8) is the water that returns; يَنتَهِ (96:15) is coming to rest at the limit. "If he does not stop" is also "if it does not settle". The goal of the course is "your Lord" (96:8).
- 96:6 لَيَطْغَىٰٓ — ط غ ي B002 [fixed expression] — «طغى السيل إذا جاء بماء كثير»; «طغى الماء خروجه عن المقدار»; «طغا البحر والماء إذا علا كل شيء فاجترفه» (tahdhib): overflow.
- 96:6 لَيَطْغَىٰٓ — ط غ ي B001 — «كل شيء جاوز القدر فقد طغا»: passing the measure, which links to خلق as measuring (chain 3).

### 8. Rain that returns and the ground it marks
Cloud, rain and growth form a cycle. The generous cloud brings rain. The first rain marks the land with plants. The next rain follows it. Rain returns again and again. Some land is skipped and left bare. The Most Generous Lord (96:3) is the source of this scene. Members sit in بسم (alt.), الأكرم, ربك, الرجعى, تولى, ناديه and خاطئة.

### 9. The front of the head: forelock, forehead, ground
This is the scene of the head's front. The forelock grows at the hairline. The smooth flat of the forehead lies below it. The man raises this part high (يطغى: rising past measure). He is seized by it, and its skin is scorched dark. The servant lays the same part on the ground, and the mark of prostration settles there. The forelock is also the chief of a people. So "a lying, erring forelock" (96:16) is the man and the head of his council (96:17).

### 11. Two summons, two courts
Calling draws someone toward the caller. The man calls his council, the people who gather round in the meeting place. "We" call the guards, whose name means those who push away: the king's police, the harsh angels who drive the people of the Fire into it. The servant is told to come near, like the king's own close circle. Prayer is itself a calling (الصلاة: الدعاء). So the scene has three calls: the servant's prayer (96:10), the man's call to his council (96:17), and God's call to the guards (96:18). The movements are gathering, pushing away and drawing near.
- 96:17/18 فَلْيَدْعُ، سَنَدْعُ — د ع و B001 — «أصل واحد وهو أن تميل الشيء إليك بصوت وكلام يكون منك»: calling something toward oneself.
- 96:17 نَادِيَهُۥ — ن د و B001 — «النادي والندى المجلس يندو القوم حواليه»; «النادي المجلس يندو إليه من حواليه»: the council gathered round.
- 96:17 نَادِيَهُۥ — the ن د ي entry (marked echo) records the verse itself — «فليدع ناديه أي عشيرته وإنما هم أهل النادي» (sihah); «النادي… لا يسمى ناديا حتى يكون فيه أهله» (tahdhib).

### 12. Fire: the one who prays and the one who burns
صلى names both sides. One meaning is to pray. The other is to enter fire and suffer its heat, to roast, to straighten a stick over flame. The servant who "ṣallā" (96:10) faces the forbidder who, in the Qur'an's own pairing, will "yaṣlā" the Fire. The scene is completed by the face scorched dark (لنسفعا), the guards who drive people into the Fire (الزبانية), and the thunderbolt-punishment (طاغية).

### 13. Snare and chase
Hunters go out into open country and set snares. The prey is caught fast in the net. The wild animal runs a stretch, then stops to look back. There is a chase. To "set a snare" for someone is to plot to bring him to ruin. The man pursues the praying servant to stop him. He is himself the one caught at the forelock.

### 14. The course: on the road, missing, turning back, returning, drawing near
A traveller is on a road. He may miss the mark and swerve from his direction, or turn his back and flee. He may falter in a charge. All of them come back to where they started, and the course has an end-point. The same root that gives "turned away" (تَوَلَّىٰ) means unbroken nearness. The servant is told to come near (ٱقْتَرِب). The surah moves from على الهدى (96:11) to كذّب وتولّى (96:13) and خاطئة (96:16), against إلى ربك الرجعى (96:8), ينته (96:15) and اقترب (96:19).

### 15. Owner, servant, obedience
There is an owner and the one owned. The servant lowers himself in worship. There are command and prohibition, and the overstepping of obedience. The surah's question is whom the servant obeys. A man forbids a servant (96:9–10), and the closing command is "do not obey him" (96:19). The owner is "your Lord". The transgressor goes past the limit in disobedience. The servant is lowered, like a road trodden smooth.

### 16. Making and fabricating; the cloth that lies
To create is to measure truly. Fabricating is the same root: a lie measured out in the mind. The dyed cloth lies about itself by looking embroidered, against the true woven border. Cloth that shifts colour in the eye belongs here too. The Lord creates (خلق, 96:1–2). The man denies (كذّب) and his forelock is lying (كاذبة). The head held high claims something it is not.

### 17. Smooth rock and its hollows
Hard rock is so smooth that nothing can grip it. Talons slip off. The same rock is closed with no opening, yet hollows in it hold rainwater. The surah names this rock with two words: خلق (96:1–2) and طغى (96:6).

## Ayat
- **96:1** ٱقْرَأْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ
  - 1 (womb): اقرأ is the womb gathering and holding (root_001211 B003; root_001210 B004 «لم تحمل علقة»); رب rears by stages; خلق forms. Scene: blood gathers and clings, the form appears, the pregnancy shows, birth draws near, or the womb casts out before form.
  - 2 (words): اقرأ is joining words and speaking them; the reader is also witness and worshipper; رب is the teacher. Scene: words gathered, recited, taught and fixed by the pen. The reader worships and ends in prostration.
  - 3 (reed): خلق is measuring before cutting and smoothing the shaft; رب is the bag of lot-shafts. Scene: a reed measured, pared, smoothed into pen and lot-shaft.
  - 4 (name): اسم is a raised token, or (Kufan) a brand. Scene: name, flag, lines re-inked, the mark on the forehead.
  - 6 (hanging on): رب is the word for need and for favour. Scene: made from a clinging scrap, given and taught, then claiming to need nothing, then returned.
  - 8 (rain): رب is the cloud that rears plants; اسم (alt.) is the first rain that marks the land; سماء is the cloud. Scene: generous cloud, first rain and following rain, returning rain, land the rain misses.
  - 9 (head): سما is rising; خلق names the smooth flat of the forehead. Scene: the head raised high, seized by the forelock, scorched dark, against the forehead on the ground with its mark.
  - 11 (courts): اقرأ is gathering; رب is a great crowd. Scene: the man calls his council, God calls the guards who push away, the servant is called near.
  - 13 (snare): سموا is the hunters going out. Scene: hunters, snare, chase, the prey caught by the head.
  - 14 (course): رب is the goal of the return; creation is the starting point the return goes back to. Scene: on the road, swerving, turning back, everyone returning to the Lord, drawing near.
  - 15 (owner): رب is owner and master obeyed. Scene: owner and servant, command and prohibition, whom to obey.
  - 16 (making): خلق is true measuring against the measured lie. Scene: God creates, the man fabricates, the lying forelock.
  - 17 (rock): خلق is smooth rock with hollows that hold rain. Scene: a smooth summit nothing can grip.
