**1. Why the pulley stayed out**

- **Controlling image.** I set the scene as road, guide and posture: walking, leading, being conveyed. A pulley is fixed and vertical, so I read it as a different setting and dropped it as off-scene. I didn't check whether it served the same operation.
- **Batch dismissal.** It went into one ledger line with five other chains under a single reason ("one ayah cannot carry them"). The brief's "most candidates stay out" and "choose the few" pushed me to cut whole chains, not to test each one against the thesis I had built.
- **The map's hedging.** Chain 9 ends with "All these senses are rare or technical." The map's own "Not carried" section notes that ayn's man-shaped wellhead structure was recorded and then judged mistaken. Together these made the whole chain feel unsafe, when only ayn's variant is doubtful. Tahdhib's «النعامة الخشبة المعترضة ثم تعلق القامة» and sihah's «القامة البكرة بأداتها» are clean.
- **Fear of invention.** "Never invent a scene" made me wary of putting a well into a prayer scene where no well is visible.
- **Map placement.** The ayat index lists chain 9 only as "Advances" for 1:6, not as core. I took that as permission to drop it.

**What I missed.** My fourth section's thesis was standing that keeps working versus standing that freezes («قامت الدابة», «قام الماء جمد»). The قامة is exactly an upright that exists to turn and draw. It hangs from the نعامة, a word from the root of 1:7's أنعمت. That would have given a short, evidence-bound beat: the upright hangs from the beam of favour, and the water it lifts is what keeps the traveller's affair standing (1:4, «الماء ملك أمر أي يقوم به الأمر»). This carries straightness into "those you favoured" without forcing anything.

**2. Instruction I would want**

In write.md, after "choose the few that make this ayah's scene":

> "Before sending a chain to the ledger, test it against the operation your controlling image depends on, not against its setting. If a chain gives a concrete instance of that same operation, use it as a sentence or short beat inside an existing scene, even if its object lies outside the scene. Dismiss chains one at a time, each with its own reason. Never dismiss several chains under a shared reason."

In the map, as a standing note:

> "Where the dictionary itself joins this ayah's word with another ayah's word in a single source phrase, mark it DICTIONARY-JOINED. Writers should weigh such a link before thematic chains. 'Rare' or 'technical' describes frequency, not reliability. Do not treat it as a reason to exclude. Flag doubtful sources separately, naming the specific phrase in doubt, so that it does not taint the sound members of the chain."
