### 15. One road for a gathered "we", and the scattered ways

From 1:5 the speakers are plural: نَعْبُدُ, نَسْتَعِينُ, ٱهْدِنَا. They ask for one road. The surah's roots hold both the gathered company and its scattering:
- ق و م is a man's people and kin.
- ر ب ب names great multitudes gathered.
- ع و ن has «وتعاون القوم», mutual help, with the ق و م word inside it.
- Against these, the root of نَعْبُدُ gives العباديد, «الفرق من الناس الذاهبون في كل وجه» and «الطرق المختلفة». These are companies scattering down divergent roads.
- ن ع م gives, in fixed expressions, a company whose march goes on together (خفت نعامتهم) and a company that breaks up (شالت نعامتهم).
- The stray of 1:7 is the beast apart from the herd (chain 7).

The single straight road is heard against many roads that scatter.

- 1:6 ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ: ق و م B001. «قوم كل رجل شيعته وعشيرته» (ayn;tahdhib). Contributes the company, a man's own people.
- 1:2 رَبِّ: ر ب ب B004. «الربيون: الجماعات الكثيرة» (tahdhib). This is a rare sense. Contributes great gathered multitudes.
- 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ: ع و ن B001. «وتعاون القوم» (sihah, inside «العون الظهيرة على الأمر والمعونة الإعانة واستعنت بفلان فأعانني وعاونني وتعاون القوم»). Contributes a people helping one another.
- 1:5 نَعْبُدُ: ع ب د B010. «العباديد الفرق من الناس الذاهبون في كل وجه» (sihah); «العباديد والعبابيد الأطراف البعيدة والأشياء المتفرقة والطرق المختلفة» (tahdhib). This is a rare derived noun. Contributes groups scattering in every direction, and divergent roads.

## Ayat

### 1:1 بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

- **Chain 2 (being made known), opens.** بِسْمِ is the Name «ما يعرف به ذات الشيء», a raising (سمو) and an indication. On the و س م derivation it is also a mark impressed and read. The verb left unsaid places whatever act follows under this sign.
- **Chain 6 (worshipped and worshippers), opens.** ٱللَّهِ is «معبود», the Worshipped, whose worshippers answer in 1:5. On the وله derivation it also carries yearning toward Him.
- **Chain 8 (womb and rearing), opens.** ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ is the womb and kinship root. On the وله derivation, the Name adds the mother's longing for her young.
- **Chain 9 (favour completed), opens.** Mercy is «رقة تقتضي الإحسان», good that must reach its object. It reaches it at 1:7.
- **Chain 11 (soft and hard), opens.** The soft pole, «الرقة».
- **Chain 10 (weather), opens.** بِسْمِ carries the sky, cloud, rain and plant. The و س م derivation adds the first rain that marks earth with growth.

### 1:2 ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ

- **Chain 9 (favour completed), advances.**
  - ٱلْحَمْدُ joins praise to thanks for favours (أشكر إليك أياديه ونعمه).
  - رَبِّ is to «رب النعمة»: complete the favour. Rare: الرُّبّى is favour itself.
  - The counter-image of the man who claims praise for his gifts sits under ح م د.
- **Chain 8 (womb and rearing), advances.** رَبِّ is rearing a child stage by stage to completion, the step-parent, and the newly delivered ewe. ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ is the household reared: «رب الجن والإنس ورب الخلق كلهم».
- **Chain 7 (owner and owned), opens.** رَبِّ is «مالكه».
- **Chain 6 (worshipped and worshippers), advances.** لِلَّهِ repeats the Worshipped. رَبِّ is «السيد المطاع», and the rabbānī is «المتأله».
- **Chain 2 (being made known), advances.** ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ is «ما يعلم به». Every kind of creature is a sign and a waymark, «المعلم الأثر يستدل به على الطريق», and a tall mountain seen from afar.
- **Chain 10 (weather), advances.** رَبِّ is the layered cloud that «يرب النبات», and the south wind and cloud that stay on.
- **Chain 12 (mainstay), opens.** رَبِّ is the one who sets things right, «أصلحه».
- **Chain 13 (the Day), prepared.** ٱلْحَمْدُ is the verdict «وجدته محمودا». رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ is the One before whom people stand (يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين). The Quran closes the judgment with this same sentence (39:75).
- **Chain 14 (journey's end), opens.**
  - ٱلْحَمْدُ: the land found praiseworthy (أحمدت الأرض), and the praised goal (غايتك المحمودة).
  - رَبِّ: staying in a place (رب بالمكان).
- **Chain 15 (gathered and scattered), touched.** A rare sense of رَبِّ: great gathered multitudes (ربيون).

### 1:3 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

The mercy pair returns and now stands between the Lord who rears and completes (1:2) and the Master of the reckoning (1:4).

- **Chain 8 (womb and rearing), renewed.** The womb and kin root is set beside rearing.
- **Chain 9 (favour completed), renewed.**
- **Chain 11 (soft and hard), renewed.**
- **Chain 13 (the Day), framed.** Tenderness stands immediately before the account. God's "days" are both punishment and pardon (أيام الله).

### 1:4 مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ

- **Chain 13 (the Day), centre.** The dictionary glosses this phrase as «الحساب ومنه مالك يوم الدين».
  - يَوْمِ is a momentous event. أيام الله are punishment and pardon, and «أيامه: نعمه».
  - ٱلدِّينِ is judgment, account and requital, and also debt taken and given.
  - مَٰلِكِ is sole sovereignty (ملكوت). The two readings, مَٰلِك and مَلِك, carry ownership and kingship.
- **Chain 7 (owner and owned), peak.** مَٰلِكِ is ownership of goods and dependents. ٱلدِّينِ is «دانه دينا أي أذله واستعبده ودينته ملكته»; «غير مدينين غير مملوكين».
- **Chain 6 (worshipped and worshippers), advances.** ٱلدِّينِ is «الطاعة», «طاعته والتعبد له».
- **Chain 12 (mainstay), advances.** مَٰلِكِ is cohesion (ملاك) and the mainstay of a matter. The dictionary joins it to 1:6's root: «قوام الأمر ملاكه». Water is «ملك أمر أي يقوم به الأمر».
- **Chain 1 (the road), prepared.** A rare sense of مَٰلِكِ: the middle of the road (الزم ملك الطريق).
- **Chain 4 (the led mount), opens.** مَٰلِكِ in a fixed expression: «ملك الدابة قوائمها وهاديها», and the lead animal the herd follows.
- **Chain 5 (the lone traveller), touched.** مَٰلِكِ in a fixed expression: the traveller's water.
- **Chain 10 (weather), touched.** Water as what keeps life standing.

### 1:5 إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ

The speakers turn from speaking about Him to speaking to Him, and the owned speak.

- **Chain 6 (worshipped and worshippers), human side.** نَعْبُدُ answers ٱللَّهِ = «معبود». The dictionary glosses this ayah: «إياك نعبد إياك نطيع الطاعة التي نخضع معها».
- **Chain 7 (owner and owned), confessed.** نَعْبُدُ is «العبد وهو المملوك»: every human, free or slave, is God's servant.
- **Chain 1 (the road), prepared.** نَعْبُدُ carries the road beaten smooth by treading, «الطريق المعبد وهو المسلوك المذلل». Worship as «غاية التذلل» makes the servant tractable to the road.
- **Chain 4 (the led mount), advances.** نَعْبُدُ: the broken-in camel, «البعير المعبد».
- **Chain 5 (the lone traveller), opens.**
  - نَسْتَعِينُ: asking for backing, «الظهيرة».
  - A rare sense of نَعْبُدُ: the traveller stranded when his mount gives out (أعبد به إذا كلت راحلته).
- **Chain 12 (mainstay), advances.** نَسْتَعِينُ is the back to lean on. A rare sense of نَعْبُدُ is toughness (العبدة).
- **Chain 15 (gathered and scattered), opens.**
  - The plural "we", and «وتعاون القوم».
  - A rare noun of the root of نَعْبُدُ: scattered groups and divergent roads (العباديد).
- The doubled إِيَّاكَ fixes one object for both acts.

### 1:6 ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ

- **Chain 1 (the road), opens explicitly.**
  - ٱهْدِنَا: showing the road and bringing onto it, «نقيض الضلالة». Also a manner of going, «هديه أي طريقته».
  - ٱلصِّرَٰطَ: the road, «الطريق المستقيم».
  - ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ: the level line and keeping to it, «لزومه المنهج المستقيم».
- **Chain 2 (being made known), advances.** Guidance is «دلالة بلطف», the same دلالة as ism, and making a place known (هديته الطريق ... أي عرفته).
- **Chain 3 (two ways out of sight), opens.** On the س form, the road is named «لأن الذاهب فيه يغيب»: it swallows and carries its walker onward out of view.
- **Chain 4 (the led mount), completes.** ٱهْدِنَا is the forepart and the leader who goes first. ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ contributes the legs (قوائم) of the same «ملك الدابة قوائمها وهاديها».
- **Chain 5 (the lone traveller), advances.**
  - ٱهْدِنَا: walking between two, leaning on them from weakness, and the staff that goes ahead.
  - ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ: rising to stand. In a fixed expression, the mount halted from exhaustion (قامت دابته إذا كلت).
- **Chain 6 (worshipped and worshippers), advances.** «الاستقامة على الطاعة»; a straight religion (دينا قيما).
- **Chain 12 (mainstay), advances.** ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ is «قوام الأمر ملاكه», livelihood «ما يقيمك», and «القيوم».
- **Chain 13 (the Day), advances.**
  - ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ: «القيامة ... يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين».
  - Valuation (القيمة).
  - Rare: the coin of exact weight (دينار قائم).
- **Chain 14 (journey's end), advances.** The station where one stays (مقام).
- **Chain 8 (womb and rearing), touched.** Standing over the household (قيام أهل بيته).
- **Chain 15 (gathered and scattered), advances.** قوم is the company that asks for one road.

### 1:7 صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ

- **Chain 1 (the road), completes.**
  - صِرَٰطَ: the road named a second time, now by its walkers.
  - غَيْرِ: the fork, «سواه وخلافه».
  - ٱلضَّآلِّينَ: those who swerve from the aim (جائر عن القصد) and fail to find the known house. The dictionary sets this word against ٱهْدِنَا.
- **Chain 9 (favour completed), completes.** أَنْعَمْتَ is favour delivered, «إيصال الإحسان إلى الغير», and increase (أنعم أي زاد). This is the إحسان that mercy required in 1:1, and the favour رب completes in 1:2.
- **Chain 11 (soft and hard), completes.**
  - أَنْعَمْتَ: softness (صار ناعما لينا) and the soft wind.
  - ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ: hardening (اشتداد السخط), solid rock, and thick skin.
- **Chain 13 (the Day), completes.**
  - أَنْعَمْتَ and ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ are the two outcomes of God's days: favour and pardon, and requital (فالمراد به الانتقام).
  - ضلّ as forgetting and losing track, which the Lord never does (20:52).
- **Chain 7 (owner and owned), closes.** أَنْعَمْتَ also names the owned herd (النعم الإبل). ٱلضَّآلِّينَ is the stray «لا يعرف ربها» / «لا يعرف لها مالك».
- **Chain 2 (being made known), closes on the hidden.** غَيْرِ performs the distinguishing that marks do (يتميز به عن غيره). ٱلضَّآلِّينَ is what has become hidden (خفي وغاب) and the place not recognised.
- **Chain 3 (two ways out of sight), completes.** The road's onward vanishing is set against ضلال, whose root meaning is «الغيبوبة»: dissolved like milk in water, buried, gone no one knows where.
- **Chain 14 (journey's end), completes.** أَنْعَمْتَ, in a fixed expression, is the land that suits the traveller and where he stays (نعمتني ... وأقمت بها), and the ease of life there.
- **Chain 10 (weather), touched.**
  - أَنْعَمْتَ: the soft, moist south wind (النعامى).
  - غَيْرِ, in a distinct lexeme and rare: God watering people with غيث.
- **Chain 15 (gathered and scattered), completes.** أَنْعَمْتَ in fixed expressions: a march kept up together, and a company broken up. ٱلضَّآلِّينَ is the beast apart from the herd.
- The doubled عَلَيْهِمْ sets the two things that come down on people side by side: favour, and wrath.
