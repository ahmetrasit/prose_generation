# Surah 102: map of image chains

## Chains

### 1. Occupation and the swerve: turned aside, then made to face

The surah opens on a person whose attention is held by one thing so that he leaves another (ل ه و), and it ends with that person made to face what he left: he sees the fire face to face and is questioned. Between the two stands زُرْتُمُ, whose root first means swerving from a course. Maqayis explains the visitor himself by that swerve: he turned from others to come to you. The movement runs: held by one thing → turned aside from what concerns him → the swerve ends at the graves → he is set facing (عيان, مواجهة, تراءى), and understanding comes to rest (ثبات الحكم). The same root's falsehood sense (a swerve from the path of truth) is the reversal of اليقين.

- 102:1 أَلْهَىٰكُمُ | ل ه و B001 | «اللهو كل شيء شغلك عن شيء فقد ألهاك، ولهيت عن الشيء إذا تركته لغيره» (maqayis) | the operation itself: one thing occupies him and another is left for it.
- 102:1 أَلْهَىٰكُمُ | ل ه و B001 | «اللهو الصدوف عن الشيء» (ayn); «لهيت عن الشيء إذا سلوت عنه وتركت ذكره وأضربت عنه» (sihah) | the face turned away, and remembrance of the thing dropped.
- 102:1 أَلْهَىٰكُمُ | ل ه و B001 | «اللهو ما يشغل الإنسان عما يعنيه ويهمه» (mufradat) | what is left behind is the thing that concerns him.
- 102:2 زُرْتُمُ | ز و ر B001 | «أصل واحد يدل على الميل والعدول» (maqayis); «تزاور عنه تزاورا كله بمعنى عدل عنه وانحرف» (sihah) | turning aside, a bend away from the line.
- 102:2 زُرْتُمُ | ز و ر B003 | «الزائر لأنه إذ زارك فقد عدل عن غيرك» (maqayis) | the dictionary joins visit and swerve in one phrase: the visit to the graves is itself a turning from somewhere else.
- 102:2 زُرْتُمُ | ز و ر B002 | «الزور الكذب لأنه مائل عن طريقة الحق» (maqayis) | the swerve as falsehood, the opposite of the certainty in 102:5 and 102:7.
- 102:5, 102:7 ٱلْيَقِينِ | ي ق ن B001 | «سكون الفهم مع ثبات الحكم» (mufradat) | where the swerving ends: understanding at rest, judgment fixed.
- 102:7 عَيْنَ | ع ي ن B002 | «عيانا أي مواجهة» (tahdhib) | face to face, the opposite of الصدوف.
- 102:6–7 لَتَرَوُنَّ / لَتَرَوُنَّهَا | ر ء ي B004 | «تراءا الجمعان أي تقاربا وتقابلا» (mufradat) | two parties brought near and set facing each other.

### 2. The mill mouth: what is cast in to keep it busy

Under the surah's first word stand two openings that are fed: the hole of the hand-mill, into which the grinder throws grain, and the back of the mouth, named for the food thrown into it. Mufradat defines the mill-feed with the very verb that defines diversion: it is what the mill is occupied with (يشغل). The opening أَلْهَىٰكُمُ ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ can therefore be heard as a mill kept busy, handful after handful, the count always growing, the grain ground fine. The sequence then shows the openings that receive the person himself: the grave, a place made to take him, and the fire in a pit, fed fire on fire and ember on ember.

- 102:1 أَلْهَىٰكُمُ | ل ه و B003 | «اللهوة ما يشغل به الرحى مما يطرح فيه، وسميت العطية لهوة تشبيها بها» (mufradat) | joins occupation and the mill in one phrase: the mill is kept busy by what is thrown into it.
- 102:1 أَلْهَىٰكُمُ | ل ه و B003 | «اللهوة ما يطرحه الطاحن في ثقبة الرحى بيده» (maqayis); «اللهوة ما يلقى في فم الرحى من الحب للطحن» (ayn) | the grinder's hand casting grain into the mill's mouth.
- 102:1 أَلْهَىٰكُمُ | ل ه و B004 | «اللهاة أقصى الفم، سميت لهاة لما يلقى فيها من الطعام» (maqayis); «اللهاة أقصى الفم وهي لحمة مشرفة على الحلق» (ayn) | the body's own mouth, named for what is thrown into it.
- 102:1 ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ | ك ث ر B001 | «الكثرة نماء العدد» (ayn;tahdhib); «استكثرت من الشيء أي أكثرت منه» (sihah) | the feed that keeps growing in number.
- 102:8 ٱلنَّعِيمِ | ن ع م B010 | «ودققت دواء فأنعمت دقه أي بالغت وزدت» (tahdhib) | pounding fine, the grinding taken as far as it goes.
- 102:2 ٱلْمَقَابِرَ | ق ب ر B001 | «القبر مقر الميت» (mufradat); «أقبرته جعلت له مكانا يقبر فيه» (maqayis;mufradat) | the opening in the ground made to receive him.
- 102:6 ٱلْجَحِيمَ | ج ح م B001 | «الجحيم اسم من أسماء النار وكل نار عظيمة في مهواة فهي جحيم» (sihah) | a fire in a pit, a hollow that things fall into.
- 102:6 ٱلْجَحِيمَ | ج ح م B001 | «كل نار توقد على نار جحيم والجمر بعضه على بعض جحيم» (tahdhib) | fire fed on fire, embers heaped one on another: the growing heap of 102:1 comes back as fuel.

### 3. Outnumbering: the count, the dust of death, the heat of killing, the graves

التكاثر is reciprocal: two sides match numbers and wealth until one outnumbers the other. The root's dust sense carries the rivalry into battle, as thick rising dust, and the dictionary's own verse "the dust of death rose until it billowed" joins كثر with death. The fire's root names the height of killing in battle and death itself at full blaze. The contest ends at the graves, which mufradat reads as death. The tafsir report (from memory; Muqatil and al-Kalbi) is that Banu ʿAbd Manaf and Banu Sahm boasted of their numbers, and that the side outnumbered among the living went on to count its dead in the graveyards. That makes the visit the last round of the count. The surah then sets a different contest, in knowing: عالمت الرجل فعلمته has the same pattern as كاثرناهم فكثرناهم.

- 102:1 ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ | ك ث ر B002 | «كاثرناهم فكثرناهم أي غلبناهم بالكثرة» (sihah); «كاثر بنو فلان بني فلان فكثروهم إذا زادوا على عددهم» (jamhara) | two clans matching numbers until one prevails.
- 102:1 ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ | ك ث ر B002 | «المكاثرة والتكاثر التباري في كثرة المال والعز» (mufradat); «فلان مكثور أي مغلوب في الكثرة» (mufradat) | the race in wealth and standing, and its loser.
- 102:1 ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ | ك ث ر B005 | «ثار نقع الموت حتى تكوثرا» (sihah;mufradat); «الكوثر الغبار سمي بذلك لكثرته وثورانه» (maqayis) | battle dust thickening and rising; the dictionary joins كثر and الموت in one line.
- 102:6 ٱلْجَحِيمَ | ج ح م B002 | «جاحم الحرب شدة القتل في معركتها» (ayn;tahdhib); «الموت جاحم» (maqayis;sihah) | the killing at its hottest, death ablaze.
- 102:2 حَتَّىٰ زُرْتُمُ ٱلْمَقَابِرَ | ق ب ر B005 | «حتى زرتم المقابر كناية عن الموت» (mufradat) | the contest reaches death.
- 102:2 ٱلْمَقَابِرَ | ق ب ر B001 | «المقبرة موضع القبور» (ayn;jamhara;tahdhib;mufradat) | the field where the dead lie and can be counted.
- 102:2 زُرْتُمُ | ز و ر B003 | «الزور الذي يزورك واحدا كان أو جميعا» (ayn) | the visitors as one body, the whole party going.
- 102:3–5 تَعْلَمُونَ / عِلْمَ | ع ل م B001 | «عالمت الرجل فعلمته» (sihah;tahdhib) | a contest in knowing, built like the contest in number: the surah's replacement for it.

### 4. What is multiplied: women, children, herds, coin

These are the objects of the count, at the level of things. The diversion word itself serves as a euphemism for a woman and for a child. التكاثر is boasting in number and wealth. The herd root names camels for the good and favour in them, so 102:8's النعيم stands over the very herds that were counted. The eye root names ready coin and gold, so 102:7's عين stands over the money at hand.

- 102:1 أَلْهَىٰكُمُ | ل ه و B002 | «وقد يكنى باللهو عن غيره، المرأة، الولد» (maqayis); «يعبر عن كل ما به استمتاع باللهو، المرأة والولد» (mufradat) | wife and child as the things that divert.
- 102:1 أَلْهَىٰكُمُ | ل ه و B002 | «اللهو ما شغلك من هوى أو طرب» (ayn) | the pleasure that holds attention.
- 102:1 ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ | ك ث ر B002 | «التفاخر بكثرة العدد والمال» (tahdhib) | number (of people) and wealth as the two things counted.
- 102:1 ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ | ك ث ر B003 [fixed expression] | «رجل مكثر كثير المال» (ayn;tahdhib) | the man of much property.
- 102:8 ٱلنَّعِيمِ | ن ع م B005 | «النعم الإبل لما فيه من الخير والنعمة والأنعام البهائم» (maqayis) | the dictionary joins herds and favour: camels named for the النعمة in them.
- 102:8 ٱلنَّعِيمِ | ن ع م B002 | «نعم فلان أولاده ترفهم» (maqayis) | children raised in ease.
- 102:7 عَيْنَ | ع ي ن B011 | «العين وهو المال العتيد الحاضر» (maqayis); «قيل للذهب: عين» (mufradat) | gold, money ready to hand.

### 5. Display: abundance shown to be seen, then the one who showed made to see

The dictionary defines التكاثر as boasting (التفاخر), and boasting needs onlookers. The seeing root supplies them: a deed done so that people will see it, the fine bearing one shows people, the look that pleases the eye. A man may even make a show with another's wealth. The surah turns the direction around: those who arranged to be seen are told لَتَرَوُنَّ, "you will see", and what they see is the fire.

- 102:1 ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ | ك ث ر B002 | «التفاخر بكثرة العدد والمال» (tahdhib) | abundance displayed in a contest of boasting.
- 102:1 ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ | ك ث ر B003 [fixed expression] | «فلان يتكثر بمال غيره» (sihah) | abundance put on for show with borrowed wealth.
- 102:6–7 لَتَرَوُنَّ | ر ء ي B005 | «وراءى فلان يرائي وفعل ذلك رئاء الناس وهو أن يفعل شيئا ليراه الناس» (maqayis); «فلان مراء والاسم الرياء وفعل ذلك رياء وسمعة» (sihah) | doing a thing so that others will see it.
- 102:6–7 لَتَرَوُنَّ | ر ء ي B006 | «والري ما أريت القوم من حسن الشارة والهيئة والرواء حسن المنظر» (ayn); «الرئي ما رأت العين من حال حسنة والرواء حسن المنظر» (maqayis) | the handsome bearing shown to people; maqayis joins رأى and عين here.
- 102:8 ٱلنَّعِيمِ | ن ع م B002 | «نعمة العيش حسنه وغضارته» (tahdhib) | the fresh, comfortable life that is put on show.

### 6. Buried and laid open: grave, chest, the hidden interior

The root of المقابر means something sunk and hidden inside: low hidden ground, the hollow worn into the core of aromatic wood. Mufradat carries burial over to the whole condition of a person in this world, whose states are covered "as if buried", and calls the ignorant buried while he lives. That puts the knowledge of 102:3–5 (العلم نقيض الجهل) against the burial of 102:2: the buried state is ignorance, and knowing is the opening of the grave. زُرْتُمُ has a branch naming the middle of the chest, and a branch for something prepared inside oneself. The Quran pairs exactly these two hiding places, graves and chests, at the moment both are laid open. The protected, screened maiden of the certainty root belongs here as the reversal: what is kept from sight under the very word whose first sense is the removal of doubt.

- 102:2 ٱلْمَقَابِرَ | ق ب ر B002 | «أصل صحيح يدل على غموض في شيء وتطامن» (maqayis); «أرض قبور غامضة» (maqayis;jamhara;tahdhib) | the sunk, hidden place.
- 102:2 ٱلْمَقَابِرَ | ق ب ر B002 | «القبر موضع متأكل مسترخى في العود الذي يتطيب به وهو جوفه» (ayn) | the eaten-out hollow at the core of a thing.

### 7. Enclosed growth: palm heart, sheathed fruit, membrane, the visible womb

The grave root's concealment sense is concrete in plants and births: a palm whose fruit lies hidden among its fronds, a child born inside an unbroken membrane. The multiplying root names the palm's heart and its spathe, the hidden growing core. The seeing root names the moment a pregnant animal's state becomes visible, "until the truth of her pregnancy is seen". Together they give an image of growth carried hidden inside a cover and then shown. Heard against 102:2 and 102:6–7, the graves become the enclosure, and seeing is the moment of showing. The Quran stages earth and womb as places of hidden growth from which people are brought out.

### 8. Visit, lodging and reception

زُرْتُمُ is a visit, and a visit has a guest, a host and the honour paid to the guest (التزوير كرامة الزائر). The grave root supplies the lodging: a place made for the dead, his مقر. The fire root names a place (the place of intense heat), and the ease root holds a fixed phrase for arriving at a land that suits you and staying there, and another for going to someone on foot. So the surah's three places, graves, Jahim and Naʿim, can be heard as stations of a journey with a reception at each. A visitor does not stay. The tafsir tradition records (from memory) that a hearer of 102:2 took زُرْتُمُ to mean that the dead will be raised, since a visitor leaves. The Quran calls the reception نُزُل, the meal set before an arriving guest, and the end of al-Wāqiʿa sets the guests of ease and the guests of Jahim side by side, then names the whole حق اليقين.

### 9. From mark to the thing itself: knowing by trace, by ear, by heart, by eye

The surah climbs: سوف تعلمون twice, then علم اليقين, then seeing, then عين اليقين. The dictionary lays out the rungs. The knowing root first names a mark: the trace a thing carries that tells it apart, the waymark from which a road is inferred. Certainty is knowledge with doubt removed, and there is a certainty of the ear, the man who is sure of whatever he hears. Seeing is with the eye or with inner sight, and as a verb of the heart it means knowing. Then comes the eye: seeing face on, after which no trace is sought, and the thing itself. The dictionary joins these words to one another again and again (رأيته رأي العين; رأيت الشيء عيانا; اليقين العلم; رأى بمعنى العلم). The branch gloss of ي ق ن B001 also lists the three named ranks of certainty (by knowledge, by eye, by reality), but no Arabic phrase is quoted for them. A further rung is being shown: in the reading لَتُرَوُنَّ (from memory: Ibn ʿĀmir and al-Kisāʾī), "you will be made to see it", a shower stands behind the sight (أريته الشيء فرآه). Death itself is called اليقين in the Quran, which ties the rungs back to 102:2.

- 102:3–5 تَعْلَمُونَ | ع ل م B001 | «ما علمت بخبرك أي ما شعرت به» (ayn;tahdhib) | knowing by report, being made aware of news.

- 102:5 ٱلْيَقِينِ | ي ق ن B001 | «رجل أذن يقن وهو الذي لا يسمع بشيء إلا أيقن به» (tahdhib) | certainty from hearing alone, the rung the surah offers its listener before the seeing.

### 10. The eye that blazes, the fire that sees, the eye that is cooled

The root of الجحيم also names the eye: in Himyar جحمة is the eye, the lion's two eyes are named from it "for their blazing", and a man جحم when he stares wide-eyed. Its anger branch is a face blazing with rage. So in لَتَرَوُنَّ ٱلْجَحِيمَ the thing seen is, in its root, an eye that blazes and stares, and the dictionary itself joins the seeing verb to the fire's blaze (رأيت جحمة النار). The seeing root has a mutual form, two parties seeing each other, and the Quran stages exactly this: the fire sees them. Against the blazing eye the ease root holds a fixed expression joining نعمة and عين, the eye's delight (its coolness), and the eye root has an eye of care and protection. Between 102:6–7 and 102:8 the eye moves from the blazing, staring one to the question about the eye's delight.

### 11. Heat against cool ease: blaze, shade, water, soft moist wind

Jahim is heat at its height, in a place of heat. النعيم, by its root, is softness and moist ease: something that has become soft and smooth, the south wind named for being the most moist and gentle of winds, and a canopy named after the ostrich, the shade set over a mountaintop or over the head of a well. The eye root names the running spring, and the knowing root names the well full of water. In the Quran Jahim and Naʿim are a fixed pair (82:13–14). The hadith reports on 102:8 (from memory) make the ease that is asked about exactly cool water and shade. So the move from 102:6 to 102:8 runs from the blaze to the water and the shade.

### 12. Given, counted, asked: the gift, the claimants, the account

The surah's first word also names a gift (العطاء لهوة: a gift likened to a handful thrown into the mill), and the multiplying root names abundant good given (الكوثر). Its last word is bestowal (النعمة اليد والصنيعة). So the surah opens and closes on what was given. The multiplying root also holds a fixed phrase that reverses the rich man's position: one besieged by people asking him, his means run out and claims piling up against him. The asking root then supplies the beggar and the man who asks many questions, and 102:8 puts the many-goods man on the side of the one questioned. Asked about the given, he must answer in kind, with the thing itself present (عين غير دين). The visit root's speech branch shows the questioned man preparing his words before he speaks.

## Ayat

### 102:1 أَلْهَىٰكُمُ ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ
- 1 Occupation: أَلْهَىٰكُمُ is the occupying that makes one leave something else and turn the face away (الصدوف). Scene: held by one thing, turned aside from what concerns him, the swerve ends at the graves, and he is set face to face with the fire, understanding at rest.
- 2 Mill mouth: أَلْهَىٰكُمُ is the grain fed into the mill and the back of the mouth fed with food; ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ is the feed that keeps growing. Scene: a mill kept busy handful after handful, ground fine, until the grave and the fire-pit, fed ember on ember, receive the feeder.
- 3 Outnumbering: ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ is two sides matching numbers and the battle dust that billows with death. Scene: clans outcounting each other, the dust of death rising, killing at its hottest, the count carried into the graveyard, then a contest in knowing.
- 4 Multiplied things: أَلْهَىٰكُمُ names woman and child; ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ names number and wealth. Scene: wives, children, herds and gold counted, the herds named for the نعمة that 102:8 asks about, the gold named عين as in 102:7.
- 5 Display: ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ is boasting, even with borrowed wealth. Scene: abundance staged for onlookers; those who arranged to be seen are made to see the fire.
- 7 Enclosed growth: ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ's root names the palm's heart and spathe. Scene: growth carried hidden in its cover (fronds, membrane, womb, grave) until it is shown and seen.
- 12 Account: أَلْهَىٰكُمُ names a gift (لهوة) and ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ abundant good given (الكوثر), and also the man besieged by claimants whose means run out. Scene: the surah opens on what was given and closes on bestowal asked about; the man of much becomes the man questioned, and must answer with the thing itself.

### 102:2 حَتَّىٰ زُرْتُمُ ٱلْمَقَابِرَ
- 1 Occupation: زُرْتُمُ is the swerve (الميل والعدول), and maqayis explains the visitor by it; the falsehood sense is the swerve from truth. Scene: as at 102:1.
- 2 Mill mouth: ٱلْمَقَابِرَ is the place made to receive the dead. Scene: as at 102:1.
- 3 Outnumbering: حتى زرتم المقابر is death (mufradat), and the graveyard where the dead are counted; زُرْتُمُ names the visitors as one body. Scene: as at 102:1.
- 6 Buried and laid open: ٱلْمَقَابِرَ is the sunk, hidden place, the ignorant "buried" while alive; زُرْتُمُ names the chest and what is prepared inside it. Scene: graves and chests hide what is in them, the ignorant lie buried in life, and knowledge turns both out (100:9–10).
- 7 Enclosed growth: ٱلْمَقَابِرَ is the palm hiding its fruit in its fronds and the child born inside an unbroken membrane. Scene: as at 102:1.
- 8 Visit: زُرْتُمُ is the visit, with the honour owed a visitor and hard travel; ٱلْمَقَابِرَ is the lodging prepared. Scene: a visitor who does not stay passes through the grave as a lodging and arrives at Jahim, a place of heat, or at Naʿim, a land that suits him where he stays, each with its own guest's meal.
- 9 Mark to thing: the graves as death, which the Quran calls اليقين. Scene: knowing climbs from a trace and a report, through certainty and heart-sight, to the eye's sight of the thing itself, after which no trace is sought.
- 12 Account: زُرْتُمُ names the man shaping his speech before he speaks. Scene: as at 102:1.

### 102:3 كَلَّا سَوْفَ تَعْلَمُونَ
- 9 Mark to thing: تَعْلَمُونَ as knowing by a mark or by report, the first rung. Scene: as at 102:2.
- 3 Outnumbering: تَعْلَمُونَ as the contest in knowing (عالمت الرجل فعلمته) that replaces the count. Scene: as at 102:1.
- 6 Buried and laid open: تَعْلَمُونَ as the opposite of the ignorance mufradat calls burial. Scene: as at 102:2.
- 11 Heat and cool: the knowing root names the well full of water (العيلم). Scene: from the blaze of Jahim to the ease asked about: soft moist wind, shade over a well-head, a running spring, cool water.

### 102:4 ثُمَّ كَلَّا سَوْفَ تَعْلَمُونَ
- 9 Mark to thing: the repetition after ثم is a second rung of the same knowing. Scene: as at 102:2.

### 102:5 كَلَّا لَوْ تَعْلَمُونَ عِلْمَ ٱلْيَقِينِ
- 9 Mark to thing: عِلْمَ is grasping the thing in its reality; ٱلْيَقِينِ is knowledge with doubt removed, including the certainty of the ear. Scene: as at 102:2.
- 1 Occupation: ٱلْيَقِينِ as understanding at rest, judgment fixed, the end of the swerve. Scene: as at 102:1.
- 6 Buried and laid open: ٱلْيَقِينِ as removal (إزاحة الشك), and its other branch as the screened, guarded maiden. Scene: as at 102:2.
- 3 Outnumbering, 11 Heat and cool: عِلْمَ as at 102:3.

### 102:6 لَتَرَوُنَّ ٱلْجَحِيمَ
- 9 Mark to thing: لَتَرَوُنَّ is seeing by eye or insight, رأى meaning knowing, and being shown (the reading لَتُرَوُنَّ). Scene: as at 102:2.
- 10 Eye: ٱلْجَحِيمَ's root names the eye, the lion's blazing eyes, the stare and the face blazing with anger; the dictionary joins رأيت with the fire's blaze; لَتَرَوُنَّ carries mutual seeing. Scene: they see Jahim, and Jahim, an eye in its root, sees them and rages; against it stands the eye of care and the eye's delight that 102:8 asks about.
- 1 Occupation: لَتَرَوُنَّ as two parties set facing. Scene: as at 102:1.
- 2 Mill mouth: ٱلْجَحِيمَ as fire in a pit, fed fire on fire, ember on ember. Scene: as at 102:1.
- 3 Outnumbering: ٱلْجَحِيمَ as killing at its hottest and death ablaze. Scene: as at 102:1.
- 5 Display: لَتَرَوُنَّ reverses the showing of رئاء and الري. Scene: as at 102:1.
- 7 Enclosed growth: لَتَرَوُنَّ as the hidden pregnancy made visible until its truth is seen. Scene: as at 102:1.
- 8 Visit: ٱلْجَحِيمَ as the place of intense heat one arrives at. Scene: as at 102:2.
- 11 Heat and cool: ٱلْجَحِيمَ as heat at full force. Scene: as at 102:3.

### 102:7 ثُمَّ لَتَرَوُنَّهَا عَيْنَ ٱلْيَقِينِ
- 9 Mark to thing: عَيْنَ is seeing face on, after which no trace is sought, and the thing itself; «رأيته رأي العين» pairs the verb and the noun of this ayah. Scene: as at 102:2.
- 10 Eye: عَيْنَ is the looking eye, set against the blazing eye of Jahim, and the eye of care. Scene: as at 102:6.
- 1 Occupation: عَيْنَ as face to face (مواجهة), the reversal of turning the face away. Scene: as at 102:1.
- 4 Multiplied things: عَيْنَ names gold and ready money. Scene: as at 102:1.
- 11 Heat and cool: عَيْنَ names the running spring. Scene: as at 102:3.
- 12 Account: عَيْنَ is payment present, not deferred. Scene: as at 102:1.
- 6 Buried and laid open, 7 Enclosed growth: ٱلْيَقِينِ and لَتَرَوُنَّهَا as at 102:5 and 102:6.

### 102:8 ثُمَّ لَتُسْـَٔلُنَّ يَوْمَئِذٍ عَنِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ
- 12 Account: لَتُسْـَٔلُنَّ is asking about what was received, with the asker as the poor man and the man of many questions, and the mutual questioning; ٱلنَّعِيمِ is favour placed by a giver's hand. Scene: as at 102:1.

- 10 Eye: ٱلنَّعِيمِ in the fixed expression نعمة العين, the eye's delight. Scene: as at 102:6.
- 8 Visit: ٱلنَّعِيمِ as arriving at a land that suits one and staying [fixed expression], going to someone on foot [fixed expression], and good living. Scene: as at 102:2.
- 4 Multiplied things: ٱلنَّعِيمِ names the camels for their النعمة, and children raised in ease. Scene: as at 102:1.
- 2 Mill mouth: ٱلنَّعِيمِ in أنعمت دقه, grinding taken as far as it goes. Scene: as at 102:1.
- 5 Display: ٱلنَّعِيمِ as the fresh, comfortable life that is put on show. Scene: as at 102:1.
