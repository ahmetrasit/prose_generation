# S100 source wording retained for editorial review

The augmentation preserves the original commentary exactly. Two source issues remain for editorial treatment:

1. **¶26, exact wording:** 3:154 says “what is in your breasts” (ما في صدوركم), while 100:10 says “what is in the breasts” (ما في الصدور). Their conceptual link is close, but the claim of identical expression is too exact. This is a wording correction, not a rejection of the connection.
2. **¶35, gambling arrows in a generous table image:** the paragraph places meysir arrows and their container within a positive scene of sharing food. The dictionary links can be described, but their inclusion does not make these practices Quranically approved generosity. 2:219, 5:3, and 5:90–91 establish the boundary; the positive conclusion needs qualification.

The parent reviewed the source passages, reported conflicts, and selected canonical ayat. Further interpretive qualifications were retained:

- **¶38 and 24:61:** eating separately is expressly permitted. The original image can describe selfish withholding rather than every solitary meal. The agent changed its initial categorical conflict into a qualifying reference making that distinction.
- **¶22 and 20:55:** the verse uses divine “We” within the Moses–Pharaoh passage. Describing the wording as simply Moses's own statement needs care; attribution was left uncertain rather than forced.
- The subject of 100:7, the preposition in 38:32, and the referent of “my lord” in 12:23 were not made artificially definite. Other pronoun and image qualifications remain in the individual verdict files.

This was a sampled parent review with targeted follow-ups, not an independent audit of every ayah–paragraph judgment. Repeated exposition across images remains available for a later surah editorial pass.
