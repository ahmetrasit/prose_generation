# S87 source wording retained for editorial review

The augmentation preserves the original commentary exactly. These findings concern that source, not repairs silently made to it.

1. **¶45, word order:** the claim that 24:37 has the same order as 87:14–15 is inaccurate. The former has remembrance, prayer, then zakat; the latter has purification, remembrance, then prayer. The thematic connection remains useful, but the order claim needs correction.
2. **¶50, where the sequence ends:** the source says the sequence ends at 53:42. The repeated sequence continues in 53:43–45 and beyond. If the author means only the selected quotation, the wording should make that boundary explicit.
3. **¶55 and ¶66, the most wretched person's response:** saying that this person does not reject or oppose the reminder is too absolute. 92:16 explicitly joins denial with turning away. Avoidance may describe the image's mechanism, but it does not exclude rejection.
4. **¶63, forgetting:** the phrase “altıncı ayetteki unutulan döküntü” loses the negation in 87:6, which says “you will not forget.” The dictionary image must remain distinct from what the ayah actually predicates.

The parent checked the above against the source paragraphs and canonical Arabic. The following are interpretive qualifications rather than established contradictions:

- **20:74–76:** the speaker is not explicitly identified in these verses. The initial categorical speaker conflict in image 10 was withdrawn; attribution remains open.
- **35:32 and ¶61:** self-wronging people occur among those chosen to inherit the Book, but this uses a different selection expression from the dictionary example in خ ي ر. The agent revised its initial conflict into a qualifying reference that limits overgeneralization.
- **¶69:** 87:5 uses غثاء whereas 13:17 uses زبد. A relation between their images does not by itself establish lexical identity. Keep any dictionary argument separate from the verses' actual wording.

These findings do not make every other source assertion verified. The parent reviewed samples and reported issues, not every ayah–paragraph relevance judgment independently. Cross-image repetition also remains for a later editorial pass.
