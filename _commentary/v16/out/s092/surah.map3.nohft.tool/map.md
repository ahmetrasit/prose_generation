## Chains

The text, the dictionary and channels.md supply all members. Every Quran passage outside Surah 92 is cited from memory of the Quran. I also read some of them through the provided tool. Where a dictionary phrase itself quotes or glosses a Quran passage, the entry says so. One historical report comes from tafsir memory and is marked "(memory)".

### 1. Night drawn over, day cut open

Night is a layer laid over everything, "one thing covering another". Day is the opposite operation: light spreads, the dark is split the way a river splits the ground, and what was hidden comes out and stands clear. The surah opens on this pair. Its later "face" (وجه, 92:20) echoes the fixed expression "the face of the day" (وجه النهار), the first, uncovered front of the day.

- 92:1 ٱلَّيْلِ — ل ي ل B001 — «الليل خلاف النهار» (maqayis); «ظلام الليل» (tahdhib) — the dark layer itself.
- 92:1 يَغْشَىٰ — غ ش و B001 — «أصل صحيح يدل على تغطية شيء بشيء» (maqayis); «الغشاء الغطاء» (sihah) — the operation: something laid over something else.
- 92:2 ٱلنَّهَارِ — ن ه ر B002 — «النهار ضياء ما بين طلوع الفجر إلى غروب الشمس» (ayn;tahdhib;maqayis); «الوقت الذي ينتشر فيه الضوء» (mufradat) — light spreading.
- 92:2 ٱلنَّهَارِ — ن ه ر B003 — «أصل صحيح يدل على تفتح شيء أو فتحه» (maqayis); «النهر السعة تشبيها بنهر الماء وفي ضياء وسعة» — day as an opening and a widening.
- 92:2 ٱلنَّهَارِ — ن ه ر B001 — «سمي النهر لأنه ينهر الأرض أي يشقها» (maqayis) — the root's image of cutting a way through.
- 92:2 تَجَلَّىٰ — ج ل و B001 — «انكشاف الشيء وبروزه» (maqayis); «الجلي نقيض الخفي ... وانجلى عنه الهم» (sihah) — the cover lifted and the thing standing out.
- 92:2 تَجَلَّىٰ — ج ل و B007 — «السماء جلواء أي مصحية» (maqayis;sihah;mufradat); «جلاء يوم واحد أي بياض يوم» (ayn;tahdhib); «والنهار إذا جلاها إذا بين الشمس» (tahdhib, glossing 91:3) — a clear sky and the white of a day. The dictionary itself reads day as the revealer.
- 92:20 وَجْهِ — و ج ه B007 [fixed expression] — «وجه النهار أوله» (jamhara) — day has a face, its first opened part.

Outside the surah:
- 91:3–4. God swears by the day when it reveals the sun and the night when it covers it. This is the preceding surah's oath, built from the same two verbs.
- 7:54. God describes creation: «يغشي الليل النهار يطلبه حثيثا». Night covers day and chases it.
- 13:3. God's signs: «يغشي الليل النهار».
- 36:37. A sign for people: «الليل نسلخ منه النهار». Day is stripped off night like a skin.
- 39:5. «يكور الليل على النهار». Night is wound round day like a turban.
- 79:29. God made its night dark and brought out its forenoon.
- 93:1–2. The next surah swears by the forenoon and by the night when it grows still.
- 7:143. Moses at the mountain: «فلما تجلى ربه للجبل». The same verb, used for a disclosure.

### 2. Film over the eye, kohl that clears it

A film (غشاوة) covers the eye or the heart. Kohl clears the sight, polish clears the blade, and the falcon "casts" its cleared gaze at its quarry. The dictionary calls guidance "showing" (البيان). Soot from a burning wick becomes kohl, so the fire of 92:14 also provides the material that clears the eye.

- 92:1 يَغْشَىٰ — غ ش و B001 — «الغشاوة ما غشي القلب من رين الطبع» (ayn); «الغشاوة ما يغطى به الشيء» (mufradat) — the film over the heart or eye.
- 92:2 تَجَلَّىٰ — ج ل و B002 — «الجلا مقصور الإثمد لأنه يجلو البصر» (ayn); «جلوت بصري بالكحل والجلا كحل» (sihah;tahdhib); «جلا الصيقل السيف واجتلاه» (ayn;tahdhib) — kohl clearing sight; a blade polished clean.
- 92:2 تَجَلَّىٰ — ج ل و B008 — «جلى ببصره تجلية إذا رمى به كما ينظر الصقر إلى الصيد» (sihah) — the cleared gaze thrown at a target.
- 92:12 لَلْهُدَىٰ — ه د ي B001 — «الهدى البيان» (tahdhib) — guidance as making things plain.
- 92:14 نَارًا — ن و ر B008 — «النؤور دخان الفتيلة يتخذ كحلا أو وشما» (ayn) — the fire's soot made into kohl.

Outside the surah:
- 2:7. God on those who reject: a seal on their hearts and «على أبصارهم غشاوة».
- 45:23. The one who takes his whim as god: «وجعل على بصره غشاوة».
- 36:9. «فأغشيناهم فهم لا يبصرون».
- 50:22. At the Rising, to the one who ignored it: «فكشفنا عنك غطاءك فبصرك اليوم حديد». The cover is removed and sight is sharp.

### 3. Night garment, the cloak, the protecting cloth

To cover is also to wrap oneself in a garment (استغشى بثوبه), and night is that garment. تردى is to "put on the cloak". The cloak is "whatever adorns you", and debt is also called a cloak. The God-fearing man's word, اتقى, has a cloth for its sense: the protecting layer between a woman's outer wrap and her hair.

- 92:1 يَغْشَىٰ — غ ش و B001 — «واستغشى بثوبه» (sihah); «والرجل يستغشي ثوبه» (tahdhib) — wrapping oneself in a garment.
- 92:11 تَرَدَّىٰٓ — ر د ي B004 — «تردى وارتدى بمعنى أي لبس الرداء» (sihah); «كل ما زينك فهو رداؤك» (tahdhib); «يسمى الدين رداء» (tahdhib) — the cloak put on, his wealth as adornment, debt as a cloak.
- 92:5 وَٱتَّقَىٰ / 92:17 ٱلْأَتْقَى — و ق ي B001 — «وقاية المرأة وهي الخرقة التي بين جلبابها وشعرها» (jamhara); «كل ما وقى شيئا فهو وقاء له ووقاية» (ayn;jamhara;tahdhib) — a protecting cloth.

Outside the surah:
- 78:10 and 25:47. «وجعلنا الليل لباسا». Night as clothing.
- 7:26. God to the children of Adam: «ولباس التقوى ذلك خير». The garment of taqwa.
- 16:81. «سرابيل تقيكم الحر». Garments that protect against heat; the verb is وقى.
- 11:5. Those who «يستغشون ثيابهم» to hide.
- 71:7. Noah on his people: «استغشوا ثيابهم».

### 4. Covering the wife, unveiling the bride, the pair

Read as a wedding, the oath runs: night covering, which is the husband coming in to the wife; day's تجلى, which is the bride shown to her husband, who looks at her and gives her a gift; and then «الذكر والأنثى». The bride is "led" (هُديت) to her husband and given her bride-price (صداق). A woman is غانية when she needs no ornament because of her husband or her beauty.

- 92:1 يَغْشَىٰ — غ ش و B004 — «الغشيان غشيان الرجل المرأة» (maqayis); «غشيت موضع كذا أتيته وكني بذلك عن الجماع وغشاها وتغشاها» (mufradat).
- 92:2 تَجَلَّىٰ — ج ل و B003 [fixed expression] — «الماشطة تجلو العروس وقد جليت على زوجها واجتلاها زوجها أي نظر إليها» (ayn;tahdhib) — the bride displayed and looked at.
- 92:2 تَجَلَّىٰ — ج ل و B009 [fixed expression] — «جلاها زوجها وصيفا أي أعطاها» (sihah); «جلى فلان امرأته وصيفا حين اجتلاها أي أعطاها وصيفا عند جلوتها» (tahdhib) — this dictionary phrase joins the word of 92:2 with أعطى of 92:5: the unveiling gift.
- 92:3 ٱلذَّكَرَ وَٱلْأُنثَىٰٓ — ذ ك ر B001 / ء ن ث B001 — «الذكر خلاف الأنثى»; «الأنثى خلاف الذكر» — the pair.
- 92:12 لَلْهُدَىٰ — ه د ي B006 — «هديت العروس إلى زوجها» (mufradat); «أهدى الرجل امرأته جمعها إليه وضمها» (tahdhib) — the bride led and joined to her husband.
- 92:6 وَصَدَّقَ — ص د ق B007 — «الصداق صداق المرأة» (maqayis) — the bride-price.
- 92:8 وَٱسْتَغْنَىٰ — غ ن ي B005 / B006 — «الغانية المستغنية بزوجها عن الزينة أو بحسنها عن التزين» (mufradat); «الغنى التزويج» (tahdhib).

Outside the surah:
- 7:189. God on the first couple: «فلما تغشاها حملت حملا خفيفا».
- 2:187. The nights of the fast: «هن لباس لكم وأنتم لباس لهن».
- 4:4. «وآتوا النساء صدقاتهن نحلة». Giving (آتى) and the صدقة of the bride-price together.
- 53:45. «وأنه خلق الزوجين الذكر والأنثى».

### 5. Easy and hard delivery: male and female

The foetus is formed (مخلقة) and delivered. The dictionary keeps an old wish and an old curse in the surah's own roots: «أيسرت وأذكرت», "may her delivery be easy and may she bear a boy", and «أعسرت وآنثت», "may her delivery be hard and may she bear a girl". So the oath on the creation of male and female (92:3) and «لليسرى / للعسرى» (92:7, 92:10) meet in one formula. Other parts of the scene: the opening at the hindquarters as birth comes near, and the newborn that comes out hands first, called وجيه.

- 92:3 خَلَقَ — خ ل ق B003 — «مضغة مخلقة أي تامة الخلق» (sihah); «مخلقة قد بدا خلقها وغير مخلقة لم تصور» (tahdhib).
- 92:3 ٱلذَّكَرَ — ذ ك ر B001 — «أذكرت ولدت ذكرا والمذكار تلد الذكور».
- 92:3 وَٱلْأُنثَىٰٓ — ء ن ث B001 — «آنثت المرأة إذا ولدت أنثى» (sihah).
- 92:10 لِلْعُسْرَىٰ — ع س ر B006 — «أعسرت المرأة إذا عسر عليها ولادها» (maqayis;sihah;tahdhib); «أعسرت وآنثت» (maqayis;tahdhib); «أيسرت وأذكرت» (maqayis;tahdhib) — the dictionary phrase joining 92:3 with 92:7 and 92:10.
- 92:7 لِلْيُسْرَىٰ — ي س ر B001 — «وأيسرت المرأة وتيسرت في كذا أي سهلته وهيأته» (mufradat).
- 92:15 يَصْلَىٰهَآ — ص ل ي B006 — «أصلت الناقة فهي مصلية إذا وقع ولدها في صلاها» (tahdhib); «انفرج صلاها» (ayn).
- 92:20 وَجْهِ — و ج ه B010 — «للولد إذا خرجت يداه من الرحم أولا وجيه» (sihah).

Outside the surah:
- 80:17–20. Man created from a drop and given his measure; then «ثم السبيل يسره». The way out is made easy.
- 46:15. «حملته أمه كرها ووضعته كرها».
- 3:35–36. ʿImrān's wife at the birth: «إني وضعتها أنثى ... وليس الذكر كالأنثى».
- 16:58–59. A man's face darkens at news of a daughter. The Quran rebukes the attitude behind «أعسرت وآنثت».
- 35:11. «وما تحمل من أنثى ولا تضع إلا بعلمه».
- 42:49–50. God gives females, males or both, as He wills.
- 53:45–46 and 75:39. The pair, male and female.

### 6. The road shown by a fire

Fire and light share a root in the dictionary: «سميا ... من طريقة الإضاءة». In the Jahiliyya people lit fires on raised markers «ليهتدى بها», so that travellers would find the way. A traveller sees a fire from far off and makes for it. Guidance is "making the road known". So the نار of 92:14 is heard in two ways: as the beacon that shows the road of 92:12, and as the blaze that must be avoided (chain 13).

- 92:14 نَارًا — ن و ر B001 — «النور والنار سميا بذلك من طريقة الإضاءة» (maqayis) — fire as light.
- 92:14 نَارًا — ن و ر B005 — «كانوا ينورون في الجاهلية ليهتدى ويقتدى بها» (ayn); «المنار: علم الطريق؛ ضرب المنار على طريقه ليهتدى بها» (sihah) — this dictionary phrase joins the root of نار with اهتدى.
- 92:14 نَارًا — ن و ر B003 [fixed expression] — «تنورت نارا قصدت إليها» (ayn); «تنورت النار من بعيد: تبصرتها» (sihah) — seen from afar and made for.
- 92:12 لَلْهُدَىٰ — ه د ي B001 — «هديته الطريق والبيت هداية أي عرفته» (sihah); «الهداية دلالة بلطف؛ تعريف الطرق» (mufradat).
- 92:2 ٱلنَّهَارِ — ن ه ر B002 — «الوقت الذي ينتشر فيه الضوء» (mufradat) — the general light in which the road can be seen.
- 92:20 وَجْهِ — و ج ه B002 — «الوجهة كل موضع استقبلته» (maqayis); «وجهوا للناس الطريق إذا وطئوه وسلكوه» (tahdhib) — the heading, and a road made plain by treading.

Outside the surah:
- 20:9–10. Moses, travelling at night with his family, sees a fire: «لعلي آتيكم منها بقبس أو أجد على النار هدى». Fire, guidance and «آتي» together.
- 27:7 and 28:29. The same scene.
- 6:97. Stars made «لتهتدوا بها في ظلمات البر والبحر».
- 16:16. «وعلامات وبالنجم هم يهتدون».

### 7. The caravan line: guide at the head, first and last

The dictionary's الهادي is the front of anything: the horses' necks, the first troop, the staff that goes ahead of the man holding it, the desert guide who walks in front. The أوّل camel goes ahead of the herd. The آخِر are those at the back, and آخرة الرحل is the rear post of the saddle. Some travellers go by night and some only by day. So «إن علينا للهدى وإن لنا للآخرة والأولى» (92:12–13) can be heard as a column, its guide at the front and its last members behind, all held by its Owner.

- 92:12 لَلْهُدَىٰ — ه د ي B003 — «الهادي من كل شيء أوله؛ هوادي الخيل أعناقها أو أول رعيل؛ العصا هاديا لأنها تتقدمه؛ الدليل يسمى هاديا لتقدمه» (ayn) — this dictionary phrase joins هدى with أوّل (92:13).
- 92:13 وَٱلْأُولَىٰ — ء و ل B001 — «ناقة أولة وجمل أول إذا تقدما الإبل» (maqayis).
- 92:13 لَلْءَاخِرَةَ — ء خ ر B001 / B003 — «أخرى القوم أي من كان في آخرهم» (sihah); «آخرة الرحل وقادمته ومؤخر الرحل ومقدمه» (maqayis).
- 92:1 ٱلَّيْلِ — ل ي ل B002 — «لست بليلي ولكني نهر أي أسير بالنهار ولا أطيق سرى الليل» (tahdhib) — a phrase joining night travel and day travel.
- 92:2 ٱلنَّهَارِ — ن ه ر B002 — «رجل نهر صاحب نهار».
- 92:16 وَتَوَلَّىٰ — و ل ي B002 — «الولاء والتوالي أن يحصل شيئان فصاعدا حصولا ليس بينهما ما ليس منهما» (mufradat) — one following another with no gap.

Outside the surah:
- 15:65. God to Lot: «فأسر بأهلك بقطع من الليل واتبع أدبارهم». Night travel, with Lot walking at the rear.
- 44:23. God to Moses: «فأسر بعبادي ليلا إنكم متبعون».
- 20:10. Moses on the night road looking for a guide at the fire.

### 8. Parting of the ways

The runners set out (سعي: walking fast, running, heading for a goal). They "issue forth scattered", as people leave a watering place in separate groups, and the gap between them becomes «شتان». One road is "made smooth and readied" (يسرى). The other twists and refuses to become easy (عسرى). God holds the direction of every matter (هدية أمره). There is also the busy road where routes meet (الميتاء).

- 92:4 سَعْيَكُمْ — س ع ي B001 — «السعي المشي السريع وهو دون العدو» (mufradat); «سعى إذا مشى وسعى إذا عدا وسعى إذا قصد» (tahdhib).
- 92:4 سَعْيَكُمْ — س ع ي B002 — «كل عمل من خير أو شر فهو السعي» (ayn).
- 92:4 لَشَتَّىٰ — ش ت ت B001 — «أصل يدل على تفرق وتزيل» (maqayis); «يصدر الناس أشتاتا أي متفرقين» (tahdhib, quoting 99:6).
- 92:4 لَشَتَّىٰ — ش ت ت B003 — «شتان ما بينهما» (maqayis;sihah;mufradat); «ارتفاع الالتئام بينهما» (mufradat).
- 92:7 لِلْيُسْرَىٰ — ي س ر B001 — «تيسر واستيسر أي تسهل وتهيأ» (mufradat) — the road smoothed and made ready.
- 92:10 لِلْعُسْرَىٰ — ع س ر B001 / B004 — «العسرى الأمور التي تعسر ولا تتيسر» (tahdhib); «العسر الخلاف والالتواء» (maqayis;ayn) — the twisting road.
- 92:12 لَلْهُدَىٰ — ه د ي B002 — «هدية أمره أي جهة أمره؛ ... ليس لهذا الأمر هدية ولا قبلة ولا دبرة ولا وجهة» (tahdhib) — this dictionary phrase joins هدى with وجهة (92:20).
- 92:18 يُؤْتِى — ء ت ي B010 — «الميتاء الطريق العامر ومجتمع الطريق» (sihah) — the travelled road and the junction.

Outside the surah:
- 99:6–8. On the Day people issue forth scattered (أشتاتا) to be shown their deeds. The dictionary quotes this passage.
- 30:14. «يومئذ يتفرقون».
- 90:8–10. «وهديناه النجدين». Two raised roads.
- 76:3. «إنا هديناه السبيل إما شاكرا وإما كفورا».
- 16:9. «وعلى الله قصد السبيل ومنها جائر». The straight road is God's charge, and some roads go astray; compare «إن علينا للهدى».
- 53:39–41. «وأن سعيه سوف يرى ثم يجزاه الجزاء الأوفى».
- 17:19. «وسعى لها سعيها».
- 59:14. «وقلوبهم شتى».
- 87:8. God to the Prophet: «ونيسرك لليسرى».
- 80:20. «ثم السبيل يسره».
- 94:5–6 and 65:7. Hardship with ease.
- 74:9–10. «يوم عسير على الكافرين غير يسير».

### 9. The mount: yielding, easy-paced, hoof-shy, unbroken, led at the side

Several of the surah's verbs name a ridden animal. "أعطى البعير" is the camel that yields without resisting. فرس واق (from the root of اتقى) is the horse that shies from hard ground because its hoof hurts, and a واق saddle does not gall. يَسَر is light-legged and quick to follow. The عسير camel is one ridden before it was broken. جنبت الدابة is to lead an animal at one's side, and mufradat explains «واجنبني» by exactly this leading, "away from the side of shirk". ردى is a horse's fast gait.

- 92:5 أَعْطَىٰ — ع ط و B006 — «أعطى البعير إذا انقاد ولم يستعصب» (sihah); «أعط فيعوج رأسه إلى راكبه» (tahdhib) — the camel that gives way and turns its head to the rider.
- 92:5 وَٱتَّقَىٰ / 92:17 ٱلْأَتْقَى — و ق ي B003 — «فرس واق إذا كان يهاب المشي من وجع يجده في حافره» (sihah); «لا تقي بالجدجد أي لا تشتكي حزونة الأرض» (tahdhib); «سرج واق إذا لم يكن معقرا» (sihah;tahdhib).
- 92:7 فَسَنُيَسِّرُهُۥ لِلْيُسْرَىٰ — ي س ر B005 — «ليسر خفيف ويسر أي لين الانقياد سريع المتابعة يوصف به الإنسان والفرس» (ayn); «اليسرات: القوائم الخفاف» (maqayis;sihah).
- 92:10 لِلْعُسْرَىٰ — ع س ر B008 — «العسير الناقة التي لم ترض وقد اعتسرتها إذا ركبتها قبل أن تراض» (sihah) — the unbroken mount.
- 92:17 وَسَيُجَنَّبُهَا — ج ن ب B005 — «جنبت الدابة إذا قدتها إلى جنبك» (maqayis;jamhara); «من جنبت الفرس كأنما سأله أن يقوده عن جانب الشرك» (mufradat) — led at the side, away from danger.
- 92:11 تَرَدَّىٰٓ — ر د ي B002 — «ردى الفرس بين العدو والمشى الشديد» (sihah) — the fast gait that becomes, in the same root, the plunge (chain 12).

Outside the surah:
- 36:71–72. God's livestock «فهم لها مالكون وذللناها لهم فمنها ركوبهم». Owned, made tractable, ridden.
- 43:12–13. Mounting the animal: «سبحان الذي سخر لنا هذا».
- 14:35. Abraham: «واجنبني وبني أن نعبد الأصنام». Mufradat explains this with the led horse.
- 67:15. The earth made «ذلولا».

### 10. Weapons and the charge: hard and soft iron, the true spear, the charge carried home or broken

Male iron is the hardest and makes a cutting sword. Female iron is soft and does not cut. A صدق spear is hard and straight. A معطية bow bends without stiffness, and المعاطاة is handing a sword over for the other man to swing. In battle, صدق is carrying the charge home and كذب is breaking it off: the dictionary uses the two roots of 92:6 and 92:9 for the same act. تولّى is turning one's back in flight. ### 11. Setting the face, turning the back

ابتغاء is searching hard, as for a lost camel (بغى ضالته). وجه is the front of anything and the direction one faces, the qibla. It can stand for the self, and it is "what is intended by God". تولّى can mean either movement: turning toward (التولية تكون إقبالا, with the gloss «فول وجهك أي وجه وجهك نحوه») or turning away and leaving (التولية تكون انصرافا). Mufradat adds that turning away can be done with the body or by not listening. The wild animal that كذب runs a stretch and then stops to look behind it. The scene ends in satisfaction, the opposite of displeasure.

### 12. The plunge below, the height above

تردّى is falling into a well, rushing into a drop, exposing oneself to destruction. المصالي are snares set for birds. The الأتقى is moved away from the danger. الأعلى is height itself. The dictionary traces «تعال» to calling someone up to a high place. The surah's line drops from «إذا تردى» (92:11) and rises to «ربه الأعلى» (92:20).

### 13. Fire: the warner's cry, the blaze, the one who enters, the one moved aside

The fire flickers and blazes, and is named Laẓā. تلظّى can also describe a man burning with anger. The one who يصلاها enters and suffers its heat. The same root names roasting meat and straightening a stick over fire, which is fire controlled and the reverse of this scene. الشقاء is hardship. ### 14. The covering of death and the wealth that does not avail

### 15. Ease as wealth, hardship as want: who is sufficed

The dictionary uses يُسر for wealth and defines one word by the other: «أيسر الرجل أي استغنى», and the other way round, «الغنى مقصور اليسار». عسر is "little in the hand", and أعسر is to go from plenty into want. So the man who «استغنى» (92:8) is the man who أيسر. He is sent toward العسرى, and his wealth "does not suffice" him. The dictionary also joins sufficiency with repayment: «فلان ذو غناء وجزاء», «الجزاء الغناء والكفاية».

### 16. The hand: reaching, handing over, gripping, owed, empty

عطو is reaching with the hand, like the gazelle that lifts its forelegs to pull down leaves. Its root gives handing over (المناولة). It also gives the palm held out to beg and the hand reaching for what is not its own. إيتاء is giving and bringing. The miser's act is إمساك, gripping. A favor is itself "a hand" (النعمة اليد). The one who did it may press for repayment as one collects a debt, even from someone who has nothing, whose hand is empty (قلة ذات اليد). Debt is a cloak around the neck. صدقة is what a man gives out of his wealth.

### 17. Herd wealth: milk that multiplies or dries

The Arabs' wealth was their livestock, and the dictionary derives النَّعَم (camels) from the good and the favor (النعمة) in them. A flock increases in milk and young. A camel's milk "lies", that is, stops before its expected time. Livestock are led to the sanctuary as an offering, and Arabs call camels هدي. A ewe is kept at home for her milk.

### 18. Growth: soft land, the rearing cloud, rising crop, the palm's yield

Soft female land is good for growing. Coarse herbage is "male". The cloud is called رباب "because it rears (يرب) the plants". تربية is bringing a thing on, stage by stage, to completion. One "rears" a favor by completing it. Crops زكا, increasing and growing. A palm's إتاء is its yield, its increase and its many fruits. Rainwater gathers in rock hollows that God made. So «يؤتي ماله يتزكى» (92:18) and «ربه» (92:20) are heard as giving that grows like a crop under a Lord who rears it.

- 92:18 يَتَزَكَّىٰ — ز ك و B001 — «زكا الزرع يزكو زكاء ازداد ونما وكل شيء ازداد ونما فهو يزكو زكاء» (ayn); «أصل الزكاة النمو الحاصل عن بركة الله تعالى» (mufradat).

### 19. The alms-collector at the flock

The dictionary ties سعي to the collecting of alms: the ساعي is the man appointed to take them in. The مصدّق takes the flocks' alms, and the متصدق gives. «تزكّى» is glossed «تصدّق». Paid alms "settles it for you" (جزت عنك).

### 20. Freeing the bound

سعاية is a bondsman working to pay for his freedom ("to free his neck"). مولى is both the one who frees and the one who is freed, and the "master of the favor" (ولي النعم, مولى النعمة). So the freed man owes the one who freed him. In this surah the freer gives his wealth when no one holds a favor over him that he is repaying (92:19). (memory) The tafsir reports say 92:17–21 came down about Abū Bakr. He bought and freed Bilāl and others, and the idolaters said he did it to repay a favor (يد) Bilāl had done him.

- 92:4 سَعْيَكُمْ — س ع ي B005 — «سعاية العبد إذا كوتب أن يسعى فيما يفك رقبته» (maqayis).

### 21. The promised best: confirmed or called a lie, then delivered

الحسنى is the best outcome, Paradise, the opposite of السوءى. One man confirms it. صدق also means a man's expectation proving true and the promise coming to pass. The other calls it a lie, "attributes it to falsehood". It comes back as recompense, which can be reward or punishment, and ends in satisfaction from both sides. The best outcome lies in الآخرة, "the second coming-into-being".

### 22. The lot-arrows and the share in the last abode

In al-maysir the players (أيسار) gather. The arrows are kept in a leather case (ربابة). The seventh and most valuable arrow is المعلى. A winning arrow that comes out separately from the rest is عنود. The slaughtered camel is divided by the arrows. خلاق is the share measured out for each person, and «لا خلاق له في الآخرة» is having no share in the last abode. The surah's words يسرى, عنده, ربه, الأعلى, خلق and الآخرة all carry members of this scene. (memory) Pre-Islamic poets praised the أيسار who gave the meat they won to the poor in winter.

- 92:7 / 92:10 يُسَّر / ٱلْيُسْرَىٰ — ي س ر B007 — «الأيسار: القوم يجتمعون على الميسر» (maqayis); «يسر القوم الجزور أي اجتزروها واقتسموا أعضاءها» (sihah).
- 92:20 رَبِّهِ — ر ب ب B010 — «الربابة شبيهة بالكنانة تجمع فيها سهام الميسر» (sihah) — this dictionary phrase joins the root of ربه with that of يسرى.
- 92:20 ٱلْأَعْلَىٰ — ع ل و B009 — «المعلى السابع من القداح» (maqayis;sihah;mufradat).
- 92:19 عِندَهُۥ — ع ن د B002 — «قدح عنود وهو الذي يخرج فائزا على غير وجهة سائر القداح» (tahdhib).
- 92:3 خَلَقَ — خ ل ق B006 — «الخلاق النصيب لأنه قد قدر لكل أحد نصيبه» (maqayis); «الخلاق: النصيب؛ لا خلاق له في الآخرة» (sihah) — this dictionary phrase joins the root of 92:3 with الآخرة (92:13).
- 92:3 خَلَقَ — خ ل ق B001 — «خلقت الأديم إذا قدرته قبل القطع» (sihah) — measuring before cutting.

### 23. The race to the goal

Horses run (سعي), and one outruns another (ساعاني فسعيته). The horse just behind the winner is the المصلّي. The winner reaches the goal first (استولى على الغاية). The end of the course is الميتاء. ردى is a fast gait, and بغي is a horse's proud, playful running. The order is first and last.

- 92:4 سَعْيَكُمْ — س ع ي B001 / B008 — «السعي عدو ليس بشديد» (ayn); «ساعانى فلان فسعيته أسعيه إذا غلبته فيه» (sihah).

### 24. Who owns: his wealth, Our last and first, his Lord

The man "takes wealth for himself" (تمول). God says the guidance is His charge and the last and the first are His (92:12–13). The Lord is the owner of everything, and the dictionary also calls Him "the creator", which is the verb of 92:3. What is "with him" (عنده) is what he holds.

## Interactions

- 1 and 14: they share يغشى (92:1). Night's cover and the غاشية «تغشى الخلق بإفزاعها» are the same verb.
- 1, 11 and 21: in 10:26–27 the people of الحسنى have faces untouched by gloom, and the others' faces are «أغشيت ... قطعا من الليل مظلما». Night's covering falls on the face.
- 1, 11 and 13: in 14:50 «وتغشى وجوههم النار». The covering verb with fire and face.
- 1, 16 and 19: 2:274 «ينفقون أموالهم بالليل والنهار سرا وعلانية» and 2:271 «إن تبدوا الصدقات ... وإن تخفوها». Covered and uncovered giving.
- 1 and 4: يغشى and تجلى each carry a night sense (the husband coming in) and a day sense (the bride shown). 2:187 sets spouses as garments on the nights of the fast.
- 3 and 4: 2:187 «هن لباس لكم» joins the night garment and union.
- 3 and 13: 16:81 «سرابيل تقيكم الحر» and 7:26 «لباس التقوى». The وقاية cloth becomes protection from heat.
- 3 and 16: «يسمى الدين رداء» (ر د ي B004) and 3:180 «سيطوقون ما بخلوا به». The cloak of debt and the collar of hoarded wealth, both at the neck.
- 2 and 6: «النؤور دخان الفتيلة يتخذ كحلا» and «سميا ... من طريقة الإضاءة». Fire's soot clears the eye and fire's light shows the road.
- 4 and 16: «جلاها زوجها وصيفا أي أعطاها» joins تجلى (92:2) and أعطى (92:5).
- 4 and 5: 7:189 «فلما تغشاها حملت». Covering leads to conception and then to birth.
- 5 and 8: «أيسرت وأذكرت» / «أعسرت وآنثت», with 80:20 «ثم السبيل يسره». The way out of the womb and the road of life are both "made easy".
- 5 and 10: ذكر and أنثى are male and female births and also hard and soft iron.
- 6 and 7: هدى is both showing the road (B001) and the leader at the front (B003). 20:9–10 is a night march toward a fire hoping for «هدى».
- 6 and 13: the same word نار (92:14) is the beacon «ليهتدى بها» and the blaze that is entered.
- 7 and 24: الأولى and الآخرة are the front and rear of the column and also the first and last abodes God owns.
- 8 and 9: يسرى and عسرى are two roads and also two mounts (light-legged and quick to follow, or ridden unbroken).
- 8 and 11: «ليس لهذا الأمر هدية ولا قبلة ولا دبرة ولا وجهة» joins هدى (92:12) and وجهة (92:20).
- 9 and 13: جنب is leading a horse at one's side and also pushing harm away. Mufradat: «كأنما سأله أن يقوده عن جانب الشرك».
- 9 and 16: أعطى is the camel that yields and also the hand that gives.
- 9, 12 and 23: ردى is a horse's fast gait (B002) and the plunge into a well (B003).
- 10 and 11: تولّى is flight in battle (8:15–16) and turning away from the call (20:48; 75:32).
- 10 and 21: صدق and كذب are the charge carried home or broken off, and the promise confirmed or denied. الحسنى is «الظفر أو الشهادة» and also Paradise.
- 11 and 12: 22:11 «انقلب على وجهه». Turning the face and falling are one movement.
- 12 and 13: 9:109 and 3:103 show the drop that ends in fire. تردّى (92:11) is followed by يصلاها (92:15).
- 12 and 20: 90:11–13. The steep ascent is «فك رقبة».
- 13 and 15: «الشقاء: الشدة والعسر» joins الأشقى (92:15) with العسرى (92:10).
- 13 and 16: 59:9 and 64:16 «ومن يوق شح نفسه» (protection from one's own grip). 70:15–18 Laẓā calls the one who «جمع فأوعى». 9:34–35 hoarded gold is heated in the fire and pressed on the hoarders' sides (جنوبهم).
- 14 and 15: يغني (92:11) appears in both. 111:2 «ما أغنى عنه ماله».
- 15, 16 and 19: «فلان ذو غناء وجزاء», «جزت عنك شاة», «صدقتك جزت عنك». Sufficiency, repayment and alms meet in جزى.
- 16 and 20: «النعمة اليد» and «ولي النعم». A favor is a hand the freed man owes. 26:22 shows the same thing.
- 16 and 17: wealth is herd animals, and herd animals given away become هدي.
- 18 and 19: زكا is a crop growing and also alms paid. 9:103 «تطهرهم وتزكيهم بها». 30:39 «تريدون وجه الله فأولئك هم المضعفون».
- 18 and 24: رب rears (B002, B008) and owns (B001). 20:50 «أعطى كل شيء خلقه ثم هدى». 87:1–3.
- 18 and 21: 2:265 «ابتغاء مرضات الله» is a garden that yields double. Seeking, satisfaction and growth together.
- 18 and 11: 30:38–39. «يريدون وجه الله» and zakat that multiplies.
- 19 and 11: 9:75–76 «بخلوا به وتولوا». The alms that were vowed, held back, andturned away from: the same three words as 92:6, 92:8 and 92:16.
- 11 and 16: 76:9 «لوجه الله لا نريد منكم جزاء». Seeking the face and refusing repayment, as in 92:19–20.
- 20 and 22: «مولى النعمة» against «ما لأحد عنده من نعمة تجزى». The freed man's debt to his patron, which the freer of 92:19 does not count on.
- 22 and 24: «لا خلاق له في الآخرة» against «وإن لنا للآخرة». The share in the last abode is God's to give.
- 22 and 15: الميسر and الأيسار come from the root of يسر (B007) next to ease and wealth (B001, B003). 2:219 puts maysir next to «ماذا ينفقون».
- 23 and 8: سعي is running a race and also running down two separate roads.
- 12 and 24: الأعلى (92:20) and 79:24, where Pharaoh calls himself «ربكم الأعلى». The true height and a false claim to it.

## Ayat

**92:1 وَٱلَّيْلِ إِذَا يَغْشَىٰ**
- Chain 1. The ayah gives the dark layer and the act of covering. Whole scene: night is laid over the world, and day cuts it open and brings what was hidden into view, with day's "face" as its first front.
- Chain 2. يغشى is the film over the eye and heart. Whole scene: a film covers sight, and kohl, polish and البيان clear it; fire's soot becomes the kohl.
- Chain 3. يغشى is wrapping oneself in a garment. Whole scene: night wraps like clothing, the rich man puts on his cloak (whatever adorns him, also called debt), and the God-fearing man has a protecting cloth between himself and harm.
- Chain 4. يغشى is the husband coming in to the wife. Whole scene: night covering is union, day's unveiling is the bride shown and given a gift, she is led to her husband and given her bride-price, and male and female are the pair.
- Chain 7. Night travel. Whole scene: a column with its guide at the head and its last members at the rear, moving by night or by day.
- Chain 14. The غاشية and the faint of death. Whole scene: the covering that wraps creation, the fall to destruction, and wealth that does not suffice.

**92:2 وَٱلنَّهَارِ إِذَا تَجَلَّىٰ**
- Chain 1. The ayah gives spreading light, splitting open, and the uncovering that brings things out. The dictionary glosses the sister oath (91:3) as revealing the sun. Whole scene: as at 92:1.
- Chain 2. تجلى is kohl clearing sight, the blade polished, the falcon's cleared look. Whole scene: as at 92:1.
- Chain 4. تجلى is the bride displayed, looked at, and given a gift (أعطاها). Whole scene: as at 92:1.
- Chain 6. Day is the general light in which the road shows. Whole scene: a fire lit on a raised marker so travellers find the way, seen from far off and made for; guidance is making the road known, and the same fire is the blaze to avoid.
- Chain 7. «رجل نهر» is the man who travels by day. Whole scene: as at 92:1.

**92:3 وَمَا خَلَقَ ٱلذَّكَرَ وَٱلْأُنثَىٰٓ**
- Chain 4. The pair. Whole scene: as at 92:1.
- Chain 5. The formed foetus; bearing a son or a daughter. Whole scene: delivery wished easy with a son (أيسرت وأذكرت) or cursed hard with a daughter (أعسرت وآنثت); the hindquarters open and the newborn comes hands first; the Creator makes the way out easy or hard.
- Chain 10. Hard male iron and soft female iron. Whole scene: a cutting sword or a blunt one, a true spear, a bow that bends, a charge carried home (صدق) or broken off (كذب), the back turned, الحسنى as victory or martyrdom, a shield at the side.
- Chain 18. Soft land good for crops, coarse "male" herbage, rock hollows where rain gathers. Whole scene: soft land, a cloud that rears the plants, crops increasing, the palm's yield; the Lord rears and completes the favor.
- Chain 22. خلاق is the measured share, and خلق is measuring before cutting. Whole scene: lot-arrows in their case, the highest arrow and the winning arrow, the camel divided, and a measured share, including no share in the last abode.
- Chain 24. «الرب المالك والخالق». Whole scene: the man owns wealth, God owns the last and the first and takes the road on Himself, and the Lord owns everything.

**92:4 إِنَّ سَعْيَكُمْ لَشَتَّىٰ**
- Chain 8. Running toward a goal; work for good or evil; scattering, «شتان». Whole scene: runners set out and leave the water in separate groups onto two roads, one smoothed and readied and one twisting; the direction of each matter is God's.
- Chain 19. The ساعي collects alms. Whole scene: the collector takes alms from the flock and the owner pays (تزكى = تصدق), and one sheep settles it for him.
- Chain 20. سعاية is the bondsman working to free his neck. Whole scene: the slave works off his price, the patron who frees him holds a favor over him, and the freer in this surah expects no repayment.
- Chain 23. Running and outrunning. Whole scene: horses run, the second follows the first, and the winner reaches the end of the course; first and last.

**92:5 فَأَمَّا مَنْ أَعْطَىٰ وَٱتَّقَىٰ**
- Chain 16. أعطى is the hand reaching out and handing over. Whole scene: a hand reaching and giving, a hand gripping, a begging palm, a favor that is a hand owed and claimed back, an empty hand.
- Chain 9. أعطى is the camel that yields; اتقى is the horse wary of hard ground and the saddle that does not gall. Whole scene: a yielding, light-legged mount led at the rider's side away from danger, against the unbroken camel that is forced and plunges.
- Chain 10. A bow that bends; a sword handed over. Whole scene: as at 92:3.
- Chain 13. اتقى is a barrier set between oneself and what one fears. Whole scene: the warner raises the alarm; a flickering, raging fire named Laẓā; the most wretched enters and is roasted; the God-fearing man puts up a barrier, is pushed aside from harm, and has a shield at his side.
- Chain 3. The protecting cloth. Whole scene: as at 92:1.
- Chain 4. أعطى is the gift at the unveiling. Whole scene: as at 92:1.

**92:6 وَصَدَّقَ بِٱلْحُسْنَىٰ**
- Chain 21. Confirming الحسنى and its coming true. Whole scene: the best end is announced, one man confirms it and another calls it a lie, and it comes back as recompense and satisfaction.
- Chain 10. A hard, true spear; the charge carried home; الحسنى as victory or martyrdom. Whole scene: as at 92:3.
- Chain 19. صدقة and the مصدّق. Whole scene: as at 92:4.
- Chain 16. صدقة given out of one's wealth. Whole scene: as at 92:5.
- Chain 4. صداق, the bride-price. Whole scene: as at 92:1.

**92:7 فَسَنُيَسِّرُهُۥ لِلْيُسْرَىٰ**

- Chain 9. The light-legged mount that follows easily. Whole scene: as at 92:5.
- Chain 5. Easy delivery, «أيسرت وأذكرت». Whole scene: as at 92:3.
- Chain 15. يسار means wealth, and «أيسر أي استغنى». Whole scene: wealth (يسار, غنى) against want (little in the hand); the self-sufficient man is sent toward want, his wealth does not suffice him, and sufficiency comes as repayment.

- Chain 22. الميسر, the division of the camel by lots. Whole scene: as at 92:3.

**92:8 وَأَمَّا مَنۢ بَخِلَ وَٱسْتَغْنَىٰ**

- 1D Double-faced surfaces: «ذو وجهين» and the deceptive cloth are not heard in the surah.
- 2B Delay and promptness: deferred sale and «ما كذب أن فعل» make no image here.
- 2C Return and interpretation: تأويل is not in the surah's sequence.
