## Chains

### The blow that knocks a compact thing apart
ٱلْقَارِعَةُ is named by what it does: one hard thing brought down on another. The next two ayat show what struck masses become. The people fly apart as moths do, and بث is the scattering that wind or a stirred foot gives to dust. The mountains become wool that has been beaten (يطرق) until it loosens. In 101:9 هاوية gives the downward swing of the striking arm and the throw from above. So the surah moves from strike, to scatter, to the fling into the pit.
- 101:1–3 ٱلْقَارِعَةُ — ق ر ع B001 — "القرع ضرب شيء على شيء" (mufradat); "كل شيء ضربته فقد قرعته" (ayn); "قرع راحلته أي ضربها بسوطه" (tahdhib) — the blow itself, a thing struck on a thing.
- 101:1–3 ٱلْقَارِعَةُ — ق ر ع B005 — "القارعة الشديدة من شدائد الدهر وهي الداهية" (sihah); "النازلة الشديدة تنزل عليهم بأمر عظيم" (tahdhib); "القارعة القيامة" (ayn) — the blow named as a calamity that comes down on people.
- 101:4 ٱلْمَبْثُوثِ — ب ث ث B001 — "التفريق وإثارة الشيء كبث الريح التراب" (mufradat); "الغبار إذا هيجته" (sihah) — what is struck flies up and apart like stirred dust.
- 101:5 ٱلْمَنفُوشِ — ن ف ش B001 — "نفش الصوف وهو أن يطرق حتى يتنفش" (maqayis) — carding is itself a beating: the mountains' image is made by blows.
- 101:5 كَٱلْعِهْنِ — ع ه ن B002 — "العهنة انكسار في قضيب من غير بينونة" (ayn); "أصل العاهن أن يتقصف القضيب من الشجرة ولا يبين منها فيبقى معلقا مسترخيا" (tahdhib) — a rod cracked by force but not severed, hanging slack: rigid form that has given way.
- 101:9 هَاوِيَةٌ — ه و ي B003 — "أهويت له بالسيف" (sihah); "أهويته إذا ألقيته من فوق" (tahdhib) — the swing of the sword arm, and the throw from above.
- Quran: 69:4 "كَذَّبَتْ ثَمُودُ وَعَادٌۢ بِٱلْقَارِعَةِ" — God narrating, within the scene that opens 69:1–3 "ٱلْحَآقَّةُ مَا ٱلْحَآقَّةُ وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلْحَآقَّةُ" (69:1–3 from memory). 69:13–14 "فَإِذَا نُفِخَ فِى ٱلصُّورِ نَفْخَةٌۭ وَٰحِدَةٌۭ وَحُمِلَتِ ٱلْأَرْضُ وَٱلْجِبَالُ فَدُكَّتَا دَكَّةًۭ وَٰحِدَةًۭ" — God describing the Day: earth and mountains lifted and crushed in a single blow. 13:31 "تُصِيبُهُم بِمَا صَنَعُوا۟ قَارِعَةٌ" — God to the Prophet about the deniers; the same ayah begins with mountains moved ("سُيِّرَتْ بِهِ ٱلْجِبَالُ"). 56:5–6 "وَبُسَّتِ ٱلْجِبَالُ بَسًّۭا فَكَانَتْ هَبَآءًۭ مُّنۢبَثًّۭا" — the Day's description: mountains crumbled into dust, scattered (the root of مبثوث).

### A battle-day
Arabic named its battles "days". The dictionary makes يوم the event that comes down (الكائنة), and makes القارعة the clash of swords between two fighters. The other words of the surah each attest a sense from the same battlefield. Horses are sent out in spread order. A place is chosen as a raid's target. The camp breaks up and leaves in haste. A feud flares among the tribe. A man defends his people in the fighting, hot with anger. Read this way, 101:4's "يوم يكون الناس" carries the dictionary's own phrase for an event that happens among people.
- 101:1–3 ٱلْقَارِعَةُ — ق ر ع B002 — "المقارعة والقراع المضاربة بالسيف في الحرب" (ayn); "قريعك الذي يقارعك" (sihah) — two sides striking at one another.
- 101:4 يَوْمَ — ي و م B003 — "اليوم: الكون، الكائنة من الكون إذا نزلت أو حدثت" (ayn;tahdhib); "الأيام في معنى الوقائع" (tahdhib); "اليوم الشديد: يوم ذو أيام" (ayn;tahdhib) — the day is the battle/event itself, and the dictionary defines it with the root of يكون.
- 101:4 يَوْمَ — ي و م B004 [fixed expression] — "وذكرهم بأيام الله: بما نزل بعاد وثمود وغيرهم من العذاب، وبالعفو عن آخرين" (tahdhib) — God's "days" are the strokes that fell on ʿĀd and Thamūd, the peoples who denied القارعة in 69:4.
- 101:4, 101:5 يَكُونُ / وَتَكُونُ — ك و ن B001 — "الكون الحدث يكون بين الناس" (ayn); "الكائنة الأمر الحادث" (ayn) — the event that occurs among people; ayn's phrase contains the surah's own "يكون … الناس".
- 101:4 ٱلْمَبْثُوثِ — ب ث ث B001 — "بثوا الخيل" (maqayis;tahdhib) — cavalry sent out spread for the raid.
- 101:3, 101:10 أَدْرَىٰكَ — د ر ي B002 [fixed expression] — "ادرى بنو فلان مكان كذا أي اعتمدوه بغزو أو غارة" (maqayis) — a place singled out as the target of a raid.
- 101:8 خَفَّتْ — خ ف ف B002 — "خف القوم إذا ارتحلوا مسرعين" (ayn;tahdhib) — the camp breaking up and leaving in haste.
- 101:11 نَارٌ — ن و ر B007 — "النائرة الكائنة تقع بين القوم" (ayn); "بينهم نائرة أي عداوة وشحناء" (sihah) — the feud that breaks out among a people; ayn joins the root of نار with الكائنة, the event word of يوم/يكون.
- 101:11 حَامِيَةٌ — ح م ي B002 — "حمى أهله في القتال حماية" (tahdhib) — the defender of his own in the fighting.
- 101:11 حَامِيَةٌ — ح م ي B003 — "حميت عليه غضبت" (sihah) — the heat of a fighter's anger.
- Quran: 69:4 (above): the two peoples that the dictionary's "أيام الله" names are the deniers of القارعة.

### The knock at the door and the question
"قرعت الباب": the knock at a door. The surah opens with the knock, then twice turns it into a question, as if the person inside asks what is knocking. No answer comes until the scene of 101:4–5. In 101:10 the same question form ("وما أدراك ما هيه") is put about هاوية, and 101:11 answers with one phrase: "نار حامية". The same word قارعة names the open court in front of a house, where a knock or a blow lands. It also names the rebuke that makes a man who heeds it turn back.
- 101:1 ٱلْقَارِعَةُ — ق ر ع B001 — "قرعت الباب أقرعه قرعا" (sihah) — the knock at the door.
- 101:1–3 ٱلْقَارِعَةُ — ق ر ع B010 — "قارعة الدار ساحتها وقارعة الطريق أعلاه" (sihah) — the court before the house: the word names the place where the knock lands.
- 101:2–3 مَا ٱلْقَارِعَةُ (text) — the one knocked upon asks "what?", twice.
- 101:3, 101:10 أَدْرَىٰكَ — د ر ي B001 — "دريته ودريت به أي علمت به وأدريته أي أعلمته" (sihah); "الدراية المعرفة المدركة بضرب من الحيل" (mufradat) — knowing and being made to know; a knowing reached by effort, and here only given from outside.
- 101:1–3 ٱلْقَارِعَةُ — ق ر ع B006 — "أقرعت إلى الحق إقراعا رجعت" (maqayis); "فلان لا يقرع أي لا يرتدع" (tahdhib); "التقريع التعنيف" (sihah) — the knock as a warning that turns back the one who heeds it, and as rebuke of the one who will not.
- 101:4 ٱلْمَبْثُوثِ — ب ث ث B002 — "بث الخبر وأبثه؛ نشره؛ أبثثتك سري؛ أظهرته لك" (sihah) — news spread, a hidden thing laid open: the answer to the question comes as a scene of spreading.
- 101:10 مَا هِيَهْ (text) — the question asked again, now of the pit; answered at once by 101:11.
- Quran: 104:5–6 "وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلْحُطَمَةُ نَارُ ٱللَّهِ ٱلْمُوقَدَةُ" — God's warning to the slanderer, the scene opening at 104:4 "كَلَّا ۖ لَيُنۢبَذَنَّ فِى ٱلْحُطَمَةِ": the same question answered by a fire, as in 101:10–11. 74:27 "وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا سَقَرُ", answered 74:28 "لَا تُبْقِى وَلَا تَذَرُ". 69:1–3 (from memory, see above). 82:17–19 (from memory): "وما أدراك ما يوم الدين ثم ما أدراك ما يوم الدين يوم لا تملك نفس لنفس شيئا". 13:31 "قَارِعَةٌ أَوْ تَحُلُّ قَرِيبًۭا مِّن دَارِهِمْ" — the striking thing lands near their houses.

### Moths falling into the lamp
In the dictionary the moth of 101:4 is the insect that flies looking for light and falls into the lamp or the fire. The surah's last word is نار. Light and fire share a root, which maqayis explains by brightness and by quick, unsteady movement, the same motion as fluttering. الناس stands on the root of آنس, the verb for catching sight of a fire far off. In 101:9 أم adds a sense of making for a destination. هاوية adds falling into a hollow one after another, as moths drop into a flame. One scene: the light, the swarm, the course toward it, the fall, the heat.
- 101:4 كَٱلْفَرَاشِ — ف ر ش B005 — "الفراش التي تطير طالبة للضوء" (ayn); "الفراشة التي تطير وتهافت في السراج" (sihah); "الفراش ما تراه كصغار البق يتهافت في النار" (tahdhib) — the swarm seeking light and dropping into lamp and fire.
- 101:4 كَٱلْفَرَاشِ — ف ر ش B006 — "تفرش الطائر إذا قرب من الأرض ورفرف بجناحه" (maqayis) — wings beating low, the flutter.
- 101:4 ٱلْمَبْثُوثِ — ب ث ث B001 — "كالفراش المبثوث" (jamhara;mufradat); "انبث الجراد" (jamhara) — the swarm spread out, and the same verb for locusts.
- 101:4 ٱلنَّاسُ — ء ن س B002 — "آنس من جانب يعني أبصر نارا" (tahdhib); "فإن آنستم منهم رشدا أي أبصرتم وآنست نارا" (mufradat) — catching sight of a fire; the dictionary pairs this root with نار.
- 101:9 فَأُمُّهُۥ — ء م م B012 — "أم يؤم أما إذا قصد للشيء" (jamhara); "الأم القصد المستقيم وهو التوجه نحو مقصود" (mufradat) — heading straight for an aim.
- 101:9 هَاوِيَةٌ — ه و ي B002 — "تهاوى القوم في المهواة سقط بعضهم في إثر بعض" (maqayis) — falling into the hollow one after another, as moths drop one after another into the lamp (تهافت).
- 101:11 نَارٌ — ن و ر B002 — "النور والنار سميا بذلك من طريقة الإضاءة ولأن ذلك يكون مضطربا سريع الحركة" (maqayis) — fire as the light that draws, and as quick unsteady flicker.
- 101:11 نَارٌ — ن و ر B003 [fixed expression] — "تنورت نارا قصدت إليها" (ayn); "تنورت النار من بعيد: تبصرتها" (sihah) — making for a fire seen from far off.
- 101:11 نَارٌ — ن و ر B006 — "نرت من الشئ" (sihah); "النوار النفار" (maqayis) — the same root names shying away and fleeing: the reversal, what a creature ought to do at the fire.
- 101:11 حَامِيَةٌ — ح م ي B001 — "حمي الشيء يحمى حميا إذا سخن" (ayn) — what the swarm reaches is heat.
- Quran: 20:10 "إِذْ رَءَا نَارًۭا فَقَالَ لِأَهْلِهِ ٱمْكُثُوٓا۟ إِنِّىٓ ءَانَسْتُ نَارًۭا لَّعَلِّىٓ ءَاتِيكُم مِّنْهَا بِقَبَسٍ أَوْ أَجِدُ عَلَى ٱلنَّارِ هُدًۭى" — Moses travelling with his family sees a fire and goes toward it for light and guidance: a fire approached and found good, the reversal of the moth's fire. 28:29 (from memory) "آنس من جانب الطور نارا", the same scene. 54:7 "كَأَنَّهُمْ جَرَادٌۭ مُّنتَشِرٌۭ" — people coming out of their graves like spreading locusts, God describing the Day (scene opens 54:6, from memory: "يوم يدع الداع إلى شيء نكر"). From memory, hadith not Quran: the Prophet's parable of a man who kindled a fire, the moths and insects falling into it, and himself holding people back from it by their waist-wraps (Bukhārī, Muslim).

### The decoy, the tamed game and the swoop
د ر ي: the hunter hides behind a camel he has set up so that the game grows used to it (يأنس), and moves in unseen. ء ن س gives the opposite pair: the tame animal that does not shy, and the wild one looking round when something alarms it. Hounds are loosed (بث), and the eagle stoops on its prey (أهوى). The surah's "وما أدراك" stands on the root of stalking unseen, and الناس stands on the root of the game's tameness and its alarm.
- 101:3, 101:10 أَدْرَىٰكَ — د ر ي B003 — "الدرية للناقة التي ينصبها الصائد ليأنس بها الصيد" (mufradat); "تدريت الصيد إذا نظرت أين هو ولم تره بعد ودريته ختلته" (maqayis) — the decoy and the hidden stalk; the first phrase joins د ر ي with the root of الناس.
- 101:3, 101:10 أَدْرَىٰكَ — د ر ي B005 — "الدريئة الحلقة التي يتعلم عليها الطعن" (maqayis) — the ring set up as a target.
- 101:4 ٱلنَّاسُ — ء ن س B003 — "الأنس أنس الإنسان بالشيء إذا لم يستوحش منه" (maqayis); "كلب أنوس نقيض العقور" (tahdhib) — the tame, unalarmed state the decoy produces.
- 101:4 ٱلنَّاسُ — ء ن س B002 — "الاستئناس النظر وأحس بما رابه" (tahdhib) — the alarmed look round when something stirs.
- 101:4 ٱلْمَبْثُوثِ — ب ث ث B001 — "بث الصياد كلابه" (maqayis) — the hounds loosed on the game.
- 101:9 هَاوِيَةٌ — ه و ي B003 — "هوت العقاب إذا انقضت فإذا أراغته قيل أهوت له إهواء" (tahdhib) — the eagle's stoop on the prey it has marked.
- Quran: 22:31 "فَكَأَنَّمَا خَرَّ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ فَتَخْطَفُهُ ٱلطَّيْرُ أَوْ تَهْوِى بِهِ ٱلرِّيحُ فِى مَكَانٍۢ سَحِيقٍۢ" — God's simile for the one who associates partners: falling from the sky, snatched by birds or flung by the wind.

### Mountains carded into wool
The mountain is mass gathered upward, a peg of the earth, thick, coarse and dry. Its root also names cloth that is tightly spun and woven. The wool is dyed in colours, and mountains are of colours (35:27). It is pressed and plucked until each fibre parts from the next, and it ends spread out, slack and hollow inside. The image goes from gathered to parted, compact to airy, made to unmade.
- 101:5 ٱلْجِبَالُ — ج ب ل B001 — "تجمع الشيء في ارتفاع" (maqayis); "اسم لكل وتد من أوتاد الأرض إذا عظم وطال" (ayn;tahdhib) — mass gathered high and driven in.
- 101:5 ٱلْجِبَالُ — ج ب ل B003 — "شيء جبل غليظ جاف" (sihah) — thick, hard, dry.
- 101:5 ٱلْجِبَالُ — ج ب ل B007 [fixed expression] — "الثوب الجيد النسج والغزل والفتل جيد الجبلة" (ayn;tahdhib) — the mountain's root names good spinning, twisting and weaving, the work that carding undoes.
- 101:1–3 ٱلْقَارِعَةُ — ق ر ع B011 — "القراع الصلب الشديد" (sihah); "مكان أقرع شديد صلب" (tahdhib) — hard, unyielding ground: the state before the blow.
- 101:5 كَٱلْعِهْنِ — ع ه ن B003 — "العهن الصوف المصبوغ ألوانا وجمعه عهون" (tahdhib); "تخصيص العهن لما فيه من اللون" (mufradat) — dyed wool of many colours.
- 101:5 ٱلْمَنفُوشِ — ن ف ش B001 — "النفش مدك الصوف حتى ينتفش بعضه عن بعض" (ayn;tahdhib); "نفشت القطن والصوف وعهن منفوش" (sihah); "النفش نشر الصوف كالعهن المنفوش" (mufradat) — fibre pressed until it parts strand from strand; the dictionary itself joins عهن and منفوش.
- 101:5 ٱلْمَنفُوشِ — ن ف ش B002 — "كل شيء تراه منتشرا رخو الجوف فهو منتفش" (ayn;tahdhib) — spread, slack, hollow within.
- Quran: 70:8–9 "يَوْمَ تَكُونُ ٱلسَّمَآءُ كَٱلْمُهْلِ وَتَكُونُ ٱلْجِبَالُ كَٱلْعِهْنِ" — God answering the one who asked for the punishment (scene opens 70:1, from memory). 35:27 "وَمِنَ ٱلْجِبَالِ جُدَدٌۢ بِيضٌۭ وَحُمْرٌۭ مُّخْتَلِفٌ أَلْوَٰنُهَا وَغَرَابِيبُ سُودٌۭ" — God showing signs in creation: mountains of colours, which commentators connect with the dyed wool (from memory). 56:5–6 "وَبُسَّتِ ٱلْجِبَالُ بَسًّۭا فَكَانَتْ هَبَآءًۭ مُّنۢبَثًّۭا". 20:105–107 "وَيَسْـَٔلُونَكَ عَنِ ٱلْجِبَالِ فَقُلْ يَنسِفُهَا رَبِّى نَسْفًۭا فَيَذَرُهَا قَاعًۭا صَفْصَفًۭا لَّا تَرَىٰ فِيهَا عِوَجًۭا وَلَآ أَمْتًۭا" — people ask the Prophet about the mountains; God tells him the answer. 27:88 "وَتَرَى ٱلْجِبَالَ تَحْسَبُهَا جَامِدَةًۭ وَهِىَ تَمُرُّ مَرَّ ٱلسَّحَابِ". 78:20 (from memory) "وسيرت الجبال فكانت سرابا".

### The spread ground and its pegs
فرش is laying out and levelling. The earth was made a فراش, a floor spread for living on, and the mountains are its pegs. Creatures were spread (بث) over it, and carpets are spread on a floor. On the Day the surah's words for laying out hold their undoing. The spread thing has become a swarm of moths (فراش مبثوث), and the pegs have become loose wool.
- 101:4 كَٱلْفَرَاشِ — ف ر ش B001 — "أصل صحيح يدل على تمهيد الشيء وبسطه" (maqayis); "جعل لكم الأرض فراشا أي ذللها" (mufradat) — the ground laid smooth and made tractable.
- 101:4 كَٱلْفَرَاشِ — ف ر ش B002 — "الفرش المفروش من متاع البيت" (sihah); "الفراش البيت؛ الفراش عش الطائر" (tahdhib) — the household's spread bedding, the house, the nest.
- 101:4 ٱلْمَبْثُوثِ — ب ث ث B001 — "خلق الخلق وبثهم في الأرض" (maqayis); "وزرابي مبثوثة" (maqayis); "بثت البسط" (tahdhib) — creatures spread over the earth, carpets spread over the floor.
- 101:5 ٱلْجِبَالُ — ج ب ل B001 — "اسم لكل وتد من أوتاد الأرض إذا عظم وطال" (ayn;tahdhib) — the tent pegs that hold the spread ground.
- 101:5 ٱلْمَنفُوشِ — ن ف ش B001 — "النفش نشر الصوف كالعهن المنفوش" (mufradat) — spreading again, but as loosened fibre: the peg has turned into something spread.
- Quran: 2:22 "ٱلَّذِى جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلْأَرْضَ فِرَٰشًۭا" — God addressing mankind. 51:48 "وَٱلْأَرْضَ فَرَشْنَٰهَا فَنِعْمَ ٱلْمَٰهِدُونَ". 78:6–7 "أَلَمْ نَجْعَلِ ٱلْأَرْضَ مِهَٰدًۭا وَٱلْجِبَالَ أَوْتَادًۭا" — God's questions before describing the Day. 88:19–20 (from memory) "وإلى الجبال كيف نصبت وإلى الأرض كيف سطحت". 2:164 "وَبَثَّ فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ دَآبَّةٍۢ"; 4:1 "وَبَثَّ مِنْهُمَا رِجَالًۭا كَثِيرًۭا وَنِسَآءًۭ" — the first spreading of creatures and of people. 84:3 "وَإِذَا ٱلْأَرْضُ مُدَّتْ" — the earth stretched on the Day.

### Flocks loose at night, the pasture stripped
نفش: camels and sheep spreading over pasture at night with no herdsman. فرش: the young herd beasts, and the crop that spreads low over the ground. قرع: meadows stripped bare by grazing animals, and a courtyard empty of visitors. The surah's people scatter like an unshepherded flock, and the ground is left with nothing on it.
- 101:5 ٱلْمَنفُوشِ — ن ف ش B003 — "نفشت الإبل والغنم أي رعت ليلا بلا راع ولا يكون النفش إلا بالليل" (sihah); "نفشت الإبل ترددت وانتشرت بلا راع" (maqayis) — the herd loose in the dark without a herdsman.
- 101:4 كَٱلْفَرَاشِ — ف ر ش B004 — "الفرش صغار الإبل؛ حمولة وفرشا؛ يحتمل أن يكون مصدرا من فرشها الله أي بثها بثا" (sihah) — the young beasts of the herd; sihah glosses فرش by بث, the two words of 101:4.
- 101:4 كَٱلْفَرَاشِ — ف ر ش B008 — "الفرش الزرع إذا فرش" (sihah) — the low spread crop the herd grazes.
- 101:4 ٱلْمَبْثُوثِ — ب ث ث B001 — "كل شيء فرقته" (jamhara) — scattered.
- 101:1–3 ٱلْقَارِعَةُ — ق ر ع B008 — "أصبحت الرياض قرعا قد جردتها المواشي" (tahdhib); "أرض قرعة لا تنبت شيئا" (tahdhib); "قرع الفناء إذا خلا من الغاشية" (sihah) — meadows stripped by the herd, land that grows nothing, a court emptied of visitors.
- 101:9 هَاوِيَةٌ — ه و ي B006 — "مضى هوى من الليل أي هزيع منه" (sihah) — a watch of the night: the time when the herd goes loose.
- Quran: 21:78 "وَدَاوُۥدَ وَسُلَيْمَٰنَ إِذْ يَحْكُمَانِ فِى ٱلْحَرْثِ إِذْ نَفَشَتْ فِيهِ غَنَمُ ٱلْقَوْمِ" — God recounting David and Solomon judging the crop the flock grazed down at night. 6:142 "وَمِنَ ٱلْأَنْعَٰمِ حَمُولَةًۭ وَفَرْشًۭا". 81:4 "وَإِذَا ٱلْعِشَارُ عُطِّلَتْ" — on the Day the pregnant she-camels are left untended. 20:106 "فَيَذَرُهَا قَاعًۭا صَفْصَفًۭا" — the ground left bare.

### The scale: heavy pan, light pan
101:6 and 101:8 set two pans against each other. The heavy one tips down (رجحان), and the dictionary uses weight for worth: the precious thing, the honoured person, humans as one of "the two weights". The light one is light in amount and light in mind. Its owner is taken lightly, and "has no weight" means "has no worth". The moth of 101:4 is named for lightness, and a light-headed man is called فراشة. The fibre of 101:5 is hollow within. The mountains are the heaviest thing on earth, and they become that fibre. The verb of 101:9, هوى, covers both a swift drop and a swift rise. In a balance the light pan goes up. In 101:9 its owner goes down.
- 101:6 ثَقُلَتْ — ث ق ل B001 — "ثقل ثقلا فهو ثقيل والثقل رجحان الثقيل" (ayn;tahdhib); "ضد الخفة" (maqayis;sihah) — the pan that tips down.
- 101:6 ثَقُلَتْ — ث ق ل B004 — "المثقال وزن معلوم قدره ومثقال الشيء ميزانه من مثله" (ayn;tahdhib) — weight set against a matching weight.
- 101:6 ثَقُلَتْ — ث ق ل B005 — "كل شيء نفيس مصون ثقل" (tahdhib); "سمي الجن والإنس الثقلين" (maqayis;sihah;tahdhib) — weight as worth; humankind called one of the two weights.
- 101:6 ثَقُلَتْ — ث ق ل B002 — "أثقال الأرض كنوزها وأجساد بني آدم" (maqayis;sihah;mufradat) — the earth's loads: its buried treasure and human bodies.
- 101:6, 101:8 مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ — و ز ن B001 — "الوزن ثقل شيء بشيء مثله" (ayn;tahdhib) — weighing defined by ثقل: one thing against its like.
- 101:6, 101:8 مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ — و ز ن B002 — "الموازين واحدها ميزان وهو المثاقيل؛ الآلة التي يوزن بها الأشياء ميزان؛ الميزان العدل" (tahdhib); "الوزن يومئذ الحق فإشارة إلى العدل في محاسبة الناس" (mufradat); "بناء يدل على تعديل واستقامة" (maqayis) — the instrument, its weights, and justice in the reckoning.
- 101:6, 101:8 مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ — و ز ن B007 [fixed expression] — "ما لفلان عندنا وزن أي قدر لخسته؛ فلا نقيم لهم يوم القيامة وزنا" (tahdhib) — to have no weight is to have no worth.
- 101:6, 101:8 مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ — و ز ن B004 [fixed expression] — "قام ميزان النهار إذا انتصف النهار" (maqayis;mufradat) — the day has its own balance point at noon (the phrase says النهار, not يوم).
- 101:8 خَفَّتْ — خ ف ف B001 — "الخفة خفة الوزن وخفة الحال" (ayn;tahdhib); "التخفيف ضد التثقيل" (sihah) — light weight, light condition.
- 101:8 خَفَّتْ — خ ف ف B003 — "فمن خفت موازينه إشارة إلى كثرة الأعمال الصالحة وقلتها" (mufradat) — light means few good deeds.
- 101:8 خَفَّتْ — خ ف ف B004 — "وخفة الرجل طيشه" (ayn;tahdhib); "لا يستخفنك أي لا يزعجنك ويزيلنك عن اعتقادك" (mufradat); "استخفه فلان إذا استجهله فحمله على اتباعه في غيه" (tahdhib) — the light-headed man, unsettled and easily led.
- 101:8 خَفَّتْ — خ ف ف B005 — "استخف به أهانه" (sihah) — being held light, despised.
- 101:4 كَٱلْفَرَاشِ — ف ر ش B005 — "الفراش هذا الذي يطير وسمي بذلك لخفته؛ الفراشة الرجل الخفيف" (maqayis); "يقال للخفيف من الرجال فراشة" (ayn); "أطيش من فراشة" (sihah) — the moth named for its lightness; the dictionary joins فراش with خفيف and with طيش, the word used for خفة in B004.
- 101:5 ٱلْمَنفُوشِ — ن ف ش B002 — "كل شيء تراه منتشرا رخو الجوف فهو منتفش" (ayn;tahdhib) — fibre that is mostly air.
- 101:5 ٱلْجِبَالُ — ج ب ل B003 — "ذو جبلة إذا كان غليظ الجسم" (jamhara;mufradat) — thick, massive body: the heaviest thing becomes the lightest.
- 101:9 هَاوِيَةٌ — ه و ي B002/B008 — "الهوي ذهاب في انحدار والهوي ذهاب في ارتفاع" (mufradat); "الهوي السريع إلى أسفل والهوي السريع إلى فوق" (tahdhib) — one verb for a swift drop and a swift rise: the movement of the pans, and of the man who goes down.
- Quran: 7:8–9 "وَٱلْوَزْنُ يَوْمَئِذٍ ٱلْحَقُّ ۚ فَمَن ثَقُلَتْ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ فَأُو۟لَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلْمُفْلِحُونَ وَمَنْ خَفَّتْ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ فَأُو۟لَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ خَسِرُوٓا۟ أَنفُسَهُم" — God stating the reckoning. 23:102–103 (same pair, ending "فِى جَهَنَّمَ خَٰلِدُونَ"). 21:47 "وَنَضَعُ ٱلْمَوَٰزِينَ ٱلْقِسْطَ لِيَوْمِ ٱلْقِيَٰمَةِ … وَإِن كَانَ مِثْقَالَ حَبَّةٍۢ مِّنْ خَرْدَلٍ أَتَيْنَا بِهَا". 18:105 "فَلَا نُقِيمُ لَهُمْ يَوْمَ ٱلْقِيَٰمَةِ وَزْنًۭا". 99:2 "وَأَخْرَجَتِ ٱلْأَرْضُ أَثْقَالَهَا" and 99:7–8 "مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ" — the Day in the surah before al-Qāriʿa's neighbour. 55:31 "سَنَفْرُغُ لَكُمْ أَيُّهَ ٱلثَّقَلَانِ". 55:7 "وَٱلسَّمَآءَ رَفَعَهَا وَوَضَعَ ٱلْمِيزَانَ". 43:54 "فَٱسْتَخَفَّ قَوْمَهُۥ فَأَطَاعُوهُ" — Pharaoh making his people light; 30:60 "وَلَا يَسْتَخِفَّنَّكَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُوقِنُونَ" — God to the Prophet. 56:1–3 "إِذَا وَقَعَتِ ٱلْوَاقِعَةُ … خَافِضَةٌۭ رَّافِعَةٌ" — the event that lowers and raises.

### Lots cast, shares sorted
The word القارعة also means casting lots to divide a shared thing among partners. After it, the surah splits people into two shares with "فأما … وأما". The split is made by setting things against each other on a scale, not by lot. Chance in dividing shares is reversed into measure.
- 101:1–3 ٱلْقَارِعَةُ — ق ر ع B004 — "أقرعت بين الشركاء في شيء يقتسمونه فاقترعوا عليه" (tahdhib); "الإقراع والمقارعة هي المساهمة" (maqayis) — the lot that divides a shared thing.
- 101:6, 101:8 فَأَمَّا … وَأَمَّا (text) — the two shares.
- 101:6, 101:8 مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ — و ز ن B003 — "وازنت بين الشيئين" (sihah) — setting two things against each other: the scale's way of dividing.
- 101:8 خَفَّتْ — خ ف ف B003 — "فمن خفت موازينه إشارة إلى كثرة الأعمال الصالحة وقلتها" (mufradat) — the share is fixed by much or little.
- Quran: 37:141 "فَسَاهَمَ فَكَانَ مِنَ ٱلْمُدْحَضِينَ" — Jonah on the laden ship casts lots, loses and is thrown into the sea (scene opens 37:139–140, from memory: "وإن يونس لمن المرسلين إذ أبق إلى الفلك المشحون"). 3:44 "إِذْ يُلْقُونَ أَقْلَٰمَهُمْ أَيُّهُمْ يَكْفُلُ مَرْيَمَ" — God telling the Prophet of the lot over Mary's guardianship. 30:14 "وَيَوْمَ تَقُومُ ٱلسَّاعَةُ يَوْمَئِذٍۢ يَتَفَرَّقُونَ"; 56:7 "وَكُنتُمْ أَزْوَٰجًۭا ثَلَٰثَةًۭ" — the Day's sorting.

### Mother: the one who takes him in
أم is the one who feeds and rears, and the thing that draws to itself whatever is near it. 101:9 gives the man of light scales a mother, and she is هاوية. The dictionary records the phrase "هوت أمه", his mother is bereaved, a way of saying he has perished, and it holds both words of the ayah. So the ayah carries two scenes in one. His mother is bereaved, because he is dead. And the pit is his mother, taking him in as a mother takes her child. Set against him is the man of 101:7, who has a life. On the Day the human mother herself lets go (22:2; 80:35).
- 101:9 فَأُمُّهُۥ — ء م م B001 — "فلانة تؤم فلانا أي تغذوه وتربيه" (maqayis) — the one who feeds and brings up.
- 101:9 فَأُمُّهُۥ — ء م م B002 — "كل شيء يضم إليه ما سواه مما يليه فإن العرب تسمى ذلك الشيء أما" (maqayis); "كل ما كان أصلا لوجود شيء أو تربيته أو إصلاحه أو مبدئه أم" (mufradat) — whatever gathers others to itself; origin and place of keeping.
- 101:9 هَاوِيَةٌ — ه و ي B002 — "هوت أمه فهي هاوية أي ثاكلة" (sihah) — the dictionary joins أم and هاوية: the bereaved mother.
- 101:9 هَاوِيَةٌ — ه و ي B002 — "هوى فلان أي مات" (ayn); "هاوية من أسماء جهنم والهاوية كل مهواة لا يدرك قعرها" (ayn) — to fall is to die; هاوية is a name of Hell and any bottomless drop.
- 101:7 عِيشَةٍ — ع ي ش B001 — "العيش الحياة" (maqayis;ayn;sihah) — the life the other man has; the reversal of the bereaved mother's dead son.
- From memory: Arabic uses "هوت أمه" as an imprecation; commentators cite it for this ayah.
- Quran: 79:39 "فَإِنَّ ٱلْجَحِيمَ هِىَ ٱلْمَأْوَىٰ" and 79:41 "فَإِنَّ ٱلْجَنَّةَ هِىَ ٱلْمَأْوَىٰ" — God dividing people on the Day, a shelter for each (79:40 "وَنَهَى ٱلنَّفْسَ عَنِ ٱلْهَوَىٰ", the same root as هاوية). 22:2 "يَوْمَ تَرَوْنَهَا تَذْهَلُ كُلُّ مُرْضِعَةٍ عَمَّآ أَرْضَعَتْ وَتَضَعُ كُلُّ ذَاتِ حَمْلٍ حَمْلَهَا" — the Hour's quake (scene opens 22:1 "يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ ٱتَّقُوا۟ رَبَّكُمْ ۚ إِنَّ زَلْزَلَةَ ٱلسَّاعَةِ شَىْءٌ عَظِيمٌۭ"). 80:33–35 "فَإِذَا جَآءَتِ ٱلصَّآخَّةُ يَوْمَ يَفِرُّ ٱلْمَرْءُ مِنْ أَخِيهِ وَأُمِّهِۦ وَأَبِيهِ" — the Deafening Blast, a name of the Day like القارعة.

### The satisfied life and the furnished house
101:7 gives one man a pleasing life. The dictionary pairs عيشة with راضية, and in the same list names the opposite, a straitened life (ضنك). Life (عيش) also means what one lives on and where one lives: food, drink, a dwelling. Other words of the surah supply the household. Bedding is spread, under the same word that the dictionary uses for the beds of Paradise. Carpets are spread. Food and drink are at hand, the settled stay of a home, and the mother's word also means plenty.
- 101:7 عِيشَةٍ — ع ي ش B001 — "عيشة صالحة وراضية وصدق وسوء وضنك" (maqayis;sihah;tahdhib;mufradat); "رجل عائش حاله حسنة" (maqayis;tahdhib) — the dictionary's own pairing of the two words of 101:7, and its opposite ضنك.
- 101:7 عِيشَةٍ — ع ي ش B002 — "المطعم والمشرب وما يكون به الحياة" (maqayis;ayn;tahdhib); "كل شيء يعاش به أو فيه فهو معاش" (maqayis;ayn) — food, drink, and the place lived in.
- 101:7 رَّاضِيَةٍ — ر ض و B001 — "أصل واحد يدل على خلاف السخط" (maqayis) — no displeasure left.
- 101:7 رَّاضِيَةٍ — ر ض و B002 — "الرضوان الرضا الكثير" (mufradat) — abundant satisfaction.
- 101:7 رَّاضِيَةٍ — ر ض و B003 — "المراضاة من اثنين" (ayn) — satisfaction on both sides: the life pleases and is pleased.
- 101:4 كَٱلْفَرَاشِ — ف ر ش B002 — "يقال للمفروش فرش وفراش؛ وفرش مرفوعة؛ فرش بطائنها من إستبرق" (mufradat) — spread bedding; the dictionary's examples are the beds of Paradise.
- 101:4 ٱلْمَبْثُوثِ — ب ث ث B001 — "وزرابي مبثوثة" (maqayis); "بثت البسط" (tahdhib) — carpets spread through the house.
- 101:5 كَٱلْعِهْنِ — ع ه ن B001 — "العاهن الطعام الحاضر والشراب الحاضر" (tahdhib); "عهن بالمكان أقام به" (sihah) — food and drink ready at hand; staying settled in a place.
- 101:9 فَأُمُّهُۥ — ء م م B010 — "الإمة النعمة" (ayn); "الأمة النعمة" (maqayis) — plenty, a good condition.
- Quran: 69:21–22 "فَهُوَ فِى عِيشَةٍۢ رَّاضِيَةٍۢ فِى جَنَّةٍ عَالِيَةٍۢ" — the identical phrase, describing the one handed his record in his right hand (scene opens 69:19 "فَأَمَّا مَنْ أُوتِىَ كِتَٰبَهُۥ بِيَمِينِهِۦ"). 88:8–9 "وُجُوهٌۭ يَوْمَئِذٍۢ نَّاعِمَةٌۭ لِّسَعْيِهَا رَاضِيَةٌۭ" and 88:15–16 "وَنَمَارِقُ مَصْفُوفَةٌۭ وَزَرَابِىُّ مَبْثُوثَةٌ" — the faces at ease, and their carpets spread. 89:28 "ٱرْجِعِىٓ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكِ رَاضِيَةًۭ مَّرْضِيَّةًۭ" — the satisfaction on both sides (scene opens 89:27, from memory: "يا أيتها النفس المطمئنة"). 20:124 "فَإِنَّ لَهُۥ مَعِيشَةًۭ ضَنكًۭا" — the life the dictionary names as the opposite.

### The pit
هاوية is any drop whose floor cannot be reached, a hollow dug deep. Its root is emptiness and falling together: the air between sky and earth, and an empty heart. حامية also names the heavy stones that line a well's shaft. The mountain's root has a fixed phrase for diggers who strike rock and must stop. The pit with no bottom reverses that: there is no rock to stop at, and the mountains have become wool. أم adds the place that gathers to itself.
- 101:9 هَاوِيَةٌ — ه و ي B002 — "الهاوية كل مهواة لا يدرك قعرها والهوة كل وهدة معمقة" (tahdhib); "هوى الشيء يهوي إذا خر من علو إلى سفل" (jamhara) — the bottomless drop, and the fall from high to low.
- 101:9 هَاوِيَةٌ — ه و ي B001 — "أصل صحيح يدل على خلو وسقوط" (maqayis); "الهَواء ما بين السماء والأرض وكل خال هواء" (sihah); "قلبه هَواء" (ayn); "هوى صدره أي خلا" (tahdhib) — emptiness and falling as one root; empty space; an empty heart.
- 101:11 حَامِيَةٌ — ح م ي B011 — "الحامية الحجارة يطوى بها البئر" (ayn;tahdhib); "الحوامي عظام الحجارة وثقالها" (tahdhib) — the great heavy stones lining a well's shaft.
- 101:5 ٱلْجِبَالُ — ج ب ل B005 [fixed expression] — "أجبل القوم إذا حفروا فبلغوا المكان الصلب" (sihah) — digging that stops at rock; the pit "لا يدرك قعرها" is its reversal.
- 101:9 فَأُمُّهُۥ — ء م م B002 — "كل شيء انضمت إليه أشياء فهو أم" (jamhara) — the place things are drawn into.
- Quran: 9:109 "أَم مَّنْ أَسَّسَ بُنْيَٰنَهُۥ عَلَىٰ شَفَا جُرُفٍ هَارٍۢ فَٱنْهَارَ بِهِۦ فِى نَارِ جَهَنَّمَ" — God contrasting two founders' buildings: a bank that gives way into the fire. 22:31 "تَهْوِى بِهِ ٱلرِّيحُ فِى مَكَانٍۢ سَحِيقٍۢ". 14:43 "وَأَفْـِٔدَتُهُمْ هَوَآءٌۭ" — the wrongdoers on the Day (scene opens 14:42, from memory: "إنما يؤخرهم ليوم تشخص فيه الأبصار"). 20:81 "وَمَن يَحْلِلْ عَلَيْهِ غَضَبِى فَقَدْ هَوَىٰ" — God to the Israelites. 53:53 "وَٱلْمُؤْتَفِكَةَ أَهْوَىٰ". 50:30 "يَوْمَ نَقُولُ لِجَهَنَّمَ هَلِ ٱمْتَلَأْتِ وَتَقُولُ هَلْ مِن مَّزِيدٍۢ" — the pit that is never filled. 104:8 "إِنَّهَا عَلَيْهِم مُّؤْصَدَةٌۭ" — closed over them (scene opens 104:4).

### Fire brought to full heat
نار is light that flickers. حامية is heating to full: iron heated in the fire, an oven at its hottest. The dictionary itself names fire as the source of such heat. The root also gives anger flaring up, and sharpness at its peak. Its other sense goes the opposite way: a guarded thing that no one may approach, which people keep away from.
- 101:11 نَارٌ — ن و ر B001 — "أصل صحيح يدل على إضاءة واضطراب وقلة ثبات" (maqayis) — brightness and restless movement.
- 101:11 نَارٌ — ن و ر B002 — "النار مؤنثة وهي من الواو؛ الجمع نور ونيران" (sihah) — the burning fire.
- 101:11 حَامِيَةٌ — ح م ي B001 — "أحميت الحديد في النار فهو محمى" (sihah); "حمى النهار وحمي التنور أي اشتد حره" (sihah); "الحامية الحارة" (ayn); "الحمي الحرارة المتولدة من الجواهر المحمية كالنار والشمس" (mufradat) — iron heated in fire, the oven at its fiercest; the dictionary joins حمي and نار.
- 101:11 حَامِيَةٌ — ح م ي B003 — "حميت عليه غضبت" (sihah); "عبر عن القوة الغضبية إذا ثارت وكثرت بالحمية" (mufradat) — rage rising.
- 101:11 حَامِيَةٌ — ح م ي B008 — "حميا الشيء حدته وشدته" (tahdhib) — a thing at its sharpest.
- 101:11 حَامِيَةٌ — ح م ي B002 — "هذا شيء حمى أي محظور لا يقرب" (sihah); "تحاماه الناس أي توقوه واجتنبوه" (sihah) — the guarded thing no one comes near; the second phrase has الناس keeping clear, the reverse of the moths of 101:4.
- Quran: 88:4 "تَصْلَىٰ نَارًا حَامِيَةًۭ" — the same two words, said of the downcast faces (scene opens 88:1 "هل أتاك حديث الغاشية", 88:2–3 "وجوه يومئذ خاشعة عاملة ناصبة", from memory). 9:35 "يَوْمَ يُحْمَىٰ عَلَيْهَا فِى نَارِ جَهَنَّمَ فَتُكْوَىٰ بِهَا جِبَاهُهُمْ وَجُنُوبُهُمْ وَظُهُورُهُمْ" — God to those who hoard gold and silver: the treasure heated in the fire. 67:8 "تَكَادُ تَمَيَّزُ مِنَ ٱلْغَيْظِ" — Hell nearly bursting with rage as groups are thrown in. 25:12 "إِذَا رَأَتْهُم مِّن مَّكَانٍۭ بَعِيدٍۢ سَمِعُوا۟ لَهَا تَغَيُّظًۭا وَزَفِيرًۭا". 104:6–7 "نَارُ ٱللَّهِ ٱلْمُوقَدَةُ" (104:7 from memory: "التي تطلع على الأفئدة").

### The blow to the head
A strike on the head breaks the thin plates of the skull, and the dictionary has the phrase "ضربة فأطار فراش رأسه", a blow that sent his skull-plates flying. The wound reaches the brain's casing, called "أم الرأس" or "أم الدماغ". A spear wound gapes open (هوت). The surah's words follow each other in this order: the blow (1), فراش (4), أم (9), هاوية (9).
- 101:1–3 ٱلْقَارِعَةُ — ق ر ع B001 — "كل شيء ضربته فقد قرعته" (ayn) — the strike.
- 101:4 كَٱلْفَرَاشِ — ف ر ش B010 — "فراش الرأس عظام رقاق تلي القحف" (sihah); "الفراشة كل عظم رقيق" (sihah) — the thin bones of the skull.
- 101:4 كَٱلْفَرَاشِ — ف ر ش B011 — "ضربة فأطار فراش رأسه" (tahdhib); "المفرشة الشجة التي تصدع العظم ولا تهشم" (sihah) — the blow that sends the skull plates flying; the head wound that cracks the bone.
- 101:9 فَأُمُّهُۥ — ء م م B003 — "أم رأسه بالعصا إذا أصاب أم رأسه وهي أم الدماغ" (jamhara); "أم الرأس وهو الدماغ والشجة الآمة التي تبلغ أم الدماغ" (maqayis) — struck on the brain's casing with a stick; the wound that reaches the brain.
- 101:9 هَاوِيَةٌ — ه و ي B007 [fixed expression] — "هوت الطعنة إذا فتحت فاها" (tahdhib) — the wound opening its mouth.
- From memory: early commentators (Qatāda and others) read فأمه as أم رأسه: he falls into the fire head first ("يهوي في النار على أم رأسه").

### The crowd thinned
الناس is a gathered human crowd, and the dictionary also uses جبل for a great crowd of people. On the Day the crowd is scattered like moths, its numbers fall off, and it breaks camp. The word for light scales (خفت) is the same word used for a crowd growing few.
- 101:4 ٱلنَّاسُ — ء ن س B001 — "الإنس جماعة الناس" (tahdhib) — the human crowd.
- 101:5 ٱلْجِبَالُ — ج ب ل B002 — "الجبل الناس الكثير" (tahdhib); "الجبل الجماعة العظيمة الكثيرة" (maqayis); "الجبل من الناس الجماعة" (jamhara;sihah) — the dictionary calls a great crowd of people a mountain.
- 101:4 يَكُونُ — ك و ن B001 — "الكون الحدث يكون بين الناس" (ayn) — the event among the crowd.
- 101:4 ٱلْمَبْثُوثِ — ب ث ث B001 — "كل شيء فرقته" (jamhara) — scattered.
- 101:8 خَفَّتْ — خ ف ف B003 — "خف القوم خفوفا أي قلوا وقد خفت زحمتهم" (sihah) — the people grow few, the crush eases.
- 101:8 خَفَّتْ — خ ف ف B002 — "خفوا عن منازلهم ارتحلوا منها في خفة" (mufradat) — they leave their dwellings light and quick.
- Quran: 99:6 "يَوْمَئِذٍۢ يَصْدُرُ ٱلنَّاسُ أَشْتَاتًۭا لِّيُرَوْا۟ أَعْمَٰلَهُمْ" — God on the Day of the earth's quake: people coming out in scattered groups to be shown their deeds, and 99:7–8 weighing them. 30:14 "يَوْمَئِذٍۢ يَتَفَرَّقُونَ". 22:2 "وَتَرَى ٱلنَّاسَ سُكَٰرَىٰ وَمَا هُم بِسُكَٰرَىٰ". 80:34–35 (above). 4:1 "وَبَثَّ مِنْهُمَا رِجَالًۭا كَثِيرًۭا وَنِسَآءًۭ" — the first spreading of the crowd from one soul.

## Interactions
- The blow / Mountains carded into wool — ن ف ش B001: "نفش الصوف وهو أن يطرق حتى يتنفش" (maqayis): carding is a beating, so the mountains' image is made by the blow of 101:1.
- The blow / The blow to the head — shared member ق ر ع B001; the dictionary phrase "ضربة فأطار فراش رأسه" (tahdhib) joins the blow with فراش and with flying.
- The blow / The knock at the door — same branch ق ر ع B001: "قرعت الباب" (sihah) beside "القرع ضرب شيء على شيء" (mufradat); one is the knuckle on a door, the other the full blow.
- Moths falling into the lamp / Fire brought to full heat — ف ر ش B005: "يتهافت في النار" (tahdhib), "تهافت في السراج" (sihah); the swarm's end is the fire of 101:11. ح م ي B002 "تحاماه الناس أي توقوه واجتنبوه" (sihah) is the reverse: people keeping clear of the guarded thing.
- Moths falling into the lamp / The scale — ف ر ش B005: "يقال للخفيف من الرجال فراشة" (ayn); "أطيش من فراشة" (sihah) with خ ف ف B004 "وخفة الرجل طيشه" (ayn;tahdhib): the moth people of 101:4 are the light of 101:8.
- Moths falling into the lamp / The pit — "تهاوى القوم في المهواة سقط بعضهم في إثر بعض" (maqayis) beside "تهافت في السراج" (sihah): falling one after another, into the hollow and into the lamp.
- Moths falling into the lamp / The decoy — ء ن س in both: "آنس من جانب يعني أبصر نارا" (tahdhib) and "الدرية للناقة التي ينصبها الصائد ليأنس بها الصيد" (mufradat); the light lures the swarm as the decoy lures the game.
- Mother / The pit — "هوت أمه فهي هاوية أي ثاكلة" (sihah) holds both words of 101:9; ء م م B002 "كل شيء انضمت إليه أشياء فهو أم" (jamhara) makes the pit the place that gathers him.
- Mother / The blow to the head — shared word فأمه: أم الرأس (ء م م B003); from memory, the commentators' "يهوي في النار على أم رأسه".
- Mother / The satisfied life — أم B001 "تغذوه وتربيه" against "العيش الحياة"; 79:39 and 79:41 give each side its own "مأوى".
- The pit / Fire brought to full heat — حامية is both well-lining stone (ح م ي B011) and heat (ح م ي B001); 9:109 stages a bank giving way into "نار جهنم".
- The pit / The scale — "الحوامي عظام الحجارة وثقالها" (tahdhib): the pit's lining is heavy stone, while the man in it is the light one.
- The pit / Mountains carded into wool — "أجبل القوم إذا حفروا فبلغوا المكان الصلب" (sihah) [fixed expression]: rock that stops digging, against "لا يدرك قعرها"; ن ف ش B002 "رخو الجوف" and ه و ي B001 "كل خال هواء" (sihah) both name hollowness.
- The scale / Mountains carded into wool — و ز ن B003: "هو وزن الجبل أي ناحية منه؛ هو زنة الجبل أي حذاءه" (sihah): the dictionary sets وزن and جبل in one phrase (here meaning "facing" or "level with" the mountain). The heaviest mass, set against the scale, has become fibre.
- The scale / Fire brought to full heat — ث ق ل B002 "أثقال الأرض كنوزها" with 9:35, where hoarded treasure is heated "فِى نَارِ جَهَنَّمَ".
- The scale / The crowd thinned — خ ف ف B003 covers both a crowd growing few (sihah) and few good deeds (mufradat); 99:6–8 stages the scattered crowd and the weighing together.
- The scale / A battle-day — و ز ن B004 "قام ميزان النهار" [fixed expression] and ي و م: the day has its own balance point.
- The spread ground / Flocks loose at night — "يحتمل أن يكون مصدرا من فرشها الله أي بثها بثا" (sihah): فرش glossed by بث, the two words of 101:4.
- The spread ground / The satisfied life — the same spreading words furnish Paradise: "وفرش مرفوعة" (mufradat under ف ر ش B002), "وزرابي مبثوثة" (maqayis under ب ث ث); 88:15–16.
- A battle-day / The crowd thinned — "الكون الحدث يكون بين الناس" (ayn): the event happening among the crowd.
- A battle-day / Fire brought to full heat — "النائرة الكائنة تقع بين القوم" (ayn); ح م ي B002 and B003 (defending, anger).
- A battle-day / The blow — يوم B004's "بما نزل بعاد وثمود" (tahdhib) and 69:4 "كَذَّبَتْ ثَمُودُ وَعَادٌۢ بِٱلْقَارِعَةِ".
- The knock at the door / Fire brought to full heat — the question-answer of 101:10–11, staged again in 104:5–6.
- Flocks loose at night / Mountains carded into wool — the one word ٱلْمَنفُوشِ: wool plucked apart (ن ف ش B001) and herds spread by night (ن ف ش B003).
- Lots cast / The scale — ق ر ع B004 against و ز ن B003 "وازنت بين الشيئين" (sihah); the two-way split of 101:6–9.

## Ayat

**101:1 ٱلْقَارِعَةُ**
- The blow — the blow itself, and its name as a calamity coming down — scene: a hard blow lands; what was compact flies apart (people as scattered moths, mountains as wool beaten loose), and the light one is flung down into the pit.
- A battle-day — the clash of swords between two sides — scene: a battle-day: swords clash, horses are sent out spread, the event falls on the people, the camp breaks up, feud and the defenders' anger flare like fire.
- The knock at the door — the knock; the court in front of the house; the warning that turns back the one who heeds it — scene: a knock at the door, asked about three times and answered by a scene; a second question ("ما هيه") answered: hot fire.
- Mountains carded into wool — hard, unyielding ground (ق ر ع B011) — scene: mountains, mass gathered high, thick and tightly made, are beaten and plucked into dyed wool, fibres parted, slack and hollow.
- Flocks loose at night — meadows stripped bare, a court emptied — scene: young beasts loose at night with no herdsman spread over the crop and leave the meadow bare.
- Lots cast — casting lots to divide a shared thing — scene: no lot is cast; the Day sorts people into two shares by weight.
- The blow to the head — the strike — scene: a blow sends the thin skull plates flying, reaches the brain's casing, and the wound gapes; he falls head first.

**101:2 مَا ٱلْقَارِعَةُ**
- The knock at the door — the first question from inside — scene: as at 101:1.
- The blow; A battle-day — the name repeated, kept unexplained — scenes: as at 101:1.

**101:3 وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلْقَارِعَةُ**
- The knock at the door — أدراك: being made to know what one cannot reach alone; the third repetition of the knock — scene: as at 101:1.
- The decoy, the tamed game and the swoop — أدراك on the root of the decoy and the hidden stalk — scene: a hunter stalks behind a decoy so the game stays tame; hounds are loosed, the eagle stoops, and the game learns too late.
- A battle-day — أدراك, a place chosen as a raid's target [fixed expression] — scene: as at 101:1.

**101:4 يَوْمَ يَكُونُ ٱلنَّاسُ كَٱلْفَرَاشِ ٱلْمَبْثُوثِ**
- A battle-day — يوم as the battle/event, defined in the dictionary by the root of يكون; يكون as the event among people; مبثوث as horses sent out spread — scene: as at 101:1.
- Moths falling into the lamp — فراش, the insect that seeks light and drops into the lamp; مبثوث, the swarm spread out; الناس, the root of catching sight of a fire — scene: moths scattered, seeking light, make for a fire seen far off and fall one after another into the hollow where it burns hot.
- The scale — فراش named for lightness; a light-headed man is called فراشة; humankind is one of "the two weights" — scene: the heavy pan tips down (weight is worth); the light pan (moth-light, light-headed, of no weight) rises, and its owner goes down.
- The spread ground and its pegs — فراش as the spread floor and bedding; مبثوث as creatures and carpets spread — scene: the earth laid out like a bed and pinned by mountain pegs, creatures spread on it; on the Day the spread thing is a moth swarm and the pegs are loose wool.
- Flocks loose at night — فرش as young herd beasts and spread crop, glossed by بث; مبثوث, scattered — scene: as at 101:1.
- The satisfied life — فراش as the beds of Paradise; مبثوث as spread carpets — scene: a pleasing life in a furnished house: bedding and carpets spread, food and drink at hand, plenty.
- The blow to the head — فراش as the thin skull plates, sent flying by the blow — scene: as at 101:1.
- The decoy — الناس as the tame and the alarmed game; مبثوث as hounds loosed — scene: as at 101:3.
- The blow — مبثوث, flying apart like stirred dust — scene: as at 101:1.
- The knock at the door — مبثوث, news spread, a hidden thing laid open — scene: as at 101:1.
- The crowd thinned — الناس, the gathered crowd; يكون, the event among it; مبثوث, scattered — scene: a great crowd ("a mountain" of people) spreads apart on the Day and grows few.

**101:5 وَتَكُونُ ٱلْجِبَالُ كَٱلْعِهْنِ ٱلْمَنفُوشِ**
- Mountains carded into wool — الجبال, mass gathered high, thick, the root of tight weaving; العهن, dyed wool; المنفوش, fibres parted, slack and hollow — scene: as at 101:1.
- The blow — المنفوش, wool beaten until it loosens; العهن, a rod cracked but not severed — scene: as at 101:1.
- The spread ground — الجبال as the earth's pegs; المنفوش, spread as loosened fibre — scene: as at 101:4.
- Flocks loose at night — المنفوش, herds spread over pasture at night without a herdsman — scene: as at 101:1.
- The scale — الجبال, massive bodies; المنفوش, airy fibre: the heaviest made lightest — scene: as at 101:4.
- The pit — الجبال, rock that stops diggers [fixed expression], reversed by the bottomless pit — scene: a bottomless hollow of empty air, lined with heavy stones, that one falls into from a height.
- The satisfied life — العهن as food and drink at hand, a settled stay — scene: as at 101:4.
- The crowd thinned — الجبال as a great crowd of people — scene: as at 101:4.
- A battle-day — تكون, the event befalling — scene: as at 101:1.

**101:6 فَأَمَّا مَن ثَقُلَتْ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ**
- The scale — ثقلت, the pan tipping down, weight as worth; موازين, the instrument, its weights, justice in the reckoning — scene: as at 101:4.
- Lots cast — فأما opens the first share; موازين sets two things against each other — scene: as at 101:1.

**101:7 فَهُوَ فِى عِيشَةٍۢ رَّاضِيَةٍۢ**
- The satisfied life — عيشة, life and what it is lived on and in, paired in the dictionary with راضية; راضية, no displeasure left, satisfaction on both sides — scene: as at 101:4.
- Mother — the life that stands against the bereaved mother's dead son — scene: the mother feeds and gathers her child; for the light one, his mother is bereaved, and the pit takes him in instead.

**101:8 وَأَمَّا مَنْ خَفَّتْ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ**
- The scale — خفت, light weight, few deeds, a light head, held of no account — scene: as at 101:4.
- Lots cast — وأما opens the second share — scene: as at 101:1.
- The crowd thinned — خفت, the crowd grown few, leaving light and quick — scene: as at 101:4.
- A battle-day — خفت, the camp breaking up in haste — scene: as at 101:1.

**101:9 فَأُمُّهُۥ هَاوِيَةٌۭ**
- Mother — أم, the one who feeds and gathers; "هوت أمه": the bereaved mother — scene: as at 101:7.
- The pit — هاوية, the bottomless drop, emptiness and falling; أم, the place that gathers — scene: as at 101:5.
- Moths falling into the lamp — أم, heading for an aim; هاوية, falling one after another into the hollow — scene: as at 101:4.
- The scale — هوى, a swift drop and a swift rise, the movement of the pans — scene: as at 101:4.
- The blow — هوى, the sword-arm's swing and the throw from above — scene: as at 101:1.
- The decoy — هوى, the eagle's stoop on its prey — scene: as at 101:3.
- The blow to the head — أم, the brain's casing; هاوية, the wound gaping [fixed expression] — scene: as at 101:1.
- The satisfied life — أم as plenty (الإمة النعمة), the reverse of the pit — scene: as at 101:4.
- Flocks loose at night — هوى, a watch of the night — scene: as at 101:1.

**101:10 وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا هِيَهْ**
- The knock at the door — the question form returns, now about the pit, and is answered at once — scene: as at 101:1.
- The decoy — أدراك, the hidden stalk — scene: as at 101:3.
- A battle-day — أدراك, the raid's chosen target [fixed expression] — scene: as at 101:1.

**101:11 نَارٌ حَامِيَةٌۢ**
- Fire brought to full heat — نار, flickering light; حامية, iron heated in fire, the oven at its fiercest, rage, sharpness; also the guarded thing no one approaches — scene: fire as light and flicker, heated to the full like iron in a furnace, raging, a thing people keep clear of.
- Moths falling into the lamp — نار, the light the swarm makes for, a fire seen far off; حامية, the heat it reaches — scene: as at 101:4.
- The pit — حامية, the heavy stones that line a well — scene: as at 101:5.
- A battle-day — نار, feud among a people; حامية, the defender and his anger — scene: as at 101:1.
- The knock at the door — the answer to "ما هيه" — scene: as at 101:1.
