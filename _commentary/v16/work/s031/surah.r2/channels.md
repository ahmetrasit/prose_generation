# s031 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. P1: Guidance as Assembly and Cultivation
- Semantic invariant: Guidance becomes durable by joining, restraining, maintaining, and cultivating rather than by merely transmitting information.
- Surface relation: direct; 31:2-5, anchored in ٱلْكِتَٰبِ, ٱلْحَكِيمِ, هُدًى, يُقِيمُ, صَّلَوٰةَ, يُؤْتُ, زَّكَوٰةَ, and مُفْلِحُونَ.
- Surprising reach: The written guide behaves both like stitched material under restraint and like cultivated ground whose yield appears through sustained care.

#### Subchannel A. Braced Text Leads the Route
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A written object is joined and made firm, then functions as a gentle guide and a leading edge on a route.
- Active motifs: joining or stitching material `quranic:root_001283:B001/m01`; written text `quranic:root_001283:B002/m01`; restraint for correction `quranic:root_000348:B001/m01`; secure workmanship `quranic:root_000348:B004/m01`; gentle route guidance `quranic:root_001583:B001/m01`; leading vanguard `quranic:root_001583:B003/m01`.
- Ayah anchors: 31:2 كِتَٰبِ (ك ت ب) and حَكِيمِ (ح ك م); 31:3 and 31:5 هُدًى (ه د ي).
- Synthesis: The book is not only writing: its root imagery joins separate pieces, while wisdom restrains disorder and brings the construction to firmness. Guidance then extends that fitted object into motion, both indicating the road gently and taking the forward position that lets others follow.

- Active motifs: sustained care and maintenance `quranic:root_001273:B004/m01`; established worship `quranic:root_000879:B003/m01`; giving or delivery `quranic:root_000009:B002/m01`; vegetative increase `quranic:root_000637:B001/m01`; purification and soundness `quranic:root_000637:B002/m01`; cleaving ground open `quranic:root_001175:B001/m01`; enduring success `quranic:root_001175:B005/m01`.

Purification carries the parallel image of plant increase, while success reaches back to splitting resistant ground; the moral outcome is figured as a field opened, tended, and kept in productive condition.

### 2. P1: Discourse as Route Steering

- Surface relation: direct; 31:6-7, anchored in يَشْتَرِى, حَدِيثِ, لَهْوَ, سَبِيلِ, يُضِلَّ, تُتْلَىٰ, يَسْمَعْ, أُذُنَيْ, وَلَّىٰ, and بَشِّرْ.

### S20. P1-P2-P3: Capture Is Not Chosen Surrender
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A person is seized and made captive, then stands as a prisoner or refuge-seeker under the pledge and protection of a relied-upon chief.

- Synthesis: Capture and protected surrender share an external transfer of control but reverse the agent's role. One begins in a captor's grip; the other seeks a pledge and a dependable protector. ### S21. P1-P2: A Written Freedom Contract Is Paid in Installments

### S22. Whole Surah: An Aimed Arrow Divides into Hit and Miss
