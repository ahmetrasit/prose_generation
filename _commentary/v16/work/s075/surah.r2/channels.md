# s075 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. P1: Public Determination of Liability
- Semantic invariant: A disputed or hidden condition becomes answerable by oath, exact apportionment, reckoning, and an established record.
- Surface relation: direct; 75:1-4 anchors oath (`أُقْسِمُ`), reckoning (`يَحْسَبُ`), and capacity (`قَادِرِينَ`), while 75:12-15 anchor settlement, report, and excuses.
- Surprising reach: The opening oath expands into a procedural scene in which shares, measures, admissions, and defective pleas make liability publicly decidable.

#### Subchannel A. Sworn shares
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Claimants divide a matter into shares and secure the division by distributed oaths.
- Active motifs: allotted share (`quranic:root_001226:B003/m01`); oath apportioned among its bearers (`quranic:root_001226:B004/m01`); oath offered as a test or reassurance (`quranic:root_000154:B005/m01`); oath and swearing (`quranic:root_000076:B007/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 75:1-2 `أُقْسِمُ` (ق س م); surface anchors unavailable for ب ل ي and ء ل ي.
- Synthesis: The oath is not merely emphatic speech: its lexical construction distributes responsibility among parties, tests the hearer, and fixes each party's share.

#### Subchannel B. Counting to an exact proportion
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A quantity is counted, valued, planned, and brought to its fitting measure.
- Active motifs: counting and accounting (`quranic:root_000318:B001/m01`); bounded amount (`quranic:root_001205:B001/m01`); measured planning and preparation (`quranic:root_001205:B005/m01`); valuation (`quranic:root_001273:B010/m01`); equal weight (`quranic:root_001273:B015/m01`); equivalence between two things (`quranic:root_000766:B001/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 75:3 `يَحْسَبُ` (ح س ب); 75:4 `قَادِرِينَ`, `نُّسَوِّىَ` (ق د ر، س و ي); 75:1,6 `ٱلْقِيَٰمَةِ` (ق و م).
- Synthesis: Reckoning becomes a calibration scene: the object is counted, assigned a limit and value, and adjusted until its parts answer to an exact standard.

#### Subchannel C. Trial, admission, and recorded finding
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Examination reveals a condition, elicits an admission, and turns it into a delivered report despite a deficient plea.
- Active motifs: trial that manifests a person's state (`quranic:root_000154:B002/m01`); practical inspection and accountability (`quranic:root_000318:B006/m01`); established admission (`quranic:root_001215:B007/m01`); consequential report (`quranic:root_001464:B002/m01`); excuse pressed to its limit (`quranic:root_000995:B004/m01`); deficient excuse presented as justification (`quranic:root_000995:B005/m01`).

- Synthesis: The scene moves from examination to a fixed declaration. What the subject tries to frame as an excuse is itself assessed as evidence and entered into the finding.
