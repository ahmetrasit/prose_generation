## Parent Channels

### 1. P1 - Speech Placed Under Hearing and Account
- Semantic invariant: Spoken, secret, and inward discourse is brought under reception, disclosure, restraint, and consequence.
- Surface relation: direct; 58:1 anchors the heard complaint and dialogue, while 58:6-12 repeatedly expose, classify, restrain, or condition private and inward speech.
- Surprising reach: Private counsel also activates a flood-safe height, sin becomes delay from good, and charity physically goes ahead of a speaker as a purifying access-token.
