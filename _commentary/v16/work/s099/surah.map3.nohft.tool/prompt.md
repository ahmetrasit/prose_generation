- Scene or process: Fine scented powder is stored and transported in a divided portable container.
- Active motifs: double-pouched bag `خ ر ج:B009/m01`; aromatic powder `ذ ر ر:B003/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 99:2 — أَخْرَجَتِ (`خ ر ج`); 99:7-8 — ذَرَّةٍ (`ذ ر ر`)
- Synthesis: The powder supplies the fine carried material, while the paired pouch separates, protects, and transports it.

## Standalone Subchannels

### S1. Luring an Animal from Its Burrow
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A tool is placed at one opening so that a hidden animal emerges from another.
- Active motifs: luring an animal from its burrow `خ ي ر:B006/m01`; extraction from concealment `خ ر ج:B002/m01`; game-stick or wooden implement `ق و ل:B008/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 99:2 — أَخْرَجَتِ (`خ ر ج`); 99:3 — قَالَ (`ق و ل`); 99:7 — خَيْرًا (`خ ي ر`)
- Synthesis: The burrow supplies concealment, the inserted implement redirects the animal, and extraction completes the induced emergence.

### S2. Tongue and Road as Conducting Paths
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A tongue conducts speech as a traveled road conducts movement.
- Active motifs: tongue as articulating instrument `ق و ل:B002/m01`; well-traveled route `ع م ل:B011/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 99:3 — قَالَ (`ق و ل`); 99:6 — أَعْمَٰلَ, 99:7-8 — يَعْمَلْ (`ع م ل`)
- Synthesis: Both motifs organize passage through a constrained channel: sounds move along the articulator's path, while travelers move along the worked road.

### S3. Oriented Foreparts of Body and Tool
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Body and implement are organized by a forward-facing part that directs contact or action.
- Active motifs: chest or bodily forepart `ص د ر:B001/m01`; spear shaft near the point `ع م ل:B009/m01`; near-facing side `ء ن س:B004/m01`; lower supporting parts `ء ر ض:B001/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 99:1-2 — أَرْضُ (`ء ر ض`); 99:3 — إِنسَٰنُ (`ء ن س`); 99:6 — يَصْدُرُ, أَعْمَٰلَ, 99:7-8 — يَعْمَلْ (`ص د ر`, `ع م ل`)
- Synthesis: The chest and near side orient the body, the lower parts brace it against the ground, and the spear's forward shaft gives a tool the same directed organization.

### S4. Soft Furnishing Spread over a Surface
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A thick woven covering with a soft nap is unfolded across the ground.
- Active motifs: thick woolen carpet `ء ر ض:B005/m01`; soft wool or nap `خ ب ر:B005/m02`; cloth spread out `ش ر ر:B002/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 99:1-2 — أَرْضُ (`ء ر ض`); 99:4 — أَخْبَارَ (`خ ب ر`); 99:8 — شَرًّا (`ش ر ر`)
- Synthesis: Wool supplies the material, softness supplies the tactile surface, the carpet supplies the finished object, and spreading places it in use.

### S5. Clear Scented Drink in a Large Vessel
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Abundant clear water is held in a capacious vessel and combined with aromatic powder.
- Active motifs: clear palatable water `ز ل ز ل:B003/m01`; abundant fresh water `ر ب ب:B013/m01`; large water vessel `خ ب ر:B004/m01`; aromatic powder `ذ ر ر:B003/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 99:1 — زُلْزِلَتِ, زِلْزَالَ (`ز ل ز ل`); 99:4 — أَخْبَارَ (`خ ب ر`); 99:5 — رَبَّ (`ر ب ب`); 99:7-8 — ذَرَّةٍ (`ذ ر ر`)
- Synthesis: The vessel supplies capacity, abundant clear water supplies the drink, and aromatic powder supplies a fine additive dispersed through it.

### S6. Polishing Blade and Heart
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Abrasion removes dullness from a blade and, by functional analogy, removes moral or cognitive rust from the heart.
- Active motifs: blade polishing `ح د ث:B007/m01`; heart cleansing by admonition `ح د ث:B007/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 99:4 — تُحَدِّثُ (`ح د ث`)
- Synthesis: Both senses share restorative removal: polishing returns a cutting edge to operation, while admonition restores the heart's receptive clarity.

### S7. Roast Releasing Its Fat
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Heat cooks meat until rendered fat drips from it.
- Active motifs: dripping-fat roast `ش ر ر:B005/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 99:8 — شَرًّا (`ش ر ر`)
- Synthesis: The motif contains a complete transformation from heated food to the visible release of rendered fat.

### S8. Type-Crossing Animal Form
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: An animal of one named kind displays the bodily configuration associated with another.
- Active motifs: she-camel formed like a male camel `خ ر ج:B008/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 99:2 — أَخْرَجَتِ (`خ ر ج`)
- Synthesis: The animal remains identified with one category while its visible construction crosses the expected boundary of form.
