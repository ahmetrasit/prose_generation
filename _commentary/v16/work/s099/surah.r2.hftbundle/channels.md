# s099 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. Release from Containment into Exposure
- Semantic invariant: What is enclosed, hidden, or concentrated crosses a boundary and becomes outwardly present.
- Surface relation: direct; 99:2 — أَخْرَجَتِ ٱلْأَرْضُ أَثْقَالَهَا; 99:6 — يَصْدُرُ, أَشْتَاتًا, لِّيُرَوْا; 99:7-8 — مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ, يَرَهُ.
- Surprising reach: The same transition extends from geological release to departure routes, disclosure, sprinkled particles, and goods spread beneath the sun.

#### Subchannel A. Ground Releasing Its Burdens
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: The ground acts as a containing substrate whose weighted contents are extracted into the open.
- Active motifs: enclosing ground `ء ر ض:B001/m01`; extraction from concealment `خ ر ج:B002/m01`; physical heaviness `ث ق ل:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 99:1-2 — أَرْضُ (`ء ر ض`); 99:2 — أَخْرَجَتِ (`خ ر ج`), أَثْقَالَ (`ث ق ل`); 99:7-8 — مِثْقَالَ (`ث ق ل`)
- Synthesis: The earth supplies the lower container, extraction supplies the outward operation, and heaviness characterizes what is released, forming a compact scene of a burdened interior opening onto the surface.

#### Subchannel B. Departure along an Outgoing Route
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A participant leaves a prior station and proceeds along a route that carries movement away from its source.
- Active motifs: exit and separation `خ ر ج:B001/m01`; departure from a watering place `ص د ر:B003/m01`; well-traveled route `ع م ل:B011/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 99:2 — أَخْرَجَتِ (`خ ر ج`); 99:6 — يَصْدُرُ (`ص د ر`), أَعْمَٰلَ (`ع م ل`); 99:7-8 — يَعْمَلْ (`ع م ل`)
- Synthesis: Exit supplies the boundary crossing, departure supplies the source-to-away direction, and the worked road supplies the conducting setting for outward movement.

#### Subchannel C. Hidden Content Made Manifest
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Concealed information or action is brought out, displayed, and made available to perception.
- Active motifs: explicit disclosure `ح د ث:B006/m01`; release from ignorance or concealment `خ ر ج:B002/m02`; caused display `ر ء ي:B012/m01`; secret transmission `و ح ي:B001/m01`

- Synthesis: A hidden message first exists in covert transmission, then crosses into disclosure and finally becomes an intentionally shown object of perception.

#### Subchannel D. Dispersion across an Open Field

#### Subchannel E. Shredding into Expelled Fragments
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A compact object is cut apart and its resulting fragments are extracted and scattered.
- Active motifs: cutting and shredding `ش ر ر:B004/m01`; extraction from concealment `خ ر ج:B002/m01`; scattering grains or powder `ذ ر ر:B002/m01`

### 3. Destabilization, Inertia, and Involuntary Motion
- Semantic invariant: Force is registered as a loss of stable control, ranging from oscillation to slowed resistance or compelled bodily movement.
- Surface relation: direct; 99:1 — زُلْزِلَتِ ٱلْأَرْضُ زِلْزَالَهَا; 99:2 — أَثْقَالَهَا; 99:3 — ٱلْإِنسَٰنُ; 99:4 — تُحَدِّثُ; 99:5 — أَوْحَىٰ.
- Surprising reach: The terrestrial quake becomes a human shiver, a faint murmur, torpor, and a possessed body's involuntary movement.

- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Oscillation passes from the ground into a shivering body and is accompanied by a low sound.
- Active motifs: seismic shaking `ز ل ز ل:B001/m01`; bodily shiver `ء ر ض:B008/m01`; torpid bodily resistance `ث ق ل:B006/m01`; faint sound `و ح ي:B005/m01`

- Scene or process: Body and implement are organized by a forward-facing part that directs contact or action.
- Active motifs: chest or bodily forepart `ص د ر:B001/m01`; spear shaft near the point `ع م ل:B009/m01`; near-facing side `ء ن س:B004/m01`; lower supporting parts `ء ر ض:B001/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 99:1-2 — أَرْضُ (`ء ر ض`); 99:3 — إِنسَٰنُ (`ء ن س`); 99:6 — يَصْدُرُ, أَعْمَٰلَ, 99:7-8 — يَعْمَلْ (`ص د ر`, `ع م ل`)
- Synthesis: The chest and near side orient the body, the lower parts brace it against the ground, and the spear's forward shaft gives a tool the same directed organization.

### S4. Soft Furnishing Spread over a Surface
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A thick woven covering with a soft nap is unfolded across the ground.
- Active motifs: thick woolen carpet `ء ر ض:B005/m01`; soft wool or nap `خ ب ر:B005/m02`; cloth spread out `ش ر ر:B002/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 99:1-2 — أَرْضُ (`ء ر ض`); 99:4 — أَخْبَارَ (`خ ب ر`); 99:8 — شَرًّا (`ش ر ر`)
- Synthesis: Wool supplies the material, softness supplies the tactile surface, the carpet supplies the finished object, and spreading places it in use.

### S5. Clear Scented Drink in a Large Vessel
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Abundant clear water is held in a capacious vessel and combined with aromatic powder.
- Active motifs: clear palatable water `ز ل ز ل:B003/m01`; abundant fresh water `ر ب ب:B013/m01`; large water vessel `خ ب ر:B004/m01`; aromatic powder `ذ ر ر:B003/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 99:1 — زُلْزِلَتِ, زِلْزَالَ (`ز ل ز ل`); 99:4 — أَخْبَارَ (`خ ب ر`); 99:5 — رَبَّ (`ر ب ب`); 99:7-8 — ذَرَّةٍ (`ذ ر ر`)
- Synthesis: The vessel supplies capacity, abundant clear water supplies the drink, and aromatic powder supplies a fine additive dispersed through it.

### S6. Polishing Blade and Heart
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Abrasion removes dullness from a blade and, by functional analogy, removes moral or cognitive rust from the heart.
- Active motifs: blade polishing `ح د ث:B007/m01`; heart cleansing by admonition `ح د ث:B007/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 99:4 — تُحَدِّثُ (`ح د ث`)
- Synthesis: Both senses share restorative removal: polishing returns a cutting edge to operation, while admonition restores the heart's receptive clarity.

### S7. Roast Releasing Its Fat
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Heat cooks meat until rendered fat drips from it.
- Active motifs: dripping-fat roast `ش ر ر:B005/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 99:8 — شَرًّا (`ش ر ر`)
- Synthesis: The motif contains a complete transformation from heated food to the visible release of rendered fat.

### S8. Type-Crossing Animal Form
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: An animal of one named kind displays the bodily configuration associated with another.
- Active motifs: she-camel formed like a male camel `خ ر ج:B008/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 99:2 — أَخْرَجَتِ (`خ ر ج`)
- Synthesis: The animal remains identified with one category while its visible construction crosses the expected boundary of form.
