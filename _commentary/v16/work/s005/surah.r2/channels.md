## Cross-Pericope Parent Channels

### 34. [P1, P2, P7] Binding Is Distinguished by How It Is Loosened
- Semantic invariant: A binding structure is judged not only by its tension but by whether loosening fulfills, repairs, or destroys the relation it holds.

- Pericopes and bridge: P1 (5:1-11), P2 (5:12-26), and P7 (5:87-108). Causal sequence with contrast: formation is followed either by faithful completion or authorized release, by destructive unbraiding, or by breach followed by expiatory release.
