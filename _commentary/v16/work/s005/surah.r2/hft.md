# Focus 5:6

## bounded transition
- reading: The verse stages a bounded bodily state-change: resolved rising is carried through ordered removal into prayer-readiness.
- mechanism: Resolved rising initiates a measured removal of trace from selected body zones, so the rite functions as a bodily transition into an upright and directed prayer-state.
- trace:
  - 5:6 **قُمْتُمْ** ق و م B003: Rising carries resolve and movement into an undertaking, making prayer-readiness an enacted transition.
  - 5:6 **وَٱمْسَحُوا۟ فَٱمْسَحُوا۟** م س ح B001: Passing the hand while removing an effect supplies the transition's repeated resetting action.
  - 5:6 **فَٱطَّهَّرُوا۟ لِيُطَهِّرَكُمْ** ط ه ر B001: Purity as the departure of defilement names the state produced by the sequence.

## materially flexible invariant
- reading: Water and good ground are alternate media that preserve directed, ordered contact with the same marked bodily endpoints.
- mechanism: The medium changes from water to ground, while intention, ordered contact, and the repeated bodily endpoints remain. The fallback therefore preserves a functional invariant rather than merely imitating wet washing.
- trace:
  - 5:6 **مَآءً** م و ه B001: Water supplies the ordinary material whose absence activates the alternate path.
  - 5:6 **فَتَيَمَّمُوا۟** ي م م B001: Deliberate aiming supplies the invariant of directed intention when the material changes.
  - 5:6 **صَعِيدًا** ص ع د B004: The face of the earth supplies an available alternate contact-surface.
  - 5:6 **طَيِّبًا** ط ي ب B001: Goodness opposed to corruption qualifies the alternate medium rather than making any material interchangeable.
  - 5:6 **وَٱمْسَحُوا۟ فَٱمْسَحُوا۟** م س ح B001: The repeated hand-passing action links the ordinary and alternate paths.
  - 5:6 **وُجُوهَكُمْ بِوُجُوهِكُمْ** و ج ه B001: The repeated front-facing bodily endpoint remains stable across both media.
  - 5:6 **وَأَيْدِيَكُمْ وَأَيْدِيكُم** ي د ي B001: The repeated hand endpoint completes the bodily invariant across both paths.

## completion without constriction
- reading: Avoiding constriction is part of how purification is designed and blessing is brought to completion.
- mechanism: The rite's detailed limits and fallback route untangle access rather than thicken it. Non-constriction is therefore a criterion internal to purification and completion, not reassurance appended after a burden.
- trace:
  - 5:6 **يُرِيدُ يُرِيدُ** ر و د B001: Repeated willing explicitly contrasts the rejected and selected outcomes of the rite.
  - 5:6 **حَرَجٍ** ح ر ج B002: Severe narrowness supplies the condition the design explicitly refuses to produce.
  - 5:6 **فَٱطَّهَّرُوا۟ لِيُطَهِّرَكُمْ** ط ه ر B001: Removal of defilement supplies the positive outcome selected instead of constriction.
  - 5:6 **وَلِيُتِمَّ** ت م م B001: Attaining wholeness makes accessible completion, rather than maximal burden, the endpoint.

## faculties map
- reading: The literal surfaces also form an inspectable map of orientation, agency, governance, and movement brought under a common reset.
- mechanism: The selected anatomy can be read as a compact map of orientation, agency, governing top, and locomotion. A common trace-removing action subjects these exposed faculties to one reset without replacing their literal bodily referents.
- trace:
  - 5:6 **وُجُوهَكُمْ بِوُجُوهِكُمْ** و ج ه B002: Face as direction supplies the body's outward orientation within the map.
  - 5:6 **وَأَيْدِيَكُمْ وَأَيْدِيكُم** ي د ي B002: Hand as power supplies active capacity within the map.
  - 5:6 **بِرُءُوسِكُمْ** ر ء س B001: Head as the uppermost part supplies the governing top of the bodily sequence.
  - 5:6 **وَأَرْجُلَكُمْ** ر ج ل B003: Walking on the feet supplies locomotion and onward movement.
  - 5:6 **وَٱمْسَحُوا۟ فَٱمْسَحُوا۟** م س ح B001: Passing the hand and removing an effect gives the distinct faculties one resetting operation.

## covenant embodied
- reading: Purification is a recurring bodily renewal of the fidelity by which prayer is undertaken within covenant.
- mechanism: Fulfilled bonds precede the focus, remembered covenant and heard obedience immediately follow it, and establishing prayer later appears as a covenant condition. The recurring bodily transition into prayer thereby becomes an enactment of fidelity rather than a private hygiene preface.
- trace:
  - 5:6 **قُمْتُمْ** ق و م B003: Resolved rising anchors covenantal fidelity in a repeated bodily undertaking.
  - 5:6 **وَلِيُتِمَّ** ت م م B001: Completion of blessing gives the rite an explicit wholeness link to the surrounding covenant sequence.
  - 5:1 **أَوْفُوا۟** و ف ي B001: Whole fulfillment without deficiency supplies the opening demand that the bodily rite later enacts.
  - 5:1 **بِٱلْعُقُودِ** ع ق د B002: A binding and ratified obligation supplies the social-legal form of fidelity.
  - 5:7 **وَمِيثَٰقَهُ وَاثَقَكُم** و ث ق B004: The confirmed covenant immediately after the focus brackets the rite with pledged obligation.
  - 5:7 **سَمِعْنَا** س م ع B003: Hearing as understanding and compliance supplies the receptive half of embodied fidelity.
  - 5:7 **وَأَطَعْنَا** ط و ع B001: Obedient yielding turns the remembered pledge into performed response.
  - 5:12 **أَقَمْتُمُ** ق و م B003: Undertaking prayer inside the covenant makes the focus's rising construction covenant-relevant.
  - 5:12 **ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ** ص ل و B003: The specified worship is directly installed among covenant performances.

## concession constitutes completion
- reading: A complete norm includes a viable path through necessity; adaptation is one of completion's mechanisms.
- mechanism: The earlier declaration of completion contains an emergency hunger allowance and closes with protective mercy. The focus repeats completion after illness, travel, water absence, and an alternate medium. Accommodation is therefore part of what makes the order complete, not a defect in an otherwise uniform rule.
- trace:
  - 5:6 **فَتَيَمَّمُوا۟** ي م م B001: Deliberate recourse preserves directed practice when the ordinary medium cannot be found.
  - 5:6 **حَرَجٍ** ح ر ج B002: Rejected constriction states why an adaptive route belongs inside the design.
  - 5:6 **وَلِيُتِمَّ** ت م م B001: Completion explicitly names the outcome reached through both ordinary and fallback paths.
  - 5:3 **أَكْمَلْتُ** ك م ل B001: Prior wholeness supplies the frame in which an emergency exception is already included.
  - 5:3 **وَأَتْمَمْتُ** ت م م B001: The earlier completion of blessing directly anticipates the focus's closing formulation.
  - 5:3 **ٱضْطُرَّ** ض ر ر B003: Necessity supplies the condition under which a complete order flexes without collapsing.
  - 5:3 **مَخْمَصَةٍ** خ م ص B002: An empty belly gives the earlier concession a concrete scarcity mechanism parallel to absent water.
  - 5:3 **غَفُورٌ** غ ف ر B002: Protective covering from an offense's effect keeps necessity from becoming exclusion.
  - 5:3 **رَّحِيمٌ** ر ح م B001: Merciful tenderness supplies the relational quality of the adaptive completion.

## hands as power under restraint
- reading: The hands are also exercised power being reset for measured public action under conditions of hostility.
- mechanism: The focus twice marks hands. The following context demands justice under hatred, then depicts hostile hands extending and being restrained. Washing the hands can thus reset exercised power before public standing, so agency is neither released by animus nor answered by uncontrolled retaliation.
- trace:
  - 5:6 **وَأَيْدِيَكُمْ وَأَيْدِيكُم** ي د ي B002: Repeated hands carry active power into both purification paths.
  - 5:6 **وَٱمْسَحُوا۟ فَٱمْسَحُوا۟** م س ح B001: Trace-removing contact supplies a reset of that active capacity.
  - 5:8 **شَنَـَٔانُ** ش ن ء B001: Hatred supplies the pressure capable of bending agency away from its measure.
  - 5:8 **تَعْدِلُوا۟ ٱعْدِلُوا۟** ع د ل B001: Justice in judgment and conduct supplies the calibration required of cleansed agency.
  - 5:11 **يَبْسُطُوٓا۟** ب س ط B004: Extending the hand literalizes power released toward another.
  - 5:11 **أَيْدِيَهُمْ أَيْدِيَهُمْ** ي د ي B002: Hands as force identify the threatened and restrained capacity with the focus's repeated organ.
  - 5:11 **فَكَفَّ** ك ف ف B002: Restraint supplies the counter-motion that prevents extended power from landing.

## reset against displacement
- reading: The rite can also image restoration to proper orientation, countering the contextual movement that shifts words and faculties from their places.
- mechanism: Broken bond leads to hardened interior and words shifted from their places; the next movement exposes what was hidden and guides toward light and a straight path. Against that sequence, wiping oriented face and walking feet can function as a bodily counter-image: remove displacement's trace and return exposed faculties to directed use.
- trace:
  - 5:6 **وَٱمْسَحُوا۟ فَٱمْسَحُوا۟** م س ح B001: Passing the hand while removing an effect supplies the body's restorative operation.
  - 5:6 **وُجُوهَكُمْ بِوُجُوهِكُمْ** و ج ه B008: The right face of an affair lets the literal face carry an exploratory proper-orientation function.
  - 5:6 **وَأَرْجُلَكُمْ** ر ج ل B003: Walking on the feet connects restored orientation to an actual path of movement.
  - 5:13 **نَقْضِهِم** ن ق ض B001: Undoing what was firmly built supplies the initial loss of ordered relation.
  - 5:13 **قَٰسِيَةً** ق س و B002: A heart losing softness supplies the interior rigidity paired with outward displacement.
  - 5:13 **يُحَرِّفُونَ** ح ر ف B003: Deviation from direction supplies the motion by which words are displaced.
  - 5:13 **مَّوَاضِعِهِۦ** و ض ع B001: Putting a thing in its settled place supplies the absent order against which displacement is measured.
  - 5:15 **تُخْفُونَ** خ ف ي B001: Concealment supplies the obscured condition reversed by disclosure.
  - 5:15 **يُبَيِّنُ مُّبِينٌ** ب ي ن B005: Making meaning clear supplies the contextual reversal of concealment and distortion.
  - 5:15 **نُورٌ** ن و ر B001: Illumination supplies the visible field into which obscured orientation is restored.
  - 5:16 **صِرَٰطٍ** ص ر ط B001: A straight traversable way supplies the destination of restored orientation.

## will specified as repair
- reading: Non-constrictive purification is itself the specifically chosen form that effective divine will takes in this ritual.
- mechanism: The later context presents divine power through destruction and creation, then punishment and forgiveness. The focus's unusually explicit double willing specifies a different deployment within ritual law: not constriction, but purification and completion. Ease is thus a selected expression of sovereign purpose rather than a soft limit imposed from outside.
- trace:
  - 5:6 **يُرِيدُ يُرِيدُ** ر و د B001: Repeated willing explicitly selects the rite's negative and positive outcomes.
  - 5:6 **حَرَجٍ** ح ر ج B002: Constriction is the sovereignly rejected effect.
  - 5:6 **فَٱطَّهَّرُوا۟ لِيُطَهِّرَكُمْ** ط ه ر B001: Removal of defilement is the selected reparative effect.
  - 5:17 **يُهْلِكَ** ه ل ك B001: Descent into destruction supplies one live expression of sovereign power in the context.
  - 5:17 **يَخْلُقُ** خ ل ق B002: Creative bringing-into-being supplies the constructive counterpart to destruction.
  - 5:17 **قَدِيرٌ** ق د ر B003: Effective capability grounds both contextual possibilities in actual power.
  - 5:18 **يُعَذِّبُكُم وَيُعَذِّبُ** ع ذ ب B005: Painful punishment supplies the severe pole of the following will statements.
  - 5:18 **يَغْفِرُ** غ ف ر B002: Protective covering of fault supplies the reparative pole of those will statements.

## directed entry not retreat
- reading: Purification rehearses directed response: rise, orient through available ground, and cross the next threshold rather than retreat, sit out the demand, or wander.
- mechanism: The focus begins with rising, preserves deliberate orientation during travel through recourse to good earth, and marks the feet. The closing pericope commands entry into purified land, warns against turning back, locates favor with the two who urge entry through the gate, and contrasts them with those who sit and finally wander. Purification becomes a small recurring rehearsal of responsive, directed movement at a threshold.
- trace:
  - 5:6 **قُمْتُمْ** ق و م B003: Resolved rising supplies the first movement of a non-paralyzed response.
  - 5:6 **فَتَيَمَّمُوا۟** ي م م B001: Deliberate aiming preserves direction even during scarcity and travel.
  - 5:6 **صَعِيدًا** ص ع د B004: The face of the earth becomes the immediate surface through which directed practice continues.
  - 5:6 **وَأَرْجُلَكُمْ** ر ج ل B003: Feet as walking organs carry the rite toward the context's entry and retreat contrast.
  - 5:6 **تَشْكُرُونَ** ش ك ر B001: Recognition of favor supplies the closing response that the entry narrative later tests in action.
  - 5:21 **ٱلْمُقَدَّسَةَ** ق د س B002: A purified place supplies the narrative threshold toward which movement is commanded.
  - 5:21 **ٱدْخُلُوا۟** د خ ل B001: Entry into an interior supplies the demanded forward crossing.
  - 5:21 **تَرْتَدُّوا۟** ر د د B001: Returning to what lies behind supplies the prohibited reversal.
  - 5:21 **أَدْبَارِكُمْ** د ب ر B003: Turning the back embodies retreat as the opposite of directed entry.
  - 5:23 **أَنْعَمَ** ن ع م B001: Bestowed good condition marks the two figures who convert favor into an appeal to move.
  - 5:23 **ٱلْبَابَ** ب و ب B001: The gate supplies a concrete threshold where orientation must become crossing.
  - 5:24 **قَٰعِدُونَ** ق ع د B002: Sitting out the action supplies the posture opposed to the focus's resolved rising.
  - 5:26 **ٱلْأَرْضِ** ء ر ض B001: The lower terrestrial expanse becomes the field of undirected movement after entry is refused.

## gratitude as minimal yield
- reading: The fallback and gratitude share an exploratory grammar of responsiveness: a small available means is deliberately used and its effect becomes visible.
- mechanism: 
- trace:
  - 5:6 **تَشْكُرُونَ** ش ك ر B002: Sufficiency with little and visible response supplies an image of gratitude as yielded effect.
  - 5:6 **مَآءً** م و ه B001: Unavailable water supplies the low-resource condition.
  - 5:6 **فَتَيَمَّمُوا۟** ي م م B001: Deliberate aiming supplies active responsiveness rather than passive deprivation.
  - 5:6 **صَعِيدًا** ص ع د B004: The readily available earth-surface supplies the small material input that still carries visible ritual effect.
  - 5:6 **نِعْمَتَهُۥ** ن ع م B001: Bestowed good condition supplies what the responsive yield recognizes.

## portable purified threshold
- reading: Good earth can provisionally function as a portable ritual threshold, allowing directed access without collapsing it into the later fixed purified land.
- mechanism: 
- trace:
  - 5:6 **فَتَيَمَّمُوا۟** ي م م B001: Deliberate aiming turns the available location into an intentional ritual approach.
  - 5:6 **صَعِيدًا** ص ع د B004: The earth's face supplies a portable contact-plane.
  - 5:6 **طَيِّبًا** ط ي ب B001: Qualification as good rather than corrupt bounds the portability claim.
  - 5:21 **ٱلْمُقَدَّسَةَ** ق د س B002: A specifically purified place supplies the fixed spatial counterpart.
  - 5:21 **ٱلْأَرْضَ** ء ر ض B001: Land as the terrestrial lower surface links the fixed destination to the focus's immediately available ground.
