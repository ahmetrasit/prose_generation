### 1. [P1 24:1-20] Measure Gives Law a Body
- Semantic invariant: A norm becomes effective by being bounded, placed, counted, witnessed, and finally registered at its proper moral magnitude.
- Surface relation: direct; 24:1-15 anchors the invariant in `سُورَةٌ`, `فَرَضْنَاهَا`, the counted sanctions, the four/fivefold testimony cycles, and the contrast `هَيِّنًا ... عَظِيمٌ`.
- Surprising reach: Lexical branches make legislation spatial and corporeal: a surah resembles an enclosing wall, obligation a scored cut, punishment a measured impact on skin, and culpability an allocated share whose apparent and actual sizes diverge.

### 2. [P1 24:1-20] Speech Moves as a Physical Force

- Surface relation: direct and indirect; 24:4-6 supplies `يَرْمُونَ`, 24:12-16 hearing/tongue/mouth transfer, and 24:14/19 overflow and diffusion.
- Surprising reach: The network materializes accusation as projectile injury and rumor as both fluid overflow and fire propagation, exposing distinct mechanisms rather than one generic “speech” domain.

### 4. [P1 24:1-20] Cognition Can Align or Invert a Report

### 7. [P2 24:21-34] Pardon Removes Residue and Reopens Kinship
- Semantic invariant: Forgiveness clears the material trace of offense, changes bodily orientation toward the offender, and permits provision to flow again through a damaged kin relation.
- Surface relation: direct and indirect; 24:22 joins `يَعْفُوا`, `يَصْفَحُوا`, `يَغْفِرَ`, kinship, capacity, and giving.
- Surprising reach: Wind-erased tracks, a broad face turned away from blame, a joined hand, and protective covering distinguish complementary operations often compressed into a single idea of pardon.

#### Subchannel A. Erasing the Trace
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Punitive pursuit stops, the residual mark is worn away, and the offender is covered against the fault's continuing effect.
- Active motifs: leaving deserved punishment `quranic:root_001032:B001/m01`; wind or time erasing a track `quranic:root_001032:B006/m01`; protective covering `quranic:root_001096:B001/m01`; covering fault and shielding its bearer `quranic:root_001096:B002/m01`

- Synthesis: Pardon first changes the offense's persistence. The pursuer releases punishment, then the trace by which the offense remains socially retraceable is erased, and protective covering prevents continued exposure. These are successive operations, not synonyms.

#### Subchannel B. Turning the Broad Side Away
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A person withdraws the accusing face from the fault and converts confrontation into an open-handed social closure.
