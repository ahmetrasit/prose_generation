# s007 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. P1 (7:1-25) - Measured Formation and Standing
- Semantic invariant: A made being receives form, position, and consequence through acts of estimation.
- Surface relation: direct; 7:8-12 joins weighing, creation, form, fire, and clay.
- Surprising reach: The creation root reaches back to pre-cut estimation, making bodily formation and moral weighing two operations of calibrated assignment.

#### Subchannel A. Form Estimated Before It Is Made
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A form is first proportioned, then brought into being, imaged, and given a material constitution.
- Active motifs: pre-action estimation (`quranic:root_000434:B001/m01`); bringing creation into being (`quranic:root_000434:B002/m01`); imaged bodily form (`quranic:root_000891:B003/m01`); mixed clay material (`quranic:root_000963:B001/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 7:11 `خَلَقْنَاكُمْ` and `صَوَّرْنَاكُمْ`; 7:12 `خَلَقْتَنِي` and `طِينٍ`
- Synthesis: Creation is not merely emergence. Its lexical field supplies an estimating operation before the finished form, while imaging and clay supply configuration and material. The result is a workshop-like scene in which constitution and proportion precede assigned standing.

#### Subchannel B. Standing Made Heavy or Light
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A balance assigns consequence by weight, with heaviness, lightness, rank, and loss as its outcomes.
- Active motifs: estimating by weight (`quranic:root_001645:B001/m01`); scales of justice (`quranic:root_001645:B002/m01`); weighted rank (`quranic:root_001645:B007/m01`); heaviness against lightness (`quranic:root_000202:B001/m01`); lightened load (`quranic:root_000427:B001/m01`); general diminution (`quranic:root_000409:B001/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 7:8 `الْوَزْنُ`, `ثَقُلَتْ`, and `مَوَازِينُهُ`; 7:9 `خَفَّتْ`, `مَوَازِينُهُ`, and `خَسِرُوا`
- Synthesis: The balance does more than count deeds: it materializes standing as load and rank. A heavy measure establishes consequential substance, while lightness becomes diminished standing and loss.

### 2. P1 (7:1-25) - From Enclosure to Exposed Ground
- Semantic invariant: Crossing a protected boundary lowers status, exposes the body, and requires a new covering and place of settlement.
- Surface relation: direct; 7:13 and 7:18-25 move from expulsion and descent to exposure, patching, and earthly settlement.
- Surprising reach: Forgiveness retains the concrete action of protective covering, so the plea after exposure answers the leaf-patching scene at another level.

#### Subchannel A. Lowered Placement
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Expulsion becomes a descent from rank, attachment to the ground, and temporary stabilization there.
- Active motifs: descent from height (`quranic:root_001575:B001/m01`); abasement from rank (`quranic:root_001575:B002/m01`); passage outside a prior state (`quranic:root_000400:B001/m01`); clinging heavily to earth (`quranic:root_000025:B006/m01`); fixed settlement (`quranic:root_001215:B003/m01`)

- Synthesis: A concealed mental intrusion produces bodily disclosure. The response is materially exact: flat leaves are layered into a provisional cover. The later request for forgiveness extends the same operation from exposed skin to exposed fault.

### 3. P2 (7:26-39) - Protective Layers and Their Removal

- Surface relation: direct; 7:26-27 names clothing, plumage, concealment, and stripping, while 7:33 distinguishes outer and inner.

- Synthesis: Stripping is an extraction rather than a passive absence. The later outer/inner contrast supplies the geometry of that exposure: an exterior layer can be removed, but concealed states also have an interior face that must be judged.

### 5. P2 (7:26-39) - Ordered Terms and Generational Entry

- Surface relation: direct; 7:34 fixes each community's term, and 7:38-39 stages successive entry and dispute between earlier and later groups.

- Active motifs: appointed temporal limit (`quranic:root_000016:B001/m01`); postponement to a later time (`quranic:root_000019:B002/m01`); advance before another (`quranic:root_001207:B004/m01`)

### 9. P4 (7:59-72) - Succession or Erasure

### 12. P5 (7:73-102) - Alternating Conditions and Sudden Closure
- Semantic invariant: Conditions can be exchanged and enlarged without warning, so apparent security never controls the timing of closure.

#### Subchannel B. Closure Arrives Off Schedule

### S30. Whole-surah - Earth Wiped onto the Limbs of Prayer
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Clean earth is deliberately sought and wiped across face and hands in preparation for prayer, after which the same embodied surface and limbs enter prostration.
- Active motifs: ritual use of clean earth to wipe face and hands for prayer (`quranic:root_001697:B002/m01`); face as the body's presented surface (`quranic:root_001630:B001/m01`); forehead, hands, knees, and feet as limbs of prostration (`quranic:root_000675:B003/m01`)

- Synthesis: This rite reframes the surah's earthward bodily geometry. The scene thus supports the primary distinction between involuntary abasement, earth-clinging desire, and deliberate worship.
