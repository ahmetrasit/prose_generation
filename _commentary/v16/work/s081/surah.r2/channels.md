# S081 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. Celestial Concealment and Renewal
- Semantic invariant: Celestial visibility changes through wrapping, falling, retreat, darkness, exposure, and renewed light.
- Surface relation: direct; 81:1 `الشمس/كورت`, 81:2 `النجوم/انكدرت`, 81:11 `السماء/كشطت`, 81:15-16 `الخنس/الجوار الكنس`, and 81:17-18 `الليل/الصبح/تنفس`.
- Surprising reach: The celestial sequence extends into hiding places, lamps, constellations, and an overhead canopy physically peeled away.

#### Subchannel A. Wound Sun and Falling Stars
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: Solar radiance is gathered and rolled while stars rush downward in a scattered fall.
- Active motifs: solar disk (`ش م س:B001/m01`); winding and gathering (`ك و ر:B001/m01`); rising stars (`ن ج م:B001/m01`); plunging, scattered motion (`ك د ر:B004/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 81:1 `الشمس/كورت`; 81:2 `النجوم/انكدرت`.
- Synthesis: The sun is treated as light that can be wound into itself, while the stars move from ordered appearance to rapid, dispersed collapse.

#### Subchannel B. Planets Retreating into Stations
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Moving celestial bodies withdraw from sight and enter their concealed lodgings.
- Active motifs: planets that hide or reverse (`خ ن س:B002/m01`); celestial running (`ج ر ي:B001/m01`); stars hidden in their courses (`ك ن س:B003/m01`); disappearance from sight (`غ ي ب:B001/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 81:15 `الخنس`; 81:16 `الجوار/الكنس`; 81:24 `الغيب`.
- Synthesis: Running, retreat, and entry into a hidden station form one astronomical cycle rather than three unrelated motions.

#### Subchannel C. The Thinning Edge of Night
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: Darkness covers the scene, then thins at one of the two margins of night.
- Active motifs: night and its darkness (`ل ي ل:B001/m01`); darkness at nightfall or withdrawal (`ع س ع س:B001/m01`); night covering what lies beneath it (`ج ن ن:B002/m01`); low cloud approaching the earth at night (`ع س ع س:B003/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 81:17 `الليل/عسعس`; 81:13 `الجنة`; 81:22 `مجنون`.
- Synthesis: The night is an active cover whose approach and recession are both encoded in the liminal motion of `عسعس`.

#### Subchannel D. Dawn Breathing Open
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: Dawn expands as though taking breath and spreading into available space.
- Active motifs: first light (`ص ب ح:B001/m01`); dawn opening like a breath (`ن ف س:B009/m01`); spreading and unfolding (`ن ش ر:B001/m02`).
- Ayah anchors: 81:18 `الصبح/تنفس`; 81:10 `نشرت`.
- Synthesis: Breath supplies the mechanism for daybreak: constriction releases, light extends, and the horizon becomes newly open.

#### Subchannel E. The Sky as a Stripped Canopy
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: An overhead cover is lifted away, exposing lights and ordered celestial structures behind it.
- Active motifs: sky as what rises and shades (`س م و:B004/m01`); removal of a covering (`ك ش ط:B001/m01`); raised canopy (`ع ر ش:B002/m01`); lamp or celestial light (`ص ب ح:B005/m01`); constellation called a throne (`ع ر ش:B008/m01`); motes visible in a sunbeam (`س ع ر:B006/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 81:11 `السماء/كشطت`; 81:18 `الصبح`; 81:20 `العرش`.
- Synthesis: The surface image of a peeled sky develops into an architectural scene in which a roof-like layer is removed and the lights and star-patterns beyond it become visible.

### 2. Distance, Orientation, and Shelter
- Semantic invariant: Places are organized by visible limits, intervening distance, lateral orientation, and the availability or loss of cover.
- Surface relation: direct; 81:23 `الأفق المبين`, 81:24 `الغيب`, 81:15-16 `الخنس/الكنس`, and 81:5 `الوحوش`.
- Surprising reach: The same spatial vocabulary reaches from horizons and deep wells to animal dens, wooded refuges, flanks, and abandoned ground.

#### Subchannel A. Horizon, Boundary, and Landmark
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A distant edge is made navigable by a conspicuous marker or raised standard.
- Active motifs: remote horizon and boundary (`ء ف ق:B001/m01`); distinguishing landmark (`ع ل م:B002/m01`); prominent marker (`ن ج م:B006/m01`); visible standard (`ر ء ي:B011/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 81:23 `الأفق/المبين/رآه`; 81:2 `النجوم`; 81:14 `علمت`; 81:27,29 `العالمين`.
- Synthesis: Distance becomes legible when an elevated or conspicuous sign fixes the edge of the field of view.

#### Subchannel B. Separation, Remoteness, and Depth
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Connected things part, and the widening interval becomes a measurable remoteness or depth.
- Active motifs: separation after contact (`ب ي ن:B001/m01`); deep or wide interval (`ب ي ن:B006/m01`); broad expanse (`ب ح ر:B002/m01`); remoteness (`ش ط ن:B001/m01`); long measuring rope (`ش ط ن:B002/m01`); spatial breadth and respite (`ن ف س:B015/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 81:23 `المبين`; 81:25 `شيطان`; 81:7 `النفوس`; 81:14 `نفس`; 81:18 `تنفس`.
- Synthesis: Parting is rendered spatially as an interval that can deepen, lengthen, and require a rope or extended span to traverse.

#### Subchannel C. Middle Ground and Lateral Position
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A participant occupies an intermediate strip or a side position relative to a route, settlement, animal, or working group.
- Active motifs: the interval between two sides (`ب ي ن:B002/m01`); intermediate condition (`ب ي ن:B011/m01`); settlements between desert and cultivated land (`ز ل ف:B005/m01`); outer or far side (`و ح ش:B003/m01`); lateral divergence (`ع ن د:B002/m01`); working from one side of an animal (`ب ي ن:B009/m01`); flank (`ء ف ق:B007/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 81:23 `المبين`; 81:13 `أزلفت`; 81:5 `الوحوش`; 81:20 `عند`.
- Synthesis: These motifs define orientation by relation to two edges: middle, near side, far side, and a sideward departure from the common line.

#### Subchannel D. Den, Copse, and Hidden Refuge
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: An animal or person enters a wooded or enclosed place that blocks outside sight.
- Active motifs: place of concealment (`ج ن ن:B017/m01`); deer and their shelter (`خ ن س:B004/m01`); entry into an animal den (`ك ن س:B002/m01`); concealing copse (`غ ي ب:B003/m01`); stone-lined animal refuge (`ر ج م:B005/m02`).
- Ayah anchors: 81:13 `الجنة`; 81:22 `مجنون`; 81:15 `الخنس`; 81:16 `الكنس`; 81:24 `الغيب`; 81:25 `رجيم`.
- Synthesis: Vegetation, enclosure, and withdrawal cooperate as parts of one refuge scene rather than merely sharing the idea of invisibility.

#### Subchannel E. Concealment and Exposure
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A covered object withdraws from sight, then becomes open when the covering is removed and the surface cleared.
- Active motifs: sensory concealment (`ج ن ن:B001/m01`); absence from sight (`غ ي ب:B001/m01`); withdrawal into hiding (`خ ن س:B001/m01`); stripping a cover (`ك ش ط:B001/m01`); sweeping a surface clear (`ك ن س:B001/m01`); open visibility (`ب ح ر:B005/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 81:13 `الجنة`; 81:22 `مجنون`; 81:24 `الغيب`; 81:15 `الخنس`; 81:11 `كشطت`; 81:16 `الكنس`; 81:6 `البحار`.
- Synthesis: Withdrawal and covering produce invisibility; stripping and sweeping reverse the operation until the object stands in open view.

#### Subchannel F. Abandoned and Barren Ground
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Loss of inhabitants, use, rain, and pasture turns a settled place into an empty waste.
- Active motifs: disuse and vacancy (`ع ط ل:B001/m01`); deserted ground (`و ح ش:B002/m01`); barren land (`ق و ي:B003/m01`); absence from sight (`غ ي ب:B001/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 81:4 `عطلت`; 81:5 `الوحوش`; 81:20 `قوة`; 81:24 `الغيب`.
- Synthesis: Vacancy, desolation, failed productivity, and human absence define an environmental state distinct from active concealment.

### 3. Waters, Wells, and Drinking
- Semantic invariant: Water is gathered, conveyed, drawn, modified, served, and consumed through connected terrains and implements.
- Surface relation: direct; 81:6 `البحار/سجرت`, 81:16 `الجوار`, and 81:18 `تنفس`.
- Surprising reach: The water channel includes a complete well mechanism, brine and diluted drink, serving vessels, algae-covered pools, and maritime command.

#### Subchannel A. Sea, Basin, and Collected Water
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Abundant water gathers in a sea, low basin, or deep well until its container is full.
- Active motifs: broad abundant water (`ب ح ر:B001/m01`); water-collecting depression (`ب ح ر:B006/m01`); deep collected water (`ع ل م:B005/m01`); plentiful or sweet water (`ر ب ب:B013/m01`); filling with water (`س ج ر:B001/m01`); algae-covered surface (`ص ح ب:B007/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 81:6 `البحار/سجرت`; 81:14 `علمت`; 81:27,29 `العالمين`; 81:29 `رب`.
- Synthesis: Sea, basin, and well are variants of one containment scene defined by breadth, depth, and the accumulation of water.

#### Subchannel B. The Well-and-Bucket Assembly
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A filled bucket is lowered, steadied, raised, and shared at a wellhead.
- Active motifs: full bucket (`ذ ن ب:B007/m01`); superior bucket (`ء ف ق:B008/m01`); drinking down a common bucket (`ق و ي:B005/m01`); long bucket rope (`ش ط ن:B002/m01`); well weight and upright timbers (`ر ج م:B006/m01`); wooden well frame and drawing platform (`ع ر ش:B004/m01`); standing pulley component (`ق و م:B012/m01`); hard substrate that stops excavation (`ج ب ل:B005/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 81:9 `ذنب`; 81:23 `الأفق`; 81:20 `قوة/العرش`; 81:25 `شيطان/رجيم`; 81:28 `يستقيم`.
- Synthesis: The motifs specify vessel, rope, counterweight, support, platform, and shared use, forming a concrete mechanical scene.

#### Subchannel C. Channels, Overflow, and Pouring
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Water moves from a filled source through a channel and pours into lower ground.
- Active motifs: valley watercourse (`ذ ن ب:B004/m01`); flowing current (`ج ر ي:B001/m01`); overflowing fill (`س ج ر:B001/m01`); pouring (`ك د ر:B003/m01`); water-collecting lowland (`ب ح ر:B006/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 81:9 `ذنب`; 81:16 `الجوار`; 81:6 `البحار/سجرت`; 81:2 `انكدرت`.
- Synthesis: A hydrological sequence connects source, channel, motion, discharge, and receiving basin.

#### Subchannel D. Draught, Refreshment, and Serving
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Water or morning drink is taken in measured breaths and served from broad, filled vessels.
- Active motifs: a breath-sized draught (`ن ف س:B006/m01`); water that sustains life (`ن ف س:B008/m01`); return to the watering place (`ح ض ر:B003/m01`); morning drink (`ص ب ح:B003/m01`); broad dish or shallow pool (`ص ح ف:B004/m01`); filled bowls (`ز ل ف:B004/m01`); ladle (`ذ ن ب:B008/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 81:7 `النفوس`; 81:14 `نفس/أحضرت`; 81:18 `الصبح/تنفس`; 81:10 `الصحف`; 81:13 `أزلفت`; 81:9 `ذنب`.
- Synthesis: The scene moves from water source to filled serving vessel, ladle, portioned draught, and bodily refreshment.

#### Subchannel E. Brine and Tempered Drink
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A liquid's salinity or strength is altered by mixing it with water.
- Active motifs: seawater salinity (`ب ح ر:B004/m01`); dilution that breaks a drink's strength (`ق ت ل:B007/m01`); measured draught (`ن ف س:B006/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 81:6 `البحار`; 81:9 `قتلت`; 81:7 `النفوس`; 81:14 `نفس`; 81:18 `تنفس`.
- Synthesis: Salty water and strong drink meet in a small chemistry of concentration, dilution, and drinkability.

#### Subchannel F. Seafaring and the Shipmaster
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A vessel enters open water under a captain who directs its continuing course.
- Active motifs: embarkation and maritime affiliation (`ب ح ر:B009/m01`); chief of sailors (`ر ب ب:B017/m01`); flowing ship or current (`ج ر ي:B001/m01`); dispatch into motion (`ر س ل:B001/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 81:6 `البحار`; 81:29 `رب`; 81:16 `الجوار`; 81:19 `رسول`.
- Synthesis: Open water, moving vessel, command, and dispatch create a complete voyage scene.

### 4. Plant Growth and Cultivation
- Semantic invariant: Vegetation emerges, thickens, is tended or supported, feeds animals, and renews after rain or harvest.
- Surface relation: direct; 81:13 `الجنة`, 81:5 `حشرت`, 81:10 `نشرت`, and 81:29 `رب`.
- Surprising reach: The botanical channel reaches precise practices such as trellising vines, pollinating palms, grazing stubble, and exposing hidden roots.

#### Subchannel A. Germination and Revived Herbage
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Small spring growth emerges, thickens, and returns green after rain.
- Active motifs: stemless spring plant (`ن ج م:B003/m01`); dense rising vegetation (`ج ن ن:B011/m01`); herbage revived by rain (`ن ش ر:B004/m01`); persistent green plant (`ر ب ب:B012/m01`); spring herb (`م ك ن:B007/m01`); named plant (`ذ ن ب:B009/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 81:2 `النجوم`; 81:13 `الجنة`; 81:22 `مجنون`; 81:10 `نشرت`; 81:29 `رب/العالمين`; 81:20 `مكين`; 81:9 `ذنب`.
- Synthesis: Emergence, thickening, rain-revival, and named ground plants form one cycle of low vegetation.

#### Subchannel B. Orchard, Vine, and Trellis
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Trees enclose a garden while vines climb supports and send hidden roots into the soil.
- Active motifs: tree-covered garden (`ج ن ن:B003/m01`); grapevine and fruit (`ك ر م:B004/m01`); vine trellis (`ع ر ش:B003/m01`); garden tree (`ع ش ر:B014/m01`); hidden roots (`غ ي ب:B008/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 81:13 `الجنة`; 81:19 `كريم`; 81:20 `العرش`; 81:4 `العشار`; 81:24 `الغيب`.
- Synthesis: Enclosure, living vine, raised support, tree cover, and underground anchorage produce a worked orchard rather than a generic plant cluster.

#### Subchannel C. Palm Seedlings and Pollination
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Young palms are propagated by using the male palm's flowering spadix.
- Active motifs: young palms (`ش ي ء:B006/m01`); young palms as a repeated lexical container (`ش ي ء:B008/m01`); male palm spadix used for pollination (`ع ط ل:B005/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 81:28 `شاء`; 81:29 `تشاءون/يشاء`; 81:4 `عطلت`.
- Synthesis: Seedling and pollen-bearing spadix supply both the plant and the horticultural operation required for palm propagation.

#### Subchannel D. Pasture, Night Grazing, and Stubble
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Ripe pasture opens to flocks, which spread to graze and return over harvested ground.
- Active motifs: pasture ready for grazing (`ط و ع:B007/m01`); sheep dispersed at night (`ن ش ر:B006/m01`); activity during the night (`ل ي ل:B002/m01`); restraint from grazing (`ع ر ش:B010/m01`); successive flocks (`ر س ل:B005/m01`); herd of wild cattle (`ر ب ب:B014/m01`); crop remains after harvest (`ح ش ر:B008/m01`).

- Synthesis: Readiness, release, flock movement, grazing control, and post-harvest residue define a pastoral field scene.

#### Subchannel E. Fertile Rain, Withering, and Renewal
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Rain-bearing cloud makes soil productive, while dry wood and hidden roots preserve the possibility of return.
- Active motifs: fertile land and rain (`ك ر م:B002/m01`); rainfall (`ذ ه ب:B005/m01`); rain cloud (`ر ب ب:B008/m01`); sky or cloud as rain source (`س م و:B004/m02`); dry tree (`ج ب ل:B008/m01`); buried roots (`غ ي ب:B008/m01`).

- Synthesis: The channel holds drought and fertility together through a causal sequence of dry plant, rain cloud, productive earth, and surviving root.

### 5. Motion, Travel, and Pursuit

- Surface relation: direct; 81:2-3 `انكدرت/سيرت`, 81:5 `الوحوش`, 81:16 `الجوار`, and 81:26 `تذهبون`.
- Surprising reach: The motion vocabulary reconstructs horse handling, caravan gait, races, hunting, and the formation of customary routes.

- Scene or process: A prized horse is driven at speed, lifts its tail, veers, and strikes the ground.

- Synthesis: Breed quality, acceleration, tail posture, lateral movement, hoof surface, and impact create a detailed equestrian action sequence.

#### Subchannel B. Caravan and Herd Gait

#### Subchannel C. Race, Rivalry, and Hunt

### 6. Material Transformation and Adornment
