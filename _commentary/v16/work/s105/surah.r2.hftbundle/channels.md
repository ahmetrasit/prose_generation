# S105 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. Directed Agency and Effected Change
- Semantic invariant: An initiating actor carries an intention through making, release, directed movement, and a resulting change of state.
- Surface relation: direct; 105:1 فَعَلَ, 105:2 يَجْعَلْ, 105:3 أَرْسَلَ, 105:4 تَرْمِي, and 105:5 جَعَلَ enact the sequence.
- Surprising reach: The same pattern extends from overt dispatch into craft, sustained handling, attachment, and terminal material transformation.

#### Subchannel A. Making and State Transformation
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: An actor produces an entity, associates its parts, and renders it into a new condition.
- Active motifs: enacted work `ف ع ل:B001/m01`; creation `ج ع ل:B001/m01`; state conversion `ج ع ل:B002/m01`; making one thing accompany another `ص ح ب:B004/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 105:1 فَعَلَ and أَصْحَٰبِ; 105:2 يَجْعَلْ; 105:5 جَعَلَ
- Synthesis: Work becomes causation when making both brings something about and fixes it into a new relation or condition.

#### Subchannel B. Dispatch and Projectile Release
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: A released carrier crosses space and delivers a projected object to a target.
- Active motifs: sending and release `ر س ل:B001/m01`; throwing `ر م ي:B001/m01`; airborne movement `ط ي ر:B001/m01`; solid projectile `ح ج ر:B003/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 105:3 أَرْسَلَ and طَيْرًا; 105:4 تَرْمِي and حِجَارَةٍ
- Synthesis: Release, flight, throwing, and stone join as successive roles in one directed delivery mechanism.

#### Subchannel C. Sustained Exertion to an Endpoint
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A difficult operation is begun, maintained, and driven until it reaches a terminal state.
- Active motifs: beginning and persisting `ج ع ل:B004/m01`; strenuous handling `ك ي د:B001/m01`; enacted operation `ف ع ل:B001/m01`; movement to an endpoint `ر م ي:B006/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 105:1 فَعَلَ; 105:2 يَجْعَلْ and كَيْدَ; 105:4 تَرْمِي; 105:5 جَعَلَ
- Synthesis: The action is not a momentary impulse but an effortful process whose continuation matters because it carries the object to a definite outcome.

#### Subchannel D. Targeted Impact and Terminal Reduction
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: A massive animal host is struck with hard projectiles and converted into crushed, consumed remnants.
- Active motifs: elephant `ف ي ل:B004/m01`; stone hardness `ح ج ر:B003/m01`; projected strike `ر م ي:B001/m01`; crushing to chaff `ع ص ف:B004/m02`; state conversion `ج ع ل:B002/m01`; consumed object `ء ك ل:B001/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 105:1 فِيلِ; 105:2 يَجْعَلْ; 105:4 تَرْمِي and حِجَارَةٍ; 105:5 جَعَلَ, عَصْفٍ, and مَّأْكُولٍۭ
- Synthesis: The scene closes the causal chain by joining target, missile, impact, fragmentation, and the final condition of something already eaten through.

### 2. Perception, Demonstration, and Judgment
- Semantic invariant: Perception becomes consequential when a sign is noticed, shown, interpreted, and converted into judgment.
- Surface relation: direct; 105:1 تَرَ opens the surah through an appeal to seeing, while 105:2 تَضْلِيلٍ supplies the contrasting possibility of failed judgment or direction.
- Surprising reach: Seeing expands into mirrors, banners, bodily traces, intellectual restraint, and the failure modes of conjecture and memory.

#### Subchannel A. Witnessing, Showing, and Calling Attention
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: A viewer is alerted, an object is made visible, and perception becomes shared knowledge.
- Active motifs: sensory seeing `ر ء ي:B001/m01`; demonstrative showing `ر ء ي:B012/m01`; attention-seeking inquiry `ر ء ي:B013/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 105:1 تَرَ
- Synthesis: Seeing is organized as a discourse act: attention is summoned, the event is presented, and the addressee is positioned as a witness.

#### Subchannel B. Deliberation Before Judgment
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A person slows action, turns a matter over, and reaches a considered opinion.
- Active motifs: reflective opinion `ر ء ي:B002/m01`; measured deliberateness `ر س ل:B004/m01`; restraining intellect `ح ج ر:B002/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 105:1 تَرَ; 105:3 أَرْسَلَ; 105:4 حِجَارَةٍ
- Synthesis: Thought, measured pacing, and an intellect that blocks impropriety form a controlled judgment process.

#### Subchannel C. Misjudgment, Conjecture, and Lost Recall
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Weak discernment produces an unverified conclusion while memory fails to retain the matter.
- Active motifs: weak judgment `ف ي ل:B001/m01`; inaccurate conjecture `ر م ي:B009/m01`; lost memory `ض ل ل:B004/m01`; fabricated account `ف ع ل:B004/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 105:1 فَعَلَ and فِيلِ; 105:2 تَضْلِيلٍ; 105:4 تَرْمِي
- Synthesis: Poor discernment moves from guess to invented account, while failed retention removes the corrective memory that could expose the error.

#### Subchannel D. Framed and Diagnostic Signs
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A visible mark, frame, or standard lets an observer identify a condition or orientation.
- Active motifs: mirror and outward appearance `ر ء ي:B006/m01`; raised banner `ر ء ي:B011/m01`; circular halo `ح ج ر:B006/m01`; bodily diagnostic trace `ر ء ي:B007/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 105:1 تَرَ; 105:4 حِجَارَةٍ
- Synthesis: Reflection, a raised sign, a ringed outline, and a bodily trace all function as bounded forms that make otherwise hidden information readable.

### 3. Loss, Concealment, and Dispersal
- Semantic invariant: A thing ceases to reach its intended place by deviating, disappearing, being lost, or scattering into an uncollectable field.
- Surface relation: direct; 105:2 كَيْدَ and تَضْلِيلٍ name a design diverted from its goal, while 105:3 طَيْرًا and 105:5 عَصْفٍ embody distributed movement.
- Surprising reach: Frustrated purpose extends into burial, a hidden-object game, travel, and wind-driven dispersal.

#### Subchannel A. A Design Diverted from Its Goal
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: A deliberate scheme is turned away from its intended outcome and made ineffectual.
- Active motifs: calculated scheme `ك ي د:B002/m01`; deviation from the intended course `ض ل ل:B001/m01`; conversion into a failed state `ج ع ل:B002/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 105:2 يَجْعَلْ, كَيْدَ, and تَضْلِيلٍ; 105:5 جَعَلَ
- Synthesis: Intention supplies the route, deviation breaks it, and causative transformation fixes the scheme in failure.

#### Subchannel B. Burial and Hidden-Object Search
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: An object is concealed in earth, divided between possible locations, and sought without immediate visibility.
- Active motifs: disappearance and burial `ض ل ل:B002/m01`; hidden-object game `ف ي ل:B003/m01`; loss of the object's location `ض ل ل:B003/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 105:1 فِيلِ; 105:2 تَضْلِيلٍ
- Synthesis: Concealment changes a visible object into a location problem, and the search proceeds through uncertainty between hidden alternatives.

#### Subchannel C. Lost Possession Becoming Dispersed Travel
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A thing leaves its holder, moves toward another region, and spreads until recovery becomes difficult.
- Active motifs: loss from an owner `ض ل ل:B003/m01`; travel toward a direction `ر م ي:B007/m01`; outward dispersal `ط ي ر:B002/m01`; wind-driven scattering `ع ص ف:B002/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 105:2 تَضْلِيلٍ; 105:3 طَيْرًا; 105:4 تَرْمِي; 105:5 عَصْفٍ
- Synthesis: Loss becomes spatial as departure, directional movement, and scattering progressively separate the object from its point of possession.

### 4. Companionship, Care, and Family Continuity

- Surface relation: indirect; 105:1 رَبُّ and أَصْحَٰبِ provide the lexical hinges of guardianship and companionship.
- Surprising reach: Companionship reaches from compatibility between things to memory, stepfamily care, adulthood, widow courtship, and the shelter of a lap.

- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: One entity is made to accompany another and remains with it through compatibility and nurture.

#### Subchannel B. Protective Escort and Retained Memory

- Ayah anchors: 105:1 رَبُّ and أَصْحَٰبِ; 105:2 تَضْلِيلٍ

#### Subchannel C. A Child Maturing into a Companion
