# Focus 105:1

## manner as evidence
- reading: A summons to grasp the manner of a lordly deed as present, inspectable evidence, whether or not it was literally witnessed.
- mechanism: Seeing or insight and the alerting question make the deed cognitively present; branchless كَيْفَ structurally keeps its manner open, while فعل and lordship supply intentional governing agency.
- trace:
  - 105:1 **تَرَ** ر ء ي B001: Eye-seeing or insight makes the clause demand apprehension rather than passive receipt of information.
  - 105:1 **تَرَ** ر ء ي B013: The alerting tell-me use turns the interrogative into a summons to attend to how the deed worked.
  - 105:1 **فَعَلَ** ف ع ل B001: The production of an act supplies the event whose operative manner must be grasped.
  - 105:1 **رَبُّكَ** ر ب ب B001: Ownership and sovereignty locate the act within governing agency rather than anonymous occurrence.

## competing attachments
- reading: The focus is a relational confrontation: people constituted by attachment to a power-bearing instrument are acted upon by the addressee's owning and ordering Lord.
- mechanism: The addressee is related to a sovereign who owns, tends, and completes, while the acted-upon people are named through sustained attachment to an elephant. The verse therefore contrasts two constitutive relations, not merely two named parties.
- trace:
  - 105:1 **رَبُّكَ** ر ب ب B001: Mastery and ownership make رَبُّكَ the governing relation set against the other group's attachment.
  - 105:1 **رَبُّكَ** ر ب ب B002: Tending, correction, and completion add sustained ordering to lordship's action.
  - 105:1 **بِأَصْحَٰبِ** ص ح ب B001: Close association makes the people identifiable through what they accompany or possess.
  - 105:1 **بِأَصْحَٰبِ** ص ح ب B004: Taking or carrying something along makes the elephant an incorporated instrument of the collective.
  - 105:1 **ٱلْفِيلِ** ف ي ل B004: The concrete elephant supplies the conspicuous power-object around which the collective is named.

## report becomes sight
- reading: The received narrative is made cognitively visible: transmission does not weaken the evidence but becomes its vehicle.
- mechanism: Perception and narration coexist: a transmitted event can be rhetorically re-presented until it functions as sight. The question need not choose between eyewitnessing and report; it can convert the latter into the former's evidentiary force.
- trace:
  - 105:1 **تَرَ** ر ء ي B001: Sensory or insightful seeing supplies the immediacy claimed by تَرَ.
  - 105:1 **تَرَ** ر ء ي B003: The non-dominant report-and-poetry branch supplies transmission as the medium through which the event becomes present.

## bulk masks weak judgment
- reading: The label can carry an ironic double activation: massive visible power coupled to weak discernment, inviting judgment of its companions rather than awe at the animal.
- mechanism: The most visible emblem of physical mass can carry an ironic counter-image of poor judgment. The addressee is asked to look through conspicuous scale and assess the discernment of those defined by it.
- trace:
  - 105:1 **تَرَ** ر ء ي B002: Reflective judgment makes seeing an evaluation of the collective's reasoning.
  - 105:1 **ٱلْفِيلِ** ف ي ل B004: The elephant supplies visible magnitude and the group's explicit identifying instrument.
  - 105:1 **ٱلْفِيلِ** ف ي ل B001: Slack or mistaken judgment supplies an ironic weakness concealed by that magnitude.

## plan converted into failure
- reading: The Lord's deed penetrates their plan and converts its designed direction into the very state of failure.
- mechanism: The broad فعل of 105:1 is specified as a transformation whose patient is the companions' own stratagem: purposive contrivance is made to occupy a state of misdirection and loss. The action works inside the plan's organization rather than only striking its agents from outside.
- trace:
  - 105:1 **فَعَلَ** ف ع ل B001: An effected deed leaves its precise operation open for the context to specify.
  - 105:2 **يَجْعَلْ** ج ع ل B002: Making something assume a state supplies the conversion operator applied to the plot.
  - 105:2 **كَيْدَهُمْ** ك ي د B002: Calculated contrivance supplies an internally organized plan capable of being reversed.
  - 105:2 **تَضْلِيلٍ** ض ل ل B001: Deviation from direction supplies the failed trajectory into which the plan is made.
  - 105:2 **تَضْلِيلٍ** ض ل ل B003: Loss supplies the terminal disappearance of the plot's intended yield.

## strategy exposes miscalculation
- reading: The irony becomes operational: a worked-out strategy, not raw impulse, is precisely what discloses the companions' miscalculation.
- mechanism: The focus-only irony of great bulk with weak discernment gains a causal test: effortful, calculated strategy terminates in deviation. Failure is not caused by absence of planning; planning itself reveals misjudgment under the addressee's reflective gaze.
- trace:
  - 105:1 **تَرَ** ر ء ي B002: Reflection and evaluative judgment position the addressee to assess strategic intelligence.
  - 105:1 **ٱلْفِيلِ** ف ي ل B001: Mistaken discernment supplies the exploratory weakness that context can test.
  - 105:2 **كَيْدَهُمْ** ك ي د B001: Severe exertion prevents the failure from reading as mere lack of effort.
  - 105:2 **كَيْدَهُمْ** ك ي د B002: Cunning supplies deliberate strategic intelligence whose adequacy is being tested.
  - 105:2 **تَضْلِيلٍ** ض ل ل B001: Departure from the intended course supplies the observable verdict on that intelligence.

## absence becomes visible evidence
- reading: The deed can also be seen abductively in a vanished outcome: the failure to arrive is itself the trace of how the Lord acted.
- mechanism: The command to see is answered by images of hiding and loss. What becomes perceptible is a negative trace: the intended outcome does not arrive, so cognition moves from visible absence back to the agency that made the plan vanish.
- trace:
  - 105:1 **تَرَ** ر ء ي B001: Seeing or insight establishes the focus's demand for evidence.

  - 105:2 **تَضْلِيلٍ** ض ل ل B003: Loss supplies the missing effect from which the reader infers the counter-action.

## attached bulk vs distributed lightness
- reading: The manner is an inversion in force architecture: attached bulk is answered by dispatched, mobile, distributed plurality.
- mechanism: A collective named by close attachment to one conspicuous heavy animal is answered by agents defined through dispatch, aerial lightness, spreading, and grouped multiplicity. The contrast makes organization itself part of the كيف: centralized attached bulk meets distributed mobility.
- trace:
  - 105:1 **بِأَصْحَٰبِ** ص ح ب B001: Sustained association gives the acted-upon collective a cohesive identity around its companion-object.
  - 105:1 **ٱلْفِيلِ** ف ي ل B004: The elephant supplies the singular conspicuous center of that attached identity.
  - 105:3 **وَأَرْسَلَ** ر س ل B001: Dispatch and emission supply an externally directed counter-agency.
  - 105:3 **طَيْرًا** ط ي ر B001: Light flight supplies mobility that contrasts with elephant-centered mass.
  - 105:3 **طَيْرًا** ط ي ر B002: Spreading after light movement supplies distributed coverage rather than one concentrated front.
  - 105:3 **أَبَابِيلَ** ء ب ل B003: Successive groups organize the light agents as plural waves rather than an undifferentiated flock.

## remote delivery as containment
- reading: The act becomes a coordinated remote operation that stops movement, saturates the target, and fixes the defeat in matter.
- mechanism: Directed throwing, hard matter, enclosure, full pouring, and fixing in a register combine into a model of saturation and closure. The Lord's فعل operates at range yet makes the advancing collective bounded and materially fixed.
- trace:
  - 105:1 **فَعَلَ** ف ع ل B001: The open deed receives a concrete delivery-and-containment mechanism from the context.
  - 105:4 **تَرْمِيهِم** ر م ي B001: Sending and throwing supply force delivered from a distance.

  - 105:4 **سِجِّيلٍ** س ج ل B001: A full vessel pouring out supplies saturation by repeated material delivery.
  - 105:4 **سِجِّيلٍ** س ج ل B004: A register that gathers and fixes supplies the exploratory image of defeat being made materially settled.

  - 105:5 **مَّأْكُولٍۭ** ء ك ل B006: Erosion and corruption supply progressive loss of material integrity.

# Focus 105:2

- reading: Their plan was made to enact its own failure by departing from the very aim for which it had been designed.
- mechanism: A deliberately contrived path toward an aim is itself converted into deviation from that aim. The failure is therefore internal to the scheme's operation: its means continue as means that no longer conduct their owners to their intended end.
- trace:

  - 105:2 **تَضْلِيلٍ** ض ل ل B001: Supplies deviation from guidance and intended course as the state imposed on the stratagem.

## spent effort lost

## operative disappearance
