## Parent Channels

### 1. P1 (19:1-15) - Guarded Continuity Across Bodily Limits
- Semantic invariant: Life is carried forward by transferring care, office, and vitality across a failing bodily threshold.
- Surface relation: direct; Zechariah's aged body and barren household frame the requested heir (19:4-8), while John receives care, authority, and a threefold life-cycle blessing (19:12-15).
- Surprising reach: The lineage scene expands into structural fatigue, animal husbandry, and the reanimation of something recumbent.

#### Subchannel A. The aging body as a failing, ignited structure
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A load-bearing frame weakens while whiteness spreads across its top like flame, snow, or frost.
- Active motifs: spent bodily force (`quranic:root_001687:B001/m01`); painful rib-and-shoulder frame (`quranic:root_001687:B003/m01`); hard bone (`quranic:root_001029:B005/m01`); head or uppermost part (`quranic:root_000529:B001/m01`); kindled flame (`quranic:root_000799:B001/m01`); white blaze across the forelock (`quranic:root_000799:B002/m01`); snow-whitened summits (`quranic:root_000833:B002/m01`); extended old age (`quranic:root_001281:B004/m01`); terminal senescence beyond repair (`quranic:root_000981:B002/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 19:4 (و ه ن, ع ظ م, ر ء س, ش ع ل, ش ي ب); 19:8 (ك ب ر, ع ت و)
- Synthesis: Zechariah's weakness is materially rendered as a frame whose bone, ribs, and upper member can no longer carry their former load. The head's grayness is not static color but a spreading ignition that also resembles frost on a summit, while old age names a terminal state that ordinary repair cannot reverse.

#### Subchannel B. Household office and inheritance
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A household seeks a successor who can receive property, knowledge, care, and governing responsibility.
- Active motifs: household and dependents gathered around a man (`quranic:root_000067:B003/m01`); inherited estate (`quranic:root_001639:B001/m01`); inherited knowledge or scripture (`quranic:root_001639:B003/m01`); guardianship and management (`quranic:root_001684:B003/m01`); kinship, manumission, and legal affiliation (`quranic:root_001684:B005/m01`); gradual nurture to completion (`quranic:root_000532:B002/m01`); unpriced gift (`quranic:root_001685:B001/m01`)
