# s032 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. P1 (32:1-10) Crafted and Delivered Discourse
- Semantic invariant: Discourse is treated as a made object whose integrity, delivery, and reception determine whether it guides or misleads.
- Surface relation: direct; 32:2-3 names the descending Book (`تنزيل`, `الكتاب`), the accusation of fabrication (`افتراه`), the truth (`الحق`), and the warning (`لتنذر`, `نذير`).
- Surprising reach: The contrast between revelation and forgery acquires the material language of writing, cutting, sewing, and tightly finished workmanship.

#### Subchannel A. The written dispatch
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A written artifact is delivered from above, enters voiced dispute, and presents a fixed claim.
- Active motifs: delivered Book (quranic:root_001492:B002/m01); written artifact (quranic:root_001283:B002/m01); spoken utterance (quranic:root_001272:B001/m01); reality fixed against falsehood (quranic:root_000347:B001/m01)
- Ayah anchors: 32:2 `تنزيل` (ن ز ل), `الكتاب` (ك ت ب); 32:3 `يقولون` (ق و ل), `الحق` (ح ق ق)
- Synthesis: The Book is not only information but a dispatched written object. Its descent gives it a direction of delivery, while its entry into speech makes the human accusation answer an already materialized record whose content stands fixed.

#### Subchannel B. Counterfeit seams and the alarm
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A counterfeit utterance is cut and pieced together, while sound workmanship and an alarm expose its instability.
- Active motifs: cutting for repair and sewing (quranic:root_001150:B001/m01); fabricated lie (quranic:root_001150:B003/m01); tightly woven speech (quranic:root_000347:B010/m01); disturbed doubt (quranic:root_000616:B001/m01); warning that awakens caution (quranic:root_001488:B001/m01)
- Ayah anchors: 32:2 `ريب` (ر ي ب); 32:3 `افتراه` (ف ر ي), `الحق` (ح ق ق), `لتنذر` and `نذير` (ن ذ ر)
- Synthesis: The accusation of fabrication activates both false invention and the older craft-image of cutting material for repair. Against that pieced counterfeit stands speech imagined as tightly made; the warning then functions like an alarm that agitates settled doubt into caution.
