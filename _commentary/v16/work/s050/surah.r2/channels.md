# s050 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. P1: Joined Forms and Re-Fitted Creation
- Semantic invariant: A whole becomes intelligible through the fitting, joining, and covering operations that give it form.
- Surface relation: direct; 50:6 presents the sky as built, adorned, and without fissures, while 50:15 frames renewed creation as the object of confusion.
- Surprising reach: The lexical movement from construction to tailoring makes renewed creation resemble the replacement of a worn covering, not the recovery of an unstructured mass.

#### Subchannel A. The Joined and Adorned Canopy
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Parts are joined into a raised covering, beautified, and inspected for gaps.
- Active motifs: building by joining parts `quranic:root_000156:B001/m01`; the sky as what rises and covers `quranic:root_000745:B004/m01`; beautifying a formed object `quranic:root_000660:B002/m01`; a cleft or gap in a whole `quranic:root_001139:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 50:6 `بَنَيْنَٰهَا`, `ٱلسَّمَآءِ`, `زَيَّنَّٰهَا`, `فُرُوجٍ`
- Synthesis: The sky is not merely a large object overhead. It is a fitted canopy whose coherence depends on joined components, visible finish, and the absence of ruptures. Adornment and gaplessness therefore operate as evidence that fabrication has reached a stable whole.

#### Subchannel B. Creation as a Replaced Garment
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A form is measured and made, an old covering is displaced, and a new garment supplies the image of renewed embodiment.
- Active motifs: measuring before cutting or forming `quranic:root_000434:B001/m01`; creation as fabrication `quranic:root_000434:B002/m01`; a worn garment `quranic:root_000434:B009/m01`; a new garment `quranic:root_000227:B004/m01`; clothing as a covering `quranic:root_001341:B001/m01`; confusion produced by mixing `quranic:root_001341:B003/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 50:15 `بِٱلْخَلْقِ ٱلْأَوَّلِ`, `خَلْقٍ جَدِيدٍ`, `لَبْسٍ`
- Synthesis: The question of a new creation acquires a material process: measure, fabricate, cover, and replace. The same lexical field that names confusion also supplies clothing, so perplexity about re-creation is reframed as failure to recognize that a formed covering can be renewed.
