# s078 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. P1 (78:1-16): Contested News Moving Toward Disclosure
- Semantic invariant: Information moves from reciprocal demand through transmission and marking toward disclosure, while counterfeit or obstructed speech interrupts that movement.
- Surface relation: direct; 78:1-5 anchors reciprocal questioning (`س ء ل`), the great report (`ن ب ء`), disagreement (`خ ل ف`), and eventual knowing (`ع ل م`).
- Surprising reach: The report is materialized as something requested, delivered, extracted from concealment, marked like a route, and vulnerable to both forgery and vocal blockage.

#### Subchannel A. Question, Requested Object, and Answered Need
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Interlocutors exchange questions, identify a sought object, and move toward satisfaction of the request through an arriving report and acquired knowledge.
- Active motifs: asking and requesting (`quranic:root_000661:B001/m01`); the sought need (`quranic:root_000661:B002/m01`); satisfying a request (`quranic:root_000661:B003/m01`); reciprocal questioning (`quranic:root_000661:B004/m01`); transmitted report (`quranic:root_001464:B002/m01`); disclosure to a knower (`quranic:root_001040:B001/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 78:1 `س ء ل` (يَتَسَآءَلُونَ); 78:2 `ن ب ء` (ٱلنَّبَإِ); 78:4-5 `ع ل م` (سَيَعْلَمُونَ).
- Synthesis: The opening inquiry is not only talk about a topic: its lexical field supplies a requester, a requested object, reciprocal exchange, and fulfillment. The report then functions as the delivered answer whose content becomes uncovered knowledge.

#### Subchannel B. Announcement, Marker, and Extraction from Concealment
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: An authorized announcer delivers information, a visible mark guides recognition, and hidden content is brought out and set in its proper place.
- Active motifs: divine announcer and prophetic transmission (`quranic:root_001464:B003/m01`); guiding mark or landmark (`quranic:root_001040:B002/m01`); bringing a hidden thing out (`quranic:root_000400:B002/m01`); delivered descent (`quranic:root_001492:B002/m01`); clear road over easy ground (`quranic:root_001464:B007/m01`); placing a thing in its fitting station (`quranic:root_001492:B004/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 78:2 `ن ب ء` (ٱلنَّبَإِ); 78:4-5 `ع ل م` (سَيَعْلَمُونَ); 78:14 `ن ز ل` (أَنزَلْنَا); 78:15 `خ ر ج` (لِّنُخْرِجَ).
- Synthesis: Announcement is rendered as guided emergence: what was inaccessible is delivered downward, extracted into view, and recognized by a mark that also behaves like a route indicator. Knowledge thus acquires spatial direction and an appointed placement.

#### Subchannel C. Counterfeit Claim, Broken Promise, and Degraded Account
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Rival accounts diverge, a promise fails to arrive, fabricated speech masquerades as creation, and a false claimant assumes prophetic authority.
- Active motifs: divergent paths and disputed accounts (`quranic:root_000433:B004/m01`); failed promise or missed appointment (`quranic:root_000433:B005/m01`); degraded successor or poor speech (`quranic:root_000433:B008/m01`); fabricated discourse (`quranic:root_000434:B007/m01`); false prophetic claim (`quranic:root_001464:B004/m01`); the liar called a lamp (`quranic:root_000693:B005/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 78:2 `ن ب ء` (ٱلنَّبَإِ); 78:3 `خ ل ف` (مُخْتَلِفُونَ); 78:8 `خ ل ق` (خَلَقْنَٰكُمْ); 78:13 `س ر ج` (سِرَاجًۭا).
- Synthesis: Disagreement opens into a full counterfeit-production scene: paths separate, promised correspondence fails, and an invented account can even claim revelatory status. Calling a liar a “lamp” sharply pressures the surface lamp image by distinguishing illumination from persuasive glare.

#### Subchannel D. Faint Report and Constricted Tongue
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A message ranges from forceful oratory to a barely perceptible sound, while dryness, wrapping, and blockage impede its vocal instrument.
- Active motifs: faint ambiguous sound (`quranic:root_001464:B006/m01`); forceful eloquent speaker (`quranic:root_000195:B003/m01`); thirst-constricted tongue (`quranic:root_001019:B014/m01`); wrapped, heavy tongue (`quranic:root_001365:B004/m01`); speech made difficult (`quranic:root_000217:B009/m01`); thickened tongue as bodily organ (`quranic:root_001029:B003/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 78:2 `ن ب ء` (ٱلنَّبَإِ); 78:7 `ج ب ل` (ٱلْجِبَالَ); 78:14 `ع ص ر` (ٱلْمُعْصِرَٰتِ), `ث ج ج` (ثَجَّاجًۭا); 78:16 `ل ف ف` (أَلْفَافًا).
- Synthesis: The report’s communicative force is tested at the level of sound production. Eloquent release stands opposite a tongue tightened by thirst, heaviness, or obstruction, while the report itself may arrive only as a faint, uncertain sound.

### 2. P1 (78:1-16): A Habitable Frame Prepared, Fastened, and Covered
- Semantic invariant: Habitable space is produced by preparing a lower surface, fixing structural supports, assembling an overhead frame, and adding a protective covering.
- Surface relation: direct; 78:6-12 names the earth as a bed, mountains as pegs, and seven strong structures built above, while 78:10 makes night a garment.
- Surprising reach: The cosmic arrangement repeatedly takes the scale of a cradle, tent, rib cage, woven shield, and fitted sleeping cloth.

#### Subchannel A. Leveled Ground as Bed, Rug, and Cradle
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A lower surface is softened, leveled, spread, and prepared to receive a resting body.
- Active motifs: lower earth opposite the sky (`quranic:root_000025:B001/m01`); thick woolen rug (`quranic:root_000025:B005/m01`); infant cradle (`quranic:root_001451:B001/m01`); spread and leveled bedding (`quranic:root_001451:B002/m01`); preparing and regularizing a matter (`quranic:root_001451:B003/m01`); smooth even surface (`quranic:root_000434:B008/m01`); softness and levelness (`quranic:root_000665:B008/m01`).

- Synthesis: The earth-bed is a worked receiving surface rather than inert ground. Rug, cradle, smoothing, and preparation make its suitability an achieved condition, with sleep supplying the body for which that condition is made.

### 3. P1 (78:1-16): Cessation, Recovery, and Alternating Time
- Semantic invariant: Activity is interrupted so that bodies and systems can rest, recover, and re-enter an alternating temporal cycle.
- Surface relation: direct; 78:9-11 moves from sleep and cessation through night-cover to daylight livelihood.
- Surprising reach: Sleep approaches death and burial, while wakefulness is figured as returning strength and stagnant systems are tested by whether circulation resumes.

- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: Motion is cut, sleep overcomes the body, and repose permits awareness and strength to return.
- Active motifs: ordinary sleep and recumbency (`quranic:root_001568:B001/m01`); sleep overcoming the person (`quranic:root_001568:B002/m01`); trusting repose in something (`quranic:root_001568:B005/m01`); stillness and rest (`quranic:root_000665:B001/m01`); cutting and removing activity (`quranic:root_000665:B002/m01`); recovery of awareness and strength (`quranic:root_001188:B005/m01`); prepared cradle (`quranic:root_001451:B001/m01`).
