# s038 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. Voiced Authority and Distorted Reception (P1, 38:1-16)
- Semantic invariant: A claim becomes socially operative through recitation, warning, counter-speech, and the hearer's mode of reception.
- Surface relation: direct; 38:1 anchors the recited `قُرْءَانِ` and `ذِّكْرِ`, while 38:4-8 stage `مُّنذِرٌ`, `سَٰحِرٌ`, `كَذَّابٌ`, `سَمِعْ`, and `شَكٍّ`.
- Surprising reach: The dispute is not only over propositions; lexical senses materialize an acoustic chain in which speech can awaken, divert, cover, split, or leave two possibilities interpenetrating.
