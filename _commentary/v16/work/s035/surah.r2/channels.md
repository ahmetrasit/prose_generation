# s035 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. P1 (35:1-14) Aperture, Containment, and Flow
- Semantic invariant: Matter or force becomes effective through a controlled relation between opening, channel, boundary, and release.
- Surface relation: direct; 35:2 `يَفْتَحِ/يُمْسِكْ`, 35:9 `تُثِيرُ/أَرْسَلَ`, 35:12 `ٱلْفُلْكَ ... مَوَاخِرَ`, and 35:13 `يَجْرِى`.
- Surprising reach: Mercy, rain, and maritime motion acquire a concrete hydraulic architecture of apertures, water-holding cavities, irrigated ground, and channels cut by moving water.

#### Subchannel A. Opening into release
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A closure splits or widens, a latent content erupts, and what was folded or confined spreads into an open course.
- Active motifs: opened closure (quranic:root_001124:B001/m01); water issuing from an outlet (quranic:root_001124:B005/m01); split or fissure (quranic:root_001165:B001/m01); unfolding and branching spread (quranic:root_001503:B001/m01); eruption from quiescence (quranic:root_000210:B001/m01); widening until flow becomes possible (quranic:root_001559:B003/m01)
- Ayah anchors: 35:1 `فَاطِرِ` (ف ط ر); 35:2 `يَفْتَحِ` (ف ت ح); 35:9 `تُثِيرُ/ٱلنُّشُورُ` (ث و ر/ن ش ر); 35:13 `ٱلنَّهَارِ` (ن ه ر).
- Synthesis: The opening of mercy is materialized as a breached closure whose contents emerge, spread, and acquire a widened outlet. The resurrection root at 35:9 participates not only in revival but in the physical act of unfolding what had been closed.

#### Subchannel B. Reservoir, well, and irrigated ground
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Water is caught in a depression or rock cavity, retained by a firm boundary, then conducted through soil until the ground is fit.
- Active motifs: water-gathering low ground (quranic:root_000086:B006/m01); rock cavity or newly dug well (quranic:root_000434:B011/m01); water-retaining site (quranic:root_001424:B004/m01); river cutting a channel (quranic:root_001559:B001/m01); water sent across soil to improve it (quranic:root_001405:B003/m01)
- Ayah anchors: 35:1, 35:3, 35:11 `خَلْقِ/خَٰلِقٍ/خَلَقَ` (خ ل ق); 35:2 `مُمْسِكَ/يُمْسِكْ` (م س ك); 35:12 `بَحْرَانِ/مَوَاخِرَ` (ب ح ر/م خ ر); 35:13 `ٱلنَّهَارِ` (ن ه ر).
- Synthesis: Retention and release become one irrigation mechanism: a firm cavity first keeps water from vanishing, while a cut channel later distributes it through the land. This gives the opening and withholding of 35:2 a material analogue in managed water.

#### Subchannel C. Ship cleaving a moving medium
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A vessel enters flowing water, cuts it under way, and is managed by a water-skilled operator.
- Active motifs: continuous flowing motion (quranic:root_000240:B001/m01); ship circulating on water (quranic:root_001177:B003/m01); hull cleaving water with motion and sound (quranic:root_001405:B001/m01); sailor and navigation craft (quranic:root_001442:B006/m01)
- Ayah anchors: 35:12 `ٱلْفُلْكَ/مَوَاخِرَ/مِلْحٌ` (ف ل ك/م خ ر/م ل ح); 35:13 `يَجْرِى` (ج ر ي).
- Synthesis: The visible ships of 35:12 form a complete transport scene: current, vessel, cleaving operation, and operator. The vessel does not merely occupy the sea; it converts resistance into a traversable course.

### 2. P1 (35:1-14) Atmospheric Delivery and Vegetal Renewal
- Semantic invariant: A mobile atmospheric carrier delivers water that reverses terrestrial dormancy into growth and yield.
- Surface relation: direct; 35:9 explicitly sends wind, raises cloud, and revives dead land, while 35:3 and 35:12 frame rain and produce as provision.
- Surprising reach: The resurrection analogy expands into fine-grained plant processes: water-laden cloud mass, returning green in dry herbage, bursting shoots, and tree growth after seasonal recession.

#### Subchannel A. Wind, cloud mass, and rain-bearing transport
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Wind moves through the air, draws a cloud body into view, and carries a layered load whose descent functions as provision.
- Active motifs: wind and moving air (quranic:root_000609:B003/m01); trailing cloud (quranic:root_000680:B002/m01); water-bearing cloud load (quranic:root_000357:B010/m01); cloud emergence and clearing sky (quranic:root_000400:B005/m01); layered cloud mass (quranic:root_000532:B008/m01); rain descending as provision (quranic:root_000560:B003/m01)
- Ayah anchors: 35:3 `يَرْزُقُ` (ر ز ق); 35:9 `ٱلرِّيَٰحَ/سَحَابًا` (ر و ح/س ح ب); 35:11 `تَحْمِلُ` (ح م ل); 35:12 `تَسْتَخْرِجُ` (خ ر ج); 35:13 `رَبُّ` (ر ب ب).
- Synthesis: Wind supplies motion, the cloud supplies a carried body, and rain supplies the delivered resource. The cloud is lexically a load and a layered mass, making atmospheric transport structurally parallel to the surah's other acts of sending and carrying.

#### Subchannel B. Dead ground returning to yield
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Unproductive ground receives rain, dry herbage greens again, tender shoots emerge, and the revived land produces a crop.
- Active motifs: soft fertile ground (quranic:root_000025:B002/m01); land's life through rain and vegetation (quranic:root_000383:B002/m01); dead unused land (quranic:root_001454:B003/m01); dry herbage revived by rain (quranic:root_001503:B004/m01); tender shoot emergence (quranic:root_000810:B004/m01); trees breaking into leaf and plants lengthening (quranic:root_000609:B017/m01); crop and tree yield (quranic:root_000043:B002/m01)
- Ayah anchors: 35:1, 35:3, 35:9 `أَرْضِ/أَرْضَ` (ء ر ض); 35:5, 35:9 `حَيَوٰةُ/أَحْيَيْ` (ح ي ي); 35:9 `مَّيِّتٍ/مَوْتِ/ٱلنُّشُورُ/ٱلرِّيَٰحَ` (م و ت/ن ش ر/ر و ح); 35:12 `تَشْكُرُ/تَأْكُلُ` (ش ك ر/ء ك ل).
- Synthesis: Revival is staged as a causal sequence rather than a generic fertility image: dead ground, rain contact, regreening, shoot emergence, and edible yield. The result makes resurrection legible through the mechanics of seasonal plant return.

### 3. P1 (35:1-14) Formation and Fixation
- Semantic invariant: An intended form is brought into being and then stabilized as an artifact, inscription, rule, or record.
- Surface relation: direct; 35:1 and 35:11 name making and creation, while 35:11 fixes lifespan in a `كِتَٰب`.
- Surprising reach: Creation and writing converge as two kinds of form-making: one shapes beings, while the other arranges marks and makes an order durable.

#### Subchannel A. Origination as deliberate making
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: An agent initiates, makes, and brings a formed entity into existence.
- Active motifs: making or producing a thing (quranic:root_000248:B001/m01); creative bringing-into-being (quranic:root_000434:B002/m01); performed manufacture (quranic:root_000885:B001/m01); origination and first emergence (quranic:root_001165:B002/m01)

- Synthesis: The motifs distinguish initiation, fabrication, and emergence while keeping them in one production scene. Creation appears as the controlled establishment of form, not only as an abstract assertion of existence.

#### Subchannel B. Inscription, decree, and roster
- Reading type: mixed

- Synthesis: The book of 35:11 activates several operations that should remain distinct: composing signs, fixing a decree, and entering a name in a roster. Retraced lines add the material act by which a mark is renewed so that its determination remains visible.

### 4. P1 (35:1-14) Reception and Report
- Semantic invariant: Meaning moves from disclosure through perception into response, report, or distortion.
- Surface relation: direct; 35:3 calls for recollection, 35:8 contrasts seeing and knowledge, 35:10 raises good speech, and 35:14 stages a call that is neither heard nor answered.
- Surprising reach: The scene includes both receptive organs and communication logistics: insight opens an obstruction, news is broadcast, speech returns as an answer, and falsehood is a report diverted from its proper direction.

#### Subchannel A. Sight becoming inward knowledge
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A visible object is perceived, reflected upon, and penetrated until its inner condition becomes known.
- Active motifs: sight by eye or insight (quranic:root_000531:B001/m01); reflective judgment of the heart (quranic:root_000531:B002/m01); disclosure to the knower (quranic:root_001040:B001/m01); knowledge of a report's interior (quranic:root_000387:B001/m01); accurate wisdom (quranic:root_000348:B003/m01); opening an intellectual obstruction (quranic:root_001124:B008/m01)
- Ayah anchors: 35:2 `حَكِيمُ/يَفْتَحِ` (ح ك م/ف ت ح); 35:8 `رَءَا/عَلِيمٌ` (ر ء ي/ع ل م); 35:11 `عِلْمِ` (ع ل م); 35:12 `تَرَى` (ر ء ي); 35:14 `خَبِيرٍ` (خ ب ر).
- Synthesis: Seeing is not the endpoint. The scene moves from visual contact to reflection, accurate judgment, and knowledge of what lies beneath the report's exterior, with opening serving as the mechanism that makes insight possible.

#### Subchannel B. Call, hearing, and returned answer

- Synthesis: The failed appeal to the invoked beings is measured against a complete response circuit: call, reception, comprehension, induced hearing, and returned speech. The surface denial removes each functional stage of that circuit.

#### Subchannel C. News circulated or turned into falsehood

- Synthesis: Communication is a transport scene whose cargo can remain true or be redirected. Broadcasting raises a report into public circulation, while lying is lexically a deflection that bends the report away from its proper referent.

### 7. P1 (35:1-14) Verticalized Power

- Surface relation: direct; 35:10 makes good speech ascend and righteous action raise it, while 35:1, 35:2, 35:10, and 35:13 name power, might, and dominion.
- Surprising reach: Physical elevation and political hierarchy are linked by a shared upward operation, but remain distinct scenes of movement and rule.

#### Subchannel A. Ascent and increasing height
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A body or utterance rises, gains height, and continues upward by increments.
- Active motifs: elevation and upwardness (quranic:root_000745:B001/m01); ascent toward a higher place (quranic:root_000862:B001/m01); increase in height or measure (quranic:root_000862:B008/m01); lifting a body from its place (quranic:root_000582:B001/m01)

#### Subchannel B. Rank, dominance, and dominion
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: An elevated position becomes recognized rank, coercive advantage, effective capacity, and rule over a domain.

### 8. P1 (35:1-14) Provision in Circulation

#### Subchannel B. Wage, assigned share, and continuing yield

### 9. P2 (35:15-30) Receptive Thresholds
- Semantic invariant: Perception depends on whether light, sound, and life can cross a receptive threshold.
- Surface relation: direct; 35:19-22 contrast blind and seeing, shadow and heat, living and dead, and hearing with the silence of graves.
- Surprising reach: The contrasts become mechanisms of access: inward insight, blinding noon, induced hearing, and the grave as a sealed or sunken enclosure.

#### Subchannel A. Eye, blindness, and inward sight
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: The eye receives a visible object, but full perception requires an inward faculty that can be present or absent independently of eyesight.
- Active motifs: eyesight (quranic:root_000121:B001/m01); insight of the heart (quranic:root_000121:B002/m01); loss of eyesight (quranic:root_001049:B001/m01); blindness of heart and judgment (quranic:root_001049:B002/m01); sight by eye or insight (quranic:root_000531:B001/m01); disclosure to the knower (quranic:root_001040:B001/m01)

- Synthesis: Physical sight and inward discernment form separate but coordinated operations. The contrast at 35:19 therefore reaches beyond ocular capacity to the heart's ability to recognize what vision presents.

#### Subchannel B. Light, shade, and blinding heat
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Illumination exposes, shade interposes a protective cover, and extreme noon heat can itself disable sight.
- Active motifs: illumination (quranic:root_001564:B001/m01); shade as covering (quranic:root_000966:B001/m01); overhead shade or canopy (quranic:root_000966:B003/m01); heat opposed to cold (quranic:root_000306:B001/m01); blinding midday heat (quranic:root_001049:B008/m01)

The counter-rite sense of spreading also reframes resurrection at 35:9 as an unbinding that no talisman performs.

### S27. Whole Surah (35:1-45) Written Manumission by Installments
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A person is held as transferable property under an owner's control; a written contract fixes staged payments, and completion changes the person's legal status from enslavement to freedom.
- Active motifs: enslaved person held as property (quranic:root_000973:B001/m01); ownership and power of disposal (quranic:root_001444:B002/m01); manumission contract with installment terms (quranic:root_001283:B005/m01); freedom and release from bondage (quranic:root_000306:B002/m01)
- Ayah anchors: 35:28, 35:31-32, 35:45 `عِبَادِ` (ع ب د); 35:1, 35:13 `مَلَٰٓئِكَةِ/مُلْكُ/يَمْلِكُ` (م ل ك); 35:11, 35:25, 35:29, 35:31-32, 35:40 `كِتَٰبٍ/كِتَٰبِ/كِتَٰبَ/كِتَٰبًا` (ك ت ب); 35:21, 35:33 `حَرُورُ/حَرِيرٌ` (ح ر ر).
- Synthesis: The contract materializes ownership, writing, payment, and release as a single status-changing practice. It usefully pressures the ownership claim at 35:13: contingent human title can be documented, transferred, and terminated, while the invoked partners possess not even the date-stone membrane. The Book-bearing servants of 35:31-32 are therefore not equated with chattel; the scene instead exposes how limited every created ownership arrangement is beside the surah's account of divine possession and human dependence.

### S29. Whole Surah (35:1-45) Wasting Illness, Recovery, and Relapse
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Illness takes hold of a body and wastes its flesh; the patient begins to recover, returns toward a former condition, and appears restored, but a relapse can return the disease or wound to a worse state.
- Active motifs: illness taking hold in the body (quranic:root_000018:B007/m01); wasting disease that strips flesh (quranic:root_000086:B011/m01); convalescence after emaciation (quranic:root_000152:B003/m01); return toward a better bodily condition (quranic:root_000544:B014/m01); recovery and renewed likeness after illness (quranic:root_001397:B012/m01); relapse after improvement (quranic:root_001096:B004/m01)
- Ayah anchors: 35:6, 35:26, 35:45 `ٱتَّخِذُ/أَخَذْ/يُؤَاخِذُ` (ء خ ذ); 35:12 `بَحْرَانِ` (ب ح ر); 35:4 `تُرْجَعُ` (ر ج ع); 35:14 `مِثْلُ` (م ث ل); 35:7, 35:28, 35:30, 35:34, 35:41 `مَّغْفِرَةٌ/غَفُورٌ/غَفُورًا` (غ ف ر). The surface root ب ل ل is unavailable in the bundle, so its convalescence motif cannot receive a token-level anchor.
- Synthesis: This reversible bodily course usefully pressures the surah's two terminal dwellings. Ordinary relief may follow wasting and still relapse; by contrast, fatigue cannot reach the settled inhabitants in 35:35, while the punishment of 35:36 is neither ended by death nor lightened into convalescence. The relapse sense attached to the forgiveness root sharpens permanent relief as more than a temporary improvement.
