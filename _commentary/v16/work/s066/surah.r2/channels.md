# s066 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. Boundaries That Bind, Protect, and Release
- Semantic invariant: A legitimate boundary is made by binding, enclosure, or measured obligation and is changed only by an equally legible act of release.
- Surface relation: direct; 66:1 `تُحَرِّمُ`/`أَحَلَّ`, 66:2 `فَرَضَ`/`تَحِلَّةَ`/`أَيْمَانِكُمْ`, 66:5 `طَلَّقَ`/`ثَيِّبَاتٍ`/`أَبْكَارًا`, and 66:12 `أَحْصَنَتْ`/`فَرْجَهَا`.
- Surprising reach: The legal and marital language materializes as knots, fences, fortifications, hand-pledges, guarded apertures, and controlled exits.

#### Subchannel A. From interdiction to an opened knot
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A perimeter is imposed, measured, and guarded; release unties the fastening rather than erasing the boundary.
- Active motifs: encompassing restraint (quranic:root_000296:B001/m01); prohibition (quranic:root_000313:B001/m01); inviolable right (quranic:root_000313:B006/m01); corrective restraint (quranic:root_000348:B001/m01); untying a knot (quranic:root_000351:B001/m01); dissolution of a ban (quranic:root_000351:B003/m01); bounded obligation (quranic:root_001144:B002/m01); release from a tether (quranic:root_000946:B001/m01); unrestricted licitness (quranic:root_000946:B005/m01).
- Ayah anchors: 66:1 `تُحَرِّمُ` and `أَحَلَّ`; 66:2 `فَرَضَ`; 66:5 `طَلَّقَ`; 66:6 `حِجَارَةُ`.
- Synthesis: Prohibition is an enclosing act, while obligation cuts a determinate limit into conduct. The corresponding release is tactile: a knot opens, a tether is let go, and a barred action becomes traversable. This gives the opening correction a mechanics of lawful boundary adjustment rather than simple permission versus denial.

- Surface relation: direct; 66:1/3/5 names spouses, 66:6 joins self and family, 66:10 places wives under righteous servants, and 66:11-12 invoke house-building and daughterhood.
- Surprising reach: Kinship appears as architecture with ribs and pillars, while food, welcome, guardianship, and clientage keep that architecture inhabited.
