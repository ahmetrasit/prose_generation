# HFT: earlier activation hypotheses, per focus ayah of surah 93

# Focus 93:1

## duha extended ascent
- reading: An oath by daylight already risen and still unfolding, a span in which delayed things can mature.
- mechanism: The oath fixes attention on an interval that has risen and continues. Duration is part of the image, so the moment can carry maturation and the arrival of what was delayed.
- trace:
  - 93:1 **وَٱلضُّحَىٰ** ض ح و B001: Risen forenoon daylight extending through the day supplies the model's sustained temporal span.

## duha public exposure
- reading: The forenoon is the condition in which things become exposed, prominent, and publicly legible.
- mechanism: Light is a field of manifestation: what had no visible standing comes out into an exposed, shared arena. The oath concerns appearance and publicity as much as luminosity.
- trace:
  - 93:1 **وَٱلضُّحَىٰ** ض ح و B002: Sun-exposure and visible prominence turn forenoon into an arena of disclosure and public standing.

## duha provisioning window
- reading: The named time is a daily provisioning window structured by hunger, movement, and nourishment.
- mechanism: The time is organized by embodied need: animals go out, food is sought, and a meal is taken. The oath can therefore evoke a recurring window in which life obtains provision.
- trace:
  - 93:1 **وَٱلضُّحَىٰ** ض ح و B003: Forenoon eating and early grazing supply a material cycle of seeking and receiving nourishment.

## duha clear legibility
- reading: Duha is brightness as clarification: the world becomes distinguishable enough to read and navigate.
- mechanism: Brightness removes visual ambiguity and gives contours to what is present. The oath can foreground a condition of discernibility, not simply a quantity of light.
- trace:
  - 93:1 **وَٱلضُّحَىٰ** ض ح و B005: Clear forenoon brightness supplies the mechanism by which obscured forms become distinct and readable.

## duha gentle delay
- reading: The oath can carry a patient tempo: what is delayed may be handled gently rather than abandoned.
- mechanism: The interval itself models patient handling. Delay need not be neglect; it can be the gentle pacing by which an outcome is allowed to arrive without force.
- trace:
  - 93:1 **وَٱلضُّحَىٰ** ض ح و B006: Gentle slowing and respite make forenoon a temporal image of non-hasty care.

## cycle visibility and cover
- reading: Duha is the returning visible phase of a rhythm that also contains stillness and covering; its force does not require continuous exposure.
- mechanism: The oath-pair places exposed, extending forenoon beside night in its settling and covering phase. Visibility and concealment become alternating modes in one rhythm; the cessation of exposure is not the cessation of the cycle.
- trace:

  - 93:2 **سَجَىٰ** س ج و B002: Covering with a cloth sharpens the image of light being veiled without being destroyed.

## pause not relational exit
- reading: The delayed or veiled light becomes a test case for pause without rejection: obscurity does not entail that care has exited.
- mechanism: The denial of leaving and aversion, framed by enduring nurture, converts the gentle-delay baseline into a relational diagnostic. A quiet interval can be repose, but it is not abandonment, expulsion, or the heart's withdrawal.
- trace:
  - 93:1 **وَٱلضُّحَىٰ** ض ح و B006: Gentle delay supplies a positive account of temporal pause before later denials exclude harsher accounts.
  - 93:3 **وَدَّعَكَ** و د ع B001: Leaving and relinquishing supply the rupture that the verse explicitly negates.

# Focus 93:2

## settling closure
- reading: An oath by the threshold at which night actively settles, closes in, and quiets a moving field.
- mechanism: Darkness supplies the field while motion in night, sea, wind, and gaze subsides together; the scene closes by becoming still.
- trace:
  - 93:2 **وَٱلَّيْلِ** ل ي ل B001: Night opposed to day supplies the dark temporal field in which the transition occurs.
  - 93:2 **سَجَىٰ** س ج و B001: Still settling across night, water, wind, or gaze supplies the event of motion closing down.

## draped cover
- reading: Night draws itself over the scene like cloth, ambiguously concealing, sheltering, or shrouding it.
- mechanism: Night behaves like a covering drawn across what was visible. The image retains an unresolved range from sheltering cloth to concealing shroud.
- trace:
  - 93:2 **وَٱلَّيْلِ** ل ي ل B001: Night's darkness supplies the wide surface-like medium that can cover the visible world.
  - 93:2 **سَجَىٰ** س ج و B002: Draping a cloth, including a shroud, turns nocturnal darkening into a material act of covering.

## night as operation
- reading: Night itself enters as an actor and performs the quieting of the scene.
- mechanism: Night is not only scenery but an operation: it arrives, takes up the field, and performs a settling upon it.
- trace:
  - 93:2 **وَٱلَّيْلِ** ل ي ل B002: Entering or doing something by night supplies an active nocturnal mode rather than passive clock-time.
  - 93:2 **سَجَىٰ** س ج و B001: Settling and closing-in gives that nocturnal mode its operative effect on the surrounding field.

## hidden yield
