# s093 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. Temporal Passage and Emergence
- Semantic invariant: Things occupy phases, move through ordered succession, and become manifest after absence.
- Surface relation: direct; 93:1 `ٱلضُّحَىٰ`, 93:2 `ٱلَّيْلِ` and `سَجَىٰ`, 93:4 `ٱلْءَاخِرَةُ` and `ٱلْأُولَىٰ`, and 93:11 `فَحَدِّثْ`.
- Surprising reach: The day-night sequence extends into reversal, ontological emergence, fresh life, and sudden incident.

#### Subchannel A. Daylight, Night, and Stilling
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: Bright daytime opens and extends, then night covers the scene and settles into stillness.
- Active motifs: extended forenoon `ض ح و:B001/m01`; clear forenoon brightness `ض ح و:B005/m01`; night and darkness `ل ي ل:B001/m01`; night settling into stillness `س ج و:B001/m01`; daylight opening `ن ه ر:B002/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 93:1 `ٱلضُّحَىٰ`; 93:2 `ٱلَّيْلِ`, `سَجَىٰ`; 93:10 `تَنْهَرْ`
- Synthesis: Forenoon supplies both duration and radiance, while night supplies darkness and `سَجَىٰ` supplies the act of becoming still. The lexical opening of daylight completes a cycle in which illumination expands, darkness arrives, and motion subsides.

#### Subchannel B. Firstness, Laterness, and Return
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A sequence begins, reaches a later phase, may be deferred, and turns back toward its outcome.
- Active motifs: first position or onset `ء و ل:B001/m01`; later or subsequent state `ء خ ر:B001/m01`; deferment `ء خ ر:B002/m01`; return to outcome `ء و ل:B002/m01`; backward movement `ق ه ر:B005/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 93:4 `ٱلْءَاخِرَةُ`, `ٱلْأُولَىٰ`; 93:9 `تَقْهَرْ`
- Synthesis: Firstness and laterness establish ordered phases. Deferment lengthens the interval, while return to an eventual end and backward motion give that order a reversible shape rather than a static before-after contrast.

#### Subchannel C. Coming into Being and Disclosure
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Something absent enters existence, is encountered as real, and is brought into view or speech.
- Active motifs: origination after nonbeing `ح د ث:B001/m01`; existent entity `و ج د:B002/m01`; manifestation and showing `ح د ث:B006/m01`; sudden incident `ح د ث:B005/m01`; initial emergence `ء و ل:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 93:4 `ٱلْأُولَىٰ`; 93:6-8 `يَجِدْكَ` and `وَجَدَكَ`; 93:11 `فَحَدِّثْ`
- Synthesis: Beginning marks entry into a phase, occurrence marks entry into reality, existence makes the new thing available to encounter, and disclosure makes it publicly perceptible. A sudden incident is the disruptive form of the same emergence process.

#### Subchannel D. Freshness and Early Life
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A life or phase remains close to its beginning and carries the freshness of recent arrival.
- Active motifs: youth and recentness `ح د ث:B002/m01`; newly delivered ewe `ر ب ب:B009/m01`; fresh beginning or early youth `ر ب ب:B009/m02`; first phase `ء و ل:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 93:3, 93:5, 93:11 `رَبُّكَ` and `رَبِّكَ`; 93:4 `ٱلْأُولَىٰ`; 93:11 `فَحَدِّثْ`
- Synthesis: Recent birth, youth, and firstness converge on a scene whose defining state is proximity to origin. Freshness is therefore temporal as well as bodily.

### 2. Orientation, Search, and Passage
- Semantic invariant: A participant moves relative to a course, loses or recovers orientation, and reaches either a destination or retreat.
- Surface relation: direct; 93:3 `وَدَّعَكَ`, 93:6-8 `يَجِدْكَ` and `وَجَدَكَ`, and 93:7 `ضَآلًّا` and `فَهَدَىٰ`.
- Surprising reach: Moral guidance opens into physical finding, gait, front-rear orientation, farewell, and refuge.

#### Subchannel A. Straying and Gentle Guidance
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: A traveler departs from the intended course and is gently directed back toward the road, purpose, or truth.
- Active motifs: deviation from the way `ض ل ل:B001/m01`; gentle direction to road or truth `ه د ي:B001/m01`; course, manner, and intended direction `ه د ي:B002/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 93:7 `ضَآلًّا`, `فَهَدَىٰ`
- Synthesis: Deviation supplies the displaced participant, guidance supplies the directing action, and the course or manner supplies the route that makes correction intelligible. The scene works equally as road-finding and restoration of purpose.

#### Subchannel B. Hidden, Lost, and Found
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: An object disappears from view or memory, becomes hard to locate, and is recovered through finding.
- Active motifs: concealment or disappearance `ض ل ل:B002/m01`; lost object `ض ل ل:B003/m01`; forgotten object `ض ل ل:B004/m01`; finding and locating `و ج د:B001/m01`; directionless lost creature `ع ي ل:B008/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 93:6-8 `يَجِدْكَ` and `وَجَدَكَ`; 93:7 `ضَآلًّا`; 93:8 `عَآئِلًا`
- Synthesis: Disappearance, loss of location, and loss from memory are distinct ways an object becomes unavailable. Finding reverses each condition, while the directionless creature gives the search a mobile participant.

#### Subchannel C. Forward Motion and Agility
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A moving body rises lightly, advances from the front, and proceeds on foot.
- Active motifs: light rising or swift advance `ق ل ي:B001/m01`; pedestrian movement `ن ع م:B012/m01`; leading front `ه د ي:B003/m01`; rear or hindpart `ء خ ر:B003/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 93:3 `قَلَىٰ`; 93:4 `ٱلْءَاخِرَةُ`; 93:7 `فَهَدَىٰ`; 93:11 `بِنِعْمَةِ`
- Synthesis: Lightness initiates advance, the leading front gives it orientation, walking realizes the passage, and the hindpart establishes the axis along which forward movement can be recognized.

#### Subchannel D. Swaying, Support, and Delayed Pace
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A weak or burdened walker advances slowly, sways, and may depend on companions for support.
- Active motifs: swaying gait `ع ي ل:B003/m01`; supported swaying walk `ه د ي:B008/m01`; delayed progress `ي ت م:B004/m01`; composed unhurried bearing `ه د ي:B010/m01`; overpowering impediment `ع ي ل:B009/m01`; weak or heavy participant `ه د ي:B009/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 93:6 and 93:9 `يَتِيمًا`, `ٱلْيَتِيمَ`; 93:7 `فَهَدَىٰ`; 93:8 `عَآئِلًا`
- Synthesis: Weakness and impediment slow the traveler; bodily swaying shows the unstable gait; reliance on others supplies a walking mechanism; and composure converts slowness from mere incapacity into measured bearing.

#### Subchannel E. Farewell, Retreat, and Refuge
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A traveler departs with farewell, may reverse course, and finally joins a sheltering destination.
- Active motifs: farewell at departure `و د ع:B003/m01`; night travel `ل ي ل:B002/m01`; backward retreat `ق ه ر:B005/m01`; return toward an end `ء و ل:B002/m01`; joining a refuge `ء و ي:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 93:2 `ٱلَّيْلِ`; 93:3 `وَدَّعَكَ`; 93:4 `ٱلْأُولَىٰ`; 93:6 `فَـَٔاوَىٰ`; 93:9 `تَقْهَرْ`
- Synthesis: Farewell begins the journey, night supplies its setting, retreat reverses its direction, and return gives the movement an endpoint. Refuge closes the passage by gathering the traveler into a protected place.

### 3. Care, Need, and Provision
- Semantic invariant: Vulnerability is encountered and answered through shelter, nurture, provision, and responsive giving rather than neglect or rebuke.
- Surface relation: direct; 93:5 `يُعْطِيكَ`, 93:6 `يَتِيمًا` and `فَـَٔاوَىٰ`, 93:8 `عَآئِلًا` and `فَأَغْنَىٰ`, 93:9 `ٱلْيَتِيمَ`, 93:10 `ٱلسَّآئِلَ` and `تَنْهَرْ`, and 93:11 `بِنِعْمَةِ`.
- Surprising reach: The surface sequence of rescue and command expands into a full economy of guardianship, sufficiency, fulfilled requests, and anti-neglect.

#### Subchannel A. The Orphan Received into Care
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: A child lacking a protector is found, sheltered, and raised under continuing care.
- Active motifs: orphaned child `ي ت م:B001/m01`; finding the vulnerable person `و ج د:B001/m01`; sheltering another `ء و ي:B001/m02`; gradual nurture `ر ب ب:B002/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 93:3, 93:5, 93:11 `رَبُّكَ` and `رَبِّكَ`; 93:6-8 `يَجِدْكَ` and `وَجَدَكَ`; 93:6 `فَـَٔاوَىٰ`; 93:6 and 93:9 `يَتِيمًا`, `ٱلْيَتِيمَ`
- Synthesis: Orphanhood names the broken protective relation, finding brings the child into view, sheltering restores place and belonging, and gradual nurture turns a moment of rescue into sustained care.

#### Subchannel B. Poverty Changed into Sufficiency
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: A person in need receives enough resources and capacity to leave dependence behind.
- Active motifs: poverty and need `ع ي ل:B001/m01`; poor nourishment `ع ي ل:B004/m01`; wealth or independence `غ ن ي:B001/m01`; sufficiency and useful adequacy `غ ن ي:B002/m01`; material capacity `و ج د:B003/m01`; good condition `ن ع م:B001/m01`; state or circumstance `ء و ل:B007/m01`; need `ر ب ب:B016/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 93:3, 93:5, 93:11 `رَبُّكَ` and `رَبِّكَ`; 93:4 `ٱلْأُولَىٰ`; 93:6-8 `يَجِدْكَ` and `وَجَدَكَ`; 93:8 `عَآئِلًا`, `فَأَغْنَىٰ`; 93:11 `بِنِعْمَةِ`
- Synthesis: Poverty and inadequate nourishment define the initial circumstance. Wealth, capacity, and practical sufficiency are distinct outcomes: possession increases, need falls, and the person becomes able to stand without dependence.

#### Subchannel C. Request, Grant, and Gentle Reception
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A requester names a need, seeks a gift, receives a grant, and is not driven away by harsh speech.
- Active motifs: asking and seeking `س ء ل:B001/m01`; desired object `س ء ل:B002/m01`; fulfillment of a request `س ء ل:B003/m01`; giving `ع ط و:B002/m01`; solicitation for a gift `ع ط و:B005/m01`; generous gift `خ ي ر:B005/m01`; affectionate present `ه د ي:B004/m01`; harsh rebuke `ن ه ر:B004/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 93:4 `خَيْرٌ`; 93:5 `يُعْطِيكَ`; 93:7 `فَهَدَىٰ`; 93:10 `ٱلسَّآئِلَ`, `تَنْهَرْ`
- Synthesis: The requested object gives asking a goal, solicitation initiates transfer, giving and the affectionate present answer it, and fulfillment completes it. Harsh rebuke is the rejected counter-action that would break this exchange at its opening.

#### Subchannel D. Stewardship against Coercion and Neglect
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A responsible holder governs and repairs what is under care instead of abandoning, ignoring, or overpowering it.
- Active motifs: lordship and responsible possession `ر ب ب:B001/m01`; governance and reform `ء و ل:B004/m01`; abandonment `و د ع:B001/m01`; neglect or omission `ي ت م:B003/m01`; refusal to touch or attend `س ج و:B004/m01`; coercive domination `ق ه ر:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 93:2 `سَجَىٰ`; 93:3 `وَدَّعَكَ`, `رَبُّكَ`; 93:4 `ٱلْأُولَىٰ`; 93:5 and 93:11 `رَبُّكَ`, `رَبِّكَ`; 93:6 and 93:9 `يَتِيمًا`, `ٱلْيَتِيمَ`; 93:9 `تَقْهَرْ`
- Synthesis: Lordship and governance establish responsibility for another's condition. Abandonment, non-attendance, and neglect withdraw that responsibility, while coercion converts it into harmful power. Repair is the positive operation that opposes both failures.

### 4. Household, Estate, and Custody
- Semantic invariant: People, property, and dependents are gathered into durable relations of maintenance, service, guardianship, and entrusted keeping.
- Surface relation: indirect; 93:3, 93:5, and 93:11 `رَبُّكَ` and `رَبِّكَ`, 93:6 and 93:9 `يَتِيمًا` and `ٱلْيَتِيمَ`, and 93:8 `عَآئِلًا`.
- Surprising reach: Care expands from the orphan into family organization, foster relations, marriage, estate labor, and deposit custody.

#### Subchannel A. Household, Dependents, and Service
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A household head gathers family and followers, maintains dependents, and assigns service for their needs.
- Active motifs: household and retinue `ء و ل:B003/m01`; dependents `ع ي ل:B002/m01`; service and handing what is needed `ع ط و:B003/m01`; labor placed at another's disposal `و د ع:B009/m01`; gathered multitude `ر ب ب:B004/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 93:3, 93:5, 93:11 `رَبُّكَ` and `رَبِّكَ`; 93:3 `وَدَّعَكَ`; 93:4 `ٱلْأُولَىٰ`; 93:5 `يُعْطِيكَ`; 93:8 `عَآئِلًا`
- Synthesis: The household supplies the social container, dependents supply those maintained within it, and service supplies the repeated transfer of labor and necessities. The multitude motif scales the same relation from one family to a gathered group.

#### Subchannel B. Foster Parent and Ward
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A young dependent from an earlier household is received by a foster parent or nurse and raised as a ward.
- Active motifs: stepchild or ward `ر ب ب:B005/m01`; foster parent, nurse, or caregiver `ر ب ب:B005/m02`; orphaned child `ي ت م:B001/m01`; youthful recent age `ح د ث:B002/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 93:3, 93:5, 93:11 `رَبُّكَ` and `رَبِّكَ`; 93:6 and 93:9 `يَتِيمًا`, `ٱلْيَتِيمَ`; 93:11 `فَحَدِّثْ`
- Synthesis: Youth and orphanhood describe the ward's vulnerability, while the paired ward-caregiver senses supply a replacement relation of nurture. The scene centers on social incorporation rather than biological descent.

#### Subchannel C. Bride, Spouse, and Marital Independence
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A woman moves from singleness into marriage, is conducted to a spouse, and gains a recognized household position.
- Active motifs: bride conducted to her spouse `ه د ي:B006/m01`; married or self-sufficient woman `غ ن ي:B005/m01`; marriage as protection from singleness `غ ن ي:B006/m01`; woman apart from a husband `ي ت م:B005/m01`; personal beauty or excellence `خ ي ر:B002/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 93:4 `خَيْرٌ`; 93:6 and 93:9 `يَتِيمًا`, `ٱلْيَتِيمَ`; 93:7 `فَهَدَىٰ`; 93:8 `فَأَغْنَىٰ`
- Synthesis: Singleness supplies the initial social state, bridal conduct supplies the transition, marriage supplies the new bond, and marital sufficiency names its protective or independent outcome. Beauty enters as a personal quality within that marital scene.

#### Subchannel D. Estate Repair and Directed Labor
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A holder directly manages an estate, repairs its condition, and deploys labor to keep it productive.
- Active motifs: direct management of an estate `س ج و:B005/m01`; estate ordering and repair `ء و ل:B004/m02`; repair, completion, and oversight `ر ب ب:B002/m01`; labor made available for a task `و د ع:B009/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 93:2 `سَجَىٰ`; 93:3, 93:5, 93:11 `رَبُّكَ` and `رَبِّكَ`; 93:3 `وَدَّعَكَ`; 93:4 `ٱلْأُولَىٰ`
- Synthesis: Direct management identifies the setting, ordering supplies administrative control, repair restores the estate's condition, and deployed labor carries the work into practice.

#### Subchannel E. Deposit and Safekeeping
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Property is handed over, held under another's care, maintained, and returned at a later time.
- Active motifs: entrusted deposit `و د ع:B005/m01`; custodial oversight and repair `ر ب ب:B002/m01`; ordered estate keeping `ء و ل:B004/m02`; deferred transfer `ء خ ر:B002/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 93:3 `وَدَّعَكَ`, `رَبُّكَ`; 93:4 `ٱلْءَاخِرَةُ`, `ٱلْأُولَىٰ`; 93:5 and 93:11 `رَبُّكَ`, `رَبِّكَ`
- Synthesis: Deposit creates the custodial relation, oversight preserves the entrusted object, estate ordering keeps it within an accountable arrangement, and deferment explains why possession and final transfer occur at different times.

### 5. Covenant, Assent, and Power
- Semantic invariant: Social relations are stabilized through pact, consent, protection, and surety, or ruptured through coercion and hostile dominance.
- Surface relation: direct; 93:3 `قَلَىٰ`, 93:5 `فَتَرْضَىٰ`, and 93:9 `تَقْهَرْ`.
- Surprising reach: Satisfaction unfolds into bilateral accord, legal guarantee, protected status, truce, and contested victory.

#### Subchannel A. Pact, Truce, and Protected Entrant
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Parties suspend conflict through a pact and extend protected standing to a person entering their domain.
- Active motifs: covenant or pact `ر ب ب:B011/m01`; truce and mutual cessation `و د ع:B004/m01`; protected entrant or asylum seeker `ه د ي:B007/m01`; captive under another's control `ه د ي:B007/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 93:3 `وَدَّعَكَ`, `رَبُّكَ`; 93:5 and 93:11 `رَبُّكَ`, `رَبِّكَ`; 93:7 `فَهَدَىٰ`
- Synthesis: The pact binds the parties, truce names the cessation it produces, and protected status applies that bond to a vulnerable entrant. The captive sense marks the neighboring condition in which protection and control must be distinguished.

#### Subchannel B. Acceptance and Mutual Pleasing
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Aversion is removed, acceptance is established, and two parties make their approval reciprocal.
- Active motifs: acceptance opposed to displeasure `ر ض و:B001/m01`; abundant or sought satisfaction `ر ض و:B002/m01`; mutual accord `ر ض و:B003/m01`; active pleasing of another `ر ض و:B004/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 93:5 `فَتَرْضَىٰ`
- Synthesis: Acceptance supplies an inward state, sought satisfaction makes it a goal, pleasing makes one party act toward the other, and mutual accord closes the relation bilaterally.

#### Subchannel C. Obedience, Attachment, and Surety
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A trusted subordinate is attached to a principal through willing obedience, affection, or guarantee.
- Active motifs: obedient trusted person `ر ض و:B006/m01`; loving attached person `ر ض و:B006/m02`; guarantor or surety `ر ض و:B006/m03`; lord or principal `ر ب ب:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 93:3, 93:5, 93:11 `رَبُّكَ` and `رَبِّكَ`; 93:5 `فَتَرْضَىٰ`
- Synthesis: The principal supplies the relation's authoritative pole. Obedience realizes it behaviorally, attachment realizes it affectively, and surety realizes it as an obligation undertaken for another.

#### Subchannel D. Coercion, Contest, and Cessation
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Opponents drive, seize, or overpower one another until conflict is restrained by cessation.
- Active motifs: coercive domination `ق ه ر:B001/m01`; victory in contest `ع ط و:B007/m01`; overcoming a rival `ر ض و:B005/m01`; expulsion or driving out `ق ل ي:B002/m01`; stealthy snatching `ن ه ر:B006/m01`; armistice `و د ع:B004/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 93:3 `وَدَّعَكَ`, `قَلَىٰ`; 93:5 `يُعْطِيكَ`, `فَتَرْضَىٰ`; 93:9 `تَقْهَرْ`; 93:10 `تَنْهَرْ`
- Synthesis: Driving and snatching initiate dispossession, contest turns it into reciprocal struggle, and coercion fixes a victor over a subdued party. Armistice is the social mechanism that interrupts this chain without requiring either side's annihilation.

### 6. Voice, Dialogue, and Public Memory
- Semantic invariant: Speech moves between participants, discloses what matters, and can persist as interpretation, reputation, song, or remembered account.
- Surface relation: direct; 93:10 `ٱلسَّآئِلَ` and 93:11 `فَحَدِّثْ`.
- Surprising reach: The command to recount expands into dialogue, public reputation, learned interpretation, and performed verse.

#### Subchannel A. Question, Answer, and Mutual Consultation
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Participants ask one another, answer affirmatively, exchange news, and reach an agreed response.
- Active motifs: reciprocal questioning `س ء ل:B004/m01`; affirmative answer `ن ع م:B004/m01`; renewed speech or news `ح د ث:B003/m01`; mutual accord `ر ض و:B003/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 93:5 `فَتَرْضَىٰ`; 93:10 `ٱلسَّآئِلَ`; 93:11 `بِنِعْمَةِ`, `فَحَدِّثْ`
- Synthesis: Reciprocal questioning opens the exchange, renewed speech supplies its content, affirmation answers it, and mutual accord gives the conversation a shared outcome.

#### Subchannel B. Telling the Benefaction
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: A received good is made visible by being recounted as news.
- Active motifs: narrated speech `ح د ث:B003/m01`; manifestation and showing `ح د ث:B006/m01`; bestowed benefaction `ن ع م:B001/m02`; generous gift `خ ي ر:B005/m01`; lordly giver `ر ب ب:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 93:3, 93:5, 93:11 `رَبُّكَ` and `رَبِّكَ`; 93:4 `خَيْرٌ`; 93:11 `بِنِعْمَةِ`, `فَحَدِّثْ`
- Synthesis: Benefaction supplies the content, the giver supplies its relational source, narration turns it into speech, and manifestation lets the spoken good become publicly perceptible.

#### Subchannel C. Conduct Becoming Public Memory
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A person's course of action is repeatedly discussed until it becomes a remembered story or reputation.
- Active motifs: becoming an anecdote or public account `ح د ث:B004/m01`; course and manner `ه د ي:B002/m01`; lapse or omission `ي ت م:B003/m01`; gathered public `ر ب ب:B004/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 93:3, 93:5, 93:11 `رَبُّكَ` and `رَبِّكَ`; 93:6 and 93:9 `يَتِيمًا`, `ٱلْيَتِيمَ`; 93:7 `فَهَدَىٰ`; 93:11 `فَحَدِّثْ`
- Synthesis: Conduct supplies the subject of talk, a lapse supplies a memorable deviation, the gathered public repeats it, and repeated speech converts the person or event into an enduring anecdote.

#### Subchannel D. Interpretation as Return to Meaning
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Speech is carried back to its intended sense under learned direction.
- Active motifs: interpretation by return to meaning `ء و ل:B002/m02`; speech or report `ح د ث:B003/m01`; directed course of discourse `ه د ي:B002/m01`; learned teacher or sage `ر ب ب:B003/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 93:3, 93:5, 93:11 `رَبُّكَ` and `رَبِّكَ`; 93:4 `ٱلْأُولَىٰ`; 93:7 `فَهَدَىٰ`; 93:11 `فَحَدِّثْ`
- Synthesis: A report presents language that can travel away from its intended point. Interpretation returns it to that point, while the learned guide supplies the disciplined course by which the return is made.

#### Subchannel E. Song and Exchanged Verse
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A voice performs melody or verse and directs the composition toward another person, who may answer in kind.
- Active motifs: singing and melody `غ ن ي:B003/m01`; directed poem or poetic exchange `ه د ي:B011/m01`; spoken composition `ح د ث:B003/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 93:7 `فَهَدَىٰ`; 93:8 `فَأَغْنَىٰ`; 93:11 `فَحَدِّثْ`
- Synthesis: Speech provides verbal content, singing gives it performed sound, and poetic direction gives it an addressee. Exchange turns the performance into a reciprocal verbal encounter.

### 7. Residence, Ease, and Composure
- Semantic invariant: A participant settles into a fitting place or bodily state and gains stability, comfort, and quiet bearing.
- Surface relation: indirect; 93:2 `سَجَىٰ`, 93:3 `وَدَّعَكَ`, 93:6 `فَـَٔاوَىٰ`, 93:7 `فَهَدَىٰ`, and 93:8 `فَأَغْنَىٰ`.
- Surprising reach: Shelter becomes both a physical abode and a manner of settled softness, flexibility, and dignity.

#### Subchannel A. Shelter and Agreeable Abode
