# s017 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. P1 (17:1-22): Directed Passage Through Bounded Fields
- Semantic invariant: A directed movement crosses distance while boundaries, stations, and a receiving field give the movement its intelligible course.
- Surface relation: direct; 17:1 anchors the night journey in `أسرى`, `ليلا`, `المسجد الحرام`, `المسجد الأقصى`, `حوله`, and `لنريه`, while 17:2-12 supply local lexical material for channels, inflow, collection, and circulation.
- Surprising reach: The sacred itinerary materializes as a hydraulic system in which a current is routed, collected, replenished, and turned around a fixed basin.

#### Subchannel A. Night Transit Between Sanctuaries
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A servant is carried at night from one protected worship-place to a remote one, through a blessed surrounding field, so that signs can be made visible.
- Active motifs: night travel (`quranic:root_000702:B001/m01`); night darkness (`quranic:root_001392:B001/m01`); worship-place (`quranic:root_000675:B002/m01`); protected sanctuary (`quranic:root_000313:B003/m01`); remote limit (`quranic:root_001235:B001/m01`); surrounding side (`quranic:root_000373:B004/m01`); showing and disclosure (`quranic:root_000531:B012/m01`); visible sign (`quranic:root_000074:B003/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 17:1 (`أسرى`, `ليلا`, `المسجد`, `الحرام`, `الأقصى`, `حوله`, `لنريه`, `آياتنا`).
- Synthesis: The movement is not bare displacement. Night supplies the setting, the two worship-places supply departure and arrival, sanctuary and remoteness define the endpoints, and the blessed surround becomes the field in which disclosure occurs. The journey is therefore a guided passage whose destination is perceptual as well as spatial.

#### Subchannel B. Routed Current and Receiving Basin
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Water is led through a channel, flows into a fixed pool, is reinforced by another current, and can be circulated by a lifting device.
- Active motifs: routed watercourse (`quranic:root_000009:B004/m01`); running current (`quranic:root_000702:B002/m01`); fixed pool (`quranic:root_000109:B003/m01`); water collected in a depression (`quranic:root_000281:B003/m01`); gathered water (`quranic:root_001292:B003/m01`); replenishing flow (`quranic:root_001407:B003/m01`); river channel (`quranic:root_001559:B001/m01`); water-lifting mechanism (`quranic:root_000373:B016/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 17:1 (`أسرى`, `باركنا`, `حوله`); 17:2 (`آتينا`); 17:5, 17:7 (`جاء`); 17:6 (`الكرة`, `أمددناكم`); 17:12 (`النهار`); 17:20 (`نمد`).
- Synthesis: The lexical field converts directed passage into fluid conveyance. An incoming stream is given a course, received in a basin, augmented by a second supply, and kept moving by a turning apparatus. This makes the opening journey resonate as controlled transmission into a prepared sacred surround rather than motion through undifferentiated space.
