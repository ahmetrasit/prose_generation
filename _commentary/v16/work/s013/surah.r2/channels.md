# s013 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. P1 - Raised Frame and Scheduled Motion
- Semantic invariant: A stable elevated frame orders movement beneath and within it.
- Surface relation: direct; 13:2 raises the heavens (`رَفَعَ`, `السَّمَاوَات`) without visible columns (`عَمَد`), establishes the throne (`العَرْش`), and assigns the sun and moon (`الشَّمْس`, `القَمَر`) a measured course (`يَجْرِي`, `أَجَل`).
- Surprising reach: The cosmic order materializes as both load-bearing architecture and a timed itinerary.

#### Subchannel A. Elevated Canopy and Supports
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A roof-like upper structure is raised, propped, and made the frame of rule.
- Active motifs: raised body or building (`quranic:root_000582:B001/m01`); support placed beneath a structure (`quranic:root_001043:B002/m01`); column or mainstay (`quranic:root_001043:B003/m01`); raised roof or canopy (`quranic:root_001000:B002/m01`); sky as what rises and overshadows (`quranic:root_000745:B004/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 13:2 `رَفَعَ` (ر ف ع), `عَمَد` (ع م د), `العَرْش` (ع ر ش), and `السَّمَاوَات` (س م و).
- Synthesis: Raising, supporting, roofing, and overshadowing form one construction scene. The unseen columns do not empty the image of material force; they make support itself the latent invariant beneath the visible heaven.

#### Subchannel B. Celestial Course and Appointment
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Luminous bodies travel along a continuing course until a fixed endpoint.
- Active motifs: flowing motion of sun and other bodies (`quranic:root_000240:B001/m01`); sun and its light (`quranic:root_000818:B001/m01`); moon and its light (`quranic:root_001255:B001/m01`); appointed temporal limit (`quranic:root_000016:B001/m01`); reflective ordering by the outcome of an affair (`quranic:root_000458:B006/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 13:2 `يَجْرِي` (ج ر ي), `الشَّمْس` (ش م س), `القَمَر` (ق م ر), `أَجَل` (ء ج ل), and `يُدَبِّرُ` (د ب ر).
- Synthesis: The course is not motion alone: sun and moon move toward an appointment under administration that already regards the end. Time is pictured as a traversable, governed route.

### 2. P1 - Water Gathered, Routed, and Made Fruitful
- Semantic invariant: Water becomes life-bearing when it is caught, directed, apportioned, and delivered to growth.
- Surface relation: direct; 13:3-4 place rivers, crops, vines, and palms in watered plots, while 13:17 sends water into valleys according to their capacity.
- Surprising reach: The same hydrology extends backward into rock basins and hidden sinks and forward into managed irrigation.

#### Subchannel A. Catchments, Wells, and Hidden Sinks
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Depressions and built holding places catch water before release.
- Active motifs: reservoir collecting well, channel, or rain water (`quranic:root_000016:B007/m01`); hollow retaining water for days (`quranic:root_000018:B006/m01`); rock hollow or newly cut well (`quranic:root_000434:B011/m01`); hidden low sink where water gathers (`quranic:root_001120:B002/m01`); timbered wellhead and station of the water drawer (`quranic:root_001000:B004/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 13:2 `أَجَل` (ء ج ل) and `العَرْش` (ع ر ش); 13:5, 13:16 `خَلْق`/`خَالِق` (خ ل ق); 13:8 `تَغِيضُ` (غ ي ض); 13:16 `اتَّخَذْتُم` (ء خ ذ).
- Synthesis: Basin, hollow, sink, and wellhead form a complete capture scene. These latent containers give material depth to the measured waters later released across the land.

#### Subchannel B. Channels, Valleys, and Outlets
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Stored or descending water enters channels, seeps through terrain, and exits through mouths and valleys.
- Active motifs: flowing river cut through earth (`quranic:root_001559:B001/m01`); water seeping and dripping (`quranic:root_000691:B003/m01`); running flood (`quranic:root_000770:B001/m01`); valley as flood-course (`quranic:root_001637:B005/m01`); watercourse fed by another body of water (`quranic:root_001407:B003/m01`); mouth or outlet of river and valley (`quranic:root_001190:B004/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 13:3 `أَنْهَار` (ن ه ر) and `مَدَّ` (م د د); 13:10 `سَارِب` (س ر ب); 13:14 `فَاهُ` (ف و ه); 13:17 `سَالَت`/`السَّيْل` (س ي ل) and `أَوْدِيَة` (و د ي).
- Synthesis: The branches assemble a hydraulic route from feeding current to channel, seepage, outlet, valley, and flood. The surface valley image therefore belongs to a wider mechanism of controlled passage rather than an isolated body of water.

#### Subchannel C. Irrigated Plot and Differentiated Yield
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Water is apportioned to planted plots whose shared source produces distinct crops and fruit.
- Active motifs: irrigation share and channel (`quranic:root_000722:B003/m01`); sowing and cultivation (`quranic:root_000630:B001/m01`); ripened fruit (`quranic:root_000205:B001/m01`); crop and tree yield (`quranic:root_000043:B002/m01`); palms branching independently from one root (`quranic:root_000887:B002/m01`); palm tree (`quranic:root_001483:B001/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 13:3 `الثَّمَرَات` (ث م ر); 13:4 `زَرْع` (ز ر ع), `يُسْقَى` (س ق ي), `الأُكُل` (ء ك ل), `صِنْوَان` (ص ن و), and `نَخِيل` (ن خ ل).
- Synthesis: One water source enters an apportioned irrigation system, yet produces differentiated fruit, crops, and palm forms. Shared supply and varied output become one agricultural mechanism for materializing unity without sameness.

### 3. P1 - Concealed Generation and Measured Increase
- Semantic invariant: Generation joins counterparts, conceals a burden, and regulates loss and growth until emergence.
- Surface relation: direct; 13:3 pairs fruit, and 13:8 names the female, what wombs carry or lose, what increases, and the measure of every thing.
- Surprising reach: Botanical pairing and human or animal gestation share one productive grammar without collapsing into the same scene.

#### Subchannel A. Joining Counterparts
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A maker joins one counterpart to another so that a productive pair is formed.
- Active motifs: paired counterpart joined to its mate (`quranic:root_000652:B001/m01`); adding one to one to make two (`quranic:root_000208:B001/m01`); female as counterpart to male (`quranic:root_000058:B001/m01`); making or bringing a thing into being (`quranic:root_000248:B001/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 13:3 `جَعَلَ` (ج ع ل), `زَوْجَيْن` (ز و ج), and `اثْنَيْن` (ث ن ي); 13:8 `أُنْثَى` (ء ن ث).
- Synthesis: Pairing is an operation rather than an umbrella category: a maker adds a second, joins counterparts, and establishes the condition from which fruit or offspring can arise.

#### Subchannel B. Womb, Burden, Loss, and Growth
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A concealed interior holds a developing burden whose diminution and increase remain measured.
- Active motifs: inward pregnancy or fruit-bearing (`quranic:root_000357:B002/m01`); womb as the offspring's growing chamber (`quranic:root_000552:B003/m01`); diminution or retention (`quranic:root_001120:B001/m01`); increase and growth (`quranic:root_000657:B001/m01`); bounded quantity and limit (`quranic:root_001205:B001/m01`); heaviness of a carried burden (`quranic:root_000202:B007/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 13:8 `تَحْمِلُ` (ح م ل), `الأَرْحَام` (ر ح م), `تَغِيضُ` (غ ي ض), `تَزْدَادُ` (ز ي د), and `مِقْدَار` (ق د ر); 13:12 `الثِّقَال` (ث ق ل).
- Synthesis: Chamber, burden, decrease, increase, and exact limit form a gestational mechanism. The heavy cloud at 13:12 lexically echoes the carried burden without being folded into the womb scene itself.

- Surface relation: direct; 13:9-10 contrast unseen and witnessed, secret and public speech, while 13:14 and 13:18 stage calling and response.
- Surprising reach: Visibility, articulate differentiation, warning, and answer behave as stages in one information flow.

- Active motifs: concealment and secrecy (`quranic:root_000428:B001/m01`); keeping something inward and secret (`quranic:root_000697:B001/m01`); public declaration and raised voice (`quranic:root_000269:B001/m01`); presence with witnessing (`quranic:root_000822:B001/m01`).

- Synthesis: Secrecy, hiddenness, public voicing, and witnessed presence form a disclosure contrast. The scene renders divine knowledge as access across both enclosed and exposed states.

#### Subchannel B. Articulation That Separates Meaning

#### Subchannel C. Call, Warning, and Answer

### 7. P1 - Assay of Truth Through Foam and Fire
- Semantic invariant: A turbulent process brings superficial waste to the top while useful substance remains.
- Surface relation: direct; 13:17 explicitly parallels flood foam and smelting dross as a likeness of truth and falsehood.
- Surprising reach: Watercourse and furnace are separate material tests that disclose the same persistence invariant.

#### Subchannel A. Flood That Casts Off Surface Waste
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: Descending water becomes a flood, lifts froth, throws refuse aside, and leaves useful material in place.
- Active motifs: water delivered downward (`quranic:root_001492:B002/m01`); water itself (`quranic:root_001458:B001/m01`); running flood (`quranic:root_000770:B001/m01`); floating froth (`quranic:root_000620:B001/m01`); refuse cast out by stream or pot (`quranic:root_000249:B001/m01`); staying with expectant persistence (`quranic:root_001437:B001/m01`); benefit as opposed to harm (`quranic:root_001536:B001/m01`); truth stable against falsehood (`quranic:root_000347:B001/m01`); falsehood losing truth and stability (`quranic:root_000127:B001/m01`).

- Synthesis: Fire, fuel, molten flow, worked ornament, and dross form a full metallurgical assay. The latent gold and iron senses sharpen the surface analogy by making falsehood the waste exposed during refinement.
