# s098 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. Arrival, Disclosure, and Transmission
- Semantic invariant: Something remote, concealed, or merely potential crosses into presence and becomes communicable.
- Surface relation: direct; 98:1-4 center on تأتي, رسول, صحفا, كتب, جاءت, and البينة.
- Surprising reach: Arrival expands from revealed proof into route markers, answering voices, and failures of reading.

#### Subchannel A. Arriving Proof
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: A messenger-borne disclosure comes into a waiting community and makes what was hidden manifest.
- Active motifs: coming into presence `ء ت ي:B001/m01`; arrival `ج ي ء:B001/m01`; manifest proof `ب ي ن:B004/m01`; message bearer `ر س ل:B002/m01`; bringing forward `ج ي ء:B004/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:1, 98:4, 98:5 (تأتي/أوتوا/يؤتوا); 98:4 (جاءت); 98:1, 98:4 (البينة); 98:2 (رسول)
- Synthesis: The two coming-roots supply approach and achieved arrival, while the messenger carries the disclosed content and البينة names its unveiled state. Bringing is therefore both motion toward recipients and transfer into public intelligibility.

#### Subchannel B. Ordered, Purified Sheets
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Written sheets are arranged in a following sequence and presented in a purified state.
- Active motifs: ordered following `ت ل و:B001/m01`; written sheet `ص ح ف:B002/m01`; removal of defilement `ط ه ر:B001/m01`; carried message `ر س ل:B002/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 98:2 (رسول/يتلوا/صحفا/مطهرة)
- Synthesis: The surface recitation rests on a lexical image of one item following another. Pages, message, and purification form a controlled transmission scene in which both sequence and medium are made fit for reception.

#### Subchannel C. Signs, Waymarks, and Beacons
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A traveler or hearer is oriented by signs that reveal meaning, mark a route, or shine from a distance.
- Active motifs: explanatory sign `ب ي ن:B005/m01`; identifying sign `ء م ر:B005/m01`; road waymark `ء م ر:B005/m02`; beacon `ن و ر:B005/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:1, 98:4 (البينة); 98:5 (أمروا); 98:6 (نار)
- Synthesis: بيان makes meaning legible, أمارة fixes a recognizable marker, and the beacon extends recognition across distance. The surface proof thus resonates with practical navigation: disclosure is something by which a course can be found.

#### Subchannel D. Distorted Reading and False Attribution
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A written or spoken transmission is misread, converted into a false statement, and attached to someone as an accusation.
- Active motifs: textual misreading `ص ح ف:B005/m01`; false following statement `ت ل و:B009/m01`; unattributed report `ر س ل:B011/m03`; accusation of unbelief `ك ف ر:B006/m01`; deficient judgment `ف ك ك:B006/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:2 (رسول/يتلوا/صحفا); 98:1, 98:6 (كفروا); 98:1 (منفكين)
- Synthesis: The channel reverses orderly transmission. A page is read wrongly, the resulting utterance becomes false, and attribution hardens into accusation; lax judgment explains how the communicative chain loses integrity.

### 2. Written Form and Documentary Force
- Semantic invariant: Marks are joined into durable textual objects that can state, value, and bind.
- Surface relation: direct; 98:1-4 and 98:6 repeatedly invoke الكتاب, كتب, and صحفا.
- Surprising reach: The same lexical field moves from physical stitching to appraisal and enforceable decree.

#### Subchannel A. From Broad Sheet to Bound Codex
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A broad writable surface receives script, is joined at its edges, and becomes a collected book.
- Active motifs: broad surface `ص ح ف:B001/m01`; written page `ص ح ف:B002/m01`; gathered codex `ص ح ف:B003/m01`; stitching and joining `ك ت ب:B001/m01`; inscription `ك ت ب:B002/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:2 (صحفا); 98:1, 98:3, 98:4, 98:6 (الكتاب/كتب)
- Synthesis: The document is reconstructed materially: breadth provides the sheet, writing fills it, joining closes it, and collected sheets produce the codex. The surface books therefore retain a latent craft image of assembled textual bodies.

#### Subchannel B. Decree, Upright Record, and Appraisal
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A written statement acquires normative force and is assessed as straight, valuable, or authoritative.
- Active motifs: prescribed decree `ك ت ب:B003/m01`; standing value `ق و م:B010/m01`; straightness `ق و م:B008/m01`; written explanation `ب ي ن:B005/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 98:1, 98:3, 98:4, 98:6 (الكتاب/كتب); 98:3, 98:5 (قيمة/يقيموا/القيمة); 98:1, 98:4 (البينة)
- Synthesis: Writing moves beyond storage into prescription. The record stands because it is both normatively straight and open to appraisal, while بيان supplies the act by which its intended meaning is set out.

### 3. Devotion, Uprightness, and Purification
- Semantic invariant: A person is directed, straightened, and cleansed for undivided service.
- Surface relation: direct; 98:2 and 98:5 explicitly join الله, مطهرة, يعبدوا, مخلصين, حنفاء, الصلاة, and الزكاة.
- Surprising reach: Ritual posture extends into lowered submission, purification media, and places built for worship.

#### Subchannel A. Undivided Worship
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: Worship is directed to one deity through obedience and an allotment withheld from partners.
- Active motifs: deity and worship `ء ل ه:B001/m01`; worshipful service `ع ب د:B003/m01`; commanded obedience `ء م ر:B002/m01`; obedient religion `د ي ن:B001/m01`; unshared devotion `خ ل ص:B004/m01`; obedient disposition `ر ض و:B006/m01`; obedient disposition `ر ض ي:B004/m01`; partner-making `ش ر ك:B002/m01`; lowered submission `ك ف ر:B014/m01`; willing submission `ك و ن:B004/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:2, 98:5, 98:8 (الله); 98:5 (أمروا/يعبدوا/الدين/مخلصين); 98:8 (رضي/رضوا); 98:1, 98:6 (المشركين/كفروا); 98:1 (يكن)
- Synthesis: The central act is exclusive direction: service and دين are assigned without a partner. The latent image of bodily lowering intensifies this orientation, while partnership marks the precise relation being refused.

#### Subchannel B. Upright Prayer
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: A worshipper turns toward a chosen direction, stands straight, and establishes ritual prayer.
- Active motifs: directional inclination `ح ن ف:B002/m01`; standing posture `ق و م:B002/m01`; straight alignment `ق و م:B008/m01`; ritual prayer `ص ل و:B003/m01`; worship place `ص ل و:B007/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:5 (حنفاء/يقيموا/الصلاة/القيمة)
- Synthesis: Inclination is not aimless bending but directed reorientation. Standing and straightness convert that turn into stable posture, and prayer gives the posture its repeated ritual form and architectural setting.

#### Subchannel C. Purified Character and Righteous Work
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Defilement is removed from the self, growth is made clean, and intentional work repairs what is wrong.
- Active motifs: moral purification `ط ه ر:B005/m01`; purity and increase `ز ك و:B002/m01`; filtering refinement `خ ل ص:B001/m01`; repair and righteousness `ص ل ح:B001/m01`; intentional work `ع م ل:B001/m01`; moral good `خ ي ر:B001/m01`; expiation `ك ف ر:B009/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:2 (مطهرة); 98:5 (مخلصين/الزكاة); 98:7 (عملوا/الصالحات/خير); 98:1, 98:6 (كفروا)
- Synthesis: Cleansing, growth, repair, and work form one ethical process: contamination is removed, capacity develops, and the resulting action restores fitness. Expiation supplies the remedial counterpart when action has incurred a stain.

#### Subchannel D. Invocation, Amen, and Blessing
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A worshipper names the divine, addresses the lord, asks for blessing, and seals the utterance with amen.
- Active motifs: divine invocation `ء ل ه:B002/m01`; amen response `ء م ن:B003/m01`; supplication and blessing `ص ل و:B002/m01`; lordship addressed `ر ب ب:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:2, 98:5, 98:8 (الله); 98:7 (آمنوا); 98:5 (الصلاة); 98:8 (ربهم/ربه)
- Synthesis: Naming opens the address, lordship identifies the addressee, supplication carries the request, and amen supplies the answering assent. The result is a compact call-and-response ritual.

### 4. Conviction, Awe, and Acceptance
- Semantic invariant: Inner judgment settles into belief, guarded fear, evaluation, or contentment.
- Surface relation: direct; 98:7-8 join آمنوا, خير, رضي/رضوا, خشي, and ربهم.
- Surprising reach: Interior stance reaches learned discernment, animal skittishness, and negotiated satisfaction.

#### Subchannel A. Conviction and Learned Discernment
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A claim is inwardly assented to and interpreted through knowledgeable, delegated judgment.
- Active motifs: belief and assent `ء م ن:B002/m01`; knowledge carried by fear-language `خ ش ي:B002/m01`; learned religious guide `ر ب ب:B003/m01`; delegated adjudication `د ي ن:B007/m01`; denial `ك ف ر:B003/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:7 (آمنوا); 98:8 (خشي/ربه); 98:5 (الدين); 98:1, 98:6 (كفروا)
- Synthesis: Belief is presented as a settled cognitive act rather than mere safety. Learned interpretation and delegated judgment give it a disciplined frame, while denial provides the opposing refusal to assent.

#### Subchannel B. Security Held Beside Awe
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Trust establishes safety while reverent fear and alarm keep the subject alert.
- Active motifs: security and trust `ء م ن:B001/m01`; awe and apprehension `خ ش ي:B001/m01`; fear after separation `ف ر ق:B009/m01`; skittish aversion `ن و ر:B006/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:7 (آمنوا); 98:8 (خشي); 98:4 (تفرق); 98:6 (نار)
- Synthesis: Security and fear are not simple opposites here. Trust supplies a stable relation, while awe, alarm, and skittish recoil regulate approach to what carries authority or danger.

#### Subchannel C. Acceptance and Mutual Satisfaction
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: Approval moves from individual contentment to reciprocal satisfaction between parties.
- Active motifs: acceptance `ر ض و:B001/m01`; abundant satisfaction `ر ض و:B002/m01`; seeking approval `ر ض و:B004/m01`; accepted state `ر ض ي:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:8 (رضي/رضوا)
- Synthesis: The surface reciprocity is expanded into a process: satisfaction is sought, received, and made abundant. The two morphological branch sets converge on a relation in which approval travels in both directions.

#### Subchannel D. Selection, Fitness, and Moral Polarity
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Persons or actions are compared, chosen, and found fit against an opposing field of evil and ingratitude.
- Active motifs: excellence and selection `خ ي ر:B002/m01`; choosing `خ ي ر:B003/m01`; fitness `ص ل ح:B003/m01`; worthiness `ء ه ل:B003/m01`; compatibility `ز ك و:B004/m01`; evil `ش ر ر:B001/m01`; ingratitude `ك ف ر:B004/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:7 (خير/الصالحات); 98:1, 98:6 (أهل/كفروا); 98:5 (الزكاة); 98:6 (شر)
- Synthesis: Evaluation proceeds from comparison to selection and then to demonstrated fitness. Evil and ingratitude are not merely labels but the negative pole against which worthiness, compatibility, and excellence become visible.

### 5. Origination, Existence, and Return
- Semantic invariant: What does not yet exist is formed, sustained, and capable of being raised again.
- Surface relation: direct; 98:1 uses يكن, 98:6-7 use البرية, and 98:8 places recompensed creatures before their lord.
- Surprising reach: Creation and resurrection share an image of standing into renewed existence.

#### Subchannel A. Formation into Existence
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A created being comes into occurrence and is nurtured toward a repaired, viable state.
- Active motifs: creation and formation `ب ر ء:B001/m01`; occurrence and existence `ك و ن:B001/m01`; nurture and repair `ر ب ب:B002/m01`; activation `ع م ل:B002/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:6, 98:7 (البرية); 98:1 (يكن); 98:8 (ربهم/ربه); 98:7 (عملوا)
- Synthesis: Origination is not an isolated instant. Coming-to-be is followed by nurture, repair, and activation, producing a causal sequence from formation to functioning life.

#### Subchannel B. Rising Again
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Created beings stand into a renewed state whose duration can extend without end.
- Active motifs: resurrection and rising `ق و م:B013/m01`; renewed creation `ب ر ء:B001/m01`; lasting duration `ء ب د:B001/m01`; permanence `خ ل د:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:3, 98:5 (قيمة/يقيموا/القيمة); 98:6, 98:7 (البرية); 98:8 (أبدا); 98:6, 98:8 (خالدين)
- Synthesis: Standing supplies the motion of return, creation supplies the recreated subject, and the two duration roots extend the resulting state. The scene links beginning and afterlife through renewed existence.

### 6. Accounting, Debt, and Substitution
- Semantic invariant: An obligation is assessed and answered by recompense, payment, discharge, or a substitute.
- Surface relation: direct; 98:5 names الدين and 98:8 opens with جزاؤهم.
- Surprising reach: Eschatological recompense extends into debt collection, tribute, guarantors, and proxy performance.

#### Subchannel A. Judgment and Recompense
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: Conduct is judged under a written norm and answered with a corresponding outcome.
- Active motifs: judgment and accounting `د ي ن:B002/m01`; recompense `ج ز ي:B001/m01`; written decree `ك ت ب:B003/m01`; adjudicative standing `ق و م:B013/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:5 (الدين/دين); 98:8 (جزاؤهم); 98:1, 98:3, 98:4, 98:6 (الكتاب/كتب); 98:3, 98:5 (قيمة/يقيموا/القيمة)
- Synthesis: The norm is fixed, the subject stands before it, and judgment produces a matched return. This gives the surface reward an institutional grammar of decree, hearing, and result.

#### Subchannel B. Debt Collection and Discharge
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A claim remains attached until it is collected, fulfilled, or cleared from the debtor.
- Active motifs: debt `د ي ن:B003/m01`; collection of what is due `ج ز ي:B003/m01`; clearing a right or debt `ب ر ء:B004/m01`; attached liability `ت ل و:B004/m01`; demand to take `ع ن د:B006/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:5 (الدين/دين); 98:8 (جزاؤهم/عند); 98:6, 98:7 (البرية); 98:2 (يتلوا)
- Synthesis: Liability follows the obligated party until collection or release. The same accounting relation can therefore terminate either in payment or in a formal clearing of the outstanding right.

#### Subchannel C. Tax and Tribute
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: An authority levies a due, receives tribute, and marks the payer's subordinate position.
- Active motifs: assessed levy `ء ت ي:B008/m01`; tribute payment `ج ز ي:B004/m01`; lowered submission `ك ف ر:B014/m02`; debt obligation `د ي ن:B003/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:1, 98:4, 98:5 (تأتي/أوتوا/يؤتوا); 98:8 (جزاؤهم); 98:1, 98:6 (كفروا); 98:5 (الدين/دين)
- Synthesis: Giving becomes compulsory transfer: a due is assessed, collected as tribute, and embedded in a hierarchy. This is the fiscal counterpart of voluntary giving elsewhere in the surah's lexical field.

#### Subchannel D. Proxy, Sufficiency, and Guarantee
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: One agent stands in for another, performs the required role, or guarantees that the obligation will be met.
- Active motifs: substitution `ج ز ي:B002/m01`; sufficiency in another's place `ج ز ي:B002/m02`; substitute office `ق و م:B007/m01`; surety `ك و ن:B003/m01`; delegated agent `ج ر ي:B003/m01`; guarantor `ر ض و:B006/m03`; guarantor `ر ض ي:B004/m03`
- Ayah anchors: 98:8 (جزاؤهم/تجري/رضي/رضوا); 98:3, 98:5 (قيمة/يقيموا/القيمة); 98:1 (يكن)
- Synthesis: Substitution is treated as a functional relation: the proxy occupies a position, the delegate acts, and the guarantor secures completion. Sufficiency names the successful outcome of that replacement.

### 7. Authority, Office, and Civic Order
- Semantic invariant: Collective life is organized through offices, records, covenants, and ranked places.
- Surface relation: indirect; 98:5 supplies command and ordered practice, while 98:8 supplies lordship and station-like nearness.
- Surprising reach: The field reaches payrolls, civic enrollment, coronation, and maritime command.

#### Subchannel A. Authority and Stewardship
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: An office-holder receives authority, supervises affairs, and maintains a governed charge.
- Active motifs: authority and office `ء م ر:B003/m01`; stewardship `ق و م:B004/m01`; administrative work `ع م ل:B003/m01`; lordship `ر ب ب:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:5 (أمروا); 98:3, 98:5 (قيمة/يقيموا/القيمة); 98:7 (عملوا); 98:8 (ربهم/ربه)
- Synthesis: Command is institutionalized as office, work becomes administration, and standing becomes guardianship. Lordship supplies the highest form of the same relation: ordered care exercised over a charge.

#### Subchannel B. Enrollment, Roster, and Payroll
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Workers are entered into a register, assigned service, and maintained by wage or stipend.
- Active motifs: enrollment and roster `ك ت ب:B004/m01`; office work `ع م ل:B003/m01`; laboring crew `ع م ل:B006/m01`; wage `ع م ل:B004/m01`; continuing stipend `ج ر ي:B006/m01`; livelihood `ق و م:B009/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:1, 98:3, 98:4, 98:6 (الكتاب/كتب); 98:7 (عملوا); 98:8 (تجري); 98:3, 98:5 (قيمة/يقيموا/القيمة)
- Synthesis: The register fixes membership, office assigns function, and recurrent payment sustains the worker. Writing thus operates as a practical technology for coordinating labor and livelihood.

#### Subchannel C. Covenant and Treaty
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Parties bind themselves by covenant and stabilize the relation through mutual assent.
- Active motifs: covenant `ر ب ب:B011/m01`; communal party `ء ه ل:B001/m01`; partnership `ش ر ك:B001/m01`; mutual satisfaction `ر ض و:B003/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:8 (ربهم/ربه/رضي/رضوا); 98:1, 98:6 (أهل/المشركين)
- Synthesis: A covenant gives durable form to a relation among collective parties. Partnership defines shared interest, and reciprocal satisfaction supplies the condition under which the pact can hold.

#### Subchannel D. Civic Station, Chief, and Crown
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A settled polity is arranged around a station, a chief, and visible insignia of rule.
- Active motifs: city and polity `د ي ن:B006/m01`; established station `ق و م:B006/m01`; located place `ك و ن:B002/m01`; chief of sailors `ر ب ب:B017/m01`; covering crown `ك ف ر:B015/m01`; dignity and service `ع ب د:B006/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:5 (الدين/دين); 98:3, 98:5 (قيمة/يقيموا/القيمة); 98:1 (يكن); 98:8 (ربهم/ربه); 98:1, 98:6 (كفروا); 98:5 (يعبدوا)
- Synthesis: Place becomes civic station, station receives leadership, and the crown makes rank visible. The maritime chief extends the hierarchy into a mobile polity whose order must be maintained at sea.

### 8. Affiliation, Household, and Social Placement
- Semantic invariant: Persons are located within households, crowds, ranks, or the margins outside them.
- Surface relation: direct; 98:1 and 98:6 distinguish أهل الكتاب and المشركين, while 98:7-8 treat collective human outcomes.
- Surprising reach: Affiliation expands into stepfamilies, hospitality, distant kin, wild aversion, and underclass position.

#### Subchannel A. People, Crowd, and Faction
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Individuals gather into a people, a dense crowd, a faction, or a named group.
- Active motifs: affiliated people `ء ه ل:B001/m01`; people as collective `ق و م:B001/m01`; mass of people `ج ن ن:B013/m01`; large gathered companies `ر ب ب:B004/m01`; faction or flock `ف ر ق:B005/m01`; grouped companies `ع د ن:B005/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:1, 98:6 (أهل); 98:3, 98:5 (قيمة/يقيموا/القيمة); 98:8 (جنات/ربهم/عدن); 98:4 (تفرق)
- Synthesis: The channel ranges from affiliation by أهل to the visual density of a crowd. Faction and grouped companies show how a collective can be internally organized or split into bounded bodies.

#### Subchannel B. Marriage, Affinity, and Caregiving
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Marriage creates an affinity network containing spouses, stepchildren, caregivers, and loving obligation.
- Active motifs: marriage and household `ء ه ل:B002/m01`; in-law affinity `ش ر ك:B003/m01`; stepchild `ر ب ب:B005/m01`; caregiver `ر ب ب:B005/m02`; loving relation `ر ض و:B006/m02`; loving relation `ر ض ي:B004/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 98:1, 98:6 (أهل/المشركين); 98:8 (ربهم/ربه/رضي/رضوا)
- Synthesis: Partnership is recast as kinship, and lordly nurture contracts into the intimate work of raising a child from an earlier union. Love gives the resulting household relation an affective rather than merely legal bond.

#### Subchannel C. Hospitality and Familiar Settlement
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: An inhabited place receives a newcomer and turns unfamiliar presence into welcome and calm.
- Active motifs: inhabited familiarity `ء ه ل:B004/m01`; welcome `ء ه ل:B005/m01`; ease through familiarity `ر س ل:B007/m01`; staying attachment `خ ل د:B002/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:1, 98:6 (أهل); 98:2 (رسول); 98:6, 98:8 (خالدين)
- Synthesis: Habitation supplies the setting, welcome performs admission, and repeated familiarity settles the guest into attachment. The scene is the social inverse of remoteness and wild recoil.

#### Subchannel D. Outsider, Distant Kin, and Aversion
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A newcomer remains socially distant and can be met with the recoil associated with wild creatures.
- Active motifs: stranger and newcomer `ء ت ي:B006/m01`; distant kin `ب ع د:B005/m01`; remote settlement `ك ف ر:B012/m01`; wild aversion from humans `ء ب د:B002/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:1, 98:4, 98:5 (تأتي/أوتوا/يؤتوا); 98:4 (بعد); 98:1, 98:6 (كفروا); 98:8 (أبدا)
- Synthesis: Distance is social before it is spatial: kin become remote, the settlement lies beyond familiar bounds, and the outsider evokes animal-like flight. The cluster gives exclusion a bodily image of recoil.

#### Subchannel E. Servitude and Lower Rank
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A person is placed under ownership, compelled into service, and ranked beneath others.
- Active motifs: slave `ع ب د:B001/m01`; enslavement `ع ب د:B004/m01`; subjugation and ownership `د ي ن:B004/m01`; lower social class `ت ح ت:B002/m01`; distant social rank `ب ع د:B005/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 98:5 (يعبدوا/الدين); 98:8 (تحتها); 98:4 (بعد)
- Synthesis: Ownership defines the relation, enslavement enforces it, and "below" converts hierarchy into spatial rank. This latent social frame sharply contrasts with voluntary worship in the surface-primary devotional channel.

### 9. Partnership, Accord, and Feud
- Semantic invariant: Shared relations are allocated, negotiated, repaired, or broken by hostility.
- Surface relation: direct; 98:4 names dispersal and 98:8 names reciprocal satisfaction, with المشركين providing a surface partnership-form.
- Surprising reach: The field moves from coowned property to consultation and then to feud as a damaged connective bond.

#### Subchannel A. Coownership and Exclusive Allotment
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A resource is shared among partners, divided into portions, and sometimes reserved exclusively for one party.
- Active motifs: coownership `ش ر ك:B001/m01`; exclusive allotment `خ ل ص:B004/m02`; interval between shares `ب ي ن:B002/m01`; paired share `ز ك و:B005/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:1, 98:6 (المشركين); 98:5 (مخلصين/الزكاة); 98:1, 98:4 (البينة)
- Synthesis: Partnership creates the common holding, "between" structures its internal division, pairing establishes matched portions, and exclusive allotment removes one portion from the shared pool.

#### Subchannel B. Consultation and Reconciliation
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Parties deliberate, choose a course, and remove mutual aversion through reciprocal assent.
- Active motifs: counsel `ء م ر:B007/m01`; mutual satisfaction `ر ض و:B003/m01`; reconciliation `ص ل ح:B002/m01`; choosing `خ ي ر:B003/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:5 (أمروا); 98:8 (رضي/رضوا); 98:7 (الصالحات/خير)
- Synthesis: Counsel opens alternatives, selection identifies a course, and mutual satisfaction converts it into accord. Reconciliation names the outcome: the prior state of alienation has been removed.

#### Subchannel C. Bond, Feud, and Peace
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A connective social bond is strained into enmity and can be repaired into peace.
- Active motifs: connective bond `ب ي ن:B003/m01`; close friendship `خ ل ص:B007/m01`; feud `ن و ر:B007/m01`; enmity `ب ع د:B010/m01`; dispute `ش ر ر:B011/m01`; peacemaking `ص ل ح:B002/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 98:1, 98:4 (البينة); 98:5 (مخلصين); 98:6 (نار/شر); 98:4 (بعد); 98:7 (الصالحات)
- Synthesis: The same "between" that links parties becomes the space in which feud operates. Enmity stretches the relation apart, dispute activates it, and reconciliation restores the bond.

### 10. Unbinding, Separation, and Release
- Semantic invariant: A fastening, obligation, affiliation, or claim is opened so that its parties can part.
