# s002 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. P1 (2:1-20) - Sensory ingress and closure
- Semantic invariant: Information reaches a perceiver through an exposed faculty, while refusal or incapacity closes that point of entry.
- Surface relation: direct; 2:7, 2:18-20 anchor the channel in `سَمْعِ`, `صُمّ`, `عُمْي`, `آذَانِ`, and `أَبْصَار`.
- Surprising reach: The ear is not only an organ but a posture of assent, and a heard signal can become publicly announced knowledge.

#### Subchannel A. Hearing as admitted signal
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A sound reaches the ear, is deliberately attended to, and becomes accepted or announced information.
- Active motifs: auditory perception (`س م ع:B001/m01`); listening and accepting what is heard (`ء ذ ن:B002/m01`); knowledge made known by a call (`ء ذ ن:B003/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:7 `سَمْعِ`; 2:19 `آذَانِ`; 2:20 `سَمْعِ`.
- Synthesis: Ear, act of listening, and announced knowledge form a single reception chain: a signal enters, receives assent, and becomes communicable knowledge.

#### Subchannel B. Faculties shut against perception
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: Hearing and sight cease to function, leaving the subject unable to receive or orient.
- Active motifs: loss of hearing and refusal to listen (`ص م م:B001/m01`); loss of sight (`ع م ي:B001/m01`); ocular seeing as the disabled capacity (`ب ص ر:B001/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:18 `صُمّ`, `عُمْي`; 2:7 `أَبْصَارِ`; 2:17 `يُبْصِرُ`; 2:20 `أَبْصَار`.
- Synthesis: Deafness and blindness are coordinated closures of sensory ports. The explicit seeing faculty supplies the capacity whose interruption makes disorientation complete.

### 2. P1 (2:1-20) - Intermittent illumination
- Semantic invariant: Light briefly opens a traversable field, then darkness or visual shock removes it.
- Surface relation: direct; 2:17 and 2:19-20 supply `اسْتَوْقَدَ`, `نُور`, `بَرْق`, `أَضَاءَ`, `مَشَى`, and renewed darkness.
- Surprising reach: Illumination includes both outward radiance and the observer's bodily reaction of staring toward a flash.

#### Subchannel A. Kindled light and visual exposure
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: Fire is kindled, emits light, and draws the eye toward what has become visible.
- Active motifs: ignition of fire (`و ق د:B001/m01`); radiance and illumination (`ن و ر:B001/m01`); looking expectantly by firelight (`ض و ء:B003/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:17 `اسْتَوْقَدَ`, `نُورِ`, `أَضَاءَتْ`; 2:20 `أَضَاءَ`.
- Synthesis: Ignition produces a luminous field, and the perceiver turns toward that field. The scene joins source, emitted light, and visual attention.

#### Subchannel B. Lightning-governed movement
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: A flash shocks the eye, briefly permits walking, and then withdraws the conditions for movement.
- Active motifs: lightning's flash (`ب ر ق:B001/m01`); eyes fixed or startled by brilliance (`ب ر ق:B002/m01`); voluntary walking (`م ش ي:B001/m01`); ocular seeing (`ب ص ر:B001/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:19-20 `بَرْق`; 2:20 `مَشَى`, `أَبْصَار`.
- Synthesis: The same flash both exposes the path and seizes the gaze. Locomotion is therefore metered by short episodes of visibility rather than by a stable light source.

### 3. P2 (2:21-39) - Habitable ordering
- Semantic invariant: Space becomes inhabitable by arranging a lower support, an upper shelter, and a fitted place of rest.
- Surface relation: direct; 2:22 and 2:35 anchor the channel in earth as `فِرَاشًا`, sky as `بِنَاءً`, and the command `اسْكُنْ`.
- Surprising reach: The cosmic floor-and-canopy arrangement contracts into the small-scale craft of bedding, patterned coverings, and bodily stillness.

#### Subchannel A. Floor, canopy, and descent
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A lower surface is spread, an upper structure is built, and what descends from above enters the enclosed world.
- Active motifs: earth as the lower counterpart of sky (`ء ر ض:B001/m01`); spreading and leveling (`ف ر ش:B001/m01`); building by joining parts (`ب ن ي:B001/m01`); the overhanging sky (`س م و:B004/m01`); downward arrival and settlement (`ن ز ل:B001/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:22 `أَرْضَ`, `فِرَاشًا`, `بِنَاءً`, `السَّمَاء`, `أَنزَلَ`; 2:29 `أَرْض`, `سَمَاوَات`.
- Synthesis: The scene is architectural: a spread base and constructed canopy define an interior, while descent supplies that interior from above.

#### Subchannel B. Fitted bedding and rest
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A resting place is furnished with a patterned cover and receives a body into stillness.
- Active motifs: patterned covering laid over a litter (`ز و ج:B006/m01`); a model or form made as a bed (`م ث ل:B007/m01`); bedding and household furnishings (`ف ر ش:B002/m01`); cessation of movement (`س ك ن:B001/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:22 `فِرَاشًا`; 2:26 `مَثَلًا`; 2:35 `زَوْجُ`, `اسْكُنْ`.
- Synthesis: Cover, bed-form, furnishings, and stillness assemble a domestic rest scene. It miniaturizes the larger floor-and-canopy invariant without collapsing into the cosmic architecture.

### 4. P2 (2:21-39) - Provision descending into life
- Semantic invariant: A gift moves downward or is allotted, enters land or body, and becomes growth.
- Surface relation: direct; 2:22 and 2:25 explicitly join descending water, produced fruits, repeated provision, and eating.
- Surprising reach: Food is represented not merely as possession but as material that builds flesh.

#### Subchannel A. Rain animating the ground
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Clouded sky releases rain as provision, and the receiving earth becomes alive with vegetation.
- Active motifs: cloud mass bearing rain (`ر ب ب:B008/m01`); the overhanging sky (`س م و:B004/m01`); rain descending as provision (`ر ز ق:B003/m01`); earth revived by rain and plants (`ح ي ي:B002/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:22 `السَّمَاء`, `رِزْقًا`; 2:28 `أَحْيَا`, `يُحْيِي`.
- Synthesis: The channel follows a complete vertical process from cloud and sky to falling provision and vegetative life in the ground.

#### Subchannel B. Allotment becoming bodily substance
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: An apportioned gift becomes edible sustenance and then contributes to bodily growth.
- Active motifs: edible share (`ء ك ل:B003/m01`); apportioned gift (`ر ز ق:B001/m01`); nutritive sustenance (`ر ز ق:B002/m01`); food building and enlarging flesh (`ب ن ي:B010/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:22 and 2:25 `رِزْقًا`; 2:35 `كُلَ`.
- Synthesis: Allocation, eating, nourishment, and flesh-building form a transfer sequence in which provision crosses from external share to incorporated body.

### 5. P3 (2:40-61) - Provision under bodily constraint
- Semantic invariant: Sustenance is evaluated by what a body can take in, tolerate, exchange, or refuse.
- Surface relation: direct; 2:57-61 repeatedly names eating, drinking, allotted springs, provision, and requested substitution.
- Surprising reach: Palatability extends beyond taste to bodily refusal, while dilution appears as an operation that breaks a drink's severity.

#### Subchannel A. Water between sweetness and aversion
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A drink is encountered as pleasant, resisted, or made tolerable by weakening its force with water.
- Active motifs: water drunk with aversion (`ش ر ب:B005/m01`); sweetness of water or food (`ع ذ ب:B001/m01`); a body abstaining from food and drink (`ع ذ ب:B002/m01`); breaking a drink's strength with water (`ق ت ل:B007/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:49 `عَذَاب`; 2:54, 2:61 `اقْتُلُوا`, `يَقْتُلُون`; 2:60 `مَشْرَب`, `اشْرَبُوا`.
- Synthesis: These senses form a palate-and-tolerance scene: liquid has a felt quality, the body may reject it, and mixing can reduce an otherwise overpowering intensity.

#### Subchannel B. Choosing and exchanging food
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Edible shares are received, compared, and exchanged through an alimentary market frame.
- Active motifs: edible allotment (`ء ك ل:B003/m01`); apportioned provision (`ر ز ق:B001/m01`); nutritive sustenance (`ر ز ق:B002/m01`); seller of foodstuffs (`ب د ل:B005/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:57-58, 2:60 `كُلُوا`; 2:57, 2:60 `رَزَقْنَاكُم`, `رِزْق`; 2:59 `بَدَّلَ`; 2:61 `تَسْتَبْدِلُون`.
- Synthesis: Provision becomes a field of selection rather than mere receipt. The seller sense supplies the social mechanism through which one food can be substituted for another.

### 6. P3 (2:40-61) - Lowering into an assigned condition
- Semantic invariant: A subject or burden moves downward and comes to occupy a materially or socially lower position.
- Surface relation: direct; 2:43, 2:45, 2:58, and 2:61 join bowing, prostration, descent, and abasement.
- Surprising reach: Physical unloading, locative placement, ritual posture, and social lowliness share one downward vector without becoming one undifferentiated scene.

#### Subchannel A. Descent, unloading, and placement
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A load is put down, a subject descends from a higher condition, and the descended thing is installed in a place.
- Active motifs: lowering from above and setting down a burden (`ح ط ط:B001/m01`); descent from rank or condition (`ه ب ط:B002/m01`); conveying something downward (`ن ز ل:B002/m01`); dwelling or rank occupied (`ن ز ل:B003/m01`); placing a thing in its proper station (`ن ز ل:B004/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:41, 2:57, 2:59 `أَنزَلْنَا`; 2:58 `حِطَّة`; 2:61 `اهْبِطُوا`.
- Synthesis: The active senses distinguish transport from outcome: something is lowered, loses elevation, and settles into an assigned station.

#### Subchannel B. Bodily yielding and abasement
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: The body lowers its head and frame in submission, with an accompanying condition of social humility.
- Active motifs: lowering oneself in humility (`خ ش ع:B001/m01`); bending and lowering the head (`ر ك ع:B001/m01`); bowing the head in prostration (`س ج د:B004/m01`); abasement and low condition (`س ك ن:B006/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:43 `ارْكَعُوا`, `الرَّاكِعِين`; 2:45 `خَاشِعِين`; 2:58 `سُجَّدًا`; 2:61 `مَسْكَنَة`.
- Synthesis: Bowing and prostration realize downward motion as posture, while abasement supplies its durable social state.

### 7. P4 (2:62-74) - Hard matter opened to flow
- Semantic invariant: Resistant matter can remain sealed and rigid or be fractured so that an interior current emerges.
- Surface relation: direct; 2:74 explicitly compares hardened hearts with stones that split and release water.
- Surprising reach: The contrast is mechanical as well as moral: hardness, fissure, pressure, channel, and flow form a material process.

#### Subchannel A. Hardened stone and heart
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: Material dries into rigidity, and the heart takes on the same loss of softness as stone.
- Active motifs: hardness and dryness in things (`ق س و:B001/m01`); coarsening of the heart and loss of softness (`ق س و:B002/m01`); solid stone (`ح ج ر:B003/m01`); heart as the affected interior (`ق ل ب:B001/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:74 `قَسَتْ`, `قَسْوَة`, `قُلُوبُ`, `حِجَارَة`.
- Synthesis: The scene aligns physical rigidity with an interior organ's hardened state, preserving the distinction between material property and moral embodiment.

#### Subchannel B. Fissure, channel, and released water
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: Stone opens under a widening fracture, water appears within it, and a channel carries the released flow.
- Active motifs: wide splitting and eruption (`ف ج ر:B001/m01`); cracking and opening (`ش ق ق:B001/m01`); water as material (`م و ه:B001/m01`); water appearing and increasing (`م و ه:B002/m01`); river cutting the earth with running water (`ن ه ر:B001/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:74 `يَتَفَجَّر`, `يَشَّقَّق`, `مَاء`, `أَنْهَار`.
- Synthesis: Fracture is not an endpoint: it opens a passage, exposes water, and develops into a running channel.

### 8. P4 (2:62-74) - Worked bodies and ground
- Semantic invariant: Animal bodies and earth are acted upon by force that stirs, opens, or scores material.
- Surface relation: direct; 2:67-71 supplies the cow, slaughter, stirred soil, field, and watering operations.
- Surprising reach: The same opening operation can cut an animal throat, score earth, or form a water-course.

#### Subchannel A. Bovine traction and field work
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A bovine body provides force that disturbs soil, prepares a field, and serves cultivation.
- Active motifs: cattle as the working animal (`ب ق ر:B001/m01`); bull as male bovine (`ث و ر:B004/m01`); stirring matter from its place (`ث و ر:B002/m01`); sowing seed and cultivating (`ح ر ث:B002/m01`); trampled or worked ground (`ح ر ث:B008/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:67-71 `بَقَرَة`; 2:71 `تُثِير`, `حَرْث`.
- Synthesis: Animal, motive force, disturbed soil, sowing, and worn ground form a compact agricultural operation rather than a generic animal motif.

#### Subchannel B. Incision and made channels
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Cutting opens a body or ground and produces a passage through which blood or water can move.
- Active motifs: opening the throat in slaughter (`ذ ب ح:B001/m01`); water channels incised in earth (`ذ ب ح:B004/m01`); splitting something open (`ب ق ر:B002/m01`); digging and scoring ground (`ب ق ر:B003/m01`); allotted irrigation channel (`س ق ي:B003/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:67, 2:71 `تَذْبَحُوا`, `ذَبَحُوا`; 2:67-71 `بَقَرَة`; 2:71 `تَسْقِي`.
- Synthesis: The channel joins distinct cutting operations through a shared mechanism: an incision changes a closed surface into a conduit.

### 9. P5 (2:75-86) - Bonds that obligate and protect
- Semantic invariant: A durable social bond places its members under preserved obligation, mutual support, and care.
- Surface relation: direct; 2:80, 2:83-85 name covenant, parents, kin, orphans, mutual aid, and violated communal obligations.
- Surprising reach: Covenant can be imaged as a protective compact, while orphanhood exposes what happens when the expected stream of care is cut or delayed.

#### Subchannel A. Covenant as protected affiliation
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Parties enter a preserved compact and become joined by support, loyalty, kinship, emancipation, or protected residence.
- Active motifs: compact and covenant (`ر ب ب:B011/m01`); preserved pact that secures its holder (`ع ه د:B003/m01`); loving support and alliance (`و ل ي:B004/m01`); affiliation by kinship, emancipation, or clientage (`و ل ي:B005/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:76 `رَبِّ`; 2:80 `عَهْدًا`; 2:83 `تَوَلَّيْتُم`.
- Synthesis: The compact is not only a spoken promise. It creates an affiliation whose practical expression is protection and mutual backing.

#### Subchannel B. Kinship and interrupted care
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Parents and kin sustain dependents, while orphanhood marks severance from a guardian and the slowing of expected kindness.
- Active motifs: mother as bearer and nurturer (`ء م م:B001/m01`); parents through procreation (`و ل د:B002/m01`); blood kinship (`ق ر ب:B003/m01`); child cut off from a guardian (`ي ت م:B001/m01`); kindness delayed from an orphan (`ي ت م:B004/m01`); foster-child relation (`ر ب ب:B005/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:78 `أُمِّيُّون`; 2:83 `وَالِدَيْن`, `قُرْبَى`, `يَتَامَى`; 2:76 `رَبِّ`.
- Synthesis: The scene maps a care network from parent and kin to fosterage. Orphanhood is its structural negative: the dependent remains while the responsible caregiver is absent or slow to act.

### 10. P5 (2:75-86) - Recorded words in exchange
- Semantic invariant: Words and writings become accountable objects that hands produce, claim, transfer, and exchange for value.
- Surface relation: direct; 2:78-81 places book, writing, hands, speech, price, acquisition, and earnings in one transactional discourse.
- Surprising reach: Authorship is treated as manual production, while a doctrinal claim can function like merchandise offered for a return.

#### Subchannel A. Hand-produced record and claim
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Hands compose a written object, speech attributes a claim to it, and the producer acquires consequences through the act.
- Active motifs: ordered letters and written object (`ك ت ب:B002/m01`); spoken utterance (`ق و ل:B001/m01`); saying or attributing what was not (`ق و ل:B005/m01`); what hands have earned (`ي د ي:B009/m01`); causing another to acquire good or wealth (`ك س ب:B002/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:79 `يَكْتُبُون`, `كِتَاب`, `كَتَبَتْ`, `يَقُولُون`, `أَيْدِيهِم`, `يَكْسِبُون`; 2:81 `كَسَبَ`.
- Synthesis: Record, utterance, manual authorship, and acquired consequence form a production chain in which text is made and then socially asserted.

#### Subchannel B. Acquisition, handover, and price
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: An object or position is acquired through exchange, passed by hand, and measured by a price.
- Active motifs: taking for oneself and acquiring (`ء خ ذ:B010/m01`); hand as the instrument of transfer (`ي د ي:B007/m01`); reciprocal buying and selling (`ش ر ي:B001/m01`); value received in exchange (`ث م ن:B001/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:79 `يَشْتَرُون`, `ثَمَنًا`, `أَيْدِيهِم`; 2:80, 2:83-84 `اتَّخَذْتُم`, `أَخَذْنَا`; 2:86 `اشْتَرَوُا`.
- Synthesis: Acquisition is resolved into its transactional parts: taking, manual conveyance, reciprocal exchange, and valuation.

### 11. P6 (2:87-103) - Message reception and relay
- Semantic invariant: A message travels through voiced transmission, attentive reception, tracking, and recognition.
- Surface relation: direct; 2:87-93 and 2:97-102 repeatedly present messengers, hearing, following, recitation, recognition, and transmitted reports.
- Surprising reach: Following a messenger becomes literally following an observable trace, while recitation can be heard as one voice answering another.

#### Subchannel A. Voiced relay
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: One voice raises and sends a signal, another voice follows it, and an ear receives or admits it.
- Active motifs: hearing sound (`س م ع:B001/m01`); making another hear (`س م ع:B004/m01`); listening with acceptance (`ء ذ ن:B002/m01`); one voice following another (`ت ل و:B007/m01`); raising the voice (`ر ف ع:B010/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:93 `اسْمَعُوا`, `سَمِعْنَا`, `رَفَعْنَا`; 2:97, 2:102 `إِذْن`; 2:102 `تَتْلُوا`.
- Synthesis: Vocal production, sequential response, ear, and assent form a relay rather than an isolated act of hearing.

#### Subchannel B. Traces becoming recognition
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A follower tracks an earlier trace, identifies it by marks, and receives news from one who knows the group's condition.
- Active motifs: following an observable trace (`ق ف و:B001/m01`); tracking an أثر (`ق و ف:B001/m01`); recognition through a sign or trace (`ع ر ف:B003/m01`); group informant who knows its condition (`ع ر ف:B006/m01`); incoming transmitted report (`ن ب ء:B002/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:87 `قَفَّيْنَا`; 2:89 `عَرَفُوا`; 2:91 `أَنْبِيَاء`; 2:93 `اسْمَعُوا`.
- Synthesis: Sequence becomes evidence: a predecessor leaves a track, the track enables recognition, and recognized knowledge is converted into report.

### 12. P6 (2:87-103) - Life-force entering and leaving the body
- Semantic invariant: An interior body is animated, constricted, relieved, filled, or emptied by what passes through it.
- Surface relation: indirect; 2:87, 2:93-96, and 2:102 anchor the channel in spirit, selves, life, death, hearts, and the image of being made to drink.
- Surprising reach: Comprehension and attachment take the form of drinking, so an inward conviction can be tasted, absorbed, or resisted like liquid.

#### Subchannel A. Breath, animation, and extinction
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Spirit and breath animate a body; breathing relieves constriction; their withdrawal leaves bodily or cardiac death.
- Active motifs: spirit that gives the body life (`ر و ح:B001/m01`); breath emerging from within (`ن ف س:B001/m01`); relief by opening a constriction (`ن ف س:B002/m01`); animate creature possessing spirit (`ح ي ي:B003/m01`); loss of force and life (`م و ت:B001/m01`); death-like failure of the heart (`م و ت:B006/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:87 `رُوح`; 2:87, 2:90, 2:102 `أَنفُس`; 2:94 `مَوْت`; 2:96 `حَيَاة`.
- Synthesis: Animation is modeled as an interior circulation. Breath and spirit sustain the living body, relief reopens it, and cessation empties life from body or heart.

#### Subchannel B. Understanding imbibed into the heart
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Meaning is drunk into an interior, where it may be sweet, resisted by the body, or diluted to reduce its force.
- Active motifs: drinking as understanding (`ش ر ب:B011/m01`); pleasant sweetness in drink or food (`ع ذ ب:B001/m01`); bodily refusal of intake (`ع ذ ب:B002/m01`); breaking a drink's severity with water (`ق ت ل:B007/m01`); heart as receiving interior (`ق ل ب:B001/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:90, 2:96 `عَذَاب`; 2:87, 2:91 `تَقْتُلُون`; 2:93 `أُشْرِبُوا`, `قُلُوبِهِم`.
- Synthesis: The surface image of being made to drink into the heart opens a lexical physiology of belief: meaning becomes an ingestible substance with taste, force, and bodily uptake.

### 13. P7 (2:104-121) - Presented speech and evidence
- Semantic invariant: A claim is brought before another party in a form that can be delivered, spoken, tested, and either verified or exposed.
- Surface relation: direct; 2:104, 2:111, and 2:118 feature commanded speech, requested proof, truth claims, and demands for direct address.
- Surprising reach: A communicative mark can be either a meaningful word or a bodily wound, making evidence something said or visibly inscribed.

#### Subchannel A. Gift, demand, and proof
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: One party brings or gives an object, another demands its presentation, and the offered item functions as proof.
- Active motifs: giving and delivery (`ء ت ي:B002/m01`); demand to bring or present (`ه ا ت:B001/m01`); courteous dispatch of a gift (`ه د ي:B004/m01`); establishing a claim through proof (`ب ر ه ن:B001/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:106, 2:109-110, 2:118, 2:121 forms of `أتى`; 2:111 `هَاتُوا`, `بُرْهَانَكُم`; 2:120 `هُدَى`.
- Synthesis: Delivery and demand converge in an evidentiary scene: what is brought must not merely be present but must establish the claim attached to it.

#### Subchannel B. Word as intelligible or injurious mark
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: An utterance connects speakers and defines a matter, while a cognate mark can be cut into a body as a wound.
- Active motifs: intelligible speech linking interlocutors (`ك ل م:B001/m01`); meaningful word or expression (`ك ل م:B002/m01`); bodily wound (`ك ل م:B003/m01`); utterance that defines a thing (`ق و ل:B016/m01`); truthfulness of speech (`ص د ق:B001/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:104, 2:111, 2:113, 2:116-118, 2:120 forms of `قَوْل`; 2:118 `يُكَلِّمُنَا`; 2:111 `صَادِقِين`.
- Synthesis: Spoken meaning and visible wound share the function of leaving a legible mark. Truth determines whether the verbal mark accurately fixes what it names.

### 14. P7 (2:104-121) - Routes under guidance and authority
- Semantic invariant: A route becomes actionable when it is marked, led, claimed, and governed by one qualified to direct others.
- Surface relation: indirect; 2:107, 2:110, 2:114, and 2:120 anchor the channel in rule, striving, guidance, following, and affiliation.
- Surprising reach: Legal entitlement appears as pursuit along a path, while office and qualification determine who may direct that pursuit.

#### Subchannel A. Marked routes and pursuit
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Repeated traffic marks a road, branches create choices or traps, a leader takes the front, and a claimant follows.
- Active motifs: well-trodden marked road (`م ل ل:B006/m01`); worked or traveled road (`ع م ل:B011/m01`); branching or snaring path (`ش ر ك:B005/m01`); guide advancing at the front (`ه د ي:B003/m01`); claimant pursuing a right (`ت ب ع:B005/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:105 `مُشْرِكِين`; 2:110 `تَعْمَلُوا`; 2:120 `مِلَّتَهُم`, `هُدَى`, `تَتَّبِع`.
- Synthesis: Surface, branch, leader, and pursuer define a route system in which orientation is inseparable from the right being followed.

#### Subchannel B. Qualified governance
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A ruler or officeholder assumes responsibility because the office, priority, and competence properly belong to that person.
- Active motifs: assuming and administering an affair (`و ل ي:B003/m01`); priority and entitlement (`و ل ي:B008/m01`); governing office over a group (`س ع ي:B003/m01`); fitness and qualification (`ء ه ل:B003/m01`); sovereignty and authority (`م ل ك:B003/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 2:105, 2:109 `أَهْل`; 2:107 `مُلْك`, `وَلِيّ`; 2:114 `سَعَى`; 2:115, 2:120 forms of `وَلِي`.

## Cross-Pericope Channels

### X1. Water from sky to body, field, and store
- Pericopes spanned: P2 (2:21-39), P3 (2:40-61), P4 (2:62-74), P10 (2:153-177), P12 (2:204-220), P14 (2:243-253), P15 (2:254-266), and P16 (2:267-286).
- Shared semantic invariant: Water moves through a linked system of source, descent, opening, channel, reception, storage, and use.
- Bridge: causal sequence and role progression; cloud and rain in P2 and P10 descend to ground, P4 opens conduits, P12 carries relief along routes, P14 makes a river a traversed intake point, P15 tests the receiving substrate, and P16 gathers water in basin, pool, well, and river. P3 supplies bodily intake within the same downstream sequence.
- Local findings joined: P2 “Rain animating the ground”; P3 “Water between sweetness and aversion”; P4 “Fissure, channel, and released water” and “Incision and made channels”; P10 “Water moving from atmosphere to land”; P12 “Rain-supported passage”; P14 “River crossing as a test of intake”; P15 “Rain on an exposed surface”; P16 “Stored water and connected channels”.
- Synthesis: These scenes form one hydrological progression without erasing their different operations. Water is formed above, descends, finds or makes a passage, gathers or runs, encounters a receptive or resistant surface, and finally sustains land, travel, and bodies.

### X2. Palm maturation as a model of increase
- Pericopes spanned: P1 (2:1-20), P13 (2:221-242), P15 (2:254-266), and P16 (2:267-286).

- Local findings joined: P1 “Hidden palm growth”; P13 “Horticultural nursery”; P15 “Cultivated palm garden”; P16 “Nurtured increase”.
- Synthesis: The repeated object and developmental roles create a continuous plant life-cycle. Financial increase enters only at the final analogical step, where proper growth remains something tended and brought to completion rather than extracted instantaneously.

- Synthesis: Worker, input, substrate, operation, and outcome recur in each scene. Yield is the result of coordinated cultivation; corruption, hardness, or nullification breaks that sequence and prevents return.
