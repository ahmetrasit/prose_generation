### P1.1 Creation as measured fabrication
- Semantic invariant: Creation is rendered as a controlled passage from measured design through opening, formation, and finished execution.
- Surface relation: direct; 6:1-2 anchors `خلق`, `جعل`, and `طين`, while 6:14 anchors `فاطر` and 6:2, 6:8 anchor `قضي`.
- Surprising reach: The created order resembles both a shaped material and a workpiece whose seams are opened and whose execution is made binding.
