# Focus 87:1

## liturgical clearing
- reading: Enact praise and prayer that clear your Lord's designated name of every unworthy association and accord it incomparable rank.
- mechanism: Two live images in س ب ح combine enacted worship with declaring free from defect. The name is the raised designation on which that clearing operates, lordship supplies the relation of authority, and الأعلى assigns qualitative rank. The line therefore commands an act that is at once vocal, ritual, and corrective.
- trace:
  - 87:1 **سَبِّحِ** س ب ح B001: Worship through praise and prayer supplies the enacted mode: the imperative is performed rather than merely described.
  - 87:1 **سَبِّحِ** س ب ح B002: Purifying and declaring free supplies the negative operation: every unworthy attribution is pushed away.
  - 87:1 **ٱسْمَ** س م و B005: Name as raised designation makes اسم the public and repeatable carrier of the clearing act.
  - 87:1 **رَبِّكَ** ر ب ب B001: Lordship, ownership, and authority specify the relational claim in ربك.
  - 87:1 **ٱلْأَعْلَى** ع ل و B002: Noble high rank gives الأعلى qualitative precedence rather than only vertical location.

## nurtured ascent
- reading: Let glorification course toward the Lord whose formative care repairs, grows, and raises beings toward completion.
- mechanism: Coursing in س ب ح, rising in both اسم and الأعلى, and the repair-and-growth branches of رب turn verticality into a process. The Lord is not only one who occupies highest rank, but one whose care brings beings toward completion; glorification aligns speech and life with that upward nurture.
- trace:
  - 87:1 **سَبِّحِ** س ب ح B004: Swimming or coursing makes the imperative kinetically sustained rather than a static utterance.
  - 87:1 **ٱسْمَ** س م و B001: Rising high lets the designation itself participate in an upward trajectory.
  - 87:1 **رَبِّكَ** ر ب ب B002: Repair, nurture, and completion make lordship a stagewise formative relation.

  - 87:1 **ٱلْأَعْلَى** ع ل و B001: Rising and being high supplies both the source and the direction of the developmental motion.

# Focus 87:2

## measured origination then rectification
- reading: He measured a form, originated it, then rectified and completed it into soundness.
- mechanism: The first root supplies both prior measurement and origination, while the second supplies straightening and completion. The fa moves from apportioned design through realized existence into sound form.
- trace:
  - 87:2 **خَلَقَ** خ ل ق B001: Measuring before cutting or acting supplies the designed specification of the creature.
  - 87:2 **خَلَقَ** خ ل ق B002: Originating and bringing into being supplies realization of the measured design.

## outer inner coherence
- reading: The creature's visible form and inward disposition were brought into a coherent soundness.
- mechanism: Creation is not only an exterior outline. Visible form and inward character are co-formed, and the following leveling makes them coherent rather than merely geometrically symmetric.
- trace:
  - 87:2 **خَلَقَ** خ ل ق B003: Complete, balanced, visibly formed features supply the exterior dimension.

## aptitude then orientation
