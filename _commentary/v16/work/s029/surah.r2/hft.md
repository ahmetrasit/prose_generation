# Focus 29:38

## evidence insight gap
- reading: They were diverted while external evidence was clear and inward discernment was available; seeing did not become governing commitment.
- mechanism: Outward disclosure from settled sites and inward heart-sight flank the account of diversion. The failure is therefore not lack of available evidence or perceptual capacity but failure of discernment to govern movement.
- trace:
  - 29:38 **تَّبَيَّنَ** ب ي ن B004: The branch image of emergence and disclosure makes the dwellings disclose evidence rather than merely contain it.
  - 29:38 **مَّسَٰكِنِهِمْ** س ك ن B002: The branch image of inhabiting a home supplies concrete settled sites as the medium of disclosure.
  - 29:38 **مُسْتَبْصِرِينَ** ب ص ر B002: The branch image of heart-sight supplies active inward discernment and makes the cognition-conduct split explicit.

## valuation capture
- reading: Diversion is mediated by attractive revaluation of their own purposive conduct: they keep acting while agency is rerouted.
- mechanism: The sequence runs through valuation rather than information: intentional deeds are made to look good, their authors remain active, and that attractive action-loop diverts them from a traversable route.
- trace:
  - 29:38 **وَزَيَّنَ** ز ي ن B002: The branch image of displaying and improving beauty supplies the altered valuation of the deeds.
  - 29:38 **ٱلشَّيْطَٰنُ** ش ط ن B004: The branch image of the rebellious, overstepping shaytan supplies the agent that engineers the altered valuation.
  - 29:38 **أَعْمَٰلَهُمْ** ع م ل B001: The branch image of purposive action makes their own intentional conduct the medium of capture.
  - 29:38 **فَصَدَّهُمْ** ص د د B001: The branch image of turning away and turning another supplies the resulting redirection.
  - 29:38 **ٱلسَّبِيلِ** س ب ل B001: The branch image of an extended traversable road supplies the course from which action is redirected.

## settlement reversal
- reading: Their instruments of settlement are precisely what survive to expose the failure of their claim to durable stability.
- mechanism: A dwelling arrests motion and establishes a place of permanence, yet here the settled places outlast their occupants only as evidence. The technology of stability is reversed into testimony against permanence.
- trace:
  - 29:38 **مَّسَٰكِنِهِمْ** س ك ن B001: The branch image of motion ceasing supplies the stabilizing function that the ruined site reverses.
  - 29:38 **مَّسَٰكِنِهِمْ** س ك ن B009: The branch image of a place of settled rest supplies the material locus that survives as witness.
  - 29:38 **تَّبَيَّنَ** ب ي ن B004: The branch image of becoming manifest converts the former place of stability into legible testimony.

## discernment under assay
- reading: Their insight is a capacity under behavioral assay; beautified work reveals that cognition failed to become truthful judgment in motion.
- mechanism: The opening sequence tests professed security by separating truthful claim from evil work and faulty judgment. Applied to مُسْتَبْصِرِينَ, insight is equipment under assay; beautification causes failure at the transfer from judgment to action.
- trace:
  - 29:38 **مُسْتَبْصِرِينَ** ب ص ر B002: Heart-sight supplies the cognitive capacity whose practical force is being tested.
  - 29:2 **يُفْتَنُونَ** ف ت ن B001: The branch image of assaying metal or a person supplies a test that reveals what cognition is worth in conduct.
  - 29:3 **صَدَقُوا۟** ص د ق B004: The branch image of making a promise or act real supplies behavioral verification rather than verbal correctness alone.
  - 29:4 **يَعْمَلُونَ** ع م ل B001: The branch image of purposive work places intended deeds, the same medium as the focus ayah, under the assay.
  - 29:4 **يَحْكُمُونَ** ح ك م B003: The branch image of accurate wisdom supplies the evaluative standard that their judgment misses.

## rival path and burden transfer
- reading: Diversion may install a socially sponsored rival route whose appeal includes the fiction that someone else can absorb its consequences.
- mechanism: A spoken invitation to follow 'our path' is coupled to a promise that others will carry the resulting errors, then reversed into multiplied burdens. Path-blocking can therefore work by recruitment onto a rival route backed by false moral outsourcing.
- trace:
  - 29:38 **فَصَدَّهُمْ** ص د د B001: Turning another away supplies the focus ayah's blocked movement that the rival invitation operationalizes.
  - 29:12 **ٱتَّبِعُوا۟** ت ب ع B001: The branch image of following behind supplies recruitment into a socially led route.

# Focus 29:39

## presented proof and failed escape
- reading: Moses makes disclosure present before them; their self-enlargement is a response to that exposure, but it cannot convert control into escape from what follows.
- mechanism: The signs are actively brought into the rulers' presence and disclose what is at issue; their self-exaltation follows that exposure, while the closing pursuit image denies that territorial command can become escape.
- trace:
  - 29:39 **جَآءَهُم** ج ي ء B004: Bringing or presenting something makes Moses' coming an active placement of the signs before the named rulers.
  - 29:39 **بِٱلْبَيِّنَٰتِ** ب ي ن B004: Visible disclosure makes the signs an exposure against which the following self-exaltation reacts.
  - 29:39 **فَٱسْتَكْبَرُوا۟** ك ب ر B006: Arrogant self-greatness supplies the resistant response to what has been made clear.
  - 29:39 **سَٰبِقِينَ** س ب ق B004: Getting away from a pursuer gives the final negation a pursuit-and-non-escape contour.

## inflated on low ground
- reading: Their power is pictured as inflation on the lower plane: apparent height without forward lead, and territorial largeness that remains earthbound.
- mechanism: Images of magnitude, the lower plane, adhesion to the ground, and forward racing create a two-axis irony: apparent vertical enlargement occurs on low ground and yields no horizontal lead.
- trace:
  - 29:39 **فَٱسْتَكْبَرُوا۟** ك ب ر B001: Magnitude opposed to smallness gives the self-exalting form a spatial image of claimed size.
  - 29:39 **ٱلْأَرْضِ** ء ر ض B001: The lower plane opposite the sky locates their claimed magnitude within a bounded low domain.
  - 29:39 **ٱلْأَرْضِ** ء ر ض B006: Adhesion and heaviness toward the ground provide a material analogy for power that cannot lift free.
  - 29:39 **سَٰبِقِينَ** س ب ق B001: Forward movement in a race supplies the denied horizontal lead.

## miscalculated race
- reading: Their non-escape instantiates the surah's recurring diagnosis of a bad calculation: wrongdoing never gains a usable lead or an outside jurisdiction.
- mechanism: The earlier question about wrongdoers supposing they can outstrip, followed by the denial of evasive power in earth or sky, turns the focus ending into the answer to a recurring escape calculation.
- trace:
  - 29:39 **سَٰبِقِينَ** س ب ق B004: Escape from a pursuer anchors the focus clause as the terminal denial in a repeated race motif.
  - 29:4 **حَسِبَ** ح س ب B002: Supposition or estimation makes attempted outstripping a mistaken calculation rather than a demonstrated capacity.
  - 29:4 **يَسْبِقُونَا** س ب ق B004: The same escape-from-pursuit image states explicitly what evildoers imagine they can achieve.
  - 29:22 **بِمُعْجِزِينَ** ع ج ز B002: Evading pursuit or making the pursuer fail turns spatial incapacity into a direct counterpart of non-outstripping.
  - 29:22 **ٱلْأَرْضِ** ء ر ض B001: The earthly domain grounds one half of the verse's exhaustive denial of an exit in earth or sky.

## warning becomes residue
- reading: They are the first phase of a two-sided disclosure: evidence arrives before refusal, and the refusers' outcome can remain as evidence afterward.
- mechanism: A clear sign is left after one destroyed community, another outcome is visible from its dwellings, and then Moses brings clear signs before the focus rulers; warning evidence and aftermath evidence form one cycle in which refusers can become evidence for later viewers.
- trace:
  - 29:39 **جَآءَهُم** ج ي ء B004: Presentation makes the focus signs the pre-refusal phase of the proposed evidentiary cycle.
  - 29:39 **بِٱلْبَيِّنَٰتِ** ب ي ن B004: Visible disclosure anchors the signs as evidence already clear before the rulers exalt themselves.
  - 29:35 **ءَايَةًۢ** ء ي ي B003: A manifest marker supplies the surviving evidentiary object left after the preceding destruction.
  - 29:35 **بَيِّنَةً** ب ي ن B004: Visible disclosure characterizes the remnant as clear aftermath evidence.
  - 29:38 **تَّبَيَّنَ** ب ي ن B004: Becoming visibly clear from former dwellings extends disclosure from a delivered sign to an inspectable outcome.

## sight redirected not absent
- reading: The text permits sight to remain active: embellished practice and self-ranking redirect discernment away from the disclosed path, so arrogance is obstruction despite insight.
- mechanism: The immediately preceding exemplar links embellished deeds to diversion from an extended path while insight remains; this makes the focus response to clear signs readable as motivated redirection of perception rather than simple lack of perception.
- trace:
  - 29:39 **بِٱلْبَيِّنَٰتِ** ب ي ن B004: Visible disclosure anchors the focus problem in acknowledged clarity rather than unavailable evidence.

  - 29:38 **فَصَدَّهُمْ** ص د د B001: Turning away or diverting converts embellished valuation into blocked movement.
  - 29:38 **ٱلسَّبِيلِ** س ب ل B001: An extended traversable path gives the diversion a directional target.
  - 29:38 **مُسْتَبْصِرِينَ** ب ص ر B002: Insight of the heart preserves perceptual capacity even while that capacity is redirected.

## territory turns capture medium
- reading: Earth is not neutral territory beneath their power: the next verse turns created media into capture mechanisms, exposing territorial mastery as enclosure.
- mechanism: The next verse answers earth-bound self-exaltation and non-outstripping with universal seizure by offense and differentiated capture, including sinking through earth and drowning; the rulers' apparent domain becomes one of the media that enclose them.
- trace:

- reading: Prayer repeatedly retrains attention and action where even genuine insight can be overridden by beautified wrongdoing.
- mechanism: A people can possess inward sight while their actions are beautified and turn them away from the path.
