# s095 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. Form, Uprightness, and Reversal
- Semantic invariant: A measured form is brought into visible integrity, held upright, and remains liable to return and lowering.
- Surface relation: direct; 95:4 `خلقنا الإنسان في أحسن تقويم` and 95:5 `رددناه أسفل سافلين`
- Surprising reach: Human proportion extends into the mechanics of standing, structural support, restoration, and directed descent.

#### Subchannel A. Measured Human Formation
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: A human form is measured, created, completed, beautified, and set in rectitude.
- Active motifs: measured design `خ ل ق:B001/m01`, creative bringing-forth `خ ل ق:B002/m01`, completed visible form `خ ل ق:B003/m01`, human creature `ء ن س:B001/m01`, desirable beauty `ح س ن:B001/m01`, broad robust body `ب ل د:B008/m01`, rectitude and evenness `ق و م:B008/m01`, upright stature `ق و م:B011/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 95:3 `البلد`; 95:4 `خلقنا`، `الإنسان`، `أحسن`، `تقويم`
- Synthesis: Measurement precedes creation, creation resolves into a perceptible human figure, and beauty coincides with straightness and proportion. Broad bodily robustness and upright stature make the well-set form visible as both balanced outline and sustaining build.

#### Subchannel B. Standing Support and Downward Return
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: An upright body or structure is sustained, returned from its established state, and directed into a lower position.
- Active motifs: bodily standing `ق و م:B002/m01`, structural support `ق و م:B009/m01`, return to a former state `ر د د:B001/m01`, directed descent `س ف ل:B004/m01`, lower position `س ف ل:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 95:4 `تقويم`; 95:5 `رددناه`، `أسفل`، `سافلين`
- Synthesis: Standing supplies the initial stable configuration, while support explains how that configuration persists. Return and downward direction then form a single reversal process in which what stood is placed back into a lower state.

### 2. Functional Repair and Substitution
- Semantic invariant: A defective or absent function is restored by activation, compatible replacement, restraint, or exact repair.
- Surface relation: indirect; 95:4 `خلقنا` and `تقويم`, 95:5 `رددناه`, 95:6 `عملوا الصالحات` and `غير`, 95:8 `أحكم`
- Surprising reach: Creation and good action open into a technical scene of operating parts, substituting equivalents, arresting failure, and securing a finished repair.

#### Subchannel A. Making and Activating
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A thing is manufactured, put into operation, and taken up as an active task.
- Active motifs: manufacture from material `خ ل ق:B002/m02`, operation and application `ع م ل:B002/m01`, rising to undertake an affair `ق و م:B003/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 95:4 `خلقنا`، `تقويم`; 95:6 `عملوا`
- Synthesis: Manufacture provides the object, application makes it operative, and rising to the affair supplies the active operator. Together they form a compact production-to-use sequence.

#### Subchannel B. Compatible Replacement and Restoration
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: One item stands in for another, fits its intended place, and restores the prior arrangement without delay.
- Active motifs: standing in another's place `ق و م:B007/m01`, replacement by another `غ ي ر:B003/m01`, return to owner or place `ر د د:B001/m02`, functional suitability `ص ل ح:B003/m01`, enactment without delay `ك ذ ب:B005/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 95:4 `تقويم`; 95:5 `رددناه`; 95:6 `الصالحات`، `غير`; 95:7 `يكذبك`
- Synthesis: Substitution is not mere exchange: the replacement must fit the vacant function and be returned into the operative position. Immediacy closes the mechanism by preventing the restored function from remaining suspended.

#### Subchannel C. Restraint, Repulsion, and Repair
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Harm or corruption is stopped, pushed away, and replaced with a sound condition.
- Active motifs: restraint against corruption `ح ك م:B001/m01`, repulsion and prevention `ر د د:B002/m01`, repair after فساد `ص ل ح:B001/m02`, corrective substitution `غ ي ر:B003/m02`, repair of equipment `غ ي ر:B001/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 95:5 `رددناه`; 95:6 `الصالحات`، `غير`; 95:8 `أحكم`
- Synthesis: Restraint blocks the damaging motion, repulsion removes it from its course, and repair reinstates sound operation. Corrective substitution supplies the practical means by which the harmful state is exchanged for a serviceable one.

#### Subchannel D. Exact and Beneficial Finish
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Work is completed with precision until it is secure, sound, and beneficial.
- Active motifs: exact workmanship `ح س ن:B002/m02`, secure finishing `ح ك م:B004/m01`, sound integrity `ص ل ح:B001/m01`, beneficial maintenance `غ ي ر:B001/m01`, fracture mended on a bend `ء ج ر:B002/m01`, absence of useful result `ب ع د:B009/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 95:4 `أحسن`; 95:6 `الصالحات`، `غير`، `أجر`; 95:7 `بعد`; 95:8 `أحكم`
- Synthesis: Exact execution leads to a tightly secured and useful result. A fracture that heals crooked and an outcome with no benefit supply the contrary finish, distinguishing mere completion from sound, effective completion.

### 3. Judgment, Authority, and Truth
- Semantic invariant: Claims, conduct, and obligations are entrusted to an authority that distinguishes truth, assigns judgment, and brings a final account.
- Surface relation: direct; 95:6 `آمنوا`, 95:7 `يكذبك بالدين`, and 95:8 `أحكم الحاكمين`
- Surprising reach: Final judgment reaches into arbitration, testimony, counterfeit rejection, fabricated speech, and the contrast between penetrating and weak counsel.

#### Subchannel A. Adjudication and Final Reckoning
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: A measured case comes before judgment, receives an account, and culminates in the standing of resurrection.
- Active motifs: adjudication between people `ح ك م:B002/m01`, reckoning and judgment `د ي ن:B002/m01`, resurrection and standing `ق و م:B013/m01`, prior estimation `خ ل ق:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 95:4 `خلقنا`، `تقويم`; 95:7 `الدين`; 95:8 `أحكم`، `الحاكمين`
- Synthesis: Estimation frames the case, adjudication determines it, and reckoning gives the determination its consequence. Resurrection supplies the terminal scene in which those subject to judgment stand for the final account.

#### Subchannel B. Delegated Judgment and Credited Testimony
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A person is trusted, authorized to decide, and either credited or charged with falsehood.
- Active motifs: delegated arbitration `ح ك م:B005/m01`, crediting testimony or oath `د ي ن:B007/m01`, assent and belief `ء م ن:B002/m01`, attribution of falsehood `ك ذ ب:B002/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 95:3 `الأمين`; 95:6 `آمنوا`; 95:7 `يكذبك`، `الدين`; 95:8 `أحكم`
- Synthesis: Trust grants standing to a witness or arbiter, delegated judgment gives that standing practical force, and attribution of truth or falsehood determines whether the entrusted word remains authoritative.

#### Subchannel C. Fabrication and Rejection
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A false account is manufactured, exposed, and refused like a defective or counterfeit item.
- Active motifs: falsehood opposed to truth `ك ذ ب:B001/m01`, fabricated speech `خ ل ق:B007/m01`, rejection of error or counterfeit `ر د د:B003/m01`, discrediting a speaker `ك ذ ب:B002/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 95:4 `خلقنا`; 95:5 `رددناه`; 95:7 `يكذبك`
- Synthesis: Fabrication gives falsehood the shape of a made object, while rejection treats it as something inspected and returned. The social act of discrediting the speaker completes the movement from manufactured claim to refused claim.

#### Subchannel D. Penetrating and Defective Judgment
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Sound discernment penetrates a matter, while weak opinion fails to reach or govern it.
- Active motifs: right-reaching wisdom `ح ك م:B003/m01`, penetrating counsel `ب ع د:B008/m01`, weak opinion `ل ي س:B007/m01`, desirable goodness `ح س ن:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 95:4 `أحسن`; 95:7 `بعد`; 95:8 `أليس`، `أحكم`
- Synthesis: Penetrating counsel gives judgment depth, wisdom directs it toward the right result, and desirable goodness names its valued outcome. Weak opinion forms the reversal: a mind present to the matter but unable to penetrate it.

### 4. Work, Exchange, and Recompense
- Semantic invariant: Deliberate labor enters relations of wage, debt, valuation, restitution, livelihood, and release.
- Surface relation: direct; 95:6 `عملوا الصالحات فلهم أجر غير ممنون` and 95:7 `الدين`
- Surprising reach: The unending reward scene expands into labor markets, credit, pricing, equal weights, rescission, public office, bondage, and release without ransom.

#### Subchannel A. Work and Uncut Wage
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: Deliberate good work earns a wage whose duration is not cut off.
- Active motifs: intentional work `ع م ل:B001/m01`, wage and recompense `ء ج ر:B001/m01`, worker's pay `ع م ل:B004/m01`, uncut duration `م ن ن:B001/m01`, sound deed `ص ل ح:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 95:6 `عملوا`، `الصالحات`، `أجر`، `ممنون`
- Synthesis: Intentional action supplies the labor, soundness qualifies the deed, and wage names the return. The cutting sense of `م ن ن` defines the reward negatively as a payment whose extension is not severed or diminished.

#### Subchannel B. Debt, Dealing, and Rescission
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Parties enter a deferred obligation, transact with one another, settle conflict, and may reclaim or unwind the exchange.
- Active motifs: debt and credit sale `د ي ن:B003/m01`, interpersonal dealing `ع م ل:B005/m01`, repossession and rescission `ر د د:B004/m01`, negotiated settlement `ص ل ح:B002/m01`, assessed compensation `ح ك م:B002/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 95:5 `رددناه`; 95:6 `عملوا`، `الصالحات`; 95:7 `الدين`; 95:8 `أحكم`
- Synthesis: Debt creates the continuing obligation, dealing establishes the relation between parties, and settlement governs disagreement within it. Rescission and assessed compensation provide two different closures: reversing the transaction or fixing what one party owes.

#### Subchannel C. Valuation and Equal Weight
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Goods or liabilities are priced, weighed to an even standard, and converted into quantified compensation.
- Active motifs: pricing and valuation `ق و م:B010/m01`, equal coin weight `ق و م:B015/m01`, measured quantity `م ن ن:B005/m01`, blood-money compensation `غ ي ر:B002/m01`, circulating market demand `ق و م:B018/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 95:4 `تقويم`; 95:6 `غير`، `ممنون`
- Synthesis: Valuation assigns an amount, equal weight stabilizes the unit, and measured quantity makes the amount transferable. Market circulation gives the unit an arena of exchange, while blood money extends quantified substitution from commodities to retaliation.

#### Subchannel D. Labor, Office, and Livelihood
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Workers perform manual or appointed service and receive the means by which life and social order are maintained.
- Active motifs: appointed public work `ع م ل:B003/m01`, manual workers `ع م ل:B006/m01`, livelihood and subsistence `ق و م:B009/m02`, wage `ء ج ر:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 95:4 `تقويم`; 95:6 `عملوا`، `أجر`
- Synthesis: Manual labor and appointed office are two organized forms of service. Wage converts that service into livelihood, while the support sense of `ق و م` makes livelihood what keeps both body and social arrangement standing.

#### Subchannel E. Bondage, Restitution, and Unransomed Release
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A person is held under coercive ownership, then either restored through return or released without a counterpayment.
- Active motifs: subjugation and enslavement `د ي ن:B004/m01`, release without ransom `م ن ن:B004/m01`, recovery and return `ر د د:B004/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 95:5 `رددناه`; 95:6 `ممنون`; 95:7 `الدين`
- Synthesis: Subjugation establishes control over the person, recovery reverses possession, and gratuitous release ends captivity without equivalent exchange. The scene turns the language of debt and payment into a contrast between priced return and unpriced freedom.

### 5. Faith, Worship, and Moral Character
- Semantic invariant: Inner disposition aligns with worship, belief, upright conduct, benefaction, and the possibility of religious return or rupture.
- Surface relation: direct; 95:4 `الإنسان في أحسن تقويم`, 95:6 `آمنوا وعملوا الصالحات`, 95:7 `الدين`, and 95:8 `الله`

- Active motifs: worship and the worshipped one `ء ل ه:B001/m01`, obedience and religion `د ي ن:B001/m01`, inward disposition `خ ل ق:B004/m01`, moral straightness `ق و م:B008/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 95:4 `خلقنا`، `تقويم`; 95:7 `الدين`; 95:8 `الله`
- Synthesis: Character supplies the inward formation, religion gives it an order of obedience, and worship directs it toward the divine. Straightness is the shared state in which inner disposition and enacted allegiance coincide.

#### Subchannel B. Faith, Good Work, and Beneficence
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: Belief becomes intentional sound action and exceeds bare correctness through beneficence.
- Active motifs: assent and faith `ء م ن:B002/m01`, intentional action `ع م ل:B001/m01`, rectitude in deed `ص ل ح:B001/m01`, beneficent action `ح س ن:B002/m01`

#### Subchannel C. Apostasy and Religious Return
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A person reverses religious allegiance, leaving an upright order for its contrary.
- Active motifs: apostatic return `ر د د:B005/m01`, religious obedience `د ي ن:B001/m01`, straight alignment `ق و م:B008/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 95:4 `تقويم`; 95:5 `رددناه`; 95:7 `الدين`
- Synthesis: Religious straightness supplies the initial alignment, while apostatic return gives reversal a specific social and doctrinal form. The same root that returns a thing to a prior state can therefore mark a departure from the adopted religious order.

#### Subchannel D. Benefaction and Reproachful Reminder
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A favor creates trust and benefit, but repeated self-credit can burden the recipient and undo the social value of the gift.
- Active motifs: benefaction `م ن ن:B003/m01`, reproachful recounting `م ن ن:B003/m02`, generous good `ح س ن:B002/m03`, trust and security `ء م ن:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 95:3 `الأمين`; 95:4 `أحسن`; 95:6 `ممنون`
- Synthesis: Benefaction establishes a relation of good and security. Recounting the favor shifts the gift from benefit to pressure, producing a moral reversal in which speech after the act can destroy the act's social integrity.

#### Subchannel B. Celestial Station and Zenith

- Scene or process: The sky is partitioned into a lunar station, a named celestial body, and the balanced instant of solar noon.

#### Subchannel C. Residence, City, and Civic Obedience
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: People remain in a settled place whose identity depends on residence, assembly, and obedience to authority.
- Active motifs: settled residence `ب ل د:B009/m01`, station and dwelling `ق و م:B006/m01`, city organized by obedience `د ي ن:B006/m01`, remaining without departure `ل ي س:B005/m01`

- Synthesis: Residence converts land into an inhabited station, prolonged remaining stabilizes it, and civic obedience gives the settlement an ordered political form. City is therefore both a place occupied and a relation maintained.

### 7. Boundary, Relative Position, and Separation

- Surface relation: indirect; 95:2 `طور`, 95:3 `البلد`, 95:4 `الإنسان`, 95:5 `أسفل`, 95:6 `عملوا`, and 95:7 `بعد`
- Surprising reach: Spatial language reaches from courtyards and distant places into spear anatomy, the human-facing side of an object, posteriority, and a lower bodily outlet.

#### Subchannel A. Edge, Enclosure, and Near-Far Relation
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A bounded place has an aligned edge, a protected vicinity, an approach zone, and a measurable distance beyond it.
- Active motifs: aligned edge or frontage `ط و ر:B001/m01`, approach to an enclosure `ط و ر:B002/m01`, terminal boundary `ط و ر:B003/m01`, distance from nearness `ب ع د:B001/m01`, bounded place `ب ل د:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 95:2 `طور`; 95:3 `البلد`; 95:7 `بعد`
- Synthesis: The aligned edge gives a place shape, the enclosure creates an inside and a vicinity, and approach measures movement toward that boundary. Distance names the corresponding movement away from it.

- Ayah anchors: 95:4 `تقويم`; 95:6 `الصالحات`; 95:8 `أحكم`، `الحاكمين`
- Synthesis: Confrontation supplies the opposed parties, reconciliation removes estrangement, and adjudication gives the agreement an authoritative form. The scene turns conflict into an ordered social relation rather than merely ending motion.

#### Subchannel B. Combat and the Truth of a Charge
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Combatants resist one another with weapons, and a charge is judged by whether the attacker carries it through or falters.
- Active motifs: sword or staff combat `ب ل د:B011/m01`, resistance in combat `ق و م:B014/m01`, far-reaching hostility `ب ع د:B010/m01`, committed charge `ك ذ ب:B004/m01`, faltering charge `ك ذ ب:B004/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 95:3 `البلد`; 95:4 `تقويم`; 95:7 `يكذبك`، `بعد`
- Synthesis: Hostility carries opposition into the encounter, armed combat gives it an instrument, and resistance holds the opponents against one another. The charge then tests resolve in motion: its truth is enacted by completion and its falsehood by stopping short.

#### Subchannel C. Office, Custom, and Civic Stewardship
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: An appointed official maintains a settled community through recurring office, customary order, and active stewardship.
- Active motifs: public appointment `ع م ل:B003/m01`, governance and stewardship `ق و م:B004/m02`, habitual order `د ي ن:B005/m01`, settled residence `ب ل د:B009/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 95:3 `البلد`; 95:4 `تقويم`; 95:6 `عملوا`; 95:7 `الدين`
- Synthesis: Settlement supplies the governed setting, office assigns responsibility, custom stabilizes repeated conduct, and stewardship keeps the arrangement functioning over time.

## Standalone Subchannels

### S1. Uncovered Roof and Open Exposure
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A roof or terrace rises within a place but lacks the parapet that would enclose its perimeter.
- Active motifs: roof without a protective wall `ء ج ر:B003/m01`, bounded or open place `ب ل د:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 95:3 `البلد`; 95:6 `أجر`
- Synthesis: The place supplies the spatial setting and the roof supplies elevation, while the missing enclosure makes exposure the scene's defining relation.

### S2. Water-Holding Hollow and Well Apparatus
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Water gathers in a rock hollow or newly dug well and is served by a standing lifting component.
- Active motifs: rock hollow or well that holds water `خ ل ق:B011/m01`, gathered water `ر د د:B007/m02`, standing pulley or well component `ق و م:B012/m03`
- Ayah anchors: 95:4 `خلقنا`، `تقويم`; 95:5 `رددناه`
- Synthesis: The hollow provides containment, gathered water supplies the stored material, and the standing pulley component makes the reservoir accessible as a working well.
