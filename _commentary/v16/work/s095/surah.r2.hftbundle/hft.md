# Focus 95:1

## edible complement
- reading: The pair displays two modes of edible yield: a singled fruit and a tree-borne collective crop.
- mechanism: The first noun supplies an edible fruit, while the second can name both olive tree and olive fruit. Their pairing presents complementary plant yields while retaining an asymmetry between a discrete fruit and a fruit-bearing crop.
- trace:
  - 95:1 **وَٱلتِّينِ** ت ي ن B001: The edible fig, including fruit eaten fresh or dried, supplies the pair's first directly consumable yield.
  - 95:1 **وَٱلزَّيْتُونِ** ز ي ت B002: The olive tree and its fruit supply a collective crop whose tree-fruit ambiguity complements the discrete fig.

## preserved extracted provision
- reading: The focus juxtaposes two harvest pathways—drying and pressing—through which perishable growth becomes provision.
- mechanism: The pair can be organized by material processing rather than taxonomy: fig passes from fresh to dried food, while olive yields oil by extraction and enters circulation as condiment or provision. Drying and pressing are two ways a harvest becomes durable and distributable.
- trace:
  - 95:1 **وَٱلتِّينِ** ت ي ن B001: The edible fig's fresh and dried states contribute a preservation pathway that changes the fruit while retaining its nourishment.
  - 95:1 **وَٱلزَّيْتُونِ** ز ي ت B001: Olive oil as extracted juice contributes transformation from contained fruit to released, usable yield.
  - 95:1 **وَٱلزَّيْتُونِ** ز ي ت B004: Oil as condiment, provision, or gift gives the extracted yield a social function of sustaining and supplying others.
  - 95:1 **وَٱلزَّيْتُونِ** ز ي ت B005: The oil presser and seller make labor and circulation visible inside the apparently simple plant name.

## landmark and grove
- reading: The conjunction may join a named landmark to its cultivated grove, allowing food and place readings to coexist.
- mechanism: One focus noun can designate a mountain or named place, while the other can designate a tree or crop. The pair therefore permits a spatial reading in which named terrain and characteristic vegetation jointly identify a landscape.
- trace:
  - 95:1 **وَٱلتِّينِ** ت ي ن B002: The fig as a named mountain or place shifts the first noun from an edible object to a geographic coordinate.
  - 95:1 **وَٱلزَّيْتُونِ** ز ي ت B002: The olive tree and fruit supply the vegetation by which a place can be materially characterized.

## nested protected landscape
- reading: The focus pair opens a protected-landscape itinerary in which productive terrain, mountain, bounded city, and security successively specify one spatial field.
- mechanism: The following mountain, bounded land, and security cues turn the baseline place-and-grove possibility into a nested topography. The opening pair can mark productive terrain, the mountain extends the upland scale, and the deictically present secure city narrows the sequence to protected inhabited ground.
- trace:
  - 95:1 **وَٱلتِّينِ** ت ي ن B002: The named mountain or place carried by the fig keeps the first focus noun available as an actual coordinate.
  - 95:1 **وَٱلزَّيْتُونِ** ز ي ت B002: The olive tree and crop give the coordinate a cultivated ecological signature.
  - 95:2 **وَطُورِ** ط و ر B005: The mountain or mountain-name branch repeats the focus place scale and extends it into an explicit upland landmark.
  - 95:3 **ٱلْبَلَدِ** ب ل د B001: The bounded tract of earth supplies a perimeter, converting loose landmarks into delimited inhabited space.
  - 95:3 **ٱلْأَمِينِ** ء م ن B001: Calm security and trust qualify the bounded land as protected rather than merely enclosed.

## formed yield and reversal
- reading: They also become small material demonstrations of formed yield: a good state can be achieved and disclosed, yet still be returned into another condition.
- mechanism: Fresh-to-dried fruit and fruit-to-oil extraction make the focus pair examples of achieved material states. Measuring creation, beauty, upright balance, return, and lowering then recast those yields as analogues for form that is produced, disclosed, and reversible rather than simply possessed.
- trace:
  - 95:1 **وَٱلتِّينِ** ت ي ن B001: The fig eaten fresh or dried contributes one substance carried through a visible change of state.
  - 95:1 **وَٱلزَّيْتُونِ** ز ي ت B001: Oil as the olive's extracted yield contributes a latent content that becomes manifest through transformation.
  - 95:4 **خَلَقْنَا** خ ل ق B001: Creation as measuring and proportioning supplies the operation by which an achieved form is set.
  - 95:4 **أَحْسَنِ** ح س ن B001: Beauty opposed to ugliness supplies a qualitative pole for the formed result.
  - 95:4 **تَقْوِيمٍ** ق و م B008: Straightness, moderation, and levelness give the human form an upright equilibrium rather than a static shape alone.

  - 95:5 **أَسْفَلَ سَٰفِلِينَ** س ف ل B001: Physical low position supplies the downward endpoint of the reversal.

## sustenance as qiwam
- reading: Fig and olive become the material qiwam of the body: provision imagined as the support that keeps formed life standing against decline.
- mechanism: The focus foods can be read as qiwam: livelihood that acts like a supporting pillar. Upright form, downward movement, and the force by which a thing stands or loses strength make nourishment function as material maintenance of embodied standing, not merely as abundance.
- trace:
  - 95:1 **وَٱلتِّينِ** ت ي ن B001: The edible fig supplies immediate nourishment as one material support of life.
  - 95:1 **وَٱلزَّيْتُونِ** ز ي ت B004: Oil as condiment and provision supplies stored sustenance that can maintain a person or group.
  - 95:4 **تَقْوِيمٍ** ق و م B009: Qiwam as pillar, subsistence, and livelihood explicitly joins economic sustenance to structural support.
  - 95:5 **أَسْفَلَ سَٰفِلِينَ** س ف ل B004: The act of moving downward supplies the collapse that contrasts with maintained standing.
  - 95:6 **مَمْنُونٍ** م ن ن B002: The force by which something stands, or the loss of that force, makes continuity of support the mechanism's hinge.

## labor value and uncut return

- mechanism: The oil presser and seller already place labor and exchange inside the focus. Later valuation, work-wage, recompense, possible cutting-off, and accounting unfold that seed into an economy: finite crops are processed and priced, while the promised return is emphatically not exhausted like ordinary provision.
- trace:
  - 95:1 **وَٱلتِّينِ** ت ي ن B001: The edible fig, preserved fresh or dried, supplies a finite crop that can be stored and circulated.
  - 95:1 **وَٱلزَّيْتُونِ** ز ي ت B004: Oil as provision or gift supplies the distributable good around which exchange and obligation can form.
  - 95:1 **وَٱلزَّيْتُونِ** ز ي ت B005: The presser and seller expose the otherwise hidden labor and market roles behind the olive product.
  - 95:4 **تَقْوِيمٍ** ق و م B010: Valuation and pricing supply the operation that assigns exchange value to produced goods.
  - 95:6 **وَعَمِلُوا۟** ع م ل B004: The wage and provision of a worker connect intentional labor to a due material return.
  - 95:6 **أَجْرٌ** ء ج ر B001: Recompense for work or hire formalizes the return side of the labor relation.
  - 95:6 **مَمْنُونٍ** م ن ن B001: Cutting and diminishing until extension ceases supply the finite limit that the grammatical negation refuses.
  - 95:7 **بِٱلدِّينِ** د ي ن B002: Accounting and recompense extend the crop-and-wage chain into a final reckoning of value.
