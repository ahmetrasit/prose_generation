# s039 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### P1.1 Purification by Separation (39:1-20)

- **Semantic invariant:** A valid relation to God is produced by removing admixture, rival claims, and inferior residues until a single allegiance or usable core remains.
- **Surface relation:** direct — 39:2-4, 9, 11, 14, 18-19; anchors include `خ ل ص`, `ص ف و`, `و ح د`, `ل ب ب`, `ح س ن`, and `ن ق ذ`.
- **Surprising reach:** The same separation logic governs both exclusive worship and discerning reception: devotion is distilled, while hearers extract the best operative kernel from speech.

#### P1.1.a Distilled allegiance

- **Reading type:** mixed
- **Scene/process:** Competing attachments are removed until worship, religion, and divine identity admit no shared claim.
- **Active motifs:** `quranic:root_000430:B001/m01` purification after admixture; `quranic:root_000430:B004/m01` exclusive allotment apart from partnership; `quranic:root_000873:B001/m01` clear essence; `quranic:root_000873:B002/m01` chosen selection; `quranic:root_001631:B001/m01` individuation and separation.
- **Ayah anchors:** 39:2-3, 11, 14 (`خ ل ص`); 39:4 (`ص ف و`); 39:4, 6 (`و ح د`).
- **Synthesis:** Purity is not merely sincerity as an inward quality. It is an operation that separates a claim from every competing allotment and leaves it indivisibly assigned.

#### P1.1.b Kernel selection and extraction

- **Reading type:** mixed
- **Scene/process:** Speech is received, compared, and followed at its best, while a threatened person is imagined as something pulled free from enclosure.
- **Active motifs:** `quranic:root_001338:B003/m01` pure core or kernel; `quranic:root_000323:B001/m01` the desirable best; `quranic:root_000175:B001/m01` following a trace or command; `quranic:root_001540:B001/m01` extraction from entrapment.
- **Ayah anchors:** 39:9, 18 (`ل ب ب`); 39:18 (`ح س ن`, `ت ب ع`); 39:19 (`ن ق ذ`).
- **Synthesis:** Discernment repeats the purification channel at the level of reception: the hearer isolates a sound core and follows it, whereas the condemned cannot simply be extracted after judgment has enclosed him.
