# s026 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. Disclosure Made Perceptible (P1, 26:1-20)
- Semantic invariant: A message hidden in source or medium becomes an inspectable sign, intelligible statement, or audible summons.
- Surface relation: direct; 26:2-6 presents signs, a clear book, renewed reminder, denial, and arriving news, while 26:10-17 turns disclosure into a call and commissioned speech.
- Surprising reach: The lexical field makes revelation simultaneously inscription, evidentiary document, emergence into clarity, and voiced dispatch.

#### Subchannel A. Inscribed and Evidentiary Disclosure
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A written object bears signs whose meaning emerges clearly and whose renewed presentation functions like documentary evidence.
- Active motifs: written artifact (`quranic:root_001283:B002/m01`); visible sign (`quranic:root_000074:B003/m01`); emergence into clarity (`quranic:root_000170:B004/m01`); meaning disclosed by sign or writing (`quranic:root_000170:B005/m01`); right-bearing document (`quranic:root_000516:B008/m01`); renewed report (`quranic:root_000299:B003/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 26:2 `ءايت`, `الكتب`, `المبين`; 26:4 `ءاية`; 26:5 `ذكر`, `محدث`
- Synthesis: The clear book is not merely a container of propositions. Its signs materialize disclosure as something written, made visible, and brought out of obscurity. The latent documentary sense of `ذكر` sharpens the reminder into a warrant that can stand against denial, while `محدث` supplies the recurring event of its presentation.

#### Subchannel B. Voiced Dispatch and Reception
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A raised call initiates a dispatch whose message travels through tongue and messenger toward hearing and eventual report.
- Active motifs: raised call (`quranic:root_001486:B002/m01`); sending or releasing (`quranic:root_000563:B001/m01`); messenger and message (`quranic:root_000563:B002/m01`); articulated tongue (`quranic:root_001355:B001/m01`); relayed communication (`quranic:root_001355:B008/m01`); auditory reception (`quranic:root_000741:B001/m01`); responsive hearing (`quranic:root_000741:B003/m01`); arriving report (`quranic:root_001464:B002/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 26:6 `انبؤا`; 26:10 `نادى`; 26:13 `لسانى`, `فارسل`; 26:15 `مستمعون`; 26:16 `رسول`; 26:17 `ارسل`
- Synthesis: The call to Moses unfolds as a transmission chain: summons, release, bearer, tongue, hearing, and report. The same scene pressures the primary reading by making failed reception part of the channel itself: an audible message has reached its audience even when responsive hearing does not follow.

### 2. Embodied Pressure and Release (P1, 26:1-20)
- Semantic invariant: Missionary resistance is registered in the body as depletion, lowered posture, constriction, and threatened injury, then countered by release into speech.
- Surface relation: direct; 26:3-4 pictures self-consuming grief and bowed necks, while 26:12-15 names fear, a constricted chest, an unfree tongue, threatened killing, and divine accompaniment.
- Surprising reach: Emotional and political pressure become anatomical mechanics, with breath, neck, chest, and tongue forming one pressure-and-release system.

#### Subchannel A. Self-Consuming Grief
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Unanswered concern exhausts the self as though grief were an act of bodily destruction.
- Active motifs: killing oneself from grief (`quranic:root_000088:B001/m01`); wasting exhaustion (`quranic:root_000088:B003/m01`); breath leaving the body (`quranic:root_001533:B001/m01`); relief after distress (`quranic:root_001533:B002/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 26:3 `بخع`, `نفسك`
- Synthesis: `باخع نفسك` is amplified by the branch's exhaustion sense: concern for unbelief consumes vitality rather than remaining an abstract emotion. The same-root movement from the living self and breath toward relief keeps open the counter-image of a self restored after pressure.

#### Subchannel B. Lowered Neck and Constricted Chest
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: External compulsion bends the neck while anticipated rejection narrows the chest and threatens the neck with cutting.
- Active motifs: lowered neck or head (`quranic:root_000419:B002/m01`); neck as vulnerable member (`quranic:root_001053:B001/m01`); severing the neck bone (`quranic:root_000088:B004/m01`); chest as bodily cavity (`quranic:root_000849:B001/m01`); constriction of the chest (`quranic:root_000925:B002/m01`); fear before danger (`quranic:root_000447:B001/m01`)

- Synthesis: The hypothetical sign makes resistance collapse into bowed anatomy, whereas Moses experiences mission as inward narrowing. The latent neck-cutting sense links compelled submission to the explicit threat of killing, producing a tight bodily scene of vulnerability rather than a generic motif of fear.

#### Subchannel C. Speech Released from Restraint
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A tongue that does not move freely is released through shared commission and guaranteed hearing.
- Active motifs: release from a fetter (`quranic:root_000946:B001/m01`); unobstructed movement (`quranic:root_000946:B003/m01`); tongue as speech organ (`quranic:root_001355:B001/m01`); sending a delegate (`quranic:root_000563:B001/m01`); attentive hearing (`quranic:root_000741:B001/m01`)

- Synthesis: The failure of the tongue to `ينطلق` is a restraint scene: speech cannot set out. Sending to Aaron and the promise of listening reverse that immobility, so delegated speech becomes the practical release of a bound communicative faculty.

### 3. Cultivation and Human Formation (P1, 26:1-20)
- Semantic invariant: Growth requires a receptive substrate, sustaining moisture, differentiation, and time, whether the product is vegetation or a formed human life.
- Surface relation: indirect; 26:7 explicitly shows earth producing noble pairs, while 26:9-10 and 26:18-19 activate latent rain, irrigation, nurture, age, and seed-covering senses.
- Surprising reach: Royal upbringing and prophetic emergence are reframed through an agricultural process in which cultivation does not guarantee ownership of the mature yield.

### 4. Rejection as Covering and Deflection (P1, 26:1-20)
- Semantic invariant: Resistance prevents a received sign from governing conduct by hiding, turning from, falsifying, or ridiculing it.
- Surface relation: direct; 26:5-6 describes turning away, denial, and mockery, and 26:12, 26:19-20 revisits accusation, covering, and acknowledged error.
- Surprising reach: Rejection operates less as a single belief-state than as a sequence of spatial and rhetorical evasions.

#### Subchannel A. Concealment and Turning Aside
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: What has arrived is covered over and the recipient presents a side rather than a receptive face.
- Active motifs: concealment of truth (`quranic:root_001307:B003/m01`); concealment of received favor (`quranic:root_001307:B004/m01`); turning or presenting the side (`quranic:root_001001:B005/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 26:5 `معرضين`; 26:19 `الكفرين`; 26:20 `الضالين`
- Synthesis: Covering and turning are distinct operations. The first obscures what is present; the second redirects the self away from it. Their conjunction makes disbelief a practiced deflection, while Moses' earlier `ضلال` remains a separable error of route rather than the same operation.

#### Subchannel B. Falsification and Ridicule
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: A message is branded false, its bearer anticipates the same accusation, and delayed consequences become the object of ridicule.
- Active motifs: making a lie (`quranic:root_001290:B001/m01`); accusing another of lying (`quranic:root_001290:B002/m01`); mockery and derision (`quranic:root_001587:B001/m01`); attributing unsaid speech (`quranic:root_001272:B005/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 26:6 `كذبوا`, `يستهزءون`; 26:12 `يكذبون`; 26:18-20 `قال`
- Synthesis: Falsification acts on the status of the message, while ridicule acts on the relation between warning and outcome. The latent attribution sense of speech adds a rhetorical pressure point: rejection can manufacture a statement to dismiss rather than answer the one actually delivered.

### 7. Rank and Allegiance Reversed (P2, 26:21-51)

- Surface relation: direct; 26:40-42 negotiates reward and proximity, 26:46-48 enacts prostration and faith, and 26:49-51 rejects royal harm in favor of return to God.
- Surprising reach: Bodily lowering produces moral elevation, while threatened loss becomes the route by which the contestants name their true destination.

- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Skilled agents bargain for compensation and elevated access if their performance secures domination.

### 9. Freedom and Claimed Benefaction (P2, 26:21-51)

- Synthesis: Flight initially marks exposure and loss of standing. Gifted judgment converts that movement into preparation, and commission supplies a new rank not granted by the court from which Moses fled.

#### Subchannel B. Enslavement Disguised as Favor
