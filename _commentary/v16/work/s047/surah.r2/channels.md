# s047 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. P1 - Directed movement and route integrity
- Semantic invariant: Purposeful movement depends on a traversable route, a reliable lead, and bodily stability; its failure appears as loss, stumbling, and reversal.
- Surface relation: direct; 47:1 and 47:4 name `سبيل`, 47:5 names guidance, 47:7 fixes `أقدامكم`, 47:8 invokes `تعساً`, and 47:10 commands travel and inspection.
- Surprising reach: The moral contrast is materialized as road infrastructure and convoy dynamics rather than remaining an abstract opposition between guidance and error.

#### Subchannel A. The worked road and its travelers
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A traveled road, especially one leading to water, is opened and maintained for wayfarers who move across the land.
- Active motifs: traversable path `quranic:root_000672:B001/m01`; wayfarers `quranic:root_000672:B002/m01`; waterward route `quranic:root_000848:B004/m01`; worked road `quranic:root_001046:B011/m01`; walking travelers `quranic:root_001046:B012/m01`; overland motion `quranic:root_000769:B001/m01`; travel in the land `quranic:root_000906:B002/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 47:1 `سبيل` (س ب ل), `صدوا` (ص د د), `أعمالهم` (ع م ل); 47:3 `يضرب` (ض ر ب); 47:4 `سبيل` (س ب ل), `أعمالهم` (ع م ل); 47:10 `يسيروا` (س ي ر).
- Synthesis: The channel joins the road itself, its water-directed function, its maintenance through use, and the people who walk it. The repeated way-language thus acquires a concrete logistics scene: blocking the way interrupts access, while right movement presupposes a road that has been made passable.

#### Subchannel B. Vanguard, direction, and stable feet
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: A guide goes ahead, fixes the direction of advance, and keeps the followers' feet from shifting.
- Active motifs: gentle guidance to a way `quranic:root_001583:B001/m01`; leading front or vanguard `quranic:root_001583:B003/m01`; weight-bearing foot `quranic:root_001207:B001/m01`; forward advance `quranic:root_001207:B004/m01`; staying fixed `quranic:root_000192:B001/m01`; active stabilization `quranic:root_000192:B003/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 47:5 `سيهديهم` (ه د ي); 47:7 `يثبت أقدامكم` (ث ب ت, ق د م).
- Synthesis: Guidance is not only information about a route. It occupies the lead position, establishes forward orientation, and transfers steadiness to the feet of those behind. The bodily image makes divine support an operational condition of movement.

#### Subchannel C. Losing the trace, falling, and turning back
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A traveler loses the intended line, misses the trace, stumbles without recovery, and reverses onto the heel.
- Active motifs: deviation from the intended way `quranic:root_000913:B001/m01`; loss of the sought thing `quranic:root_000913:B003/m01`; unrecovered stumble `quranic:root_000182:B001/m01`; heel `quranic:root_001033:B002/m01`; trailing trace `quranic:root_001033:B002/m02`; reversal on the heel `quranic:root_001033:B003/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 47:1, 47:4, and 47:8 `أضل/يضل` (ض ل ل); 47:8 `تعساً` (ت ع س); 47:10 `عاقبة` (ع ق ب).
- Synthesis: Error becomes a failed locomotion sequence rather than a static state. The lost trace leads to a fall, and the heel becomes the pivot of reversal, pressing the contrast between a completed journey and motion that undoes itself.

### 2. P1 - Force converted into custody and disposition
- Semantic invariant: Open combat is progressively constrained into immobilization, binding, custody, and a governed decision about release.
- Surface relation: direct; the entire sequence is concentrated in 47:4 with `ضرب الرقاب`, `أثخنتموهم`, `شدوا الوثاق`, `منّاً`, `فداء`, and the laying down of war's burdens.
- Surprising reach: Martial action is reframed as a controlled material process with tool anatomy, restraint technology, and a release economy.

#### Subchannel A. Spear assembly, impact, and immobilization
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A weapon is assembled from point and shaft, brought into impact, and used until the opponent can no longer move freely.
- Active motifs: direct impact `quranic:root_000906:B001/m01`; spearhead and cutting point `quranic:root_000302:B004/m01`; forward shaft below the point `quranic:root_001046:B009/m01`; disabling injury `quranic:root_000196:B003/m01`; lethal force `quranic:root_001200:B001/m01`

- Synthesis: The branch senses assemble a single weapon-and-effect scene: point, shaft, strike, wound, and loss of mobility. This material precision tightens the verse's transition from encounter to control.

#### Subchannel B. Neck, bond, and secured captive

#### Subchannel C. Release without price or recovery by exchange
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: Custody ends through one of two distinct dispositions: free release or recovery through a substitute payment or exchange.
- Active motifs: unpriced release of a captive `quranic:root_001449:B004/m01`; exchange or recovery of captives `quranic:root_001136:B002/m01`; a substitute given to save another `quranic:root_001136:B001/m01`

- Synthesis: The two alternatives are not collapsed. One removes the bond without countervalue; the other restores the captive by substitution. Together they complete the custody process without extending the combat scene beyond its proper operation.

### 3. P1 - Covering as occlusion, protection, and cultivation
- Semantic invariant: A covering changes the fate of what lies beneath it: it can hide truth, shield vulnerability, efface harm, or enable buried life to grow.
- Surface relation: indirect; 47:1-4 repeatedly use ك ف ر, 47:2 uses `كفّر عنهم`, 47:6 names the garden, and 47:10 places the scene in the earth.
- Surprising reach: The same-root transformation turns disbelief, expiation, shelter, and cultivation into sharply separated operations of covering.

#### Subchannel A. An occluding layer over sight and truth
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A broad cover and a woven eye-film conceal what would otherwise be visible, allowing the underlying thing to disappear from recognition.
- Active motifs: physical covering `quranic:root_001307:B001/m01`; occlusion of truth `quranic:root_001307:B003/m01`; woven eye-film `quranic:root_000672:B010/m01`; disappearance into concealment `quranic:root_000913:B002/m01`

- Synthesis: The scene is optical and material: a layer covers, a film crosses the eye, and the object recedes from view. It usefully pressures the primary reading by depicting denial as an obstruction of access rather than mere absence of assent.

#### Subchannel B. Shield, blind, screen, and effacement

- Synthesis: Unlike occlusion, this cover has a protective function. Shield, blind, and screen interpose a barrier, while expiation covers a harmful record; the shared invariant is defense, not concealment for its own sake.

#### Subchannel C. Seed cover, ear, husk, and enclosed growth
- Reading type: latent/lexical

- Synthesis: The cover here is generative. Burial protects seed long enough for an ear and enclosed fruit to emerge, converting the repeated covering root into a latent account of whether works reach growth or remain unproductive.

## Parent Channels

### 18. P1 + P3 - Passage secured or intercepted
- Semantic invariant: Directed transmission succeeds only while route, directional agent, and moving bearer remain aligned; obstruction converts passage into failed arrival.
- Surface relation: direct; P1 47:1, 47:4-5, and 47:7-10 join the way, guidance, stable feet, travel, and obstruction, while P3 47:32-34 and 47:38 join the messenger, guidance, the way, and renewed obstruction.
- Surprising reach: The opening movement scene becomes a closing transport scene for speech, and opposition reverses the moving role into one that intercepts movement.
- Pericopes and bridge: P1 (47:1-11) and P3 (47:22-38); repeated scene signature, role progression, and contrast: route plus directional agent plus bearer leads either to passage or interception.
- Canonical placements: P1 Parent 1, "Directed movement and route integrity," Subchannels A-C; P3 Parent 17, "Message passage and route obstruction," Subchannels A-B.
- Synthesis: P1 establishes the mechanics of a traversable course and a bearer kept on it. P3 preserves that structure while changing the carried object to articulated guidance and sharpening the opposed role into an interceptor. The bridge makes barring the way a concrete failure of transmission across the surah rather than a repeated topic.

### 19. P1 + P2 + P3 - External forms make absent conditions legible

- Pericopes and bridge: P1 (47:1-11), P2 (47:12-21), and P3 (47:22-38); repeated semiotic scene signature and role progression: represented referent plus external sign plus interpreter.
- Canonical placements: P1 Parent 4, Subchannel B, "The parable as fabricated model"; P2 Parent 8, Subchannel A, "Marker, landmark, and beacon"; P3 Parent 14, Subchannel B, "Extraction, mark, and diagnostic tone."
- Synthesis: Each pericope assigns a different source to the sign while retaining the same interpretive relation. - Synthesis: The bridge does not collapse different closure operations. ### 21. P1 + P2 + P3 - Sustenance completed by routed outflow
- Semantic invariant: Provision becomes effective when supply leaves its source, follows an open conduit, reaches a recipient, and produces usable value; blocked outflow arrests the sequence.
- Surface relation: indirect; P1 47:4, 47:7, and 47:10-11 support rain as arriving aid, P2 47:12 and 47:15 present flowing water, food, fruit, and drink, and P3 47:36-38 presents wealth, expenditure, withholding, and need.
