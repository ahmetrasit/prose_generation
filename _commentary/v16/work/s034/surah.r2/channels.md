### S10. P1-P2-P3-P4, Causal Bridge: Fulfillment Adjudicates Speech
- Reading type: mixed

- Synthesis: The causal bridge is fulfillment as the adjudicator of competing speech labels. P1 supplies the accusation of lying, P2 shows an expectation acquiring truth through enacted response, P3 ties truthfulness to a promised appointment, and P4 turns what was denied into experienced consequence. The progression supports the surah's primary claim that an audience cannot determine truth by naming it false: conduct and event make speech answer to its referent.

### S11. P1-P2 to P4, Contrast Bridge: Covered Liability or Accountable Seizure
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Liability is first presented as coverable so that its bearer is protected from its effect; at the final panic, taking hold carries the alternate sense of holding a person to account for what issued from them.
- Active motifs: forgiveness as covering sin and protecting its bearer from consequence (`quranic:root_001096:B002/m01`), taking a person to account for sin (`quranic:root_000018:B002/m01`)

- Synthesis: The contrast bridge is the alternate handling of one liability: it is either covered with its bearer shielded from the result, or it terminates in the bearer's seizure for account. The forgiving descriptions in P1 and P2 therefore pressure the near capture of P4 with a juridical sense. Mercy is protection from an effect that remains real, while deferred seizure is not acquittal.
