# S080 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. Directed Awareness and Cognitive Response
- Semantic invariant: A subject opens, directs, withholds, or loses perceptual and cognitive access to an object.
- Surface relation: direct; 80:1-12 turns on attention, blindness, fear, reminder, and distraction, while 80:24 commands reflective looking.
- Surprising reach: The same system extends from eyes and ears into sentinels, debate, impaired hearts, and total self-surrender.

#### Subchannel A. Seeing, Gaze, and Occlusion
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A viewer directs sight or reflection toward an object while blindness, distance, or concealment can block access.
- Active motifs: directed seeing and reflection `ن ظ ر:B001/m01`; intense gaze `ح د ق:B004/m01`; sensory detection `ء ن س:B002/m01`; bodily blindness `ع م ي:B001/m01`; blindness of the heart `ع م ي:B002/m01`; far sight `ش ي ء:B003/m01`.
- Ayah anchors: `ن ظ ر` 80:24 `يَنظُرِ`; `ح د ق` 80:30 `حَدَائِقَ`; `ء ن س` 80:17,24 `إِنسَانُ`; `ع م ي` 80:2 `أَعْمَىٰ`; `ش ي ء` 80:12,18,22 `شَاءَ/شَىْءٍ`.
- Synthesis: Directed sight, inward reflection, and long-range seeing form one access scene whose negative forms are physical blindness and the heart's inability to apprehend.

#### Subchannel B. Listening and Acoustic Impact
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Sound is produced, propagated, and received with effects ranging from recognition to overwhelming impact.
- Active motifs: intentional listening `ش ي ء:B005/m01`; deafening cry `ص خ خ:B001/m01`; reverberating echo `ص د ي:B004/m01`; raised voice `ر ف ع:B010/m01`; loud speech or noise `ب ر ر:B004/m01`; sensory detection by sound `ء ن س:B002/m01`.

- Synthesis: Listening occupies the receptive end of a scene that includes loud utterance, echo, and the cry whose intensity defeats ordinary hearing.

#### Subchannel C. Recollection, Reminder, and Forgetting
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: A remembered matter is called back, voiced as a reminder, or lost through forgetting and neglect.
- Active motifs: inward recollection `ذ ك ر:B003/m01`; spoken mention `ذ ك ر:B004/m01`; reminder `ذ ك ر:B009/m01`; forgetting `ء م ه:B001/m01`; deliberate neglect `ش ء ن:B006/m01`.

- Synthesis: Memory becomes a communicative process: an absent matter is recovered, mentioned, and made useful as reminder, while forgetting and disregard interrupt the sequence.

#### Subchannel D. Distraction and Cognitive Dimming
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Attention turns away until awareness is dulled, obscured, or suspended.
- Active motifs: diversion from a concern `ل ه و:B001/m01`; heedless preoccupation `ل ه ي:B001/m01`; a deadened heart `م و ت:B006/m01`; a water-like dull heart `م و ه:B008/m01`; involuntary derangement `ء ر ض:B012/m01`; fainting or madness `م و ت:B009/m01`.

#### Subchannel E. Watchfulness and Deliberation
- Reading type: latent/lexical

- Active motifs: vigilant custodian `ن ظ ر:B009/m01`; mutual deliberation `ن ظ ر:B010/m01`; counsel over an affair `ء م ر:B007/m01`; calculation and planning `ق د ر:B005/m01`; estimation before action `خ ل ق:B001/m01`; selective choosing `ن خ ل:B003/m01`.

### 2. Intention, Motion, and Encounter
- Semantic invariant: A participant selects a goal, follows a course, and changes relation to others through approach, arrival, pursuit, or withdrawal.
- Surface relation: direct; 80:1-10 stages turning away, coming, striving, and attentive approach, while 80:20 gives the facilitated path and 80:34 the act of flight.
- Surprising reach: Paths become offspring-like branches, repeated arrival becomes dominance, and bodily facing becomes social alignment.

### 3. Measure, Capacity, Order, and Control

- Surface relation: direct; 80:19 presents determination and proportion, 80:20 facilitation, 80:23 an unfulfilled command, and 80:37 an all-absorbing affair.
- Surprising reach: Abstract control is repeatedly embodied in the hand, fitted equipment, bonds, and orderly succession.
