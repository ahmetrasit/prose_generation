# S011 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. P1 - Measured Composition and Bounded Disclosure
- Semantic invariant: Authoritative meaning is made reliable by joining, tightening, segmenting, and enclosing its parts.
- Surface relation: direct; 11:1 presents a book whose ayahs are made firm and then detailed, 11:13 asks for ten comparable surahs, and 11:17 places the present proof beside an earlier book.
- Surprising reach: The lexical field materializes text first as joined hide and tight weave, then as a raised enclosure with internal partitions.

- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Separate pieces are joined into a written body, tightened into a sound fabric, and articulated by meaningful divisions.
- Active motifs: joined or stitched parts `quranic:root_001283:B001/m01`; written record `quranic:root_001283:B002/m01`; tightened workmanship `quranic:root_000348:B004/m01`; firm weave or speech `quranic:root_000347:B010/m01`; boundary-making separation `quranic:root_001159:B001/m01`; verse and discourse divisions `quranic:root_001159:B011/m01`; detailed components `quranic:root_001159:B013/m01`; disclosure by speech, writing, or sign `quranic:root_000170:B005/m01`

Its lexical construction resembles a joined skin or assembled company whose seams are tightened, whose weave is sound, and whose divisions make each unit legible. Detailing therefore completes rather than loosens the initial act of making firm.
