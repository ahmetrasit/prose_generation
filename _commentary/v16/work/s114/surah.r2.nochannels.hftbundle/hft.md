# HFT: earlier activation hypotheses, per focus ayah of surah 114

Note: HFT used an older root map; a trace step on a root the gateway now withholds is an echo, not identity.

# Focus 114:1

## enacted refuge
- reading: A commanded speech-act that begins protection by attaching the speaker to the one who forms and repairs human life.
- mechanism: Utterance is the first movement into shelter: the commanded voice externalizes dependence and fastens the speaker to lordship that repairs and completes visible human life.
- trace:
  - 114:1 **قُلْ** ق و ل B001: Articulated speech supplies the overt act, which performs the turn toward protection rather than merely reporting it.
  - 114:1 **أَعُوذُ** ع و ذ B001: Turning for refuge supplies the protective orientation enacted by the utterance.
  - 114:1 **أَعُوذُ** ع و ذ B004: Clinging close beside a shelter makes refuge an attachment, not only a request made from a distance.
  - 114:1 **بِرَبِّ** ر ب ب B002: Repair, nurture, and completion identify the sheltering power as formative care.
  - 114:1 **ٱلنَّاسِ** ن و س B001: Visible human presence defines the collective sphere whose Lord is invoked.

## caregiver attachment
- reading: Refuge is the vulnerable person's sustained attachment to a caregiver who raises, repairs, and keeps the human dependent.
- mechanism: Birth-near staying, foster-care, nurture, and duration refigure refuge as vulnerable dependency entering a caregiver's continuing custody rather than simply fleeing a danger.
- trace:
  - 114:1 **أَعُوذُ** ع و ذ B003: A newly delivered female staying with her young supplies a birth-near pattern of vulnerable protective closeness.
  - 114:1 **بِرَبِّ** ر ب ب B005: The fostered-child and caregiver relation turns lordship into relational custody.
  - 114:1 **بِرَبِّ** ر ب ب B002: Stage-by-stage nurture makes that custody developmental rather than merely defensive.
  - 114:1 **بِرَبِّ** ر ب ب B007: Staying and lasting give the protective attachment temporal continuity.
  - 114:1 **ٱلنَّاسِ** ن و س B006: The self or intimate companion image keeps the protected human personally near rather than anonymous.

## inner outer selection
- reading: The speaker is trained to choose an inward protective orientation, draw it to the self, and make it an explicit voiced commitment.
- mechanism: The speech root can hold both unspoken inner saying and overt utterance. The imperative selects a protective saying from the interior field, draws it to the self, and gives it public form as a deliberate orientation.
- trace:
  - 114:1 **قُلْ** ق و ل B012: Unspoken inner saying supplies the interior side of the threshold crossed by the command.
  - 114:1 **قُلْ** ق و ل B006: Drawing a saying to oneself supplies the selective appropriation of one inward voice.
  - 114:1 **قُلْ** ق و ل B001: Articulated speech carries the selected inward orientation across into audible commitment.
  - 114:1 **أَعُوذُ** ع و ذ B008: Avoidance out of aversion gives the selected saying a directional rejection as well as an attachment.

## human facing nearness
- reading: "Lord of people" also evokes governance of the perceptible, familiar, human-facing world in which estrangement must be repaired.
- mechanism: The human collective is not only a species label. Its branch field makes it a perceptible, familiar, near-facing world; refuge can therefore restore orientation toward a human-facing nearness governed by care when fear produces estrangement.
- trace:
  - 114:1 **ٱلنَّاسِ** ن و س B002: Perceiving by sight, sense, or hearing makes the human sphere a field of awareness.
  - 114:1 **ٱلنَّاسِ** ن و س B003: Familiar comfort that removes estrangement supplies the affective pole toward which refuge turns.
  - 114:1 **ٱلنَّاسِ** ن و س B004: The near or person-facing side gives the human sphere a spatial orientation of approachability.
  - 114:1 **بِرَبِّ** ر ب ب B002: Nurturing administration assigns the Lord the work of restoring that perceptible and familiar orientation.

## royal stabilization
- reading: The speaker yields unstable self-management to a caregiver whose rule can hold the human domain together and actually dispose its affairs.
- mechanism: The next title loads nurturing lordship with the power to hold together, possess, dispose, and rule. Repetition of the same people makes refuge a transfer from unstable self-management into competent, cohesive custody.
- trace:
  - 114:1 **بِرَبِّ** ر ب ب B002: Nurture and completion supply the caring work that the following royal powers can secure.
  - 114:2 **مَلِكِ** م ل ك B001: Strength and cohesion supply the stabilizing capacity of the kingly title.
  - 114:2 **مَلِكِ** م ل ك B002: Possession with power of disposition makes custody operational rather than honorific.
  - 114:2 **مَلِكِ** م ل ك B005: The mainstay of an affair supplies the structural support on which refuge can rest.
  - 114:2 **ٱلنَّاسِ** ن و س B001: Repeated visible human presence keeps governance and nurture directed to the same collective.

## refuge as realliance
- reading: The phrase voices an exclusive relocation of care, rule, and worship before it ever specifies the danger.
- mechanism: Adding the worshipped one after Lord and King converts the protective utterance into re-allegiance. The speaker does not merely seek an effective shelter but voices whom care, rule, and worship ultimately belong to.
- trace:
  - 114:1 **قُلْ** ق و ل B001: Overt articulation makes allegiance confessed rather than privately assumed.
  - 114:1 **أَعُوذُ** ع و ذ B001: Protective turning supplies the act whose ultimate addressee is being fixed.
  - 114:1 **بِرَبِّ** ر ب ب B001: Lordship, ownership, and authority supply the first layer of the allegiance relation.
  - 114:3 **إِلَٰهِ** ء ل ه B001: Worship and the worshipped one turn protective dependence into ultimate devotional allegiance.
  - 114:3 **ٱلنَّاسِ** ن و س B001: The repeated human collective makes that allegiance public in scope, even though voiced in the first person.

## countervoice at threshold
- reading: The opening deliberately places an overt protective countervoice at the same threshold where hidden, recurrent inner speech tries to form.
- mechanism: The later hidden self-talk and faint voice reveal why the focus begins with overt speech. The voiced refuge becomes a countervoice at the inner-outer threshold, interrupting an adverse signal that hides, returns, and can propagate in spark-like increments.
- trace:
  - 114:1 **قُلْ** ق و ل B001: Articulated speech supplies the audible counter-action.
  - 114:1 **قُلْ** ق و ل B012: Unspoken inner saying supplies the contested interior medium shared with the whisper.
  - 114:4 **شَرِّ** ش ر ر B003: Flying fire-sparks supply an exploratory image of small adverse impulses propagating from a source.
  - 114:4 **ٱلْوَسْوَاسِ** و س و س B001: Hidden self-talk identifies the interior speech-like process that the overt saying counters.
  - 114:4 **ٱلْوَسْوَاسِ** و س و س B002: A faint voice supplies the low-salience acoustic contrast with commanded articulation.
  - 114:4 **ٱلْخَنَّاسِ** خ ن س B001: Withdrawal into hiding makes the adverse signal intermittent rather than continuously visible.
  - 114:4 **ٱلْخَنَّاسِ** خ ن س B002: Stars that disappear and return supply the recurrence pattern of the hidden signal.

## recurrent reattachment
- reading: "I seek refuge" is a renewable attachment repeatedly enacted as a mobile hidden pressure recedes and resumes.
- mechanism: Because the whisper is repeated and its agent withdraws and returns, refuge is not a one-time relocation. The focus verb becomes a repeatable reattachment to an enduring shelter whenever the hidden pressure changes position.
- trace:
  - 114:1 **أَعُوذُ** ع و ذ B004: Clinging close supplies the renewable act of reattachment.
  - 114:1 **بِرَبِّ** ر ب ب B007: Staying and lasting supply the stable pole to which the speaker repeatedly returns.
  - 114:5 **يُوَسْوِسُ** و س و س B001: The finite recurrence of hidden self-talk supplies repeated pressure rather than a single event.
  - 114:4 **ٱلْخَنَّاسِ** خ ن س B002: Disappearing and returning supplies the cycle that makes renewed attachment necessary.
  - 114:4 **ٱلْخَنَّاسِ** خ ن س B005: A running horse's lateral deviation supplies a mobile threat pattern that cannot be met by static distance alone.

## upstream reformation
- reading: The Lord's refuge reaches the upstream source of conduct, repairing suggestion before it can issue as action.
- mechanism: Locating hidden self-talk in the origin from which actions issue moves protection upstream. Nurturing lordship is asked not only to block an external attacker but to repair the interior source before suggestion becomes action.
- trace:
  - 114:1 **بِرَبِّ** ر ب ب B002: Repair, administration, and stage-by-stage completion supply the work of reforming an interior process.
  - 114:1 **أَعُوذُ** ع و ذ B004: Clinging near the protector positions refuge at the point where formation is still occurring.
  - 114:5 **يُوَسْوِسُ** و س و س B001: Hidden self-talk supplies the pre-action disturbance to be repaired.
  - 114:5 **صُدُورِ** ص د ر B004: The origin from which actions issue locates intervention causally upstream of conduct.
  - 114:5 **صُدُورِ** ص د ر B002: The foremost or first position reinforces priority of intervention at the beginning of the action chain.

## visible hidden social field
- reading: The Lord of visible, familiar humanity protects within that same social field from influence carried by concealed agents or by people themselves.
- mechanism: The final contrast between concealed beings and visible people destabilizes a simple outside-enemy model. The people are both the collective under lordship and one possible source class, so refuge operates within familiar society against influence that may arrive through either hidden or human carriers.
- trace:
  - 114:1 **ٱلنَّاسِ** ن و س B001: Visible human presence defines the focus collective and one side of the later visible-hidden contrast.
  - 114:1 **ٱلنَّاسِ** ن و س B003: Familiar comfort supplies the social nearness that the final source list makes morally non-guaranteeing.
  - 114:6 **ٱلْجِنَّةِ** ج ن ن B001: Covering and concealment supply the hidden pole of the source field.
  - 114:6 **ٱلْجِنَّةِ** ج ن ن B005: Concealed beings instantiate that hidden pole without exhausting the broader concealment mechanism.
  - 114:6 **وَٱلنَّاسِ** ن و س B001: Visible humans appear again as a possible source class, folding risk back into the protected collective.

## trembling lift

# Focus 114:2

## public dominion
- reading: The one whose sovereignty actively orders the visible human collective.
- mechanism: The kingship branch supplies dominion and public governance, while the people branch supplies a visible human collective. The relation is therefore active jurisdiction over a human polity, not merely an honorific attached to an unspecified king.
- trace:
  - 114:2 **مَلِكِ** م ل ك B003: Kingship, dominion, and governance supply the governing relation expressed by مَلِكِ.
  - 114:2 **ٱلنَّاسِ** ن و س B001: Visible human presence supplies the governed collective denoted by ٱلنَّاسِ.

## dispositional jurisdiction
- reading: The holder of legitimate disposal over each human self's field of action.
- mechanism: Ownership is read functionally as the right to dispose, authorize, and delegate. Joined to the self-bearing branch of people, the phrase reaches human agency and not only public territory.
- trace:
  - 114:2 **مَلِكِ** م ل ك B002: Ownership and disposal supply a jurisdiction capable of authorizing action.
  - 114:2 **ٱلنَّاسِ** ن و س B006: The idiom of one's own self carries the governed object inward from population to person.

## cohesive mainstay
- reading: Kingship as the cohesive mainstay by which human order remains standing.
- mechanism: Rule can be imagined as load-bearing coherence: the king is not only above the people but is the support by which their collective and personal order holds together.
- trace:

## leading center
- reading: The orienting center and leading course toward which the human-facing side is ordered.

  - 114:1 **بِرَبِّ** ر ب ب B002: Repair, nurture, and completion make the protective rule restorative rather than merely coercive.

# Focus 114:3

## public worship
- reading: The worshipped center toward which the visible human collective directs devotion.
- mechanism: The head names a worship relation, while the dependent noun profiles manifest human presence. The construct therefore presents a center of enacted human devotion, not merely a divine label associated with a species.
- trace:
  - 114:3 **إِلَٰهِ** ء ل ه B001: Worship and the worshipped one make the head noun a relational center of devotion.
  - 114:3 **ٱلنَّاسِ** ن و س B001: Visible human presence supplies the manifest collective whose devotion is in view.

## perceptive humanity
- reading: The deity is related to humans precisely at the level of their perceiving and discerning awareness.
- mechanism: The worship relation is attached to awareness-bearing subjects. Humanity is not only a population here; it is the class whose perception can recognize, miss, or misread what solicits its allegiance.
- trace:
  - 114:3 **إِلَٰهِ** ء ل ه B001: The worship branch supplies the orientation that human awareness may recognize or misdirect.
  - 114:3 **ٱلنَّاسِ** ن و س B002: Perception by seeing, hearing, or sensing profiles the people as awareness-bearing subjects.

## familiar nearness
- reading: It can also name the worshipped relation on humanity's near side, where familiarity displaces estrangement.
- mechanism: Those branch images make the construct capable of an affective-spatial reading: worship is encountered on the human-facing side as a relation of nearness that counters estrangement.
