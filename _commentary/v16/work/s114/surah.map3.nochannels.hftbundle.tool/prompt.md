# Focus 114:1

## enacted refuge
- reading: A commanded speech-act that begins protection by attaching the speaker to the one who forms and repairs human life.
- mechanism: Utterance is the first movement into shelter: the commanded voice externalizes dependence and fastens the speaker to lordship that repairs and completes visible human life.
- trace:
  - 114:1 **قُلْ** ق و ل B001: Articulated speech supplies the overt act, which performs the turn toward protection rather than merely reporting it.
  - 114:1 **أَعُوذُ** ع و ذ B001: Turning for refuge supplies the protective orientation enacted by the utterance.
  - 114:1 **أَعُوذُ** ع و ذ B004: Clinging close beside a shelter makes refuge an attachment, not only a request made from a distance.
  - 114:1 **بِرَبِّ** ر ب ب B002: Repair, nurture, and completion identify the sheltering power as formative care.
  - 114:1 **ٱلنَّاسِ** ن و س B001: Visible human presence defines the collective sphere whose Lord is invoked.

## caregiver attachment
- reading: Refuge is the vulnerable person's sustained attachment to a caregiver who raises, repairs, and keeps the human dependent.
- mechanism: Birth-near staying, foster-care, nurture, and duration refigure refuge as vulnerable dependency entering a caregiver's continuing custody rather than simply fleeing a danger.
- trace:
  - 114:1 **أَعُوذُ** ع و ذ B003: A newly delivered female staying with her young supplies a birth-near pattern of vulnerable protective closeness.
  - 114:1 **بِرَبِّ** ر ب ب B005: The fostered-child and caregiver relation turns lordship into relational custody.
  - 114:1 **بِرَبِّ** ر ب ب B002: Stage-by-stage nurture makes that custody developmental rather than merely defensive.
  - 114:1 **بِرَبِّ** ر ب ب B007: Staying and lasting give the protective attachment temporal continuity.
  - 114:1 **ٱلنَّاسِ** ن و س B006: The self or intimate companion image keeps the protected human personally near rather than anonymous.
