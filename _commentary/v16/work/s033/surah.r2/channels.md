# s033 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. Hidden Pressure, Received Direction, and Entrusted Agency
- Semantic invariant: Concealed opposition is answered by a direction received from beyond the visible field and then handed over to a sufficient agent.
- Surface relation: indirect; 33:1 anchors concealment in الكافرين and المنافقين, 33:2 anchors reception and following in يوحى and اتبع, and 33:3 anchors delegation in توكل and وكيلا.
- Surprising reach: The opening imperatives form one transmission circuit: hidden pressure, disclosed guidance, traced response, and entrusted completion.

#### Subchannel A. Covered Opposition
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Opposition works by covering what should be manifest and by moving through a concealed passage whose public entrance and actual exit differ.
- Active motifs: covering over `quranic:root_001307:B001/m01`; blocking truth `quranic:root_001307:B003/m01`; tunneled passage `quranic:root_001537:B003/m01`; visible entrance with hidden exit `quranic:root_001537:B004/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 33:1 (ك ف ر: الكافرين; ن ف ق: المنافقين)
- Synthesis: The two prohibited constituencies are not merely named opponents; their lexical images pressure the command as resistance whose operative route is concealment.

#### Subchannel B. Direction Received and Traced

- Active motifs: hidden delivery of knowledge `quranic:root_001633:B001/m01`; divine revelation `quranic:root_001633:B004/m01`; following behind `quranic:root_000175:B001/m01`; tracing step by step `quranic:root_000175:B003/m01`; inwardly grounded knowledge `quranic:root_000387:B001/m01`

- Synthesis: Revelation is materialized as a concealed transfer that leaves a traversable trace, while divine awareness secures the path beyond what its follower can see.

#### Subchannel C. Entrusted Sufficiency
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: The matter is deposited with an agent whose holding and sufficiency release the human actor from controlling the result.
- Active motifs: entrusting a matter `quranic:root_001681:B001/m01`; relying upon another `quranic:root_001681:B002/m01`; an agent who guards and suffices `quranic:root_001681:B006/m01`; sufficiency that closes need `quranic:root_001310:B001/m01`
