# s020 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. P1 (20:1-16) - Disclosure That Gathers and Restores
- Semantic invariant: A hidden communication is placed, gathered, and made present so that it can restore attention rather than impose disabling hardship.
- Surface relation: direct; 20:2-4 (أَنزَلْنَا، ٱلْقُرْءَانَ، تَذْكِرَةً، يَخْشَىٰ، تَنزِيلًا) and 20:7 (تَجْهَرْ، بِٱلْقَوْلِ، يَعْلَمُ، ٱلسِّرَّ، أَخْفَى).
- Surprising reach: Recitation is also lexical gathering, remembrance is renewed presence after absence, and divine disclosure spans audible speech, unspoken interior saying, concealment, and the removal of concealment.

#### Subchannel A. Ordered descent into gathered recitation
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A communication descends into its fitting place, gathers its words into recitation, and re-presents what may be absent from attention.
- Active motifs: delivered descent (quranic:root_001492:B002/m01); placement in a proper rank (quranic:root_001492:B004/m01); gathering and joining (quranic:root_001210:B001/m01); reading and recitation (quranic:root_001210:B002/m01); inward recollection after forgetting (quranic:root_000516:B003/m01); reminder that makes present again (quranic:root_000516:B009/m01); hardship and strenuous suffering (quranic:root_000808:B002/m01); fear with awe (quranic:root_000413:B001/m01)
- Ayah anchors: 20:2 (أَنزَلْنَا، ٱلْقُرْءَانَ، لِتَشْقَىٰ); 20:3 (تَذْكِرَةً، يَخْشَىٰ); 20:4 (تَنزِيلًا)
- Synthesis: The revelation is not an undifferentiated burden: descent places it, recitation gathers it, and reminder restores it to active presence. The hardship denied in 20:2 is therefore pressured by a constructive alternative, in which awe receives an ordered and recollective communication.

#### Subchannel B. From voiced declaration to the concealed interior
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Speech moves across a visibility gradient from public voicing to an inward saying, a secret kept inside, and knowledge that discloses what was hidden.
- Active motifs: public declaration and raised voice (quranic:root_000269:B001/m01); articulated utterance (quranic:root_001272:B001/m01); saying held in the self without appearance (quranic:root_001272:B012/m01); concealment in the interior (quranic:root_000697:B001/m01); covering and hiddenness (quranic:root_000428:B001/m01); removal of hiddenness and manifestation (quranic:root_000428:B003/m01); disclosure to the knower (quranic:root_001040:B001/m01)
- Ayah anchors: 20:7 (تَجْهَرْ، بِٱلْقَوْلِ، يَعْلَمُ، ٱلسِّرَّ، أَخْفَى)
- Synthesis: The scene does not oppose sound to silence absolutely. It grades communication from voiced speech through an unvoiced interior proposition to a secret under cover, while knowledge is the operation that leaves no layer undisclosed.

### 2. P1 (20:1-16) - Vertical Architecture of the Created World
- Semantic invariant: Creation is organized as a measured vertical structure with an elevated canopy and seat above a low, fertile, moist substrate.
- Surface relation: direct; 20:4-6 (خَلَقَ، ٱلْأَرْضَ، ٱلسَّمَاوَاتِ ٱلْعُلَى، ٱلْعَرْشِ ٱسْتَوَى، تَحْتَ ٱلثَّرَى).
- Surprising reach: The throne is not only royal furniture but also raised roof and structural support, while the lowest named layer retains wetness and generative capacity rather than reading as inert depth.

#### Subchannel A. Measured upper canopy and elevated seat
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A formed world is measured, raised into a covering sky, and stabilized around an elevated royal and architectural support.
- Active motifs: measuring and proportioning a thing (quranic:root_000434:B001/m01); bringing creation into being (quranic:root_000434:B002/m01); sky as whatever rises and covers (quranic:root_000745:B004/m01); elevation and ascent (quranic:root_001042:B001/m01); the upper direction (quranic:root_001042:B005/m01); elevated royal seat (quranic:root_001000:B001/m01); raised roof or canopy (quranic:root_001000:B002/m01); elevation and stable seating (quranic:root_000766:B003/m01)

- Synthesis: Measurement becomes architecture: the sky covers from above, height establishes orientation, and the throne combines sovereignty with the image of a raised supporting canopy. The lexical material makes cosmic rule spatially load-bearing rather than merely titular.

#### Subchannel B. Low earth and living subsoil
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Beneath the upper structure lies a low terrestrial plane whose fertile surface continues downward into moist soil.
- Active motifs: the lower realm opposite the sky (quranic:root_000025:B001/m01); soft earth that brings forth growth (quranic:root_000025:B002/m01); the relation beneath a thing (quranic:root_000177:B001/m01); moist soil and wetness (quranic:root_000198:B001/m01); creation brought into being (quranic:root_000434:B002/m01)

- Synthesis: The lower pole is not simply “down.” Earth is both the low counterpart of sky and a soft, productive material, while الثرى supplies wetness beneath it; vertical possession thus reaches into a latent reservoir of terrestrial life.

### 3. P1 (20:1-16) - Fire, Threshold, and Attentive Worship
- Semantic invariant: Approach to the divine summons proceeds through a transferred signal, the stripping of ordinary protection, and an embodied posture of attentive service.
- Surface relation: direct; 20:10-14 (نَارًا، بِقَبَسٍ، هُدًى، نُودِيَ، فَٱخْلَعْ نَعْلَيْكَ، ٱلْوَادِ ٱلْمُقَدَّسِ، فَٱسْتَمِعْ، فَٱعْبُدْنِي، أَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَاةَ).
- Surprising reach: The root of prayer also names meeting fire and its heat, so the encountered blaze is lexically transposed into standing worship after the protective sole is removed.

- Synthesis: The sought object is first a detachable flame for the family, but the fire's visible signal becomes a route to guidance. The ember's lexical capacity to transmit both light and good makes the scene a transfer of orientation, not only heat.

- Synthesis: The threshold operation is materially exact: enclosure is removed from the foot before entry into a purified channel. The latent fold in طوى adds an inwardly layered site whose access requires shedding the ordinary protective interface.

- Synthesis: The same lexical field that permits “meeting heat” also carries prayer. The pericope turns an approach toward fire into standing before God, while service and recollection keep the encounter active after the visible flame has yielded to revelation.

### 4. P1 (20:1-16) - Directed Motion and Its Reckoned End

- Surface relation: direct; 20:15-16 (ٱلسَّاعَةَ ءَاتِيَةٌ، لِتُجْزَىٰ كُلُّ نَفْسٍ بِمَا تَسْعَىٰ، ٱتَّبَعَ هَوَاهُ فَتَرْدَى).

### 5. P2 (20:17-36) - The Staff as a Multi-Operation Instrument
- Semantic invariant: One held object changes role without losing identity: it supports a shepherd's body and flock, then becomes a cast, living, moving sign before being recovered.

- Surprising reach: The staff's ordinary functions already contain support, striking, and governance; the sign intensifies those latent operations into casting, animate motion, and restored habitual form.

- Scene or process: The right hand controls a staff that bears weight, knocks foliage down, and manages the needs of a flock.

- Synthesis: Release changes the object's ontological role from tool to living mover, but recovery reverses the operation. “Former sīra” is not merely shape: its lexical range includes mode and conduct, so restoration closes both form and behavior.

### S21. The Still Hunter and Approaching Prey
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A hunting party sets out, a hunter hides behind a blind and stages a snare, prey comes within range, a struck animal is inspected for death, and the expedition may end with the hunter returning without a catch.

- Active motifs: hunting party setting out (quranic:root_000745:B006/m01); hunter's blind used for concealment (quranic:root_000584:B011/m01); snare in which prey becomes entangled (quranic:root_000791:B006/m01); prey becoming possible as it comes within range (quranic:root_000421:B005/m01); inspecting struck prey to determine whether it died (quranic:root_001454:B014/m01); hunter returning without a catch (quranic:root_001641:B004/m01)

- Synthesis: Concealment and induced approach make capture possible without an overt chase, while the empty return reverses the hunter's presumed control. Watchfulness therefore concerns staged arrangements as well as visible pursuers, and apparent control remains vulnerable to failure.

### S25. Keeping and Breaking a Bounded Fast
