## boundary jurisdiction
- reading: The western limit is a jurisdictional threshold where geographic reach must become a moral use of power.
- mechanism: Repeated finding pivots from a perceived terminal landscape to a social encounter. Geographic reach becomes delegated jurisdiction: at the limit, power must decide how to meet the people found there.
- trace:
  - 18:86 **بَلَغَ** ب ل غ B001: Arrival at an endpoint opens the jurisdictional encounter.
  - 18:86 **مَغْرِبَ تَغْرُبُ** غ ر ب B006: West and sunset supply both destination and visible event.
  - 18:86 **وَجَدَهَا وَوَجَدَ** و ج د B001: Finding links the perceived sun and encountered people as one hinge.
  - 18:86 **قَوْمًا** ق و م B001: The human collective converts horizon sight into social responsibility.
  - 18:86 **تُعَذِّبَ** ع ذ ب B005: Painful punishment supplies the coercive branch of the charge.
  - 18:86 **حُسْنًا** ح س ن B002: Beneficent action supplies the constructive branch of the charge.

## situated horizon
- reading: The line reports an encountered horizon through a dark-water spring whose eye resonance keeps situated vision in view.
- mechanism: The spring and eye branches of the same noun, governed by finding, allow a layered scene: a real dark-water source is also the aperture through which the horizon event is encountered. This concerns situated perception, not denial of the spring sense.
- trace:
  - 18:86 **وَجَدَهَا وَوَجَدَ** و ج د B001: Finding supplies an observer-bound encounter.
  - 18:86 **عَيْنٍ** ع ي ن B006: A flowing source supplies the literal environmental medium.
  - 18:86 **عَيْنٍ** ع ي ن B001: The seeing eye supplies a secondary perspectival aperture.
  - 18:86 **حَمِئَةٍ** ح م ء B001: Black mud darkens and materializes the visual medium.

## instituted good
- reading: The second option can be to establish and maintain a beneficent practice within the people.
- mechanism: The constructive option uses a verb of adopting or acquiring, not merely a static adjective. Located among a people, goodness can be an instituted practice or durable arrangement, not only lenient treatment.
- trace:
  - 18:86 **قَوْمًا** ق و م B004: Care, preservation, and governance supply a communal function.

## enabled reach

## criterion bound rule
- reading: Discretion is conduct-bound: wrongdoing draws painful penalty, while reparative action draws recompense and ease; restraint survives only as a possible purpose.
- mechanism: The answer fills the two open options with criteria and matched consequences. Wrongful displacement receives painful punishment; right action receives fitting recompense and ease. A restraint nuance remains possible as purpose, but not as a replacement for penalty.
- trace:
  - 18:86 **تُعَذِّبَ** ع ذ ب B003: Prevention and restraint supply the exploratory coercive nuance being tested.

  - 18:88 **يُسْرًا** ي س ر B001: Ease after difficulty supplies constructive administration.
