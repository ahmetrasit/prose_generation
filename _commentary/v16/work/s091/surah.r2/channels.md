# S091 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. Cycles of Exposure and Occlusion
- Semantic invariant: A field becomes visible, bright, veiled, or perceptually inaccessible through recurring changes of light and covering.
- Surface relation: direct; 91:1-4 moves from `الشمس` and `ضحاها` through the moon that `تلاها`, the day that `جلاها`, and the night that `يغشاها`.
- Surprising reach: The cycle extends from celestial alternation into polishing, glare, white markings, enveloping calamity, and loss of awareness.

#### Subchannel A. Celestial Alternation
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: Sun, moon, day, and night succeed one another as illumination advances and covering returns.
- Active motifs: solar disc and light `ش م س:B001/m01`; forenoon interval `ض ح و:B001/m01`; moon and moonlight `ق م ر:B001/m01`; full-moon night `س و ي:B012/m01`; bodily or ranked following `ت ل و:B001/m01`; bright day `ن ه ر:B002/m01`; daylight disclosure `ج ل و:B007/m01`; night darkness `ل ي ل:B001/m01`; covering veil `غ ش و:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:1 `الشمس` and `ضحاها`; 91:2 `القمر` and `تلاها`; 91:3 `النهار` and `جلاها`; 91:4 `الليل` and `يغشاها`; 91:7 and 91:14 `سواها`
- Synthesis: The lights are not static objects but participants in an ordered relay: one follows, another exposes, and another covers. The lexical senses preserve the same alternation as a movement between visible brightness and enveloping darkness.

#### Subchannel B. Dawn as Aperture and Breath
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Darkness or constriction opens into dawn, widened space, and an outward breath.
- Active motifs: dawn breaking from night `ف ج ر:B002/m01`; broad fissure and water-burst `ف ج ر:B001/m01`; opening and widening `ن ه ر:B003/m01`; dawn breathing outward `ن ف س:B009/m01`; public emergence into sunlight `ض ح و:B002/m01`; disclosure after concealment `ج ل و:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:1 `ضحاها`; 91:3 `النهار` and `جلاها`; 91:7 `نفس`; 91:8 `فجورها`
- Synthesis: Dawn is rendered as an aperture: light, space, water, and breath all move outward from confinement. The scene turns temporal emergence into a physical process of splitting, widening, and exhalation.

#### Subchannel C. Polishing, Glare, and Visible Marking
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A surface is brightened until it becomes conspicuous, while excessive whiteness can overwhelm sight.
- Active motifs: polishing and visual clarification `ج ل و:B002/m01`; exposed forehead `ج ل و:B005/m01`; clear white day `ج ل و:B007/m01`; forenoon radiance `ض ح و:B005/m01`; pale or white coloration `ق م ر:B002/m01`; snow-glare confusion `ق م ر:B005/m01`; white facial covering `غ ش و:B007/m01`; white head or face marking `غ ش ي:B008/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:1 `الشمس` and `ضحاها`; 91:2 `القمر`; 91:3 `جلاها`; 91:4 `يغشاها`
- Synthesis: Polishing and daylight make forms legible, and white markings make an animal immediately visible. At the limit, the same brightness reverses into snow glare and confused vision, so disclosure and perceptual loss occupy opposite ends of one luminous process.

#### Subchannel D. Veiling and Loss of Awareness
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A covering descends over an object, a population, or consciousness itself.
- Active motifs: material or perceptual covering `غ ش و:B001/m01` and `غ ش ي:B001/m01`; all-enveloping affliction `غ ش و:B002/m01`; swoon or deathly obscuration `غ ش و:B005/m01`; loss of understanding `غ ش ي:B007/m01`; stealthy self-concealment `د س و:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:4 `يغشاها`; 91:10 `دساها`
- Synthesis: The night’s covering expands into a general mechanism of occlusion. Cloth, disaster, fainting, and deliberate hiding each place a layer between a subject and visibility, awareness, or social notice.

### 2. Formed Space and Vertical Order
- Semantic invariant: Space becomes habitable or intelligible through construction, leveling, spreading, elevation, and stable support.
- Surface relation: direct; 91:5-7 names the sky that was `بناها`, the earth that was `طحاها`, and the self that was `سواها`.
- Surprising reach: Cosmic architecture continues into mountain ridges, smooth plains, house posts, hide shelters, and the base on which property and settlement stand.

#### Subchannel A. Cosmic Architecture
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: An overhead structure and a lower expanse are assembled, spread, and brought into finished form.
- Active motifs: building by joined components `ب ن ي:B001/m01`; sky or overhead canopy `س م و:B004/m01`; lower earth opposite the sky `ء ر ض:B001/m01`; spreading and extension `ط ح و:B001/m01`; straightened completed form `س و ي:B002/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:5 `السماء` and `بناها`; 91:6 `الأرض` and `طحاها`; 91:7 and 91:14 `سواها`
- Synthesis: Building, spreading, and leveling form a single spatial operation. The sky supplies the raised canopy, the earth the extended lower field, and leveling the finishing action that gives the whole a coherent constitution.

#### Subchannel B. Level Expanse and Open Way
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Ground is flattened into a broad, fair, traversable interval.
- Active motifs: wide smooth ground `س و ي:B009/m01`; fair middle or level place `س و ي:B006/m01`; earth-spreading `ط ح و:B001/m01`; spacious interval and distance `ن ف س:B015/m01`; gap between two sides `ع ق ر:B014/m01`; thick spread carpet `ء ر ض:B005/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:6 `الأرض` and `طحاها`; 91:7 `نفس` and `سواها`; 91:14 `عقروها`
- Synthesis: Flattening does more than enlarge the ground: it establishes a middle, a gap, and enough interval for passage or settlement. The carpet image miniaturizes the same operation as a portable plane laid out beneath occupants.

#### Subchannel C. Ascent, Ridge, and Summit
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A traveler or visible figure rises from level ground through a pass toward a ridge or summit.
- Active motifs: rising and eminence `س م و:B001/m01`; high visible figure `س م و:B002/m01`; rising and settling above `س و ي:B003/m01`; elevated moon `ط ح و:B008/m01`; high rock `ط غ و:B006/m01`; smooth high slab `ط غ ي:B005/m01`; mountain summit `ن و ق:B001/m01`; climbable long ridges `ش ق و:B004/m01` and `ش ق ي:B004/m01`; steep mountain pass `ع ق ب:B012/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:2 `القمر`; 91:5 `السماء`; 91:6 `طحاها`; 91:7 and 91:14 `سواها`; 91:11 `طغواها`; 91:12 `أشقاها`; 91:13 `ناقة`; 91:15 `عقباها`
- Synthesis: Elevation is distributed across figure, motion, terrain, and endpoint. The pass and ridge make ascent a bodily route, while the summit, high slab, and raised celestial body make height a stable state of visibility and dominance.

#### Subchannel D. Structural Supports and Settlement
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Posts, ribs, bases, and enclosing structures hold a dwelling or settled property in place.
- Active motifs: ribs and house posts `ب ن ي:B009/m01`; hide shelter `ب ن ي:B004/m01`; dwelling base or interior site `ع ق ر:B013/m01`; fortified refuge `ع ق ر:B015/m01`; landed property and household goods `ع ق ر:B016/m01`; staying and dwelling `ر ب ب:B007/m01`; earthbound staying `ء ر ض:B006/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:5 `بناها`; 91:6 `الأرض`; 91:14 `عقروها` and `ربهم`
- Synthesis: Support begins with load-bearing ribs and posts, expands into a shelter, and terminates in the base, refuge, and property of settlement. Bodily and architectural frames share the same function: they keep an inhabited structure upright and located.

### 3. Release, Flow, and Cultivation
- Semantic invariant: Something confined or still is opened, sent outward, or guided through a channel into productive motion.
- Surface relation: indirect; the roots occur at 91:3 `النهار`, 91:6 `طحاها`, 91:8 `فجورها`, 91:12 `انبعث`, and 91:13 `رسول` and `سقياها`.
- Surprising reach: The same release mechanism joins dispatch, river incision, exhalation, relief from distress, and the opening of soil for cultivation.

#### Subchannel A. Rousing and Dispatch
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A resting participant is stirred, directed, and sent toward a task or destination.
- Active motifs: rousing the still `ب ع ث:B001/m01`; setting out rapidly `ب ع ث:B004/m01`; release and dispatch `ر س ل:B001/m01`; successive movement `ت ل و:B001/m01`; hired carrier or cultivator `ف ل ح:B004/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:2 `تلاها`; 91:9 `أفلح`; 91:12 `انبعث`; 91:13 `رسول`
- Synthesis: Rousing converts rest into directed motion, while dispatch gives that motion a destination and function. Following then turns a single departure into an ordered sequence of participants.

#### Subchannel B. Fissure, Channel, and Overflow
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Pressure opens a fissure, water enters a channel, and excess rises beyond its bounds.
- Active motifs: broad opening and water eruption `ف ج ر:B001/m01`; river cutting the earth `ن ه ر:B001/m01`; widened opening `ن ه ر:B003/m01`; irrigation course `س ق ي:B003/m01`; water channels at valley ends `ذ ن ب:B004/m01`; surging overflow `ط غ و:B002/m01` and `ط غ ي:B002/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:3 `النهار`; 91:8 `فجورها`; 91:11 `طغواها`; 91:13 `سقياها`; 91:14 `بذنبهم`
- Synthesis: The channel is a managed fissure: opening allows flow, a course directs it, and overflow marks the point at which directed movement escapes measure. Tail-like valley channels give the process a branching terminal geometry.

#### Subchannel C. Irrigated Ground and Growth
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Fertile ground is split, watered, covered by low growth, and brought into increase.
- Active motifs: fertile productive soil `ء ر ض:B002/m01`; ploughing and soil-splitting `ف ل ح:B003/m01`; irrigation share `س ق ي:B003/m01`; prostrate ground-cover `ط ح و:B006/m01`; vegetal increase `ز ك و:B001/m01`; persistent green plant `ر ب ب:B012/m01`; tail-shaped plant `ذ ن ب:B009/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:6 `الأرض` and `طحاها`; 91:9 `زكاها` and `أفلح`; 91:13 `سقياها`; 91:14 `بذنبهم` and `ربهم`
- Synthesis: Cultivation coordinates soil, incision, water allotment, and growth. The plants remain close to the earth or branch into tail-like forms, making agricultural increase a visible consequence of correctly guided flow.

#### Subchannel D. Breath and Relief from Constriction
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Breath, space, or assistance opens a constricted condition and restores movement.
- Active motifs: outward breath `ن ف س:B001/m01`; relief of distress `ن ف س:B002/m01`; spaciousness and respite `ن ف س:B015/m01`; easy unforced movement `ر س ل:B003/m01`; familiar ease `ر س ل:B007/m01`; widening and opening `ط ح و:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:6 `طحاها`; 91:7 `نفس`; 91:13 `رسول`
- Synthesis: Exhalation is the bodily form of release, while respite and familiarity are its social and emotional forms. Smooth movement and widening complete a shared passage from pressure into room, ease, and renewed motion.

#### Subchannel E. Rain Cloud and Overcast
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Clouds gather overhead, carry heavy precipitation, obscure the sun, and feed the waters below.
- Active motifs: cloud and rain overhead `س م و:B004/m02`; layered rain cloud `ر ب ب:B008/m01`; heavy rain-bearing cloud `س ق ي:B007/m01`; cloud mass `ن ه ر:B008/m01`; cloud obscuring the sun `ع ق ر:B018/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:3 `النهار`; 91:5 `السماء`; 91:13 `سقياها`; 91:14 `ربهم` and `عقروها`
- Synthesis: The cloud scene connects elevation to hydrology. Vapor gathers into an overhead mass, darkens the luminous field, and releases the precipitation that replenishes channels, fields, and drinking supply.

### 4. Abundance, Nourishment, and Giving
- Semantic invariant: A supply exceeds immediate need and becomes sustaining water, food, multitude, wealth, or voluntary benefaction.
- Surface relation: indirect; the relevant roots appear at 91:3 `النهار`, 91:5 `السماء`, 91:8 `فجورها`, 91:9 `زكاها`, 91:13 `سقياها`, and 91:14 `ربهم`.
- Surprising reach: Material plenty passes into abundant milk, a body built by food, large crowds, open-handed giving, and praise spreading among people.

#### Subchannel A. Water, Drink, and Milk
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Water or milk is made available, drawn, drunk, and retained as a life-sustaining supply.
- Active motifs: giving drink `س ق ي:B001/m01`; establishing a water supply `س ق ي:B002/m01`; abundant fresh water `ر ب ب:B013/m01`; water sustaining life `ن ف س:B008/m01`; draught or drinking interval `ن ف س:B006/m01`; continuous milk yield `ر س ل:B006/m01`; full water bucket `ذ ن ب:B007/m01`; ladle `ذ ن ب:B008/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:7 `نفس`; 91:13 `رسول`, `سقياها`, and `ناقة`; 91:14 `ربهم` and `بذنبهم`
- Synthesis: Provision forms a complete handling scene: a source is established, liquid is drawn with bucket and ladle, and the drinker receives sustaining water or milk. Continuity of supply, rather than a single sip, defines the channel.

#### Subchannel B. Food Building the Body
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Ingestion becomes bodily substance, growth, and renewed strength.
- Active motifs: swallowing and consuming `ل ه م:B001/m01`; food building flesh `ب ن ي:B010/m01`; growth and fattening `ز ك و:B001/m01`; thick syrup or nutritive paste `ر ب ب:B006/m01`; giving drink `س ق ي:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:5 `بناها`; 91:8 `فألهمها`; 91:9 `زكاها`; 91:13 `سقياها`; 91:14 `ربهم`
- Synthesis: The body is treated as something constructed by intake. Swallowing admits material, food and drink supply it, and growth registers the successful conversion of nourishment into flesh and strength.

#### Subchannel C. Multitude and Material Plenty
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Water, pasture, livestock, troops, or people accumulate into a conspicuous mass.
- Active motifs: vast crowd or army `ط ح و:B005/m01`; great quantity and scale `ل ه م:B004/m01`; gathered multitudes `ر ب ب:B004/m01`; plentiful water or pasture `ق م ر:B008/m01`; successive herds `ر س ل:B005/m01`; sudden arriving multitude `ف ج ر:B003/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:2 `القمر`; 91:6 `طحاها`; 91:8 `فجورها` and `فألهمها`; 91:13 `رسول`; 91:14 `ربهم`
- Synthesis: Abundance is spatial before it is evaluative: a mass spreads, gathers, or arrives in waves. Water, pasture, animals, and people share the same image of quantity becoming large enough to shape the surrounding scene.

#### Subchannel D. Generosity and Benefaction
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Internal largeness becomes willing giving, bounty, and public good repute.
- Active motifs: gushing generosity `ف ج ر:B005/m01`; generous sufficiency `ل ه م:B004/m02`; willing gift `ر س ل:B010/m02`; generous high spirit `ن ف س:B014/m02`; blessing or benefaction `ر ب ب:B016/m01`; good reputation `س م و:B008/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:5 `السماء`; 91:7 `نفس`; 91:8 `فجورها` and `فألهمها`; 91:13 `رسول`; 91:14 `ربهم`
- Synthesis: Giving is pictured as overflow from an ample interior. Bounty moves outward with the force of a spring, sustains others, and returns socially as a good name carried beyond the giver.

### 5. Inner Formation and Moral Outcome
- Semantic invariant: The inner person is formed through cognition, protection, purification, concealment, or corruption, and those operations issue in flourishing or failure.
- Surface relation: direct; 91:7-10 moves from the self that was `سواها` through `فجورها وتقواها` to the one who `زكاها` or `دساها`.
- Surprising reach: Moral interiority is rendered through concrete acts of straightening, shielding, burying, breathing, and building.

#### Subchannel A. Self, Life, and Inner Cognition
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: A living self possesses identity, awareness, intention, and an inward saying before outward action.
- Active motifs: living soul `ن ف س:B011/m01`; individual self `ن ف س:B012/m01`; cognitive discernment `ن ف س:B013/m01`; completed formation `س و ي:B002/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:7 `نفس` and `سواها`; 91:14 `فسواها`
- Synthesis: The self is simultaneously life, identity, cognition, and intention. Formation gives this interior a coherent shape from which conduct can proceed.

#### Subchannel B. Purification, Protection, and Flourishing
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: The self is cleansed, guarded from harm, brought into rectitude, and carried toward success.
- Active motifs: purification and righteousness `ز ك و:B002/m01`; self-protective piety `و ق ي:B002/m01`; straightening and sound formation `س و ي:B002/m02`; nurture toward completion `ر ب ب:B002/m01`; success and survival `ف ل ح:B005/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:7 `سواها`; 91:8 `تقواها`; 91:9 `أفلح` and `زكاها`; 91:14 `ربهم` and `فسواها`
- Synthesis: Purification is not merely removal of stain; it is a constructive regimen of guarding, straightening, and nurture. Its outcome is a self able to endure and flourish.

#### Subchannel C. Concealment, Corruption, and Failure
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: The self is buried from view, turned away from right order, and deprived of its sought outcome.
- Active motifs: stealthy burial `د س و:B001/m01`; misguidance and corruption `د س و:B003/m01`; breach of moral bounds `ف ج ر:B004/m01`; failure and deprivation `خ ي ب:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:8 `فجورها`; 91:10 `خاب` and `دساها`
- Synthesis: Burial provides the governing image: what should be formed and purified is instead pushed down and obscured. Corruption redirects it, moral breach opens a path away from right order, and failure names the resulting deprivation.

#### Subchannel D. Deceptive Impulse and Divided Interior
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: An inner impulse conceals itself, fabricates a claim, and competes with reflective intention.
- Active motifs: deceitful self `ك ذ ب:B008/m01`; concealed inward impulse `د س و:B001/m01`; inwardly drawn saying `ق و ل:B006/m01`; inward intention `ن ف س:B013/m02`; desire for the precious `ن ف س:B010/m01`; protective restraint `و ق ي:B002/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:7 `نفس`; 91:8 `تقواها`; 91:10 `دساها`; 91:11 and 91:14 forms of `كذب`; 91:13 `قال`
- Synthesis: The interior is a contested space rather than a single voice. Desire and a concealed impulse generate a private account, while intention and restraint determine whether that account remains deceptive or is brought under moral protection.

### 6. Message, Speech, and Social Circulation
- Semantic invariant: Meaning moves from inner conception through an authorized speaker into utterance, response, report, attribution, and public reputation.
- Surface relation: direct; 91:11-14 joins communal denial with the messenger who `قال` and the people who `كذبوه`.
- Surprising reach: Speech extends into private belief, negotiated judgment, musical responsion, rumor, and public reputation.

#### Subchannel A. Messenger and Utterance
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: A sender appoints a messenger who bears and voices a statement to an audience.
- Active motifs: dispatched envoy `ب ع ث:B002/m01`; messenger and carried message `ر س ل:B002/m01`; spoken utterance `ق و ل:B001/m01`; tongue as articulator `ق و ل:B002/m01`; authoritative speaker `ق و ل:B004/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:12 `انبعث`; 91:13 `فقال` and `رسول`
- Synthesis: Dispatch establishes provenance, the messenger carries the communication, and utterance makes it public. The authoritative speaker motif gives the voiced message social force beyond its bare wording.

#### Subchannel B. Inner Saying and Belief
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A saying is formed inwardly, aligned with intention, adopted as a belief, and then made available for utterance.
- Active motifs: inward saying `ق و ل:B012/m01`; inwardly drawn speech `ق و ل:B006/m01`; belief or doctrine `ق و ل:B013/m01`; inward intention `ن ف س:B013/m02`; spoken utterance `ق و ل:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:7 `نفس`; 91:13 `قال`
- Synthesis: Speech begins before sound as an inwardly held conception. Intention gives the conception direction, belief fixes it as a position, and utterance carries it from private thought into an audible statement.

#### Subchannel C. False Attribution and Denial
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A speaker fabricates words, assigns them to another, or discredits a truthful address.
- Active motifs: false report against another `ت ل و:B009/m01`; fabricated attribution `ق و ل:B005/m01`; falsehood `ك ذ ب:B001/m01`; imputing a lie `ك ذ ب:B002/m01`; deceptive embellished statement `ف ل ح:B007/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:2 `تلاها`; 91:9 `أفلح`; 91:11 `كذبت`; 91:13 `قال`; 91:14 `كذبوه`
- Synthesis: Falsehood operates through attribution as well as content. A statement can be invented, placed in another’s mouth, used to discredit a messenger, or ornamented until its persuasive surface masks its invalidity.

#### Subchannel D. Directive Speech, Rebuke, and Objection
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Speech binds an addressee, warns or rebukes, and then becomes subject to objection or rebuttal.
- Active motifs: idiomatic injunction `ك ذ ب:B003/m01`; imposed judgment `ق و ل:B010/m01`; authoritative negotiation `ق و ل:B009/m01`; warning through induced fear `خ و ف:B002/m01`; forceful rebuke `ن ه ر:B004/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:3 `النهار`; 91:11 and 91:14 forms of `كذب`; 91:13 `قال`; 91:15 `يخاف` and `عقباها`
- Synthesis: Directive discourse moves from command to enforcement: an authority states an obligation, warning gives it urgency, and rebuke confronts refusal. Negotiation then tests how the directive will operate between speakers.

#### Subchannel E. Rumor, Praise, and Reputation
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Repeated sayings circulate until they become a person’s public name or standing.
- Active motifs: circulating saying `ق و ل:B007/m01`; good repute `س م و:B008/m01`; fame that cannot be hidden `ج ل و:B006/m01`; loquacious speaker `ق و ل:B003/m01`; strength of character `ن ف س:B014/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:3 `جلاها`; 91:5 `السماء`; 91:7 `نفس`; 91:13 `قال`
- Synthesis: Speech leaves the immediate exchange and travels socially. Repetition makes a report widespread, loquacity increases its reach, and accumulated talk hardens into fame, praise, or moral reputation.

#### Subchannel F. Responsorial Voice and Performance
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: One voice calls or performs and another follows, accompanies, or answers it.
- Active motifs: answering voice after a singer `ت ل و:B007/m01`; paired performer or correspondent `ر س ل:B008/m01`; raised singing, reading, or lament `ع ق ر:B009/m01`; spoken articulation `ق و ل:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:2 `تلاها`; 91:6 `طحاها`; 91:13 `قال` and `رسول`; 91:14 `عقروها`
- Synthesis: Sequence becomes audible as responsion: a leading sound is followed by a second voice, and paired performers sustain the exchange. Raised recitation, song, and lament give the scene a public, embodied acoustic form.

### 7. Sequence, Remainder, and Consequence
- Semantic invariant: What comes after may follow, replace, remain as a trace, reverse course, or arrive as the consequence of an act.
- Surface relation: direct; 91:2 has the moon that `تلاها`, 91:14 places destruction `بذنبهم`, and 91:15 names `عقباها`.
- Surprising reach: Temporal order is embodied in heels and tails, then extended into residue, retreat, punishment, and repeated action.

#### Subchannel A. Following, Procession, and Iteration
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Participants move one after another, replace predecessors, or repeat a prior action.
- Active motifs: direct following `ت ل و:B001/m01`; successive groups `ر س ل:B005/m01`; succession and replacement `ع ق ب:B005/m01`; repeated return to an action `ع ق ب:B009/m01`; followers behind leaders `ذ ن ب:B003/m01`; rapid collective setting-out `ب ع ث:B004/m01`; immediate action without delay `ك ذ ب:B005/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:2 `تلاها`; 91:11 `كذبت`; 91:12 `انبعث`; 91:13 `رسول`; 91:14 `بذنبهم` and `كذبوه`; 91:15 `عقباها`
- Synthesis: Sequence can be linear, substitutive, or iterative. A follower occupies the wake of another, successive groups preserve the line, and repetition turns one passage into a recurring cycle.

#### Subchannel B. Heel, Tail, Trace, and Remainder
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: The rear of a moving body becomes the place where traces and remnants persist.
- Active motifs: heel and footprint `ع ق ب:B002/m01`; tail or terminal rear `ذ ن ب:B002/m02`; remaining portion `ت ل و:B003/m01`; residue and aftereffect `ع ق ب:B011/m01`; last or final part `ع ق ب:B006/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 91:2 `تلاها`; 91:8 `تقواها`; 91:14 `بذنبهم`; 91:15 `عقباها`
- Synthesis: Rear anatomy supplies a concrete model of aftermath. Heel and tail mark the path behind a body, while residue and remainder are what an event leaves in the same trailing position.

#### Subchannel C. Retreat, Desertion, and Interrupted Motion
- Reading type: latent/lexical

- Synthesis: Life is represented through moving breath and circulating blood. Injury or consuming affliction disrupts those motions, and the last gasp marks the narrow boundary at which the living self departs.

### 14. Measure, Fitness, and Selection
- Semantic invariant: Objects, persons, claims, or actions are compared against a standard and found equal, fitting, mature, or chosen.
- Surface relation: indirect; the roots occur at 91:5 `السماء`, 91:7 and 91:14 `سواها`, 91:8 `تقواها`, 91:9 `زكاها`, 91:13 `رسول` and `ناقة`, and 91:15 `عقباها`.
- Surprising reach: Abstract parity becomes concrete in an ounce, a paired game-call, a fair midpoint, meticulous handling, and selection from alternatives.

#### Subchannel A. Equality, Standard, and Fair Middle
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Two things are compared, balanced, or located against a shared standard.
- Active motifs: equality and equivalence `س و ي:B001/m01`; fair midpoint `س و ي:B006/m01`; measured ounce `و ق ي:B004/m01`; wealth calibrated to a head `س و ي:B013/m01`; diminution by taking `خ و ف:B004/m01`; small portion `ط غ و:B007/m01`; fitting equivalence `ز ك و:B004/m01`; parity in name `س م و:B005/m02`; corresponding gap `ع ق ر:B014/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:5 `السماء`; 91:7 and 91:14 `سواها`; 91:8 `تقواها`; 91:9 `زكاها`; 91:11 `طغواها`; 91:14 `عقروها`; 91:15 `يخاف`
- Synthesis: Equality can be quantitative, spatial, verbal, or nominal. Weight supplies a fixed standard, the midpoint supplies a fair position, and naming or fit supplies equivalence at the level of social and linguistic classification.

#### Subchannel B. Suitability, Deliberation, and Meticulous Handling
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: An action is slowed and refined until it suits the object, circumstance, or norm.
- Active motifs: aptitude for good `ء ر ض:B003/m01`; suitability and propriety `ز ك و:B004/m01`; deliberation and composure `ر س ل:B004/m01`; meticulous handling `ن و ق:B005/m01`; straight finished form `س و ي:B002/m01`; sustained concern `ق و ل:B015/m01`; respite and room `ن ف س:B015/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:6 `الأرض`; 91:7 and 91:14 `سواها`; 91:9 `زكاها`; 91:13 `قال`, `رسول`, and `ناقة`
- Synthesis: Fitness is produced through attention rather than speed. Deliberation creates room to assess, meticulous handling adjusts the action, and a straight finished form shows that the result suits its intended condition.

#### Subchannel C. Choice, Pairing, and Category Shift
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: An item is selected or paired, while a proverb exposes a mismatch or transformation of category.
- Active motifs: careful selection `ن و ق:B006/m01`; pair or even state `ز ك و:B005/m01`; otherness and substitution `س و ي:B007/m01`; chosen designation `س م و:B005/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 91:5 `السماء`; 91:7 and 91:14 `سواها`; 91:9 `زكاها`; 91:13 `ناقة`
- Synthesis: Selection presupposes alternatives and a criterion of fit. Pairing confirms compatibility, otherness marks the rejected alternative, and designation fixes the chosen item within a named class.
