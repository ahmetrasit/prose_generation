# HFT: earlier activation hypotheses, per focus ayah of surah 91

Note: HFT used an older root map; a trace step on a root the gateway now withholds is an echo, not identity.

# Focus 91:1

## radiant extension
- reading: An oath by a luminous source and by the temporal-spatial unfolding of its clear reach.
- mechanism: The sun supplies the radiating source, while ḍuḥā supplies brightness extended across a rising interval. The focus therefore presents illumination as an unfolding reach rather than a static object.
- trace:
  - 91:1 **وَٱلشَّمْسِ** ش م س B001: The literal sun and its radiating daylight supply the source from which the focus mechanism extends.
  - 91:1 **وَضُحَىٰهَا** ض ح و B001: The rising, extended forenoon supplies duration and turns the sun's light into an unfolding interval.
  - 91:1 **وَضُحَىٰهَا** ض ح و B005: Clear ḍuḥā brightness supplies the qualitative clarity carried through that interval.

## exposure event
- reading: The verse names a source together with its act of placing a field openly under light and heat.
- mechanism: Radiance moves outward until bodies and actions occupy an exposed, public surface. The paired terms thus describe an event of manifestation: a source brings what is outside into visibility.
- trace:
  - 91:1 **وَٱلشَّمْسِ** ش م س B001: Solar light supplies the physical agent of manifestation.
  - 91:1 **وَضُحَىٰهَا** ض ح و B002: Exposure to heat, outward prominence, and public visibility supply the manifested field produced by the sun.

## measured ripening
- reading: Ḍuḥā is a measured maturation of visibility, with delay functioning as part of how clarity forms.
- mechanism: The light reaches fullness by measured continuation. This allows the focus to carry a tempo: manifestation ripens through delay rather than arriving as an instantaneous flash.
- trace:
  - 91:1 **وَضُحَىٰهَا** ض ح و B001: The forenoon's rising extension supplies a process that takes time to reach fullness.
  - 91:1 **وَضُحَىٰهَا** ض ح و B006: Gentle delay supplies the pacing rule by which the illumination unfolds without haste.

## creaturely provision
- reading: The sun's ḍuḥā is a usable life interval in which creatures feed, graze, and receive provision.
- mechanism: The sunlight interval organizes embodied access to food rather than merely illuminating scenery. The possessive pairing can therefore be heard as the sun's creature-serving turn or usable allotment.
- trace:
  - 91:1 **وَٱلشَّمْسِ** ش م س B001: Daylight supplies the environmental condition in which the provision interval operates.
  - 91:1 **وَضُحَىٰهَا** ض ح و B003: Forenoon feeding and early grazing supply the creaturely use that makes the interval materially consequential.

## guarded restiveness
- reading: The sun is also a difficult, guarded power whose outward exposure makes its force impossible to keep private.
- mechanism: A force that resists capture or guards what lies behind it nevertheless becomes publicly manifest through its ḍuḥā. This yields a tense rather than placid solar image: withheld power declares itself by exposure.
- trace:
  - 91:1 **وَٱلشَّمْسِ** ش م س B002: Restive refusal supplies a force that will not settle or submit to being ridden.
  - 91:1 **وَٱلشَّمْسِ** ش م س B006: Guarding and withholding supply the protected interior or reserve carried by that force.
  - 91:1 **وَضُحَىٰهَا** ض ح و B002: Open prominence supplies the contrary motion by which the guarded or unruly force becomes visible.

## leading succession
- reading: The sun's ḍuḥā leads a luminous succession and leaves a phase that can be followed or carried onward.
- mechanism: The moon's own light is explicitly placed in following relation to the sun. Followership and a remainder that comes after strengthen the baseline extension model: ḍuḥā is the leading phase of a train, not an isolated span.
- trace:
  - 91:1 **وَضُحَىٰهَا** ض ح و B001: Extended forenoon supplies the leading duration from which a succession can proceed.
  - 91:2 **وَٱلْقَمَرِ** ق م ر B001: The moon and its sky-light supply the distinct luminous follower.
  - 91:2 **تَلَىٰهَا** ت ل و B001: Following and serial continuation supply the directional link from solar phase to lunar phase.
  - 91:2 **تَلَىٰهَا** ت ل و B003: A remainder following what precedes supplies an afterlight or residual phase rather than mere adjacency.

## reciprocal disclosure
- reading: Solar clarity is relational: it opens the day, while the opened day in turn reveals and sharpens the sun.
- mechanism: Day is an opening field, and its revealing or polishing action makes the sun manifest. The apparent source of visibility is thus also disclosed by a medium that it helps create, producing reciprocal rather than one-way illumination.
- trace:
  - 91:1 **وَٱلشَّمْسِ** ش م س B001: The radiating sun supplies the apparent source whose independence is being revised.
  - 91:1 **وَضُحَىٰهَا** ض ح و B005: Clear brightness supplies the quality that must itself become perceptible in a field.
  - 91:3 **وَٱلنَّهَارِ** ن ه ر B002: Day opening through light supplies the receiving field in which the sun appears.
  - 91:3 **وَٱلنَّهَارِ** ن ه ر B003: Opening and widening supply the spatial expansion that gives radiance room to become manifest.
  - 91:3 **جَلَّىٰهَا** ج ل و B001: Uncovering and appearance supply the day-to-sun direction of disclosure.
  - 91:3 **جَلَّىٰهَا** ج ل و B002: Polishing supplies a material analogy in which the medium intensifies the source's legibility.

## reversible legibility
- reading: Ḍuḥā is one phase in a reversible contest between disclosure and overlay, with a contained possibility that seeing and understanding rise and fall together.
- mechanism: The exposed field can be overlaid by darkness, while the non-dominant mapped inventory extends covering from surfaces to understanding. The focus's manifestation model therefore becomes reversible physical and cognitive legibility.
- trace:
  - 91:1 **وَضُحَىٰهَا** ض ح و B002: Open visibility supplies the legible state that can subsequently be covered.
  - 91:4 **وَٱلَّيْلِ** ل ي ل B001: Night and its darkness supply the alternating condition opposed to exposed ḍuḥā.
  - 91:4 **يَغْشَىٰهَا** غ ش و B001: A covering laid over and concealing a thing supplies the physical reversal of manifestation.
  - 91:4 **يَغْشَىٰهَا** غ ش و B007: Covering of understanding supplies the exploratory epistemic analogue of the same reversal.

## built luminous field
- reading: The sun's ḍuḥā appears as a mark borne and stabilized by a built luminous field.
- mechanism: The sky is both an overhead enclosure and, through a non-dominant mapped root, a surface carrying a visible mark; building joins parts and supports. Solar exposure can thus be read as a legible event inside a constructed field rather than light poured into emptiness.
- trace:

  - 91:5 **بَنَىٰهَا** ب ن ي B009: Ribs and supports supply the material image of a field held in a stable configuration.

- mechanism: The extension first heard temporally in ḍuḥā is repeated materially as earth spread outward. Fertile ground gives that spread a life-bearing use, so illumination can be read as laying out a habitable surface for action.

# Focus 91:2

## ordered celestial succession
- reading: An oath by the moon specifically in the condition of taking an ordered place after a feminine antecedent.
- mechanism: The moon and its light are placed in bodily, ranked, or temporal sequence after an antecedent that remains unresolved within the focus ayah alone.
- trace:
  - 91:2 **وَٱلْقَمَرِ** ق م ر B001: The celestial body together with its illumination supplies the visible entity whose position is tracked.
  - 91:2 **تَلَىٰهَا** ت ل و B001: Physical, ranked, or sequential following supplies the ordered relation to the unresolved feminine referent.

## dependent afterlight
- reading: The moon can be heard as an afterlight or remainder that carries forward what preceded it, making dependence coexist with sequence.
- mechanism: What follows can be the residue of what came before, so the moon is not merely a second coequal object but a dependent afterlight carrying a prior presence forward.
- trace:
  - 91:2 **وَٱلْقَمَرِ** ق م ر B001: The moon's visible illumination provides the material that can persist as an afterlight.
  - 91:2 **تَلَىٰهَا** ت ل و B003: A remainder coming after an earlier portion turns succession into residual continuation rather than mere adjacency.

## antiphonal response
- reading: The moon answers a prior presence like a second voice, preserving responsive relay alongside physical succession.
- mechanism: Following can be antiphonal: a second voice answers a first. Crossed into the visual domain, the lunar presence becomes a response to an unresolved prior signal rather than a silent trailer.
- trace:
  - 91:2 **وَٱلْقَمَرِ** ق م ر B001: The sky-visible moon and its light function as the second signal in the proposed visual relay.
  - 91:2 **تَلَىٰهَا** ت ل و B007: A voice answering a preceding voice supplies the responsive, antiphonal structure assigned to lunar following.
