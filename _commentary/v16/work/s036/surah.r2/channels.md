# s036 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. P1 - Directed Revelation
- Semantic invariant: A source releases an ordered signal that reaches a route, then becomes effective only when a hearer tracks and retains it.
- Surface relation: direct; 36:2-6 anchors the recited revelation, wisdom, messengership, straight path, descent, and warning, while 36:10-11 contrasts refused warning with following the reminder.
- Surprising reach: Recitation is not only speech but an act of gathering, while wisdom supplies both exact judgment and the restraining function of guidance.

#### Subchannel A. Dispatch onto a straight route
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A gathered recitation is made exact, carried by an envoy, delivered downward, and placed on an upright route as an alarm.
- Active motifs: recitation (quranic:root_001210:B002/m01); accurate wisdom (quranic:root_000348:B003/m01); firm construction (quranic:root_000348:B004/m01); released dispatch (quranic:root_000563:B001/m01); carried message (quranic:root_000563:B002/m01); delivered descent (quranic:root_001492:B002/m01); danger-awakening warning (quranic:root_001488:B001/m01); straight road (quranic:root_000858:B001/m01); upright alignment (quranic:root_001273:B008/m01)
- Ayah anchors: 36:2 `القرآن`, `الحكيم`; 36:3 `المرسلين`; 36:4 `صراط`, `مستقيم`; 36:5 `تنزيل`; 36:6 `لتنذر`, `أنذر`; 36:10-11 `أنذرتهم`, `تنذر`
- Synthesis: The signal begins as ordered recitation, is secured by discriminating and well-fastened wisdom, and is borne by a messenger rather than left as free-floating information. Descent supplies delivery, warning gives the delivery its activating function, and the straight, upright road supplies its intended trajectory.

#### Subchannel B. Tracking a reminder beyond sight
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A recipient follows a prior trace, restores it to memory, and responds with awe even though the object remains visually absent.
- Active motifs: following in another's wake (quranic:root_000175:B001/m01); trace-by-trace pursuit (quranic:root_000175:B003/m01); recalled memory (quranic:root_000516:B003/m01); reminder that restores presence (quranic:root_000516:B009/m01); awe-filled fear (quranic:root_000413:B001/m01); concealment from eyes (quranic:root_001117:B001/m01); news that opens the skin with joy (quranic:root_000120:B005/m01)
- Ayah anchors: 36:11 `اتبع`, `الذكر`, `خشي`, `بالغيب`, `فبشره`
- Synthesis: Reception is modeled as disciplined tracking rather than passive hearing. The reminder makes an absent referent present to memory; awe operates without visual possession, and the final message registers somatically as a brightened, opened surface.

### 2. P1 - Constrained Passage
- Semantic invariant: Orientation is disabled by controlling the body's angle or sealing the spatial field through which it could advance and see.
- Surface relation: direct; 36:8 fixes necks and chins, while 36:9 blocks front and rear and covers sight.
- Surprising reach: The same lexical field that gives wisdom the function of a rein also exposes how guidance can be inverted into a yoke that prevents motion.

#### Subchannel A. Rein, neck, yoke, and raised chin
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A head-control apparatus closes around the neck, braces the chin, and fixes the head in a non-drinking, non-navigating posture.
- Active motifs: restraining rein around the jaws (quranic:root_000348:B006/m01); neck and collar-bearing member (quranic:root_001053:B001/m01); iron or leather yoke (quranic:root_001102:B006/m01); chin as jaw-junction (quranic:root_000515:B001/m01); support forced under the chin (quranic:root_000515:B006/m01); head raised away from water (quranic:root_001254:B001/m01)
- Ayah anchors: 36:2 `الحكيم`; 36:8 `أعناقهم`, `أغلالا`, `الأذقان`, `مقمحون`
- Synthesis: The scene resolves into a complete control mechanism: a rein governs the jaws, a yoke grips the neck, a brace occupies the chin, and the resulting head-angle prevents ordinary lowering toward water. The surface image of disbelief is thereby materialized as forced posture and failed access.

#### Subchannel B. Blocked horizon

- Active motifs: road-blocking partition (quranic:root_000687:B001/m01); obstructed threshold (quranic:root_000687:B004/m01); rearward position (quranic:root_000433:B002/m01); what lies ahead (quranic:root_001693:B008/m01); overlying visual cover (quranic:root_001088:B001/m01); ocular sight (quranic:root_000121:B001/m01)

- Scene or process: A smooth shaft receives its point, waits in a quiver joined to the bow, is taken by hand, set against the bow limb, drawn to the limit, and sent to its intended endpoint.
- Active motifs: smoothed arrow shaft (quranic:root_000434:B008/m01); arrowhead fitted into its shaft (quranic:root_000589:B004/m01); quiver joined to a bow (quranic:root_001221:B007/m01); hand reaching into the quiver for an arrow (quranic:root_000544:B010/m01); bow limb (quranic:root_000546:B009/m01); bow drawn to its fullest extent (quranic:root_001080:B004/m01); arrow or message reaching its target or addressee (quranic:root_001560:B002/m01)

The assembled bow scene therefore usefully pressures the Surah's sent-message reading: a warning reaches an addressee by intelligible speech, while the surrounding threats imagine directed force reaching a body.

### S22. Moonlit hunting and verifying the kill
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Hunters go out under moonlight when prey vision is compromised, set snares or use a feeding bow or trained raptor, strike successive animals, and inspect the fallen quarry to determine whether it has died.
- Active motifs: hunters setting out in pursuit (quranic:root_000745:B006/m01); moonlit hunting against visually impaired prey (quranic:root_001255:B003/m01); snares and traps set for game (quranic:root_000880:B005/m01); hunting tool or raptor that feeds its owner (quranic:root_000934:B006/m01); successive prey taken in one discharge (quranic:root_000993:B008/m01); inspection to establish whether prey is dead (quranic:root_001454:B014/m01)

- Synthesis: The hunt ends not with self-evident death but with a human examination of an uncertain body. That uncertainty usefully pressures 36:79: the hunter must infer whether life has ceased, whereas the one who first created the body knows its state and can restore it.

### S23. A wound that suppurates, heals, and relapses
- Reading type: latent/lexical

- Synthesis: The bodily sequence supports the Surah's repair imagery while marking the limits of ordinary repair. Human tissue can reorganize and look sound, but the improvement remains reversible; renewed creation in 36:78-81 is presented as decisive rather than a precarious remission.

## Cross-Pericope Parent Channels

### 20. Signal efficacy across state change
- Semantic invariant: A directed signal joins source, transmission, recipient, and outcome, while its efficacy is measured by the state change produced in the addressed field.
- Surface relation: direct; 36:2-11 and 36:13-29 establish transmitted warning, 36:48-53 moves through seizing cry and gathering blast, 36:69-70 restores the warning function, and 36:82 makes uttered command coincide with realized being.
- Pericopes and bridge: P1, P2, P3, P4, and P5; causal role progression with reversal. Warning first seeks voluntary hearing, the single cry then terminates motion and unfinished transfer, the gathering signal reverses stillness into presence, and sovereign address finally produces its object.
- Canonical scene placements: P1 1A, "Dispatch onto a straight route", and 1B, "Tracking a reminder beyond sight"; P2 4A, "A relay completed by reinforcement", and S3, "One cry extinguishes a deployment"; P3 S4, "A cry seizes an unfinished social transfer"; P4 11B, "One signal gathers all"; P5 16A, "Marked remembrance rather than poetic fabrication", 16B, "Warning the living, fixing the verdict", and 19B, "Performative command".
- Surprising reach: The recipient progresses from responsible hearer to seized population, reassembled collective, and commanded object; acoustic or verbal form stays recognizable while causal force changes scale.
- Synthesis: The same source-signal-recipient-outcome architecture governs all five pericopes. Human response matters at the warning stage, but later signals progressively remove delay and negotiation: one arrests, one gathers, and one actualizes.

### 21. Route agency and compelled return
- Semantic invariant: A route scene couples orientation, a readable course, capacity for motion, impediment, and destination; its decisive variable is who controls passage.
- Surface relation: direct; 36:4-9 sets straightness against bodily and visual obstruction, 36:20-21 gives purposeful pedestrian traversal, 36:38-40 assigns measured celestial courses, 36:60-67 contrasts covenantal direction with diversion and erased passage, and 36:83 fixes final return.
- Pericopes and bridge: P1, P2, P3, P4, and P5; repeated scene signature with role progression and reversal. Passage moves from imposed blockage, through voluntary pursuit and regulated noncollision, to moral diversion or erased agency, before ending as compulsory return.
- Canonical scene placements: P1 2A, "Rein, neck, yoke, and raised chin", and 2B, "Blocked horizon"; P2 S2, "Courier from the periphery"; P3 8B, "Solar route and station", and 8C, "Lunar stages and non-collision"; P4 14A, "Preserved instruction on a leveled road", 14B, "Hostile tether and mass diversion", 15A, "Eye and track erased", and 15B, "Recast in place"; P5 19C, "Total custody and return".
- Surprising reach: Straightness is not merely a quality of a path. It is a relation among bodily posture, sight, motive, traffic rules, allegiance, and the authority that fixes an endpoint.
- Synthesis: Every placement preserves course, mover, constraint, and destination, but redistributes control. The courier chooses and the celestial bodies remain regulated; the tethered or erased body loses navigational power; final return closes the progression by making destination certain even when creaturely route-choice has ended.

### 22. Reconstitution from inactivity and dispersal
- Semantic invariant: A still, absent, dispersed, or fragmented substrate is acted upon until organized agency becomes present again.
- Surface relation: direct; 36:12 announces revival, 36:28-32 moves from extinguished generations to total presence, 36:33-34 revives dead ground, 36:51-53 releases and gathers grave-bound bodies, and 36:77-81 argues from first formation and broken bone to renewed embodiment.
- Pericopes and bridge: P1, P2, P3, P4, and P5; repeated causal sequence. Loss of active form is followed by directed intervention, collection or repair, and a renewed output capable of growth, motion, presence, or dispute.
- Canonical scene placements: P1 3A, "Reactivating still life"; P2 6A, "Generations removed from return", and 6B, "Total reassembly into presence"; P3 7A, "Hydraulic cultivation"; P4 11A, "Horn-blast, waking, and grave-exit"; P5 18B, "A drop becomes a manifest disputant", 18C, "Discarded bone repaired into life", and 19A, "Cosmic capacity by measured analogy".
- Surprising reach: Population, soil, fetus-like formation, and bone are not merged into one material class; they share an operation in which lost organization is answered by gathering, growth, repair, or renewed motion.
- Synthesis: The bridge is procedural rather than topical. Each pericope stages a before-state lacking active organized presence, an effective intervention, and an after-state whose agency is visible, so ecological revival and bodily return become mutually clarifying demonstrations of reconstitution.

### 23. Custody becoming obligation and account
- Semantic invariant: Receiving control over a deed, load, resource, dependent being, or person creates an obligation whose outcome exposes whether custody served transfer, use, withholding, or release.
- Surface relation: direct; 36:12 preserves deeds and traces, 36:41 makes descendants entrusted cargo, 36:47 tests provision through expenditure, 36:54 and 36:65 attach settlement and testimony to action, and 36:71-75 tests ownership by service and reciprocal aid.
- Pericopes and bridge: P1, P3, P4, and P5; role progression with contrast and reversal. What begins as preservation in a record becomes carriage and onward provision, then exact account, and finally a test in which owners, dependents, servants, and supposed protectors exchange or lose their claimed roles.
- Canonical scene placements: P1 3B, "Enrolling deeds and traces"; P3 9A, "Loaded vessel and entrusted cargo", and 10B, "Provision that must pass through"; P4 12A, "Exact settlement of action", and 12B, "Sealed mouth, evidentiary limbs"; P5 17A, "Tamed livestock as a service chain", 17B, "Gods that cannot perform reciprocal service", and S15, "Earning release through a written manumission".
- Surprising reach: Custody never remains static possession. Record, cargo, food, livestock, bodily action, and bonded labor each reveal control through what the holder preserves, transfers, yields, withholds, or permits to leave.
- Synthesis: The progression turns possession into responsibility. Entrusted things generate claims on their holders; account makes those claims legible, while failed protection and contractual release reverse the assumption that control is proved simply by retaining another being.
