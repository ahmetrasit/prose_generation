# s070 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. [P1 70:1-18] Measured Ascent and Collapsing Distance
- Semantic invariant: A remote-looking event is recalibrated by a higher scale whose route, duration, and endpoint make it near.
- Surface relation: direct; `70:3-4` supplies the ascents and ascending, `70:4` the measured day of fifty thousand years, and `70:6-7` the far/near reversal.
- Surprising reach: The scale is not only numerical; it becomes a route with bends, halts, visible heights, and an endpoint that closes distance.

#### Subchannel A. Calibrated ascent
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: An ascent is assigned a day, a quantity, a five-based count, a thousand-fold magnitude, and a span of years.
- Active motifs: upward ascent `quranic:root_000997:B004/m01`; bounded amount `quranic:root_001205:B001/m01`; the number five `quranic:root_000439:B001/m01`; one thousand `quranic:root_000045:B001/m01`; year-long term `quranic:root_000751:B008/m01`; bounded daylight `quranic:root_001700:B001/m01`; indefinite temporal duration `quranic:root_001700:B002/m01`.

- Synthesis: The ascent is rendered as a calibrated operation: a path rises while day, amount, five, thousand, and year supply nested units. The apparently excessive duration does not defer the endpoint; it identifies the scale on which the ascent is already measured.
