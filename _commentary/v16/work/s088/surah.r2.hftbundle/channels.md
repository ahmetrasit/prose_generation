# S088 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. Covering and the Protected Interior
- Semantic invariant: A layer, enclosure, or obscurity limits access to what lies within.
- Surface relation: direct; 88:1 `الغاشية`, 88:10 `جنة`, and 88:23 `كفر` activate covering and enclosure.
- Surprising reach: The same invariant extends from veils and night to shields, shelters, burial, and the opposed condition of exposure.

#### Subchannel A. Layered Veil
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Successive coverings settle over an object and hide its interior.
- Active motifs: concealing cover `ج ن ن:B001/m01`; overlying veil `غ ش و:B001/m01`; enveloping cover `غ ش ي:B001/m01`; covering layer `ك ف ر:B001/m01`; carried upper layer `ع ل و:B008/m01`

- Synthesis: The motifs form a single layering mechanism in which an upper cover closes sight and access to what it contains.

#### Subchannel B. Night and Dark Canopy
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Night and dark cloud spread as a continuous overhead cover.
- Active motifs: night covering `ج ن ن:B002/m01`; blackened night or cloud `ح م ي:B012/m01`; engulfing darkness `ك ف ر:B002/m01`; overhead sky or cloud `س م و:B004/m01`

#### Subchannel C. Shield, Shelter, and Enclosure
