# s051 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. P1 (51:1-23): Directed Provision
- Semantic invariant: Sustenance becomes available through directed movement, containment, release, cultivation, and measured transfer.
- Surface relation: direct; 51:1-4 wind, bearing, running, and division; 51:15 springs and gardens; 51:19 an acknowledged claim in wealth; 51:22 provision in the sky.
- Surprising reach: The opening motions resolve into one supply system: atmospheric freight becomes routed water, productive land, and finally an apportioned human share.

#### Subchannel A. Atmospheric Freight
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Wind sorts and transports a heavy, rain-bearing load until what is held aloft falls as provision.
- Active motifs: winnowing scatter (`quranic:root_000513:B004/m01`); visible load-bearing (`quranic:root_000357:B001/m01`); heavy freight (`quranic:root_001674:B002/m01`); rain-bearing cloud (`quranic:root_000357:B010/m01`); cloud and rain (`quranic:root_000745:B004/m02`); falling rain (`quranic:root_001675:B003/m01`); rain as provision (`quranic:root_000560:B003/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 51:1 `ذَّٰرِيَٰتِ/ذَرْوًا` (ذ ر و); 51:2 `حَٰمِلَٰتِ` (ح م ل), `وِقْرًا` (و ق ر); 51:6 `وَٰقِعٌ` (و ق ع); 51:7,22 `سَّمَآءِ` (س م و); 51:22 `رِزْقُ` (ر ز ق).
- Synthesis: Scattering is not mere dispersal. It separates and mobilizes a burden whose latent identity is a rain cloud; the burden's eventual falling turns transport into delivery. The sky's promised provision is thereby materialized as freight moving through an atmospheric distribution chain.

#### Subchannel B. Routed Water
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Released water enters channels, arrives from elsewhere, and gathers in springs before continuing through the landscape.
- Active motifs: flowing water (`quranic:root_000240:B001/m01`); watercourse or channel (`quranic:root_000009:B004/m01`); incoming flood from another land (`quranic:root_000009:B005/m01`); spring or water source (`quranic:root_001069:B006/m01`); rainfall as provision (`quranic:root_000560:B003/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 51:3 `جَٰرِيَٰتِ` (ج ر ي); 51:15 `عُيُونٍ` (ع ي ن); 51:16 `ءَاتَىٰ` (ء ت ي); 51:22 `رِزْقُ` (ر ز ق).
- Synthesis: The running motion of 51:3 acquires a hydrological mechanism. Water comes, enters a course, and emerges as a spring; provision is not static abundance but a routed arrival whose path joins sky and inhabited ground.

#### Subchannel C. Fertility and Yield
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Water-bearing movement culminates in fertile soil, protected growth, and produce carried to maturity.
- Active motifs: fertile planted land (`quranic:root_000025:B002/m01`); sheltered orchard (`quranic:root_000266:B003/m01`); dense plant growth (`quranic:root_000266:B011/m01`); crop and palm yield with abundant water (`quranic:root_000009:B007/m01`); fruit carried on the plant (`quranic:root_000357:B002/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 51:2 `حَٰمِلَٰتِ` (ح م ل); 51:15 `جَنَّٰتٍ` (ج ن ن); 51:16 `ءَاتَىٰ` (ء ت ي); 51:20,23 `أَرْضِ` (ء ر ض).
- Synthesis: Bearing recurs at the productive end of the supply chain: land receives water, vegetation thickens behind the garden's enclosure, and the plant itself becomes a bearer. Atmospheric carriage is reframed as the first phase of biological yield.

#### Subchannel D. The Allotted Human Share
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Provision is divided, received, held as wealth, and transferred to one whose claim or deprivation makes a share due.
- Active motifs: apportioning shares (`quranic:root_001226:B003/m01`); allotted provision (`quranic:root_000560:B001/m01`); continuing grant (`quranic:root_000240:B006/m01`); taking into possession (`quranic:root_000018:B001/m01`); wealth or property (`quranic:root_001457:B001/m01`); a holder's specific right (`quranic:root_000347:B003/m01`); asking for a share (`quranic:root_000661:B001/m01`); deprivation of expected provision (`quranic:root_000313:B008/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 51:3 `جَٰرِيَٰتِ` (ج ر ي); 51:4 `مُقَسِّمَٰتِ` (ق س م); 51:16 `ءَاخِذِينَ` (ء خ ذ); 51:19 `أَمْوَٰلِ` (م و ل), `حَقٌّ` (ح ق ق), `سَّآئِلِ` (س ء ل), `مَحْرُومِ` (ح ر م); 51:22 `رِزْقُ` (ر ز ق).
- Synthesis: Cosmic division and ethical giving share one operation: making a measured portion reach its proper recipient. The claimant and the deprived are not appended to the provision scene; they reveal its terminal obligation, where flow becomes possession and possession remains answerable to a right.

### 2. P1 (51:1-23): A Promise Coming Due
