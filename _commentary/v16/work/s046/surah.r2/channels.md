# s046 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. P1 (46:1-14): Inscribed Truth Made Legible
- Semantic invariant: A true communication leaves ordered, transmissible marks that can be read, witnessed, and followed.
- Surface relation: direct; the book and its signs are presented as evidence in 46:2, 46:4, 46:7-10, and 46:12.
- Surprising reach: The branch images turn abstract proof into a material chain of stitching, inscription, landmarks, testimony, and tracked passage.

#### Subchannel A. Joined marks become durable evidence
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Separate marks are joined into an ordered inscription whose surviving trace can function as a landmark.
- Active motifs: Joined material (`quranic:root_001283:B001/m01`); ordered written letters (`quranic:root_001283:B002/m01`); a remaining trace (`quranic:root_000011:B003/m01`); a distinguishing landmark (`quranic:root_001040:B002/m01`); a visible sign (`quranic:root_000074:B003/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: `الكتاب` (46:2, 46:4, 46:12), `أثارة من علم` (46:4), and `آياتنا` (46:7).
- Synthesis: Scripture and requested evidence occupy one evidentiary scene: joined signs endure, distinguish a route, and remain available for inspection.

#### Subchannel B. Disclosure passes through witness, tongue, and messenger
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Meaning becomes public through an articulate carrier and is then secured by present or knowledgeable testimony.
- Active motifs: A present witness (`quranic:root_000822:B001/m01`); declaration grounded in knowledge (`quranic:root_000822:B002/m01`); meaning disclosed by word or sign (`quranic:root_000170:B005/m01`); clear articulation (`quranic:root_000996:B001/m01`); the tongue as language and message (`quranic:root_001355:B006/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: `كفى به شهيدا بيني وبينكم` (46:8), `شاهد من بني إسرائيل` (46:10), `بلسان عربي` (46:12), and `ينذر` (46:12).
- Synthesis: The truth claim is not only a text-object; it is voiced in a legible tongue and enters a field of accountable witnessing.

#### Subchannel C. Precedence is tested by traceable following
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A marked predecessor opens a path, later travelers follow it, and rival claims of precedence become visible as movement on that path.
- Active motifs: Following or imitation (`quranic:root_000175:B001/m01`); successive following (`quranic:root_000186:B001/m01`); gentle route guidance (`quranic:root_001583:B001/m01`); a forward race (`quranic:root_000671:B001/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: `تتلى عليهم آياتنا` (46:7), `أتبع إلا ما يوحى إلي` (46:9), `سبقونا إليه` and `يهتدوا به` (46:11), and `من قبله كتاب موسى إماما` (46:12).
- Synthesis: Moses' book, present revelation, and human response form a procession rather than isolated assertions; the issue is who follows an attested route and who merely claims to be ahead.

### 12. P3 (46:26-35): Proximity and Protection Put to Test
- Semantic invariant: Claimed nearness is valid only if it can answer, assist, cover fault, and secure escape under pressure.
- Surface relation: direct; false proximity and failed gods in 46:28 are contrasted with answering God's caller, forgiveness, rescue, and the absence of other protectors in 46:31-32.
- Surprising reach: Offering, neighborhood asylum, pursuit, and covering branches translate religious relation into enforceable protection.

#### Subchannel A. Manufactured proximity cannot produce help
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Objects are adopted and offered as means of nearness, but disappear precisely when assistance is required.
- Active motifs: Acquiring or adopting (`quranic:root_000018:B010/m01`); an offering that establishes proximity (`quranic:root_001212:B005/m01`); an object of worship (`quranic:root_000047:B001/m01`); assistance (`quranic:root_001510:B001/m01`); something lost or vanished (`quranic:root_000913:B003/m01`); fabricated falsehood (`quranic:root_001150:B003/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: `اتخذوا من دون الله قربانا آلهة`, `فلولا نصرهم`, `بل ضلوا عنهم`, and `ذلك إفكهم وما كانوا يفترون` (46:28).
- Synthesis: Sacral proximity is judged by performance under need; disappearance at the point of aid exposes both the adopted objects and the fabrication that sustained them.

#### Subchannel B. Response determines whether pursuit can be escaped
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A call demands an answer; refusal leaves the hearer unable to outstrip pursuit and without an alternate ally.
- Active motifs: Answering a call (`quranic:root_000273:B003/m01`); calling or attraction by speech (`quranic:root_000478:B001/m01`); escaping or outstripping a pursuer (`quranic:root_000985:B002/m01`); an ally who gives help (`quranic:root_001684:B004/m01`); straying from the route (`quranic:root_000913:B001/m01`).

- Synthesis: Answering is a protection decision: refusal does not preserve autonomy but leaves the nonrespondent exposed to a pursuit no other patron can block.

#### Subchannel C. True protection covers fault and grants asylum
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Belief establishes a protected relation in which offenses are covered and the exposed person is brought into shelter from pain.
