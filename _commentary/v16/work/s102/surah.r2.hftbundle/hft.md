# HFT: earlier activation hypotheses, per focus ayah of surah 102

# Focus 102:1

## competitive capture
- reading: The self-renewing contest to outnumber others actively captured the addressees away from an unstated prior concern.
- mechanism: The attempt to exceed others is a feedback process: every comparative gain creates a new comparison, so rivalry itself becomes the agent that repeatedly displaces another concern.
- trace:
  - 102:1 **أَلْهَىٰكُمُ** ل ه و B001: Diversion from one concern by another supplies the displacement relation, and the causative form assigns that displacement to word 2.
  - 102:1 **ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ** ك ث ر B002: Mutual outnumbering supplies the recursive contest that keeps renewing the distracting occupation.

## pleasurable more
- reading: The felt pleasure of each new increment was itself the medium of capture, making 'more' affectively self-propelling.
- mechanism: Each increment supplies a small affective reward; enjoyment is therefore not incidental to accumulation but the reinforcement that makes increasing quantity continue to occupy attention.
- trace:
  - 102:1 **أَلْهَىٰكُمُ** ل ه و B002: Amusement and enjoyment supply the rewarding affect through which diversion can operate.
  - 102:1 **ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ** ك ث ر B001: Numerical growth supplies the repeatable increments that feed the reward loop.

## claim proliferation
- reading: The distracting surplus could be a proliferating layer of claims and status-talk through which abundance is socially manufactured.
- mechanism: What multiplies need not be possessions alone: assertions of standing, comparative stories, and claims upon wealth can proliferate until maintaining the representation of abundance becomes the occupation.
- trace:
  - 102:1 **أَلْهَىٰكُمُ** ل ه و B001: Displacement by a substitute occupation lets the maintenance of claims take over attention.
  - 102:1 **ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ** ك ث ر B003: Abundance in speech, claims, wealth, or borrowed status supplies a symbolic rather than merely material object of increase.

## feed mill
- reading: Abundance functioned like feed for a mill: each acquisition was consumed by the accumulating process and generated demand for the next.
- mechanism: As a material analogy, every new unit of abundance becomes fresh input to a process that consumes it and demands another unit; accumulation behaves like throughput rather than stable possession.
- trace:
  - 102:1 **أَلْهَىٰكُمُ** ل ه و B003: Casting material into a mill mouth supplies the repeated-feed image and the conversion of each gain into process input.
  - 102:1 **ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ** ك ث ر B001: Continuing numerical increase supplies the stream of units that keeps the imagined mechanism turning.

## mortality bounded itinerary

- mechanism: The distracting contest becomes a journey with a boundary: it carries the addressed group forward until a visit at a place of burial. Visitation makes tenure provisional, while burial and concealment reverse the fantasy that accumulated standing is permanent.
- trace:

  - 102:2 **ٱلْمَقَابِرَ** ق ب ر B001: Burial supplies the bodily terminus that limits the competitive process.
  - 102:2 **ٱلْمَقَابِرَ** ق ب ر B002: Lowering and concealment supply a spatial reversal of public increase and display.

## delayed sign recognition

- mechanism: Quantity does not merely consume time that could be used for learning; it crowds out discriminating marks. The repeated promise of knowing and the later stabilization of certainty recast diversion as a delay between available signs and settled recognition.

# Focus 102:2

## temporary grave visit
- reading: You crossed the limit into burial, yet the verb casts even that arrival as a visit: the grave is a reached station whose permanence the wording pointedly withholds.
- mechanism: Ordinary visitation supplies arrival without lexical ownership of the destination, while the burial-place branch identifies that destination as the resting place of the dead. The limit construction therefore reaches death while retaining a live asymmetry: those buried are described as visitors, so the grave is a terminal boundary in the sentence but not securely a permanent home in the visitor image.
- trace:
  - 102:2 **زُرْتُمُ** ز و ر B003: Visiting and turning toward someone supplies a purposeful arrival whose visitor relation remains temporary.
  - 102:2 **ٱلْمَقَابِرَ** ق ب ر B001: Burial and the place of graves fix the destination as actual mortuary space rather than an abstract limit.

## bent descent into hiddenness
- reading: The verse images a trajectory permitted to bend all the way into hidden, sunken enclosure: the cemetery is the geometry of a completed diversion.
- mechanism: The visit remains the overt event, but the same motion root also carries turning aside, and the grave root carries hiddenness, depression, and enclosure. Together they spatialize the clause as a course that bends away and sinks inward; حَتَّى measures not merely elapsed time but the full extent of that diversion.
- trace:
  - 102:2 **زُرْتُمُ** ز و ر B003: The ordinary visit keeps the model attached to the clause's actual directed motion.

  - 102:2 **ٱلْمَقَابِرَ** ق ب ر B002: Hiddenness, inward sinking, and sealed enclosure give the route a downward and occluding terminus.

## buried recourse
