# s102 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. Hidden Matters Becoming Known
- Semantic invariant: Something unavailable or uncertain becomes apprehensible through sight, inward judgment, disclosure, or certainty.
- Surface relation: direct; 102:3-5 `تَعْلَمُونَ` and `عِلْمَ`, 102:5 and 102:7 `ٱلْيَقِينِ`, 102:6-7 `لَتَرَوُنَّ` and `لَتَرَوُنَّهَا`, and 102:7 `عَيْنَ`.
- Surprising reach: The same disclosure pattern extends from waking perception into deliberation, dream vision, apparition, and deliberate showing.

#### Subchannel A. Sight Ripening into Certainty
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: Sensory or inward sight yields direct witnessing, knowledge, and settled certainty.
- Active motifs: sensory seeing `ر ء ي:B001/m01`; insight `ر ء ي:B001/m02`; direct witnessing `ع ي ن:B002/m01`; knowledge `ع ل م:B001/m01`; fixed certainty `ي ق ن:B001/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 102:3-5 `تَعْلَمُونَ` and `عِلْمَ` (`ع ل م`); 102:5, 102:7 `ٱلْيَقِينِ` (`ي ق ن`); 102:6-7 `لَتَرَوُنَّ` and `لَتَرَوُنَّهَا` (`ر ء ي`); 102:7 `عَيْنَ` (`ع ي ن`)
- Synthesis: Perception begins with the act of seeing, deepens into inward insight and direct encounter, and culminates in knowledge from which doubt has been displaced.

#### Subchannel B. Deliberation Producing Judgment
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: An inward view is turned over through reflection and counsel until it becomes a considered judgment.
- Active motifs: opinion and judgment `ر ء ي:B002/m01`; conjectural assessment `ر ء ي:B002/m02`; deliberation and counsel `ر ء ي:B002/m04`; learning `ع ل م:B001/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 102:3-5 `تَعْلَمُونَ` and `عِلْمَ` (`ع ل م`); 102:6-7 `لَتَرَوُنَّ` and `لَتَرَوُنَّهَا` (`ر ء ي`)
- Synthesis: Seeing is internalized as a mental position, then tested through reflection, consultation, and learning. The result is not merely an image but a judgment formed in the heart.

#### Subchannel C. Deliberate Disclosure
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: One party makes an object or proposition visible so that another can attend to and know it.
- Active motifs: causative showing `ر ء ي:B012/m01`; mirror presentation `ر ء ي:B012/m02`; teaching and informing `ع ل م:B001/m03`
- Ayah anchors: 102:3-5 `تَعْلَمُونَ` and `عِلْمَ` (`ع ل م`); 102:6-7 `لَتَرَوُنَّ` and `لَتَرَوُنَّهَا` (`ر ء ي`)
- Synthesis: Disclosure has a showing party, an object made present, and an addressee whose attention is summoned. Showing, presenting through a mirror, and informing all enact the transfer of something into another's awareness.

#### Subchannel D. Vision Beyond Waking Sight
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Hidden content appears through dreams or through an uncanny intermediary associated with divination and treatment.
- Active motifs: dream vision `ر ء ي:B003/m01`; jinn apparition `ر ء ي:B008/m01`; divinatory or therapeutic showing `ر ء ي:B008/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 102:6-7 `لَتَرَوُنَّ` and `لَتَرَوُنَّهَا` (`ر ء ي`)
- Synthesis: The root's visual field opens onto scenes in which the perceiver receives an image rather than seeking an ordinary visible object. Dream and apparition preserve disclosure while changing its setting and intermediary.

### 2. Attention Captured, Redirected, and Performed
- Semantic invariant: Attention is drawn away from one object, absorbed by pleasure, redirected toward social desire, or deliberately solicited from an audience.
- Surface relation: direct; 102:1 `أَلْهَىٰ` names diversion, while 102:6-7 `لَتَرَوُنَّ` and `لَتَرَوُنَّهَا` provide the contrasting field of compelled attention.
- Surprising reach: Diversion ranges from inward neglect to play, intimate attraction, reputation management, and the refusal of social shame.

#### Subchannel A. Diversion and Abandonment
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A person or heart is occupied by one thing and consequently leaves or turns away from another.
- Active motifs: diversion `ل ه و:B001/m01`; turning away `ل ه و:B001/m02`; occupying another `ل ه و:B001/m03`; variant diversion `ل ه ي:B001/m01`; variant abandonment `ل ه ي:B003/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 102:1 `أَلْهَىٰ` (`ل ه و`); no separately tagged `ل ه ي` surface occurrence
- Synthesis: Attention is treated as a limited capacity: occupation by one object produces neglect of another, and repeated occupation hardens into abandonment.

#### Subchannel B. Absorbing Play
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Play and entertainment hold attention through immediate enjoyment.
- Active motifs: play and enjoyment `ل ه و:B002/m01`; variant amusement `ل ه ي:B002/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 102:1 `أَلْهَىٰ` (`ل ه و`); no separately tagged `ل ه ي` surface occurrence
- Synthesis: The diverting object becomes pleasurable in its own right. Play creates a self-contained scene of enjoyment capable of displacing more consequential attention.

#### Subchannel C. Intimate and Social Diversion
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Desire is directed toward intimate companions, offspring, or repeated social courtship and conversation.
- Active motifs: intimate euphemism `ل ه و:B002/m02`; variant intimate euphemism `ل ه ي:B002/m02`; recurrent courtship and conversation `ز و ر:B003/m03`
- Ayah anchors: 102:1 `أَلْهَىٰ` (`ل ه و`); 102:2 `زُرْتُمُ` (`ز و ر`); no separately tagged `ل ه ي` surface occurrence
- Synthesis: Diversion acquires participants and a social setting: the sought pleasure may be a person, a family attachment, or repeated conversational courtship.

#### Subchannel D. Performing for Public Regard
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: An actor shapes conduct for an audience, while the opposite posture ceases to be restrained by shame.
- Active motifs: ostentatious display `ر ء ي:B005/m01`; reputation-seeking `ر ء ي:B005/m02`; diminished shame `ج ح م:B005/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 102:6-7 `لَتَرَوُنَّ` and `لَتَرَوُنَّهَا` (`ر ء ي`); 102:6 `ٱلْجَحِيمَ` (`ج ح م`)
- Synthesis: Public regard becomes a social force. One person stages an act to be seen or heard of, while another acts with little restraint from the same observing public.

### 3. Increase, Rivalry, and Material Throughput
- Semantic invariant: Units multiply, gather into a mass, become a basis for rivalry, or pass through a mechanism that converts quantity into output.
- Surface relation: direct; 102:1 `ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ` presents reciprocal increase as the opening action.
- Surprising reach: Quantity appears not only as number and wealth but also as speech, claimants, grain, gifts, and rising dust.

#### Subchannel A. Growth into Abundance
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: A quantity grows, is made numerous, or is deliberately accumulated until it becomes a gathered abundance.
- Active motifs: abundance `ك ث ر:B001/m01`; multiplication `ك ث ر:B001/m02`; seeking accumulation `ك ث ر:B001/m03`; addition `ن ع م:B010/m01`; intensification `ن ع م:B010/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 102:1 `ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ` (`ك ث ر`); 102:8 `ٱلنَّعِيمِ` (`ن ع م`)
- Synthesis: Increase can be a state, an operation, or an appetite. Repeated addition produces a compact whole, while intensification marks the same movement in the force of an action.

#### Subchannel B. Competitive Outnumbering
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Groups face one another and seek superiority through number, wealth, honor, or knowledge.
- Active motifs: outnumbering `ك ث ر:B002/m01`; rivalry and boasting `ك ث ر:B002/m02`; reciprocal facing `ر ء ي:B004/m01`; contest in knowledge `ع ل م:B001/m04`
- Ayah anchors: 102:1 `ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ` (`ك ث ر`); 102:3-5 `تَعْلَمُونَ` and `عِلْمَ` (`ع ل م`); 102:6-7 `لَتَرَوُنَّ` and `لَتَرَوُنَّهَا` (`ر ء ي`)
- Synthesis: Quantity becomes relational when opposed parties face each other. Counting, display, and comparison turn abundance into a contest whose prize is precedence.

#### Subchannel C. Proliferating Speech and Claims
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Verbal production or repeated demands accumulate around a speaker or a person burdened by claimants.
- Active motifs: prolific speech `ك ث ر:B003/m02`; crowding claimants `ك ث ر:B003/m03`; repeated questioning `س ء ل:B001/m04`
- Ayah anchors: 102:1 `ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ` (`ك ث ر`); 102:8 `لَتُسْـَٔلُنَّ` (`س ء ل`)
- Synthesis: Multiplicity migrates from possessions into discourse. Words can become excessive, and questions or claims can multiply until the person addressed is surrounded by demand.

#### Subchannel D. Grain, Mill, and Gift Throughput
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Repeated portions of grain are fed into a mill, with gifts or coins understood through the same image of measured input.
- Active motifs: grain cast into a mill `ل ه و:B003/m01`; variant mill-feed `ل ه ي:B004/m01`; gift or coins as portions `ل ه و:B003/m02`; abundant giving `ل ه و:B003/m03`
- Ayah anchors: 102:1 `أَلْهَىٰ` (`ل ه و`); no separately tagged `ل ه ي` surface occurrence
- Synthesis: Quantity becomes operational: small units are continually supplied to an opening and transformed. The image then carries over from grain to money and repeated giving.

#### Subchannel E. Dense Masses in Motion
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Fine particles gather into a dense, rising, or intensely concentrated mass.
- Active motifs: rising dust cloud `ك ث ر:B005/m01`; extreme concentration `ك ث ر:B005/m02`; compact gathering `ك ث ر:B007/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 102:1 `ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ` (`ك ث ر`)
- Synthesis: Dust makes abundance visible as particulate density. The scene joins accumulation with motion rather than with static counting.

### 4. Inquiry, Desire, and Response
- Semantic invariant: A question opens a relation between asker and addressee, becomes a request for a desired object, and may end in fulfillment.
- Surface relation: direct; 102:8 `لَتُسْـَٔلُنَّ` places questioning at the surah's final turn.
- Surprising reach: Inquiry expands from information-seeking into mutual dialogue, desiderata, intercession, and the granting of a need.

#### Subchannel A. Interrogation and Mutual Questioning
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: An addressee is called to answer, or several participants question one another.
- Active motifs: inquiry `س ء ل:B001/m01`; mutual questioning `س ء ل:B004/m01`; attention-seeking inquiry formula `ر ء ي:B013/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 102:8 `لَتُسْـَٔلُنَّ` (`س ء ل`); 102:6-7 `لَتَرَوُنَّ` and `لَتَرَوُنَّهَا` (`ر ء ي`)
- Synthesis: Questioning creates a discourse scene with accountable participants. The attention formula supplies the summons, while direct and reciprocal questions organize who must speak.

#### Subchannel B. Need Becoming a Request
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: An inward want is identified as a desired object and then directed toward someone able to provide it.
- Active motifs: request `س ء ل:B001/m02`; asking through or about a person `س ء ل:B001/m03`; desired object or need `س ء ل:B002/m01`
- Ayah anchors: 102:8 `لَتُسْـَٔلُنَّ` (`س ء ل`)
- Synthesis: The question-root also structures desire: a need becomes definite, an addressee is selected, and the want is voiced as a request.

#### Subchannel C. Fulfillment and Benefaction
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: The requested need is met by an act of granting or benefaction.
- Active motifs: fulfillment of a request `س ء ل:B003/m01`; benefaction to another `ن ع م:B001/m03`
- Ayah anchors: 102:8 `لَتُسْـَٔلُنَّ` (`س ء ل`) and `ٱلنَّعِيمِ` (`ن ع م`)
- Synthesis: Request and favor form two sides of one transfer. The asker's lack is answered by a giver who completes the matter and conveys benefit.

### 5. Movement, Visitation, and Resting Places
- Semantic invariant: Directed movement culminates in arrival, social reception, settlement, or emplacement in a final resting place.
- Surface relation: direct; 102:2 joins `زُرْتُمُ` with `ٱلْمَقَابِرَ` in a single movement toward graves.
- Surprising reach: The movement ranges from speed and foot travel to hospitality, congenial residence, burial, and the provision of a grave.

#### Subchannel A. Fast and Pedestrian Travel
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A traveler advances either with speed or directly on foot, with the feet bearing the cost of movement.
- Active motifs: rapid travel `ز و ر:B007/m01`; pedestrian journey `ن ع م:B012/m01`; worn feet `ن ع م:B012/m02`; light walking `ن ع م:B012/m03`
- Ayah anchors: 102:2 `زُرْتُمُ` (`ز و ر`); 102:8 `ٱلنَّعِيمِ` (`ن ع م`)
- Synthesis: Travel is differentiated by manner: forceful speed contrasts with unmounted movement, while foot-wear and lightness register the body's participation in the journey.

#### Subchannel B. Visit and Reception
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: A visitor intentionally approaches another party and enters a social frame of reception and hospitality.
- Active motifs: visit and visitor `ز و ر:B003/m01`; guest hospitality `ز و ر:B003/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 102:2 `زُرْتُمُ` (`ز و ر`)
- Synthesis: Visiting has direction, an arriving participant, and a receiving side. Hospitality develops the destination from a place into a social encounter.

#### Subchannel C. Settlement and Final Emplacement
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Arrival resolves into a fitting place of residence or into burial within a grave and cemetery.
- Active motifs: congenial land `ن ع م:B011/m01`; pleasant settlement `ن ع م:B011/m02`; grave as resting place `ق ب ر:B001/m01`; burial `ق ب ر:B001/m02`; provision of a grave `ق ب ر:B001/m03`; cemetery `ق ب ر:B001/m04`
- Ayah anchors: 102:2 `ٱلْمَقَابِرَ` (`ق ب ر`); 102:8 `ٱلنَّعِيمِ` (`ن ع م`)
- Synthesis: Residence and burial share the operation of assigning a body to a place. One setting welcomes continued dwelling; the other receives the dead and fixes their final station.

### 6. Heat as an Escalating Force
- Semantic invariant: Heat intensifies from material combustion into destructive conflict, bodily anger, and a burning or fixed gaze.
- Surface relation: direct; 102:6 `ٱلْجَحِيمَ` names the blazing destination.
- Surprising reach: Fire becomes a model for warfare, facial inflammation, anger, and the eye's severe visual force.

#### Subchannel A. Blaze, Embers, and Hot Place
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: Fire flares, embers multiply, and the surrounding place becomes intensely hot.
- Active motifs: blazing fire `ج ح م:B001/m01`; massed embers `ج ح م:B001/m02`; intensely hot place `ج ح م:B001/m03`
- Ayah anchors: 102:6 `ٱلْجَحِيمَ` (`ج ح م`)
- Synthesis: Combustion is rendered as both process and environment: flame intensifies, glowing material accumulates, and heat defines the place containing it.

#### Subchannel B. Battle at Full Heat
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Conflict reaches a state of violent intensity in which severe fighting produces death.
- Active motifs: raging warfare `ج ح م:B002/m01`; killing and death in battle `ج ح م:B002/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 102:6 `ٱلْجَحِيمَ` (`ج ح م`)
- Synthesis: The blaze is translated into collective violence. Battle supplies the setting, fury the process, and death the terminal outcome.

#### Subchannel C. Anger Igniting the Face
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Inner anger rises as heat and becomes visible in the face and an aggressive nasal posture.
- Active motifs: face inflamed by anger `ج ح م:B004/m01`; nose-raised anger posture `ق ب ر:B004/m02`
- Ayah anchors: 102:2 `ٱلْمَقَابِرَ` (`ق ب ر`); 102:6 `ٱلْجَحِيمَ` (`ج ح م`)
- Synthesis: An inward emotional surge is externalized through heated facial appearance and a projecting posture, making anger bodily legible.

#### Subchannel D. The Burning Gaze
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: An eye opens wide, reddens, fixes, or sharpens its look until vision itself takes on the force of flame.
- Active motifs: wide fixed eye `ج ح م:B003/m02`; red and sharpened gaze `ج ح م:B003/m04`
- Ayah anchors: 102:6 `ٱلْجَحِيمَ` (`ج ح م`)
- Synthesis: Combustive intensity is transferred to ocular behavior. Heat no longer consumes external matter but concentrates in the eye's color, width, and unwavering direction.

### 7. Eye Forms and Regimes of Regard
- Semantic invariant: The eye functions as organ, beautiful form, caring or harmful regard, observer, and model for reflective or circular structures.
- Surface relation: direct; 102:7 `عَيْنَ` is joined to 102:6-7 `لَتَرَوُنَّ` and `لَتَرَوُنَّهَا`.
- Surprising reach: Ocular meaning extends from sight into protection, envy, espionage, household presence, mirrors, the sun, sockets, and balance tilt.

#### Subchannel A. Eye, Sight, and Ocular Condition

## competitive capture
- reading: The self-renewing contest to outnumber others actively captured the addressees away from an unstated prior concern.

- trace:

  - 102:1 **ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ** ك ث ر B002: Mutual outnumbering supplies the recursive contest that keeps renewing the distracting occupation.

## pleasurable more
- reading: The felt pleasure of each new increment was itself the medium of capture, making 'more' affectively self-propelling.
- mechanism: Each increment supplies a small affective reward; enjoyment is therefore not incidental to accumulation but the reinforcement that makes increasing quantity continue to occupy attention.
- trace:
  - 102:1 **أَلْهَىٰكُمُ** ل ه و B002: Amusement and enjoyment supply the rewarding affect through which diversion can operate.
  - 102:1 **ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ** ك ث ر B001: Numerical growth supplies the repeatable increments that feed the reward loop.
