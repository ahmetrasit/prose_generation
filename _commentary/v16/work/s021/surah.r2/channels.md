# S021 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. P1 - Attention Becomes Answerability
- Semantic invariant: A message becomes morally operative through reception, inward retention, and accountable expression.
- Surface relation: direct; 21:1-3 moves from heedlessness and turning aside to listening, distracted hearts, and secret counsel, while 21:7 and 21:10 join revelation, reminder, knowledge, writing, and reason.
- Surprising reach: Hearing is reframed as an entire transmission system: the ear admits a signal, the heart either keeps or abandons it, and private speech eventually becomes a public claim.

#### Subchannel A. Distracted reception
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Sound reaches the ear, but distraction diverts the heart and bodily turning prevents reception from becoming understanding.
- Active motifs: auditory reception `quranic:root_000741:B001/m01`; attentive listening `quranic:root_000831:B006/m01`; lapse from vigilance `quranic:root_001097:B001/m01`; diversion by another concern `quranic:root_001382:B001/m01`; turning one's side away `quranic:root_001001:B005/m01`; heart as the seat of understanding `quranic:root_001248:B001/m01`; intellect restraining ignorance `quranic:root_001036:B001/m01`

- Synthesis: The scene is not simple deafness. Sound crosses the outer threshold, but heedlessness, diversion, and a turned side interrupt its passage into the heart, making listening physically present yet cognitively uncompleted.

#### Subchannel B. Secret counsel becomes circulating speech
- Reading type: mixed

#### Subchannel C. Inscription restores memory and judgment
- Reading type: mixed
