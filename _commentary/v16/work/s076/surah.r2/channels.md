# s076 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. P1 - From Unmarked Duration to a Perceptible Person
- Semantic invariant: An initially unregistered entity crosses a temporal threshold, becomes present, and enters human perception and social memory.
- Surface relation: direct; 76:1-2 anchors duration, non-thinghood, creation, humanity, and mention.
- Surprising reach: Time is not merely a backdrop: its lexical branches arrive, overpower, and bring down events, while humanity becomes an image in the eye and a subject of speech, record, and reputation.

#### Subchannel A. Duration Delivers an Event into Existence
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Extended time reaches its appointed moment and carries an entity from non-description into occurrence.
- Active motifs: arriving event (quranic:root_000009:B001/m01); destructive arrival (quranic:root_000009:B011/m01); appointed time (quranic:root_000382:B001/m01); time of death or calamity (quranic:root_000382:B002/m01); overpowering duration (quranic:root_000494:B001/m01); extended age (quranic:root_000494:B002/m01); descending vicissitude (quranic:root_000494:B004/m01); occurrence in time (quranic:root_001332:B001/m01); knowable thing (quranic:root_000831:B001/m01)
- Ayah anchors: 76:1 (ء ت ي, ح ي ن, د ه ر, ك و ن, ش ي ء)
- Synthesis: The scene treats the opening interval as an active delivery mechanism. Duration stretches and overpowers, a critical time arrives, and what had not counted as a reportable thing becomes an occurrence that can be known and predicated. The destructive senses of arrival and time keep emergence under the pressure of mortality rather than presenting existence as neutral persistence.

### S20. P1 -> P2 - Refused Recompense Returns as Thanked Striving
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Pericopes and bridge: P1 (76:8-9; canonical scene 7C) -> P2 (76:21-22; canonical scene 15A); causal sequence and role reversal
- Scene or process: Human providers refuse to turn recipients into sources of repayment or praise; divine evaluation then makes the providers' own striving the object of recompense and thanks.
- Active motifs: corresponding recompense for an act (quranic:root_000244:B001/m01); recognition and praise for a favor (quranic:root_000810:B001/m01); work, earning, and serious effort (quranic:root_000709:B002/m01); reward returning from one's act (quranic:root_000209:B002/m01)
- Ayah anchors: 76:3 (ش ك ر); 76:9 (ج ز ي, ش ك ر); 76:12 (ج ز ي); 76:21 (ث و ب); 76:22 (ج ز ي, ش ك ر, س ع ي)
- Synthesis: P1 refuses “recompense” and “thanks” from vulnerable beneficiaries; P2 answers with the same two relations from a different source, declaring the gift to be their recompense and their striving thanked. The bridge is therefore not a general reward theme but an exact reversal of creditor and evaluator: the human giver declines a claim on the recipient, and divine recompense returns value to the giver without transferring that debt back to the vulnerable.

### S23. P1 -> P2 - Binding Reverses from Captivity into Ornament and Constitution
- Reading type: mixed
- Pericopes and bridge: P1 (76:4, 76:8; canonical scenes 5A and 7A) -> P2 (76:21, 76:28; canonical scenes 15C and 21A); shared spatial invariant with functional reversal and role progression
- Scene or process: External binding first confines a punished body and names a captive; P2 changes encirclement into honored ornament and changes the captivity-root into the internal cohesion of a created body.
- Active motifs: iron collar binding limb or neck (quranic:root_001102:B006/m01); binding and captivity (quranic:root_000030:B001/m01); bracelet encircling the hand (quranic:root_000758:B004/m01); wearable ornament (quranic:root_000353:B001/m01); firm joints and articulated bodily constitution (quranic:root_000030:B002/m01)

- Synthesis: Encirclement and fastening persist while their functions reverse. ### S25. P1 -> P2 - Prepared Punishment Frames the Surah
- Reading type: mixed
- Pericopes and bridge: P1 (76:4; canonical scene 5A) -> P2 (76:31; canonical scene 23B); repeated scene signature and whole-surah framing recurrence
- Scene or process: Punitive equipment is declared ready near the opening, and prepared painful punishment returns in the final ayah after the intervening alternatives of mercy and reward.

### S26. P1 -> P2 - Ingratitude Progresses from Response to Capturing Command
- Reading type: mixed
- Pericopes and bridge: P1 (76:3; canonical scene 3B) -> P2 (76:24; canonical scene 18B); role progression and contrast in moral agency
- Scene or process: Covering a received favor first appears as one possible response to guidance; it later appears in a would-be commander whose order must not capture another person's obedience.
- Active motifs: concealment and denial of a favor (quranic:root_001307:B004/m01); compliance and following an order (quranic:root_000956:B001/m01); forcing an obedient person into disobedience (quranic:root_001307:B007/m01); sin as delay from good (quranic:root_000013:B001/m01)
- Ayah anchors: 76:3-5, 76:24 (ك ف ر); 76:24 (ط و ع, ء ث م)
- Synthesis: P1 locates ingratitude inside the guided person's own response; P2 externalizes it as a social pressure issuing commands. The prohibition on obedience reverses that capture, preserving the agent's alignment with guidance rather than allowing another person's ingratitude to become governing authority.
