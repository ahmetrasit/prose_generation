# S104 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. Denigration, Force, and Breaking
- Semantic invariant: Concentrated pressure crosses from speech and gesture into propulsion, forced movement, and the loss of bodily or material integrity.
- Surface relation: direct; 104:1 هُمَزَةٍ and لُّمَزَةٍ enact social injury, while 104:4-5 يُنۢبَذَ and ٱلْحُطَمَةِ turn pressure into casting and crushing.
- Surprising reach: The same force pattern extends through compressed articulation, a clenched palm, a prod or bow impulse, a charging mass, and an exhausted driven animal.

#### Subchannel A. Speech as Compressed Injury
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: An aggressor compresses fault-finding into utterance, gesture, and repeated attack until another person's standing is blunted.
- Active motifs: fault-finding and covert disparagement (`ل م ز:B001/m01`); denigrating assault (`ه م ز:B004/m01`); pressure-shaped articulation (`ه م ز:B002/m01`); blunted tongue or perception (`ك ل ل:B001/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:1 كُلِّ (`ك ل ل`), هُمَزَةٍ (`ه م ز`), and لُّمَزَةٍ (`ل م ز`)
- Synthesis: The speech act is figured as pressure rather than neutral description. Articulation itself is compressed, the target is persistently probed for defects, and the resulting social injury resembles an edge or faculty made dull.

#### Subchannel B. Hand Pressure, Prodding, and Casting
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Force gathers in the hand, becomes a squeeze or thrust, and ends by displacing its object.
- Active motifs: squeezing in the hand (`ه م ز:B001/m01`); prodding impulse (`ه م ز:B003/m01`); bodily push or blow (`ل م ز:B002/m01`); clenched fist (`ج م ع:B005/m01`); physical casting (`ن ب ذ:B001/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:1 هُمَزَةٍ (`ه م ز`) and لُّمَزَةٍ (`ل م ز`); 104:2 جَمَعَ (`ج م ع`); 104:4 يُنۢبَذَ (`ن ب ذ`)
- Synthesis: Gathering and pressure converge in the clenched hand. The hand compresses, the prod transfers force, and the pushed object is finally thrown away, giving the surface casting a tactile prehistory.

#### Subchannel C. Crushing by Coalesced Mass
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Separate bodies gather momentum, overrun what lies before them, and leave brittle matter fragmented.
- Active motifs: brittle fracture and fragmentation (`ح ط م:B001/m01`); trampling herd or ravaging mass (`ح ط م:B006/m01`); coalesced charging force (`ج م ع:B010/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:2 جَمَعَ (`ج م ع`); 104:4 ٱلْحُطَمَةِ and 104:5 ٱلْحُطَمَةُ (`ح ط م`)
- Synthesis: Crushing is not only a single impact. It can be the cumulative force of a herd, flood-like movement, or gathered charge whose parts become one destructive mass and reduce resistant material to fragments.

#### Subchannel D. Harsh Driving and Exhaustion
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A harsh driver forces living bodies onward until movement and faculties lose their edge.
- Active motifs: violent herding or driving (`ح ط م:B004/m01`); exhaustion of walker, mount, wind, or faculty (`ك ل ل:B001/m02`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:1 كُلِّ (`ك ل ل`); 104:4 ٱلْحُطَمَةِ and 104:5 ٱلْحُطَمَةُ (`ح ط م`)
- Synthesis: The driver is named through the damage he causes: bodies are crowded and driven without relief, while motion, sight, hearing, or cutting power becomes spent and dull.

### 2. Accumulation, Measure, and Claimed Permanence
- Semantic invariant: Gathered quantities are counted, provisioned, extended, and treated as though numerical control could secure adequacy or duration.
- Surface relation: direct; 104:2 جَمَعَ مَالًا وَعَدَّدَهُ and 104:3 يَحْسَبُ أَنَّ مَالَهُ أَخْلَدَهُ explicitly join accumulation, enumeration, estimation, wealth, and imagined permanence.
- Surprising reach: Quantity expands into total capacity, reserve logistics, calendrical spans, devotional fixation on property, and the return of worldly goods to breakable debris.

#### Subchannel A. Hoarding, Calculation, and False Permanence
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Property is gathered and repeatedly counted, then mentally promoted from a reserve into a guarantor of lasting existence.
- Active motifs: gathering dispersed holdings (`ج م ع:B001/m01`); numerical enumeration (`ع د د:B001/m01`); accumulated property (`م و ل:B001/m01`); accounting and calculation (`ح س ب:B001/m01`); supposition (`ح س ب:B002/m01`); enduring permanence (`خ ل د:B001/m01`); devotion and object-making (`ء ل ه:B001/m01`); transient worldly goods (`ح ط م:B009/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:2 جَمَعَ (`ج م ع`), مَالًا (`م و ل`), and عَدَّدَهُ (`ع د د`); 104:3 يَحْسَبُ (`ح س ب`), مَالَهُ (`م و ل`), and أَخْلَدَهُ (`خ ل د`); 104:4-5 ٱلْحُطَمَةِ/ٱلْحُطَمَةُ (`ح ط م`); 104:6 ٱللَّهِ (`ء ل ه`)
- Synthesis: Counting converts possessions into an apparently closed system of security. The devotional branch sharpens the relation: wealth can be treated as an object of ultimate reliance, yet the crushing branch recasts its display and ornament as passing debris.

#### Subchannel B. Totality and Full Capacity
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Parts are enclosed in a whole until nothing appears absent and the containing space is filled to capacity.
- Active motifs: complete and undiminished whole (`ج م ع:B009/m01`); all-inclusive totality (`ك ل ل:B003/m01`); capacity filled to its limit (`ط ل ع:B007/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:1 كُلِّ (`ك ل ل`); 104:2 جَمَعَ (`ج م ع`); 104:7 تَطَّلِعُ (`ط ل ع`)
- Synthesis: Gathering reaches a terminal state in which the object is intact, the parts are comprehensively included, and the available space is full. The channel turns counting from an open sequence into bounded completeness.

#### Subchannel C. Prepared Reserves and Material Aid
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Supplies are measured, stored, and made ready so they can sustain a person, household, or force when need arrives.
- Active motifs: prepared equipment and reserves (`ع د د:B002/m01`); reinforcements and material aid (`م د د:B002/m01`); sufficient provision (`ح س ب:B003/m01`); measured allotment (`م د د:B006/m01`); small residual portion (`ن ب ذ:B005/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:2 عَدَّدَهُ (`ع د د`); 104:3 يَحْسَبُ (`ح س ب`); 104:4 يُنۢبَذَ (`ن ب ذ`); 104:9 مُّمَدَّدَةٍ (`م د د`)
- Synthesis: Enumeration becomes logistics: one prepares an outfit, reserve, weapon, food supply, or reinforcement, then portions it by measure. The tiny remainder and the sufficient store establish the lower and upper bounds of usable provision.

#### Subchannel D. Counted Terms and Recurring Time
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Time is divided into obligatory intervals, recurring appointments, and prolonged terms.
- Active motifs: prescribed waiting span (`ع د د:B003/m01`); recurrent appointed time (`ع د د:B005/m01`); extended term or respite (`م د د:B004/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:2 عَدَّدَهُ (`ع د د`); 104:9 مُّمَدَّدَةٍ (`م د د`)
- Synthesis: Numbering governs duration as well as possessions. A span can be counted because it imposes waiting, returns on schedule, or is deliberately prolonged, making time itself an administered quantity.

### 3. Combustion, Heat, and Consumption
- Semantic invariant: Material is fed into heat, transformed by combustion or digestion, and carried from an external fire into bodily and emotional interiors.
- Surface relation: direct; 104:6 نَارُ ٱللَّهِ ٱلْمُوقَدَةُ names fire and ignition, and 104:7 تَطَّلِعُ عَلَى ٱلْأَفْـِٔدَةِ carries that process into the hearts.
- Surprising reach: The fire scene includes fuel, hearth, cooking vessel, digestive action, inward anger, appetite, vomiting, summer heat, and drought.

#### Subchannel A. Ignition, Fuel, Hearth, and Cooking
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Fuel is placed at a hearth, rapidly ignited, and used to heat, roast, or bake food before digestion completes the transformation.
- Active motifs: ignition and flame (`و ق د:B001/m01`); combustible fuel (`و ق د:B002/m01`); hearth or fire-place (`و ق د:B003/m01`); rapid kindling (`و ق د:B005/m01`); visible fire (`ن و ر:B002/m01`); heat and fire (`ف ء د:B001/m01`); roasting or baking (`ف ء د:B001/m02`); hearth and skewer implements (`ف ء د:B001/m03`); large cooking vessel (`ج م ع:B012/m01`); digestive action (`ح ط م:B008/m02`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:2 جَمَعَ (`ج م ع`); 104:4-5 ٱلْحُطَمَةِ/ٱلْحُطَمَةُ (`ح ط م`); 104:6 نَارُ (`ن و ر`) and ٱلْمُوقَدَةُ (`و ق د`); 104:7 ٱلْأَفْـِٔدَةِ (`ف ء د`)
- Synthesis: The lexical branches assemble a complete combustion system: material fuel, a bounded hearth, ignition, flame, vessel, food, and digestive breakdown. External fire and internal digestion become parallel mechanisms for reducing and transforming matter.

#### Subchannel B. The Heart as an Interior Fire
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Thought lodges in the heart, heat gathers there, and rapid ignition becomes ardor, anger, or social conflict.
- Active motifs: heart as inward heat (`ف ء د:B002/m01`); thought lodged in the mind or heart (`خ ل د:B004/m01`); emotional ardor, anger, or war (`و ق د:B005/m02`); anger's domination (`ع م د:B015/m01`); feud between groups (`ن و ر:B007/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:3 أَخْلَدَهُ (`خ ل د`); 104:6 نَارُ (`ن و ر`) and ٱلْمُوقَدَةُ (`و ق د`); 104:7 ٱلْأَفْـِٔدَةِ (`ف ء د`); 104:9 عَمَدٍ (`ع م د`)
- Synthesis: The heart is not merely reached by fire; it already has a lexical capacity for inner burning. A settled thought can kindle into fervor, anger, and conflict, so psychological fixation and social hostility reproduce the dynamics of combustion.

#### Subchannel C. Gluttony and Emesis
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Appetite consumes beyond restraint until what entered the body rises back out.
- Active motifs: voracious eater (`ح ط م:B008/m01`); rising vomit (`ط ل ع:B011/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:4-5 ٱلْحُطَمَةِ/ٱلْحُطَمَةُ (`ح ط م`); 104:7 تَطَّلِعُ (`ط ل ع`)
- Synthesis: Consumption first resembles a fire that breaks food down, then reverses direction as the stomach expels what it could not contain. Rising changes from emergence into emesis.

#### Subchannel D. Summer Heat and Drought
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Sustained seasonal heat dries the environment until a severe year breaks people, animals, and property.
- Active motifs: peak summer heat (`و ق د:B004/m01`); destructive drought-year (`ح ط م:B003/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:4-5 ٱلْحُطَمَةِ/ٱلْحُطَمَةُ (`ح ط م`); 104:6 ٱلْمُوقَدَةُ (`و ق د`)
- Synthesis: Combustion expands to climate: heat becomes a season, the season becomes drought, and drought performs the same breaking action on living communities that impact performs on brittle matter.

### 4. Enclosure and Structural Support
- Semantic invariant: Space is bounded by doors, yards, pens, covers, restraints, walls, and load-bearing members that determine what remains inside and what can stand.
- Surface relation: direct; 104:8 مُّؤْصَدَةٌ establishes closure, while 104:9 عَمَدٍ مُّمَدَّدَةٍ supplies columns and extension.
- Surprising reach: The closed structure ranges from a sealed doorway to a mountain livestock pen, a bodily canopy, a hand-to-neck fetter, and a crowded sanctuary.

#### Subchannel A. Sealed Doorway
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A doorway is brought shut and made fast so passage is no longer available.
- Active motifs: firmly closed door (`و ص د:B001/m01`); doorway attached to a dwelling (`و ص د:B002/m02`); sealing and shutting (`ء ص د:B001/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:8 مُّؤْصَدَةٌ (`و ص د`); no separate surface occurrence for `ء ص د`
- Synthesis: Object and action coincide: the doorway marks the access point, while closing pressure converts it into an impassable boundary. The alternate closure branch intensifies the sense of something clamped over its contents.

#### Subchannel B. Courtyard, Stone Pen, and Mountain Recess
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A bounded exterior space gathers occupants or livestock within a forecourt, stone enclosure, or recess between mountains.
- Active motifs: house forecourt (`و ص د:B002/m01`); mountain stone pen (`و ص د:B003/m01`); encompassing enclosure (`ء ص د:B002/m01`); courtyard or open fore-space (`ء ص د:B004/m01`); place between mountains (`ء ص د:B005/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:8 مُّؤْصَدَةٌ (`و ص د`); no separate surface occurrence for `ء ص د`
- Synthesis: Closure acquires a spatial interior. The house forecourt, livestock pen, and mountain recess all use surrounding edges to hold what lies within, even when the bounded space remains open above.

#### Subchannel C. Canopy and Bodily Restraint
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Flexible enclosure surrounds a body either protectively as a canopy or coercively as a fetter.
- Active motifs: tent-like protective cover (`ك ل ل:B006/m01`); hand-to-neck restraint (`ج م ع:B008/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:1 كُلِّ (`ك ل ل`); 104:2 جَمَعَ (`ج م ع`)
- Synthesis: Both objects gather the body into a restricted volume. The canopy excludes insects and exposure, whereas the fetter removes freedom of movement, creating a sharp protective-versus-punitive reversal.

#### Subchannel D. Columns, Props, and Extended Frameworks
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Upright members support a structure, establish its central load path, and continue lengthwise into an extended framework.
- Active motifs: propping and support (`ع م د:B002/m01`); column or pillar (`ع م د:B003/m01`); load-bearing core (`ع م د:B007/m01`); lengthwise extension (`م د د:B001/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:9 عَمَدٍ (`ع م د`) and مُّمَدَّدَةٍ (`م د د`)
- Synthesis: A prop receives weight, the column regularizes that support, and extension distributes it through space. The surface pair therefore contains both the vertical member and the operation that carries its reach onward.

#### Subchannel E. Crowded Sacred Gathering Place
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: People converge at a sacred gathering place whose wall or precinct becomes compressed by crowding.
- Active motifs: crowded or broken sanctuary precinct (`ح ط م:B007/m01`); pilgrimage gathering place or day (`ج م ع:B004/m02`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:2 جَمَعَ (`ج م ع`); 104:4-5 ٱلْحُطَمَةِ/ٱلْحُطَمَةُ (`ح ط م`)
- Synthesis: Gathering supplies the human convergence, while the sanctuary branch gives it a built boundary vulnerable to pressure and breakage. Social assembly and architectural compression occupy one scene.

### 5. Collective Cohesion and Social Continuity
- Semantic invariant: Individuals become a durable social body through assembly, alliance, rank, kinship, and reproductive connection.
- Surface relation: indirect; 104:1 كُلِّ, 104:2 جَمَعَ and عَدَّدَهُ, 104:3 يَحْسَبُ, and 104:9 عَمَدٍ provide the lexical anchors for groups, gathering, comparison, standing, and communal reliance.
- Surprising reach: Collective cohesion ranges from public assembly and strategic alliance to tent-based status, collateral inheritance, sexual union, pregnancy, and the suspended state of an unconsummated marriage.

#### Subchannel A. Assembly and Alliance
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Dispersed people gather at a common place, become a recognized group, and coordinate around a shared undertaking.
- Active motifs: assembled group or mixed collective (`ج م ع:B002/m01`); civic gathering place or summons (`ج م ع:B004/m01`); alliance or collusion (`ج م ع:B013/m01`); grouped bodies (`ك ل ل:B009/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:1 كُلِّ (`ك ل ل`); 104:2 جَمَعَ (`ج م ع`)
- Synthesis: Collection becomes social organization when a place or summons brings people together and alliance gives the group a common direction. The resulting whole is more than proximity: its members act in concert.

#### Subchannel B. Tent Community and Comparative Standing
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A mobile tent community distinguishes households and persons by inherited standing, visible qualities, and comparison with peers.
- Active motifs: socially counted prestige (`ح س ب:B004/m01`); people of tents and poles (`ع م د:B004/m01`); peer, counterpart, or allotted share (`ع د د:B006/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:2 عَدَّدَهُ (`ع د د`); 104:3 يَحْسَبُ (`ح س ب`); 104:9 عَمَدٍ (`ع م د`)
- Synthesis: The tent pole supplies a social setting as well as a structure. Within it, standing is something reckoned from ancestry, conduct, generosity, or wealth and negotiated by comparison with others.

#### Subchannel C. Collateral Kin and Inherited Standing
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A death activates lateral kinship, and inherited standing identifies which relations count when direct parent-child continuity is absent.
- Active motifs: collateral kin and inheritance (`ك ل ل:B004/m01`); ancestral standing (`ح س ب:B004/m02`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:1 كُلِّ (`ك ل ل`); 104:3 يَحْسَبُ (`ح س ب`)
- Synthesis: Social accounting reaches into genealogy. When the direct line is missing, lateral relations become operative, while ancestry supplies the remembered standing through which the family locates itself.

#### Subchannel D. Union, Pregnancy, and Withheld Consummation
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Sexual union creates reproductive continuity, while pregnancy, virginity, or an unconsummated marriage marks whether that connection has taken effect.
- Active motifs: sexual union (`ج م ع:B006/m01`); retained pregnancy (`ج م ع:B007/m01`); untouched spouse or virgin state (`ج م ع:B007/m02`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:2 جَمَعَ (`ج م ع`)
- Synthesis: Gathering becomes the joining of bodies and the continuity of generations. The same lexical container also records suspended union: a spouse remains untouched, or pregnancy remains held within the body.

### 6. Emergence, Visibility, and Growth
- Semantic invariant: Something hidden or low comes into view by rising, sprouting, approaching, peeking, flashing, or occupying a vantage point.
- Surface relation: indirect; 104:6 نَارُ supplies visible light and 104:7 تَطَّلِعُ supplies upward emergence and disclosure.
- Surprising reach: The invariant links luminaries, beacons, lightning, palms, flowers, shy peeking, sudden arrival, mountain ascent, and conspicuous height.

#### Subchannel A. Luminary Rise and Guiding Light
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A luminary rises, spreads light, and becomes a visible marker by which travelers orient themselves.
- Active motifs: rising sun, dawn, moon, or star (`ط ل ع:B001/m01`); illumination (`ن و ر:B001/m01`); beacon or boundary marker (`ن و ر:B005/m01`); cloud-flash or lightning (`ك ل ل:B011/m02`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:1 كُلِّ (`ك ل ل`); 104:6 نَارُ (`ن و ر`); 104:7 تَطَّلِعُ (`ط ل ع`)
- Synthesis: Rise creates visibility, light extends it, and a beacon stabilizes it into orientation. The momentary lightning flash is the compressed counterpart of the enduring guide.

#### Subchannel B. Sprouting, Flowering, and Close-Rooted Growth
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Plants emerge from hidden growth, flower, and thicken into a closely rooted stand.
- Active motifs: emerging palm spathe or crop (`ط ل ع:B005/m01`); blossom (`ن و ر:B004/m01`); close-rooted vegetation (`و ص د:B004/m01`); mixed seed-grown date palm (`ج م ع:B011/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:2 جَمَعَ (`ج م ع`); 104:6 نَارُ (`ن و ر`); 104:7 تَطَّلِعُ (`ط ل ع`); 104:8 مُّؤْصَدَةٌ (`و ص د`)
- Synthesis: Emergence proceeds from first visible growth to flower and then to a dense stand whose roots draw near one another. The unnamed mixed palm adds generative variety to the same cultivated scene.

#### Subchannel C. Arrival, Peeking, and Visible Appearance
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A figure approaches or suddenly appears, looks out from concealment, and withdraws when seen.
- Active motifs: appearing arrival or incursion (`ط ل ع:B002/m01`); curious peeking and hiding (`ط ل ع:B008/m01`); visible countenance (`ط ل ع:B009/m01`); shy or startled flight (`ن و ر:B006/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:6 نَارُ (`ن و ر`); 104:7 تَطَّلِعُ (`ط ل ع`)
- Synthesis: Visibility is unstable: an arrival breaks into view, a face presents itself, and a curious observer alternates between looking and concealment. Shy flight turns appearance into immediate retreat.

#### Subchannel D. Ascent, Vantage, and Prominence
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A person or object climbs to an overlooking position and becomes conspicuous through height.
- Active motifs: ascent and overlooking access (`ط ل ع:B006/m01`); protruding height (`ط ل ع:B012/m01`); lofty visible structure (`ع م د:B005/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:7 تَطَّلِعُ (`ط ل ع`); 104:9 عَمَدٍ (`ع م د`)
- Synthesis: Ascent supplies the route, the overlook supplies perspective, and height turns the elevated object into a landmark. The channel joins movement upward with the state of standing above one's surroundings.

### 7. Inquiry, Targeting, and Deliberate Action
- Semantic invariant: Knowledge is sought or assembled, converted into intention, and then directed toward governance, reconnaissance, hunting, conflict, or formal exchange.
- Surface relation: direct; 104:5 أَدْرَىٰكَ foregrounds knowing, 104:7 تَطَّلِعُ foregrounds inspection, and 104:9 عَمَدٍ carries the lexical extension into deliberate intent.
- Surprising reach: Epistemic inquiry develops into administrative oversight, scouting, a concealed hunt, projectile mechanics, treaty renunciation, and sale by thrown token.

#### Subchannel A. Knowing, Testing, and Disclosure
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: An unknown matter is investigated, tested, learned, and disclosed to another observer.
- Active motifs: knowing a matter (`د ر ي:B001/m01`); informing another (`د ر ي:B001/m02`); seeking and testing news (`ح س ب:B010/m01`); inspecting or revealing hidden affairs (`ط ل ع:B003/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:3 يَحْسَبُ (`ح س ب`); 104:5 أَدْرَىٰكَ (`د ر ي`); 104:7 تَطَّلِعُ (`ط ل ع`)
- Synthesis: Inquiry moves from uncertain report to tested knowledge, then from private discovery to disclosure. Looking into a hidden matter and causing another to know it are consecutive roles in one epistemic process.

#### Subchannel B. Deliberation and Accountable Leadership
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A group consolidates its judgment, an overseer examines conduct, and a relied-upon leader carries the decision.
- Active motifs: resolved collective judgment (`ج م ع:B003/m01`); practical oversight and accountability (`ح س ب:B006/m01`); relied-upon leader (`ع م د:B006/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:2 جَمَعَ (`ج م ع`); 104:3 يَحْسَبُ (`ح س ب`); 104:9 عَمَدٍ (`ع م د`)
- Synthesis: Distributed opinions are gathered into resolve, oversight tests the resulting conduct, and leadership supplies the person on whom implementation rests. Cognition becomes administration.

#### Subchannel C. Reconnaissance and Deliberate Raid
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Scouts gain sight of an opponent so a force can choose and deliberately approach its target.
- Active motifs: reconnaissance party (`ط ل ع:B004/m01`); purposeful targeting or raid (`د ر ي:B002/m01`); deliberate intent (`ع م د:B001/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:5 أَدْرَىٰكَ (`د ر ي`); 104:7 تَطَّلِعُ (`ط ل ع`); 104:9 عَمَدٍ (`ع م د`)
- Synthesis: The scout supplies information, intent fixes the objective, and the raid converts knowledge into directed movement. Seeing and striking are ordered stages rather than unrelated acts.

#### Subchannel D. Concealed Hunt and Projectile Flight
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A hunter approaches under cover, aims a pointed implement, releases a force-driven projectile, and either reaches the heart or overshoots the mark.
- Active motifs: concealed stalking (`د ر ي:B003/m01`); pointed horn or implement (`د ر ي:B004/m01`); bow-driven impulse (`ه م ز:B003/m02`); arrow overshooting its target (`ط ل ع:B010/m01`); strike to the heart (`ف ء د:B003/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:1 هُمَزَةٍ (`ه م ز`); 104:5 أَدْرَىٰكَ (`د ر ي`); 104:7 تَطَّلِعُ and ٱلْأَفْـِٔدَةِ (`ط ل ع`, `ف ء د`)
- Synthesis: Concealment manages distance, the pointed tool and bow concentrate force, and the heart defines the intended endpoint. Overshooting preserves the same trajectory while reversing its outcome.

#### Subchannel E. Declared Conflict and Tokenized Exchange
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Throwing an object or declaration changes a social relation by openly ending a pact or making a sale binding.
- Active motifs: treaty renunciation and open confrontation (`ن ب ذ:B002/m01`); sale concluded by thrown token or goods (`ن ب ذ:B003/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:4 يُنۢبَذَ (`ن ب ذ`)
- Synthesis: Casting becomes a formal sign rather than disposal. In one case it returns a pact and opens conflict; in the other it transfers goods and closes a transaction.

### 8. Material Flow and Replenishment
- Semantic invariant: A material moves through a channel or container to replenish, mix with, or extend another material system.
- Surface relation: indirect; 104:2 جَمَعَ and عَدَّدَهُ, 104:4 يُنۢبَذَ, and 104:9 عَمَدٍ مُّمَدَّدَةٍ anchor gathering, stored water, added material, dams, and extension.
- Surprising reach: The same replenishment mechanism governs rivers and wells, earth dams, steeped fruit drink, enriched animal water, and ink drawn from an inkwell into a pen.

#### Subchannel A. Wells, Feeding Waters, and Earthworks
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Water collects in a durable source, one flow replenishes another, and a dam redirects or holds the current against packed earth.
- Active motifs: perennial well or collected water (`ع د د:B004/m01`); water feeding another watercourse (`م د د:B003/m01`); dam across a stream (`ع م د:B013/m01`); rain-packed soil (`ع م د:B010/m01`); loose excavated earth (`ن ب ذ:B009/m02`); coalesced flood momentum (`ج م ع:B010/m02`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:2 جَمَعَ (`ج م ع`) and عَدَّدَهُ (`ع د د`); 104:4 يُنۢبَذَ (`ن ب ذ`); 104:9 عَمَدٍ (`ع م د`) and مُّمَدَّدَةٍ (`م د د`)
- Synthesis: The channel supplies source, flow, barrier, and construction material. Water gathers and extends itself through tributary feeding, while packed and excavated earth reshape that movement into a retained pool or redirected course.

#### Subchannel B. Steeped Drink and Enriched Animal Water
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A dry ingredient is cast into water and left to infuse, producing a drink or nutritive mixture.
- Active motifs: fruit steeped in a vessel (`ن ب ذ:B006/m01`); water enriched with meal or seed for animals (`م د د:B008/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:4 يُنۢبَذَ (`ن ب ذ`); 104:9 مُّمَدَّدَةٍ (`م د د`)
- Synthesis: Casting initiates mixture rather than rejection. Water receives fruit, flour, seed, or sesame, and the added substance extends plain liquid into a prepared drink or feed.

#### Subchannel C. Ink Supply from Inkwell to Pen
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: An inkwell is replenished, holds a writing medium, and yields that medium to a pen.
- Active motifs: ink medium (`م د د:B005/m01`); replenished inkwell (`م د د:B005/m02`); pen drawing its supply (`م د د:B005/m03`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:9 مُّمَدَّدَةٍ (`م د د`)
- Synthesis: Extension is material and sequential: liquid is added to the inkwell, retained there, and drawn onward by the pen. Writing becomes a controlled flow system in miniature.

### 9. Bodily Integrity and Attrition
- Semantic invariant: Bodies move between intact strength and loss of integrity through pain, heart disease, aging, pressure wounds, suppuration, or altered skin.
- Surface relation: indirect; 104:1 كُلِّ, 104:3 يَحْسَبُ, 104:4-5 ٱلْحُطَمَةِ, 104:7 ٱلْأَفْـِٔدَةِ, and 104:9 عَمَدٍ مُّمَدَّدَةٍ anchor faculties, bodily estimation, breaking, heart, pain, and wound extension.
- Surprising reach: The body appears as chest and heart, aged mount, swollen saddle wound, vigorous youth, intact animal, and skin whose color shades into disease.

#### Subchannel A. Heart Ailment, Weakness, and Crushing Pain
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Injury or disease reaches the heart, spreads through the chest, and leaves the person weak, fearful, or overwhelmed by pain.
- Active motifs: heart ailment (`ف ء د:B003/m02`); weak or absent-hearted state (`ف ء د:B004/m01`); crushing grief, pain, or illness (`ع م د:B008/m01`); chest as bodily enclosure (`ك ل ل:B007/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:1 كُلِّ (`ك ل ل`); 104:7 ٱلْأَفْـِٔدَةِ (`ف ء د`); 104:9 عَمَدٍ (`ع م د`)
- Synthesis: The chest locates the vulnerable interior, the heart receives disease, and crushing pain changes an organ-level injury into a whole-person state of weakness or fear.

#### Subchannel B. Aging, Pressure Wound, and Suppuration
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Age or repeated pressure weakens a body, damages tissue beneath an intact surface, and produces swelling or pus.
- Active motifs: senescence and emaciation (`ح ط م:B005/m01`); saddle or pressure wound (`ع م د:B009/m01`); accumulating wound discharge (`م د د:B007/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:4-5 ٱلْحُطَمَةِ/ٱلْحُطَمَةُ (`ح ط م`); 104:9 عَمَدٍ (`ع م د`) and مُّمَدَّدَةٍ (`م د د`)
- Synthesis: Attrition can be gradual rather than explosive. A body loses strength with age, sustained pressure crushes tissue under the skin, and the wound extends internally as swelling and discharge.

#### Subchannel C. Vigor and Undiminished Form
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A youthful or stout body retains fullness, strength, and all of its parts.
- Active motifs: vigorous full-bodied youth (`ع م د:B011/m01`); stout compact physique (`ك ل ل:B008/m01`); intact body without loss (`ج م ع:B009/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:1 كُلِّ (`ك ل ل`); 104:2 جَمَعَ (`ج م ع`); 104:9 عَمَدٍ (`ع م د`)
- Synthesis: Integrity is rendered positively as compact strength, bodily fullness, and the absence of missing parts. This is the bodily opposite of the crushed, emaciated, or suppurating state.

#### Subchannel D. Mottled Color and Skin Affliction
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: White, red, gray, or patchy coloration becomes a visible bodily state and may shade into a skin disorder.
- Active motifs: mottled skin or hair coloration (`ح س ب:B009/m01`); disease-like discoloration (`ح س ب:B009/m02`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:3 يَحْسَبُ (`ح س ب`)
- Synthesis: Reckoning shifts into visible pattern: colors are distributed across skin, hair, or hide, and the same appearance can be read as ordinary coloration or bodily affliction.

### 10. Adornment and Surface Presentation
- Semantic invariant: A visible surface is dressed, encircled, groomed, colored, marked, or made to flash.
- Surface relation: indirect; 104:1 كُلِّ, 104:2 جَمَعَ, 104:3 أَخْلَدَهُ, 104:5 أَدْرَىٰكَ, 104:6 نَارُ and ٱلْمُوقَدَةُ, and 104:7 تَطَّلِعُ provide the lexical anchors for covering, attached ornament, grooming tools, pigment, branding, and display.
- Surprising reach: Presentation includes a child's undergarment, earrings and bracelets, combing, kohl, depilation, tattoo smoke, livestock branding, crowns, smiles, and gemstone-like sparkle.

#### Subchannel A. Garment and Attached Jewelry
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Clothing covers the body while ornaments remain attached at the ear or wrist as durable visible additions.
- Active motifs: small undergarment (`ء ص د:B003/m01`); attached earrings or bracelets (`خ ل د:B003/m01`); garments gathered over the body (`ج م ع:B009/m02`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:2 جَمَعَ (`ج م ع`); 104:3 أَخْلَدَهُ (`خ ل د`); no separate surface occurrence for `ء ص د`
- Synthesis: Covering and attachment form two layers of presentation. Garments gather around the body, while jewelry remains fixed to selected points and turns persistence into ornament.

#### Subchannel B. Combing, Kohl, and Depilation
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A pointed grooming tool orders the hair, dark pigment defines the eye or skin, and a preparation removes unwanted hair.
- Active motifs: hair-combing tool (`د ر ي:B004/m02`); soot used as kohl (`ن و ر:B008/m01`); depilatory preparation (`ن و ر:B009/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:5 أَدْرَىٰكَ (`د ر ي`); 104:6 نَارُ (`ن و ر`)
- Synthesis: Grooming combines shaping, adding pigment, and removing material. The pointed comb, smoke-derived cosmetic, and depilatory preparation operate on different surfaces but share the work of deliberate bodily presentation.

#### Subchannel C. Fire-Made Marks
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Heat and smoke leave a durable sign on skin or hide.
- Active motifs: livestock brand made by fire (`ن و ر:B002/m02`); tattoo pigment from smoke (`ن و ر:B008/m02`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:6 نَارُ (`ن و ر`)
- Synthesis: Fire produces more than light and destruction. Applied to a surface, it becomes an identifying brand; reduced to smoke pigment, it becomes an inserted bodily mark.

#### Subchannel D. Circlet, Smile, and Sparkle
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: An encircling ornament frames a visible subject, while teeth or a polished object flash within the viewer's field.
- Active motifs: crown, halo, or encircling wreath (`ك ل ل:B005/m01`); tooth-revealing smile (`ك ل ل:B011/m01`); fire-like glitter (`و ق د:B006/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:1 كُلِّ (`ك ل ل`); 104:6 ٱلْمُوقَدَةُ (`و ق د`)
- Synthesis: The circlet supplies stable framing and the smile or glitter supplies momentary brilliance. Encirclement and flash work together to make a person, place, or object visually prominent.

### 11. Attachment, Exclusion, and Place
- Semantic invariant: Persons and objects are positioned by clinging to a place, moving aside, or being cast beyond a social or spatial center.
- Surface relation: direct; 104:3 أَخْلَدَهُ supplies adhesion and settledness, 104:4 يُنۢبَذَ supplies expulsion, and 104:9 عَمَدٍ supplies continued adherence.
- Surprising reach: Spatial placement becomes a social relation encompassing residence, withdrawal, foundlings, dependents, and neglected animals.

#### Subchannel A. Clinging and Continued Residence
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A person adheres to a place or companion, settles there, and remains.
- Active motifs: clinging, resting, or dwelling (`خ ل د:B002/m01`); continued adherence or residence (`ع م د:B014/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:3 أَخْلَدَهُ (`خ ل د`); 104:9 عَمَدٍ (`ع م د`)
- Synthesis: Attachment is both relational and spatial. One leans toward a person or place, then converts that inclination into sustained residence.

#### Subchannel B. Withdrawal to the Side
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A person leaves the center of activity and takes up a position at the side.
- Active motifs: moving or sitting apart (`ن ب ذ:B004/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:4 يُنۢبَذَ (`ن ب ذ`)
- Synthesis: Casting is internalized as self-placement: instead of being thrown away by another person, the person withdraws and occupies a marginal location.

#### Subchannel C. Cast-Off Dependents
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A dependent person or animal is rejected, left exposed, and made reliant on whoever receives or sustains it.
- Active motifs: rejection from consideration (`ن ب ذ:B001/m02`); abandoned child (`ن ب ذ:B007/m01`); neglected ewe (`ن ب ذ:B009/m01`); dependent, burden, or orphan (`ك ل ل:B002/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:1 كُلِّ (`ك ل ل`); 104:4 يُنۢبَذَ (`ن ب ذ`)
- Synthesis: Expulsion becomes a social condition. The foundling, neglected animal, orphan, or burden lacks a secure center and survives only by entering another person's care.

### 12. Calamity Voiced as Doom and Lament
- Semantic invariant: Calamity is made present through speech, either as a pronouncement over another or as a first-person cry of disgrace and distress.
- Surface relation: direct; 104:1 وَيْلٌ voices doom, while 104:6 ٱللَّهِ supplies the divine name that can enter oath, appeal, or lament.
- Surprising reach: A compact doom formula unfolds into personal lament, public disgrace, and invocation.

#### Subchannel A. Pronouncement of Ruin
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: A speaker declares that destruction, punishment, or evil has fallen upon its addressee.
- Active motifs: doom and ruin formula (`و ي ل:B001/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:1 وَيْلٌ (`و ي ل`)
- Synthesis: The utterance does not merely describe danger; it performs a judgment by placing the addressee under ruin and punishment.

#### Subchannel B. Lament, Disgrace, and Invocation
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A sufferer turns calamity inward as a cry of shame or distress and may invoke the divine name within that cry.
- Active motifs: personal lament and disgrace (`و ي ل:B002/m01`); divine name in invocation or oath (`ء ل ه:B002/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:1 وَيْلٌ (`و ي ل`); 104:6 ٱللَّهِ (`ء ل ه`)
- Synthesis: The external sentence of doom becomes a first-person lament. Invocation gives that cry an addressee and intensifies the movement from public calamity to personally voiced distress.

## Standalone Subchannels

### S1. Cushioned Seating
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A small cushion is thrown down, arranged, and used to seat or support a person.
- Active motifs: small supporting cushion (`ح س ب:B008/m01`); floor cushion placed for sitting (`ن ب ذ:B008/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:3 يَحْسَبُ (`ح س ب`); 104:4 يُنۢبَذَ (`ن ب ذ`)
- Synthesis: Throwing is constructive here: the cushion is cast onto the ground precisely so it can receive a seated body, while the smaller pillow supplies localized support.

### S2. Blind Burrowing Animal

- Active motifs: blind mole-like creature (`خ ل د:B005/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:3 أَخْلَدَهُ (`خ ل د`)
- Synthesis: The branch forms a complete concrete animal image centered on blindness, resemblance to a mouse, and hidden low movement.
