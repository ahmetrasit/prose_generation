Read the surah as one text and write its map of image chains: the lexical images that run through several of
its ayat and join them into one scene, process or movement. A later writer will read one ayah at a time, with
only that ayah's own dictionary. The map lets that writer hear what the ayah's words carry in the surah as a
whole, including senses whose evidence sits under the words of other ayat.

Your evidence is the surah text, the dictionary of every root in the surah (each branch with the classical
dictionaries' own phrases), earlier readers' channel review (channels.md) and activation hypotheses (hft.md),
and your own knowledge of Arabic and the Quran. Where a member or passage comes from memory rather than from
the dictionary or the text, say so. Do not use tools, delegate, browse or inspect files.

The channel review and the HFT records are proposals by earlier readers. Ignore their judgements: grades,
strength or confidence labels, reading types, words such as "surprising", "exploratory" or "latent", and every
statement of what a reading may or may not do. Make your own judgement from the surah's words, the dictionary
phrases and the Quran. Do not rediscover what they already assembled: start from their chains, test each one
against the dictionary phrases and the text, and join, extend, split or correct them. Where their wording
abstracts a member, go back to the dictionary phrase and name what it actually says.

A chain belongs on the map when its members are senses the dictionary attests for words that stand in the
surah, and together they make one image or process that the surah's wording or sequence lets a listener hear.
A member need not be the sense that translates its word in its own ayah; a chain is heard across the surah,
not in one word. Say plainly when a member is a rare sense or is attested only inside a fixed expression, and
keep it. When the dictionary itself joins two of the surah's words in one phrase, quote that phrase: it is
the strongest evidence a chain can have. Keep a scene at the level of its objects, their parts and their
operation. When proposals share members, do not fold one into another's more abstract function unless nothing
concrete is lost. Carry every chain that meets this test, however unusual; leave out proposals that do not.

Write the map in English, with Arabic quoted exactly (surah wording from the text, dictionary phrases from the
dictionary). It is working material for the writer, not commentary prose.

1. `## Chains`. For each chain, a `###` heading naming the image. One paragraph on what the image is and how it
   moves through the surah. Then its members, one line each: the ayah ref, the word as it stands in the text,
   the root and branch id, the dictionary's own phrase quoted exactly, and what this member contributes to the
   image. Add Quran passages outside the surah, with exact refs, where they stage or confirm the chain.
2. `## Ayat`. For each ayah in order: which chains it opens, advances or completes, and what its words add to
   each.
3. `## Not carried`. Each channel subchannel and each HFT record you did not carry into a chain, one line each:
   its name and why.

No ranking and no labels of strength or confidence. No list of what a writer must include. There is no length
target and no required number of chains or members.

# S104 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. Denigration, Force, and Breaking
- Semantic invariant: Concentrated pressure crosses from speech and gesture into propulsion, forced movement, and the loss of bodily or material integrity.
- Surface relation: direct; 104:1 هُمَزَةٍ and لُّمَزَةٍ enact social injury, while 104:4-5 يُنۢبَذَ and ٱلْحُطَمَةِ turn pressure into casting and crushing.
- Surprising reach: The same force pattern extends through compressed articulation, a clenched palm, a prod or bow impulse, a charging mass, and an exhausted driven animal.

#### Subchannel A. Speech as Compressed Injury
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: An aggressor compresses fault-finding into utterance, gesture, and repeated attack until another person's standing is blunted.
- Active motifs: fault-finding and covert disparagement (`ل م ز:B001/m01`); denigrating assault (`ه م ز:B004/m01`); pressure-shaped articulation (`ه م ز:B002/m01`); blunted tongue or perception (`ك ل ل:B001/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:1 كُلِّ (`ك ل ل`), هُمَزَةٍ (`ه م ز`), and لُّمَزَةٍ (`ل م ز`)
- Synthesis: The speech act is figured as pressure rather than neutral description. Articulation itself is compressed, the target is persistently probed for defects, and the resulting social injury resembles an edge or faculty made dull.

#### Subchannel B. Hand Pressure, Prodding, and Casting
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Force gathers in the hand, becomes a squeeze or thrust, and ends by displacing its object.
- Active motifs: squeezing in the hand (`ه م ز:B001/m01`); prodding impulse (`ه م ز:B003/m01`); bodily push or blow (`ل م ز:B002/m01`); clenched fist (`ج م ع:B005/m01`); physical casting (`ن ب ذ:B001/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:1 هُمَزَةٍ (`ه م ز`) and لُّمَزَةٍ (`ل م ز`); 104:2 جَمَعَ (`ج م ع`); 104:4 يُنۢبَذَ (`ن ب ذ`)
- Synthesis: Gathering and pressure converge in the clenched hand. The hand compresses, the prod transfers force, and the pushed object is finally thrown away, giving the surface casting a tactile prehistory.

#### Subchannel C. Crushing by Coalesced Mass
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Separate bodies gather momentum, overrun what lies before them, and leave brittle matter fragmented.
- Active motifs: brittle fracture and fragmentation (`ح ط م:B001/m01`); trampling herd or ravaging mass (`ح ط م:B006/m01`); coalesced charging force (`ج م ع:B010/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:2 جَمَعَ (`ج م ع`); 104:4 ٱلْحُطَمَةِ and 104:5 ٱلْحُطَمَةُ (`ح ط م`)
- Synthesis: Crushing is not only a single impact. It can be the cumulative force of a herd, flood-like movement, or gathered charge whose parts become one destructive mass and reduce resistant material to fragments.

#### Subchannel D. Harsh Driving and Exhaustion
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A harsh driver forces living bodies onward until movement and faculties lose their edge.
- Active motifs: violent herding or driving (`ح ط م:B004/m01`); exhaustion of walker, mount, wind, or faculty (`ك ل ل:B001/m02`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:1 كُلِّ (`ك ل ل`); 104:4 ٱلْحُطَمَةِ and 104:5 ٱلْحُطَمَةُ (`ح ط م`)
- Synthesis: The driver is named through the damage he causes: bodies are crowded and driven without relief, while motion, sight, hearing, or cutting power becomes spent and dull.

### 2. Accumulation, Measure, and Claimed Permanence
- Semantic invariant: Gathered quantities are counted, provisioned, extended, and treated as though numerical control could secure adequacy or duration.
- Surface relation: direct; 104:2 جَمَعَ مَالًا وَعَدَّدَهُ and 104:3 يَحْسَبُ أَنَّ مَالَهُ أَخْلَدَهُ explicitly join accumulation, enumeration, estimation, wealth, and imagined permanence.
- Surprising reach: Quantity expands into total capacity, reserve logistics, calendrical spans, devotional fixation on property, and the return of worldly goods to breakable debris.

#### Subchannel A. Hoarding, Calculation, and False Permanence
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Property is gathered and repeatedly counted, then mentally promoted from a reserve into a guarantor of lasting existence.
- Active motifs: gathering dispersed holdings (`ج م ع:B001/m01`); numerical enumeration (`ع د د:B001/m01`); accumulated property (`م و ل:B001/m01`); accounting and calculation (`ح س ب:B001/m01`); supposition (`ح س ب:B002/m01`); enduring permanence (`خ ل د:B001/m01`); devotion and object-making (`ء ل ه:B001/m01`); transient worldly goods (`ح ط م:B009/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:2 جَمَعَ (`ج م ع`), مَالًا (`م و ل`), and عَدَّدَهُ (`ع د د`); 104:3 يَحْسَبُ (`ح س ب`), مَالَهُ (`م و ل`), and أَخْلَدَهُ (`خ ل د`); 104:4-5 ٱلْحُطَمَةِ/ٱلْحُطَمَةُ (`ح ط م`); 104:6 ٱللَّهِ (`ء ل ه`)
- Synthesis: Counting converts possessions into an apparently closed system of security. The devotional branch sharpens the relation: wealth can be treated as an object of ultimate reliance, yet the crushing branch recasts its display and ornament as passing debris.

#### Subchannel B. Totality and Full Capacity
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Parts are enclosed in a whole until nothing appears absent and the containing space is filled to capacity.
- Active motifs: complete and undiminished whole (`ج م ع:B009/m01`); all-inclusive totality (`ك ل ل:B003/m01`); capacity filled to its limit (`ط ل ع:B007/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:1 كُلِّ (`ك ل ل`); 104:2 جَمَعَ (`ج م ع`); 104:7 تَطَّلِعُ (`ط ل ع`)
- Synthesis: Gathering reaches a terminal state in which the object is intact, the parts are comprehensively included, and the available space is full. The channel turns counting from an open sequence into bounded completeness.

#### Subchannel C. Prepared Reserves and Material Aid
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Supplies are measured, stored, and made ready so they can sustain a person, household, or force when need arrives.
- Active motifs: prepared equipment and reserves (`ع د د:B002/m01`); reinforcements and material aid (`م د د:B002/m01`); sufficient provision (`ح س ب:B003/m01`); measured allotment (`م د د:B006/m01`); small residual portion (`ن ب ذ:B005/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:2 عَدَّدَهُ (`ع د د`); 104:3 يَحْسَبُ (`ح س ب`); 104:4 يُنۢبَذَ (`ن ب ذ`); 104:9 مُّمَدَّدَةٍ (`م د د`)
- Synthesis: Enumeration becomes logistics: one prepares an outfit, reserve, weapon, food supply, or reinforcement, then portions it by measure. The tiny remainder and the sufficient store establish the lower and upper bounds of usable provision.

#### Subchannel D. Counted Terms and Recurring Time
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Time is divided into obligatory intervals, recurring appointments, and prolonged terms.
- Active motifs: prescribed waiting span (`ع د د:B003/m01`); recurrent appointed time (`ع د د:B005/m01`); extended term or respite (`م د د:B004/m01`)
- Ayah anchors: 104:2 عَدَّدَهُ (`ع د د`); 104:9 مُّمَدَّدَةٍ (`م د د`)
- Synthesis: Numbering governs duration as well as possessions. A span can be counted because it imposes waiting, returns on schedule, or is deliberately prolonged, making time itself an administered quantity.

### 3. Combustion, Heat, and Consumption
- Semantic invariant: Material is fed into heat, transformed by combustion or digestion, and carried from an external fire into bodily and emotional interiors.
- Surface relation: direct; 104:6 نَارُ ٱللَّهِ ٱلْمُوقَدَةُ names fire and ignition, and 104:7 تَطَّلِعُ عَلَى ٱلْأَفْـِٔدَةِ carries that process into the hearts.
