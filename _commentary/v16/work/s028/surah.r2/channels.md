## Parent Channels

### 1. P1: Power Reverses Its Own Vertical Order
- Semantic invariant: Attempts to secure height by dividing and weakening others become the conditions for the weak to receive leadership, place, and succession.
- Surface relation: direct; 28:4-6 moves from Pharaoh's elevation, partition, weakening, slaughter, and life-sparing to the installation and inheritance of those made weak.
- Surprising reach: The regime's management of bodies and lineages materializes the reversal it is trying to prevent.

- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: An elevated ruler fragments a population, weakens one section, kills sons, preserves women, and thereby pushes the land out of right order.

- Synthesis: The scene is not merely oppression in general. It is a population-control mechanism: height is maintained through segmentation, induced weakness, and differential survival. The corruption lies in turning social order into a managed life-and-death partition.
