# Channel review of surah 29: subchannels anchored in 29:38

(source: latent_activation/network/v3/reviews/s029/reader_a_pilot.md)

## Parent Channels

### 9. P3 (29:28-44) Routes and Forums Turned Hostile
- Semantic invariant: Shared channels for movement and speech are converted from public access into mechanisms of obstruction and abuse.
- Surface relation: direct; 29:29 names cutting the road and wrongdoing in the forum, while 29:38 names obstruction from the path despite perceptive capacity.
- Surprising reach: The passage links highway robbery, worked tracks, water access, public council, loud circulation, and the restraint or excess of the tongue.

#### Subchannel A. Cutting the Worked Road
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Travelers move on a known track toward resources until an obstacle, seizure, or exhausted means interrupts passage.
- Active motifs: crossing a distance or water `quranic:root_001240:B003/m01`; a stranded traveler whose means fail `quranic:root_001240:B006/m01`; highway seizure and obstruction `quranic:root_001240:B023/m01`; an extended traversable road `quranic:root_000672:B001/m01`; road users and travelers `quranic:root_000672:B002/m01`; preventing and turning away `quranic:root_000848:B001/m01`; a route leading to water `quranic:root_000848:B004/m01`; a worked road `quranic:root_001046:B011/m01`; walkers on foot `quranic:root_000546:B003/m01`
- Ayah anchors: ق ط ع 29:29; س ب ل 29:29,38; ص د د 29:38; ع م ل 29:38; ر ج ل 29:29.
- Synthesis: Cutting the road is a full disruption of public mobility: a known worked track and its resource route are blocked until the traveler becomes "cut off" through lost mount, food, or money. The lexical assembly makes obstruction an attack on the infrastructure that lets strangers continue.

### 10. P3 (29:28-44) House, Household, and Failed Support
- Semantic invariant: A dwelling is viable only when fitted structure, inhabited relations, and load-bearing supports work together.
- Surface relation: direct; 29:31-35 repeatedly distinguishes town and household, while 29:38 names dwellings and 29:41 tests protectors through the spider's house.
- Surprising reach: The spider comparison opens into joinery, tent supports, stitched panels, household membership, bodily ribs, hidden pain, and timber-eating decay.

#### Subchannel A. Fitted Dwelling Structure
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A shelter is assembled from a standing support, a fitted socket or joint, transverse members, and joined wall material.
- Active motifs: dwelling and nightly shelter `quranic:root_000166:B001/m01`; crosspieces carrying a house-post `quranic:root_001222:B008/m01`; fitted joint, socket, or door fitting `quranic:root_000347:B011/m01`; standing tool or structural member `quranic:root_001273:B012/m01`; residence as a stable place `quranic:root_000726:B002/m01`; joining two skins or wall-halves `quranic:root_000121:B006/m01`
- Ayah anchors: ب ي ت 29:41; ق ر ي 29:31,34; ح ق ق 29:44; ق و م 29:28-30,35-36; س ك ن 29:38; ب ص ر 29:38.
- Synthesis: The house is a fitted mechanism, not merely an enclosure. Posts, crosspieces, sockets, and joined skins distribute load; the spider-house pressures the comparison by offering the name of shelter without dependable structural work.

#### Subchannel B. Household, Inhabitants, and Welcome
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Kin, residents, and guests are gathered by a dwelling whose social function is recognition, qualification, and safe reception.
- Active motifs: kin and co-inhabitants `quranic:root_000064:B001/m01`; qualification for a role or place `quranic:root_000064:B003/m01`; inhabited familiarity `quranic:root_000064:B004/m01`; household and dependents `quranic:root_000166:B002/m01`; residents of a house `quranic:root_000726:B003/m01`; what gives inward familiarity and calm `quranic:root_000726:B004/m01`; gathered town and townspeople `quranic:root_001222:B001/m01`; hospitality through gathered food `quranic:root_001222:B003/m01`
- Ayah anchors: ء ه ل 29:31-34; ب ي ت 29:41; س ك ن 29:38; ق ر ي 29:31,34.
- Synthesis: "Household" names more than blood descent: it is a relation of residence, qualification, familiarity, and welcome. The repeated negotiation over Lot's family therefore asks who truly belongs inside a protective social dwelling.

### 12. P3 (29:28-44) Examples Made Legible
- Semantic invariant: A truth becomes cognitively usable when it is marked, modeled, and impressed into a form that can restrain mistaken judgment.
- Surface relation: direct; 29:35 and 29:44 call remains and creation signs, while 29:41-43 explicitly gives a likeness, strikes examples, and limits understanding to knowers.
- Surprising reach: Parable-making behaves like heraldry, model-making, molding, and tethering: cognition is given a durable form and then held to it.

#### Subchannel A. Mark, Landmark, and Inward Sight
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A distinguishing trace remains in the landscape, directs attention, and becomes inward recognition rather than bare looking.
- Active motifs: manifest sign `quranic:root_000074:B003/m01`; retained route-guiding landmark `quranic:root_001040:B002/m02`; emergence into visible clarity `quranic:root_000170:B004/m01`; gestural clarification `quranic:root_000170:B005/m02`; inward sight and evidentiary understanding `quranic:root_000121:B002/m01`; retained trace after departure `quranic:root_000180:B002/m01`
- Ayah anchors: ء ي ي 29:35,44; ع ل م 29:28,32,41-43; ب ي ن 29:35,38-39; ب ص ر 29:38; ت ر ك 29:35.
- Synthesis: The "clear sign" left from the town is a trace that functions like a landmark: it first catches sight, then opens meaning, then becomes inward evidence. Remains are useful because they preserve direction after the event itself has departed.

## Cross-Pericope and Whole-Surah Channels

### X3. Rescue as Separation and Relocation
- Pericopes: P2 (29:14-27) and P3 (29:28-44).
- Bridge: repeated scene signature and causal sequence; an enclosing threat is followed by an external rescuer, separation from danger, rapid movement, and relocation beyond the threatened field.
- Canonical placements: P2 Parent 7 / Subchannel B, "Extraction to Flood-Safe Height"; P2 S6, "Fire Attack and Extractive Rescue"; P3 S8, "Guests, Provision, and Extraction"; P3 S9, "Constricted Reach Opened by Rescue".
- Synthesis: Flood, fire, a hostile town, and constricted personal reach differ as threat media, but rescue keeps the same operation: it does not merely cancel harm in place; it opens an exit and changes the endangered party's location. The rescuing role therefore progresses from preserving companions to extracting a target and finally enabling a household whose own reach has failed.

### X4. Route Agency From Following to Guided Striving
- Pericopes: P1 (29:1-13), P2 (29:14-27), P3 (29:28-44), P4 (29:45-56), and P5 (29:57-69).
- Bridge: repeated scene signature with role progression; traveler, route, directional authority, obstruction or difficult terrain, and terminus recur as follower, migrant, blocked traveler, displaced servant, and guided striver.
- Canonical placements: P1 Parent 3 / Subchannel A, "Worked Track, Foot Travelers, and Road Gear"; P2 S5, "The Migrant as Stranger in Habitable Terrain"; P3 Parent 9 / Subchannel A, "Cutting the Worked Road"; P4 S14, "Spacious Earth as Ground for Service"; P5 Parent 23 / Subchannels A-B, "Exertion Until the Route Appears" and "Hard Ground, Mountain Center, and Supported Gait".
- Synthesis: The surah's road sequence develops moral agency through changing travel conditions. A path can recruit passive following, migration can sever inherited location, hostile power can block public passage, breadth can restore room for service, and exertion can culminate in disclosed routes. Guidance is thus neither bare direction nor frictionless motion; it is the restoration of traversable agency under pressure.

### X5. From Assay Mark to Inward Testimony
- Pericopes: P1 (29:1-13), P2 (29:14-27), P3 (29:28-44), and P4 (29:45-56).
- Bridge: causal sequence and role progression; pressure leaves a distinguishing mark, the mark becomes a visible sign, inspection converts it into an intelligible model, and learned observers retain it as testimony.
- Canonical placements: P1 Parent 1 / Subchannel A, "Fire-Assay and the Mark of Sound Material"; P2 Parent 5 / Subchannel B, "Sign, Banner, and Deliberate Inspection"; P3 Parent 12 / Subchannels A-B, "Mark, Landmark, and Inward Sight" and "Struck Model and Rational Restraint"; P4 Parent 13 / Subchannel C, "Signs Archived in Breasts".
- Synthesis: Evidence progresses through material, spatial, cognitive, and testimonial roles without changing its invariant function of distinction. What pressure first marks can later guide attention, discipline comparison, enter knowledge, and be produced as witness. The whole sequence explains why seeing alone is insufficient: a sign completes its work only when its distinction survives into judgment and testimony.

### X7. From Interior Claim to Attributed Public Record
- Pericopes: P1 (29:1-13), P2 (29:14-27), P3 (29:28-44), P4 (29:45-56), and P5 (29:57-69).
- Bridge: causal sequence and role progression; an inward source becomes utterance, dispatch, public circulation, ordered inscription, remembered testimony, and finally an attribution that can be tested as true or fabricated.
- Canonical placements: P1 Parent 1 / Subchannels B-C, "Spoken Profession and the Unvoiced Interior" and "Injury Amplified as Public Circulation"; P2 Parent 5 / Subchannel A, "Dispatch, Carriage, and Clear Delivery"; P3 Parent 9 / Subchannel B, "The Abusive Public Council"; P4 Parent 13 / Subchannels A-B, "Hidden Delivery and Durable Inscription" and "Following Voice and Recovered Presence"; P4 S12, "Provenance by Line, Hand, and False Attribution"; P5 Parent 22 / Subchannel B, "Turning Away From the Proper Fit".
- Synthesis: The communication chain begins before speech and ends after reception. Intention supplies provenance; delivery gives the claim a carrier; public repetition can clarify or distort; inscription and remembrance stabilize it; line, hand, testimony, and fit test its attribution. The bridge makes truth a property of the whole chain rather than of fluent wording alone.
