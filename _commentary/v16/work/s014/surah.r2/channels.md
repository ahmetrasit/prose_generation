# s014 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. P1 (14:1-15): Message Made Visible and Traversable
- Semantic invariant: Guidance converts what is concealed into something publicly perceptible, linguistically transmissible, and spatially followable.
- Surface relation: direct; 14:1-5, 9-12: كِتَابٌ، أَنزَلْنَاهُ، لِتُخْرِجَ، الظُّلُمَاتِ، النُّورِ، صِرَاطِ، سَبِيلِ، بِلِسَانِ، لِيُبَيِّنَ، أَرْسَلْنَا، رُسُلُهُمْ، أُرْسِلْتُمْ، تَدْعُونَنَا، سُلْطَانٍ مُبِينٍ، هَدَانَا سُبُلَنَا.
- Surprising reach: Lexical branches materialize revelation as extraction from cover, road engineering, a route to water, a public thoroughfare, oral articulation, and a quietly imparted report.

- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A written and delivered message draws people out of concealment and makes its content appear under illumination.

### 2. P1 (14:1-15): Coercive Extraction Reversed into Settlement
- Semantic invariant: Human power expels, strips, and threatens; divine deliverance separates people from that pressure and reallocates land and outcome.
- Surface relation: direct; 14:6, 13-15: أَنجَاكُم، يَسُومُونَكُمْ، يُذَبِّحُونَ، يَسْتَحْيُونَ، بَلَاءٌ، لَنُخْرِجَنَّكُمْ، أَرْضِنَا، لَتَعُودُنَّ، لَنُهْلِكَنَّ، لَنُسْكِنَنَّكُمُ الْأَرْضَ، وَعِيدِ، اسْتَفْتَحُوا، خَابَ، جَبَّارٍ عَنِيدٍ.
- Surprising reach: The interval turns political persecution into a set of physical operations: slaughter, preservation, stripping, forced movement, resettlement, and finally the opening of a judicial passage.

### 3. P1 (14:1-15): Contested Delegation and Entrusted Endurance
- Semantic invariant: Messenger authority is delegated rather than possessed, so refusal cannot be answered by autonomous force but by evidence, permission, trust, and endurance.
- Surface relation: direct; 14:9-12: بِالْبَيِّنَاتِ، فَرَدُّوا أَيْدِيَهُمْ فِي أَفْوَاهِهِمْ، شَكٍّ مُرِيبٍ، بَشَرٌ مِثْلُنَا، بِسُلْطَانٍ مُبِينٍ، بِإِذْنِ اللَّهِ، فَلْيَتَوَكَّلِ، هَدَانَا سُبُلَنَا، لَنَصْبِرَنَّ، آذَيْتُمُونَا.
- Surprising reach: Refusal becomes bodily recoil and invasive doubt, while reliance becomes the transfer of a matter to a sufficient guardian rather than passive optimism.

### 4. P2 (14:16-27): Blocked Intake and Suspended Death

It supports and materializes P3's account of offspring as a received gift, while usefully pressuring P1's child-slaughter as the violent cancellation of a prior womb-to-hand process of protection, emergence, and care.
