# s010 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. P1 - Reality Made Legible
- Semantic invariant: Reality becomes intelligible by being inscribed, distinguished, and measured.
- Surface relation: direct; 10:1 (ءَايَٰتُ، كِتَٰبِ، حَكِيمِ), 10:5-6 (شَّمْسَ، ضِيَآءً، قَمَرَ، نُورًا، حِسَابَ، سِّنِينَ، يُفَصِّلُ، ٱخْتِلَٰفِ).
- Surprising reach: The written page and the alternating sky function as parallel instruments of exact disclosure.

#### Subchannel A. Inscription as Precise Disclosure
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A written object presents visible signs whose parts are disclosed with wisdom and exact construction.
- Active motifs: written text (quranic:root_001283:B002/m01); visible sign (quranic:root_000074:B003/m01); accurate wisdom (quranic:root_000348:B003/m01); exact construction (quranic:root_000348:B004/m01); articulated detail (quranic:root_001159:B013/m01).
- Ayah anchors: 10:1 (ءَايَٰتُ، كِتَٰبِ، حَكِيمِ); 10:5 (يُفَصِّلُ).
- Synthesis: The opening treats scripture not as an undifferentiated message but as an artifact whose signs have been written, made visible, accurately judged, and fitted together. The lexical imagery of construction and articulation makes wisdom a property of how disclosure is formed.

#### Subchannel B. Celestial Calibration
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Sun, moon, radiance, and light are assigned magnitudes and alternating periods from which counted time is recovered.
- Active motifs: sun (quranic:root_000818:B001/m01); diffused radiance (quranic:root_000919:B001/m01); moon (quranic:root_001255:B001/m01); illumination (quranic:root_001564:B001/m01); measured limit (quranic:root_001205:B001/m01); enumeration (quranic:root_000989:B001/m01); year (quranic:root_000751:B008/m01); succession by alternation (quranic:root_000433:B006/m01); night (quranic:root_001392:B001/m01); daylight opening (quranic:root_001559:B002/m01).

- Synthesis: The sky operates as a calibrated display: distinct luminous bodies, assigned measures, counted years, and the succession of night and day form a legible temporal mechanism. Its latent kinship with inscription lies in ordered distinction rather than mere visibility.

### 2. P1 - Transmission Held to Its Course
- Semantic invariant: A received discourse remains authoritative by preserving its sequence, source, and form.
- Surface relation: direct; 10:2 (أَوْحَيْ), 10:15-17 (تُتْلَىٰ، أَتَّبِعُ، يُوحَىٰٓ، بَدِّلْ، ٱفْتَرَىٰ، كَذِبًا).
- Surprising reach: Fidelity is rendered both as following a recited sequence and as refusing to refashion an artifact.

#### Subchannel A. Received Sequence and Recited Following
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Speech is received from beyond the speaker, recited in sequence, and followed rather than generated from the speaker's own position.
- Active motifs: divine communication (quranic:root_001633:B004/m01); reading and recitation (quranic:root_001210:B002/m01); serial following (quranic:root_000186:B001/m01); following behind (quranic:root_000175:B001/m01); receiving speech (quranic:root_001372:B011/m01); verbal clarification (quranic:root_000170:B005/m01).

#### Subchannel B. Substitution and Counterfeit Form

- Active motifs: one thing standing in another's place (quranic:root_000095:B001/m01); alteration of form (quranic:root_000095:B002/m01); changing a thing for another (quranic:root_001119:B003/m01); fabricated falsehood (quranic:root_001150:B003/m01); contradiction of truth (quranic:root_001290:B001/m01); deceptive diversion (quranic:root_000682:B002/m01).
