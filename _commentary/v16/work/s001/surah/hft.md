# HFT: earlier activation hypotheses, per focus ayah of surah 1

# Focus 1:1

## operative designation
- reading: A portable operation that places the next act under the raised divine designation and makes mercy its governing manner.
- mechanism: The opening does more than identify a speaker's deity: it places an otherwise unstated act under a raised divine designation and specifies mercy as the designation's operative manner. The missing verb lets the formula travel into whatever act follows.
- trace:
  - 1:1 **بِسْمِ** س م و B005: Name as raised designation makes ism an active heading elevated over the unnamed act, not a neutral label.
  - 1:1 **ٱللَّهِ** ء ل ه B002: The fixed divine name in invocation formulas supplies the determinate addressee and the formulaic force of the opening.
  - 1:1 **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** ر ح م B001: Mercy and tenderness, doubled in two forms, supply the manner in which the raised designation governs the act.

## legible mercy mark
- reading: Marking the act so that its divine affiliation becomes legible as mercy in the act's effects.
- mechanism: The formula can function like an identifying impression placed upon an act. The mark is not merely ownership signage: discerning observation should be able to read mercy in the act's visible trace.
- trace:
  - 1:1 **بِسْمِ** س م و B001: A visible identifying mark turns ism into an impressed sign by which the act's affiliation becomes recognizable.
  - 1:1 **بِسْمِ** س م و B002: A sign read through discerning observation makes the mark evidential: its claimed source is tested by what appears in the act.
  - 1:1 **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** ر ح م B001: Mercy and tenderness provide the visible signature that the identifying mark is expected to disclose.

## generative enclosure
- reading: A beginning inside a protected, kin-like, generative enclosure constituted by the divine name.
- mechanism: The formula spatializes beginning as entry into a merciful enclosure. Cover, womb, and kinship yield a protected relational interior from which the unnamed act can emerge, so mercy is generative containment rather than an external ornament.
- trace:
  - 1:1 **بِسْمِ** س م و B004: The overhead cover supplies an encompassing upper boundary beneath which the act begins.
  - 1:1 **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** ر ح م B003: The womb supplies a protected generative interior in which something is formed before emerging.
  - 1:1 **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** ر ح م B002: The kinship bond makes that enclosure relational, joining the one who begins to a sustaining tie rather than an impersonal shelter.

  - 1:2 **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** ع ل م B002: A distinguishing trace that guides toward its referent closes the cycle: the cultivated effect points back to the named source.

# Focus 1:2

- reading: A considered verdict that the worshipped Lord proves praiseworthy across the full scope of lordship.
- mechanism: Hamd is both praise opposed to blame and the finding of something satisfactory after testing. Joined to divine lordship, it reads as an evaluative verdict grounded in the Lord's relation to all worlds, not as unexamined applause.
