# s004 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. Stewardship That Ends in Release (P1, 4:1-18)
- Semantic invariant: A vulnerable person is held under accountable care only until demonstrated capacity makes transfer possible.
- Surface relation: direct; guardianship and property transfer are anchored by `رقيبا` (4:1), `قيما` (4:5), and `فادفعوا إليهم أموالهم` (4:6).
- Surprising reach: The same lexical field makes care neither passive benevolence nor permanent control: it is a watched office whose success is measured by its own termination.

#### Subchannel A. The Dependent Inside a Guarded Charge
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A guardian stands over a dependent's material life, cultivating and preserving what cannot yet be independently managed.
- Active motifs: protective watching `quranic:root_000584:B002/m01`; standing care and wardship `quranic:root_001273:B004/m01`; corrective cultivation `quranic:root_000532:B002/m01`; dependents whose neglect incurs guilt `quranic:root_000365:B003/m01`.
- Ayah anchors: `ر ق ب` -> `رقيبا` (4:1); `ق و م` -> `قيما` (4:5); `ر ب ب` -> `ربكم` (4:1); `ح و ب` -> `حوبا` (4:2).
- Synthesis: Watching, standing care, and cultivation form a custodial scene around those whose weakness makes their neglect culpable. Property administration is therefore figured as personal guardianship: the charge must be sustained, watched, and brought toward fitness.

#### Subchannel B. Capacity Tested, Property Handed Over
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: Maturity is tested, competent direction becomes visible, and the held property is transferred before witnesses.
- Active motifs: testing that reveals condition `quranic:root_000153:B002/m01`; reaching maturity `quranic:root_000151:B001/m01`; sound disposal of a ward's property `quranic:root_000565:B002/m01`; delivery into another's possession `quranic:root_000480:B002/m01`; knowledgeable attestation `quranic:root_000822:B002/m01`.
- Ayah anchors: `ب ل و` -> `وابتلوا` (4:6); `ب ل غ` -> `بلغوا` (4:6); `ر ش د` -> `رشدا` (4:6); `د ف ع` -> `فادفعوا`, `دفعتم` (4:6); `ش ه د` -> `فاشهدوا` (4:6).
- Synthesis: The sequence is procedural rather than merely chronological. Trial discloses condition, maturity supplies the threshold, right direction authorizes transfer, and testimony closes the accountable handoff.

### 2. Estate Converted into Legible Shares (P1, 4:1-18)
- Semantic invariant: Death's undivided remainder is transformed into determinate claims through kinship and measure.
- Surface relation: direct; `مما ترك` and named kin (4:7-12) feed into `القسمة`, `نصيبا`, and explicit fractional terms (4:7-12).
- Surprising reach: Kinship appears not only as affection or descent but as the routing structure through which an otherwise inert remainder becomes socially addressable.

- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: An estate passes from the deceased through offspring, near kin, and collateral kin.

### 4. Covenant Gives Intimacy Its Form (P2, 4:19-35)
- Semantic invariant: Intimacy is constituted through both a binding relation and embodied access; neither operation substitutes for the other.
- Surface relation: direct; the `ميثقا غليظا` and marriage vocabulary of 4:21-25 sit beside `أفضى`, `دخلتم`, and `تجمعوا` in 4:21-24.
- Surprising reach: Knotting and entry disclose two axes of the same relation: public obligation supplies durability, while bodily passage supplies consummation.
