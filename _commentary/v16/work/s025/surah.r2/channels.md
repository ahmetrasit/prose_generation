# s025 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. P1 25:1-20 - Authored Speech and Disclosed Truth
- Semantic invariant: A communicative artifact is defined by how it is inscribed, transmitted, attributed, and opened to knowledge.
- Surface relation: direct; 25:1 and 25:6 present a descended warning grounded in knowledge, while 25:4-5 contest its authorship as fabricated, copied, and dictated speech.
- Surprising reach: The accusation and the revelation occupy the same workshop of rows, dictation, attribution, and disclosure, but differ in whether those operations turn away from truth or make a hidden distinction manifest.

#### Subchannel A. Inscribed Rows and Dictated Copy
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Speech is aligned into written rows, copied into a document, dictated to a receiver, and exposed as an open written instrument.
- Active motifs: inscribed row `quranic:root_000704:B001/m01`; copied written document `quranic:root_001283:B002/m01`; dictation to writer or hearer `quranic:root_001446:B004/m01`; open written decree `quranic:root_001503:B009/m01`.
- Ayah anchors: 25:5 (س ط ر، ك ت ب، م ل و); 25:3 (ن ش ر).
- Synthesis: The charge of "legends" is materially staged as a production line: ordered rows become a copied document through dictation, while the lexical reach of ن ش ر places the result in the form of an opened, unsealed writing. The scene sharpens the dispute from vague borrowing into a claim about textual manufacture and circulation.

#### Subchannel B. Fabrication and Pre-shaped Testimony
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A statement is diverted from truth, invented, embellished before utterance, and attributed to someone who did not say it.
- Active motifs: lie diverted from truth `quranic:root_000041:B002/m01`; invented false charge `quranic:root_001150:B003/m01`; false testimony `quranic:root_000654:B002/m01`; speech pre-shaped and embellished `quranic:root_000654:B006/m01`; attribution of unsaid words `quranic:root_001272:B005/m01`; fabricated discourse `quranic:root_000434:B007/m01`; disorderly legends `quranic:root_000704:B002/m01`.
- Ayah anchors: 25:4 (ء ف ك، ف ر ي، ز و ر، ق و ل); 25:5 (س ط ر، ق و ل); 25:2-3 (خ ل ق).
- Synthesis: These motifs form a social mechanism of false authorship rather than a loose catalogue of lies. Material is first turned from its proper direction, then invented and cosmetically straightened, and finally assigned to a speaker; the creation root adds the unsettling counter-image of discourse being manufactured like an object.

#### Subchannel C. Descent Opens the Hidden Distinction
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: A warning is delivered downward from one who knows what is concealed, making a discriminating boundary available to its hearers.
- Active motifs: delivery downward `quranic:root_001492:B002/m01`; knowledge by disclosure `quranic:root_001040:B001/m01`; concealed matter `quranic:root_000697:B001/m01`; separation that clarifies `quranic:root_001148:B001/m01`; warning that awakens caution `quranic:root_001488:B001/m01`.
- Ayah anchors: 25:1, 25:6-7 (ن ز ل); 25:1, 25:6 (ع ل م); 25:6 (س ر ر); 25:1 (ف ر ق، ن ذ ر); 25:7 (ن ذ ر).
- Synthesis: Descent is not merely motion from above: it transfers hidden knowledge into an operative distinction. The warning completes the process by converting disclosure into alertness, directly opposing the preceding scene of manufactured attribution.

### 2. P1 25:1-20 - Marked Routes and Failed Turning
- Semantic invariant: Guidance is spatialized as a traversable route whose forks and landmarks must be perceived and whose loss disables purposeful movement.
- Surface relation: direct; 25:9 and 25:17 name the lost way, while 25:19 denies any power to turn the outcome aside.
- Surprising reach: The route is not empty ground but an engineered and social corridor, furnished with marks, forks, aligned approaches, and travelers.

#### Subchannel A. The Furnished Road
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A traveler moves along an extended road, reads its landmarks, and negotiates a visible fork where another way branches off.

#### Subchannel B. Losing the Route and the Capacity to Turn

- Synthesis: The sequence distinguishes two operations that a broad "misguidance" label would collapse. First the intended course is lost; then practical agency contracts until neither forward movement nor defensive redirection remains available.
