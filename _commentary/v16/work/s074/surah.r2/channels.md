# s074 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. P1: Emergence from covering into commissioned standing
- Semantic invariant: A concealed or inert figure is brought upright, committed to an entrusted task, and kept there.
- Surface relation: direct; 74:1 `المدثر`, 74:2 `قم` and `فأنذر`, and 74:3-7 the sequence of imperatives.
- Surprising reach: The commission is not only awakening; its lexical machinery includes guardianship, structural support, and repeated public summons.

#### Subchannel A. The wrapped sleeper rises into the warning
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A sleeper hidden under an outer covering stands bodily, resolves on action, and voices danger.
- Active motifs: outer covering (`quranic:root_000459:B002/m01`); hidden sleeper (`quranic:root_000459:B006/m01`); bodily standing (`quranic:root_001273:B002/m01`); resolved undertaking (`quranic:root_001273:B003/m01`); danger-announcement (`quranic:root_001488:B001/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 74:1 `المدثر` (د ث ر); 74:2 `قم` (ق و م), `فأنذر` (ن ذ ر).
- Synthesis: The covering supplies a literal enclosure and the sleeper supplies inertia; `قم` reverses both through upright posture and deliberate undertaking. Warning is therefore the outward operation of a body and will newly made present.

#### Subchannel B. Standing becomes guardianship and sustained charge
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Authority entrusts a charge to one who maintains, supports, and patiently guarantees it.
- Active motifs: care and governance (`quranic:root_001273:B004/m01`); sustaining structure and livelihood (`quranic:root_001273:B009/m01`); lordship and ownership (`quranic:root_000532:B001/m01`); gradual nurture and completion (`quranic:root_000532:B002/m01`); self-restraint (`quranic:root_000840:B001/m01`); guarantor who remains with the group (`quranic:root_000840:B003/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 74:2 `قم` (ق و م); 74:3 and 74:7 `ربك`/`لربك` (ر ب ب); 74:7 `فاصبر` (ص ب ر).
- Synthesis: Standing is extended from posture into maintenance: the charge has a sovereign source, must be brought toward completion, and requires a guarantor-like endurance that does not leave its company.

#### Subchannel C. The warning is a reiterated call
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A call is renewed until a sign is made audible and publicly recognizable.
- Active motifs: repeated summons (`quranic:root_000209:B004/m01`); vocative call to near or far (`quranic:root_000074:B008/m01`); warning that awakens caution (`quranic:root_001488:B001/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 74:4 `ثيابك` (ث و ب); 74:16 `لآياتنا` (ء ي ي); 74:2 `فأنذر` (ن ذ ر).
- Synthesis: The garment root also carries the reiterated call, while the sign root carries vocative address. Together they materialize warning as a signal that must be sounded again, not merely possessed.

### 2. P1: Purification as surface work and moral separation
- Semantic invariant: A compromised surface or state is restored by washing, removal, separation, or a new protective finish.
- Surface relation: direct; 74:4 purifies garments and 74:5 commands separation from `الرجز`.
- Surprising reach: Garment, self, rusted metal, worn fabric, speech, migration, and fragrant coatings all test what it means for cleansing to reach below appearance.

#### Subchannel A. Garment cleansing reaches the self and act
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: Clothing is washed with a cleansing medium while the person and conduct signified by it are cleared of defect.
- Active motifs: garment (`quranic:root_000209:B003/m01`); garment standing for the self (`quranic:root_000209:B003/m02`); removal of dirt and defect (`quranic:root_000953:B001/m01`); washing with water (`quranic:root_000953:B003/m01`); a medium that cleanses another (`quranic:root_000953:B004/m01`); purification of self and work (`quranic:root_000953:B005/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 74:4 `ثيابك` (ث و ب), `فطهر` (ط ه ر).
- Synthesis: The scene begins with cloth but does not stop there. The garment can denote the person, and the cleansing operation can act on material, inward state, and public work without collapsing those distinct roles.

#### Subchannel B. Departure severs contaminating motion and speech
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A person breaks contact with a disturbing field by leaving its place, practice, and corrupt speech.
- Active motifs: separation and abandonment (`quranic:root_001578:B001/m01`); migration from one abode to another (`quranic:root_001578:B002/m01`); obscene or rejected speech (`quranic:root_001578:B003/m01`); delirious ungoverned speech (`quranic:root_001578:B004/m01`); convulsive repeated motion (`quranic:root_000542:B001/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 74:5 `الرجز` (ر ج ز), `فاهجر` (ه ج ر).
- Synthesis: `فاهجر` is a spatial exit, a social break, and a refusal of speech that has lost moral or cognitive control. The disturbing motion of `الرجز` makes separation an escape from an active destabilizing field.

#### Subchannel C. A cover can protect, conceal, or decay
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Layers accumulate over a surface until washing or polishing must distinguish protection from concealment and neglect.
- Active motifs: erased trace under accumulated cover (`quranic:root_000459:B004/m01`); grime and sword-rust from lack of polishing (`quranic:root_000459:B005/m01`); physical covering (`quranic:root_001307:B001/m01`); worn cloth stripped of nap (`quranic:root_000434:B009/m01`); garment layer (`quranic:root_000209:B003/m01`); restored cleanness (`quranic:root_000953:B001/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 74:1 `المدثر` (د ث ر); 74:10 `الكافرين` (ك ف ر); 74:11 `خلقت` (خ ل ق); 74:4 `ثيابك` (ث و ب), `فطهر` (ط ه ر).
- Synthesis: Covering is semantically double-edged. It may shelter, but it may also erase evidence, retain grime, or leave metal and fabric degraded; purification thus becomes the discriminating removal of the wrong layer.

#### Subchannel D. Cleansing ends in a fragrant visible finish
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A cleaned body or object is coated with scent and color after corrupt residue has been removed.
- Active motifs: perfumed coating (`quranic:root_000434:B010/m01`); camphor fragrance (`quranic:root_001307:B011/m01`); saffron coloring (`quranic:root_000606:B007/m01`).
- Ayah anchors: 74:11 `خلقت` (خ ل ق); 74:10 `الكافرين` (ك ف ر); 74:17 `سأرهقه` (ر ه ق).
- Synthesis: The three coatings supply a positive counterpart to grime and rust: surface treatment can disclose care through scent and color rather than hide damage.

### 3. P1: Water prepared, caught, and returned

- Surface relation: indirect; roots at 74:3-8, 74:10-12, and 74:18 supply the latent hydrological mechanism.
- Surprising reach: The imperative sequence is pressured by a complete material system in which gathering, directing, retaining, and cleansing are separate operations.

#### Subchannel C. Layered cloud becomes storm and continuing supply
- Reading type: latent/lexical

- Active motifs: banked rain cloud (`quranic:root_000532:B008/m01`); layered white cloud (`quranic:root_000840:B010/m01`); thunder, cloud motion, and enduring wind (`quranic:root_000542:B001/m02`); flood and river extension (`quranic:root_001407:B003/m01`).

- Synthesis: Endurance is imaged as atmospheric layering: one mass holds over another until disturbance releases an extended supply below.

### 4. P1: Making movement carryable
- Semantic invariant: Travel succeeds when rider, load, animal gait, equipment, and provisions are brought into alignment.
- Surface relation: indirect; 74:1-2, 74:5, 74:8-10, 74:12, and 74:14-18 activate the component roots.
- Surprising reach: The passage's commands acquire the physical pressure of mounting, tethering, balancing, guiding, provisioning, and treating an animal that can fail under load.

- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A rider mounts, the animal is tethered, and a weighted fitting corrects the tilt of the carried load.

#### Subchannel D. Departure requires time, route, and stored provision

### S16. Whole-surah: A thrown stick uproots an upright game-piece
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A wooden piece is set upright as a target, a bat or second stick is cast against it, and the attempt either uproots the piece or passes beyond its mark.

- Synthesis: The hard-day root supplies a rule-bound scene of exposed balance, impact, displacement, and failure to hit. It materializes the surah's standing and turning vocabulary as vulnerability under a decisive blow, while the possibility of an overshot target sharpens the difference between calculated play and the inescapable summons of 74:8-10.

### S17. Whole-surah: Menstrual cessation is completed by ritual washing

- Synthesis: The rite usefully pressures the garment command of 74:4: purification is not cosmetic surface improvement but a timed bodily transition completed through an enacted wash. The primary instruction thereby acquires a threshold between compromised state and restored participation.

### S18. Whole-surah: A foreign scion is joined until the host accepts it

- Scene or process: A branch from one tree is introduced into another, joined to the host, and judged successful when the living union takes hold.

This reframes feeding the needy as provision that must be received and take hold, and it pressures the earlier desire for increase by making growth depend on a successful relation between donor material and host.

### S19. Whole-surah: Guests voice needs and a host answers them
- Reading type: mixed

### S20. Whole-surah: Camel breeding, loss, and milk control form a husbandry cycle

### S21. Whole-surah: A captain, planked hull, and rudder negotiate a hidden shoal

## Cross-Pericope Channels

### X1. P1 -> P2 -> P3: Commissioned standing reverses into evasive flight
- Pericopes: P1 (74:1-18), P2 (74:19-38), and P3 (74:39-56).
- Bridge: role progression and reversal; directed bodily motion moves from commanded orientation, through accountable choice, into oblique avoidance and uncontrolled escape.
- Canonical placements: P1 1A, "The wrapped sleeper rises into the warning"; P2 18A, "Advance and delay are bodily directions"; P3 26A, "Turning away is a sideways bodily operation"; P3 27D, "Flight deforms gait, skin, and nerves."
- Synthesis: P1 gives upright motion to the commissioned warner, P2 transfers directional responsibility to every potential responder, and P3 shows refusal degrading from a chosen side-turn into a body no longer moving with composure. The bridge is therefore a reversal of agency within one orientation signature, not a general motif of travel.

### X2. P1 -> P2 -> P3: The maker of a verdict becomes the subject of certainty
- Pericopes: P1 (74:1-18), P2 (74:19-38), and P3 (74:39-56).
- Bridge: causal sequence and role reversal; fabricated thought becomes premature judgment, while terminal certainty reverses evaluator and evaluated.
- Canonical placements: P1 9D, "Thought can manufacture a defamatory account"; P2 11A, "Estimation examines its own projected outcome"; P2 11B, "Prematurity corrupts judgment"; P3 25A, "Certainty arrives as the terminal event."
- Synthesis: The sequence begins with cognitive capacities redirected into fabrication, passes through an estimate that mistakes an early sign for a finished result, and ends when certainty closes the interval that human deliberation abused. The person who tried to fix the message's status is finally fixed by an account no longer open to revision.

### X3. P1 -> P2 -> P3: Covering changes role after disclosure
- Pericopes: P1 (74:1-18), P2 (74:19-38), and P3 (74:39-56).
- Bridge: causal sequence and reversal; a layer first conceals or preserves defect, an assay exposes condition, and forgiveness then covers a known offense and its consequence.
- Canonical placements: P1 2C, "A cover can protect, conceal, or decay"; P2 13C, "Furnace heat tests and brands"; P3 28C, "Forgiveness covers the offense and its aftereffect."
- Synthesis: The invariant is a surface placed between a bearer and what would reveal or harm it. Before disclosure, that surface can retain grime and erase evidence; after testing has made condition legible, covering can become mercy rather than concealment. The moral reversal depends on the intervening assay, so these scenes form a sequence rather than a loose covering theme.

### X4. P1 -> P2 -> P3: Retained water becomes socially delivered food
- Pericopes: P1 (74:1-18), P2 (74:19-38), and P3 (74:39-56).
- Bridge: causal sequence; catchment, distribution, cultivation, maturation, digestion, and feeding are successive roles in one provision chain.
- Canonical placements: P1 3A, "Carved catchments retain arriving water"; P2 19A, "Channels and wells secure a settlement"; P2 19B, "Immature palms move toward fruit"; P3 23D, "Fruit, rain, and digestion make food available"; P3 23B, "Feeding gives the poor a place in the household economy."
- Synthesis: Water is first kept from loss, then routed into settled use, converted by growth into mature food, and finally made bodily and social provision. The confession in P3 concerns the omitted terminal transfer, which makes the earlier hydrological and agricultural operations ethically incomplete rather than merely productive.

### X5. P1 -> P2 -> P3: Expected return contracts into an untransferable account
- Pericopes: P1 (74:1-18), P2 (74:19-38), and P3 (74:39-56).
- Bridge: role progression and reversal; giver, acquirer, pledged self, advocate, and beneficiary occupy successive positions in an attempted transfer of value.
- Canonical placements: P1 5A, "A gift is ruined when converted into leverage"; P2 18D, "The self becomes collateral for acquisition"; P3 25B, "An advocate adds a second voice to a need"; P3 25C, "Joining can enlarge property, kin, or yield."
- Synthesis: P1 corrupts an outward gift when it is engineered to return as surplus, P2 makes gain bind the acquiring self, and P3 tests whether one more person or resource can still produce benefit. The reversal is exact: the actor who tried to make generosity increase his account reaches a state in which addition itself no longer transfers value.

### X6. P1 -> P2 -> P3: The hunting apparatus culminates in the audience as quarry
- Pericopes: P1 (74:1-18), P2 (74:19-38), and P3 (74:39-56).
- Bridge: repeated scene signature and role progression; lure, concealment, pursuer, quarry, capture mechanism, strike, and inspection recur while the human audience moves into the prey role.
- Canonical placements: P1 S3, "Bird life moves from nest repair to baited capture"; P2 20A, "The hunter approaches behind concealment"; P2 20B, "Decoy, trap, raptor, and talon complete the mechanism"; P2 20C, "Horn, blade, and mark register the strike"; P3 27A, "Donkeys flee a pursuing force"; P3 27B, "Pursuit operates through sound, darkness, and inspection."
- Synthesis: P1 establishes induced capture through a baited animal, P2 fills every operative role of the hunt, and P3 transfers the completed signature onto those fleeing the reminder. This is a participant reversal within the same pursuit mechanism: observers of animal capture become the startled quarry whose motion and exposed condition are read.

### X7. P1 -> P2 -> P3: A public call is countered, dispersed, and restored as reminder
- Pericopes: P1 (74:1-18), P2 (74:19-38), and P3 (74:39-56).
- Bridge: role progression, contrast, and reversal; accountable summons becomes attributed counter-speech, collective verbal mixing dissolves its source, and remembrance restores content to voice.
- Canonical placements: P1 1C, "The warning is a reiterated call"; P2 12A, "Utterance becomes attribution and circulating report"; P3 24A, "Talk becomes a medium one enters and stirs"; P3 24F, "Correction can dissolve into haste and noise"; P3 29A, "Reminder moves from retained content to speech."
- Synthesis: P1 sends a repeatable public signal, P2 answers with a claim whose force depends on attribution and circulation, and P3 shows group speech losing stable ownership in a mixed verbal medium. The final reminder reverses that dispersal by recovering absent content, retaining it, and giving it an accountable tongue again.
