# Layer 2

Layer 2 has two passes.

`discovery/` records the full admitted field for one ayah in a structured ledger.
It optimizes recall, provenance, before/after activation, and reader payoff. Its
memo is optional working prose, not the publication surface.

`editorial/` writes final ayah-based prose after the shared compiler has seen the
whole editorial scope. It may reorganize and consolidate, but every carried
finding must remain represented.

The final output unit is:

```text
{S}_{A}.prose.md
{S}_{A}.evidence.md
{S}_{A}.index.md
{S}_{A}.result.json
{S}_{A}.friction.md
```

`result.json` is the mechanical coverage bridge between discovery, planning, and
authored prose.
