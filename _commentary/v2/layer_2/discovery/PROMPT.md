# Layer 2 Discovery Prompt

Read the inlined editorial contract and schema first. The attached source packet
belongs to one ayah.

## Task

Discover and record the ayah's complete admitted field. Produce:

1. `{UNIT}.ledger.json`
2. `{UNIT}.memo.md`
3. `{UNIT}.friction.md`

The JSON ledger is canonical for downstream work. The memo is a concise
exploratory account of the most important relationships you found; it is not a
publication draft and it does not limit the ledger.

## Discovery rules

- Record what the ayah does as an act, what its grammar forces, what each word
  contributes, and what its sound or form contributes when supported.
- Record local resonances and later-context activations with explicit
  `readingBefore`, `activationTriggers`, and `readingAfter`.
- Preserve surprising valid outliers when they retain a grounded return path.
- Do not manufacture a surprise to make the ledger interesting.
- Do not impose any maximum number of findings.
- Do not merge findings that have distinct reader payoffs merely to shorten the
  ledger.
- Mark a source obligation `blocked` only when the packet itself supplies
  blocking evidence. Missing interest or available space is not blocking
  evidence.
- Every `carry` source obligation must point to one or more findings.
- Every finding must cite source claims and evidence references.
- Use `remote-lexical` and `synthetic` support levels candidly. These labels do
  not by themselves reject a finding.

## Grounding

Every finding must identify at least one local anchor in this ayah. A
context-activated finding must also identify the later trigger. The `proseClaim`
must keep the primary reading reachable and state the reader payoff.

## Memo

The memo may connect findings, note likely paragraph movements, and identify
possible channel tags. It must not silently add findings absent from the ledger.

## Friction

Record missing sources, contradictory evidence, malformed identities, and
instructions that could not be satisfied. Do not resolve malformed source IDs by
inventing replacements.
