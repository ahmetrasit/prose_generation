### Disputed roots in this ayah (minor analyses)

- 96:1:1 ٱقْرَأْ: minor analysis from ق ر ن (q-r-' vs q-r-n)
- 96:1:2 بِٱسْمِ: minor analysis from و س م (s-m-w vs w-s-m)
- the dispute (ق ر ء or ق ر ن): The majority derive قرآن from ق ر أ (to recite/read), making it "that which is recited," on pattern فُعلان. Al-Ash'ari and some Kufans derive it from ق ر ن (to join/connect), arguing it means "that which joins surahs/verses together." Al-Shafi'i held it was a proper noun not derived from any root.
