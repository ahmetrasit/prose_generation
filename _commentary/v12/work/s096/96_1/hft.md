## Records whose focus is 96:1

### b_authorized_recitation [baseline model, strong]
- before: Read or recite.
- after: Read or recite as sourced, authorized speech in the designation of your Creator-Lord.
- mechanism: Reading or recitation is not left as an autonomous vocal act: the prepositional phrase places it within the Creator-Lord's designation and authority.
- trace:
  - 96:1 w1 ٱقْرَأْ → ق ر ء B002 (okumak, okutmak ve birlikte okumak) — Reading, reciting, and teaching recitation supply the commanded act and its transmissible voice.
  - 96:1 w2 بِٱسْمِ → س م و B005 (ad, adlandırma ve ad ya da nitelik bakımından denklik) — A name as a raised designation supplies the identifier under which the recitation proceeds.
  - 96:1 w3 رَبِّكَ → ر ب ب B001 (sahip olup yönetme) — Lordship, ownership, and authority make the named relation one of authorization and answerability.
  - 96:1 w5 خَلَقَ → خ ل ق B002 (var etme ve ortaya çıkarma) — Originating and bringing into being identifies the authority as the source of the reader and the readable world.
