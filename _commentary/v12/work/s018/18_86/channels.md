## The channels anchored in 18:86, in full

### 14. Governed Reach Along Connective Means
- Semantic invariant: Granted capacity becomes just rule only by following available connections to their limits and calibrating action to the conditions encountered there.
- Surface relation: direct; 18:84-93 repeatedly joins established power, supplied means, following, arrival at boundaries, and differentiated treatment within the fifth pericope.
- Surprising reach: Sovereignty is pictured as disciplined traversal and local adjustment rather than possession of a static center.
#### Subchannel B. Boundary Conditions Determine the Form of Rule
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: At western and eastern limits, distinct environmental exposure and moral condition call forth punishment, good treatment, easy command, or informed restraint.
- Active motifs: western setting `غ ر ب B006`; luminary rising `ط ل ع B001`; spring source `ع ي ن B006`; dark mud `ح م ء B001`; protective covering `س ت ر B001`; encountered people `ق و م B001`; punitive consequence `ع ذ ب B005`; adopted course of treatment `ء خ ذ B010`; beneficial excellence `ح س ن B002`; proportionate recompense `ج ز ي B001`; ease of command `ي س ر B001`; encompassing knowledge `ح و ط B004`; inner acquaintance `خ ب ر B001`
- Ayah anchors: 18:86-88 (western limit, its people, choice of punishment or good treatment, and differentiated recompense); 18:90-91 (eastern exposure without covering and knowledge encompassing their condition)
- Synthesis: Reach does not authorize a uniform response. The ruler reads both moral conduct and material exposure, then fits action and speech to what the boundary reveals.

### 20. Following Is Judged by Its Object and Endpoint
- Semantic invariant: Following has no fixed moral value by itself; its direction is determined by what leads and by the endpoint the relation makes reachable.
- Surface relation: direct; pericopes spanned: 2 (18:27-44), 4 (18:60-82), and 5 (18:83-98); bridge: the same following operation moves from desire, to an informed guide, to connective means repeatedly pursued to spatial limits.
- Surprising reach: Desire, teacher, and route are placed in the same grammatical role so that the object of following becomes the decisive variable.
#### Subchannel C. Following a Means Converts Connection into Reach
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A supplied connection is followed repeatedly until western, eastern, and intermontane limits are reached.
- Active motifs: following `ت ب ع B001`; rope-like connection `س ب ب B003`; arrival at an endpoint `ب ل غ B001`; interval between sides `ب ي ن B002`
- Ayah anchors: 18:85-86 (following a means to the western limit); 18:89-90 (following to the eastern limit); 18:92-93 (following to the interval between barriers)
- Synthesis: The means does not lead through persuasion or authority. It offers a connection whose endpoint appears only through disciplined continuation.

### 25. Solar Boundaries Calibrate Exposure
- Semantic invariant: Sunrise and sunset define exposure conditions that are actively routed or governed rather than treated as neutral scenery.
- Surface relation: direct; pericopes spanned: 1 (18:1-26) and 5 (18:83-98); bridge: identical rising and setting anchors first map protection around the cave and later organize encounters with peoples at the western and eastern limits.
- Surprising reach: The sun links private refuge to public rule by making exposure a condition that calls for calibrated response.
#### Subchannel B. Solar Endpoints Reveal Different Conditions of Rule
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Reaching the setting and rising places of the sun exposes distinct populations, including an eastern people without covering from it.
- Active motifs: solar body `ش م س B001`; western setting `غ ر ب B006`; luminary rising `ط ل ع B001`; spring source `ع ي ن B006`; dark mud `ح م ء B001`; protective covering `س ت ر B001`
- Ayah anchors: 18:86 (western setting at the spring and encountered people); 18:90 (eastern rising upon a people without covering)
- Synthesis: The same celestial movement that is routed around the sleepers becomes a geographic measure of sovereign reach. Rule begins with reading how bodies are exposed at each limit.
