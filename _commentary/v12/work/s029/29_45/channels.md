## The channels anchored in 29:45, in full

### 13. P4 (29:45-56) Revelation From Hidden Signal to Public Text
- Semantic invariant: Revelation moves from concealed delivery through inscription and ordered sequence into voiced remembrance and embodied knowledge.
- Surface relation: direct; 29:45-51 repeatedly joins revelation, book, recitation, writing, signs, breasts, and remembrance.
- Surprising reach: The text is simultaneously a hidden signal, stone inscription, joined document, following voice, mnemonic recovery, and archive carried in the chest.
#### Subchannel A. Hidden Delivery and Durable Inscription
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Knowledge is conveyed discreetly, impressed into a durable surface, joined as a document, and delivered into its proper place.
- Active motifs: concealed communication of knowledge `و ح ي B001`; writing and engraving `و ح ي B003`; divine report and inspiration `و ح ي B004`; revelation delivered downward `ن ز ل B002`; ordered placement into the proper rank `ن ز ل B004`; written document and arranged letters `ك ت ب B002`; binding determination in writing `ك ت ب B003`; extended written line `خ ط ط B001`
- Ayah anchors: و ح ي 29:45; ن ز ل 29:46-47,50-51; ك ت ب 29:45-48,51; خ ط ط 29:48.
- Synthesis: Revelation traverses two media without losing identity: a hidden transfer of knowledge becomes a durable inscription. Descent is also placement, so the book arrives not merely from above but into an ordered textual form.
#### Subchannel B. Following Voice and Recovered Presence
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A reader follows a prior sequence, voice answers voice, and repeated naming restores what could otherwise pass out of active memory.
- Active motifs: following in ordered succession `ت ل و B001`; a voice following another voice `ت ل و B007`; inward recovery after absence `ذ ك ر B003`; voiced naming and mention `ذ ك ر B004`; reminder that restores presence `ذ ك ر B009`; articulated utterance `ق و ل B001`; clarification through speech `ب ي ن B005`
- Ayah anchors: ت ل و 29:45,48,51; ذ ك ر 29:45,51; ق و ل 29:46,50,52,55; ب ي ن 29:49-50,52.
- Synthesis: Recitation is lexical following: the present voice walks behind an established sequence and can be answered by another voice. Remembrance keeps that chain active by repeatedly restoring the text to heart and tongue.
#### Subchannel C. Signs Archived in Breasts
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Distinguishing signs are held in the chest as known evidence and can be produced as testimony when challenged.
- Active motifs: visible sign `ء ي ي B003`; disclosure to the knower `ع ل م B001`; distinguishing mark `ع ل م B002`; chest as bodily container `ص د ر B001`; source from which action and expression issue `ص د ر B004`; testimony that declares known fact `ش ه د B002`; sign that witnesses a condition `ش ه د B008`; fine awareness `ش ع ر B005`
- Ayah anchors: ء ي ي 29:47,49-50; ع ل م 29:45,49,52; ص د ر 29:49; ش ه د 29:52; ش ع ر 29:53.
- Synthesis: The signs are not only external marks; they are stored in the breasts of those given knowledge. This turns the body into an archive whose contents can emerge as informed testimony, while lack of شعور names failure to access even fine-grained evidence.

### 14. P4 (29:45-56) Prayer as Established Restraint
- Semantic invariant: Prayer establishes a bodily and social order whose operation is to stop conduct at the boundary of the shameful and unrecognized.
- Surface relation: direct; 29:45 commands recitation and establishment of prayer, then states that prayer restrains indecency and wrong.
- Surprising reach: Ritual becomes a standing structure, directional alignment, cognitive brake, weaning process, and endpoint that makes further pursuit unnecessary.
#### Subchannel A. Standing, Direction, and Maintained Service
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: A worshiper takes an upright position, maintains the rite, aligns to its direction, and submits the body in service.
- Active motifs: prescribed prayer with standing and prostration `ص ل و B003`; houses and places of prayer `ص ل و B007`; bodily standing `ق و م B002`; sustained care and maintenance `ق و م B004`; straight alignment `ق و م B008`; submissive worship `ع ب د B003`; prayer-direction faced by the body `ق ب ل B005`
- Ayah anchors: ص ل و 29:45; ق و م 29:45,51; ع ب د 29:56; ق ب ل 29:48.
- Synthesis: "Establish prayer" combines erection and maintenance: the body stands, the practice is kept in repair, and attention is aligned toward a direction. Prayer thereby supplies an ordered stance before it supplies a prohibitive effect.
#### Subchannel B. Braking, Weaning, and Ending the Wrong
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Reason and prohibition arrest an act, detach appetite from it, and terminate the search for further indulgence.
- Active motifs: prohibition and active restraint `ن ه ي B001`; reason that prevents ugliness `ن ه ي B003`; sufficiency that ends further seeking `ن ه ي B005`; overt indecency in speech or conduct `ف ح ش B003`; rejected ugliness `ن ك ر B006`; preventing or weaning from an object `ع ذ ب B003`; ugly conduct exceeding its measure `ف ح ش B002`
- Ayah anchors: ن ه ي 29:45; ف ح ش 29:45; ن ك ر 29:45; ع ذ ب 29:53-55.
- Synthesis: Prayer's restraint is not a vague moral influence. Its lexical mechanism is a brake that reason applies, a weaning from appetite, and an endpoint after which the act no longer solicits pursuit.

### 16. P4 (29:45-56) Argument Without Rupture
- Semantic invariant: Disagreement can be tightly reasoned while preserving peace, shared orientation, and the interlocutor's human belonging.
- Surface relation: direct; 29:46 commands disputation only in the best manner, excepts active wrongdoers, and ends with one God and shared submission.
- Surprising reach: Argument is a braided cord whose strength can either hold relation together or become a wrestling grip; the best form keeps unity without erasing difference.
#### Subchannel B. Peaceful Unity With Distinct Parties
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Distinct communities remain separate participants yet converge on one object of worship and a non-hostile relation.
- Active motifs: soundness free of injury `س ل م B001`; peace opposed to war `س ل م B004`; handing oneself or an affair over `س ل م B012`; divine unity without partner `و ح د B004`; different parties united in one respect `و ح د B005`; worship directed to the deity `ء ل ه B001`; trusting assent `ء م ن B002`
- Ayah anchors: س ل م 29:46; و ح د 29:46; ء ل ه 29:45-46,50,52; ء م ن 29:46-47,51-52,56.
- Synthesis: Unity here is not social erasure but convergence "in one respect": one deity and a shared posture of handing oneself over. Peace safeguards the distinct participants so that common worship can be affirmed without coercive fusion.
