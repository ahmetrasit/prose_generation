## The channels anchored in 29:41, in full

### 10. P3 (29:28-44) House, Household, and Failed Support
- Semantic invariant: A dwelling is viable only when fitted structure, inhabited relations, and load-bearing supports work together.
- Surface relation: direct; 29:31-35 repeatedly distinguishes town and household, while 29:38 names dwellings and 29:41 tests protectors through the spider's house.
- Surprising reach: The spider comparison opens into joinery, tent supports, stitched panels, household membership, bodily ribs, hidden pain, and timber-eating decay.
#### Subchannel A. Fitted Dwelling Structure
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A shelter is assembled from a standing support, a fitted socket or joint, transverse members, and joined wall material.
- Active motifs: dwelling and nightly shelter `ب ي ت B001`; crosspieces carrying a house-post `ق ر ي B008`; fitted joint, socket, or door fitting `ح ق ق B011`; standing tool or structural member `ق و م B012`; residence as a stable place `س ك ن B002`; joining two skins or wall-halves `ب ص ر B006`
- Ayah anchors: ب ي ت 29:41; ق ر ي 29:31,34; ح ق ق 29:44; ق و م 29:28-30,35-36; س ك ن 29:38; ب ص ر 29:38.
- Synthesis: The house is a fitted mechanism, not merely an enclosure. Posts, crosspieces, sockets, and joined skins distribute load; the spider-house pressures the comparison by offering the name of shelter without dependable structural work.
#### Subchannel B. Household, Inhabitants, and Welcome
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Kin, residents, and guests are gathered by a dwelling whose social function is recognition, qualification, and safe reception.
- Active motifs: kin and co-inhabitants `ء ه ل B001`; qualification for a role or place `ء ه ل B003`; inhabited familiarity `ء ه ل B004`; household and dependents `ب ي ت B002`; residents of a house `س ك ن B003`; what gives inward familiarity and calm `س ك ن B004`; gathered town and townspeople `ق ر ي B001`; hospitality through gathered food `ق ر ي B003`
- Ayah anchors: ء ه ل 29:31-34; ب ي ت 29:41; س ك ن 29:38; ق ر ي 29:31,34.
- Synthesis: "Household" names more than blood descent: it is a relation of residence, qualification, familiarity, and welcome. The repeated negotiation over Lot's family therefore asks who truly belongs inside a protective social dwelling.
#### Subchannel C. Weak Supports and Internal Failure
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A nominal shelter fails because its support is weak, its inner frame hurts, its speech is pretext, and decay consumes its material.
- Active motifs: loss of strength and resolve `و ه ن B001`; a vulnerable rib or shoulder support `و ه ن B003`; pain in that support `و ه ن B004`; empty speech used as a pretext `و ه ن B008`; a protector responsible for an affair `و ل ي B003`; protective alliance `و ل ي B004`; timber-eating termite `ء ر ض B010`; spider-house designation within a fitted joint term `ح ق ق B011`
- Ayah anchors: و ه ن 29:41; و ل ي 29:41; ء ر ض 29:36,39-40,44; ح ق ق 29:44.
- Synthesis: Weakness penetrates every load-bearing register: bodily ribs, household patrons, structural timber, and justificatory speech. The spider-house is weakest not because "house" is meaningless, but because the relations and members that should carry its load do not.

### 11. P3 (29:28-44) Distinct Seizure Mechanics
- Semantic invariant: Each destroyed community is removed by a different physical operation that attacks its assumed stability.
- Surface relation: direct; 29:34,37,40 enumerates descending punishment, tremor, stone-bearing force, cry, sinking, and drowning.
- Surprising reach: The list becomes a mechanics of vibration, projectile wind, immersion, substrate failure, and acoustic shock, with distinct body states and outcomes.
#### Subchannel C. Immersion and Engulfing Water
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Water fills the available space, occupies the victim's field, and submerges until movement and life cease.
- Active motifs: sinking beneath engulfing water `غ ر ق B001`; land or eye filled with water `غ ر ق B003`; a medium occupying the whole field `غ ر ق B005`; a basin or depression retaining water `ء خ ذ B006`; the gathered body of water `ج ث م B007`; lower terrestrial support `ء ر ض B001`
- Ayah anchors: غ ر ق 29:40; ء خ ذ 29:37,40-41; ج ث م 29:37; ء ر ض 29:36,39-40,44.
- Synthesis: Drowning is modeled as progressive occupation: water gathers, fills, and takes over the victim's whole field. Unlike the tremor, which arrests bodies on the surface, immersion removes the breathable surface itself.
#### Subchannel D. Substrate Failure and Downward Disappearance
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Supporting ground softens or opens, the victim is forced below its surface, and the former dwelling becomes an encircling adverse state.
- Active motifs: ground swallowing and hiding what enters it `خ س ف B001`; imposed humiliation or hardship `خ س ف B006`; terrestrial supporting surface `ء ر ض B001`; heaviness and adhesion to ground `ء ر ض B006`; the adverse circle closing around a people `د و ر B004`; bodily condition taking hold `ء خ ذ B007`
- Ayah anchors: خ س ف 29:40; ء ر ض 29:36,39-40,44; د و ر 29:37; ء خ ذ 29:37,40-41.
- Synthesis: Sinking attacks the support relation itself: what appeared load-bearing becomes the medium of disappearance. The lexical field also carries humiliation, so spatial descent and loss of standing reinforce one another without collapsing into the other punishments.

### 12. P3 (29:28-44) Examples Made Legible
- Semantic invariant: A truth becomes cognitively usable when it is marked, modeled, and impressed into a form that can restrain mistaken judgment.
- Surface relation: direct; 29:35 and 29:44 call remains and creation signs, while 29:41-43 explicitly gives a likeness, strikes examples, and limits understanding to knowers.
- Surprising reach: Parable-making behaves like heraldry, model-making, molding, and tethering: cognition is given a durable form and then held to it.
#### Subchannel A. Mark, Landmark, and Inward Sight
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A distinguishing trace remains in the landscape, directs attention, and becomes inward recognition rather than bare looking.
- Active motifs: manifest sign `ء ي ي B003`; retained route-guiding landmark `ع ل م B002`; emergence into visible clarity `ب ي ن B004`; gestural clarification `ب ي ن B005`; inward sight and evidentiary understanding `ب ص ر B002`; retained trace after departure `ت ر ك B002`
- Ayah anchors: ء ي ي 29:35,44; ع ل م 29:28,32,41-43; ب ي ن 29:35,38-39; ب ص ر 29:38; ت ر ك 29:35.
- Synthesis: The "clear sign" left from the town is a trace that functions like a landmark: it first catches sight, then opens meaning, then becomes inward evidence. Remains are useful because they preserve direction after the event itself has departed.
#### Subchannel B. Struck Model and Rational Restraint
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A maker strikes or stamps a representative form so that observers can compare structures and bind judgment to the demonstrated relation.
- Active motifs: proverbial formulation `م ث ل B003`; represented model or image `م ث ل B008`; example serving as an instructive sign `م ث ل B011`; rendering an example explicit `ض ر ب B003`; type or mold for repeated form `ض ر ب B007`; striking tool `ض ر ب B014`; craft of striking `ض ر ب B014`; reason that restrains ignorance `ع ق ل B001`; tethering a moving animal `ع ق ل B002`
- Ayah anchors: م ث ل 29:41,43; ض ر ب 29:43; ع ق ل 29:35,43.
- Synthesis: To "strike" an example is to make a repeatable model whose form carries an invariant relation. عقل contributes both understanding and tether: the comparison checks wandering judgment by fastening it to a visible structure.
