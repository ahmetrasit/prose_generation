## The channels anchored in 1:4, in full

### 1. Devotional Dependence and Rule
- Semantic invariant: A dependent community turns toward a sovereign divine addressee through worship, service, obedience, and petition.
- Surface relation: direct; 1:1 `بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ`, 1:2 `لِلَّهِ رَبِّ`, 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ ... ٱلدِّينِ`, 1:5 `نَعْبُدُ ... نَسْتَعِينُ`, and 1:6 `ٱهْدِنَا`.
- Surprising reach: The devotional relation extends lexically from willing worship into ownership and coercive service, while lordship also carries the countervailing function of nurture.
#### Subchannel A. Worship and Obedient Submission
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: Worshippers direct humble obedience to the one recognized as deity and organizing religious authority.
- Active motifs: worship as an act `ء ل ه B001`; the worshipped deity `ء ل ه B001`; obedient religious submission `د ي ن B001`; humble worship and service `ع ب د B003`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 `ٱللَّهِ` and 1:2 `لِلَّهِ` (`ء ل ه`); 1:4 `ٱلدِّينِ` (`د ي ن`); 1:5 `نَعْبُدُ` (`ع ب د`)
- Synthesis: The scene joins the divine object of worship to the human act of worship, then specifies the relation as obedience enacted with humility. Religion is active here as an ordering relation rather than merely a named doctrine.
#### Subchannel B. Sovereignty, Ownership, and Service
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A lord and king possesses a domain, governs it, and stands over dependents whose service ranges from honored attendance to enslavement.
- Active motifs: divine lordship `ر ب ب B001`; proprietary mastery `ر ب ب B001`; kingship and sovereignty `م ل ك B003`; ownership and disposal of property `م ل ك B002`; the enslaved dependent `ع ب د B001`; coercive dominion `د ي ن B004`; the honored or venerated servant `ع ب د B006`
- Ayah anchors: 1:2 `رَبِّ` (`ر ب ب`); 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ` and `ٱلدِّينِ` (`م ل ك`, `د ي ن`); 1:5 `نَعْبُدُ` (`ع ب د`)
- Synthesis: Lordship and kingship supply the governing agent, ownership supplies the legal relation to a domain, and servitude supplies the dependent role. The honored servant and the enslaved subject expose opposite social realizations of the same vertical relation.

### 2. Benefaction, Welfare, and Praise
- Semantic invariant: Good is conferred through compassion and maintenance, stabilized as welfare, and answered by praise or recognition.
- Surface relation: direct; 1:1 and 1:3 `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ`, 1:2 `ٱلْحَمْدُ ... رَبِّ`, 1:5 `نَسْتَعِينُ`, and 1:7 `أَنْعَمْتَ`.
- Surprising reach: Tenderness expands into practical provisioning, repair, guardianship, and structural support, while praise can function either as gratitude or as a claim imposed by a patron.
#### Subchannel B. Patronage, Support, and Claimed Recognition
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A patron supplies aid and the mainstay of another's life, then may present that support as a favor deserving praise.
- Active motifs: aid and mutual support `ع و ن B001`; the mainstay of an affair `م ل ك B005`; structural and livelihood support `ق و م B009`; benefaction or favor `ن ع م B001`; blessing as a conferred good `ر ب ب B016`; seeking praise for a favor `ح م د B005`
- Ayah anchors: 1:2 `ٱلْحَمْدُ` and `رَبِّ` (`ح م د`, `ر ب ب`); 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ` (`م ل ك`); 1:5 `نَسْتَعِينُ` (`ع و ن`); 1:6 `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` (`ق و م`); 1:7 `أَنْعَمْتَ` (`ن ع م`)
- Synthesis: Aid, mainstay, and livelihood form one support mechanism. The social relation becomes patronage when the giver converts that material dependence into a demand for acknowledgment.

### 3. Declared Identity and Public Presence
- Semantic invariant: Names, utterances, reputation, and bearing make identity or commitment socially legible.
- Surface relation: direct; 1:1 `بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ`, 1:2 `ٱلْحَمْدُ`, 1:4 `ٱلدِّينِ`, 1:6 `ٱهْدِنَا`, and 1:7 `غَيْرِ ... أَنْعَمْتَ`.
- Surprising reach: The naming and address field reaches from divine invocation into assent, grammatical qualification, poetic exchange, and the ceremonial presence of a reputed captain.
#### Subchannel B. Assent, Certification, and Qualification
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: An utterance confirms a claim, delegates judgment, or narrows it through difference, exception, negation, and approximation.
- Active motifs: affirmative response `ن ع م B004`; certification or delegated judgment `د ي ن B007`; otherness and difference `غ ي ر B005`; exception and negation `غ ي ر B005`; the approximating or quantifying particle `ر ب ب B015`
- Ayah anchors: 1:2 `رَبِّ` (`ر ب ب`); 1:4 `ٱلدِّينِ` (`د ي ن`); 1:7 `غَيْرِ` and `أَنْعَمْتَ` (`غ ي ر`, `ن ع م`)
- Synthesis: Assent commits the speaker, certification authorizes another's word or judgment, and qualification sets the limits of the commitment. Approximation and exception show that discourse governs not only truth but also scope.

### 4. Orientation, Passage, and Breakdown
- Semantic invariant: Movement is organized by a course, a guide, and a state of passage, then tested by deviation, engulfment, or loss of mobility.
- Surface relation: direct; 1:2 `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, 1:5 `نَعْبُدُ`, 1:6 `ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`, and 1:7 `صِرَٰطَ ... ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`.
- Surprising reach: The straight-road scene expands into landmarks, centerlines, and leading animals, while swallowing, mounting, cutting, and burial supply a shape-motion analogy for passage into disappearance.
#### Subchannel A. The Marked and Straight Route
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A traveler receives guidance, reads landmarks, enters the center of a prepared road, and maintains a straight course that can also become an established manner of conduct.
- Active motifs: gentle route guidance `ه د ي B001`; direction and manner `ه د ي B002`; road or straight path `ص ر ط B001`; straightness and rectitude `ق و م B008`; route landmark and boundary `ع ل م B002`; middle of the road or valley `م ل ك B006`; paved or trodden road `ع ب د B005`; established custom or habitual course `د ي ن B005`
- Ayah anchors: 1:2 `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` (`ع ل م`); 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ` and `ٱلدِّينِ` (`م ل ك`, `د ي ن`); 1:5 `نَعْبُدُ` (`ع ب د`); 1:6 `ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` (`ه د ي`, `ص ر ط`, `ق و م`); 1:7 `صِرَٰطَ` (`ص ر ط`)
- Synthesis: Guidance initiates movement, landmarks and boundaries make the route legible, a prepared surface and centerline stabilize traversal, and straightness describes its successful geometry. Direction and custom extend the same course structure from travel into sustained conduct.
#### Subchannel B. The Visible Leader at the Front
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A conspicuous leader takes the foremost position and draws a herd or procession behind it.
- Active motifs: the foremost guide `ه د ي B003`; the leading animal followed by the group `م ل ك B008`; an elevated visible figure `س م و B002`; a gathered wild-cattle herd `ر ب ب B014`; a herd of wild donkeys `ع و ن B006`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 `بِسْمِ` (`س م و`); 1:2 `رَبِّ` (`ر ب ب`); 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ` (`م ل ك`); 1:5 `نَسْتَعِينُ` (`ع و ن`); 1:6 `ٱهْدِنَا` (`ه د ي`)
- Synthesis: Visibility allows the leader to be tracked, the foremost position supplies direction, and the gathered animals supply the following participants. Leadership is thus rendered as a spatial relation before it becomes a social one.

### 5. Time, Measure, and Final Reckoning
- Semantic invariant: Time is made intelligible by bounded spans and celestial positions, by pace within a duration, and by a culminating event of judgment.
- Surface relation: direct; 1:1 `بِسْمِ`, 1:2 `ٱلْحَمْدُ ... ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ`, and 1:6 `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`.
- Surprising reach: The day expands from ordinary daylight into zenith, lunar marking, personal pace, prolonged residence, and finally the event in which creation rises for sovereign accounting.
#### Subchannel A. Daylight, Zenith, and Celestial Marking
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A daylight span is located by the sun at zenith and by visible signs in the overhead sky.
- Active motifs: bounded daylight `ي و م B001`; the sun standing at midday `ق و م B017`; the overhead sky `س م و B004`; a lunar station `ن ع م B007`; a distinguishing sign `ع ل م B002`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 `بِسْمِ` (`س م و`); 1:2 `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` (`ع ل م`); 1:4 `يَوْمِ` (`ي و م`); 1:6 `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` (`ق و م`); 1:7 `أَنْعَمْتَ` (`ن ع م`)
- Synthesis: Daylight supplies the bounded interval, zenith gives it an internal midpoint, and sky signs provide a larger celestial coordinate system. Time is read through the positions of bodies that appear to stand overhead.
#### Subchannel B. Duration, Pace, and Praised Attainment
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: An actor inhabits a duration, varies between delay and haste, intensifies effort, and reaches a praiseworthy limit without losing composure.
- Active motifs: an open duration of time `ي و م B002`; brief delay `ع ب د B009`; quick running `ع ب د B009`; increase or intensification `ن ع م B010`; the utmost praised goal `ح م د B004`; calm bearing `ه د ي B010`; continued residence `ر ب ب B007`
- Ayah anchors: 1:2 `ٱلْحَمْدُ` and `رَبِّ` (`ح م د`, `ر ب ب`); 1:4 `يَوْمِ` (`ي و م`); 1:5 `نَعْبُدُ` (`ع ب د`); 1:6 `ٱهْدِنَا` (`ه د ي`); 1:7 `أَنْعَمْتَ` (`ن ع م`)
- Synthesis: Duration supplies the field, delay and running supply contrasting rates, and intensification directs those rates toward an upper limit. Continued residence and calm bearing keep attainment from collapsing into mere haste.
#### Subchannel C. The Momentous Day of Rising and Account
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: A climactic event arrives, creation rises, and a sovereign conducts judgment, accounting, and recompense.
- Active motifs: the momentous or calamitous day `ي و م B003`; resurrection and rising of creation `ق و م B013`; judgment, account, and recompense `د ي ن B002`; sovereign kingship `م ل ك B003`
- Ayah anchors: 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` (`م ل ك`, `ي و م`, `د ي ن`); 1:6 `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` (`ق و م`)
- Synthesis: The day is not only a duration but the event that triggers rising. Resurrection supplies the participants, sovereignty supplies the judge, and account and recompense supply the operation and outcome.

### 6. Property, Measure, and Legitimate Transfer
- Semantic invariant: Goods and obligations move between parties through ownership, valuation, replacement, compensation, gift, or offering.
- Surface relation: indirect; 1:1 and 1:2 `ٱللَّهِ`, 1:1 and 1:3 `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ` / `ٱلرَّحِيمِ`, 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ ... ٱلدِّينِ`, 1:5 `نَعْبُدُ`, 1:6 `ٱهْدِنَا ... ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`, and 1:7 `أَنْعَمْتَ ... غَيْرِ ... ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`.
- Surprising reach: Financial debt and equal coinage join a livestock market and its stray property, while compensation and substitution offer a regulated alternative to uncompensated loss.
#### Subchannel A. Credit, Price, and Livestock Property
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Livestock is owned, priced, sold on credit, and monitored for loss within an active market.
- Active motifs: debt, loan, and deferred sale `د ي ن B003`; valuation and pricing `ق و م B010`; an equal-weight coin `ق و م B015`; a flourishing market `ق و م B018`; property and ownership `م ل ك B002`; livestock wealth `ن ع م B005`; an ownerless stray animal `ض ل ل B005`; a middle-aged animal fit for classification `ع و ن B002`
- Ayah anchors: 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ` and `ٱلدِّينِ` (`م ل ك`, `د ي ن`); 1:5 `نَسْتَعِينُ` (`ع و ن`); 1:6 `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` (`ق و م`); 1:7 `أَنْعَمْتَ` and `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` (`ن ع م`, `ض ل ل`)
- Synthesis: Ownership identifies the goods, age and kind make animals assessable, pricing and equal coin establish measure, and debt permits delayed settlement. The stray is the failure case in which market property loses recognized custody.
#### Subchannel B. Replacement, Indemnity, and Redress
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A lost or injured claim is answered by a substitute, transfer, or assessed compensation rather than remaining without redress.
- Active motifs: blood money or indemnity `غ ي ر B002`; replacement of one thing by another `غ ي ر B003`; standing in another's place `ق و م B007`; blood lost without vengeance or compensation `ض ل ل B003`; judgment and recompense `د ي ن B002`; transfer of ownership `م ل ك B002`
- Ayah anchors: 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ ... ٱلدِّينِ` (`م ل ك`, `د ي ن`); 1:6 `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` (`ق و م`); 1:7 `غَيْرِ` and `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` (`غ ي ر`, `ض ل ل`)
- Synthesis: Loss creates a vacant claim, substitution fills the vacant place, ownership transfer legalizes the replacement, and judgment fixes its compensatory value. Unredressed blood marks the opposite outcome.
#### Subchannel C. Reciprocal Gift and Beneficent Transfer
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A gift moves toward a valued recipient as an expression of affection, mercy, or benefit.
- Active motifs: affectionate gift-giving `ه د ي B004`; conferred benefit `ن ع م B001`; mercy enacted as benefaction `ر ح م B001`; transfer into another's ownership `م ل ك B002`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 and 1:3 `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ` / `ٱلرَّحِيمِ` (`ر ح م`); 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ` (`م ل ك`); 1:6 `ٱهْدِنَا` (`ه د ي`); 1:7 `أَنْعَمْتَ` (`ن ع م`)
- Synthesis: Affection supplies the social motive, the gift supplies the transferred object, ownership change completes the act, and benefaction names its intended effect on the recipient.

### 7. Household Continuity and Protected Belonging
- Semantic invariant: Kinship is produced and maintained through gestation, nurture, marriage, covenant, and defense of household boundaries.
- Surface relation: indirect; 1:1 and 1:3 `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ` / `ٱلرَّحِيمِ`, 1:2 `رَبِّ`, 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ ... ٱلدِّينِ`, 1:5 `نَعْبُدُ`, 1:6 `ٱهْدِنَا`, and 1:7 `غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ`.
- Surprising reach: The mercy root opens into womb and blood relation, while guidance becomes both bride-conveyance and the protected status of a refugee under covenant.
#### Subchannel C. Marriage and Bride-Conveyance
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A marriage contract establishes a new household relation and the bride is formally conveyed to her spouse, accompanied by affectionate transfer.
- Active motifs: marriage contract `م ل ك B004`; bride conveyed to her husband `ه د ي B006`; kinship relation `ر ح م B002`; affectionate gift `ه د ي B004`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 and 1:3 `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ` / `ٱلرَّحِيمِ` (`ر ح م`); 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ` (`م ل ك`); 1:6 `ٱهْدِنَا` (`ه د ي`)
- Synthesis: Contract creates the relation, conveyance enacts the transition between households, kinship names the resulting bond, and the gift supplies a reciprocal social gesture around the transfer.
#### Subchannel D. Covenant, Refuge, and Legal Protection
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A named outsider or dependent receives protected standing through covenant, certification, and a rule of compensatory redress.
- Active motifs: covenant and protected association `ر ب ب B011`; protected refugee or bearer of safe conduct `ه د ي B007`; certification or delegated oath `د ي ن B007`; indemnity `غ ي ر B002`; naming and legal identification `س م و B005`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 `بِسْمِ` (`س م و`); 1:2 `رَبِّ` (`ر ب ب`); 1:4 `ٱلدِّينِ` (`د ي ن`); 1:6 `ٱهْدِنَا` (`ه د ي`); 1:7 `غَيْرِ` (`غ ي ر`)
- Synthesis: Naming identifies the protected person, covenant establishes the relation, certification makes it publicly actionable, and indemnity defines the consequence of violating that protection.

### 8. Herd Formation, Wildlife, and Dispersal
- Semantic invariant: Animal life is organized by gathering, classification, reproduction, leadership, pursuit, and scattering.
- Surface relation: indirect; 1:1 and 1:3 `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ` / `ٱلرَّحِيمِ`, 1:2 `رَبِّ ... ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ`, 1:5 `نَسْتَعِينُ`, and 1:7 `أَنْعَمْتَ ... ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`.
- Surprising reach: The animal field ranges from owned livestock and coherent herds to ostrich, falcon, hyena, and ownerless strays, with womb imagery joining wildlife to generation.
#### Subchannel A. Herd, Livestock, and the Stray Margin
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Domestic and wild animals gather into recognizable herds, are classified and led, and can fall outside the group as ownerless strays.
- Active motifs: wild-cattle herd `ر ب ب B014`; herd of wild donkeys `ع و ن B006`; livestock wealth `ن ع م B005`; middle-aged animal `ع و ن B002`; leading animal followed by the herd `م ل ك B008`; ownerless stray `ض ل ل B005`
- Ayah anchors: 1:2 `رَبِّ` (`ر ب ب`); 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ` (`م ل ك`); 1:5 `نَسْتَعِينُ` (`ع و ن`); 1:7 `أَنْعَمْتَ` and `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` (`ن ع م`, `ض ل ل`)
- Synthesis: Species and age make animals classifiable, gathering makes them a herd, and the lead animal provides internal order. The stray defines the boundary case where an animal loses both group placement and known ownership.

### 9. Habitation, Water, and the Living Landscape
- Semantic invariant: Habitation becomes viable when authority, residence, water, weather, and vegetation converge into a sustaining place.
- Surface relation: indirect; 1:1 `بِسْمِ`, 1:2 `ٱلْحَمْدُ ... رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, 1:4 `ٱلدِّينِ`, 1:5 `نَسْتَعِينُ`, 1:6 `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`, and 1:7 `أَنْعَمْتَ ... غَيْرِ`.
- Surprising reach: The field moves from a city defined by obedience into a camp secured by stored water, then upward to layered clouds, persistent rain, gentle wind, and enduring plants.
#### Subchannel A. Governed and Pleasant Settlement
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A community establishes residence in a governed place, finds the site suitable, and remains there.
- Active motifs: city as the place where authority is obeyed `د ي ن B006`; residence or settled place `ق و م B006`; the village or locality of Anah `ع و ن B008`; a place pleasant for settlement `ن ع م B011`; finding a person or place satisfactory `ح م د B002`; continued residence `ر ب ب B007`
- Ayah anchors: 1:2 `ٱلْحَمْدُ` and `رَبِّ` (`ح م د`, `ر ب ب`); 1:4 `ٱلدِّينِ` (`د ي ن`); 1:5 `نَسْتَعِينُ` (`ع و ن`); 1:6 `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` (`ق و م`); 1:7 `أَنْعَمْتَ` (`ن ع م`)
- Synthesis: Authority gives the settlement civic form, residence supplies continuity, and suitability supplies the inhabitants' positive judgment of the place. The named locality makes the scene geographically concrete.
#### Subchannel B. Water-Secured Encampment and Livelihood
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A community locates abundant collected water, controls its encampment through that resource, and sustains livelihood by storing and distributing it.
- Active motifs: abundant fresh water `ر ب ب B013`; sea or water-rich well `ع ل م B005`; water that secures command of camp and livelihood `م ل ك B007`; sustenance of life `ق و م B009`; irrigation of land and people `غ ي ر B001`
- Ayah anchors: 1:2 `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` (`ر ب ب`, `ع ل م`); 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ` (`م ل ك`); 1:6 `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` (`ق و م`); 1:7 `غَيْرِ` (`غ ي ر`)
- Synthesis: The well or collected water supplies the resource, control of water makes encampment possible, irrigation extends it into the surrounding land, and livelihood is the resulting stable state.

### 10. Structural Support and Working Materials
- Semantic invariant: Stability is produced by cohesive material, load-bearing members, mechanical parts, and treatments that bind, coat, or repair.
- Surface relation: indirect; 1:2 `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ`, 1:5 `نَعْبُدُ`, 1:6 `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`, and 1:7 `أَنْعَمْتَ ... غَيْرِ`.
- Surprising reach: Abstract mainstay and firmness resolve into wall cohesion, knots, table or well parts, kneaded material, thick concentrate, and coated transport surfaces.
#### Subchannel A. Cohesion, Mainstay, and Upright Members
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A structure or undertaking remains standing because material cohesion, a firm joint, and upright supporting members carry its load.
- Active motifs: material cohesion and strength `م ل ك B001`; mainstay of an affair `م ل ك B005`; structural support `ق و م B009`; upright tool or furniture member `ق و م B012`; firm knot or joint `ر ب ب B016`; strength and solidity `ع ب د B007`
- Ayah anchors: 1:2 `رَبِّ` (`ر ب ب`); 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ` (`م ل ك`); 1:5 `نَعْبُدُ` (`ع ب د`); 1:6 `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` (`ق و م`)
- Synthesis: Cohesion binds the material, the knot or joint fixes connected parts, upright members transfer load, and the mainstay names the same sustaining function at the scale of an entire undertaking.
#### Subchannel B. Well and Water-Lifting Assembly

- Synthesis: The well supplies the setting and resource, the upright timber and associated standing part supply the mechanism, and control of the lifted water supplies the functional outcome.
#### Subchannel C. Thickening, Coating, and Repair

### 11. Bodily Integrity, Posture, and Injury
- Semantic invariant: A living body is read through stance, proportion, strength, visible markers, pain, and loss of function.
- Surface relation: indirect; 1:1 and 1:3 `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ` / `ٱلرَّحِيمِ`, 1:2 `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ`, 1:5 `نَعْبُدُ ... نَسْتَعِينُ`, 1:6 `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`, and 1:7 `أَنْعَمْتَ ... ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ`.
- Surprising reach: Uprightness and firmness describe healthy stature, yet the same standing field also holds an intact-looking blind eye, diseased legs, swollen tissue, and a body halted by weakness.
#### Subchannel A. Upright Stature and Balanced Strength
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A body rises upright, displays proportion and firmness, and sustains its posture through balanced mature strength.
- Active motifs: bodily rising and upright stance `ق و م B002`; stature and bodily proportion `ق و م B011`; strength and solidity `ع ب د B007`; balanced mature build `ع و ن B005`; bodily cohesion `م ل ك B001`; thick, strongly colored body `غ ض ب B005`
- Ayah anchors: 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ` (`م ل ك`); 1:5 `نَعْبُدُ` and `نَسْتَعِينُ` (`ع ب د`, `ع و ن`); 1:6 `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` (`ق و م`); 1:7 `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` (`غ ض ب`)
- Synthesis: Rising supplies posture, stature supplies visible proportion, and cohesion, solidity, and mature balance supply the forces that maintain it. Thickness and strong coloration render bodily vigor externally perceptible.
