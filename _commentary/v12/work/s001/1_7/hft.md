## Records whose focus is 1:7

### base_embodied_route [baseline model, strong]
- before: The verse names a road used by a favored class and excludes two bad classes.
- after: The verse makes a favored human trajectory the road's visible embodiment and distinguishes oppositional rupture from aimless deviation.
- mechanism: The verse does not identify a road by geometry alone. It makes bestowed well-being visible in a recipient group, so their lived trajectory becomes the road's positive continuity; opposition and deviation are rival ways of leaving that continuity.
- trace:
  - 1:7 w1 صِرَٰطَ → ص ر ط B001 (yol, özellikle düz yol) — The straight-road image supplies continuous movement, while the following relative phrase relocates that continuity into an embodied human route. [plain here: the same branch; usage group: The worshippers ask God to guide them to the path of those He favored]
  - 1:7 w3 أَنْعَمْتَ → ن ع م B001 (iyi yaşam durumu ve başkasına ulaştırılan iyilik) — Bestowed favorable condition supplies the observable state by which the road-bearing group is identified.
  - 1:7 w5 غَيْرِ → غ ي ر B005 (başka olma, dışta bırakma veya olumsuzlama) — Otherness and exception turn the verse from a single route description into a contrast among trajectories.
  - 1:7 w6 ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ → غ ض ب B003 (karşı koyup muhalefet etmek) — Defiance and opposition supply a relational mechanism for one trajectory's rupture rather than merely naming an emotion. [plain here: B001; usage group: Those upon whom anger is incurred in the prayer for the straight path]

### base_successful_and_failed_disappearance [baseline model, medium]
- before: Road and straying are simple opposites: presence on the route versus absence from it.
- after: Both right passage and straying can involve disappearance; the decisive difference is whether disappearance completes directed transit or erases recoverable direction.
- mechanism: Two kinds of disappearance coexist. A soft, capacious passage can carry a traveler through so completely that the traveler vanishes into successful transit, whereas straying can also make something vanish but without destination or recoverability. Compact hardness acts as the obstructing condition that separates the two.
- trace:
  - 1:7 w1 صِرَٰطَ → ص ر ط B002 (geçişte gözden kaybolmak; özellikle yiyeceği yutmak) — Disappearance through swallowing supplies a successful passage in which being taken out of sight is evidence of transit.
