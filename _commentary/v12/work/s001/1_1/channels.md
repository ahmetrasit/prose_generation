## The channels anchored in 1:1, in full

### 1. Devotional Dependence and Rule
- Semantic invariant: A dependent community turns toward a sovereign divine addressee through worship, service, obedience, and petition.
- Surface relation: direct; 1:1 `بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ`, 1:2 `لِلَّهِ رَبِّ`, 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ ... ٱلدِّينِ`, 1:5 `نَعْبُدُ ... نَسْتَعِينُ`, and 1:6 `ٱهْدِنَا`.
- Surprising reach: The devotional relation extends lexically from willing worship into ownership and coercive service, while lordship also carries the countervailing function of nurture.
#### Subchannel A. Worship and Obedient Submission
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: Worshippers direct humble obedience to the one recognized as deity and organizing religious authority.
- Active motifs: worship as an act `ء ل ه B001`; the worshipped deity `ء ل ه B001`; obedient religious submission `د ي ن B001`; humble worship and service `ع ب د B003`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 `ٱللَّهِ` and 1:2 `لِلَّهِ` (`ء ل ه`); 1:4 `ٱلدِّينِ` (`د ي ن`); 1:5 `نَعْبُدُ` (`ع ب د`)
- Synthesis: The scene joins the divine object of worship to the human act of worship, then specifies the relation as obedience enacted with humility. Religion is active here as an ordering relation rather than merely a named doctrine.
#### Subchannel C. Petition for Aid and Direction
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: Dependents seek assistance and gentle direction toward an upright path, with compassionate benefaction as the answering function.
- Active motifs: requested aid and cooperation `ع و ن B001`; gentle guidance `ه د ي B001`; the traversable path `ص ر ط B001`; uprightness and rectitude `ق و م B008`; mercy enacted as benefaction `ر ح م B001`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 and 1:3 `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ` / `ٱلرَّحِيمِ` (`ر ح م`); 1:5 `نَسْتَعِينُ` (`ع و ن`); 1:6 `ٱهْدِنَا`, `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ`, and `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` (`ه د ي`, `ص ر ط`, `ق و م`); 1:7 `صِرَٰطَ` (`ص ر ط`)
- Synthesis: Aid supplies the enabling force, guidance supplies the directing action, the path supplies its object, and straightness supplies the desired state. Mercy turns the request-response structure into beneficent rather than merely administrative rule.

### 2. Benefaction, Welfare, and Praise
- Semantic invariant: Good is conferred through compassion and maintenance, stabilized as welfare, and answered by praise or recognition.
- Surface relation: direct; 1:1 and 1:3 `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ`, 1:2 `ٱلْحَمْدُ ... رَبِّ`, 1:5 `نَسْتَعِينُ`, and 1:7 `أَنْعَمْتَ`.
- Surprising reach: Tenderness expands into practical provisioning, repair, guardianship, and structural support, while praise can function either as gratitude or as a claim imposed by a patron.
#### Subchannel A. Compassionate Provision and Nurture
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A caregiver feels tenderness, nurtures a dependent over time, guards its affairs, and supplies or repairs what sustains a good condition.
- Active motifs: tenderness and compassion `ر ح م B001`; beneficent mercy `ر ح م B001`; conferred blessing `ن ع م B001`; good condition `ن ع م B001`; progressive nurture `ر ب ب B002`; guardianship and care `ق و م B004`; provision for dependents `غ ي ر B001`; repair of their equipment `غ ي ر B001`; practical aid `ع و ن B001`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 and 1:3 `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ` / `ٱلرَّحِيمِ` (`ر ح م`); 1:2 `رَبِّ` (`ر ب ب`); 1:5 `نَسْتَعِينُ` (`ع و ن`); 1:6 `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` (`ق و م`); 1:7 `غَيْرِ` and `أَنْعَمْتَ` (`غ ي ر`, `ن ع م`)
- Synthesis: Compassion is the motive, progressive nurture and guardianship are the continuing operations, and food, equipment, and assistance are the practical means. Blessing names the resulting condition of the cared-for dependent.

### 3. Declared Identity and Public Presence
- Semantic invariant: Names, utterances, reputation, and bearing make identity or commitment socially legible.
- Surface relation: direct; 1:1 `بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ`, 1:2 `ٱلْحَمْدُ`, 1:4 `ٱلدِّينِ`, 1:6 `ٱهْدِنَا`, and 1:7 `غَيْرِ ... أَنْعَمْتَ`.
- Surprising reach: The naming and address field reaches from divine invocation into assent, grammatical qualification, poetic exchange, and the ceremonial presence of a reputed captain.
#### Subchannel A. Naming, Invocation, and Direct Address
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A speaker identifies the addressee by name and turns that name into invocation, oath, or direct appeal.
- Active motifs: name and naming `س م و B005`; a namesake or comparable bearer of a name `س م و B005`; the divine name `ء ل ه B002`; oath and vocative formulas using that name `ء ل ه B002`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 `بِسْمِ` and `ٱللَّهِ` (`س م و`, `ء ل ه`); 1:2 `لِلَّهِ` (`ء ل ه`)
- Synthesis: Naming establishes reference, while oath and vocative use convert reference into an active relation between speaker and addressee. The namesake motif defines identity comparatively by asking who else can bear the same designation.
#### Subchannel C. Reputation and Poetic Exchange
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Public speech circulates praise or blame until it becomes a person's audible reputation and social eminence.
- Active motifs: good reputation or public voice `س م و B008`; social eminence `س م و B001`; exchanged praise or satire in verse `ه د ي B011`; the person of many praised qualities `ح م د B003`; soliciting praise for favors `ح م د B005`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 `بِسْمِ` (`س م و`); 1:2 `ٱلْحَمْدُ` (`ح م د`); 1:6 `ٱهْدِنَا` (`ه د ي`)
- Synthesis: Poetic exchange is the transmitting action, praise and satire supply opposite evaluations, and public voice is the resulting social state. Eminence describes the vertical effect of a reputation that others cannot easily match.
#### Subchannel D. The Reputed Captain's Ceremonial Bearing
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A maritime captain appears as a composed, publicly reputed leader whose station is marked by ceremonial scent.
- Active motifs: captain or chief of sailors `ر ب ب B017`; perfume brazier `ع ب د B012`; calm and dignified bearing `ه د ي B010`; good public reputation `س م و B008`; an elevated visible figure `س م و B002`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 `بِسْمِ` (`س م و`); 1:2 `رَبِّ` (`ر ب ب`); 1:5 `نَعْبُدُ` (`ع ب د`); 1:6 `ٱهْدِنَا` (`ه د ي`)
- Synthesis: The captain supplies the leading role, calm bearing and visibility make that role perceptible, reputation carries it beyond the immediate crew, and the perfume brazier gives the scene a ceremonial object of status.

### 4. Orientation, Passage, and Breakdown
- Semantic invariant: Movement is organized by a course, a guide, and a state of passage, then tested by deviation, engulfment, or loss of mobility.
- Surface relation: direct; 1:2 `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, 1:5 `نَعْبُدُ`, 1:6 `ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`, and 1:7 `صِرَٰطَ ... ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`.
- Surprising reach: The straight-road scene expands into landmarks, centerlines, and leading animals, while swallowing, mounting, cutting, and burial supply a shape-motion analogy for passage into disappearance.
#### Subchannel B. The Visible Leader at the Front
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A conspicuous leader takes the foremost position and draws a herd or procession behind it.
- Active motifs: the foremost guide `ه د ي B003`; the leading animal followed by the group `م ل ك B008`; an elevated visible figure `س م و B002`; a gathered wild-cattle herd `ر ب ب B014`; a herd of wild donkeys `ع و ن B006`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 `بِسْمِ` (`س م و`); 1:2 `رَبِّ` (`ر ب ب`); 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ` (`م ل ك`); 1:5 `نَسْتَعِينُ` (`ع و ن`); 1:6 `ٱهْدِنَا` (`ه د ي`)
- Synthesis: Visibility allows the leader to be tracked, the foremost position supplies direction, and the gathered animals supply the following participants. Leadership is thus rendered as a spatial relation before it becomes a social one.
#### Subchannel C. Engulfing and Penetrating Passage
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: An entity enters, cuts through, or is swallowed by another body or medium until it becomes hidden.
- Active motifs: swallowing and disappearance through a passage `ص ر ط B002`; disappearance or burial within another medium `ض ل ل B002`; a stallion rising over and entering the herd `س م و B003`; a cutting, penetrating sword `ص ر ط B003`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 `بِسْمِ` (`س م و`); 1:6 and 1:7 `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ` / `صِرَٰطَ` (`ص ر ط`); 1:7 `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` (`ض ل ل`)
- Synthesis: Swallowing, mounting, cutting, and burial share a directional transition across a boundary. Their common outcome is loss of exterior visibility, whether the receiving medium is a throat, a body, or the earth.

### 5. Time, Measure, and Final Reckoning
- Semantic invariant: Time is made intelligible by bounded spans and celestial positions, by pace within a duration, and by a culminating event of judgment.
- Surface relation: direct; 1:1 `بِسْمِ`, 1:2 `ٱلْحَمْدُ ... ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ`, and 1:6 `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`.
- Surprising reach: The day expands from ordinary daylight into zenith, lunar marking, personal pace, prolonged residence, and finally the event in which creation rises for sovereign accounting.
#### Subchannel A. Daylight, Zenith, and Celestial Marking
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A daylight span is located by the sun at zenith and by visible signs in the overhead sky.
- Active motifs: bounded daylight `ي و م B001`; the sun standing at midday `ق و م B017`; the overhead sky `س م و B004`; a lunar station `ن ع م B007`; a distinguishing sign `ع ل م B002`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 `بِسْمِ` (`س م و`); 1:2 `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` (`ع ل م`); 1:4 `يَوْمِ` (`ي و م`); 1:6 `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` (`ق و م`); 1:7 `أَنْعَمْتَ` (`ن ع م`)
- Synthesis: Daylight supplies the bounded interval, zenith gives it an internal midpoint, and sky signs provide a larger celestial coordinate system. Time is read through the positions of bodies that appear to stand overhead.

### 6. Property, Measure, and Legitimate Transfer
- Semantic invariant: Goods and obligations move between parties through ownership, valuation, replacement, compensation, gift, or offering.
- Surface relation: indirect; 1:1 and 1:2 `ٱللَّهِ`, 1:1 and 1:3 `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ` / `ٱلرَّحِيمِ`, 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ ... ٱلدِّينِ`, 1:5 `نَعْبُدُ`, 1:6 `ٱهْدِنَا ... ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`, and 1:7 `أَنْعَمْتَ ... غَيْرِ ... ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`.
- Surprising reach: Financial debt and equal coinage join a livestock market and its stray property, while compensation and substitution offer a regulated alternative to uncompensated loss.
#### Subchannel C. Reciprocal Gift and Beneficent Transfer
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A gift moves toward a valued recipient as an expression of affection, mercy, or benefit.
- Active motifs: affectionate gift-giving `ه د ي B004`; conferred benefit `ن ع م B001`; mercy enacted as benefaction `ر ح م B001`; transfer into another's ownership `م ل ك B002`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 and 1:3 `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ` / `ٱلرَّحِيمِ` (`ر ح م`); 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ` (`م ل ك`); 1:6 `ٱهْدِنَا` (`ه د ي`); 1:7 `أَنْعَمْتَ` (`ن ع م`)
- Synthesis: Affection supplies the social motive, the gift supplies the transferred object, ownership change completes the act, and benefaction names its intended effect on the recipient.
#### Subchannel D. Consecrated Offering
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Livestock or goods are selected from owned wealth and directed toward the sanctuary as an act of worship.
- Active motifs: consecrated offering to the sanctuary `ه د ي B005`; livestock wealth `ن ع م B005`; the worshipped deity `ء ل ه B001`; worshipful submission `ع ب د B003`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 `ٱللَّهِ` and 1:2 `لِلَّهِ` (`ء ل ه`); 1:5 `نَعْبُدُ` (`ع ب د`); 1:6 `ٱهْدِنَا` (`ه د ي`); 1:7 `أَنْعَمْتَ` (`ن ع م`)
- Synthesis: Livestock supplies the transferable material, consecration fixes its destination, and worship supplies the relation that distinguishes an offering from an ordinary gift.

### 7. Household Continuity and Protected Belonging
- Semantic invariant: Kinship is produced and maintained through gestation, nurture, marriage, covenant, and defense of household boundaries.
- Surface relation: indirect; 1:1 and 1:3 `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ` / `ٱلرَّحِيمِ`, 1:2 `رَبِّ`, 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ ... ٱلدِّينِ`, 1:5 `نَعْبُدُ`, 1:6 `ٱهْدِنَا`, and 1:7 `غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ`.
- Surprising reach: The mercy root opens into womb and blood relation, while guidance becomes both bride-conveyance and the protected status of a refugee under covenant.
#### Subchannel A. Kinship, Fosterage, and Continuing Care
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A child belongs to a network of blood and acquired kin whose caregiver assumes responsibility for nurture and continuity.
- Active motifs: close kinship `ر ح م B002`; maintaining kin ties `ر ح م B002`; stepchild `ر ب ب B005`; foster caregiver `ر ب ب B005`; progressive child-rearing `ر ب ب B002`; guardianship `ق و م B004`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 and 1:3 `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ` / `ٱلرَّحِيمِ` (`ر ح م`); 1:2 `رَبِّ` (`ر ب ب`); 1:6 `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` (`ق و م`)
- Synthesis: Blood relation supplies one form of belonging, fosterage supplies another, and guardianship turns both into continuing obligations. Progressive nurture is the operation that converts formal relation into lived care.
#### Subchannel B. Gestation, Recent Birth, and Postpartum Vulnerability
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: The womb contains developing offspring, birth produces a newly delivered mother and new life, and the postpartum body remains vulnerable to pain or retained afterbirth.
- Active motifs: the womb as organ `ر ح م B003`; the womb as seedbed and vessel `ر ح م B003`; recently delivered ewe `ر ب ب B009`; freshness or newness after emergence `ر ب ب B009`; postpartum uterine pain `ر ح م B004`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 and 1:3 `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ` / `ٱلرَّحِيمِ` (`ر ح م`); 1:2 `رَبِّ` (`ر ب ب`)
- Synthesis: The womb supplies enclosure and generative function, newness marks successful emergence, and the recently delivered ewe embodies the maternal participant. Postpartum pain preserves the bodily cost that follows the transition.
#### Subchannel C. Marriage and Bride-Conveyance
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A marriage contract establishes a new household relation and the bride is formally conveyed to her spouse, accompanied by affectionate transfer.
- Active motifs: marriage contract `م ل ك B004`; bride conveyed to her husband `ه د ي B006`; kinship relation `ر ح م B002`; affectionate gift `ه د ي B004`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 and 1:3 `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ` / `ٱلرَّحِيمِ` (`ر ح م`); 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ` (`م ل ك`); 1:6 `ٱهْدِنَا` (`ه د ي`)
- Synthesis: Contract creates the relation, conveyance enacts the transition between households, kinship names the resulting bond, and the gift supplies a reciprocal social gesture around the transfer.
#### Subchannel D. Covenant, Refuge, and Legal Protection
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A named outsider or dependent receives protected standing through covenant, certification, and a rule of compensatory redress.
- Active motifs: covenant and protected association `ر ب ب B011`; protected refugee or bearer of safe conduct `ه د ي B007`; certification or delegated oath `د ي ن B007`; indemnity `غ ي ر B002`; naming and legal identification `س م و B005`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 `بِسْمِ` (`س م و`); 1:2 `رَبِّ` (`ر ب ب`); 1:4 `ٱلدِّينِ` (`د ي ن`); 1:6 `ٱهْدِنَا` (`ه د ي`); 1:7 `غَيْرِ` (`غ ي ر`)
- Synthesis: Naming identifies the protected person, covenant establishes the relation, certification makes it publicly actionable, and indemnity defines the consequence of violating that protection.
#### Subchannel E. Protective Jealousy and Household Defense
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Attachment to household and kin activates vigilance, bodily firmness, and visible anger against intrusion.
- Active motifs: protective jealousy over family `غ ي ر B004`; frowning irritability `غ ض ب B007`; bodily strength and firmness `ع ب د B007`; kin group or community `ق و م B001`; close kinship `ر ح م B002`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 and 1:3 `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ` / `ٱلرَّحِيمِ` (`ر ح م`); 1:5 `نَعْبُدُ` (`ع ب د`); 1:6 `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` (`ق و م`); 1:7 `غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` (`غ ي ر`, `غ ض ب`)
- Synthesis: Kinship supplies the protected circle, jealousy detects a threatened boundary, firmness supplies defensive capacity, and the frown externalizes readiness to resist.

### 8. Herd Formation, Wildlife, and Dispersal
- Semantic invariant: Animal life is organized by gathering, classification, reproduction, leadership, pursuit, and scattering.
- Surface relation: indirect; 1:1 and 1:3 `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ` / `ٱلرَّحِيمِ`, 1:2 `رَبِّ ... ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ`, 1:5 `نَسْتَعِينُ`, and 1:7 `أَنْعَمْتَ ... ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`.
- Surprising reach: The animal field ranges from owned livestock and coherent herds to ostrich, falcon, hyena, and ownerless strays, with womb imagery joining wildlife to generation.
#### Subchannel B. Hunt, Flight, and Scattered Routes
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Hunters set out, the pursued group takes flight, and a once-coherent body disperses into people, animals, or routes going in different directions.
- Active motifs: hunters setting out `س م و B006`; flight, rout, and dispersal `ن ع م B008`; scattered groups and diverging roads `ع ب د B010`; a human company `ق و م B001`; loss of a thing from its holder `ض ل ل B003`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 `بِسْمِ` (`س م و`); 1:5 `نَعْبُدُ` (`ع ب د`); 1:6 `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` (`ق و م`); 1:7 `أَنْعَمْتَ` and `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` (`ن ع م`, `ض ل ل`)
- Synthesis: Departure initiates pursuit, flight breaks collective formation, and diverging roads spatialize the resulting separation. Loss names the outcome from the perspective of the group or holder that can no longer retain what fled.
#### Subchannel C. Wildlife Generation and Exposure
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: New animal life emerges from reproductive enclosure into an open field populated by birds, herd animals, and predators.
- Active motifs: womb as generative vessel `ر ح م B003`; ostrich `ن ع م B006`; falcon or hawk `ع ل م B006`; male hyena `ع ل م B007`; recently delivered ewe `ر ب ب B009`; exposed ownerless animal `ض ل ل B005`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 and 1:3 `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ` / `ٱلرَّحِيمِ` (`ر ح م`); 1:2 `رَبِّ` and `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` (`ر ب ب`, `ع ل م`); 1:7 `أَنْعَمْتَ` and `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` (`ن ع م`, `ض ل ل`)
- Synthesis: The womb and recently delivered ewe supply generation, the ostrich supplies a large bird of the open field, and falcon and hyena supply predatory animal classes. The stray marks the vulnerability that follows emergence beyond enclosure and custody.

### 9. Habitation, Water, and the Living Landscape
- Semantic invariant: Habitation becomes viable when authority, residence, water, weather, and vegetation converge into a sustaining place.
- Surface relation: indirect; 1:1 `بِسْمِ`, 1:2 `ٱلْحَمْدُ ... رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, 1:4 `ٱلدِّينِ`, 1:5 `نَسْتَعِينُ`, 1:6 `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`, and 1:7 `أَنْعَمْتَ ... غَيْرِ`.
- Surprising reach: The field moves from a city defined by obedience into a camp secured by stored water, then upward to layered clouds, persistent rain, gentle wind, and enduring plants.
#### Subchannel C. Sky, Rain, Wind, and Enduring Growth
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: The overhead sky forms layered rainclouds, weather persists, a gentle southern wind moves through, and watered vegetation remains green.
- Active motifs: overhead sky `س م و B004`; cloud and rain `س م و B004`; layered raincloud `ر ب ب B008`; persistent cloud or wind `ر ب ب B007`; gentle south wind `ن ع م B009`; enduring green plant `ر ب ب B012`; old established palm `ع و ن B004`; irrigation `غ ي ر B001`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 `بِسْمِ` (`س م و`); 1:2 `رَبِّ` (`ر ب ب`); 1:5 `نَسْتَعِينُ` (`ع و ن`); 1:7 `أَنْعَمْتَ` and `غَيْرِ` (`ن ع م`, `غ ي ر`)
- Synthesis: Layered cloud gathers the water-bearing material, persistence and wind describe weather movement, irrigation delivers water to the ground, and green plant and old palm display growth across both season and duration.

### 11. Bodily Integrity, Posture, and Injury
- Semantic invariant: A living body is read through stance, proportion, strength, visible markers, pain, and loss of function.
- Surface relation: indirect; 1:1 and 1:3 `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ` / `ٱلرَّحِيمِ`, 1:2 `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, 1:4 `مَٰلِكِ`, 1:5 `نَعْبُدُ ... نَسْتَعِينُ`, 1:6 `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`, and 1:7 `أَنْعَمْتَ ... ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ`.
- Surprising reach: Uprightness and firmness describe healthy stature, yet the same standing field also holds an intact-looking blind eye, diseased legs, swollen tissue, and a body halted by weakness.
#### Subchannel B. Lesion, Swelling, and Localized Pain
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A specific body part bears a visible lesion or swelling and becomes the site of persistent pain.
- Active motifs: cleft in the upper lip `ع ل م B004`; swelling around the eye `غ ض ب B006`; pain lodged in an organ `ق و م B019`; postpartum uterine pain `ر ح م B004`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 and 1:3 `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ` / `ٱلرَّحِيمِ` (`ر ح م`); 1:2 `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` (`ع ل م`); 1:6 `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` (`ق و م`); 1:7 `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` (`غ ض ب`)
- Synthesis: Cleft and swelling provide visible signs, while localized pain provides the felt state. The postpartum uterus extends the same diagnostic scene to an internal reproductive organ.
#### Subchannel D. Bodily Markers of Maturity and Reproduction
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Bodily marks, sexual maturity, reproductive capacity, and age make a living body classifiable.
- Active motifs: pubic hair `ع و ن B007`; womb as reproductive organ `ر ح م B003`; middle age `ع و ن B002`; mature balanced strength `ع و ن B005`; a distinguishing bodily mark `ع ل م B002`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 and 1:3 `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ` / `ٱلرَّحِيمِ` (`ر ح م`); 1:2 `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` (`ع ل م`); 1:5 `نَسْتَعِينُ` (`ع و ن`)
- Synthesis: Visible hair and marks supply external indicators, the womb supplies reproductive capacity, and age with mature strength locates the body within a life stage. Classification emerges from the conjunction of form, function, and development.

### 12. Conflict, Anger, and Defensive Order
- Semantic invariant: Opposition escalates through rivalry and anger into combat, retaliation, defense, defeat, or compensatory settlement.
- Surface relation: direct; 1:2 `ٱلْحَمْدُ`, 1:5 `نَعْبُدُ ... نَسْتَعِينُ`, 1:6 `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`, and 1:7 `غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ ... ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`.
- Surprising reach: Competitive striving becomes repeated war and mutual resistance, while anger over loss can terminate either in vengeance or in indemnity; hardness also becomes literal defensive material.
#### Subchannel A. Rivalry, Resistance, and Repeated War
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: Competitors seek superiority, rise against one another, and turn repeated opposition into organized combat.
- Active motifs: rivalry and competitive matching `س م و B007`; war fought repeatedly `ع و ن B003`; mutual resistance and combat `ق و م B014`; defiance and opposition `غ ض ب B003`; resolve and rising to action `ق و م B003`; the publicly praised champion `ح م د B003`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 `بِسْمِ` (`س م و`); 1:2 `ٱلْحَمْدُ` (`ح م د`); 1:5 `نَسْتَعِينُ` (`ع و ن`); 1:6 `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` (`ق و م`); 1:7 `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` (`غ ض ب`)
- Synthesis: Competition supplies the initiating comparison, resolve converts comparison into action, resistance supplies reciprocal force, and repeated war stabilizes opposition as an enduring social frame. Praise records the reputation won within that contest.
#### Subchannel C. Rout and Collective Dispersal
- Reading type: latent/lexical
- Scene or process: A group under pressure loses formation, turns away, and scatters along divergent routes.
- Active motifs: defeat and dispersal `ن ع م B008`; scattered groups and routes `ع ب د B010`; loss from a holder `ض ل ل B003`; mutual confrontation `ق و م B014`; hunters setting out in pursuit `س م و B006`
- Ayah anchors: 1:1 `بِسْمِ` (`س م و`); 1:5 `نَعْبُدُ` (`ع ب د`); 1:6 `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` (`ق و م`); 1:7 `أَنْعَمْتَ ... ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` (`ن ع م`, `ض ل ل`)
- Synthesis: Confrontation supplies pressure, pursuit supplies direction, defeat breaks cohesion, and divergent routes express the spatial result. Loss describes the same event from the perspective of the former group.
