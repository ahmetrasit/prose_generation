The central task is synthesis: discover what several findings together allow
the reader to understand about the focus ayah. Existing HFT readings and

roles, processes, surprises and changed readings as intellectual substance.
They are not lists of related keywords to summarize. Examine original lexical
and Quranic evidence before endorsing an upstream interpretation.

Read the focus, its syntax, the surah movement and Quran usage panels together.
Then compose the strongest readings. Several subnetworks can be components of

do not veto a latent image because its physical object is not literally in the
ayah. Conversely, many roots sharing a broad field does not establish a scene.
Make the operations and their interpretive consequence intelligible.
