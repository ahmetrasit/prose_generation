## Headline verdict

No variant is fully equivalent to the default across both ayat. The default remains the broadest discovery inventory, but its accumulation of remote branches makes it the least efficient continuation surface. `s100-hft-5.5-high-xhigh` is the closest overall alternative and the best balanced condition across both ayat. `s100-hft-5.6-sol-xhigh-writer-xhigh` is clearly stronger than default as an edited 100:1 continuation surface, but clearly weaker in 100:2 coverage. `s100-hft-5.5-xhigh-writer-xhigh` is consistently cleaner and more selective, but its coverage loss is substantive rather than merely stylistic.

## Baseline profile

The default uses the same basic argument shape in both ayat:

1. Establish the oath syntax, morphology, referent uncertainty, and cadence.
2. Develop the local lexical mechanism.
3. Re-read that mechanism through later ayat.
4. Activate increasingly remote root branches while repeatedly preserving the local meaning.

For 100:1, this produces a very broad network: oath suspension; participial identity; boundary crossing; audible exertion; heat, spark, dust, ash, and residue; preparation and recurrence; hard route and watering cycle; center penetration; desire; service versus ingratitude; bodily witness; interior disclosure; and the remote rising-breath split. For 100:2, it produces local causation and cadence plus latent fire, trace, concealment, dawn, dust, center penetration, witness, exhumation, interior assay, enablement versus value, lot selection, corrosion, and tender emergence.

This breadth is the default's main strength and its main weakness. It is highly useful as an inventory for later selection, but it does not rank central, secondary, and remote claims firmly enough in the prose itself. Evidence labels usually do that work, leaving an upstream writer with a large editorial burden.

## Ayah 100:1

### `s100-hft-5.5-high-xhigh`

The variant preserves the default's central sequence: compressed oath, delayed answer, participial and underdetermined subject, adversarial boundary pressure, cadence into `dabhan`, variant contrasts, heat-to-spark development, trace, prepared recurrence, internal drive, and redress before a knowing authority. Its argument is easier to follow because each paragraph generally advances one mechanism and returns to the literal running-and-breathing scene.

It omits or greatly reduces the default's seasonal hard-route and watering-cycle reading, the service-versus-ingratitude contrast, the full ash/residue/value question, the distributed witness-to-interior-exhalation sequence, and the remote rising-breath split. These are mostly remote findings, but their removal means the output is not interpretively equivalent.

The redress line is comparatively well integrated here: the movement toward an authority, witness, and expert knowledge becomes one evidential trajectory rather than several separated observations. Readability and upstream usefulness are stronger than default. Depth is weaker.

Evidence handling is mostly disciplined and exclusions are explicit. One status issue remains: the heat-and-spark pressure is labeled `bundle-traceable` even though the prose's threshold mechanism is a synthesis from lexical branches and later context, which the default more cautiously treats as inference.

**Verdict:** stronger editorially, weaker in total coverage; mixed rather than equivalent.

### `s100-hft-5.5-xhigh-writer-xhigh`

This condition retains the local grammar, referent uncertainty, variants, boundary-to-center movement, prepared recurrence, audible exertion, heat/ash residue, and desire as inward drive. The prose is the cleanest and most compact 100:1 rendering after the 5.6 condition.

The reduction is substantial. It drops the default's dust-to-disclosure and bodily-witness network, hard route and water cycle, redress before authority, service and ingratitude, the later interior-exhalation development, and the rising-breath split. It also compresses preparation, counting, and recurrence into one paragraph, losing some distinctions among sequence, habit, road, service, and reckoning.

Its evidence surface is strong about rejected or omitted material and correctly keeps most remote claims as inference. The findings index is appended to the evidence file despite the evidence-only instruction, and friction acknowledges that compromise. This is transparent but not contract-equivalent to the cleaner three-file resolution used by `5.5-high`.

**Verdict:** clearly weaker in depth and finding coverage, though stronger in readability. This is a useful synopsis, not an equivalent commentary.

### `s100-hft-5.6-sol-xhigh-writer-xhigh`

This is the strongest 100:1 synthesis. It preserves nearly all of the default's useful local and mid-distance structure: oath compression, participial identity, boundary and center, cadence and exertion, both variants, heat/spark/dust, recurrence and return, counted expenditure, bodily witness and inner assay, desire, service/ingratitude, and contagious appetite. The final synthesis ranks these as direction, environmental trace, time/result, and movement-to-inner-cause, making the material easier to continue upstream.

It does not fully preserve the default's seasonal hard-route/watering-cycle construction, redress before the expert authority, or the rising-breath split. It also adds a distinct acceleration-and-environmental-degradation line. That addition is explicitly contained as analogy and marked inference, but it is a real interpretive addition rather than a restatement of default.

Evidence discipline is strongest here. The map distinguishes traceable, mixed, inferential, and counter-reading statuses; it publishes live alternatives for the major later-ayah activations; and it explicitly notes when inter-ayah rows call those activations weak or of no direct clarifying value. The main residual risk is volume: the prose is still dense, and the environmental-degradation line is more speculative than the central argument.

**Verdict:** clearly stronger than default for readable synthesis and upstream continuation, but not coverage-equivalent because it omits several remote default branches and adds a new ecological one.

## Ayah 100:2

### `s100-hft-5.5-high-xhigh`

This is the closest 100:2 equivalent. It preserves the default's local causal sequence, participial morphology, marked form, intensive reading variant, accusative manner, cadence, hapax concentration, latent fire, dawn threshold, trace and dust, witness, center penetration, successful ignition, moral interiorization, forced disclosure, internal decay, lot imagery, and tender emergence.

Its prose is better organized around a stable progression: movement produces contact; contact reveals latent fire; the resulting flash becomes trace, evidence, and disclosure. Remote readings are repeatedly returned to that mechanism.

At the margins it differs from default. It adds a more explicit word-wound or lineage-damage connection and develops tender emergence through wet-soil ecology. It weakens the default's especially useful distinction between visible activation and actual benefit: the default states that a spark proves activation while usefulness remains a separate result. That distinction is not carried with equal clarity here.

The evidence table, counter-evidence, and rejection of the plan-to-irreversible-action synthesis are strong. A few assertions, especially concealment/intent and some sequence claims, are classified more directly than their weak or inferential sources warrant, but the overall discipline is high.

**Verdict:** near-equivalent in breadth and slightly stronger as edited prose. It is the strongest 100:2 variant, but its added and missed marginal findings prevent strict equivalence.

### `s100-hft-5.5-xhigh-writer-xhigh`

The core is strong: sequential `fa`, agent morphology, manner accusative, cadence, latent fire, successful ignition, trace, partial disclosure, and internal damage all remain. The final question, what an impact makes the interior become outwardly, gives the ayah a clear continuation hook.

The condition deliberately removes the marked rare form, reading variant, hapax concentration, lot selection, tender emergence, activation-versus-value distinction, several whole-sura moral links, and much of the dust/center network. These omissions are explicitly listed in the evidence file, so the loss is controlled rather than accidental.

Two smaller regressions matter. The prose says the oath catches "the same unnamed group" in a new action, while the default preserves uncertainty over whether the feminine plural beats identify one continuing group or parallel groups. It also calls the flash a "beautiful light moment," an evaluative phrase not needed by the evidence and not separately grounded.

The evidence surface is admirably explicit about omitted candidates and live alternatives, but findings are mixed into the evidence file to resolve the output-count conflict.

**Verdict:** clearly weaker than default in interpretive depth. It is stronger as a compact editorial outline, but the difference is substantive, not merely stylistic.

### `s100-hft-5.6-sol-xhigh-writer-xhigh`

This condition gives the clearest local 100:2 account. It retains sequential/result `fa`, the marked and rare participial form, manner and impact cadence, hapax concentration, latent ignition, scored concealment, successful operation, witness, and forced disclosure. The prose stays tightly centered on hidden capacity becoming visible result through contact.

Its interpretive perimeter is much thinner than default. It omits the reading variant, dust and center development, usefulness-versus-activation distinction, lot selection, tender emergence, internal corrosion, and most links to ingratitude and desire. The evidence file explicitly records these as containment decisions, which makes the reduction auditable but does not restore equivalence.

The evidence map is clear and candid. One classification is slightly overconfident: "the run becomes the condition of the spark" is called bundle-traceable, although the stronger energy-transfer or necessary-condition reading goes beyond the safer grammatical claim that `fa` marks sequence/result. Elsewhere the condition handles inference and rejected planning material carefully.

**Verdict:** stronger than default for local clarity, clearly weaker for depth and discovery value. It is not an adequate replacement when the upstream continuation needs the full interpretive inventory.

## Evidence and friction comparison

All four conditions identify the same two material prompt frictions:

- The governing task allows exactly three files, while the embedded specification asks for prose, evidence, findings, and friction as four separate artifacts.
- `word_analysis` and QAC count the ayah's orthographic/morphological units differently, leaving an upstream word identity unmatched unless `word_morpheme_spans` is used.

All conditions correctly use `word_morpheme_spans` and do not invent repaired identities.

`s100-hft-5.5-high-xhigh` handles the output-count conflict most consistently: it omits the findings index from both ayat and explains why, preserving evidence-only files. The default is internally inconsistent: 100:1 refuses to mix findings into evidence, while 100:2 inserts them there. Both writer conditions append findings to evidence and disclose the compromise in friction. This is transparent, but less faithful to the explicit evidence-only constraint.

For evidence discipline, the default and 5.6 condition provide the richest counter-evidence, while 5.5-high provides the cleanest contract handling. The default's main evidence risk is not missing support but the number of remote, singly synthesized inferences promoted into prose. The variants generally improve containment, though each has a few status classifications that are more confident than its own supporting record.

## Continuation usefulness

- **Best maximal research inventory:** `s100-hft-default-xhigh`.
- **Best balanced continuation across both ayat:** `s100-hft-5.5-high-xhigh`.
- **Best 100:1 continuation surface:** `s100-hft-5.6-sol-xhigh-writer-xhigh`.
- **Best compact synopsis:** `s100-hft-5.5-xhigh-writer-xhigh`.

No single variant should replace default on a claim-for-claim equivalence premise. If one available condition must be selected wholesale, `s100-hft-5.5-high-xhigh` is the strongest choice because its 100:2 is close to the default's depth and its 100:1 sacrifices mostly remote branches in exchange for materially better readability. If ayat can be selected independently, the strongest edited pair is 100:1 from `s100-hft-5.6-sol-xhigh-writer-xhigh` and 100:2 from `s100-hft-5.5-high-xhigh`, with the default retained as the discovery ledger.
