# S100:1-2 HFT Output Comparison: `default` vs `5.5-high`

## 1. Executive verdict

**Conditionally equivalent.**

`5.5-high` is effectively equivalent to `default` for **100:2** and is somewhat better at exposing evidentiary limits. It is **not equivalent for 100:1 when the Layer 2 output is meant to preserve the widest useful finding set for Layer 3/channel work**. On 100:1 it produces cleaner, shorter prose, but drops several interpretive lines that are useful downstream.

This is therefore not a safe drop-in replacement across the sample. `5.5-high` is usable, and sometimes preferable for reader-facing synthesis, but its compression is not uniform across ayahs.

## 2. Per-ayah comparison

### 100:1

The `5.5-high` prose is more controlled and easier to follow. It moves in a clear sequence from oath syntax, to the identity-neutral participle, to boundary pressure, audible breath, ignition, trace, repetition, inward drive, and evidentiary/redress implications. At roughly 851 words versus 1,222 for `default`, it removes substantial repetition and speculative accumulation.

It retains the main local and HFT gains: the compressed oath and delayed answer; uncertainty about the runners' identity; running as boundary-charged movement without prematurely moralizing the runners; the sound and bodily cost of `dabhan`; breath as a heat threshold before 100:2's spark; movement as trace; habituated or repeatable motion; inwardly transmitted drive; and movement as evidence before a knowing authority.

The loss is material for deep discovery. `default` additionally develops exertion into ash or sterile residue, counted pulses and accumulated output, a seasonal hard-route/watering complex, repeated service contrasted with human ingratitude, distributed bodily and terrestrial witness, the return from surface motion to opened interiors, and dependence signaled through the distant rising-breath association. Some of these are correctly pruned by `5.5-high`: its refusal to promote the water/soft-earth line is a reasonable signal-to-noise improvement. But the service/ingratitude, exertion/value, and embodied-witness lines are not merely noise; they are plausible channel seeds that disappear from the `5.5-high` output.

`5.5-high` also contributes one genuine synthesis that `default` does not. It combines the root's remote redress-before-an-authority branch with 100:7's witness and 100:11's expert knower, then reads the oath-sworn motion as a complaint or body of evidence carried toward judgment. `default` contains the components separately: running to seek redress, bodily/terrestrial traces as witness, and later expert knowledge. It never joins them into the same juridical-evidentiary model. This is a distinct `5.5-high` finding, not merely a shorter restatement of `default`.

**Verdict for 100:1:** weaker but still usable. Equivalent for concise reader-facing commentary; not equivalent for maximum lexical/HFT depth or downstream candidate retention.

### 100:2

The two variants are much closer. Their prose lengths are nearly identical, and both preserve the same central mechanism: prior motion produces impact; impact extracts latent fire; the spark becomes a visibility threshold, trace, witness, and small-scale model of forced disclosure.

Both also retain the difficult peripheral material with appropriate containment: successful kindling, penetration toward the gathered center, inward damage, lots or arrow-shafts, and tender plant emergence. Both keep the primary fire-striking syntax in place and document rejection of the stronger "planned action becomes irreversible" claim.

There are small shifts rather than a clear depth loss. `default` gives more explicit space to repeated impacts and to the distinction between visible activation and actual benefit. `5.5-high` gives more explicit space to the moral interior opened by 100:6-8 and presents the baseline/context/outlier sequence more cleanly. `default` supplies a separate findings index inside the evidence file, which is operationally useful but violates the stated evidence-only boundary; `5.5-high` keeps a cleaner evidence surface while still preserving the substantive candidates.

**Verdict for 100:2:** equivalent overall. `5.5-high` is at least as useful for prose and evidence review; `default` has a slight advantage only if the embedded findings index is consumed directly by downstream tooling.

## 3. Dimension comparison

| Dimension | Judgment |
| --- | --- |
| Prose readability | **`5.5-high` better on 100:1; roughly tied on 100:2.** It is more linear, repeats fewer lexical formulas, and keeps secondary readings visibly subordinate to the local scene. |
| Lexical depth | **`default` better overall because of 100:1.** On 100:2 the variants are effectively tied; on 100:1 `default` preserves several additional semantic and inter-ayah mechanisms. |
| Surprising findings retained | **`default` retains more findings on 100:1, but `5.5-high` is not a strict subset; tied on 100:2.** `5.5-high` prunes both weak outliers and some productive channel candidates, while uniquely synthesizing redress, witness, and expert knowledge into an evidentiary movement toward judgment. |
| Before/after HFT use | **`5.5-high` slightly better.** Its prose and evidence more consistently distinguish the ayah alone, the change introduced by later ayahs, and the status of outliers. `default` often integrates these layers richly but less visibly. |
| Evidence surface quality | **`5.5-high` cleaner; `default` sometimes more exhaustive.** `5.5-high` uses compact claim/status/support mapping and records exclusions. `default` 100:2 adds a useful findings index, but mixes that apparatus into a file described as evidence-only. |
| Friction/coverage honesty | **`5.5-high` better.** It explicitly records omitted 100:1 material, the uncertain publication threshold for remote outliers, rejected planning language, and unresolved span/address problems. `default` is candid about prompt conflicts but less explicit about selection costs on 100:1. |
| Downstream usefulness | **`default` better for broad Layer 3/channel discovery; `5.5-high` equal or better for clean synthesis.** The deciding issue is the non-uniform loss of candidate diversity on 100:1. |

## 4. Specific examples

- `default/100_1.prose.md` turns breath, spark, and dust into successive records left by force; `5.5-high/100_1.prose.md` retains the trace idea but does not carry the full distributed-witness development.
- `default/100_1.prose.md` connects repeated service and expended bodily power with the later charge of ingratitude. That potentially valuable relation is absent from the `5.5-high` prose.
- `default/100_1.prose.md` explores ash, useless spark, and residue as a question about the value left by intense expenditure. `5.5-high` preserves breath-to-ignition but loses this result-versus-value distinction.
- `5.5-high/100_1.evidence.md` explicitly says the water/soft-earth countercurrent was excluded because it lacked a sufficiently strong return to the local running sense. This is better editorial discipline than `default`'s extended seasonal watering route.
- `5.5-high/100_1.prose.md` uniquely joins seeking redress before an authority with 100:7's witness and 100:11's expert knowledge. `default/100_1.prose.md` has all three ingredients in separate developments but does not synthesize motion as evidence carried toward judgment.
- `5.5-high/100_1.evidence.md` marks later-ayah transformations and counterevidence more clearly; `default/100_1.evidence.md` offers more candidate coverage but a flatter list of traceable claims and inferences.
- Both `100_2.prose.md` files retain latent fire, visibility, trace/witness, forced disclosure, internal corrosion, lots, and plant-tip emergence. This is strong evidence of equivalence for 100:2.
- Both `100_2.evidence.md` files preserve the rejection of the stronger planning/irreversibility reading while retaining successful-kindling material on narrower support.
- `default/100_2.evidence.md` provides stable finding IDs and a dedicated findings index, which is useful for channel handoff. `5.5-high/100_2.evidence.md` is cleaner and more contract-faithful, but downstream consumers would need to derive an index from its claim map.

## 5. Recommendation

Use **`default` for the next Layer 2 runs when the outputs will feed Layer 3 or channel discovery**. Across these two ayahs it has the safer recall profile, and the 100:1 comparison shows that `5.5-high` can remove productive findings along with genuine noise.

Use `5.5-high` as a synthesis or presentation variant, or where a clean evidence surface and readable prose matter more than preserving every viable channel seed. It is a strong choice for 100:2-like outputs, but this sample does not support treating it as globally equivalent to `default`.
