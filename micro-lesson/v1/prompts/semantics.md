its family, its semantic branch, its form and its use.

root identity, documented alternative etymology and mere sound resemblance distinct.

qualification when teaching it. A source variant is not automatically a semantic
feature present in this occurrence.

Form recognition and its local semantic contribution can be one coherent lesson
with several ontology annotations. Split branch, form and contrast observations
only when they add independent learning value. Do not expand an entire dictionary
entry into a sequence of repetitive comments or teach an unattested paradigm.

paragraph. Cite them as supporting comparisons and explain their own local meanings.

qualified alternative, teach the qualified relationship only if it fits clearly
within a short lesson. Otherwise defer the affected claim with the missing question.
