## Focus expression and identity

At 1:1 the exact text is **بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ**. QAC gives `1:1:1:1` بِ (preposition), `1:1:1:2` سْمِ (noun, lemma ٱسْم, root س م و, genitive), `1:1:2:1` ٱللَّهِ (proper noun, genitive, QAC root ء ل ه), `1:1:3:1–2` ٱل + رَّحْمَٰنِ (determiner and adjective, root ر ح م, masculine singular genitive), and `1:1:4:1–2` ٱل + رَّحِيمِ (same features, different form). The source reading is *bismillâhi'r-rahmâni'r-rahîm*. `bi-` is the relation; `ism` is the name. Allah is the name's dependent; the two following forms describe the same referent. The four genitive endings must be explained by their respective attachment, not all attributed directly to `bi-`. No finite verb is written at 1:1. The context supplies an understood action; its exact wording is interpretive.

`word-root-analyses.json` gives ٱسْم a primary س م و → `root_000745/B005` and a documented و س م → `root_001650/B001` alternative restricted to the name word. It gives ٱللَّه a primary ء ل ه → `root_000047/B002` and a documented و ل ه → `root_005296/B001` alternative. The latter derivation is attested in the indexed Tehzîb source; the و ل ه branch by itself documents emotional bewilderment/longing, not the divine name. Neither alternative replaces the QAC primary identity. The 1:1 ر ح م forms belong to the mercy field `root_000552/B001`; the organ `B003`, kinship `B002`, and postnatal pain `B004` are related dictionary material, not asserted lexical meanings of either 1:1 adjective. `root-source-index.json` locates other attested roots in cited verses. Use exact word selectors, not Latin spelling alone.
