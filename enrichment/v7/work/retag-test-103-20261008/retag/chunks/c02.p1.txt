[ELMALILI:11:114-115/r1] verses 11:114 | “Zulaf” is the plural of “zulfa,” whose Arabic broken plural denotes at least three; with two parts of the day, this indicates five prayers, including three during the night’s approaches to day. «“Zülef” “zülfe”nin cem‘i ve Arabîde cem‘in ekalli üç olduğu için bununla ikisi gündüz tarafında, üçü de geceden olmak üzere tam beş vakit namaz emredilmiş olduğu zâhirdir»
[ELMALILI:11:114-115/r10] verses 11:114 | Each prayer is a good deed that continually erases prior minor sins; the five daily prayers expiate sins between them when major sins are avoided, as a hadith states. «beş vakit namaz arada vâki‘olan küçük günahlara keffaret olur»
[ELMALILI:11:114-115/r11] verses 11:114 | A hadith says one prayer expiates what lies between it and the next, provided major sins are avoided. «namaz, namaza kadar aralarındakine keffarettir, kebâirden ictinab ettikçe»
