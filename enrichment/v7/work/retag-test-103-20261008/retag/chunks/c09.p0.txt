# Notes

## 12:42
[SAMIN-DURR:12:42/r2] verses 12:42 | If ظن retains its ordinary sense, it suits Joseph only under independent reasoning; revelation is certain and would make the cupbearer the one who thought, unless ظن means certainty. «يعني أنه إن كان الظن على بابه فلا يستقيم إسناده إلى يوسف»
[SAMIN-DURR:12:42/r3] verses 12:42 | Qatada keeps the ordinary sense of ظن and attributes it to Joseph because dream interpretation is conjectural. «ذهب قتادة، فإنه قال: " الظن هنا على بابه لأن عبارة الرؤيا ظن»
[SAMIN-DURR:12:42/r4] verses 12:42 | منهما may modify ناج or be a circumstantial phrase from the relative pronoun; attaching it to ناج would misleadingly suggest someone escaped from the two. «على أنه حال من الموصول»
[SAMIN-DURR:12:42/r5] verses 12:42 | Abu al-Baqa rejects attaching منهما to ناج because that is not the intended sense. «قال أبو البقاء: " ولا يكون متعلقا ب " ناج " لأنه ليس المعنى عليه "»
[SAMIN-DURR:12:42/r6] verses 12:42 | The pronoun in فأنساه refers to the cupbearer; the alternative reference to Joseph is weak. «والضمير في " فأنساه " يعود على الشرابي. وقيل: على يوسف، وهو ضعيف.»
[STUDYQURAN:12:42/r1] verses 12:42 | Joseph asked the wine carrier to mention him to Egypt’s king. «Joseph told the wine carrier to mention him to the king of Egypt (Kl; cf. Genesis 40:14–15)»
[STUDYQURAN:12:42/r2] verses 12:42 | Satan made the wine carrier forget Joseph, prolonging Joseph’s imprisonment. «Satan caused him to forget to make mention to his lord; that is, he forgot to mention Joseph to the king (Kl)»
[STUDYQURAN:12:42/r3] verses 12:42 | Most commentators say Joseph remained imprisoned for seven years. «a total of seven years according to most commentators (IK, N)»
[STUDYQURAN:12:42/r4] verses 12:42 | An alternative translation says Satan made Joseph forget to remember God, meaning Joseph forgot God when asking the carrier to mention him. «Satan caused him to forget remembering his Lord,” in which case it would mean that at the particular moment when Joseph told the wine carrier»
[STUDYQURAN:12:42/r5] verses 12:42 | Ibn ʿAbbās says Joseph would have been released earlier had he remembered God when asking the wine carrier to mention him. «According to Ibn ʿAbbās, had Joseph remembered God when he told the wine carrier»
[STUDYQURAN:12:42/r6] verses 12:42 | The Prophet prayed for Joseph and said his request to be mentioned to the king resulted in a longer imprisonment. «the Prophet is reported to have said, “God have mercy on Joseph. Had it not been for a statement he made»
[STUDYQURAN:7:11/r36] verses 7:16, 6:43, 8:48, 16:63, 27:24, 29:38, 6:68, 12:42, 18:63, 58:19, 22:52 | Satan makes evil deeds seem fair, causes people to forget God’s commands, and even attempts to alter revelation. «causing them to forget God and His commandments (6:68; 12:42; 18:63; 58:19); and even by attempting to alter revelation (22:52).»
[TAB-FULL:v13p169#2/r1] verses 12:42 | Yusuf knew the surviving cupbearer would be saved and asked him to tell his master of Yusuf's unjust imprisonment without a crime; rabb means the king or master. «عن ابن إسحاق، قال: " قال، يعني لنبو: {اذكرني عند ربك} [يوسف: 42] : أي اذكر للملك الأعظم مظلمتي، وحبسي في غير شيء»
[TAB-FULL:v13p169#2/r2] verses 12:42 | Qatada understands the word ظن as uncertainty, since dream interpretation involves conjecture; al-Tabari rejects this application to Yusuf. «عن قتادة، " {وقال للذي ظن أنه ناج منهما اذكرني عند ربك} [يوسف: 42] وإنما عبارة الرؤيا بالظن، فيحق الله ما يشاء ويبطل ما يشاء»
[TAB-FULL:v13p169#2/r3] verses 12:42 | Al-Tabari rejects reading Yusuf's prediction as conjecture: prophets cannot give a definite report that might prove false without undermining the proof of their message; Yusuf's firm prediction shows he knew it would occur. «فأما الأنبياء فغير جائز منها أن تخبر بخبر عن أمر أنه كائن ثم لا يكون»
[TAB-FULL:v13p169#2/r4] verses 12:42 | Ibrahim al-Taymi reports that a companion at the prison door asked what to convey, and Yusuf asked to be remembered before his master, the king. «عن إبراهيم التيمي، " أنه لما انتهى به إلى باب السجن قال له صاحب له. حاجتك أوصني بحاجتك»
[TAB-FULL:v13p176#2/r1] verses 12:42 | Mujahid says Yusuf was not mentioned to the king until the king's dream; Satan made Yusuf forget his Lord and seek relief from the king, so he remained imprisoned as punishment. «عن ابن أبي نجيح، عن مجاهد، قال: " قال له: {اذكرني عند ربك} [يوسف: 42] قال: فلم يذكره حتى رأى الملك الرؤيا»
[TAB-FULL:v13p176#2/r2] verses 12:42 | Ibn Ishaq instead says Satan made the cupbearer forget to tell the king about Yusuf; Yusuf's imprisonment then continued as punishment for asking the cupbearer to remember him. «عن ابن إسحاق، قال: " لما خرج، يعني الذي ظن أنه ناج منهما، رد على ما كان عليه، ورضي عنه صاحبه. فأنساه الشيطان ذكر ذلك للملك»
[TAB-FULL:v13p176#2/r3] verses 12:42 | Qatada and Wahb report a seven-year prison term; Wahb lists it alongside Job's seven-year affliction and Nebuchadnezzar's seven years among beasts. «عن قتادة، قال: «لبث يوسف في السجن سبع سنين»»
[TAB-FULL:v13p176/r1] verses 12:42 | Al-Tabari reads Satan's causing forgetfulness as a lapse in Yusuf, who forgot to appeal to God; this lengthened his confinement and was painful punishment. «عن مالك بن دينار، قال: " لما قال يوسف للساقي: {اذكرني عند ربك} [يوسف: 42] قال: قيل: يا يوسف اتخذت من دوني وكيلا»
[TAB-FULL:v13p176/r2] verses 12:42 | Malik ibn Dinar reports a divine reproach to Yusuf for taking an intermediary besides God, followed by Yusuf's lament that affliction had distracted his heart. «عن مالك بن دينار، قال: " لما قال يوسف للساقي: {اذكرني عند ربك} [يوسف: 42] قال: قيل: يا يوسف اتخذت من دوني وكيلا»
[TAB-FULL:v13p176/r3] verses 12:42 | Several reports attributed to the Prophet say Yusuf's request that the cupbearer remember him caused him to remain in prison longer; Hasan wept and applied the lesson to people who turn to others in hardship. «قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم: «لولا أنه» يعني يوسف «قال الكلمة التي قال ما لبث في السجن طول ما لبث»»
[TAB-FULL:v13p176/r4] verses 12:42 | Ibn Abbas transmits a Prophetic report that Yusuf would not have remained so long had he not sought relief from someone besides God. «عن ابن عباس، قال: قال النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم: «لو لم يقل يوسف» يعني الكلمة التي قال «ما لبث في السجن»
[TAB-FULL:v13p176/r5] verses 12:42 | Qatada reports a Prophetic saying that Yusuf's seeking intercession through the cupbearer prolonged his imprisonment as a punishment. «عن قتادة، قال: ذكر لنا أن نبي الله صلى الله عليه وسلم كان يقول: «لولا أن يوسف استشفع على ربه ما لبث في السجن طول ما لبث»
