# Notes

## 12:42
[DURR-FULL:v4p542/r7] verses 12:42, 12:47 | Qatadah says Joseph left home at seventeen, spent seven years in the well and seven in prison, and stored food for seven years; they think he met his father then. «عن قتادة رضي الله عنه قال: ذهب يوسف عليه السلام وهو ابن سبع عشرة ولبث في الجب سبعا»
[DURR-FULL:v4p542/r8] verses 12:42 | Abu al-Malih transmits Joseph's prison prayer asking God, by the face of Jacob, for relief, an easy way out, and provision from an unexpected source. «عن أبي المليح رضي الله عنه قال: كان دعاء يوسف عليه السلام في السجن اللهم إن كان خلق وجهي»
[DURR-FULL:v4p542/r9] verses 12:42 | A report says Gabriel taught Joseph to seek relief from every worldly and otherworldly distress, unexpected provision, forgiveness, firm hope in God, and detachment from everyone else. «عن عبد الله مؤذن الطائف قال: جاء جبريل عليه السلام إلى يوسف عليه السلام فقال: يا يوسف»
[FARRA:v2p45#2/r4] verses 12:42 | One explanation says Satan made Joseph forget to mention and seek help from God. «يقول: أنسى الشيطان يوسف أن يجعل ذكره ومستغاثه إلى الله.»
[FARRA:v2p45#2/r5] verses 12:42 | Another explanation says Satan made the cupbearer forget to mention Joseph. «ويقال: أنسى الشيطان الساقي أن يذكر أمر يوسف.»
[FARRA:v2p45#2/r6] verses 12:42 | ذكر ربه means Joseph remembered his master. «وقوله: (ذكر ربه) يقول: ذكر يوسف لمولاه.»
[FARRA:v2p45#2/r7] verses 12:42 | Reports say Joseph remained in prison seven years after an initial five; بضْع means fewer than ten. «ذكروا أنه لبث سبعا بعد خمس والبضع ما دون العشرة»
[IBNABIHATIM:v7p2148#2/r1] verses 12:42 | The Prophet prayed for God's mercy on Joseph and said that, had Joseph not asked to be remembered by his master, he would not have remained imprisoned so long. «عن أبي هريرة قال : قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم : يرحم الله يوسف ، لولا الكلمة التي قالها : اذكرني عند ربك»
[IBNABIHATIM:v7p2148#2/r2] verses 12:42 | Hasan also attributes this saying to the Prophet, then weeps that people turn to others when trouble comes. «عن الحسن ، قال : قال نبي الله صلى الله عليه وسلم : رحم الله يوسف ، لولا كلمته ما لبث في السجن طول ما لبث»
[IBNABIHATIM:v7p2148#2/r3] verses 12:42 | Joseph asked the prisoner who would be freed to mention him to the king, but the man did not do so until the king saw his dream. «عن مجاهد قوله : اذكرني عند ربك للذي نجا من صاحبي السجن ، يوسف يقول : اذكرني للملك فلم يذكره حتى راى الملك الرؤيا»
[IBNABIHATIM:v7p2148#2/r4] verses 12:42 | Joseph asked the king to be told of his unjust imprisonment. «عن عبد الله بن أبي نجيح ، عن مجاهد اذكرني عند ربك اي : الملك الاعظم ، مظلمتي وحبسي في غير شيء»
[IBNABIHATIM:v7p2148#2/r5] verses 12:42 | Hasan reports a rebuke to Joseph for relying on an intermediary; Joseph answered that distress had overwhelmed him and words escaped his tongue, and he wept. «عن مالك بن دينار ، عن الحسن قال : لما قال يوسف للساقي : اذكرني عند ربك قيل له يا يوسف»
[IBNABIHATIM:v7p2148#2/r6] verses 12:42 | The cupbearer forgot the king whom Joseph had asked him to remember. «عن مجاهد قوله : فانساه الشيطان ذكر ربه وذلك ان يوسف انساه الشيطان ذكر ربه وامره بذكر الملك»
[IBNABIHATIM:v7p2148#2/r7] verses 12:42 | After the cupbearer returned to his post and regained his master's favor, he forgot the king Joseph had asked him to mention, and Joseph remained imprisoned for years. «عن محمد بن اسحاق ، عن عبد الله بن أبي نجيح ، عن مجاهد ، قال : فلما خرج الساقي رد علي ما كان عليه»
[IBNABIHATIM:v7p2149/r1] verses 12:42 | God reminded Joseph of rescue from the pit and his brothers' plot, asking why he forgot God and remembered a human; Joseph blamed anguish and a spoken word, and God kept him imprisoned seven years. A similar report is attributed to al-Thawri. «عن انس قال : اوحى إلى يوسف»
[IBNABIHATIM:v7p2149/r2] verses 12:42 | Joseph was punished three times; the second punishment was prolonged imprisonment for asking the cupbearer to remember him. «عن خصيف ، عن عكرمة ، عن ابن عباس قال : عوقب يوسف ثلاث مرات الثانية : فلقوله : اذكرني عند ربك فلبث في السجن بضع سنين»
[IBNABIHATIM:v7p2149/r3] verses 12:42 | “A few years” can mean three to nine years. «عن مجاهد فلبث في السجن بضع سنين قال : البضع من ثلاثة إلى تسعة»
[IBNABIHATIM:v7p2149/r4] verses 12:42 | One interpretation of “a few years” is twelve years. «عن ابن عباس في قوله : فلبث في السجن بضع سنين قال : اثنتا عشرة سنة»
[IBNABIHATIM:v7p2149/r5] verses 12:42 | Tawus and al-Dahhak give the duration as fourteen years. «عن محمد بن عمر ، عن طاوس ، والضحاك بن مزاحم في قوله : فلبث في السجن بضع سنين قالا : اربع عشرة سنة»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v12p278/r1] verses 12:42 | The man Joseph expected to survive was the cupbearer; “thought” here approaches certainty because Joseph trusted his interpretation. «والظن هنا مستعمل في القريب من القطع لأنه لا يشك في صحة تعبيره الرؤيا»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v12p278/r2] verses 12:42 | Joseph asks the cupbearer to tell his master, the king of Egypt, about Joseph's case and injustice. «مظلمته ، أي اذكرني لربك ، أي سيدك»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v12p278/r3] verses 12:42 | The pronouns in “Satan made him forget” may refer to the cupbearer: he forgot Joseph and the king; or to Joseph: Satan made Joseph forget God, a different remembrance. «أي أنسى الشيطان الذي نجا أن يذكره لربه»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v12p278/r4] verses 12:42 | Both readings may be intended: Joseph forgot to ask God for deliverance, which caused the cupbearer to forget him; this becomes divine reproach for relying on people. «ولعل كلا الاحتمالين مراد»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v12p278/r5] verses 12:42 | This double reading tactfully reports the lapse, since an allusive statement is gentler than a direct one. «لأن الكلام الموجه في المعاني الموجهة ألطف من الصريح»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v12p278/r6] verses 12:42 | Biḍʿ means a number from three to nine. «البضع : من الثلاث إلى التسع»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v12p278/r7] verses 12:42 | The account implies the prison had no reliable register, oversight, or periodic report to the king; Islamic law addressed this neglect by requiring a judge to review prisoners daily. «وهذا من الإهمال والتهاون بحقوق الناس وقد أبطله الإسلام»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v3p243/r5] verses 12:35, 12:42, 12:70, 12:77 | Ibn Abbas counts three slips by Joseph: the episode with her, asking the cupbearer to remember him, and accusing the brothers of theft, which prompted the reply about Benjamin. «عثر يوسف عليه السلام ثلاث عثرات»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v3p246/r10] verses 12:42 | Qatada takes ẓanna in its ordinary sense because dream interpretation remains conjectural. «على بابه لأن عبارة الرؤيا ظن»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v3p246/r11] verses 12:42 | The author says Qatada’s reading requires taking Joseph’s قضي الأمر to mean only that his interpretation was finished, not that the future was divinely settled. «ولا يترتب قول قتادة إلا بأن يكون معنى قوله قضي الأمر أي قضي كلامي وقلت ما عندي وتم»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v3p246/r12] verses 12:42 | Another grammatical reading makes the man who was promised release the subject of ظن: joy elevated him to expect rescue. «أن يكون ظن مسندا إلى الذي قيل له: إنه يسقي ربه خمرا»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v3p246/r9] verses 12:42 | Here ẓanna can mean certainty, since Joseph had said the outcome was settled, though it had not yet happened. «بمعنى اليقين، لأن ما تقدم من قوله: قضي الأمر يلزم ذلك»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v3p247/r1] verses 12:42 | Joseph asks the king’s cupbearer to remember him by reporting his knowledge and standing, his wrongful imprisonment, or both. «فيحتمل أن يريد أن يذكره»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v3p247/r10] verses 12:42 | Al-Farra says biḍʿ is used with tens but not with hundreds or thousands. «لا يذكر مع مائة ولا مع ألف»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v3p247/r11] verses 12:42 | Reports say Joseph had already served five years, then received seven years for asking the cupbearer to remember him; another report gives two additional years. «فكانت مدة سجنه اثنتي عشرة سنة، وقيل: عوقب ببقاء سنتين»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v3p247/r12] verses 12:42 | Al-Hasan attributes to the Prophet the saying that Joseph would not have remained imprisoned so long if not for his word; Hasan wept and warned that people turn to others when trouble comes. «قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم: «لولا كلمته ما لبث في السجن طول ما لبث»»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v3p247/r2] verses 12:42 | One explanation makes the pronoun in أنساه refer to Joseph: he forgot to appeal to God and relied on a human; reports say Gabriel reproached him and this prolonged the prison term. «نسي في ذلك الوقت أن يشتكي إلى الله»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v3p247/r3] verses 12:42 | A reported divine address says Joseph took an intermediary besides God and would therefore remain imprisoned longer. «يا يوسف اتخذت من دوني وكيلا لأطيلن حبسك»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v3p247/r4] verses 12:42 | Ibn Ishaq makes the pronoun refer to the cupbearer, who forgot to mention Joseph to his master; رب then means the king. «أي نسي ذكر يوسف عند ربه، فأضاف الذكر»
