# Verses
- 12:36: وَدَخَلَ مَعَهُ ٱلسِّجْنَ فَتَيَانِ ۖ قَالَ أَحَدُهُمَآ إِنِّىٓ أَرَىٰنِىٓ أَعْصِرُ خَمْرًۭا ۖ وَقَالَ ٱلْءَاخَرُ إِنِّىٓ أَرَىٰنِىٓ أَحْمِلُ فَوْقَ رَأْسِى خُبْزًۭا تَأْكُلُ ٱلطَّيْرُ مِنْهُ ۖ نَبِّئْنَا بِتَأْوِيلِهِۦٓ ۖ إِنَّا نَرَىٰكَ مِنَ ٱلْمُحْسِنِينَ
- 12:41: يَٰصَىٰحِبَىِ ٱلسِّجْنِ أَمَّآ أَحَدُكُمَا فَيَسْقِى رَبَّهُۥ خَمْرًۭا ۖ وَأَمَّا ٱلْءَاخَرُ فَيُصْلَبُ فَتَأْكُلُ ٱلطَّيْرُ مِن رَّأْسِهِۦ ۚ قُضِىَ ٱلْأَمْرُ ٱلَّذِى فِيهِ تَسْتَفْتِيَانِ
- 12:42: وَقَالَ لِلَّذِى ظَنَّ أَنَّهُۥ نَاجٍۢ مِّنْهُمَا ٱذْكُرْنِى عِندَ رَبِّكَ فَأَنسَىٰهُ ٱلشَّيْطَٰنُ ذِكْرَ رَبِّهِۦ فَلَبِثَ فِى ٱلسِّجْنِ بِضْعَ سِنِينَ
- 12:43: وَقَالَ ٱلْمَلِكُ إِنِّىٓ أَرَىٰ سَبْعَ بَقَرَٰتٍۢ سِمَانٍۢ يَأْكُلُهُنَّ سَبْعٌ عِجَافٌۭ وَسَبْعَ سُنۢبُلَٰتٍ خُضْرٍۢ وَأُخَرَ يَابِسَٰتٍۢ ۖ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلْمَلَأُ أَفْتُونِى فِى رُءْيَٰىَ إِن كُنتُمْ لِلرُّءْيَا تَعْبُرُونَ
- 12:50: وَقَالَ ٱلْمَلِكُ ٱئْتُونِى بِهِۦ ۖ فَلَمَّا جَآءَهُ ٱلرَّسُولُ قَالَ ٱرْجِعْ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ فَسْـَٔلْهُ مَا بَالُ ٱلنِّسْوَةِ ٱلَّٰتِى قَطَّعْنَ أَيْدِيَهُنَّ ۚ إِنَّ رَبِّى بِكَيْدِهِنَّ عَلِيمٌۭ

# Notes

## 12:42
[MAWARDI-FULL:v3p39/r18] verses 12:42 | Wahb says Joseph was imprisoned seven years and Job endured affliction for seven years. «وقال وهب : حبس يوسف سبع سنين , ومكث أيوب في البلاء سبع سنين»
[MAWARDI-FULL:v3p39/r19] verses 12:42 | Al-Kalbi says Joseph spent seven years imprisoned after the five years in which he had asked the cupbearer to remember him. «قال الكلبي : حبس سبع سنين بعد الخمس السنين التي قال فيها { اذكرني عند ربك»
[MAWARDI-FULL:v3p39/r2] verses 12:42 | Alternatively, Joseph only conjectured that one prisoner would be saved, without certainty. «أنه ظن ذلك من غير يقين»
[MAWARDI-FULL:v3p39/r3] verses 12:42 | Joseph spoke conjecturally because dream interpretation itself is conjectural. «لأن عبارة الرؤيا بالظن فلذلك لم يقطع به , قاله قتادة»
[MAWARDI-FULL:v3p39/r4] verses 12:42 | Another explanation is that Joseph doubted whether the prisoners had told the truth about their dreams. «أنه لم يتيقن صدقهما في الرؤيا فكان الظن في الجواب لشكه في صدقهما»
[MAWARDI-FULL:v3p39/r5] verses 12:42 | Joseph asked the saved cupbearer to mention him to his master, the king al-Walid ibn al-Rayan, hoping to be released. «أي عند سيدك يعني الملك الأكبر الوليد بن الريان تأميلا للخلاص إن ذكره عنده»
[MAWARDI-FULL:v3p39/r6] verses 12:42 | Satan made the saved prisoner forget to mention Joseph to his master until the king later dreamed. «أن الذي نجا منهما أنساه الشيطان ذكر يوسف عند سيده حتى رأى الملك الرؤيا قاله محمد بن إسحاق»
[MAWARDI-FULL:v3p39/r7] verses 12:42 | Alternatively, Satan caused Joseph to forget invoking God and relying on Him. «أن يوسف أنساه الشياطن ذكر ربه في الاستغاثة به والتعويل عليه»
[MAWARDI-FULL:v3p39/r8] verses 12:42 | A report from Abu Hurayra through Abu Salama asks God to have mercy on Joseph and says he would not have stayed so long had he not asked the cupbearer to remember him. «روى أبو سلمة عن أبي هريرة قال : قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم ( رحم الله يوسف لولا الكلمة التي قال : اذكرني عند ربك ما لبث في السجن ما لبث»
[MAWARDI-FULL:v3p39/r9] verses 12:42 | Joseph was punished with a longer imprisonment for asking the saved prisoner to remember him; had he remembered God, God would have freed him. «قال ابن عباس : عوقب يوسف بطول السجن بضع سنين لما قال للذي نجا منهما اذكرني عند ربك»
[MUJAHID:v1p316#2/r1] verses 12:42, 12:43 | Mujahid says Joseph asked the released prisoner to mention him to the king; the prisoner forgot until the king dreamed. Satan made Joseph forget remembrance of his Lord and direct his plea to the king, so Joseph remained imprisoned for years because he had said to mention him to the king. «عن مجاهد ﴿ وقال للذي ظن أنه ناج منهما اذكرني عند ربك ﴾ يوسف يقول هذا للذي نجا من السجن»
[MUJAHID:v1p317#2/r1] verses 12:42 | Mujahid defines bid' as a span of three to nine years. «عن مجاهد قال ﴿ فلبث في السجن بضع سنين ﴾ وقال البضع ما بين الثلاث إلى التسع»
[MUQATIL:12:41-42/r3] verses 12:42 | Joseph asked the cupbearer to mention him to the king, but Satan made Joseph forget to ask God; God kept him imprisoned as punishment for relying on the king. «{ اذكرني عند ربك } ، يعني سيدك، فإنه يسرني أن يخرجني من السجن، يقول الله:»
[MUQATIL:12:41-42/r4] verses 12:42 | Joseph served seven years before the incident and five more years as punishment, totaling twelve years; the additional years fulfill 12:35. «فذلك قوله: { فلبث في السجن بضع سنين } [آية: 42]، يعني خمس سنين حتى رأى الملك،»
[MUQATIL:12:41-42/r5] verses 12:42 | The Prophet said Joseph would have left prison that very day had he called upon God rather than seeking help from the king. «وقال النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم:»
[MUQATIL:12:41-42/r6] verses 12:42 | Gabriel rebuked Joseph for relying on a servant after God had made him beloved to his father and protected him from sin; Joseph prayed for forgiveness through his forefathers. «فكيف تركتنى واستغثت بعبد مثلك؟ فلما سمع يوسف ذكر الخطيئة، قال: يا إلهى،»
[NASAFI-FULL:v2p190#2/r1] verses 12:42 | If the interpretation came through reasoning, Joseph was the one who thought the cupbearer would be saved; if by revelation, the cupbearer was the one who thought so; alternatively, ẓanna means certainty. «الظان هو يوسف عليه السلام أن كان تأويله بطريق الاجتهاد»
[NASAFI-FULL:v2p190#2/r2] verses 12:42 | Joseph asks the cupbearer to describe him and his case to the king in hope of release from his predicament. «صفنى عند المك بصفتى وقص عليه قصتى لعله يرحمني»
[NASAFI-FULL:v2p190#2/r3] verses 12:42 | Satan made the cupbearer forget to mention Joseph to the king, or made Joseph forget God when he entrusted his affair to another. «فأنسى الشرابى ﴿ ذكر ربه ﴾ أن يذكره لربه أو عند ربه»
[NASAFI-FULL:v2p190#2/r4] verses 12:42 | A prophetic report says God have mercy on Joseph: had he not asked the cupbearer to remember him, he would not have remained in prison seven years; most say the years were seven, while biḍʿ means three through nine. «وفي الحديث رحم الله اخى يوسف لو لم يقل اذكرنى عند ربك لما لبث فى السجن سبعا»
[QASHANI:12:41-42#2/r1] verses 12:42 | Asking the cupbearer to remember him means seeking stable spiritual existence through love; intoxicated love raises spirit to unity and heart to spirit, but both remain veiled in divine light rather than annihilated. «اطلب الوجود في مقام الروح بالمحبة والاستقرار فيه»
[QASHANI:12:41-42#2/r2] verses 12:42 | Remaining at that level can produce ego and transgression; Satanic illusion makes Joseph forget annihilation because he seeks the spirit's station and retains a remainder of self. «ومن الوقوف في هذا المقام ينشأ الطغيان والأنائية»
[QASHANI:12:41-42#2/r3] verses 12:42 | A prophetic report says God have mercy on Joseph: had he not asked the cupbearer to remember him, he would not have remained imprisoned for years. «أشار النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم بقوله: " رحم الله أخي يوسف»
[QASHANI:12:41-42#2/r4] verses 12:42 | Alternatively, illusion forgets the love-messenger when love dominates in bewildered witnessing; the lover forgets creation and self in unity until annihilation ends and divine life restores differentiation. «أو أنسى شيطان الوهم المقهور الممنوع المحجوب عن جناب الحق»
[QASHANI:12:41-42#2/r5] verses 12:42 | The prison's duration is the spiritual journey; the later visions of cattle and grain symbolize changes in soul and bodily qualities during seclusion and return. «ثم لما انتهى فناؤه بالانغماس في بحر الهوية والانطماس»
[QASHANI:12:41-42/r4] verses 12:42 | When both faculties function through God rather than themselves, confinement ends and subsistence begins, though a trace of ego remains. «لكونهما بالله حينئذ لا بنفسهما وانتهى زمان الخلوة بابتداء زمان البقاء»
[QURTUBI-FULL:12:42#2/r1] verses 12:42 | A report says a slave should not call someone my lord, but should say my master; the reason given is that Rabb is an agreed divine name while Sayyid's status as a divine name is disputed. «لا يقل العبد ربي وليقل سيدي "
لأن الرب من أسماء الله تعالى المستعملة بالاتفاق واختلف»
[QURTUBI-FULL:12:42#2/r2] verses 12:42 | Ibn al-Arabi says this form of address may have been permitted under Joseph's law. «وقال ابن العربي: يحتمل أن يكون ذلك جائزا في شرع يوسف عليه السلام»
[QURTUBI-FULL:12:42#2/r3] verses 12:42 | One reading takes the pronoun in فأنساه to mean Joseph: Satan made him forget God when he asked the cupbearer for help, so reliance on a creature brought a longer imprisonment. «أحدهما: أنه عائد إلى يوسف عليه السلام، أي أنساه الشيطان ذكر الله عز وجل»
[QURTUBI-FULL:12:42#2/r4] verses 12:42 | Abd al-Aziz ibn Umayr al-Kindi tells that Gabriel rebuked Joseph for seeking a human's help, and God swore to keep him imprisoned for years; Joseph said he did not mind if God was pleased. «قال عبد العزيز بن عمير الكندي: دخل جبريل على يوسف النبي عليه السلام في السجن»
[QURTUBI-FULL:12:42#2/r5] verses 12:42 | Another Gabriel story asks who saved Joseph from his brothers, the pit, sexual temptation, and women's schemes; Joseph answers God, then admits he trusted a creature and asks for mercy. «وروي أن جبريل عليه السلام جاءه فعاتبه عن الله تعالى في ذلك وطول سجنه، وقال»
[QURTUBI-FULL:12:42#2/r6] verses 12:42 | Abu Salama reports the Prophet saying God have mercy on Joseph: without the request remember me before your master, Joseph would not have remained imprisoned for years. «وروى أبو سلمة عن أبي هريرة قال: قال رسول الله»
[QURTUBI-FULL:12:42#3/r1] verses 12:42 | A report says the Prophet asked Abu Bakr how long bid' is; Abu Bakr answered three to seven, and the Prophet told him to raise the wager. «وكم البضع» فقال: ما بين الثلاث إلى السبع»
[QURTUBI-FULL:12:42#3/r10] verses 12:42 | Maqatil, through Mujahid from Ibn Abbas, says Joseph remained five years and a bid' in prison. «وقال مقاتل عن مجاهد عن ابن عباس قال: مكث يوسف في السجن خمسا وبضعا»
[QURTUBI-FULL:12:42#3/r11] verses 12:42 | Bid' derives from cutting: the penalty period was seven or nine years after five already passed, rather than the total time in prison. «واشتقاقه من بضعت الشيء أي قطعته، فهو قطعة من العدد، فعاقب الله يوسف بأن حبس»
