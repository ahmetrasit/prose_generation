[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p279/r2] verses 12:42 | Al-Zamakhsharī makes Joseph the one who thought the cupbearer would be released; Abū Ḥayyān says this is difficult if the interpretation came by conjecture because the decree is final, unless the phrase only means Joseph has finished speaking. «وقال الزمخشري: الظان هو يوسف عليه السلام إن كان تأويله بطريق الاجتهاد»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p279/r3] verses 12:42 | Joseph may have asked the cupbearer to tell the king about his knowledge and standing, or to report his unjust imprisonment. «فالمعنى أن يوسف عليه السلام قال لساقي الملك حين علم أنه سيعود»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p279/r4] verses 12:42 | Abū Ḥayyān sees Joseph's request as permissible practical help, with God making it a cause of relief; he compares it to Jesus seeking helpers and the Prophet seeking a guard. «وهذا من يوسف على سبيل الاستعانة والتعاون في تفريج كربه، وجعله بإذن الله»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p279/r5] verses 12:42 | Abū Ḥayyān prefers that Joseph wanted to lead the cupbearer to faith, as he had sought the guidance of both prisoners. «والذي أختاره أن يوسف إنما قال لساقي الملك: اذكرني عند ربك ليتوصل»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p279/r6] verses 12:42 | The pronoun in فأنساه refers to the cupbearer; ذكر ربه means remembering Joseph for his master, and the loose relation permits the possessive. «والضمير في فأنساه عائد على الساقي، ومعنى ذكر ربه: ذكر يوسف لربه»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p279/r7] verses 12:42 | Satan's whisper distracted the cupbearer until he forgot Joseph's request; God prolonged Joseph's imprisonment to increase his reward. «وإنساء الشيطان له بما يوسوس إليه من اشتغاله حتى يذهل عما قال له يوسف»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p279/r8] verses 12:42 | A few years is left vague; reports give seven or twelve, or count seven after the prisoners left or two years, while the apparent sense is Joseph's full prison term. «وبضع سنين مجمل، فقيل: سبع، وقيل: اثنا عشر. والظاهر أن قوله: فلبث»

[ALUSI-FULL:v12p247#2/r1] verses 12:42 | بضع is commonly three to nine; Qatada says three to nine, Mujahid three to seven, and Abu Ubayda one to ten. Al-Farra says it accompanies tens, not hundreds or thousands; the word derives from cutting. «البضع ما بين الثلاث إلى التسع كما روي عن قتادة وعن مجاهد أنه من الثلاث إلى السبع»
[ALUSI-FULL:v12p247#2/r2] verses 12:42 | Most accounts make Joseph's whole imprisonment seven years, with two years after the request to the cupbearer; another view makes the post-request stay seven years after five earlier years, totaling twelve. «وهي مدة لبثه كلها فيما صححه البعض وسنتان منها كانت مدة لبثه بعد ذلك القول»
[ALUSI-FULL:v12p247#2/r3] verses 12:42 | The report claiming seven additional years after five is not established in that wording; the versions judged established merely say Joseph remained imprisoned as long as he remained. «الخبر لم يثبت بهذا اللفظ وإنما الثابت في عدة روايات ما لبث في السجن طول ما لبث»
[ALUSI-FULL:v12p247#2/r4] verses 12:42 | Tawus and al-Dahhak, as cited by Ibn Hatim, explain this occurrence of بضع as fourteen years, an uncommon interpretation. «روي ابن حاتم عن طاوس والضحاك تفسير البضع ههنا بأربع عشرة سنة»
[ALUSI-FULL:v12p247#2/r5] verses 12:42 | Al-Alusi advises against assigning the prison term an exact number, despite the divergent reports. «والأولى أن لا يجوز بمقدار معين كما قدمنا»
[ALUSI-FULL:v12p247#2/r6] verses 12:42 | Anas reports that God reminded Joseph of being saved from his brothers, the well, and the woman, then asked why he forgot God and remembered a human; Joseph said it was only a word spoken by his tongue, and God swore to confine him for several years. «من استنقذك من القتل حين هم إخوتك أن يقتلوك»
[ALUSI-FULL:v12p247#2/r7] verses 12:42 | Although seeking help from people in hardship is generally permissible, as cooperation in righteousness shows, what suits prophets is to leave that concession and choose the higher resolve. «واللائق بمناصب الأنبياء عليهم السلام ترك ذلك والأخذ بالعزائم»
[ALUSI-FULL:v12p247#2/r8] verses 12:42 | Abu Hayyan says Joseph asked the cupbearer to remember him in order to guide the king to faith, not to secure his release; Al-Alusi calls this contrary to the apparent sense and reports, though fitting Joseph's rank. «واختار أبو حيان أن يوسف عليه السلام إنما قال للشرابي ما قال ليتوصل بذلك إلى هداية الملك وإيمانه»
[ALUSI-FULL:v12p247/r1] verses 12:42 | The imperfect 'he thought' emphasizes certainty that one prisoner would be saved and prepares for Joseph's request to be remembered; some early authorities instead identify the saved man as the one who thought so. «أوثِر على صيغة المضارع مبالغة في الدلالة على تحقيق النجاة»
[ALUSI-FULL:v12p247/r2] verses 12:42 | Joseph is the one who knows the prisoner's rescue; here ظن means certainty, as in the verse about those certain they will meet their Lord. «والظان هو يوسف عليه السلام لأصحابه وإن ذهب إليه بعض السلف»
[ALUSI-FULL:v12p247/r3] verses 12:42 | Calling Joseph's certainty ظن may be deferential etiquette toward God; alternatively it reflects interpretation by inference, supporting the view that dream interpretation is conjectural rather than certain. «ولعل التعبير به من باب إرخاء العنان والتأدب مع الله تعالى»
