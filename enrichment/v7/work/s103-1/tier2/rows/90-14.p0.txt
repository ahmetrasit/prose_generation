## NAHHAS: Abū Jaʿfar al-Naḥḥās, d. 338 AH
[NAHHAS:v5p229/r11] author | holds | The steep path means freeing a slave or feeding someone on a day of hunger; Abū Hurayra reports that freeing a slave earns release from the Fire for each limb.
[NAHHAS:v5p229/r12] الحسن | reports | With Abū Rajāʾ, Abū ʿAmr, Ibn Kathīr, and al-Kisāʾī, he reads the words as verbal commands or past verbs: freeing a slave or feeding on a hungry day.
[NAHHAS:v5p229/r13] الفراء | prefers | He prefers the verbal reading because ثم كان is a past verb and should coordinate with another past verb.
[NAHHAS:v5p229/r14] الأخفش | prefers | Al-Akhfash, Abū Ḥātim, and Abū ʿUbayd prefer the nominal reading.
[NAHHAS:v5p229/r15] author | holds | Both are sound readings and one may not attack the reading transmitted by the community; the past-verb coordination argument fails because it cannot coordinate by sense as proposed.
[NAHHAS:v5p232/r1] الفراء | reports | He allows the alternate reading by supplying an implied an and cites a poem; the author says the reading would then require an accusative and that an cannot be omitted without a substitute.
[NAHHAS:v5p232/r2] أبو عبيد | prefers | He says the nominal ‘freeing a slave’ is the choice because it explains what the obstacle is.
[NAHHAS:v5p232/r3] سفيان بن عيينة | reports | He claims that every verse asking ‘what will make you know’ supplies an explanation, while ‘what makes you know’ does not.
[NAHHAS:v5p232/r4] author | rejects | He rejects Sufyān's rule: the Qurʾān explains both the Calamity and the Reality after asking what they are. | mentions 101:3, 69:3
[NAHHAS:v5p232/r5] الحسن | reports | With Abū Rajāʾ, he reads dhā as the object fed and an adjective of the orphan.
[NAHHAS:v5p232/r6] الفراء | reports | He analyzes dhā masghaba as an adjective before the orphan, despite the adjective preceding its noun.
[NAHHAS:v5p232/r7] author | rejects | He calls that analysis an obvious error because an adjective cannot precede its noun; dhā masghaba is governed by feed and the orphan is an apposition.
