## SAMIN-DURR: al-Samīn al-Ḥalabī, d. 756 AH
[SAMIN-DURR:10:45#2/r1] ابن عطية وأبو البقاء ومكي | reports | They parse كأن لم يلبثوا as an adjective of an omitted verbal noun, تقديره يحشرهم حشرات كأن لم يلبثوا; some also posit an omitted pronoun linking it.

[SAMIN-DURR:10:45#2/r3] الحوفي | reports | Al-Hawfi's proposed implicit meaning is speed: they hasten as though they had stayed only an hour.
[SAMIN-DURR:10:45#2/r4] author | reports | On that reading يسرعون is circumstantial from the object of يحشرهم, and كأن لم may describe its subject or explain the implied hastening.

[SAMIN-DURR:10:45#3/r2] أبو البقاء | reports | Another parse makes يتعارفون circumstantial from the object of يحشرهم; Abu al-Baqa calls it an estimated state because recognition does not occur during the act of gathering.

[SAMIN-DURR:10:45#4/r4] author | reports | وما كانوا مهتدين either coordinates with قد خسر or is part of the relative clause of الذين, reinforcing that those who deny God’s meeting are unguided.
