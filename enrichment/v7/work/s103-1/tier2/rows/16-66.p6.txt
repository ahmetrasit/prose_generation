## KURANYOLU-TEFSIR: Hayreddin Karaman, Mustafa Çağrıcı, İbrahim Kâfi Dönmez, Sadrettin Gümüş
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:16:66/r1] İbn Atıyye; Şevkânî | reports | The term an‘am for milking livestock is said to cover sheep, goats, cattle, and camels.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:16:66/r2] author | holds | God nourishes the earth with rain and grows animal feed; milk formed from that feed is a human food, singled out for its nutritional value and as a sign of wise creation in both its benefit and bodily formation.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:16:66/r3] author | holds | Fers refers to food residues whose nutrients have been absorbed and that will become waste; milk is between this useless residue and blood needed by the animal, and benefits drinkers after secretion.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:16:66/r4] Zemahşerî | reports | Another explanation is that milk passes between the systems of waste and blood without mixing with either; divine power separates it so its color, taste, and smell remain pure.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:16:66/r5] author | holds | The author also suggests that nutrients partly enter blood and partly leave as waste, while milk is another product made from nutrients that nourishes others and tastes pleasant.
