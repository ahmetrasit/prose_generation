## SAMIN-DURR: al-Samīn al-Ḥalabī, d. 756 AH
[SAMIN-DURR:78:16/r1] الزمخشري | reports | Al-Zamakhshari says ‘alfafan’ means intertwined and has no singular, like ‘awza‘’ and ‘akhyaf’. 
[SAMIN-DURR:78:16/r2] أبو علي الطوسي | reports | Most language authorities analyze ‘alfafan’ as the plural of ‘liff’; Abu ‘Ali al-Tusi’s line ‘jannat liff’ witnesses that form.
[SAMIN-DURR:78:16/r3] الكسائي | reports | Al-Kisa’i analyzes ‘alfafan’ as a plural of ‘lafif’, like sharif/ashraf and shahid/ashhad; a line uses ‘alifaf’ for interwoven groups.
[SAMIN-DURR:78:16/r4] ابن قتيبة | reports | Ibn Qutayba derives the form through pluralization twice: ‘alf’ and ‘lifā’’ first yield ‘liff’, then ‘alfaf’. 
[SAMIN-DURR:78:16/r5] الزمخشري | reports | Al-Zamakhshari doubts the double-plural analysis because he knows no parallel such as ‘khudr/akhḍar’ or ‘ḥumr/aḥmar’. 
[SAMIN-DURR:78:16/r6] author | rejects | The lack of a productive plural-of-plural pattern does not invalidate Ibn Qutayba’s analysis; ‘liff’ resembles ‘qufl’ and can take the pattern ‘af‘al’, while plural-of-plurals need not be regular.
[SAMIN-DURR:78:16/r7] الزمخشري | reports | Al-Zamakhshari also suggests that ‘alfafan’ could be ‘multaffah’ with its additions removed; the author finds this needlessly strained, since such deletions are chiefly discussed in diminutives and verbal nouns.
