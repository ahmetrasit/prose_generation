## JISHUMI: al-Ḥākim al-Jishumī, d. 494 AH
[JISHUMI:v5p3659/r2] أبو عمرو | reports | Abū ʿAmr reads حاشا with a final alif; the other readers omit it.
[JISHUMI:v5p3659/r3] author | holds | Khaṭb is a matter of great consequence addressed between people; وما خطبك asks what the matter or situation is.
[JISHUMI:v5p3659/r4] أبو مسلم | reports | Abū Muslim says ḥāshā combines exception, declaring someone free of blame, and astonishment; adding it to God's name conveys wonder, like an Arab exclamation asking for help or expressing surprise.
[JISHUMI:v5p3659/r5] الزجاج | reports | Al-Zajjāj analyzes ḥaṣḥaṣ as a reduplicated form whose root is ḥaṣṣ, comparable to other doubled forms; ḥaṣṣ means cutting something away completely, so the truth became clear.
[JISHUMI:v5p3659/r6] author | holds | Ḥaṣṣ is to cut away completely, and ḥiṣṣa is a portion; the verb ḥaṣḥaṣ can mean becoming clear, with further examples for hair cut short, an egg clearing the head, land stripped of growth, or a camel leaving marks.
[JISHUMI:v5p3659/r7] حميد | reports | Ḥumayd's cited line uses ḥaṣḥaṣ for a camel settling its knees against hard stones until their marks show.
