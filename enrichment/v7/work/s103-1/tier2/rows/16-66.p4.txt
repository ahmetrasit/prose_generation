## SAMIN-DURR: al-Samīn al-Ḥalabī, d. 756 AH
[SAMIN-DURR:16:66#2/r1] author | reports | In مما في بطونه, من can mark partitive meaning or origin.
[SAMIN-DURR:16:66#2/r10] author | reports | The poem فيها خطوط uses feminine reference to the described thing.
[SAMIN-DURR:16:66#2/r11] author | reports | Non-rational broken plurals allow collective or plural agreement: this verse takes plural sense, while al-Muminun takes collective sense; poetry illustrates plural treatment. | mentions 23:21
[SAMIN-DURR:16:66#2/r12] author | reports | The pronoun may stand for an understood singular with collective meaning, like نعم; the supporting analogies continue in the next segment.
[SAMIN-DURR:16:66#2/r2] الزمخشري | reports | Al-Zamakhshari says the masculine singular pronoun refers to الأنعام as a singular-pattern word on أفعال; the Shaikh disputes the citation of Sibawayh.
[SAMIN-DURR:16:66#2/r3] الشيخ | reports | The Shaikh explains Sibawayh's point: plural patterns that can be further pluralized resemble singulars, unlike the terminal مفاعل and مفاعيل patterns.
[SAMIN-DURR:16:66#2/r4] الشيخ | reports | Sibawayh says أفعال may sometimes stand for one thing, as in هو الأنعام, figuratively meaning نعم.
[SAMIN-DURR:16:66#2/r5] author | reports | The Shaikh argues قد يقع للواحد indicates occasional figurative use, not an inherent singular pattern; Sibawayh elsewhere excludes أفعال from singular nouns.
[SAMIN-DURR:16:66#2/r6] author | prefers | The author finds al-Zamakhshari's reading sufficient to license singular agreement and says he has not misquoted Sibawayh.
[SAMIN-DURR:16:66#2/r7] author | reports | The pronoun may refer to female livestock only, because males do not produce milk.
[SAMIN-DURR:16:66#2/r8] author | reports | Al-Kisa'i glosses it as “in the bellies of what was mentioned.”
[SAMIN-DURR:16:66#2/r9] author | reports | Al-Mubarrad says Quranic demonstratives can refer to “the thing,” citing 80:11 and 6:78; feminine agreement is grammatical, not natural. | mentions 80:11, 6:78
[SAMIN-DURR:16:66#3/r1] author | reports | The poem تركنا الخيل والنعم uses نعم collectively, supporting the implied singular explanation.
[SAMIN-DURR:16:66#4/r1] author | holds | من بين فرث may attach to the drinking verb as origin; if the preceding من has the same governor and sense, it must instead be بدل. A partitive first من avoids that problem.

[SAMIN-DURR:16:66#4/r3] author | reports | A third analysis makes من بين فرث a circumstantial phrase for the preceding relative pronoun.
[SAMIN-DURR:16:66#4/r4] author | holds | فرث is fodder residue in a stomach or thick remaining food in the intestine; related verbs mean crushing a liver or bringing calamity.
[SAMIN-DURR:16:66#4/r5] author | holds | لبنا is the second object of نسقي; سيغا is read with doubled ياء or, by Isa ibn Umar, lightened; it cannot be a verb, which would have to be سوغا.
