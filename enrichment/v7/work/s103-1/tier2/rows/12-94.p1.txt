## QUSHAYRI: al-Qushayrī, d. 465 AH
[QUSHAYRI:v2p91/r10] author | holds | Only Jacob sensed Joseph because he alone grieved over his loss; only those who love the beloved recognize the beloved’s fragrance.
[QUSHAYRI:v2p91/r11] author | holds | The word scent is also used figuratively, as in saying someone’s influence blows or that one senses the scent of discord.
[QUSHAYRI:v2p91/r12] author | holds | Jacob anticipated blame, but his sons answered with increasingly insulting reproaches and called his loving devotion delusion.
[QUSHAYRI:v2p91/r8] author | holds | While hardship continued, Joseph’s story remained obscure to Jacob; once the trial lifted, his state became apparent.
[QUSHAYRI:v2p91/r9] author | holds | Joseph had not been physically far away when thrown into the well, but his identity and condition were obscured; Jacob sensed him from eighty farsakhs away.
