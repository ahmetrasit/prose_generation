## ABUHAYYAN-FULL: Abū Ḥayyān al-Gharnāṭī, d. 745 AH

[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v10p502/r3] author | holds | After praising the Prophet's perfected creation and character, the surah describes the one who opposes him being brought low in this world and the next; mankind was created ready to accept truth but may be moved to a base state.

[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v10p503/r11] الجمهور | reports | The majority read sāfilīn indefinite; ʿAbd Allāh reads it definite with the article.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v10p503/r12] الزمخشري | reports | Al-Zamakhsharī embellishes both readings as physical degradation in Hell or the bodily reversal of old age after an upright, handsome creation.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v10p503/r13] author | rejects | The author calls al-Zamakhsharī's elaboration excessive and begins to explain the continued reward of righteous people who reach old age.
