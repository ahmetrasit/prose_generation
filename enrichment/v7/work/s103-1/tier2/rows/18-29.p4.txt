## BAYDAWI-FULL: al-Bayḍāwī, d. 685 AH

[BAYDAWI-FULL:18:29/r2] author | holds | The choice to believe or disbelieve does not grant humans independent creation of their acts, since their will is itself not self-willed.

[BAYDAWI-FULL:18:29/r5] author | holds | The drink is a second description of the water or a state of the molten substance; it is vile, and Hell is an evil resting place.
[BAYDAWI-FULL:18:29/r6] author | holds | The root of resting place refers to propping an elbow under the cheek; the wording echoes the good resting place of Paradise, though Hell's people do not recline.
