[RAZI-FULL:v20p53#2/r1] author | holds | The cause that produces milk from blood in females is absent in males, explaining the difference.
[RAZI-FULL:v20p53#2/r2] المفسرون | reports | Commentators say dung, blood, and milk form in one place: dung at the bottom of the rumen, blood at the top, and milk in the middle.
[RAZI-FULL:v20p53#2/r3] author | rejects | Razi rejects that account as contrary to observation and experience; if blood formed in the stomach, it would have to be vomited, which is clearly false.
[RAZI-FULL:v20p53#2/r4] author | prefers | He explains the phrase as a sequence: milk comes from some parts of blood, which comes from subtle parts of the food in the rumen; God filters coarse matter and gives the remainder properties suited to milk and a child's body.
[RAZI-FULL:v20p53/r1] author | rejects | Razi rejects the claim that blood and milk form in the rumen: repeated slaughter shows neither there, and a process observable in some cases would have been seen.
[RAZI-FULL:v20p53/r2] author | prefers | His account is that food is digested first in the stomach or rumen; subtle material is absorbed into the liver and dense residue passes to the intestines.
[RAZI-FULL:v20p53/r3] author | holds | The liver's second digestion turns subtle nourishment into blood and separates bile, black bile, and water; blood then travels through veins and undergoes a third digestion before the udder transforms it into milk.
[RAZI-FULL:v20p53/r4] author | holds | Males are hot and dry while females are cold and moist; females need added moisture both to form the fetus and to let the body stretch as it grows.
[RAZI-FULL:v20p53/r5] author | holds | After birth, moisture that nourished the fetus is redirected to the breast or udder to nourish the infant.
[RAZI-FULL:v20p54/r1] author | holds | The stomach's lower outlet closes while food is needed for digestion and absorption, then opens to release residue; this timed control bespeaks a wise agent.
[RAZI-FULL:v20p54/r2] author | holds | The liver selectively draws subtle parts while the intestines draw dense residue; reversing those powers would disrupt the body's interests.
[RAZI-FULL:v20p54/r3] author | holds | The liver cooks subtle material into blood, while the gallbladder, spleen, and kidney draw off bile, black bile, and excess water, leaving nourishing blood; this specific allocation requires an all-knowing planner.

[RAZI-FULL:v20p54/r7] author | holds | Milk contains fat that is hot and moist, water that is cold and moist, and curd that is cold and dry, qualities not present in grass; successive transformations show a wise, merciful creator manages the world for human interests.

[QURTUBI-FULL:16:66#2/r5] author | holds | Farth is dung that descends into the stomach; when it exits, it is no longer called farth.
