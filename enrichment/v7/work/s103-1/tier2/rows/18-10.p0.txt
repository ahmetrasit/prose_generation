## SULAMI: al-Sulamī, d. 412 AH
[SULAMI:v1p406#2/r1] سهل | reports | The youths' request for mercy asks for remembrance and gratitude in every state, while asking for the easiest path of divine enabling.
[SULAMI:v1p406#2/r2] جعفر | reports | Seeing the youths from one's own perspective brings flight, but seeing them from the Real's perspective reveals unity and lordship within them.
[SULAMI:v1p406#2/r3] ابن عطاء | reports | A view of all created things from one's own position would turn one's sight away from the youths; the Prophet is both a beholder of God and beheld from Him.
[SULAMI:v1p406#2/r4] ابن عطاء | reports | The youths were surrounded by lights of the Real, exaltation, and awe, explaining why the Prophet would flee if he saw them.
[SULAMI:v1p406#2/r5] الحسين | reports | The Prophet would recoil from the overpowering spiritual states displayed in them, despite recognizing their lofty station of nearness and witnessing.
[SULAMI:v1p406#2/r6] جعفر | reports | The awe of God's power, care, and guardianship over them would be unbearable; the flight would truly be from God, since what appears in them comes from Him.
[SULAMI:v1p406#2/r7] جعفر | reports | One flees when looking from the human self but stands firm when looking from God; the Prophet's flight from unbelievers reflects the former human perspective.
[SULAMI:v1p406#2/r8] بعضهم | reports | Angelic lights are honors, while human lights are guidance, outward and inward; their greater awe explains the report that Satan fled from ʿUmar.
