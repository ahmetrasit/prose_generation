[TABATABAI:92:2-7/r5] author | holds | Saʿy is swift walking and here means purposeful deeds; shatta is a plural meaning dispersed. The oath answers that efforts and their effects diverge, from generosity, piety, and faith to miserliness, self-sufficiency, and denial.
