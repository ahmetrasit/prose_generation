[TABATABAI:68:1-33#5/r11] ابن عباس | reports | Ibn ʿAbbas tells of a father who always gave each rightful person a share, then five sons who planned to keep an unusually abundant harvest from Muslim poor; four agreed and the moderate brother objected.
[TABATABAI:68:20-28/r10] author | holds | The admonition to glorify God means to declare Him free of partners: their reliance on themselves and worldly causes denied God's sole will, while adding if God wills would affirm that all outcomes depend on Him.
[TABATABAI:68:20-28/r9] author | prefers | Their most balanced brother reminded them that he had warned them; the meaning is fairest in judgment, not oldest in age, and his earlier words are omitted for concision.
[TABATABAI:68:29-33/r1] قيل | reports | Another explanation takes their failure to glorify God as failure to remember and repent for intending to harvest everything and deny the poor; this fits if exception means reserving the poor's share.
