## NASAFI-FULL: al-Nasafī, d. 710 AH

## ABUHAYYAN-FULL: Abū Ḥayyān al-Gharnāṭī, d. 745 AH
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p421/r1] ابن عيسى | reports | He defines ash as matter pulverized by burning into dust, gives the plural forms r-m-d and armida, notes the rare armidāʾ, and says ramād ramadd is the finest, lightest dust.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p421/r10] author | holds | Mathal is metaphorical for an unusual quality; ‘their deeds are like ash’ is a resumed answer to an implied question about their likeness.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p421/r11] ابن عطية | reports | He reports the Kisaʾī and al-Farrāʾ analysis that mathal is suspended, yielding ‘those who disbelieved: their deeds are like ash.’
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p421/r12] الحوفي | reports | He makes mathal the subject, aʿmāluhum an encompassing بدل, and ka-ramād the predicate, citing a poetic construction as a parallel.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p421/r2] author | holds | Jazaʿ is inability to endure hardship, the opposite of patience; a poem illustrates the word mujzaʿan.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p421/r9] author | holds | The phrase begins with mathal as a subject whose predicate is omitted, estimated as ‘in what is recited to you’ or ‘is being told,’ following Sībawayh.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p422/r1] الزمخشري | reports | He offers deeds as a بدل of ‘the likeness of those who disbelieve,’ with an implied ‘the likeness of their deeds,’ and ka-ramād as predicate.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p422/r10] author | holds | Abū Ḥayyān refers readers to the explanation of ʿaṣf in Yūnus, at the phrase ‘a raging wind struck it.’ | mentions 10:22
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p422/r11] author | reports | On allowing a noun to be annexed to its adjective, the reading can mean that on Judgment Day disbelievers find no reward or trace from their works, like scattered ash.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p422/r12] author | reports | Another explanation supplies an omitted word: they cannot obtain any reward from what they earned.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p422/r2] ابن عطية | prefers | He prefers treating mathal and aʿmāluhum as successive subjects, with ‘like ash’ predicating the latter and the whole clause predicating the former; their deeds vanish when needed like wind-scattered ash.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p422/r3] author | rejects | Abū Ḥayyān rejects the analysis Ibn ʿAṭiyya prefers: the clause lacks a link to mathal and is not identical in meaning to it.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p422/r4] author | holds | The disbelievers' seemingly good deeds—kinship, freeing captives, hospitality, helping the distressed, and protection—come to nothing because they lacked knowledge of God, faith, and action for Him.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p422/r5] نافع وأبو جعفر | reports | They read al-rīyāḥ in the plural; most readers read al-rīḥ in the singular.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p422/r6] author | holds | Calling the day stormy transfers an adjective of wind to the day by figurative usage, as in expressions such as a day that is sun-dark and sun-eclipsed.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p422/r7] الهروي | reports | He supplies ‘wind’ after ‘stormy day,’ deleted because wind was already mentioned; a poem likewise means a day whose sun is dark and eclipsed.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p422/r8] author | reports | Another grammatical explanation makes ʿāṣif an adjective of wind placed after day and genitive by attraction, as in ‘a lizard hole that is ruined.’
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p422/r9] ابن أبي إسحاق وإبراهيم بن أبي بكر عن الحسن | reports | They read ‘in a day of storm,’ construing the adjective as standing for an omitted noun: a day of a violent wind.

## SAMIN-DURR: al-Samīn al-Ḥalabī, d. 756 AH
[SAMIN-DURR:14:18#2/r1] author | holds | رماد is fire-pulverized matter; plural forms include رمد, أرمدة, and rare أرمداء.
[SAMIN-DURR:14:18#2/r10] author | holds | عاصف and معصف come from العصف, broken crop stalks, describing a wind that breaks what it passes.
[SAMIN-DURR:14:18#2/r2] author | reports | رماد can mean a year of drought; أرمد الماء means it became ash-colored, and الأرمد is gray.
[SAMIN-DURR:14:18#2/r3] author | reports | رمد can mean mosquito because of its color; رماد رمدد means reduced to dust.
[SAMIN-DURR:14:18#2/r4] author | holds | اشتدت به الريح is an adjective of رماد, and في يوم attaches to اشتدت.
[SAMIN-DURR:14:18#2/r5] author | reports | عاصف may stand for عاصف ريحه; الريح is omitted and the day is figuratively described as windy; al-Harawi cites poetry for a similar omission.
[SAMIN-DURR:14:18#2/r6] الهروي | reports | Al-Harawi explains the omission because الريح was mentioned earlier, comparing كاسف for كاسف الشمس in a poem.
[SAMIN-DURR:14:18#2/r7] author | reports | Alternatively عاصف is a relational adjective: a day possessing violent wind, like laban and tamir.
[SAMIN-DURR:14:18#2/r8] author | rejects | A proposed genitive of proximity is rejected: unlike جحر ضب خرب, عاصف cannot properly modify الريح here because definiteness differs.
[SAMIN-DURR:14:18#2/r9] الحسن وابن أبي إسحاق | reports | They read يوم عاصف in an إضافة, with ريح understood, or an adjective added to its noun per that grammatical view.
[SAMIN-DURR:14:18#3/r1] author | holds | لا يقدرون is independent; making it modify يوم with omitted فيه is weak.
[SAMIN-DURR:14:18#3/r2] author | holds | مما كسبوا attaches to an omitted phrase as a circumstantial modifier of شيء; after شيء it would be adjectival.
[SAMIN-DURR:14:18/r1] سيبويه | reports | Sibawayh makes مثل a subject with omitted predicate “the likeness of disbelievers [in what is recited]”; أعمالهم كرماد answers an implied question.
[SAMIN-DURR:14:18/r2] ابن عطية | reports | Ibn Atiyya prefers مثل as subject, أعمالهم as a second subject, and كرماد as its predicate; the author defends the lack of a separate link because the likeness and works are the same.
[SAMIN-DURR:14:18/r3] الشيخ | reports | The Shaikh objects that this predicate has no رابط to مثل; the author compares the identity to هجيرى أبي لا إله إلا الله.
[SAMIN-DURR:14:18/r4] author | reports | Al-Kisa'i and al-Farra treat مثل as extra, yielding “the works of those who disbelieve are like ashes”; the author rejects extra nouns.
[SAMIN-DURR:14:18/r5] الزمخشري | reports | Al-Zamakhshari makes أعمالهم a بدل کل from مثل, with an omitted إضافة: مثل أعمالهم.
[SAMIN-DURR:14:18/r6] author | reports | Another parse makes أعمالهم بدل اشتمال, exemplified by al-Zabba's line about camels and their gait.
[SAMIN-DURR:14:18/r7] author | reports | A further parse makes أعمالهم the predicate of مثل, understood as مثل أعمالهم, while كرماد predicates an omitted subject.
[SAMIN-DURR:14:18/r8] أبو البقاء | reports | Abu al-Baqa notes a hypothetical غير-transmitted reading with أعمالهم as a genitive بدل اشتمال from الذين.
