## TAB-FULL: al-Ṭabarī, d. 310 AH

[TAB-FULL:v20p348/r2] author | holds | “Glorify your Lord with praise” means pray while thanking him; evening runs from solar decline to night, and morning from the second dawn to sunrise, though some extend morning into the rising sun and forenoon. | mentions 20:130, 3:41

[TAB-FULL:v20p348/r4] بعض نحويي البصرة | reports | A Basran grammarian takes the coordinated phrases as “in the evening and in the morning,” since the preposition biʾ can mean fī.

## TABRISI: al-Ṭabrisī, d. 548 AH
[TABRISI:40:51-55/r8] author | holds | The Prophet is commanded to endure his people’s abuse because God’s promise of victory in this life and reward in the next is true; the reading treats seeking forgiveness as a command for a minor sin only if such sins are possible for prophets.

[TABRISI:40:55/r2] author | holds | Glorifying God with praise means declaring Him transcendent, thanking Him, attributing blessings to Him, and denying any likeness; another view purifies His attributes or deeds from created or unjust qualities.

[TABRISI:40:55/r4] مجاهد | reports | For Mujahid, al-ʿashī extends from the sun’s decline until night and al-ibkār from the second dawn until sunrise; Ibn Abbas instead identifies the five prayers.
