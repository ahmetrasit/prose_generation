## QUTB-ZILAL: Sayyid Quṭb, d. 1386 AH
[QUTB-ZILAL:100:1/r1] author | holds | The Meccan surah has eleven verses and moves in rapid, forceful jumps before settling at its ending, like a runner reaching the finish.
[QUTB-ZILAL:100:1/r2] author | holds | The opening scene shows charging, panting horses striking sparks, attacking at dawn, raising dust, and suddenly breaking into enemy ranks.
[QUTB-ZILAL:100:1/r6] author | holds | The rough, rumbling, cracking rhythm suits the noisy dust and forceful disclosure of graves and hearts, as well as the surah’s ingratitude and greed.
[QUTB-ZILAL:100:1/r7] author | holds | The oath’s noisy cavalry scene frames the surah’s moral and resurrection scenes: image and frame mirror one another.
[QUTB-ZILAL:100:2/r1] author | holds | The horses strike stone with their hooves until sparks fly from it.
