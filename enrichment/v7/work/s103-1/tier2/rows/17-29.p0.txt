## MUQATIL: Muqātil b. Sulaymān, d. 150 AH
[MUQATIL:17:29-30/r1] author | holds | The command teaches proper spending: do not hold back from spending on what is due out of miserliness.
[MUQATIL:17:29-30/r2] author | holds | Do not give away everything, leaving yourself unable to give when asked.
[MUQATIL:17:29-30/r3] author | holds | People will blame the person who gives nothing; maḥsūr means left cut off or stranded.
[MUQATIL:17:29-30/r4] author | holds | He links maḥsūr with 67:4, glossing ḥasīr as one left exhausted or cut off. | mentions 67:4

## YAHYA-SALLAM: Yaḥyā b. Sallām, d. 200 AH
[YAHYA-SALLAM:v1p131/r1] الحسن | reports | Al-Hasan says not to withhold spending on God's due, nor spend outside it; either extreme leaves one blamed and unable to help others.
[YAHYA-SALLAM:v1p131/r2] قتادة | reports | Qatāda says not to withhold one's hand from obedience and God's due or spend it in disobedience and waste.
[YAHYA-SALLAM:v1p131/r3] السدي | reports | Al-Suddī takes the image as a lesson about spending: neither keep one's hand closed nor spend until nothing remains; the result is blame and loss.

## TAB-FULL: al-Ṭabarī, d. 310 AH
