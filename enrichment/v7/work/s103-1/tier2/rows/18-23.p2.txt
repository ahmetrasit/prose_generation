## STUDYQURAN: Seyyed Hossein Nasr (ed.-in-chief), Caner K. Dagli, Maria Massi Dakake, Joseph E. B. Lumbard, Mohammed Rustom
[STUDYQURAN:18:23/r1] author | holds | The verses address the Prophet: do not promise future action, using tomorrow for the future generally, without making fulfillment conditional on God’s will.

[STUDYQURAN:18:23/r3] some commentators | reports | Some commentators read this as an instruction to say “if God wills” with any promise about future action.
[STUDYQURAN:18:23/r4] author | holds | Muslims commonly say in shāʾ Allāh about future aims, recalling that nothing lies outside God’s will and the future is in His hands.
[STUDYQURAN:18:intro/r6] author | reports | After promising an answer the next day, the Prophet waited fifteen days without revelation; v. 6 reproaches his concern about Quraysh's disbelief, and vv. 23–24 warn against future promises without invoking God's will.
