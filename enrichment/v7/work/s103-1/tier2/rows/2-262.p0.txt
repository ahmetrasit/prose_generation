## THALABI: al-Thaʿlabī, d. 427 AH
[THALABI:v2p258/r7] author | holds | Following a gift with mann means reminding its recipient of it, recounting favors, or repeatedly making the gift a reproach; the root first meant to bestow.
[THALABI:v2p258/r8] author | holds | Man derives from giving or bestowing; the word came to mean recalling and claiming credit for a favor. | mentions 38:39
[THALABI:v2p258/r9] author | holds | Harm includes publicizing a gift to someone who need not know of it and other hurtful speech.
[THALABI:v2p259/r1] سفيان والمفضل | reports | Sufyān and al-Mufaḍḍal explain mann and harm as saying, “I gave you, but you did not thank me.”
[THALABI:v2p259/r2] الضحاك | reports | Al-Ḍaḥḥāk says it is better not to give than to give and then follow the gift with reproach and harm.
[THALABI:v2p259/r3] عبد الرحمن بن زيد بن أسلم | reports | ʿAbd al-Raḥmān ibn Zayd reports his father advising donors to withhold even a greeting if they think it will burden the recipient.
[THALABI:v2p259/r4] عبد الرحمن بن زيد بن أسلم | reports | Ibn Zayd recounts that his father rebuked a woman who suspected men going to jihad only wanted fruit; her words had hurt them before she gave them a quiver and arrows.
[THALABI:v2p259/r5] author | holds | God reserves mann as an attribute of grace: human reminders taunt and spoil a gift, whereas God's mention of a blessing is beneficence and favor.
[THALABI:v2p259/r6] معاد بن المثنى عن أبيه محمود بن الوراق | reports | A poem attributed to Maʿād ibn al-Muthannā, from his father Maḥmūd ibn al-Warrāq, praises a good deed free from reproach.
[THALABI:v2p259/r7] أبو ذر القرطبي | reports | A poem transmitted by Abū Dharr al-Qurṭubī warns that reminding people of a favor diminishes it and spoils the giver's generosity.
[THALABI:v2p259/r8] محمد بن طاهر عن أبي علي البصري | reports | A poem describes a recipient turning hostile to a benefactor when the benefactor's fortunes later decline.
[THALABI:v2p259/r9] author | holds | Another poem says that reproach ruins the good already done and that a generous person does not remind others of gifts.
