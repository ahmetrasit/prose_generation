[TAB-FULL:v22p359/r9] author | reports | Another group interprets the phrase as the stars' setting places.

[WAHIDI-BASIT:v21p256#2/r2] أهل المعاني | reports | The scholars of meaning take لا as a rejection of the claim that the Quran is magic, poetry, or divination, followed by a new oath that it is noble scripture; compare 4:65. | mentions 4:65
