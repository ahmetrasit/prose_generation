## IBNASHUR-FULL: Ibn ʿĀshūr, d. 1393 AH
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v14p199#2/r1] author | holds | This is another proof and blessing arising from livestock; the sign is integrated into the benefits of animals in Q 16:5–7.
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v14p199#2/r2] author | holds | Milk sustains human life as rain sustains the earth, and rainfall helps form milk through the pasture it produces.
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v14p199#2/r3] author | holds | The phrase about pure milk between waste and blood displays God's craftsmanship and wisdom, while the verse also emphasizes the gift to people.
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v14p199#2/r4] author | holds | The verse continues the sign in Q 16:65: just as attentive listeners learn from rain, they can learn from livestock; the address shifts from third to second person and is doubly emphatic.

[IBNASHUR-FULL:v14p200/r2] author | holds | Fodder is digested through the stomach and liver and becomes liquid in the udder, secreted as dung and blood are also produced.
