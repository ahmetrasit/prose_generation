[TABATABAI:3:180/r2] author | holds | The divine name is repeated, with the last three instances replacing pronouns, to emphasize God as the source of matters only His divinity can enact: testing, knowing the unseen, choosing messengers, and making faith possible.
[TABATABAI:3:180/r3] author | holds | Following God's reprieve of disbelievers, the discussion turns to misers who rejoice and boast over wealth; calling it God's bounty highlights why their stinginess deserves blame, and being collared explains why it harms them.
