## KASHSHAF-FULL: al-Zamakhsharī, d. 538 AH

[KASHSHAF-FULL:v2p449/r6] author | reports | Yuʿṣirūn may instead mean that they receive rain, with either a verb meaning rain or an omitted preposition understood.
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v2p449/r9] author | holds | The end of a famine implies some fertility in general, but the detailed promise that people will receive relief and press produce in that year can only be known by revelation.

## TABRISI: al-Ṭabrisī, d. 548 AH
