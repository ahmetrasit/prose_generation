## WAHIDI-WAJIZ: al-Wāḥidī, d. 468 AH
[WAHIDI-WAJIZ:57:19-20/r3] author | holds | Worldly life is passing play, diversion, adornment, boasting, and competition in wealth and children. Like rain-grown crops that yellow, dry, and crumble, people age, die, and decay; the afterlife holds punishment for disbelievers and forgiveness and approval for God's friends. Here “the disbelievers” means farmers.
