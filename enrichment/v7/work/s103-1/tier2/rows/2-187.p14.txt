[QURTUBI-FULL:2:187#18/r10] author | holds | In legal usage iʿtikāf is adherence to a specified act of worship for a specified time, under specified conditions, in a specified place; it is unanimously non-obligatory and a recommended devotional act practiced by the Prophet, Companions, and wives.
