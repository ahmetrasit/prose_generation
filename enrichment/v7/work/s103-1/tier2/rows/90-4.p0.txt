## TAB-FULL: al-Ṭabarī, d. 310 AH

## THALABI: al-Thaʿlabī, d. 427 AH

[THALABI:v10p207/r15] أبو بكر الوراق | reports | He says a person cannot attain all that he desires or hopes for.
[THALABI:v10p207/r16] خصيف وابن كيسان | reports | They connect the verse with hardship and changing stages from embryo to full formation; another explanation says the head turns downward before birth.
[THALABI:v10p207/r17] author | reports | Other views call humans boldhearted and thick-livered despite their weak creation and lowly material, in trial and affliction, or in darkness and ignorance.

[THALABI:v10p207/r6] عمرو بن دينار عن ابن عباس | reports | He identifies the struggle as the growth of a person's teeth.
