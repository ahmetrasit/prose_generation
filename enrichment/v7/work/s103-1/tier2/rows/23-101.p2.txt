## BIQAI-FULL: al-Biqāʿī, d. 885 AH

[BIQAI-FULL:v5p222/r2] author | holds | Kinship will no longer be invoked for boasting or mutual aid amid terror and distraction; only righteous deeds matter, and no one can help another without God's permission.
