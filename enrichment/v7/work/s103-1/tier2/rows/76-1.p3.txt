[ELMALILI:76:1-4#2/r1] author | holds | “Ḥīn” is a bounded period, short or long, unlike “dahr,” which can denote the whole.

[ELMALILI:76:1-4#2/r3] author | holds | “There was nothing mentioned” is grammatically a circumstantial clause of “human” or an adjective of “period”; the negation targets being mentioned, not existence itself.
