[JISHUMI:v10p6945/r1] أبو عمرو | reports | Abu Amr reads fa-aṣaddaq wa-akūn with a waw and open nūn as a response to the wish or question introduced by fa; he says the waw was omitted from the codex for brevity.
[JISHUMI:v10p6945/r2] الفراء | reports | Al-Farraʾ reports seeing a form in some of Ibn Masud's codices, while Ubayy's and Ibn Masud's recitation is also reported with wa-akūn.
[JISHUMI:v10p6945/r3] author | reports | Most readers use wa-akun in the jussive, coordinated to the underlying position of fa-aṣaddaq before fa; it could also be parsed as a response to the command.
[JISHUMI:v10p6945/r4] أبو عبيد | prefers | Abu Ubayd prefers the jussive because the written codices omit waw, most readers use it, and coordination to the pre-fa subjunctive is grammatically sound; a cited line supports this syntax.
[JISHUMI:v10p6945/r5] الفراء | reports | Al-Farraʾ reports a form in some of Abdullah ibn Masud's codices.
[JISHUMI:v10p6945/r6] عمرو بن كلثوم | reports | The poet's line uses alhā to mean distracted or diverted from an affair.
[JISHUMI:v10p6945/r7] كعب بن زهير | reports | Kaʿb's line uses lā ulhiyannaka to mean I will not distract you, despite being occupied elsewhere.
[JISHUMI:v10p6946/r1] author | holds | Aḥadakum is accusative as the object of the verb yāti, although death is what comes; fa-aṣaddaq is subjunctive after a wish or question introduced by fa.
[JISHUMI:v10p6946/r10] author | holds | The dying person asks for a brief extension after seeing signs of the afterlife, hoping to make up what was neglected.
[JISHUMI:v10p6946/r11] author | reports | Lawlā may be an extra particle expressing a wish for delay or may mean halā as an interrogative request for more time.
[JISHUMI:v10p6946/r12] author | holds | No warning against neglecting God's due is stronger than this passage.
[JISHUMI:v10p6946/r13] مقاتل | reports | For the view that the verse concerns hypocrites, the requested righteous standing means being among sincere believers.
[JISHUMI:v10p6946/r14] ابن عباس | reports | For the view that it concerns believers, ṣāliḥīn means those obedient to God; Ibn Abbas also says a person who dies able to perform Hajj but has not done so, or has not paid zakat, will ask to return.
[JISHUMI:v10p6946/r15] ابن عباس | reports | Ibn Abbas challenged those who said only disbelievers seek return after death, citing this verse about a person who failed to pay zakat or perform able-bodied Hajj.
[JISHUMI:v10p6946/r16] الضحاك | reports | Al-Dahhak reports that anyone dying without Hajj or zakat wishes for a short reprieve, but the request is denied.
[JISHUMI:v10p6946/r2] عمرو بن معدي كرب | reports | His line illustrates a subjunctive verb after an imperative: leave me so I may go aside alone.
[JISHUMI:v10p6946/r3] مقاتل وجماعة | reports | One report says the verse was revealed about hypocrites; the address calls them believers outwardly, and ṣāliḥīn means sincere believers.
[JISHUMI:v10p6946/r4] ابن عباس وجماعة | reports | Another report places the revelation among believers and takes ṣāliḥīn to mean righteous deeds.
[JISHUMI:v10p6946/r9] author | reports | Spending is explained as spending in jihad or in charitable causes, including zakat and all other obligatory dues.
[JISHUMI:v10p6948/r5] author | holds | The command to spend shows that wealth carries a due that must be paid before regret comes.
[JISHUMI:v10p6948/r6] author | holds | Provision cannot be unlawful, since spending from unlawful wealth is forbidden.
[JISHUMI:v10p6948/r7] author | holds | Their request to return so they can give charity shows their acts belong to them; otherwise the request would make no sense, and they are capable of doing them.

[BAGHAWI-FULL:v8p134/r7] author | reports | Abū ʿAmr reads وأكون with wāw and subjunctive as a response to the wish; others read jussive وأكن, coordinated with فأصدق and written without wāw.

## KASHSHAF-FULL: al-Zamakhsharī, d. 538 AH
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v4p546#2/r1] رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | A report promises freedom from hypocrisy for whoever recites Surat al-Munafiqun.
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v4p546/r1] author | holds | The مِن in مما رزقناكم is partitive; the required spending is intended.
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v4p546/r10] author | holds | The reading وأكن joins the jussive to the grammatical position of فأصدق, meaning ‘if delayed, I would give and become’; وأكون instead follows the overt form.
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v4p546/r11] عبيد بن عمير | reports | Ubayd ibn Umayr reads وأكون as if the speaker promises, ‘and I shall be’ righteous.
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v4p546/r2] author | holds | The warning applies before death's signs become unmistakable, when one can no longer hope for respite, give acceptably, or avoid regret over withheld charity.
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v4p546/r3] ابن عباس | reports | Ibn Abbas urges charity before death's authority arrives, when a capable person will not be accepted.
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v4p546/r4] ابن عباس | reports | Ibn Abbas urges anyone with wealth to pay zakah and anyone able to perform pilgrimage to do so before death; a request to return will then be refused.
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v4p546/r5] ابن عباس | reports | Ibn Abbas says the passage concerns those withholding zakah; he argues that a person who had seen good would not seek to return, and identifies believers as its addressees.
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v4p546/r6] الحسن | reports | Al-Hasan likewise says anyone who failed to give zakah, fast, or perform pilgrimage will ask to return at death.
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v4p546/r7] عكرمة | reports | Ikrima says the verse was revealed concerning the people of the qibla.
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v4p546/r8] author | holds | The variant أخرتن means ‘Why did You not delay my death?’; a nearby term means a short period.
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v4p546/r9] أبي | reports | Ubayy reads فأتصدق, preserving the fuller form of the verb.

## IBNATIYYA-FULL: Ibn ʿAṭiyya, d. 542 AH
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v5p315#2/r3] جمهور من المتأولين | reports | Most interpreters take the command to spend as referring specifically to zakāt; others read it broadly to include obligatory and recommended giving.
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v5p315#2/r4] author | holds | The coming of death means its signs and first stages; the plea asks for another chance and reprieve.
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v5p315#3/r1] أبي بن كعب | reports | Ubayy ibn Kaʿb's codex reads ākhartan without the final yāʾ; the requested term is called near because it is coming and is sought only to do good deeds.
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v5p315#3/r2] ابن عباس | reports | Ibn ʿAbbās says becoming one of the righteous means pilgrimage; he says people who neglect zakāt and pilgrimage seek a return at death, despite a listener's objection.
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v5p315#3/r3] author | reports | Most read wa-akun in the jussive: Abū ʿAlī coordinates to the implied conditional slot, while Sībawayh's account from al-Khalīl jussives it by the condition implied in the wish, without a syntactic slot. | mentions 7:186

## TABRISI: al-Ṭabrisī, d. 548 AH
[TABRISI:63:11#2/r10] ابن عباس | reports | Another view says it addresses believers, and ‘righteous’ means obedient people; Ibn Abbas says anyone who died with wealth but withheld zakat or could make pilgrimage but failed would ask to return, and here ‘righteousness’ means pilgrimage.
[TABRISI:63:11#2/r11] أبو عبد الله | reports | The explanation that ‘righteous’ means pilgrimage is also transmitted from Abu Abdillah.
[TABRISI:63:11#2/r7] author | holds | Spend in good causes before death, including zakat and all other obligatory rights; death's signs prompt a futile wish for a short reprieve to give and become righteous.
[TABRISI:63:11#2/r8] author | holds | The verse is the strongest warning against neglecting God's rights; ‘righteous’ can mean those who perform good deeds.
[TABRISI:63:11#2/r9] مقاتل | reports | One view says the verse addresses hypocrites.

[TABRISI:63:11/r3] author | holds | The jussive ‘and I be’ coordinates with the implied jussive condition after ‘if only you delayed me’; the request implies ‘if you delay me, I will give charity.’
[TABRISI:63:11/r4] author | holds | Abu Amr follows the visible subjunctive after the fāʾ, a preferable construction because the overt form is clear and close; a codex of Ubayy reportedly has both verbs in that form.
