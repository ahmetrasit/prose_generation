[QURTUBI-FULL:25:62#2/r1] author | holds | Night has great merit: the Prophet was commanded to pray at night, believers are praised for rising from their beds, and a report praises late prayer, a time when supplications are answered, and the Lord’s descent. | mentions 17:79, 73:2, 32:16

[QURTUBI-FULL:25:62#2/r3] author | reports | The lightened reading may mean recalling in one time what was missed in the other, or remembering God’s transcendence and praising Him during both periods.
[QURTUBI-FULL:25:62#2/r4] author | holds | Shukūr is an intensive verbal noun meaning the same as shukr, like kufūr and kufr; the cycles of night and day sustain people’s livelihood and prompt gratitude.
[QURTUBI-FULL:25:62#2/r5] author | reports | The close connects the signs to the question “what is the Merciful?”: He is the One able to make these things.
[QURTUBI-FULL:25:62/r1] أبو عبيدة | reports | Abu Ubayda defines khilfa as one thing following another, as night and day each succeed the other; the word can describe alternating bodily positions or new plant leaves.
[QURTUBI-FULL:25:62/r10] ابن العربي | reports | Ibn al-Arabi quotes Dhu al-Shahid al-Akbar: people’s created completeness is life and knowledge, but sleep, bodily needs, and physical deficiency afflict them; a person should reduce sleep through restraint and worship where possible.
[QURTUBI-FULL:25:62/r11] ابن العربي | reports | Ibn al-Arabi calculates that a sixty-year life spent sleeping half the night and a sixth of the day wastes two thirds, leaving twenty years, and calls it folly to spend that much life in fleeting pleasure rather than lasting worship.
[QURTUBI-FULL:25:62/r12] ابن العربي | reports | Ibn al-Arabi says things do not differ in rank by themselves but by qualities; which is superior, night or day, is disputed, with fasting enough to indicate the answer.

[QURTUBI-FULL:25:62/r5] author | reports | Other interpretations are alternation in light and darkness and in increase and decrease, or an omitted phrase meaning that night and day possess difference.
[QURTUBI-FULL:25:62/r6] author | holds | The alternation lets people remember and reflect that God did not create it in vain, and thank Him using their reason, thought, and understanding.
[QURTUBI-FULL:25:62/r7] عمر بن الخطاب وابن عباس والحسن | reports | They explain the verse as allowing anyone who missed a good deed by night to make it up by day, and anyone who missed one by day to make it up by night.
