## BAGHAWI-FULL: al-Baghawī, d. 516 AH
[BAGHAWI-FULL:v8p141#2/r6] author | holds | The Gathering is Resurrection, when heaven and earth’s inhabitants are assembled; mutual loss is missing one’s share, especially Paradise’s family and homes.

## IBNATIYYA-FULL: Ibn ʿAṭiyya, d. 542 AH
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v5p319#2/r2] author | reports | Yawm yajmaʿukum may attach grammatically to the earlier promise that people will be informed or to khabīr; God knows on every day but singles out the day when that knowledge harms them. | mentions 64:7
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v5p319#3/r1] author | reports | Most read yajmaʿukum with a vowel on ʿayn; Abū ʿAmr allows it to be silent, while Salām and Yaʿqūb read najmaʿukum with God's first-person plural.
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v5p319#3/r2] author | holds | The Gathering is Resurrection: each rises hoping for rank, then believers gain Paradise and disbelievers the Fire, so believers are the winners; taghābun is not grammatically reciprocal, like tawāḍuʿ and taḥāmul.
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v5p319#3/r3] نافع وابن عامر والمفضل عن عاصم | reports | These authorities read nukaffir and nudkhil with nūn of majesty; other reciters use yāʾ, meaning God absolves and admits.

## TABRISI: al-Ṭabrisī, d. 548 AH
[TABRISI:64:9-10/r1] author | holds | Faith and righteous deeds cause God to erase sins and admit people to eternally flowing gardens; this is unsurpassed success.
[TABRISI:64:9/r1] author | holds | The Day of Gathering is Judgment Day, when earlier and later peoples are assembled for resurrection and requital.
[TABRISI:64:9/r2] author | holds | Ghubn is an exchange in which someone takes the worse and leaves the better, or takes the better and leaves the worse: believers give up worldly share for the afterlife, while unbelievers choose the reverse and are cheated.
[TABRISI:64:9/r3] قتادة ومجاهد | reports | Alternatively, the Day of Mutual Loss is when the people of Paradise outgain the people of Hell.
[TABRISI:64:9/r4] النبي | reports | The Prophet said each believer is shown the place in Hell they would have occupied had they done wrong, increasing gratitude; each person in Hell sees their lost place in Paradise, increasing regret.

## IBNJAWZI-ZAD: Ibn al-Jawzī, d. 597 AH
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v8p282#3/r1] author | holds | The phrase yauma yajmaʿukum is governed by the earlier statement that people will surely be resurrected and told what they did; the Gathering is Judgment Day.
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v8p282#3/r2] author | holds | It is called the Day of Gathering because God brings together jinn, humans, the inhabitants of heaven, and the people of earth.
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v8p282#3/r3] أبو صالح عن ابن عباس | reports | Taghābun means the unbeliever is cheated of his share when a believer inherits his home and family in Paradise.
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v8p282#3/r4] مجاهد والقرظي | reports | Taghābun means the people of Paradise have gained at the expense of the people of Hell.
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v8p282#3/r5] الماوردي | reports | Taghābun is the wrongdoer's loss to the wronged person: the wronged person, cheated in worldly life, becomes the winner in the afterlife.
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v8p282#3/r6] الثعلبي | reports | The Day reveals the unbeliever's loss for abandoning faith and the believer's loss through falling short in good works.
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v8p282#3/r7] الزجاج | reports | The term is a commercial metaphor, like the descriptions of a failed or successful trade. | mentions 2:16, 61:10
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v8p282#3/r8] نافع وابن عامر والمفضل عن عاصم | reports | They recite the verbs for covering sins and admitting believers with nūn in both forms.
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v8p282#3/r9] الباقون | reports | The other readers recite both verbs with yāʾ.

## QURTUBI-FULL: al-Qurṭubī, d. 671 AH
[QURTUBI-FULL:64:9#2/r1] author | holds | The people of Paradise trade worldly life for the afterlife, while the people of the Fire trade the afterlife for worldly life; this is figurative exchange and loss. | mentions 2:16
[QURTUBI-FULL:64:9#2/r2] author | holds | God divided people between Paradise and Fire and assigned everyone a dwelling in both; the guided may inherit the place of the forsaken, producing the ultimate exchange.
[QURTUBI-FULL:64:9#2/r3] author | holds | This exchange may also be described as inheritance, as explained under the opening of al-Mu’minun. | mentions 23:1
[QURTUBI-FULL:64:9#2/r4] author | holds | Loss can occur before Judgment, but this verse concerns the final loss whose consequences cannot be repaired.
[QURTUBI-FULL:64:9#2/r5] الحسن وقتادة | reports | They report three losses: discarded knowledge later practiced by a student, ill-gotten wealth wisely used by an heir, and an obedient slave saved while his sinful master is ruined.
[QURTUBI-FULL:64:9#2/r6] النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | A report describes a husband condemned for unlawful earnings and neglecting support, while his wife is admitted to Paradise and tells him their fates have been exchanged.
[QURTUBI-FULL:64:9#3/r1] ابن العربي | reports | Ibn al-Arabi says the verse’s restriction of mutual loss to Judgment Day proves worldly transaction loss is impermissible; the Baghdadis would rescind a sale when the loss exceeds one-third.
[QURTUBI-FULL:64:9#3/r2] البغداديون | reports | The Baghdadi jurists support rescission for substantial loss with the Prophet’s instruction to Hibbān ibn Munqidh to disclaim fraud and retain a three-day option.
[QURTUBI-FULL:64:9#3/r3] author | holds | Qurtubi says fraud is religiously forbidden by consensus, but slight loss cannot be avoided; substantial avoidable loss warrants rescission, with one-third set by analogy to bequests.
[QURTUBI-FULL:64:9#3/r4] author | reports | One interpretation takes the verse to mean that unrestricted loss is permitted only on that Day.
[QURTUBI-FULL:64:9#3/r5] author | holds | Alternatively, it means irreparable loss: worldly losses may be reversed or offset by later profit, whereas losing Paradise can never be recovered.
[QURTUBI-FULL:64:9#3/r6] بعض علماء الصوفية | reports | Some Sufi scholars say everyone meets God at a loss because no one can fully perform enough works to receive complete recompense.
[QURTUBI-FULL:64:9#3/r7] النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | A report says everyone meets God regretful: the wrongdoer regrets failing to do good, and the good-doer regrets failing to do more.
[QURTUBI-FULL:64:9#4/r1] author | reports | Nafi and Ibn Amir read both verbs in the first-person plural; the other readers use the third-person form.
[QURTUBI-FULL:64:9/r1] author | holds | The time expression may be governed by “you will be informed,” by “all-knowing” with its warning sense, or by an implied command to remember.
[QURTUBI-FULL:64:9/r10] الزجاج | reports | Al-Zajjāj says even a believer is at a loss when someone else is elevated to a higher rank in Paradise.
[QURTUBI-FULL:64:9/r11] author | holds | The question about what transaction caused this loss introduces an answer that it is a parable of buying and selling.
[QURTUBI-FULL:64:9/r2] author | holds | Ghubn means a shortfall in a transaction, taking something from someone for less than its value.
[QURTUBI-FULL:64:9/r3] قراءة العامة | reports | The common reading says yajmaʿukum with yāʾ, matching the preceding mention of God's name and the report that God knows what people do.
[QURTUBI-FULL:64:9/r4] نصر وابن أبي إسحاق والجحدري ويعقوب وسلام | reports | They read najmaʿukum with nūn, echoing the first-person plural in “the light We sent down.” | mentions 64:8
[QURTUBI-FULL:64:9/r5] author | reports | The Day of Gathering brings together the first and last generations, humans and jinn, and inhabitants of heaven and earth.
[QURTUBI-FULL:64:9/r6] author | reports | Other interpretations say it gathers each servant with their deeds, wrongdoer with victim, each prophet with their community, or reward with punishment.
[QURTUBI-FULL:64:9/r7] author | holds | The Day of Mutual Loss is Resurrection Day: Paradise's people gain the Garden while Hell's people receive the Fire after exchanging good for evil, quality for poor, and bliss for torment.
[QURTUBI-FULL:64:9/r8] author | holds | The commercial idiom means the loser takes less in a sale or purchase while the other side prevails; related words describe excess cloth cut away and folds of the body.
[QURTUBI-FULL:64:9/r9] المفسرون | reports | The truly shortchanged person is deprived of their family and homes in Paradise; every disbeliever loses by abandoning faith, and every believer loses through deficient goodness and wasted days.
