[BIQAI-FULL:v4p43/r8] author | holds | ظن may mean knowledge, since all human knowledge is nothing beside God's, or ordinary inference from clues; this could permit decisive speech based on probability, or the subject may be the cupbearer.
[BIQAI-FULL:v4p43/r9] author | holds | Joseph asks the cupbearer to tell the king about Joseph's virtue and clean character, which contradict the accusation against him; رب here means the king, unlike God's use elsewhere.
