## QURTUBI-FULL: al-Qurṭubī, d. 671 AH
[QURTUBI-FULL:2:263#2/r1] author | holds | Here 'forgiveness' means concealing a needy person's deficiency and poor condition; an Arab used the phrase 'God forgive' when shame about his livelihood prevented him from giving his lineage.

[QURTUBI-FULL:2:263#2/r3] النحاس | reports | Al-Nahhas says this interpretation is syntactically difficult: 'forgiveness' is the subject and 'better than charity' its predicate, with an implied act leading to forgiveness.

[QURTUBI-FULL:2:263#2/r5] author | holds | God is absolutely independent of human charity and commanded it to reward people; His forbearance means He does not punish the donor immediately for reminders and harm.

[QURTUBI-FULL:2:263/r3] رسول الله | reports | The Prophet calls a good word charity and says meeting one's brother with a cheerful face is an act of kindness; Muslim transmits the report.

## NASAFI-FULL: al-Nasafī, d. 710 AH

## ABUHAYYAN-FULL: Abū Ḥayyān al-Gharnāṭī, d. 745 AH
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v2p650/r1] author | holds | غني is an intensive form of “need-free”; it can also mean residing, while غانية describes a woman whose beauty needs no adornment, as a cited poet says people are each self-sufficient.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v2p660#2/r1] مفسرون غير مسمين | reports | Reported meanings of قول معروف include prayer for the petitioner, consolation and hope in God, praying privately for a fellow believer, commanding right, praise and glorification, concealing one’s own faults, politely encouraging another to give, or a good answer to a petitioner.

[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v2p660#2/r3] author | reports | Another reported reading addresses the petitioner, urging restrained, courteous requests and forbidding a display of wealth when not needed; either a kind reply or concealment can benefit more durably than harmful charity.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v2p660#2/r4] author | holds | The comparison shares only the general quality of benefit: the benefit of kind speech and forgiveness endures, while the benefit of charity paired with harm disappears; خير may also mean better than nothing.

[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v2p660#2/r6] author | holds | قول is an indefinite subject licensed by its description; مغفرة coordinates as a second subject, with an omitted description such as forgiveness from the giver, petitioner, or God.
