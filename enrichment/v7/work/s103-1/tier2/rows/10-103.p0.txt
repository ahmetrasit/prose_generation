## JISHUMI: al-Ḥākim al-Jishumī, d. 494 AH
[JISHUMI:v5p3437/r2] الكسائي ويعقوب | reports | Al-Kisāʾī, in one transmission, and Yaʿqūb read ننجي رسلنا lightly; the others double the jīm, two valid forms of the verb.
[JISHUMI:v5p3437/r3] الكسائي ويعقوب وحفص | reports | They read كذلك حقا علينا ننج lightly; the other readers double the jīm.
[JISHUMI:v5p3437/r6] أبو مسلم | reports | The comparison in كذلك likens the rescue of the present believers to the rescue of earlier people, and either verb may govern the phrase; حقا can be circumstantial or a verbal noun.
[JISHUMI:v5p3437/r7] أبو مسلم | reports | Abū Muslim takes ثم as coordination with the earlier destruction: God's practice was to destroy peoples successively and then save His messengers.

## BAGHAWI-FULL: al-Baghawī, d. 516 AH
[BAGHAWI-FULL:v4p154#2/r1] يعقوب | reports | Yaqub reads ننجي lightly, with a variant report about this reading; the believers are saved with the messengers when punishment descends.
[BAGHAWI-FULL:v4p154#2/r2] author | holds | The future verb means “We saved,” while likewise denotes “as We saved them”; حقا means obligatory.
[BAGHAWI-FULL:v4p154#2/r3] الكسائي وحفص ويعقوب | reports | Al-Kisai, Hafs, and Yaqub read ننج with a light form; the other readers read it doubled, and نجا and أنجى have the same meaning.

## KASHSHAF-FULL: al-Zamakhsharī, d. 538 AH

[KASHSHAF-FULL:v2p354#4/r3] author | holds | The believers saved are those who believed with the messengers; similarly God will rescue believers and destroy polytheists.
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v2p354#4/r4] author | holds | ‘An obligation upon Us’ is parenthetical emphasis that this rescue is truly binding upon God.

## IBNATIYYA-FULL: Ibn ʿAṭiyya, d. 542 AH
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v3p145#2/r2] author | holds | Because the punishment's duration is unspecified while the Prophet and believers remain among disbelievers, God explicitly states that His established practice is to rescue messengers and their followers, making the threat more severe.
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v3p145#2/r3] الجمهور | reports | All but al-Kisa'i and Hafs read ننجي with a doubled jīm; they read it with a quiescent first nūn and a light jīm. Other reported forms are called baseless by al-Zajjaj, and the written form omits yāʾ after jīm. | mentions 19:72, 21:88
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v3p145#2/r4] author | holds | كذلك can be nominative or accusative as an adjective describing an omitted verbal noun.

## TABRISI: al-Ṭabrisī, d. 548 AH
[TABRISI:10:103#2/r10] الحسن | reports | Hasan says that as God rescued earlier prophets and their followers when destroying their communities, He will rescue Muhammad and those who believe in him.
[TABRISI:10:103#2/r11] author | reports | Another interpretation calls rescue an obligation upon God through wisdom and takes it as saving believers from the hereafter's punishment, just as from worldly punishment.
[TABRISI:10:103#2/r12] أبو عبد الله | reports | Al-Sadiq urges his companions to testify that those among them who die holding this commitment belong to Paradise, invoking God's promise to rescue believers.
[TABRISI:10:103#2/r8] author | holds | God saves the messengers and believers from punishment when it falls.
[TABRISI:10:103#2/r9] author | reports | An alternative says He saves believers from the harms and plots of their enemies.
[TABRISI:10:103/r1] الكسائي، يعقوب | reports | Al-Kisa'i through Nasir and Yaqub through Rawh read ثم ننجي رسلنا lightly; Rawh is also reported to have the doubled reading, while the others read it doubled.
[TABRISI:10:103/r10] جامع العلوم النحوي الضرير | reports | The grammarian says حقا may instead be appositional to كذلك or an adjective, but one verb cannot govern both كذلك and حقا as two verbal nouns or two states.
[TABRISI:10:103/r11] author | holds | If كذلك modifies the first ننجي and حقا modifies the second, the pause should come after كذلك.
[TABRISI:10:103/r2] الكسائي، حفص عن عاصم، يعقوب، سهل | reports | Al-Kisa'i, Hafs from Asim, Yaqub, and Sahl read ننجي المؤمنين lightly; the others double it.
[TABRISI:10:103/r3] author | holds | The light reading is supported by the Qur'anic verb أنجاه in the account of Abraham being saved from the fire. | mentions 29:24
[TABRISI:10:103/r4] author | holds | The doubled reading is supported by the Qur'anic form ونجينا; both readings are sound. | mentions 41:18
[TABRISI:10:103/r5] author | holds | The cited poetic line likewise uses نجى for saving a person from danger.
[TABRISI:10:103/r6] author | holds | Looking is seeking a thing through thought as sight seeks perception; nذر is the plural of a warner, and waiting means remaining in place for what is expected.
[TABRISI:10:103/r7] author | holds | A mithl within one genus substitutes for its counterpart in its essential nature, while a cross-genus likeness is proximity by analogy; salvation and safety both derive from rising above harm or receiving something unharmed.
[TABRISI:10:103/r8] author | holds | The phrase كذلك may describe believers' rescue as resembling the rescue of earlier believers, a right due upon God; grammatically it may be governed by either instance of ننجي.
[TABRISI:10:103/r9] أبو مسلم | reports | Abu Muslim takes حقا as a circumstantial accusative although its form resembles a verbal noun.
