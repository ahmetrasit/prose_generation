[IBNASHUR-FULL:v23p328/r3] author | holds | Takwīr literally means wrapping; the alternation of night and day over earth is pictured as successive folds of a turban, with earth as the head.
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v23p328/r4] author | holds | Because takwīr derives from a round ball, Ibn ʿĀshūr reads the wording as a scientific allusion to Earth's sphericity, unknown to Arabs and most people then.
