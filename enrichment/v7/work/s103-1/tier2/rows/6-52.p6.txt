[SAMIN-DURR:6:52#3/r3] author | holds | من حسابهم may be a partitive circumstantial phrase modifying شيء because a delayed modifier would be adjectival; it then depends on the implicit ‘being’ in عليك.
[SAMIN-DURR:6:52#3/r4] author | holds | Alternatively من شيء is a subject raised by عليك under negation, with من حسابهم circumstantial; or من حسابهم is predicate and عليك circumstantial, though this last information structure is unclear.
