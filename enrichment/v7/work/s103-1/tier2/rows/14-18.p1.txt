## TABRISI: al-Ṭabrisī, d. 548 AH
[TABRISI:14:17-18/r10] الفراء | reports | The comparison means the works of those who disbelieve; the construct phrase omits ‘works’ before ‘those who disbelieve’ because it is repeated afterward.
[TABRISI:14:17-18/r11] سيبويه | reports | Alternatively, it means the likeness of the unbelievers themselves among what has been related about them.

[TABRISI:14:17-18/r13] author | holds | The far misguidance is the deeds’ remoteness from benefit.
[TABRISI:14:17-18/r14] ابن عباس | reports | Alternatively, it is error far removed from what is right.
[TABRISI:14:17-18/r15] author | holds | The verse refutes determinism because it attributes the deeds to the unbelievers; if God created those deeds, attributing them to the people would be invalid.
