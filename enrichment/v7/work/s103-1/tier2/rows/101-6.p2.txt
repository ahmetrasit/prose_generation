[STUDYQURAN:101:6/r2] author | holds | Mawāzīn is plural of mīzān: the Qur'an uses balance for revelation and revealed just order, but scales for weighing deeds at Resurrection. | mentions 6:152, 7:85, 11:84, 11:85, 42:17, 55:7, 55:8, 55:9, 57:25, 21:47

[STUDYQURAN:101:6/r5] author | reports | A hadith says nothing placed in the Balance weighs more heavily than good character.
[STUDYQURAN:101:6/r6] the Prophet | reports | A hadith says deeds are judged according to intentions, and each person receives what was intended.
[STUDYQURAN:101:6/r7] the Prophet | reports | A hadith says no one enters Paradise through deeds alone; when asked whether this included him, the Prophet said only God's mercy would shelter him.
