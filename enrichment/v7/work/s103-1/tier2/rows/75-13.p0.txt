## MUQATIL: Muqātil b. Sulaymān, d. 150 AH
[MUQATIL:75:8-18/r4] author | holds | On that day a person is told what they sent ahead for the afterlife and what good or evil they left after death for others to follow.

## FARRA: al-Farrāʾ, d. 207 AH
[FARRA:v3p210/r7] author | holds | What a person sent ahead is prior work; what was left behind includes a practice others continue, whose reward or sin remains undiminished.

[ABDURRAZZAQ:v3p333#4/r1] قتادة | reports | Qatada says the person is shown what acts of obedience he sent ahead and what obligations to God he left behind.
[ABDURRAZZAQ:v3p334/r1] ابن مسعود | reports | Ibn Masud interprets what a person left behind as a good or evil practice that people continued after him.

## NAHHAS: Abū Jaʿfar al-Naḥḥās, d. 338 AH
[NAHHAS:v5p81/r4] قتادة | reports | Qatada says a person is told what obedience he sent ahead and which obligations he left; Ibn ʿAbbās says it includes all good and evil.

## SULAMI: al-Sulamī, d. 412 AH
[SULAMI:v2p361#2/r1] أبو عثمان | reports | Five afflictions in sin are worse than the sin: abandonment by God, loss of the saints' qualities, closed mercy and opened punishment, being seen while disobeying, and standing before God with one's ugly deeds exposed.

## THALABI: al-Thaʿlabī, d. 427 AH
[THALABI:v10p85/r1] عطية عن ابن عباس | reports | A report through Atiyya from Ibn Abbas says what was sent ahead means sins, while what was left behind means acts of obedience.
[THALABI:v10p85/r2] مجاهد | reports | Mujahid says the person is informed of the first and last deed performed.
[THALABI:v10p85/r3] قتادة | reports | Qatada says it is the obedience sent ahead and God's due postponed and neglected.
[THALABI:v10p85/r4] ابن زيد | reports | Ibn Zayd says the deeds sent ahead may be good or bad, while what is left behind includes obedience a person failed to perform.
[THALABI:v10p85/r5] عطاء | reports | Ata interprets what is sent ahead and left behind as deeds from the beginning and end of life.
[THALABI:v10p85/r6] زيد بن أسلم | reports | Zayd ibn Aslam says it means the wealth spent for oneself and the wealth left to heirs.
[THALABI:v10p85/r7] author | holds | The discussion is linked to the verse saying each soul will know what it sent forward and left behind. | mentions 82:5
[THALABI:v10p85/r8] أبو سعيد بن أبي بكر | reports | Abu Said lists five harms of sin worse than the sin: divine abandonment, loss of the saints' qualities, closed mercy and opened punishment, God's witnessing the sin, and public reckoning of one's offenses.
