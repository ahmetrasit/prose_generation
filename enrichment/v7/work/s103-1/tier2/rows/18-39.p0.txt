## TAB-FULL: al-Ṭabarī, d. 310 AH
[TAB-FULL:v15p264/r1] author | holds | The garden’s owner should have said that what God willed had happened when he entered and admired it; the conditional response, that it happened, is omitted but understood.
[TAB-FULL:v15p264/r2] author | holds | On this parsing, ما is accusative as the object of شاء, and the answer to the conditional is understood though unspoken.
[TAB-FULL:v15p264/r3] author | reports | Another Arabist parses ما as nominative with هو understood, yielding the sense it is what God willed.
[TAB-FULL:v15p264/r4] author | holds | لا قوة إلا بالله means that one has no ability to carry out the sought obedience except through God.
[TAB-FULL:v15p265/r1] author | holds | The line إن ترن انا أقل منك is spoken by the poor believer to the garden’s wealthy owner; the believer is compared to Salmān, Ṣuhayb, and Khabbāb.
[TAB-FULL:v15p265/r2] author | prefers | When أنا is an inserted supporting pronoun, أقل is accusative; this is the reading adopted by the regional readers and al-Ṭabarī.
[TAB-FULL:v15p265/r3] author | reports | If أنا is instead treated as the subject, then أقل takes the nominative.
