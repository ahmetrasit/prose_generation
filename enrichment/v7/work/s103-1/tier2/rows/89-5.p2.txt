## KURANYOLU-TEFSIR: Hayreddin Karaman, Mustafa Çağrıcı, İbrahim Kâfi Dönmez, Sadrettin Gümüş
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:89:1/r11] author | holds | The root meaning of hijr is “to prevent”; reason shares this name because it restrains false ideas and bad conduct, so understanding divine messages and the oath's purpose requires using reason well.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:89:1/r9] author | holds | Although the sentence begins with a question particle, commentators agree that it functions like qad, a particle of certainty, underscoring the importance of the oath's objects.
