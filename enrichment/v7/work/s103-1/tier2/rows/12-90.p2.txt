[RAZI-FULL:v18p162#2/r1] author | holds | Grammatically, the lām is for emphasis, أنت is the subject, يوسف its predicate, and the whole clause is the predicate of إنّ.
[RAZI-FULL:v18p162#2/r2] author | holds | Joseph names himself to emphasize the contrast between his brothers' extreme injustice and God's raising him to high rank; he similarly identifies his brother as another victim whom God favored.
[RAZI-FULL:v18p162#2/r3] ابن عباس | reports | Ibn Abbas says God favored Joseph and his brother with every kind of honor in this world and the next; others say the favor was their reunion.
