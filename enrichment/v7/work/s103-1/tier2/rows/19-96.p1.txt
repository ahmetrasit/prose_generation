## BAYDAWI-FULL: al-Bayḍāwī, d. 685 AH
[BAYDAWI-FULL:19:93-96/r4] author | holds | God will create affection for believers who act righteously in people's hearts without their pursuing its causes.
[BAYDAWI-FULL:19:93-96/r5] النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | The Prophet reports that God tells Gabriel He loves a person; Gabriel and heaven's inhabitants love him, then affection is placed for him on earth.
[BAYDAWI-FULL:19:93-96/r6] author | reports | The future promise may answer that the Meccan believers were despised among unbelievers until Islam spread, or refer to the affection bestowed at Judgment after resentment is removed.

## QASHANI: ʿAbd al-Razzāq al-Qāshānī, d. 736 AH
[QASHANI:19:96/r1] author | holds | True intellectual or witnessed faith and purifying deeds prepare the self to receive manifestations of divine attributes by shedding its own qualities.
[QASHANI:19:96/r2] author | holds | The promised love is the effect of an initial divine election: God first loves in hiddenness, then that love moves the servant to renew the primordial covenant through following the Beloved. | mentions 5:54
[QASHANI:19:96/r3] author | holds | Following God in works and states brings a further love of choosing, beyond the first hidden love, and acceptance among people of innate faith. | mentions 3:31

[QASHANI:19:96/r5] قتادة | reports | Qatada says that when a servant turns toward God, God turns the hearts of other people toward that servant.

## ABUHAYYAN-FULL: Abū Ḥayyān al-Gharnāṭī, d. 745 AH
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v7p304/r4] author | reports | Because believers in Mecca were hated by unbelievers when the surah came down, the future promise may refer to love after Islam prevails and spreads.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v7p304/r5] author | reports | The promise may apply generally in this world, as in the sound Tirmidhi hadith where God's love for a servant is announced to Gabriel and then placed in the earth.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v7p304/r6] ابن عطية | reports | Ibn Atiyya links the verse to the preceding one: after all creatures come as servants, believers are consoled by the love shown through God's honor and signs of forgiveness on that Day.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v7p304/r7] الزمخشري | reports | Al-Zamakhshari allows that on the Day of Resurrection God will make the creatures love believers by displaying their good deeds and records.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v7p304/r8] الزمخشري | reports | Alternatively, God plants affection for His friends in hearts without their earning it through kinship, friendship, favors, or other usual causes, as a special honor parallel to awe placed in enemies.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v7p304/r9] قيل | reports | An ellipsis may mean that God admits believers to His honored abode and grants them love by removing rancor from their breasts, unlike the mutual repudiation of unbelievers.

## IBNKATHIR-FULL: Ibn Kathīr, d. 774 AH
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v5p267#2/r1] author | holds | God plants affection for believers who perform righteous deeds pleasing to Him by following Muhammad's law in the hearts of righteous people; this is certain and unavoidable.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v5p267#2/r2] النبي | reports | When God loves a servant, He tells Gabriel, who loves him and announces God's love among heaven's inhabitants; they love him, and acceptance is placed for him on earth. God does the inverse for one He hates. The report is transmitted by Ahmad and Muslim from Abu Hurayra, with parallel routes in Ahmad and Bukhari.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v5p267#2/r3] ثوبان | reports | Thawban reports that a servant seeking God's pleasure receives God's mercy; Gabriel, the Throne-bearers, those around them, and the inhabitants of the seven heavens invoke mercy for him, which then descends to earth. Ibn Kathir calls this route rare and says the compilers did not transmit it.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v5p267#2/r4] أبو أمامة | reports | A report says God's love is announced in heaven and descends to earth, while hatred is likewise announced and spreads through earth; Sharik glosses al-miqah as love. Ibn Kathir calls the route rare and says the compilers did not transmit it.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v5p268/r1] النبي | reports | In a report from Abu Hurayra, God tells Gabriel He loves a servant, Gabriel announces this in heaven, and love is then placed for the servant among earth's people; Muslim and Tirmidhi transmit it, and Tirmidhi grades it hasan sahih.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v5p268/r2] ابن عباس | reports | Ibn Abbas glosses God's granting wuddan as love; Mujahid reports from him that it means love among people in this world.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v5p268/r3] سعيد بن جبير عن ابن عباس | reports | Sa'id ibn Jubayr says God loves these believers and makes believing people love them; Mujahid, al-Dahhak, and others give the same interpretation.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v5p268/r4] ابن عباس | reports | Al-Awfi transmits Ibn Abbas's broader explanation of wuddan as Muslims' affection in this world, good provision, and an honest reputation.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v5p268/r5] قتادة | reports | Qatada says God places this affection in believers' hearts; Hurm ibn Hayyan said that when a servant turns his heart to God, God turns believing hearts toward him and grants their love and mercy.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v5p268/r6] عثمان بن عفان | reports | Uthman said that God clothes every person in the mantle of whatever deed, good or evil, that person performs.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v5p268/r7] الحسن البصري | reports | Hasan tells of a man who worshipped conspicuously for seven months without gaining respect; when he redirected the same deeds to God alone, people began to pray for mercy on him. Hasan recited this verse about him.
