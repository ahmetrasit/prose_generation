## BIQAI-FULL: al-Biqāʿī, d. 885 AH
[BIQAI-FULL:v2p642/r5] author | holds | Those the Prophet must not drive away are poor Muslims, devoted to their Lord in contrast to unbelievers who call on powerless beings.
[BIQAI-FULL:v2p642/r6] author | holds | Morning and evening may mean both ends of each day, the two daily prayers, or continuous worship; their long constancy indicates sincerity rather than ostentation.
[BIQAI-FULL:v2p642/r7] author | holds | The wealthy polytheists promised to follow Muhammad if he expelled his humble companions, whom they disparaged as insincere and driven by need.
[BIQAI-FULL:v2p642/r8] author | holds | The Prophet has no stake in worldly gain from expelling the poor or accepting the rich; his mission calls people away from worldly life.
[BIQAI-FULL:v2p642/r9] author | holds | The denial of responsibility for one another's account means the Prophet sees only outward conduct; he need not expel them over a fear that they are insincere or that they burden him.
[BIQAI-FULL:v2p643/r1] author | holds | An alternative reading of the denial of mutual accounting, linked to 18:28, concerns provision: the Prophet's invitation is for the afterlife, and neither the poor nor the Prophet depends on the other's provision. | mentions 18:28

## DURR-FULL: al-Suyūṭī, d. 911 AH
[DURR-FULL:v3p272/r3] عبد الله بن مسعود | reports | Quraysh leaders asked the Prophet to expel poor Muslim companions such as Suhayb, Ammar, Bilal, and Khabbab so that they might follow him; the warning verse was revealed.
[DURR-FULL:v3p272/r4] عكرمة | reports | Quraysh nobles asked Abu Talib to have Muhammad expel poor and enslaved followers so they would join; Umar suggested testing their request, then verses about not expelling the believers were revealed.

## SUYUTI-LUBAB: al-Suyūṭī, d. 911 AH
[SUYUTI-LUBAB:v1p101/r1] خباب | reports | Khabbab reports that Aqra ibn Habis and Uyayna ibn Hisn asked the Prophet to dismiss poor believers such as Suhayb, Bilal, Ammar, and Khabbab when Arab delegations arrived; the verse forbidding their expulsion was revealed.
[SUYUTI-LUBAB:v1p101/r3] ابن كثير | rejects | Ibn Kathir calls the report unusual because the verse is Meccan while Aqra and Uyayna converted much later, after the Hijra.
