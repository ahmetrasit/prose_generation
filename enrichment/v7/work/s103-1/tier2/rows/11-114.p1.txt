## ZAJJAJ: al-Zajjāj, d. 311 AH
[ZAJJAJ:v3p82/r2] author | holds | The two ends of the day are morning and evening, specifically dawn, noon, and afternoon prayers; zulaf is near the beginning of night and refers to Maghrib and ʿIshāʾ. 
[ZAJJAJ:v3p82/r3] author | reports | Zulafan may also be read with ḍamma on zāy and lām; it is an adverbial plural of zulfa. 
[ZAJJAJ:v3p82/r4] author | holds | These prayers expiate the sins committed between them, confirming the report that prayers erase sins. 
[ZAJJAJ:v3p82/r5] author | prefers | Zulf is a plural of zulfa, or may be a plural like qarīb/qurb; he prefers the plural analysis and knows no use of zalīf for night.
