## ALUSI-FULL: al-Ālūsī, d. 1270 AH
[ALUSI-FULL:v12p259#2/r1] author | reports | The grammar and derivation of الآن are disputed: its alif is traced to wāw in أوان or to yā in آن يئين; a proposed original أوان with a deleted alif is rejected because wāw before alif does not normally change, while another analysis compares it to راح/رواح.
[ALUSI-FULL:v12p259#2/r10] author | holds | The Aziz's wife confesses that she seduced Joseph, not that he seduced her, to confirm his innocence; she may have confessed because denial was useless or because obsessive love made her indifferent to exposure.
[ALUSI-FULL:v12p259#2/r2] الزجاج | reports | Al-Zajjaj says الآن is built because it contains the meaning of a demonstrative, 'this time'; the objection is that demonstrative pronouns do not take the article.
[ALUSI-FULL:v12p259#2/r3] أبو علي | reports | Abu Ali says الآن is built because it contains the meaning of the definite lam; Al-Alusi objects that an implied particle's meaning cannot sensibly be expressed by adding that very particle.
[ALUSI-FULL:v12p259#2/r4] المبرد وابن السراج والزمخشري | reports | Al-Mubarrad, Ibn al-Sarraj, and al-Zamakhshari say الآن is built because it was originally indefinite but has used the article since its first use; Ibn Malik objects that other initially articulated phrases remain inflected, and that mere unusualness does not cause building.
[ALUSI-FULL:v12p259#2/r5] ابن مالك | reports | Ibn Malik instead attributes building to الآن's fixed, particle-like form: unlike حين and وقت it cannot be dualized, pluralized, or diminished; Abu Hayyan objects to the argument.
[ALUSI-FULL:v12p259#2/r6] الفراء | reports | Al-Farra says الآن retains the building of the past verb آن, 'the time came'; the objection is that it takes the article, unlike that verb, and can be declined.
[ALUSI-FULL:v12p259#2/r7] بعضهم | reports | Some grammarians treat الآن as an inflected accusative of time, citing a poetic 'both full from now' with genitive nūn; critics reply that the vowel may be built kasra, and Ibn al-Sa'igh says those deriving it from أوان also treat that form as inflected.
[ALUSI-FULL:v12p259#2/r8] السيوطي | prefers | Al-Suyuti chooses to treat الآن as inflected and accusative in an adverbial role because no valid cause for building it has been established; its use outside that role is unproven.
