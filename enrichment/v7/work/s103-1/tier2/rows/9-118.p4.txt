## QURTUBI-FULL: al-Qurṭubī, d. 671 AH
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:118#2/r1] كعب بن مالك | reports | Ka‘b says he had never missed a campaign except Tabuk and Badr; no one was reproached for Badr because the Muslims had set out for the Quraysh caravan and unexpectedly met their enemy.
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:118#2/r2] كعب بن مالك | reports | Ka‘b says the pledge at al-‘Aqaba mattered more to him than participation at Badr, despite Badr’s greater public fame.
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:118#2/r3] كعب بن مالك | reports | Ka‘b had never been stronger or wealthier than before Tabuk and gathered two mounts, while the expedition meant extreme heat, long travel, desert, and a large enemy.
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:118#2/r4] كعب بن مالك | reports | The Prophet announced the destination openly so Muslims could prepare; their large numbers were not recorded in a register, making a would-be absentee think revelation might not expose him.
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:118#2/r5] كعب بن مالك | reports | Ripe fruit and shade drew Ka‘b’s attention; he kept postponing preparations, assuming he could catch up, until the army had gone too far.
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:118#2/r6] كعب بن مالك | reports | After the departure, Ka‘b felt isolated because he saw only a suspected hypocrite or someone excused by God among those left in Medina.
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:118#2/r7] كعب بن مالك | reports | At Tabuk the Prophet asked about Ka‘b; one man blamed his clothes and vanity, but Mu‘adh ibn Jabal defended him as a man known for good, and the Prophet kept silent.
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:118#2/r8] كعب بن مالك | reports | The Prophet recognized the approaching figure as Abu Khaythama, who had donated a measure of dates and been mocked by hypocrites.
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:118#3/r1] كعب بن مالك | reports | As the Prophet returned, Ka‘b considered a lie but chose truth; on arrival the Prophet prayed two units in the mosque and received over eighty absentees, accepting their oaths outwardly and leaving their inner truth to God.
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:118#3/r2] كعب بن مالك | reports | Ka‘b confessed to the Prophet that he had no excuse and had been able and wealthy enough to go; he said a false excuse might satisfy the Prophet but anger God.
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:118#3/r3] النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | The Prophet said Ka‘b had spoken truthfully and ordered him to wait until God decided his case.
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:118#3/r4] كعب بن مالك | reports | Men from Banu Salama urged Ka‘b to invent an excuse, and he nearly returned to retract his truth; hearing Murara and Hilal had done likewise steadied him.
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:118#3/r5] كعب بن مالك | reports | The Prophet singled out the three for a ban on speaking; after fifty nights of estrangement, Ka‘b continued prayer and market visits while the others stayed home weeping.
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:118#4/r1] كعب بن مالك | reports | Ka‘b sought reassurance from his cousin Abu Qatada, but after repeated questions about his love for God and the Messenger, Abu Qatada answered only that God and His Messenger know.
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:118#4/r2] كعب بن مالك | reports | A Syrian merchant delivered a letter from the king of Ghassan offering Ka‘b refuge; Ka‘b regarded it as another trial and burned it.
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:118#4/r3] النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | After forty of the fifty nights, the Prophet ordered the three to separate from their wives without divorcing them; Hilal’s wife could serve him but not have marital relations.
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:118#4/r4] كعب بن مالك | reports | Ka‘b declined to request the same service permission given to Hilal’s wife, unsure what the Prophet would say to a young man.
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:118#4/r5] كعب بن مالك | reports | On the fiftieth morning, while he sat under the constriction described in the verse, a herald on Mount Sala announced good news and Ka‘b prostrated, recognizing relief.
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:118#4/r6] كعب بن مالك | reports | The Prophet announced God’s acceptance after the dawn prayer; people congratulated Ka‘b, and Talha ibn Ubayd Allah ran to greet him, the only Muhajir to do so.
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:118#4/r7] النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | The Prophet told Ka‘b that this was the best day since his birth and confirmed that the good news came from God.
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:118#4/r8] كعب بن مالك | reports | Ka‘b offered all his property as charity for repentance, but the Prophet advised him to keep some; Ka‘b kept his share at Khaybar and pledged to speak only truth.
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:118#4/r9] كعب بن مالك | reports | Ka‘b greeted the Prophet after prayer but received no answer and saw him turn away whenever Ka‘b looked toward him.
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:118#5/r1] كعب بن مالك | reports | Ka‘b says the revealed passage included God’s repentance for the Prophet and believers, the three left behind, and the command to fear God and stay with the truthful.
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:118#5/r2] كعب بن مالك | reports | After Islam, Ka‘b considered truthful speech his greatest blessing; lying would have destroyed him like the liars, whom God censured severely. | mentions 9:117, 9:118, 9:119, 9:95, 9:96
