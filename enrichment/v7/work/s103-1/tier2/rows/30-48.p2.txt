## TABATABAI: Muḥammad Ḥusayn al-Ṭabāṭabāʾī, d. 1402 AH
[TABATABAI:30:48-49/r1] author | holds | This is the third proof from God's acts, chiefly aimed at establishing resurrection; since denial of resurrection undermines law and religion, the Prophet is directed toward those ready to believe.
[TABATABAI:30:48-49/r2] author | holds | Wind stirs and spreads clouds, God stretches them in the upper air as He wills and divides them into masses, and rain falls through their gaps to give life to people, animals, and plants.
[TABATABAI:30:48-49/r3] author | holds | Ethāra is stirring and spreading, sky is whatever is above and shelters, kasaf is plural for pieces, wadq is rain, and khilāl are openings.
