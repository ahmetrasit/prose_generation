[NASAFI-FULL:v4p341#7/r2] author | holds | The first wāw in this oath sequence is unanimously understood as an oath particle; some also take the second wāw as an oath.
[NASAFI-FULL:v4p341#7/r3] الخليل | reports | Al-Khalil takes the second wāw as coordination, since introducing an oath before the first oath is complete is impermissible; replacing it with fa or thumma would leave the sense unchanged.
[NASAFI-FULL:v4p341#7/r4] author | reports | Those who treat the second wāw as an oath argue that coordination would attach a genitive noun to a noun governed by the oath wāw and an accusative temporal clause to one governed by the implicit verb 'I swear.'
