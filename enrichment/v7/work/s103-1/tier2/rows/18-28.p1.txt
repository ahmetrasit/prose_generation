## NAHHAS: Abū Jaʿfar al-Naḥḥās, d. 338 AH
[NAHHAS:v2p453/r10] نصر بن عاصم ومالك بن دينار وأبو عبد الرحمن | reports | They read ‘do not drive away those who call on their Lord’ with ghudwata; their evidence is the written wāw.
[NAHHAS:v2p453/r11] author | rejects | The orthographic wāw does not prove this reading because ṣalāt and ḥayāt are also written with wāw; al-ghudwa is normally indefinite and does not take the article.
[NAHHAS:v2p453/r12] الحسن | reports | He reads ‘do not let your eyes overlook’ with naṣb because the verb governs it.

## FARISI-HUJJA: Abū ʿAlī al-Fārisī, d. 377 AH
[FARISI-HUJJA:v5p140/r1] ابن عامر | reports | Ibn ʿĀmir alone reads al-ghudwah, treating the word as the named time without the ordinary article.
[FARISI-HUJJA:v5p140/r2] الباقون | reports | The other readers use al-ghadāh; the author considers this form clearer.
[FARISI-HUJJA:v5p140/r3] author | holds | Although ghudwah is a proper name, it may be generalized and take the article; spelling it with wāw proves no wāw sound, as in الصلاة.

[IBNJINNI-MUHTASAB:v1p139/r3] author | holds | Ibn Jinnī reads أغفلنا as 'We found him heedless,' not 'We made him heedless': if it expressed causation, the following consequence would normally use fa, not wa. | mentions 18:28
[IBNJINNI-MUHTASAB:v2p27/r3] الحسن | reports | Al-Ḥasan reads ولا تعد عينيك, a causative of 'your eyes passed beyond,' meaning turn your eyes away from the worldly adornment.
[IBNJINNI-MUHTASAB:v2p27/r4] عمرو بن فائد | reports | ʿAmr ibn Fāʾid reads من أغفلنا as 'one whom We found heedless,' not 'one whom We caused to be heedless'; the author defends this as an established أفعل usage and answers the theological objection. | mentions 2:74, 45:29, 50:4

## THALABI: al-Thaʿlabī, d. 427 AH

[THALABI:v6p166/r13] الفراء | reports | Al-Farra glosses faratan as abandoned or left behind.
