## QURTUBI-FULL: al-Qurṭubī, d. 671 AH
[QURTUBI-FULL:70:19/r1] الضحاك | reports | Al-Dahhak identifies the human created as halu‘ as a disbeliever.
[QURTUBI-FULL:70:19/r10] author | holds | Arab usage also calls a fast-moving, light-footed she-camel halwa‘a or halwa‘; a cited line witnesses to that word.
[QURTUBI-FULL:70:19/r11] author | holds | “Jazu‘an” and “manu‘an” are adjectives for halu‘, understood as moved forward before the following conditional, or as the predicate of an implied kana.
[QURTUBI-FULL:70:19/r2] قتادة ومجاهد | reports | Halu‘ linguistically means extreme greed and the worst, most excessive panic; the intensified form describes a person impatient in good and hardship.

[QURTUBI-FULL:70:19/r4] الضحاك | reports | Al-Dahhak says the halu‘ person is never satiated.
[QURTUBI-FULL:70:19/r5] author | reports | The miserly person is one who receives wealth but withholds God’s due from it.
[QURTUBI-FULL:70:19/r6] ابن كيسان | reports | Ibn Kaysan says people love what pleases them and flee what displeases them, yet God commands them to spend what they love and endure what they dislike.
[QURTUBI-FULL:70:19/r7] أبو عبيدة | reports | Abu Ubayda, as transmitted by Tha‘lab, defines halu‘ as failing to give thanks when blessed and failing to endure harm.
[QURTUBI-FULL:70:19/r8] ثعلب | reports | Tha‘lab also reads the following verses as defining halu‘: extreme distress when harmed and withholding good from people when prosperous.
