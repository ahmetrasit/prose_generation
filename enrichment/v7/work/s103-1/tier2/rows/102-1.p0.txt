## MUQATIL: Muqātil b. Sulaymān, d. 150 AH
[MUQATIL:102:1-7/r1] author | reports | Two Quraysh clans, Banu ʿAbd Manāf and Banu Sahm, disputed and boasted by comparing their leaders, honor, protection, and numbers.
[MUQATIL:102:1-7/r2] author | reports | They settled the rivalry by counting graves; Banu Sahm surpassed Banu ʿAbd Manāf by three houses, having been more numerous in pre-Islamic times.
[MUQATIL:102:1-7/r3] author | holds | The rivalry of multiplying and boasting distracted them from remembering the afterlife.

## TAB: al-Ṭabarī, d. 310 AH
[TAB:102:1/r1] author | holds | Competition in boasting about abundant wealth and numbers distracts people from obeying God and avoiding His anger.
[TAB:102:1/r2] قتادة | reports | Qatāda says tribes boasted that they outnumbered others until every one of them had died and entered the graves.
[TAB:102:1/r3] رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | The Prophet tells a reader that a person keeps only food consumed, clothes worn out, and charity sent ahead; this points to wealth as the object of competition.
[TAB:102:1/r4] أبي بن كعب | reports | Ubayy b. Kaʿb says people thought a warning about endless desire for wealth was Qur'an until this surah was revealed, ending that uncertainty.
[TAB:102:1/r5] author | holds | Al-Ṭabarī takes the Prophet's words after reciting the verse as evidence that التكاثر means competing in wealth.
