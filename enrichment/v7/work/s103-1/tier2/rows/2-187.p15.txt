[QURTUBI-FULL:2:187#21/r5] عبد الملك | reports | Mālik’s well-known view requires a person vowing ten days to retreat in a congregational mosque, and retreat elsewhere is invalidated by leaving for Friday prayer; ʿAbd al-Malik instead allows leaving and returning without invalidation, which the author prefers because Friday prayer is obligatory and outweighs recommended retreat.
[QURTUBI-FULL:2:187#21/r6] ابن خويز منداد | reports | Ibn Khuwayz Mindād, reporting Mālik’s view, says committing a grave sin invalidates iʿtikāf because grave sin opposes worship and avoiding what God forbids is central to devotion.
