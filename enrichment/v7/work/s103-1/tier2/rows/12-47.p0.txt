## WAHIDI-BASIT: al-Wāḥidī, d. 468 AH
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v12p135/r1] المفسرون | reports | The seven fat cows signify seven prosperous years, and يوسف's “you sow” means “sow!”
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v12p135/r10] الفراء | reports | Al-Farrāʾ parses دأبا as an accusative verbal noun from an implied تدأبون, indicated by the labor of sowing and by تزرعون.
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v12p135/r11] author | reports | Other grammarians parse دأبا as a verbal noun used circumstantially, meaning that they sow persistently.
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v12p135/r2] صاحب النظم | reports | تزرعون is a present-form response to the request for interpretation but functions as a command, as the explicit following command فما حصدتم فذروه shows.
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v12p135/r3] صاحب النظم | reports | The present form can also express prayer, as in 23:118; a prayer functions like a command, as in “say: Lord, forgive and have mercy.” | mentions 23:118
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v12p135/r4] الزجاج | reports | دأب means persistence in something or habitual practice.
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v12p135/r5] ابن عباس | reports | Here دأبا means seven consecutive years.
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v12p135/r6] أهل المعاني واللغة | reports | دأب means continuing in a habitual activity; the years of sowing are successive, or it means sow according to your usual practice.
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v12p135/r7] أبو علي الفارسي | reports | The more common pronunciation has a silent second consonant; the open vowel may be dialectal, like shamʿ/shamʿ and nahr/nahr.
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v12p135/r8] الفراء | reports | Al-Farrāʾ allows a silent second consonant to take a vowel when it is a guttural consonant.
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v12p135/r9] الزجاج | reports | Al-Zajjāj reports Kufan grammarians allow this vowel change with gutturals, while Basrans allow only forms heard in usage, not unattested forms.
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v12p136/r1] ابن عباس | reports | Thresh only what you intend to eat; leave the rest in its ears so it will not be attacked by pests.
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v12p136/r2] المفسرون | reports | Grain lasts longer and spoils less when kept in its ear, so Joseph advised the more prudent storage method.
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v12p136/r3] author | reports | Exegetes disagree whether Joseph's interpretation came from knowledge God had taught him or direct revelation; the prediction of an unasked-for abundant year supports direct revelation. | mentions 12:49
