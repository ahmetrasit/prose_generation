[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:46:35/r1] author | holds | When the Prophet and Companions felt overwhelmed by denial and oppression, God comforted them: a lifetime's wait is negligible on the eternal scale, so reward and punishment are not delayed.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:46:35/r2] Bazı tefsirciler | reports | Some take “messengers of resolve” to divide prophets into resolute and non-resolute groups, and list Noah, Abraham, Moses, Jesus, and Muhammad.
