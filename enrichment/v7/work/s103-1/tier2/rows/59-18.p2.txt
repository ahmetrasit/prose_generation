## ALUSI-FULL: al-Ālūsī, d. 1270 AH
[ALUSI-FULL:v28p60/r1] author | holds | Taqwā covers everything one does and leaves undone.

[ALUSI-FULL:v28p60/r2] author | holds | “Tomorrow” means Resurrection because it is near as tomorrow is to yesterday, or because worldly life is like a day before the distinct conditions of the next life; its indefiniteness magnifies an unknowable, tremendous day.
