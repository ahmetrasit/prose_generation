## WAHIDI-ASBAB: al-Wāḥidī, d. 468 AH
[WAHIDI-ASBAB:v1p297/r1] سلمان الفارسي | reports | Salmān reports that influential converts, including ʿUyayna and al-Aqraʿ, asked the Prophet to remove poor companions in coarse wool so they could sit near him and learn.
[WAHIDI-ASBAB:v1p297/r2] النبي | reports | After finding the poor companions remembering God at the back of the mosque, the Prophet praised God for commanding him to remain patient with them, saying life and death were with them.
[WAHIDI-ASBAB:v1p297/r3] ابن عباس | reports | Ibn ʿAbbās says the warning against obeying one heedless of God was revealed about Umayya b. Khalaf, who urged the Prophet to expel the poor and favor Meccan nobles; heedlessness means a heart sealed from monotheism, and desire means polytheism.
