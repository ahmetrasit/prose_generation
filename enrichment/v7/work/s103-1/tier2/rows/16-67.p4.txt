## BIQAI-FULL: al-Biqāʿī, d. 885 AH

[BIQAI-FULL:v4p284/r6] author | holds | The verse distinguishes intoxicating drink, which blocks reason and is blameworthy, from wholesome provision such as vinegar and date syrup, which harms neither body nor mind.
