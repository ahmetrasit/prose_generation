## IBNKATHIR-FULL: Ibn Kathīr, d. 774 AH
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p417#2/r1] ابن عباس | reports | Ibn Abbas interprets being facilitated toward ease as being guided to good; Zayd ibn Aslam says it means Paradise.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p417#2/r2] بعض السلف | reports | Some early authorities say good deeds lead to further good deeds, while sins bring further sins.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p417#2/r6] author | holds | Many verses and reports show God grants success to whoever intends good and abandons whoever intends evil, while all occurs by a determined decree.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p417#2/r7] رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | When Abu Bakr asks whether deeds follow a settled decree or a new decision, the Prophet replies that each person is made ready for what he was created to do.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p418/r1] رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | The Prophet says each persons place in Paradise or Hell and status as fortunate or wretched have been written, prompting the question whether people should then rely on fate.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p418/r2] رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | In a fuller version, the Prophet says the fortunate are enabled toward fortunate deeds and the wretched toward wretched deeds, then recites the two paths.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p418/r3] رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | Umar asks whether human deeds are already decreed; the Prophet says each person is facilitated, with the fortunate working toward happiness and the wretched toward misery. Tirmidhi grades the report hasan sahih.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p418/r4] رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | Jabir asks whether deeds concern a settled matter or a new one; the Prophet says the matter is settled, and tells Suraqah that every worker is facilitated for his work.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p419/r1] رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | Muslim also transmits the report in which the Prophet says every worker is facilitated for the work for which he was created.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p419/r2] رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | Two young men ask whether deeds follow what is written; the Prophet says to act, since everyone is facilitated for the work he was created to do.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p419/r3] رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | The Prophet tells Abu al-Darda that each person is prepared for what he was created for; Ibn Kathir says Ahmad alone transmits this route.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p420/r2] author | reports | A report cited by Ibn Jarir says the verses concern Abu Bakr, who freed vulnerable women and elderly converts in Mecca to seek God's reward despite his father's advice to free stronger men for protection.

## DURR-FULL: al-Suyūṭī, d. 911 AH
[DURR-FULL:v8p534/r5] ابن مسعود | reports | He reports that Abu Bakr bought and freed Bilal for God; the verses contrast their efforts with those of Umayya and Ubayy, identify the good as the testimony of faith, and the hard path as Hell.
[DURR-FULL:v8p534/r6] ابن عباس عن عكرمة | reports | Giving means giving from one's bounty and fearing God; the good promise is God's compensation, ease is good, and the opposite is withholding wealth, self-sufficiency, denial, and ease toward evil.
[DURR-FULL:v8p534/r7] قتادة | reports | He said the giver pays God's due, avoids prohibitions, and believes God's promise; the miser withholds that due, feels self-sufficient, and denies the promise.
[DURR-FULL:v8p535/r2] زيد بن أسلم | reports | He glossed the easy path as Paradise.
[DURR-FULL:v8p535/r3] عامر بن عبد الله بن الزبير | reports | Abu Bakr freed weak women who accepted Islam; when his father urged him to free strong protectors instead, he replied that he sought God's reward, and a family member said these verses were revealed about him.
[DURR-FULL:v8p535/r5] علي بن أبي طالب عن النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | The Prophet said each person's place in Paradise or Fire is written, but commanded people to act: each is made ready for the deeds of the saved or the wretched, then recited this passage.
[DURR-FULL:v8p535/r6] أبو عبد الرحمن السلمي | reports | After the decree in 54:49 was revealed, a man asked whether deeds begin anew or are already settled; the Prophet replied to act, since each is made ready for the easy or hard path. | mentions 54:49
[DURR-FULL:v8p537/r2] جابر بن عبد الله عن النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | Suraqa asked whether deeds were predetermined; the Prophet affirmed the decree but urged action, since each person is eased toward what he was created for, then recited the passage.
[DURR-FULL:v8p537/r3] بشير بن كعب الأسلمي | reports | Asked about deeds, he reports the Prophet answering that they follow the written decree, yet people must act because each is eased toward what he was created for; he recited these verses.

## IBNASHUR: Ibn ʿĀshūr, d. 1393 AH
[IBNASHUR:92:7/r1] author | holds | Taysir makes something easy to obtain; its direct object is what is made easy, while the following li- phrase names the beneficiary, as in the cited verses. | mentions 20:26, 54:17
[IBNASHUR:92:7/r10] author | reports | One solution keeps facilitate literal and treats the construction as rhetorical inversion: the original sense is making the easy or hard path easy for the person, with inversion intensifying the facilitation.
[IBNASHUR:92:7/r11] author | reports | The inversion is compared with the Arabic expression about presenting a she-camel to a watering trough.
[IBNASHUR:92:7/r12] author | reports | A second solution uses facilitate figuratively for preparation; li- is causal, and al-yusra and al-usra denote Paradise and Hell, with entry understood after the preposition.
[IBNASHUR:92:7/r13] author | holds | On the preparation interpretation, making entry quick is likened to making a difficult thing easy, since difficulty is associated with delay and ease with speed; the cited verse uses easy in the sense of quick. | mentions 50:44
[IBNASHUR:92:7/r14] author | holds | The same reading makes facilitation follow the acts already produced by giving, restraint, affirmation, or denial, and therefore concerns continuing those acts or increasing them.
[IBNASHUR:92:7/r15] author | reports | A further possibility keeps facilitation literal without inversion: al-yusra and al-usra describe the corresponding conditions, and facilitation means persisting in the deed.
[IBNASHUR:92:7/r16] علي | reports | In Sahih al-Bukhari, Ali reports that at a funeral in Baqi al-Gharqad the Prophet said each person's place in Paradise or Hell is written; when asked whether they should rely on that, he commanded them to work, since each person is eased toward the deeds for which they were created, then recited these verses.
