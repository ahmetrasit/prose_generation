## AKHFASH: al-Akhfash al-Awsaṭ, d. 215 AH
[AKHFASH:v1p398/r2] author | holds | وادكر is form VIII from ذكرت, underlyingly اذتكر; adjacent sounds assimilate, but dāl preserves voicing instead of ta, and ṭāʾ is avoided because it is emphatic.
[AKHFASH:v1p398/r3] author | reports | Some say مذكر, replacing tāʾ with dhāl before assimilation.
[AKHFASH:v1p398/r4] author | prefers | In the reading أن يصلحا, ṣād does not assimilate into tāʾ because of voicing and emphasis; tāʾ becomes ṣād instead, while يصطلحا is the better form. | mentions 4:128
