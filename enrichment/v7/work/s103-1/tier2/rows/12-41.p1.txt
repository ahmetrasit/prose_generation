## QURTUBI-FULL: al-Qurṭubī, d. 671 AH
[QURTUBI-FULL:12:41/r1] author | holds | Joseph told the cupbearer he would return to serving the king after three days, while the other man would be crucified after three days and eaten by birds; whether dreamed or not, the decree was settled.
[QURTUBI-FULL:12:41/r2] author | reports | Some linguists use سقى and أسقى synonymously; al-Nahhas says most distinguish giving someone water to drink from providing a source of water. | mentions 77:27
[QURTUBI-FULL:12:41/r3] author | holds | A false dream interpretation does not bind someone; Joseph's interpretation became a decree because he was a prophet and God made it occur to confirm his prophethood.
[QURTUBI-FULL:12:41/r4] قتادة | reports | A report from Qatada says a man told Umar he dreamed of alternating lush growth and drought; Umar predicted alternating faith and apostasy ending in death as an unbeliever, and the man said he had dreamed nothing.
[QURTUBI-FULL:12:41/r5] author | holds | The author says this sort of prediction was exceptional for Umar, a divinely inspired man whose intuitions and statements came true, citing two further examples.
