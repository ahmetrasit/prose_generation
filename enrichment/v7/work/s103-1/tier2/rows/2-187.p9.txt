## RAZI-FULL: Fakhr al-Dīn al-Rāzī, d. 606 AH
[RAZI-FULL:v5p87#2/r1] جمهور المفسرين | reports | Most exegetes say the first Muslim fasting rule barred eating, drinking, and intercourse after sleep or the last evening prayer, and this verse abrogated that rule.
