## KURANYOLU-TEFSIR: Hayreddin Karaman, Mustafa Çağrıcı, İbrahim Kâfi Dönmez, Sadrettin Gümüş
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:107:4/r1] author | holds | After condemning failure in one's duties toward people, the surah criticizes those who fail to fulfill genuine duties of worship toward God.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:107:4/r11] author | holds | The surah stresses that sincere worship of God and mutual aid and solidarity cannot be separated in religious devotion.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:107:4/r12] author | holds | A truly believing person fulfills duties toward God and creation sincerely, does good, and enables rather than blocks its spread through social cooperation.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:107:4/r2] author | holds | Three faults are given among people who pray: distance from prayer's essence, ostentation in worship, and obstructing good.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:107:4/r6] Taberî | reports | Tabari reports that ma'un has been explained as alms, other financial duties, benefits people withhold from each other, a right, a loan, or property.

## STUDYQURAN: Seyyed Hossein Nasr (ed.-in-chief), Caner K. Dagli, Maria Massi Dakake, Joseph E. B. Lumbard, Mohammed Rustom
[STUDYQURAN:107:7/r1] IK | reports | The verse describes people who perform devotions well but fail to act kindly toward others.
[STUDYQURAN:107:7/r2] widespread interpretation | reports | Māʿūn commonly means utensils or tools that neighbors share or lend, such as a pickax, pot, or bucket.
[STUDYQURAN:107:7/r3] others | reports | Other interpretations take māʿūn as anything beneficial, obligatory zakāh, property generally in Qurayshi dialect, or refusing guests.
[STUDYQURAN:107:7/r4] author | holds | All readings point to hypocrites' stinginess: they attend prayer lazily and spend reluctantly. | mentions 9:54
[STUDYQURAN:107:intro/r1] author | reports | Most call al-Māʿūn an early Makkan sūrah after al-Takāthur, though some assign vv. 4–7 to Madinah; since the whole sūrah addresses religious hypocrisy, it may all be Madinan, when hypocrisy could gain political advantage. | mentions 102:1
[STUDYQURAN:107:intro/r2] author | holds | Religious hypocrisy is not treated as limited to Muslims alone. | mentions 63:1, 63:2, 63:3, 63:4, 63:5, 63:6, 63:7, 63:8
[STUDYQURAN:107:intro/r3] author | holds | The sūrah takes its name from māʿūn in its last verse, broadly meaning kindness, charity, or assistance.
