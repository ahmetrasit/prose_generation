## NAHHAS: Abū Jaʿfar al-Naḥḥās, d. 338 AH
[NAHHAS:v3p278/r6] author | holds | The verse does not deny punishment in the grave; the Prophet authentically sought refuge from it and instructed others to do so.
[NAHHAS:v3p278/r7] عبد الله بن مسعود | reports | He reports that when Umm Ḥabība asked God to preserve her husband, father, and brother, the Prophet said lifespans and provisions are fixed and advised her to seek refuge from Hell or grave punishment.
[NAHHAS:v3p278/r8] author | reports | One view says the dead mean the quiet interval before resurrection and call it an hour because they did little; another says worldly life seems like an hour because it passes, despite their claiming knowledge of the unseen.
[NAHHAS:v3p278/r9] author | holds | Ka-dhālika kānū yuʾfakūn means they used to lie in worldly life; the Qur’an rejects the claim that people cannot lie at the resurrection by describing them swearing falsely there. | mentions 58:18

## WAHIDI-BASIT: al-Wāḥidī, d. 468 AH
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v18p84#2/r1] ابن عباس والمفسرون | reports | Ibn Abbas and the exegetes say criminals swear at the Resurrection that they stayed in their graves for only an hour.
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v18p84#2/r2] قتادة | reports | Qatada instead says they will claim to have lived in the world for only an hour; the author rejects this because the next verse supports the grave interpretation. | mentions 30:56
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v18p84#2/r3] الزجاج | reports | Al-Zajjaj takes كذلك as comparing their present oath to their earlier false speech, since they swore without reliable knowledge.
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v18p84#2/r4] الكلبي | reports | Al-Kalbi says their claim of only an hour was false just as they had lied in worldly life.
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v18p84#2/r5] مقاتل | reports | Muqatil says they denied resurrection in worldly life and now lie that they spent only an hour in their graves.
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v18p84#2/r6] ابن قتيبة | reports | Ibn Qutayba says they lie now as before and explains أفك as being turned away from truth and goodness.
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v18p84#2/r7] مقاتل | reports | Muqatil and others explain that seeing the afterlife made them regard their stay in the world or graves as very short.
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v18p84#2/r8] author | prefers | The author says they deliberately swear an unexcused, plain lie so believers can recognize their falsehood and infer that their former idolatry and unbelief were lies too.
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v18p84#2/r9] author | holds | The passive يؤفكون indicates that God turns them from truth in their oath just as they were turned away from faith in worldly life, affirming divine decree; an active form would have expressed their own lying.

## TABRISI: al-Ṭabrisī, d. 548 AH
[TABRISI:30:51-55/r9] الكلبي | reports | The criminals swear at resurrection that they remained in their graves for only an hour; this interpretation is attributed to al-Kalbi and Muqatil.
[TABRISI:30:55/r1] author | reports | The criminals may mean they spent only an hour in worldly life because they regard its duration as insignificant, or only an hour after the punishment of the grave ceased; al-Jubbāʾī and Matta hold the latter.
[TABRISI:30:55/r2] أبو علي | reports | To explain false oaths despite certain knowledge in the afterlife, Abu Ali and Abu Hashim say they swear according to their conjecture, since they did not know how long they remained in the graves.
[TABRISI:30:55/r3] أبو بكر بن الإخشيد | reports | Another answer is that their oaths could occur before their minds were fully developed.
[TABRISI:30:55/r4] author | holds | The author rejects using this verse to deny punishment in the grave, since they may mean that only an hour passed after God’s punishment there.
[TABRISI:30:55/r5] author | reports | Yuʾfakūn can mean that they lied in worldly life or that ignorance turned them away from truth in both abodes.

## IBNJAWZI-ZAD: Ibn al-Jawzī, d. 597 AH
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v6p311#2/r1] الزجاج | reports | In the Quran, الساعة means the moment when Resurrection occurs; it is indefinite because no one knows which hour it will be.
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v6p311#2/r2] author | holds | The criminals are polytheists who swear they remained in their graves less than an hour.
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v6p311#2/r3] ابن قتيبة | reports | أفك means a person was turned away from truth, so their claim is a lie as their worldly claims were.
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v6p311#2/r4] author | reports | Another explanation is that God exposes them before believers on Judgment Day by making them swear to something whose falsity is evident, confirming their worldly lies.

## QURTUBI-FULL: al-Qurṭubī, d. 671 AH
[QURTUBI-FULL:30:55/r1] author | holds | The criminals who swear are the polytheists.
[QURTUBI-FULL:30:55/r10] author | holds | The Quran also says that they will swear to God as they swear to people and falsely think they have something to stand on. | mentions 58:18
[QURTUBI-FULL:30:55/r11] author | holds | Another passage depicts their claim that they were not polytheists and exposes their lie. | mentions 6:23, 6:24
[QURTUBI-FULL:30:55/r2] author | rejects | The claim that they remained only an hour does not disprove punishment in the grave, which is established by authentic Prophetic reports and the Prophet's instruction to seek refuge from it.
[QURTUBI-FULL:30:55/r3] عبد الله بن مسعود | reports | He reports that the Prophet heard Umm Ḥabība ask for blessing in her husband, father, and brother, then directed her to seek refuge from Hellfire and grave punishment; Qurtubi says the reports are well-known in Muslim, Bukhari, and others.
[QURTUBI-FULL:30:55/r4] author | reports | One interpretation says there must be an interval of stillness before Resurrection, so they describe their stay then as only an hour.
[QURTUBI-FULL:30:55/r5] author | reports | Alternatively, they mean worldly life, which seems brief because it has vanished; this resembles the description in al-Nāziʿāt. | mentions 79:46
[QURTUBI-FULL:30:55/r6] author | holds | They swear about the unseen without knowing; kādhālika kānū yuʾfakūn says they used to lie in worldly life, and afaka means to turn someone away from truth and good.
[QURTUBI-FULL:30:55/r7] author | holds | The word maʾfūka can describe land deprived of rain.
[QURTUBI-FULL:30:55/r8] جماعة من أهل النظر | reports | Some rationalists claim lying cannot occur on Resurrection Day; Qurtubi rejects this because the Quran says they are diverted from truth and depicts them swearing falsely then. | mentions 30:55
[QURTUBI-FULL:30:55/r9] author | holds | Their false oath on that day repeats their habitual turning away from truth in worldly life.
