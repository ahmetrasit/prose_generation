[WAHIDI-BASIT:v13p570/r2] العلماء بأخبار القدماء | reports | Many generations later, the righteous king Tindusis faced a dispute about the Resurrection and prayed for a sign; God inspired a man to dismantle the cave entrance while building a sheep enclosure.
