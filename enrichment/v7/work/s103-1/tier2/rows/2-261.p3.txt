[SAMIN-DURR:2:261#2/r5] author | holds | Without adjacency, when a noun has both sound and broken plurals the broken plural is used for counting; sound forms such as ṭarīqāt and laylāt are possible but disfavored beside seven paths or nights.
[SAMIN-DURR:2:261#2/r6] author | holds | Among competing plurals, mafaʿil broken plurals are preferred to sound plurals (e.g. three Aḥmads/Zaynabs); when the non-sound plural is uncommon, sound or common broken forms are preferred, with the rare alternatives noted.
