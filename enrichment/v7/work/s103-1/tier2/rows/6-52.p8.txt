## ELMALILI: Elmalılı Muhammed Hamdi Yazır, d. 1361 AH
[ELMALILI:6:50-55#2/r1] author | holds | The Prophet must not expel people who worship their Lord morning and evening, continually seeking God's presence and favor with sincere, exclusive devotion.
[ELMALILI:6:50-55#2/r2] author | holds | Neither their reckoning belongs to the Prophet nor his reckoning to them; he must not expel them on the pretext of accounting for them.
[ELMALILI:6:50-55#2/r3] author | holds | Expelling these worshippers would make the Prophet one of the wrongdoers.
[ELMALILI:6:50-55#2/r4] author | reports | Elmalılı reports that Quraysh leaders asked Muhammad to remove poor Muslims such as Suhayb, Khabbāb, Bilāl, ʿAmmār, and Salmān so they might visit; after Umar urged trying it and they pressed for a written promise, the verse was revealed and the Prophet discarded the page.
[ELMALILI:6:50-55#2/r5] Selmân ve Habbâb | reports | Salmān and Khabbāb say the verse concerned them: the Prophet sat close with them and would not leave before they did; after 18:28, he stayed with them and thanked God for the command.
[ELMALILI:6:50-55#2/r6] author | holds | God tests one group through another: arrogant elites scorn poor, sincere believers as undeserving, while faith and sincerity are divine gifts that challenge worldly pride and status.
