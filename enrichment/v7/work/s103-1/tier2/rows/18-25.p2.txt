## QURTUBI-FULL: al-Qurṭubī, d. 671 AH
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:25/r1] ابن مسعود | reports | Ibn Masud's codex adds wa-qalu, 'and they said,' before the statement of duration.
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:25/r10] النقاش | reports | Al-Naqqash explains nine as the difference between 300 solar years and their equivalent lunar years, about one year per 33⅓ solar years; al-Ghaznawi gives a similar account.
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:25/r11] الجمهور | reports | The majority read '300' with an indefinite hundred and accusative sinin, interpreting the order as '300 years'; sinin is apposition, explication, or specification.
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:25/r12] حمزة والكسائي | reports | Hamza and al-Kisai join miʾa to sinin without tanwin, treating the plural as equivalent to singular sana.
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:25/r13] أبو علي | reports | Abu Ali notes that numbers normally joined to singular nouns can also be joined to plurals; Ibn Masud's codex instead has '300 year.'
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:25/r14] الضحاك | reports | Al-Dahhak reads thalathamiʾata sunun with nominative plural ending.
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:25/r15] أبو عمرو | reports | Abu Amr has a variant reading of tisʿan with fatḥa on tāʾ; the majority use kasra.

[QURTUBI-FULL:18:25/r2] الطبري | reports | Al-Tabari says Israelites disputed how long had passed since the youths were discovered; God clarifies that 300 years and nine describe their sleep, while the later period is unknown to humans.
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:25/r3] ابن عطية | reports | On this reading, the first 'they remained' refers to sleep, and the second to the time after discovery until Muhammad's era or their bodies' decay.

[QURTUBI-FULL:18:25/r5] الضحاك | reports | Al-Dahhak says the later period extended until they died; another report says the nine's units were unclear to people, so knowledge was referred to God.
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:25/r6] author | holds | The apparent Arabic sense is nine years because years were just named; the historical appearance is that the youths entered the cave not long after Jesus while some disciples remained.
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:25/r7] القشيري | reports | Al-Qushayri says the nine cannot mean nights or hours because the preceding word years supplies the understood unit, as in a hundred dirhams and five.
