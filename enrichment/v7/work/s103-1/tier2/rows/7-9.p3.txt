## KURANYOLU-TEFSIR: Hayreddin Karaman, Mustafa Çağrıcı, İbrahim Kâfi Dönmez, Sadrettin Gümüş
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:7:8/r1] author | holds | Judgment aims to reward or punish justly according to the quantity and quality of deeds, which requires a measure; here ‘weight’ names that measure.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:7:8/r3] author | holds | Good deeds may receive more than their equivalent, bad deeds equivalent punishment, while repentance, intercession, or sheer divine mercy may forgive sins or increase rewards without violating justice. | mentions 6:160
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:7:8/r5] some commentators | reports | Some commentators say the things weighed are deeds made into tangible objects; others say they are the records of deeds.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:7:8/r6] author | reports | Commentators differ over whether the weighing is a literal balance or metaphor; the manner and instrument are known to God.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:7:8/r7] author | holds | For people, the essential point is that God records intentions, thoughts, feelings, words, and actions, then counts and judges them completely fairly, rewarding or punishing by their nature and amount.
