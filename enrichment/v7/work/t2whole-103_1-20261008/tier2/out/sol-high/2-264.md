[ابن عباس: DURR-FULL,IBNABIHATIM,TAB-FULL; الضحاك: TAB-FULL; الربيع: TAB-FULL; السدي: TAB-FULL; قتادة: TAB-FULL; ABUHAYYAN-FULL,ALUSI-FULL,BURSEVI,IBNATIYYA-FULL,SAMIN-DURR,TABATABAI,TABRISI,THALABI,WAHIDI-BASIT,ZAJJAJ; أبان بن تغلب: MAWARDI-FULL; ابن قتيبة: IBNJAWZI-ZAD; أبو عبيد: RAZI-FULL; الأخفش: QURTUBI-FULL; الكسائي: QURTUBI-FULL; الصادق عليه السلام: TABATABAI]
- 2:264/v101 Poetry cited for ṣafwān or related ṣafī describes a smooth stone or stone surface, including lines by Imruʾ al-Qays, Abū Dhuʾayb, and Aws ibn Ḥajar. [الشاعر: TAB-FULL; THALABI; امرؤ القيس: IBNATIYYA-FULL; أبو ذؤيب: IBNATIYYA-FULL; ابن عباس: DURR-FULL]

## صَفْوَانٍ (word 21) · grammar
- 2:264/v102 Ṣafwān may be treated as a collective noun with singular ṣafwāna; this analysis explains the singular pronoun ʿalayhi. [ABUHAYYAN-FULL,ALUSI-FULL,TAB-FULL,THALABI; الأخفش: QURTUBI-FULL; أبو البقاء: SAMIN-DURR+]
- 2:264/v103 Ṣafwān may instead be singular, with plural forms reported as ṣafwān, ṣafī, or ṣafiyy. [ABUHAYYAN-FULL,TAB-FULL,THALABI; الأخفش: QURTUBI-FULL; الكسائي: QURTUBI-FULL,SAMIN-DURR; النحاس: QURTUBI-FULL,SAMIN-DURR]
- 2:264/v104 Al-Mubarrad rejects al-Kisāʾī's ṣafī plural analysis, treating ṣafī as plural of ṣafā; others likewise reject the singular analysis of ṣafwān. [ABUHAYYAN-FULL,IBNATIYYA-FULL,QURTUBI-FULL,SAMIN-DURR-,TABRISI; الكسائي: QURTUBI-FULL,SAMIN-DURR; النحاس: QURTUBI-FULL]
- 2:264/v105 Ṣafwān can be analyzed as a plural of ṣafā, although the faʿlān plural pattern is rare; some prefer this to a singular analysis. [IBNATIYYA-FULL,QURTUBI-FULL,SAMIN-DURR-; أبو البقاء: SAMIN-DURR]

## صَفْوَانٍ (word 21) · readings
- 2:264/v106 Saʿīd ibn al-Musayyib and al-Zuhrī read ṣafwān with fatḥa on the fāʾ, a dialectal but unusual noun form; al-Zaʿrī also vocalizes it thus. [سعيد بن المسيب والزهري: IBNJINNI-MUHTASAB; الزعري: THALABI; سعيد بن المسيب: KASHSHAF-FULL,QURTUBI-FULL; الزهري وابن المسيب: IBNATIYYA-FULL; ابن المسيب، والزهري: ABUHAYYAN-FULL; SAMIN-DURR]
- 2:264/v107 A reported reading vocalizes ṣafwān with fatḥa, with its plural still ṣafwān, analogous to karawān and warshān. [الزعري: THALABI; سعيد بن المسيب والزهري: IBNJINNI-MUHTASAB]

## عَلَيْهِ (word 22) · grammar
- 2:264/v108 The dust clause describes the stone: either ʿalayhi turāb is a full adjectival clause or ʿalayhi alone modifies the stone and turāb begins a new clause; the latter is preferred by al-Samīn. [SAMIN-DURR+,TABRISI]

## تُرَابٌۭ (word 23) · meaning
- 2:264/v109 Turāb also appears as tū-rāb; related tariba means becoming poor, atraba becoming rich, and turab can mean a same-age peer. [ABUHAYYAN-FULL,SAMIN-DURR,TABRISI]
- 2:264/v110 Other related vocabulary includes turāʾib for chest bones and dhā matraba for a poor person sitting in dust. [SAMIN-DURR,TABRISI]

## فَأَصَابَهُۥ (word 24) · grammar
- 2:264/v111 Aṣāba derives from ṣāba/yaṣūbu; its alif originated as wāw. [SAMIN-DURR]
- 2:264/v112 Fa-aṣābahu coordinates with the implied verb for dust settled on the stone; its pronoun may return to stone or dust, while the pronoun in fa-tarakahu returns to stone. [ABUHAYYAN-FULL,SAMIN-DURR]

## وَابِلٌۭ (word 25) · meaning
- 2:264/v113 Wābil is heavy, abundant, forceful rain, often described as falling in large drops. - 2:264/v114 The root of wābil also conveys severity or bad consequence, as in severe blows or punishment; Arabic has related verb and adjective forms. [QURTUBI-FULL,SAMIN-DURR,TAB-FULL,TABRISI]
- 2:264/v115 Al-Naḍr orders named rain stages from light spray and drizzle through drops and steady rain to heavy wābil and generous downpour. [النضر: ABUHAYYAN-FULL; النضر بن شميل: SAMIN-DURR]

## صَلْدًۭا (word 27) · grammar
- 2:264/v123 Ṣaldan is accusative as the state in which rain left the stone. [TABRISI]

## مِّمَّا (word 32) · meaning
- 2:264/v124 Their 'earnings' are what they spent or the deeds performed to acquire a return; spending is called acquisition because they intended to earn reward. [ABUHAYYAN-FULL,MAWARDI-FULL,QURTUBI-FULL,SAMIN-DURR]

## وَٱللَّهُ (word 34) · rhetoric
- 2:264/v125 Calling the corrupt givers disbelievers sharply warns believers away from reproach, harm, and ostentation, and explains why the show-off exemplifies disbelief. [ALUSI-FULL-,BAYDAWI-FULL,BIQAI-FULL,BURSEVI,IBNASHUR-FULL,TAB-FULL,TABATABAI]

## وَٱللَّهُ (word 34) · referent
- 2:264/v126 The closing description can identify those who die in unbelief; another reading limits the lack of guidance to their present unbelieving state or deeds. [ABUHAYYAN-FULL,IBNATIYYA-FULL,NASAFI-FULL; المعتزلة: RAZI-FULL]

## وَٱللَّهُ (word 34) · interpretation
- 2:264/v127 God does not guide disbelievers toward right spending or good while they persist in disbelief or prefer falsehood. [ABUHAYYAN-FULL,ELMALILI,IBNATIYYA-FULL,NASAFI-FULL,TAB-FULL,ZAJJAJ; أبو إسحاق: WAHIDI-BASIT]
- 2:264/v128 God does not make guidance the recompense for their disbelief. [WAHIDI-BASIT,WAHIDI-WAJIZ,ZAJJAJ]
- 2:264/v129 God does not guide unbelievers to Paradise by their works, or grant them the additional care and success given to believers. [TABRISI; المعتزلة: RAZI-FULL]
