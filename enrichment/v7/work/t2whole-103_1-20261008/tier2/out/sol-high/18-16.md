## whole verse · interpretation
- 18:16/v001 The Qurʾān selects lessons for guidance rather than supplying a full historical account, omitting names, ancestry, occupations, social rank, dates, rulers, cities, persecutors, and the dog's identity; later historians filled these gaps and produced divergent accounts. [TABATABAI]

## وَإِذِ (word 1) · grammar

## ٱعْتَزَلْتُمُوهُمْ (word 2) · meaning

## فَأْوُۥٓا۟ (word 7) · interpretation

## فَأْوُۥٓا۟ (word 7) · law

- 18:16/v052 Seclusion to escape religious trials is prescribed, as in the hadith of sheep taken to the mountains; outside such trials, abandoning communal prayers for isolation is not prescribed. [IBNKATHIR-FULL]

## إِلَى (word 8) · referent

- 18:16/v105 Al-Kisāʾī is reported as accepting only a particular form for the bodily elbow, while al-Farrāʾ accepts both forms for elbow and aid. (Reports conflict on which vowel pattern al-Kisāʾī restricts to the elbow: some say kasra mīm, others open mīm.) [الكسائي: ABUHAYYAN-FULL,RAZI-FULL,SAMIN-DURR,TAB-FULL; الكسائي والفراء: NAHHAS; الفراء: ALUSI-FULL]
- 18:16/v106 The kasra form is more common or more idiomatic, especially for the bodily joint, although both forms are known. (Rāzī also reports that the fatḥa form is more regular by analogy.) [RAZI-FULL,TAB-FULL+,ZAJJAJ; الزجاج: TABRISI]
- 18:16/v107 One analysis distinguishes the two vowel patterns as benefit received from another versus benefit one extends to another. [MAWARDI-FULL]

## مِّرْفَقًۭا (word 19) · grammar
- 18:16/v108 The noun for an aid can be patterned like masjid, a lexical noun, a dialectal form from rafiqa, or a verbal noun meaning gentleness or ease. (The authorities offer alternative morphological analyses, not one agreed derivation.) [AKHFASH,QIRAAT-ER; نحويي أهل البصرة: TAB-FULL; الأخفش سعيد: NAHHAS; أبو الحسن: FARISI-HUJJA,TABRISI; أبو علي: WAHIDI-BASIT]
- 18:16/v109 The open-mīm form can be analyzed as a verbal noun like rifq on the pattern mafʿal; others call it an anomalous verbal noun. [نافع وابن عامر: BAYDAWI-FULL,IBNATIYYA-FULL; أبو زيد: ABUHAYYAN-FULL; أبو حاتم وأبو زيد: SAMIN-DURR]
- 18:16/v110 Abū Ḥātim takes the open-mīm form as a place noun, whereas others associate it with the bodily joint. [الكسائي: ABUHAYYAN-FULL; أبو حاتم وأبو زيد: SAMIN-DURR; الفراء: ALUSI-FULL]
