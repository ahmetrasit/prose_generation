## whole verse · meaning
- 12:45/v001 Yughāth in the neighboring interpretation may derive from rescue and relief or from rain; an Arab woman's request for rain illustrates the latter sense. [ABUHAYYAN-FULL]
- 12:45/v002 Ṣiddīq is an intensive form meaning very truthful or strongly affirming truth; the cupbearer uses it for Joseph after long acquaintance and the accuracy of his earlier dream interpretations. [ABUHAYYAN-FULL,QURTUBI-FULL,TABRISI]
- 12:45/v003 In the related grammatical discussion, يصطلحا is preferred to أن يصلحا; ṣād does not assimilate into tāʾ because of voicing and emphasis, so tāʾ changes instead. (An analogy for the assimilation discussed under وادّكر.) [AKHFASH+]

## whole verse · interpretation
- 12:45/v008 Confused dreams and passing inner thoughts are disregarded, whereas a true vision can be interpreted and investigated. (This relates the cupbearer's offer to the courtiers' preceding judgment of the king's dream.) [IBNATIYYA-FULL]
- 12:45/v009 The officials' response can instead mean that they cannot interpret dreams generally, or that they can interpret only true dreams. (Alternative understandings of their preceding statement.) [الملأ: TABATABAI]
- 12:45/v010 Joseph hears the king's dream and explains it without reproaching the cupbearer for forgetting him or first demanding release. [IBNKATHIR-FULL]
- 12:45/v011 The cupbearer hoped to return with Joseph's interpretation either to explain the dream or to show Joseph's knowledge and secure his release. [QURTUBI-FULL]

## وَٱدَّكَرَ (word 5) · meaning
- 12:45/v014 The verb can mean simply 'remembered and told' or 'mentioned'; its reflexive form does not by itself establish an earlier lapse of memory, just as other reflexive forms can equal simpler verbs. (This bears on whether Joseph or the cupbearer forgot earlier.) [ابن الأنباري: RAZI-FULL,WAHIDI-BASIT; IBNJAWZI-ZAD]
- 12:45/v015 The expressions used for remembering are synonymous. [ISLAHI-TADABBUR]

## وَٱدَّكَرَ (word 5) · grammar
- 12:45/v016 The common form ادّكر derives from an underlying اذتكر or اذْتكر: the infixed tāʾ changes to dāl and the adjacent dhāl assimilates, yielding doubled dāl. The substitution accommodates voicing and articulation and is described as the more eloquent form. (Al-Akhfash says ṭāʾ is avoided because of emphasis, and compares the consonant behavior of أن يصلحا and يصطلحا at 4:128.) [ABUHAYYAN-FULL,AKHFASH,ALUSI-FULL,BURSEVI,IBNASHUR-FULL,IBNATIYYA-FULL,MAJAZ,NAHHAS,NASAFI-FULL,QURTUBI-FULL,TABRISI,ZAJJAJ+; الزجاج: IBNJAWZI-ZAD; العامة: SAMIN-DURR]
- 12:45/v017 An alternative morphological analysis replaces the infixed tāʾ with dhāl before assimilation, producing a form with doubled dhāl. [AKHFASH,ZAJJAJ+]
- 12:45/v018 The clause 'and remembered' is most likely circumstantial, referring to the survivor, though it can also be coordinated with 'survived.' [ABUHAYYAN-FULL,BURSEVI,SAMIN-DURR+]
- 12:45/v019 The verb can be understood as 'he sought to remember.' [BIQAI-FULL]

## أُمَّةٍ (word 7) · meaning
- 12:45/v042 The time sense of أمّة is explained through its sense of a collected group: many days and hours form a span, or a whole generation passes during it. A generation itself can be an ummah. (Ibn Durustawayh says the time sense requires an omitted word such as 'period'; Ibn ʿĀshūr relates the generation sense to one reading of Q 3:110.) [ALUSI-FULL,BURSEVI,IBNASHUR-FULL,TABRISI,WAHIDI-BASIT; ابن درستويه: QURTUBI-FULL]
- 12:45/v043 أمّة can mean a group united in purpose or a large community; it has singular form and collective meaning, and each animal species can be called a community. [ابن درستويه: QURTUBI-FULL; الأخفش: QURTUBI-FULL; TABRISI]

## أَنَا۠ (word 8) · rhetoric
- 12:45/v046 By beginning with 'I' and presenting himself as the subject, the cupbearer surprises the king and lends force to his claim that he can resolve a dream that baffled the court. [IBNASHUR-FULL]
- 12:45/v047 The cupbearer figuratively says that he himself will inform them because he will cause the interpretation to reach them. [IBNASHUR-FULL]
- 12:45/v048 The cupbearer leaves Joseph unnamed so that news of a prisoner's skill will surprise the court and carry more force. [IBNASHUR-FULL,MAWARDI-FULL]
- 12:45/v049 The cupbearer cautiously offers to return with Joseph's interpretation so people will recognize Joseph's learning; his wording allows for the possibility of failure after the other interpreters failed. [RAZI-FULL]
