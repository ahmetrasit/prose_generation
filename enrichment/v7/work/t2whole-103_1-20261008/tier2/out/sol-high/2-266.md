## لَكُمُ (word 32) · interpretation

- 2:266/v106 Because لعل expresses hope, one explanation treats it as hope from a human perspective; a Muʿtazilite argument instead takes it as evidence that God intended all people to believe. (The interpretations differ over what divine use of a hope particle implies.) [IBNATIYYA-FULL,RAZI-FULL]
