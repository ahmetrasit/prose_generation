## فَذُوقُوا۟ (word 21) · inward
- 35:37/v080 In an ishārī reading, people sleep in worldly heedlessness and wake fully after death and resurrection, when they perceive the punishment they taste. [BURSEVI]

## فَمَا (word 22) · grammar
- 35:37/v081 Min naṣīr may be a subject governed by the preposition or a topic whose predicate is the preceding prepositional phrase. [SAMIN-DURR]

## فَمَا (word 22) · rhetoric
- 35:37/v082 The denial of help may continue the rebuke to the damned and end hope of rescue, or it may be an independent conclusion for listeners applying to every wrongdoer. [BIQAI-FULL,IBNASHUR-FULL]

## فَمَا (word 22) · interpretation
- 35:37/v083 No one can defend or rescue the wrongdoers from God's punishment, restraints, and chains; their former allies and idols cannot help. [ALUSI-FULL,BAGHAWI-FULL,BAYDAWI-FULL,BIQAI-FULL,BURSEVI,IBNJAWZI-ZAD,IBNKATHIR-FULL,ISLAHI-TADABBUR,MUQATIL,NASAFI-FULL,QURTUBI-FULL,RAZI-FULL,TAB-FULL,TABATABAI,TABRISI,YAHYA-SALLAM; مقاتل: WAHIDI-BASIT]
- 35:37/v084 Al-Rāzī's preferred broad reading excludes all help, from God or anyone else. [RAZI-FULL]
- 35:37/v085 An alternative says the wrongdoer's compounded ignorance, mistaking falsehood for truth, leaves him without beneficial knowledge in the afterlife. [RAZI-FULL]
- 35:37/v086 Their lack of helpers follows from turning away from God and the help He sent during worldly life. [STUDYQURAN]

## فَمَا (word 22) · theology
- 35:37/v087 One theological reading concludes that major sinners receive no intercession. [JISHUMI]

## فَمَا (word 22) · law
- 35:37/v088 Because aiding oppression violates justice, the community should restrain wrongdoing; a Prophetic report explains that helping an unjust person means stopping his injustice. [IBNASHUR-FULL; محمد صلى الله عليه وسلم: IBNASHUR-FULL]

## لِلظَّٰلِمِينَ (word 23) · meaning
- 35:37/v089 Wrongdoing violates another's due; its gravest form is shirk, which affronts God's oneness and harms the self by bringing punishment. [IBNASHUR-FULL]

## لِلظَّٰلِمِينَ (word 23) · referent
- 35:37/v090 The wrongdoers here are unbelievers and polytheists who wronged themselves through disbelief and idolatry. [ALUSI-FULL,BURSEVI,IBNASHUR-FULL,TAB-FULL,YAHYA-SALLAM]
