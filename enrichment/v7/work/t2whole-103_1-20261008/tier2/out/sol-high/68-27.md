# 68:27: 16 views from 33 rows (sol-high)

## whole verse · reports
- 68:27/v001 Asked whether the garden owners were people of Paradise or Hell, Qatada said the question burdened him and gave no answer. [قتادة: DURR-FULL]
- 68:27/v002 Reports elaborating the garden story say it belonged to a righteous man who left it to his people and town; they variously place it near Sana'a in Yemen, in Abyssinia, or among the People of Scripture. (These are reported alternatives, not a single agreed location.) [BINTSHATI]

## بَلْ (word 1) · rhetoric
- 68:27/v003 Bal shifts from their earlier thought to the more serious fact that, although they meant to deprive the poor of the surplus, they themselves lost all the fruit. [IBNASHUR-FULL]

## بَلْ (word 1) · interpretation
- 68:27/v004 Seeing the ruined garden, they first thought they had lost their way; once they recognized it, they corrected themselves and acknowledged their deprivation. (The initial mistake is tied in several notes to the garden's burned, unrecognizable appearance.) [ALUSI-FULL,BAGHAWI-FULL,ELMALILI,IBNATIYYA-FULL,ISLAHI-TADABBUR,RAZI-FULL,TAB-FULL; قيل: NAHHAS; ابن عباس: IBNKATHIR-FULL,WAHIDI-BASIT; قتادة: ABUHAYYAN-FULL]
- 68:27/v005 Their earlier claim of being lost can mean they had gone wrong in planning to harvest while excluding the poor; their correction admits that the expected benefit was lost and their scheme rebounded on them. (Razi says they had assumed they could still benefit; Tabatabai describes the error as straying from right conduct. This differs from taking their first claim as a mistaken route.) [ABUHAYYAN-FULL,RAZI-FULL,TABATABAI]

## نَحْنُ (word 2) · rhetoric
- 68:27/v006 The explicit pronoun is brought forward although the passive construction could leave its subject implicit, restricting true deprivation to them. [IBNASHUR-FULL]

## نَحْنُ (word 2) · interpretation
- 68:27/v007 Recognizing the garden's landmarks, they admitted deprivation; Muqatil identifies what they saw as punishment, while Ibn Jurayj says they were also afflicted by misfortune. [MUQATIL; ابن جريج: DURR-FULL]

## نَحْنُ (word 2) · links
- 68:27/v008 The declaration recalls 56:67 and extends the lesson to harvests generally: God determines whether crops flourish. (The note also mentions 56:63–66 in this connection.) [STUDYQURAN]

## مَحْرُومُونَ (word 3) · meaning
- 68:27/v009 The word may also mean that they were rendered destitute. [STUDYQURAN]

## مَحْرُومُونَ (word 3) · interpretation
- 68:27/v010 They were deprived of the garden's fruit, yield, or benefit after its destruction. (Ibn Atiyya also names the garden's blessing; Abu Hayyan calls its destroyed produce God's bounty.) [IBNASHUR-FULL,IBNATIYYA-FULL,IBNJAWZI-ZAD,ISLAHI-TADABBUR,JISHUMI,TAB-FULL,TABATABAI,ZAJJAJ; قيل: NAHHAS; المفسرون: WAHIDI-BASIT; قتادة: ABUHAYYAN-FULL; ابن عباس: IBNKATHIR-FULL]
- 68:27/v011 Their deprivation of the garden was a punishment or consequence of their own wrongdoing. (Qatada describes the loss as repayment; Abu Hayyan's report explicitly attributes the destruction to God's punishment.) [ALUSI-FULL,BAYDAWI-FULL,BIQAI-FULL,ELMALILI,MUQATIL,NASAFI-FULL,QURTUBI-FULL,RAZI-FULL; قتادة: ABUHAYYAN-FULL,MAWARDI-FULL,TAB-FULL]
- 68:27/v012 Their attempt or intention to withhold the garden's produce from the poor brought deprivation upon themselves. (Some notes describe barring the poor as done; Razi, Biqai, and Ibn Ashur stress the resolve or intention to do so. Abu Hayyan calls its reversal the return of their treachery upon them.) [ABUHAYYAN-FULL,BAGHAWI-FULL,BIQAI-FULL,IBNASHUR-FULL,IBNJAWZI-ZAD,JISHUMI,RAZI-FULL,ZAJJAJ; المفسرون: WAHIDI-BASIT]
- 68:27/v013 Their failure to make an exception contributed to their deprivation of the garden's benefit. (Both notes also connect the loss with denying the poor their due.) [BAGHAWI-FULL,JISHUMI]
- 68:27/v014 They recognized a punishment and deprivation greater than what they had planned for the poor, and despaired of both their present and future prospects. [ELMALILI]
- 68:27/v015 A calamity can soften and lead to repentance for a person God intends to guide, while increasing another person's disbelief. [BIQAI-FULL]

## مَحْرُومُونَ (word 3) · links
- 68:27/v016 God taking the expected good in response to their changed intention recalls that God does not change a people's condition until they change themselves (13:11). [BIQAI-FULL]
