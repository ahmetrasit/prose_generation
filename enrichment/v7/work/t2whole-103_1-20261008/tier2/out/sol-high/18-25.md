## وَٱزْدَادُوا۟ (word 7) · interpretation
- 18:25/v066 Nine added years reconcile three hundred solar years with roughly three hundred and nine lunar years used by Arabs, about three years' difference per solar century. (Some attribute the calculation to ʿAlī; al-Rāzī and al-Ālūsī question its arithmetic, while others allow approximation.) [BIQAI-FULL,BURSEVI,ELMALILI,IBNASHUR-FULL,IBNKATHIR-FULL,MAWARDI-FULL,RAZI-FULL,TABATABAI; النقاش: ABUHAYYAN-FULL,IBNATIYYA-FULL,QURTUBI-FULL; بعضهم: ALUSI-FULL; الخفاجي: ALUSI-FULL; السهيلي: IBNASHUR-FULL; bir tefsir: KURANYOLU-TEFSIR; some commentators: STUDYQURAN]
- 18:25/v067 The calendar conversion is approximate: one calculation yields 309 lunar years plus two months and nine days for 300 solar years; another puts the residual below three months.
