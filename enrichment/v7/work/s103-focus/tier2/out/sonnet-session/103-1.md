## lexicon and root
- 103:1/v059 Root ʿ-ṣ-r means squeezing cloth or grapes to extract juice; the term came to mean time (span in which affairs are pressed and turned) or late afternoon. (Study Quran: evokes time declining or fading quickly.) [BINTSHATI,BINTSHATI-IJAZ,JISHUMI,STUDYQURAN,TABRISI]
- 103:1/v060 Related words: the afternoon prayer is named for being deferred from the start of its time; ʿaṣāra is pressed grape juice; muʿṣirāt rain-bearing clouds; iʿṣār a column of dust twisting skyward or a strong wind driving clouds; asaru for a people bringing rain. [BINTSHATI,JISHUMI]
- 103:1/v061 Age is called asr because experience and hardship press out a person's essence; late afternoon is asr as the day is brought to a close and the prayer is named from it. Quranic uses: Yusuf 12:36, 12:49, al-Naba 78:14, a whirlwind in 2:266, and the sura itself. (Bint al-Shāṭiʾ in Ijaz: dahr is named for extracting man's essence through trial; she rejects Raghib's derivation of time from the residue of what is pressed (BINTSHATI-IJAZ:p416/r2).) [BINTSHATI,BINTSHATI-IJAZ]
- 103:1/v062 Bint al-Shāṭiʾ rejects Raghib's derivation of time from the residue of what is pressed. [الراغب: BINTSHATI-IJAZ-]
- 103:1/v063 As noun asr can mean the whole age, day and night, parts of the day, afternoon prayer or time, a group, rain; as verb restraining, taxing, squeezing juice. Variants asr, isr, usr; the dual asran day/night or morning/evening. With the generic article it may include every sense; with the covenant article, one particular time. Turkish usage: time generally, present era, eighty or a hundred years. [ELMALILI]
- 103:1/v064 Dehr, in Raghib's definition, is the interval from the universe's beginning to its end; zaman can name the whole or any part; dehr also means long duration, remaining lifetime, lifespan. [ELMALILI]
- 103:1/v065 Bint al-Shāṭiʾ (Ijaz): in this verse ʿaṣr means time (zaman, dahr); a single-form Qurʾānic usage. [BINTSHATI-IJAZ]

## grammar of the oath
- 103:1/v066 The wāw in al-ʿaṣr is an oath particle (as in wa-ḍ-ḍuḥā); the oath's answer is that humanity is surely in loss. (Itqan: fifth kind of oath-and-consequent structure.) [ELMALILI,JISHUMI; unnamed: ITQAN]
- 103:1/v067 The waw retains its force of magnifying what is sworn by; Tabari did not construct elaborate arguments for asr's greatness. The waw moves from perceptible reality to moral truth. [BINTSHATI]
- 103:1/v068 Bint al-Shāṭiʾ (Ijaz): the waw marks the opening as assertion/testimony to what was denied; it places the human under the pressure of time that squeezes and tests him, extracting his essence and revealing loss or salvation. Exegetes mostly read it as glorifying the sworn-by, which she rejects, noting they ignore qualifiers of the sworn-by object; she asks whether waw adds rhetoric over aqsama. (Turns on whether the oath only magnifies or also asserts/tests.) [BINTSHATI-IJAZ,BINTSHATI-IJAZ-; المفسرون: BINTSHATI-IJAZ]
- 103:1/v069 All surahs opening with the oath-waw, including al-ʿAsr, are Meccan. [BINTSHATI-IJAZ]
- 103:1/v070 Ibn Abi al-Asbaʿ: surahs open in ten kinds of speech; fifteen open with oaths, al-ʿAsr among them (also in Zarkashi); Zarkashi's list. [ابن أبي الأصبع: ITQAN; BURHAN]
- 103:1/v071 Qurtubi: generic definite al-nafs parallels generic human statements qualified by exceptions; verse forbids killing any protected life except for a valid cause. [QURTUBI-FULL]
- 103:1/v072 Ibn Khalawayh: no surah of three verses with ten waws exists except al-ʿAṣr. [ابن خالويه: ITQAN]

## occasion, place and order of revelation
- 103:1/v079 The surah is Makkan (Ibn ʿAbbās, Ibn al-Zubayr, the majority). (Mujāhid, Qatada and Muqātil, and one narration of Ibn ʿAbbās/Qatada, say Madinan; Tabatabai says it could be either but Meccan is likelier; Alusi: three verses undisputed.) [IBNKATHIR-FULL,ITQAN,MAWARDI-FULL,QUTB-ZILAL,STUDYQURAN,TABATABAI; ابن عباس وابن الزبير والجمهور: ABUHAYYAN-FULL,ALUSI-FULL; ابن عباس: DURR-FULL]
- 103:1/v080 Order of revelation: after Ash-Sharh/94 and before al-ʿĀdiyāt (al-Duha, Alam Nashrah, al-Asr, al-Adiyat). (Bayhaqi: first-revelation hadith has witnesses in Muqatil's tafsir and a sound mursal.) [ASAD-NOTES,STUDYQURAN; عكرمة والحسن بن أبي الحسن: ITQAN; ابن عباس: ITQAN; البيهقي: ITQAN; جابر بن زيد: ITQAN]
- 103:1/v081 Ibn Masʿūd mushaf order places al-Asr in the mufassal. [ابن مسعود: ITQAN]
- 103:1/v082 Ubayy says he recited the surah to the Prophet and asked for its interpretation. [أبي بن كعب: THALABI]
- 103:1/v083 Before Islam, ʿAmr b. al-ʿĀṣ recited al-ʿAṣr to Musaylima, who answered with a parody; al-Wabr is a small cat-like animal; al-Kharāʾiṭī records the story too. [عمرو بن العاص: IBNKATHIR-FULL; IBNKATHIR-FULL]

## virtue and sufficiency of the surah
- 103:1/v086 Al-Shāfiʿī: if people pondered the surah, or if no other revelation came, it would suffice them; another view says it contains all Qur'anic sciences. [الشافعي: ALUSI-FULL,BIQAI-FULL,IBNKATHIR-FULL; İmam Şâfiî: KURANYOLU-TEFSIR; al-Shāfiʿī: STUDYQURAN; KURANYOLU-TEFSIR,STUDYQURAN]
- 103:1/v087 Report: reciting Sūrat al-ʿAṣr brings patience and companionship with the people of truth on the Resurrection Day. [أبي: JISHUMI]
- 103:1/v088 Prophet identifies the person in loss with Abu Jahl and believers, righteous deeds, truth and patience with Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman and Ali; ʿAlī b. ʿAbd Allāh b. ʿAbbās repeated it from the pulpit. (Ibn al-Jawzī in contrast identifies the insan as Abu Lahab (IBNJAWZI-NUZHA:v1p181/r10).) [النبي: THALABI; علي بن عبد الله بن عباس: THALABI]
- 103:1/v089 Ibn al-Jawzī: Abu Lahab is the insan of 103:2. [IBNJAWZI-NUZHA]

## coherence with neighbouring verses
- 103:1/v090 Follows al-Takāthur: warns against wasting life on rivalry and wealth; Jishumi, Abu Hayyan, Alusi, Biqāʿī, Islahi all link it. (Abu Hayyan: distinguishes believer from unbeliever.) [ABUHAYYAN-FULL,ALUSI-FULL,BIQAI-FULL,ISLAHI-TADABBUR,JISHUMI]
- 103:1/v091 Biqāʿī: the surah distinguishes the saved human essence from the doomed and teaches salvation by leaving the transient for the lasting; the name signals extraction; begins with examples of ruined and saved because preventing harm precedes securing benefit; Ghazali's six minerals; clauses map to knowledge, worship, patience; basmala says all perish except God. [BIQAI-FULL; الغزالي: BIQAI-FULL]
- 103:1/v092 The surah condenses the Qur'an's teachings; Qutb: it compresses Islam's way of life and the community's identity into a constitution; one successful path across ages: faith, righteous action, mutual exhortation to truth and patience; Akif's poem; Samarrai: the added exhortation prevents further loss of reward. [CORPUSCORANICUM,QUTB-ZILAL,SAMARRAI-ASRAR,TABATABAI; Mehmet Âkif Ersoy: KURANYOLU-TEFSIR]
- 103:1/v093 Muhammad ibn Kaʿb: all people are in loss; belief alone is not enough, the verse requires good works, counsel in truth and counsel in patience. [محمد بن كعب القرظي: IBNABIHATIM]
- 103:1/v094 Humanity is in loss when people neglect God's rights, follow desires, and do not fear Him. [بعضهم: SULAMI]
- 103:1/v095 Loss (khusrān) is diminution, loss of part of the capital; Bursevi: grave because the servant sins against the Lord and immense benefaction; indefinite form may mark a distinctive kind of loss. [BURSEVI,ELMALILI,IBNJAWZI-NUZHA]
- 103:1/v096 Corpus Coranicum: no hymn-like resonance; Neuwirth argues oaths by prayer times preserve the recipient's liturgical experience and give the sura a hymn-like tone, treats the oath as an independent verse; pre-Islamic seer expression; Spitaler's Medina II joins verse 1 to 2. (Turns on whether the sura has a liturgical hymn tone.) [Neuwirth: CORPUSCORANICUM,CORPUSCORANICUM+; CORPUSCORANICUM,CORPUSCORANICUM-; Spitaler: CORPUSCORANICUM]

## verse counting
- 103:1/v097 Al-ʿAsr counted as a verse by analogy with al-Fajr and al-Duha for rhyme; a single word can be a verse; verse division is tawqifi (Zamakhshari) or relies on early practice (Ibn al-ʿArabī); some say revelation alone. (Last Medinan counter counts Watawasaw bil-haqq (103:3) as a verse and not wal-asr; other counters the reverse.) [BURHAN; الزمخشري: BURHAN; القاضي أبو بكر بن العربي: BURHAN; غيره: ITQAN; بعضهم: ITQAN+; الموصلي: ITQAN; المدني الأخير: ITQAN]

## abrogation
- 103:1/v098 One classification places al-Asr among surahs with both abrogating and abrogated verses; Suyuti doubts it; Ibn al-ʿArabī on rare abrogation of ruling without recitation. [BURHAN,ITQAN-; بعضهم: ITQAN; القاضي أبو بكر بن العربي: ITQAN]

## law
- 103:1/v099 Malik: swearing not to speak for an asr means a year. Shafiʿi: an hour unless another duration intended. Ibn al-ʿArabī prefers Shafiʿi's hour, but for an Arab the plausibly intended meaning is accepted except the shortest. (Differ on how large an asr is.) [مالك: QURTUBI-FULL; الشافعي: QURTUBI-FULL; ابن العربي: QURTUBI-FULL]

## inward (ishārī) readings
- 103:1/v100 Qushayri: whoever hears God's name does not withhold wealth, knows it does not prefer himself, accompanies it does not withhold his spirit. [QUSHAYRI]
- 103:1/v101 Qashani: affliction falls first on prophets, then saints, then the best in descending order; affliction is God's whip driving servants toward Him. [QASHANI]
