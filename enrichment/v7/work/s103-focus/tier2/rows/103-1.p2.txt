## SAMIN-DURR: al-Samīn al-Ḥalabī, d. 756 AH
[SAMIN-DURR:103:1/r1] author | reports | The common reading has ṣād still; Salam ibn Sulaymān reads ‘al-‘aṣri’ and ‘al-ṣabri’ with kasra. Ibn ‘Atiyya allows this only at pause through vowel transfer, while an Abu ‘Amr report uses an imala-like kasra on the bā’. 
[SAMIN-DURR:103:1/r2] الهذلي | reports | Al-Hudhali reports the kasra reading of ‘al-‘aṣr’, ‘al-ṣabr’, ‘al-fajr’, and ‘al-watr’ from Harun and Ibn Musa on Abu ‘Amr in both pause and connected recitation.
[SAMIN-DURR:103:1/r3] ابن خالويه | reports | Ibn Khalawayh also describes Abu ‘Amr’s ‘al-ṣabri’ as vowel transfer without limiting the report to pause.
[SAMIN-DURR:103:1/r4] أبو الفضل | reports | Abu al-Fadl describes transferring the final rā’s vowel to bā’ before a pause as widespread Arabic, marking inflection, avoiding two adjacent vowelless letters, and preserving pause-sukūn.
[SAMIN-DURR:103:1/r5] الشاعر | reports | The anonymous poetic examples ‘wa-ṣṭifāqan bi-r-rajul’ and ‘ana Jarir … wa-Sa‘d fi al-qaṣr’ witness transferring a vowel before a consonant cluster.
