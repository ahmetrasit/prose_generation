## وَلْيَتَلَطَّفْ (word 34) · law
- 18:19/v226 A captive or powerless believer may outwardly comply under coercion while keeping inward faith. [TABATABAI]
- 18:19/v227 A person able to leave who voluntarily enters a hostile society and loses ability to profess faith has wronged himself. [TABATABAI]

## وَلْيَتَلَطَّفْ (word 34) · inward
- 18:19/v228 Treat people of spiritual knowledge gently and spiritual novices firmly. [أبو عثمان المعزي: SULAMI]
- 18:19/v229 Choose a verified, pure hearted spiritual guide rather than a pretender; companionship yields insight. [QASHANI]
- 18:19/v230 The envoy is thought, the city the gathered faculties, and pure food intellect rather than illusion, imagination, or sensation; discretion hides faith from the ignorant. [QASHANI]
