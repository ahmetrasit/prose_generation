# Notes

## 7:156
[ALUSI-FULL:v9p78/r2] verses 7:156 | Because mercy encompasses everything, Moses' people surely remain within it; even the punished receive some mercy, since God does not punish them as severely as He could. «ورحمتي وسعت كل شيء ) إنسانا كان أو غيره مطيعا كان أو غيره»
[ALUSI-FULL:v9p78/r3] verses 7:156 | God will write the fullest share of mercy for their later descendants at the end of time, who meet the stated qualities. «بل إني سأرحمهم وأذهب عنهم ماأهمهم وأكتب الحظ الأوفر من رحمتي»
[ALUSI-FULL:v9p78/r4] verses 7:156 | The qualities are named to spur Moses' people to adopt or maintain them; the answer does not explicitly say their request is granted because that is more eloquent. «ووصف أخلاقهم بما وصفوا به لإستنهاض همهم إلى الإتصاف بما يمكن إتصافهم به منه أو إلى الثبات عليه ولم يصرح في الجواب بحصول السؤال»
[ALUSI-FULL:v9p78/r5] verses 7:156 | Alusi acknowledges flaws in this explanation but considers it better than many exegetical accounts and says transmitted reports are the best guide; if a hadith is sound, that is his position. «إلا أنه أولى من كثير مما وقفنا عليه من كلام المفسرين»
[ALUSI-FULL:v9p78/r6] verses 7:156 | The sīn in “I will write it” may signal emphasis or future time. «والسين في ( سأكتبها ) يحتمل أن تكون للتأكيد ويحتمل أن تكون للاستقبال»
[ASAD-NOTES:7:154#n123/r1] verses 7:156 | Asad links the promise that God's grace overspreads everything to 6:12 and 6:54 and the corresponding note 10. «Cf. 6:12 (and the corresponding note 10), as well as 6:54.»
[BAGHAWI-FULL:v3p287#2/r1] verses 7:156 | Moses asks God to guarantee them a blessing in this world, meaning well-being and safety. «واكتب لنا} أوجب لنا {في هذه الدنيا حسنة} النعمة والعافية»
[BAGHAWI-FULL:v3p287#2/r2] verses 7:156 | The requested good in the hereafter means forgiveness and Paradise. «وفي الآخرة {حسنة} أي المغفرة والجنة»
[BAGHAWI-FULL:v3p287#2/r3] verses 7:156 | Hudnā ilayka means ‘we have repented to You.’ «إنا هدنا إليك} أي: تبنا إليك»
[BAGHAWI-FULL:v3p287#2/r4] verses 7:156 | God says He sends punishment on whom He wills and that His mercy encompasses everything. «عذابي أصيب به من أشاء} من خلقي، {ورحمتي وسعت كل شيء} عمت كل شيء»
[BAGHAWI-FULL:v3p287#2/r5] verses 7:156 | They say mercy reaches righteous and wicked people in this world, but on Resurrection Day it is reserved for the God-fearing. «قال الحسن وقتادة: 138/ب وسعت رحمته في الدنيا البر والفاجر، وهي يوم القيامة للمتقين خاصة»
[BAGHAWI-FULL:v3p287#2/r6] verses 7:156 | Although mercy encompasses everything, it is only assured for the God-fearing; unbelievers receive provision and protection through believers, but in the hereafter mercy is exclusively for believers, like using another’s lamp until its owner takes it away. «وقال عطية العوفي: وسعت كل شيء ولكن لا تجب إلا للذين يتقون»
[BAGHAWI-FULL:v3p287#2/r7] verses 7:156 | They report that Iblīs claimed inclusion when ‘My mercy encompasses everything’ was revealed; God restricted its inscription to the God-fearing, zakat-givers, and believers, disappointing Jews and Christians who claimed those qualities. «قال ابن عباس -رضي الله عنهما -وقتادة، وابن جريج: لما نزلت: "ورحمتي وسعت كل شيء" قال إبليس: أنا من ذلك الشيء»
[BAYDAWI-FULL:7:156/r1] verses 7:156 | Good in this world means a good livelihood and success in obedience; in the afterlife it means Paradise. «{ واكتب لنا فى هذه الدنيا حسنة } حسن معيشة وتوفيق طاعة. { وفي الاخرة } الجنة.»
[BAYDAWI-FULL:7:156/r2] verses 7:156 | Hudnā means we repented and returned; the kasra reading comes from a verb meaning to incline, may be active or passive, and describes inclining ourselves or toward God; the ḍamma form may also be passive in a dialect. «تبنا إليك من هاد يهود إذا رجع. وقرىء بالكسر من هاده يهيده إذا أماله، ويحتمل أن يكون مبنيا للفاعل وللمفعول بمعنى أملنا أنفسنا وأملنا إليك، ويجوز أن يكون المضموم أيضا مبنيا للمفعول منه على لغة من يقول عود المريض.»
[BAYDAWI-FULL:7:156/r3] verses 7:156 | “My punishment strikes whom I will” means the one I choose to punish. «{ قال عذابي أصيب به من أشاء } تعذيبه.»
[BAYDAWI-FULL:7:156/r4] verses 7:156 | God's mercy encompasses every kind of thing in this world, believers and unbelievers, accountable people and others. «في الدنيا المؤمن والكافر بل المكلف وغيره»
[BAYDAWI-FULL:7:156/r5] verses 7:156 | “I shall write it” means establish mercy in the afterlife, or grant it a special written allotment for the Israelites. «{ فسأكتبها } فسأثبتها في الآخرة، أو فسأكتبها كتبة خاصة منكم يا بني إسرائيل.»
[BAYDAWI-FULL:7:156/r6] verses 7:156 | Those who fear disbelief and sins qualify for this mercy. «الكفر والمعاصي»
[BAYDAWI-FULL:7:156/r7] verses 7:156 | Zakat is singled out for its importance and because it was especially difficult for them. «خصها بالذكر لإنافتها ولأنها كانت أشق عليهم»
[BAYDAWI-FULL:7:156/r8] verses 7:156 | Believers in God's signs do not reject any of them. «فلا يكفرون بشيء منها»
[BIQAI-FULL:v3p122/r4] verses 7:156 | They ask for a good, contented and wholesome life in this present, lowly world and a similar good in the afterlife. «في هذه الدنيا } أي الحاضرة والدنية { حسنة } أي عيشة راضية طيبة { وفي } الحياة { الآخرة } أي كذلك»
[BIQAI-FULL:v3p122/r5] verses 7:156 | Their repentance explains the request; beginning with God's lordship and then their servanthood gives two powerful causes of happiness and teaches repentance, submission, and avoidance of their former error. «فبدأ بذكر عزة الربوبية وثنى بذلة العبودية وهما أقوى اسباب السعادة»
[BIQAI-FULL:v3p122/r6] verses 7:156 | Because the sin was publicly asking to see God, the wording of repentance alludes to returning to truth, becoming sound and gentle, and restraining speech; the name Judah is linked to this as an exhortation to repent. «تنبيها لهم على أن اسمهم يدل على التوبة والرجوع إلى الحق والصيرورة إلى الصلاح واللين والضعف في الصوت»
[BIQAI-FULL:v3p123/r1] verses 7:156 | God says His punishment may strike whomever He wills, whether that person sinned or did not sin. «أصيب به } أي في الدنيا والآخرة { من أشاء } أي اذنب أو لم يذنب»
[BIQAI-FULL:v3p123/r2] verses 7:156 | A report in the Ṣaḥīḥ says God's mercy preceded His anger, with a variant reading that it overcame His anger; the author allows either sense. «حديث أبي هريرة في الصحيح ( إن رحمتي سبقت ) وفي رواية : غلبت - ( غضبي )»
[BIQAI-FULL:v3p123/r3] verses 7:156 | Mercy has wider scope than wrath: everything subject to wrath also receives mercy through creation and provision, whereas many creatures receive mercy without wrath, including animals, inanimate things, believers, angels, and houris. «كل ما تعلق به الغضب تعلقت به الرحمة بإيجاد وإضافة الرزق عليه , ولا عكس كالحيونات العجم والجمادات»
[BIQAI-FULL:v3p123/r4] verses 7:156 | Although mercy encompasses everything, God promises its enduring share to the God-conscious, so the breadth of mercy does not oblige Him to give its continuance to everyone. «فسأكتبها } اي اخص بدوامها بوعد لا خلف فيه»
[BIQAI-FULL:v3p123/r5] verses 7:156 | Piety means avoiding what God forbids and doing what He commands; zakat is singled out because it was due at this appointed meeting and is a trust between people and God, like following the Prophet's described traits. «ولما ذكر نظرهم إلى الخالق بالانتهاء عما عنه والاتئمار بما أمر به , أتبعه النظر إلى الخلائق فقال : { ويؤتون الزكاة }»
[BIQAI-FULL:v3p123/r6] verses 7:156 | The final condition generalizes the fruit of piety: believers affirm all God's signs in heart and speech and confirm that faith by action, without accepting some signs and rejecting others. «والذين هم بآباتنا } اي كلها { يؤمنون»
[BURSEVI:v3p189#3/r7] verses 7:156 | Writing means to establish and appoint something for them, and the wording is used because written things endure longer. «{واكتب لنا} أي : أثبت وعين لنا وذكر الكتابة لأنها أدوم.»
[BURSEVI:v3p190#2/r1] verses 7:156 | Divine mercy reaches every kind of being in this world, believer or unbeliever, accountable or not; all live by its blessings, but in the hereafter mercy is exclusive to believers. «{ورحمتى} (ورحمت من وصفت أو آنست كه) {وسعت} في الدنيا معناه (رسيده است) {كل شىء} المؤمن والكافر»
[BURSEVI:v3p190#2/r2] verses 7:156 | Writing mercy means establishing and appointing it for the hereafter. «كما قال تعالى : {فسأكتبها} أي : أثبتها وأعينها في الآخرة.»
[BURSEVI:v3p190#2/r3] verses 7:156 | Those who fear are those who avoid unbelief and sins; zakat is singled out because it was especially difficult for them. «{للذين يتقون} الكفر والمعاصي. {ويؤتون الزكواة} خصها بالذكر لأنها كانت أشق عليهم»
[BURSEVI:v3p190#2/r4] verses 7:156 | Belief in God's signs is continuous and does not reject any of them. «يؤمنون} إيمانا مستمرا فلا يكفرون بشيء منها.»
