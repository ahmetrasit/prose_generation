[NASAFI-FULL:v3p22#3/r4] verses 18:74 | The boy is called pure either because Moses had not seen him sin or because he was too young to have reached the age of sin. «أما لأنها طاهرة عنده لأنه لم يرها قد اذنبت أو لأنها صغيرة لم تبلغ الحنث»
[NASAFI-FULL:v3p22#3/r5] verses 18:74 | By saying without a life in return, Moses means the boy had killed no one and was not liable to retaliation. «أي لم تقتل نفسا فيقتص منها»
[NASAFI-FULL:v3p22#3/r6] verses 18:74 | Ibn Abbas says Najda the Haruri wrote asking how the killing was permitted despite the Prophet’s prohibition on killing children; Ibn Abbas replied that only someone who knew the child’s condition as Moses’ knower did could do it. «وعن ابن عباس رضي الله تعالى عنهما ان نجدة الحروري كتب اليه كيف جاز قتله وقد نهى رسول الله»
[NASAFI-FULL:v3p22#3/r7] verses 18:74 | Madani and Abu Bakr read nukran with a ḍamma on the kāf wherever the word occurs; it means objectionable. «وبضم الكاف حيث كان مدني وابو بكر وهو المنكر»
[NASAFI-FULL:v3p22#3/r8] verses 18:74 | One comparison says nukr is less than imr because killing one person is less than drowning the boat’s people. «وقيل النكر أقل من الأمر لأن قتل نفس واحدة أهون من إغراق أهل السفينة»
[NASAFI-FULL:v3p22#3/r9] verses 18:74 | Another comparison says this was more reprehensible than the first act because a hole can be repaired, while a killing cannot be undone. «أو معناه جئت شيئا أنكر من الأول لأن الخرق يمكن تداركه بالسد ولا يمكن تدارك القتل»
