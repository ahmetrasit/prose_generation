# Notes

## 90:17
[NAHHAS:v5p232/r9] verses 90:17 | The repeated verb and preposition in urging patience and compassion add emphasis. «أعيد الفعل والباء توكيدا»
[NASAFI-FULL:v4p340#9/r13] verses 90:17 | Then being among believers means persisting in faith. «ومعنى ثم كان من الذين آمنوا أي دوام على الإيمان»
[NASAFI-FULL:v4p340#9/r14] verses 90:17 | An alternative takes then in the sense of and. «وقيل ثم بمعنى الواو وقيل إنما جاءبثم لتراخى الإيمان»
[NASAFI-FULL:v4p340#9/r15] verses 90:17 | Another explanation makes then signal faith's higher rank and virtue, not later timing, since faith precedes other deeds and no good deed stands without it. «وقيل إنما جاءبثم لتراخى الإيمان وتباعده فى الرتبة والفضيلة عن العتق والصدفة لا فى الوقت إذا الإيمان هو السابق على غيره ولا يثبت عمل صالح إلا به»
[NASAFI-FULL:v4p340#9/r16] verses 90:17 | Mutual exhortation to patience means avoiding sins, keeping obedient, and enduring the trials believers face. «عن المعاصى وعلى الطاعات والمحن التى يبتلى بها المؤمن»
[NASAFI-FULL:v4p340#9/r17] verses 90:17 | Mutual exhortation to compassion means showing mercy to one another. «﴿ وتواصوا بالمرحمة ﴾ بالتراحم فيما بينهم»
[QASHANI:90:8-14/r5] verses 90:14, 90:17 | The order begins with chastity and generosity, then the higher wisdom of faith, then patience which requires certainty, and ends with compassion as justice; each named quality stands for its broader virtue. «بدأ بالعفة التي هي أولى الفضائل وعبر عنها بمعظم أنواعها»
[QURTUBI-FULL:90:17-19/r1] verses 90:17 | Freeing a slave or feeding the hungry does not cross the steep path unless accompanied by faith; acceptance of acts requires belief in God. «قوله تعالى: { ثم كان من الذين آمنوا } يعني: أنه لا يقتحم العقبة من فك رقبة، أو أطعم في يوم ذا مسغبة، حتى يكون من الذين آمنوا»
[QURTUBI-FULL:90:17-19/r2] verses 90:17 | Faith added only after spending does not help; the Qur'an says disbelief kept hypocrites' spending from acceptance. «فالإيمان بالله بعد الإنفاق لا ينفع، بل يجب أن تكون الطاعة مصحوبة بالإيمان، قال الله تعالى»
[QURTUBI-FULL:90:17-19/r3] verses 90:17 | Aisha asks whether Ibn Judʿan's pre-Islamic charity, freeing captives, and hospitality benefit him; the Prophet says no, since he never asked forgiveness for the Day of Judgment. «وقالت عائشة: يا رسول الله، إن ابن جدعان كان في الجاهلية يصل الرحم، ويطعم الطعام، ويفك العاني»
[QURTUBI-FULL:90:17-19/r4] verses 90:17 | Alternatively, he did these deeds as a believer and remained faithful until death, like the phrasing in 20:82. «وقيل: «ثم كان من الذين آمنوا» أي فعل هذه الأشياء وهو مؤمن، ثم بقي على إيمانه حتى الوفاة»
[QURTUBI-FULL:90:17-19/r5] verses 90:17 | Other interpretations say he believed the deeds would benefit him with God, or performed them for God and then believed in Muhammad. «وقيل: أتى بهذه القرب لوجه الله، ثم آمن بمحمد صلى الله عليه وسلم.»
[QURTUBI-FULL:90:17-19/r6] verses 90:17 | After Islam, Hakim asked whether his pre-Islamic good deeds counted; the Prophet said he entered Islam with the good he had already done. «أسلمت على ما أسلفت من الخير»
[QURTUBI-FULL:90:17-19/r7] verses 90:17 | Some say thumma means wa: the person who freed or fed was among the believers. «وقيل: إن «ثم» بمعنى الواو أي وكان هذا المعتق الرقبة، والمطعم في المسغبة، من الذين آمنوا.»
[QURTUBI-FULL:90:17-19/r8] verses 90:17 | Tawāṣaw means they advised one another to obey God, avoid sins, and endure afflictions, and to show mercy to creation. «{ وتواصوا } أي أوصى بعضهم بعضا. { بالصبر } على طاعة الله، وعن معاصيه وعلى ما أصابهم»
[QURTUBI-FULL:90:17-19/r9] verses 90:17 | They explain the people of the right through right-hand records, being fortunate, coming from Adam's right side, or holding a right-side station; the author summarizes them as Paradise's people. «قلت: ويجمع هذه الأقوال أن يقال: إن أصحاب الميمنة أصحاب الجنة، وأصحاب المشأمة أصحاب النار»
[QUSHAYRI:v3p422/r16] verses 90:17 | The outcome depends on ending one's life among the faithful. «أي تكون خاتمته على ذلك»
[QUTB-ZILAL:90:17/r1] verses 90:17 | Here “then” marks spiritual rank and breadth, not chronological order: faith must precede emancipation and feeding, and gives righteous action weight by connecting it to a stable divine way rather than whim, praise, or advantage. «ليست للتراخي الزمني ، إنما هي للتراخي المعنوي»
[QUTB-ZILAL:90:17/r2] verses 90:17 | Patience is essential to faith and especially to confronting the obstacle; mutual exhortation exceeds individual patience by strengthening communal endurance and preventing discouragement, defeat, and panic. «والتواصي به يقرر درجة وراء درجة الصبر ذاته . درجة تماسك الجماعة المؤمنة»
[QUTB-ZILAL:90:17/r3] verses 90:17 | Mutual exhortation to compassion adds a shared and collective duty of mercy to mercy itself, with everyone recognizing and supporting that obligation. «إنه إشاعة الشعور بواجب التراحم في صفوف الجماعة عن طريق التواصي به»
[QUTB-ZILAL:90:17/r4] verses 90:17 | The passage stresses Islam as a communal religion and the Qur’an and Prophetic teachings repeatedly underscore that community, while retaining clear individual responsibility and judgment. «فهو دين جماعة ، ومنهج أمة ، مع وضوح التبعة الفردية والحساب الفردي فيه»
[RAZI-FULL:v31p169#3/r1] verses 90:17 | The one who passed the difficult ascent must also belong to the believers; without faith, none of these acts benefits its doer. «فإنه إن لم يكن منهم لم ينتفع بشيء من هذه الطاعات»
[RAZI-FULL:v31p169#3/r2] verses 90:17 | The delayed mention of faith may be rhetorical rather than chronological, as in a poem that mentions a father's and grandfather's status after the son's. «أن هذا التراخي في الذكر لا في الوجود»
[RAZI-FULL:v31p169#3/r3] verses 90:17 | Alternatively, faith must be the person's final state at death, since dying in faith is required for reward from earlier obedience. «أن يكون المراد ، ثم كان في عاقبة أمره من الذين آمنوا»
[RAZI-FULL:v31p169#3/r4] verses 90:17 | A third view allows reward for good deeds done before conversion if the person later accepts Muhammad; Hakim ibn Hizam asked about his pre-Islamic good works and the Prophet replied that he entered Islam with them. «أن حكيم بن حزام بعدما أسلم قال لرسول الله»
[RAZI-FULL:v31p169#3/r5] verses 90:17 | A fourth reading delays faith in rank, not time: its reward greatly exceeds freeing a slave or giving charity. «تراخي الإيمان وتباعده في الرتبة والفضيلة»
[RAZI-FULL:v31p169#3/r6] verses 90:17 | Mutual exhortation to patience means steadfastness in faith, obedience, avoiding sins and enduring trials. «الصبر على الإيمان والثبات عليه أو الصبر على المعاصي»
[RAZI-FULL:v31p169#3/r7] verses 90:17 | Mutual exhortation to mercy means helping the oppressed and poor and restraining a person approaching wrongdoing; it requires directing others toward truth and away from evil. «على أن يرحم المظلوم أو الفقير ، أو يرحم المقدم على منكر»
[RAZI-FULL:v31p169#3/r8] verses 90:17 | These believers are the leading Companions, including the four caliphs, distinguished by endurance in religion and compassion for people. «وهذه الطائفة هم أكابر الصحابة كالخلفاء الأربعة وغيرهم»
[RAZI-FULL:v31p169#3/r9] verses 90:17 | A learned authority sums up religious conduct as two foundations: truthfulness toward God and good character toward people. «إن الأصل في التوصف أمران : صدق مع الحق ؟ وخلق مع الخلق»
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT-HALAQAT:p385/r4] verses 90:13-14, 90:17 | Surah al-Fajr contrasts rich and poor, while al-Balad portrays expenditure and poverty; al-Fajr condemns neglect of orphans and the poor, and al-Balad commands feeding them. «وفي سورة البلد وصّانا تعالى بالرحمة بهذين الضعيفين»
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT-HALAQAT:p389/r3] verses 90:3-4, 90:17 | Birth itself entails hardship, linking the oath to humanity's creation in hardship; raising children also requires patience and compassion, matching the surah's ending and the Prophet's struggle. «الولادة هي المكابدة والشدّة والمشقة»
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT-HALAQAT:p403/r3] verses 90:13-15, 90:17 | Freeing and feeding are acts of compassion, and their vulnerable recipients especially need patience, linking the obstacle's tasks to the surah's counsel of mercy. «فك الرقاب وإطعام المحتاجين من المرحمة وهؤلاء من أحوج الناس إلى الصبر»
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT-HALAQAT:p403/r4] verses 90:17 | Then can indicate order of mention rather than chronology: faith has a higher rank and is foundational, since no deed benefits a person in the afterlife without faith. «تراخي رتبة الإيمان عما قبلها لأنه لا ينفع شيء بدون إيمان»
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT-HALAQAT:p403/r5] verses 90:17 | Unlike the common pairing “believed and did righteous deeds,” this verse omits the latter because it has already listed freeing and feeding as righteous works. «يأتي ذكر الذين آمنوا مقرونا بـ (عملوا الصالحات)»
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT-HALAQAT:p404/r1] verses 90:17 | Repeating the verb in “they counsel one another in patience and they counsel one another in compassion” is stronger than repeating only the preposition or using conjunction alone, because each virtue matters independently. «تكرار الفعل وهي أقوى التعبيرات لأن فيها توكيد لأهمية كل واحد من الصبر والمرحمة»
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT-HALAQAT:p404/r3] verses 90:17 | Marḥamah is broader and more encompassing than raḥmah, and the surah's many relationships call for compassion rather than harm. «المرحمة هي ليست الرحمة فقط ولكنها عامة وهي أهم من الرحمة»
