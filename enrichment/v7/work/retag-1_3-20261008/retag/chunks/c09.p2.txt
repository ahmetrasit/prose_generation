[IBNKATHIR:1:1/r116] verses 1:1 | When Muʿawiya omitted the basmala in Medina, the Muhajirun present objected; on his next prayer he recited it. «فترك البسملة ، فأنكر عليه من حضره من المهاجرين ذلك»
[IBNKATHIR:1:1/r117] verses 1:1 | Another group does not recite the basmala aloud; Ibn Kathir says this is established from the four caliphs, Ibn Mughaffal, and other early authorities. «وذهب آخرون إلى أنه لا يجهر بالبسملة في الصلاة ، وهذا هو الثابت عن الخلفاء الأربعة»
[IBNKATHIR:1:1/r118] verses 1:1 | Malik does not recite the basmala in prayer either aloud or quietly, relying on reports that the Prophet and first caliphs began with al-Hamd and did not mention it. «وعند الإمام مالك : أنه لا يقرأ البسملة بالكلية ، لا جهرًا ولا سرًّا»
[IBNKATHIR:1:1/r119] verses 1:1 | The legal disagreement is narrow: scholars agree a prayer is valid whether the basmala is recited aloud or quietly. «لأنهم أجمعوا على صحة صلاة من جهر بالبسملة ومن أسر»
[IBNKATHIR:1:1/r12] verses 1:1 | Yahya ibn Abi Kathir calls it al-Kafiya: it suffices in place of other surahs, while none can replace it; this is also stated in a mursal report. «وسماها يحيى بن أبي كثير : الكافية ؛ لأنها تكفي عما عداها ولا يكفي ما سواها عنها»
[IBNKATHIR:1:1/r120] verses 1:1 | A report says the basmala is one of God's names, extremely close to the Greatest Name, like the closeness between the black and white of the eye. «هو اسم من أسماء الله ، وما بينه وبين اسم الله الأكبر ، إلا كما بين سواد العينين وبياضهما»
[IBNKATHIR:1:1/r121] verses 1:1 | A report explains the letters as bāʾ for God's splendor, sīn for His glory, and mīm for His dominion; Rahman is merciful in both worlds and Rahim in the next. Ibn Kathir calls the report very strange and possibly Israelite rather than Prophetic. «الباء بهاء الله ، والسين سناؤه ، والميم مملكته»
[IBNKATHIR:1:1/r122] verses 1:1 | Al-Dahhak is said to have transmitted a similar explanation of the basmala. «وقد روى جُوَيبر{[824]} ، عن الضحَّاك ،   نحوه من قبله»
[IBNKATHIR:1:1/r123] verses 1:1 | A report from Burayda says the basmala was revealed to Muhammad and Solomon alone. «أنزلت علي آية لم تنزل على نبي غير سليمان بن داود وغيري ، وهي بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم»
[IBNKATHIR:1:1/r124] verses 1:1 | Jabir's report describes clouds fleeing, winds and sea becoming still, animals listening, devils pelted, and God's promise of blessing when His name is said over something. «عن جابر بن عبد الله ،   قال : لما نزل»
[IBNKATHIR:1:1/r125] verses 1:1 | Ibn Masʿud reportedly says reciting the basmala shields a person from each of the nineteen keepers of Hell; Ibn ʿAtiyya supports this with a report of more than thirty angels hastening to record praise. «من أراد أن ينجيه الله من الزبانية التسعة عشر فليقرأ : بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم»
[IBNKATHIR:1:1/r126] verses 1:1 | A report says not to curse Satan after a fall because that makes him swell with pride; saying “in God's name” makes him shrink like a fly (another version says like a house). «لا تقل تعس الشيطان . فإنك إذا قلت : تعس الشيطان تعاظم»
[IBNKATHIR:1:1/r127] verses 1:1 | Because the basmala humbles Satan, it is recommended at the beginning of actions and speech. «فهذا من تأثير بركة بسم الله ؛ ولهذا تستحب في أوّل كل عمل وقول»
[IBNKATHIR:1:1/r128] verses 1:1 | A report recommends beginning a sermon with the basmala and calls an affair begun without it defective. «لا يبدأ فيه ببسم الله الرحمن الرحيم ،   فهو أجذم»
[IBNKATHIR:1:1/r129] verses 1:1 | The basmala is recommended on entering the toilet, based on reports transmitted on that practice. «وتستحب البسملة عند دخول الخلاء ولما ورد من الحديث في ذلك»
[IBNKATHIR:1:1/r13] verses 1:1 | Al-Zamakhshari also records the names Surat al-Salah and al-Kanz. «ويقال لها : سورة الصلاة والكنز ذكرهما الزمخشري»
[IBNKATHIR:1:1/r130] verses 1:1 | A hadith judged hasan says there is no ablution for one who does not mention God's name; scholars differ over whether it is obligatory when remembered or absolutely. «من رواية أبي هريرة ،   وسعيد بن زيد ،   وأبي سعيد مرفوعًا»
[IBNKATHIR:1:1/r131] verses 1:1 | The basmala is recommended before slaughter according to al-Shafiʿi and a group; others require it if remembered, and some require it absolutely. «وكذا تستحب عند الذبيحة في مذهب الشافعي وجماعة ، وأوجبها آخرون عند الذكر»
[IBNKATHIR:1:1/r132] verses 1:1 | The Prophet instructed his stepson ʿUmar ibn Abi Salama to say God's name, eat with his right hand, and eat from what is nearest; some scholars make the basmala obligatory at meals. «قل : باسم الله ، وكل بيمينك ، وكل مما يليك»
[IBNKATHIR:1:1/r133] verses 1:1 | The Prophet taught spouses to say God's name and ask protection from Satan before intercourse; if a child is conceived, Satan will not harm it. «لو أن أحدكم إذا أتى أهله قال : باسم الله ، اللهم جنبنا الشيطان»
