[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p187/r4] verses 7:155 | Ibn Abbas, through Ali ibn Talha, says God appointed seventy to pray; they asked for a privilege unmatched before or after them, which God disliked, so the quake seized them. «وقال ابن عباس فيما روى عنه علي بن طلحة هو ميقات وقته الله لموسى»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p187/r5] verses 7:155 | A report from Ali transmitted by Ibn Abi Shayba says Harun died on a mountain couch; Israel accused Moses of killing him, but Harun denied it and said God had taken him, after which the selected seventy quaked. «وعن علي رضي الله عنه فيما روى ابن أبي شيبة أن موسى وهارون وابناه شبر وشبير»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p187/r6] verses 7:155 | In Ali's account, the phrase “Our appointment” fits because the group went at God's appointed time. «ولفظ لميقاتنا في هذا القول الذي روي عن علي لأنه يقتضي أنه كان عن توقيت»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p187/r7] verses 7:155 | Ibn al-Sa'ib says Moses never approached his Lord except by God's permission. «وقال ابن السائب: كان موسى لا يأتي ربه إلا بإذن منه»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p187/r8] verses 7:155 | Abu Hayyan holds this was a different appointment from the vision meeting in 7:143: the earlier account has Moses speak with God and swoon when the mountain crumbles, while here the seventy quake and Moses does not; the intervening discourse also separates the events. «والذي يظهر أن هذا الميقات غير ميقات موسى الذي قيل فيه: ولما جاء موسى لميقاتنا»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p188/r1] verses 7:155 | The cause of the quake is variously said to be their acquiescence in calf worship, their request to see God, excess in their supplication, or hearing the dead Harun speak. «سبب الرجفة مختلف فيه وهو مرتب على تفسير الميقات فهل الرجفة عقوبة على سكوتهم»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p188/r10] verses 7:155 | Ibn Atiyya takes “and me” to refer to the time Moses killed the Egyptian, when God concealed and forgave him; now his return without the group could damage Israel. «وقوله وإياي أي وقت قتلي القبطي فأنت قد سترت وغفرت حينئذ فكيف الآن»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p188/r11] verses 7:155 | وإياي is coordinated with the attached object pronoun, so it is separated; Moses mentions the group first because they were the ones struck, not himself. «وعطف وإياي على الضمير المنصوب في أهلكتهم وعطف الضمير مما يوجب فصله»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p188/r12] verses 7:155 | Moses includes himself despite not sharing the reason for destruction, submitting to God's will and power to destroy either the sinful or obedient if He chose. «وإن كان لم يشركهم في مقتضى الإهلاك تسليما منه لمشيئة الله تعالى وقدرته»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p188/r2] verses 7:155 | Al-Suddi says the seventy were punished because they themselves had worshipped the calf; Moses did not know this until God informed him. «وقال السدي: عقوبة على عبادة هؤلاء السبعين باختيارهم العجل وخفي ذلك عن موسى»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p188/r3] verses 7:155 | The quake may have caused death or fainting; both interpretations are reported. «وأخذ الرجفة يحتمل أن نشأ عنه الموت ويحتمل أن نشأ عنه الغشي وهما قولان»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p188/r4] verses 7:155 | Al-Suddi says Moses feared returning after the death of Israel's best men and losing their trust; God restored them to life. «قال موسى: كيف أرجع إلى بني إسرائيل وقد أهلكت خيارهم فماذا أقول»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p188/r5] verses 7:155 | Another report says they trembled until their joints nearly separated and their backs broke; Moses feared death, wept, and prayed, so the quake was lifted. «وقيل أخذتهم الرعدة حتى كادت تبين مفاصلهم وتنتقض ظهورهم وخاف موسى الموت»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p188/r6] verses 7:155 | Al-Zamakhshari reads Moses's words as wishing they had been destroyed before he saw the consequences of asking to see God; Abu Hayyan notes this depends on identifying this meeting with the vision meeting, which he rejects. «وهذا تمن منه للإهلاك قبل أن يرى ما رأى من تبعة طلب الرؤية كما يقال النادم»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p188/r7] verses 7:155 | Ibn Atiyya says Moses was distressed about Israel's future if he returned without the group and appealed that their destruction, and his, would have been easier before this harmful moment. «وقال ابن عطية لما رأى موسى ذلك أسف عليهم وعلم أن أمر بني إسرائيل يتشعب»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p188/r8] verses 7:155 | The object after لو شئت is omitted (“destroying us”); لو without an answer lām is eloquent, though an answer with lām is more common. «ومفعول شئت محذوف تقديره لو شئت إهلاكنا وجواب لو شئت أهلكتهم وأتى دون لام»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p188/r9] verses 7:155 | من قبل means before Moses selected them and the quake occurred, during their acquiescence in or worship of the calf. «والمحذوف في من قبل أي من قبل الاختيار وأخذ الرجفة وذلك زمان إغضائهم»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p189/r1] verses 7:155 | The question is an appeal framed as an argument in humility; “us” refers to Moses and the seventy, while “what the fools did” varies with explanations of the quake. «هذا استفهام على سبيل الإدلاء بالحجة في صيغة استعطاف وتذلل والضمير المنصوب»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p189/r10] verses 7:155 | Another explanation has Moses ask who led Israel to the calf; when God says He did, Moses responds that God led them astray. «وقيل لما أعلم موسى بعبادة بني إسرائيل العجل وبصفته قال: يا رب ومن أخاره»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p189/r11] verses 7:155 | Ibn Atiyya suggests “Your trial” may refer to the people's request to see God openly, which led to the quake. «قال ابن عطية: ويحتمل أن يشير به إلى قولهم أرنا الله جهرة إذ كانت فتنة»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p189/r12] verses 7:155 | Abu Hayyan says this verse refutes the Mu'tazilites. «وفي هذه الآية رد على المعتزلة»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p189/r13] verses 7:155 | Al-Zamakhshari explains the trial as God's speaking to Moses: the witnesses wrongly inferred that they could see God, so the test caused some to stray and others to remain guided. «وقال الزمخشري أي محنتك وبلاؤك حين كلمتني وسمعت كلامك فاستدلوا بالكلام»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p189/r14] verses 7:155 | Abu Hayyan says al-Zamakhshari's account follows the Mu'tazilite denial that God causes misguidance. «انتهى وهو على طريقة المعتزلة في نفيهم الإضلال عن الله تعالى»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p189/r2] verses 7:155 | Another view takes “us” as Moses and all Israel and the fools' deeds as their division, disbelief, and disobedience. «الضمير في أتهلكنا له ولبني إسرائيل وبما فعل السفهاء أي بالتفرق والكفر»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p189/r3] verses 7:155 | Al-Zamakhshari says Moses includes himself and the seventy because he requested the vision to restrain the foolish men who demanded it ignorantly. «وقال الزمخشري يعني نفسه وإياهم لأنه إنما طلب الرؤية زجرا للسفهاء»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p189/r4] verses 7:155 | Abu Hayyan reads it as a genuine inquiry: may the righteous selected men be destroyed for the deeds of others? [ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p189/r5] verses 7:155 | The Prophet answered that a community may be destroyed while righteous people are among it when wrongdoing becomes widespread. «وقوله عليه السلام، وقد قيل له: أنهلك وفينا الصالحون قال: «نعم إذا كثر الخبث»»

[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p190/r1] verses 7:155 | Moses says God is their patron and asks forgiveness and mercy for himself and his people; because his people sinned, he stresses forgiveness and calls God the best forgiver. «سأل الغفران له ولهم والرحمة لما كان قد اندرج قومه في قوله أنت ولينا»
