# Notes

## 31:14
[TABATABAI:31:14-15/r1] verses 31:14 | Mentioning the mother's hardship in carrying and raising the child should prompt gratitude to both parents, especially the mother. «ذكر بعض ما تحملته أمه من المحنة والأذى في حمله وتربيته ليكون داعيا له إلى شكرهما وخاصة الأم»
[TABATABAI:31:14-15/r2] verses 31:14 | Wahn means weakness and is either circumstantial or an emphatic cognate object; fiṣāl is weaning, and its completion by two years means nursing lasts two full years. «والوهن الضعف وهو حال بمعنى ذات وهن أو مفعول مطلق والتقدير تهن وهنا على وهن، والفصال الفطم وترك الإرضاع»
[TABATABAI:31:14-15/r3] verses 31:14 | Combining two years of nursing with the thirty-month pregnancy-and-weaning period in 46:15 leaves six months as the minimum gestation. «وإذا ضم إلى قوله تعالى وحمله وفصاله ثلاثون شهرا الأحقاف 15، بقي لأقل الحمل ستة أشهر»
[TABATABAI:31:14-15/r4] verses 31:14 | “Be grateful to Me and your parents” explains the earlier command to honor them, and the return to God warns and reinforces the duty; the shift in person matches verse 31:12. «تفسير لقوله { وصينا } الخ، في أول الآية أي كانت وصيتنا هو أمرنا بشكرهما كما أمرناه بشكر الله»
[TABRISI:31:11-14/r12] verses 31:14 | Ḥamalat-hu ummuhu is a circumstantial clause with an implied qad: the mother carries him while the person is in the state of being borne, implied by the command to honor parents. «جملة في موضع النصب على الحال بإضمار قد»
[TABRISI:31:11-14/r5] verses 31:14 | A non-canonical reading has wahn with fatḥa on the hāʾ. «وفي الشواذ قراءة عيسى الثقفي ورواية بعضهم عن أبي عمرو وهنا على وهن بفتح الهاء»
[TABRISI:31:11-14/r6] verses 31:14 | These readers pronounce faṣluhu fī ʿāmayn with a fatḥa on the verb's ṣād. «وقراءة الحسن بخلاف وأبي رجاء والجحدري وقتادة ويعقوب وفصله في عامين»
[TABRISI:31:11-14/r9] verses 31:14 | The fatḥa in wahn may reflect the influence of a guttural; faṣl is broader than separation from nursing, but fiṣāl better fits this context because it specifically concerns weaning. «وأما الفصل فإنه أعم من الفصال لأنه يستعمل في الرضاع وغيره والفصال ها هنا أوجه»
[TABRISI:31:14-15#2/r1] verses 31:14 | The mother nurses and raises her child for two years after birth, adding to her hardship; this explains the command to thank God and parents, honoring God through praise and obedience and parents through kindness and connection. «والمراد أنها بعد ما تلده ترضعه عامين وتربية فتلحقها المشقة بذلك أيضا»
[TABRISI:31:14-15#2/r2] verses 31:14 | To God is the return is a threat: He will recompense each person according to deeds. «وفيه تهديد أي إلي مرجعكم فأجازيكم على حسب أعمالكم»
[TABRISI:31:14-15/r1] verses 31:14 | Wahnan is a verbal noun used circumstantially for an omitted tahnu; ʿalā wahn may qualify it or attach to its governing verb, while maʿrūfan qualifies an omitted noun meaning decent companionship. «مصدر فعل محذوف في موضع الحال أي تهن وهنا»
[TABRISI:31:14/r10] verses 31:14 | Pregnancy burdens the mother, and her weakness increases as the pregnancy advances; another view says her naturally weak constitution is further weakened by pregnancy. «لأن الحمل يؤثر فيها فكلما ازداد الحمل ازدادت ضعفا على ضعف»
[TABRISI:31:14/r11] verses 31:14 | Wahn ʿalā wahn can mean hardship upon hardship and effort upon effort. «شدة على شدة وجهدا على جهد عن ابن عباس وقتادة»
[TABRISI:31:14/r12] verses 31:14 | Weaning occurs at the end of two full years, the total nursing period, as in the verse about two complete years for anyone wishing to complete nursing. «وفطامه من الرضاع في انقضاء عامين لأن العامين جملة مدة الرضاع»
[TABRISI:31:14/r8] verses 31:14 | After commanding gratitude, God enjoins honoring parents as benefactors; their gratitude is paired with God's because He creates while they are the means of birth and upbringing. «لما قدم الأمر بشكر النعمة أتبعه بالتنبيه على وجوب الشكر لكل منعم»
[TABRISI:31:14/r9] verses 31:14 | One explanation says the mother's weakness is compounded by the weakness of the father's and mother's seed. «ضعف نطفة الوالد على ضعف نطفة الأم عن أبي مسلم»
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v18p103#2/r1] verses 31:14 | The verse was revealed about Saʿd ibn Abī Waqqāṣ when he became Muslim; its story was given at the start of Sūrat al-ʿAnkabūt. «نزلت في سعد بن أبي # وقاص لما أسلم، وذكرنا القصة في أول سورة العنكبوت»
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v18p103#2/r2] verses 31:14 | Ibn ʿAbbās, al-Kalbī, and al-Suddī gloss wahn as weakness upon weakness. «قال ابن عباس والكلبي والسدي: ضعفا على ضعف»
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v18p103#2/r3] verses 31:14 | Abū Isḥāq explains that pregnancy weakened the mother repeatedly. «أبو إسحاق: أي لزمها لحملها إياه أن (¬3) تضعف مرة بعد مرة»
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v18p103#2/r4] verses 31:14 | Wahnan is an accusative verbal noun, with the pregnancy clause implying that she weakened because she carried the child; this material is not syntactically continuous with the preceding statement. «وانتصب هاهنا وهنا على المصدر، ودل قوله: {حملته أمه} على أن المعنى وهنت بحملها إياه وهنا على وهن. وهذا غير متصل بالكلام الأول»
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v18p103#2/r5] verses 31:14 | The author of al-Naẓm says the opening command about parents is left awaiting its complement; the pregnancy and two years of nursing detail the mother’s hardship and establish her claim, before the text resumes with the command to thank God and parents. «فإنما عدد الله -عز وجل- ذلك ليبين وجوب حق الوالدة بما لزمها من التعب والنصب في الولادة»
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v18p104/r1] verses 31:14 | Fiṣāl means weaning, separating a child from the mother so he no longer nurses; the implied construction is at the completion of two years, when the child is weaned. «الفصال: الفطام، وهو أن يفصل الولد عن الأم كي لا يرضع. والتقدير: وفصاله في انقضاء عامين»
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v18p104/r2] verses 31:14 | Al-Akhfash supplies the implied word completion in the phrase about weaning in two years. «والتقدير: وفصاله في انقضاء عامين. قاله # الأخفش (¬1).»
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v18p104/r3] verses 31:14 | The noun is the subject and the prepositional phrase its predicate; the passage highlights a mother’s two years of nursing hardship. «وفصاله} مبتدأ، وخبره في الظرف على تقدير: وفصاله يقع في انقضاء عامين»
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v18p104/r4] verses 31:14 | Ibn ʿAbbās interprets the command to thank God and parents as an instruction to obey them. «يريد أطعني وأطع والديك»
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v18p104/r5] verses 31:14 | Muqātil says one thanks God for guiding one to Islam and parents for the blessings they bestowed. «مقاتل: أن اشكر لي إذ (¬3) هديتك للإسلام، ولوالديك بما أولياك من النعم»
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v18p104/r6] verses 31:14 | The phrase explains the content of the command to the human being: God enjoined gratitude to Himself and to the parents. «قال صاحب النظم [وقال صاحب] (¬5) المعني: ووصينا الإنسان أن اشكر لي ولوالديك»
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v18p104/r7] verses 31:14 | Ibn ʿAbbās and Muqātil say the return is to God, who will recompense each person for his deeds. «إلى المرجع والمنقلب فأجزيك بعملك»
[WAHIDI-WAJIZ:31:14-16/r1] verses 31:14 | Pregnancy weakens a mother again and again, and weaning lasts two years because she nurses her child then; God commands gratitude to Him and the parents. «أن تضعف مرة بعد مرة»
[YAHYA-SALLAM:v2p674/r1] verses 31:14 | “We enjoined the human being concerning his parents” means enjoining kindness to them. «يعني: برا بوالديه»
[YAHYA-SALLAM:v2p674/r2] verses 31:14 | “Weakness upon weakness” describes the mother’s repeated weakness; Ibn Mujahid, Mujahid, and Qatada offer related glosses of the child’s burden, weakness, or effort. «ضعفا على ضعف، في تفسير الحسن.»
[YAHYA-SALLAM:v2p674/r3] verses 31:14 | “Separation” means weaning; a Prophetic report says there is no nursing after weaning, and Ibn ʿUmar and Ibn ʿAbbas did not count nursing after two years. «قال: {وفصاله} [لقمان: 14] ، أي: وفطامه.»
[YAHYA-SALLAM:v2p674/r4] verses 31:14 | “To Me is the destination” means the Resurrection; reports say God’s pleasure and anger correspond to a parent’s pleasure and anger and describe Paradise or Hell’s gates for one pleasing or angering parents, even if wronged. «[لقمان: 14] البعث.»
[YAHYA-SALLAM:v2p674/r5] verses 31:14 | A Prophetic report says good deeds have ever higher forms, culminating in giving one’s life for God, while worse sins culminate in disobeying one’s parents. «إن فوق كل بر برا»
[ZAJJAJ:v4p195#2/r4] verses 31:14 | Tafsir explains وهنا على وهن as weakness upon weakness: pregnancy repeatedly weakened the mother. «جاء في التفسير (وهنا على وهن) ضعفا على ضعف، أي لزمها
لحملها إياه أن ضعفت مرة بعد مرة.»
[ZAJJAJ:v4p195#2/r5] verses 31:14 | أن is accusative under وصينا; the meaning is that God enjoined the person to thank Him and his parents. «وموضع " أن " نصب ب (وصينا).
المعنى وصينا الإنسان أن اشكره لي ولوالديك، أي وصيناه بشكرنا
وبشكر والديه.»

## 33:43

[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v8p486/r1] verses 33:43 | Al-Ḥasan interprets God’s prayer for believers as mercy; Ibn Jubayr says forgiveness, Abū al-ʿĀliya says praise, and another view says compassion. «قال الحسن: يصلي عليكم: يرحمكم»
