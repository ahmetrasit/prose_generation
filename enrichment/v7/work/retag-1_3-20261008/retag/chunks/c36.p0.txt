# Notes

## 4:1
[JISHUMI:v2p1441/r9] verses 4:1 | Some read الأرحام nominative as a new subject after ending the previous clause; al-Akhfash says the genitive reading implies an omitted preposition. «وعن بعضهم والأرحام بالرفع على الابتداء، كأنه نوى تمام الكلام عند قوله: تساءلون به»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v1p491#2/r1] verses 4:1 | The many men and women are other human communities, too numerous to count. «غيركم من الأمم الفائتة للحصر»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v1p491#2/r2] verses 4:1 | The creation account warrants piety because it displays immense power: one able to create this can punish sinners, so people should fear His punishment. «لأن ذلك مما يدل على القدرة العظيمة ومن قدر على نحوه كان قادرا على كل شيء ومن المقدورات عقاب العصاة»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v1p491#2/r3] verses 4:1 | Creation also demonstrates abundant blessing, so people owe God piety rather than ingratitude and neglect of their duty to thank Him. «ولأنه يدل على النعمة السابغة عليهم فحقهم أن يتقوه في كفرانها والتفريط فيما يلزمهم من القيام بشكرها»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v1p491#2/r4] verses 4:1 | Alternatively, piety here means preserving mutual rights and not cutting obligatory ties, since God joined people as branches of one root; this fits the sura's themes. «أو أراد بالتقوى تقوى خاصة وهي أن يتقوه فيما يتصل بحفظ الحقوق بينهم فلا يقطعوا ما يجب عليهم وصله»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v1p491#2/r5] verses 4:1 | A reading uses the active participles خالق and باث, as predicates of an omitted subject, هو. «وهذا المعنى مطابق لمعاني السورة وقرىء ( وخالق منها زوجها وباث منهما ) بلفظ اسم الفاعل وهو خبر مبتدأ محذوف تقديره وهو خالق»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v1p491/r1] verses 4:1 | People means the children of Adam; humanity descends from one root, Adam, their father. «﴿ يا أيها الناس ﴾ يابني آدم»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v1p491/r2] verses 4:1 | One parse coordinates created its spouse with an omitted verb: God began Adam's creation from clay and created Eve from one of his ribs. «فيه وجهان أحدهما أن يعطف على محذوف كأنه قيل من نفس واحدة أنشأها أو ابتدأها وخلق منها زوجها»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v1p491/r3] verses 4:1 | On that parse, God made humanity branch from Adam, created Eve from clay from one of his ribs, and dispersed humankind from the pair. «والمعنى شعبكم من نفس واحدة هذه صفتها وهي أنه أنشأها من تراب وخلق زوجها حواء من ضلع من أضلاعها»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v1p491/r4] verses 4:1 | An alternative parse coordinates created her spouse with created you, addressing the people to whom the Prophet was sent: they descend from Adam, their mother Eve was made from him, and humanity spread from both. «والثاني أن يعطف على خلقكم ويكون الخطاب فى ﴿ يا أيها الناس ﴾ للذين بعث اليهم رسول الله»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v1p492/r1] verses 4:1 | تساءلون derives from تتساءلون, with the tāʾ assimilated into the sīn; another reading drops the second tāʾ. «تتساءلون به فأدغمت التاء في السين وقرىء ( تساءلون ) بطرح التاء الثانية»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v1p492/r10] verses 4:1 | Al-Hasan says to grant what is requested when someone appeals to God or to family ties. «وعن الحسن إذا سألك بالله فأعطه وإذا سألك بالرحم فأعطه»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v1p492/r11] verses 4:1 | Ibn ʿAbbas reports that kinship hangs from the Throne and greets and speaks to the one who maintains it. «ما روي عن ابن عباس رضي الله عنه الرحم معلقة بالعرش فإذا أتاها الواصل بشت به وكلمته»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v1p492/r2] verses 4:1 | The expression can mean asking one another by God and kinship as an appeal, or invoking them to ask another person; the tafaʿal form can serve for plural tafʿal. «أي يسأل بعضكم بعضا بالله وبالرحم فيقول بالله وبالرحم أفعل كذا على سبيل الاستعطاف وأناشدك الله والرحم»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v1p492/r3] verses 4:1 | Ibn Masʿud's reading تسلون supports the interpretation that people ask others by God and kinship. «وتنصره قراءة من قرأ ( تسلون به ) مهموز أو غير مهموز»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v1p492/r4] verses 4:1 | The accusative reading of والأرحام may coordinate either with the command to fear God or with the grammatical object of the prepositional phrase; Ibn Masʿud's reading explicitly repeats the preposition. «وقرىء ( والأرحام ) بالحركات الثلاث فالنصب على وجهين إما على واتقوا الله والأرحام أو ان يعطف على محل الجار والمجرور»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v1p492/r5] verses 4:1 | The genitive reading that coordinates the explicit noun with the attached pronoun is unsound because the pronoun and preposition are too tightly joined; repeating the preposition is required. «والجر على عطف الظاهر على المضمر وليس بسديد لأن الضمير المتصل متصل كاسمه والجار والمجرور كشيء واحد»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v1p492/r6] verses 4:1 | Those defending the genitive reading posit a repeated preposition, with a line of poetry as a parallel. «وقد تمحل لصحة هذه القراءة بأنها على تقدير تكرير الجار ونظيرها ( فاذهب فما بك والأيام من عجب )»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v1p492/r7] verses 4:1 | With nominative case, kinship is a subject with an omitted predicate: it too is to be guarded or invoked in requests. «والرفع على أنه مبتدأ خبره محذوف كأنه قيل والأرحام كذلك على معنى والأرحام مما يتقى»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v1p492/r8] verses 4:1 | People acknowledged a creator and invoked God and kinship in requests; they are told to fear God, honor these appeals, and not sever family ties. «والمعنى أنهم كانوا يقرون بأن لهم خالقا وكانوا يتساءلون بذكر الله والرحم فقيل لهم اتقوا الله الذي خلقكم واتقوا الذي تتناشدون به»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v1p492/r9] verses 4:1 | Mentioning kinship alongside God signals its special importance, as Qur'an 17:23 also pairs worship of God with kindness to parents. «وقد آذن عز وجل إذ قرن الأرحام باسمه أن صلتها منه بمكان كما قال ^ أن لا تعبدوا الا اياه وبالوالدين إحسانا ^ الإسراء»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v1p493/r1] verses 4:1 | The continuing account of kinship says it veils itself from the person who severs family ties. «وإذا أتاها القاطع احتجبت منه»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v1p493/r2] verses 4:1 | The Prophet's saying urges people to choose carefully for their offspring. «وسئل ابن عيينة عن قوله عليه الصلاة والسلام 250 ( تخيروا لنطفكم )»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v1p493/r3] verses 4:1 | Asked about the Prophetic saying, Ibn ʿUyayna explains that it concerns one's children and placing them in a lawful setting. «وسئل ابن عيينة عن قوله عليه الصلاة والسلام 250 ( تخيروا لنطفكم ) + + فقال يقول لأولادكم وذلك ان يضع ولده في الحلال»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v1p493/r4] verses 4:1 | The first act of maintaining kinship is choosing a lawful place for conception; one should preserve lineage, seek health, and avoid debauchery rather than follow unguided desire. «وأول صلته أن يختار له الموضع الحلال فلا يقطع رحمه ولا نسبه فإنما للعاهر الحجر ثم يختار الصحة ويجتنب الدعر ولا يضعه موضع سوء»
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:1/r1] verses 4:1 | The Qur’anic address “O people” targets all humanity, not believers alone. «Kur’an-ı Kerîm’de “ey insanlar!” hitabının hedef kitlesi yalnızca müminler değil, bütün insanlardır.»
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:1/r10] verses 4:1 | Under the second definition, God creates an individual nafs for each person as well as a body; Shah Waliullah calls this nafs nasama, whose spiritual constitution and traits vary with a person’s deeds. «Şah Veliyyullah’ın “neseme” adını verdiği bu nefis, insanın hayatı boyunca yapıp ettiklerine göre mânevî bir yapı ve kişilere göre farklı özellikler kazanır.»
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:1/r11] verses 4:1 | Spirit is universal rather than personal, like electricity from one source lighting bulbs; it is divine, belongs to God and the command-world, not the created world. «Ruh ise şahsî değil umumidir; tek bir enerji merkezinden gelip ampulleri aydınlatan elektrik gibidir ve ilâhîdir, Allah’a aittir, “halk âlemi”ne değil “emir âlemi”ne dahildir»
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:1/r12] verses 4:1 | Spirit illuminates or draws the nafs toward the path that leads to God’s approval. «nefis için Allah’ın rızâsına götüren yolu aydınlatır veya onu bu yola çeker.»
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:1/r13] verses 4:1 | Human nature also contains emotions, drives, and needs that pull against God’s approval, while Satan works to divert people from God’s path. «İnsanın tabiatında ve yapısında Allah’ın rızâsına aykırı yola çeken güçler de (heyecanlar, güdüler, ihtiyaçlar) vardır, ayrıca şeytanın da işi, insanı Allah yolundan saptırmaya çalışmaktır.»
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:1/r14] verses 4:1 | Through education and will, the human nafs struggles between these attractions and seeks to fulfill servitude and spiritual development in worldly life through trial. «İnsan (nefis), aldığı eğitim ve iradesi sayesinde bu iki çekim merkezi arasında mücadele ve imtihan vererek dünya hayatında kulluğunu ve tekâmülünü gerçekleştirmeye çalışır»
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:1/r15] verses 4:1 | The nafs strives to rise from ammara, which commands evil, through self-reproaching, divinely inspired, tranquil, content, and God-approved stages. «“emmâre” (kötüye çeken, kötüyü emreden) nefis olmaktan kurtularak, “levvâme” (kendini tenkit eden, kınayan), “mülheme” (ilâhî ilhama mazhar olan), “mutmainne”»
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:1/r16] verses 4:1 | Because every person has a mother and father and arises through reproduction, “from one nafs” and “from it” mean from its essence and a like kindred root, not a body part such as a rib. «burada “nefisten, ondan yaratan” sözünü “onun bir parçasından” (meselâ kaburgasından) şeklinde değil, “onun özünden, ona benzer (misli) olan asıldan ve kökten»
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:1/r17] verses 4:1 | Rūm 30:21 uses nafs for one’s own kind: God made spouses of your kind and placed love and compassion between you. «“Onlara ısınıp kaynaşasınız diye size kendi türünüzden (nefislerinizden) eşler yaratıp aranıza sevgi ve şefkat duyguları yerleştirmesi de O’nun kanıtlarındandır” meâlindeki âyette de bu kelime aynı mânada kullanılmıştır»
