[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p495/r1] verses 4:1 | The coordinating wāw does not imply temporal order; Adam’s creation is mentioned first because the addressed people are the focus, as in the command to worship the Lord who created them and those before them. «إذ الواو لا تدل على ترتيب زماني كما تقرر في علم العربية»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p495/r2] verses 4:1 | Zamakhshari posits an omitted earlier phrase so Eve’s creation follows Adam’s creation in time; Abu Hayyan says this grammatical workaround is unnecessary. «يعطف على محذوف كأنه قيل: من نفس واحدة أنشأها أو ابتدأها»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p495/r3] verses 4:1 | Zamakhshari also connects the phrase directly to “created you,” with Adam as the single self and Eve as the mother of those addressed; Abu Hayyan says this fits Arabic syntax. «يعطف على خلقكم»

[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p495/r6] verses 4:1 | Abu Hayyan says Zamakhshari’s ellipsis is unnecessary because the direct analysis already fits Arabic syntax. «ولا حاجة إلى تكلف هذا الوجه مع مساغ الوجه الذي ذكرناه»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p496#2/r1] verses 4:1 | Ibn Atiyya says the light reading deletes the second tāʾ of تتساءلون; Abu Hayyan notes the Kufan grammarian Hisham instead says it deletes the imperfect prefix tāʾ. «أما قول ابن عطية: حذفوا التاء الثانية فهذا مذهب أهل البصرة»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p496#2/r2] verses 4:1 | Abu Ali says adjacent sounds may be lightened through deletion, assimilation, or substitution, and cites طست from طس with an al-Ajjaj verse. «قال أبو علي: وإذا اجتمعت المتقاربة خففت بالحذف والإدغام والإبدال»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p496#2/r3] verses 4:1 | Abu Hayyan says Ibn Atiyya should note that retention is original and assimilation is close to it; deletion follows identical consonants, not merely nearby sounds. «كان ينبغي أن ينبه على الإثبات، إذ يجوز الإثبات وهو الأصل»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p496#2/r4] verses 4:1 | Assimilation is not confined to the tafaʿʿul form but occurs across several verb forms; deletion, however, is limited to imperfects beginning with the extra tāʾ. «أما الإدغام فلا يختص به، بل ذلك في الأمر والمضارع»

[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p496#2/r8] verses 4:1 | A group says the phrase means invoking God to claim rights and treating Him as exalted. «وقالت طائفة: معناه تسألون به حقوقكم وتجعلونه معظما لها»
