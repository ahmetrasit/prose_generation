[QURTUBI-FULL:19:93/r3] verses 19:93 | The verse proves a parent cannot own a child: God contrasts sonship with servitude, which cannot coexist; if a father cannot own his son, the son cannot own his father all the more. «وقد أبان الله تعالى المنافاة بين الأولاد والملك»
[QURTUBI-FULL:19:93/r4] verses 19:93 | The sound Muslim report says a child cannot repay a parent except by finding the parent enslaved, buying him, and freeing him. «لا يجزي ولد والدا إلا أن يجده مملوكا فيشتريه فيعتقه»
[QURTUBI-FULL:19:93/r5] verses 19:93 | Isḥāq ibn Rāhwayh interprets emancipation of a share in a slave as referring only to male slaves, so freeing a share in a female would not complete emancipation. «ذهب إسحاق بن راهويه في تأويل قوله عليه الصلاة والسلام»
[QURTUBI-FULL:19:93/r6] verses 19:93 | The majority reject Isḥāq’s distinction and include male and female slaves, since “ʿabd” is generic and this verse encompasses both. «وهو على خلاف ما ذهب إليه الجمهور من السلف ومن بعدهم»
