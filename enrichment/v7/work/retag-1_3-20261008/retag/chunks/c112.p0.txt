# Notes

## 31:14
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v21p152/r5] verses 31:14 | The present tense in يشكر signals renewed gratitude, and the لام in اشكر لي is a strengthening marker conventionally attached to the verb شكر, as in 2:152. «لام ملتزم زيادتها مع مادة الشكر للتأكيد والتقوية»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p348#2/r2] verses 31:14 | The instruction gives mother and father equal standing, then singles out the mother for pregnancy and nursing, yielding three shares of kindness for her and one for the father; the Prophetic answer names the mother three times, then the father. «ثم خصص الأم بدرجة ذكر الحمل ودرجة ذكر الرضاع فتحصل للأم ثلاث مراتب وللأب واحدة»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p348#2/r3] verses 31:14 | Wahnan ʿalā wahn can mean weakness upon weakness from pregnancy then childbirth, the weakness of child and mother, or steadily increasing weakness without specifying two episodes. «وهنا على وهن معناه ضعفا على ضعف، وقيل إشارة»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p348#2/r4] verses 31:14 | They read wahn with fatḥa on the hāʾ; it has the same meaning as the majority reading with sukūn. «وقرأ عيسى الثقفي «وهنا على وهن» بفتح الهاء، ورويت عن أبي عمرو»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p348#2/r5] verses 31:14 | They read wa-faṣluhu instead of wa-fiṣāluhu; the author says fiṣāl points to the counted duration of nursing by naming its endpoint. «وقرأ جمهور الناس «وفصاله» ، وقرأ الحسن وأبو رجاء»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p348#2/r6] verses 31:14 | Jurists agree on two years as the nursing period for maintenance, but differ on milk kinship: exactly two years, two years plus connected days, or no effect after early weaning. «والناس مجمعون على العامين في مدة الرضاع في باب الأحكام والنفقات، وأما»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p348#2/r7] verses 31:14 | Sufyan says praying the five daily prayers thanks God, while praying for one's parents after them thanks one's parents. «وقال سفيان بن عيينة من صلى الصلوات الخمس فقد شكر الله تعالى.»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p348#2/r8] verses 31:14 | The clause ilaa al-maṣīr inserts a warning within the parental counsel. «وقوله تعالى: إلي المصير توعد أثناء الوصية»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v6p319/r1] verses 31:14 | Muqātil says the verse was revealed about Saʿd ibn Abī Waqqāṣ; the author refers readers to his account in Sūrat al-ʿAnkabūt. «قال مقاتل نزلت في سعد بن أبي وقاص وقد شرحنا ذلك في العنكبوت»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v6p319/r2] verses 31:14 | Al-Ḍaḥḥāk and ʿĀṣim al-Jaḥdarī read “wahn” with fatḥa on the hāʾ both times; al-Zajjāj glosses it as weakness upon weakness from successive burdens of pregnancy. «وقرأ الضحاك وعاصم الجحدري وهنا على وهن بفتح الهاء فيهما قال الزجاج أي ضعفا على ضعف»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v6p319/r3] verses 31:14 | The accusative “an” introduces the command enjoining gratitude to God and to one’s parents. «وموضع أن نصب ب وصينا المعنى ووصينا الإنسان أن أشكر لي ولواليدك»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v6p319/r4] verses 31:14 | “Its weaning is in two years” means weaning occurs at the completion of two years. «أي فطامه يقع في انقضاء عامين»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v6p319/r5] verses 31:14 | Ibrāhīm al-Nakhaʿī, Abū ʿImrān, and al-Aʿmash read “fiṣālihi” with fatḥa on the fāʾ. «وقرأ إبراهيم النخعي وأبو عمران والأعمش وفصاله بفتح الفاء»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v6p319/r6] verses 31:14 | These authorities read “faṣlihi” with fatḥa on fāʾ, sukūn on ṣād, and no alif; the wording highlights the mother’s hardship in nursing after pregnancy. «وقرأ أبي بن كعب والحسن وأبو رجاء وطلحة بن مصرف وعاصم الجحدري وقتادة وفصله بفتح الفاء وسكون الصاد من غير ألف»
[IBNJINNI-MUHTASAB:v2p167/r1] verses 31:14 | They read وهنا على وهن with fatḥa on both hāʾs; the author allows وَهَن to be a verbal noun, by analogy with وَضَر and وَحَر. «الحلواني عن شباب عن أحمد بن موسى عن أبي عمرو وعيسى الثقفي»
[IBNJINNI-MUHTASAB:v2p167/r2] verses 31:14 | They read وفصله instead of وفصاله. Faṣl is broader than weaning, while faṣāl better fits this nursing context; the author relates both to separation and cessation. «ومن ذلك قراءة الحسن بخلاف وأبي رجاء الجحدري وقتادة ويعقوب»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v6p336#2/r1] verses 31:14 | Weaning in two years means the child's rearing and nursing after birth; this matches 2:233. «تربيته وإرضاعه بعد وضعه في عامين»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v6p336#2/r2] verses 31:14 | Ibn ʿAbbās and other imams infer a minimum pregnancy of six months by comparing thirty months for pregnancy and weaning in 46:15 with a full two-year nursing term. «أن أقل مدة الحمل ستة أشهر»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v6p336#2/r3] verses 31:14 | Mention of the mother's toil, including nights awake, recalls her prior care and calls the child to show her kindness, as in 17:24; God will richly reward gratitude to Him and the parents. «ليذكر الولد بإحسانها المتقدم إليه»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v6p336#2/r4] verses 31:14 | Muʿādh, sent by the Prophet, tells his hearers to worship God, avoid shirk, obey the Messenger for their good, and recognize that their final destination is everlasting Paradise or Hell. «أن تعبدوا الله ولا تشركوا به شيئا، وأن تطيعوني لا آلوكم خيرا»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v6p336/r3] verses 31:13, 31:14 | Worship of God alone is paired with kindness to parents, as in 17:23 and often elsewhere in the Quran. «وكثيرا ما يقرن تعالى بين ذلك في القرآن»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v6p336/r4] verses 31:14 | Mujāhid interprets the mother's weakness upon weakness as the hardship of carrying her child; Qatādah says strain upon strain and ʿAṭāʾ al-Khurāsānī says weakness upon weakness. «قال مجاهد: مشقة وهن الولد»

[JISHUMI:v8p5649/r3] verses 31:14 | Yaʿqūb reads “his weaning” without the alif; the other readers use the form with alif. «وقراءة القراء: (فصاله) بالألف، وعن يعقوب: (فصله) بغير ألف»

[JISHUMI:v8p5649/r5] verses 31:14 | Fiṣāl means weaning, named for cutting the child off from the maternal breast. «والفصال: الفطام، وأصله: القطع، فصلت بين الشيئين»
[JISHUMI:v8p5649/r8] verses 31:14 | The verse about dutifulness was revealed concerning Saʿd ibn Abī Waqqāṣ: his mother swore not to eat until he abandoned his religion, but ate after three days when he would not recant. «حلفت أمه لا تأكل طعاما حتى تموت، أو يدع دينه»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v3p500#3/r1] verses 31:14 | Hamalat-hu means his mother carried him while weakened; the phrase is circumstantial and describes weakness increasing as pregnancy grew heavier. «تهن { وهنا على وهن } كقولك رجع عودا على يده ، بمعنى ؛ يعود عودا على يده ، وهو في موضع الحال»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v3p500#3/r12] verses 31:14 | The mother's bearing and weaning are inserted between the command and its gloss to detail her hardship, especially establish the mother's claim to care, and recall her great right. «لما وصى بالوالدين : ذكر ما تكابده الأم وتعانيه من المشاق والمتاعب في حمله وفصاله هذه المدة المتطاولة»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v3p500#3/r2] verses 31:14 | Abu Amr reads wahn with both words vowelled, and the source notes the two verb forms wahan-yūhinu and wahan-yahinu. «وقرىء : ( وهنا على وهن ) . بالتحريك عن أبي عمرو . يقال : وهن يوهن . ووهن يهن»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v3p500#3/r3] verses 31:14 | A reading has fasluhu in place of fisaluhu. «وقرىء : ( وفصله»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v3p500#3/r4] verses 31:14 | An ashkur is an explanatory gloss for waṣṣaynā: the command is to give thanks. «أن اشكر } تفسير لوصينا»

[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:31:14/r4] verses 31:14 | Gratitude to God and parents is jointly commanded because God creates and sustains, while parents enable birth and care for, raise, feed, and educate a child. «ana babanın da insanın hem dünyaya gelmesine vesile olması hem de hayatının en zayıf dönemlerinde»

[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:31:14/r6] verses 31:14 | Asked who most deserves kindness, the Prophet answered 'your mother' three times before answering 'your father' on the fourth. «“annene” diye cevap vermiş; “Sonra kime?” denilince yine “annene” demiş; üçüncü defa tekrarlanan soruya da aynı cevabı vermiş»
