[THALABI:v5p114/r17] verses 9:128 | The cited Prophetic report says that whoever leaves property leaves it to heirs, while whoever leaves dependents and debt has responsibility taken on by the Prophet. «لقوله صلى الله عليه وسلم: «من ترك ما لا فلنؤتينه ومن ترك كلا ودينا فعلي وإلى» .»
