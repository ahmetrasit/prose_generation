[ELMALILI:7:156-157#2/r13] verses 7:156, 7:157 | From Muhammad’s mission onward, following him makes mercy a universal conditional obligation: God established a monotheistic universal law as the divinely required path to worldly and eternal mercy and escape from punishment. «Bi‘set-i Muhammediyeden itibaren ise vasf-ı ittibâ‘şartıyla vâcib bir meşrûta-i âmme oldu. [ELMALILI:7:156-157#2/r16] verses 7:156, 7:157 | The story of Moses and the preceding prophets culminates in the universal mercy-law and the promised mission of the final Prophet. «mâsîka lehi ve hedef-i aksâsıdır.»
