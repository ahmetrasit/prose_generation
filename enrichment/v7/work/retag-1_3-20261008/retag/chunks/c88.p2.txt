[BIQAI-FULL:v4p499/r1] verses 18:82 | A Bukhārī report through Ibn ʿAbbās and Ubayy recounts Moses asking whether anyone was more learned, God’s correction, the fish sign, Khidr’s identity question, the boat, and the bird’s comparison of their knowledge with God’s. «قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وعلى آله وسلم»
[BIQAI-FULL:v4p499/r2] verses 18:82 | The bird’s tiny sip from the sea illustrates how little Moses’s and Khidr’s knowledge removes from God’s knowledge. «ما نقص علمي وعلمك من علم الله إلا كما نقص هذا البحر»
[BIQAI-FULL:v4p499/r3] verses 18:82 | The hadith distinguishes knowledge that Moses could not properly act upon from the inward knowledge Khidr followed; each had knowledge the other should not use. «أي لا ينبغي لك أن تعمل بالباطن ولا ينبغي لي أنا أن أقف مع الظاهر»
[BIQAI-FULL:v4p499/r4] verses 18:82 | The story urges prompt public command of right and endurance in it, without deferring for rank or age when one trusts the outward evidence of revelation and religious authorities. «بالمبادرة بالأمر بالمعروف والنهي عن المنكرو المصابرة عليه»

[BURSEVI:v5p220#2/r2] verses 18:82 | Ja‘far ibn Muhammad says seven generations separated the boys from their righteous ancestor, the seventh forefather who buried the treasure. «قال جعفر بن محمد : كان بينهما وبين الأب الصالح سبعة آباء»
[BURSEVI:v5p220#2/r3] verses 18:82 | The boys were to reach maturity, described as puberty and sound judgment; reports differ on its precise age, ending at thirty-three or eighteen. «الأشد في معنى القوة جمع شدة»
[BURSEVI:v5p220#2/r6] verses 18:82 | Without the repaired wall it would have fallen before the boys could preserve or grow the wealth, and the treasure would have been lost. «لولا أني أقمته لانقض وخرج الكنز من تحته قبل اقتدارهما على حفظ المال»
[BURSEVI:v5p220#2/r7] verses 18:82 | The Imam’s answer to the puzzle of how the boys could find the treasure is that they did not know it and their guardian was away. «لعلهما لم يعلماه وعلم القيم إلا أنه كان غائبا»
[BURSEVI:v5p220#2/r8] verses 18:82 | The author says the puzzle is no objection: God could guide them to it, discoveries without prior knowledge are common, and the lām grants them the exclusive right to find it; a seventh-generation ancestor could hardly have buried it for them. «لأن الله تعالى قادر على أن يعرفهما مكان ذلك الكنز بطريق من الطرق»
[BURSEVI:v5p220#2/r9] verses 18:82 | Rahmatan min rabbik may be a circumstantial phrase meaning ‘shown mercy,’ a reason for the divine will, or an omitted verb meaning God had mercy on them. «{رحمة من ربك} لهما مصدر في موقع الحال»
