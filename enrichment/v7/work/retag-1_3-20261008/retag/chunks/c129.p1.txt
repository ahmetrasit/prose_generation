[IBNASHUR-FULL:v26p33/r1] verses 46:15 | Awziʿnī means inspire me; the root denotes removing restraint, with the hamza causative of removal. «و { أوزعني } : ألهمني»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v26p33/r2] verses 46:15 | Niʿmataka is a verbal noun used inclusively: the prayer asks gratitude for every religious blessing, such as faith and guidance, and worldly blessing, such as health and wealth, given to the speaker and his parents. «أي ألهمني شكر النعم التي أنعمت بها علي وعلى والدي من جميع النعم الدينية كالإيمان والتوفيق ومن النعم الدنيوية كالصحة والجدة»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v26p33/r3] verses 46:15 | The request to reform descendants is an aside within the prayer for parents, directing one to attend to future generations as well; their reform includes inspiring them to pray for their parent. «استطراد في أثناء الوصاية بالدعاء للوالدين بأن لا يغفل الإنسان عن التفكر في مستقبله بأن يصرف عنايته إلى ذريته»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v26p33/r4] verses 46:15 | Including descendants hints that a person may receive the same kindness from children that the person gave parents, and that a parent’s prayer for a child is likely answered. «إيماء إلى أن المرء يلقى من إحسان أبنائه إليه مثل ما لقي أبواه من إحسانه إليهما»

[IBNASHUR-FULL:v26p33/r6] verses 46:15 | The lām in li of wa-aṣliḥ lī is causal: reform the descendants for my sake and benefit, as in the cited verse. «واللام في { وأصلح لي } لام العلة»
