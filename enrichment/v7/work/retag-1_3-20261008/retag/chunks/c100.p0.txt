# Verses
- 21:107: وَمَآ أَرْسَلْنَٰكَ إِلَّا رَحْمَةًۭ لِّلْعَٰلَمِينَ

# Notes

## 21:107
[ASAD-NOTES:21:105#n102/r4] verses 21:107 | Asad says the Qur’an’s wording has remained unchanged since revelation and will remain protected, citing 15:9; these factors make it the final revelation, and Muhammad the last prophet, the “seal” (33:40). «the fact that - contrary to all other sacred scriptures known to history - the Qur'an has remained entirely unchanged in its wording»
[BAGHAWI-FULL:v5p359#2/r5] verses 21:107 | Ibn Zayd says the Prophet is a mercy specifically for believers. «قال ابن زيد: يعني رحمة للمؤمنين خاصة فهو رحمة لهم.»
[BAGHAWI-FULL:v5p359#2/r6] verses 21:107 | Ibn Abbas takes the mercy as universal: believers receive it in both lives, while unbelievers receive respite and protection from transformative and total punishments in this life. «وقال ابن عباس: هو عام في حق من آمن ومن لم يؤمن فمن آمن فهو رحمة له] في الدنيا والآخرة»
[BAGHAWI-FULL:v5p359#2/r7] verses 21:107 | The Prophet describes himself as a mercy sent as a gift. «وقد قال النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم: "إنما أنا رحمة مهداة"»
[BAYDAWI-FULL:21:107/r1] verses 21:107 | The Prophet's message is mercy because it brings people felicity and improves their worldly life and afterlife. «لأن ما بعثت به سبب لإسعادهم وموجب لصلاح معاشهم ومعادهم»
[BAYDAWI-FULL:21:107/r2] verses 21:107 | Alternatively, the Prophet was mercy even for unbelievers because his coming protected them from being swallowed by the earth, transformed, or struck by exterminating punishment. «وقيل كونه رحمة للكفار أمنهم به من الخسف والمسخ وعذاب الاستئصال»
[BIQAI-FULL:v5p124/r4] verses 21:107 | The Prophet was sent as mercy to all worlds, including the inhabitants of heaven and earth, jinn, humans, and others. «كلهم , أهل السماوات وأهل الأرض من الجن والأنس وغيرهم»
[BIQAI-FULL:v5p124/r5] verses 21:107 | The obedient receive reward through this mercy, while the disobedient receive delayed punishment instead of the destruction that overtook earlier nations. «طائعهم بالثواب , وعاصيهم بتأخير العقاب , الذي كنا نستأصل به الأمم»
[BIQAI-FULL:v5p124/r6] verses 21:107 | The delay displays the Prophet's honor and lets many former opponents return to his religion and become its greatest supporters, proving that only God aids him. «ثم نرد كثيرا منهم إلى دينك , ونجعلهم من أكابر أنصارك وأعاظم أعوانك»
[BIQAI-FULL:v5p124/r7] verses 21:107 | The universal mercy is most evident in the great intercession: people seek relief from the assembly by asking prophets in turn, until Muhammad accepts, bears the Banner of Praise, and is granted the Praised Station. «ومن أعظم ما يظهر فيه الشرف في عموم الرحمة وقت الشفاعة العظمى يوم يجمع الأولون والآخرون»
[BIQAI-FULL:v5p124/r8] verses 21:107 | The author says he had already related most of the great-intercession hadith in his commentary on 40:18. «وقد سبقت أكثر الحديث بذلك في سورة غافر»
[BURSEVI:v5p403#2/r1] verses 21:107 | Some spiritual masters say the Prophet is mercy to all worlds because he embodies magnificent character and gives each level—royal, spiritual, natural, psychic, spiritual, and secret—its due place. «وقال بعض الكبار : إنما كان رحمة للعالمين بسب اتصافه بالخلق العظيم»
[BURSEVI:v5p403#2/r2] verses 21:107 | The Najmiyya commentary contrasts Jesus’s partial mercy, limited by min and ending when his religion was abrogated, with Muhammad’s unrestricted mercy, which never ceases: his religion remains in this world and all creatures, even Abraham, need his intercession in the next. «وفي "التأويلات النجمية" في سورة مريم»
[BURSEVI:v5p403#2/r3] verses 21:107 | The Arāʾis al-Baqli says Muhammad’s light was the first creation; creatures from Throne to soil came from some of it, so his advent gives life and mercy to all existence. «قال في "عرائس البقلى" : أيها الفهيم إن الله أخبرنا»
[BURSEVI:v5p403#2/r4] verses 21:107 | All creatures were originally lifeless forms awaiting Muhammad; once he arrived, the world became alive because he is the spirit of all creatures. «فإذا قدم إلى العالم صار العالم حيا بوجوده لأنه روح جميع الخلائق»
[BURSEVI:v5p403#2/r5] verses 21:107 | Creatures could not know the mysteries of eternity or reach the shores of divine majesty, so Muhammad came as the elixir of bodies and spirit of forms, conveying primordial knowledge and clarifying the path of truth. «لم يخرج من العدم إلا ناقصا من حيث الوقوف على أسرار قدمه بنعت كمال المعرفة والعلم»
[BURSEVI:v5p403#2/r6] verses 21:107 | His journey compressed the beginning and end of time into one step, guiding all across the path of the Night Journey to the station of two bows’ length or nearer; by his blessed foot God forgave all creatures. «بحيث جعل سفر الآزال والآباد للجميع خطوة واحدة فإذا قدم من الحضرة إلى سفر القربة»
[BURSEVI:v5p403/r10] verses 21:107 | The direct address is to the Prophet, while its inward indication extends to each heir who follows his path as a manifestation of his inheritance. «خطاب للنبي عليه السلام فقط وإشارته خطاب لكل واحد من ورثته»
[BURSEVI:v5p403/r3] verses 21:107 | Muhammad was sent with laws, rulings, and other foundations of happiness in both lives as mercy to all worlds; anyone who turns away brings hardship upon himself. «بما ذكر وأمثاله من الشرائع والأحكام وغير ذلك من الأمور التي هي مناط السعادة في الدارين»
[BURSEVI:v5p403/r4] verses 21:107 | Some say the Prophet also showed mercy to unbelievers by postponing their punishment and sparing them collective destruction, being swallowed by the earth, or transformation. «قال بعضهم : جاء رحمة للكفار أيضا»
[BURSEVI:v5p403/r5] verses 21:107 | In a report, the Prophet asks Gabriel whether the mercy in this verse reached him; Gabriel says it did because he feared his fate until God praised him at 81:20–21. «ورد في الخبر أنه عليه السلام قال لجبريل»
[BURSEVI:v5p403/r6] verses 21:107 | Gabriel answers that the mercy reached him: he had feared the outcome until reassured by God’s praise of him as powerful, honored, obeyed, and trustworthy. «أنه عليه السلام قال لجبريل : "إن الله يقول وما أرسلناك إلى آخره فهل أصابك من هذه الرحمة" قال : نعم»
[BURSEVI:v5p403/r7] verses 21:107 | Kashifi, citing Kashf al-Asrar, says the Prophet remembered his community at every station, including the Throne and the two-bows’ length, and will spread intercession at the Praiseworthy Station, crying for his community. «قال الكاشفي : (در كشف الأسرار آورده كه از رحمت وى بودكه امت را در هي مقام»
[BURSEVI:v5p403/r8] verses 21:107 | The Persian poem portrays sinners grasping the Prophet’s hem and relying on his intercession rather than despairing of his court. «عاصيان ركنه در دامن آخر زمان دست درد امان تودارندو جان در آستين»
[BURSEVI:v5p403/r9] verses 21:107 | Some spiritual masters call the Prophet’s mercy absolute, complete, universal, and encompassing every limited form of mercy; this universality entails his superiority to all worlds. «قال بعض الكبار : وما أرسلناك إلا رحمة مطلقة تامة كاملة عامة»
[BURSEVI:v5p404/r1] verses 21:107 | Some scholars contrast earlier prophets as prelude to punishment, citing 17:15, with Muhammad as prelude to mercy; God made the last community’s beginning and end mercy, in keeping with the report that mercy precedes wrath. «قال بعض العلماء : إن كل نبي كان مقدمة للعقوبة»
[BURSEVI:v5p404/r2] verses 21:107 | The Ahmadic reality emerged from the hidden divine presence when creation was willed, was marked with possibility, and became mercy and honor for humanity; spirits and then bodily forms flowed from it. «أبرز الحقيقة الأحمدية من كمون الحضرة الأحدية فميزه بميم الامكان وجعله رحمة للعالمين وشرف به نوع الإنسان»
[BURSEVI:v5p404/r3] verses 21:107 | A saying attributed to the Prophet states that he is from God and believers are from the outpouring of his light. «كما قال عليه السلام : "أنا من الله والمؤمنون من فيض نوري"»
[BURSEVI:v5p404/r4] verses 21:107 | The author presents Muhammad as the ultimate purpose in the ordering of created beginnings and cites the saying that the celestial spheres were created for his sake. «كما قال تعالى : "لولاك لما خلقت الأفلاك"»
[BURSEVI:v5p404/r5] verses 21:107 | Jami’s couplet calls Muhammad the goal and joy of every realm and the intermediary through whom all existence comes. «علت غائيه هر عالم اوست سرور اولاد بني آدم اوست واسطه فيض وجودي همه»
[BURSEVI:v5p404/r6] verses 21:107 | ʿUrfi Shirazi’s praise poem says Muhammad’s honor lies in being the source of divine determination; the author reads ʿabathan reversed as baʿthan, resurrection. «قال العرفي الشيرازي في قصيدته النعتية»
[BURSEVI:v5p404/r7] verses 21:107 | The author infers that God created beings and sent messengers as preparation for Muhammad’s manifestation; their spirits and bodies follow his noble spirit and subtle body. «يعني يكفيك شرفا وفضلا أن الله سبحانه إنما خلق الخلق وبعث الأنبياء والرسل ليكونوا مقدمة لظهورك»
[BURSEVI:v5p404/r8] verses 21:107 | A report says the Prophet’s life and death are both mercy: the Companions ask how his death benefits them, and he says their deeds are shown to him twice weekly; he praises good and asks forgiveness for bad. «حياته عليه السلام رحمة ومماته رحمة»
[BURSEVI:v5p404/r9] verses 21:107 | Jami’s poem asks the Prophet for compassion after his separation, saying he cannot leave the deprived without intercession if he is mercy to the worlds. «قال المولى الجامي : زمهجورى برآمد جان عالم»
[DURR-FULL:v5p687/r10] verses 21:107 | The Prophet says that angry insults or curses from him, as a human who gets angry, are made a prayer for the recipient on Resurrection Day. «عن سلمان: أن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم: أيما رجل من أمتي سببته سبة في غضبي أو لعنته لعنة»
[DURR-FULL:v5p687/r11] verses 21:107 | The Prophet calls himself a mercy sent as a gift. «عن أبي هريرة رضي الله عنه قال: قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم: إنما أنا رحمة مهداة»
[DURR-FULL:v5p687/r12] verses 21:107 | Asked whether he would curse Quraysh for their treatment of him, the Prophet says he was sent as mercy, not as a curser, citing this verse. «عن عكرمة رضي الله عنه قال: قيل يا رسول الله ألا تلعن قريشا بما أتوا إليك فقال: لم أبعث لعانا إنما بعثت رحمة»
