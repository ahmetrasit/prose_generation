# Notes

## 46:15
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v7p279#2/r1] verses 46:15 | After the preceding command to worship God alone and remain upright, the Qur’an pairs that with kindness to parents, tenderness, and care, as it does elsewhere. «لما ذكر تعالى في الآية الأولى التوحيد له وإخلاص العبادة والاستقامة إليه، عطف بالوصية بالوالدين»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v7p279#2/r2] verses 46:15 | Saʿd’s mother refused food and drink until he disbelieved, saying God commanded obedience to parents; after she was forced to eat, the verse about kindness to parents was revealed. «قالت أم سعد لسعد: أليس قد أمر الله بطاعة الوالدين، فلا آكل طعاما، ولا أشرب شرابا حتى تكفر بالله»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v7p279#2/r3] verses 46:15 | Muslim and the Sunan collections except Ibn Majah transmit this account through Shuʿba with the same chain, in a longer form. «ورواه مسلم وأهل السنن إلا ابن ماجه، من حديث شعبة بإسناده، نحوه وأطول منه»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v7p280#2/r1] verses 46:15 | The child’s full strength comes with adulthood; by forty, reason, understanding, and judgment are complete, and people generally do not change much afterward. «أي: قوي وشب وارتجل {وبلغ أربعين سنة}»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v7p280#2/r2] verses 46:15 | Masruq says a man is called to account for his sins upon reaching forty and should be on guard. «قلت لمسروق: متى يؤخذ الرجل بذنوبه؟ قال: إذا بلغت الأربعين»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v7p280#2/r3] verses 46:15 | A report from Uthman says a Muslim’s reckoning is eased at forty, repentance is granted at sixty, heaven’s inhabitants love him at seventy, good deeds are fixed and sins erased at eighty, and forgiveness and intercession come at ninety. «العبد المسلم إذا بلغ أربعين سنة خفف الله حسابه»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v7p280#2/r4] verses 46:15 | The Umayyad official says he left sins for forty years out of shame before people, then continued leaving them out of shame before God. «تركت المعاصي والذنوب أربعين سنة حياء من الناس، ثم تركتها حياء من الله»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v7p280#2/r5] verses 46:15 | The quoted poet describes a man who turned away from falsehood once gray hair covered his head. «صبا ما صبا حتى علا الشيب رأسه … فلما علاه قال للباطل: ابطل»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v7p280/r1] verses 46:15 | Pregnancy brings nausea, illness, heaviness, distress, and other hardship, while childbirth also involves the difficulty and pain of labor. «من وحام وغشيان وثقل وكرب، إلى غير ذلك مما تنال الحوامل من التعب والمشقة»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v7p280/r2] verses 46:15 | Ali combines the thirty months of pregnancy and weaning with the two-year weaning period to infer a six-month minimum pregnancy; Ibn Kathir calls the deduction strong and correct. «على أن أقل مدة الحمل ستة أشهر، وهو استنباط قوي صحيح»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v7p280/r3] verses 46:15 | Uthman and a group of Companions agreed with Ali’s deduction that pregnancy can last as little as six months. «ووافقه عليه عثمان وجماعة من الصحابة»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v7p280/r4] verses 46:15 | A woman from his tribe delivered after six months; Uthman ordered her stoned until Ali showed from the Qur’an that six months was possible, but she had already been delivered. Her husband recognized the child, and the report says he later died from a consuming ulcer. «فولدت له لتمام ستة أشهر، فانطلق زوجها إلى عثمان فذكر ذلك له»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v7p280/r5] verses 46:15 | Ibn Abbas calculates that a nine-month birth needs twenty-one months of nursing, a seven-month birth twenty-three, and a six-month birth two full years, totaling thirty months. «إذا وضعت المرأة لتسعة أشهر كفاه من الرضاع أحد وعشرون شهرا»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v7p281/r1] verses 46:15 | Awziʿni means inspire me; the prayer asks for gratitude for God’s favors to the speaker and parents, pleasing righteous work, and the reform of descendants. «أي: ألهمني {أن أشكر نعمتك التي أنعمت علي وعلى والدي»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v7p281/r2] verses 46:15 | The verse guides anyone reaching forty to renew repentance and return firmly to God. «وهذا فيه إرشاد لمن بلغ الأربعين أن يجدد التوبة والإنابة إلى الله»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v7p281/r3] verses 46:15 | Ibn Masʿud reports a supplication the Prophet taught in tashahhud, asking for united hearts, peace, protection from wrong, blessings on family and senses, repentance, and gratefulness. «كان يعلمهم أن يقولوا في التشهد:»
[IBNKHALAWAYH-HUJJA:v1p326#2/r2] verses 46:15 | بوالديه حسنا has two verbal-noun readings: ḥusnan from حَسُنَ and إِحسانا from أَحسن. «يقرا بضم الحاء من غير ألف وبألف قبل الحاء وإسكانها وألف بعد السين وهما مصدران»
[IBNKHALAWAYH-HUJJA:v1p326#2/r4] verses 46:15 | حملته أمه كرها ووضعته كرها are read with fatḥa or ḍamma on kāf; the author says the rationale was given earlier. «يقرآن بضم الكاف وفتحها وقد تقدم ذكره»
[IBNMUJAHID:v1p152/r8] verses 2:152, 15:36, 38:79, 19:43, 27:19, 46:15, 40:26, 40:60, 9:49, 11:47, 7:143, 28:34, 18:96 | Mujahid lists added yāʾs left unvowelled before a separate alif when attached to a jussive verb, including فاذكروني أذكركم and رب فأنظرني. «ومما ترك تحريك يائه عند الألف المقطوعة المتصلة بالفعل المجزوم قوله ﴿ فاذكروني أذكركم ﴾ البقرة 152»
[IBNMUJAHID:v1p152/r9] verses 2:40, 12:59, 46:15, 40:41, 40:43 | They leave the yāʾ unvowelled before a separate alif with a non-jussive verb, including بعهدي أوف, أني أوفي الكيل, and في ذريتي إني. «ومما لم يحرك ياءه عند الألف المقطوعة وهو مع فعل غير مجزوم فيما ذكر ابن جماز وإسماعيل بن جعفر قوله ﴿ بعهدي أوف ﴾ البقرة 40»
[IBNQUTAYBA-GHARIB:v1p407#4/r1] verses 46:15 | The mother's burden and delivery in hardship mean she undergoes difficulty in both stages. «{حملته أمه كرها} أي مشقة. {ووضعته كرها} أي مشقة.»
[IBNQUTAYBA-GHARIB:v1p407#4/r2] verses 46:15 | Awziʿnī means inspire me; the root īzāʿ originally means urging someone toward something, as in being devoted to it. «أي ألهمني. والأصل في "الإيزاع": الإغراء بالشيء؛ يقال: فلان موزع بكذا ومولع»
