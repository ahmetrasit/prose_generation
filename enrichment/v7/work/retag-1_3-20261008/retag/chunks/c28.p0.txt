# Notes

## 3:6
[BURSEVI:v2p3#2/r2] verses 3:6 | God shapes people in their mothers’ wombs into particular forms, including male or female, black or white, complete or deficient, tall or short, beautiful or ugly. «يجعلكم على هيئة مخصوصة في أرحام أمهاتكم من ذكر وأنثى وأسود وأبيض وتام وناقص وطويل وقصير وحسن وقبيح»
[BURSEVI:v2p3#2/r3] verses 3:6 | This refutes those who call Jesus God or God’s son: someone formed in a womb cannot be God or His son, since he is composite, situated in a composite, and subject to passing away. «وهو رد على الذين قالوا عيسى الله أو ابن الله لأن من صور في الرحم يمتنع أن يكون إلها»
[BURSEVI:v2p3#2/r4] verses 3:6 | ‘There is no deity but Him’ declares God exalted above having Jesus as a son; He is mighty in power and complete in wisdom, creating people in a wondrous pattern. «المتناهي في القدرة والحكمة فربكم يخلقكم على النمط البديع»
[BURSEVI:v2p3#2/r5] verses 3:6 | The Prophet reports that each person’s creation unfolds as forty days of a drop, then forty as a clot, then forty as a morsel, after which an angel records provision, deeds, lifespan, and whether the person is blessed or wretched. «إن خلق أحدكم يجمع في بطن أمه أربعين يوما نطفة ثم يكون علقة مثل ذلك ثم يكون مضغة مثل ذلك»
[BURSEVI:v2p3#2/r6] verses 3:6 | The Prophet warns that a person may perform the deeds of Paradise until only an arm’s length remains, then the prior decree prevails and he acts as the people of Hell; the reverse can happen as well. «وإن أحدكم ليعمل بعمل أهل الجنة حتى ما يكون بينه وبينها غير ذراع فيسبق عليه الكتاب فيعمل بعمل أهل النار»
[BURSEVI:v2p3#2/r7] verses 3:6 | Another Prophetic report says an angel enters the settled embryo after forty or forty-five nights, asks whether it will be blessed or wretched and male or female, writes its work, trace, lifespan, and provision, then rolls up the record without later additions or deletions. «يدخل الملك على النطفة بعد ما تستقر في الرحم بأربعين أو بخمس وأربعين ليلة فيقول : يا رب أشقي أم سعيد؟»
[BURSEVI:v2p3#2/r8] verses 3:6 | The angel is told to attach the record to the person’s neck until God’s decree; 17:13 calls the person’s ‘bird’ the good and evil deeds he freely performs within what is decreed, as if they flew to him from fate’s unseen nest. «ثم يقول الملك : يا رب ما أصنع بهذا الكتاب؟ فيقول علقه في عنقه إلى قضائي عليه»
[BURSEVI:v2p3#2/r9] verses 3:6 | Al-Qāḍī explains that recording these matters makes them known to the angel, since God’s decree preceded the writing and each person is made ready for what he was created to do. «قال القاضي المراد بكتبه هذه الأشياء إظهارها للملك وإلا فقضاؤه تعالى سابق على ذلك وكل ميسر لما خلق له»
[DURR-FULL:v2p143/r4] verses 3:6 | Because Jesus was formed in a womb like other human beings, whom his opponents do not deny were formed there, he cannot be God. «{هو الذي يصوركم في الأرحام كيف يشاء} قد كان عيسى ممن صور في الأرحام لا يدفعون ذلك ولا ينكرونه كما صور غيره من بني آدم»
[DURR-FULL:v2p143/r5] verses 3:6 | God forms people in the womb as males or females. «عن ابن مسعود في قوله {يصوركم في الأرحام كيف يشاء} قال: ذكورا وإناثا»
[DURR-FULL:v2p143/r6] verses 3:6 | A report describes 40 days as a drop, then 40 as a clot and 40 as a lump; an angel forms it with earth, asks its sex, fortune, provision, lifespan, deeds and afflictions, and records God's answers; the body is buried where its earth was taken. «عن ابن عباس عن مرة عن ابن مسعود وناس من الصحابة في قوله {هو الذي يصوركم في الأرحام كيف يشاء} قال: إذا وقعت النطفة في الأرحام»
[DURR-FULL:v2p143/r7] verses 3:6 | God forms people as male or female, red, white or black, and complete or incomplete in form. «عن قتادة {هو الذي يصوركم في الأرحام كيف يشاء} قال: من ذكر وأنثى وأحمر وأبيض وأسود وتام وغير تام الخلق»
[DURR-FULL:v2p143/r8] verses 3:6 | Al-Aziz describes God as mighty in punishment when He punishes, and al-Hakim as wise in His command. «عن أبي العالية في قوله {العزيز الحكيم} قال: العزيز في نقمته إذا انتقم الحكيم في أمره»
[ELMALILI:3:4-6#3/r10] verses 3:6 | Human formation involves familiar sciences and hidden divine sciences, all applied beyond human observation while people are absent from the womb. «perde-i hafâda bulunan nice ulûm-i ledünniyenin de alâkası bulunduğunu ve sonra bütün bunların sizin gıyâbınızda tatbik edildiğini»
[ELMALILI:3:4-6#3/r11] verses 3:6 | Womb formation combines shared traits linking humanity back to Adam and creation with unique qualities that distinguish each individual. «ta‘ayyün-i şahsî ve temâyüz-i ferdîyi ifade eden ve bir ikincisi bulunmayan suver u keyfiyyât-ı mahsûsa»
[ELMALILI:3:4-6#3/r12] verses 3:6 | Jesus too was formed in a womb and is one of God's creatures, so God should be understood first as the Creator who formed Jesus and every person. «Îsâ dahi onun böyle erhâmda tasvir ettiği sizlerden birisidir, O’nun bir mahlûkudur»
[ELMALILI:3:4-6#3/r13] verses 3:5, 3:6 | Reflection on human formation shows that God knows the unseen and visible, has complete power and will, and is not subject to any external necessity. «O’na gayb u şehadet mâlumdur. Kudret-i bâliğası vardır, aczden münezzehtir, irade ve meşîeti vardır»
[ELMALILI:3:4-6#3/r17] verses 3:6 | God's independent, complete power encompasses every existing and possible thing, from particles and bodies to material and spiritual realities, all of which are subject to Him. «maddiyyât u ma‘neviyyâtıyla hep O’ndandır ve O’na musahhardır»
[ELMALILI:3:4-6#3/r18] verses 3:6 | Nature and its laws are effects of divine power, not masters of it: natural processes are repeated regular effects, while miracles are singular irregular effects. «Tabiat O’nun te’sîrât-ı mütekerrire ve muttaridesi, hilâf-ı tabiat hârikalar da O’nun te’sîrât-ı münferide ve gayr-i muttaridesidir»
[ELMALILI:3:4-6#3/r7] verses 3:6 | God forms people in the womb as He wills, choosing their form and developing their material through successive stages of growth and differentiation. «nasıl dilerse öylece tasvir eder. Hangi surete isterse ona kor»
[ELMALILI:3:4-6#3/r8] verses 3:6 | God composes human organs, tissues, vessels, nerves, senses, and functions into a living person, who may be complete or incomplete, male, female, or intersex. «tam veya nâkıs, erkek veya dişi veya hunsâ, canlı bir nefis suretine ifrağ eder»
[ELMALILI:3:4-6#3/r9] verses 3:6 | Across each stage God adds a new act of creation and transforms and refines humans from one form and quality to another. «her tavırda bir halk-ı cedîd ilâve ederek suretten surete»
[ELMALILI:3:4-6#4/r1] verses 3:6 | The material life-law says every animal emerges from an initially shaped matter, identified as protoplasm. «Her hayvan ilk tasvir edilmiş olan bir maddeden, bir madde-i musavvere-i ûlâ, ‘protoplazma’dan çıkar»
[ELMALILI:3:4-6#4/r10] verses 3:6 | The One who forms people in wombs is the Almighty Creator, possessing the power of the Living, Self-Subsisting One. «insanları erhâmda tasvir eden Hâlık-ı Musavvir böyle bir kudret-i bâliğaya mâlik bir Hayy u Kayyûm’dur»
[ELMALILI:3:4-6#4/r11] verses 3:6 | God acts as a free agent in creation and formation, not under compulsion or necessity; He acts through knowledge and will. «Hâlık-ı Musavvir, bu tekvîn ü tasvîrde mecbur ve muztar değil fâ‘il-i muhtârdır.»
[ELMALILI:3:4-6#4/r12] verses 3:6 | Giving each person a distinct form reflects God's will, not prior conditions, an overriding fate, or inability to choose another form. «Zeyd’e falan sureti, Amr’a fülan sureti vermesi, her şahsı bir ta‘ayyün-i mahsûsa mazhar etmesi»
[ELMALILI:3:4-6#4/r13] verses 3:6 | The succession and replacement of forms is neither necessary nature nor uncaused; it expresses the choice and will of a free agent with knowledge and boundless power. «bir fâ‘il-i muhtârın tercîhi ve iradesi eseridir.»
[ELMALILI:3:4-6#4/r14] verses 3:6 | If forms were necessary, free and varied uses, differing natures, opposing natural laws, sexual variation from one seed, and life itself could not occur. «bir taş, yerinden koparılıp iki muhtelif maksad için isti‘mâl edilemez, tabâyi‘ ihtilâf edemez»
[ELMALILI:3:4-6#4/r15] verses 3:6 | What are called errors of nature are not defects in perfect order and power; they are evidence that nature and parents are not the true agents and that the Creator wills. «bir noksan değil, tabiatın vâlid ve vâlidenin müessir-i hakīkī olmadıklarını ve Hâlık’ın iradesini gösteren şevâhid-i sanattırlar.»
[ELMALILI:3:4-6#4/r16] verses 3:6 | Nature's failures reveal its ignorance and impotence and refute blind necessary regularity as the first principle, thereby proving a perfect free agent. «tabiatın mebde’-i evvel olması nazariyesini ibtal ederek ekmel bir fâ‘il-i muhtârı isbat eden»
[ELMALILI:3:4-6#4/r17] verses 3:6 | Neither particular nor universal nature is a complete cause; both are regularities of an originating creation made by the Creator's power and will. «ne tabî‘at-i cüz’iye ne de tabî‘at-ı külliye»
[ELMALILI:3:4-6#4/r18] verses 3:6 | Natural regularity must be understood under a fuller law of causality and adjusted by the law of will; God can modify the nature He gives creatures. «Ittırâd-ı tabîat kanunu, ekmel bir illiyet kanununun taht-ı tâbi‘iyetine verilerek irade kanunuyla ta‘dîlen mütalaa edilmedikçe hakka erilemez.»
[ELMALILI:3:4-6#4/r19] verses 3:6 | Heredity names the succession of similar but nonidentical forms through changing regularity, and is a route for species survival, selection, development, and decline. «bir ıttırâd-ı mütehavvil ile halef olması aynen bir ıttırad ve intikal gibi mülâhaza edilerek veraset tâbir edilir»
[ELMALILI:3:4-6#4/r2] verses 3:6 | This biological law is created and began only after the earth formed and animals appeared; it is not eternal. «Çünkü arzın teşekkülünden ve üzerinde hayvânâtın zuhûrundan sonra başlamıştır.»
