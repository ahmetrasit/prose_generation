[ELMALILI:18:82/r13] verses 18:82 | One proposed answer is that the boy was an adult, a rebellious unbeliever liable to execution, rather than the innocent child Musa assumed. «onun نَفْسًا زَكِيَّةًۢ diye bir masum zannettiği oğlan sabî değil, bâliğ azgın bir kâfir»

[ELMALILI:18:82/r5] verses 18:82 | God preserved the treasure until the two orphans reached maturity; had the wall fallen earlier, others would have found and taken it. «Bunlar büyümeden duvar yıkılmış olsa idi o defineyi başkaları bulacak ve zâyi‘olacaktı»

[ELMALILI:18:82/r7] verses 18:82 | Because Musa says nothing after the explanation, Elmalılı infers that he saw nothing in it to reject and understood that the apparently harmful acts had hidden reasons and wisdom. «Demek ki Mûsâ’nın zâhiren muzır ve münker gördüğü şeyler hakikatte öyle değil imiş»

[FARRA:v2p157/r4] verses 18:82 | Raḥmatan is accusative as an explanatory maṣdar for the preceding action; inserting huwa or hiya before it reveals the construction. «وقوله (رحمة من ربك) نصب: فعل ذلك رحمة منه. وكل فعل رأيته مفسرا للخبر الذي قبله فهو منصوب.»
[FARRA:v2p157/r5] verses 18:82 | The same explanatory-maṣdar construction appears with faḍlan, tanzīl, and amran; their underlying clauses make the accusative relation clear. «كقوله (فضلا من ربك) وكقوله (إنك لمن المرسلين على صراط مستقيم تنزيل العزيز الرحيم)»

[IBNASHUR-FULL:v16p14/r1] verses 18:82 | Calling the actions mercy and saying they were not Khidr's own initiative removes Moses's objection by revealing their benefit and divine authorization. «وقوله : { رحمة من ربك وما فعلته عن أمري } تصريح بما يزيل إنكار موسى عليه تصرفاته هذه»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v16p14/r2] verses 18:82 | Because prophets act by judgment or revelation, denying that the acts were Khidr's own makes God's command the necessary source; this phrasing resolves Moses's confusion more fully than a direct positive attribution would. «لأنه لما قال { وما فعلته عن أمري } علم موسى أن ذلك بأمر من الله تعالى لأن النبي إنما يتصرف عن اجتهاد أو عن وحي»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v16p14/r3] verses 18:82 | رحمة is accusative of purpose and can modify each of أردت, أردنا, and أراد ربك. «وانتصب { رحمة } على المفعول لأجله فينازعه كل من ( أردت ) ، و ( أردنا ) ، و ( أراد ربك )»
