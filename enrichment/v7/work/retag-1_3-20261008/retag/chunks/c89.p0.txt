[BURSEVI:v5p221#2/r7] verses 18:82 | A sound report from Ibn ‘Abbas says the boys were preserved through their father’s righteousness; if this benefited descendants seven generations later, the Prophet’s pure family has even greater claim to blessing. «وصح عن ابن عباس رضي الله عنهما»

[BURSEVI:v5p221#2/r9] verses 18:82 | In al-‘Ara’is, an Alid whom Harun al-Rashid meant to kill says he prayed, ‘Preserve me through my ancestors as You preserved the treasure for the boys through their father.’ «يا من حفظ الكنز على الصبيين لصلاح أبيهما احفظني لصلاح آبائي»
[BURSEVI:v5p222/r1] verses 18:82 | God preserves a righteous servant’s rightful wealth when its preservation serves a good purpose. «أن الله تعالى يحفظ المال الصالح للعبد الصالح إذا كان فيه صلاح»
[BURSEVI:v5p222/r2] verses 18:82 | Everything involving prophets and saints happens by God’s command inwardly and outwardly: Khidr acted under an explicit command, while Moses objected in keeping with the outward law because he lacked the inward instruction. «أي : فعلته بأمر ربي»
[BURSEVI:v5p222/r3] verses 18:82 | Patience with a shaykh’s actions is difficult; excuse, forgive, and overlook a sincere disciple’s mistakes or objections up to three times, after which separation is excusable and the shaykh should explain the matter. «فليعذره ويعف عنه ويتجاوز إلى ثلاث مرات»
[BURSEVI:v5p222/r4] verses 18:82 | The disciple should inwardly clear the shaykh of suspicion in all his actions; objection poisons discipleship, and the Moses–Khidr story teaches that apparently wrong acts may prove sound when their reasons are disclosed. «ويحذر المريد الاعتراض على الشيخ ويزيل اتهام الشيخ عن باطنه»
[BURSEVI:v5p222/r5] verses 18:82 | Hafez’s couplet advises listening to sincere counsel and accepting it without excuses. «نصيحتي كنمت بشنو وبهانه مكير هر آنكه ناصح مشفق بكويدت بذير»
[BURSEVI:v5p222/r6] verses 18:82 | A spiritual guide should be realized and compassionate, not an unfeeling imitator, lest followers’ efforts be wasted. «وينبغي أن يكون المرشد محققا ومشفقا لا مقلدا غير مشفق»
[BURSEVI:v5p222/r7] verses 18:82 | An Arabic verse warns that if a crow guides people, it will lead them to a place of carrion. «إذا كان الغراب دليل قوم سيهديهم إلى أرض الجياف»
