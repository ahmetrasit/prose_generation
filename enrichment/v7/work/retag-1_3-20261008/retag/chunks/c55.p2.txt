[BIQAI-FULL:v3p121/r3] verses 7:155 | On the second-appointment reading, some of the group may have made sight of God a condition for belief, as in 2:55; this recalls the twelve scouts sent to inspect the giants, most of whom failed, and Moses feared Israel would blame him for the group's deaths. «لما سمعوا كلام الله طلب بعضهم الرؤية جاعليها شرطا لإيمانهم»
[BIQAI-FULL:v3p121/r4] verses 7:155 | Ibn Abbas says these seventy were different from the earlier group who demanded to see God openly and were struck by the thunderbolt; those people came before them. «وعن ابن عباس رضى الله عنهما أن هؤلاء غير السبعين الذين قالوا»

[BIQAI-FULL:v3p122/r1] verses 7:155 | The trial is God's test; misguidance and guidance disclose in the witnessed world what was already known in the unseen. «أي الفتنة التي أوقعها السفهاء { إلا فتنتك } أي ابتلاؤك واختياك { تضل بها من تشاء } أي تظهر في عالم الشهادة من ضلاله ما كان معلوما لك في عالم الغيب»
[BIQAI-FULL:v3p122/r2] verses 7:155 | Moses acknowledges God alone as guardian and source of their welfare; God gains or loses nothing, while pardon benefits them and vengeance harms them, and divine acts are not driven by purposes. «لا يقدر على عمل مصالحنا غيرك , وأنت لا نفع لك في شيء من الأمرين ولا ضر , بل الكل بالنسبة إليك على حد سواء»
[BIQAI-FULL:v3p122/r3] verses 7:155 | Because a guardian brings benefit and removes harm, asking forgiveness means erasing sins and asking mercy means raising them. «فاغفر لنا } أي امح ذنوبنا { وارحمنا } أي ارفعنا»
[BIQAI-FULL:v3p122/r7] verses 7:155 | Moses' apparent objection to destroying the obedient for the sinners is coaxing and entreaty; he knows God's act is just and that God may do as He wills. «على سبيل الاستعطاف منه والتملق مع العلم بأنه عدل منه تعالى وله أن يفعل ما يشاء»

[BURSEVI:v3p188#3/r2] verses 7:155 | A hadith says that whoever does not fear God will be feared by others; al-Sakhawi judges its meaning sound because lacking fear leads to every forbidden and disliked act. «وفي الحديث : "من لم يخف الله خف منه" قال الإمام السخاوي معناه صحيح فإن عدم الخوف»
[BURSEVI:v3p188#3/r3] verses 7:155 | The Mathnawi says fear suits the fearful: God makes the one who fears safe and steadies the fearful heart, while the fearless person needs instruction. «وفي "المثنوي" : لا تخافوا هست نزل خائقان هست درخور ازبراى خائف آن»
