# Notes

## 78:38
[RAZI-FULL:v31p22#2/r6] verses 78:38 | Al-Hasan and Qatada interpret the Spirit as human beings, meaning those possessing a spirit. «وعن الحسن وقتادة هم بنو آدم»
[RAZI-FULL:v31p22#2/r7] verses 78:38 | Another report from Ibn Abbas identifies the Spirit as human souls. «وعن ابن عباس أرواح الناس»
[RAZI-FULL:v31p22#2/r8] verses 78:38 | Al-Dahhak and al-Shabi identify the Spirit as Gabriel. «وعن الضحاك والشعبي هو جبريل عليه السلام»
[RAZI-FULL:v31p22#2/r9] verses 78:38 | Al-Qadi prefers Gabriel: the Quran uses this name for him, and Gabriel can stand, speak, and receive permission, unlike an unknown creature or the Quran itself. «وهذا القول هو المختار عند القاضي»
[SAMIN-DURR:78:38/r1] verses 78:38 | Yawm is accusative either through the later la yatakallamun or through la yamlikun. «منصوب: إما ب " لا يتكلمون " بعده، وإما ب " لا يملكون "»
[SAMIN-DURR:78:38/r2] verses 78:38 | Saffan is a circumstantial accusative meaning that they stand arrayed in rows. «و " صفا " حال، أي: مصطفين»
[SAMIN-DURR:78:38/r3] verses 78:38 | La yatakallamun may be circumstantial or an independent clause. «مصطفين، و " لا يتكلمون ": إما حال وإما مستأنف»
[SAMIN-DURR:78:38/r4] verses 78:38 | The exception illa man udhina is preferably parsed as a substitute for the plural subject marker of yatakallamun, since the clause is negative. «أن يكون بدلا من واو " يتكلمون " ، وهو الأرجح لكونه غير موجب»
[SAMIN-DURR:78:38/r5] verses 78:38 | Alternatively, illa man udhina may be accusative as an ordinary exception. «وأن يكون منصوبا على أصل الاستثناء»
[STUDYQURAN:78:38/r1] verses 78:38 | The Quran leaves the Spirit's identity unsettled, saying that human knowledge of it is limited. «Even the Quran does not give a definitive explanation, responding instead by saying, They ask thee about the Spirit.»
[STUDYQURAN:78:38/r10] verses 78:38 | The permitted speaker may be an intercessor whom God allows to intercede for others, paralleling 2:255. «is interpreted as a reference to those whom God permits to intercede on behalf of others»
[STUDYQURAN:78:38/r11] verses 78:38 | On this reading, the speaker who speaks aright is an intercessor who speaks rightly about those for whom he intercedes. «they will only be able to speak aright about those for whom they intercede (Āl, Q, R).»
[STUDYQURAN:78:38/r12] verses 78:38 | Another interpretation identifies the one who speaks aright as a prophet. «Some maintain that this refers to the prophets (IK).»
[STUDYQURAN:78:38/r13] verses 78:38 | Another interpretation identifies the speaker as someone who professes that there is no god but God. «who speaks aright means those who say,“There is no god but God” (Q).»
[STUDYQURAN:78:38/r14] verses 78:38 | A syntactic alternative ends the exception after God's permission and says that the permitted speaker then necessarily speaks aright. «Some maintain that the verse should be read,“save one whom the Compassionate permits. And he speaks aright,”»
[STUDYQURAN:78:38/r2] verses 78:38 | Al-Ghazzālī observes that the Quran uses “the Spirit” with multiple meanings. «‘The Spirit’ is found in the Quran with many meanings»
[STUDYQURAN:78:38/r3] verses 78:38 | One interpretation identifies the Spirit as the Archangel Gabriel, as in the title Trustworthy Spirit. «some say it refers to the Archangel Gabriel (IK, Q, R)»
[STUDYQURAN:78:38/r4] verses 78:38 | Another interpretation takes the Spirit as God's first creature, which may or may not be Gabriel. «Others say that here the Spirit does not specify a particular angel, but the first creature created by God (Q, R)»
[STUDYQURAN:78:38/r5] verses 78:38 | The angels may be the noblest angels, including the recorders of each person's deeds, or all angels. «responsible for recording the deeds of each individual (see commentary on 82:10–12), or all angels.»
[STUDYQURAN:78:38/r6] verses 78:38 | Ṣaffan, translated “in rows,” can also mean “in a row.” «In rows, translating ṣaffan, could also mean “in a row.”»
[STUDYQURAN:78:38/r7] verses 78:38 | One reading places the Spirit and angels together in one row. «Some read the verse to mean that the Spirit and the angels stand in a single row»
[STUDYQURAN:78:38/r8] verses 78:38 | Another reading has the Spirit standing alone and the angels in a row. «others take it to mean that the Spirit stands alone, while the angels stand in a row (Āl).»
[STUDYQURAN:78:38/r9] verses 78:38 | Some read the angels as standing in many rows, as in the description of 89:22. «some read ṣaffan to mean that the angels stand in many rows (Āl, R).»
[SULAMI:v2p369#2/r1] verses 78:38 | Permission to speak is a gift of God's generosity; the permitted speaker speaks in agreement with truth only to the degree of his knowledge. «قال : لما كان إليهم من بره فمن كان مأذون في الكلام كان موافقا على قدر علمه»
[SULAMI:v2p369#2/r2] verses 78:38 | Abū ʿUthmān says correct, truthful speech marks one permitted to speak, while faults, slips, or lies show that the speaker lacks permission. «قال أبو عثمان : علامة المأذون له في الكلام : صوابا قوله وصدقه»
[TAB-FULL:v24p47/r1] verses 78:38 | Ibn Masʿūd describes the Spirit as an angel in the fourth heaven, greater than the heavens, mountains, and angels, who glorifies God twelve thousand times daily and comes alone in a row. «عن ابن مسعود، قال: الروح ملك في السماء الرابعة، هو أعظم من السموات ومن الجبال ومن الملائكة»
[TAB-FULL:v24p47/r2] verses 78:38 | He identifies the Spirit as the greatest of the angels in creation. «عن ابن عباس، قوله: {يوم يقوم الروح والملائكة} [النبأ: 38] قال: هو ملك أعظم الملائكة خلقا»
[TAB-FULL:v24p47/r3] verses 78:38 | He identifies the Spirit as Gabriel. «عن الضحاك، {يوم يقوم الروح} [النبأ: 38] قال: جبريل عليه السلام»
[TAB-FULL:v24p47/r4] verses 78:38 | He identifies the Spirit as Gabriel. «عن الشعبي، {يوم يقوم الروح} [النبأ: 38] قال: الروح جبريل عليه السلام»
[TAB-FULL:v24p47/r5] verses 78:38 | He describes the Spirit as a human-shaped created kind that eats and drinks, and elsewhere likens it to Adam's creation. «عن مجاهد، قال {الروح} [النبأ: 38] خلق على صورة بني آدم، يأكلون ويشربون»
[TAB-FULL:v24p47/r6] verses 78:38 | He says these beings have hands, feet, perhaps heads, eat food, and are not angels. «عن مجاهد، قال {الروح} [النبأ: 38] خلق لهم أيد وأرجل، وأراه قال: ورءوس، يأكلون الطعام، ليسوا ملائكة»
[TAB-FULL:v24p47/r7] verses 78:38 | He says the Spirit-beings resemble people but are not people. «عن أبي صالح، قال: يشبهون الناس، وليسوا بالناس»
[TAB-FULL:v24p47/r8] verses 78:38 | He describes the Spirit as created beings vastly outnumbering the angels, with hands and feet. «عن الأعمش، في قوله: {يوم يقوم الروح والملائكة صفا} [النبأ: 38] قال: الروح خلق من خلق الله يضعفون على الملائكة أضعافا، لهم أيد وأرجل»
[TAB-FULL:v24p49/r1] verses 78:38 | He describes the Spirit as created beings like humans, but not humans. «عن أبي صالح مولى أم هانئ {يوم يقوم الروح والملائكة} [النبأ: 38] قال: " الروح: خلق كالناس، وليسوا بالناس "»
[TAB-FULL:v24p49/r2] verses 78:38 | They identify the Spirit as human beings, the children of Adam. «عن قتادة، {يوم يقوم الروح} [النبأ: 38] قال: هم بنو آدم وهو قول الحسن»
[TAB-FULL:v24p49/r3] verses 78:38 | He identifies the Spirit as human souls rising with the angels between the two blasts, before returning to their bodies. «عن ابن عباس، قوله: {يوم يقوم الروح والملائكة صفا لا يتكلمون} [النبأ: 38] قال: يعني حين تقوم أرواح الناس مع الملائكة»
[TAB-FULL:v24p49/r4] verses 78:38 | Ibn Zayd transmits his father's view that the Spirit is the Quran, citing the revelation called a spirit from God's command. «قال ابن زيد، كان أبي يقول: الروح: القرآن، وقرأ {وكذلك أوحينا إليك روحا من أمرنا ما كنت تدري ما الكتاب ولا الإيمان} [الشورى: 52]»
[TAB-FULL:v24p49/r5] verses 78:38 | The Spirit is certainly one of God's created beings, but no reliable report or proof establishes which proposed identity is intended; ignorance of it causes no harm. «والروح: خلق من خلقه. وجائز أن يكون بعض هذه الأشياء التي ذكرت، والله أعلم أي ذلك هو؟»
[TAB-FULL:v24p49/r6] verses 78:38 | He says the Spirit and angels stand as two ranks before the Lord on Judgment Day. «عن الشعبي، في قوله: {يوم يقوم الروح والملائكة صفا لا يتكلمون إلا من أذن له الرحمن} [النبأ: 38] قال: هما سماطان لرب العالمين»
[TAB-FULL:v24p51/r1] verses 78:38 | He describes angels asking where people are being taken; they affirm that the damned earned their fate and that the saved enter by God's mercy, then are permitted to speak. «عن عكرمة، وقرأ، هذه الآية: {إلا من أذن له الرحمن وقال صوابا} [النبأ: 38] قال: يمر بأناس من أهل النار على ملائكة»
[TAB-FULL:v24p51/r2] verses 78:38 | He says permission is for the testimony of God's oneness, “There is no deity but God,” the utmost truthful word. «عن ابن عباس، في قوله: {إلا من أذن له الرحمن وقال صوابا} [النبأ: 38] يقول: إلا من أذن له الرب بشهادة أن لا إله إلا الله»
[TAB-FULL:v24p51/r3] verses 78:38 | He says the truthful word is truth in worldly life, followed by acting upon it. «عن مجاهد، {وقال صوابا} [النبأ: 38] قال حقا في الدنيا، وعمل به»
[TAB-FULL:v24p51/r4] verses 78:38 | He interprets the truthful word as “There is no deity but God.” «عن أبي صالح، في قوله: {إلا من أذن له الرحمن وقال صوابا} [النبأ: 38] قال: لا إله إلا الله»
