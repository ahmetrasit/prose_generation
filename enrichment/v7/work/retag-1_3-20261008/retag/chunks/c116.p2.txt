[TUSTARI:36:11/r1] verses 36:11 | Worshipping God in secret begets certainty; worshipping with a truthful tongue makes the heart reach toward the Throne; worshipping with fairness places heaven and earth in one's balance. «من عبدالله في سره أورثه اليقين، ومن عبدالله بصدق اللسان لم يستقر قلبه دون العرش»
