[KASHSHAF-FULL:v2p654/r5] verses 17:110 | The conjunction offers a choice of either name; tanwin in ayyā replaces its genitive complement and mā emphasizes the generality of which name is used. «وأو للتخيير ، فمعنى { ادعوا الله أو ادعوا الرحمان } سموا بهذا الاسم أو بهذا»

[KASHSHAF-FULL:v2p654/r7] verses 17:110 | Prayer here means its recitation by ellipsis; the Prophet raised his voice and pagans abused him, so he was told to lower it enough to avoid them but not hide it from followers. «بصلاتك بقراءة صلاتك على حذف المضاف ؛ لأنه لا يلبس»
