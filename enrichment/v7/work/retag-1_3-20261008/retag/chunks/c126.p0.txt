# Notes

## 40:7
[RAZI-FULL:v27p33/r2] verses 40:7 | The philosophers reconcile creation’s merciful purpose with divine decree of every good and evil by saying good is intended and approved essentially, while evil is intended but disliked and decreed incidentally. «قالت الحكماء : الخير مراد مرضي ، والشر مراد مكروه ، والخير مقضي به بالذات ، والشر مقضي به باللعرض»
[RAZI-FULL:v27p33/r3] verses 40:7 | The verse affirms that God knows every item among the unbounded universals and particulars. «يدل على كونه سبحانه عالما بجميع المعلومات التي لا نهاية لها من الكليات والجزئيات»
[RAZI-FULL:v27p33/r4] verses 40:7 | If any things could fall outside God’s knowledge, a supplicant could not know whether God knew or heard the prayer, leaving supplication without benefit. «فلولا ذلك لم يكن في الدعاء والتضرع فائدة لأنه إذا جاز أن يخرج عن علمه بعض الأشياء»
[SAMIN-DURR:40:7/r1] verses 40:7 | الذين يحملون is the subject and يسبحون its predicate. «{ الذين يحملون }: مبتدأ " ويسبحون " خبره.»
[SAMIN-DURR:40:7/r2] verses 40:7 | The common reading has fatḥa on the ʿayn of العرش; Ibn Abbas and others use ḍamma, possibly as a plural of عرش like سقف. «والعامة على فتح عين " العرش ". وابن عباس في آخرين بضمها فقيل: يحتمل أن يكون جمعا»
[SAMIN-DURR:40:7/r3] verses 40:7 | من حوله most naturally coordinates in the nominative with الذين يحملون, so both groups are said to glorify God. «مرفوع المحل عطفا على " الذين يحملون " أخبر عن الفريقين بأنهم يسبحون، وهذا هو الظاهر»
[SAMIN-DURR:40:7/r4] verses 40:7 | Alternatively, من حوله is accusative by coordination with العرش, meaning angels around it are also borne; this is called unlikely. «وأن يكون منصوب المحل عطفا على العرش، يعني أنهم يحملون أيضا الملائكة الحافين بالعرش. وليس بظاهر.»
[SAMIN-DURR:40:7/r5] verses 40:7 | ربنا depends on implied يقولون; the omitted saying is circumstantial of يستغفرون's subject or a successive predicate. «قوله: " ربنا " / معمول لقول مضمر تقديره: يقولون ربنا.»
[SAMIN-DURR:40:7/r6] verses 40:7 | رحمة وعلماً are specification accusatives transferred from subject position: God's mercy and knowledge encompass everything. «و " رحمة وعلما " تمييز منقول من الفاعلية، أي: وسع كل شيء رحمتك وعلمك.»
[STUDYQURAN:40:7/r1] verses 40:7 | The bearers of the Throne are eight angels, as stated in 69:17. «Those who bear the Throne are eight angels, as in 69:17»
[STUDYQURAN:40:7/r2] verses 40:7 | The bearers may be the same angels who encircle the Throne in 39:75 or a distinct group among them. «These may be the same as the angels encircling all around the Throne (39:75) or a distinct group among them.»
[STUDYQURAN:40:7/r3] verses 40:7 | The Throne is where God’s Commands and Decrees issue and from where He directs the cosmos. «the meaning of the Throne, from which God’s Commands and Decrees issue and from which He directs the cosmos (Ṭb)»
[STUDYQURAN:40:7/r4] verses 40:7 | The prayers of the highest angels show the difference between believers and disbelievers and reassure the Prophet and his followers amid disbelievers’ disputes. «show the great difference between the believers and the disbelievers, whose fate is mentioned in the previous verse (R)»
[STUDYQURAN:40:7/r5] verses 40:7 | Scripture attests that God encompasses all things in mercy and in knowledge. «That God encompasses all things in Mercy is attested in 7:156; that He encompasses all things in knowledge is also attested in 6:80»
[STUDYQURAN:40:7/r6] verses 40:7 | Some interpret the sentence’s structure to mean that God’s Mercy precedes or outstrips His Knowledge. «Some understand the structure of this sentence to indicate that God’s Mercy precedes or outstrips His Knowledge (R, Ṭb)»
[STUDYQURAN:40:7/r7] verses 40:7 | The priority of divine Mercy is a principle in Islamic metaphysics and cosmology. «for the priority and principiality of God’s Mercy in Islamic metaphysics and cosmology»
[STUDYQURAN:40:7/r8] verses 40:7 | Here the angels seek forgiveness for believers; elsewhere they seek forgiveness for people on earth. «Here the angels seek forgiveness for those who believe; elsewhere they seek forgiveness for those on earth (42:5).»
[STUDYQURAN:40:7/r9] verses 40:7 | The verse connects intercession and seeking forgiveness with 2:48 and 2:255, and shielding from Hellfire with 44:56. «For further discussion of seeking forgiveness for others»
[STUDYQURAN:40:intro/r7] verses 40:2, 40:3, 40:4, 40:5, 40:6, 40:7, 40:8, 40:9 | The opening affirms revelation and divine mercy and forgiveness, rejects disputants over God’s signs, and presents the angels’ extended prayer for human beings. «The sūrah opens with an affirmation of the revelation and of God’s Mercy and Forgiveness»
[SULAMI:v2p207/r1] verses 40:7 | Sahl identifies the Throne-bearers and those around it as people who repented from heedlessness, became intimate with remembrance, and followed the Prophet's Sunna. «قال سهل : هم الذين تابوا من الغفلة وانسوا بالذكر واتبعوا سنة المصطفى صلى الله عليه وسلم»
[TAB-FULL:v20p283/r1] verses 40:7 | The bearers of God’s Throne and the surrounding angels are angels who encircle it. «يحف به من الملائكة {يسبحون بحمد ربهم} [الزمر: 75]»
[TAB-FULL:v20p283/r10] verses 40:7 | The second grammarian argues that measured quantities are definite, whereas mithl is indefinite despite its definite form; this accounts for accusative “عبدا,” and mithl can likewise be used indefinitely. «فإن المقادير لا تكون إلا معلومة مثل عندي رطل زيتا، والمثل غير معلوم، ولكن لفظه لفظ المعرفة والعبد نكرة»
[TAB-FULL:v20p283/r11] verses 40:7 | The cited verse uses قحطان مثلك واحد معدود to support the analysis of مثل as indefinite, alongside the grammarian’s examples ما مثلك رجل and مثلك رجلا. «ما في معد والقبائل كلها … قحطان مثلك واحد معدود»
[TAB-FULL:v20p283/r2] verses 40:7 | Their glorification is prayer to their Lord accompanied by praise and thanks. «يقول: يصلون لربهم بحمده وشكره»
[TAB-FULL:v20p283/r3] verses 40:7 | Their faith means affirming and testifying that God alone is deity, without arrogance toward worship. «ويقرون بالله أنه لا إله لهم سواه، ويشهدون بذلك، لا يستكبرون عن عبادته»
[TAB-FULL:v20p283/r4] verses 40:7 | They ask God to forgive the believers who share their monotheism and disavow every other object of worship, erasing their sins. «ويسألون ربهم أن يغفر للذين أقروا بمثل إقرارهم من توحيد الله، والبراءة من كل معبود سواه ذنوبهم، فيعفوها عنهم»
[TAB-FULL:v20p283/r5] verses 40:7 | Qatadah identifies the believers for whom the angels seek forgiveness as the people who say “there is no god but God.” «عن قتادة، قوله: {ويستغفرون للذين آمنوا} [غافر: 7] «لأهل لا أله إلا الله»»
[TAB-FULL:v20p283/r6] verses 40:7 | The phrase “Our Lord” follows an omitted “they say”; the angels are asking, “Our Lord, You encompass all things in mercy and knowledge.” «وفي هذا الكلام محذوف، وهو يقولون؛ ومعنى الكلام ويستغفرون للذين آمنوا يقولون: يا ربنا»
[TAB-FULL:v20p283/r7] verses 40:7 | God’s knowledge reaches everything and nothing is hidden from Him; His mercy encompasses all creation. «وسعت رحمتك وعلمك كل شيء من خلقك، فعلمت كل شيء، فلم يخف عليك شيء، ورحمت خلقك»
[TAB-FULL:v20p283/r8] verses 40:7 | A Basran grammarian treats “mercy and knowledge” as accusative explanatory terms after the verb has already taken “everything” as its object, by analogy with “لك مثله عبدا.” «فقال بعض نحويي البصرة: انتصاب ذلك كانتصاب لك مثله عبدا»
[TAB-FULL:v20p283/r9] verses 40:7 | Another grammarian calls the construction transposed: God’s mercy and knowledge encompassed everything, with “mercy” as an explanatory accusative, analogous to “طابت به نفسي” and “طبت به نفسا.” «وقال غيره: هو من المنقول، وهو مفسر، وسعت رحمته وعلمه، ووسع هو كل شيء رحمة»
[TAB-FULL:v20p285#2/r1] verses 40:7 | Following God’s path means taking the way He commanded and adhering to His prescribed way, namely entering Islam. «عن قتادة {واتبعوا سبيلك} [غافر: 7] أي طاعتك»
[TAB-FULL:v20p285#2/r2] verses 40:7 | Qatadah glosses following God’s path as obedience to Him. «عن قتادة {واتبعوا سبيلك} [غافر: 7] أي طاعتك»
[TAB-FULL:v20p285/r1] verses 40:7 | The angels ask God to pardon the wrongdoing of those who turned from associating partners with Him to monotheism and obedience to His commands. «عن قتادة {فاغفر للذين تابوا} [غافر: 7] «من الشرك»»
[TAB-FULL:v20p285/r2] verses 40:7 | Qatadah says these are people who repented from shirk. «عن قتادة {فاغفر للذين تابوا} [غافر: 7] «من الشرك»»
