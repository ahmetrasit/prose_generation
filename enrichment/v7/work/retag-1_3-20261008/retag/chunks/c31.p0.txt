- 4:1: يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ ٱتَّقُوا۟ رَبَّكُمُ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَكُم مِّن نَّفْسٍۢ وَٰحِدَةٍۢ وَخَلَقَ مِنْهَا زَوْجَهَا وَبَثَّ مِنْهُمَا رِجَالًۭا كَثِيرًۭا وَنِسَآءًۭ ۚ وَٱتَّقُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ ٱلَّذِى تَسَآءَلُونَ بِهِۦ وَٱلْأَرْحَامَ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَلَيْكُمْ رَقِيبًۭا
- 4:3: وَإِنْ خِفْتُمْ أَلَّا تُقْسِطُوا۟ فِى ٱلْيَتَٰمَىٰ فَٱنكِحُوا۟ مَا طَابَ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلنِّسَآءِ مَثْنَىٰ وَثُلَٰثَ وَرُبَٰعَ ۖ فَإِنْ خِفْتُمْ أَلَّا تَعْدِلُوا۟ فَوَٰحِدَةً أَوْ مَا مَلَكَتْ أَيْمَٰنُكُمْ ۚ ذَٰلِكَ أَدْنَىٰٓ أَلَّا تَعُولُوا۟
- 4:6: وَٱبْتَلُوا۟ ٱلْيَتَٰمَىٰ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا بَلَغُوا۟ ٱلنِّكَاحَ فَإِنْ ءَانَسْتُم مِّنْهُمْ رُشْدًۭا فَٱدْفَعُوٓا۟ إِلَيْهِمْ أَمْوَٰلَهُمْ ۖ وَلَا تَأْكُلُوهَآ إِسْرَافًۭا وَبِدَارًا أَن يَكْبَرُوا۟ ۚ وَمَن كَانَ غَنِيًّۭا فَلْيَسْتَعْفِفْ ۖ وَمَن كَانَ فَقِيرًۭا فَلْيَأْكُلْ بِٱلْمَعْرُوفِ ۚ فَإِذَا دَفَعْتُمْ إِلَيْهِمْ أَمْوَٰلَهُمْ فَأَشْهِدُوا۟ عَلَيْهِمْ ۚ وَكَفَىٰ بِٱللَّهِ حَسِيبًۭا
- 4:7: لِّلرِّجَالِ نَصِيبٌۭ مِّمَّا تَرَكَ ٱلْوَٰلِدَانِ وَٱلْأَقْرَبُونَ وَلِلنِّسَآءِ نَصِيبٌۭ مِّمَّا تَرَكَ ٱلْوَٰلِدَانِ وَٱلْأَقْرَبُونَ مِمَّا قَلَّ مِنْهُ أَوْ كَثُرَ ۚ نَصِيبًۭا مَّفْرُوضًۭا
- 4:9: وَلْيَخْشَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَوْ تَرَكُوا۟ مِنْ خَلْفِهِمْ ذُرِّيَّةًۭ ضِعَٰفًا خَافُوا۟ عَلَيْهِمْ فَلْيَتَّقُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ وَلْيَقُولُوا۟ قَوْلًۭا سَدِيدًا
- 4:10: إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَأْكُلُونَ أَمْوَٰلَ ٱلْيَتَٰمَىٰ ظُلْمًا إِنَّمَا يَأْكُلُونَ فِى بُطُونِهِمْ نَارًۭا ۖ وَسَيَصْلَوْنَ سَعِيرًۭا

# Notes

## 4:1
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p497/r2] verses 4:1 | The shared sense of avoiding violation reconciles the different applications and answers the objection that one word cannot carry different meanings; kinship’s coordination with God highlights the gravity of severing ties. «وفي عطف الأرحام على اسم الله دلالة على عظم ذنب قطع الرحم»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p497/r3] verses 4:1 | Other scriptural and prophetic examples emphasize kindness to parents and relatives and the duty to maintain what God commanded to be joined. «ويقطعون ما أمر الله به أن يوصل»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p497/r4] verses 4:1 | Another accusative parse attaches الأرْحام to the implied object of the preposition به, supported by Ibn Masud’s reading with a repeated preposition. «وقيل: النصب عطفا على موضع به»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p497/r5] verses 4:1 | The nominative can begin a sentence with an omitted predicate: Ibn Atiyya supplies “kin deserve to be joined,” while Zamakhshari supplies that they are to be revered or invoked; Abu Hayyan prefers the latter as closer to the wording. «وقدره الزمخشري: والأرحام مما يتقى، أو مما يتساءل به»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p497/r6] verses 4:1 | They explain the genitive reading as coordination with the attached pronoun in به without repeating the preposition, supported by the reading وبالأرحام. «وعلى هذا فسرها الحسن والنخعي ومجاهد»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p497/r7] verses 4:1 | The wording also reflects people invoking God and kinship together when making appeals. «وكانوا يتناشدون بذكر الله والرحم»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p498/r1] verses 4:1 | Zamakhshari rejects attaching الأرْحام to the bound pronoun without repeating the preposition, arguing that the pronoun and preposition form a tightly joined unit. «قال الزمخشري: وليس بسديد يعني: الجر عطفا على الضمير»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p498/r2] verses 4:1 | Ibn Atiyya says leading Basran grammarians disallow coordinating an overt noun with a genitive pronoun; Sibawayh permits it only in poetry, though some grammarians accept it. «وهذه القراءة عند رؤساء نحويين البصرة لا تجوز»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p498/r3] verses 4:1 | Abu Hayyan says al-Mazani’s analogy fails because رأيتك وزيدا is accepted although رأيت زيداوك is not. «وتعليل المازني معترض بأنه يجوز أن تقول: رأيتك وزيدا»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p498/r4] verses 4:1 | Ibn Atiyya further argues that a genitive pronoun cannot separate from its preposition and therefore behaves like part of a word. «المضمر المخفوض لا ينفصل، فهو كحرف من الكلمة»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p499/r1] verses 4:1 | Ibn Atiyya objects that the genitive reading contributes no useful exhortation and implies swearing by kinship; he cites the prohibition on swearing by anything but God. «من كان حالفا فليحلف بالله أو ليصمت»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p499/r2] verses 4:1 | Some take the wāw as an oath particle rather than coordination, making the following clause the oath’s response and treating kinship as honored by God. «وذهبت طائفة إلى أن الواو في والأرحام واو القسم»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p499/r3] verses 4:1 | Abu Hayyan endorses the Kufan rule permitting coordination to a genitive pronoun without repeating the preposition and rejects the Basran restriction as unsound. «بل الصحيح مذهب الكوفيين في ذلك وأنه يجوز»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p499/r4] verses 4:1 | He sharply rebukes Ibn Atiyya for rejecting a mutawatir reading transmitted from the Prophet through senior Companions and the leading reciters. «عمد إلى ردها بشيء خطر له في ذهنه»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p499/r5] verses 4:1 | Abu Hayyan defends Hamza’s integrity and transmission, says readers need not follow Basran grammarians, and insists Arabic usage exceeds what any one school transmitted. «ولسنا متعبدين بقول نحاة البصرة ولا غيرهم ممن خالفهم»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p500/r1] verses 4:1 | Allah’s being ever watchful is not restricted to past time; He continually observes and knows everything about people. «فهو تعالى رقيب في الماضي وغيره علينا»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p531/r1] verses 4:1 | The passage uses antithesis between one and its mate, rich and poor, and few and many. «الطباق في: واحدة وزوجها، وفي غنيا وفقيرا،»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p531/r14] verses 4:1, 4:10 | The passage uses ellipsis in several places. «والحذف في عدة مواضع»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v3p531/r2] verses 4:1, 4:3, 4:6, 4:7, 4:9 | It repeats key words and meanings: fear, creation, orphan, women, inheritance, and guarding against injustice. «والتكرار في: اتقوا، وفي: خلق، وفي: خفتم، وأن لا تقسطوا،»
[AKHFASH:v1p243/r1] verses 4:1 | The light form تساءلون deletes the final tāʾ from تساؤلهم; this deletion is common in Arabic, as in تكلمون, and a heavy form with assimilation is also possible. «قال تعالى {تسآءلون به} خفيفة لأنها من تساؤلهم فانههم "يتساءلون" فحذف التاء الأخيرة، وذلك كثير في كلام العرب نحو {تكلمون} وان شئت ثقلت فادغمت.»
[AKHFASH:v1p243/r2] verses 4:1 | He parses والأرحام as accusative, “and fear the ties of kinship,” because an explicit noun should not be coordinated with a genitive attached pronoun without repeating the preposition. «قال الله تعالى {والأرحام} منصوبة أي: اتقوا الأرحام. وقال بعضهم {والأرحام} جر. والأول أحسن لأنك لا تجري الظاهر المجرور على المضمر المجرور.»
[AKHFASH:v1p243/r3] verses 4:1 | Some parse والأرحام as genitive, coordinated with the pronoun in تساءلون به; al-Akhfash prefers the accusative analysis. «وقال بعضهم {والأرحام} جر. والأول أحسن»
[AKHFASH:v1p243/r4] verses 4:1 | The verb family of رقيب includes رقب يرقب رقبا ورقوبا. «تقول من "الرقيب": "رقب" "يرقب" "رقبا" و"رقوبا".»
[ALUSI-FULL:v4p178#2/r1] verses 4:1, 3:200 | Nisa is Medinan according to the correct view; al-Nahhas calls it Meccan based on the Meccan revelation of the command to return the Kaaba key, but al-Suyuti says that does not determine the whole surah’s provenance. «( مدنية على الصحيح وزعم النحاس أنها مكية مستندا إلى أن قوله تعالى»
[ALUSI-FULL:v4p178#2/r2] verses 4:1, 3:200 | Al-Suyuti argues that a Meccan verse within a long surah, mostly revealed in Medina, does not make that surah Meccan; material after the Hijra is generally Medinan. «لأنه لا يلزم من نزول آية أو آيات بمكة من سورة طويلة نزل معظمها بالمدينة»
[ALUSI-FULL:v4p178#2/r3] verses 4:1, 3:200 | Aisha’s report in al-Bukhari that Baqara and Nisa were revealed while she was with the Prophet, after their post-Hijra marriage, supports Nisa’s Medinan classification. «ما أخرجه البخاري عن عائشة رضي الله تعالى عنها قالت»
[ALUSI-FULL:v4p178#2/r4] verses 4:1, 3:200 | Another view says the surah was revealed around the time of the Hijra. «وقيل : إنها نزلت عند الهجرة»
[ALUSI-FULL:v4p178#2/r5] verses 4:1, 3:200 | The Syrian count is 177 verses, the Kufan count 176, and other counts 175; authorities differ on whether two clauses are separate verses. «وعدة آياتها عند الشاميين مائة وسبع وسبعون وعند الكوفيين ست وسبعون»
[ALUSI-FULL:v4p178#2/r6] verses 4:1, 3:200 | Nisa opens with taqwa after Ali ʿImran ends with it, a strong rhetorical link between surahs known as similarity of endings or tassbigh. «آل عمران ختمت بالأمر بالتقوى وأفتتحت هذه السورة به وذلك من آكد وجوه المناسبات»
[ALUSI-FULL:v4p178#2/r7] verses 4:1, 3:200 | Other links are that Nisa refers to the aftermath of Uhud, follows Ali ʿImran’s mention of the expedition after Uhud, and develops in detail themes stated earlier; this ordering is better than that in Ibn Masud’s codex. «في آل عمران ذكر قصة أحد مستوفاة وفي هذه السورة ذكر ذيلها»
[ALUSI-FULL:v4p179#2/r1] verses 4:1 | Some include earlier peoples in the address and in the coming command in God’s eternal speech, though its Arabic form is incidental to this community; the author objects that the discussion concerns Quranic rulings after revelation, or all direct address would be metaphorical. «وجوز بعضهم كون الخطاب عاما بحيث يندرجون فيه ثم قال : ولا يبعد»
[ALUSI-FULL:v4p179#2/r10] verses 4:1 | A view that the command addresses men alone and includes women only through external evidence would require restricting الناس, which the author says sound taste rejects. «وزعم بعضهم أن لا تغليب بل الأمر للرجال فقط كما يقتضيه ظاهر الصيغة»
[ALUSI-FULL:v4p179#2/r11] verses 4:1 | The command to practice taqwa may broadly mean avoiding disbelief, sins, and vices while honoring both human and divine rights. «والمأمور به إما الإتقاء بحيث يشمل ما كان بإجتناب الكفر والمعاصي»
[ALUSI-FULL:v4p179#2/r2] verses 4:1 | The word الناس includes males and females by agreement, but whether a masculine plural command such as اتقوا ربكم includes women is disputed; most say it does not apparently, unlike the Hanbalis. «وفي شمول نحو قوله تعالى : ( أتقوا ربكن ( خلاف»
