# Notes

## 6:12
[IBNQUTAYBA-GHARIB:v1p151#3/r2] verses 6:12 | The clause about those who lost themselves refers back to the command to travel and observe the outcome of the deniers. «هذا مردود إلى قوله: {قل سيروا في الأرض ثم انظروا كيف كان عاقبة المكذبين}»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-3-surah-anam-06:p13/r1] verses 6:12 | The coming passage presents arguments for monotheism, the Hereafter, and prophethood through new styles and aspects. «arguments in favour of monotheism, hereafter and prophethood are presented in some new styles with some new aspects.»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-3-surah-anam-06:p15/r1] verses 6:12 | Qur’anic questions answered without waiting signal that the addressees cannot deny the answer; contrary beliefs or actions conflict with what they acknowledge. «on such instances the addressees are no position to deny it.»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-3-surah-anam-06:p15/r2] verses 6:12 | Answering immediately also shows that truth must be stated despite the addressees’ stubbornness. «the truth must be told regardless of the stubbornness the addressees may show in response.»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-3-surah-anam-06:p15/r3] verses 6:12 | Although the Arabs practiced idolatry, they still acknowledged God as creator of the heavens and earth. «the Arabs regarded God to be the creator of the heavens and the earth»
[JISHUMI:v3p2168/r10] verses 6:12 | The lām in liman marks ownership, while the lām of layajmaʿannakum marks an oath and its nūn is emphatic. «اللام في قوله: لمن لام الملك، وفي قوله: ليجمعنكم لام القسم، والنون فيه نون التأكيد»
[JISHUMI:v3p2168/r11] verses 6:12 | One parsing gives layajmaʿannakum no syntactic position, taking it as a fresh oath clause. «قيل: لا موضع له على تقدير الاستئناف»
[JISHUMI:v3p2168/r12] verses 6:12 | Al-Zajjāj instead treats the second oath clause as accusative under kataba, explaining mercy as delaying judgment until the Resurrection. «والقسم الثاني يكون موضعه نصبا ب كتب كأن في قوله: كتب على نفسه الرحمة، وذلك أنه تفسير الرحمة بالإمهال»
[JISHUMI:v3p2168/r13] verses 6:12 | Al-Akhfash parses alladhīna khasirū as an accusative apposition to the pronoun in layajmaʿannakum. «قيل: محله نصب على البدل من الضمير في ليجمعنكم عن الأخفش»
[JISHUMI:v3p2168/r14] verses 6:12 | Al-Zajjāj instead parses alladhīna khasirū as a nominative subject whose predicate is fa-hum lā yuʾminūn. «وقيل: موضعه رفع بالابتداء، وخبره: فهم لا يؤمنون؛ لأنه يجمع الجميع إلى يوم القيامة، عن الزجاج»
[JISHUMI:v3p2168/r15] verses 6:12 | The passage returns to promise, warning, and proof of God's power: God owns, creates, and preserves the heavens and earth. «ثم عاد الكلام إلى الوعد والوعيد والدلالة على قدرته تعالى»
[JISHUMI:v3p2168/r16] verses 6:12 | If the unbelievers do not answer, the Prophet is told to answer that ownership belongs to God; ownership and creation make worship due to Him and He controls as He wills. «فقل أنت: لله؛ أي ملكهما له، والتصرف كيف يشاء، فنبه بأن العبادة تحق لمن له ملكهما وخلقهما»
[JISHUMI:v3p2168/r3] verses 6:12 | The verb kataba belongs to the sense of gathering, and kitāb can mean decree. «كتبت الكتاب أكتبه، وهو من الجمع، والكتاب: القدر»
[JISHUMI:v3p2168/r4] verses 6:12 | Al-Jaʿdī's verse uses kitāb Allāh to mean God's decree or fate. «قال الجعدي: يا بنت عمي كتاب الله أخرجني ... عنكم وهل أمنعن الله ما فعلا»
[JISHUMI:v3p2168/r5] verses 6:12 | Katb also means sewing because it joins things; a gathered troop is called a katība. «والكتب: الخرز؛ لأنه يجمع واحدها: كتبة، ومنه قيل للقطعة المجتمعة من الجيش: كتيبة»
[JISHUMI:v3p2168/r6] verses 6:12 | Khus r and khusrān mean loss of capital, and the verb can mean to reduce or lose in a sale. «والخسر والخسران: ذهاب رأس المال، نحو: كفر وكفران»
[JISHUMI:v3p2169/r1] verses 6:12 | Asking first prompts the mind to seek an answer, making the answer more firmly established when it follows. «لأن السؤال يحث النفس على الطلب، فيكون الجواب بعده أشد تقريرا في النفس»
[JISHUMI:v3p2169/r10] verses 6:12 | Another reading takes ilā to mean fī: God will gather people within the Resurrection Day. «وقيل: إلى بمعنى (في)؛ أي يجمعنكم في يوم القيامة»
[JISHUMI:v3p2169/r11] verses 6:12 | A third analysis supplies the omitted gathering place, since a gathering is toward a place. «وقيل: فيه حذف: أي إلى المحشر يوم القيامة؛ لأن الجمع يكون إلى مكان»
[JISHUMI:v3p2169/r12] verses 6:12 | Another interpretation says God gathers people in worldly generations in succession up to the Resurrection. «وقيل: ليجمعنكم في الدنيا قرنا بعد قرن إلى يوم القيامة»
[JISHUMI:v3p2169/r13] verses 6:12 | The resurrection warning follows the proof already given; other views take it as a statement of continuance or as address to believers. «قلنا: ذكر عقيب الدلالة، وقيل: هو الدوام، وقيل: هو خطاب للمؤمنين»
[JISHUMI:v3p2169/r14] verses 6:12 | Lā rayba fīhi means there is no doubt in the Resurrection. «لا ريب فيه أي لا شك يعني في البعث»
[JISHUMI:v3p2169/r15] verses 6:12 | The truth remains true even if a false claimant doubts it; another view says the proofs remove doubt. «الحق حق وإن ارتاب فيه المبطل، وقيل: الدلائل أزالت الشك»
[JISHUMI:v3p2169/r16] verses 6:12 | Since worldly benefits reach both the righteous and the wicked, another realm must distinguish between them. «ولأن نعم الدنيا تعم المحسن والمسيء فلابد من دار تميز»
[JISHUMI:v3p2169/r17] verses 6:12 | Because obligation entails recompense, if recompense does not occur in this world it must occur in another. «ولأن التكليف يتضمن الثواب فإذا لم يكن في الدنيا فلا بد من دار»
[JISHUMI:v3p2169/r18] verses 6:12 | Worldly injustice, illness, and harm without deserved cause or immediate compensation require a later realm that pays dues and vindicates the wronged. «ولأن التمكين من الظلم من غير انتصاف في العاجل وإنزال الأمراض من غير استحقاق»
[JISHUMI:v3p2169/r19] verses 6:12 | Those who lose themselves ruin them through disbelief and sin, so they do not affirm the truth. «الذين خسروا أنفسهم أي أهلكوها بارتكاب الكفر والفسق فهم لا يؤمنون»
[JISHUMI:v3p2169/r2] verses 6:12 | Kataba ʿalā nafsihi means that God prescribed mercy for Himself; the source reports several explanations of that mercy. «كتب على نفسه الرحمة كتب: أوجب، واختلفوا في معنى الرحمة»
[JISHUMI:v3p2169/r20] verses 6:12, 6:13 | The heavens and earth encompass place and the night and day encompass time; together they indicate all beings, bodies and accidents. «لأن الأول يجمع المكان، والثاني الزمان، وهما طرفان لكل موجود، فكأنه أراد الأجسام والأعراض»
[JISHUMI:v3p2169/r3] verses 6:12 | Al-Aṣamm says mercy means providing people's sustenance whether they obey or disobey. «فقيل: معاشهم عن الأصم؛ لأنه يرزقهم أطاعوا أم عصوا»
[JISHUMI:v3p2169/r4] verses 6:12 | Another view defines mercy as God's gifts of creation, life, senses, empowerment, desires, and desired things. «وقيل: نعمه بالخلق والإحياء والحواس والتمكين والشهوات والمشتهيات»
[JISHUMI:v3p2169/r5] verses 6:12 | Another view makes mercy a favor on Resurrection Day for believers and those who repent. «وقيل: الرحمة يوم القيامة على المؤمنين والتوابين»
[JISHUMI:v3p2169/r6] verses 6:12 | Another explanation says God imposed duties and prescribed mercy so people could benefit; disbelief is their own doing. «وقيل: كلف وكتب بذلك الرحمة على نفسه لينتفعوا به، فإن كفروا فمن قبل أنفسهم أتوا»
[JISHUMI:v3p2169/r7] verses 6:12 | Another view says God promised believers in Muḥammad not to destroy them with exterminating punishment even if they sin, delaying them until Resurrection. «وقيل: كتب لمن آمن بمحمد - صلى الله عليه وسلم - ألا يعذبه بعذاب الاستئصال وإن عصى»
[JISHUMI:v3p2169/r8] verses 6:12 | Al-Aṣamm explains mercy as not exterminating servants or hastening punishment before they reach the Resurrection. «وقيل: كتب الرحمة بألا يستأصل عباده، ولا يعجل بالعقوبة حتى يبلغوا القيامة، عن الأصم»
[JISHUMI:v3p2169/r9] verses 6:12 | One reading treats ilā as redundant, so the phrase means God will gather people on the Resurrection Day. «قيل: (إلى) صلة، والمعنى: ليجمعنكم يوم القيامة»
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:6:12/r1] verses 6:12 | After saying that God created the heavens and earth and alone rules them, the verse affirms that all belongs to Him and that He has no partner in lordship or worship; its question-and-answer form stresses that only He is Lord and worthy of worship. «göklerde ve yerde bulunanların yalnızca O’nun mülkü olduğu, dolayısıyla rubûbiyyette olduğu gibi mâbûdiyyette de O’nun ortağı bulunmadığı»
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:6:12/r2] verses 6:12 | Because the question has one definitive answer, the verse calls for the brief, clear reply, 'It belongs to Allah.' «Sorunun tek ve kesin bir cevabı olduğu için hemen arkasından kısa ve net bir şekilde “Allah’ındır” şeklinde cevap verilmesi istenmiştir.»
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:6:12/r3] verses 6:12 | Since Allah created and owns all beings, He alone can hold accountable those made responsible; the verse announces their certain gathering for judgment on Resurrection Day. «bu varlıklar içinde yükümlü tutulanları yani insanları hesaba çekme yetkisi de O’na ait bulunduğu için âyette, kesin olarak vuku bulacak olan kıyamet gününde Allah’ın muhakkak surette insanları hesaba çekmek üzere bir araya toplayacağı bildirilmiştir.»
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:6:12/r4] verses 6:12 | The statement that God prescribed mercy for Himself between creation and the final return urges people to use His mercy while alive through faith, good works, and repentance. «hayatta iken Allah’ın kesin olan rahmetinden yararlanmaya bakınız; inanıp hayırlı işler yapınız; bu geniş rahmete nâil olmak için günahlarınızdan tövbe ediniz.»
