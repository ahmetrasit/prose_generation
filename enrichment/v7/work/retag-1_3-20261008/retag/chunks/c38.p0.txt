# Verses

# Notes

## 4:1
[QUSHAYRI:v1p193/r15] verses 4:1 | One who guards against the self stands with God without claiming a station or witnessing a spiritual state. «ومن اتقى نفسه وقف مع الله بلا مقام ولا شهود حال»
[QUSHAYRI:v1p193/r2] verses 4:1 | Under the elevation derivation, remembering God raises one's rank, knowing Him raises one's state, and companionship with Him raises one's aspiration; these bring abundant rewards, inward lights, and escape from base attachments. «فمن قال إنه مشتق من السمو فهو اسم من ذكره سمت رتبته ، ومن عرفه سمت حاللته ، ومن صحبه سمت همته»
[QUSHAYRI:v1p193/r3] verses 4:1 | Under the mark derivation, seeking God marks one with worship, companionship with intention, love with the mark of the elect, and knowledge with the mark of the chosen. «ومن قال أصله من السمة فهو اسم من قصده وسم بسمة العبادة ، ومن صحبه وسم بسمة الإرادة»
[QUSHAYRI:v1p193/r4] verses 4:1 | The worshipper's mark protects from the fire, the seeker is too honored for the Garden to enslave him, and the elect's mark removes self-conceit about deserving closeness. «فسمة العبادة توجب هيبة النار أن ترمي صاحبها بشررها ، وسمة الإرادة توجب حشمة الجنان»
[QUSHAYRI:v1p193/r5] verses 4:1 | The mark of the special ones is the heart's grief of separation; union with God elevates His rank beyond imagining, while distance brands the heart with separation. «وسمة وسم بكي الفرقة قلبه . ويقال اسم من واصله سما عنده ( عن ) الأوهام قدره ( سبحانه )»
[QUSHAYRI:v1p193/r6] verses 4:1 | The word people is a collective noun, and its derivation is not strong. «الناس اسم جنس ، والاشتقاق فيه غير قوي»
[QUSHAYRI:v1p193/r7] verses 4:1 | It is said that insan derives from visibility; inwardly, people appeared from nonexistence under obligation, while God honors some with knowledge and guidance and disposes of all as He wills. «وقيل سمي الإنس إنسا لظهوره فعلى هذه الإشارة : يا من ظهرتم عن كتم العدم بحكم تكليفي»
[QUSHAYRI:v1p193/r8] verses 4:1 | It is said God brought people forth from nonexistence as their likenesses and disclosed to no one else the states He disclosed to them. «ويقال لم أظهر من العدم أمثالكم ، ولم أظهر على أحد ما أظهرت عليكم من أحوالكم»
[QUSHAYRI:v1p193/r9] verses 4:1 | It is said insan derives from forgetfulness: forgetting God is the lowest condition and forgetting His remembrance the most abased state. «ويقال سميت إنسانا لنسيانك ، فإن نسيتني فلا شيء أخس منك ، وإن نسيت ذكري فلا أحد أحط منك»
[QUSHAYRI:v1p194/r1] verses 4:1 | The one who stands for God does so without seeking a share in this world or the next. «لا لشهود حظ في الدنيا والعقبى»
[QUSHAYRI:v1p194/r10] verses 4:1 | Knowing that God watches over someone should make that person ashamed before Him. «ومن علم أنه رقيب عليه فبالحري أن يستحي منه»
[QUSHAYRI:v1p194/r11] verses 4:1 | God litigates against a caretaker who violates those in his care and avenges servants more than He avenges Himself; a just and kind orphan guardian has a claim with God, while an abusive one faces God as adversary. «فجاء على رعيته فخصمه ربه ؛ فإنه - سبحانه - ينتقم لعباده ما لا ينتقم لنفسه»
[QUSHAYRI:v1p194/r2] verses 4:1 | The one soul is Adam; since he was created by God's hand, those descended from him share this distinction and are the best of creation. «وهو آدم عليه السلام ، وإذا كنا مخلوقين منه وهو مخلوق باليد فنحن أيضا كذلك»
[QUSHAYRI:v1p194/r3] verses 4:1 | The word soul is generic, and its generic sense implies exhaustive inclusion. «ولفظ ( النفس ) للعموم والعموم يوجب الاستغراق»
[QUSHAYRI:v1p194/r4] verses 4:1 | God made creatures dwell with one another so lineage could continue and like could return to like by pairing form with form. «حكم الحق - سبحانه - بمساكنة الخلق مع الخلق لبقاء النسل ، ولرد المثل إلى المثل»
[QUSHAYRI:v1p194/r5] verses 4:1 | Creating all humanity from one person despite their varied bodies, appearances, characters, aspirations, and conditions proves God's power, lordship, and wisdom. «تعرف إلى العقلاء على كمال القدرة بما ألاح من براهين الربوبية ودلالات الحكمة»
[QUSHAYRI:v1p194/r6] verses 4:1 | No two people resemble one another in appearance, character, aspiration, and condition, demonstrating the limitless scope of God's power and knowledge. «وإن اثنين منهم لا يتشابهان ، فلكل وجه في الصورة والخلق ، والهمة والحالة»
[QUSHAYRI:v1p194/r7] verses 4:1 | Repeating the command to fear God emphasizes its force. «ثم قال : { واتقوا الله } تكريرالأمر بالتقوى يدل على تأكيد حكمه»
[QUSHAYRI:v1p194/r8] verses 4:1 | The instruction concerning God and kinship means to avoid severing family ties: whoever cuts them is cut off, and whoever joins them is joined. «أي اتقوا الأرحام أن تقطعوها ، فمن قطع الرحم قطع ، ومن وصلها وصل»
[QUSHAYRI:v1p194/r9] verses 4:1 | God's watching means He witnesses and counts breaths, sees the senses, oversees thoughts, and originates movements and stillness. «مطلعا شهيدا ، يعد عليك أنفاسك ، ويرى حواسك ، وهو متول خطراتك»
[RAZI-FULL:v9p128/r1] verses 4:1 | The surah centers on duties of compassion and rights toward children, women, and orphans, returns to this theme at its end, and also prescribes purification, prayer, and fighting; its burdensome duties open by grounding taqwa in God's creation. «هذه السورة مشتملة على أنواع كثيرة من التكاليف ، وذلك لأنه تعالى أمر الناس»
[RAZI-FULL:v9p128/r2] verses 4:1 | Ibn Abbas, as transmitted by al-Wahidi, takes the address يا أيها الناس to refer to the people of Mecca. «روى الواحدي عن ابن عباس في قوله : { يذهبكم أيها الناس } أن هذا الخطاب لأهل»
[RAZI-FULL:v9p128/r3] verses 4:1 | The usul exegetes hold, correctly in the author's view, that the address is general to all legally responsible people. «الأصوليون من المفسرين فقد اتفقوا على أن الخطاب عام لجميع المكلفين ، وهذا هو الأصح»
[RAZI-FULL:v9p128/r4] verses 4:1 | The definite plural الناس denotes all people, supporting the general address. «أن لفظ الناس جمع دخله الألف واللام فيفيد الاستغراق»
[RAZI-FULL:v9p128/r5] verses 4:1 | The command to fear God is justified by creation from one soul, a rationale shared by all humanity descended from Adam. «أنه تعالى علل الأمر بالاتقاء بكونه تعالى خالقا لهم من نفس واحدة»
[RAZI-FULL:v9p128/r6] verses 4:1 | Taqwa is required of everyone worldwide, not only Meccans, reinforcing the universal scope of the address and its rationale. «أن التكليف بالتقوى غير مختص بأهل مكة ، بل هو عام في حق جميع العالمين»
[RAZI-FULL:v9p128/r7] verses 4:1 | Ibn Abbas argues that the final clause addresses Arabs because appealing by God and kinship was their custom, so the opening shares that audience. «وحجة ابن عباس أن قوله : { واتقوا الله الذى تساءلون به والارحام } مختص بالعرب»
[RAZI-FULL:v9p128/r8] verses 4:1 | The author answers that a specific audience in the verse's ending does not prevent its opening from addressing everyone; the last clause may be Arab-specific. «ثبت في أصول الفقه أن خصوص آخر الآية لا يمنع من عموم أولها»
[RAZI-FULL:v9p129/r1] verses 4:1 | The opening of al-Nisa is the fourth surah in the Quran's first half, while al-Hajj is fourth in the second half; their openings establish knowledge of creation and resurrection respectively, with creation placed first. «إحداهما : هذه السورة وهي السورة الرابعة من النصف الاول من القرآن . والثانية : سورة الحج»
[RAZI-FULL:v9p129/r2] verses 4:1 | The command to fear God is connected to both the fact that God created humanity and the particular way He created it from one soul. «قولنا إنه تعالى خلقنا من نفس واحدة ، مشتمل على قيدين»
[RAZI-FULL:v9p129/r3] verses 4:1 | Because God created our beings and attributes, He is our master and we His servants; lordship entails obedience to His commands and servitude entails submission. «لما كان خالقا لنا وموجدا لذواتنا وصفاتنا فنحن عبيده وهو مولى لنا»
[RAZI-FULL:v9p129/r4] verses 4:1 | Creation is the utmost beneficence: God brought people from nonexistence to existence, revived them, empowered them, and taught them, so they owe humility and obedience. «فانك كنت معدوما فأوجدك ، وميتا فأحياك ، وعاجزا فأقدرك . وجاهلا فعلمك»
[RAZI-FULL:v9p129/r5] verses 4:1 | God's blessings require humility, submission, and abandoning rebellion, as the verse about being dead and then made alive also says. «فلما كانت النعم بأسرها من الله سبحانه وجب على العبد أن يقابل تلك النعم»
[RAZI-FULL:v9p129/r6] verses 4:1 | Since God alone is creator, deity, and lord, people must worship Him and avoid His prohibitions. «وجب علينا أن نشتغل بعبوديته وأن نتقي كل ما نهى عنه وزجر عنه»
[RAZI-FULL:v9p129/r7] verses 4:1 | No obedience independently earns reward: the capacity and motive to obey come from God, making obedience itself a divine blessing rather than payment owed to the servant. «فعل الطاعات لا يحصل إلا إذا خلق الله القدرة على الطاعة ، وخلق الداعية على الطاعة»
[RAZI-FULL:v9p130#2/r1] verses 4:1 | Al-Asamm says reason alone cannot show that humans had to descend from one soul; since an unlettered Prophet reported this unseen fact without a teacher, it proves prophecy, while خلقكم indicates knowledge of divine unity. «قال الأصم : الفائدة فيه : أن العقل لا دليل فيه على أن الخلق يجب أن يكونوا مخلوقين من نفس واحدة»
[RAZI-FULL:v9p130#2/r2] verses 4:1 | Although all people are numerous and Adam was a small beginning, humanity may be attributed to him because Eve came from him and their offspring and descendants followed. «لأن زوج آدم اذا خلقت من بعضه ، ثم حصل خلق أولاده من نطفتهما ثم كذلك أبدا»
[RAZI-FULL:v9p130#2/r3] verses 4:1 | Muslims agree that the single soul is Adam; its description is grammatically feminine because نفس is feminine, as also in the verse about a pure soul. «أجمع المسلمون على أن المراد بالنفس الواحدة ههنا هو آدم عليه السلام ، إلا أنه أنث الوصف على لفظ النفس»
