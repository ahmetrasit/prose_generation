[RAZI-FULL:v22p199/r3] verses 21:107 | The Prophet is a mercy in religion: sent amid ignorance and the confusion of the scriptural communities, he called people to truth, explained the path to reward, legislated, and distinguished lawful from forbidden. «فدعاهم إلى الحق وبين لهم سبيل الثواب»

[RAZI-FULL:v22p199/r6] verses 21:107 | To the objection that the sword and taking property contradict mercy, one answer is that force targets arrogant, obstinate opponents; even divine mercy coexists with punishment, and blessed rain can cause damage. «إنما جاء بالسيف لمن استكبر وعاند ولم يتفكر ولم يتدبر»
[RAZI-FULL:v22p199/r7] verses 21:107 | A second answer contrasts earlier prophets, whose rejecting peoples were destroyed, with Muhammad's community, whose punishment was delayed until death or resurrection. «كل نبي قبل نبينا كان إذا كذبه قومه أهلك الله المكذبين بالخسف والمسخ والغرق»
[RAZI-FULL:v22p199/r8] verses 21:107 | The objection from verses about fighting and punishment is answered by noting that specification of a general rule does not invalidate it. «لأنا نقول تخصيص العام لا يقدح فيه»

[RAZI-FULL:v22p200#2/r1] verses 21:107 | The Muʿtazila infer that Muhammad is superior to angels because angels belong to the worlds and he is mercy to the worlds. «قالوا : لأن الملائكة من العالمين»
[RAZI-FULL:v22p200#2/r2] verses 21:107 | Al-Razi counters that angels seek forgiveness for believers and bless the Prophet, who is himself among the believers, so angelic mercy also reaches him. «والرسول عليه السلام داخل في المؤمنين»
[RAZI-FULL:v22p200/r1] verses 21:107 | The Muʿtazila argue that if God intended disbelievers' rejection and created their disbelief, the mission would be punishment rather than mercy, contrary to the verse. «لو كان الله تعالى أراد من الكافرين الكفر ولم يرد منهم القبول من الرسول»

[RAZI-FULL:v22p200/r4] verses 21:107 | If a disbeliever's capacity allows only disbelief, the objection follows; if it allows both alternatives, choosing between them requires a divine determiner, so the objection returns. «وإن كانت صالحة للضدين توقف للترجيح على مرجح من قبل الله تعالى»
[RAZI-FULL:v22p200/r5] verses 21:107 | The mission may be mercy for disbelievers through postponement of total destruction; the verse does not require mercy to operate on the same basis for everyone. «لم لا يجوز أن يكون رحمة للكافر بمعنى تأخير عذاب الاستئصال عنه»
[RAZI-FULL:v22p200/r6] verses 21:107 | Al-Razi rejects the claim that worldly favors explain the mercy: although those favors predated the mission, the Prophet's presence stopped threatened punishment, which was mercy for disbelievers. «فلما اندفع ذلك عنهم بسبب حضوره كان ذلك رحمة في حق الكفار»
