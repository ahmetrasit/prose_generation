## 46:15
[ABDURRAZZAQ:v3p216#3/r1] verses 46:15 | Al-Hasan and Qatadah say the mother carried the child with hardship and gave birth with hardship. «عن الحسن وقتادة في قوله تعالى ﴿ حملته أمه كرها ووضعته كرها ﴾ قالا حملته من مشقة ووضعته من مشقة»
[ABDURRAZZAQ:v3p216#3/r2] verses 46:15 | Qatadah says full maturity is reached at thirty-three years and relates the prayer at forty, noting that some years of one’s deeds have already passed. «عن قتادة في قوله تعالى ﴿ حتى إذا بلغ أشده ﴾ ثلاث وثلاثون سنة وتلا قتادة ﴿ وبلغ أربعين سنة قال رب أوزعني أن أشكر نعمتك ﴾»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v9p438/r4] verses 46:15 | Kindness to parents follows the greatest acts of worship; a hadith ranks prayer at its appointed time first and kindness to parents next, while another warns that associating partners and disobeying parents are among the greatest sins. «إذ كان بر الوالدين ثانيا أفضل الأعمال، إذ في الصحيح: أي الأعمال أفضل؟»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v9p438/r5] verses 46:15 | The majority read حسنا with a damma on ح and still س; Ali, al-Sulami, and Isa read both vowels open; Isa is also reported with both vowels rounded, while Kufans read إحسانا. «وقرأ الجمهور: حسنا، بضم الحاء وإسكان السين»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v9p438/r6] verses 46:15 | Grammarians construe حسنا or إحسانا as the second object of وصينا, a phrase meaning an instruction characterized by goodness, a purpose clause, or a verbal noun under an implied verb; Abu Hayyan rejects Ibn Atiyya's claim that the preposition ب can depend on إحسانا. «فانتصب حسنا وإحسانا على المفعول الثاني لوصينا»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v9p439/r1] verses 46:15 | The suggestion that hardship begins at conception is unlikely because the mother has no control over conception or continuation at that moment. «ولا يلحقها كره إذ ذاك، فهذا احتمال بعيد.»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v9p439/r2] verses 46:15 | They understand pregnancy and childbirth as involving hardship for the mother. «المعنى: حملته مشقة، ووضعته مشقة.»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v9p439/r3] verses 46:15 | The majority read كَرها with a damma; several canonical reciters read كَرها with a fatha, and both are Arabic synonyms. Some distinguish hardship from coercive force, but Abu Hayyan rejects that distinction as a basis for faulting the canonical reading, including Abu Hatim's criticism. «والضم والفتح لغتان بمعنى واحد، كالعقر والعقر.»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v9p439/r4] verses 46:15 | The accusatives كَرها may be circumstantial descriptions of the mother or modifiers of an omitted verbal noun for carrying and delivery. «وانتصابهما على الحال من ضمير الفاعل»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v9p439/r5] verses 46:15 | A thirty-month combined term means either six months' pregnancy plus two years of nursing or a usual nine-month pregnancy plus twenty-one months nursing; longer pregnancies reduce nursing, whose full term is two years for one who wishes to complete it. «وهذا لا يكون إلا بأن يكون أحد الطرفين ناقصا إما بأن تلد المرأة لستة أشهر وترضع عامين»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v9p439/r6] verses 46:15 | Galen says his investigations found a woman who delivered after 184 nights, supporting six months as the minimum pregnancy. «فرأيت امرأة ولدت لمائة وأربع وثمانين ليلة»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v9p439/r7] verses 46:15 | Ibn Sina says he witnessed a birth after six months and recounts a trusted report of a birth after four years, with the child already having teeth; Abu Hayyan says the Qur'an sets no maximum pregnancy duration. «وأما أكثر الحمل فليس في القرآن ما يدل عليه»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v9p439/r8] verses 46:15 | Aristotle is quoted as saying gestation is fixed for other animals but variable for humans, with seven months possible and eight-month births rarely surviving except in certain lands such as Egypt. «إن مدة الحمل لكل الحيوان مضبوطة سوى الإنسان»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v9p439/r9] verses 46:15 | The term فصال calls nursing by the adjoining weaning stage because nursing ends and completes with it; the majority read فصاله, while some read فصله, with both treated as verbal nouns. «وعبر عن الرضاع بالفصال، لما كان الرضاع يلي الفصال ويلابسه،»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v9p440/r1] verses 46:15 | The verse mentions the mother in three respects—parentage, pregnancy, and nursing—but the father only once, matching the report that three quarters of filial kindness is owed to the mother. «ذكر تعالى الأم في ثلاثة مراتب في قوله: بوالديه وحمله وإرضاعه المعبر عنه بالفصال،»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v9p440/r2] verses 46:15 | The phrase حتى إذا بلغ أشده omits an intervening life clause such as he lived thereafter; the apparent meaning that maturity is forty is weak because the next phrase coordinates forty as a distinct stage. «والظاهر ضعف قول من قال: بلوغ الأشد أربعون، لعطف وبلغ أربعين سنة.»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v9p440/r3] verses 46:15 | The stronger reading is that forty marks complete intellect and evident flourishing; it is also said that no prophet was commissioned before forty. «وبلوغ الأربعين اكتمال العقل لظهور الفلاح»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v9p440/r4] verses 46:15 | A hadith depicts Satan dragging his hand over the face of an unrepentant person over forty and saying that this face will not prosper. «إن الشيطان يجر يده على وجه من زاد على الأربعين ولم يتب»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v9p440/r5] verses 46:15 | The prayer asks God to place righteousness among the descendants; alternatively, أصلح لي is interpreted as be kind to me regarding my descendants because أصلح ordinarily acts reflexively. «سأل أن يجعل ذريته موقعا للصلاح ومظنة له»
[ALUSI-FULL:v26p16#3/r1] verses 46:15 | A report from Ibn Abbas says the verse was revealed concerning Abu Bakr al-Ṣiddīq, through the later phrase about the true promise they were promised. «نزلت كماأخرج ابن عساكر من طريق الكلبي عن أبي صالح عن ابن عباس في أبي بكر»
[ALUSI-FULL:v26p16#3/r2] verses 46:15 | إحسانا may be a second object after construing وصينا as ألزمنا; a cognate object after construing it as أحسنا; an adjective of an omitted مصدر; or a causal object for kindness to the parents. «قيل : مفعول ثان لوصينا على تضمينه معنى ألزمنا وقيل : منصوب على المصدر على تضمين ( وصينا ) معنى أحسنا»
[ALUSI-FULL:v26p16#3/r3] verses 46:15 | Ibn ʿAṭiyya parses إحسانا as an explicit cognate object; Abu Hayyan objects that its جار ومجرور cannot depend on a postponed verbal noun, and that أحسن takes لام rather than باء for the recipient. «وقال ابن عطية : إنه منصوب على المصدر الصريح و ( بوالديه ) متعلق بوصينا أو به»
