# Notes

## 46:15
[ELMALILI:46:11-16#2/r35] verses 46:15 | The third request asks God to make righteousness spread among the descendants and establish it in them. «zürriyetimde de benim için ıslah nasib et yani salâhı onlara da sirâyet ettir, içlerinde sâbit kıl.»
[ELMALILI:46:11-16#2/r36] verses 46:15 | Repentance means turning to God from acts He disapproves or from things that distract from His remembrance; being among Muslims means sincere submission to divine unity. «râzı olmayacağın fiillerden veya Senin zikrinden»
[ELMALILI:46:11-16#2/r37] verses 46:15 | The concluding repentance and submission clauses indicate that a supplication’s soundness depends on repentance and Islam. «Bu iki cümle duanın sıhhati tevbe ve islâma mütevakkıf olduğuna işarettir.»
[ELMALILI:46:11-16#2/r38] verses 46:15 | Ibn Abbas says God accepted Abu Bakr’s prayer by freeing nine believers, including Bilal and Amir b. Fuhayra, and aiding his good aims. «Allah teâlâ Ebû Bekir radıyallâhu anh’ın duasını kabul buyurdu da»
[ELMALILI:46:11-16#2/r39] verses 46:15 | Ibn Abbas says Abu Bakr’s prayer for his descendants was accepted: all his children believed, and his parents, son, and grandson met the Prophet. «Zürriyeti hakkında da duasını kabul buyurdu, evlâdlarının hepsi iman ettiler, ona hem anasının babasının hem de evlâdlarının cemî‘sinin islâmı müyesser oldu.»
[ELMALILI:46:11-16#2/r40] verses 46:15 | Ibn Abbas says no Companion attained Abu Bakr’s degree of distinction; the author adds that all the Companions were filled with beneficence and steadfastness. «Hazret-i Ebû Bekir’in mazhariyeti derecesi sahabeden hiçbirine müyesser olmadı.»
[ELMALILI:46:11-16#2/r7] verses 46:15 | Kindness to parents is especially enjoined among the foremost marks of beneficent people, whose defining signs are faith and steadfastness. «İman ve istikāmet en birinci şiarları olan muhsinlerin şânı beyan olunurken anaya babaya ihsan bilhassa tavsiye edilmiştir.»
[ELMALILI:46:11-16#2/r8] verses 46:15 | Parent-kindness appears in several verses, each serving a distinct purpose; here it is presented as a major example of beneficence and steadfastness linked to recompense for deeds. «münasebetiyle ihsan ve istikāmetin mühim bir misâli olmak üzere îrad buyurulmuştur.»
[ELMALILI:46:11-16#2/r9] verses 46:15, 46:16 | A report says these two verses were revealed about Abu Bakr al-Siddiq and contain foundations for many legal rulings. «bir hayli ahkâmın da esaslarını ihtivâ eylemiştir.»
[FARISI-HUJJA:v6p182#2/r1] verses 46:15 | These readers use iḥsānan, with an alif. «فقرأ عاصم وحمزة والكسائي: إحسانا بألف.»
[FARISI-HUJJA:v6p182#2/r2] verses 46:15 | The other readers use ḥusnan without an alif. «وقرأ الباقون: حسنا* بغير ألف .»
[FARISI-HUJJA:v6p182#2/r3] verses 46:15 | The preposition bi before the parents can attach to the command “We enjoined,” supported by the parallel wording in al-Anʿām. «الباء في قوله: بوالديه يجوز أن يتعلق بوصينا، بدلالة قوله: ذلكم وصاكم به»
[FARISI-HUJJA:v6p182#2/r4] verses 46:15 | It may also be construed with iḥsānan through an implied verb, like an example where the preposition precedes the verb that explains it. «ويجوز أن تتعلق بالإحسان، يدل على ذلك قوله: وقد أحسن بي إذا أخرجني من السجن»
[FARISI-HUJJA:v6p182#2/r5] verses 46:15 | Iḥsānan is a verbal noun meaning kindness rather than harm; it cannot be governed directly by waṣṣaynā because the verb has already filled its objects. «والإحسان خلاف الإساءة، والحسن خلاف القبح،»
[FARISI-HUJJA:v6p182#2/r6] verses 46:15 | Ḥusnan means doing what is good rather than ugly in one’s treatment of parents, supported by al-ʿAnkabūt. «ومن قال: بوالديه حسنا فمعناه: ليأت في أمرهما أمرا ذا حسن، أي: ليأت الحسن في أمرهما دون القبح»
[FARISI-HUJJA:v6p184/r1] verses 46:15 | These readers use fatḥa in both occurrences of karhan. «وقرأ ابن كثير ونافع وأبو عمرو: كرها* وكرها* [الأحقاف/ 15] نصبا.»
[FARISI-HUJJA:v6p184/r2] verses 46:15 | The other readers use ḍamma on the kāf in both occurrences. «وقرأ الباقون: كرها بضم الكاف في الحرفين .»
[FARISI-HUJJA:v6p184/r3] verses 46:15 | Al-karh is analyzed as a verbal noun and al-kurh as a noun for what is disliked; fatḥa suits a verbal noun or circumstantial use, while ḍamma suits the noun. «الكره: كأنه المصدر، والكره: الاسم، كأنه الشيء المكروه»
[FARISI-HUJJA:v6p184/r4] verses 46:15 | The two vowelings may instead be dialect variants, like paired forms for drinking, weakness, poverty, tambourine, and honeycomb. «وقد قيل: إنهما لغتان، فمن ذهب إلى ذلك جعلهما مثل الشرب والشرب، والضعف والضعف، والفقر والفقر»
[FARRA:v3p52/r1] verses 46:15 | Some codices have ḥusnan; Farrāʾ says it and iḥsānan have the same meaning. «(حسنا) وكذلك هي في مصاحفهم، ومعناهما واحد والله أعلم.»
[FARRA:v3p52/r2] verses 46:15 | Abdullah’s reading adds istawā before reaching full maturity; Farrāʾ says the reordered wording has the same sense because Arabic can mention a later event before an earlier one. «وفي قراءة عبد الله: حتى إذا استوى وبلغ أشده وبلغ أربعين سنة»
[FARRA:v3p52/r3] verses 46:15 | One view identifies ashudd here with age forty. «إن الأشدها هنا هو الأربعون»
[FARRA:v3p52/r4] verses 46:15 | An account from some elders assigns age thirty-three to ashudd and forty to istiwāʾ. «وسمعت بعض المشيخة يذكر بإسناد له في الأشد: ثلاث وثلاثون، وفي الاستواء: أربعون.»
[FARRA:v3p52/r5] verses 46:15 | Farrāʾ prefers thirty-three as ashudd followed by forty as istiwāʾ, since the numbers form a closer sequence; he says eighteen could also work if joined to forty. «والأول أشبه بالصواب لأن الأربعين أقرب في النسق إلى ثلاث وثلاثين ومنها إلى ثماني عشرة»
[FARRA:v3p52/r6] verses 46:15 | Ashudd is eighteen in another passage; the example of less than two-thirds, half, and a third illustrates that nearby quantities form a better sequence. «وسمعت أن الأشد في غير هذا الموضع: ثمانى عشرة»
[FARRA:v3p52/r7] verses 46:15 | Ibn Abbas reports that the verse was revealed concerning Abu Bakr, through the continuation about those whose best deeds are accepted. «عن ابن عباس قال: نزلت في أبي بكر رحمه الله»
[IBNABIHATIM:v10p3293#3/r1] verses 46:15, 2:233 | ʿAli derives a six-month minimum pregnancy by subtracting the two full years of nursing from the Qur’an’s thirty-month pregnancy-and-weaning total, stopping ʿUthman’s mistaken stoning order. «فبلغ ذلك عليا فاتاه ، فقال له»
[IBNABIHATIM:v10p3293#3/r2] verses 46:15 | Ibn ʿAbbas explains that the thirty-month total allows twenty-one nursing months after a nine-month pregnancy, twenty-three after seven months, or two full years after six months. «عن ابن عباس قال : اذا وضعت المراة لتسعة اشهر ، كفاه من الرضاع أحد وعشرون شهرا»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v26p32/r1] verses 46:15 | The prayer asks God to inspire the speaker and include his parents, so personal supplication does not crowd out prayer for them; their direct kindness is already implied by the argument from forbidding even a mild rebuke. «أنه لا يشغله الدعاء لنفسه عن الدعاء لهما وبأنه يحسن إليهما بظهر الغيب منهما حين مناجاته ربه»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v26p32/r2] verses 46:15 | God commands kindness to parents both in their presence and absence, through every means that benefits them, as in the prayer to show them mercy as they raised the child. «أن الله أمر بالإحسان إلى الوالدين في المشاهدة والغيبة وبجميع وسائل الإحسان الذي غايته حصول النفع لهما»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v26p32/r3] verses 46:15 | A Prophetic report says a person’s deeds end at death except for ongoing charity, knowledge imparted, and a righteous child who prays for the deceased parent; the author uses it to support the prayer for parents. «إذا مات ابن آدم انقطع عمله إلا من ثلاث»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v26p32/r4] verses 46:15 | When a child thanks God for blessings bestowed on the parents, the child acts on their behalf and performs a good deed for them. «وما شكر الولد ربه على النعمة التي أنعمها الله على والديه إلا من باب نيابته عنهما في هذا الشكر»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v26p32/r5] verses 46:15 | At the Farewell Pilgrimage, a Khathʿamī woman asked whether she could perform Hajj for her elderly father, who could not remain on a mount; the Prophet answered yes. «وفي حديث الفضل بن عباس أن المرأة الخثعمية قالت لرسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم يوم حجة الوداع»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v26p32/r6] verses 46:15 | The Hajj done for the father was not an obligatory Hajj for him because incapacity prevented him from performing it. «وهو حج غير واجب على أبيها لعجزه»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v26p32/r7] verses 46:15 | Ashudd denotes the strengthening of mental and physical faculties; it is a plural with no attested singular. «والأشد : حالة اشتداد القوى العقلية والجسدية وهو جمع لم يسمع له بمفرد»
