[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v6p436/r2] verses 33:43 | God's and the angels' blessings on believers spur them to remember Him: since He remembers them, they should remember Him, as the cited command says. «هذا تهييج إلى الذكر، أي: إنه سبحانه يذكركم فاذكروه أنتم»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v6p436/r3] verses 33:43 | A divine hadith promises that whoever remembers God privately is remembered privately, and whoever remembers Him in a gathering is remembered in a better gathering. «يقول الله: من ذكرني في نفسه ذكرته في نفسي، ومن ذكرني في ملأ ذكرته في ملأ خير منهم»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v6p436/r4] verses 33:43 | Abu al-Aliya explains God's salat as His praise of the servant before the angels; al-Bukhari relates the view through al-Rabi' ibn Anas. «والصلاة من الله ثناؤه على العبد عند الملائكة، حكاه البخاري عن أبي العالية»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v6p436/r5] verses 33:43 | Another explanation takes God's salat to mean mercy, supported by the verse describing God's blessings and mercy upon the patient. «وقال غيره: الصلاة من الله: الرحمة»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v6p436/r6] verses 33:43 | Ibn Kathir sees no contradiction between God's praise of believers before the angels and His mercy toward them. «وقد يقال: لا منافاة بين القولين والله أعلم»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v6p436/r7] verses 33:43 | The angels' salat means supplication for people and asking forgiveness, as shown by the angels' prayer for believers. «وأما الصلاة من الملائكة، فبمعنى الدعاء للناس والاستغفار»
[IBNQUTAYBA-GHARIB:v1p351#3/r1] verses 33:43 | God's ṣalāt upon believers means He blesses them, and it is also said to mean He forgives them. «أي يبارك عليكم. ويقال: يغفر لكم.»
[IBNQUTAYBA-GHARIB:v1p351#3/r2] verses 33:43 | The angels' ṣalāt means that they ask forgiveness for the believers. «أي تستغفر لكم»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-6-2:p217/r1] verses 33:43 | God sends mercy upon believers, while angels pray for God to grant them mercy. «He sends down His mercy on His believers and His angels too continue to pray to God to grant His mercy to the believers.»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-6-2:p217/r2] verses 33:43 | The verse's darkness means darkness of belief and its light means guidance; its close describes God as merciful to believers. «darkness obviously refers to the darkness of beliefs and light refers to the light of guidance.»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-6-2:p217/r3] verses 33:43 | Yusallī means God's mercy when attributed to God and angels' prayer for mercy when attributed to angels; 33:56 illustrates the distinction, and the close here signals God's mercy to the Prophet. «The word یۡ ِّلَصُی when used with reference to God will allude to His mercy and when used with reference to the angels will mean their prayer for mercy.»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-6-2:p217/r4] verses 33:43 | Islahi cites angels' prayers for forgiveness of believers in Sūrah al-Mu’min and Sūrah al-Shūrā. «The fact that angels seek forgiveness for the believers is mentioned at other places in the Qur’ān as well.»
[JISHUMI:v8p5744/r13] verses 33:43 | The segment raises, but does not answer, how God's and the angels' prayer upon believers connects to the preceding passage. «ويقال: كيف يتصل قوله: (هو الذي يصلي عليكم) بما قبله؟»
[JISHUMI:v8p5744/r3] verses 33:43 | Prayer originally means supplication; Islamic usage transferred it to specified acts and pillars, while God's prayer is mercy and the angels' prayer is supplication. «والصلاة في الأصل: الدعاء، ونقل في الشرع إلى أفعال وأركان مخصوصة، والصلاة من الله: الرحمة، ومن الملائكة: الدعاء»
[JISHUMI:v8p5744/r5] verses 33:43 | Nūr is radiance, described here as a body possessing a particular color; munīr is what illuminates by its own source or by causing illumination. «والنور: الضياء، وهو جسم فيه لون مخصوص، والمنير: ما ينور عنده»
[JISHUMI:v8p5744/r8] verses 33:43 | Raḥīman is the predicate of kāna, whose omitted subject is God. «(رحيما) خبر (كان) والاسم مضمر، أي: كان الله رحيما»
[JISHUMI:v8p5744/r9] verses 33:43 | Anas reports that after 33:56 was revealed, Abu Bakr said the Prophet's honor was shared with the believers, and God then revealed 33:43. «عن أنس: لما نزل قوله: (إن الله وملائكته يصلون على النبي) قال أبو بكر»

[KASHSHAF-FULL:v3p555/r2] verses 33:43 | When God sends prayer upon believers it means that He has mercy on them; angels' prayer means their supplication that God grant believers mercy, since their prayer is answered. «هي قولهم : اللهم صل على المؤمنين ، جعلوا لكونهم مستجابي الدعوة كأنهم فاعلون الرحمة والرأفة .»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v3p555/r4] verses 33:43 | God's mercy and compassion appear in His invitation to good and command to increase remembrance, prayer, and obedience; bringing believers from darkness to light means from disobedience to obedience. «والمعنى : هو الذي يترحم عليكم ويترأف : حيث يدعوكم إلى الخير ويأمركم بإكثار الذكر والتوفر على الصلاة والطاعة { ليخرجكم } من ظلمات المعصية إلى نور الطاعة»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v3p555/r5] verses 33:43 | Calling God merciful to believers confirms that His prayer here means mercy. «{ وكان بالمؤمنين رحيما } دليل على أن المراد بالصلاة الرحمة .»
