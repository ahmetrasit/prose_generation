[ALUSI-FULL:v16p18/r3] verses 18:82 | He says the spiritual path and religious law are the same in reality, with no contradiction even by a hair's breadth; anything that conflicts with law is rejected. «فكل من الطريقة والشريعة عين الآخر لا مخالفة بينهما بقدر رأس الشعيرة»
[ALUSI-FULL:v16p18/r4] verses 18:82 | He says the inward path perfects outward law; apparent contradictions during an ecstatic state disappear when the seeker returns to sobriety. «فإذا تجاوزوا ذلك المقام ورجعوا إلى الصحو ارتفعت تلك المنافاة بالكلية»
[ALUSI-FULL:v16p19#2/r1] verses 18:82 | Al-Ghazālī says anyone claiming inner truth can contradict outward law is closer to unbelief than faith. «وقال في الإحياء : من قال إن الباطل يخالف الظاهر فهو إلى الكفر أقرب منه إلى الإيمان»
[ALUSI-FULL:v16p19#2/r2] verses 18:82 | The author says mystics do receive unseen disclosures, but these are fruits of sincerity within religious law and cannot change legal rulings, restrict an unrestricted ruling, or release a restricted one. «فإن الكشوف والعلوم الغيبية ثمرة الإخلاص الذي هو الجزء الثالث من أجزاء الشريعة»
[ALUSI-FULL:v16p19#2/r3] verses 18:82 | Sājūqlīzāda says hidden causes may make Khidr's ship damage and killing lawful exceptions to general prohibitions; the author says earlier texts explicitly reject this. «خلافا لما توهمه ساجقلي زاده حيث قال في شرح عبارة الإحياء السابقة آنفا»
[ALUSI-FULL:v16p19#2/r4] verses 18:82 | Sirhindī says mystical unveilings and experiences are not final goals; they train beginners, and the accomplished seeker passes beyond them toward contentment, a rare attainment. «أنها تربي بها أطفال الطريق وأنه ينبغي مجاوزتها والوصول إلى مقام الرضا»
[ALUSI-FULL:v16p19#2/r5] verses 18:82 | Those who mistake ecstatic states and visions for spiritual goals remain trapped in illusion and miss the law's full attainments. «إن الذين هم قليلو النظر يعدون الأحوال والمواجيد من المقامات»
[ALUSI-FULL:v16p19#2/r6] verses 18:82 | Moses is more complete than Khidr; Khidr's greater knowledge of inner reality was relative to their present states, while Moses passed beyond it in his lawgiving role. «ويعلم مما ذكر أن موسى عليه السلام أكمل من الخضر وأعلمية الخضر عليه السلام بعلم الحقيقة»
[ALUSI-FULL:v16p19#2/r7] verses 18:82 | A Mathnawī verse illustrates that a lover's passion can eclipse the formal lessons of Abū Ḥanīfa and al-Shāfiʿī; the author says a perfected person may even be hidden among ordinary people. «وعلى هذا يخرج بيت المثنوي حيث يقول : زان طرف كه عشق من افزوددرد بو حنيفة شافعي درسي نكرد»
[ALUSI-FULL:v16p19/r1] verses 18:82 | Sirhindī says law and spiritual reality are one in truth, differing by summary versus detail, inference versus unveiling, and effort versus ease; any contradiction, however slight, proves nonattainment. «اعلم أن الشريعة والحقيقة متحدان في الحقيقة ولا فرق بينهما إلا بالإجمال والتفصيل»

[ALUSI-FULL:v16p19/r3] verses 18:82 | ʿAbd al-Qādir al-Jīlānī says saints draw only on God's and the Messenger's words and act only by their outward meaning. «وقال سيدي القطب الرباني الشيخ عبد القادر الكيلاني قدس سره : جميع الأولياء»
[ALUSI-FULL:v16p19/r4] verses 18:82 | Al-Junayd says every path is closed except following the Messenger; he also says this discipline is bound by Quran and Sunna and its teacher must know both. «وقال سيد الطائفة الجنيد قدس سره : الطرق كلها مسدودة إلا على من اقتفى أثر الرسول»
