# Notes

## 9:128
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v3p99#2/r8] verses 9:128 | “From among yourselves” praises the Prophet’s lineage as being from the heart of Arab nobility, supported by the report that God chose Kinana from Ishmael’s descendants, Quraysh from Kinana, Banu Hashim from Quraysh, and the Prophet from Banu Hashim. «إن الله اصطفى كنانة من ولد إسماعيل، واصطفى قريشا من كنانة، واصطفى بني هاشم من قريش، واصطفاني من بني هاشم»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v3p99#2/r9] verses 9:128 | The Prophet says he is from legitimate marriage rather than fornication, meaning his ancestry back to Adam contained no illicit parentage. «ومنه قوله صلى الله عليه وسلم: «إني من نكاح ولست من سفاح» معناه أن نسبه صلى الله عليه وسلم إلى آدم عليه السلام لم يكن النسل فيه إلا من نكاح»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v3p520#2/r1] verses 9:128 | Most readers pronounce the fāʾ with ḍamma; Ibn ʿAbbās, Abū al-ʿĀliya, al-Ḍaḥḥāk, Ibn Muḥayṣin, and Maḥbūb from Abū ʿAmr pronounce it with fatḥa. «قرأ الجمهور بضم الفاء وقرأ ابن عباس وأبو العالية والضحاك وابن محيصن ومحبوب عن أبي عمرو بفتحها»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v3p520#2/r2] verses 9:128 | With ḍamma, “from among yourselves” means from all the Arabs; every Arab tribe had a descendant of the Messenger. «من جميع العرب قاله ابن عباس وقال ليس في العرب قبيلة إلا وقد ولدت رسوله الله صلى الله عليه وسلم»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v3p520#2/r3] verses 9:128 | With ḍamma, “from among yourselves” means from people you know. «ممن تعرفون قاله قتادة»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v3p520#2/r4] verses 9:128 | With ḍamma, “from among yourselves” means from a marriage untouched by any pre-Islamic birth. «من نكاح لم يصبه شيء من ولادة الجاهلية قاله جعفر الصادق»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v3p520#2/r5] verses 9:128 | With ḍamma, it means a human like you, which strengthens the proof because you understand someone like yourselves. «بشر مثلكم فهو آكد للحجة لأنكم تفقهون عمن هو مثلكم قاله الزجاج»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v3p520#2/r6] verses 9:128 | With fatḥa, one reported meaning is that he is the best of you in creation. «وفي المفتوحة ثلاثة أقوال أحدها أفضلكم خلقا»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v3p520#2/r7] verses 9:128 | With fatḥa, another reported meaning is that he is the noblest of you in lineage. «والثاني أشرفكم نسبا والثالث أكثركم طاعة لله عز وجل»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v3p520#2/r8] verses 9:128 | With fatḥa, a third reported meaning is that he is the most obedient of you to God. «الثالث أكثركم طاعة لله عز وجل»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v3p521/r1] verses 9:128 | “What troubles you” means what is severe and hard for you; al-ʿanat is encountering hardship. «شديد عليه ما شق عليكم رواه الضحاك عن ابن عباس قال الزجاج شديد عليه عنتكم والعنت لقاء الشدة»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v3p521/r2] verses 9:128 | Another explanation is that the Prophet is pained by what makes you sinful. «شديد عليه ما آثمكم رواه أبو صالح عن ابن عباس»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v3p521/r3] verses 9:128 | The Prophet is keen for you to believe. «قال الحسن حريص عليكم أن تؤمنوا»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v3p521/r4] verses 9:128 | The Prophet is named with two of God’s names, “compassionate” and “merciful.” «قال ابن عباس سماه باسمين من أسمائه»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v3p521/r5] verses 9:128 | Raʾūf is on the faʿūl pattern from raʾfa, which is gentler than mercy; ruʾuf is also said, and a verse of poetry attests it. «رؤوف فعول من الرأقة وهي أرق من الرحمة ويقال رؤف وأنشد ترى للمؤمنين عليك حقا كفعل الوالد الرؤف الرحيم»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v3p521/r6] verses 9:128 | One view says he is compassionate toward the obedient and merciful toward sinners. «وقيل رؤوف بالمطيعين رحيم بالمذنبين»
[IBNJINNI-MUHTASAB:v1p305/r4] verses 9:128 | Ibn Qusayṭ al-Makkī reads من أنفسكم, meaning 'from your finest people,' since النفس can denote what is most excellent. «ومن ذلك قراءة عبد الله بن قسيط المكي: "لقد جاءكم رسول من أنفسكم"»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v4p241#2/r1] verses 9:128 | The Prophet is pained by hardship for his community; Ibn Kathir cites reports describing his message as lenient monotheism and the religion as easy, and says the whole law is complete and easy for those God enables. «وشريعته كلها سهلة سمحة كاملة، يسيرة على من يسرها الله تعالى عليه»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v4p241#2/r2] verses 9:128 | He is eager for the community's guidance and for their worldly and otherworldly benefit. «أي: على هدايتكم ووصول النفع الدنيوي والأخروي إليكم»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v4p241#2/r3] verses 9:128 | Abū Dharr says the Prophet left them without omitting knowledge about anything, and said he had explained everything that brings near Paradise and distances from Hell; al-Ṭabarānī transmits it. «عن أبي الطفيل، عن أبي ذر قال. تركنا رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم وما طائر (1) يقلب جناحيه في الهواء»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v4p241#2/r4] verses 9:128 | The Prophet warns that God prohibited nothing without knowing someone would violate it, and says he is holding the community back from plunging into Hell like moths or flies; Aḥmad transmits it. «عن عبدة النهدي، عن عبد الله بن مسعود قال: قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم: "إن الله لم يحرم حرمة إلا وقد علم»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v4p241#2/r5] verses 9:128 | In a dream allegory, the Prophet and his community are travelers rescued from a barren desert by a guide to water and pasture; some follow him toward better provisions ahead, while others stay behind. «عن ابن عباس: أن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم أتاه ملكان، فيما يرى النائم، فقعد أحدهما عند (5) رجليه»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v4p241/r1] verses 9:128 | God favored believers with a messenger from their own people and speaking their language; Ibn Kathir links this to Abraham's prayer and God's reminder of His favor in sending a messenger from among them. «أي: من جنسهم وعلى لغتهم، كما قال إبراهيم، عليه السلام»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v4p241/r2] verses 9:128 | Jaʿfar ibn Abī Ṭālib and al-Mughīrah ibn Shuʿbah told foreign rulers that their messenger was one of them, whose lineage, character, conduct, truthfulness, and trustworthiness they knew. «كما قال جعفر بن أبي طالب للنجاشي، والمغيرة بن شعبة لرسول كسرى: إن الله بعث فينا رسولا منا، نعرف نسبه وصفته»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v4p241/r3] verses 9:128 | Jaʿfar ibn Muḥammad reports from his father that the Prophet's lineage was untouched by any pre-Islamic illicit birth; the Prophet said he came through marriage, not fornication. «وقال سفيان بن عيينة، عن جعفر بن محمد، عن أبيه في قوله تعالى: {لقد جاءكم رسول من أنفسكم} قال: لم يصبه شيء من ولادة الجاهلية»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v4p241/r4] verses 9:128 | A report traced to ʿAlī says the Prophet was born through lawful marriage, with no pre-Islamic fornication touching his lineage from Adam until his parents. «عن أبيه، عن جده، عن علي قال: قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم: "خرجت من نكاح ولم أخرج من سفاح»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v4p242/r1] verses 9:128 | A Bedouin who received the Prophet's help insulted him; the Prophet calmly gave more, asked him to publicly express satisfaction, and compared his gentle handling to a camel's owner calming a frightened camel. Al-Bazzār says it is known only through this route. «عن عكرمة عن أبي هريرة، رضي الله عنه؛ أن أعرابيا جاء إلى رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم ليستعينه في شيء»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v4p242/r2] verses 9:128 | Ibn Kathir judges the Bedouin report weak because its chain includes Ibrāhīm ibn al-Ḥakam ibn Abān. «قلت: وهو ضعيف بحال إبراهيم بن الحكم بن أبان، والله أعلم»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v4p243/r1] verses 9:128 | The Prophet's compassion toward believers accords with God's command to lower his wing to believers and, if they disobey, disavow their deeds and rely on the Mighty, Merciful God. «كما قال تعالى: {واخفض جناحك لمن اتبعك من المؤمنين. فإن عصوك فقل إني بريء مما تعملون.»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v4p243/r4] verses 9:128, 9:129 | A report from Ubayy ibn Kaʿb says the final revelation was this passage from the messenger's arrival through the end of the surah. «عن أبي بن كعب قال: آخر آية نزلت من القرآن هذه الآية: {لقد جاءكم رسول من أنفسكم}»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v4p243/r5] verses 9:127, 9:128, 9:129 | During Abū Bakr's Qur'an compilation, scribes thought 9:127 was the last revelation; Ubayy said the Prophet had taught him the next two verses as the final revelation, closing with the monotheism with which revelation began. «قال لهم أبي بن كعب: أن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم أقرأني بعدها آيتين»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v4p244/r1] verses 9:128, 9:129 | Al-Ḥārith ibn Khazmah brought the final two verses to ʿUmar, who confirmed hearing them from the Prophet and said he would have made them a separate surah had they been three verses; they were placed at the end of Bara'ah. «فقال عمر: وأنا أشهد لسمعتها من رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v4p244/r2] verses 9:128, 9:129 | ʿUmar had urged Abū Bakr to compile the Qur'an; in a Ṣaḥīḥ report, Zayd found the end of Bara'ah with Khuzaymah ibn Thābit or Abū Khuzaymah, and other Companions recalled it from the Prophet. «وفي الصحيح أن زيدا قال: فوجدت آخر سورة "براءة" مع خزيمة بن ثابت -أو: أبي خزيمة»
[IBNQUTAYBA-GHARIB:v1p193#8/r1] verses 9:128 | ʿAzīz ʿalayhi means it is painful or severe for him when hardship and harm befall you. «أي: شديد عليه ما أعنتكم وضركم»
[JISHUMI:v5p3300/r10] verses 9:128 | These readers use fatḥa and understand the Messenger to be among the most noble and excellent of you, from the word nafīs, something prized. «وقرأ ابن عباس والزهري وابن علية: من أنفسكم بفتح الفاء؛ أي: من أشرفكم وأفضلكم»
[JISHUMI:v5p3300/r9] verses 9:128 | The common reading of anfusikum with ḍamma means the Messenger is from your lineage. «قراءة العامة: أنفسكم بضم الفاء من النفس أي: من نسبكم»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v2p311/r1] verses 9:128 | 'From yourselves' means from your own kind and lineage: an Arab Qurayshite like you. «من جنسكم ومن نسبكم عربي قرشي مثلكم»
