# Verses
- 3:7: هُوَ ٱلَّذِىٓ أَنزَلَ عَلَيْكَ ٱلْكِتَٰبَ مِنْهُ ءَايَٰتٌۭ مُّحْكَمَٰتٌ هُنَّ أُمُّ ٱلْكِتَٰبِ وَأُخَرُ مُتَشَٰبِهَٰتٌۭ ۖ فَأَمَّا ٱلَّذِينَ فِى قُلُوبِهِمْ زَيْغٌۭ فَيَتَّبِعُونَ مَا تَشَٰبَهَ مِنْهُ ٱبْتِغَآءَ ٱلْفِتْنَةِ وَٱبْتِغَآءَ تَأْوِيلِهِۦ ۗ وَمَا يَعْلَمُ تَأْوِيلَهُۥٓ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ ۗ وَٱلرَّٰسِخُونَ فِى ٱلْعِلْمِ يَقُولُونَ ءَامَنَّا بِهِۦ كُلٌّۭ مِّنْ عِندِ رَبِّنَا ۗ وَمَا يَذَّكَّرُ إِلَّآ أُو۟لُوا۟ ٱلْأَلْبَٰبِ

- 18:77: فَٱنطَلَقَا حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَآ أَتَيَآ أَهْلَ قَرْيَةٍ ٱسْتَطْعَمَآ أَهْلَهَا فَأَبَوْا۟ أَن يُضَيِّفُوهُمَا فَوَجَدَا فِيهَا جِدَارًۭا يُرِيدُ أَن يَنقَضَّ فَأَقَامَهُۥ ۖ قَالَ لَوْ شِئْتَ لَتَّخَذْتَ عَلَيْهِ أَجْرًۭا

- 18:82: وَأَمَّا ٱلْجِدَارُ فَكَانَ لِغُلَٰمَيْنِ يَتِيمَيْنِ فِى ٱلْمَدِينَةِ وَكَانَ تَحْتَهُۥ كَنزٌۭ لَّهُمَا وَكَانَ أَبُوهُمَا صَٰلِحًۭا فَأَرَادَ رَبُّكَ أَن يَبْلُغَآ أَشُدَّهُمَا وَيَسْتَخْرِجَا كَنزَهُمَا رَحْمَةًۭ مِّن رَّبِّكَ ۚ وَمَا فَعَلْتُهُۥ عَنْ أَمْرِى ۚ ذَٰلِكَ تَأْوِيلُ مَا لَمْ تَسْطِع عَّلَيْهِ صَبْرًۭا

# Notes

## 18:82
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:79-82#5/r1] verses 18:82 | Amr ibn Dinar says Khidr and Elijah remain alive as long as the Quran remains, and die when it is lifted. «وعن عمرو بن دينار قال: إن الخضر وإلياس لا يزالان حيين في الأرض»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:79-82#5/r2] verses 18:82 | Abu Muhammad al-Lakhmi recounts many encounters with Khidr by righteous people; together with other reports, they make his survival probable. «حكايات كثيرة عن جماعة من الصالحين والصالحات بأنهم رأوا الخضر عليه السلام ولقوه»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:79-82#5/r3] verses 18:82 | Abu Ishaq identifies as Khidr the exceptionally good man whom a Muslim hadith says will confront the Dajjal near Medina. «وفي آخره قال أبو إسحاق: يعني أن هذا الرجل هو الخضر»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:79-82#5/r4] verses 18:82 | A report says Ali met Khidr and learned a supplication to say after prayer, promising great reward, forgiveness, and mercy; a similar report is attributed to Umar. «بسند يرفعه إلى علي بن أبي طالب رضي الله تعالى عنه أنه لقي الخضر وعلمه هذا الدعاء»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:79-82#5/r5] verses 18:82 | If Elijah survived to the Prophet's time, Khidr could have too; they are said to meet yearly at the Kaaba and exchange words of reliance on God. «وإذا جاز بقاء إلياس إلى عهد النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم جاز بقاء الخضر»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:79-82#5/r6] verses 18:82 | Ibn Abd al-Barr transmits Ali's story of an unseen caller comforting the Prophet's household after his death, whom the Companions took to be Khidr. «هتف هاتف من ناحية البيت يسمعون صوته ولا يرون شخصه»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:79-82#6/r1] verses 18:82 | The unseen caller's consolation says God replaces each loss and comforts every calamity; companions thought the caller was Khidr. «إن في الله خلفا من كل هالك، وعوضا من كل تالف»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:79-82#6/r2] verses 18:82 | In the hundred-year report, “the earth” can mean familiar Arabia, not remote regions of Gog and Magog or far Indian islands. «والألف واللام في قوله: «على الأرض» للعهد لا للجنس وهي أرض العرب»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:79-82#6/r3] verses 18:82 | Qurtubi admits his discussion has not answered the objection posed by the Dajjal report. «ولا جواب عن الدجال»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:79-82#6/r4] verses 18:82 | Al-Suhayli gives competing genealogies and legends of Khidr's birth from a king and Persian wife, including a foundling suckled by a sheep. «فعن ابن منبه أنه قال: أيليا بن ملكان بن فالغ بن شالخ»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:79-82#6/r5] verses 18:82 | A legend says Khidr found the spring of life, remained alive until the Dajjal, and will be killed by him before God revives him. «إلى أن وجد عين الحياة فشرب منها، فهو حي إلى أن يخرج الدجال»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:79-82#6/r6] verses 18:82 | Al-Bukhari and Ibn al-Arabi say Khidr died before the hundred years elapsed; Qurtubi reiterates his contrary view that Khidr lives to the present. «وقال البخاري وطائفة من أهل الحديث منهم شيخنا أبو بكر بن العربي رحمه الله تعالى: إنه مات»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:82/r1] verses 18:82 | The boys were apparently minors because the verse calls them orphans, though orphan status may remain as a compassionate description after puberty; for humans it is loss of father, for animals of mother. «هذان الغلامان صغيران بقرينة وصفهما باليتم»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:82/r10] verses 18:82 | The apparent meaning of “their father” is their immediate father. «ظاهر اللفظ والسابق منه أنه والدهما دنية»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:82/r11] verses 18:82 | Ja'far ibn Muhammad identifies their father as their seventh ancestor; another view calls him the tenth ancestor, Kashih, according to Muqatil. «هو الأب السابع قاله جعفر بن محمد. وقيل: العاشر»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:82/r12] verses 18:82 | Al-Naqqash gives the boys' mother's name as Dunya. «واسم أمهما دنيا ذكره النقاش»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:82/r13] verses 18:82 | The story indicates God protects a righteous person in himself and in distant descendants. «أن الله تعالى يحفظ الصالح في نفسه وفي ولده وإن بعدوا عنه»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:82/r14] verses 18:82 | A report says God preserves a righteous person through seven generations; Qurtubi connects this to the verse on God's guardianship of the righteous. «وقد روي أن الله تعالى يحفظ الصالح في سبعة من ذريته»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:82/r15] verses 18:82 | Khidr's statement that he did not act on his own authority implies that he was a prophet. «يقتضي أن الخضر نبي وقد تقدم الخلاف في ذلك»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:82/r16] verses 18:82 | Most readers use the shortened reading tastaṭiʿ in keeping with the codex. «وقرأ الجمهور «تسطع» قال أبو حاتم: كذا نقرأ كما في خط المصحف»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:82/r17] verses 18:82 | Ikrima asks Ibn Abbas why Moses' companion is omitted; Ibn Abbas says the youth drank from the water of life and became immortal, then the sage sealed him in a boat and sent him into the sea until Resurrection because he drank what he was not allowed. «فقال عكرمة لابن عباس: لم يسمع لفتى موسى بذكر وقد كان معه؟»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:82/r2] verses 18:82 | The Prophet's saying “no orphanhood after puberty” supports the inference that the boys were children. «لا يتم بعد بلوغ»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:82/r3] verses 18:82 | The word madina shows that qarya can also mean a city. «أن القرية تسمى مدينة ومنه الحديث»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:82/r4] verses 18:82 | A hadith describes a qarya that consumes other settlements. «أمرت بقرية تأكل القرى»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:82/r5] verses 18:82 | In the migration report, a man says he is from the people of Medina, meaning Mecca. «فقال الرجل: من أهل المدينة»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:82/r6] verses 18:82 | Ikrima and Qatada interpret the treasure as substantial material wealth, the apparent lexical meaning of kanz. «فقال عكرمة وقتادة: كان مالا جسيما وهو الظاهر من اسم الكنز»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:82/r7] verses 18:82 | Ibn Abbas instead says the treasure was knowledge written on buried pages. «وقال ابن عباس: كان علما في صحف مدفونة»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:82/r8] verses 18:82 | Another report describes a gold tablet inscribed with maxims about providence, provision, death, judgment, and worldly instability. «كان لوحا من ذهب مكتوبا فيه بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم»
[QURTUBI-FULL:18:82/r9] verses 18:82 | A similar tablet report is transmitted by Uthman from the Prophet, and versions come from Ikrima and Umar, freedman of Ghufra. «وروي نحوه عن عكرمة وعمر مولى غفرة، ورواه عثمان بن عفان رضي الله عنه عن النبي»
[QUSHAYRI:v2p230/r1] verses 18:82 | Khidr repaired the wall to preserve the two orphans' treasure and avoid seeking compensation from people. «أما تسوية الجدار فلاستبقاء كنز الغلامين وترك طلب الرفق من الخلق»
[RAZI-FULL:v21p127/r1] verses 18:82 | Al-Asamm takes وما فعلته عن أمري to mean Khidr acted by God's revelation, evidence that he was a prophet; al-Razi calls the argument plainly weak. «الحجة الخامسة : احتج الأصم على نبوته بقوله في أثناء القصة : { وما فعلته عن أمرى } ومعناه فعلته بوحي الله»
[RAZI-FULL:v21p136/r2] verses 18:82 | Building the wall imposed a lesser hardship than its collapse, which would destroy the orphans' property. «المشقة الحاصلة بسبب الإقدام على إقامة ذلك الجدار ضررها أقل من سقوطه»
[RAZI-FULL:v21p137#2/r10] verses 18:82 | Another account says the righteous father held people's deposits and returned them safely. «وذكروا أيضا أن ذلك الأب الصالح كان الناس يضعون الودائع»
[RAZI-FULL:v21p137#2/r11] verses 18:82 | The boys may have been unaware of the treasure while their absent guardian knew it, explaining how they could later recover it. «لعل اليتيمين كانا جاهلين به إلا أن وصيهما كان عالما به»
[RAZI-FULL:v21p137#2/r12] verses 18:82 | The sage acted by God's command rather than personal judgment, since damaging property and spilling blood require decisive revelation. «ما فعلت ما رأيت من هذه الأحوال عن أمري واجتهادي ورأيي وإنما فعلته بأمر الله»
[RAZI-FULL:v21p137#2/r3] verses 18:82 | God had the sage shore up the nearly fallen wall to protect the orphans' treasure in view of their rights and their father's righteousness. «فأراد الله إبقاء ذلك الكنز على ذينك اليتيمين»
