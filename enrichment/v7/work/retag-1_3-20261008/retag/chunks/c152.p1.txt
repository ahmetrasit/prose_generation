[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT:p273/r4] verses 90:17 | The call to mutual counsel in patience and compassion follows the discussion of the difficult righteous acts. «وذكر بعد الآيات التواصي بالصبر والتواصي بالمرحمة»
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT:p274/r2] verses 90:17 | Believers need patience to obey God and avoid sin; patience is a governing thread of the surah. «والذين آمنوا يحتاجون إلى الصبر على الطاعات والصبر عن المعاصي»
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT:p274/r3] verses 90:2, 90:3, 90:17 | Mercy fits every sense of hill: the resident Prophet deserves mercy rather than abuse, and at the conquest he treated Quraysh with clemency, calling it the day of mercy and releasing them. «وقال في ذلك اليوم: "اليومَ يومُ المَرْحمة"»
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT:p274/r4] verses 90:3, 90:17 | The parent-and-child bond is one of mercy and dutiful care. «فإن العلاقة بين الوالد وولده، علاقة رحمة وبرٍّ»
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT:p275/r2] verses 90:17 | Repeating the verb and preposition with both patience and mercy stresses that each separately deserves mutual exhortation; repeating only the preposition is weaker, and simple coordination weaker still. «والتعبير الأول أقوى التعبيرات للدلالة على أهمية كُلٍّ منهما، وذلك لتكرار الفعل مع حرف الجر»
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT:p276/r1] verses 90:4, 90:8, 90:9, 90:10, 90:11, 90:17 | Patience comes first because the surah has already described human toil, the difficult ascent, and the two paths; mercy follows the subsequent duties of freeing and feeding. «فقدم التواصي بالصبر لمّا تقدّم ما يدعو إليه»
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT:p276/r2] verses 90:17 | Surat al-Asr first enjoins mutual counsel in truth because that surah treats loss generally; this surah addresses one part of truth, mercy and feeding. «ففي سورة (العصر) كان الكلام على خسارة الإنسان على وجه العموم»
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT:p276/r3] verses 90:17 | Truth precedes patience in al-Asr because truth is primary and patience must be directed toward truth; holding to truth itself requires patience, while this surah orders patience first for contextual reasons. «إذ ليس المهم هو الصبر، وإنما المهم أن يصبر على ماذا»
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT:p276/r4] verses 90:17, 90:18 | The believers who trust, counsel patience and mercy, free slaves, and feed the needy are people of the right side, associated with goodness, blessing, happiness, and receiving their records in the right hand. «الميمنة: مفعلة من اليُمن، وهو الخير والبركة أو من اليمن»
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT:p284/r6] verses 90:4, 90:6, 90:17 | The surah is woven around five threads: human toil, breadth of meaning, collective action, patience, and mercy. «خط المكابدة التي تدل عليه الآية {لَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا الإنسان فِي كَبَدٍ}»
