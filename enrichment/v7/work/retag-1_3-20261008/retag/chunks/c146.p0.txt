# Verses
- 78:38: يَوْمَ يَقُومُ ٱلرُّوحُ وَٱلْمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ صَفًّۭا ۖ لَّا يَتَكَلَّمُونَ إِلَّا مَنْ أَذِنَ لَهُ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنُ وَقَالَ صَوَابًۭا

# Notes

## 78:38
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v9p12/r6] verses 78:38 | Al-Sha'bi, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, and al-Dahhak identify the Spirit as Gabriel. «أنه جبريل عليه السلام قاله الشعبي وسعيد بن جبير والضحاك»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v9p12/r7] verses 78:38 | Al-Hasan and Qatada identify the Spirit as the children of Adam. «أنهم بنو آدم قاله الحسن وقتادة»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v9p12/r8] verses 78:38 | Zayd ibn Aslam identifies the Spirit as the Qur'an. «أنه القرآن قاله زيد بن أسلم»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v9p12/r9] verses 78:38 | Muqatil ibn Hayyan identifies the Spirit as the noblest angels. «أنهم أشرف الملائكة قاله مقاتل بن حيان»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v9p13/r1] verses 78:38 | Al-Sha'bi says there are two ranks, one of the Spirit and one of the angels; accordingly, the Spirit stands as a rank and the angels as a rank. «قال الشعبي هما سماطان سماط من الروح وسماط من الملائكة فعلى هذا يكون المعنى يوم يقوم الروح صفا والملائكة صفا»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v9p13/r2] verses 78:38 | Ibn Qutayba explains “in a rank” to mean ranks. «وقال ابن قتيبة معنى قوله تعالى صفا صفوفا»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v9p13/r3] verses 78:38 | “They do not speak” refers to all creatures, except those whom the Most Merciful permits to speak. «قوله تعالى لا يتكلمون يعني الخلق كلهم إلا من أذن له الرحمن في الكلام»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v9p13/r4] verses 78:38 | Most commentators take “and says what is right” to mean saying the testimony of God's oneness in earthly life. «وقال صوابا أي قال في الدنيا صوابا وهو الشهادة بالتوحيد عند أكثر المفسرين»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v9p13/r5] verses 78:38 | Mujahid says it means speaking truth in worldly life and acting on it. «وقال مجاهد قال حقا في الدنيا وعمل به»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p309/r10] verses 78:38 | A report from Ibn Mas'ud places the Spirit in the fourth heaven, greater than heavens, mountains, and angels, praising God 12,000 times daily and generating an angel from each praise; Ibn Kathir calls this very unusual. «عن ابن مسعود قال: الروح: في السماء الرابعة هو أعظم من السموات ومن الجبال ومن الملائكة، يسبح كل يوم اثني عشر ألف تسبيحة»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p309/r11] verses 78:38 | A report attributed to the Prophet says God has an angel who could swallow the seven heavens and earth in one bite if commanded, and whose praise is God's glorification wherever He is. «سمعت رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم يقول»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p309/r3] verses 78:38 | Al-Awfi transmits from Ibn Abbas that the Spirit means the souls of human beings. «رواه العوفي، عن ابن عباس: أنهم أرواح بني آدم.»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p309/r4] verses 78:38 | Al-Hasan and Qatada identify the Spirit as human beings; Qatada adds that this was something Ibn Abbas kept concealed. «قاله الحسن، وقتادة، وقال قتادة: هذا (1) مما كان ابن عباس يكتمه.»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p309/r5] verses 78:38 | They say the Spirit is a created species resembling humans, neither angels nor humans, and capable of eating and drinking. «قاله ابن عباس، ومجاهد، وأبو صالح والأعمش.»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p309/r6] verses 78:38 | Al-Sha'bi, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, and al-Dahhak identify the Spirit as Gabriel, supported by the verse calling him the Trustworthy Spirit. «هو جبريل. قاله الشعبي، وسعيد بن جبير، والضحاك.»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p309/r7] verses 78:38 | Maqatil ibn Hayyan describes the Spirit as the noblest angel, nearest to the Lord, and bearer of revelation. «وقال مقاتل بن حيان: الروح: أشرف الملائكة، وأقرب إلى الرب عز وجل، وصاحب الوحي.»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p309/r8] verses 78:38 | Ibn Zayd says the Spirit is the Qur'an, as in the verse describing revelation as a spirit from God's command. «والخامس: أنه القرآن. قاله ابن زيد، كقوله: {وكذلك أوحينا إليك روحا من أمرنا} الآية [الشورى: 52]»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p309/r9] verses 78:38 | Ibn Abbas, through Ali ibn Abi Talha, says the Spirit is an immense angel, one of the greatest in creation. «قال علي بن أبي طلحة، عن ابن عباس: قوله: {يوم يقوم الروح} قال: هو ملك عظيم من أعظم الملائكة خلقا.»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p310/r1] verses 78:38 | Ibn Kathir says the huge-angel report is very unusual and questions its attribution to the Prophet; it may instead stop with Ibn Abbas and derive from Israelite reports. «وهذا حديث غريب جدا، وفي رفعه نظر، وقد يكون موقوفا على ابن عباس، ويكون مما تلقاه من الإسرائيليات»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p310/r2] verses 78:38 | Al-Tabari declines to choose among the interpretations of the Spirit, while Ibn Kathir considers human beings the most likely meaning. «وتوقف ابن جرير فلم يقطع بواحد من هذه الأقوال كلها، والأشبه -والله أعلم-أنهم بنو آدم.»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p310/r3] verses 78:38 | Only those whom the All-Merciful permits may speak, as in the verse about no soul speaking except by God's permission and the sound report that only messengers speak then. «وقوله: {إلا من أذن له الرحمن} كقوله: {لا تكلم نفس إلا بإذنه}»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p310/r4] verses 78:38 | Ibn Kathir glosses correct speech as truth and includes the shahada among truths, noting Abu Salih and Ikrima as authorities for that gloss. «وقوله {وقال صوابا} أي: حقا»
[IBNQUTAYBA-GHARIB:v1p511/r1] verses 78:38 | He construes صفا as صفوفا (rows); the Eid expression “day of the row” and 89:22’s “row upon row” support that plural sense. «{يوم يقوم الروح والملائكة صفا} أي صفوفا. ويقال ليوم [العيد: يوم] الصف»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p147/r1] verses 78:38 | The angels are worshiped as God’s daughters by idolaters, but Gabriel and other angels stand before God as servants and can only speak by permission and truthfully. «The verse says that Gabriel and other angels will stand»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p147/r2] verses 78:38 | The verse rules out the belief that deities can cajole God or intercede as they wish. «In other words, if the Idolaters wrongly believe»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p147/r3] verses 78:38 | Al-rūḥ means Gabriel, a common Quranic usage; he is named first to show that no other angel has authority, while the alternative “human souls” lacks evidence. «there is no reason to accept this interpretation.»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p147/r6] verses 78:38 | No one may speak on God’s behalf unless permitted, and only truthfully. «No one will have even the authority to speak on His behalf»
