# Verses
- 39:6: خَلَقَكُم مِّن نَّفْسٍۢ وَٰحِدَةٍۢ ثُمَّ جَعَلَ مِنْهَا زَوْجَهَا وَأَنزَلَ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلْأَنْعَٰمِ ثَمَٰنِيَةَ أَزْوَٰجٍۢ ۚ يَخْلُقُكُمْ فِى بُطُونِ أُمَّهَٰتِكُمْ خَلْقًۭا مِّنۢ بَعْدِ خَلْقٍۢ فِى ظُلُمَٰتٍۢ ثَلَٰثٍۢ ۚ ذَٰلِكُمُ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّكُمْ لَهُ ٱلْمُلْكُ ۖ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ۖ فَأَنَّىٰ تُصْرَفُونَ

# Notes

## 39:6
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p519#2/r10] verses 39:6 | One interpretation of “sent down” says the first animals were created in heaven and brought down to earth. «قيل معناه: أن المخلوق الأول من هذه الأنعام خلق في السماء وأهبط إلى الأرض»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p519#2/r11] verses 39:6 | Another interpretation is that God's decree to create the animals came from Him; benefactions and rain are conventionally described as descending from heaven. «وقالت فرقة: بل لما نزل الأمر بخلقه وإيجاده من عند الله، وكانت العادة في نعم الله ورحمته وأمطاره وغير ذلك أن يقال فيها إنها من السماء عبر عن هذه ب أنزل»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p519#2/r12] verses 39:6 | A third explanation links rainfall to grasses and plants, and plants to the animals' fattening and livelihood, so their provision is said to descend. «لما كانت الأمطار تنزل وكانت الأعشاب والنبات عن المطر، وكانت هذه الأنعام عن النبات في سمنها ومعاشها، قال في هذه أنزل»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p519#2/r13] verses 39:6 | The author introduces a rajaz poem as evidence for describing animal provision as descending through a gradual process. «قال في هذه أنزل فهو على التدريج كما قال الراجز»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p519#2/r14] verses 39:6 | He explains the gradual account through the chain from rain to vegetation to the animals livelihood. «وكانت هذه الأنعام عن النبات في سمنها ومعاشها، قال في هذه أنزل»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p519#2/r2] verses 39:6 | The surface sequence of “then” seems to place the creation of the spouse after the descendants, although that is not the actual order. «وقوله تعالى: ثم جعل ظاهر اللفظ يقتضي أن جعل الزوجة من النفس هو بعد أن خلق الخلق منها، وليس الأمر كذلك»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p519#2/r3] verses 39:6 | One group says “created you from one soul” refers to extracting descendants from Adam's back, before Eve was created. «فقالت فرقة قوله: خلقكم من نفس واحدة هو أخذ الذرية من ظهر آدم وذلك شيء كان قبل خلق حواء»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p519#2/r4] verses 39:6 | Another group takes “then” as ordering the account rather than the events: Eve had been made earlier, as the Abu Nuwas verse illustrates. «وقالت فرقة: إنما هي لترتيب الأخبار لا لترتيب المعاني»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p519#2/r5] verses 39:6 | Abu Nuwas's verse illustrates that a later-mentioned father and grandfather may have preceded the person in the stated sequence. «قل لمن ساد ثم ساد أبوه ... ثم قد ساد قبل ذلك جده»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p519#2/r6] verses 39:6 | A third group understands the sequence as reporting what was already fixed in God's knowledge, so the certain future is narrated as settled before it came into being. «وقالت فرقة قوله: خلقكم من نفس واحدة عبارة عن سبق ذلك في علم الله تعالى»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p519#2/r7] verses 39:6 | On the foreknowledge view, the events are ordered in the report although humanity's emergence from Adam actually followed the creation of Eve. «فجاءت معان مترتبة وإن كان خروج خلق العالم من آدم إلى الوجود إنما يجيء بعد ذلك»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p519#2/r8] verses 39:6 | Ibn ʿAṭiyya favors the report that Eve was created from Adam's short rib, supported by the hadith that a woman was created from a rib and bending it breaks it. «وخلقت من ضلعه القصيري فيما روي، ويؤيد هذا الحديث الذي فيه أن المرأة خلقت من ضلع، فإن ذهبت تقيمه كسرته»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p519#2/r9] verses 39:6 | Another group says Eve was created from leftover clay of Adam; Ibn ʿAṭiyya says the rib account is sounder. «وقالت فرقة: خلقت حواء من بقية طين آدم والأول أصح»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p520/r1] verses 39:6 | The eight kinds are male-female pairs: sheep, goats, cattle, and camels. «وجعلها ثمانية، لأن كل واحد فيه زوج للذكر من فرعه، وهي الضأن والمعز والبقر والإبل»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p520/r2] verses 39:6 | Ibn Zayd says womb creation in successive stages includes development in Adam's and the fathers' backs before the mother's womb. «قال ابن زيد، معناه: يخلقكم في البطون خلقا من بعد خلق آخر في ظهر آدم وظهور الآباء»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p520/r3] verses 39:6 | Mujahid, Ikrima, and al-Suddi take the successive creation in the womb to mean successive stages such as the clot and morsel. «وقال مجاهد وعكرمة والسدي: يخلقكم في البطون رتبا خلقا من بعد خلق على المضغة والعلقة وغير ذلك»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p520/r4] verses 39:6 | Isa ibn Umar and Talha ibn Musarrif assimilate qaf into kaf in “yakhluqukum” throughout the Qurʾan. «وقرأ عيسى بن عمر وطلحة بن مصرف: يخلقكم بإدغام القاف في الكاف في جميع القرآن»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p520/r5] verses 39:6 | Most readers pronounce “mothers” with a damma on the hamza. «وقرأ الجمهور: أمهاتكم بضم الهمزة»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p520/r6] verses 39:6 | Yahya ibn Waththab pronounces the hamza in “mothers” with kasra; both pronunciations are dialects. «وقرأ يحيى بن وثاب: بكسرها وهي لغتان»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p520/r7] verses 39:6 | One group identifies the three darknesses as the father's back, the mother's womb, and the placenta in the womb. «قالت فرقة: الأولى هي ظهر الأب، ثم رحم الأم، ثم المشيمة في البطن»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p520/r8] verses 39:6 | Mujahid, Qatada, and Ibn Zayd identify them as the placenta, womb, and abdomen. «وقال مجاهد وقتادة وابن زيد: هي المشيمة والرحم والبطن»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p520/r9] verses 39:6 | These signs alert people to the sole Creator who deserves worship and refute idol worship; the proofs make it clear that God is their Lord, so how can they turn away? «وهذه الآيات كلها هي معتبر وتنبيه لهم على الخالق الصانع الذي لا يستحق العبادة غيره، وهذا كله في رد أمر الأصنام والإفساد لها»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v7p163/r1] verses 39:6 | The single soul is Adam, and his spouse was created from him before his descendants were created. «﴿ خلقكم من نفس واحدة ﴾ يعني آدم ﴿ ثم جعل منها زوجها ﴾ أي قبل خلقكم جعل منها زوجها»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v7p163/r2] verses 39:6 | The sequence marker then can precede a later-mentioned event that actually happened earlier, as in a statement contrasting what was given today with more given yesterday. «هذا اختيار الفراء»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v7p163/r3] verses 39:6 | Another interpretation is that the sequence simply announces afterward that God created Adam's spouse from him. «وقال غيره ثم أخبركم أنه خلق منها زوجها»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v7p163/r4] verses 39:6 | God created the eight pairs of livestock; their details were given earlier in Sūrat al-Anʿām. «﴿ وأنزل لكم من الأنعام ﴾ أي خلق ﴿ ثمانية أزواج ﴾ وقد بيناها في سورة الأنعام 143»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v7p163/r5] verses 39:6 | Creation after creation is the stages of a drop, clot, lump, bones, flesh, hair growth, and further changes until birth. «هذا قول الجمهور»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v7p163/r6] verses 39:6 | Ibn Zayd instead understands the stages in the womb as following the person's creation in Adam's back. «وقال ابن زيد خلقا في البطون من بعد خلقكم في ظهر آدم»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v7p163/r7] verses 39:6 | The three darknesses are the abdomen, womb, and placenta. «ظلمة البطن وظلمة الرحم وظلمة المشيمة قاله الجمهور وابن زيد معهم»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v7p163/r8] verses 39:6 | An alternative set of three darknesses is the father's loins, the woman's abdomen, and the womb. «وقال أبو عبيدة إنها ظلمة صلب الأب وظلمة بطن المرأة وظلمة الرحم»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v7p86#2/r1] verses 39:6 | The eight livestock pairs are created for people from the animals' offspring: two each from sheep, goats, camels, and cattle, as listed in 6:143–144. «من ظهور الأنعام ثمانية، أزواج وهي المذكورة في سورة الأنعام»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v7p86#2/r2] verses 39:6 | In the womb, a person develops from sperm to clot, then lump, flesh, bones, sinews, and veins, receives a spirit, and becomes a new creation, as in 23:14. «أي: يكون أحدكم أولا نطفة، ثم يكون علقة»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v7p86#2/r3] verses 39:6 | The three darknesses are the womb, the placenta or protective covering, and the abdomen, according to Ibn ʿAbbās and the listed early authorities. «قال ابن عباس، ومجاهد، وعكرمة، وأبو مالك، والضحاك»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v7p86#3/r1] verses 39:6 | The creator of the heavens, earth, people, and their ancestors is their Lord, sovereign and controller; only He deserves worship, so worshipping another is irrational diversion. «هو الرب له الملك والتصرف (8) في جميع ذلك»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v7p86/r3] verses 39:6 | Human groups differ in kind, language, and color while descending from Adam and his wife Eve, as also stated in 4:1. «خلقكم مع اختلاف أجناسكم وأصنافكم وألسنتكم وألوانكم من نفس واحدة، وهو آدم عليه السلام»
[IBNQUTAYBA-GHARIB:v1p382#4/r1] verses 39:6 | The eight pairs of livestock are eight kinds, those named by God in Sūrat al-Anʿām. «أي ثمانية أصناف، وهي التي ذكرها الله -عز ذكره- في سورة الأنعام»
[IBNQUTAYBA-GHARIB:v1p382#4/r2] verses 39:6 | Creation in stages means a clinging form after a drop, then a lump after the clinging form. «أي علقة بعد نطفة ومضغة بعد علقة»
[IBNQUTAYBA-GHARIB:v1p382#4/r3] verses 39:6 | The three darknesses are said to be the placenta, womb, and abdomen. «يقال (5) : ظلمة المشيمة وظلمة الرحم وظلمة البطن.»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-6-2:p521/r2] verses 39:6, 39:7 | People depend on God, return to Him, and face individual judgment; He knows every deed and hidden secret. «everyone is destined to return to God; on that Day, no one will bear the burden of any other person»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-6-2:p527/r3] verses 39:6 | God created every human from one soul and made its mate from the same species; since God alone creates, no other being can be Lord. «it is God Who has created every one from a single soul, and then created its mate from the same species.»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-6-2:p527/r4] verses 39:6 | God alone creates, provides for creatures, and knows all
