## 18:81
[DURR-FULL:v5p428/r6] verses 18:81 | Atiyya glosses zakatan as religion and rahman as affection; the replacement was a girl who bore a prophet. «في قوله: {خيرا منه زكاة} قال: دينا {وأقرب رحما} قال: مودة»
[DURR-FULL:v5p428/r7] verses 18:81 | A report says the parents received a girl in place of the boy, and she bore two prophets. «قال: أبدلهما جارية مكان الغلام ولدت نبيين»
[ELMALILI:18:80-81/r5] verses 18:81 | God would replace him with a child better in purity and closer in mercy, sparing the parents his harm and compensating their grief with a more beloved child. «Hem oğlanın yüzünden görecekleri şerden kurtulacaklar, hem de onun ölümüyle duyacakları acıya mukabil daha sevimli bir evlâda nâil olacaklardı»
[ELMALILI:18:80-81/r6] verses 18:81 | A report says God gave the parents a daughter in his place; she became a prophet's mother, and a community was guided through that prophet. «ona bedel Allah bunlara bir kız vermiş ve bu kız bir peygamber vâlidesi olmuş ve o peygamberin eliyle ümmetlerden bir ümmet hidâyete ermiştir»
[FARISI-HUJJA:v5p164/r1] verses 18:81 | They lighten the verb in the listed occurrences, including this verse. «فقرأ ابن كثير وعاصم في رواية أبي بكر: أن يبدلهما»
[FARISI-HUJJA:v5p164/r2] verses 18:81 | They use the doubled form in all the listed occurrences. «وقرأ نافع وأبو عمرو في الكهف والتحريم ونون والنور مشددا كله»
[FARISI-HUJJA:v5p164/r3] verses 18:81 | They lighten the form here, in al-Taḥrīm and Nūn, but double it in al-Nūr; Ḥafṣ from ʿĀṣim follows that distribution. «وقرأ ابن عامر وحمزة والكسائي في الكهف والتحريم ونون مخففا»
[FARISI-HUJJA:v5p164/r4] verses 18:81 | Badala and abdala are close in meaning, but badala is preferable because the Qur'an repeatedly uses tabdīl and says there is no changing God's words, without using ibdāl in that formula. «إلا أن بدل ينبغي أن يكون أرجح لما جاء في التنزيل من قوله: لا تبديل لكلمات الله»
[FARISI-HUJJA:v5p164/r5] verses 18:81 | Istibdāl can mean ibdāl, just as istijāba can mean ijāba; the cited verse asks to substitute one wife for another. «وإن أردتم استبدال زوج مكان زوج»
[FARISI-HUJJA:v5p164/r6] verses 18:81 | Some distinguish tabdīl as removing one thing and bringing another, while ibdāl can apply whether the first thing remains or not; the source's examples show both forms overlap, so no firm distinction holds here. «فليس ينفصل بدل من أبدل في هذا النحو بشيء»
[FARRA:v2p157/r2] verses 18:81 | Zakat means righteousness, and raḥm means that the child would be more deserving of compassion; raḥm is the maṣdar of raḥimta. «وقوله: خيرا منه زكاة [81] صلاحا (وأقرب رحما) يقول: أقرب أن يرحما به. وهو مصدر رحمت.»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v16p13/r1] verses 18:81 | The word رحم here is vocalized with ḍamma on rāʾ and a still ḥāʾ; Ibn Ashur relates its form to a plural expressing abundance. «والرحم : بضم الراء وسكون الحاء : نظير الكثر للكثرة»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v16p13/r4] verses 18:81 | The majority reads يبدلهما with fatḥa on bāʾ and a doubled dāl, from tabdīl. «وقرأ الجمهور أن يبدلهما } بفتح الموحدة وتشديد الدال من التبديل»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v16p13/r5] verses 18:81 | Ibn Kathir, Ibn Amir, Asim, Hamza, al-Kisai, and Khalaf read the verb with a still bāʾ and a light dāl, from ibdāl. «وقرأه ابن كثير ، وابن عامر ، وعاصم ، وحمزة ، والكسائي ، وخلف بسكون الموحدة وتخفيف الدال من الإبدال»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v3p536#2/r1] verses 18:81 | Ruʾba’s line supports rahm as mercy shown to the parents or child. «يا منزل الرحم على إدريسا ... ومنزل اللعن على إبليسا»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v3p536#2/r2] verses 18:81 | Ibn ʿAmir rounds the ha in rahman; others leave it silent, with variation from Abu ʿAmr. Ibn ʿAbbas reads “their Lord is purer than him.” «وقرأ ابن عامر «رحما» بضم الحاء، وقرأ الباقون «رحما» بسكونها، واختلف عن أبي عمرو، وقرأ ابن عباس»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v3p536#2/r3] verses 18:81 | Ibn Jurayj reports the parents received a Muslim boy, and separately a girl; another report says the mother was pregnant with a Muslim boy when the first was killed. «وروي عن ابن جريج أنهما بدلا غلاما مسلما، وروي عن ابن جريج أنهما بدلا جارية،»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v3p536#2/r4] verses 18:81 | Al-Naqqash says she bore seventy prophets, also attributed to Ibn ʿAbbas by al-Mahdawi; Ibn ʿAṭiyya rejects this as far-fetched because prophetic abundance is known among Israelites and she was not one. «وحكى النقاش أنها ولدت هي وذريتها سبعين نبيا، وذكره المهدوي عن ابن عباس.»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v3p536/r8] verses 18:81 | Most read yubaddilahuma with open ba and doubled dal; Ibn Muhaysin, al-Hasan, and ʿAsim lighten it and make ba silent. «وقرأ الجمهور «أن يبدلهما» بفتح الباء وشد الدال، وقرأ ابن محيصن»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v3p536/r9] verses 18:81 | Zakat means noble character, dignity, and calmness containing goodness and intent; rahm means mercy, either God showing the parents mercy or they showing it to the child. «و «الزكاة» : شرف الخلق، والوقار والسكينة المنطوية على خير ونية،»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v5p180/r1] verses 18:81 | Ibn Kathir and Abu Bakr from ʿAsim read yubdilahumā lightly; Nafiʿ and Abu ʿAmr read it with a doubled consonant. «قرأ ابن كثير وأبو بكر عن عاصم أن يبدلهما بالتخفيف وقرأ نافع وأبو عمرو بالتشديد»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v5p180/r2] verses 18:81 | The replacement being “better in purity” is variously explained as better in religion by Ibn Abbas, deeds by Muqatil, or righteousness by al-Farraʾ. «أحدها دينا قاله ابن عباس والثاني عملا قاله مقاتل والثالث صلاحا قاله الفراء»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v5p180/r3] verses 18:81 | Readings of raḥman vary in vowel pattern; the word rahm and rahma carry senses of tenderness and mercy, supported by a cited poetic line. «ومعنى الرحم والرحم في اللغة العطف والرحمة قال الشاعر وكيف بظلم جارية ومنها اللين والرحم»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v5p180/r4] verses 18:81 | Ibn Abbas and Qatada understand “nearer in raḥm” as more connected to kinship and dutiful to the parents; al-Zajjaj says more affectionate and closer in kinship. «أحدهما أوصل للرحم وأبر للوالدين قاله ابن عباس وقتادة»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v5p180/r5] verses 18:81 | Al-Farraʾ reads the phrase as “more likely to be shown mercy.” «والثاني أقرب أن يرحما به قاله الفراء»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v5p180/r6] verses 18:81 | Most commentators say the replacement was a girl; ʿAtaʾ reports Ibn Abbas saying she bore seventy prophets. Ibn Jurayj instead says it was a Muslim boy. «أحدهما جارية قاله الأكثرون وروى عطاء عن ابن عباس قال أبدلهما به جارية ولدت سبعين نبيا»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v5p184#2/r4] verses 18:81 | The replacement is purer and more compassionate to the parents; Qatada instead specifies a child more dutiful to them. «أي: ولدا أزكى من هذا، وهما أرحم به منه، قاله ابن جريج.»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v5p184#2/r5] verses 18:81 | Reports say the parents received a daughter; another says the mother was pregnant with a Muslim boy when Khidr killed the first child. «وقد تقدم أنهما بدلا جارية. وقيل لما قتله الخضر كانت أمه حاملا بغلام مسلم. قاله ابن جريح»
[IBNKHALAWAYH-HUJJA:v1p229/r1] verses 18:81 | Raḥman with ḍamma or sukūn on ḥāʾ are both dialect forms; the word means mercy, affection, or closeness of kin. «يقرأ بضم الحاء وإسكانها وهما لغتان كالعمر»
[IBNMUJAHID:v1p396/r2] verses 18:81, 24:55, 66:5, 68:32 | Ibn Kathīr and Abū Bakr from ʿĀṣim lighten the four cited forms; Nāfiʿ and Abū ʿAmr double them throughout; Ibn ʿĀmir, Ḥamza, al-Kisāʾī, and Ḥafṣ lighten all except the heavy form at al-Nūr 24:55. «29 واختلفوا فى التخفيف والتشديد من قوله ﴿ أن يبدلهما ربهما ﴾ 81»
[IBNMUJAHID:v1p396/r3] verses 18:81 | Ibn Kathīr, Nāfiʿ, ʿĀṣim, Ḥamza, and al-Kisāʾī quiesce ḥāʾ in raḥman; Ibn ʿĀmir uses the heavy form. Abū ʿAmr is reported with both, and transmitters permit either vocalization. «30 واختلفوا فى التخفيف والتثقيل من قوله ﴿ وأقرب رحما ﴾ 81»
[IBNQUTAYBA-GHARIB:v1p270#5/r1] verses 18:81 | Raḥman means mercy and affection; in 18:85, sabab means a path. «{وأقرب رحما} أي رحمة وعطفا. {فأتبع سببا} أي طريقا.»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-4-surah-yunus-10-surah-maryam-19:p481/r4] verses 18:81 | The word “raḥm” means affection, sympathy, and concern. «means “affection, sympathy and concern.”»
