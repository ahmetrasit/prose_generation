[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p37/r15] verses 20:5 | Mālik said istiwā is known while its modality is unknown, questioning it is an innovation, and the questioner should be expelled. «الاستواء معلوم والكيفية مجهولة والسؤال عن هذا بدعة وأظنك رجل سوء أخرجوه عني»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p37/r16] verses 20:5 | Abū al-Maʿālī rejects refusing to discuss the interpretation because believers agree istiwā is not used in its ordinary Arabic sense and an explanation is necessary. «وضعف أبو المعالي قول من قال لا يتكلم في تفسيرها»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v5p273/r1] verses 20:5 | The safest approach to God's establishment over the Throne follows the Salaf: affirm the Qur'an and Sunnah wording without asking how, distorting, likening, negating, or making it comparable. «بما أغنى عن إعادته أيضا، وأن المسلك الأسلم في (8) ذلك طريقة السلف»
[IBNQUTAYBA-GHARIB:v1p277#2/r1] verses 20:5 | Abū ʿUbayda interprets istawā as rising above, as in being above an animal or a house. «قال أبو عبيدة: علا. قال: وتقول استويت فوق الدابة، واستويت فوق البيت.»
[IBNQUTAYBA-GHARIB:v1p277#2/r2] verses 20:5 | Another interpreter takes istawā to mean settled, citing Noah's settling in the ark and Moses reaching maturity and settling. «وقال غيره: استوى: استقر. واحتج بقول الله عز وجل: {فإذا استويت أنت ومن معك على الفلك}»
[JISHUMI:v6p4636/r11] verses 20:5 | The anthropomorphists cannot establish direction or place from istiwāʾ: it admits multiple meanings, as does the Throne, and direction belongs to bodies because it entails spatial occupation. «ولا تعلق للمشبهة بالآية في إثبات الجهة والمكان؛ لأن الاستواء إذا احتمل معاني فليس لهم أن يحملوه على المكان،»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v3p53#2/r1] verses 20:5 | One reading makes “the All-Merciful” genitive as an adjective of the one who created; the nominative is preferable, either as praise or as a subject pointing back to the Creator. «قرىء ( الرحمان ) مجرورا صفة لمن خلق والرفع أحسن»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v3p53#2/r3] verses 20:5 | Establishing oneself on a throne conventionally signifies kingship, whether or not the ruler literally sits on a throne; the expression is vivid and explanatory. «جعلوه كناية عن الملك فقالوا : استوى فلان على العرش يريدون ملك وإن لم يقعد على السرير البتة»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v3p53#2/r4] verses 20:5 | Likewise, an open or tied hand conventionally means generosity or stinginess without imagining a literal hand or its binding. «ونحوه قولك : يد فلان مبسوطة ، ويد فلان مغلولة ، بمعنى أنه جواد أو بخيل»
[KASHSHAF-FULL:v3p53#2/r5] verses 20:5 | Reading the hand as bounty and then forcing the dual form is strained and departs from rhetorical understanding. «والتفسير بالنعمة والتمحل للتثنية من ضيق الطعن والمسافرة عن علم البيان مسيرة أعوام»
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:20:5/r1] verses 20:5 | Because the whole universe is under the sovereignty of the All-Merciful, every being is surrounded by His mercy and receives its share, whether it realizes this or not; the commentary points to the discussion of the Throne and istiwāʾ at 7:54. «Bütün evren rahmân olan Allah’ın hükümranlığı altında olduğuna göre, varlıklar âlemi O’nun merhametiyle kuşatılmış demektir, her varlık fark etse de etmese de O’nun rahmetinden payına düşeni almaktadır»
[MAJAZ:v2p15/r5] verses 20:5 | Istawā means rose or ascended above; Arabic uses it of rising over a mount, camel, mountain, or house. «أي علا، يقال: استويت فوق الدابة وعلى البعير وعلى الجبل وفوق البيت، أي علوت عليه وفوقه»
[MAJAZ:v2p15/r6] verses 20:5 | Al-Raḥmān may be nominative by detaching it from the preceding genitive and beginning anew. «ورفع الرحمن فى مكانين: أحدهما على القطع من الأول المجرور والابتداء»
[MAJAZ:v2p15/r7] verses 20:5 | Alternatively, its nominative is analyzed through the verb: “The Merciful rose over the Throne.” «وعلى إعمال الفعل، مجازه: استوى الرحمن على العرش.»
[MUQATIL:20:5-8/r1] verses 20:5 | God's establishment over the Throne means that He settled there before creating the heavens and earth. «في التقديم قبل خلق السموات والأرض يعني استقر»
[NAHHAS:v3p31/r16] verses 20:5 | Abu Ishaq allows the accusative as praise and the genitive as an apposition to min. «قال أبو إسحاق ويجوز الخفض على البدل من من»
[NAHHAS:v3p31/r17] verses 20:5 | Saʿid ibn Musʿada understands the nominative as an implied “He is the All-Merciful.” «وقال سعيد بن مسعدة الرفع بمعنى هو الرحمن»
[NAHHAS:v3p31/r18] verses 20:5 | The author also allows a nominative subject or apposition to the implicit pronoun in “created.” «قال أبو جعفر ويجوز الرفع بالابتداء وعلى البدل من المضمر الذي في خلق»
[NASAFI-FULL:v3p50#5/r1] verses 20:5 | Al-Raḥmān is nominative as praise, meaning He is the Merciful; ʿalā al-ʿarsh is the predicate of an omitted subject. «رفع على المدح أي هو الرحمن»
[NASAFI-FULL:v3p50#5/r2] verses 20:5 | Al-Zajjāj interprets istawā as istawlā, exercising dominion. «﴿ استوى ﴾ استولى عن الزجاج»
[NASAFI-FULL:v3p50#5/r3] verses 20:5 | Mentioning the throne, the greatest creation, signals the rest; alternatively, sitting on a throne conventionally signifies kingship, as an extended hand signifies generosity without implying a literal hand. «ونبه بذكر العرش وهو أعظم المخلوقات على غيره وقيل لما كان الاستواء على العرش»
