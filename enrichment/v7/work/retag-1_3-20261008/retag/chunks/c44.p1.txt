[IBNASHUR-FULL:v7p150/r3] verses 6:12 | The ownership lām marks people as God's servants and implies their return to their owner, reaffirming resurrection through the Creator's rightful ownership and warning those who associate others with Him. «دلت على عبودية الناس لله دون غيره ، وتستلزم أن العبد صائر إلى مالكه لا محالة»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v7p150/r4] verses 6:12 | The argument against polytheism is that beings other than God cannot be deities because they do not own creation; this prepares for the promise to gather everyone, since an owner does not neglect accounting for what He owns. «لأن غير الله لا يملك ما في السماوات وما في الأرض إذ ملك ذلك لخالق ذلك»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v7p150/r5] verses 6:12 | كتب على نفسه الرحمة is a parenthetical part of the commanded speech; it softens the impending warning by reminding hearers that God is merciful and may lead them to repent, as in 6:54. «جملة : { كتب على نفسه الرحمة } معترضة ، وهي من المقول الذي أمر الرسول بأن يقوله»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v7p150/r6] verses 6:12 | The reminder also answers why God does not immediately punish those under His ownership: mercy is complete toward the righteous and temporary respite toward sinful, astray people. «فكان قوله : { كتب على نفسه الرحمة } جوابا لكلا الفريقين بأنه تفضل بالرحمة»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v7p151/r1] verses 6:12 | The ownership statement prepares the warning and promise of gathering on Judgment Day; mentioning mercy implicitly promises believers good and threatens polytheists. «ذكرت رحمة الله تعريضا ببشارة المؤمنين وبتهديد المشركين»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v7p151/r2] verses 6:12 | Mercy hints that Muhammad's community was spared the annihilation inflicted on earlier nations because Muhammad was sent as mercy to all worlds and God gave deniers time to join his growing following, as the Prophet hoped. «أن الله قد نجى أمة الدعوة المحمدية من عذاب الاستئصال الذي عذب به الأمم المكذبة رسلها من قبل»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v7p151/r3] verses 6:12 | When Meccan opponents asked for stones or painful punishment, 8:33 said God would not punish them while Muhammad was among them; after Mecca's conquest the remaining polytheists converted, strengthened Islam, and spread it. «قال الله تعالى { وما كان الله ليعذبهم وأنت فيهم }»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v7p151/r4] verses 6:12 | Because Islam was destined to be the final religion, it was wise to spare its deniers initially: annihilation at Islam's first emergence would have destroyed Mecca's Muslims and polytheists and frustrated the religion's purpose. «وإذ قد قدر الله تعالى أن يكون هذا الدين خاتمة الأديان كان من الحكمة إمهال المعاندين له»
