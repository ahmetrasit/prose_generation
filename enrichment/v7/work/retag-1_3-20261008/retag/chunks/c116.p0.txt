# Notes

## 36:11
[BURSEVI:v7p293/r9] verses 36:11 | Their traces include good works left behind: knowledge taught, books authored, endowed works, and building mosques, hospices, bridges, and other charitable works. «آثارهم التي أبقوها من الحسنات كعلم علموه أو كتاب ألفوه»
[DURR-FULL:v7p45/r5] verses 36:11 | Qatada says following the reminder means following the Qur’an. «عن قتادة في قوله {إنما تنذر من اتبع الذكر} قال: اتباع الذكر اتباع القرآن»
[DURR-FULL:v7p45/r6] verses 36:11 | Qatada says fearing the Merciful unseen means fearing God’s punishment and fire. «عن قتادة في قوله {إنما تنذر من اتبع الذكر} قال: اتباع الذكر اتباع القرآن {وخشي الرحمن بالغيب} قال: خشي عذاب الله وناره»
[DURR-FULL:v7p45/r7] verses 36:11 | Qatada identifies the promised forgiveness and noble reward as Paradise. «عن قتادة في قوله {إنما تنذر من اتبع الذكر} قال: اتباع الذكر اتباع القرآن {وخشي الرحمن بالغيب} قال: خشي عذاب الله وناره»
[ELMALILI:36:1-12#3/r14] verses 36:11 | Although most are unresponsive, the Prophet must warn everyone, since warning benefits and protects those who respond. «sen yine umûmunu da inzar edeceksin, çünkü inzarının o kimselere fâidesi»
[ELMALILI:36:1-12#3/r15] verses 36:11 | Following the Reminder means seriously reflecting on and reciting the Qur'an and heeding its admonition. «zikri tâkip etmekte kitabı, Kur’ân’ı cidden düşünerek vird etmekte, nasihat dinlemekte»
[ELMALILI:36:1-12#3/r16] verses 36:11 | Fearing the Merciful unseen means revering His majesty while not yet in His presence, fearing punishment without presuming on His mercy, and obeying His commands; al-Hijr 15:49 holds mercy and painful punishment together. «âhirette olacağı gibi henüz huzûruna varmış olmayıp gıyâbında bulunduğu hâlde O’nun celâl ü azametini sayarak ikābından korkar, Rahmân’dır diye rahmetine mağrur olmaz.»
[ELMALILI:36:1-12#3/r17] verses 36:11 | Alternatively, “unseen” means inwardly, in the hidden heart that no one but God can know, rather than merely outwardly. «zâhiri değil, Allah’tan başka kimsenin vâkıf olamayacağı kalbinin iç yüzünden haşyet duyar.»
[ELMALILI:36:1-12#3/r18] verses 36:11 | People of any community who respond are promised forgiveness and a noble reward; the accusative nunation magnifies a forgiveness covering every sin and an honorable reward without favor or deficiency. «“Mağfiret” ve “ecr”deki tenvinler tefhıym içindir. Yani hiçbir günah bırakmayıp örten geniş, ehemmiyetli bir mağfiret»
[ELMALILI:36:1-12#3/r19] verses 36:11 | The wisdom of the mission includes generous glad tidings as well as warning. «Demek ki risalet yalnız inzar için değil, hem de böyle büyük müjde ile tebşir hikmeti içindir.»
[IBNABIHATIM:v10p3189#3/r1] verses 36:11 | Qatada says following the Reminder means following the Qur’an. «عن قتادة في قوله : انما تنذر من اتبع الذكر قال : اتباع الذكر اتباع القران»
[IBNABIHATIM:v10p3189#3/r2] verses 36:11 | Qatada interprets fearing the All-Merciful unseen as fearing God’s punishment and Fire. «عن قتادة في قوله : انما تنذر من اتبع الذكر قال : اتباع الذكر اتباع القران وخشي الرحمن بالغيب قال : خشي عذاب الله وناره»
[IBNABIHATIM:v10p3189#3/r3] verses 36:11 | Qatada takes the promised forgiveness and noble reward to mean Paradise. «قتادة في قوله : انما تنذر من اتبع الذكر قال : اتباع الذكر اتباع القران وخشي الرحمن بالغيب قال : خشي عذاب الله وناره فبشره بمغفرة واجر كريم»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p447#3/r5] verses 36:11 | Innamā here specifies those who benefit from warning rather than strictly excluding anyone else. «وقوله إنما تنذر ليس على جهة الحصر ب إنما بل على تجهة تخصيص»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p447#3/r6] verses 36:11 | Following the Reminder means acting according to God’s Book and following it; Qatāda identifies the Reminder as the Qur’an. «و «اتباع الذكر» هو العمل بما في كتاب الله تعالى»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p447#3/r7] verses 36:11 | Bi-l-ghayb means fearing God in private, when a person is out of the sight of other people. «وقوله تعالى: بالغيب أي بالخلوات عند مغيب الإنسان»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p447#3/r8] verses 36:11 | The singular pronoun in ‘give him good news’ follows the grammatical singular form of man. «فوحد الضمير مراعاة للفظ من»
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v4p447#3/r9] verses 36:11 | A noble reward is what a worker receives with praise and honor; for believers it is Paradise. «و «الأجر الكريم» هو كل ما يأخذه الأجير»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v7p8#2/r1] verses 36:11 | The warning benefits only those who follow the Qur'an and act upon it. «ثم أخبر عمن ينفعه الإنذار بقوله ﴿ إنما تنذر ﴾ أي إنما ينفع إنذارك»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v7p8#2/r2] verses 36:11 | The explanation of fearing the Merciful unseen was given at al-Anbiya 21:49. «﴿ وخشي الرحمن بالغيب ﴾ وقد شرحناه في الأنبياء 49»
[IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v7p8#2/r3] verses 36:11 | The “noble reward” is Paradise. «والأجر الكريم الحسن وهو الجنة»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v6p565/r2] verses 36:11 | Only believers who follow the Qur'an benefit from the warning; fearing the Merciful unseen means knowing that God sees and knows what they do even when no one else does. «{إنما تنذر من اتبع الذكر} أي: إنما ينتفع بإنذارك المؤمنون الذين يتبعون الذكر، وهو القرآن العظيم، {وخشي الرحمن}»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v6p565/r3] verses 36:11 | The warning brings such people forgiveness of their sins and a great, abundant, beautiful reward, like the reward promised to those who fear God unseen. «{فبشره بمغفرة} أي: لذنوبه، {وأجر كريم} أي: كبير واسع حسن جميل»
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v6p565/r4] verses 36:11 | God will raise the dead on Resurrection Day; the verse also hints that He can revive the dead hearts of some disbelievers by guiding them from error to truth. «وفيه إشارة إلى أن الله تعالى يحيي قلب من يشاء من الكفار الذين قد ماتت قلوبهم بالضلالة»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-6-2:p370/r4] verses 36:11 | Verses 1–12 establish Muhammad’s prophethood: the wise Qur’an is the strongest evidence that he is God’s messenger calling people to the straight path. «the Qur’ān replete with wisdom is the greatest evidence to the fact that he is a messenger of God»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-6-2:p370/r5] verses 36:11 | The Qur’an is God’s favor to people who lacked divine guidance and wandered astray; arrogance makes them deny it, while inherited traditions and future desires block reflection. «The Qur’ān is a great favour from the Almighty and powerful God to people who till then were deprived of any divine guidance»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-6-2:p370/r6] verses 36:11 | The Prophet should not worry over those who will not listen; he should promise a great reward to those who fear God and are ready to receive counsel. «He should give glad tidings of a great reward to those who fear God and are prepared to listen»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-6-2:p370/r7] verses 36:11 | The Prophet should leave nonlisteners to God, who has recorded their deeds and can deal with them. «He should leave the matter of those who do not listen to God. He has recorded all their deeds»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-6-2:p376/r1] verses 36:11 | Reminders benefit those who heed counsel, open their hearts, and reason beyond the senses; valuable faith is professed through sight, hearing, mind, and heart before direct visual proof, while the Prophet leaves sight-demanders to God. «Only that faith is of value to God which is professed by using the faculties of sight, hearing, mind as well as the heart»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-6-2:p376/r2] verses 36:11 | Fear of God follows from His graciousness: He must reward the pious and punish wrongdoers, so the appointed Day displays mercy and justice perfectly. «For the manifestation of His graciousness, He has appointed a day for reward and punishment»
[JISHUMI:v8p5870#2/r10] verses 36:11 | Another explanation says the believer fears unseen matters of the afterlife. «وقيل: خبر ما غاب عنه من أمر الآخرة»
[JISHUMI:v8p5870#2/r11] verses 36:11 | The promised forgiveness and noble reward are a reward free of impurities. «فبشره يعني: هؤلاء بمغفرة من الله وأجر كريم أي: ثواب خالص من الشوائب»
[JISHUMI:v8p5870#2/r8] verses 36:11 | The Prophet's warning benefits those who follow and accept the Qur'an; fearing the Merciful unseen means either in private, unlike hypocrites, or fearing unseen matters of the afterlife. «إنما تنذر من اتبع الذكر أي: القرآن، يعني: أنك وإن جئت منذرا للجميع، فإنما ينتفع بإنذارك من يتبع القرآن ويقبله»
[JISHUMI:v8p5870#2/r9] verses 36:11 | One explanation of fearing God unseen is that a person's private conduct differs from the hypocrites' conduct. «قيل: حال غيبتهم عن الناس خلاف المنافقين»
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:36:11/r1] verses 36:11 | The word only limits who benefits from the warning, not who falls within its scope. «Buradaki “ancak” kaydı, belirtilenler dışındakilerin uyarı kapsamında olmadıkları anlamında değil, uyarının sadece onlara yarar sağlayacağını belirtmek içindir (İbn Atıyye, IV, 448).»
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:36:11/r2] verses 36:11 | Most commentators understand dhikr here to mean the Qur’an. «Müfessirler arasında, bu âyette geçen “zikir” kelimesiyle Kur’an-ı Kerîm’in kastedildiği kanaati hâkimdir.»
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:36:11/r3] verses 36:11 | Others take dhikr to mean Qur’anic signs or clear proofs that complete human nature. «Bunu Kur’an’daki âyetler veya insanın fıtratını tamamlayan açık kanıtlar şeklinde yorumlayanlar da olmuştur (Râzî, XXVI, 47).»
