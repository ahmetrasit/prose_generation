# Notes

## 9:128
[DURR-FULL:v4p331#2/r1] verses 9:128, 9:129 | In the Qurʾan collection account, Zayd finds the two closing verses of al-Tawba with Khuzayma ibn Thābit alone; the collected sheets pass from Abū Bakr to ʿUmar and then Ḥafṣa. «حتى وجدت من سورة التوبة آيتين مع خزيمة بن ثابت الأنصاري لم أجدهما مع أحد غيره»
[DURR-FULL:v4p331#2/r2] verses 9:128, 9:129 | ʿUmar says he will not ask for further proof when an Anṣārī brings the two verses, affirming that this was how the Prophet had taught them. «فقال عمر: لا أسألك عليها بينة أبدا كذلك كان رسول الله»
[DURR-FULL:v4p331#2/r3] verses 9:128, 9:129 | Al-Ḥārith ibn Khuzayma brought the two verses to ʿUmar, who testified he had heard them from the Prophet and ordered that they be attached at the end of al-Tawba. «فقال عمر: وأنا أشهد لسمعتها من رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم لو كانت ثلاث آيات لجعلتها سورة على حدة»
[DURR-FULL:v4p331#2/r4] verses 9:128, 9:129 | Yaḥyā ibn ʿAbd al-Raḥmān says ʿUmar and then ʿUthmān collected written Qurʾan testimony, requiring two witnesses; Khuzayma then said they had omitted two verses. «عن يحيى بن عبد الرحمن بن حاطب قال: أراد عمر بن الخطاب أن يجمع القرآن فقام في الناس»
[DURR-FULL:v4p332/r1] verses 9:128, 9:129 | ʿUthmān confirms the two verses as from God and places them at the end of al-Tawba because they were the last revealed. «فقال عثمان: وأنا أشهد بهما من عند الله فأين ترى أن نجعلهما قال: اختم بهما آخر ما نزلت من القرآن»
[DURR-FULL:v4p332/r2] verses 9:128 | Qatāda says God made the Messenger one of the people so they would not envy his prophecy and honor; he cares about their hardship, wants the lost to be guided, and is compassionate to believers. «عن قتادة في قوله {لقد جاءكم رسول من أنفسكم} الآية قال: جعله الله من أنفسهم فلا يحسدونه»
[DURR-FULL:v4p332/r3] verses 9:128 | Ibn ʿAbbās glosses “grievous to him is your suffering” as your hardship being severe to him, and “eager for you” as wanting the disbelievers to believe. «عن ابن عباس في قوله {عزيز عليه ما عنتم} قال: شديد عليه ما شق عليكم»
[DURR-FULL:v4p332/r4] verses 9:128 | When offered destruction of his people by the Angel of the Mountains, the Prophet chose to hope that their descendants would affirm God’s oneness; the angel says this exemplifies his being merciful. «عن عكرمة قال: قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم جاء جبريل فقال لي: يا محمد إن ربك يقرئك السلام»
[DURR-FULL:v4p332/r5] verses 9:128 | The Prophet distinguishes mercy for one’s family and possessions from the divine quality in this verse, saying God places His mercy only upon a merciful person. «عن أبي صالح الحنفي قال: قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم أن الله رحيم ولا يضع رحمته إلا على رحيم»
[DURR-FULL:v4p332/r6] verses 9:128 | Saʿd ibn Abī Waqqāṣ reports that when Juḥayna asked the Prophet for mutual guarantees of safety after his arrival in Medina, this verse was revealed. «عن سعد بن أبي وقاص قال لما قدم رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم المدينة جاءته جهينة فقالوا له»
[ELMALILI:9:128/r1] verses 9:128 | The messenger is from the people's own kind, a human rather than an angel, with known Arab, Qurayshi, Meccan origins. «bir Resul ki kendinizden kendi cinsinizden, melek değil beşerden, aslı ve nesebi mâlum Arabî, Kureşî, ehl-i Harem’den»
[ELMALILI:9:128/r10] verses 9:128 | Hasan b. al-Faḍl says God joined two of His names for no prophet other than Muhammad, calling him Raʾūf and Raḥīm, as God also describes Himself. «Allah teâlâ hiçbir peygambere esmâsından iki ismi cem‘etmedi, ancak Peygamberimiz hakkında بِٱلْمُؤْمِنِينَ رَءُوفٌۭ رَّحِيمٌۭ buyurdu»
[ELMALILI:9:128/r11] verses 9:128 | This naming is a great divine honor; not all beautiful names are exclusive to God, since the Prophet embodies divine ethics and is merciful to believers, while his religion and law are mercy for them. «Resûlüne pek büyük bir tekrîm-i ilâhî vardır. Bundan anlaşılır ki esmâ-i hüsnâ-yı ilâhiyenin hepsi “Allah”, “er-Rahman”, “er-Rab” isimleri gibi esmâ-i muhtassadan değildir»
[ELMALILI:9:128/r2] verses 9:128 | One reported explanation says that even the believers' hardship deeply pains the Messenger, not merely their punishment. «Yani azab görmeniz şöyle dursun zahmet çekmeniz bile onu son derece müteessir eder»
[ELMALILI:9:128/r3] verses 9:128 | Another reported explanation says that, by human nature, whatever troubles the believers troubles him too. «Yahut sizi sıkan, zorunuza giden şeyler hasbe’l-beşeriyye onu da müteessir eder»
[ELMALILI:9:128/r4] verses 9:128 | Ibn Qushayri's preferred parsing treats “aziz” and “whatever distresses you is against him” as two separate adjectives; this yields the added sense that his great honor makes him feel all his people's grief. «“azîz” bir sıfat, عَلَيْهِ مَا عَنِتُّمْ de diğer bir sıfattır»
[ELMALILI:9:128/r5] verses 9:128 | Some muṣḥafs place a jim pause after “aziz,” marking the additional sense of the preferred parsing. «bazı mushaflarda “azîz” üzerine bir “cim” secâvendi konulmuştur ki bunda daha fazla bir mâna vardır»
[ELMALILI:9:128/r6] verses 9:128 | The Messenger is ardently concerned for the believers' guidance, righteousness, benefit, and salvation, striving to lead them to the highest felicity and Paradise. «Üzerinize toz kondurmak istemediği gibi sizi aksâyı saadet ve selâmete çıkarmak, cennet ve rıdvâna erdirmek için bütün hırsıyla uğraşır»
[ELMALILI:9:128/r7] verses 9:128 | His compassion extends to all believers, whatever their tribe, rather than only Quraysh, Arabs, or a particular people. «bütün mü’minleredir ki raûfdur.»
[ELMALILI:9:128/r8] verses 9:128 | He is deeply merciful by God-given nature and pities even sinners. «Fıtraten min-tarafillah pek ziyade[^3] merhametlidir. Günahkârlara bile acır.»
[ELMALILI:9:128/r9] verses 9:128 | Qur'anic obligations, especially the commands about disavowal, repentance, and jihad in this surah, should not feel burdensome because they manifest great compassion for believers against disbelief and hypocrisy. «Bütün bunlar küfr ü nifâkın zarar ve şeâmetine karşı ale’l-umûm mü’minlere[^4] gayet büyük bir re’fet ve şefkatin tecelliyâtıdır»
[FARRA:v1p454/r9] verses 9:128 | رسول من أنفسكم means no Arab clan was left without having given birth to him, so the messenger belongs to them. «يقول: لم يبق بطن من العرب إلا وقد ولدوه.»
[IBNABIHATIM:v6p1917/r1] verses 9:128 | Jaʿfar’s father says the Prophet was untouched by Jahiliyya’s illicit births; the Prophet says he came through marriage, not fornication. «قال : لم يصبه شئ من ولادة الجاهلية وقال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم : خرجت من نكاح ولم اخرج من السفاح»
[IBNABIHATIM:v6p1917/r10] verses 9:128 | The Prophet lists Muhammad, Ahmad, al-Mahi who erases disbelief, al-Hashir after whom people gather, and al-Aqib after whom none comes; God calls him compassionate and merciful. «عن محمد بن جبير بن مطعم عن أبيه ان رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم قال : لي اسماء انا محمد واحمد وانا الماحي»
[IBNABIHATIM:v6p1917/r2] verses 9:128 | The Prophet describes his mission as reviving sealed hearts, opening blind eyes, making deaf ears hear, and restoring the crooked Sunna until people affirm God’s oneness. «عن خالد بن معدان عن جبير بن نفير ان رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم قال : لقد جاءكم رسول اليكم ليس بوهن ولا كسل ليحيي قلوبا غلفا»
[IBNABIHATIM:v6p1917/r3] verses 9:128 | Qatada says God made the Messenger one of the people, so they would not envy the prophethood and honor God gave him. «عن قتادة : لقد جاءكم رسول من انفسكم قال : جعله الله من انفسهم فلا يحسدونه على مااعطاه الله من النبوة والكرامة»
[IBNABIHATIM:v6p1917/r4] verses 9:128 | Ibn Abbas glosses ʿaziz ʿalayhi as severe or burdensome to him. «عن ابن عباس قوله : عزيز عليه قال : شديد عليه»
[IBNABIHATIM:v6p1917/r5] verses 9:128 | Ibn Abbas glosses the hardship that befalls you as what weighs heavily on you. «عن ابن عباس قوله : ما عنتم قال : ما شق عليكم»
[IBNABIHATIM:v6p1917/r6] verses 9:128 | Qatada interprets the hardship as hardship endured by the believers. «عن قتادة : عزيز عليه ما عنتم قال : عنت مؤمنهم»
[IBNABIHATIM:v6p1917/r7] verses 9:128 | A transmitted fragment links the Prophet’s concern over hardship to the believers being favored over others; its wording is difficult. «عن سعيد أبي عروبة عزيز عليه ما عنتم ان تفضلوا عن غير قتادة»
[IBNABIHATIM:v6p1917/r8] verses 9:128 | Abu Rūq says the Prophet is eager for the disbelievers among the people to believe. «عن أبي روق قوله : حريص عليكم ان يؤمن كفاركم»
[IBNABIHATIM:v6p1917/r9] verses 9:128 | Qatada says the Prophet is eager to guide those who have gone astray. «عن قتادة قوله : حريص عليكم قال : حريص على ضالهم ان يهديه»
[IBNABIHATIM:v6p1918/r1] verses 9:128 | Abu Rūq reads the phrase as saying that the Prophet is compassionate and merciful toward all believers. «عن أبي روق في قوله : بالمؤمنين كلهم رءوف رحيم»
[IBNABIHATIM:v6p1918/r2] verses 9:128 | The Prophet refuses the mountain angel’s offer to destroy the people, hoping their descendants will worship God; the angel says he is as his Lord named him, compassionate and merciful. «عن عكرمة قال : قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم : جاء جبريل فقال لي : يامحمد»
[IBNABIHATIM:v6p1918/r3] verses 9:128 | Said ibn Abi ʿAruba glosses raʾuf as gentle and kind. «عن سعيد بن ابى عروبة بالمؤمنين رؤوف رحيم قال : رؤوف : رفيق»
