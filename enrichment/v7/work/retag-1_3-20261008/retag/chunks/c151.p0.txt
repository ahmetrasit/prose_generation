# Notes

## 90:15
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v24p32/r2] verses 90:15 | A poetic line contrasts an unknown stranger’s mourning with a relative’s happiness, illustrating the word for kinship. «قال الشاعر (¬4):
يبكي الغريب عليه ليس يعرفه ... وذو قرابته في»
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v24p32/r3] verses 90:15 | It means an orphan related to the giver. «قال مقاتل: يعني يتيما بينه وبينه قرابة»
[WAHIDI-QT:90:15/r1] verses 90:15 | The orphan is a relative. «{ يتيما ذا مقربة } ذا قرابة»
[WAHIDI-WAJIZ:90:14-20/r2] verses 90:15 | An orphan “of close relationship” is a relative. «{ يتيما ذا مقربة } ذا قرابة.»
[ZAJJAJ:v5p329#2/r1] verses 90:15 | Dhā maqrabah means a person with kinship; dhū qarābatī or dhū maqribatī is acceptable, while Zayd qarābatī is awkward because qarābah is a verbal noun. «معناه ذا قرابة، تقول زيد ذو قرابتي وذو مقربتي، وزيد قرابتي قبيح لأن القرابة المصدر»
[ZAJJAJ:v5p329#2/r2] verses 90:15 | The poet’s line uses dhū qarābatihi for a relative, witnessing the construction with dhū. «قال الشاعر: يبكي عليه غريب ليس يعرفه. . .»

## 90:17
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v10p483/r1] verses 90:17 | The clause about becoming one of the believers is coordinated with the failure to climb; thumma marks rank or order of mention, not time, since belief must precede good deeds and persist until death for them to benefit. «ثم كان من الذين آمنوا: هذا معطوف على قوله: فلا اقتحم ودخلت ثم لتراخي الإيمان»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v10p483/r2] verses 90:17 | Mutual exhortation to patience means steadfastness in faith and obedience and abstention from sins; exhortation to mercy means compassion or acts that bring God's mercy. «وتواصوا بالصبر: أي أوصى بعضهم بعضا بالصبر على الإيمان والطاعات»
[ALUSI-FULL:v30p138#4/r1] verses 90:17 | Thumma kāna is coordinated with the negative “did not cross”: he neither crossed the ascent nor believed; repeating the negative is rhetorically allowed to single out faith as the noblest part, not because faith lies outside the ascent's sense. «فإنه عطف على المنفي أعني اقتحم فكأنه قيل فلا اقتحم ولا آمن»
[ALUSI-FULL:v30p138#4/r2] verses 90:17 | An alternative reads lā as a prayer against him, or as a shortened lā used for urging (“why did he not cross?”), or supplies a deleted interrogative; Alusi raises grammatical objections to these alternatives. «ونقل ذلك عن ابن زيد والجبائي وأبي مسلم»
[ALUSI-FULL:v30p138#4/r3] verses 90:17 | Al-Tibi transmits Abu Ali al-Farisi's view that the negative need not be repeated, answering al-Zajjaj; examples such as “they did not spend extravagantly nor stint” show non-repetition. «نقل الطيبي عن أبي علي الفارسي عدم وجوب تكريرها»
[ALUSI-FULL:v30p138#4/r4] verses 90:17 | The grammarians criticize Umar ibn Abi Rabiʿa's line, quoted as an example in the discussion over omitting an interrogative hamza. «وقد عيب على عمر بن أبي ربيعة قوله ثم قالوا تحبها قلت بهرا»
[ALUSI-FULL:v30p139/r4] verses 90:17 | The rank conveyed by thumma places faith above the other deeds because it is what secures salvation; faith without later required deeds still saves, whereas deeds without faith do not count. «والتراخي المفهوم من ثم في قوله تعالى ثم كان الخ رتبي فالإيمان فوق جميع ما قبله»
[ALUSI-FULL:v30p139/r5] verses 90:17 | Mutual counsel in patience can mean steadfastness in faith, obedience, avoiding sin, or enduring trials; mutual counsel in compassion means mercy to God's creatures, including enjoining right, or acts that bring God's mercy. «أوصى بعضهم بعضا بالصبر على الإيمان والثبات عليه»
[ALUSI-FULL:v30p139/r6] verses 90:17 | Counsel in patience honors God's command and counsel in compassion shows kindness to creation; these are the two foundations of obedience, expressed by the Sufi maxim to be truthful with God and good in character with people. «وهما أصلان عليهما مدار الطاعة وهو الذي قاله بعض المحققين»
[BAGHAWI-FULL:v8p433#2/r3] verses 90:17 | Such acts only benefit along with faith; ثم can mean ‘and,’ and people urge one another to fulfill God’s obligations patiently and show mercy. «{ثم كان من الذين آمنوا} بين أن هذا القرب إنما تنفع مع الإيمان. وقيل: "ثم" بمعنى الواو»
[BAYDAWI-FULL:90:17-20/r1] verses 90:17 | Then is attached to scaling the pass or freeing the captive; faith follows at a higher rank and is prerequisite to other acts of obedience. «{ ثم كان من الذين ءامنوا } عطفه على { اقتحم } ، أو { فك } ب { ثم } لتباعد الإيمان عن العتق»
[BAYDAWI-FULL:90:17-20/r2] verses 90:17 | They urge one another to patience in obeying God and compassion toward His servants, or acts that bring God’s mercy. «{ وتواصوا } وأوصى بعضهم بعضا. { بالصبر } على طاعة الله تعالى. { وتواصوا بالمرحمة } بالرحمة على عباده»
[BINTSHATI:v1p188/r2] verses 90:17 | Al-Zamakhsharī reads thumma as distance in virtue and rank, not time, since faith precedes and validates good works. «وجاء بثم لتراخي الإيمان وتباعده في الفضيلة والرتبة عن العتق والصدقة»
[BINTSHATI:v1p188/r3] verses 90:17 | Abū Ḥayyān proposes rank rather than temporal delay because faith must precede valid deeds, or that the phrase describes dying in faith, or merely delayed mention. «ثم، لتراخي الإيمان والفضيلة لا للتراخي في الزمان»
[BINTSHATI:v1p189/r2] verses 90:17 | The conjunction waw links faith directly with mutual exhortation: sound faith brings patience in the struggle for good and merciful solidarity among people. «عطف التواصي بالصبر والتواصي بالمرحمة على الإيمان بالواو المفيدة للربط دون تفاوت او تراخ»
[BINTSHATI:v1p189/r3] verses 90:17 | The ideal community speaks against injustice, since God gave people tongues and lips so they would not remain silent about truth. «وقد جعل الله للإنسان لساناً وشفتين. كي لا يسكت عن الحق»
[BIQAI-FULL:v8p433/r1] verses 90:17 | The preceding good deeds signal noble character and high aspiration, and sincerity is required for such qualities; thumma introduces a second, later obstacle concerning purified reason. «ولما كانت هذه الأفعال خيرا في نفسها تدل على جودة الطبع وعلو الهمة وكرم العنصر»
[BIQAI-FULL:v8p433/r2] verses 90:17 | True believers accepted the caller despite becoming followers after once being followed, reasoning that faith raised them from lowliness toward an exalted rank. «ولم تحمله حمية الأنف وشماخة النفس على الإباء عن أن يكون تابعا بعد ما كان متبوعا»
[BIQAI-FULL:v8p433/r3] verses 90:17 | The passage’s phrase about people of understanding is linked to the cited signs for those endowed with understanding. «إن في ذلك لآيات لأولي النهى»
[BIQAI-FULL:v8p434/r1] verses 90:17 | Abu Bakr’s pre-Islamic character included kinship, hospitality, carrying burdens, and helping people facing true calamities; Ibn al-Daghina said such a man should neither leave nor be expelled. «إن مثلك يا أبا بكر لا يخرج ولا يخرج»
[BIQAI-FULL:v8p434/r10] verses 90:17 | The faithful cultivate mercy through love and aversion for God; faith purifies them from ostentation and self-admiration, making them radiant in knowledge. «بأن يوطنوا أنفسهم على كل ما يحمل على الرحمة العظيمة التي توجب لهم الحب في الله»
[BIQAI-FULL:v8p434/r2] verses 90:17 | Khadija praised similar virtues in the Prophet after his first encounter with Gabriel, paralleling Abu Bakr’s innate virtues. «كما قالت خديجة رضي الله عنها للنبي صلى الله عليه وسلم حين رجع إليها ترجف بوادره»
[BIQAI-FULL:v8p434/r3] verses 90:17 | God enabled Abu Bakr to accept the call to Islam immediately and without hesitation. «توفيقا من الله تعالى لم يتعلثم حين دعاه إلى الدين»
[BIQAI-FULL:v8p434/r4] verses 90:17 | At Hudaybiyya Abu Bakr answered Umar with the Prophet’s exact words despite not hearing them beforehand, evidence of his exceptional rank. «ما قال له النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم سواء حرفا بحرف»
[BIQAI-FULL:v8p434/r5] verses 90:17 | Abu Bakr never drank wine; when challenged in pagan times, he said he would spend all his wealth on anything that increased his intelligence. «قال لعشراء : والله لو وجدت شيئا يزيد في عقلي»
[BIQAI-FULL:v8p434/r6] verses 90:17 | The Prophet said, ‘I entered Islam carrying forward the good I had done’; this validates Abu Bakr’s good deeds from before Islam through his later faith. «أسلمت على ما سلف منك من خير»
[BIQAI-FULL:v8p434/r7] verses 90:17 | Faith elevates a person and leads to religious duties that require the greater struggle against one’s nature; courage is a third virtue, and its hardship is sustained by patience and cooperation. «وكانت موجبة للجهاد الأكبر من حيث مخالفتها للطبع»
[BIQAI-FULL:v8p434/r8] verses 90:17 | The believers mutually counsel patience in demanding acts of duty, including enjoining right even when it may lead to death; bravery is patience for an hour. «أي صبروا وأوصى بعضهم بعضا»
[BIQAI-FULL:v8p434/r9] verses 90:17 | Mercy is a fourth obstacle and fruit of patient justice, moderating excess and deficiency; repeating the verb signals that few people practice it, in forms fitting each time and place. «فكانت الرحمة من ثمرات الاصطبار المثمر للعدالة»
[BURSEVI:v10p339#2/r1] verses 90:17 | Then marks faith's higher rank after emancipation and charity, although faith comes first in time and is required for every righteous deed; accepted spending is for God, unlike wealth squandered in ostentation and pride. «للدلالة على تراخي رتبة الإيمان عن العتق والصدقة ورفعة محله لاشتراط جميع الأعمال الصالحة به»
[BURSEVI:v10p339#2/r2] verses 90:17 | The afterlife's obstacle can be crossed only by one who keeps his stomach from forbidden and doubtful food and takes only enough to preserve life. «المحاسبي : تلك عقبة لا يجوزها إلا من خمص بطنه عن الحرام والشبهات»
[BURSEVI:v10p339#2/r3] verses 90:17 | Al-Qasim says the obstacle is the self: free it from servitude to creatures and occupy it in worship of the Lord. «وقال القاسم القعبة نفسك ألا ترى إلى قوله فك رقبة فإنه أن تعتق نفسك من رق الخلق»
