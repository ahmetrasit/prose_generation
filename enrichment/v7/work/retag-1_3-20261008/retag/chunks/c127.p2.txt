[BAYDAWI-FULL:46:15/r1] verses 46:15 | The thirty months describe the full burden a mother bears in raising a child, intensifying the command to care for her. «كل ذلك بيان لما تكابده الأم في تربية الولد مبالغة في التوصية بها»
[BAYDAWI-FULL:46:15/r2] verses 46:15 | The verse establishes a six-month minimum pregnancy: subtract the two complete nursing years stated in 2:233 from thirty months; doctors also hold this view. «وفيه دليل على أن أقل مدة الحمل ستة أشهر لأنه إذا حط منه الفصال حولان»
[BAYDAWI-FULL:46:15/r3] verses 46:15 | The minimum pregnancy and maximum nursing periods are specified because they are measurable and determine rules of lineage and breastfeeding. «ولعل تخصيص أقل الحمل وأكثر الرضاع لانضباطهما وتحقق ارتباط حكم النسب والرضاع بهما»

[BAYDAWI-FULL:46:15/r5] verses 46:15 | One reported view says no prophet was commissioned before the age of forty. «قيل لم يبعث نبي إلا بعد الأربعين»
[BAYDAWI-FULL:46:15/r6] verses 46:15 | Awziʿnī means “inspire me”; Bayḍāwī relates its root sense to making someone ardently attached to something. «ألهمني وأصله أولعني من أوزعته بكذا»

[BAYDAWI-FULL:46:15/r8] verses 46:15 | The indefinite “righteous deed” magnifies the deed or specifies a kind that wins God’s approval. «نكرة للتعظيم أو لأنه أراد نوعا من الجنس يستجلب رضا الله عز وجل.»
[BAYDAWI-FULL:46:15/r9] verses 46:15 | The prayer asks that righteousness run through the speaker’s descendants and become firmly rooted among them; Bayḍāwī cites a poem as a parallel. «واجعل لي الصلاح ساريا في ذريتي راسخا فيهم ونحوه قوله»
[BIQAI-FULL:v7p126/r10] verses 46:15 | The thirty months are the combined pregnancy and full nursing period; calling it weaning points to its endpoint, since nursing can stop and resume before completion. «وعبر بالفصال لإرادة النهاية لأن الفطام قد يكون قبل النهاية لغرض ثم تظهر الحاجة فتعاد الرضاعة»
[BIQAI-FULL:v7p126/r11] verses 46:15 | Since the full term totals thirty months, the minimum pregnancy is six months; doctors agree, and the calculation may imply a minimum nursing period of one year and nine months if pregnancy usually lasts nine months. «فانصرف الفصال إلى الكامل الذي تقدم في البقرة فعرف أن أقل مدة الحمل ستة أشهر»
[BIQAI-FULL:v7p126/r3] verses 46:15 | God pairs the command to obey Him, the Creator, with kindness to parents, whom He made the means of human creation; prayer on time ranks first and filial kindness second. «الوصية بالوالدين لكونه تعالى جعله سبب الإيجاد»
[BIQAI-FULL:v7p126/r4] verses 46:15 | The author cites a hadith in al-Tirmidhi that God's approval follows the parents' approval and His displeasure follows theirs. «وفي الترمذي : ( رضى الله في رضى الوالدين وسخطه في سخطهما )»
[BIQAI-FULL:v7p126/r5] verses 46:15 | The Qur'an regularly places kindness to parents after the command to worship God, as in the covenants with Israel and the command not to associate partners with Him. «القرآن يوصي بطاعة الوالدين بعد الأمر بعبادته»
[BIQAI-FULL:v7p126/r6] verses 46:15 | The command addresses humankind as a species, and the implied completion is that a person should treat both parents well. «أي هذا النوع الذي أنس بنفسه»
