[DURR-FULL:v5p351/r2] verses 17:110 | The verse forbids praying for show and abandoning prayer out of shyness. «عن الحسن رضي الله عنه في قوله: {ولا تجهر بصلاتك ولا تخافت بها} قال: لا تصلها رياء ولا تدعها حياء»
[DURR-FULL:v5p351/r3] verses 17:110 | Do not make all of the prayer audible or all of it private. «عن ابن عباس رضي الله عنهما في قوله: {ولا تجهر بصلاتك} لا تجعلها كلها جهرا {ولا تخافت بها} قال: لا تجعل كلها سرا»
[DURR-FULL:v5p351/r4] verses 17:110 | In Ibn Umar's reading, the phrase means neither lower the voice too much nor raise it excessively. «عن أبي رزين رضي الله عنه قال: في قراءة عبد الله بن عمر {ولا تخافت} بصوتك ولا تعال به»
[DURR-FULL:v5p351/r5] verses 17:110 | A whisper is not so low that the speaker cannot hear it himself. «عن ابن مسعود قال: لم يخافت من أسمع أذنيه»
[DURR-FULL:v5p351/r6] verses 17:110 | Knowledge is better than action; the middle course is best and virtue lies between two bad extremes, as the verse teaches. «عن مطرف بن عبد الله بن الشخير قال: العلم خير من العمل وخير الأمور أوسطها والحسنة بين تلك السيئتين»
[DURR-FULL:v5p351/r7] verses 17:110 | The best matters are those in the middle. «عن أبي قلابة قال: خير الأمور أوسطها»

[ELMALILI:17:110-111/r13] verses 17:110, 17:111 | Aisha reports that the Prophet recited this surah and Surah al-Zumar every night. «bu sûre ile Sûre-i Zümer’i her gece okurdu.»
[ELMALILI:17:110-111/r14] verses 17:110, 17:111 | Ibn Masud reports the Prophet called this surah and the following ones among his earliest treasures and old possessions, meaning early Meccan revelations and treasured memorized devotions. «İbn Mes‘ud’dan rivayet olunduğu üzere Resûl-i Ekrem bu sûre ile bunu tâkip eden Kehf, Meryem, Tâ-Hâ, Enbiyâ sûreleri hakkında buyurmuştur ki»
[ELMALILI:17:110-111/r15] verses 17:110, 17:111 | He reads the sequence from Isra's promise of the Praised Station to Anbiya's declaration that Muhammad was sent as mercy as deliberately ordered on that basis. «tebcîline müntehî olan Sûre-i Enbiyâ’ya kadar bu beş sûrenin tertîbi de bu esas üzeredir.»
[ELMALILI:17:110-111/r2] verses 17:110 | Allah's beautiful names express the highest beauty, majesty, and generosity, and prayer using any of them is permissible. «En yüksek cemâl ve celâl ve ikram ifade eden Esmâ-i Hüsnâ hep O’nundur.»
[ELMALILI:17:110-111/r3] verses 17:110 | Having many names does not entail multiple named beings; invoking all the beautiful names is still prayer to the one God without partner. «ismin ta‘addüdünden müsemmânın ta‘addüdü lâzım gelmez.»
[ELMALILI:17:110-111/r4] verses 17:110 | Prayer recitation or supplication should not be shouted, lest it suggest ostentation or disrespect. «namazda kıraat veya dua ederken bağırma; gösteriş, saygısızlık şâibesi olmasın»
[ELMALILI:17:110-111/r5] verses 17:110 | It should not be so quiet that one cannot hear oneself, as though afraid of others; the right way is between the extremes. «işitmeyecek kadar da gizleme, mâsivâdan korkuyormuş gibi olmasın»
