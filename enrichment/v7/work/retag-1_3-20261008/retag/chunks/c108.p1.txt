[QURTUBI-FULL:25:63#4/r3] verses 25:63 | A historical report says Ibrahim ibn al-Mahdi, who opposed Ali, dreamed of repeatedly challenging Ali’s claim to leadership; Ali answered سلاما, and the narrator says he may have forgotten the verse. «قال ابن عطية: ورأيت في بعض التواريخ أن إبراهيم بن المهدي»
[QURTUBI-FULL:25:63#4/r4] verses 25:63 | The Ma’mun identifies Ali’s سلاما as the most eloquent answer from this verse; Ibrahim is embarrassed, and the narrator says the dream was surely true. «فنبه المأمون على الآية من حضره وقال: هو والله يا عم علي بن أبي طالب»
[QURTUBI-FULL:25:63/r1] verses 25:63 | After describing the idolaters’ ignorance and attacks on revelation and prophethood, the passage turns to believing servants and honors them by attributing them to God; this echoes 17:1. «لما ذكر جهالات المشركين وطعنهم في القرآن والنبوة ذكر عباده المؤمنين أيضا»
[QURTUBI-FULL:25:63/r10] verses 25:63 | The Prophet’s gait is described as firm and quick in lifting the feet, purposeful in its sway, broad-striding, and gentle rather than swaggering or hurried. «ويخطو تكفؤا، ويمشي هونا، ذريع المشية إذا مشى كأنما ينحط من صبب»
[QURTUBI-FULL:25:63/r11] verses 25:63 | He glosses the gait terms: تكفؤ is a purposeful lean, هون is gentleness and dignity, and ذريع means broad-striding; the pace is steady without haste. «التقلع، رفع الرجل بقوة والتكفؤ: الميل إلى سنن المشي وقصده»
[QURTUBI-FULL:25:63/r12] verses 25:63 | Umar used to walk quickly by natural disposition, not by affectation. «وكان عمر بن الخطاب رضي الله عنه يسرع جبلة لا تكلفا»

[QURTUBI-FULL:25:63/r14] verses 25:63 | He understands the disapproved pace as strenuous haste, which compromises dignity; moderation is best. «يريد الإسراع الحثيث لأنه يخل بالوقار والخير في التوسط»
[QURTUBI-FULL:25:63/r15] verses 25:63 | After searching for the meaning, he reports a dream answer: they are people who do not intend to corrupt the earth. «هم الذين لا يريدون أن يفسدوا في الأرض»
[QURTUBI-FULL:25:63/r16] verses 25:63 | They do not walk toward corruption and sin, but toward obedience and permissible affairs, without frivolous conduct. «قال القشيري: وقيل لا يمشون لإفساد ومعصية، بل في طاعة الله»
[QURTUBI-FULL:25:63/r2] verses 25:63 | Those who obey God and devote their faculties to His commands deserve the name ‘servants’; the opposite are like cattle for failing to reflect, as discussed at 7:179. «فمن أطاع الله وعبده وشغل سمعه وبصره ولسانه وقلبه بما أمره»

[QURTUBI-FULL:25:63/r5] verses 25:63 | He says the predicate may be the closing promise ‘those will be rewarded with the upper chamber’; the intervening material describes them, though he also allows الذين يمشون as predicate. «وما بين المبتدأ والخبر أوصاف لهم وما تعلق بها قاله الزجاج»
[QURTUBI-FULL:25:63/r6] verses 25:63 | Walking represents their whole life and conduct; movement on earth is a fitting choice because it entails living and mixing with people. «و { يمشون } عبارة عن عيشهم ومدة حياتهم وتصرفاتهم»
[QURTUBI-FULL:25:63/r7] verses 25:63 | هَوْنًا is the verbal noun of هَيِّن and denotes calmness and dignity. «قوله تعالى: { هونا } الهون مصدر الهين وهو من السكينة والوقار»
[QURTUBI-FULL:25:63/r8] verses 25:63 | The tafsir describes them as forbearing, humble people who walk moderately; measured pace and good bearing are prophetic traits. «وفي «التفسير»: يمشون على الأرض حلماء متواضعين»
[QURTUBI-FULL:25:63/r9] verses 25:63 | A prophetic report urges people to keep calm, since righteousness is not in rushing. «أيها الناس عليكم بالسكينة فإن البر ليس في الإيضاع»
