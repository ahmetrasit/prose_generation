[BIQAI-FULL:v6p246/r19] verses 36:11 | Fearing God unseen means believing in unseen powers, especially resurrection, without demanding a sign that removes the veil and turns faith into direct witnessing; fear also includes the possibility of punishment or lost favor. «أي بسبب ما يخبر به من مقدوراته الغائبة لا سيما البعث»
[BIQAI-FULL:v6p246/r20] verses 36:11 | The unseen fear is also sincere fear when no human observer is present to impress. «ويخشاه أيضا خشية خالصة في حال غيبته عمن يرائيه من الناس»
[BIQAI-FULL:v6p246/r21] verses 36:11 | These are the guided God-fearing people; others cannot be set right, so the Prophet should grieve no more than his duty to warn, while God knows who is receptive and who is hardened. «فلا تذهب نفسك عليهم حسرات , فإنه ليس عليك إلا الإنذار , إن الله عليم بما يصنعون»
[BIQAI-FULL:v6p246/r22] verses 36:11 | The singular “give him good news” follows the singular form “whoever” and signals how few such people are in this comprehensive, heart-like surah. «فقال مفردا الضمير على النسق الماضي في مراعاة لفظ ( من ) دلالة على قلة هذا الصنف»
[BIQAI-FULL:v6p246/r23] verses 36:11 | Forgiveness covers even grave and repeatedly committed sins; repeated return to sin and repentance does not prevent a person from having true fear of God. «أي لذنوبه وإن عظمت وإن تكررت مواقعته لها وتوبته منها , فإن ذلك لا يمنع الاتصاف بالخشية .»
[BIQAI-FULL:v6p246/r24] verses 36:11 | The generous reward is an immense, pleasant, continuous abode with no discomfort. «أي دار عظيم هنيء لذيذ متواصل , لا كدر فيه بوجه .»
[BURSEVI:v7p292/r3] verses 36:11 | Only warning that benefits those who follow the Qurʾan through reflection or admonition, without persisting in Satan's path, reaches its target. «القرآن بالتأمل فيه أو الوعظ والتذكير ولم يصر على اتباع خطوات الشيطان»
[BURSEVI:v7p292/r4] verses 36:11 | Fearing the Merciful unseen means fearing His punishment before it arrives or while unseen by people, without being deceived by mercy because He is also avenging and overpowering. «خاف عقابه تعالى والحال أنه غائب عن العقاب»
[BURSEVI:v7p292/r5] verses 36:11 | The Merciful is named alongside fear because one whose blessings come largely through mercy fears most that its continuous favors may be cut off. «ومن كان نعمته بسبب رحمته أكثر فالخوف منه أتم مخافة أن يقطع عنه النعم المتواترة»
[BURSEVI:v7p292/r6] verses 36:11 | Al-Taʾwīlāt al-Najmiyya interprets unseen fear as mystically witnessing the dire end of disbelief and disobedience and verifying the sweetness of faith and rank of gnosis. «بنور غيبتي يشاهد وخامة عاقبة الكفر والعصيان ويتحقق عنده بشواهد الحق»
[BURSEVI:v7p292/r7] verses 36:11 | The singular pronoun in the promise refers to the person who both follows and fears; forgiveness is great, and the incomparable gracious reward is Paradise and its gifts. «من اتبع وخشي وحد الضمير مراعاة اللفظ»
[BURSEVI:v7p292/r8] verses 36:11 | Bursevi pairs the outcomes with their causes: reflection and being moved by admonition lead to faith and forgiveness, while fear leads to good deeds and gracious reward. «رتب التبشير بمثنى على مثنى فالتأمل في القرآن والتأثر من الوعظ يؤدي إلى الإيمان المؤدي إلى المغفرة»
[BURSEVI:v7p293/r1] verses 36:11 | The plural We expresses God's majesty and many attributes; a view that angels share in reviving the dead is challenged by the emphatic We alone. «من مقام كمال قدرتنا والجمع للتعظيم ولكثرة الصفات»
[BURSEVI:v7p293/r10] verses 36:11 | Saʿdī's couplet supports the point that a person does not die while a good legacy remains after him. «قال الشيخ سعدي : نمرد آنكه ماند س از وى بجاى»
[BURSEVI:v7p293/r2] verses 36:11 | Al-Baḥr says the repeated pronoun adds emphasis: God raises people after death and recompenses them by their deeds, manifesting full honor and retribution. «قال في "البحر" : كرر الضمير لتكرير التأكيد»
[BURSEVI:v7p293/r3] verses 36:11 | Reviving means making something living with sensation and motion; the Prophet also called affluent people and unjust rulers dead. «والإحياء جعل الشيء حيا ذا حس وحركة والميت من أخرج روحه وقد أطلق النبي عليه السلام لفظ الموتى»
[BURSEVI:v7p293/r4] verses 36:11 | A Prophetic report says four things kill the heart: repeated sin, excessive company and talk with women, arguing with a fool, and keeping company with the dead, meaning an affluent person or unjust ruler. «أربع يمتن القلب الذنب على الذنب وكثرة مصاحبة النساء وحديثهن وملاحاة الأحمق»
[BURSEVI:v7p293/r5] verses 36:11 | Al-Taʾwīlāt al-Najmiyya reads reviving as bringing hardened, dead hearts to life with a shower of divine turning and closeness. «نحيى قلوبا ماتت بالقسوة بما نمطر عليها من صوب الإقبال والزلفة»
[BURSEVI:v7p293/r6] verses 36:11 | We write means either preserve and establish in the Preserved Tablet or have the noble recording angels write; the act is attributed to God to warn and because He commands it. «نحفظ ونثبت في اللوح المحفوظ يدل عليه آخر الآية»
[BURSEVI:v7p293/r7] verses 36:11 | What people send ahead means the good and evil deeds they have already done. «أي : أسلفوا من خير وشر»
[BURSEVI:v7p293/r8] verses 36:11 | Writing is mentioned after revival although it precedes it because writing serves resurrection and would have no purpose without return to life. «وإنما أخر الكتابة مع أنها مقدمة على الإحياء لأنها ليست مقصودة لذاتها»
