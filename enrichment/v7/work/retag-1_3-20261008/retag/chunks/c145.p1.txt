[ELMALILI:78:31-38/r14] verses 78:38 | Yawm yaqumu reinforces rather than restricts the meaning of la yamlikun; it may instead attach to la yatakallamun, but Elmalılı prefers taking it as a replacement for 78:18’s yawm yunfakhu. «{يَوْمَ يَقُومُ} لَا يَمْلِكُونَ mazmûnunu takrirdir, takyid değildir.»

[ELMALILI:78:31-38/r17] verses 78:38 | Ghazali says in the Ihya that the angel called Spirit places souls into bodies through breathing, and that people of spiritual insight perceive this truth. «Gazzâlî İhyâ’sında “rûh denilen melek, ervâhı ecsâma sokandır.»
[ELMALILI:78:31-38/r18] verses 78:38 | Ibn Abbas identifies the Spirit as Gabriel, who stands trembling before God at resurrection and confesses that worshippers did not worship God as fully as due. «İbn Abbas’tan rivayet olunan kavilde ise Cibrîl’dir.»
[ELMALILI:78:31-38/r19] verses 78:38 | The definite article in al-Rūḥ can be referential: because the Prophet is addressed by min rabbika, it means the Spirit already known to him. «مِّن رَّبِّكَ karînesiyle muhatab Resûlullah olduğu cihetle onca ma‘hud olan rûh demek olur.»

[ELMALILI:78:31-38/r22] verses 78:38 | Although some take saffan as a single row, 89:22 shows that the Spirit and angels stand in rows upon rows. «وَٱلْمَلَكُ صَفًّۭا صَفًّۭا [el-Fecr 89/22] saf saf olmasını anlatır.»
[ELMALILI:78:31-38/r23] verses 78:38 | La yatakallamun is an independent sentence reaffirming la yamlikun; its pronoun could refer to the Spirit and angels, but the broader reference to all inhabitants of heaven and earth fits better. «Cümle-i istînâfiye olarak لَا يَمْلِكُونَ mazmûnunu takrirdir.»
[ELMALILI:78:31-38/r24] verses 78:38 | No one, including the Spirit, angels, or others, can speak for intercession, request, or any other purpose unless the Merciful permits. «gerek Rûh ve gerek melâike ve gerek sâireden hiçbiri ne bir şefaat ne bir talep ne de herhangi bir maksad ile bir söz söyleyemezler.»
[ELMALILI:78:31-38/r25] verses 78:38 | The exception allows only someone permitted by the Merciful who speaks correctly; such speech occurs by permission, not ownership. «Ancak o Rahmân’ın izin verdiği savab söyleyen kimse müstesnâ o kimse izne iktiran etmek ve savab söylemek şartıyla»
[ELMALILI:78:31-38/r26] verses 78:38 | This exception opens the door to intercession: those near God may intercede, with Muhammad first authorized for universal intercession at the Praised Station, followed by particular intercessions. «bu izin ile istisnâda şefaat kapısının bir küşâdı vardır. Makām-ı kurbda bulunanlar bu suretle şefaat edebileceklerdir»
[ELMALILI:78:31-38/r27] verses 78:38 | Elmalılı’s first answer treats “speaking correctly” as a defining quality and first condition of intercession; because God’s approval is known only by His will, intercessors speak in fear and only sound intercession is accepted. «وَقَالَ صَوَابًۭا o izne nâil olabileceklerin sıfat-ı kâşifesini ve şefaatin birinci şartını beyan olmasıdır.»
