The S100 surah page is finished. The validator keeps all 63 records with no warnings, and the preview renders with no placement errors.

**Blocks by type (tur):** 18 tefsir_dirayet, 9 meal, 7 yenilik, 6 tefsir_rivayet, 3 nahiv, 3 lugat, and 2 each of esbab, hadis, kiraat, kaynak_notu, modern and nazm. There is 1 each of nuzul, isari, belagat, anlam_tarihi and vucuh.

**Blocks by layer (kat):** 15 temel, 40 ek, 8 arastirma.

**Novelty (klasik_tanik):**
- **21 oncul blocks.** In 10 the source states the commentary's point outright (acik); in 11 it states part of it (kismi). The strongest:
  - Rāzī on the obedient horse set against the ungrateful human.
  - Farrāʾ on "nār al-ḥubāḥib", the useless fire named after a miser.
  - Mujāhid and Ṭabarī reading the struck fire as human plotting, speech and thought.
  - Zamakhsharī calling the sieve "muḥaṣṣil".
  - Abū ʿUbayda on "kanūd" as land that grows nothing.
  - Rāghib on 100:9 as the moment secrets are laid bare.
  - Neuwirth (via Corpus Coranicum) on the dawn raid as an image of the Last Day.
- **7 yenilik blocks.** In 4 the parts are attested separately but not the combination (yapitaslari); for 3 nothing was found (bulunamadi). All were searched only in this surah's per-ayah texts, and each block says so.

**Objections and corrections:** there are no itiraz blocks. Instead there are two sinir blocks from Ibn Fāris: he says the root w-r-y has no single governing meaning, and he files "love" and "grain" under separate origins of ḥ-b-b. No base errors were found, so there are no duzeltme blocks. The commentary's attribution of Q 20:55 to Moses is disputed in the sources, so I recorded it as a disagreement (arastirma), not as an error.

**Meal verdict:** Çantay is the most literal for the surah (keeps "göğüs" for ṣudūr, marks its additions, keeps the connectives). Hayrat is the best explanatory meal (marked additions, "sine", both readings of 100:8). Gölpınarlı keeps the widest range of meaning but says "gönül". The Diyanet translations lose ṣudūr→"kalp" and add unmarked words. İslamoğlu, Okuyan and Süleymaniye change who the subject of 100:1–5 is.

- No sahih merit hadith exists for this surah in the corpus.
