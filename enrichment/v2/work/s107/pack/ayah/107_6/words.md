# 107:6 — words and the elements a faithful translation must carry

ٱلَّذِينَ هُمْ يُرَآءُونَ

Per word: QAC morphemes with their features in plain terms. These are the checks for the meal review: a translation keeps, drops or substitutes each element (a preposition, an attached pronoun, number, definiteness, voice, emphasis, a conjunction). Turkish may force a change; say so when it does.

## w1 ٱلَّذِينَ  (107:6:1; lemma ٱلَّذِى; root —)
- ٱلَّذِينَ: relative pronoun; masc. PLURAL

## w2 هُمْ  (107:6:2; lemma —; root —)
- هُمْ: pronoun; 3rd person masc. PLURAL

## w3 يُرَآءُونَ  (107:6:3; lemma يُرَآءُ; root ر ء ي)
- يُرَآءُ: verb; imperfect (ongoing/future); verb form (III); 3rd person masc. PLURAL
- ونَ: attached pronoun 3MP = their/them (m.pl.)
