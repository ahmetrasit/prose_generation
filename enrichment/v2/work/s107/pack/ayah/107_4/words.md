# 107:4 — words and the elements a faithful translation must carry

فَوَيْلٌۭ لِّلْمُصَلِّينَ

Per word: QAC morphemes with their features in plain terms. These are the checks for the meal review: a translation keeps, drops or substitutes each element (a preposition, an attached pronoun, number, definiteness, voice, emphasis, a conjunction). Turkish may force a change; say so when it does.

## w1 فَوَيْلٌ  (107:4:1; lemma وَيْل; root —)
- فَ: fa- (resumption: so)
- وَيْلٌ: noun; M; indefinite; nominative

## w2 لِّلْمُصَلِّينَ  (107:4:2; lemma مُصَلِّين; root ص ل و)
- لِّ: li- (preposition: for, to)
- لْ: al- (definite article; Turkish has none — definiteness must be carried otherwise or is lost)
- مُصَلِّينَ: noun; ACT; PCPL; verb form (II); masc. PLURAL; genitive
