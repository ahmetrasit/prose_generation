# 107:1 — words and the elements a faithful translation must carry

أَرَءَيْتَ ٱلَّذِى يُكَذِّبُ بِٱلدِّينِ

Per word: QAC morphemes with their features in plain terms. These are the checks for the meal review: a translation keeps, drops or substitutes each element (a preposition, an attached pronoun, number, definiteness, voice, emphasis, a conjunction). Turkish may force a change; say so when it does.

## w1 أَرَءَيْتَ  (107:1:1; lemma رَءَا; root ر ء ي)
- أَ: a- (question)
- رَءَيْ: verb; perfect (completed); 2nd person masc. singular
- تَ: attached pronoun 2MS = your (m.s.)

## w2 ٱلَّذِى  (107:1:2; lemma ٱلَّذِى; root —)
- ٱلَّذِى: relative pronoun; masc. singular

## w3 يُكَذِّبُ  (107:1:3; lemma كَذَّبَ; root ك ذ ب)
- يُكَذِّبُ: verb; imperfect (ongoing/future); verb form (II); 3rd person masc. singular

## w4 بِٱلدِّينِ  (107:1:4; lemma دِين; root د ي ن)
- بِ: bi- (preposition: with, by, in)
- ٱل: al- (definite article; Turkish has none — definiteness must be carried otherwise or is lost)
- دِّينِ: noun; M; genitive
