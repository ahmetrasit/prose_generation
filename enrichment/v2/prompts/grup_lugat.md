# Group lugat: the lexica of the bound roots

Each material item is one root (identity from PACK/binding.json only; never resolve a root by spelling): the
project dictionary (PROJE, authoritative), the six classical lexica in full, and the further lexica's entries
(LISAN, LANE, ASAS, QAMUS, TAJ; MUHIT, VASIT, HANSWEHR are modern Arabic: only inside anlam_tarihi, as evidence of
later drift, never as attestation). Write lugat blocks (sozluk) for senses and branches in the lexica's own words
with their shawāhid, literal versus figurative (ASAS), near-synonyms (FURUQ when present), Maqāyīs's uṣūl: the
evidence for and against the commentary's lexical claims, on the page of the ayah whose word it concerns (a root
bound in several ayat: the page where the commentary discusses that word). Do not repeat what the commentary
already says from the same lexicon; add what it does not.
