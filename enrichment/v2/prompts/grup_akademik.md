# Group akademik: academic reference works

Your material is pages of reference works that cite the surah's ayat (EQ, Nöldeke's Geschichte des Qorāns,
Sinai's Key Terms, Jeffery's Foreign Vocabulary, Zammit's comparative lexicon), usually excerpts around the
citation; many cite an ayah only in passing (ilgisiz). Report positions with their authors and the work, never as
the page's conclusions: chronology and dating (Nöldeke's periods), loanword and cognate claims, the history of a
term, a structural observation. Nöldeke and Jeffery are OCR texts (German, English): quote at most a few words.
These are Western academic voices: one block per position that changes or sharpens the reading, kat:ek or
arastirma unless the point is central.
