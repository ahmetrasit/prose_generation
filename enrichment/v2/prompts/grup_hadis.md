# Group hadis

Sahih only for tur:hadis (the shared core, rule 4). Your material is the hits of scripted searches (the ayah's
phrase and word pairs, sahih reports only), each marked with the query that found it; most hits merely share a
phrase: mark them ilgisiz in kapsam.jsonl. On an ayah page: a direct Prophetic explanation of the ayah's words when
one exists (l dogrudan), and each hadith that bears on the ayah's words or subject (one line each; l tematik is the
default). On the surah page: merit (fazilet) reports, as t hadis with f fazilet, and hadith on the surah as a whole.
