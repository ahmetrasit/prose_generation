# Group nazm-bikai: Biqāʿī and naẓm (a REQUIRED voice)

al-Biqāʿī's Naẓm al-durar (BIQAI, BIQAI-FULL), al-Suyūṭī's Tanāsuq al-durar, al-Gharnāṭī: the surah's maqṣūd (its
governing purpose), its names and what they show, the munāsaba of the surah with its neighbours and of each ayah
with the ones around it, and how a word or order serves the maqṣūd. Biqāʿī's surah introductions (maqṣūd, names,
munāsaba) are segments marked «introduction … copied from a page of S…»: they belong on the surah page. Every page
of your unit hears this voice or gets a kapsam line saying why not.
