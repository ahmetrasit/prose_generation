# Machine-readable alternatives: Bint al-Shāṭiʾ and al-Khūlī

Checked 2026-10-05 in response to the user's Internet Archive, OpenITI and Doha leads. **A useful structured version of Bint al-Shāṭiʾ's exact commentary was found and acquired. A comparably usable complete al-Khūlī text was not verified.** Detailed query/file evidence is in [source_checks.json](source_checks.json); checksums, page counts and sample comparisons are in [archive_results.json](archive_results.json).

## Internet Archive

### Bint al-Shāṭiʾ: preferable existing text found

The [HTML item 1266Htm](https://archive.org/details/1266Htm) supplies a 216,987-byte ZIP containing a Windows-1256 HTML export generated by Shamela. Its internal metadata identifies **ʿĀʾisha Muḥammad ʿAlī ʿAbd al-Raḥmān, al-Tafsīr al-bayānī li-l-Qurʾān al-karīm, Dār al-Maʿārif, seventh edition, two volumes**, and says its numbering follows print. A [separate database item](https://archive.org/details/tahirkhan576-1389) supplies an 840,627-byte ZIP containing a 2,536,448-byte SQLite database with book text, volume/page fields and headings. Both were downloaded and verified against the Archive's byte count and MD5, with local SHA-256 recorded. These are apparently related Shamela exports, **not independent textual witnesses**. A [Word export](https://archive.org/details/1266Word) and [BOK export](https://archive.org/details/1391Bok) were also listed; they were not needed for this check and were not downloaded.

The database has **373 records: 197 in volume 1 (printed pp12–216) and 176 in volume 2 (pp6–192)**. The HTML has 373 volume/page markers. Volume/page fields match the four inspected PDF sample pages. Full alignment to the 418 PDF pages is not yet certified. Assuming numeric identity, 45 PDF indices have no corresponding database row; the audit records all of them. Some are blanks, while others contain front/end matter. Furthermore, volume 1 record 12 is bibliographic metadata rather than the scan's page text. The fifth-edition preface visible in the acquired scan at PDF pp9–12 is not present in those database records. **Do not interpret 373 records as a complete 418-page conversion.**

In the 234-word diagnostic excerpts, the structured text has 8 word edits (3.4%, after permissive normalization), compared with Luna low's 25 and medium's 22. It preserves the negation, surah reference and attribution that Luna damaged. It still contains typos such as `الغطلء` for the printed `الغطاء`, a duplicated `الإغماء`, modified verse delimiters/numbers, and added vocalization. It is the best located **candidate base for search and correction**, not a near-lossless diplomatic edition.

### Archive OCR is available, but format is not a quality guarantee

The exact acquired scans have `_djvu.txt`, `_djvu.xml`, hOCR and character-level chOCR derivatives. The three TXT files had already been downloaded in the earlier acquisition; they should have been directly benchmarked before fresh OCR was proposed. They are OCR output, not a clean born-digital alternative. Their metadata names Tesseract 5.3.0. The [volume 1](https://archive.org/details/elshandawily0546), [volume 2](https://archive.org/details/elshandawily0547) and [al-Khūlī](https://archive.org/details/AAlexandrina-087373) file lists supply the evidence.

The page XML and scan metadata were additionally downloaded for all three PDFs. Their page counts match exactly: **222, 196 and 368**. XML retains word coordinates and page boundaries, making it more useful for alignment than plain TXT, which has no form feeds here. The hOCR/chOCR/TXT/XML are derivatives of the same recognition lineage; converting between them does not repair wrong letters or restore omitted marks. Keep coordinates and the original recognition output, then create page text for search. Empty recognition still requires image confirmation before it is classified as blank.

The likely [author collection behind the user's lead](https://archive.org/details/alabadiaschool_gmail_20190108_1258) contains *Nisāʾ al-Nabī*, *al-Qurʾān wa-qaḍāyā al-insān* and other works, with OCR derivatives. Its original file list does **not** include the required two-volume commentary. The exact commentary exports above are the relevant find.

### Al-Khūlī: alternate copies remain OCR

Archive title search returned four relevant items: the already acquired [AAlexandrina-087373](https://archive.org/details/AAlexandrina-087373), [AAlexandrina-473548](https://archive.org/details/AAlexandrina-473548), [moharram1965_gmail_20171113_0102](https://archive.org/details/moharram1965_gmail_20171113_0102), and [elshandawily3374](https://archive.org/details/elshandawily3374). TXT derivatives from the other three were downloaded, checksum-verified and spot-inspected. They contain conspicuous recognition errors; none was established as a clean full-text replacement. The original same-scan XML has a 44.2% normalized word edit rate in the diagnostic excerpts, partly driven by word segmentation. Its better character rate does not make it suitable for quotations.

Saved under `enrichment/corpus/KHULI/raw/ocr-candidates/archive-2026-10-05/`. No alternative text was substituted into the indexed corpus.

## OpenITI

The complete, non-truncated GitHub trees of **1400AH** and **1425AH** were fetched and searched. The checked commits and path counts are retained in `archive_results.json`.

- [Al-Khūlī's author directory](https://github.com/OpenITI/1400AH/tree/6b4084ba42c8e0bd7c888a8c4b37c43ca771c322/data/1385AminKhuli) contains **Fī al-adab al-Miṣrī**, **Hādhā al-naḥw** and **Raʾy fī Abī al-ʿAlāʾ**, from Hindawi. It does not contain *Manāhij tajdīd* in this snapshot.
- No Bint al-Shāṭiʾ author or *al-Tafsīr al-bayānī* path was found in the [relevant 1425AH repository](https://github.com/OpenITI/1425AH/tree/6d71d2af1be5a49f1db3b52cdda15ea11da569de/data). This is a bounded catalogue finding, not a claim that no unofficial or future contribution exists anywhere.

OpenITI is an appropriate first search for available texts, but its general format does not establish that a specific title is present or diplomatically faithful. Do not replace *Manāhij tajdīd* with the author's other three books.

## Doha Historical Dictionary

The [public title search for مناهج تجديد](https://www.dohadictionary.org/bibliography/%D9%85%D9%86%D8%A7%D9%87%D8%AC%20%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF) returns the exact title, identifying **Amīn al-Khūlī, introduction by Shukrī Muḥammad ʿAyyād, Egyptian General Book Organization, Cairo, 1416 AH / 1995**. That is a different edition from the locally acquired 1961 scan. The [public corpus page](https://www.dohadictionary.org/corpus) describes a linguistic corpus drawn from its bibliography.

The exact title query **التفسير البياني** returned no results. Default title-mode queries using author names also returned none; those are not exhaustive author-filter searches. No downloadable full-book text, EPUB or structured text export for either target was found in these checks. The catalogue establishes a useful al-Khūlī lead, not that its entire processed text can be downloaded. The claim that a blanket restriction is specifically caused by academic publishing rules was **not verified** and should not be treated as an established explanation.

## Revised acquisition rule

For every pending work, search **exact title + author + edition** for structured exports before paying for fresh recognition. Validate coverage and page correspondence against the held scan; record shared textual lineage so duplicate exports are not mistaken for corroboration. Keep the best existing text as a searchable candidate, preserve page images as the authority, and correct/recognize only uncovered or unreliable regions. This reduces transcription work without hiding missing prefaces, notes, quotations or variant spellings.
