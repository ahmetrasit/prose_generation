# Enrichment: findings, review of v3/v4/v5, and the path below $5 per ayah

2026-10-06. Written after independently checking the 1:6, 87:6 and 100:1 benchmark runs against `corpus.sqlite`, and after the first v5 pilot runs. The benchmark reports saved by earlier sessions were read as claims, not evidence; everything stated as checked below was rechecked here. API-equivalent costs are at the saved rates. Actual cash is $0: GPT runs go through the Codex subscription, and Claude-run costs are the CLI's nominal figures.

## 1. Findings

### 1.1 Memory vs corpus (v3 memory runs, v4 corpus runs)

**Memory attributions are mostly right, wrong exactly where it matters.**
- About 13 of 18 sampled claims on 1:6 and 11 of 15 on 100:1 were correct.
- The misses are the publishable details: which scholar said which technical point, exact translator wording, and which collection holds a verse. Examples:
  - Zajjāj on akabba/kabba at 67:22 is not in his text.
  - Elmalılı 98:3 is "kütüb-i kayyime", not "kıymetli yazılar".
  - TDV 38:32 says remembrance, not forgetting.
  - Turkish Asad 16:8 has "yaratmaktadır".
  - Islāḥī on 87:7 was misremembered.

**Corpus anchors are reliable; corpus discovery is not.**
- All 3,475 anchors on 1:6 and 87:6 exist in the cited segments.
- Sampled corpus prose matched its sources.
- Keyword search still misses held material:
  - Thawbān at IBNMAJAH:277 was recorded as `no_match`.
  - The Imruʾ al-Qays witness MUALLAQAT:v1p71#2 was missed.
  - Wāḥidī on 2:190–194 was missed: under-indexed, see 1.3.
- Memory found those three items, and missed others that the corpus agents found (*kenūd*, ḍ-b-ḥ). The two methods are complementary.

**Forced to pick one run per source family on 1:6:**
- Corpus wins 17 of 19: Sol max for most, Sol high for bayani, imami-mutazili, kessaf and rivayet.
- Astra max memory wins hadith and ishari.
- On 100:1, historical went to Astra max memory and poetry was close.

**A rule flaw cost a major voice.** v4 excluded Islāḥī's Tadabbur because the held text is the English translation, and that translation holds his actual 87:7 reading. v5 allows labelled translations.

**High vs max is mostly run variance.** Corpus high and max share few cited segments (1:6 meal: 11 of about 110; 100:1 rivayet: 14 of 67). Max did write more paragraphs and anchors on 100:1. "Repeated high beats max" was never tested and is withdrawn.

### 1.2 Where the cost goes

**Agentic search re-reads its own context.**
- 1:6 corpus runs: 64–104M cumulative input tokens, to cite about 565k tokens of source.
- 100:1 Sol agents: 2.3–7.3M input each, to cite 10–30k.

**Writers pay a floor in output and thinking per call.**
- The Opus poetry search writer: about 76k output tokens including thinking, out of a $3.19 total.
- With one writer per family, 19 calls per ayah pay that floor 19 times.

**Measured on 100:1 poetry, same brief, same 24 Opus leads:**

| | Opus 5.5 high | Sonnet 5.5 |
|---|---|---|
| Anchors, checked by script | 77/77 | 34/34 |
| Known witnesses found | 3/3 | 3/3 |
| Paragraphs written | 16 | 14 |
| Words | 3,201 | 1,166 |
| Passages cited | 45 | 21 |
| Cost | $3.19 | $0.74 |
| Leads (Opus) | $0.64 | $0.64 |

- Sonnet: no attribution errors in the sample, and one valid find Opus missed (al-Aswad ibn Yaʿfur's *ḍawābiḥ*, MUFADDALIYYAT:v1p418). Opus wrongly stated that no other witness existed.
- Opus is roughly twice as deep.

**Luna as extractor is cheap but does not compress.**
- Luna max costs about $0.027 per 40k-character chunk.
- Told to quote verbatim and keep anything uncertain, it quoted 59–92% of each chunk. Writers would then read almost the whole packet.
- The dropped Sonnet-reader pilot (okuma.py, 1.6:1) failed the same way.

### 1.3 Data issues found

- **Under-indexed verse ranges:** 81 WAHIDI-ASBAB segments run past their indexed ayah (e.g. v1p55#2 is indexed 2:190 but spans 2:190–195). `v5/ranges.py` writes an overlay; the corpus is untouched. Ibn Kathīr's parenthesised numbers are footnotes, not verse markers.
- **Packet scope explodes on heavy pages.** 1:6 cites 171 verses; packets for its 13 indexed families total about 7M tokens. 100:1 cites 32 verses, about 1.3M.
- **Meal packets are mostly near-duplicate renderings.** 1:6 has 13.8k segments (81 translations × 171 verses).

### 1.4 Runner and harness findings (v5)

- **Codex clips long command output.** Deliveries of 36k characters were cut. Luna honestly marked 31 of 74 segments `unreadable`, meaning they were never read. Fix: 10k-character deliveries, reads at 12,000 output tokens (v4's setting), and `unreadable` reserved for broken text.
- **Context cap.** With 40k-character chunks the peak is 67k tokens, inside the 120k cap. Chunks of 61–70k characters reached 114k, because max reasoning and the written quotes stay in context.
- **Structured briefs work for Luna.** Exact commands, numbered steps and a `selfcheck` command: in the c01 test it read each delivery once, the self-check caught one problem, and it fixed it.
- **Brief leak caught before launch.** The leads brief's example terms were the test's own answer (*kenūd*). Examples must never contain reference answers.

## 2. Review

**v3 (memory families)**
- Cheap with Sol and Luna, and the best source of leads for out-of-corpus works.
- About 20–25% misattribution in exactly the publishable details, so memory cannot be the evidence.

**v4 (corpus families)**
- Checkable and accurate.
- Expensive ($19.8 with Sol high, $32 with Sol max per 1:6 page) because of agentic search loops.
- Unstable recall between runs.
- Blocked one major source by rule.

**v5 (packet → extract → write), as built**
- What's right:
  - script packets reach 90–100% recall for indexed families;
  - the range overlay;
  - translations allowed;
  - verbatim-checked extracts;
  - structured briefs, self-check, per-request context accounting.
- What's wrong:
  - **19 writers per ayah.**
  - **Search loops kept** for six families.
  - **Extraction that does not compress.**
  - **No scope cap on cross-references.**
- Estimated cost, light page: about $50 with Opus writers, about $24 with Sonnet. Heavy pages cost two to three times that.
- **Not sustainable.** The pilot stays useful as a measurement of extraction recall and of the two writer models, not as a production design.

## 3. How to get below $5 per ayah

The per-ayah bill is roughly *(number of model calls × fixed overhead per call) + tokens read once + tokens written*. v4 and v5 lose on the first term (19 or more calls, each with search loops or large reads) and on reading the same material repeatedly. The design below removes both.

### 3.1 Design

1. **Scripts do all discovery. No model ever searches in a loop.**
   - Indexed families get script packets (90–100% recall, measured).
   - Non-indexed families get script keyword search driven by:
     - the page's own Arabic words and verse references;
     - one cheap memory-leads call for all six families together.
   - A script verifies every lead against the corpus and turns it into candidate segments (`leads.py` exists).
2. **Cap scope before any model reads.**
   - Material: the target ayah's segments plus the cross-referenced verses the page actually argues from (at most about 15 per ayah, chosen by script from the paragraphs).
   - Meal: collapse identical renderings into one row per distinct wording with its translators listed. This should be about a tenth of the size.
3. **Make the cheap reader select, not quote.**
   - Luna returns segment locators plus short anchor spans and a one-line reason, ranked per paragraph. Its output becomes small, so chunks can be larger and the peak stays under the 120k cap.
   - Writers then read the selected segments in full, so nothing is cut, under a fixed budget per writer (for example 40k tokens).
   - Measured Luna cost: about $0.03 per chunk; with the scope cap, roughly $0.5–1 per ayah.
4. **Five writers, not nineteen.** One call per tradition group:
   - transmitted (rivayet, hadith, historical);
   - classical exegesis (kessaf, analytical, imami-mutazili, ishari);
   - language (grammar, qiraat, wujuh, rhetoric, poetry);
   - coherence and modern (classical-coherence, modern-coherence, bayani, modern-tafsir, academic, turkish-tafsir);
   - meal.

   Each call reads the page once, with a cached prefix, and writes the blocks for all its families. The output floor is paid 5 times instead of 19.
5. **Writer model and effort.**
   - Sonnet 5.5 at medium effort: accurate in the measured sample and four times cheaper than Opus.
   - Opus only where depth decides quality (meal judgement, coherence). A writer per group can choose.
6. **Amortize per surah.** On short surahs (S87–S114), packets and cross-reference reading are largely shared across ayahs. Build and select once per surah and write per ayah.

### 3.2 Expected cost per ayah (estimate; to be measured)

| Step | Cost |
|---|---|
| Script packets, overlay, keyword search, lead verification | $0 |
| One leads call (Sonnet, 6 families together) | ~$0.2–0.3 |
| Luna selection (scope-capped packets) | ~$0.5–1.0 |
| 5 Sonnet medium writers × (~60k input, mostly cache, plus ~25–35k output with thinking) | ~$2.0–2.5 |
| **Total, Sonnet writers** | **~$3–4** |
| Total with 2 of the 5 groups on Opus | ~$4.5–5.5 |
| Total with GPT Sol writers (API-equivalent; $0 actual on Codex) | ~$2–3 |

### 3.3 What has to be true, and how to test it cheaply

1. **Luna selection recall stays high.** Measure against the 933-segment 1:6 reference and the 100:1 reference at the finding level, not by locator. The v5 evaluator and review sheets already do this.
2. **One group writer keeps per-family quality.** Compare a grouped Sonnet writer on 100:1 rivayet+hadith+historical with the per-family Opus and Sonnet outputs, using the same review sheet.
3. **The scope cap does not drop findings the page argues from.** Check on 1:6, the heaviest page, against the v4 reference.
4. **The prose volume target is explicit.** Output is the irreducible floor. The current runs write 20–35k words per ayah across families; a target (for example 10–15k) fixes the writer budget.

### 3.4 Smallest decisive pilot

- **Page:** 100:1.
- **Group:** transmitted (rivayet, hadith, historical).
- **Steps:** script packets with the scope cap, Luna selection, one Sonnet medium writer.
- **Expected cost:** under $1.
- **Comparison:** the existing v4 Sol runs, v3 Astra runs and the v5 Opus outputs on the same families.

If recall and quality hold, extend to the five groups on 100:1 and measure the full ayah against $5.
