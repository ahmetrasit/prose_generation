# Independent review of the ayah enrichment v3 plan — 2026-10-06

Historical review, preserved as written. Its proposals are not worker instructions. The revised [plan](PLAN.md), [research brief](RESEARCH_BRIEF.md) and [review brief](REVIEW_BRIEF.md) incorporate the user's subsequent scope: external literature awareness, no adjudication of the frozen prose, no dictionary checking and no added ledgers or hashes.

## Decision

**Retain the focused orchestration/evidence-flow rewrite, with a tighter claim, search-closure and delivery contract before implementation.** Targeted v2 fixes are necessary for integrity but cannot make source-first extraction reliably cover the frozen prose. A larger rebuild would duplicate useful corpus and metadata work. The plan's three model stages are a reasonable minimum: inventory, source research, and independent semantic review. The main risk is that concise records could again turn incomplete research into a plausible completion label unless their scope and dependencies are explicit.

This review checked the proposed [plan](PLAN.md), the [independent assessment](../v2/audits/2026-10-06-ayah-independent/assessment.md), its `audit.py`/`results.json`/`claim-sample.json`, and the v2 retrieval, validation, rendering and source-group code. The grouped workflow has **not run** (`results.json`: 0 prepared/finished grouped calls for S1 and S87), so neither its output quality nor v3's expected token savings are established by the audit. I also made one read-only renderer probe described below. The accepted 1:1 sample and synthetic probes show failure modes; they do not prove the prevalence of errors in uninspected pages.

## Required before a production pilot

### 1. Define exactly what an assigned claim and a scoped negative result close

`PLAN.md:36-44, 77-81` says general tafsir covers every substantive claim, specialized passes decide applicability, and every assigned claim receives a finding, scoped no-match, inapplicability or open state. That is the right compact shape, but it leaves three decisions unspecified: who assigns claim→domain work, when a domain may be deemed inapplicable, and what material a `no_match` actually negates. Without a fixed manifest, a job that never received paragraph 23 could still return a complete-looking zero-note result. A `no_match` after a narrow excerpt must not become “no earlier position in this school.” V2 already demonstrates how this happens: `grup.py:1010-1040` can count a segment as used when one retained line cites it, while a second position in that segment remains unaccounted; `grup.py:1426-1436` checks only whether that locator survives somewhere.

**Minimal revision:** make the script register each job's claim IDs, applicable domain(s), source IDs/versions, delivered evidence spans, and retrieval exclusions/caps. Let the model use grouped result rows: findings, `no_match` **in the delivered named material**, justified inapplicability, or open work, with defaults shared across IDs where valid. Reject missing IDs; do not require a model-written claim×source matrix. A job-wide default can close a group only when the manifest proves all assigned claims received the same complete packet and the reviewer accepts the common reason. Give each specialist a compact page-wide claim index, plus full text only for routed paragraphs and an on-demand expansion path; record claims excluded from that job with the routing reason. Otherwise `PLAN.md:36` can save context by hiding a relevant claim before the specialist sees it. No additional full-page routing model pass is justified. The page completion record should derive claim→domain dispositions from these compact records and reject missing/failed packets. Define a small applicability rule set by claim type, with model exceptions and reasons: all claims get the core tafsir pass; hadith and meal see the claims but can mark a specific claim inapplicable when no report/translation issue bears on it. This preserves the intended sparse storage while making omissions visible.

### 2. Make direct ayah-source traversal a finite obligation; keep broader discovery bounded

`PLAN.md:38-42` lists target-ayah passages, overlapping ranges, concepts and cross-references in one retrieval sentence. These routes have different completion meanings. The held, in-scope tafsir passages explicitly tied to the target ayah or an overlapping range form a finite core. A concept search is open-ended and can miss a relevant wording. If the core is merely ranked together with concept hits, a cap can recreate v2's omission: `grup.py:170-200` sends `MUQATIL:1:1-4` and `TABATABAI:1:1-5#5` to `surah`, not 1:1, and the audit's 1:1 note already cites the latter. The v2 search also skipped locally present Bukhārī 5376, Abū Dāwūd 1694 and Bukhārī 5988 (`results.json`, `retrieval_probes`).

**Minimal revision:** enumerate every direct target-ayah/range passage in the *declared core source set* before ranking broader hits. Each receives a delivered passage/expanded excerpt, a justified source-level exclusion, or an open/unreadable status; no top-k search cap silently discharges it. Route one stored passage to every possibly relevant ayah, then assign its positions only to the claims they actually address. Keep concept, spelling-variant, cross-reference and model-memory discovery as bounded searches with recorded limits. The finite traversal may be costly: reduce duplicate delivery and tune the core source set explicitly, rather than treating “searched” as “read.” The same rule applies to the actual meal panel for translation claims; hadith requires its own claim-led report search because a verse tie is usually absent.

### 3. Give negative novelty decisions a different evidentiary burden from positive precedents

The distinctions in `PLAN.md:55-61` are sound, especially partial precedent and bounded negative search. Yet the example output at `PLAN.md:69-79` has `no_match` IDs without a visible query or source scope, and the plan does not say how several domain-level no-matches become a page-level novelty statement. A positive Taberî passage at 21:112 supports only part of 1:1 paragraph 23's mercy/help connection; it does not establish the Fatiha 1:5 link, the shared-root observation, or the “fullness” inference. The nine separate claims in `claim-sample.json` make that distinction concrete. Conversely, absence from one tafsir packet cannot justify a novel-combination verdict.

**Minimal revision:** define an aggregation rule per *proposition*, including direct precedent, partial precedent, different/contrary reading, known components but unlocated synthesis, scoped no precedent located, and open. A negative conclusion must name the query families, source/domain scope and material actually examined (script-owned IDs are enough), show which capped, unavailable, or credible memory leads remain, and survive a targeted counterexample check. Missing mandatory packets or unfinished assigned checks keep the claim/page incomplete. An honestly identified external source or memory lead that cannot presently be verified is different: the page may be released as **bounded/provisional** if the lead is shown to the reader and the related novelty judgment remains open. Do not output an unqualified “never said before” judgment. A claim with a positive note can still have an open contrary-source check. This is intentionally asymmetric: positive citation does not need a long negative ledger; negative novelty does need a defensible search trace.

### 4. Make source-delivery and position-retention reviewable without rereading the corpus

`PLAN.md:40-44, 50-57, 87, 93` correctly requires fuller excerpts, preservation of differing positions and a final sample of unrepresented passages. It does not specify how that sample is chosen or whether it challenges passages already represented by one note. The latter matters: the v2 synthetic near-duplicate probe loses a distinct contrary tail because `grup.py:188-192` drops an 80%-similar full segment, and the “used”/pointer checks above cannot detect a second position lost inside a used segment. A reviewer seeing only retained supporting excerpts cannot discover that loss. Exact-text deduplication is safe for storage, but source identity, edition, quotation chain and distinct reasoning still need separate attribution.

**Minimal revision:** script each packet's candidate/delivered/truncated/skipped span IDs and source text status. Research output may declare a passage fully inspected for relevant positions or flag an open remainder; do not use “one note cites it” as that declaration. Run the final review in a fresh context, separate from the research transcript, and have it challenge the full prose for missing claims before accepting the inventory. It should inspect all passages bearing on a proposed novelty or substantive disagreement in the pilot, plus a small, reproducible sample of (a) delivered passages marked no-match and (b) long passages already used for one finding. Expand an excerpt when its omitted context may change the position. This is a bounded quality check, not a guarantee about every unread work. State that limit in the completion record. No whole-corpus reread or large segment ledger is warranted without a measured failure rate.

### 5. Treat the frozen prose as an object of criticism, not source evidence

`PLAN.md:23-32, 50-56` emphasizes coverage and accurate note attribution but does not explicitly require checking a claim's own direct premise. The user has said the frozen prose is not authoritative. For example, 1:1 paragraph 23 asserts a shared root between *mustaʿān* and *nastaʿīn* (`claim-sample.json`, c4); that is a language claim to verify, not something its own citation proves. Paragraph 14's augmentation about 48:29 also needs the distinction between a physical forehead trace and visible character, which the audit's Ibn Kesîr passage supplies. Paragraph 4's 22:37 addition makes a separate claim from its already annotated slaughter-law statements. A source may correct, qualify, or reject a frozen inference without being its antecedent.

**Minimal revision:** give research/review an explicit `correction/challenge` relation and require direct-script or source checks for quoted verse/word/root/cross-reference premises when the note depends on them. Keep the model's brief claim gloss for relational or potentially ambiguous spans; exact spans alone may not tell different source passes which inference is at stake. A new claim found in final review must enter the same assignment and search process, then reopen only dependent notes and novelty decisions. Reconcile the whole page's claim set at acceptance without asking the model to rewrite unchanged notes.

### 6. Specify a v3 renderer and fail-closed acceptance boundary

`PLAN.md:15, 63, 103-109` proposes reusing pure rendering helpers while promising frozen-byte preservation and allowing several novelty findings per paragraph. Reuse must be narrower than the whole v2 renderer/validator. `render.py:34-36, 177-195` splits on blank lines and rejoins with fixed separators; `validate.py:39-65` finds base paragraphs and checks only block lines that remain. An independent read-only probe on the actual 1:1 frozen base found that a split/rejoin adds a terminal newline (49,851 versus 49,852 characters) and the base contains a triple-newline delimiter; this is not byte-preserving insertion. The v2 validator accepts an empty annotation list and, in the audit's synthetic case, a rendered page with all 53 expected blocks deleted. `validate.py:107-111` also drops a second novelty record in one paragraph; `tools/blocks.py:160-166` checks a hadith grade only if a hadith source happens to be present.

**Minimal revision:** reuse paragraph classification and bibliography lookup, but render notes by inserting at offsets in the immutable original byte stream (or prove an equivalent byte-exact round trip). Specify how the compact v3 `c/rel/e/text` finding compiles into reader-visible records and how multiple relations to claims survive that compile. Validate the exact expected/actual record-ID multiset, order and claim links; refuse any dropped, unanchored, missing or duplicated note. Require a resolved hadith report and grade source for factual hadith/grading language. A zero-note page may pass only with explicit claim dispositions and semantic review, as `PLAN.md:81` intends. Use distinct completion states: **incomplete** for missing assigned work or unrepresented material differences; **bounded/provisional** for finished in-scope work with honestly visible, inaccessible leads; **complete within declared scope** when neither remains. Do not retry inaccessible leads indefinitely or withhold the useful, clearly qualified notes already established.

## Refinements that can wait for the pilot

- **Retrieval regression set.** `PLAN.md:40, 105` already names range routing, spelling and concepts. Turn the audit cases into gates: Bukhārī 5376 and 5988, Abū Dāwūd 1694, `MUQATIL:1:1-4`, `MUQATIL:1:5-7`, `TABATABAI:1:1-5#5`, and the ordinary spellings of *ṣirāṭ*, *ʿālamīn* and *mālik* (`results.json`, `retrieval_probes`). Route a range to candidate ayahs, then test relevance to specific claims; do not redeliver its full text to every overlapping page. For paragraph 14, exercise 15:75, 68:16 and 48:29; for paragraph 23, 21:112 and its augment9 references. A claim-concept search should supplement anchored passages, not replace the inexpensive known-reference route.
- **Memory discovery.** `PLAN.md:25-30, 40, 46` usefully makes memory a lead rather than evidence. Permit researchers and the final reviewer to add new leads after the initial inventory; do not make stage 1's empty lead list a declaration that no scholar remembered anything. V2's `held_sources()` (`grup.py:450-452`) excludes `access='hafiza'`, while `results.json` lists memory-only catalog items. Separate unavailable catalog pointers from verified local evidence, and close useful leads as verified, explicitly open, or rejected after checking. One shared lead row can serve several claims; no per-claim “memory checked” prose is needed.
- **Cost discipline.** The plan rightly avoids duplicate packets, model-written matrices, placement calls and repeated prose (`PLAN.md:83-97`). Its S1/S87 character totals (`PLAN.md:115-124`) are sizing data, not a savings estimate. There are 23 v2 source groups and 53/48 planned S1/S87 units; adding claim context and source challenges may initially cost more than an incomplete v2 run. Set a hard *model-visible token* ceiling per packet, report unique versus redelivered source tokens and cache use, and split a long source at position/claim boundaries without severing the argument. Compare total tokens plus repair work and verified claim coverage before changing grouping. A shorter output is not a gain if it hides a contrary position.

The pilot should require the concrete omitted-claim and routing cases above, an intentionally truncated packet, a source with two different positions, an unverified memory lead, bare-r13 selection, and a missing/deleted annotation. Accept only when each produces the correct finding or open/incomplete state, the rendered base round-trips byte-exactly, and a reviewer can follow each reader note to its supporting span. If the plan's compressed record and single final-review call meet these gates, retain this design. I would resist both a full claim×source prose ledger and wholesale replacement of the corpus/index; neither directly repairs the demonstrated omissions.
