## IBNASHUR-FULL: Ibn ʿĀshūr, d. 1393 AH
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p419/r1] IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The surah is called Wa-l-Tin in most tafsir books and codices, al-Tin in some sources including al-Tirmidhi's heading, and it is Meccan according to most scholars. «سميت في معظم كتب التفسير ومعظم المصاحف ( سورة والتين ) بإثبات الواو تسمية بأول كلمة فيها»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p419/r2] IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · ابن عطية · reports · Ibn Atiyya says he knows no disagreement among exegetes over its Meccan status; al-Qurtubi reports a Madinan view from Qatada and Ibn Abbas, but the sound report from Ibn Abbas says Meccan. «قال ابن عطية : لا أعرف في ذلك خلافا بين المفسرين»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p419/r3] IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · It is the twenty-eighth surah in revelation order, after al-Buruj and before al-Ilaf, and has eight verses. «وعدت الثامنة والعشرين في ترتيب نزول السور ، نزلت بعد سورة البروج وقبل سورة الإيلاف»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p419/r4] IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The surah presents Islam as humanity's sound primordial nature and treats departures from its foundations as corruption and misguidance. «احتوت هذه السورة على التنبيه بأن الله خلق الإنسان على الفطرة المستقيمة ليعلموا أن الإسلام هو الفطرة»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p419/r5] IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The oath's sacred places gesture toward four stages of divine law, suggesting Islam confirms those traditions and shares their foundations. «والإشارة بالأمور المقسم بها إلى أطوار الشرائع الأربعة إيماء إلى أن الإسلام جاء مصدقا لها»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p419/r8] IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · An emphatic opening oath marks the subject's importance, while its length builds anticipation for the sworn statement. «ابتداء الكلام بالقسم المؤكد يؤذن بأهمية الغرض المسوق له الكلام ، وإطالة القسم تشويق إلى المقسم عليه»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p419/r9] IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The fig's form, dark peel, honeyed interior, pleasant taste, and easy cultivation display God's precise craft, knowledge, and power, and are a blessing to people. «فحالتها دالة على دقة صنع الله ومؤذنة بعلمه وقدرته»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p422/r1] IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The oath at places associated with major revealed religions is a fine opening for the surah's claim that humanity was created in sound nature, able to recognize the Creator and His unity. «وفي ابتداء السورة بالقسم بما يشمل إرادة مهابط أشهر الأديان الإلاهية براعة استهلال لغرض السورة»

## TABATABAI: Muḥammad Ḥusayn al-Ṭabāṭabāʾī, d. 1402 AH
[TABATABAI:95:3-6/r2] TABATABAI d.1402 · reports · Views on the fig and olive include the familiar fruits with their many benefits, the two trees, or the mountains near Damascus and Jerusalem; the mountains may take their names from those crops and be oaths for the regions where many prophets appeared. Other views also exist. «قيل المراد بالتين والزيتون الفاكهتان المعروفتان أقسم الله بهما لما فيهما من الفوائد»
