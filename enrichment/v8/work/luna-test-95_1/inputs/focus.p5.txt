## DURR-FULL: al-Suyūṭī, d. 911 AH
[DURR-FULL:v8p553/r1] DURR-FULL d.911 · holds · The surah is Meccan and has eight verses. «مكية وآياتها ثمان»
[DURR-FULL:v8p553/r2] DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن عباس · reports · He said the surah was revealed in Mecca. «أخرج ابن الضريس والنحاس وابن مردويه والبيهقي عن ابن عباس»
[DURR-FULL:v8p553/r3] DURR-FULL d.911 · عبد الله بن الزبير · reports · He likewise said the surah was revealed in Mecca. «عن عبد الله بن الزبير قال:»
[DURR-FULL:v8p553/r4] DURR-FULL d.911 · البراء بن عازب · reports · While traveling, the Prophet recited the Fig and Olive surah in one rakʿah of the night prayer; al-Baraʾ praised his voice and recitation. «وأخرج مالك وابن أبي شيبة والبخاري ومسلم وأبو داود والترمذي والنسائي وابن ماجة عن البراء بن عازب»
[DURR-FULL:v8p553/r5] DURR-FULL d.911 · عبد الله بن يزيد · reports · He said the Prophet recited this surah in the sunset prayer. «وأخرج ابن أبي شيبة في المصنف وعبد بن حميد في مسنده والطبراني عن عبد الله بن يزيد»
[DURR-FULL:v8p553/r6] DURR-FULL d.911 · البراء بن عازب · reports · He said the Prophet recited this surah in the sunset prayer. «وأخرج الخطيب عن البراء بن عازب»
[DURR-FULL:v8p553/r7] DURR-FULL d.911 · زرعة بن خليفة · reports · After accepting Islam, he heard the Prophet recite this surah and the Decree surah in the dawn prayer. «وأخرج ابن قانع وابن السكن والشيرازي في الألقاب عن زرعة بن خليفة»
[DURR-FULL:v8p553/r8] DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن عباس · reports · An explicitly weak report identifies the Fig with Syria and Olive with Palestine, Sinai with Moses' mountain, and the secure town with Mecca; it allegorizes the human as Muhammad, the lowest as idolaters, and the exception as the four caliphs. «فسألنا ابن عباس عن تفسيرها فقال»
[DURR-FULL:v8p553/r9] DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن عباس · reports · He identified the Fig as Noah's mosque on al-Judi, Olive as Jerusalem, Sinai as its mosque, and the secure town as Mecca; return to the lowest means extreme old age, excusing contemporaries whose minds failed and preserving their prior reward. «وأخرج ابن جرير وابن أبي حاتم وابن مردويه عن ابن عباس»
[DURR-FULL:v8p554/r1] DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن عباس · reports · He identified Fig and Olive as the Sacred Mosque and al-Aqsa, where the Night Journey ended; Sinai as Moses' mountain, Mecca as the secure town, upright stature as standing erect, and the lowest as decrepit age. «وأخرج ابن مردويه عن ابن عباس»
[DURR-FULL:v8p554/r2] DURR-FULL d.911 · قتادة · reports · He identified the Fig with Damascus's mountain and Olive with Jerusalem's, Sinai as a blessed wooded mountain and Mecca as the secure town; the oath ends at verse 4, the lowest is Hell, and the final command means be certain of God's proof. «وأخرج عبد الرزاق وعبد بن حميد وابن جرير وابن أبي حاتم وابن عساكر عن قتادة»
[DURR-FULL:v8p554/r3] DURR-FULL d.911 · أبو عبد الله الفارسي · reports · He identified the Fig with Damascus's mosque, the Olive with Jerusalem, Sinai with Moses' mountain, and the secure town with the Sacred City. «وأخرج عبد بن حميد عن أبي عبد الله الفارسي»
[DURR-FULL:v8p554/r4] DURR-FULL d.911 · محمد بن كعب · reports · He identified the Fig as the Companions of the Cave's mosque, the Olive as Elijah's mosque, Sinai as the Mount's mosque, and the secure town as Mecca. «وأخرج عبد بن حميد وابن أبي حاتم عن محمد بن كعب»
[DURR-FULL:v8p554/r5] DURR-FULL d.911 · الضحاك · reports · He said the Fig and Olive are two mosques in Syria; Sinai is the mountain, and Sinīn means beautiful. «وأخرج عبد بن حميد عن الضحاك»
[DURR-FULL:v8p554/r6] DURR-FULL d.911 · كعب الأحبار · reports · He identified the Fig as Damascus, the Olive as Jerusalem, Sinai as where God spoke to Moses, and the secure town as Mecca. «وأخرج ابن الضريس وابن جرير وابن المنذر وابن أبي حاتم وابن عساكر عن كعب الأحبار»
[DURR-FULL:v8p554/r7] DURR-FULL d.911 · أبو حبيب الحارث بن محمد · reports · He named four sacred mountains: Zayta/Jerusalem, Sinai, Tina/Damascus, and Tima/Mecca, linking them to the four oaths. «وأخرج سعيد بن منصور عن أبي حبيب الحارث بن محمد»
[DURR-FULL:v8p555/r1] DURR-FULL d.911 · زيد بن ميسرة · reports · He repeated the sacred-mountain identification and specified Sinai as where God spoke to Moses. «وأخرج ابن المنذر عن زيد بن ميسرة»
[DURR-FULL:v8p555/r2] DURR-FULL d.911 · الحكم · reports · He identified the Fig with Damascus, Olive with Palestine, and the secure town with Mecca. «وأخرج ابن عساكر عن الحكم»
[DURR-FULL:v8p555/r3] DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن عباس · reports · He identified the Fig and Olive as fruits people eat, and glossed Sinai as the blessed mountain. «وأخرج ابن أبي حاتم والحاكم وصححه عن ابن عباس»
[DURR-FULL:v8p555/r4] DURR-FULL d.911 · مجاهد · reports · He identified the first oaths as fruit, the blessed mountain, and Mecca; human creation is in the finest image, return is to Fire, believers are excepted, and their reward is uncounted. «وأخرج الفريابي وعبد بن حميد وابن جرير وابن المنذر وابن أبي حاتم عن مجاهد»
[DURR-FULL:v8p555/r7] DURR-FULL d.911 · الربيع · reports · He identified the oath as the mountain bearing fig and olive trees. «وأخرج عبد بن حميد عن الربيع»
[DURR-FULL:v8p555/r9] DURR-FULL d.911 · عمرو بن ميمون · reports · He recited with ʿUmar the variant طور سينا, also reported as ʿAbd Allah's reading; ʿUmar paired al-Fil and Quraysh in the next rakʿah and raised his voice to honor the sanctuary. «وأخرج عبد بن حميد وابن الأنباري في المصاحف عن عمرو بن ميمون»
[DURR-FULL:v8p556/r3] DURR-FULL d.911 · عكرمة · reports · He identified the oaths with actual figs and olives, Sinai as the blessed mountain, and Mecca; finest form is youth and strength, senility is the lowest, and the believer retains reward without reproach. «وأخرج عبد بن حميد وابن جرير وابن المنذر وابن أبي حاتم عن عكرمة»
[DURR-FULL:v8p556/r4] DURR-FULL d.911 · الحسن · reports · He identified the oaths with actual figs and olives, finest form with a beautiful image, and the lowest with Hell. «وأخرج عبد بن حميد عن الحسن»

## BURSEVI: İsmāʿīl Ḥaqqī Bursevī, d. 1127 AH
[BURSEVI:v10p359#2/r1] BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · The fig is a fruit, food, and medicine; dream interpretations associate receiving it with wealth, eating it with children, and taking an olive leaf with holding the firm bond. «وأما الزيتون فهو فاكهة وإدام ودواء ولو لم يكن له سوى اختصاصه»
[BURSEVI:v10p359/r1] BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · The oaths name the edible fig and the olive from which oil is pressed, singled out from other fruits for their notable qualities. «هما هذا التين الذي يؤكل وهذا الزيتون الذي يعصر منه الزيت»
[BURSEVI:v10p359/r2] BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · The fig is sweet, light, and quickly digested; the source lists many medicinal benefits, including loosening the bowels, dissolving phlegm, cleansing the kidneys, removing bladder gravel, and aiding the body. «فإن التين فاكهة طيبة لا فضل له وغذاء لطيف سريع الهضم ودواء كثير النفع»
[BURSEVI:v10p359/r3] BURSEVI d.1127 · أبو ذر · reports · Abu Dharr reports that the Prophet ate a gift of figs and urged the companions to eat them, saying that if a fruit descended from Paradise it would be this seedless fruit; he also attributed relief for hemorrhoids and gout to it. «وروى أبو ذر رضي اللهعنه أنه أهدى للنبي عليه السلام»
[BURSEVI:v10p359/r4] BURSEVI d.1127 · علي بن موسى الرضى · reports · ‘Ali ibn Musa al-Rida says figs freshen the breath, lengthen hair, and protect from paralysis. «وعن علي بن موسى الرضى»
[BURSEVI:v10p359/r5] BURSEVI d.1127 · الإمام · reports · A report says Adam covered himself with fig leaves after sin; when lonely after descending, he fed leaves to visiting gazelles, and God made the first group beautiful and gave them musk because they came for Adam rather than for food; later, greedy gazelles gained beauty without musk. «قال الامام : لما عصى آدم عليه السلام»
[BURSEVI:v10p359/r6] BURSEVI d.1127 · أسئلة الحكم · reports · A cited wisdom text says the fig first appears without a covering because it alone covered Adam: after it covered him, God made it bring forth meaning before claim, while other trees show claim before meaning. «وشجرة التين أول ما يبدو ثمرها يبدو بارزا من غير كمام»
[BURSEVI:v10p359/r7] BURSEVI d.1127 · خريدة العجائب · reports · Khuraydat al-‘Aja’ib says fig-wood ash kills garden worms and its smoke drives away flies and mosquitoes. «إذا نثر رماد خشب التين في البساتين هلك منه الدود ودخان التين يهرب منه البق والبعوض»

## ALUSI-FULL: al-Ālūsī, d. 1270 AH
[ALUSI-FULL:v30p173#2/r1] ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The oath concerns blessed, honored places: Makkah is certainly the secure city, home of the House, the Prophet's birth, and his mission. «وهذا البلد الأمين إقسام ببقاع مباركة شريفة على ما ذهب إليه كثير فأما البلد الأمين فمكة»
[ALUSI-FULL:v30p173#2/r14] ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · قتادة · reports · He identifies the fig mountain with Damascus and the olive mountain with Jerusalem. «فروي جماعة عن قتادة أن الأول منهما الجبل الذي عليه دمشق والثاني الجبل الذي عليه بيت المقدس»
[ALUSI-FULL:v30p173#2/r15] ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · أبو حبيب الحارث بن محمد · reports · He says the names refer to the two places' fig and olive growth, by ellipsis or figurative naming of the source regions. «عن أبي حبيب الحرث بن محمد للأول تينا وللثاني زيتا»
[ALUSI-FULL:v30p173#2/r16] ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · أبو عبد الله الفارسي · reports · He identifies the fig with Damascus Mosque and the olive with Jerusalem's holy site, likely because each has a tree of that kind. «وأخرج عبد بن حميد عن أبي عبد الله الفارسي أن التين مسجد دمشق والزيتون بيت المقدس»
[ALUSI-FULL:v30p173#2/r17] ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · كعب الأحبار · reports · He takes the two names as Damascus and Aelia, Jerusalem, using the place-name for its inhabitants. «وعن كعب الأحبار أنهما دمشق وإيلياء بلد بيت المقدس وكأن تسميتها بذلك من تسمية المحل»
[ALUSI-FULL:v30p173#2/r18] ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · محمد بن كعب · reports · He identifies them as the Mosque of the Companions of the Cave and the Mosque of Aelia. «وابن أبي حاتم عن محمد ن كعب أنهما مسجد أصحاب الكهف»
