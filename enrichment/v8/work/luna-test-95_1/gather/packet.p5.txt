[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v4p668/r20] ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · الزهري والحسن والأوزاعي · reports · They tax vegetables and fruits once ripe if their value reaches two hundred dirhams. «يزكى اثنان الخضر والفواكه إذا أينعت وبلغ ثمنها مائتي درهم»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v4p669/r1] ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · أبو حنيفة · reports · Abu Hanifa sets no minimum threshold for zakat on land produce. «في قليل ما تخرجه الأرض وكثيره»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v4p669/r2] ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · مالك والليث وابن أبي ليلى وأبو يوسف ومحمد والشافعي · reports · They require five wasqs of measured produce before zakat is due; Abu Yusuf and Muhammad differ on the measure for unmeasured crops. «لا يخرج حتى يبلغ خمسة أوسق إذا كان مكيلا فإن كان غير مكيل»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v4p669/r3] ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · مالك · reports · Malik excludes nuts and fruits such as walnuts, almonds, apples, pears, and apricots; he classifies figs as fruit, while Ibn Habib and several Maliki followers require zakat on figs. «لا زكاة عند أصحاب مالك في الجوز واللوز والحلوز وما أشبهها»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v4p669/r4] ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · مالك والشافعي · reports · They do not require zakat on pomegranates or olives; al-Zuhri, al-Awzaʿi, al-Thawri, and al-Layth require it on olives. «وقال مالك: لا زكاة في الزيتون. وقال هو والشافعي ولا في الرمان.»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v4p669/r5] ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · مالك · reports · Malik says olives are not assessed while on the tree; their oil is tithed if the crop measures five wasqs. «لا يخرص الزيتون ولكن يؤخذ العشر من زيته إذا بلغ مكيله خمسة أوسق»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v4p669/r7] ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · prefers · Yawm ḥaṣādihi most naturally modifies give: attend to payment at harvest and do not delay once payment is possible. «فلا يؤخر عن وقت إمكان الإيتاء فيه»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v4p669/r8] ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · reports · Alternatively, the right becomes due at harvest but is paid after cleaning; harvest creates the obligation and cleaning enables payment. «فيكون الحصاد سببا للوجوب الموسع والتصفية سبب للأداء»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v4p669/r9] ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · أبو حنيفة ومالك · reports · The apparent duty covers the whole crop, including what the owner and family eat; another group excludes their consumption. «والظاهر وجوب إخراج الحق منه كله ما أكل صاحبه وأهله منه وما تركوه»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v4p669/r10] ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · holds · The wording appears to require giving the right on harvest rather than estimating the crop in advance. «والظاهر أنه أمر بأن يؤتى حقه يوم حصاده فلا يخرص عليه»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-3-surah-anam-06:p135/r3] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · The pronoun may refer to God or the fruit without changing the point: gratitude requires sharing a divine favor with those who lack it, an innate right called God's right. «Every favour of God necessarily entails that he who receives it must also share it with others who are without it»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-3-surah-anam-06:p135/r5] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · Because the due is gratitude for the farmer's harvest, zakāh should be paid when produce is harvested; he says taxation instead of zakāh is un-Islamic and unjust. «this right should be discharged at the time of the harvest from the produce»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-3-surah-anam-06:p129/r1] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · The section refutes religious innovations: crops and livestock are God's gifts to Quraysh, who should use them gratefully and fulfil their duties instead of imposing polytheistic rules, since Satan is their enemy. «land-produce, cattle and live-stock are all created by God and it is because of His graciousness that the Quraysh are their beneficiaries.»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-3-surah-anam-06:p134/r3] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · Abundant variety beyond what survival requires displays the Creator's generosity and mercy and should awaken gratitude and recognition of people's obligations to God. «the Creator of this world embellished it with such an innumerable range of products for us»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-3-surah-anam-06:p135/r1] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · The varied produce is evidence for monotheism and the hereafter, directing attention from diversity to the Creator's oneness; he refers back to 6:99-101. «This aspect points to the Creator of this universe and to the oneness of its governor»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-3-surah-anam-06:p135/r2] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · Enjoying ripe fruit and giving its due at harvest follows from God's favors, prophetic teaching, and uncorrupted human nature. «benefit from these fruits when they ripen and give away what is due of it upon the day of its harvest»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-3-surah-anam-06:p135/r6] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · A singular pronoun can refer to every listed crop individually, so zakāh applies to all kinds of produce subject to an exemption tied to the producer's needs. «in Islam God has a right in every produce whatever the thing; only a certain amount is exempt»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-3-surah-anam-06:p135/r7] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · rejects · He cannot understand jurists' exclusion of certain produce from zakāh, except where small household amounts justify exemption. «Jurists do not regard certain entities to zakatable, which is beyond my comprehension»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-3-surah-anam-06:p136/r2] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · The ban on excess after giving produce's due is needed because moderation and frugality let people meet others' rights. «the fulfilment of rights is based on a person being moderate and frugal in his needs and desires»

### notes filed under 16:11
[MUQATIL:16:8-11/r4] MUQATIL d.150 · holds · Rain grows grain, olives, dates, grapes, and every kind of fruit as a sign for people to reflect on God's oneness. «بالمطر، { الزرع والزيتون والنخيل والأعناب ومن كل الثمرات إن في ذلك لآية } ، فيما ذكر لكم من النبات لعبرة»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p512/r7] ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · holds · The four crops are singled out as the noblest and most useful: grain as the world’s main food, olives for lighting, eating, seasoning and ointment, dates as fine fruit and staple in some lands, and grapes as fruit alone. «وخص الأربعة بالذكر لأنها أشرف ما ينبت، وأجمعه للمنافع»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p512/r8] ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · holds · “Some of every fruit” is partitive because the earth contains only some of all fruits, while all kinds will be in Paradise; this wording prompts reflection. «لأن كل الثمرات لا تكون إلا في الجنة، وإنما أنبت في الأرض بعض من كلها للتذكرة»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p512/r9] ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · holds · The general phrase about fruits follows the pattern of mentioning unknown creatures: detailed varieties, forms, qualities, and uses cannot be counted. «تنبيها على أن تفصيل القول في أجناسها وأنواعها وصفاتها ومنافعها مما لا يكاد يحصر»
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p512/r10] ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · holds · The closing sign invites contemplation because a seed absorbs moisture, splits, sends a shoot upward and roots downward, then develops diverse leaves, flowers, fruit, and benefits through God’s deliberate power. «ألا ترى أن الحبة الواحدة إذا وضعت في الأرض ومر عليها مقدار من الزمان معين لحقها»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-4-surah-yunus-10-surah-maryam-19:p310/r3] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · The verse offers a proof of the hereafter: a world filled with wisdom and favours cannot be purposeless, so it must culminate in accountability. «he instantly calls out that this world cannot be a purposeless place; God has not»
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-4-surah-yunus-10-surah-maryam-19:p311/r1] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · The world's purposeful wisdom and mercy imply a day when the responsible are rewarded and the indifferent held accountable. «those who fulfilled their responsibilities are rewarded and those who led their lives indifferently are held accountable.»
