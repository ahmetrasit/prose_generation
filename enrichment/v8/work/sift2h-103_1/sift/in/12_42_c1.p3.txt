#129 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · وهب · reports · Wahb gives three seven-year examples: Job’s affliction, Joseph’s prison term, and Nebuchadnezzar’s confinement among beasts. «وقال وهب: أصاب أيوب البلاء سبع سنين، وترك يوسف في السجن سبع سنين، وعذب بختنصر فحول في السباع سبع سنين»
#130 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · مالك بن دينار · reports · Malik ibn Dinar relates that God rebuked Joseph for taking the cupbearer as an intermediary; Joseph wept and promised not to repeat the lapse. «قال مالك بن دينار: لما قال يوسف للساقي اذكرني عند ربك، قيل له: يا يوسف اتخذت من دوني وكيلا لأطيلن حبسك،»
#131 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · الحسن · reports · Al-Hasan recounts Gabriel asking Joseph whether he was ashamed to seek an intercessor among humans; Joseph replies that he does not mind imprisonment if God is pleased with him. «وقال الحسن: دخل جبريل على يوسف في السجن، فلما رآه يوسف عرفه فقال له: يا أخا المنذرين»
#132 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · reports · If Yusuf interpreted by reasoning, he was the one who thought the cupbearer would be saved; if he interpreted by revelation, the cupbearer was the one who knew with certainty. «الظان هو يوسف إن كان تأويله بطريق الاجتهاد ، وإن كان بطريق الوحي فالظان هو الشرابي»
#133 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · holds · Yusuf asked the cupbearer to describe his case to the king in hopes the king would rescue him. «صفني عند الملك بصفتي ، وقص عليه قصتي لعله يرحمني»
#134 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · reports · The Satan caused the cupbearer to forget to mention Yusuf to his master; an alternative says Yusuf forgot God when he entrusted his case to someone else. «فأنسى الشرابي { ذكر ربه } أن يذكره لربه . وقيل فأنسي يوسف ذكر الله»
#135 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · holds · Buḍʿ means three to nine years; most reports say Yusuf remained imprisoned seven years. «البضع ما بين الثلاث إلى التسع ، وأكثر الأقاويل على أنه لبث فيه سبع سنين»
#136 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · holds · Satan can prompt forgetfulness by distracting a person, but initiating forgetfulness directly is within God's power alone; the verse links this to abrogation. «وأما الإنساء ابتداء فلا يقدر عليه إلا الله عز وجل»
#137 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · holds · The possessive in dhikri rabbihi is licensed by close association with the king, or by ellipsis of the word reports before lord. «لأن الإضافة تكون بأدنى ملابسة . أو على تقدير : فأنساه الشيطان ذكر أخبار ربه»
#138 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · holds · Seeking another person's help is generally permissible, supported by verses and reports, including God's help for servants who help one another and relief of a believer's distress. «الله في عون العبد ما دام العبد في عون أخيه المسلم»
#139 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · prefers · Though help may be sought even from non-Muslims to repel harm, a prophet's better course is to entrust a trial only to God, especially to avoid unbelievers gloating. «والأحسن والأولى بالنبي أن لا يكل أمره إذا ابتلي ببلاء إلا إلى ربه»
#140 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · الحسن · reports · Al-Ḥasan would weep at this verse and say that when trouble comes, people like us turn to other people. «وعن الحسن أنه كان يبكي إذا قرأها ويقول : نحن إذا نزل بنا أمر فزعنا إلى الناس»
#141 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas counts three slips by Joseph: the episode with her, asking the cupbearer to remember him, and accusing the brothers of theft, which prompted the reply about Benjamin. «عثر يوسف عليه السلام ثلاث عثرات» [filed under 12:35, 12:42, 12:70, 12:77]
#142 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · قتادة · reports · Qatada takes ẓanna in its ordinary sense because dream interpretation remains conjectural. «على بابه لأن عبارة الرؤيا ظن»
#143 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · rejects · The author says Qatada’s reading requires taking Joseph’s قضي الأمر to mean only that his interpretation was finished, not that the future was divinely settled. «ولا يترتب قول قتادة إلا بأن يكون معنى قوله قضي الأمر أي قضي كلامي وقلت ما عندي وتم»
#144 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · holds · Another grammatical reading makes the man who was promised release the subject of ظن: joy elevated him to expect rescue. «أن يكون ظن مسندا إلى الذي قيل له: إنه يسقي ربه خمرا»
#145 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · holds · Here ẓanna can mean certainty, since Joseph had said the outcome was settled, though it had not yet happened. «بمعنى اليقين، لأن ما تقدم من قوله: قضي الأمر يلزم ذلك»
#146 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · holds · Joseph asks the king’s cupbearer to remember him by reporting his knowledge and standing, his wrongful imprisonment, or both. «فيحتمل أن يريد أن يذكره»
#147 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · الفراء · reports · Al-Farra says biḍʿ is used with tens but not with hundreds or thousands. «لا يذكر مع مائة ولا مع ألف»
#148 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · reports · Reports say Joseph had already served five years, then received seven years for asking the cupbearer to remember him; another report gives two additional years. «فكانت مدة سجنه اثنتي عشرة سنة، وقيل: عوقب ببقاء سنتين»
#149 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · الحسن · reports · Al-Hasan attributes to the Prophet the saying that Joseph would not have remained imprisoned so long if not for his word; Hasan wept and warned that people turn to others when trouble comes. «قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم: «لولا كلمته ما لبث في السجن طول ما لبث»»
#150 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · reports · One explanation makes the pronoun in أنساه refer to Joseph: he forgot to appeal to God and relied on a human; reports say Gabriel reproached him and this prolonged the prison term. «نسي في ذلك الوقت أن يشتكي إلى الله»
#151 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · reports · A reported divine address says Joseph took an intermediary besides God and would therefore remain imprisoned longer. «يا يوسف اتخذت من دوني وكيلا لأطيلن حبسك»
#152 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · ابن إسحاق · reports · Ibn Ishaq makes the pronoun refer to the cupbearer, who forgot to mention Joseph to his master; رب then means the king. «أي نسي ذكر يوسف عند ربه، فأضاف الذكر»
#153 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · ابن عباس · reports · The most common definition of biḍʿ is three to ten; Ibn Abbas’s definition informs Malik’s rule in claims and oaths. «فالأكثر على أنه من الثلاثة إلى العشرة، قاله ابن عباس»
#154 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · أبو عبيدة · reports · Abu Ubayda says biḍʿ is below a full decade and below half of one, ranging from one to four. «لا يبلغ العقد ولا نصف العقد، وإنما هو من الواحد إلى الأربعة»
#155 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · الأخفش · reports · Al-Akhfash gives biḍʿ the range one to ten. «وقال الأخفش «البضع» من الواحد إلى العشرة»
#156 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · قتادة · reports · Qatada defines biḍʿ as three to nine, supported by a prophetic explanation to Abu Bakr about the Romans’ victory over Persia. «من الثلاثة إلى التسعة، ويقوي هذا ما روي»
#157 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · مجاهد · reports · Mujahid narrows biḍʿ to three through seven. «من الثلاثة إلى السبعة، قال الفراء»
#158 TABRISI d.548 · أبو عبيدة · reports · Abu ʿUbayda says buḍʿ denotes a span from three to five years. «البضع ما بين الثلاث إلى الخمس عن أبي عبيده» [filed under 12:39-42]
#159 TABRISI d.548 · قطرب، مجاهد · reports · Qutrub defines buḍʿ as three to seven; this definition is also reported from Mujahid. «وقول قطرب مروي عن مجاهد» [filed under 12:39-42]
#160 TABRISI d.548 · الأصمعي، قتادة · reports · Al-Asmaʿi gives three to nine, as reported by al-Zajjaj, and this range is transmitted from Qatada. «وقول الأصمعي مروي عن قتادة» [filed under 12:39-42]
#161 TABRISI d.548 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas defines buḍʿ as less than ten; most commentators specify seven years in this verse. «وقال ابن عباس: وهو ما دون العشرة» [filed under 12:39-42]
#162 TABRISI d.548 · الكلبي · reports · Al-Kalbi says the seven years in prison came after the five years already spent there. «قال الكلبي: وهذه السبع سوى الخمسة التي كانت قبل ذلك» [filed under 12:39-42]
#163 TABRISI d.548 · أبو عبد الله · reports · Abu ʿAbd Allah says Gabriel taught Yusuf a prayer to recite after every obligatory prayer, asking God for relief and provision from expected and unexpected sources. «وروي عن أبي عبد الله ع قال: علم جبرائيل ع يوسف في حبسه» [filed under 12:39-42]
#164 TABRISI d.548 · أبو عبد الله · reports · A report through Shuʿayb al-ʿAqraqufi says that when the term ended, Yusuf prayed for release by invoking Abraham, Ishmael, Isaac, and Jacob; Abu ʿAbd Allah tells the questioner to make the same prayer through Muhammad, ʿAli, Fatima, Hasan, Husayn, and the Imams. «وروى شعيب العقرقوفي عنه ع قال: لما انقضت المدة» [filed under 12:39-42]
#165 TABRISI d.548 · أكثرون، الجبائي · prefers · The majority and al-Jubbaʾi take ظن as certain knowledge received by revelation, as in the verse about expecting to meet one's account; this is the author's preferred reading. «هذا قول الأكثرين واختيار الجبائي»
#166 TABRISI d.548 · holds · The lesson is to rely on and seek help from God in hardship, although seeking help from other people is also permissible. «ترغيب في الاعتصام بالله تعالى والاستعانة به دون غيره عند نزول الشدائد»
#167 TABRISI d.548 · قتادة · reports · Qatada takes ظن in its ordinary sense of conjecture, since Yusuf could not verify that the prisoner's dream report was true. «قال قتادة للذي ظنه ناجيا لأنه لا يحكم بصدقه فيما قصه من الرؤيا»
#168 TABRISI d.548 · holds · Yusuf asked the escaped cupbearer to mention that he was unjustly imprisoned to his master, the king. «أي اذكرني عند سيدك بأني محبوس ظلما»
#169 TABRISI d.548 · prefers · The author first explains Satan's making Yusuf forget as Yusuf forgetting to mention God and instead asking a human for help, when reliance on God was more fitting. «يعني أنسى الشيطان يوسف ذكر الله تعالى في تلك الحال حتى استغاث بمخلوق»
#170 TABRISI d.548 · الحسن، محمد بن إسحاق، الجبائي، أبو مسلم · reports · An alternative takes the forgotten remembrance as the cupbearer's failure to mention Yusuf to the king; the author paraphrases this as Satan making him forget Yusuf before his master. «عن الحسن ومحمد بن إسحاق والجبائي وأبي مسلم وعلى هذا فتقديره فأنساه الشيطان ذكر يوسف»
