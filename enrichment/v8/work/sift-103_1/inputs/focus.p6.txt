[ALUSI-FULL:v30p227#2/r7] ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · قيل · reports · Some take al-asr as the Prophet's era or his lifetime through the Resurrection, whose remaining span is compared in a Bukhari report to the time from Asr to sunset; its honor comes from the Prophet and his community. «وقيل المراد به عصر النبوة وكأنه عنى به وقت حياته عليه الصلاة والسلام»
[ALUSI-FULL:v30p227#2/r8] ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas says al-asr is time itself, encompassing wonders and blessings as well as their opposites; the oath warns people facing ruin or salvation. «وقال ابن عباس هو الدهر أقسم عز وجل به لاشتماله على أصناف العجائب»
[ALUSI-FULL:v30p227#2/r9] ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The oath honors time while denying that time itself causes loss or events, as some claim; attributing loss to humanity shows it is a human quality, not a defect of time. «ويعرض عز وجل لما في الاقسام به من التعظيم بنفي أن يكون له خسران أو دخل فيه كما يزعمه من يضيف الحوادث اليه»
[ALUSI-FULL:v30p227/r1] ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · ابن عباس وابن الزبير والجمهور · reports · They classify Surat al-ʿAsr as Meccan; Mujahid, Qatada, and Muqatil are cited for classifying it as Medinan, and its three verses are undisputed. «مكية في قول ابن عباس وابن الزبير والجمهور ومدنية في قول مجاهد وقتادة ومقاتل»
[ALUSI-FULL:v30p227/r2] ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · الشافعي · reports · Al-Shafiʿi is reported to say that even if no other surah had been revealed, this one would suffice because it contains the Qurʾan’s sciences. «انه قال لو لم ينزل غير هذه السورة لكفت الناس لانها شملت جميع علوم القرآن»
[ALUSI-FULL:v30p227/r3] ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · أبو حذيفة · reports · He reports that two Companions would recite Surat al-ʿAsr to one another whenever they met, parting only after one recited it and the other greeted him. «كان الرجلان من اصحاب رسول الله صلى الله تعالى عليه وسلم اذا التقيا لم يفترقا»
[ALUSI-FULL:v30p227/r4] ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The surah follows al-Takathur because it points to those whom worldly rivalry did not distract. «وفيها اشارة إلى حال من لم يلهه التكاثر ولذا وضعت بعد سورته»
[ALUSI-FULL:v30p228/r1] ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · rejects · The author objects that using al-asr to mean time in general is not linguistically apparent. «وتعقب بان استعمال العصر بذلك المعنى غير ظاهر»

## ELMALILI: Elmalılı Muhammed Hamdi Yazır, d. 1361 AH
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#2/r1] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Time gathers every kind of change, benefit, harm, and wonder; the oath draws attention to how people fare in gain or loss within it. «Zaman böyle varlıkla yokluk arasında nîk ü bed, ni‘met ü nikmet, serra vü darrâ» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#2/r10] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The oath warns that time consumes life and leaves people in loss unless they believe and do good works. «o geçen leyl ü nehar vücûdunu kemirmekte ve bu suretle o her dem hüsran içinde kalmaktadır» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#2/r11] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · In an elegy for his teacher and the Balkan War dead, the author portrays life as deceiving people while death draws its nourishment and each breath weaves shrouds. «إِنَّ الْحَيَاةَ تَغُرُّنَا وَمَ مَاتُنَا مِنْ ضَرْعِهَا يَسْتَرْضِعُ الأَْلْبَانَ» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#2/r12] ELMALILI d.1361 · rejects · People wrongly blame time for their misfortunes; a cited poem says time is not corrupt, but people themselves are. «Zaman bozulmadı, kendileri fesad» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#2/r13] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The oath honors time as a valuable blessing and says those who fail to work toward reform are themselves in loss. «Haddizâtında asrın, zamanın aybı, kabahati, zararı yoktur, o değerli bir nimettir» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#2/r14] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The warning against blaming time is connected to the hadith forbidding people to curse time. «hadîs-i şerîfiyle[^26] işaret olunan mânadır. Bu tafsîlâttan anlaşılır ki asrı»
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#2/r15] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Swearing by asr honors time and avoids the materialist implication of saying wa-al-dahr, recalling those who claimed that only time destroys. «daha sarih olmak için وَالدّهْرِ denilmeyip de وَٱلْعَصْرِ buyurulması»
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#2/r16] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The definite article first directs hearers to the time closest to them; with the Prophet as first audience, this suggests the Prophetic era. «muhataplara yakından alâkası olan asrı düşündürmek lâzım gelir»
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#2/r17] ELMALILI d.1361 · prefers · The Muhammadic era is the most immediate sense of asr while also including the broader temporal meanings. «Bu asırdan murad, asr-ı nübüvvet, yani asr-ı Muhammedî olmak tebâdür eder»
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#2/r2] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Human life is precious capital; each unprofitable instant wastes it, but a final righteous act may make up for years of waste and win eternal paradise. «İnsanın ömrü en kıymetli sermayesidir. Ne kazanacaksa onunla kazanacaktır» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#2/r3] ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · Sufis say a person should be the child of the moment: value the present and use it to gain good for the afterlife. «sûfiyye, “sûfî ibnü’l-vakt olmalıdır; yani ömrünün ve bilhassa fiilen içinde bulunduğu vakt-i hâlin» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#2/r4] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · People who deny the afterlife also urge seizing the present for worldly pleasure, though their aim differs from the Sufi's afterlife-oriented use of time. «gün bugündür, saat bu saattir, ne yapacaksak şimdi yapmalıyız” diyerek herçibâdâbad vaktine» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#2/r5] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Time is the opportunity for action, whether productive or wasteful, profitable or harmful; the oath highlights the value and nature of life's moments. «İşte vakit böyle bir fırsat ve ömür bütün ânâtıyla böyle kâr ve zarara mâruz bir sermaye» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#2/r6] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Time's senses of restraint and bestowal can support both the loss side and the exception of those who gain through faith and good works. «asrın dehr ü zaman mânası mülâhaza olunurken men‘ve atâ mânalarına da işaret edilmiş bulunduğu hafî değildir» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#2/r7] ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · A cited couplet says each passing day takes away part of the speaker, since nothing is more precious than life. «“Her gün geçtikçe benim birazım gider, benim için ise» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#2/r8] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · People fail to calculate what they gained against the life they spent and wrongly imagine that passing time means they are growing. «insan geçen cüz’ünün yerine ne kazandığını hesab etmez de gün geçtikçe büyüdüğünü zu‘m eder» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#2/r9] ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · A poem says people rejoice at days passing although each one reduces their remaining lifespan. «Biz günleri geçiriyoruz diye ferahlanıyoruz, hâlbuki her geçen gün ecelden, ömürden bir eksikliktir» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#3/r1] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The first Prophetic-era phase began with Muhammad's mission amid religious disorder, extinguished guidance, and widespread ignorance, disbelief, injustice, and corruption. «nübüvvet, fazilet, hakk u adalet, ma‘rifetullah» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#3/r2] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The Qur'an began descending with the command iqra on a Night of Power, and prophetic light rose to call humanity toward reform, paradise, and divine approval. «bir leyle-i kadirde اِقْرَأْ بِاسْمِ رَبِّكَ emriyle Kur’an nâzil olarak nûr-ı nübüvvet tulû‘etmişti» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#3/r3] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · As discussed in Surat al-Bayyina, spreading the upright religion called people away from total loss and began transformations that led from the middle ages to the last era. «لَمْ يَكُن Sûresi’nde geçtiği üzere dîn-i kayyimenin neşriyle insanlığı şerrü’l-beriyyenin istîlâsından» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#3/r4] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The oath by this era before the declaration that humanity is in loss except believers and doers of good carries important meaning. «O vakit bu asra kasem ederek إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#4/r1] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The Prophetic era from the mission to Muhammad's death had two phases: struggle, then conquest and victory, as prophetic light spread guidance and distinguished truth from falsehood. «ki biri mücahede, biri de feth ü nusret ile»
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#4/r2] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The Muhammadic era from the Hijra through the end of time encompasses later centuries and fulfills the effects and aims of all earlier prophetic eras. «hicret-i seniyyeden ilâ âhiri’d-dehr bütün kurûn-ı ahîreyi ihtivâ eden âhir zamandır»
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#4/r3] ELMALILI d.1361 · prefers · The author and some exegetes find the Muhammadic era the most comprehensive and immediate meaning of asr. «müfessirînin dediği gibi bizce de en ziyade mütebâdir olan bu asırdır»
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#4/r4] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Qur'anic history is divided into the eras before Moses' Torah, from the Torah to the final Prophet, and the final era of Muhammad and his community. «zamanın üç kısma taksîmine işaret olunmuştu»
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#4/r5] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · A day analogy portrays the Muhammadic era as the afternoon of human history, following earlier ages as afternoon follows morning and noon. «asr-ı Muhammedî bugünün ikindisi misâlinde temsil edilmiştir»
