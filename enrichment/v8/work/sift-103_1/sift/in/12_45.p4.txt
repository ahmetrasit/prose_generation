#175 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · ابن عطية · rejects · Ibn Atiyya takes the favor as God's favor on Joseph by bringing his release near, but Al-Alusi considers this interpretation far-fetched. «المراد بعد نعمة أنعم الله تعالى بها على يوسف عليه السلام»
#176 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · ابن عباس وزيد بن علي · reports · Ibn Abbas, Zayd ibn Ali, Mujahid, Ikrima, and Shubayl ibn Azra are reported to read with a light, open form from a verb meaning to forget; Al-Alusi dismisses those who rejected this reading. «قرأ ابن عباس وزيد بن علي»
#177 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The phrase after a long time is parenthetical between the speech verb and its content; it could be circumstantial with an understood قد, but joining it to the relative clause is rejected because the later remembering is new information, not a known description. «والجملة اعتراض بين القول والمقول وجوز أن تكون حالا من الموصول»
#178 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The cupbearer offers to inform them through access to someone who knows the interpretation, not from his own knowledge; he says send me so they will dispatch him to Joseph. «أي أخبركم بتأويل ذلك الذي خفى أمره بالتلقي ممن عنده علمه لا من تلقاء نفسي»
#179 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · الحسن · reports · Al-Hasan reads أنا آتيكم, 'I will bring you,' instead of 'I will inform you'; when corrected, he asks whether Joseph himself had been informing them. «وأخرج ابن أبي حاتم وأبو الشيخ عن الحسن أنه كان يقرأ أنا آتيكم»
#180 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · أبي · reports · Ubayy is also reported to read أنا آتيكم; al-Bahr says this is likewise the reading in the codex. «وأخرج ابن المنذر وغيره عن أبي أنه قرأ أيضا كذلك»
#181 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The survivor is the cupbearer who had asked Yusuf to interpret the dreams; after a long interval he remembered Yusuf and offered to explain the king's dream. «uzun bir müddet sonra -bu vesile ile- hatırlayıp dedi»
#182 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · When the courtiers could not interpret the dream, the cupbearer remembered Joseph and offered to explain it. «فلما ظهر عوص تعبير هذا الحلم تذكر ساقي الملك ما جرى له مع يوسف»
#183 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Starting with “I” and making himself the subject attracts the king's surprise and strengthens the claim that he can explain a baffling dream. «وابتداء كلامه بضميره وجعله مسندا إليه وخبره فعلي لقصد استجلاب تعجب الملك»
#184 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Calling himself the one who will inform them is figurative because he is the cause of the explanation; “send me” seeks the king's permission to leave his presence. «مجاز عقلي لأنه سبب الإنباء ، ولذلك قال»
#185 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · His confidence that Joseph remained in prison suggests it was a private prison: any release or death would have reached the king and his court. «ولعل سبب يقينه ببقاء يوسف عليه السلام في السجن أنه كان سجن الخاصة»
#186 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Ibn ʿĀshūr derives ادكر from اذتكر: the infix ت becomes د for ease and proximity of articulation, then ذ assimilates into the lighter د; this is the most eloquent substitution. «أصله : اذتكر ، وهو افتعال من الذكر ، قلبت تاء الافتعال دالا»
#187 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم · reports · The Prophet is reported to have recited the related form مدكر in Q 54:15, as found in the Ṣaḥīḥ. «وهو قراءة النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم في قوله تعالى : { فهل من مدكر»
#188 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Ummah here means a long period, originally the span in which a generation dies out; a generation itself can be called an ummah, as in Q 3:110 on one interpretation referring to the Companions. «والأمة : أطلقت هنا على المدة الطويلة ، وأصل إطلاق الأمة على المدة الطويلة»
#189 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Calling the period an ummah exaggerates how long the cupbearer forgot Yusuf; the Torah gives the interval as two years. «وإطلاقه في هذه الآية مبالغة في زمن نسيان الساقي . وفي التوراة كانت مدة نسيانه سنتين»
#190 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The plural address in “inform you” and “send me” is directed to the king as an honorific plural, comparable to Q 23:99. «ضمائر جمع المخاطب في أنبئكم } { فأرسلون } مخاطب بها الملك على وجه التعظيم»
#191 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The cupbearer leaves the recipient unnamed so the news of Yusuf's interpretation can surprise the court and land with greater force; a prisoner was not expected to have such skill. «ولم يسم لهم المرسل إليه لأنه أراد أن يفاجئهم بخبر يوسف عليه السلام بعد حصول تعبيره»
#192 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The later statement that the cupbearer remembered Joseph after a lapse of years confirms that the cupbearer, not Joseph, was the one who forgot. «قرينة صالحة على أن الناسي هو الساقي دون يوسف» [filed under 12:42, 12:45]
#193 TABATABAI d.1402 · الملأ · reports · The officials tell the king that the vision is a jumble of dreams and that they cannot interpret this kind of mixed dream. «قالوا أي قال الملأ للملك ما رأيته أضغاث أحلام وأخلاط من منامات مختلفة»
#194 TABATABAI d.1402 · الملأ · reports · An alternative reading is that the officials cannot interpret dreams generally, or that they know only how to interpret true dreams. «أو وما نحن بتأويل جميع المنامات بعالمين وإنما نعلم تأويل الرؤى الصالحة»
#195 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · Umma here means a span of years: the period during which the cupbearer forgot to mention Joseph to his lord after Joseph asked him to do so. «والمراد بها ههنا الجماعة من السنين وهى المدة»
#196 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The survivor is the cupbearer, who recalls Joseph’s request after years and offers to bring the interpretation of the king’s dream from him in prison. «وقال الذي نجا من السجن من صاحبي يوسف فيه وادكر بعد جماعة من السنين ما سأله يوسف»
#197 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The plural address in I will inform you and send me includes the officials around the king, who manage affairs of state; the later reference to returning to the people supports this. «تشريك لمن حضر مع الملك وهم الملأ من أركان الدولة»
#198 TABATABAI d.1402 · تفسير القمي · reports · Al-Qummī reports the king’s dream of seven fat cows eaten by seven lean cows and seven green ears with dry ones; the cupbearer recalled it after a time, and Joseph interpreted seven plentiful years, seven famine years, then rain. «ثم إن الملك رأى رؤيا فقال لوزرائه إني رأيت في نومي سبع بقرات سمان» [filed under 12:45, 12:46, 12:47, 12:48, 12:49]
#199 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · Joseph interpreted Pharaoh's dream as seven productive years followed by famine, proposed storing surplus grain, and was appointed to Egypt's stores after the women and al-ʿAzīz's wife cleared him; prison thus led to kingship. «وفي هذه السنين انتصب يوسف لمقام عزة مصر» [filed under 12:42-57]
#200 ASAD-NOTES d.1412 · almost all authorities · reports · The word ummah here means a time or a long period of time. «According to almost all the authorities, the noun ummah denotes here "a time" or "a long period of time"»
