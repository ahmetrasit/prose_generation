#294 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · الراغب · reports · Al-Raghib derives بضع from a cut-off part of ten and gives the ranges three to ten or above five and below ten. «وقال الراغب:/ " البضع: بالكسر المقتطع من العشرة»
#295 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · holds · The word is linked to cutting: بضع اللحم means cut meat, بضاعة is a piece of capital, and مبضع is the cutting tool; بعض was discussed under البعوضة. «قلت: فجعله مشتقا من مادة البضع وهي القطع، ومنه: بضعت اللحم»
#296 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · الزمخشري · reports · ظن may be attributed to Joseph if his dream interpretation was reasoning, or to the cupbearer if the interpretation came by revelation; it may also mean certainty. «فاعل " ظن " يجوز أن يكون يوسف عليه السلام إن كان تأويله بطريقة الاجتهاد»
#297 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · prefers · If ظن retains its ordinary sense, it suits Joseph only under independent reasoning; revelation is certain and would make the cupbearer the one who thought, unless ظن means certainty. «يعني أنه إن كان الظن على بابه فلا يستقيم إسناده إلى يوسف»
#298 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · قتادة · reports · Qatada keeps the ordinary sense of ظن and attributes it to Joseph because dream interpretation is conjectural. «ذهب قتادة، فإنه قال: " الظن هنا على بابه لأن عبارة الرؤيا ظن»
#299 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · holds · منهما may modify ناج or be a circumstantial phrase from the relative pronoun; attaching it to ناج would misleadingly suggest someone escaped from the two. «على أنه حال من الموصول»
#300 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · أبو البقاء · reports · Abu al-Baqa rejects attaching منهما to ناج because that is not the intended sense. «قال أبو البقاء: " ولا يكون متعلقا ب " ناج " لأنه ليس المعنى عليه "»
#301 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · prefers · The pronoun in فأنساه refers to the cupbearer; the alternative reference to Joseph is weak. «والضمير في " فأنساه " يعود على الشرابي. وقيل: على يوسف، وهو ضعيف.»
#302 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · holds · Yusuf privately asks the surviving cupbearer to mention his case to the king, apparently so the other prisoner will not realize he is to be crucified. «قال له يوسف خفية عن الآخر والله أعلم»
#303 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · holds · The cupbearer forgot to speak to the king, which Ibn Kathir calls one of Satan's stratagems to keep God's prophet imprisoned. «فنسي ذلك الموصى أن يذكر مولاه بذلك»
#304 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · prefers · The pronoun in 'Satan made him forget' correctly refers to the rescued cupbearer, as Mujahid, Ibn Ishaq, and others held. «هذا هو الصواب أن الضمير في قوله: {فأنساه الشيطان ذكر ربه} عائد على الناجي»
#305 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · ابن عباس · reports · An alternative, transmitted by Ibn Jarir from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ikrima, and others, makes the pronoun refer to Yusuf. «رواه ابن جرير، عن ابن عباس»
#306 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم · rejects · A report from Ibn Abbas says Yusuf's request for relief from someone other than God prolonged his imprisonment; Ibn Kathir calls it very weak because Sufyan ibn Waki and Ibrahim al-Khuzi are both weak, the latter weaker. «وهذا الحديث ضعيف جدا؛ لأن سفيان بن وكيع ضعيف»
#307 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · الحسن · rejects · Mursal versions from al-Hasan and Qatada are also unacceptable here, even if mursal reports are accepted in other contexts. «وقد روي عن الحسن وقتادة مرسلا عن كل منهما»
#308 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · مجاهد · reports · Mujahid and Qatada define bid' as a span from three to nine years. «فقال مجاهد وقتادة: هو ما بين الثلاث إلى التسع»
#309 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · وهب بن منبه · reports · Wahb says Ayyub endured affliction seven years, Yusuf remained in prison seven, and Nebuchadnezzar's punishment lasted seven. «وقال وهب بن منبه: مكث أيوب في البلاء سبعا»
#310 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · الضحاك · reports · Al-Dahhak transmits from Ibn Abbas that Yusuf remained twelve years, and separately says fourteen years. «وقال الضحاك، عن ابن عباس، رضي الله عنهما:»
#311 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · ظن may mean knowledge, since all human knowledge is nothing beside God's, or ordinary inference from clues; this could permit decisive speech based on probability, or the subject may be the cupbearer. «ويمكن أن يكون الظن على بابه لكونه قال ما مضى اجتهادا بقرائن»
#312 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · Joseph asks the cupbearer to tell the king about Joseph's virtue and clean character, which contradict the accusation against him; رب here means the king, unlike God's use elsewhere. «أي سيدك ملك مصر , بما رأيت مني من معالي الأخلاق وطهارة الشيم»
#313 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · As Joseph predicted, the cupbearer survived and the baker was crucified; Satan made the cupbearer forget Joseph because Joseph relied on him, prolonging Joseph's imprisonment. «أي الساقي { الشيطان } أي البعيد من الرحمة المحترق باللعنه»
#314 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · Joseph's continued imprisonment teaches that causes have no effect independently of God; بضع means three through nine, and the reported term here was seven years. «وحقيقة البضع من الثلاث إلى التسع , والمروي هنا أنه كان سبعا»
#315 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · التوراة · reports · The Torah account says Midianites brought Joseph to Egypt and Potiphar bought him; God prospered his work, and Potiphar trusted him with his household and possessions. «فأهبط المدينيون يوسف إلى مصر , فاشتراه قوطيفر الأمير صاحب شرطة فرعون»
#316 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · التوراة · reports · In the Torah account, Joseph refuses his master's wife as betrayal and sin before God, flees when she seizes his tunic, and she falsely accuses him of attempted assault. «فكيف أرتكب هذا الشر العظيم»
#317 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · التوراة · reports · The Torah says Potiphar imprisoned Joseph, but God won him favor with the jailer, who entrusted him with the prisoners; whatever Joseph did prospered. «فمكث هناك في السجن , وكان الرب يبصره»
#318 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · التوراة · reports · The Torah says Pharaoh jailed his cupbearer and baker in Joseph's prison after they offended him, and Joseph served them; they had separate dreams on the same night. «في الحبس الذي كان فيه يوسفن فسلط صاحب السجن يوسف عليهما»
#319 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · التوراة · reports · In the cupbearer's dream, a vine had three branches that budded, flowered, and ripened into grapes; he squeezed them into Pharaoh's cup and served the king. «فبينا هي كذلك إذ فرعت وبنت ورقها»
#320 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · التوراة · reports · Joseph interprets the three branches as three days, after which Pharaoh will restore the cupbearer; he asks him to remember Joseph and help free him. «الثلاثة قضبان هي ثلاثة أيام»
#321 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · التوراة · reports · The baker dreams of three baskets of bread on his head, with birds eating the uppermost food; Joseph interprets the baskets as three days before the baker is executed and eaten by birds. «ثلاثة أطباق هي ثلاثة أيام»
#322 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · التوراة · reports · On Pharaoh's birthday, the cupbearer was restored and the baker executed as Joseph predicted, but the cupbearer did not remember Joseph. «فأمر برد رئيس أصحاب الشراب على موضعه»
#323 DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas describes three punishments for Joseph: prison over his desire, prolonged prison after asking the cupbearer to remember him, and the brothers' taunt that Benjamin had stolen as Joseph accused them of theft. «عن ابن عباس رضي الله عنهما قال: عوقب يوسف عليه السلام ثلاث مرات» [filed under 12:35, 12:42, 12:70]
#324 DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن سابط · reports · Ibn Sabit explains Joseph's request to be remembered before the cupbearer's lord as a request to be remembered before the king; Qatadah gives the same gloss. «عن ابن سابط رضي الله عنه {وقال للذي ظن أنه ناج منهما اذكرني عند ربك} قال: عند ملك الأرض»
#325 DURR-FULL d.911 · إبراهيم التيمي · reports · On the way to prison, Joseph told the guard who asked his need that he wanted the man to mention him to his lord, meaning the king who ruled Joseph. «عن إبراهيم التيمي رضي الله عنه قال لما انتهى به إلى باب السجن قال له: اوصني بحاجتك»
#326 DURR-FULL d.911 · قتادة · reports · Qatadah says dream interpretation proceeds by conjecture, while God may confirm or undo what He wills. «عن قتادة رضي الله عنه في قوله {وقال للذي ظن أنه ناج} قال إنما عبارة الرؤيا بالظن»
#327 DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن عباس · reports · A report from the Prophet says that, had Joseph not uttered his request to the cupbearer, he would not have remained in prison so long; the report connects this to seeking relief from someone besides God. «عن ابن عباس رضي الله عنهما قال: قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم لو لم يقل يوسف عليه السلام»
#328 DURR-FULL d.911 · أبو هريرة · reports · Abu Hurayra transmits the Prophet's prayer for mercy on Joseph and says he would have left prison quickly instead of asking the cupbearer to remember him. «عن أبي هريرة رضي الله عنه قال قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم: رحم الله يوسف لو لم يقل»
#329 DURR-FULL d.911 · الحسن · reports · Hasan transmits a Prophetic prayer for mercy on Joseph because he asked the cupbearer to remember him; Hasan weeps and says people turn to other people when hardship comes. «عن الحسن رضي الله عنه قال: ذكر لنا أن نبي الله صلى الله عليه وسلم قال: رحم الله يوسف»
#330 DURR-FULL d.911 · قتادة · reports · Qatadah says a report from the Prophet blamed Joseph's seeking another's intercession, and that he was punished for seeking an intermediary against his Lord. «عن قتادة رضي الله عنه قال: ذكر لنا أن النبي اله صلى الله عليه وسلم قال: لولا أن يوسف»
#331 DURR-FULL d.911 · أنس · reports · In a report from Anas, God asks Joseph who rescued him from his brothers, the well, and the woman; Joseph answers God, then explains that distress made him utter a word and remember a human. «عن أنس رضي الله عنه قال: أوحى إلى يوسف: من استنقذك من القتل حين هم اخوتك»
#332 DURR-FULL d.911 · الحسن · reports · Hasan reports that God asked Joseph whether he had taken a human as an agent and threatened longer confinement; Joseph wept that distress had occupied his heart. «عن الحسن رضي الله عنه قال: لما قال يوسف عليه السلام للساقي: اذكرني عند ربك قيل له»
