<<notes for 35_37, part 6 of 0..7>>
#268 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · A Persian couplet contrasts the youthful who hurry toward spiritual gain with old age, whose coldness prevents the vessel from advancing. «ذل زدنيا زودتر كردد جوانا نرا خنك كهنكى از سردىء آبست مانع كوزه را»
#269 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · Companions and their successors intensified worship and night prayer at forty or on seeing gray hair, reduced social contact, and left their beds; no age is safe because death’s time is hidden. «وكان جماعة من الصحابة ومن بعدهم إذا بلغ أربعين سنة أو رأى شيبا بالغ في الاجتهاد»
#270 BURSEVI d.1127 · الجمهور · reports · The majority identify the warner as the Messenger; alternatives are the Qur’an, reason distinguishing good from evil, deaths among family and neighbors, or gray hair. «والمراد بالنذير رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم وعليه الجمهور»
#271 BURSEVI d.1127 · الكاشفي · reports · Kashifi says many scholars identify gray hair as the warner, since it signals life’s flame waning and the mirror of one’s being turning white. «قال الكاشفي : (وأكثر علما برآنند كه مراد از نذير شيب است»
#272 BURSEVI d.1127 · reports · A report says Abraham was the first of Adam’s descendants to gray; God called gray hair dignity in this world and light in the next, and Abraham asked for more. «قيل : أول من شاب من ولد آدم عليه السلام إبراهيم الخليل»
#273 BURSEVI d.1127 · النبي · reports · A hadith says God dislikes an old man whose hair has not turned gray, as explained in al-Maqasid al-Hasana. «وفي الحديث : "إن الله يبغض الشيخ الغربيب"»
#274 BURSEVI d.1127 · الكواشي · reports · Al-Kawashi allows every sign warning of departure from life to count as a warner and calls for heedfulness when it appears. «بالانتقال»
#275 BURSEVI d.1127 · محمد بن الحسن · reports · The sounder view attributed to Muhammad al-Shaybani makes Hajj an expanded duty that may be delayed unless delay is likely to cause it to be missed; sudden death brings no sin, but clear warning makes delay impermissible. «ولذا قال أهل الأصول الصحيح من قولي محمد أن الحج يجب موسعا»
#276 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · The condemned beg to return for the whole span of worldly existence; God answers that He gave them life and sent a warner, yet they admit they had both. «بمقدار زمان دنيا از اول ابداع تا آخر انقطاع فرياد كنند»
#277 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · The command to taste punishment follows from God’s granting life and sending a warner; the wrongdoers are those who wronged themselves through disbelief and idolatry, with no defender to repel punishment. «فالفاء لترتيب الأمر بالذوق على ما قبلها من التعمير»
#278 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · Ishari reading: people were asleep in worldly heedlessness and only awaken fully after death and resurrection, when they taste and perceive punishment. «وفيه إشارة إلى أنهم كانوا في الدنيا نائمين ولذا لم يذوقوا الألم»
#279 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · Yaṣṭarikhūn is an iftiʿāl form of ṣarākh, intense shouting, with original tāʾ changed to ṭāʾ; it often means calling for rescue. «إفتعال من الصراخ وهو شدة الصياح والأصل يصترخون فأبدلت التاء طاء»
#280 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · أبو حيان · reports · Abū Ḥayyān takes mā as a temporal verbal noun; this is challenged because fīhi cannot refer to it unless treated as a noun or refers to age implied by ‘We gave you long life.’ «وقال أبو حيان : ما مصدرية ظرفية أي ألم نعمركم في مدة تذكر»
#281 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · قتادة · reports · Qatāda says they seek rescue from God; the suggestion they call to one another in bewilderment is rejected. «وبه فسره هنا قتادة فقال : يستغيثون فيها واستغاثتهم بالله عزوجل بدليل ما بعده»
#282 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · Their plea may be introduced by implied ‘saying’ and coordinated with prior speech, stand independently as an explanation, or function as a circumstantial phrase. «بإضمار القول أي ويقولون بالعطف أو يقولون بدونه على أنه تفسير لما قبله»
#283 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · Calling requested work different from former work expresses regret and desire to repair admitted wrongdoing; alternatively, they mistakenly thought past conduct good. «وتقييد العمل الصالح بالوصف المذكور للتحسر على ما عملوه من غير الصالح»
#284 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · أبو البقاء · reports · Abū al-Baqāʾ offers parses making ṣāliḥan and ghayra modifiers of omitted nouns, or ṣāliḥan a modifier and ghayra the object of ‘we do.’ «يجوز أن يكونا صفتين لمصدر محذوف أو لمفعول محذوف»
#285 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · They ask to leave Hell and return to worldly life for righteous work, especially affirming God’s unity, obeying the Messenger, and submitting. «فالمراد أخرجنا من النار وردنا إلى الدنيا نعمل صالحا»
#286 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn ʿAbbās explains ‘we do righteous work’ as saying lā ilāha illā Allāh. «نعمل صالحا»
#287 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The divine question rebukes their plea and denies God failed to give ample time for reflection and reminders. «جواب من جهته تعالى وتوبيخ لهم في الآخرة حين يقولون»
#288 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · Mā means the age or span of years in which anyone wishing to remember could reflect and be reminded. «أي العمر الذي أو عمرا يتذكر فيه من تذكر»
#289 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · ابن الحاجب · reports · Ibn al-Ḥājib says making mā negative is untenable in wording and meaning. «وجعل ما نافية لا يصح كما قال إبن الحاجب لفظا ومعنى»
#290 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · علي وابن عباس · reports · A report from ʿAlī, authenticated by al-Ḥākim from Ibn ʿAbbās, sets sufficient lifetime at sixty; Sahl ibn Saʿd reports God keeps a person alive to sixty to remove excuses. «على ما روى عن علي كرم الله تعالى وجهه وأخرجه جماعة وصححه الحاكم عن إبن عباس ستون سنة»
#291 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · Other estimates are fifty, forty-six (Ibn ʿAbbās), forty or puberty (al-Ḥasan), seventeen, eighteen (Qatāda), twenty (ʿUmar ibn ʿAbd al-ʿAzīz), and twenty to sixty (Mujāhid). «خمسون سنة وفي رواية عن إبن عباس أنه ست وأربعون سنة»
#292 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · الأعمش · reports · Al-Aʿmash reads mā yadhkur fīhi man udhkira with assimilation and pronounces the connecting hamzat al-waṣl in connected speech. «وقرأ الأعمش ( ما يذكر فيه من أذكر ) بالإدغام»
#293 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · ‘The warner came to you’ coordinates with the question’s meaning, effectively ‘We gave you life and the warner came’; another parse coordinates it with ‘We gave you life.’ «فكأنه قيل : عمرناكم وجاءكم النذير»
#294 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · السدي وابن زيد · reports · Al-Suddī and Ibn Zayd identify the warner as Muḥammad; another view says the Qur’an, while Abū Ḥayyān takes all prophets, supported by the plural al-nudhur. «والمراد بالنذير على ما روى عن السدي»
#295 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · ابن عباس وعكرمة وسفيان بن عيينة ووكيع والحسين بن الفضل والفراء والطبري · reports · These authorities say the warner is gray hair; a report makes each white hair warn its neighbor of death, and poetry likewise calls gray hair a herald. «وعن إبن عباس وعكرمة وسفيان بن عيينة ووكيع والحسين بن الفضل والفراء والطبري هو الشيب»
#296 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · Other warners are fever, deaths of relatives, or maturity of reason; the singular ‘warner’ fits the immediate point. «موت الأهل والأقارب»
#297 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The command to taste punishment follows from long life and the warner’s arrival; denial of a helper is causal, ‘wrongdoers’ means unbelievers, and naming them rebukes them. «لترتيب الأمر بالذوق على ما قبلها من التعمير ومجيء النذير»
#298 ELMALILI d.1361 · Peygamber · reports · A prophetic report gives sixty years as the maximum lifetime for reflection. «Altmış, Peygamber’den mervî olduğu üzere»
#299 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · After the stated lifetime, disbelief has no excuse. «Yani bundan sonra küfre hiç mazeret kalmıyor demektir.»
#300 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The warning that came refers to the subsidiary branches of religious rulings. «Bu da fürû‘-ı ahkâma nazarandır.»
#301 ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · Reported views on the lifetime sufficient for reflection range from sixty, forty-six, or forty years to puberty, twenty years, or any age from twenty to sixty; God knows its truth. «Altmış, kırk altı, kırk, sinn-i büluğ, yirmi, yirmiden altmışa kadar denilmiş ise de hakikatini Allah bilir.»
#302 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The stated term applies to everyone who dies after puberty. «Büluğdan sonra her vefat eden hakkında bu müddet tahakkuk etmiş demektir.»
#303 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The pronoun refers to those who disbelieved in 35:36, and the clause is coordinated with ‘they have the Fire of Hell’; it is not circumstantial because the preceding conclusion closes that discourse. «الضمير إلى { الذين كفروا } ( فاطر : 36 ) والجملة عطف على جملة { لهم نار جهنم }»
#304 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Yaṣṭarikhūn intensifies yaṣrukhūn: they scream with great effort because of what has befallen them; their plea explains the screaming and displays their helplessness. «و { يصطرخون } مبالغة في ( يصرخون ) لأنه افتعال من الصراخ»
#305 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Their promise to do good if released is belated repentance; the jussive yaʿmal ṣāliḥan answers the plea with the understood condition ‘if You release us.’ «ولإرادة الوعد جزم { نعمل صالحا } في جواب الدعاء . والتقدير : إن تخرجنا نعمل صالحا .»
#306 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Calling their future deeds different from what they did before expresses regret: they now know their former deeds were false and harmful in the realm of realities. «وهذا ندامة على ما كانوا يعملونه لأنهم أيقنوا بفساد عملهم وضره»
#307 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Taʿmīr is prolonging a life; the related expression ‘a time’ is a temporal, masdar-based adverbial phrase. «والتعمير : تطويل العمر . وقد تقدم غير مرة ، منها عند قوله تعالى : { يود أحدهم لو يعمر ألف سنة في سورة البقرة»
#308 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The clause ‘whoever reflects in it’ describes a span of time sufficient for reflection and instruction. «أي زمانا كافيا بامتداده للتذكر والتبصير»
#309 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The warner is Muhammad; calling him a warner highlights the aspect of his mission most relevant to these people. «و { النذير } الرسول محمد صلى الله عليه وسلم»
