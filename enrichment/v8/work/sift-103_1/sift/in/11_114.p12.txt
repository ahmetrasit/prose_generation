#467 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · reports · Those who regard the whole surah as Meccan take the verse as a general command to pray, recited by the Prophet to a penitent; “revealed” can mean applying its general rule to his case. «وأما الذين رجحوا أن السورة كلها مكية»
#468 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · عبد الله بن مسعود · reports · In another Tirmidhi transmission through Alqama and al-Aswad, Ibn Masud says the Prophet recited the verse, without saying it was newly revealed. «عن علقمة والأسود عن ابن مسعود»
#469 TABATABAI d.1402 · أبو عبد الله عليه السلام · reports · A report identifies the two ends of the day as sunset and dawn prayers and the night periods as the late evening prayer. «وفيه عن جرير عن أبي عبد الله عليه السلام قال»
#470 TABATABAI d.1402 · أبو جعفر عليه السلام · reports · A report from Zurara connects the verse to the five daily prayers; al-Tabataba'i says it suggests the day's two sides mean periods before and after noon. «وفي التهذيب بإسناده عن زرارة عن أبي جعفر عليه السلام في حديث في الصلوات الخمس اليومية»
#471 TABATABAI d.1402 · أبو عبد الله عليه السلام · reports · A report explains that a believer's night prayer removes sins committed during the day; the author notes parallel transmissions in several collections. «قال صلاة المؤمن بالليل تذهب بما عمل من ذنب النهار»
#472 TABATABAI d.1402 · رسول الله صلى الله عليه وآله وسلم · reports · In a report narrated by Salman, the Prophet shakes a dry branch and says that good ablution followed by the five prayers sheds a Muslim's sins like falling leaves. «هكذا فعله رسول الله صلى الله عليه وآله وسلم وأنا معه تحت شجرة»
#473 TABATABAI d.1402 · علي بن أبي طالب عليه السلام · reports · Ali recounts a man confessing a sin; after the man prayed with the Prophet following good ablution, the Prophet told him the prayer expiated the sin. «عن الحارث عن علي بن أبي طالب عليه السلام قال كنا مع رسول الله صلى الله عليه وآله وسلم»
#474 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The confession story circulates through many Companion routes with variations; al-Tirmidhi reports it from Abu al-Yusr, who experienced it. «والرواية مروية بطرق كثيرة عن ابن مسعود وأبى أمامة ومعاذ بن جبل وابن عباس»
#475 TABATABAI d.1402 · أحدهما عليهما السلام · reports · When Ali asks which verse gives Muslims most hope, he dismisses several proposed forgiveness verses and identifies the command to establish prayer. «عن أبى حمزة الثمالى قال سمعت أحدهما عليهما السلام يقول إن عليا عليه السلام أقبل على الناس»
#476 TABATABAI d.1402 · رسول الله صلى الله عليه وآله وسلم · reports · The report says five prayers wash away sins like a river cleans dirt, leaving the worshiper cleansed as at birth after each prayer. «إنما منزلة الصلوات الخمس لأمتي كنهر جار على باب أحدكم»
#477 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The prayer-as-river simile is also transmitted in Sunni collections from Companions including Abu Hurayra, Anas, Jabir, and Abu Sa'id. «وقد روي المثل المذكور في آخر الحديث من طرق أهل السنة عن عدة من الصحابة كأبي هريرة وأنس وجابر»
#478 TABATABAI d.1402 · أبو عبد الله عليه السلام · reports · Abu Abdillah warns that angels record each deed; small sins should not be dismissed, and a good deed can erase an old grave sin, as the verse teaches. «عن إبراهيم الكرخي قال كنت عند أبي عبد الله عليه السلام فدخل عليه رجل من أهل المدينة»
#479 TABATABAI d.1402 · أبو عبد الله عليه السلام · reports · Asked whether charity and pilgrimage from officials' earnings erase the sin, Abu Abdillah says one sin does not cancel another, but a good deed cancels sin. «عن سماعة بن مهران قال سأل أبا عبد الله عليه السلام رجل من أهل الجبال»
#480 TABATABAI d.1402 · رسول الله صلى الله عليه وآله وسلم · reports · A report says a Muslim who performs ablution and obligatory prayer well is forgiven for sins committed since the preceding prayer. «عن أبى أمامة قال قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وآله وسلم»
#481 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · Good deeds, especially prescribed prayers, remove traces of sins from believers' souls; this teaching serves as a reminder for those who remember God. «تعليل لقوله وأقم الصلاة وبيان أن الصلوات حسنات واردة على نفوس المؤمنين»
#482 ASAD-NOTES d.1412 · holds · The literal phrase is at the two ends of the day. «at the two ends»
#483 ASAD-NOTES d.1412 · holds · The command covers the obligatory prayers whose forms and exact times are set by the Prophet's authenticated sayings and practice: dawn, after midday, afternoon, sunset, and early night. «both of which are clearly laid down in the sunnah»
#484 ASAD-NOTES d.1412 · holds · Because the verse stresses prayer's importance generally, Asad takes it to include remembrance of God throughout waking life, beyond the five obligatory prayers. «it is safe to assume that it refers not merely to the five obligatory prayers but to a remembrance of God at all times»
#485 ASAD-NOTES d.1412 · Rashid Rida' · rejects · Rashid Rida' rejects the proposed Medina dating of verses 12, 17, and 114 as unconvincing and holds the whole surah Meccan. «this view as unconvincing and holds that the surah in its entirety was revealed at Mecca» [filed under 11:12, 11:17, 11:114]
#486 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · The closing section states why the stories were told and guides the Prophet and companions; Quraysh must learn from local history or face the same consequences. «The Quraysh are warned to learn a lesson from the history of their country» [filed under 11:101-123]
#487 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · The sūrah as a whole is a comprehensive sermon directed toward one prescribed purpose. «the whole of it is a comprehensive sermon on a prescribed purpose» [filed under 11:1-123]
#488 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · holds · Day runs from dawn to sunset and night from sunset to dawn; the day’s two edges are dawn and sunset. The prayer at the second edge is variously identified as noon and afternoon before sunset, or sunset and night prayers after it. «Gündüzün iki tarafından maksat, geceyle birleşen iki tarafı, yani başı ve sonu olup tan yerinin ağardığı ve güneşin battığı zamanlardır»
#489 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · holds · Zulef, plural of zulfa, means the night’s early hours near day and refers here to the night prayer; because the verse gives no form or exact times, it stresses prayer at the indicated periods without detailing them, as Shawkani notes. «“Gündüze yakın saatler” diye tercüme ettiğimiz zülef kelimesi ise zülfenin çoğulu olup gecenin gündüze yakın olan ilk saatlerini ifade eder»
#490 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · some interpreters · reports · Some hold that this verse sets the times for all obligatory prayers; the mutawatir Sunna supplies their specific times and manner. «Bu âyetin bütün farz namazların vakitlerini belirlediği kanaatinde olanlar da vardır»
#491 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · holds · The verse calls for worship and abundant good deeds to remove or forgive evils, especially prayer, and its closing words address those who turn toward Qur’anic guidance. «Âyet, kötülüklerin ortadan kalkması veya bağışlanması için ibadetlerle iyiliklerin çokça yapılmasının gereğine işaret etmektedir»
#492 QIRAAT-ER · holds · The vowel shift from zulafan to zulufan changes the internal pattern of the plural without changing meaning. «Vowel shift: zulafan → zulufan (ḍamma on lām) — plural of zulfa with different internal vowel pattern»
#493 STUDYQURAN · holds · The prayers named here are generally understood as the five canonical daily prayers. «The prayers here are usually understood as referring»
#494 STUDYQURAN · the Prophet · reports · The Prophet teaches that a good deed following an evil deed erases it. «Follow up an evil deed with a good deed»
#495 STUDYQURAN · the Prophet · reports · The heart is polished by remembrance of God; prayer and remembrance can affect a heart darkened by sin. «In a ḥadīth the Prophet said, “For everything there is a polish, and the polish of the heart is the remembrance of God”»
#496 STUDYQURAN · reports · One identification of the two day-end prayers is dawn and sunset. «the prayers at the two ends of the day are interpreted to be the prayers performed at dawn»
#497 STUDYQURAN · reports · Another identification of the two day-end prayers is dawn and late afternoon. «or at dawn and in the late afternoon»
#498 STUDYQURAN · reports · A third identification is midday and late afternoon. «A similar range of opinion regarding the five prayers can be seen»
#499 STUDYQURAN · reports · The early-night prayers may collectively mean sunset and night prayers. «the early hours of the night can collectively refer to the prayers performed at sunset and during the night»
#500 STUDYQURAN · reports · Alternatively, the early-night phrase may refer only to the night prayer. «or to the latter only (IK)»
#501 STUDYQURAN · holds · The range of interpretations of the five prayers resembles the disagreement over which prayer is the middlemost at 2:238. «A similar range of opinion regarding the five prayers can be seen in identifying the middlemost prayer in 2:238»
#502 STUDYQURAN · the Prophet · reports · The daily prayers expiate minor sins committed between them, provided major sins are avoided. «the Prophet is reported to have said, “Verily from prayer to prayer there is an expiation»
#503 STUDYQURAN · holds · More generally, the positive effects of good deeds can counterbalance the negative effects of evil deeds. «In a more general sense, it can refer to the manner in which the positive effects of good actions can offset the negative effects of evil actions»
#504 STUDYQURAN · holds · The sūrah encourages the Prophet and believers to persist in the prescribed prayers. «to persist in the prescribed prayers (v. 114)»
#505 STUDYQURAN · many commentators · reports · Many commentators regard verses 12, 17, and 114 as possible exceptions to the sūrah’s Makkan dating, placing them in Madinah. «vv. 12, 17, and 114, which are said by many commentators to have been revealed in Madinah (JJ)» [filed under 11:12, 11:17, 11:114]
