#226 BURSEVI d.1127 · reports · The oath may refer to the afternoon itself, which has signs of divine power, or to the era of Prophethood, whose duration is likened to a day or half-day and which excels as the age of the best prophet, community, and revealed book. «ويقال اقسم بالعشى الذي هو ما بين الزوال والغروب كما سم بالضحى لما فيها جميعا من دلائل القدرة»
#227 BURSEVI d.1127 · رسول الله · reports · A report says that if the Prophet's community remains upright, it has a day, and if not, half a day. «عليه السلام : إن استقامت أمتي فلها يوم وإن لم تستقم فلها نصف يوم»
#228 BURSEVI d.1127 · reports · Another reading takes ‘asr as time itself, filled with extraordinary events, and rejects blaming time for misfortune; God swears by it as a locus of divine signs and self-disclosure through attributes and acts. «ويقال اقسم بالدهر لانطوائه على أعاجيب الأمور القارة والمارة»
#229 BURSEVI d.1127 · رسول الله · reports · The Prophet says not to revile time, for God is time. «لا تسبوا الدهر فإن الله هو الدهر»
#230 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · The ‘asr gathers the signs sworn by elsewhere in the Qur’an, concluding the sequence of oaths about dawn, sun, moon, night, day, and duha. «فلما كان العصر جامعا لجميع الآيات التي أقسم الله بها في القرآن»
#231 BURSEVI d.1127 · التأويلات النجمية · reports · The Najmiyya reading says God swears by time's complete continuity because it encompasses the Prophet's sainthood, prophethood, mission, and caliphate, citing reports about his pre-eternal prophetic status and the cosmos being created for him. «أقسم الله بكمال دوامه الزمان واستمراره لاشتماله على ولاية النبي عليه السلام ونبوته ورسالته وخلافته»
#232 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · The loss is grave because the servant sins against the Lord and against immense benefaction; the indefinite form may also mark a distinctive kind of loss beyond ordinary human usage. «والذنب يعظم إما لعظم من في حقه الذنب أو لأنه في مقابلة النعمة العظيمة» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#233 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · مقاتل · reports · Muqatil says the oath is by the Asr prayer because of its virtue; the majority identify it as the middle prayer, supported by reports naming it and warning against missing it. «قال مقاتل أقسم سبحانه بصلاة العصر لفضلها لانها الصلاة الوسطى عند الجمهور»
#234 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · rejects · The author dismisses a story about a sinful woman being told her gravest sin was missing Asr prayer as unreliable material from an otherwise prominent transmitter. «ذكر ذلك الامام وهو لعمري امام في نقل مثل ذلك مما لايعول عليه عند أئمة الحديث»
#235 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · Asr prayer is especially meritorious because people neglect it while absorbed in trade and work late in the day. «وخصت بالفضل لان التكليف في أدائها أشق لتهافت الناس في تجاراتهم ومكاسبهم آخر النهار»
#236 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · قتادة · reports · Qatada says the oath is by the time of the Asr prayer, or by the creation of Adam on Friday; he also explains al-asr as evening, rich in signs of divine power like dawn. «وقيل أقسم عز وجل بوقت تلك الصلاة لفضيلة صلاته أو لخلق آدم أبي البشر عليه السلام فيه من يوم الجمعة»
#237 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · الزجاج · reports · Al-Zajjaj says al-asr means day or night, and cites Hamid ibn Thawr's verse about the two spans of time, day and night. «وقال الزجاج العصر اليوم والعصر الليلة وعليه قول حميد بن ثور»
#238 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · قيل · reports · Some interpret al-asr as morning and evening, the two cool periods; on this and the preceding view the oath is to one of two unspecified times. «وقيل العصر بكرة والعصر عشية وهما الابرادان»
#239 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · قيل · reports · Some take al-asr as the Prophet's era or his lifetime through the Resurrection, whose remaining span is compared in a Bukhari report to the time from Asr to sunset; its honor comes from the Prophet and his community. «وقيل المراد به عصر النبوة وكأنه عنى به وقت حياته عليه الصلاة والسلام»
#240 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas says al-asr is time itself, encompassing wonders and blessings as well as their opposites; the oath warns people facing ruin or salvation. «وقال ابن عباس هو الدهر أقسم عز وجل به لاشتماله على أصناف العجائب»
#241 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The oath honors time while denying that time itself causes loss or events, as some claim; attributing loss to humanity shows it is a human quality, not a defect of time. «ويعرض عز وجل لما في الاقسام به من التعظيم بنفي أن يكون له خسران أو دخل فيه كما يزعمه من يضيف الحوادث اليه»
#242 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · ابن عباس وابن الزبير والجمهور · reports · They classify Surat al-ʿAsr as Meccan; Mujahid, Qatada, and Muqatil are cited for classifying it as Medinan, and its three verses are undisputed. «مكية في قول ابن عباس وابن الزبير والجمهور ومدنية في قول مجاهد وقتادة ومقاتل»
#243 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · الشافعي · reports · Al-Shafiʿi is reported to say that even if no other surah had been revealed, this one would suffice because it contains the Qurʾan’s sciences. «انه قال لو لم ينزل غير هذه السورة لكفت الناس لانها شملت جميع علوم القرآن»
#244 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · أبو حذيفة · reports · He reports that two Companions would recite Surat al-ʿAsr to one another whenever they met, parting only after one recited it and the other greeted him. «كان الرجلان من اصحاب رسول الله صلى الله تعالى عليه وسلم اذا التقيا لم يفترقا»
#245 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The surah follows al-Takathur because it points to those whom worldly rivalry did not distract. «وفيها اشارة إلى حال من لم يلهه التكاثر ولذا وضعت بعد سورته»
#246 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · rejects · The author objects that using al-asr to mean time in general is not linguistically apparent. «وتعقب بان استعمال العصر بذلك المعنى غير ظاهر»
#247 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Time gathers every kind of change, benefit, harm, and wonder; the oath draws attention to how people fare in gain or loss within it. «Zaman böyle varlıkla yokluk arasında nîk ü bed, ni‘met ü nikmet, serra vü darrâ» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#248 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The oath warns that time consumes life and leaves people in loss unless they believe and do good works. «o geçen leyl ü nehar vücûdunu kemirmekte ve bu suretle o her dem hüsran içinde kalmaktadır» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#249 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · In an elegy for his teacher and the Balkan War dead, the author portrays life as deceiving people while death draws its nourishment and each breath weaves shrouds. «إِنَّ الْحَيَاةَ تَغُرُّنَا وَمَ مَاتُنَا مِنْ ضَرْعِهَا يَسْتَرْضِعُ الأَْلْبَانَ» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#250 ELMALILI d.1361 · rejects · People wrongly blame time for their misfortunes; a cited poem says time is not corrupt, but people themselves are. «Zaman bozulmadı, kendileri fesad» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#251 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The oath honors time as a valuable blessing and says those who fail to work toward reform are themselves in loss. «Haddizâtında asrın, zamanın aybı, kabahati, zararı yoktur, o değerli bir nimettir» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#252 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The warning against blaming time is connected to the hadith forbidding people to curse time. «hadîs-i şerîfiyle[^26] işaret olunan mânadır. Bu tafsîlâttan anlaşılır ki asrı»
#253 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Swearing by asr honors time and avoids the materialist implication of saying wa-al-dahr, recalling those who claimed that only time destroys. «daha sarih olmak için وَالدّهْرِ denilmeyip de وَٱلْعَصْرِ buyurulması»
#254 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The definite article first directs hearers to the time closest to them; with the Prophet as first audience, this suggests the Prophetic era. «muhataplara yakından alâkası olan asrı düşündürmek lâzım gelir»
#255 ELMALILI d.1361 · prefers · The Muhammadic era is the most immediate sense of asr while also including the broader temporal meanings. «Bu asırdan murad, asr-ı nübüvvet, yani asr-ı Muhammedî olmak tebâdür eder»
#256 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Human life is precious capital; each unprofitable instant wastes it, but a final righteous act may make up for years of waste and win eternal paradise. «İnsanın ömrü en kıymetli sermayesidir. Ne kazanacaksa onunla kazanacaktır» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#257 ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · Sufis say a person should be the child of the moment: value the present and use it to gain good for the afterlife. «sûfiyye, “sûfî ibnü’l-vakt olmalıdır; yani ömrünün ve bilhassa fiilen içinde bulunduğu vakt-i hâlin» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#258 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · People who deny the afterlife also urge seizing the present for worldly pleasure, though their aim differs from the Sufi's afterlife-oriented use of time. «gün bugündür, saat bu saattir, ne yapacaksak şimdi yapmalıyız” diyerek herçibâdâbad vaktine» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#259 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Time is the opportunity for action, whether productive or wasteful, profitable or harmful; the oath highlights the value and nature of life's moments. «İşte vakit böyle bir fırsat ve ömür bütün ânâtıyla böyle kâr ve zarara mâruz bir sermaye» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#260 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Time's senses of restraint and bestowal can support both the loss side and the exception of those who gain through faith and good works. «asrın dehr ü zaman mânası mülâhaza olunurken men‘ve atâ mânalarına da işaret edilmiş bulunduğu hafî değildir» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#261 ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · A cited couplet says each passing day takes away part of the speaker, since nothing is more precious than life. «“Her gün geçtikçe benim birazım gider, benim için ise» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#262 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · People fail to calculate what they gained against the life they spent and wrongly imagine that passing time means they are growing. «insan geçen cüz’ünün yerine ne kazandığını hesab etmez de gün geçtikçe büyüdüğünü zu‘m eder» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
