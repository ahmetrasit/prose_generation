#48 TABRISI d.548 · reports · Shidda, solidity, and difficulty are near synonyms; another account lists seven uses of shidda, including time, anger, pain, drink, and the body. «والشدة والصلابة والصعوبة نظائر. وقيل: الشدة تكون في سبعة أصناف»
#49 TABRISI d.548 · البلخي · reports · Al-Balkhi says the episode disproves that dreams become true according to their first interpretation: the assembly called the king's dream confused, yet Yusuf correctly interpreted it. «قال البلخي: وهذا التأويل من يوسف يدل على بطلان قول من يقول» [filed under 12:43-49]
#50 TABRISI d.548 · حفص · reports · Hafs reads دأبا with fatḥa on the hamza; the other readers use sukūn. «قرأ حفص دأبا بفتح الهمزة والباقون بسكونها» [filed under 12:43-49]
#51 TABRISI d.548 · أهل الكوفة غير عاصم · reports · The Kufan readers except ʿAsim use the second-person reading تعصرون; the remaining readers use the third-person يعصرون. «قرأ تعصرون بالتاء أهل الكوفة غير عاصم والباقون بالياء» [filed under 12:43-49]
#52 TABRISI d.548 · holds · Yusuf interprets the seven fat cows and green ears as seven fertile years and the lean cows and dry ears as seven years of famine. «فالسنون الجدبة وأما البقرات السمان والسنابل السبع الخضر فإنهن سبع سنين مخصبات» [filed under 12:47-49]
#53 TABRISI d.548 · holds · The seven severe years will consume the stored harvest saved for them; the years are described as eating because the famine occurs during them. «تأكلن فيها ما قدمتم في السنين المخصبة لتلك السنين وإنما أضاف الأكل إلى السنين»
#54 TABRISI d.548 · reports · Eating can also mean using up and destroying something, as in the idiom that a journey ate a camel's flesh. «أراد بالأكل الإفناء والإهلاك كما يقال: أكل السير لحم الناقة»
#55 TABRISI d.548 · زيد بن أسلم · reports · Zayd ibn Aslam says Yusuf prepared food for two; when the guest ate his whole portion one day, Yusuf recognized that the seven severe years had begun. «قال زيد بن أسلم كان يوسف يصنع طعام اثنين فيقربه إلى رجل فيأكل نصفه»
#56 TABRISI d.548 · holds · They should preserve a small amount from the harvest, since تحصنون means storing and protecting it. «إلا شيئا قليلا مما تحرزون وتدخرون»
#57 IBNJAWZI-ZAD d.597 · holds · The years are described as eating what was stored, though people eat during those years, like calling a night asleep. «( يأكلن ) أي يذهبن ما قدمتم لهن في السنين المخصبات فوصف السنين بالأكل»
#58 IBNJAWZI-ZAD d.597 · holds · “Except a little that you preserve” means a small amount you protect and store. «قوله تعالى ( إلا قليلا مما تحصنون ) أي تحرزون وتدخرون»
#59 IBNJAWZI-ZAD d.597 · holds · Grain should be left in its ear because it keeps better and is less vulnerable to spoilage; the following years will be severe droughts. «قوله تعالى ( فذروه في سنبله ) فإنه أبقى له وأبعد من الفساد والشداد المجدبات»
#60 RAZI-FULL d.606 · holds · The seven severe years are hard years of drought; saying that years eat what was stored is a metaphor attributing people's consumption to the years. «أي سبع سنين مجدبات ، والشداد الصعاب»
#61 RAZI-FULL d.606 · holds · Iḥṣān means securing or storing something in a protected place, so only a little of the reserves will be consumed. «الإحصان الإحراز ، وهو إلقاء الشيء في الحصن»
#62 RAZI-FULL d.606 · قتادة · reports · The first seven years are fertile and the next seven years are drought, as indicated by the dream; the forecast of a further abundant year came through revelation, and Qatada says God granted Joseph knowledge of that year. «وعن قتادة زاده الله علم سنة» [filed under 12:47, 12:48, 12:49]
#63 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · holds · The seven severe years are the drought years, and saying that the years eat is metaphor for their inhabitants eating the stored provisions. «يعني السنين المجدبات. { يأكلن } مجاز، والمعنى يأكل أهلهن»
#64 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · holds · What was advanced for the years means what people stored for them; the cited poem similarly attributes sleep to night and heedlessness to day while meaning people do those things. «أي ما ادخرتم لأجلهن ونحوه قول القائل»
#65 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · زيد بن أسلم · reports · Zayd ibn Aslam relates from his father that Joseph noticed a man consume a double portion; when he ate it all, Joseph identified the first of the seven hard years. «وحكى زيد بن أسلم عن أبيه: أن يوسف كان يضع طعام الاثنين فيقربه إلى رجل واحد»
#66 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · holds · The word qalīlan is accusative as an exception; the retained little is what people preserve as seed for sowing and thus protect their provisions. «نصب على الاستثناء. { مما تحصنون } أي مما تحبسون لتزرعوا»
#67 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · أبو عبيدة · reports · Abu ʿUbayda glosses tuḥṣinūn as guarding or securing provisions; the wording differs but the meaning is the same. «وقال أبو عبيدة: تحرزون. وقال قتادة: «تحصنون» تدخرون»
#68 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · قتادة · reports · Qatāda glosses tuḥṣinūn as storing provisions, which supports the same sense of preserving food. «وقال قتادة: «تحصنون» تدخرون، والمعنى واحد»
#69 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · holds · The verse indicates that storing food until it is needed is permissible. «وهو يدل على جواز احتكار الطعام إلى وقت الحاجة»
#70 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · holds · The passage establishes that an unbeliever's dream can be true and interpreted as seen, especially when it concerns a believer; here it is also a sign and miracle for a messenger. «هذه الآية أصل في صحة رؤيا الكافر، وأنها تخرج على حسب ما رأى، لاسيما إذا تعلقت بمؤمن»
#71 BAYDAWI-FULL d.685 · holds · In the seven hard years, their people consume the stored grain; attributing eating to the years is figurative and aligns the interpretation with the dream symbol. «أي يأكل أهلهن ما ادخرتم لأجلهن فأسند إليهن على المجاز»
#72 BAYDAWI-FULL d.685 · holds · They should preserve a small amount for seed. «{ إلا قليلا مما تحصنون } تحرزون لبذور الزراعة.»
#73 NASAFI-FULL d.710 · holds · The famine years are said by figurative attribution to eat what their people consume from the stored harvest of the fertile years. «هو من إسناد المجاز جعل اكل اهلهن مسندا إليهن»
#74 NASAFI-FULL d.710 · holds · The stores are what the people put forward for those years, retaining only a small amount of what they protect and save. «أى فى السنين المخصبة ﴿ إلا قليلا مما تحصنون ﴾ يحرزون وتخبئون»
#75 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · holds · Joseph's reply combines three kinds of speech: interpretation by meaning rather than wording, practical advice, and a report of the unseen future eighth year. «والثالث: الإعلام بالغيب في أمر العام الثامن، قاله قتادة» [filed under 12:47, 12:48, 12:49]
#76 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · السلمي · reports · Al-Sulamī reads يأكلون in the third person, meaning people will eat, rather than addressing the listeners. «وقرأ السلمي: مما يأكلون بالياء على الغيبة أي: يأكل الناس»
#77 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · holds · The phrase seven severe ones omits the word years, understood from the preceding seven years. «وحذف المميز في قوله: سبع شداد أي: سبع سنين شداد، لدلالة قوله: سبع سنين عليه»
#78 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · holds · The crops ‘eat’ metaphorically because they are consumed during those years, as in describing day as seeing. «الأكل الذي في قوله: يأكلن على سبيل المجاز من حيث أنه يؤكل فيهما»
#79 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · holds · Taḥṣinūn means preserve and store, deriving from ḥiṣn, a refuge or fortress. «ومعنى تحصنون تحرزون وتخبئون، مأخوذ من الحصن وهو الحرز والملجأ»
#80 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · holds · سبع شداد omits its counted noun, understood from the prior context; assigning eating to the years is figurative, as with the day being bright in 10:67. «حذف المميز وهو الموصوف لدلالة ما تقدم عليه. ونسب الأكل إليهن مجازا كقوله: والنهار مبصرا»
#81 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · holds · The seven thin cows and dry ears represent seven barren years that consume what was stored during the fertile years. «وهن السبع السنين المحل التي تعقب هذه السبع متواليات»
#82 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · holds · The coming drought years will produce nothing, and whatever is sown will yield no return, so they consume what they stored. «وما بذروه فلا يرجعون منه إلى شيء»
#83 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · The seven severe years are famine years, represented by the lean cows and dry ears; their duration follows the two dreams’ imagery. «{ سبع } أي سنون { شداد } بالقحط العظيم»
#84 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · The famine years “eat” metaphorically through their inhabitants, consuming the grain previously stored for them. «أسند الأكل إليهن مجازا عن أكل أهلهن تحقيقا للأكل»
#85 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · The reserve should last through the famine, with only a small amount kept outside storage for consumption. «والإحصان : الإحراز , وهو إلقاء الشيء فيما هو كالحصن المنيع»
#86 DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن عباس · reports · The cupbearer went to Joseph, who interpreted the seven years as productive harvests followed by seven famines, advised storing grain in ears and eating little, then forecast a rain-rich year of pressing; Joseph asked the king to investigate the women before leaving prison. «قال ابن عباس رضي الله عنهما: لم يكن السجن في المدينة فانطلق الساقي إلى يوسف عليه السلام» [filed under 12:45-50]
#87 DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن عباس · reports · He glosses what is saved during the famine as what people store up. «قال: مما ترفعون»
#88 DURR-FULL d.911 · قتادة · reports · Qatadah says the seven hard years are barren droughts that consume the provisions gathered earlier, except a small amount people store. «عن قتادة - رضي الله عنه - في قوله {ثم يأتي من بعد ذلك سبع شداد} قال: هن السنون المحول الجدوب»
#89 DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas glosses what people preserve as what they put into storage. «عن ابن عباس - رضي الله عنهما - في قوله {مما تحصنون} يقول: تخزنون»
#90 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · The seven severe years are famine, harder for people than captivity or killing; their inhabitants consume the grain stored in its ears, expressed figuratively as years eating it. «سبع سنين صعاب على الناس لأن الجوع أشد من الأسر والقتل»
#91 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · The wording hints that the dream’s thin cows did not literally eat the fat cows; the famine years consume stored grain instead. «وفيه تلويح بأنه تأويل لا كل العجاف السمان»
