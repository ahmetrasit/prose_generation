#162 BURSEVI d.1127 · طاوس · reports · Tawus and Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz treated al-Duha and ألم نشرح as one surah and joined them in one prayer unit without the basmala. «وعن طاووس وعمر بن عبد العزيز رحمهما الله إنهما كانا يقولان إن الضحى وألم نشرح سورة واحدة» [filed under 93:1-11, 94:1-8]
#163 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The words are Gabriel's answer: revelation had paused after the Prophet was asked about the Cave Companions, Dhul-Qarnayn, and the Spirit; pagans said his Lord had abandoned him, and Gabriel explained he descends only when commanded. The verse and Sura al-Duha were revealed. «قال له عليه الصلاة والسلام : يا جبريل احتبست عنى حتى ساء ضنى واشتقت اليك فقالك انى كنت اشوق» [filed under 19:64, 93:1-11]
#164 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · prefers · Al-ḍuḥā means the interval after sunrise when the sun has risen, rather than sunlight or the act of rising, because this fits what follows. «والمراد به هنا وقت ارتفاع الشمس الذي يلي وقت بروزها للناظرين دون ضوئها وارتفاعها لأنه أنسب بما بعد»
#165 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · The time is a fitting oath because it is the day's youthful, vigorous phase and a daily honor for the sun. «تخصيصه بالأقسام به لأنه شباب النهار وقوله فيه قوة غير قريبة من ضدها»
#166 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · Some say this was the hour God spoke to Moses and the sorcerers fell prostrate, linking the oath to God's continuing care and speech to the Prophet. «ولأنه على ما قالوا الساعة التي كلم الله تعالى فيها موسى عليه السلام»
#167 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · An alternative takes ḍuḥā as the whole day, as in the verse about punishment arriving by day; the author objects that this passage contrasts it with a darkened night. «وقيل المراد به النهار كما في قوله تعالى أن يأتيهم بأسنا ضحى واعترض بالعرق»
#168 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · rejects · The objection to reading the whole day assumes without proof that the following night phrase requires intense darkness. «وأجيبب بمنع دلالة القيد على الأشتداد وستسمع»
#169 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The Morning is Meccan and unanimously counted as eleven verses; it follows the mention of the most God-fearing with God's gifts to the Prophet. «مكية وآيها إحدى عشرة آية بلا خلاف ولما ذكر سبحانه فيما قبلها وسيجنبها الأتقى»
#170 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · الإمام · reports · The Imam says the preceding surah concerns Abū Bakr and this one the Messenger, with no intervening surah to show no intermediary between them; precedence of Abū Bakr's surah does not imply superiority, just as an oath by creation may precede an oath by God. «وقال الإمام لما كانت الأولى سورة أبي بكر رضي الله تعالى عنه وهذه سورة رسول الله صلى الله تعالى عليه وسلم عقب جل وعلا بها ولم يجعل بينهما واسطة»
#171 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · rejects · Alusi dismisses the Imam's flower metaphor as a claim that cannot withstand scrutiny. «ثم أن ما ذكره زهرة ربيع لا تتحمل الفرك كما لا يخفى»
#172 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Duha is the familiar midmorning, when the sun begins to shine and rise and the day is in its youthful phase. «“Duhâ” diye mâruf olan kuşluk zamanına ki Güneş’in parlayıp yükselmeye başladığı, gündüzün gençliği zamanıdır.»
#173 ELMALILI d.1361 · prefers · The definite article may be generic, but in this address to the Prophet it more likely points to the singular dawn of truth: the Muhammadan mission beginning to radiate mercy and light to the worlds. «Hitab Resûlullah’a olmak itibariyle bu ahd mânası râcih olur.»
#174 ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · Others connect the oath to the Prophet's face or divine speech; the verse about people gathering at duha also evokes the appointed morning when Moses overcame the magicians. «vech-i Muhammedîye veya teklîm-i ilâhî zamanına işaret olduğu da söylenmiştir. Nitekim طٰهٰ’da وَأَن يُحْشَرَ ٱلنَّاسُ ضُحًۭى»
#175 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Duha expresses the bright, youthful joy of life and revealed knowledge, while the settled night signals rest before a promised renewal; this relation fittingly introduces the assurance that the Lord has not abandoned Muhammad. «gecenin dindiği sükûnu hengâmına kasem olsun ki yâ Muhammed» [filed under 93:1, 93:2, 93:3]
#176 QUTB-ZILAL d.1386 · holds · The Meccan surah is wholly a tender consolation for the Prophet, renewing hope, closeness, reassurance, and calm for his weary spirit. «إنها كلها خالصة للنبي [ صلى الله عليه وسلم ] كلها نجاء له من ربه»
#177 QUTB-ZILAL d.1386 · روايات كثيرة · reports · Many reports say revelation paused and Gabriel was delayed; the polytheists then claimed that Muhammad’s Lord had abandoned him, and the surah was revealed in response. «ورد في روايات كثيرة أن الوحي فتر عن رسول الله»
#178 QUTB-ZILAL d.1386 · holds · Revelation and Gabriel’s visits were the Prophet’s provision and spiritual refreshment through the hardships of rejection, hostility, and persecution; their pause left him isolated and distressed. «والوحي ولقاء جبريل والاتصال بالله ، كانت هي زاد الرسول»
#179 QUTB-ZILAL d.1386 · holds · The message assures him that God never deserted or hated him, that the afterlife is better, and that his Lord will give until he is content, recalling prior care as proof. «وما تركك ربك من قبل أبدا ، وما قلاك من قبل قط ، وما أخلاك من رحمته»
#180 QUTB-ZILAL d.1386 · holds · Dawn and the settled night form a delicate frame for tenderness and reflection; their clear, pure moments let the spirit sense creation worshipping its Maker, matching the surah’s emotional setting. «جعل الإطار من الضحى الرائق ، ومن الليل الساجي . أصفى آنين من آونة الليل والنهار»
#181 QUTB-ZILAL d.1386 · holds · The oath links cosmic phenomena with human feeling and makes the Prophet feel accompanied rather than forsaken or alone in the living universe. «فيعيش ذلك القلب في أنس من هذا الوجود ، غير موحش ولا غريب فيه فريد»
#182 QUTB-ZILAL d.1386 · holds · The surah’s graceful wording and musical cadence embody its gentleness; the beauty of this artistic expression points to God’s unmatched creative work. «إن هذا الإبداع في كمال الجمال ليدل على الصنعة . صنعة الله التي لا تماثلها صنعة»
#183 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The oath rebuts the idolaters' claim that revelation stopped when the Prophet did not recite the Quran at night for several days; the warning targets their insinuation, since the Prophet himself had no doubt. «القسم لتأكيد الخبر ردا على زعم المشركين أن الوحي انقطع عن النبي» [filed under 93:1, 93:2, 93:3]
#184 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Morning brightness evokes revelation and guidance, while night evokes the Prophet's Quran recitation that nearby idolaters heard; the time when night settled may be when he prayed, as in al-Muzzammil. «ومناسبة القسم ب { الضحى والليل } أن الضحى وقت انبثاق نور الشمس فهو إيماء إلى تمثيل نزول الوحي وحصول الاهتداء به ، وأن الليل وقت قيام النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم بالقرآن» [filed under 93:1, 93:2]
#185 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The term ضحى was explained at al-Shams 1. «والضحى تقدم بيانه عند قوله تعالى : { والشمس وضحاها } ( الشمس : 1 ) .»
#186 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The muṣḥaf writes the final alif of الضحى in the yāʾ shape although its root vowel is wāw, matching other word endings, most of which derive from yāʾ, and supporting imāla in relevant dialects; Ibn Mālik discusses the orthographic pattern. «وكتب في المصحف { والضحى } بألف في صورة الياء مع أن أصل ألفه الواو لأنهم راعوا المناسبة مع أكثر الكلمات المختومة بألف في هذه السورة»
#187 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Its message serves as evidence for al-Duha: past care reassures the Prophet that the One who began blessing him will continue it. «فمضمونها شبيه بأنه حجة على مضمون سورة الضحى تثبيتا له بتذكيره سالف عنايته به» [filed under 94:1, 93:1]
#188 TABATABAI d.1402 · reports · The surah was said to follow several days without revelation, when people said the Prophet's Lord had forsaken him; it came to console him. The surah could be Meccan or Medinan. «قيل انقطع الوحي عن النبي صلى الله عليه وآله وسلم أياما حتى قالوا إن ربه ودعه» [filed under 93:1-3]
#189 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The oath is by duha, the sun's spread and the day's extension, and by the night at rest as its darkness covers the world. «والضحى - على ما في المفردات - انبساط الشمس وامتداد النهار» [filed under 93:1-2]
#190 TABATABAI d.1402 · القمي · reports · Al-Qummi glosses al-ḍuḥā as the sun after it rises and the night when it becomes dark. «قال إذا ارتفعت الشمس { والليل إذا سجى } قال إذا أظلم» [filed under 93:1-2]
#191 ASAD-NOTES d.1412 · reports · A story says revelation paused after surah 89 and Meccan opponents taunted the Prophet that God had forsaken and scorned him; Asad regards the report as doubtful. «Whether or not we accept this somewhat doubtful story» [filed under 93:1-11]
#192 ASAD-NOTES d.1412 · holds · Beyond the Prophet, the surah consoles any faithful person enduring hardship and sorrow that can lead even the righteous to question God's justice. «every faithful man and woman suffering from the sorrows» [filed under 93:1-11]
#193 BINTSHATI d.1419 · holds · The surah is Meccan without disagreement and is commonly placed eleventh in revelation order, after al-Fajr. «السورة مكية بلا خلاف، والمشهور أنها الحادية عشرة في ترتيب النزول. نزلت بعد الفجر» [filed under 93:1, 93:3]
#194 BINTSHATI d.1419 · ابن قيم الجوزية · reports · The prevailing earlier view treats Qur'anic oaths as glorifying what is sworn by, since created things are great signs. «الرأي السائد عند الأقدمين، أن هذا القسم القرآني يحمل معنى التعظيم للمقسم به» [filed under 93:1, 93:2]
#195 BINTSHATI d.1419 · الشيخ محمد عبده · reports · Abduh explains the duha oath as drawing attention to the light's greatness and its status as a major sign and blessing. «فالقسم بالضياء للإشارة إلى تعظيم أمر الضياء وإعظام قدر النعمة فيه»
#196 BINTSHATI d.1419 · prefers · The oath's wāw can serve a rhetorical purpose beyond glorification: strongly directing attention to familiar sensory realities that prepare the reader for unseen meanings. «القسم بها يمكن أن يكون، والله أعلم، قد خرج عن أصل الوضع اللغوي في القسم للتعظيم، إلى معنى بياني» [filed under 93:1, 93:2]
