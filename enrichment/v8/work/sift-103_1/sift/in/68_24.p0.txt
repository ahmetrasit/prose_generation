# 59 notes filed under 68:24
verse 68:24: أَن لَّا يَدْخُلَنَّهَا ٱلْيَوْمَ عَلَيْكُم مِّسْكِينٌۭ

#1 MUJAHID d.104 · مجاهد · reports · Mujahid says they privately resolved not to let any poor person enter the garden. «عن مجاهد في قوله ﴿ أن لا يدخلنها اليوم عليكم مسكين ﴾ قال اضمروا في أنفسهم أن لا يدخل عليهم مسكين»
#2 MUQATIL d.150 · holds · They decided to let no poor person enter the garden that day. «أن لا يدخلنها اليوم عليكم مسكين»
#3 FARRA d.207 · holds · The justest brother's call to tasbih means to make an exception by saying إن شاء الله, as in the instruction to remember God after forgetting. «ألم أقل لكم لولا تسبحون؟ فالتسبيح هاهنا في معنى الاستثناء» [filed under 68:18, 68:24]
#4 IBNQUTAYBA-GHARIB d.276 · holds · They whispered to one another not to let a poor person enter the garden that day. «{وهم يتخافتون} أي يتسارون: ب {أن لا يدخلنها اليوم عليكم مسكين} (4) .» [filed under 68:23, 68:24]
#5 TAB-FULL d.310 · عكرمة · reports · ʿIkrima says the owners were Ethiopians whose father fed the poor from his garden; after he died, his sons called him foolish and vowed to exclude the poor. «هم ناس من الحبشة كانت لأبيهم جنة كان يطعم المساكين منها» [filed under 68:17, 68:18, 68:24]
#6 TAB-FULL d.310 · قتادة · reports · Qatāda says the garden belonged to a charitable old man; his sons opposed his charity and, after his death, set out to keep poor people away. «كانت الجنة لشيخ، وكان يتصدق، فكان بنوه ينهونه عن الصدقة» [filed under 68:17, 68:18, 68:24]
#7 ZAJJAJ d.311 · holds · They whispered among themselves that no needy person should enter the garden; takhāfut means speaking secretly. «أي يسرون الكلام بينهم بأن: (لا يدخلنها اليوم عليكم مسكين).» [filed under 68:23-24]
#8 NAHHAS d.338 · holds · The answer is in an as before; ʿAbd Allāh's reading omits an because yatakhāfatūn means they speak secretly. «وفي قراءة عبد الله بغير أن لأن معنى يتخافتون يقولون سرا»
#9 THALABI d.427 · holds · The brothers called to one another at dawn and went to their crops, whispering their plan to keep any poor person out. «فتنادوا نادى بعضهم بعضا مصبحين أن اغدوا على حرثكم إن كنتم صارمين فانطلقوا فمضوا إليها وهم يتخافتون» [filed under 68:21-24]
#10 MAWARDI-FULL d.450 · يحيى بن سلام · reports · They resolved that no poor person should enter the garden that day. «أن لا يدخلنها اليوم عليكم مسكين } قاله يحيى بن سلام .»
#11 QUSHAYRI d.465 · reports · On ḥard can mean that they thought themselves able to carry out the plan, or that they were angry with the poor. «على حرد } أي : قادرين عند أنفسهم ، ويقال : على غضب منهم»
#12 WAHIDI-WAJIZ d.468 · holds · They conspire that no poor person should enter their orchard that day. «{ أن لا يدخلنها اليوم عليكم مسكين }.»
#13 JISHUMI d.494 · reports · The poor had an enforceable due in the harvest, variously identified as a tenth or as the fruit that fell during picking. «واختلفوا في ذلك الحق، فقيل: كان حقهم العشر، وقيل: بل ما كان يتساقط من الشجرة» [filed under 68:24, 68:25]
#14 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · holds · They set out whispering privately to one another that no poor person should enter the garden that day. «{فانطلقوا} مشوا إليها {وهم يتخافتون أن لا يدخلنها اليوم عليكم مسكين} يتسارون، يقول بعضهم لبعض سرا» [filed under 68:23, 68:24]
#15 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · ابن مسعود وابن أبي عبلة · reports · Ibn Masud and Ibn Abi Abla read لا يدخلنها without the particle أن. «وقرأ ابن مسعود وابن أبي عبلة: «لا يدخلنها» بسقوط أن»
#16 TABRISI d.548 · holds · The command that no poor person should enter was the secret they exchanged. «هذا ما كانوا يتخافتون به»
#17 RAZI-FULL d.606 · holds · An is explanatory; Ibn Masʿūd reads without it, with an implicit verb of saying. Barring the poor from entering means the owners must not let them in. «بإضمار القول أي يتخافتون يقولون لا يدخلها»
#18 BAYDAWI-FULL d.685 · holds · أن is explanatory, or is omitted in a reading with an implicit speech verb; telling the poor not to enter hyperbolically forbids allowing them entry. «{ أن لا يدخلنها اليوم عليكم مسكين } { إن } مفسرة وقرىء بطرحها»
#19 NASAFI-FULL d.710 · holds · The particle “an” may explain their whispering; if omitted, a verb of saying is understood. They prohibit enabling a poor person to enter, not merely his entering. «وان مفسرة وقرئ بطرحها باضمار القول أي يتخافتون يقولون لا يدخلنها»
#20 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · holds · They whispered so the poor would not hear them; their secret plan was to keep anyone poor from entering the garden. «يتخافتون: يخفون كلامهم خوفا من أن يشعر بهم المساكين.» [filed under 68:23, 68:24]
#21 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · holds · An may introduce either a verbal noun or an explanation of their whisper; the prohibition means not enabling the poor to enter. «وأن مصدرية، ويجوز أن تكون تفسيرية.»
#22 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · عبد الله وابن أبي عبلة · reports · They read لا يدخلنها without أن, either supplying “they say” or treating whispering like speech. «وقرأ عبد الله وابن أبي عبلة: لا يدخلنها، بإسقاط أن على إضمار يقولون،»
#23 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · holds · أن لا يدخلنها may be a verbal-noun clause reporting what they whispered or an explanatory clause after the speech-like verb. «أن تكون المصدرية، أي: يتخافتون بهذا الكلام،»
#24 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · عبد الله وابن أبي عبلة · reports · They read لا يدخلها without أن, either with an omitted القول under Basran analysis or by treating يتخافتون like a verb of speech under Kufan analysis. «وقرأ عبد الله وابن أبي عبلة " لا يدخلها "»
#25 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · holds · They spoke secretly so no one could hear their plan to prevent a poor person from entering the garden that day. «أي: يتناجون فيما بينهم بحيث لا يسمعون أحدا كلامهم.» [filed under 68:23, 68:24]
#26 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · The prohibition is phrased as barring a poor person from entering, but rhetorically they are forbidding themselves from letting one in; they intend to search throughout the day so none can reach fruit or an opening. «وهو نهي للمسكين في اللفظ للمبالغة في نهي أنفسهم أن لا يدعوه يدخل عليهم»
#27 DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas says they were people of Abyssinia whose father fed the needy from his garden; after his death, his sons swore to harvest early and give no poor person food. «عن ابن عباس في قوله» [filed under 68:17-24]
#28 DURR-FULL d.911 · قتادة · reports · Qatada says the garden belonged to an Israelite elder who saved his annual provisions and gave away the excess; his sons opposed charity and resolved to exclude the needy. «عن قتادة قال: كانت الجنة» [filed under 68:17-24]
#29 DURR-FULL d.911 · قتادة · reports · Qatada says they whispered their plan to exclude the poor; they went early, resolved and thinking themselves able to carry it out. «عن قتادة في قوله: {وهم يتخافتون}» [filed under 68:23-24]
#30 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · They barred the poor from the garden; a miskīn has nothing and is more destitute than a faqīr. Forbidding entry emphatically forbids permitting it. «المسكين هو الذي لا شيء له وهو أبلغ من الفقير»
#31 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · Ḥard means forceful prevention with anger; they thought themselves able to deny the poor their share, but only achieved deprivation. «الحرد المنع عن حدة وغضب»
#32 BURSEVI d.1127 · الكشاف · reports · Al-Kashshāf says they intended to distress the poor despite their ability to benefit them, but instead lost their wealth and suffered deprivation. «وفي الكشاف وغدوا قادرين على نكد لا غير عاجزين عن النفع»
#33 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · أن لا is explanatory of the speech implied by their whispering, or a verbal noun with omitted ب; a reading by Abdullah and Ibn Abi ʿAbla omits أن and supports the first parse. «إن مفسرة لما في التخافت من معنى القول أو مصدرية والتقدير بأن ويؤيد الأول قراءة عبد الله وابن أبي عبلة بإسقاطها»
#34 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · Some supply an implied قول, while the Kufan analysis makes يتخافتون govern the phrase because it contains the sense of speech. «وعليه قيل هو بتقدير القول وقيل العامل فيه يتخافتون لتضمنه معنى القول وهو المذهب الكوفي»
#35 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · Telling the poor person not to enter dramatizes a broader order not to let one in, like saying لا أرينك ههنا. «فالمراد بنهي المسكين عن الدخول المبالغة في النهي عن تمكينه منه كقولهم لا أرينك ههنا»
#36 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · They meant to finish the harvest completely and leave nothing for anyone, acting with extreme greed. «onu ekin biçer gibi kesip bitirmek niyetiyle düşmanın üzerine saldırır gibi kıymak azmiyle» [filed under 68:22, 68:24]
#37 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · They forgot that the previous day they had needed oaths to reassure each other and establish their company; the orchard's yield would likewise depend on trust. «dün birbirleriyle mukavele ederlerken birbirlerini» [filed under 68:23, 68:24]
#38 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · They should have remembered that God gave them the orchard and watched over it while they slept, and that God and needy servants had rights in it. «o bağ ve hars kendilerinden evvel onu kendilerine veren ve onlar uyurken onu gözetecek olan Allah’ındır»
#39 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Gratitude required generosity toward the poor, sharing some means from the crop, and cultivating beneficiaries and well-wishers instead of enemies through greed and despotism. «öyle istibdad ile hareket etmeyip mümkin olabildiği kadar onlardan bazılarını da sebeplendirmek»
#40 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · They rushed out without considering the orchard's condition and whispered their exclusion of the poor so no one would hear. «bunu da kimse işitmesin diye seslerini dahi kıskanır bir surette fısıl fısıl, mızır mızır» [filed under 68:23, 68:24]
#41 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The phrase forbidding any poor person to enter explains the whispering, since تخافت implies speech without stating its words. «وأن لا يدخلنها اليوم عليكم مسكين } تفسير لفعل { يتخافتون }»
#42 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The emphatic ن in the prohibition reinforces their oath; grammatically the poor person is barred from entering, but the intended command is that no brother let one in. «وتأكيد فعل النهي بنون التوكيد لزيادة تحقيق ما تقاسموا عليه»
