#124 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · أبو جعفر · reports · Abu Ja'far reads الرؤيا as الريا by assimilation after replacing hamza with wāw; the author calls it weak because the replacement is not obligatory. «وقرأ أبو جعفر " الرؤيا " وبابها " الريا " بالإدغام»
#125 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · الزمخشري · prefers · Al-Zamakhshari endorses the light form عبرت and says authoritative speakers reject the doubled form عبرت, التعبير, and المعبر. «وعبرت الرؤيا بالتخفيف قال الزمخشري: " هو الذي اعتمده الأثبات»
#126 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · الزمخشري · reports · Al-Zamakhshari adduces an Arab poem containing عبارا as evidence for the heavier noun form and explains crossing a dream as stating its eventual outcome. «وقد عثرت على بيت أنشده المبرد في كتاب " الكامل " لبعض الأعراب»
#127 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · الزمخشري · holds · Al-Zamakhshari says interpreting a dream is called crossing it because one describes its outcome, as crossing a river means reaching the far side. «قال: " وحقيقة عبرت الرؤيا: ذكرت عاقبتها وآخر أمرها كما تقول: عبرت النهر إذا قطعته»
#128 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · holds · سمان modifies بقرات; it is the plural of سمينة, while سمين can also form a shared-gender plural, like كرام. «صفة لبقرات وهو جمع سمينة، ويجمع»
#129 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · holds · سمن is the verbal noun and سمين the active adjective, both with irregular vowels compared with the expected pattern. «فالمصدر واسم [الفاعل] جاءا على غير قياس»
#130 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · الزمخشري · reports · Al-Zamakhshari explains that describing the classifier بقرات selects a subtype of cows that are fat, while describing the numeral would first identify the number by cow-kind and then characterize it. «قال الزمخشري: " هل من فرق بين إيقاع " سمان " صفة للمميز وهو " بقرات "»
#131 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · الزمخشري · reports · He says عجاف cannot be annexed directly as the numeral's discriminator because an adjective alone does not identify the counted kind; nouns like فرسان can act as nouns. «والعجاف وصف لا يقع البيان به وحده.»
#132 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · holds · An adjective on the genitive discriminator entails that the counted set has that quality, but a nominative adjective on the numeral does not necessarily describe the men. «قلت: وهي أسئلة وأجوبة حسنة. وتحقيق السؤال الأول وجوابه: أنه يلزم من وصف التمييز بشيء»
#133 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · When the forgotten request failed, God produced another cause for Joseph's elevation; the king is a powerful ruler responsible for politics and administration. «أثار الله سبحانه سببا ينفذ به ما أراد من رئاسته»
#134 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · The king presents his vision to a court of elites, including sorcerers, priests, soothsayers, physiognomists, and sages, emphasizing that he is serious rather than testing them. «وهم السحرة والكهنة والحزرة والقافة والحكماء»
#135 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · The present tense أرى narrates the vision as if unfolding now because it deeply alarmed the king; fat means bodily increase and عجاف means dry, emaciated thinness. «حكاية للحال لشدة ما هاله من ذلك»
#136 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · The dry ears overcome the green ears; the author says the number modifies a plural of paucity because the dream signifies famine and hardship, unlike the multiplied crop in 2:261. «ولما كان تأويل المنام الجدب والقحط والشدة»
#137 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · A سنبلة is a reed-like plant bearing an ordered cluster of grains; the repeated seven is omitted from the second ears because the lean cattle already suggest it. «نبات كالقصبة حملة حبوب منتظمة»
#138 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · Biqāʿī says rulers need learned interpreters; the courtiers call the king’s single dream confused fragments to deny that it has a determinate meaning. «وفي الآية ما يوجبه حال العلماء من حاجة الملوك إليهم» [filed under 12:43-44]
#139 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · Dreams may be true inspiration from an angel, satanic confusion, or self-talk; the courtiers classify the king’s dream with the latter confused kinds. «لأن الرؤيا تارة تكون من الملك وهي الصحيحة , وتارة تكون من تحريف الشيطان وتخليطاته , وتارة من حديث النفس» [filed under 12:43-44]
#140 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · The courtiers use plural “dreams” for one dream to make it sound like meaningless fragments, then deny absolute expertise to conceal their ignorance. «جمعوا - وهي حلم واحد - ليجعلوها أضغاثا لا مدلول لها» [filed under 12:43-44]
#141 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · In the courtiers’ phrasing, negating knowledge without qualification also denies knowledge of this particular dream, after they have framed it as confused. «ونفوا عن أنفسهم ( العلم المطلق ) المستلزم لنفي ( العلم بالمقيد )» [filed under 12:43-44]
#142 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · The verb “said” entails recounting the actual words, whereas “spoke” does not necessarily quote what was said. «فإذا ذكر أنه قال , اقتضى الحكاية لما قال» [filed under 12:43-44]
#143 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · Biqāʿī derives the forms of ḥ-l-m from a common sense of diverting something from its accustomed course or nature. «ومادة ( حلم ) بجميع تقاليبها تدور على صرف شيء عن وجهه وعادته وما تقتضيه الجبلة» [filed under 12:43-44]
#144 DURR-FULL d.911 · reports · The king's troubling dream showed seven fat cows eaten by seven lean cows and green ears eaten by dry ones; the assembled diviners called it confused dreams and admitted they could not interpret dreams. «ثم أن الله أرى الملك رؤيا في منامه هالته فرأى سبع بقرات سمان»
#145 DURR-FULL d.911 · مجاهد عن ابن إسحاق · reports · Joseph asked the cupbearer to tell the great king about his grievance and unjust imprisonment; the man forgot, then recalled Joseph when King Rayyan's troubling dream needed interpretation. «عن مجاهد رضي الله عنه قال: قال يوسف عليه الصلاة والسلام للساقي {اذكرني عند ربك} أي الملك الأعظم ومظلمتي» [filed under 12:42-43]
#146 DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas glosses the king's phrase أضغاث أحلام as false dreams; al-Dahhak agrees. «عن ابن عباس رضي الله عنهما في قوله {أضغاث أحلام} قال: من الأحلام الكاذبة»
#147 DURR-FULL d.911 · مجاهد · reports · Mujahid glosses أضغاث أحلام as a mixture of dreams. «عن مجاهد - رضي الله عنه - في قوله {أضغاث أحلام} قال: أخلاط أحلام»
#148 DURR-FULL d.911 · قتادة · reports · Qatadah interprets the fat cows as fertile years, the lean cows as barren years, green ears as abundant harvests, and dry ears as drought. «عن قتادة - رضي الله عنه - في قوله {أفتنا في سبع بقرات} الآية قال: أما السمان فسنون»
#149 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The king is Rayyan, who was an unbeliever; his title illustrates the permissibility of calling an unbeliever king, though some prohibit king or amir for fear the title implies rightfulness, citing Muhammad's letter to Heraclius as 'great Roman'. «الريان وكان كافرا ففي إطلاق ذلك عليه دلالة»
#150 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The king's 'I see' means 'I saw'; the present tense vividly recounts the past vision. «أي رأيت وإيثار صيغة المضارع لحكاية الحال الماضية»
#151 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · سمان describes cows full of flesh and fat; the plural can be regular as in كريم/كرام, and its lexical forms derive from samāna and samn. «ممتلئات لحما وشحما من سمن كسمع سمانة بالفتح وسمنا كعنبا»
#152 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The imperfect 'they eat' means that they ate, but makes the strange scene present to the listener; the clause is circumstantial or adjectival. «أي أكلهن والعدول إلى المضارع لاستحضار الصورة تعجيبا»
#153 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · عجاف means very thin. Its plural is عجفاء, unusually patterned as عجاف rather than the expected عجف; it is formed in opposition to سمان. «أي سبع بقرات مهزولة جدا»
#154 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · prefers · Describing the numeral's discriminator سمان rather than making it an adjective of the numeral better distinguishes by kind than gender and more fully removes ambiguity; a derived adjective is not a sound discriminator in formal usage. «وقد ذكروا أنه إذا وصف التمييز كان التمييز بالنوع وإذا وصف المميز كان التمييز بالجنس»
#155 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · One explanation says the fat cows are named first because they appeared first: seven fat cows emerged from a dry river, then seven lean ones swallowed them without visibly changing. «بأنه أول ما رأى السمان فقد روي أنه رأى سبع بقرات سمان خرجن من نهر يابس»
#156 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The green ears had formed kernels and were green; the dry ears had ripened and curled until no green remained, according to a report. Their number is omitted because the earlier seven cows supply it. «قد انعقد حبها واخضر أي وسبعا أخر»
#157 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The author rejects coordinating أخر with سنبلات: the seven would then be both the green ears and a different set of dry ears, a numerical contradiction; coordination with a single set of seven men divided between standing and sitting is different. «ولا يجوز عطف أخر على سنبلات لأن العطف على المميز يقتضي أن يكون المعطوف والمعطوف عليه بيانا للمعدود»
#158 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · صاحب الفرائد · reports · Al-Fara'id argues coordination normally repeats the numeral: seven other ears would then be added to seven green ones, making fourteen; under the alternative ellipsis analysis the reading would instead wrongly make all ears total seven. «ونظر في ذلك صاحب الفرائد فقال : إن الصحيح أن العطف في حكم تكرير العامل»
#159 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · صاحب الفرائد · reports · Al-Fara'id further prefers coordinating with خضر rather than يابسات, so the dry description has an expressed ears noun and need not be supplied from context. «وادعى أن الأولى أن يكون العطف»
#160 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · rejects · Al-Alusi says the objection still assumes a discriminator already fixed at seven; neither repetition nor ellipsis resolves that contradiction, and he prefers the ellipsis or 'withdrawal' analysis followed by Ibn al-Hajib. «ومن ذلك يظهر أنه لا مدخل للتكرير ولا الإنسحاب في هذا الفرض ثم إن المختار قول الإنسحاب»
