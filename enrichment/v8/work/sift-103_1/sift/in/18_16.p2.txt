#88 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · holds · The verse pictures their mutual counsel: having separated from the unbelievers, they would take refuge in the cave and rely on God to spread mercy and provide ease; the prayer concerns worldly life. «ومضمن هذه الآية أن بعضهم قال لبعض إذ فارقنا الكفار وانفردنا بالله تعالى فلنجعل الكهف مأوى»
#89 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · نافع وابن عامر · reports · Nafi and Ibn Amir read marfiqan with an open mīm and kasra on fāʾ; Abu Zayd calls this a verbal noun like rifq. «وقرأ نافع وابن عامر «مرفقا» بفتح الميم وكسر الفاء»
#90 TABRISI d.548 · reports · For marfiqan, people of Medina, Ibn ʿĀmir, al-Aʿshā, and al-Barjamī from Abū Bakr read fatḥa on mīm and kasra on fāʾ; the other readers reverse those vowels. «قرأ أهل المدينة وابن عامر والأعشى والبرجمي عن أبي بكر مرفقا بفتح الميم وكسر الفاء»
#91 TABRISI d.548 · الزجاج · reports · Al-Zajjāj and Quṭrub acknowledge both vowel forms for ‘benefit/support’ and ‘joint’; kasra on the arm-joint form is more idiomatic. «قال الزجاج وذكر قطرب وغيره اللغتين جميعا في مرفق الأمر ومرفق اليد»
#92 TABRISI d.548 · أبو الحسن · reports · Abū al-Ḥasan says the fatḥa form means a thing used for benefit, while the kasra form is a noun; it may be a dialect or a verbal noun form. «قال أبو الحسن مرفقا أي شيئا يرتفقون به مثل المقطع ونحوه ومرفقا جعله اسما»
#93 TABRISI d.548 · holds · Iʿtizāl means withdrawing or distancing oneself; a line of poetry uses taʿazzul for withdrawal, and the name Muʿtazila was applied to ʿAmr ibn ʿUbayd’s circle because they left al-Ḥasan’s gathering. «والاعتزال التنحي عن الأمر والتعزل بمعناه»
#94 TABRISI d.548 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn ʿAbbās says Tamlikhā, the youths’ leader, spoke: if they left their people and idols, they would still not abandon worship of God. «قال ابن عباس وهذا من قول تمليخا وهو رئيس أصحاب الكهف قال لهم فإذا فارقتموهم»
#95 TABRISI d.548 · reports · If some youths worshiped God alongside the idols, the exception ‘except God’ is connected; if all worshiped only idols, it is a disconnected exception. «الاستثناء متصلا ويجوز أن يكون جميعهم»
#96 TABRISI d.548 · holds · They should make the cave their refuge; God will spread mercy and arrange ease, gentleness, and what they need for their livelihood. «أي صيروا إليه واجعلوه مأواكم»
#97 TABRISI d.548 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn ʿAbbās glosses the arrangement as God making their fear of the ruler and his injustice easy to bear, with gentleness and relief. «ويسهل عليكم ما تخافون من الملك وظلمه ويأتيكم باليسر والرفق واللطف عن ابن عباس»
#98 TABRISI d.548 · holds · The passage shows the high status of migration for religion and the wrongness of staying in an unbelieving land when remaining requires professing disbelief. «وفي هذا دلالة على عظم منزلة الهجرة في الدين وعلى قبح المقام في دار الكفر»
#99 IBNJAWZI-ZAD d.597 · ابن عباس · reports · Yamlīkha, the leader of the Companions of the Cave, says they have withdrawn from the idolaters. «قال ابن عباس هذا قول يمليخا وهو رئيس اصحاب الكهف قال لهم وإذ اعتزلتموهم أي فارقتموهم»
#100 IBNJAWZI-ZAD d.597 · عطاء الخراساني والفراء · reports · On one reading, they withdrew from the gods their people worshipped while continuing to worship God, whom the people also worshipped. «هذا قول عطاء الخراساني والفراء»
#101 IBNJAWZI-ZAD d.597 · قتادة · reports · A second reading is that they withdrew from everything their people worshipped besides God; Abdullah's codex makes this wording explicit. «قال قتادة هي في مصحف عبد الله وما يعبدون من دون الله وهذا تفسيرها»
#102 IBNJAWZI-ZAD d.597 · holds · Taking refuge in the cave means making it their dwelling; God's spreading mercy includes expanding their provision. «فأووا إلى الكهف أي اجعلوه مأواكم ينشر لكم ربكم من رحمته أي يبسط عليكم من رزقه»
#103 IBNJAWZI-ZAD d.597 · ابن كثير وأبو عمرو وعاصم وحمزة والكسائي · reports · They read مرفقا with kasra on mīm and fatḥa on fāʾ. «قرأ ابن كثير وأبو عمرو وعاصم وحمزة والكسائي مرفقا بكسر الميم وفتح الفاء»
#104 IBNJAWZI-ZAD d.597 · نافع وابن عامر · reports · They read مرفقا with fatḥa on mīm and kasra on fāʾ. «وقرأ نافع وابن عامر مرفقا بفتح الميم وكسرالفاء»
#105 IBNJAWZI-ZAD d.597 · الفراء · reports · The people of the Hijaz use the fatḥa-kasra form for any convenience one resorts to, while they use kasra for the human elbow; Arabs may use kasra for both. «قال الفراء أهل الحجاز يقولون مرفقا بفتح الميم وكسر الفاء في كل مرفق ارتفقت به»
#106 IBNJAWZI-ZAD d.597 · ابن الأنباري · reports · The phrase means God will provide a substitute for their difficult situation; a cited verse of poetry uses the same sense of substitute. «قال ابن الأنباري معنى الآية ويهيئ لكم بدلا من أمركم الصعب مرفقا»
#107 IBNJAWZI-ZAD d.597 · ابن عباس · reports · God would ease their fear of the king's oppression and bring them ease, gentleness, and kindness. «قال ابن عباس ويهيئ لكم يسهل عليكم ما تخافون من الملك وظلمه»
#108 RAZI-FULL d.606 · holds · The youths tell one another that withdrawing from the people and their objects of worship leaves their worship of God intact; they should make the cave their refuge. «اعتزلتم الشيء الذي يعبدونه إلا الله فإنكم لم تعتزلوا عبادة الله»
#109 RAZI-FULL d.606 · الفراء · reports · Al-Farrāʾ treats the cave command as the answer to idhā, like “when you do this, then do that”; it means go there and make it your abode. «قال الفراء هو جواب إذ كما تقول إذ فعلت كذا فافعل كذا»
#110 RAZI-FULL d.606 · holds · God will spread His mercy over them and prepare ease in their affairs. «أي يبسطها عليكم»
#111 RAZI-FULL d.606 · نافع وابن عامر وعاصم · reports · Nāfiʿ, Ibn ʿĀmir, and ʿĀṣim in one transmission read marfaq with fatḥa on mīm and kasra on fāʾ; the others use kasra on mīm and fatḥa on fāʾ. «قرأن افع وابن عامر وعاصم في رواية مرفقا بفتح الميم وكسر الفاء»
#112 RAZI-FULL d.606 · الفراء · reports · Al-Farrāʾ regards both marfaq pronunciations as dialect forms derived from irtifāq. «وهما لغتان واشتقاقهما من الإرتفاق»
#113 RAZI-FULL d.606 · الكسائي · reports · Al-Kisāʾī accepts only the kasra-on-mīm reading for the body's elbow, while al-Farrāʾ accepts it for both the elbow and an affair. «وكان الكسائي ينكر في مرفق الإنسان الذي في اليد إلا كسر الميم»
#114 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · Another explanation says both forms are dialects, with fatḥa more regular by analogy and kasra more common; marfaq can mean a means of support. «وقيل هما لغتان إلا أن الفتح أقيس والكسر أكثر»
#115 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · ابن إسحاق · reports · When the youths withdrew to the cave, God would extend mercy and arrange their affairs; the sun's path avoided them while they lay in its spacious interior. «فأووا إلى الكهف ينشر لكم ربكم من رحمته ويهيىء لكم من أمركم مرفقا» [filed under 18:16-17]
#116 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · reports · The words may be God's instruction to the youths or, in Ibn Atiyya's account, a speech by their leader Yemlikha. «هو من قول الله لهم»
#117 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · holds · Mirfaq is read with mīm kasra or fatḥa and denotes something used for comfort; usage also distinguishes the arm joint from a place or matter of assistance. «قرىء بكسر الميم وفتحها، وهو ما يرتفق به»
#118 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · الغزنوي · reports · Al-Ghaznawi instead names Maksalmina as their leader who urged retreat to the cave. «قال لهم ذلك أي إذ اعتزلتموهم واعتزلتم ما يعبدون»
#119 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · ابن عطية · reports · If the townspeople knew only idols and did not know God, illa Allah is a disjoint exception from what they worshipped. «وهذا على تقدير إن الذين فر أهل الكهف منهم لا يعرفون الله»
#120 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · ابن عطية · reports · If the townspeople knew God but associated idols with Him, the exception is connected because the youths left every object of worship except God. «وإن فرضنا أنهم يعرفون الله كما كانت العرب تفعل لكنهم يشركون أصنامهم»
#121 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · قتادة · reports · The reading in Ibn Masud's codex, 'what they worship besides God,' is Qatada's explanatory gloss. «وفي مصحف عبد الله بن مسعود «وما يعبدون من دون الله». قال قتادة هذا تفسيرها»
#122 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · عطاء الخراساني · reports · Al-Khurasani says the youths came from a people worshipping God alongside idols, and they abandoned idol worship while keeping worship of God. «كان فتية من قوم يعبدون الله ويعبدون معه آلهة»
#123 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · ابن عطية · reports · On Qatada's reading, illa means 'other than' and ma is accusative, coordinated with the pronoun in 'you left them.' «فعلى ما قال قتادة تكون «إلا» بمنزلة غير»
#124 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · holds · The youths decide that after separating from unbelievers and their worship they will make the cave their refuge, trust God's mercy, and ask for a worldly convenience while secure about the afterlife. «فلنجعل الكهف مأوى ونتكل على الله فإنه سيبسط لنا رحمته»
#125 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · أبو جعفر محمد بن علي بن الحسين · reports · Abu Jaʿfar says the People of the Cave were polishers and names the cave Hayyum. «وقال أبو جعفر محمد بن علي بن الحسين رضي الله عنه»
#126 BAYDAWI-FULL d.685 · holds · The youths address one another about abandoning the people and their idols; the exception may exclude worship of God, be a verbal noun meaning worship, or be a divine parenthesis affirming their monotheism. «عطف على الضمير المنصوب، أي وإذا اعتزلتم القوم»
#127 BAYDAWI-FULL d.685 · holds · God's mercy includes expanded provision and benefit in both worlds; the youths speak confidently because of their firm trust in His favor. «يبسط الرزق لكم ويوسع عليكم»
#128 BAYDAWI-FULL d.685 · نافع وابن عامر · reports · Nafi and Ibn Amir read marfiqan with an a-vowel on the first syllable and an i-vowel on the second; the form is an anomalous verbal noun. «وقرأ نافع وابن عامر { مرفقا } بفتح الميم»
#129 NASAFI-FULL d.710 · holds · The believers address one another after firmly resolving to flee with their religion; what they worship is grammatically coordinated to the pronoun, meaning abandon the people and their objects of worship. «خطاب من بعضهم لبعض حيث صممت عزيمتهم على الفرار بدينهم»
