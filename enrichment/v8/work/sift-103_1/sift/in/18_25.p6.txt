#239 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · ابن سينا · reports · Al-Ālūsī supports the possibility of the long sleep by citing Ibn Sīnā's report that Aristotle described mystics who experienced a state resembling the cave sleepers before their time. «ومما يدل على إمكان هذا اللبث أن أبا علي بن سينا ذكر في الباب الزمان من الشفاء»
#240 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · بعضهم · reports · An allegory says direct divine training without shaykhs may take many years, while guidance through them may be completed in forty days or even a few days; al-Ālūsī answers that God can guide without intermediaries in a few hours. «وفي الآية على ما قيل إشارة إلى أن المزيد الذي يربيه الله سبحانه بلا واسطة المشايخ يصل في مدة مديدة»
#241 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Three hundred years plus nine gives the lunar equivalent of three hundred solar years. «sene-i şemsiye üç yüz, dokuz fazlası da sene-i kameriye hesâbıyladır»
#242 ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · Some commentators hold that the duration is God's own statement. «Bazı müfessirîn bu müddetin beyân-ı ilâhî olduğuna kāil olmuş»
#243 ELMALILI d.1361 · İbn Mes‘ud · reports · A report from Ibn Masud takes the duration as a quotation of those disputing the count, with an omitted “they said.” «İbn Mes‘ud’dan mervî olduğu üzere وقالوا takdîriyle adedde ihtilâf edenlerin sözünü hikâye olduğunu söylemiş»
#244 ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · Other commentators prefer connecting the duration to the statement of those who said they would build a mosque over the sleepers. «diğer bazıları da tâ yukarıda قَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ غَلَبُوا۟ عَلَىٰٓ أَمْرِهِمْ’deki وَقُلْ’nin mekūlüne ma‘tuf olarak “mescid yapalım” diyenlerin sözü olmasını tercih etmişlerdir»
#245 ELMALILI d.1361 · prefers · Elmalılı prefers a fresh, resumptive reading: the victorious Cave party remained hidden in their cave for 309 years, until their side prevailed. «Ashâb-ı Kehf’in hizbi demek olan ve “mescid yapalım” diyen ٱلَّذِينَ غَلَبُوا۟’ya râci‘olmak»
#246 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · He connects their emergence with Christians overcoming the idolatrous Romans at the beginning of the fourth century, which fits a hiding period of more than three hundred years. «Nasârânın müşrik Romalılara galebe ile meydana çıkmaları Milâdın dördüncü asrı bidâyetlerinde vâki‘olduğuna göre o zamana kadar üç yüz küsur sene durmuşlar demektir»
#247 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Their long hidden victory foreshadows that Muhammad's mission and Islam would prevail sooner and more swiftly and beautifully, a triumph that began after the Hijra. «üç yüz dokuz sene gizli durdukları hâlde risâlet-i Muhammediye ile dîn-i İslâm’ın bundan çok az bir müddet zarfında»
#248 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · reports · The clause may continue what people say after 18:22, meaning they say the sleepers remained 309 years; Ibn Masʿūd’s reading ‘and they said’ supports this attachment. «فيجوز أن تكون جملة ولبثوا } عطفا على مقولهم في قوله : { سيقولون ثلاثة رابعهم كلبهم . ( الكهف : 22 )»
#249 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · ابن مسعود · reports · Ibn Masʿūd read ‘and they said they remained in their cave,’ clarifying the interpretation that the duration is reported speech. «وعن ابن مسعود أنه قرأ وقالوا لبثوا في كهفهم } إلى آخره ، فذلك تفسير لهذا العطف»
#250 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · reports · Alternatively, the clause may continue the whole narrative as God’s statement that they remained in the cave for 309 years. «ويجوز أن يكون العطف على القصة كلها . والتقدير : وكذلك أعثرنا عليهم إلى آخره ، وهم لبثوا في كهفهم ثلاثمائة سنة وتسع سنين»
#251 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · prefers · The apparent meaning is the sleepers’ first interval before awakening, matching the earlier discussion of how long they slept; Christian historians instead claimed 240 years. «ثم إن الظاهر أن القرآن أخبر بمدة لبث أهل الكهف في كهفهم»
#252 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · reports · Another proposed meaning is the period from their final death until the verse’s revelation. «وقيل : المراد لبثهم من وقت موتهم الأخير إلى زمن نزول هذه الآية»
#253 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The 309 years express 300 solar years plus nine lunar years, aligning the Roman calendar with the lunar years used by Arabs and Muslims; a full lunar year accrues per 33 solar years. «فعبر عن هذا العدد بأنه ثلاثمائة سنة وزيادة تسع ، ليعلم أن التقدير بالسنين القمرية المناسبة لتاريخ العرب والإسلام»
#254 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · السهيلي · reports · Al-Suhaylī says Christians knew the sleepers’ story and dated events by it; Ibn ʿAṭiyya transmits from al-Naqqāsh the calculation that 100 solar years differ by three lunar years. «قال السهيلي في الروض الأنف } : النصارى يعرفون حديث أهل الكهف ويؤرخون به . وأقول : واليهود»
#255 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Ibn Ashur presents the lunar-solar conversion as a scientific aspect of Qur’anic inimitability beyond the general knowledge of Arabs. «وبهذا تظهر نكتة التعبير عن التسع السنين بالازدياد . وهذا من علم القرآن وإعجازه العلمي الذي لم يكن لعموم العرب علم به»
#256 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · الجمهور · reports · The majority read miʾah with tanwīn; sinīn is either apposition under the view that one hundred cannot take an accusative specification, or a specification for those who allow it. «وقرأ الجمهور { ثلاث مائة } بالتنوين . وانتصب { سنين } على البدلية من اسم العدد على رأي من يمنع مجيء تمييز المائة منصوبا»
#257 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · حمزة والكسائي وخلف · reports · Ḥamza, al-Kisāʾī, and Khalaf read miʾata sinīna in annexation; a plural specification after one hundred is rare but eloquent. «وقرأه حمزة والكسائي وخلف بإضافة مائة إلى سنين على أنه تمييز للمائة . وقد جاء تمييز المائة جمعا»
#258 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The stated three hundred years plus nine gives the youths’ sleeping duration, first summarized as a number of years; the following verse and sequence support this duration as the true account. «بيان لمدة لبثهم في الكهف على حال النوم فإن هذا اللبث هو متعلق العناية في آيات القصة»
#259 TABATABAI d.1402 · rejects · The duration is not a quotation from the People of the Book refuted by the next verse, nor three hundred years from God with nine added by them; their reported figure was two hundred or less. «على أن المنقول عنهم أنهم قالوا بلبثهم مائتي سنة أو أقل لا ثلاثمائة وتسعة»
#260 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · Sinin is an appositional substitute for three hundred, not its numeral specification, which would be singular; plural wording may magnify the long duration. «ليس بمميز للعدد وإلا لقيل ثلاثمائة سنة»
#261 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The added nine has an emphatic sense, as if to say not merely three hundred long years but nine more; the earlier account minimizes the period while this verse stresses its length. «كأنه قيل ولبثوا في كهفهم ثلاثمائة سنة هذه السنين المتمادية والدهر الطويل بل ازدادوا تسعا»
#262 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The nine-year difference indicates three hundred solar years equal roughly three hundred and nine lunar years; Quranic years are lunar, and approximate ratios need not match exactly. «إضافة تسع سنين إلى ثلاثمائة سنة مدة اللبث تعطي أنهم لبثوا في كهفهم ثلاثمائة سنة شمسية»
#263 TABATABAI d.1402 · التفسير الكبير · reports · The Great Commentary strongly objects because the two figures do not exactly correspond and disputes a report attributed to Ali; the author answers that the discrepancy is under three months and approximation is common. «وفي التفسير الكبير شدد النكير على ذلك لعدم تطابق العددين تحقيقا»
#264 TABATABAI d.1402 · rejects · A report’s claim that the duration in 18:25 is the People of the Book’s statement rejected by 18:26 is incompatible with the surrounding passage and its eloquent structure. «وقد عرفت في البيان المتقدم أن السياق يدفعه والنظم البليغ لا يقبله» [filed under 18:25, 18:26]
#265 TABATABAI d.1402 · reports · Reports differ over whether the youths died or slept again and whether the three hundred and nine years came from God or the People of the Book; the broadly shared account is that monotheists fled a coercive polytheist king and slept in a cave. «والذي يمكن أن تعد الروايات متفقة أو كالمتفقة عليه أنهم كانوا قوما موحدين هربوا من ملك جبار» [filed under 18:9, 18:25, 18:26]
#266 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The cave story circulated in detailed reports from al-Qummī, Ibn ʿAbbās, ʿIkrima, Mujāhid, Ibn Isḥāq, Wahb ibn Munabbih, and al-Nuʿmān ibn Bashīr, among others. «وردت قصة الكهف مفصلة كاملة في عدة روايات عن الصحابة والتابعين وأئمه أهل البيت» [filed under 18:9, 18:10, 18:11, 18:12, 18:13, 18:14, 18:16, 18:17, 18:18, 18:19, 18:21, 18:25, 18:26]
#267 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The transmitted accounts disagree even on major features, while reports about dates, rulers, lineage, appearance, names, and the title أصحاب الرقيم diverge still more. «من الاختلاف في متونها بحيث لا تكاد تتفق في جهة بارزه من جهات القصة» [filed under 18:9, 18:10, 18:11, 18:12, 18:13, 18:14, 18:16, 18:17, 18:18, 18:19, 18:21, 18:25, 18:26]
#268 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · One cause is the story’s prominence among People of the Book: Quraysh learned it from them, and its Christian images and monuments spread it widely, so communities reshaped it to fit their beliefs. «أن القصة مما اعتنت به أهل الكتاب كما يستفاد من رواياتها أن قريشا تلقتها عنهم» [filed under 18:9, 18:10, 18:11, 18:12, 18:13, 18:14, 18:16, 18:17, 18:18, 18:19, 18:21, 18:25, 18:26]
#269 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · A second cause is Muslims’ broad collection of outside reports, especially through converts such as Wahb ibn Munabbih and Kaʿb al-Aḥbār; later transmitters treated such material as prophetic reports. «اختلط بهم قوم من علماء أهل الكتاب دخلوا في الإسلام كوهب بن منبه وكعب الأحبار» [filed under 18:9, 18:10, 18:11, 18:12, 18:13, 18:14, 18:16, 18:17, 18:18, 18:19, 18:21, 18:25, 18:26]
#270 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The Qurʾān selects key lessons that serve guidance instead of supplying a complete historical account; it is a book of guidance, not history. «فما كتاب الله بكتاب تاريخ وإنما هو كتاب هدى» [filed under 18:9, 18:10, 18:11, 18:12, 18:13, 18:14, 18:16, 18:17, 18:18, 18:19, 18:21, 18:25, 18:26]
