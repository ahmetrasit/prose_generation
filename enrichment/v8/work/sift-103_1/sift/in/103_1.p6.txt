#263 ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · A poem says people rejoice at days passing although each one reduces their remaining lifespan. «Biz günleri geçiriyoruz diye ferahlanıyoruz, hâlbuki her geçen gün ecelden, ömürden bir eksikliktir» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#264 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The first Prophetic-era phase began with Muhammad's mission amid religious disorder, extinguished guidance, and widespread ignorance, disbelief, injustice, and corruption. «nübüvvet, fazilet, hakk u adalet, ma‘rifetullah» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#265 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The Qur'an began descending with the command iqra on a Night of Power, and prophetic light rose to call humanity toward reform, paradise, and divine approval. «bir leyle-i kadirde اِقْرَأْ بِاسْمِ رَبِّكَ emriyle Kur’an nâzil olarak nûr-ı nübüvvet tulû‘etmişti» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#266 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · As discussed in Surat al-Bayyina, spreading the upright religion called people away from total loss and began transformations that led from the middle ages to the last era. «لَمْ يَكُن Sûresi’nde geçtiği üzere dîn-i kayyimenin neşriyle insanlığı şerrü’l-beriyyenin istîlâsından» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#267 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The oath by this era before the declaration that humanity is in loss except believers and doers of good carries important meaning. «O vakit bu asra kasem ederek إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#268 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The Prophetic era from the mission to Muhammad's death had two phases: struggle, then conquest and victory, as prophetic light spread guidance and distinguished truth from falsehood. «ki biri mücahede, biri de feth ü nusret ile»
#269 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The Muhammadic era from the Hijra through the end of time encompasses later centuries and fulfills the effects and aims of all earlier prophetic eras. «hicret-i seniyyeden ilâ âhiri’d-dehr bütün kurûn-ı ahîreyi ihtivâ eden âhir zamandır»
#270 ELMALILI d.1361 · prefers · The author and some exegetes find the Muhammadic era the most comprehensive and immediate meaning of asr. «müfessirînin dediği gibi bizce de en ziyade mütebâdir olan bu asırdır»
#271 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Qur'anic history is divided into the eras before Moses' Torah, from the Torah to the final Prophet, and the final era of Muhammad and his community. «zamanın üç kısma taksîmine işaret olunmuştu»
#272 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · A day analogy portrays the Muhammadic era as the afternoon of human history, following earlier ages as afternoon follows morning and noon. «asr-ı Muhammedî bugünün ikindisi misâlinde temsil edilmiştir»
#273 ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · A hadith compares prior peoples' work periods to morning and midday, and the Muslim community's work from asr to sunset, for which God grants twice the wage after the Jews and Christians object. «Sizden evvel geçmişlere nisbetle sizin vaktiniz, ikindi namazıyla gurûb-ı şems arası kadardır»
#274 ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · Some exegetes use this hadith to identify asr with the time of the Prophet and the community praised as the best brought forth for humanity. «Bir kısım müfessirîn bu hadis ile istidlâl ederek demişlerdir ki: Bu hadis»
#275 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · As Allah swore by the Prophet's location and life, this surah swears by his era. «diye mekânına ve لَعَمْرُكَ [el-Hicr 15/72] diye ömrüne kasem buyurduğu gibi burada da asrına kasem buyurmuştur»
#276 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The oath encompasses every meaning of asr, especially the final era as the afternoon of time and the age of Muhammad, before the declaration that humankind is in loss. «Her mânasıyla asra ve bâhusus pek büyük hâdisâta» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#277 ELMALILI d.1361 · Ebû Medîne · reports · He reports that two of the Prophet's Companions would not part after meeting until they recited Sūrat al-ʿAṣr to each other and then exchanged greetings. «Ebû Medîne’den[^57]-ki sohbeti vardır- şöyle dediğini rivayet eylemişlerdir» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#278 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The rendering says: By the age; humankind is in absolute loss, except those who believe, do righteous works, counsel one another to truth, and counsel patience. «Kasem olsun ki asra (1) insan mutlak bir hüsranda (2)» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#279 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Prophetic reports call the delayed middle prayer the asr prayer and warn that missing asr is like losing one's family and property. «Hendek vak‘asında Aleyhissalâtü vesselâm شَغَلُونَا عَنِ الصَّلاَةِ الْوُسْطَى صَلاَةِ الْعَصْرِ buyurmuştu»
#280 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Afternoon prayer is especially attention-awakening because it interrupts the period when people are most absorbed in work and trade. «gündüzün âhiri olmak hasebiyle dünyada diğer mahlûkāt ve hayvânâtın yaratılışından»
#281 ELMALILI d.1361 · Katâde · reports · Qatada says Allah swears by afternoon time as a counterpart to duha, with signs of divine power. «Katâde demiştir ki: İkindi namazının vakti olmak ve “duhâ”ya mukabil birtakım delâil-i kudreti hâvî bulunmak»
#282 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Afternoon evokes humanity's creation late in the world's day, the closing period of human life, and the sorrow of returning without gain; this complements the previous surah's warning about rivalry. «dünyada bir gün mesabesinde demek olan insan ömrünün de son demlerini andırdığı» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#283 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Dehr, in Raghib's definition, is the interval from the universe's beginning to its end; zaman can name that whole or any part, while dehr also means long duration, remaining lifetime, or lifespan. «“dehr”in asıl mânası Râgıb’ın dediği gibi»
#284 ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · Ibn Jarir transmits Ali's interpretive recitation: by time and its calamities, humankind remains in loss until time's end. «İbn Cerîr’in rivayet ettiği vechile Hazret-i Ali radıyallâhu anh’tan وَالْعَصْرِ وَنـَوَائِبِ الدَّهْرِ» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#285 ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · This reading interprets asr as dehr, whose recurring calamities and changes warn people and whose loss continues to the end; squeezing or confinement may also reinforce the loss side. «İbn Cerîr’in rivayet ettiği vechile Hazret-i Ali radıyallâhu anh’tan» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#286 ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · Another view says dehr contains every wonder that displays the Creator's power, where ordinary and extraordinary events, blessings, disasters, gains, and losses unfold. «Demek ki “asr”, dehr mânasınadır ve dehre kasemin hikmeti de onun insana» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#287 ELMALILI d.1361 · Râzî · reports · Razi presents time as puzzling: it is divisible into past, present, and future, yet the present is indivisible and past and future are nonexistent, so time seems neither simply existent nor nonexistent. «Denilmiştir ki “dehr” Hâlık teâlâ’nın kudretine»
#288 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Philosophers and theologians have debated whether time exists, is nonexistent or imagined, and whether our knowledge of it is immediate or abstracted. «Râzî derki “akıl ona yok diye hükmedemez; çünkü devirler»
#289 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The opening waw marks an oath, as in Wad-Duha. «Vâv وَالضُّحٰى gibi kasem için»
#290 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · A cited Arab poet compares time to a ship carrying people toward death while its movement remains unseen. «Onun için felâsife ve mütekellimîn zaman mevcud mudur, ma‘dum mudur, mevhum mudur»
#291 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · As a noun, asr can mean the whole age, day and night, parts of the day, afternoon prayer or time, a group, and rain; as a verb it can mean restraining, preventing, taxing, or squeezing out juice. «Kāmûs’ta zikrolunduğu üzere “asr” kelimesi lügatta isim olarak dehr»
#292 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Vocalization variants asr, isr, and usr can mark a specific time; paired asran can refer to day and night or morning and evening. «Gündüzle geceye ve sabah ile akşama “asrân” denilir.»
#293 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · With the generic article, al-asr may include every sense; with the covenant article, it may identify one particular time. «Lâm-ı cins ile وَٱلْعَصْرِ makāmına göre bunların her birinin cinsine»
#294 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · In Turkish usage asr commonly means time generally, the present era, or a period of eighty or a hundred years. «Ondan dolayı lisânımızda “asr” mutlak zaman ve bilhassa içinde bulunulan zaman»
#295 ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · Exegetes propose afternoon prayer, afternoon time, the age or time generally, Muhammad's era, and the end times. «müfessirîn başlıca ikindi namazı, ikindi vakti, dehr ve zaman, bâhusus asr-ı Muhammedî»
#296 ELMALILI d.1361 · İbn Cerîr · prefers · Ibn Jarir holds that asr is broad enough to include all its lexical senses, since the verse gives no reason to select one; the author endorses this inclusive reading. «Bu hususta savab olan kavil şudur: Rabbimiz teâlâ asra kasem etmiştir»
#297 ELMALILI d.1361 · Mukātil · reports · Muqatil says Allah swears by the afternoon prayer, whose special merit follows from the majority's identification of the middle prayer as asr. «Mukātil demiştir ki: Allah teâlâ ikindi namazına kasem buyurmuştur»
#298 QUTB-ZILAL d.1386 · holds · The surah is Meccan, has three verses, and compresses Islam's full way of life and the Muslim community's identity and role into a brief constitution. «سورة العصر مكية وآياتها ثلاث» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#299 QUTB-ZILAL d.1386 · holds · The surah presents the Muslim community's essence and function in its final verse, a concise form of inimitable divine expression. «وتصف الأمة المسلمة : حقيقتها ووظيفتها . في آية واحدة هي الآية الثالثة من السورة» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
