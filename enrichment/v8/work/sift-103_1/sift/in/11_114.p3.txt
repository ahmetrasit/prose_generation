#123 JISHUMI d.494 · reports · One occasion report concerns an Anṣārī date seller who lured a woman into his home and embraced her; she reminded him to fear God, he stopped and regretted it, and after he confessed, the Prophet said his prayer expiated it. ʿUmar asked whether this applied to him alone or everyone, and the Prophet answered everyone. «فقالت: اتق الله، فتركها وندم على ذلك، وأتى النبي - صلى الله عليه وسلم -، فأخبره بما فعل»
#124 JISHUMI d.494 · أبو علي · prefers · Abū ʿAlī's second interpretation is that repentance expiates major sins; the author says God intended both interpretations under discussion. «والثاني: التوبة تكفر الكبائر، وأراد تعالى الوجهين، عن أبي علي»
#125 JISHUMI d.494 · holds · Establishing prayer entails prayer in the specified times and establishes the times in general; the Prophet explained their details. «ويدل قوله: أقم الصلاة على وجوب الصلاة بهذه الأوقات. وتدل على المواقيت في الجملة»
#126 JISHUMI d.494 · prefers · The soundest account is Abū ʿAlī's: obedience removes sin through repentance; the claim cannot apply to every good deed, since unbelievers perform many good deeds without their sins being erased. «والأصح ما حكيناه عن أبي علي رحمه الله، ولا تصح دعوى العموم فيه»
#127 JISHUMI d.494 · reports · Another view says good deeds mean all good actions by the Prophet and believers, while sins mean all evil deeds by the polytheists; the verse promises victory for the Prophet and dominance of his religion. «وقيل: الحسنات أفعال الخير كله، وهو فعل النبي والمؤمنين. يذهبن السيئات»
#128 JISHUMI d.494 · أبو مسلم · reports · Abū Muslim links the promise of good overcoming evil to the statement that truth is hurled against falsehood and crushes it. «ونظيره قوله تعالى: (بل نقذف بالحق على الباطل فيدمغه)» [filed under 11:114, 21:18]
#129 JISHUMI d.494 · أبو مسلم · reports · Abū Muslim says the reminder makes believers firm in their religion. «فيثبتون على الدين، عن أبي مسلم»
#130 JISHUMI d.494 · الأصم · reports · Al-Aṣamm says prayer reminds people of their sins, leading them to repent and avoid evil, as in the statement that prayer restrains indecency and wrongdoing. «كقوله: (إن الصلاة تنهى عن الفحشاء والمنكر)» [filed under 11:114, 29:45]
#131 JISHUMI d.494 · reports · Other explanations take ذلك as the Qur'an or as the promise itself being a reminder. «وقيل: ذلك الوعد ذكرى»
#132 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · reports · One reading of the day’s two ends is dawn and evening, meaning the dawn and sunset prayers; Mujahid includes dawn, noon, and afternoon, with sunset and night prayers as the night’s portions. «قال مجاهد: طرفا النهار صلاة»
#133 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · مقاتل · reports · Muqatil assigns dawn and noon prayer to one end of the day, afternoon and sunset to the other, and night prayer to the night’s portions. «وقال مقاتل: صلاة الفجر والظهر طرف، وصلاة العصر والمغرب طرف، وزلفا من الليل، يعني: صلاة العشاء.»
#134 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · الحسن · reports · Al-Hasan takes the two ends as dawn and afternoon prayers, and the night portions as sunset and night prayers. «وقال الحسن: طرفا النهار. الصبح والعصر، وزلفا من الليل: المغرب والعشاء.»
#135 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas identifies the ends as morning and evening, meaning dawn and sunset prayers. «وقال ابن عباس رضي الله عنهما: طرفا النهار الغداة والعشي، يعني صلاة الصبح والمغرب.»
#136 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · أبو جعفر · reports · The night portions are its hours, each called a zulfa; Abu Jafar reads زلفا with a damma on the lam. «ساعاته واحدتها زلفة. وقرأ أبو جعفر»
#137 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · reports · “That” points either to what was just described or to the Quran; it is a reminder for those who remember. «قوله عز وجل: {ذلك} أي: ذلك الذي ذكرنا. وقيل: هو إشارة إلى القرآن، {ذكرى} عظة {للذاكرين} أي لمن ذكره.»
#138 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · holds · The two ends of the day are morning and evening: dawn is the morning prayer, noon and afternoon are the evening prayers, and the night intervals are sunset and night prayers. «وصلاة الغدوة : الفجر ، وصلاة العشية : الظهر والعصر»
#139 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · holds · The accusative of the two ends is adverbial because the words are annexed to times; the annexed term takes the syntactic status of its complement. «وانتصاب طرفي النهار على الظرف ، لأنهما مضافان إلى الوقت»
#140 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · reports · He records forms with damma, quiescent lām, and the form zu lfā; the last means a near interval of night, as does qurbā. «وقرىء : ( وزلفا ) ، بضمتين . وزلفا ، بسكون اللام . وزلفى : بوزن قربى»
#141 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · reports · On another parsing, the night intervals are prayers by which one draws near to God, so the phrase coordinates with prayer. «وحقها على هذا التفسير أن تعطف على الصلاة»
#142 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · holds · One explanation is that acts of obedience expiate minor sins. «أن يراد تكفير الصغائر بالطاعات»
#143 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · reports · A prophetic report says one prayer expiates what lies between it and the next, provided major sins are avoided. «إن الصلاة إلى الصلاة كفارة ما بينهما ما اجتنبت الكبائر»
#144 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · reports · A second explanation is that good deeds act as a moral prompting that helps people leave sins; he links this to reciting scripture. «إن الحسنات يذهبن السيئات ، بأن يكن لطفا في تركها»
#145 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · reports · The verse is reported to have followed the repentance of Abu al-Yusr ʿAmr ibn Ghaziyya after he embraced and kissed a woman, then confessed to the Prophet. «نزلت في أبي اليسر عمرو بن غزية الأنصاري ، كان يبيع التمر»
#146 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · reports · A report says Abu Bakr and ʿUmar each advised the penitent to conceal the matter and repent; when ʿUmar asked whether the verse applied only to him, the Prophet said it was for everyone. «فقال عمر : أهذا له خاصة أم للناس عامة ؟ فقال : بل للناس عامة»
#147 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · reports · Another prophetic report instructs him to perform ablution well and pray two rakʿas, then cites the verse about good deeds removing sins. «توضأ وضوءا حسنا وصل ركعتين»
#148 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · holds · The demonstrative refers to the command to remain upright and what follows it; this is an admonition for those who take admonition. «إشارة إلى قوله { فاستقم } فما بعده { ذكرى للذكرين }»
#149 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · جمهور المتأولين · reports · Most early interpreters take the good deeds that erase sins as the five prayers; Uthman invoked this verse while performing ablution, and this is Malik's interpretation. «ذهب جمهور المتأولين من صحابة وتابعين إلى أن الحسنات يراد بها الصلوات الخمس»
#150 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · مجاهد · reports · Mujahid says the good deeds are a person's repeated glorification, praise, profession of divine unity, and magnification of God. «وقال مجاهد: الحسنات: قول الرجل»
#151 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · prefers · These are examples of good deeds; the verse's wording is general for good deeds but specific to lesser sins in light of the Prophetic phrase 'if major sins are avoided.' «وهذا كله إنما هو على جهة المثال في الحسنات»
#152 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · reports · A report says the verse came after an Ansari man—identified as Abu al-Yusr ibn Amr or as Abbad—met a woman alone and kissed and fondled her without intercourse; after he told Umar and Abu Bakr, he confessed to the Prophet, who recited the verse. Muadh asked if it was only for him; the Prophet said it was for everyone. Another report says the verse predated the incident and was applied to him. «وروي أن هذه الآية نزلت في رجل من الأنصار»
#153 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم · reports · The Prophet said Friday to Friday, the five prayers, and Ramadan to Ramadan expiate what lies between them if major sins are avoided. «وروي أن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم قال: «الجمعة إلى الجمعة»
#154 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · جمهور أهل السنة · reports · Most Sunni scholars make avoiding major sins a condition of the entire promise: without it, the named acts do not expiate lesser sins. «فقال جمهورهم: هو شرط في معنى الوعد كله»
#155 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · فرقة · reports · Another group says major sins are the sins the acts do not erase; the condition preserves the hadith's broad wording while the acts still erase lesser sins. «وقالت فرقة: معنى قوله إن اجتنبت»
#156 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · prefers · Ibn Atiyya adopts the second interpretation: the hadith about sins falling away with drops of water supports it, provided minor sins are repented of and not persisted in; he calls this the precise view of expert legal theorists. On the first interpretation, the verse applies only to those who avoid major sins. «وبهذا أقول وهو الذي يقتضيه حديث خروج الخطايا»
#157 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · reports · ذلك may refer to the prayers as a cause and setting for remembrance, to the claim that good deeds erase sins as encouragement toward good deeds, or to all the preceding commands and prohibitions, as al-Tabari explains. «أي هي سبب ذكر وموضع ذكرى»
#158 TABRISI d.548 · holds · طرفي النهار is a temporal accusative, with زلفا coordinated to it. «{ طرفي النهار } منصوب على الظرف»
#159 TABRISI d.548 · holds · Establish prayer by completing its acts of bowing, prostration, and other obligations. «أي أدها وائت بأعمالها على وجه التمام في ركوعها وسجودها»
#160 TABRISI d.548 · reports · Al-Tabrisi reports from Abu Umama that a man confessed an offense and asked the Prophet to impose its legal penalty; the Prophet said prayer had brought forgiveness from God. «" بينما رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم في المسجد ونحن قعود معه إذ جاءه رجل»
#161 TABRISI d.548 · reports · Al-Tabrisi transmits from al-Harith, from Ali, that after a man confessed, the Prophet called his well-performed prayer and ablution expiation for the sin. «" كنا مع رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم في المسجد ننتظر الصلاة»
#162 TABRISI d.548 · أبو عبد الله · reports · Al-Sadiq advises a visitor to account for each day and night because a noble angel records his public and hidden acts; he should neither dismiss a sin nor belittle a good deed. «واعلم أن معك ملكا كريما موكلا بك يحفظ عليك ما تصنع ويطلع على سرك الذي تخفيه من الناس»
