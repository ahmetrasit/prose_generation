#178 DURR-FULL d.911 · قتادة · reports · Qatadah says Joseph gave them knowledge of one additional year beyond the years they asked about. «عن قتادة - رضي الله عنه - في قوله {ثم يأتي من بعد ذلك عام} الآية قال: زادهم يوسف عليه السلام علم سنة»
#179 DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas says God taught Joseph to volunteer an unasked-for forecast: rain, sesame pressed for oil, grapes for wine, and olives for oil. «عن ابن عباس - رضي الله عنهما - في قوله {ثم يأتي من بعد ذلك عام} قال: أخبرهم بشيء لم يسألوه عنه»
#180 DURR-FULL d.911 · مجاهد · reports · Mujahid glosses the year of relief as rain and says people press their grapes. «عن مجاهد - رضي الله عنه {فيه يغاث الناس} قال: بالمطر {وفيه يعصرون} قال: يعصرون أعنابهم»
#181 DURR-FULL d.911 · الضحاك · reports · Al-Dahhak says people receive rain and press oil in the year of relief. «عن الضحاك - رضي الله عنه - {فيه يغاث الناس} قال: يغاث الناس بالمطر {وفيه يعصرون} قال: الزيت»
#182 DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن عباس · reports · A reading attributed to Ibn Abbas uses the second-person form taʿṣirūn and means that people milk. «عن علي بن طلحة - رضي الله عنه - قال: كان ابن عباس - رضي الله عنه - يقرأ / وفيه تعصرون / بالتاء يعني تحتلبون»
#183 DURR-FULL d.911 · عيسى بن عبيد الثقفي · reports · A reciter reads with second-person forms; the note explains relief as rain and cites the verse about water poured down from the rain clouds. «عن عيسى بن عبيد عن عيسى بن عمير الثقفي - رضي الله عنه - قال: سمعته يقرأ / فيه يغاث الناس وفيه تعصرون / بالتاء»
#184 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · Yughāth may mean rain is sent, from the three-letter root ghayth, or people are rescued, from the four-letter root ghawth; the alif reflects the respective root vowel. «أي يمطرون فيكون بناؤه من ثلاثي»
#185 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · Alternatively, the form may derive from ghawth and mean rescue from hardship. «فالألف مقلوبة من الواو»
#186 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · In the prosperous year, abundance lets people press grapes, sugarcane, olives, sesame, and similar produce. «ما شأنه أن يعصر من العنب والقصب والزيتون والسمسم»
#187 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · The repeated ‘in it’ distinguishes rain and relief, acts of God, from pressing, an act people perform. «وتكرير فيه لأن الغيث والغوث من فعل الله والعصر من فعل الناس»
#188 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · The final year’s glad tidings came by revelation, not from the king’s dream; the cows and ears symbolize fertile years, thin cows and dry ears famine, and stored abundance is consumed. «وإنما تلقاه من جهة الوحي فبشرهم بها»
#189 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · عام and سنة both mean year, but عام often suggests ease and fertility whereas سنة often suggests hardship and famine; the positive term signals the changed conditions from the preceding years. «هو كالسنة لكن كثيرا ما يستعمل فيما فيه الرخاء والخصب»
#190 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · ابن عباس ومجاهد والجمهور · reports · Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and most exegetes explain يغاث الناس as receiving rain, from the triliteral root غاث. «قال ابن عباس ومجاهد والجمهور فهو من غاث الثلاثي اليائي»
#191 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · Another explanation derives it from relief: God will succor them by lifting the hardships pressing upon them, using the fourth-form verb from غوث. «أمدنا برفع المكاره حين أظلتنا فهو رباعي واوي»
#192 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · They will press products normally squeezed, such as grapes, sugarcane, olives, and sesame; explicit mention matters because rain does not ensure these crops as directly as grain, or because this special good news suits the king's own circumstances and makes his reading's second-person form apt. «من العصر المعروف أي يعصرون ما من شأنه أي يعصر من العنب والقصب»
#193 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas interprets the verb as milking; this can derive from pressing because milking squeezes the udder to release milk. «عن ابن عباس تفسير ذلك بيحلبون وكأنه مأخوذ من العصر المعروف»
#194 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · Repeating فيه can signal different times or kinds of benefit, enumerate the year's blessings, or emphasize that the preceding drought's rain was negligible beside this year's; the fronting of the phrase marks the focus, with rhyme also possible for the second occurrence. «المقام مقام تعداد منافع ذلك العام ولأجله قدم»
#195 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · جعفر بن محمد والأعرج وعيسى البصرة · reports · Ja'far ibn Muhammad, al-Aʿraj, and ʿIsa of Basra read يعصرون as passive; ʿIsa also transmits a second-person passive reading, 'you will be delivered' from hardship, matching 'people will receive rain'. «وقرأ جعفر بن محمد رضي الله تعالى عنهما والأعرج وعيسى البصرة»
#196 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · زيد بن أسلم · reports · Zayd ibn Aslam reports Joseph began testing the coming famine by preparing two portions for each man; when one man ate all of his, Joseph said that was the first day of the hard years. «أخرج ابن أبي حاتم عن زيد بن أسلم أنه عليه السلام كان بعد ذلك يصنع لرجل طعام اثنين»
#197 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · البلخي · reports · Al-Balkhi uses the counselors' dismissal of the king's dream as confused dreams to challenge the rule that dreams occur according to their first interpretation; Al-Alusi replies with the hadith that a dream hangs on a bird's wing until interpreted, and says dismissing it as meaningless may not count as interpretation. «واستدل البلخي بتأويله لذلك على بطلان قول من يقول»
#198 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · ابن العربي · reports · Ibn al-Arabi restricts the dream rule to visions admitting several possible meanings: one interpretation is made and then comes to pass. «إنه ينبغي أن يخص ذلك بما يحتمل من الرؤيا وجوها»
#199 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · The passage is also cited as evidence that an unbeliever can have a true dream; interpreters are advised not to ask at sunrise, sunset, or night, and some say a dream seen in sleep is its own interpretation. Al-Alusi finds many such claims opaque. «واستدلوا بذلك أيضا على صحة رؤيا الكافر وهو ظاهر وقد ذكروا للإستفتاء عن الرؤيا آدابا»
#200 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · أبو عبيدة · reports · Abu Ubayda and others take the active form as pressing in the sense of rescue; al-Bahr gives a related lexical sense of relief and cites Abu Zubayd's poem about Uthman as a refuge for the distressed. «وعن أبي عبيدة وغيره أخذ المبني للفاعل من العصر بمعنى النجاة أيضا»
#201 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · ابن المنير · reports · Ibn al-Munir understands the verb as receiving rain from a cloud whose time to be squeezed has come, with the preposition omitted or the verb carrying the sense of raining. «معناه عصيرون من أعصرت السحابة عليهم أي حان وقت عصر الرياح لها لتمطر»
#202 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · الجوهري · reports · Al-Jawhari says عصر القوم means that a people received rain, which makes the reading apparent without a verbal supplement. «وفي الصحاح عصر القوم أي أمطروا ومنه قراءة بعضهم»
#203 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · النقاش · reports · Al-Naqqash transmits a doubled form يعصّرون with a passive yā and kasra on ṣād, conveying abundance or intensity. «وحكى النقاش أنه قريء ( يعصرون ) بضم الياء وكسر الصاد وتشديدها»
#204 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · زيد بن علي · reports · Zayd ibn Ali reads تعصرون with a doubled consonant from an assimilated form of تعتصرون; it can mean squeezing fruit or being rescued. «وقرأ زيد بن علي رضي الله تعالى عنهما ( وفيه تعصرون ) بكسر التاء والعين والصاد»
#205 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · قتادة وابن عباس · reports · Qatada, as transmitted by Ibn Jarir and others, and Ibn Abbas say God granted Joseph knowledge of this year's details by revelation; Al-Alusi treats revelation as the apparent source. «كما أخرج ابن جرير وغيره عن قتادة علم آتاه الله تعالى علمه لم يكن فيما سئل عنه»
#206 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · Joseph ends his answer to the king with the glad news; unlike his answer to the prisoners, where the prediction came first, this placement at the end highlights his excellence. «ولقد أتى عليه السلام بما يدل على فضله في آخر فتواه على عكس ما فعل أولا»
#207 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · بعضهم · rejects · A non-revelatory view says Joseph inferred that drought naturally ends in abundance or that God sends ease after hardship; Al-Alusi rejects it because the prophecy would be vague, especially in readings where mutual relief is described, which only revelation could establish. «إن هذه البشارة منه عليه السلام لم تكن عن وحي بل لأن العادة جارية»
#208 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · In the dream's interpretation, cows represent agricultural years, fatness fertility, leanness drought, and ears of grain provisions. «فالبقرات لسنين الزراعة ، لأن البقرة تتخذ للإثمار . والسمن رمز للخصب» [filed under 12:47, 12:48, 12:49]
#209 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The promise of a year of relief and pressing produce brings comfort and hope after the discouraging forecast, reflecting God's pattern of ease after hardship. «فهو بشارة وإدخال المسرة والأمل بعد الكلام المؤيس»
#210 TABATABAI d.1402 · الرضا عليه السلام · reports · A report says Joseph stockpiled the harvests, sold food during famine first for money, then valuables, livestock, servants, property, and land, and finally for people’s freedom; he then freed the Egyptians and restored their possessions and kingship. «سمعت الرضا عليه السلام يقول وأقبل يوسف على جمع الطعام في السبع السنين المخصبة فكبسه في الخزائن» [filed under 12:47, 12:48, 12:49]
#211 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The author omits many similar reports about the famine economy because most do not directly serve the interpretation of these verses. «والروايات في هذا المقام كثيرة أغلبها غير مرتبطة بغرض تفسير الآيات ولذلك تركنا نقلها» [filed under 12:47, 12:48, 12:49]
#212 TABATABAI d.1402 · reports · Yughath may mean that people are helped through relief from hardship, or that they receive rain; the next phrase about pressing makes the rain sense more apt. «كان معناه ينصرون فيه من قبل الله سبحانه بكشف الكربة»
#213 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The final year is inferred from the repeated seven: after the seven difficult years people need not exert themselves to manage sowing and reserves. «والظاهر أنه عليه السلام استفاده من عدد السبع الذي تكرر في البقرات السمان والعجاف»
