#94 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · holds · The seven firm heavens, sun, rain-bearing clouds, and life-sustaining growth display God's power to create a new realm, the afterlife. «Gökleri, alev alev yanarak dünyayı aydınlatan güneşi, bolca yağmur indirerek yeryüzünde birçok nimetin yetişmesine ve hayatın devam etmesine vesile olan bulutları yaratan yüce kudret» [filed under 78:12, 78:13, 78:14, 78:15, 78:16]
#95 STUDYQURAN · holds · God remains the primary cause: rain is the means by which He produces grain, plants, and gardens, since He made every living thing from water. «God remains the primary cause for all things. Therefore, it is not the rain that produces grain» [filed under 78:15, 78:16]
#96 STUDYQURAN · holds · This late Makkan surah is named for the Great Tiding in verse 2, understood by most as the Quran or Resurrection; it is also called al-Muʿṣirāt and al-Tasāʾul for its rain-cloud and questioning themes. «a late Makkan sūrah, takes» [filed under 78:1-40]
#97 STUDYQURAN · Aj · reports · If the Great Tiding means the Quran, this surah follows directly from the previous surah’s last verse. «Insofar as the Great Tiding refers to the Quran, this sūrah follows directly upon the last verse of the previous sūrah» [filed under 78:1-40]
#98 STUDYQURAN · holds · Verses 1–5 describe deniers, 6–16 point to creation as evidence, and 17–40 depict the Resurrection, the Day of Division, and the differing return of believers and disbelievers. «Rather than argue against their disbelief, vv. 6–16 point to all of those things that God has created as signs» [filed under 78:1-40]
