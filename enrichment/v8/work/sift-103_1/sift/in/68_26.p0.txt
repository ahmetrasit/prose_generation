# 59 notes filed under 68:26
verse 68:26: فَلَمَّا رَأَوْهَا قَالُوٓا۟ إِنَّا لَضَآلُّونَ

#1 MUQATIL d.150 · holds · When they saw the ruined place, they thought they had lost their way. «{ فلما رأوها } ليس فيها شىء ظنوا أنهم أخطأوا الطريق { قالوا إنا لضالون } [آية: 26] عنها.»
#2 FARRA d.207 · holds · Their statement إنا لضالون first means they thought this was not their garden; then one recognized it as theirs, ruined because they had deprived the poor. «ثم قال بعضهم: بل هو مالنا حرمناه بما صنعنا بالأرامل والمساكين»
#3 ABDURRAZZAQ d.211 · قتادة · reports · When they saw it, they thought they had lost the way and this was not their garden; they then recognized that they were deprived. «وقال قتادة يقول أخطأنا الطريق ما هذه جنتنا قال بعضهم ﴿ بل نحن محرومون ﴾ حورفنا حرمنا حتى»
#4 TAB-FULL d.310 · holds · Some brothers first think they lost the way; another recognizes their own garden and says its benefit has been denied them. «أغفلوا طريق جنتهم، وأن التي رأوا غيرها» [filed under 68:26, 68:27]
#5 TAB-FULL d.310 · قتادة · reports · Qatāda says they thought they had taken the wrong path. «{فلما رأوها قالوا إنا لضالون} [القلم: 26] أي ضللنا الطريق»
#6 ZAJJAJ d.311 · holds · Seeing the burned garden, they first thought they had lost their way and this was not their orchard, then recognized it as punishment. «محترقة. (قالوا إنا لضالون)، أي قد ضللنا طريق جنتنا،»
#7 NAHHAS d.338 · holds · On seeing the burned garden, they thought they had lost their way and this was not their garden. «أي قد ضللنا الطريق وليست هذه جنتنا لما رأواها محترقة»
#8 THALABI d.427 · holds · At first they thought they had lost their way; then one said they had instead been deprived of the garden's benefit for excluding the poor and failing to make an exception. «فلما رأوها قالوا إنا لضالون لمخطئوا الطريق فليس هذه بجنتنا» [filed under 68:26-27]
#9 MAWARDI-FULL d.450 · holds · Seeing a bare garden, they first thought they had lost their way and reached the wrong place. «لما رأوا أرض الجنة لا ثمرة فيها ولا شجر قالوا إنا ضالون الطريق»
#10 QUSHAYRI d.465 · holds · Seeing the orchard razed, they first denied it was theirs, then recognized it and realized they had been deprived of its good. «قالوا : ليست هذه جنتنا ! ! ثم قالوا : بل هذه جنتنا» [filed under 68:26-27]
#11 WAHIDI-WAJIZ d.468 · holds · Seeing it black and burned, they first thought they had lost their way, then realized God had deprived them of their fruit because they withheld it from the poor. «{ فلما رأوها } سوداء محترقة { قالوا إنا لضالون } مخطئون طريقنا، وليست هذه جنتنا» [filed under 68:26-27]
#12 JISHUMI d.494 · قتادة · reports · When they saw the garden, they thought they had lost the way and this could not be their orchard. «قيل: [ضللنا] الطريق فليس هذا بستاننا، عن قتادة»
#13 JISHUMI d.494 · reports · Other interpretations are that they had strayed from the truth and were punished for their conduct, or had strayed by withholding the poor's due. «وقيل: الضالون عن الحق في أمرنا؛ فلذلك عوقبنا بذهاب ثمرتها»
#14 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · holds · Seeing the burned garden, they first thought they had lost their way, then realized they were deprived because they denied the poor and omitted the exception. «{فلما رأوها قالوا إنا لضالون} أي لما رأوا الجنة محترقة قالوا: إنا لمخطئون الطريق» [filed under 68:26, 68:27]
#15 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · holds · Seeing the burned orchard, they first thought they had lost the way; once they recognized it, they said they were deprived of its yield and blessing. «فلما رأوها أي محترقة حسبوا أنهم قد ضلوا الطريق، وأنها ليست تلك» [filed under 68:26, 68:27]
#16 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · holds · At dawn the brothers first thought they had taken the wrong road because they could not see their garden, then recognized God's affliction. «فلما أصبحوا إلى جنتهم لم يروها فحسبوا أنهم قد أخطؤوا الطريق، ثم تبينوها فعلموا أن الله تعالى أصابهم»
#17 TABRISI d.548 · قتادة · reports · On seeing the ruined garden, they first thought they had lost their way and this was not their orchard. «ضللنا عن الطريق فليس هذا بستاننا عن قتادة» [filed under 68:26-27]
#18 TABRISI d.548 · reports · Another interpretation is that they had strayed from the right path and were punished for their decision; they then recognized their garden but were deprived of its benefit for denying the poor their share and failing to make an exception. «إنا لضالون عن الحق في أمرنا فلذلك عوقبنا بذهاب ثمر جنتنا» [filed under 68:26-27]
#19 IBNJAWZI-ZAD d.597 · holds · Seeing the burned garden, they first thought they had lost their way and reached the wrong place, then realized it was punishment. «فلما رأوها محترقة قالوا إنا لضالون أي قد ضللنا طريق جنتنا فليست هذه»
#20 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · They may first have thought they had lost their way on seeing the burned orchard; after recognizing it, they said instead that they were deprived because their resolve to be miserly had brought this loss. «لما رأوا جنتهم محترقة ظنوا أنهم قد ضلوا الطريق» [filed under 68:26, 68:27]
#21 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · Alternatively, they called themselves lost because they had resolved to bar the poor and assumed they could benefit from the orchard, but realized they were the deprived ones. «حيث كنا عازمين على منع الفقراء» [filed under 68:26, 68:27]
#22 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · holds · Seeing the burnt, blackened garden, the brothers failed to recognize it and wondered whether they had lost their way. «قد صارت كالليل الأسود ينظرون إليها كالرماد»
#23 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · قتادة · reports · Qatada interprets their words as saying that they had taken the wrong road to their garden. «أي ضللنا الطريق إلى جنتنا قاله قتادة»
#24 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · reports · Alternatively, they recognized their wrongful dawn plan to exclude the poor and understood the destruction as punishment. «إنا لضالون عن الصواب في غدونا على نية منع المساكين فلذلك عوقبنا»
#25 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم · reports · A report from Ibn Masud warns that sin may deprive someone of provision prepared for him, and the Prophet recites the garden passage. «إياكم والمعاصي إن العبد ليذنب الذنب فيحرم به رزقا كان هيىء له» [filed under 68:26-27]
#26 BAYDAWI-FULL d.685 · holds · At first sight they thought they had lost their way to their garden and this was not it. «{ فلما رأوها } أول ما رأوها. { قالوا إنا لضالون } طريق جنتنا»
#27 NASAFI-FULL d.710 · holds · On first seeing the burned garden, they thought they had gone astray and reached the wrong place; after looking carefully, they recognized it. «﴿ قالوا ﴾ في بديهة وصولهم ﴿ إنا لضالون ﴾ أي ضللنا جنتنا»
#28 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · قتادة · reports · At first they thought they had taken the wrong road; after recognizing the garden, they understood that God's punishment had destroyed its bounty. «قالوا إنا لضالون: أي عن الطريق إليها، قاله قتادة.» [filed under 68:26, 68:27]
#29 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · reports · Another interpretation is that they were mistaken in their plan to harvest while intending to deny the poor; “we are deprived” recognizes that their treachery rebounded on them. «بل نحن محرومون خيرها بخيانتنا على أنفسنا.» [filed under 68:26, 68:27]
#30 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas and others say the brothers first thought they had lost their way, then recognized the orchard and realized they had been deprived of its produce. «قاله ابن عباس وغيره. ثم رجعوا عما كانوا فيه، وتيقنوا أنها هي فقالوا: {بل نحن محرومون}» [filed under 68:26, 68:27]
#31 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · After a short walk they saw no trace of crop or fruit and first assumed they had lost their way, because the destroyed orchard bore no resemblance to the one they knew. «بعد سير يسير وليس للزرع ولا للثمر بها أثر»
#32 DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas says they first thought they had lost the way to their garden. «عن ابن عباس في قوله»
#33 DURR-FULL d.911 · قتادة · reports · Qatada says they first thought they had taken the wrong road, then recognized the garden and realized they had been deprived. «عن قتادة في قوله: {إنا لضالون}» [filed under 68:26-27]
#34 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · They first thought they had mistaken the place, then realized they were deprived because of their wrongful intention to bar the poor. «قالوه بعد ما تأملوها ووقفوا على حقيقة الأمر» [filed under 68:26-27]
#35 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · قتادة · reports · Qatada says their first reaction was that they had lost the way to their orchard and this was not it. «قاله قتادة»
#36 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · rejects · Another interpretation is that they were mistaken in their judgment when they set out intending to bar the poor; the author finds it unpersuasive. «وقيل لضالون عن الصواب في غدونا على نية منع المساكين وليس بذاك»
#37 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · They first thought they had lost their way, then recognized punishment and deprivation greater than what they had planned for the poor, and despaired of present and future. «evvelâ yalnız dalâletlerine hükmettiler, sonra da cezâya uğradıklarına, men‘etmek istedikleri miskinden ziyade mahrum bırakıldıklarına hükmettiler» [filed under 68:26, 68:27]
#38 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The owners wake from heedlessness and blame themselves for pride and failure to thank God; this fulfills the lesson that trials may turn people back. «أي استفاقوا من غفلتهم ورجعوا على أنفسهم باللائمة على بطرهم وإهمال شكر النعمة»
#39 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · ابن عطاء الله الإسكندري · reports · Ibn Ata Allah's maxim says that ingratitude exposes blessings to loss, while gratitude binds them fast. «من لم يشكر النعم فقد تعرض لزوالها»
#40 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · لما makes the answer follow its condition immediately and instinctively; the story urges idolaters to repent as quickly as the owners regretted and sought a better replacement. «وأفادت ( لما ) اقتران جوابها بشرطها بالفور والبداهة»
#41 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The plural attribution indicates that all the owners agreed on the reason for what struck them. «وإسناد هذه المقالة إلى ضمير { أصحاب الجنة }»
