#333 DURR-FULL d.911 · مجاهد · reports · Mujahid says Joseph asked the surviving prisoner to mention him to the king; Satan made the cupbearer forget, and Joseph was punished for seeking release through the king. «عن مجاهد رضي الله عنه في قوله {وقال للذي ظن أنه ناج منهما اذكرني عند ربك} قال يوسف»
#334 DURR-FULL d.911 · قتادة · reports · Qatadah says reports reached them that Joseph remained in prison for seven years. «عن قتادة رضي الله عنه في قوله {فلبث في السجن بضع سنين} قال: بلغنا أنه لبث»
#335 DURR-FULL d.911 · وهب بن منبه · reports · Wahb says Job suffered for seven years, Joseph remained in prison for seven, and Nebuchadnezzar was tormented among beasts for seven. «عن وهب بن منبه رضي الله عنه قال: أصاب أيوب عليه السلام البلاء سبع سنين»
#336 DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas says Joseph remained imprisoned for twelve years. «عن ابن عباس رضي الله عنهما في قوله {فلبث في السجن بضع سنين} اثنتي عشرة سنة»
#337 DURR-FULL d.911 · الكلبي · reports · Al-Kalbi says Joseph's one word caused seven years' imprisonment; Abu Bakr ibn Ayyash adds that he had already been imprisoned for five years. «عن الكلبي رضي الله عنه قال: قال يوسف عليه السلام كلمة واحدة حبس بها سبع سنين»
#338 DURR-FULL d.911 · طاوس والضحاك · reports · Tawus and al-Dahhak say Joseph remained imprisoned for fourteen years. «عن طاوس والضحاك في قوله {فلبث في السجن بضع سنين} قالا: أربع عشرة سنة»
#339 DURR-FULL d.911 · مجاهد وقتادة · reports · Mujahid and Qatadah define biḍʿ as a period of three to nine years; Ibn Abbas says it is less than ten. «عن مجاهد رضي الله عنه قال: البضع ما بين الثلاث إلى التسع»
#340 DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas counts three lapses by Joseph: asking to be remembered, accusing his brothers of theft, and saying he had not betrayed the Aziz; Gabriel mentioned Joseph's desire, and he answered that he did not absolve himself. «عن ابن عباس رضي الله عنهما قال: عثر يوسف عليه السلام ثلاث عثرات» [filed under 12:42, 12:70]
#341 DURR-FULL d.911 · قتادة · reports · Qatadah says Joseph left home at seventeen, spent seven years in the well and seven in prison, and stored food for seven years; they think he met his father then. «عن قتادة رضي الله عنه قال: ذهب يوسف عليه السلام وهو ابن سبع عشرة ولبث في الجب سبعا» [filed under 12:42, 12:47]
#342 DURR-FULL d.911 · أبو المليح · reports · Abu al-Malih transmits Joseph's prison prayer asking God, by the face of Jacob, for relief, an easy way out, and provision from an unexpected source. «عن أبي المليح رضي الله عنه قال: كان دعاء يوسف عليه السلام في السجن اللهم إن كان خلق وجهي»
#343 DURR-FULL d.911 · عبد الله مؤذن الطائف · reports · A report says Gabriel taught Joseph to seek relief from every worldly and otherworldly distress, unexpected provision, forgiveness, firm hope in God, and detachment from everyone else. «عن عبد الله مؤذن الطائف قال: جاء جبريل عليه السلام إلى يوسف عليه السلام فقال: يا يوسف»
#344 DURR-FULL d.911 · مجاهد عن ابن إسحاق · reports · Joseph asked the cupbearer to tell the great king about his grievance and unjust imprisonment; the man forgot, then recalled Joseph when King Rayyan's troubling dream needed interpretation. «عن مجاهد رضي الله عنه قال: قال يوسف عليه الصلاة والسلام للساقي {اذكرني عند ربك} أي الملك الأعظم ومظلمتي» [filed under 12:42-43]
#345 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · بضع is commonly three to nine; Qatada says three to nine, Mujahid three to seven, and Abu Ubayda one to ten. Al-Farra says it accompanies tens, not hundreds or thousands; the word derives from cutting. «البضع ما بين الثلاث إلى التسع كما روي عن قتادة وعن مجاهد أنه من الثلاث إلى السبع»
#346 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · Most accounts make Joseph's whole imprisonment seven years, with two years after the request to the cupbearer; another view makes the post-request stay seven years after five earlier years, totaling twelve. «وهي مدة لبثه كلها فيما صححه البعض وسنتان منها كانت مدة لبثه بعد ذلك القول»
#347 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · rejects · The report claiming seven additional years after five is not established in that wording; the versions judged established merely say Joseph remained imprisoned as long as he remained. «الخبر لم يثبت بهذا اللفظ وإنما الثابت في عدة روايات ما لبث في السجن طول ما لبث»
#348 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · طاوس والضحاك · reports · Tawus and al-Dahhak, as cited by Ibn Hatim, explain this occurrence of بضع as fourteen years, an uncommon interpretation. «روي ابن حاتم عن طاوس والضحاك تفسير البضع ههنا بأربع عشرة سنة»
#349 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · prefers · Al-Alusi advises against assigning the prison term an exact number, despite the divergent reports. «والأولى أن لا يجوز بمقدار معين كما قدمنا»
#350 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · أنس · reports · Anas reports that God reminded Joseph of being saved from his brothers, the well, and the woman, then asked why he forgot God and remembered a human; Joseph said it was only a word spoken by his tongue, and God swore to confine him for several years. «من استنقذك من القتل حين هم إخوتك أن يقتلوك»
#351 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · Although seeking help from people in hardship is generally permissible, as cooperation in righteousness shows, what suits prophets is to leave that concession and choose the higher resolve. «واللائق بمناصب الأنبياء عليهم السلام ترك ذلك والأخذ بالعزائم»
#352 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · أبو حيان · reports · Abu Hayyan says Joseph asked the cupbearer to remember him in order to guide the king to faith, not to secure his release; Al-Alusi calls this contrary to the apparent sense and reports, though fitting Joseph's rank. «واختار أبو حيان أن يوسف عليه السلام إنما قال للشرابي ما قال ليتوصل بذلك إلى هداية الملك وإيمانه»
#353 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The imperfect 'he thought' emphasizes certainty that one prisoner would be saved and prepares for Joseph's request to be remembered; some early authorities instead identify the saved man as the one who thought so. «أوثِر على صيغة المضارع مبالغة في الدلالة على تحقيق النجاة»
#354 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · Joseph is the one who knows the prisoner's rescue; here ظن means certainty, as in the verse about those certain they will meet their Lord. «والظان هو يوسف عليه السلام لأصحابه وإن ذهب إليه بعض السلف»
#355 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · Calling Joseph's certainty ظن may be deferential etiquette toward God; alternatively it reflects interpretation by inference, supporting the view that dream interpretation is conjectural rather than certain. «ولعل التعبير به من باب إرخاء العنان والتأدب مع الله تعالى»
#356 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The phrase 'among them' can modify saved or be circumstantial, but cannot attach to 'saved' as its complement because that does not fit the intended meaning. «ولا يجوز أن يكون متعلقا بناج لأنه ليس المعنى عليه»
#357 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · Joseph asks the rescued cupbearer to mention him to his master, including the state and qualities the cupbearer had witnessed. «بما أنا عليه من الحال والصفة»
#358 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · A report says that on the third day, at the prison gate, the cupbearer asked Joseph for his need; Joseph asked him to describe him to the king, but Satan distracted the man until he forgot. «إلى باب السجن قال له»
#359 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The forgotten remembrance is Joseph's mention before the king, not remembrance of God; the genitive can mean remembering Joseph, or reporting him to his master. «أي ذكر يوسف عليه السلام عند الملك والإضافة لأدنى ملابسة»
#360 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The causal ف links the lapse to Joseph's request, since seeking human assistance was the occasion of the rescued man's forgetting. «والفاء للسببية فإن توصيته عليه السلام المتضمنة للإستعانة بغيره سبحانه وتعالى»
#361 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · بعضهم · reports · Some take both pronouns in 'Satan made him forget his lord' to refer to Joseph; forgetfulness here means leaving the better course for a spiritual elite, not Satanic misguidance. «ترك الأولى بالنسبة لمقام الخواص الرافعين للأسباب من البين»
#362 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · prefers · Al-Alusi favors the usual reading in which the rescued cupbearer forgets Joseph before the king, since it suits the causal ف and the later mention of remembering after a long time. «الأول هو المناسب لكان الفاء ولقوله تعالى الآتي»
#363 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · قيل · reports · Some call Joseph’s request to be remembered heedlessness of his station; God prolonged prison to perfect him, for prophets are held to account for the smallest measure. «كان ذلك على ماقيل غفلة منه عليه السلام عما يقتضيه مقامه»
#364 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · بعض ذوي الألباب · reports · Some discerning scholars interpret Joseph’s words in a way that entails no blame. «وقد يحمل كلامه هذا على مالايوجب العتاب كما ذهب اليه بعض ذوي الالباب»
#365 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The close accounting of prophets is due to their special standing before God. «والانبياء مؤاخذون بمثاقيل الذر لمكانتهم عند ربهم»
#366 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The man Joseph expected to survive was the cupbearer; “thought” here approaches certainty because Joseph trusted his interpretation. «والظن هنا مستعمل في القريب من القطع لأنه لا يشك في صحة تعبيره الرؤيا»
#367 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Joseph asks the cupbearer to tell his master, the king of Egypt, about Joseph's case and injustice. «مظلمته ، أي اذكرني لربك ، أي سيدك»
#368 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The pronouns in “Satan made him forget” may refer to the cupbearer: he forgot Joseph and the king; or to Joseph: Satan made Joseph forget God, a different remembrance. «أي أنسى الشيطان الذي نجا أن يذكره لربه»
#369 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · prefers · Both readings may be intended: Joseph forgot to ask God for deliverance, which caused the cupbearer to forget him; this becomes divine reproach for relying on people. «ولعل كلا الاحتمالين مراد»
#370 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · This double reading tactfully reports the lapse, since an allusive statement is gentler than a direct one. «لأن الكلام الموجه في المعاني الموجهة ألطف من الصريح»
#371 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Biḍʿ means a number from three to nine. «البضع : من الثلاث إلى التسع»
