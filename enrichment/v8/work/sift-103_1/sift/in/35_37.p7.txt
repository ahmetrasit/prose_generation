#310 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · ‘Your warner came’ coordinates with the preceding question because it conveys an assertion; coordinating an assertion with an interrogative form is valid here. «لأن معناها الخبر فعطف عليها الخبر»
#311 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The fāʾ in ‘taste’ marks consequence, its object ‘the punishment’ is omitted, and the imperative conveys their continuing punishment and inability to escape. «والفاء في { فذوقوا } للتفريع . وحذف مفعول ( ذوقوا ) لدلالة المقام عليه»
#312 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · ‘The wrongdoers have no helper’ may continue the rebuke, ending their hope of rescue and asking where their supposed allies are. «فيجوز أن يكون من جملة الكلام الذي وبخهم الله به»
#313 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Alternatively it is a separate conclusion for listeners, making the rule apply to every wrongdoer, including the disbelievers just described. «ويجوز أن يكون كلاما مستقلا مفرعا على القصة»
#314 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Wrongdoing violates a rights-holder's due; its gravest form is shirk, an assault on God's oneness and on the self by leading it into punishment. «وأعظمه الشرك لأنه اعتداء على الله بإنكار صفته النفيسة وهي الوحدانية»
#315 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The generality of wrongdoers and helpers means helping an oppressor violates justice; the community must restrain wrongdoing, refuting the pre-Islamic maxim to help one's brother whether wrongdoer or victim. «وفي هذا إبطال لخلق أهل الجاهلية القائلين في أمثالهم ( انصر أخاك ظالما أو مظلوما ) .»
#316 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · محمد صلى الله عليه وسلم · reports · The Prophet corrected that maxim: helping an unjust person means restraining him from his injustice. «إذا كان ظالما تنصره على نفسه فتكفه عن ظلمه»
#317 TABATABAI d.1402 · مجمع البيان · reports · Al-Majma explains iṣṭirākh as crying out and calling for help, derived from ṣarākh. «في المجمع الاصطراخ الصياح والنداء بالاستغاثة افتعال من الصراخ»
#318 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The request to be brought out explains the cries; God's question about granting a life in which one could remember answers them, and the commands to taste punishment and the denial of any helper follow from that answer. «بيان لاصطراخهم، وقوله { أولم نعمركم ما يتذكر»
#319 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The people of the Fire ask to return and do good unlike their former deeds, but are told that they had time and a warner, failed to believe, and have no rescuer from punishment. «فذوقوا العذاب فما للظالمين من نصير»
#320 TABATABAI d.1402 · علي عليه السلام · reports · A saying in Nahj al-Balagha sets the age by which God has given a person sufficient excuse at sixty years; al-Majma and al-Durr also transmit it from Ali. «وقال العمر الذي أعذر الله فيه إلى ابن آدم ستون سنة»
#321 TABATABAI d.1402 · ابن عباس عن النبي صلى الله عليه وآله وسلم · reports · A report says that on Judgment Day it will be asked where the sixty-year-olds are, identifying them as those given the life mentioned in the verse; other routes cite Sahl ibn Sa'd and Abu Hurayra. «عن ابن عباس أن النبي صلى الله عليه وآله وسلم»
#322 TABATABAI d.1402 · الباقر عليه السلام · reports · Another reported interpretation makes the rebuke apply to someone who reached eighteen years; it is attributed to al-Baqir and transmitted in al-Faqih. «وفي المجمع وقيل هو توبيخ لابن ثماني عشر سنة وروي ذلك عن الباقر عليه السلام»
#323 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · Without faith and righteous deeds, wealth and children only increase a person's ruin. «In the absence of faith and righteous deeds, wealth and children will only add to the ruin of such people.»
#324 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · Worldly wealth and children are exposed to danger, and people cannot protect themselves from those risks. «in this world danger lurks everywhere for one’s wealth and children»
#325 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · Ḍiʿf can mean equal in amount or many multiples without a fixed limit; the faithful receive manifold reward, not necessarily only double. «as well as many times the magnitude of a thing»
#326 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · The chambers promise enduring safety and reward, unlike worldly wealth and children exposed to danger; the faithful need not fear loss in the Hereafter. «in this world danger lurks everywhere for one’s wealth and children and no one can protect oneself from these dangers.»
#327 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · The sūrah’s discourse has internal coherence and a relationship with the previous sūrah. «portrays its internal coherence and its relationship with the previous sūrah» [filed under 35:1-45]
#328 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · In Hell, the condemned plead for release so they can return to the world and act differently from before. «they will plead to God to grant them respite so that they could go back in the previous world»
#329 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · God answers that they had enough life to heed the reminder and a warner came; they receive no further support and must taste their deserved punishment. «were they not granted enough respite which was sufficient for them to pay heed to God’s reminder»
#330 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · Those given sufficient lifespan and a warner who conclusively communicates truth receive no concession; people who die before maturity are treated more leniently. «those whom the Almighty has given enough life span and the truth has also been conclusively communicated to them»
#331 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · holds · Unbelievers who dismissed worldly warnings plead for another chance when faced with punishment, but their cries do no good; compare 23:107–108. «dünyada iken yapılan uyarıları hafife alan inkârcıların kötü âkıbetle karşılaştıklarında kendilerine yeni bir fırsat verilmesi için çırpınmaları tasvir edilir» [filed under 35:36, 35:37]
#332 STUDYQURAN · holds · After witnessing the Resurrection and Judgment they denied and admitting sin, disbelievers seek reprieve and promise righteousness, as in their plea to be sent back. «Having witnessed the realities of Resurrection and Judgment that they had denied (see 32:12; 19:38) and been made to admit their sin»
#333 STUDYQURAN · holds · The warners sent to them and their lifetime to reflect were already a reprieve; repentance is not accepted once death confronts an evildoer or disbeliever. «the warners who had been sent to them and the opportunity to reflect throughout their life constitute such a reprieve»
#334 STUDYQURAN · holds · God rejects requests for return because even if sent back, wrongdoers would again do what they had been forbidden. «God thus denies all such requests, because even if they were sent back, they would return to the very thing they had been forbidden»
#335 STUDYQURAN · holds · Wrongdoers have no helpers because they turned away from God and the help He sent them during worldly life. «because they turned away from God and the help that He sent them during their life in this world»
#336 STUDYQURAN · holds · This and related verses can be read as part of an extended discourse between God and Hell’s inhabitants. «this and other verses can be seen as part of an extended discourse between God and the denizens of Hell»
#337 STUDYQURAN · holds · The longest comparison of believers and disbelievers in verses 29–39 leads into a final contrast between God’s Power and idols, then the outcomes of disbelief. «The longest comparison between the two groups (vv. 29–39), followed by a final contrast between the Power of God and that of the idols (vv. 40–41), segues into a final passage on the nature of disbelief and the ends to which it leads (vv. 42–45)» [filed under 35:29, 35:30, 35:31, 35:32, 35:33, 35:34, 35:35, 35:36, 35:37, 35:38, 35:39]
