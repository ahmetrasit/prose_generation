#84 IBNJAWZI-ZAD d.597 · الفراء · reports · Alternatively, it means the whole day. «النهار كله قاله الفراء»
#85 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · Exegetes take الضحى either as the midmorning, when the sun rises and spreads its light, or as the whole daytime, contrasted with the whole night. «المراد بالضحى وقت الضحى وهو صدر النهار»
#86 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · A third explanation identifies the two suras with Abu Bakr and Muhammad and says their consecutive order shows no intermediary between them. «سورة والليل سورة أبي بكر» [filed under 93:1-2]
#87 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · One reason for reversing the night-day order across the two suras is that each has a distinct merit: night comes first, while daylight brings light; alternating them also balances the sequence. «بالليل والنهار ينتظم مصالح المكلفين» [filed under 93:1-2]
#88 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · A second explanation links the previous sura's night to Abu Bakr, whose past included disbelief, and this sura's daylight to Muhammad, who had no prior sin. «أنه تعالى قدم الليل على النهار في سورة أبي بكر» [filed under 93:1-2]
#89 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · The alternation of day and night shows that pauses in revelation follow wisdom, just as the shifting hours do not reflect divine whim or rejection. «فمرة تزداد ساعات الليل وتنقص ساعات النهار» [filed under 93:1-2]
#90 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · Because the disbelievers claimed that God abandoned and hated the Prophet, their unsupported charge is answered by a divine oath, as a claimant is asked for proof and the denier swears. «هاتوا الحجة فعجزوا فلزمه اليمين» [filed under 93:1-2]
#91 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · The alternating night and day show that neither is free from the other; likewise people should not expect exemption from hardship. «انظروا إلى جوار الليل مع النهار لا يسلم أحدهما عن الآخر بل الليل تارة يغلب وتارة يغلب» [filed under 93:1-2]
#92 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · Midmorning is when people gather and find ease after the isolation of night, so it promises revelation and comfort after the Prophet's anxiety during its pause. «أنه وقت اجتماع الناس وكمال الأنس بعد الاستيحاش في زمان الليل»
#93 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · Midmorning recalls the hour Moses spoke with God and the sorcerers prostrated; the God who honored Moses can honor Muhammad and turn his enemies' hearts. «أنها الساعة التي كلم فيها موسى ربه»
#94 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · A brief daylight hour set against the whole night suggests Muhammad's stature equals all prophets and that worldly grief lasts longer than joy; an anecdote pictures sorrow lasting centuries and joy an hour. «أن ساعة من النهار توازي جميع الليل كما أن محمدا إذا وزن يوازي جميع الأنبياء» [filed under 93:1-2]
#95 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · Busy daylight resembles resurrection and quiet night resembles the stillness of graves; life and the postmortem state are more excellent than death and what precedes it. «وقت الضحى وقت حركة الناس وتعارفهم» [filed under 93:1-2]
#96 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · Mentioning midmorning first prevents despair of God's relief, while following it with night prevents complacency about His plan. «ذكروا الضحى حتى لا يحصل اليأس من روحه» [filed under 93:1-2]
#97 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · Some exegetes take midmorning as Muhammad's face and night as his hair; others interpret them as the male and female members of his family. «فسر الضحى بوجه محمد والليل بشعره» [filed under 93:1-2]
#98 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · Other symbolic readings make midmorning Muhammad's mission and night the pause in revelation, which brought estrangement after the comfort of revelation. «ويحتمل الضحى رسالته والليل زمان احتباس الوحي» [filed under 93:1-2]
#99 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · Midmorning can symbolize the light of Muhammad's knowledge that reveals hidden matters, and night his forgiving concealment of faults. «ويحتمل والضحى نور علمه الذي به يعرف المستور من الغيوب» [filed under 93:1-2]
#100 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · The pair may symbolize Islam's initial rise from strangeness and its later return to strangeness. «ويحتمل أن الضحى إقبال الإسلام بعد أن كل غريبا والليل إشارة إلى أنه سيعود غريبا» [filed under 93:1-2]
#101 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · Another interpretation makes midmorning full intellect and night the state of death. «ويحتمل والضحى كمال العقل» [filed under 93:1-2]
#102 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · The oath may refer to Muhammad's outward and inward selves, neither of which the unseen knows to be flawed. «أقسم بعلانيتك التي لا يرى عليها الخلق عيبا» [filed under 93:1-2]
#103 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · holds · Duha means daytime here, as the verse pairs it with night; the Qur'an likewise contrasts a night attack with a daytime one. «والمراد به النهار لقوله { والليل إذا سجى } فقابله بالليل.»
#104 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · reports · Other figurative readings invoke God's worshippers at morning and night, Paradise's light against night, or believers' inner light against disbelievers' darkness. «ويقال: «والضحى. والليل إذا سجا»: يعني عباده الذين يعبدونه في وقت الضحى، وعباده الذين يعبدونه بالليل» [filed under 93:1-2]
#105 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · قتادة ومقاتل وجعفر الصادق · reports · Qatada, Muqatil, and Ja'far al-Sadiq say the oath invokes the morning when God spoke to Moses and the night of the Ascension. «وقال قتادة ومقاتل وجعفر الصادق: أقسم بالضحى الذي كلم الله فيه موسى، وبليلة المعراج.»
#106 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · reports · Another view identifies duha as the hour when the magicians fell prostrate, supported by the command to assemble people at duha. «وقيل: هي الساعة التي خر فيها السحرة سجدا. بيانه قوله تعالى:»
#107 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · أهل المعاني · reports · Semantic scholars understand an omitted oath object: the sense is by the Lord of the morning and its like. «وقال أهل المعاني فيه وفي أمثاله: فهي إضمار، مجازه ورب الضحى.»
#108 BAYDAWI-FULL d.685 · holds · The morning oath may mean the sun’s elevated hour, when daylight is strong, the time Moses spoke with God and the magicians prostrated, or daylight contrasted with nighttime. «{ والضحى } ووقت ارتفاع الشمس وتخصيصه لأن النهار يقوى فيه»
#109 NASAFI-FULL d.710 · holds · Duha is the early part of the day, when the sun has risen higher; it may be singled out because Moses spoke and the magicians fell prostrate at that hour. «المراد به وقت الضحى وهو صدر النهار حين ترتفع الشمس وإنما خص وقت الضحى بالقسم لأنها الساعة التى كلم فيها موسى عليه السلام وألقى فيها السحرة سجدا»
#110 NASAFI-FULL d.710 · reports · Alternatively, duha denotes the whole day, paired with night in the following oath. «أو النهار كله لمقابلته بالليل فى قوله»
#111 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · holds · The surah is Meccan and follows the previous mention of the most pious, the Prophet, by recounting God's favors to him. «هذه السورة مكية. ولما ذكر فيما قبلها وسيجنبها الأتقى» [filed under 93:1, 93:2, 93:3]
#112 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم · reports · After the Prophet did not rise for one or two nights while ill, a woman accused his devil of abandoning him; the Prophet's report says the opening verses were then revealed. «فقالت: يا محمد، ما أرى شيطانك إلا قد تركك.» [filed under 93:1, 93:2, 93:3]
#113 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · مشركون · reports · In another version, Gabriel's delay led the polytheists to say Muhammad had been abandoned, and the verses were revealed in response. «قال: أبطأ جبريل على رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم، فقال المشركون: ودع محمد.» [filed under 93:1, 93:2, 93:3]
#114 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · reports · Another narration says a stone injured the Prophet's finger, after which he stopped night worship for two or three nights and a woman made the same accusation; Ibn Kathir calls the injury report authentic but the causal link unusual. «رمي رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم بحجر في أصبعه فقال:» [filed under 93:1, 93:2, 93:3]
#115 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · ابن إسحاق · reports · Ibn Ishaq says the woman who made the accusation was Umm Jamil, the wife of Abu Lahab. «قيل: إن هذه المرأة هي: أم جميل امرأة أبي لهب،» [filed under 93:1, 93:2, 93:3]
#116 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · holds · God swears by the forenoon and its light, and by night when it settles, darkens, and grows still. «وهذا قسم منه تعالى بالضحى وما جعل فيه من الضياء» [filed under 93:1, 93:2]
#117 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · holds · The contrasting light of day and darkness of night visibly demonstrate the Creator's power. «وذلك دليل ظاهر على قدرة خالق هذا وهذا» [filed under 93:1, 93:2]
#118 BURHAN d.794 · holds · Zarkashi: al-ʿAsr is counted as a verse by analogy with al-Fajr and al-Duha for the shared rhyme, though their lengths differ. «للمناسبة لكن تفاوتت فى الكمية» [filed under 103:1, 93:1, 105:1]
#119 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · The surah’s purpose is to show that the most God-fearing person remains in divine favor in both lives through perfected qualities that lead to the goal. «مقصودها الدلالة على آخر الليل بأن أتقى الأتقياء الذي هو الأتقى على الإطلاق»
#120 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · The title al-Ḍuḥā captures this purpose because its physical daylight symbolizes the spiritual light of perfected character. «التي هي الإيصال للمقصود بما لها من النور المعنوي كالضحى بما له من النور الحسي»
#121 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · The oath by midmorning, the daylight’s finest and brightest portion, suits the most honored creature and signals his exceptional rank. «فذكر ماهو أشرف النهار وألطفه وهو زهرته»
#122 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · The alternation of mixed and pure light and darkness can represent Qur’anic clear and ambiguous teachings and human spiritual conditions. «ويمكن أن يكون الأول مشيرا إلى ما يأتي به هذا الرسول صلى الله عليه وسلم من المحكم» [filed under 93:1, 93:2]
#123 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · The heart’s light can overcome the soul’s darkness, while appetite can darken the whole person; spirit or temperament then elevates or degrades the person. «فروحه نور بنور القلب , فإن قويت شهوة النفس على نورانية القلب أظلم جميعه» [filed under 93:1, 93:2]
