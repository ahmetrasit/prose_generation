#205 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The verse implies that even people with true faith incur a kind of loss if they do not perform righteous deeds and counsel truth and patience. «Hakk’a iman etmekle beraber sâlih ameller yapmayan ve hak ve sabır tavsiye etmeyen kimseler de bir nevi hüsrandan hâriç kalmamış olurlar» [filed under 103:2, 103:3]
#206 ELMALILI d.1361 · Mürcienin · rejects · The Murji'a claim that lack of action causes no loss once faith exists is false; Ahl al-Sunna correctly holds that sinful, disobedient believers also incur a kind of loss. «“iman olunca amelsizlik ziyan etmez” diyen Mürcienin kavilleri hak değildir. Doğrusu ehl-i sünnetin dedikleri gibi imanı olup da imanına göre âmil olmayan fâsıklar, âsîler için de bir nevi hüsran» [filed under 103:2, 103:3]
#207 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Although faith ultimately saves sinful believers, those whose sins outweigh their good deeds undergo Hell's loss until their sins are cleansed. «seyyiâtı hasenâtından ağır gelen mü’minler de günahları temizlenene kadar cehennem azâbı olan hüsrânı göreceklerdir»
#208 ELMALILI d.1361 · Ebû Medîne · reports · He reports that two of the Prophet's Companions would not part after meeting until they recited Sūrat al-ʿAṣr to each other and then exchanged greetings. «Ebû Medîne’den[^57]-ki sohbeti vardır- şöyle dediğini rivayet eylemişlerdir» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#209 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Only those with faith, righteous works, mutual counsel in truth, and mutual counsel in patience are exceptions to humanity's otherwise absolute loss; all four express complete faith. «ancak böyle iman, sâlih ameller, tevâsî bi’l-hak, tevâsî bi’s-sabr dört vasıf ile muttasıf olan kimseler hüsranda değil, müstesnâdırlar» [filed under 103:2, 103:3]
#210 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The rendering says: By the age; humankind is in absolute loss, except those who believe, do righteous works, counsel one another to truth, and counsel patience. «Kasem olsun ki asra (1) insan mutlak bir hüsranda (2)» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#211 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Afternoon evokes humanity's creation late in the world's day, the closing period of human life, and the sorrow of returning without gain; this complements the previous surah's warning about rivalry. «dünyada bir gün mesabesinde demek olan insan ömrünün de son demlerini andırdığı» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#212 ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · Ibn Jarir transmits Ali's interpretive recitation: by time and its calamities, humankind remains in loss until time's end. «İbn Cerîr’in rivayet ettiği vechile Hazret-i Ali radıyallâhu anh’tan وَالْعَصْرِ وَنـَوَائِبِ الدَّهْرِ» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#213 ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · This reading interprets asr as dehr, whose recurring calamities and changes warn people and whose loss continues to the end; squeezing or confinement may also reinforce the loss side. «İbn Cerîr’in rivayet ettiği vechile Hazret-i Ali radıyallâhu anh’tan» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#214 ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · Another view says dehr contains every wonder that displays the Creator's power, where ordinary and extraordinary events, blessings, disasters, gains, and losses unfold. «Demek ki “asr”, dehr mânasınadır ve dehre kasemin hikmeti de onun insana» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#215 QUTB-ZILAL d.1386 · holds · The surah is Meccan, has three verses, and compresses Islam's full way of life and the Muslim community's identity and role into a brief constitution. «سورة العصر مكية وآياتها ثلاث» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#216 QUTB-ZILAL d.1386 · holds · The surah presents the Muslim community's essence and function in its final verse, a concise form of inimitable divine expression. «وتصف الأمة المسلمة : حقيقتها ووظيفتها . في آية واحدة هي الآية الثالثة من السورة» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#217 QUTB-ZILAL d.1386 · holds · Across all ages there is one successful path: faith, righteous action, and mutual exhortation to truth and patience; every other way ends in loss. «ليس هنالك إلا منهج واحد رابح ، وطريق واحد ناج . هو ذلك المنهج الذي ترسم السورة حدوده» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#218 QUTB-ZILAL d.1386 · holds · Qutb sees worldly loss spreading because humanity turns away from God's good and lacks righteous authority; he says Muslims who claim Islam have strayed furthest from its guidance. «يهولنا أن نرى الخسر يحيق بالبشرية في كل مكان على ظهر الأرض بلا استثناء» [filed under 103:2, 103:3]
#219 QUTB-ZILAL d.1386 · holds · Earthly profit and loss are small beside the lasting stakes of Paradise and God's pleasure; the final loss is the collapse of human dignity. «هناك الربح والخسر : ربح الجنة والرضوان ، أو خسر الجنة والرضوان» [filed under 103:2, 103:3]
#220 QUTB-ZILAL d.1386 · holds · The surah decisively sets one route out of loss: faith, righteous work, and a community that mutually guards truth with patience. «طريق واحد لا يتعدد . طريق الإيمان والعمل الصالح وقيام الجماعة المسلمة» [filed under 103:2, 103:3]
#221 QUTB-ZILAL d.1386 · الرجلان من أصحاب رسول الله · reports · Qutb reports that two Companions would not part after meeting until one recited Surat al-Asr to the other, renewing their commitment to its guidance. «كان الرجلان من أصحاب رسول الله [ صلى الله عليه وسلم ] إذا التقيا لم يتفرقا حتى يقرأ أحدهما على الآخر سورة» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#222 QUTB-ZILAL d.1386 · holds · Qutb treats mutual exhortation to truth and patience as defining the Muslim community's distinct identity and bond. «أما التواصي بالحق والتواصي بالصبر فتبرز من خلالها صورة الأمة المسلمة - أو الجماعة المسلمة - ذات الكيان الخاص» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]

#228 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The exception completes the division into those who lose and those untouched by loss; believers who do good avoid loss if they leave no good undone by committing its opposite, or repent. «فالذين آمنوا وعملوا الصالحات لا يلحقهم الخسران بحال إذا لم يتركوا شيئا من الصالحات» [filed under 103:2, 103:3]
#229 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Loss has degrees: eternal loss comes from denying God's oneness and the Messenger; lesser loss varies with evil deeds, and minor sins may be forgiven when major sins are avoided, as in 11:114. «فأعظمه وخالده الخسر المنجر عن انتفاء الإيمان بوحدانية الله وصدق الرسول» [filed under 103:2, 103:3]
#230 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The loss is in the future life, as the warning context indicates, regardless of worldly fortunes; compare 3:196–197. «أي لفي خسر في الحياة الأبدية الآخرة فلا التفات إلى أحوال الناس في الحياة الدنيا»
#231 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The indefinite khusr can indicate kinds of loss or intensify and universalize it for dramatic effect. «وتنكير { خسر } يجوز أن يكون للتنويع»
#232 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Al-ṣāliḥāt is generic yet comprehensive of every commanded good deed, including avoiding sins; the exception's relative clause states why they are spared loss. «والتعريف في قوله : { الصالحات } تعريف الجنس مراد به الاستغراق» [filed under 103:2, 103:3]
#233 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The surah condenses Quranic teachings and gathers its aims in the briefest statement; although it could be Meccan or Medinan, it seems more likely Meccan. «تخلص السورة جميع المعارف القرآنية وتجمع شتات مقاصد القرآن في أوجز بيان» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#234 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The indefinite khusr either magnifies the loss or denotes a distinct kind, beyond financial and status losses; the Quran calls loss of self and family at Judgment manifest loss. «والتنكير في { خسر } للتعظيم ويحتمل التنويع أي في نوع من الخسر غير الخسارات المالية والجاهية»
#235 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · Al-insan means humankind generally; khusr is loss of capital, whether attributed to a person or to an action such as trade. «المراد بالإنسان جنسه، والخسر والخسران والخسار والخسارة نقص رأس المال»
#236 TABATABAI d.1402 · الراغب · reports · Raghib notes that loss is attributed either to a person, as in someone losing, or to an action, as in a trade losing. «قال الراغب وينسب ذلك إلى الانسان فيقال خسر فلان وإلى الفعل فيقال خسرت تجارته»
#237 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · Earthly life is a test that determines the felicity or misery of the enduring afterlife. «ويبين أن شطرا من هذه الحياة وهي الحياة الدنيا حياة امتحانية تتعين بها صفة الشطر الأخير» [filed under 103:2, 103:3]
