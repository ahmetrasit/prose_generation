#405 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · Resûlullah · reports · A hadith cited for that reading says Joseph would not have stayed so long had he not asked someone other than God for release. «Yûsuf bu sözü söylememiş olsaydı, zindanda bu kadar uzun süre kalmazdı»
#406 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · other commentators · rejects · Other commentators consider that reading and its supporting hadith weak. «Gerek bu yorum gerekse delil olarak getirilen bu hadis, diğer müfessirler tarafından zayıf kabul edilmiştir (İbn Kesîr, IV, 317)»
#407 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · many commentators · prefers · Many prefer that Satan made the released prisoner forget to tell the king Joseph’s situation; prophets may seek human help without forgetting God, and 12:45 supports this reading. «Müfessirlerin birçoğu bu mânayı tercih etmişlerdir»
#408 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · reports · Reports give Joseph’s prison stay as five, seven, twelve, or fourteen years. «Hz. Yûsuf’un zindanda kaldığı süre hakkında beş, yedi, on iki veya on dört yıl şeklinde farklı rivayetler vardır»
#409 STUDYQURAN · Kl · reports · Joseph asked the wine carrier to mention him to Egypt’s king. «Joseph told the wine carrier to mention him to the king of Egypt (Kl; cf. Genesis 40:14–15)»
#410 STUDYQURAN · Kl · reports · Satan made the wine carrier forget Joseph, prolonging Joseph’s imprisonment. «Satan caused him to forget to make mention to his lord; that is, he forgot to mention Joseph to the king (Kl)»
#411 STUDYQURAN · IK and N · reports · Most commentators say Joseph remained imprisoned for seven years. «a total of seven years according to most commentators (IK, N)»
#412 STUDYQURAN · Kl and Q · reports · An alternative translation says Satan made Joseph forget to remember God, meaning Joseph forgot God when asking the carrier to mention him. «Satan caused him to forget remembering his Lord,” in which case it would mean that at the particular moment when Joseph told the wine carrier»
#413 STUDYQURAN · Ibn ʿAbbās · reports · Ibn ʿAbbās says Joseph would have been released earlier had he remembered God when asking the wine carrier to mention him. «According to Ibn ʿAbbās, had Joseph remembered God when he told the wine carrier»
#414 STUDYQURAN · the Prophet · reports · The Prophet prayed for Joseph and said his request to be mentioned to the king resulted in a longer imprisonment. «the Prophet is reported to have said, “God have mercy on Joseph. Had it not been for a statement he made»
#415 STUDYQURAN · holds · Satan makes evil deeds seem fair, causes people to forget God’s commands, and even attempts to alter revelation. «causing them to forget God and His commandments (6:68; 12:42; 18:63; 58:19); and even by attempting to alter revelation (22:52).» [filed under 7:16, 6:43, 8:48, 16:63, 27:24, 29:38, 6:68, 12:42, 18:63, 58:19, 22:52]
