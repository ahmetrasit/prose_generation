#395 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · عكرمة · reports · Ikrima reports that Quraysh nobles asked Abu Talib to have Muhammad expel poor slaves and allies so they would follow him; Umar suggested doing so temporarily, and verses warning and identifying the grateful were revealed, after which Umar apologized. «مشى عتبة وشيبة أبنا ربيعة وقرظة بن عبد عمرو»
#396 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · عكرمة · reports · The report names the poor believers as Bilal, ʿAmmar, Salim, Subayh, Ibn Masʿud, al-Miqdad, Waqid, ʿAmr, and Marthad, with allies and freedmen among them. «وكانوا بلالا وعمار بن ياسر وسالم مولى»
#397 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · عكرمة · reports · The report says the verse 'Thus We have tried some of them by others' addressed Quraysh leaders and their clients; when it came, Umar apologized, and 'When those who believe in Our signs come to you' was revealed. «وكذلك فتنا بعضهم ببعض»
#398 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · Al-ghadah derives from ghudwah with the waw changing to alif; al-ashi derives from ashwa with waw changing to ya and assimilation. The author says al-ashi appears singular like ashiyyah, though some call it a plural. «وأصل العشي عشوى قلبت الواو ياء»
#399 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · Some say al-ashi is a plural of ashiyyah, but the author finds this far-fetched; morning means early morning or the time from dawn prayer until sunrise, and evening means the end of the day. «هو جمع عشية وفيه بعد ومعنى الأول لغة البكرة»
#400 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · Morning and evening can denote continual practice, or the acts of supplication, prayer, remembrance, or Qur'an recitation; the temporal terms can stand for what occurs at those times, and prayer can also mean its time or place. «والمراد بالدعاء حقيقته أو الصلاة أو الذكر أو قراءة القرآن أقوال»
#401 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · مجاهد · reports · Mujahid interprets morning and evening as the dawn and afternoon prayers; the times are named for the prayers, which are singled out for their honor. «عن مجاهد أنهما عبارة عن صلاتي الصبح والعصر»
#402 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · ابن عامر · reports · Ibn ʿAmir, also in al-Kahf, and several other readers recite الغدوة with a wāw; Abu ʿUbayd calls that reading an error because he treats ghudwah as a proper name that cannot take the article. «ابن عامر هنا وفي الكهف»
#403 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · rejects · The author rejects Abu Ubayd criticism: ghudwah is also attested as a common noun with the article, as reported from al-Khalil by Sibawayh, al-Mubarrad, and other language authorities; the transmitted reading itself suffices as evidence. «منكرا مصروفا فتدخلها أل حينئذ»
#404 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The verse does not establish that the Prophet actually expelled anyone; reports say he considered assigning separate times to the devoted poor and Quraysh leaders to draw the latter toward faith, without humiliating the believers. «والذي تحكيه اة ثار أنه عليه الصلاة والسلام هم أن يجعل لاولئك الداعين المتقين وقتا خاصا»
#405 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · المشهور · reports · The common interpretation takes God's face as His essence, hence the believers seek Him sincerely; the author notes that this interpretation must explain how God can be willed if will attaches only to possible things. «فقيل وهو المشهور إنه الذات أي مريدين ذاته تعالى»
#406 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · الزجاج · reports · Al-Zajjaj interprets God's face as the direction or path He commands them to seek. «المراد به الجهة والطريق والمعنى مريدين الطريق»
#407 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · الإمام · reports · Another interpretation takes seeking God's face as a metaphor for love and seeking His approval; the author says this account is questionable. «إنه كناية عن المحبة وطلب الرضا»
#408 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · بعضهم · reports · The word 'face' may be used for honor or emphasis, as in expressions such as 'the face of an argument'; the believers seek God in this sense. «هذا وجه الرأي وهذا وجهه الدليل والمعنى يريدونه»
#409 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · عطاء · reports · The plural pronoun in 'their account' refers to the poor believers, as reported from ʿAṭāʾ and most commentators; grammatically, من is an added marker of comprehensiveness, and the clause interrupts the prohibition to support it. «ضمير الجمع للموصول السابق كما روي عن عطاء وغالب المفسرين»
#410 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The believers' faith and deeds are not the Prophet's concern to judge; he must apply rulings to outward appearances and leave hidden accounts to God, against the taunts resembling Noah's people's complaint. «وإنما وظيفتك حسبما هو شأن منصب الرسالة النظر إلى ظواهر الأمور»
#411 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · ابن زيد · reports · Ibn Zayd interprets the account as their provision: their poverty would not harm the Prophet, so it cannot justify expelling them. «وروي عن ابن زيد أن المعنى ما عليك شيء من حساب رزقهم»
#412 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · الزمخشري · reports · Al-Zamakhshari reads the two reciprocal denials as one thought like 'no bearer bears another's burden'; the author objects that this is not a fitting reading of the Qur'anic wording. «ان الجملتين في معنى جملة واحدة تؤدي مؤدى»
#413 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · Fronting 'upon you' in both clauses may honor the Prophet or emphasize that the believers' account is not exclusively his responsibility. «للتشريف له عليه أشرف الصلاة وأفضل السلام»
#414 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · ابن عباس · reports · Some commentators, including a report from Ibn Abbas, take the plural pronoun to mean the idolaters: the Prophet is not accountable for them, so concern for their conversion cannot prompt him to expel believers. «وروي ذلك عن ابن عباس رضي الله تعالى عنهما والمعنى إنك لا تؤاخذ بحسابهم»
#415 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The pronoun in 'then you would expel them' refers to the believers under either reading; the subjunctive presents expulsion as a consequence ruled out by the preceding denial. «للمؤمنين على كل حال والفعل منصوب»
#416 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · الإمام والزمخشري · reports · Al-Razi and al-Zamakhshari allow 'then you would be among the wrongdoers' to coordinate with the preceding consequence, since expelling the believers would cause wrongdoing. «على وجه التسبب لأن الكون ظالما معلول طردهم»
#417 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The author answers that injustice through expulsion ceases when expulsion is negated, so the coordinated clauses share the same cause for their subjunctive form. «فانتفاء الظلم بالطرد يتوقف على ذلك أيضا فيلزم من الانتفاء الانتفاء»
#418 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · بعضهم · reports · An objection says the coordination implies that expulsion would not be wrong if the Prophet were accountable for them; the reply reads the denial as saying that lack of responsibility is why expulsion would wrong them. «واعترض أيضا بأن العطف مؤذن بأن عدم الظلم لعدم تفويض الحساب اليه»
#419 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · الدر المصون · reports · A further parsing makes the first result clause answer the prohibition, but the author finds the literal repetition 'do not expel them and then you would expel them' untenable. «ولا تطردهم فتطردهم وهو كما ترى»
#420 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · rejects · The second denial cannot govern the result if that would imply that expulsion is proper whenever the Prophet bears their account; because that meaning is impossible, the first denial governs. «إذ يلزم المعنى حينئذ أنه لو كان عليهم شيء من حسابه عليه الصلاة والسلام كان طرده إياهم حسنا»
#421 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The warning that he would be among wrongdoers means wronging himself or those believers, or becoming characterized by injustice. «فالمراد فتكون من الظالمين لأنفسهم أو لأولئك المؤمنين»
#422 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The Prophet must not expel people who worship their Lord morning and evening, continually seeking God's presence and favor with sincere, exclusive devotion. «sabah akşam, yani her zaman Rablerine dua ve ibadet ederler»
#423 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Neither their reckoning belongs to the Prophet nor his reckoning to them; he must not expel them on the pretext of accounting for them. «Onların hesaplarından hiçbir şey sana ait değil»
#424 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Expelling these worshippers would make the Prophet one of the wrongdoers. «ki zâlimlerden olursun.»
#425 ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · Elmalılı reports that Quraysh leaders asked Muhammad to remove poor Muslims such as Suhayb, Khabbāb, Bilāl, ʿAmmār, and Salmān so they might visit; after Umar urged trying it and they pressed for a written promise, the verse was revealed and the Prophet discarded the page. «Rivayet olunuyor ki Kureyş’in ileri gelenlerinden birtakımları Hazret-i Peygamber’e uğramışlar»
#426 ELMALILI d.1361 · Selmân ve Habbâb · reports · Salmān and Khabbāb say the verse concerned them: the Prophet sat close with them and would not leave before they did; after 18:28, he stayed with them and thanked God for the command. «radıyallahu anhümâ demişlerdir ki: “Bu âyet»
#427 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · God tests one group through another: arrogant elites scorn poor, sincere believers as undeserving, while faith and sincerity are divine gifts that challenge worldly pride and status. «bir fitne ile yani tard ü istihkāra şâyan zannedilen fukara ve zu‘afâyı iman ve ihlâs ile memnun ve muazzez etmek gibi bir imtihan iledir»
#428 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The command coordinates with the warning in 6:51: the Prophet is to stay with those fearing resurrection despite the arrogance of leading polytheists. «عطف على قوله : { وأنذر به الذين يخافون أن يحشروا إلى ربهم }»
#429 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The renewed relative description suits the ruling better than a pronoun: those who continually invoke God sincerely deserve the Prophet's company as callers to God. «فإن النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم جاء داعيا إلى الله فأولى الناس بملازمته»
#430 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · سعد بن أبي وقاص · reports · In Muslim's report, six companions were with the Prophet when polytheists asked him to expel them; Sa'd names himself, Ibn Mas'ud, a Hudhayli, Bilal, and two unnamed men, after which the verse was revealed. «روى مسلم عن سعد بن أبي وقاص قال : كنا مع النبيء ستة نفر»
