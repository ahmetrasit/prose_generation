#94 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · müfessirler · reports · On interpretation A, neither their number nor duration is stated by God; verse 26 says only God knows, as the youths themselves said in verse 19. «Buna göre Allah, Ashâb-ı Kehf’in sayısını da mağarada kaldıkları süreyi de bildirmemiştir.» [filed under 18:25, 18:26]
#95 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · prefers · The author prefers interpretation A because the ordering of the verses and their mutually reinforcing indications support it. «Bize göre, âyetlerin sıralanışı ve birbirini tamamlayan işaretleri bu yorumun isabetli olduğunu gösteriyor.» [filed under 18:25, 18:26]
#96 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · müfessirler · reports · Interpretation B takes verse 25 as God’s own statement, meaning the youths stayed in the cave 309 years. «25. âyet Allah’ın sözüdür. Bu takdirde Ashâb-ı Kehf mağarada üç yüz dokuz yıl kalmışlardır.»
#97 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · bir tefsir · reports · One commentary says the People of the Book also gave 300 years; the extra nine may reconcile their solar-year count with the Arabs’ lunar-year count of 309. «Ehl-i kitap da Ashâb-ı Kehf’in mağarada üç yüzyıl uyuduklarını söylemiştir.» [filed under 18:25, 18:26]
#98 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · müfessirler · reports · On interpretation B, verse 26 settles disagreement by specifying the duration and stressing that God knows it best, since He knows all hidden things and rules without partner. «26. âyet onların mağarada kaldıkları süreyi belirterek insanların bu konudaki ihtilâflarını ortadan kaldırmış ve Allah’ın konuyu herkesten daha iyi bildiğini vurgulamış olur.» [filed under 18:25, 18:26]
#99 QIRAAT-ER · holds · The standard reading has tanwīn on miʾatin in an unusual construct, with sinīn as apposition or explanatory specification. «Standard reading with tanwīn on miʾatin — thalātha and miʾatin are in construct»
#100 QIRAAT-ER · holds · Without tanwīn, miʾati is mudāf and sinīn its genitive complement, forming a tighter unit. «Without tanwīn on miʾati — standard iḍāfa construction where miʾati is mudāf and sinīn is mudāf ilayhi»
#101 QIRAAT-ER · holds · The form miyatin drops hamza as a dialectal pronunciation without semantic change. «Variant without hamza in miʾa — dialectal pronunciation miyah instead of miʾah»
#102 STUDYQURAN · some commentators · reports · Some say the 309 years report the People of the Book’s reckoning, not necessarily the youths’ actual time in the cave; verse 26 then reserves knowledge to God. «said by some to be God’s informing the Prophet of the number of years the People of the Book say they remained in the cave» [filed under 18:25, 18:26]
#103 STUDYQURAN · others · reports · Others take 309 years as the youths’ actual sleep in the cave and understand God knows best to concern the interval after their death, shortly after discovery. «Others claim that this figure represents the actual number of years the youths slept in the cave» [filed under 18:25, 18:26]
#104 STUDYQURAN · still others · reports · Still others say 309 is the actual duration and settles the dispute about how long they remained. «Still others suggest that the figure of 309 years represents the actual number of years in the cave» [filed under 18:25, 18:26]
#105 STUDYQURAN · some commentators · reports · Some explain 300 years by the solar calendar plus nine years to express the same period in the lunar calendar used by the Prophet and Arabs. «300 years represents the amount of time they slept in the cave according to the solar calendar, and another 9 years would have to be added»
#106 STUDYQURAN · holds · The youths' centuries-long sleep represents an intermediate realm between life and death, analogous to the state between individual death and universal resurrection. «their sleep represents an intermediate realm between life and death» [filed under 18:9, 18:11, 18:12, 18:25]
