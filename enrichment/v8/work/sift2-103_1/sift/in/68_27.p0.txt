# 50 notes filed under 68:27
verse 68:27: بَلْ نَحْنُ مَحْرُومُونَ

#1 MUQATIL d.150 · holds · Recognizing the landmarks, they realized it was punishment and admitted they had been deprived. «ثم أنهم عرفوا الأعلام فعلموا أنها عقوبة. فقالو: { بل نحن } يعنى ولكن نحن { محرومون } [آية: 27]»
#2 TAB-FULL d.310 · holds · Some brothers first think they lost the way; another recognizes their own garden and says its benefit has been denied them. «أغفلوا طريق جنتهم، وأن التي رأوا غيرها» [filed under 68:26, 68:27]
#3 TAB-FULL d.310 · قتادة · reports · Qatāda says they had been repaid by being deprived of their garden. «فقال بعضهم: {بل نحن محرومون} [القلم: 27] حرمنا جنتنا»
#4 TAB-FULL d.310 · holds · The garden’s owners were deprived of its benefit when its crop was destroyed. «حرمنا منفعة جنتنا بذهاب حرثها»
#5 ZAJJAJ d.311 · holds · They were deprived of the garden’s fruit because they had withheld it from the poor. «أي حرمنا ثمر جنتنا بمنعنا المساكين.»
#6 NAHHAS d.338 · قيل · reports · Someone recognizing the garden said they had not lost the way but had been deprived of its fruit. «فقال من يعرفها ويعلم أنهم لم يضلوا الطريق ( بل نحن محرومون ) أي حرمنا ثمارها»
#7 THALABI d.427 · holds · At first they thought they had lost their way; then one said they had instead been deprived of the garden's benefit for excluding the poor and failing to make an exception. «فلما رأوها قالوا إنا لضالون لمخطئوا الطريق فليس هذه بجنتنا» [filed under 68:26-27]
#8 MAWARDI-FULL d.450 · قتادة · reports · They realized they were deprived as punishment for their wrongdoing. «أي حرمنا خير جنتنا , قال قتادة : معناه جوزينا فحرمنا .»
#9 QUSHAYRI d.465 · holds · Seeing the orchard razed, they first denied it was theirs, then recognized it and realized they had been deprived of its good. «قالوا : ليست هذه جنتنا ! ! ثم قالوا : بل هذه جنتنا» [filed under 68:26-27]
#10 WAHIDI-BASIT d.468 · المفسرون · reports · The exegetes explain that they had lost their garden's fruit because they had barred the poor. «أي: حرمنا ثمر جنتنا بمنعنا المساكين (¬5). هذا معنى قول المفسرين.»
#11 WAHIDI-BASIT d.468 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas says they first thought they had lost their way because they did not recognize the burned garden. «قال ابن عباس: قالوا: إنا أخطأنا الطريق (¬6). وذلك أنهم أنكروها لأنها احترقت.»
#12 WAHIDI-WAJIZ d.468 · holds · Seeing it black and burned, they first thought they had lost their way, then realized God had deprived them of their fruit because they withheld it from the poor. «{ فلما رأوها } سوداء محترقة { قالوا إنا لضالون } مخطئون طريقنا، وليست هذه جنتنا» [filed under 68:26-27]
#13 JISHUMI d.494 · holds · They realize this is their garden but have been deprived of its benefit because they denied the needy their due and failed to make an exception. «بل نحن محرومون يعني هذه جنتنا، ولكن حرمنا نفعها وخيرها لمنع المساكين حقهم»
#14 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · holds · Seeing the burned garden, they first thought they had lost their way, then realized they were deprived because they denied the poor and omitted the exception. «{فلما رأوها قالوا إنا لضالون} أي لما رأوا الجنة محترقة قالوا: إنا لمخطئون الطريق» [filed under 68:26, 68:27]
#15 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · holds · Seeing the burned orchard, they first thought they had lost the way; once they recognized it, they said they were deprived of its yield and blessing. «فلما رأوها أي محترقة حسبوا أنهم قد ضلوا الطريق، وأنها ليست تلك» [filed under 68:26, 68:27]
#16 TABRISI d.548 · قتادة · reports · On seeing the ruined garden, they first thought they had lost their way and this was not their orchard. «ضللنا عن الطريق فليس هذا بستاننا عن قتادة» [filed under 68:26-27]
#17 TABRISI d.548 · reports · Another interpretation is that they had strayed from the right path and were punished for their decision; they then recognized their garden but were deprived of its benefit for denying the poor their share and failing to make an exception. «إنا لضالون عن الحق في أمرنا فلذلك عوقبنا بذهاب ثمر جنتنا» [filed under 68:26-27]
#18 IBNJAWZI-ZAD d.597 · holds · They realized they had been deprived of the garden's fruit because they had denied it to the poor. «أي حرمنا ثمر جنتنا بمنعنا المسكين»
#19 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · They may first have thought they had lost their way on seeing the burned orchard; after recognizing it, they said instead that they were deprived because their resolve to be miserly had brought this loss. «لما رأوا جنتهم محترقة ظنوا أنهم قد ضلوا الطريق» [filed under 68:26, 68:27]
#20 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · Alternatively, they called themselves lost because they had resolved to bar the poor and assumed they could benefit from the orchard, but realized they were the deprived ones. «حيث كنا عازمين على منع الفقراء» [filed under 68:26, 68:27]
#21 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · holds · They conclude that they have been deprived of their garden because of what they did. «أي حرمنا جنتنا بما صنعنا»
#22 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم · reports · A report from Ibn Masud warns that sin may deprive someone of provision prepared for him, and the Prophet recites the garden passage. «إياكم والمعاصي إن العبد ليذنب الذنب فيحرم به رزقا كان هيىء له» [filed under 68:26-27]
#23 BAYDAWI-FULL d.685 · holds · After recognizing the garden, they admitted they had instead been deprived through their own wrongdoing. «{ بل نحن } أي بعد ما تأملوه وعرفوا أنها هي قالوا { بل نحن } { محرومون }»
#24 NASAFI-FULL d.710 · holds · They then realized their deprivation was the result of their own wrongdoing. «حرمنا خيرها لجنايتنا على أنفسنا»
#25 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · قتادة · reports · At first they thought they had taken the wrong road; after recognizing the garden, they understood that God's punishment had destroyed its bounty. «قالوا إنا لضالون: أي عن الطريق إليها، قاله قتادة.» [filed under 68:26, 68:27]
#26 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · reports · Another interpretation is that they were mistaken in their plan to harvest while intending to deny the poor; “we are deprived” recognizes that their treachery rebounded on them. «بل نحن محرومون خيرها بخيانتنا على أنفسنا.» [filed under 68:26, 68:27]
#27 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas and others say the brothers first thought they had lost their way, then recognized the orchard and realized they had been deprived of its produce. «قاله ابن عباس وغيره. ثم رجعوا عما كانوا فيه، وتيقنوا أنها هي فقالوا: {بل نحن محرومون}» [filed under 68:26, 68:27]
#28 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · They then recognized that they were deprived of the expected good because God took it in response to their intention to deprive the poor; God does not change a people until they change themselves. «الله لا يغير ما بقوم حتى يغيروا ما بأنفسهم»
#29 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · A calamity softens and leads to repentance for one whom God intends to guide, but increases another person's disbelief. «وما كان القرع بالمصائب مظنة الرقة والتوبة لمن أريد به الخير»
#30 DURR-FULL d.911 · قتادة · reports · Qatada says they first thought they had taken the wrong road, then recognized the garden and realized they had been deprived. «عن قتادة في قوله: {إنا لضالون}» [filed under 68:26-27]
#31 DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن جريج · reports · Ibn Jurayj says after recognizing the garden's landmarks, they called themselves deprived and afflicted by misfortune. «عن ابن جريج في قوله»
#32 DURR-FULL d.911 · قتادة · reports · Asked whether the garden owners were people of Paradise or Hell, Qatada says the question burdened him and gives no answer. «عن معمر قال: قلنا لقتادة»
#33 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · They first thought they had mistaken the place, then realized they were deprived because of their wrongful intention to bar the poor. «قالوه بعد ما تأملوها ووقفوا على حقيقة الأمر» [filed under 68:26-27]
#34 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · After examining the scene, they retract their first guess: they were not lost but deprived of the garden's good through their own wrongdoing. «قالوه بعدما تأملوا ووقفوا على حقيقة الأمر مضربين عن قولهم الأول»
#35 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · They first thought they had lost their way, then recognized punishment and deprivation greater than what they had planned for the poor, and despaired of present and future. «evvelâ yalnız dalâletlerine hükmettiler, sonra da cezâya uğradıklarına, men‘etmek istedikleri miskinden ziyade mahrum bırakıldıklarına hükmettiler» [filed under 68:26, 68:27]
#36 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · بل shifts to the more serious point: they had planned to deprive the poor of surplus, but in fact they themselves lost all the fruit. «و { بل نحن محرومون } إضراب للانتقال إلى ما هو أهم»
#37 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Fronting the explicit pronoun نحن, where the passive's subject could remain implicit, signals an additional restriction: they are the ones truly deprived. «بأن أتي به ضميرا بارزا مع أن مقتضى الظاهر أن يكون ضميرا مستترا»
#38 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · On seeing the ruined orchard, they first say they have gone astray from right conduct in trying to harvest and exclude the poor; then they correct themselves: they have been deprived of the crop. «عن الصواب في غدونا إليها بقصد الصرم ومنع المساكين» [filed under 68:26, 68:27]
#39 ASAD-NOTES d.1412 · holds · Asad places the surah probably third in revelation order and calls it one of the oldest Qur'anic portions. «In the chronological order of revelation, this surah most probably occupies the third place» [filed under 68:1-52]
#40 ASAD-NOTES d.1412 · Suyuti · reports · Suyuti holds that the surah followed immediately after the first five verses of surah 96; Asad says this conflicts with the best-authenticated reports that most of surah 74 came second. «Some authorities - among them Suyuti - incline to the view that it was revealed immediately after the first five verses of surah 96» [filed under 68:1-52]
