#220 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · Reciters vary on wariqikum: many use a still rāʾ, Abu Rajaʾ and others use kasra on wāw with assimilation, a form with kasra on rāʾ is defended as rare Arabic, al-Zajjaj reports a non-assimilated form, and ʿAli reads bawariqikum as a collective noun. «وقرأ أبو عمرو وحمزة وأبو بكر والحسن والأعمش واليزيدي ويعقوب في رواية وخلف»
