# 191 notes filed under 6:52 (group 2 of 3 of this verse's notes)
verse 6:52: وَلَا تَطْرُدِ ٱلَّذِينَ يَدْعُونَ رَبَّهُم بِٱلْغَدَوٰةِ وَٱلْعَشِىِّ يُرِيدُونَ وَجْهَهُۥ ۖ مَا عَلَيْكَ مِنْ حِسَابِهِم مِّن شَىْءٍۢ وَمَا مِنْ حِسَابِكَ عَلَيْهِم مِّن شَىْءٍۢ فَتَطْرُدَهُمْ فَتَكُونَ مِنَ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

#1 IBNJAWZI-ZAD d.597 · أبو جعفر · reports · Another interpretation is learning the Qur'an morning and evening. «والرابع أنه تعلم القرآن غدوة وعشية قاله أبو جعفر»
#2 IBNJAWZI-ZAD d.597 · الزجاج · reports · Another interpretation is praying to God with monotheism, sincerity, and worship. «والخامس أنه دعاء الله بالتوحيد والإخلاص له وعبادته قاله الزجاج»
#3 IBNJAWZI-ZAD d.597 · ابن عامر · reports · Most readers say bil-ghadah; Ibn Amir reads bil-ghudwah here and in al-Kahf 18:28. Farra sees ghudwah as inherently definite, Abu Ali says ghadah can take the article while ghudwah is a proper name, and al-Khalil permits it as a time word like duha. «وقرأ ابن عامر هاهنا وفي الكهف أيضا بالغدوة بضم الغين وإسكان الدال»
#4 IBNJAWZI-ZAD d.597 · الزجاج · reports · Those who seek God's face seek God Himself, and God attests to the sincerity of their intentions. «أي يريدون الله فيشهد الله لهم بصحة النيات وأنهم مخلصون»
#5 IBNJAWZI-ZAD d.597 · الحسن · reports · The account mentioned here is an account of deeds. «أحدها أنه حساب الأعمال قاله الحسن»
#6 IBNJAWZI-ZAD d.597 · reports · Other interpretations of the account mentioned are provision or sufficiency: neither group is responsible for providing for the other. «والثالث أنه بمعنى الكفاية والمعنى ما عليك من كفايتهم»
#7 IBNJAWZI-ZAD d.597 · ابن الأنباري · reports · The warning not to expel them threatens inclusion among the wrongdoers because the Prophet had considered giving the leaders precedence over the weak. «عظم هذا الأمر على النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم وخوف بالدخول في جملة الظالمين»
#8 RAZI-FULL d.606 · عبدالله بن مسعود · reports · Ibn Masud reports that Quraysh leaders asked Muhammad to dismiss poor believers such as Suhayb, Khabbab, Bilal, and Ammar so they might follow him; he refused, then agreed to separate meeting times in hope they would convert. «روي عن عبدالله بن مسعود أنه قال : مر الملأ من قريش على رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم»
#9 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · After the leaders demanded a written agreement and Umar suggested trying their request, this verse descended; the Prophet threw away the page, Umar apologized, and Salman and Khabbab said it concerned them. «فنزلت هذه الآية ، فرمى الصحيفة ، واعتذر عمر عن مقالته»
#10 RAZI-FULL d.606 · سلمان وخباب · reports · Salman and Khabbab say the Prophet then sat with them until his knees touched theirs; after 18:28 he stopped rising before them and praised God for being commanded to stay with them in life and death. «فنزل قوله { واصبر نفسك مع الذين يدعون ربهم } ( الكهف : 28 ) فترك القيام عنا إلى أن نقوم عنه»
#11 RAZI-FULL d.606 · الطاعنون في عصمة الأنبياء · reports · Critics cite the incident as sin: the Prophet expelled poor believers despite the prohibition, became one of the wrongdoers, contradicted Noah's refusal to expel believers and the command to follow prophets, sought worldly adornment, and later said God rebuked him. «احتج الطاعنون في عصمة الأنبياء عليهم السلام بهذه الآية من وجوه»
#12 RAZI-FULL d.606 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas, Hasan, and Mujahid take calling upon God at dawn and evening to mean the prescribed dawn and afternoon prayers; another explanation treats the two ends of the day as shorthand for keeping all five prayers. «أن المراد من الدعاء الصلاة ، يعني يعبدون ربهم بالصلاة المكتوبة»
#13 RAZI-FULL d.606 · إبراهيم · reports · Ibrahim takes الدعاء to mean remembrance: the poor believers remember God at the two ends of the day. «قال إبراهيم : الدعاء ههنا هو الذكر والمعنى يذكرون ربهم طرفي النهار»
#14 RAZI-FULL d.606 · المجسمة · reports · The anthropomorphists cite wanting God's face and related verses such as 55:27 as evidence that God has bodily parts; Razi answers that God's complete oneness in 112:1 rules out composition. «المجسمة تمسكوا في إثبات الأعضاء لله تعالى بقوله { يريدون وجهه }»
#15 RAZI-FULL d.606 · holds · Face is either an honorific way to refer to God Himself, like face of an argument, or a metonym for love and seeking approval because a lover wants to see the beloved's face; compare 2:115. «جعل الوجه كناية عن المحبة وطلب الرضا»
#16 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · One reading makes the pronouns in the account clause refer to the disbelievers: the Prophet need not bear their account or worry whether the proposed meeting leads them to Islam, since God guides and governs them. «والقول الأول : أنه عائد إلى المشركين ، والمعنى ما عليك من حساب المشركين من شيء»
#17 RAZI-FULL d.606 · holds · Razi says Muhammad did not dismiss them from contempt for poverty; he set separate meeting times to win Quraysh, whose conversion mattered greatly, and any mistaken judgment in this effort is forgiven. «ما طردهم لأجل الاستخفاف بهم والاستنكاف من فقرهم وإنما عين لجلوسهم وقتا معينا»
#18 RAZI-FULL d.606 · holds · Calling their dismissal wrongful means the poor deserved honor; expelling them from that gathering wronged them by leaving the more fitting and excellent course, not by violating a duty. The other criticisms likewise concern what was preferable. «كان ذلك ظلما ، إلا أنه من باب ترك الأولى والأفضل لا من باب ترك الواجبات»
#19 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · Ibn Amir reads بالغدوة with waw and a damma on ghayn, also in 18:28; the other readers have the common form بالغداة with alif and a fatha. «قرأ ابن عامر { بالغداة والعشى } بالواو وضم الغين وفي سورة الكهف مثله»
#20 RAZI-FULL d.606 · أبو علي الفارسي · reports · Al-Farisi favors the common reading because ghadāh is an indefinite noun that can take the definite article, while ghudwah is a proper name; spelling ṣalāh with waw despite its alif sound shows orthography proves nothing. «الوجه قراءة العامة بالغدة لأنها تستعمل نكرة فأمكن تعريفها بإدخال لام التعريف عليها»
#21 RAZI-FULL d.606 · سيبويه · reports · Sibawayh says ghudwah and bukrah can function as generic names like Umm Hubayn; Yunus reports Abu Amr leaves them uninflected when used definitely, supporting the common reading, while al-Khalil permits them like ḍuḥwah, supporting Ibn Amir. «زعم الخليل أنه يجوز أن يقال أتيتك اليوم غدوة وبكرة فجعلهما بمنزلة ضحوة»
#22 RAZI-FULL d.606 · prefers · The more apparent reading refers the account pronouns to the poor worshippers, since the pronoun in the warning about expelling them certainly refers to those believers and the nearby pronouns should match. «القول الثاني : أن الضمير عائد إلى الذين يدعون ربهم بالغداة والعشي ، وهم الفقراء»
#23 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · The disbelievers accused the poor of joining for food and clothes rather than faith; if so, Muhammad need only judge appearances, while their hidden account belongs to them, as no bearer carries another's burden. «إن الكفار طعنوا في إيمان أولئك الفقراء وقالوا يا محمد إنهم إنما اجتمعوا عندك»
#24 RAZI-FULL d.606 · holds · Both clauses are needed to express one reciprocal point: neither Muhammad nor the poor will be held responsible for the other's account. «جعلت الجملتان بمنزلة جملة واحدة قصد بهما معنى واحد»
#25 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · A second interpretation is that Muhammad bears no responsibility for their livelihood and they bear none for his; God provides for both, so he should let them remain rather than expel them. «ما عليك من حساب رزقهم من شيء فتملهم وتطردهم»
#26 RAZI-FULL d.606 · holds · The episode resembles Noah's exchange with his people over his low-status followers: 26:111-113 describes laborers and craftsmen, and their account belongs to God. The expulsion clause answers the denial; being unjust may mean wronging himself or the poor who deserved welcome. «وقوله { فتطردهم } جواب النفي ومعناه ، ما عليك من حسابهم من شيء فتطردهم»
#27 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · خباب · reports · Khabbab’s Ibn Majah report says al-Aqra‘ and ‘Uyayna sought a private session because Arab delegations might see them with poor believers; the Prophet agreed before revelation forbade dismissing them. «عن خباب في قول الله عز وجل: { ولا تطرد الذين يدعون ربهم بالغداة والعشي }»
#28 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم · reports · After revelation, the Prophet sat with the poor companions and did not rise until they chose to leave. «وكان رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم يجلس معنا فإذا أراد أن يقوم قام وتركنا»
#29 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · سعد · reports · Sa‘d reports that Quraysh rejected following six poor companions, including Sa‘d, Ibn Mas‘ud, Suhayb, ‘Ammar, al-Miqdad, and Bilal; revelation answered their demand. «عن سعد قال: نزلت هذه الآية فينا ستة، في وفي ابن مسعود وصهيب وعمار والمقداد وبلال»
#30 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · holds · ‘You are not responsible for their account’ means their judgment and provision belong to God, as do the Prophet’s; the first min is partitive and the second emphatic extra. «الأولى للتبعيض، والثانية زائدة للتوكيد.»
#31 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · holds · Since God alone provides and judges, the Prophet must receive and sit with them; expelling them to accommodate people of lesser faith would be injustice. «فأقبل عليهم وجالسهم ولا تطردهم مراعاة لحق من ليس على مثل حالهم»
#32 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · holds · The warning is instruction for others, not evidence that the Prophet actually expelled anyone; it resembles the conditional warning against shirk though he would never commit it. «وحاشاه من وقوع ذلك منه، وإنما هذا بيان للأحكام،»
#33 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · holds · The syntax makes ‘then you would expel them’ a result of the preceding negation and ‘so you would be unjust’ a result of the prohibition; the clauses can also be reordered. «نصب بالفاء في جواب النهي»
#34 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · holds · The verse and report forbid elevating people for status or fine clothing and despising those who are obscure or poorly dressed. «وقد حصل من قوة الآية والحديث النهي عن أن يعظم أحد لجاهه ولثوبه،»
