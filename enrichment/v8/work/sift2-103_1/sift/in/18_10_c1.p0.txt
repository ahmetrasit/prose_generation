# 201 notes filed under 18:10 (group 1 of 2 of this verse's notes)
verse 18:10: إِذْ أَوَى ٱلْفِتْيَةُ إِلَى ٱلْكَهْفِ فَقَالُوا۟ رَبَّنَآ ءَاتِنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحْمَةًۭ وَهَيِّئْ لَنَا مِنْ أَمْرِنَا رَشَدًۭا

#1 MUQATIL d.150 · reports · Abu Jahl and his companions tested the Prophet with three questions: the Cave Companions, Dhul-Qarnayn, and the spirit. «فأتوا النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم فقال أبو جهل: يا ابن عبد المطلب، إنا سائلوك» [filed under 18:9-10]
#2 MUQATIL d.150 · reports · The Prophet promised to answer the next day without saying God willing, and the revelation was delayed for three days. «ولم يستثن، فمكث النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم ثلاثة أيام.» [filed under 18:9-10]
#3 MUQATIL d.150 · reports · Gabriel came with the answer, and the account begins by describing the young men seeking mercy and guidance. «قال النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم: " يا جبريل، إن القوم سألونى عن ثلاث خصال " ، فقال جبريل، عليه السلام:» [filed under 18:9-10]
#4 MUQATIL d.150 · holds · The young men ask for mercy from God, which Muqatil glosses as provision from Him. «من عندك رحمة، يعني رزقا، { وهيىء لنا من أمرنا رشدا } [آية: 10]، يعني تيسيرا،»
#5 MUQATIL d.150 · holds · They ask God to prepare right guidance in their affair; rushd here means facilitation, and the wording involves fronting. «{ وهيىء لنا من أمرنا رشدا } [آية: 10]، يعني تيسيرا، فيها تقديم.»
#6 YAHYA-SALLAM d.200 · السدي · reports · Al-Suddī interprets the mercy the youths ask for as provision and 'from You' as from God's presence. «{من لدنك} [الكهف: 10] من عندك. {رحمة} [الكهف: 10] ، يعني: رزقا. وهو تفسير السدي.»
#7 YAHYA-SALLAM d.200 · holds · The youths were believers who fled their unbelieving people, fearing they would be killed for their faith. «قال: {وهيئ لنا من أمرنا رشدا} [الكهف: 10] كانوا قوما قد آمنوا، فروا بدينهم من قومهم»
#8 FARRA d.207 · holds · The hamza in هيئ is written on an alif here; normally a hamza is seated according to the preceding vowel, though Arabs sometimes use alif in every position. «وقوله: هيئ [10] كتبت الهمزة بالألف (وهيأ) بهجائه. وأكثر ما يكتب الهمز على ما قبله.»
#9 FARRA d.207 · reports · The ʿAbd Allāh codex reportedly wrote شيأ and يستهزأون with alif in positions where the more common orthography uses another seat. «قال: ورأيتها في مصحف عبد الله (شيأ) في رفعه وخفضه بالألف.»
#10 FARRA d.207 · holds · The first spelling pattern is more common in books, although the alif spelling is analogical to the word’s origin. «فإن كان ما قبله مفتوحا كتبت بالألف. وإن كان مضموما كتب بالواو»
#11 TUSTARI d.283 · holds · The youths’ request for mercy means asking God to preserve them in remembrance of Him. «أي احفظنا على ذكرك»
#12 TAB-FULL d.310 · holds · The youths took refuge in the mountain cave to flee their people while preserving their religion; they asked God for mercy from Him and guidance toward the right course. «فقالوا إذ آووه: {ربنا آتنا من لدنك رحمة} [الكهف: 10] رغبة منهم إلى ربهم»
#13 TAB-FULL d.310 · holds · Their request for a rightly directed course means that God make easy what pleases Him and their escape from disbelief and idol worship, and guide them to sound action. «يسر لنا بما نبتغي وما نلتمس من رضاك والهرب من الكفر بك، ومن عبادة الأوثان»
#14 TAB-FULL d.310 · عمرو · reports · The youths followed Jesus, while their idolatrous king pressed them to worship idols; they refused and withdrew to a cave to protect their faith. «قال: ثنا عمرو، في قوله: {أصحاب الكهف والرقيم} [الكهف: 9] كانت الفتية على دين عيسى على الإسلام» [filed under 18:10, 18:14]
#15 TAB-FULL d.310 · عمرو · reports · One youth proposed his father's cave as refuge; the people sealed them inside, then a later Christian king removed the barrier, whereupon they awoke and estimated they had slept a day or part of one. «فقال أحدهم: إنه كان لأبي كهف يأوي فيه غنمه، فانطلقوا بنا نكن فيه» [filed under 18:10, 18:14]
#16 TAB-FULL d.310 · ابن إسحاق · reports · Ibn Ishaq relates that Christian belief had weakened amid sin and idolatrous rulers; Deqianus persecuted those who retained Jesus' religion, forcing the faithful to flee or face death and mutilation. «عن ابن إسحاق، قال: مرج أمر أهل الإنجيل وعظمت فيهم الخطايا وطغت فيهم الملوك»
#17 TAB-FULL d.310 · ابن إسحاق · reports · The young nobles sought God's help through prayer, fasting, charity, praise, and tears as persecution intensified; they were freeborn youths from prominent Roman families. «وكانوا فتية أحداثا أحرارا من أبناء أشراف الروم»
#18 TAB-FULL d.310 · مجاهد · reports · Mujahid reports that at least one youth still showed a downy beard because he was so young. «عن مجاهد، قال: لقد حدثت أنه كان على بعضهم من حداثة أسنانه وضح»
#19 TAB-FULL d.310 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas says they were eight, naming Mكسلمينا as the eldest and the one who spoke to the king, followed by the other named youths. «قال ابن عباس: فكانوا كذلك في عبادة الله ليلهم ونهارهم يبكون إلى الله، ويستغيثونه، وكانوا ثمانية نفر»
#20 TAB-FULL d.310 · reports · As Deqianus prepared a citywide sacrifice to idols, the youths prayed for the believers' deliverance; informers found them prostrate and reported their disobedience to the king. «اللهم رب السماوات والأرض، لن ندعو من دونك إلها {لقد قلنا إذا شططا} [الكهف: 14]» [filed under 18:10, 18:14]
#21 TAB-FULL d.310 · reports · The king summoned the eight tearful, dust-covered youths and demanded that they sacrifice to his gods or be killed. «اختاروا مني: إما أن تذبحوا لآلهتنا كما ذبح الناس، وإما أن أقتلكم»
#22 TAB-FULL d.310 · reports · Deqianus strips the youths of their rank and jewelry but postpones punishment, giving the young men time to reconsider their refusal. «فإني سأؤخركم أن تكونوا من أهل مملكتي وبطانتي وأهل بلادي، وسأفرغ لكم»
#23 TAB-FULL d.310 · reports · Fearing the king's return, each youth takes money from his father's house, gives some away, and sets out for a nearby mountain cave; their dog follows. «أن يأخذ كل واحد منهم نفقة من بيت أبيه، فيتصدقوا منها، ويتزودوا بما بقي»
#24 TAB-FULL d.310 · reports · They devote themselves to worship in the cave and entrust their provisions to Yemliha, who secretly enters town disguised as a poor man to buy food and learn news. «وجعلوا نفقتهم إلى فتى منهم يقال له يمليخا، فكان على طعامهم، يبتاع لهم أرزاقهم من المدينة سرا»
#25 TAB-FULL d.310 · reports · When Deqianus returns to Ephesus and orders sacrifices, Yemliha reports that the youths have been found and are sought, alarming his companions. «فأخبرهم أن الجبار دقينوس قد دخل المدينة، وأنهم قد ذكروا وافتقدوا»
#26 TAB-FULL d.310 · reports · The youths prostrate themselves, pray for protection from persecution, and eat the small meal Yemliha brought as evening falls. «فرفعوا رءوسهم، وأعينهم تفيض من الدمع حذرا وتخوفا على أنفسهم، فطعموا منه»
#27 TAB-FULL d.310 · reports · Deqianus searches for the missing youths and tells the nobles he would have forgiven them if they repented, but the nobles urge him to question their parents and punish them. «فألقى الله عز وجل في نفسه أن يأمر بالكهف فيسد عليهم كرامة من الله»
#28 TAB-FULL d.310 · reports · The two secret believers, Bedros and Ronas, record the youths' names, lineages, and story on lead tablets in a sealed copper chest by the cave, hoping future believers will find them. «اسم أحدهما بيدروس، واسم الآخر: روناس، فأتمرا أن يكتبا شأن الفتية أصحاب الكهف»
#29 TAB-FULL d.310 · عبد الله بن عبيد بن عمير · reports · They were richly dressed young princes who encountered one another while leaving a festival where their people carried idols; each had independently received faith from God and feared persecution. «عن عبد العزيز بن أبي رواد، عن عبد الله بن عبيد بن عمير، قال: كان أصحاب الكهف فتيانا ملوكا»
#30 TAB-FULL d.310 · عبد الله بن عبيد بن عمير · reports · The youths cautiously paired off to test one another's beliefs, discovered that all shared faith, and entered a nearby cave with their hunting dog, where God made them sleep for 309 years. «فإذا هم جميعا على الإيمان، وإذا كهف في الجبل قريب منهم، فقال بعضهم لبعض: ائتوا إلى الكهف»
#31 TAB-FULL d.310 · عبد الله بن عبيد بن عمير · reports · Their people searched for them but could not find the cave; they later recorded the youths' names and lineage, and a Muslim king succeeded the earlier ruler after generations passed. «كتبوا أسماءهم وأنسابهم في لوح: فلان ابن فلان، وفلان ابن فلان أبناء ملوكنا»
#32 TAB-FULL d.310 · reports · Another reported explanation says they fled a ruler's pursuit after one of them was accused of committing a crime. «وقال آخرون: بل كان مصيرهم إلى الكهف هربا من طلب سلطان»
#33 TAB-FULL d.310 · وهب بن منبه · reports · Wahb relates that a disciple of Jesus converted young nobles while working at a bath; after the king's son and a woman died there, the blamed disciple and youths fled with a farmer and dog, slept in a cave, and were sealed inside by the king. «قال: أخبرني إسماعيل بن شروس، أنه سمع وهب بن منبه، يقول: جاء حواري عيسى ابن مريم»
#34 ZAJJAJ d.311 · holds · Awaw means they went to the cave and made it their refuge; fitya is a collective plural like ghilma and sabiyaa, but the pattern is not productive. «ومعنى أووا إليه صاروا إليه وجعلوه مأواهم، والفتية جمع فتى»
#35 ZAJJAJ d.311 · holds · They ask for mercy meaning forgiveness and provision, and for guidance toward what draws them near God. «أي أعطنا من عندك رحمة، أي مغفرة ورزقا.»
#36 ZAJJAJ d.311 · prefers · Another vocalization of رشدا is possible, but رشدا suits the verse-ending pattern and means guidance toward what brings them near God. «يجوز في (رشدا) (رشدا) إلا أنه لا يقرأ بها ههنا»
#37 NAHHAS d.338 · holds · The youths took refuge in the cave while fleeing for their faith and asked for mercy to save them from the unbelievers. «أي هاربين بدينهم ^ ( فقالوا ربنا آتنا من لدنك رحمة ) ^ أي أعطنا من عندك رحمة تنجينا بها من هؤلاء الكفار»
#38 NAHHAS d.338 · prefers · Rushd means guidance by which they could be saved; rushd is preferred over rashad here to match the verse endings. «وهيئ لنا من أمرنا رشدا ) ^ أي على ما ننجو به . ويقال : رشد ورشد إلا أن رشدا ههنا أولى لتتفق الآيات»
