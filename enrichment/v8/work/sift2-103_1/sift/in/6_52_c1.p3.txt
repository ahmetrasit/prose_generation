#123 WAHIDI-BASIT d.468 · أهل المعاني · reports · One interpretation takes 'His face' as God's obedience and the direction or path He commanded people to follow; al-Zajjaj says it testifies to sincere intentions. «أحدهما: أن معناه: يريدون طاعته»
#124 WAHIDI-BASIT d.468 · أبو إسحاق · reports · The other interpretation uses 'face' as an honorific expression: they seek God alone; Abu Ishaq gives this explanation at 18:28. «يريدونه إلا أنه يؤتى بلفظ الوجه للتعظيم»
#125 WAHIDI-BASIT d.468 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas takes the reckoning pronouns to refer to the polytheists: the Prophet is not accountable for them, nor they for him; God alone rewards His allies and punishes His enemies. «ما عليك من حساب المشركين من شيء ولا على المشركين من حسابك من شيء»
#126 WAHIDI-BASIT d.468 · reports · On Ibn Abbas's reading, the Prophet must not court the polytheists by excluding poor believers; the believers' reward is not theirs to give. «ليس ثوابك عليهم حتى تلين لهم»
#127 WAHIDI-BASIT d.468 · prefers · Most exegetes take the pronouns to refer to the poor worshippers; this best fits the explicit object of 'expel them'. «والمفسرون (¬1) يردون الكناية إلى {الذين يدعون ربهم} وهم الفقراء»
#128 WAHIDI-BASIT d.468 · الحسن · reports · One explanation says the Prophet is not responsible for their actions or account of their actions; Ibn Abbas also reports this meaning. «ما عليك من عملهم ومن حساب عملهم من شيء»
#129 WAHIDI-BASIT d.468 · أهل المعاني · reports · The story resembles Noah's people calling his followers the lowly: the prophets defer their deeds and livelihood to God, and the poor believers' humble trades do not disgrace the Prophet. «قال له قومه: {قالوا أنؤمن لك واتبعك الأرذلون}»
#130 WAHIDI-BASIT d.468 · الزجاج · reports · Al-Zajjaj says the repeated reckoning clause confirms parallel phrasing, though the second phrase could be omitted. «وإلى هذا المعنى أشار الزجاج»
#131 WAHIDI-BASIT d.468 · أهل المعاني · reports · A second view concerns provision: neither the Prophet's nor the poor believers' livelihood depends on one another, because God provides for all; he should let them draw near. «ليس رزقك عليهم ولا رزقهم عليك وإنما يرزقك وإياهم الله الرازق»
#132 WAHIDI-BASIT d.468 · ابن عباس · reports · The warning against becoming one of the wrongdoers means harming oneself through disobedience and losing reward; ظلم can mean diminution in Arabic and in Quranic usage. «فتكون من الضارين لنفسك بالمعصية»
#133 WAHIDI-BASIT d.468 · ابن زيد · prefers · Ibn Zayd calls expelling the poor injustice to them: they did not deserve it, so the author calls this a sound interpretation. «إنهم لم يستحقوا منك الطرد فإذا طردتهم فقد ظلمتهم»
#134 WAHIDI-WAJIZ d.468 · reports · The verse concerns poor believers after Meccan chiefs told the Prophet to remove them so the chiefs could sit with him and believe. «نزلت في فقراء المؤمنين لما قال رؤساء الكفار»
#135 WAHIDI-WAJIZ d.468 · holds · Calling on God morning and evening means performing the obligatory prayers; seeking his face means seeking his reward. «يريدون وجهه } يطلبون ثواب الله»
#136 WAHIDI-WAJIZ d.468 · holds · The Prophet is not responsible for the poor people's provision, nor are they responsible for his; God provides for all, so he should keep them near. «ليس رزقك عليهم، ولا رزقهم عليك»
#137 WAHIDI-WAJIZ d.468 · holds · Expelling these believers would wrong them. «فتكون من الظالمين } لهم بطردهم»
#138 JISHUMI d.494 · ابن عامر · reports · Ibn ʿĀmir reads بالغدوة والعشي with wāw and a ḍamma on ghayn in both this verse and al-Kahf; the other readers have alif and fatḥa. «قرأ ابن عامر: بالغدوة والعشي بالواو وضم الغين، وفي سورة (الكهف) مثله» [filed under 6:52, 18:28]
#139 JISHUMI d.494 · ابن أبي عبلة · reports · Ibn Abī ʿAbla reads the plural forms بالغدوات والعشيات; the original wāw turns into alif after fatḥa on the preceding dāl. «وقرأ ابن أبي عبلة: بالغدوات والعشيات وأصله الواو، ولذلك يجمع غدوات» [filed under 6:52, 18:28]
#140 JISHUMI d.494 · holds · Ṭard means banishing, exclusion, or expulsion. «الطرد والإقصاء والتشريد نظائر، وهو الإبعاد»
#141 JISHUMI d.494 · holds · Wajh can mean the familiar bodily face or the essence/aspect of a thing, as in saying this is the wajh of an opinion. «والوجه: الجارحة المعروفة، سميت بذلك لأنها تواجهك، والوجه: نفس الشيء أيضا»
#142 JISHUMI d.494 · holds · The prohibition طرد is jussive, with kasra to separate adjacent vowelless consonants; فتطردهم can answer the negation in ما عليك من حسابهم من شيء. «ولا تطرد جزم للنهي، وكسر لالتقاء الساكنين. فتطردهم جواب للجحود»
#143 JISHUMI d.494 · أبو مسلم · reports · Abū Muslim parses فتطردهم فتكون as consequences of the prohibition: do not expel them, or you become unjust by expelling them. «عن أبي مسلم. والكاف في قوله: وكذلك كاف التشبيه»
#144 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · reports · Salman and Khabbab say the verse came after al-Aqra, Uyayna, and their elite group asked the Prophet to dismiss poor believers such as Bilal, Suhayb, Ammar, and Khabbab, whose coarse wool clothes they despised. «قال سلمان وخباب بن الأرت: فينا نزلت هذه الآية، جاء الأقرع بن حابس التميمي»
#145 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · reports · When the elites demanded a separate meeting and a written promise to remove the poor men, the Prophet refused to drive believers away; Gabriel brought this verse and the command to keep company with those seeking God. «ما أنا بطارد المؤمنين»
#146 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · reports · The Prophet dropped the sheet, summoned the poor believers, and honored them with God's message of mercy; he thereafter sat close to them and waited for them before leaving. «فألقى رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم الصحيفة من يده، ثم دعانا فأثبته»
#147 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · reports · The Prophet told them he was grateful not to die before being commanded to remain patient with members of his community, saying their life and death were with them. «الحمد لله الذي لم يمتني حتى أمرني أن أصبر نفسي مع قوم من أمتي»
#148 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · الكلبي · reports · Al-Kalbi says the petitioners proposed separate days or a single gathering where the Prophet would face them and turn his back on the poor; God revealed the prohibition against dismissing the poor worshippers. «وقال الكلبي: قالوا له اجعل لنا يوما ولهم يوما، فقال: لا أفعل»
#149 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · مجاهد · reports · Mujahid says Quraysh claimed they would follow Muhammad if not for Bilal and Ibn Umm Abd; the verse followed. «قال مجاهد: قالت قريش: لولا بلال وابن أم عبد لبايعنا محمدا»
#150 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas says morning and evening worship means the dawn and afternoon prayers; he also reports that it can mean all five prayers after elite men asked the poor to pray behind them. «قال ابن عباس: يعني يعبدون ربهم بالغداة والعشي، يعني: صلاة الصبح وصلاة العصر»
#151 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · سعيد بن المسيب · reports · Said ibn al-Musayyib rejects reading the phrase as a session for public storytellers; he says it refers to the prayer they had just completed. «مع سعيد بن المسيب، فلما سلم الإمام ابتدر الناس القاص»
#152 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · إبراهيم النخعي · reports · Ibrahim al-Nakhai interprets the phrase as remembering God; another view takes it as literal supplication. «وقال إبراهيم النخعي: يعني يذكرون ربهم، وقيل المراد منه: حقيقة الدعاء»
#153 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · holds · Seeking God's face means seeking God through obedience; Ibn Abbas says they seek God's reward. «يريدون الله بطاعتهم، قال ابن عباس رضي الله عنهما: يطلبون ثواب الله»
#154 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · holds · The Prophet is not responsible for the worshippers' account and they are not responsible for his; their livelihood is not his burden, nor is his provision theirs. «أي: لا تكلف أمرهم ولا يتكلفون أمرك»
#155 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · holds · The phrase 'so you would drive them away' answers the preceding negation; 'then you would be among wrongdoers' answers the prohibition, respectively. «قوله {فتطردهم} جواب لقوله {ما عليك من حسابهم من شيء}»
#156 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · holds · After warning those Muslims who are not yet devout, the text turns to the devout: draw them near and honor them, and do not obey those seeking the opposite. «ثم أردفهم ذكر المتقين منهم وأمره بتقريبهم وإكرامهم»
#157 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · reports · A reading has بالغدوة والعشي. «وقرىء : ( بالغدوة والعشي )»
#158 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · holds · Calling on their Lord morning and evening means continuous worship; alternatively it specifically means the dawn and afternoon prayers. «وقيل معناه : يصلون صلاة الصبح والعصر»
#159 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · holds · Seeking God's face denotes sincerity; ‘face’ can express a thing's essence and reality. «ووسمهم بالإخلاص في عبادتهم بقوله»
#160 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · رؤسا من المشركين · reports · Polytheist leaders asked the Prophet to expel poor believers such as Ammar, Suhayb, Bilal, Khabbab, and Salman so they could speak with him; he refused, then briefly considered their request in hope they might believe, before revelation forbade it. «لو طردت عنا هؤلاء الأعبد يعنون فقراء المسلمين»
#161 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · سلمان وخباب · reports · Salman and Khabbab say this was revealed about them: the Prophet sat very close, then revelation told him to remain with them until they rose; he praised God for living to receive that command. «قال سلمان وخباب : فينا نزلت فكان رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم»
#162 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · holds · The accounting clauses assure the Prophet that allegations about the believers do not bind him: he judges outward signs, and any hidden fault remains their burden, as in the rule that no bearer bears another's burden. «فما يلزمك إلا اعتبار الظاهر والاتسام بسيمة المتقين»
#163 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · holds · Both accounting clauses are needed together to convey that neither group is answerable for the other's account. «ولا يستقل بهذا المعنى إلا الجملتان جميعا»
