#131 DURR-FULL d.911 · قتادة · reports · Qatadah says Joseph left home at seventeen, spent seven years in the well and seven in prison, and stored food for seven years; they think he met his father then. «عن قتادة رضي الله عنه قال: ذهب يوسف عليه السلام وهو ابن سبع عشرة ولبث في الجب سبعا» [filed under 12:42, 12:47]
#132 DURR-FULL d.911 · عكرمة · reports · A Prophetic report marvels at Joseph's patience and generosity: although asked to interpret the cows, he did not rush out when summoned and wanted his innocence established. «عن عكرمة - رضي الله عنه - قال: قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم لقد عجبت من يوسف وصبره وكرمه»
#133 DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن زيد · reports · Ibn Zayd says Joseph paired interpretation with prudent storage advice because grain left in its ear is preserved and not eaten. «عن ابن زيد - رضي الله عنه - قال: لم يرض يوسف عليه السلام أن أفتاهم بالتأويل»
#134 DURR-FULL d.911 · قتادة وابن جريج · reports · Qatadah says Joseph intended to preserve the grain; Ibn Jurayj notes an early reading that it lasts longer in its ear and is not eaten. «عن قتادة رضي الله عنه في قوله {فذروه في سنبله} قال: أراد يوسف عليه السلام البقاء»
#135 DURR-FULL d.911 · زيد بن أسلم · reports · Joseph used to serve a man food for two, leaving half uneaten each day; when the man ate the whole serving, Joseph recognized the first hard year had begun. «عن زيد بن أسلم - رضي الله عنه - أن يوسف عليه السلام في زمانه كان يصنع لرجل طعام اثنين»
#136 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · Dāʾban is an adverbial verbal noun meaning they should farm continuously and diligently, following their usual practice. «مصدر دأب في العمل إذا جد فيه وتعب وانتصابه على الحالية»
#137 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · Harth is sowing seed and preparing soil, while zarʿ is tending and growing the crop; zarʿ is broader because it can also mean sowing, while God causes growth. «والفرق بين الحرث والزرع أن الحرث القاء البذر وتهيئة الأرض»
#138 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · Joseph forecast seven abundant years and recommended leaving grain in its ears to protect it from pests; green ears may have suggested this unusual storage method. «اتركوه فيه ولا تذروه كيلا يأكله السوس كما هو شأن غلال مصر»
#139 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · They should eat only a little during the seven plentiful years and store the rest; Joseph’s instruction teaches restraint and economy. «مما تأكلون في تلك السنين فأنتم تدرسون وقت حاجتكم إليه»
#140 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · حفص reads دأبا with fatḥa and most with sukūn; a light alif form is also transmitted. It is a verbal noun of toil, extended to habitual persistence, and can be a circumstantial description of the cultivators or an absolute object of an omitted verb. «قرأ حفص بفتح الهمزة والجمهور بإسكانها وقريء دابا بألف من غير همز»
#141 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · المبرد · rejects · Al-Mubarrad parses دأبا as an absolute object of تزرعون, like قعد القرفصاء; Al-Alusi rejects that analysis. «وعند المبرد مفعول مطلق لتزرعون وذلك عنده نظير قعد القرفصاء وليس بشيء»
#142 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · Joseph interprets the seven fat cows and green ears as seven fertile years and the lean cows and dry ears as seven famine years, during which they should cultivate diligently. «وقد أول عليه السلام البقرات السمان والسنبلات الخضر بسنين مخاصيب»
#143 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · بعضهم · reports · Some read تزرعون as an imperative to cultivate, cast as a report to make fulfillment sound already accomplished; the harvest instruction would then also be imperative. «المراد الأمر بالزراعة كذلك فالجملة خبر لفظا أمر معنى»
#144 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · The command to leave grain in its ears prevents weevils from consuming it; Egyptian grain stores can be ruined after roughly two years, and the green ears may suggest this storage counsel is another imperative. «فذروه في سنبله ولا تذروه كيلا يأكله السوس كما هو شأن غلال مصر»
#145 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · Grammarians object that an imperative harvest instruction cannot coordinate with a declarative forecast; replies treat it as independent advice, an answer to an implied condition, or understand the imperative as a report because it is Joseph's guidance. «لأنه لو لم يؤول ذلك بالأمر لزم عطف الإنشاء على الخبر»
#146 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · الكشف · prefers · Al-Kashf finds the strongest reading predictive: the cultivation forecast interprets the dream, while the storage instruction interrupts it as urgent advice, making what will happen seem already present. «على أصله لأنه تأويل المنام بدليل قوله الآتي»
#147 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · بعضهم · reports · If تزرعون is instead an imperative, some treat the storage instruction as part of the dream's interpretation because the lean cows and dry ears predict eating what the fertile years produced; others regard it as advice outside the interpretation. «أن ما حصدتم الخ على تقدير كون»
#148 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · They should leave all but the small amount needed to eat during the fertile years; the advice encourages moderation in food. «أي اتركوا ذلك في السنبل إلا ما لا غنى عنه من القليل الذي تأكلونه»
#149 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · السلمي · reports · Al-Sulami reads مما يأكلون with yā, meaning what people eat, rather than addressing the audience as 'you'. «وقرأ السلمي مما يأكلون بالياء على الغيبة أي يأكل الناس»
#150 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The seed grain is not separately excepted because the command to cultivate seven years already makes retaining seed understood. «والاقتصار على استثناء المأكول دون البذر لكون ذلك معلوما»
#151 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · In the dream's interpretation, cows represent agricultural years, fatness fertility, leanness drought, and ears of grain provisions. «فالبقرات لسنين الزراعة ، لأن البقرة تتخذ للإثمار . والسمن رمز للخصب» [filed under 12:47, 12:48, 12:49]
#152 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Seven green ears signify seven years whose harvest is eaten fresh, with each ear standing for that year's food. «فالسنبلات الخضر رمز لطعام ينتفع به ، وكونها سبعا رمز للانتفاع به في السبع السنين»
#153 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · تزرعون predicts work they ordinarily do; mentioning their customary farming prepares for the advice that follows, and دأبا qualifies it. «وقوله : { تزرعون } خبر عما يكون من عملهم ، وذلك أن الزرع عادتهم»
#154 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · دأب means habit and persistence; it is adverbial حال describing the pronoun in يزرعون, meaning “according to your customary practice,” and links to Q 3:11. «والدأب : العادة والاستمرار عليها . وتقدم في قوله : { كدأب آل فرعون»
#155 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Yusuf's interpretation also gives the king valuable public guidance on provisioning and storage; the king's dream is divine kindness to Egypt and revelation to Yusuf through the king, perhaps as the king was ready for faith and reform. «وقد مزج تعبيره بإرشاد جليل لأحوال التموين والادخار لمصلحة الأمة»
#156 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Keeping surplus grain in its ear protects it from weevils that attack piled grain; people should also eat sparingly in good years to save the surplus for hardship. «ليكون أسلم له من إصابة السوس الذي يصيب الحب إذا تراكم بعضه على بعض»
#157 TABATABAI d.1402 · الرضا عليه السلام · reports · A report says Joseph stockpiled the harvests, sold food during famine first for money, then valuables, livestock, servants, property, and land, and finally for people’s freedom; he then freed the Egyptians and restored their possessions and kingship. «سمعت الرضا عليه السلام يقول وأقبل يوسف على جمع الطعام في السبع السنين المخصبة فكبسه في الخزائن» [filed under 12:47, 12:48, 12:49]
#158 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The author omits many similar reports about the famine economy because most do not directly serve the interpretation of these verses. «والروايات في هذا المقام كثيرة أغلبها غير مرتبطة بغرض تفسير الآيات ولذلك تركنا نقلها» [filed under 12:47, 12:48, 12:49]
#159 TABATABAI d.1402 · الراغب · reports · Al-Raghib defines daʾb as continued travel and, by extension, a lasting habitual practice; another reported sense is strenuous effort. «قال الراغب الدأب إدامة السير دأب في السير دأبا»
#160 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · Accordingly, Joseph’s instruction is to farm continuously for seven years, either as a sustained practice or with persistent effort. «وعليه فالمعنى تزرعون سبع سنين زراعة متوالية مستمرة»
#161 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · Tazraʿun is a report in the form of a command, emphasizing that they must comply; the following imperative to leave the harvest in its ears confirms this. «وكثيرا ما يؤتى بالأمر في صورة الخبر مبالغة في وجوب الامتثال»
#162 TABATABAI d.1402 · reports · Some explain storing grain in its ears as protection from insects and spoilage, since husked and cleaned grain deteriorates faster. «قيل وإنما أمر بوضعه وتركه في سنبله لأن السنبل لا يقع فيه سوس ولا يهلك»
#163 TABATABAI d.1402 · prefers · The dream depicts the practical measures the king must take for a coming famine: repeated planting, preserving grain in ears, and rationing reserves, rather than simply forecasting years. «أن الذي رآه الملك من الرؤيا إنما كان مثال ما يجب عليه من اتخاذ التدبير لإنجاء الناس من مصيبة الجدب» [filed under 12:43, 12:47, 12:48]
#164 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · Cows represent yearly provisions: fat cows picture plentiful harvests from sustained planting, lean cows picture shortages, and their eating the fat ones pictures famine consuming saved food. «فحكت السنين المخصبة والمجدبة أي الرزق الذي يرتزقون به فيها في صورة البقرة» [filed under 12:43, 12:47, 12:48]
#165 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The green ears represent stores to be saved and the dry ears indicate preserving grain unthreshed; the dream shows the work required to protect food supplies. «وحكت ما يجب عليهم في حفظ ذخائر الرزق بالسنبلات اليابسة قبال السنبلات الخضر» [filed under 12:43, 12:47, 12:48]
#166 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The exception allowing a little consumption is not dream interpretation but permission to use the amount needed for food during the seven productive years. «ولم يزد يوسف عليه السلام في تأويله على ذلك شيئا الا أمورا ثلاثة أحدها»
