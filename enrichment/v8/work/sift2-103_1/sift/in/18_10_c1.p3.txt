#117 RAZI-FULL d.606 · holds · The youths’ taking refuge means they went to the cave and made it their dwelling. «ومعنى أوى الفتية في الكهف صاروا إليه وجعلوه مأواهم»
#118 RAZI-FULL d.606 · holds · Their prayer asks for mercy from God’s stores of grace, including guidance, knowledge, patience, provision, and safety from enemies. «وهي الهداية بالمعرفة والصبر والرزق والأمن من الأعداء»
#119 RAZI-FULL d.606 · holds · “From Your presence” signals the greatness of the requested mercy, fitting God’s generosity and ample bounty. «وقوله من لدنك يدل على عظمة تلك الرحمة»
#120 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · Rashad can mean an affair endowed with right guidance so that they become guided, or that God make all their affair itself rightly guided. «وفي تفسير اللفظ وجهان . الأول : التقدير وهيىء لنا أمرا ذا رشد»
#121 RAZI-FULL d.606 · holds · The temporal إذ cannot attach to the preceding question because the Prophet and the youths were separated by a long interval; an implicit اذكر, “recall,” is understood. «لا يجوز أن يكون إذ هنا متعلقا بما قبله على تقدير أم حسبت إذ أوى الفتية»
#122 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · ابن عباس · reports · The dog was either their hunting dog or a shepherd's dog that followed the shepherd and youths; it was called Himran or Qitmir. «وروي أنهم وجدوا في طريقهم راعيا له كلب فاتبعهم الراعي على رأيهم»
#123 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · reports · The youths' foreign names have weak transmission; al-Tabari lists Maksalmina as eldest spokesman and Yemlikha as the one sent with money, among six others. «وأما أسماء أهل الكهف فأعجمية، والسند في معرفتها واه»
#124 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · مقاتل · reports · The dog belonged to Maksalmina, the eldest youth and owner of sheep. «قال مقاتل: وكان الكلب لمكسلمينا»
#125 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · holds · The story establishes fleeing with one's religion and leaving family, homeland, wealth, and friends to escape persecution; the Prophet and Companions did likewise. «هذه الآية صريحة في الفرار بالدين وهجرة الأهل والبنين والقرابات»
#126 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · holds · Retreat to mountains, caves, or solitude is a prophetic and saintly practice and is especially favored by many scholars amid fitna and corruption. «فسكنى الجبال ودخول الغيران، والعزلة عن الخلق»
#127 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · العلماء · reports · Seclusion can be practiced in mountains, at coasts and frontier posts, or at home; a report advises hiding and guarding one's tongue during fitna. «الاعتزال عن الناس يكون مرة في الجبال والشعاب، ومرة في السواحل»
#128 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · طائفة من العلماء · reports · A group defines seclusion as inwardly and practically separating from evil while remaining among people. «اعتزال الشر وأهله بقلبك وعملك، إن كنت بين أظهرهم»
#129 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · ابن المبارك · reports · Ibn al-Mubarak defines seclusion as joining people when they remember God and remaining silent when they discuss other matters. «أن تكون مع القوم فإذا خاضوا في ذكر الله فخض معهم»
#130 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · النبي ﷺ · reports · A report says the believer who mixes with people and patiently bears harm is better than one who avoids people and does not bear their harm. «المؤمن الذي يخالط الناس ويصبر على أذاهم أفضل من المؤمن الذي لا يخالطهم»
#131 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · holds · Any place away from people, including mosque retreat, coastal service, or home, fits the meaning of mountain solitude; mountains are named because they are common retreats. «فكل موضع يبعد عن الناس فهو داخل في معناه كما ذكرنا»
#132 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · الحسن · reports · A saying that al-Baghawi transmits as a mursal report from al-Hasan and others calls believers' homes their cloisters. «" من مراسيل الحسن وغيره»
#133 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · النبي ﷺ · reports · In response to Uqba ibn Amir asking about salvation, the Prophet advises holding the tongue, staying home, and weeping over one's sin. «يا عقبة أمسك عليك لسانك وليسعك بيتك وابك على خطيئتك»
#134 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · النبي ﷺ · reports · A Bukhari report describes a time when a Muslim's best property will be sheep taken to mountain heights and rain-fed places to flee fitna for faith. «يأتي على الناس زمان خير مال الرجل المسلم الغنم يتبع بها شعف الجبال»
#135 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · الحسن بن واقد · reports · A report attributed to the Prophet says that in year 180 AH celibacy, seclusion, and retreat to mountain tops become permitted for the community. «إذا كانت سنة ثمانين ومائة فقد حلت لأمتي العزبة والعزلة»
#136 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · الحسن · reports · A long report says that in a corrupt age only flight from peak to peak or stone to stone preserves religion; family may ruin a person by demanding unaffordable provision. «لا يسلم لذي دين دينه إلا من فر بدينه من شاهق إلى شاهق»
#137 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · prefers · People differ in capacity: mountain-cave retreat is the highest state, home seclusion suits others, and some can mix outwardly while remaining inwardly distinct. «أحوال الناس في هذا الباب تختلف، فرب رجل تكون له قوة»
#138 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · reports · Some veterans of Badr stayed inside their homes after Uthman's killing and did not leave again except for burial. «اعتزل رجال من أهل بدر فلزموا بيوتهم بعد قتل عثمان»
#139 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · وهب بن منبه · reports · Wahb counsels a would-be recluse that people need one another, advising him to hear without listening, see without looking, and speak little. «أصم سميعا، أعمى بصيرا، سكوتا نطوقا»
#140 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · النبي ﷺ · reports · Uqba ibn Amir reports that God admires a shepherd on a mountain crag who calls and performs prayer from fear of Him, forgives him, and admits him to Paradise. «يعجب ربك من راعي غنم في رأس شظية الجبل يؤذن بالصلاة»
#141 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · holds · The youths fled their pursuers and turned to supplication; rahma here means forgiveness and provision, while rushd means success in right guidance. «لما فروا ممن يطلبهم اشتغلوا بالدعاء ولجأوا إلى الله تعالى»
#142 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas interprets their request for right guidance as asking for a safe exit from the cave. «مخرجا من الغار في سلامة»
#143 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · reports · Another interpretation takes rushd to mean what is correct; similarly, the Prophet resorted to prayer when troubled. «ومن هذا المعنى أنه عليه السلام كان إذا حزبه أمر فزع إلى الصلاة»
#144 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · reports · One account makes the youths sons of nobles under the pagan king Daqyus, Romans who followed Jesus; another places them before Jesus. «قوم من أبناء أشراف مدينة دقيوس الملك الكافر»
#145 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · ابن عباس · reports · Daqyanus, after Jesus, imposed idol worship in Ephesus or Tarsus; seven young secret worshippers fled with a shepherd and dog, and God blinded pursuers before they sealed the cave. «إن ملكا من الملوك يقال له دقيانوس ظهر على مدينة من مدائن الروم»
#146 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · مجاهد عن ابن عباس · reports · In another account, the youths learned faith from a disciple or earlier believers, rejected the king's idols, and answered his summons with words resembling 18:14–16. «وروى مجاهد عن ابن عباس أيضا أن هؤلاء الفتية كانوا في دين ملك يعبد الأصنام»
#147 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · reports · A reported version says the king called them immature, delayed punishment, and sent them home to reconsider before a deadline; they resolved to flee while he was away. «إنكم شبان أغمار لا عقول لكم»
#148 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · reports · One escape account has them playing polo and a ball toward their route to hide their departure; another says imprisoned youths slipped away while playing at a public feast. «خرجوا فيما روي يلعبون بالصولجان»
#149 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · وهب بن منبه · reports · A disciple of Jesus worked in a bathhouse, converted local youths, then fled with them after the king's son and a prostitute died there and the group was blamed. «أن أول أمرهم إنما كان حواري لعيسى بن مريم جاء إلى مدينة أصحاب الكهف»
#150 BAYDAWI-FULL d.685 · holds · The youths were Roman nobles whom Daqyanus tried to make idolaters; they refused and fled to the cave. «فتية من أشراف الروم أرادهم دقيانوس على الشرك فأبوا»
#151 BAYDAWI-FULL d.685 · holds · Their request for mercy seeks forgiveness, provision, and protection from their enemy; they ask for right guidance in leaving the unbelievers or for all their affairs to be rightly ordered. «توجب لنا المغفرة والرزق والأمن من العدو»
#152 BAYDAWI-FULL d.685 · holds · The root of prepare means bringing a thing into a particular state or form. «وأصل التهيئة إحداث هيئة الشيء»
#153 NASAFI-FULL d.710 · holds · The prayer asks for mercy from God's stores, including forgiveness, provision, and safety from enemies. «رحمة من خزائن رحمتك وهي المغفرة والرزق والامن من الاعداء»
#154 NASAFI-FULL d.710 · holds · Their affair means their separation from the unbelievers; they ask God to prepare a right course so they become guided, or to make their whole affair rightly guided, or to ease the path to His pleasure. «الذي نحن عليه من مفارقة الكفار ﴿ رشدا ﴾ حتى نكون بسببه راشدين»
#155 QASHANI d.736 · holds · The youths' cave is the body; they seek mercy from God's beautiful names, fitting their readiness, and guidance through descent to bodily existence for development. «من خزائن رحمتك التي هي أسماؤك الحسنى»
#156 QASHANI d.736 · holds · By bodily attachment and tools they seek intellectual and practical perfection and a steady course toward God. «طلبوا بالاتصاف البدني والتعلق بآلات الكمال»
#157 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · reports · Quraysh sent al-Naḍr ibn al-Ḥārith and ʿUqba ibn Abī Muʿayṭ to Jewish scholars, who proposed questions about the youths, a world-traveling man, and the spirit as a test of Muhammad’s prophethood. «وسبب نزولها أن قريشا بعثت النضر بن الحارث وعقبة بن أبي معيط إلى أحبار اليهود بالمدينة» [filed under 18:10, 18:22, 18:83]
#158 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · قيل · reports · A second report says the Jews judged that answering all three questions would disprove prophethood, while answering two and withholding the other would confirm it. «وروي في هذا السبب أن اليهود قالت: إن أجابكم عن الثلاثة فليس بنبي» [filed under 18:10, 18:22, 18:83]
