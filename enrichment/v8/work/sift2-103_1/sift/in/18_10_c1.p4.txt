#159 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · holds · The sura’s opening connects to the prior sura’s affirmation of true revelation and humility: praise God for the upright warning Quran, which warns those asserting a son and promises believers reward. «ومناسبة أول هذه السورة لآخر ما قبلها أنه لما قال وبالحق أنزلناه وبالحق نزل» [filed under 18:10, 18:22, 18:83]
#160 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · holds · Andalusian speakers commonly called Christians Rūm rather than Naṣārā; the youths ask God to grant guidance and steadiness after leaving their families’ religion for monotheism. «دعوا بإيتاء الرحمة وهي تتضمن الرزق وغيره»
#161 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · الزمخشري · reports · Al-Zamakhsharī glosses the prayer as making the whole affair rightly guided, like calling someone a lion. «وقال الزمخشري: واجعل أمرنا رشدا كله»
#162 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · أبو جعفر · reports · Abū Jaʿfar, Shayba, and al-Zuhrī read the forms of hayyiʾ with two yāʾs and no hamza, treating this as replacement of a quiescent hamza. «وقرأ أبو جعفر وشيبة والزهري: وهي ويهيي بياءين»
#163 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · الأعشى · reports · Ibn Khalawayh records al-Aʿshā’s reading from Abū Bakr from ʿĀṣim without hamza; the form may reflect hamza replacement or disputed deletion, with replacement regular and deletion allowed in imperative or jussive forms. «ابن خالويه الأعشى عن أبي بكر عن عاصم»
#164 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · أبو رجاء · reports · Abū Rajāʾ reads rushd with ḍamma on rāʾ and a quiescent shīn; the majority read rashadan with both vowels open. «وقرأ أبو رجاء: رشد بضم الراء وإسكان الشين»
#165 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · ابن عطية · reports · Ibn ʿAṭiyya prefers the majority reading because it resembles the surrounding verse endings; he says the prayer concerns worldly need, while the youths already trusted their afterlife guidance, though mercy could also mean the hereafter. «قال ابن عطية: وهي أرجح لشبهها بفواصل الآيات»
#166 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · holds · Ibn ʿAṭiyya says every believer should use this sufficient prayer for worldly affairs. «وينبغي لكل مؤمن أن يجعل دعاءه في أمر دنياه هذه الآية فإنها كافية»
#167 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · holds · The temporal idh is governed either by an implied udhkur or by ʿajaban; the youths made the cave a refuge and place of protection. «ومعنى أوى جعلوه مأوى لهم ومكان اعتصام»
#168 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · المفسرون · reports · Exegetes gloss the requested mercy as provision. «ثم دعوا الله تعالى أن يؤتيهم رحمة من عنده وفسرها المفسرون بالرزق»
#169 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · الزمخشري · reports · Al-Zamakhsharī says the mercy includes forgiveness, provision, and safety from enemies. «وقال الزمخشري: هي المغفرة والرزق والأمن من الأعداء»
#170 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · holds · Fitya is a broken plural of paucity, fitting their small number; Ibn al-Sarrāj instead calls it a collective noun, and the term indicates they were young. «والفتية جمع فتى جمع تكسير جمع قلة»
#171 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · reports · A report describes them as young Roman nobles, richly dressed with gold ornaments and sidelocks, following Jesus; another says they lived before Jesus. «وكذا روي أنهم كانوا شبابا من أبناء الأشراف والعظماء»
#172 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · reports · إذ أوى may be governed by عجبا or by an implied اذكر. «يجوز أن ينتصب ب " عجبا "»
#173 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · holds · Most read هيئ with hamza after doubled ياء; Abu Jaafar, Shayba, and al-Zuhri use two yā letters, substituting for hamza. «يجوز أن ينتصب ب " عجبا "»
#174 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · reports · Asim's هي deletes hamza directly or replaces it, then treats the replacement as an original weak letter and deletes it; a poem illustrates weak-letter retention. «يجوز أن ينتصب ب " عجبا "»
#175 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · أبو رجاء · reports · Abu Raja reads رشدا with damma راء and silent شين. «يجوز أن ينتصب ب " عجبا "»
#176 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · prefers · The common رشدا better matches the verse-end rhymes. «يجوز أن ينتصب ب " عجبا "»
#177 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas says he knows the Quran except the meanings of hannan, awwah, and raqim. «عن ابن عباس قال: القرآن أعلمه إلا حنانا»
#178 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas, through Ikrima, says he does not know whether raqim is a book or a building. «قال ابن عباس: ما أدري ما الرقيم؟»
#179 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas also interprets raqim as a book; Sa'id ibn Jubayr says it was a stone tablet bearing the youths' story at the cave entrance, and Abd al-Rahman ibn Zayd likewise says book, citing 83:9. «وقال سعيد بن جبير: [الرقيم]»
#180 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · prefers · Ibn Kathir agrees with Ibn Jarir that raqim most likely means something written or inscribed, like the forms qatil and jarih. «وهو اختيار ابن جرير قال:»
#181 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · holds · The youths fled to a mountain cave to protect their faith from their people and asked God for mercy to shelter them and for a wise, righteous outcome. «الذين فروا بدينهم من قومهم»
#182 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · holds · Their prayer asks God to grant mercy, conceal them from their people, and arrange a sound outcome; a transmitted supplication asks that every divine decree end well. «هب لنا من عندك رحمة ترحمنا بها وتسترنا عن قومنا»
#183 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم · reports · A report from Busr ibn Abi Artat says the Prophet prayed for good outcomes in every affair and protection from worldly disgrace and afterlife punishment. «عن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم أنه كان يدعو:»
#184 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · They ask for exceptional divine mercy and a way toward deliverance and right guidance in both worlds; their request is granted beyond expectation. «وجها ترشدنا فيه إلى الخلاص في الدارين»
#185 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · The youths were few and inexperienced, yet youth makes people more receptive to truth and better guided than elders. «والشبان أقبل للحق وأهدى للسبيل من الشيوخ»
#186 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · Their flight to the cave for their faith parallels Muhammad and Abu Bakr retreating to the cave of Thawr. «فرارا بدينهم كما أويت أنت والصديق إلى غار ثور فرارا بدينكما»
#187 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · reports · Al-Harali distinguishes customary, extraordinary, and divinely bestowed orders of events; the youths ask for the highest, hidden kind of mercy. «على ثلاث رتب : حكيمات جارية على قوانين العادات , وعنديات خارقة للمطردات ولدنيات مستغرقة في الأمور الخارقات»
#188 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · The opening ‘when’ can function as an adverb modifying ‘wonder’ or as the object of ‘remember’; it means they went to and took refuge in the cave. «ظرف لعجبا أو مفعول لأذكر»
#189 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · The youths were noble Romans who refused Daqyanus’s coercion to worship idols and fled to a cave in their mountain. «فتية من أشراف الروم أكرههم دقيانوس على الشرك فأبوا وهربوا»
#190 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · Fitya means vigorous young men and can metaphorically describe rulers even when old; a prophetic report prefers ‘my young man’ over ‘my slave,’ while Abu Yusuf says the term admits bondage. «والفتية جمع الفتى وهو الشاب القوي الحدث ويستعار للملوك»
#191 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · ‘From Your presence’ means from the hidden treasuries of special mercy, inaccessible to hostile eyes; they ask mercy bringing forgiveness, provision, and safety from enemies. «من خزائن رحمتك الخاصة المكنونة عن عيون أهل المعاداة»
#192 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · Both prepositional phrases attach to ‘prepare’; preparation means ordering their migration from unbelievers and perseverance in obedience, while rushd is the path to the goal. «هيأت الشيء أصلحته والإصلاح نقيض الإفساد»
#193 BURSEVI d.1127 · التأويلات النجمية · reports · The cave youths fled Daqyanus, while seekers in retreat long for encounter with God; they seek rescue from the self and escape from existence’s darkness into divine beauty and majesty. «آووا إلى كهف الخلوة شوقا إلى لقائي وفرارا إلي» [filed under 18:10, 18:12]
#194 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · إذ may attach to عجبا or كانوا, or to an omitted اذكر; it cannot modify حسبت because the speaker's perception was not simultaneous with the youths' retreat. «ولا يجوز أن يكون ظرفا لحسبت لأن حسبانه لم يكن في ذلك الوقت»
#195 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · أبو رجاء · reports · Abu Rajaʾ reads رشدا with ḍamma on rāʾ and a still shīn; the meaning is the same as the common reading, which better fits the verse endings. «وقرأ أبو رجاء ( رشدا ) بضم الراء وإسكان الشين والمعنى واحد»
#196 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · ابن عطية · reports · Ibn Atiyya says the youths' prayer concerns worldly affairs because of its wording and urges believers to use this verse for worldly supplication; al-Alusi rejects the restriction and reads the prayer broadly for both worlds. «إن هذا الدعاء منهم كان في أمر دنياهم وألفاظه تقتضي ذلك»
#197 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The fronted phrases emphasize the desired favor and make the petition's object of desire clear from the beginning; the construction may also be rhetorical extraction, meaning make all our affair right. «وتقديم المجرورين على المفعول الصريح لإظهار الاعتناء بهما وإبراز الرغبة»
#198 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The فتية are the Companions of the Cave, taking the cave as their dwelling and refuge. «الفتية إلى الكهف ( واتخذوه مأوى ومكانا لهم»
#199 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · الراغب وغيره · reports · The plural فتية denotes young, fresh youths; other authorities call it a collective noun, while most treat it as the plural of فتى by analogy with صبي and صبية. «وهو كما قال الراغب وغيره الطري من الشبان ويجمع أيضا على فتيان»
#200 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · They are said to have been young sons of Roman nobles and grandees, wearing gold collars and bracelets and having forelocks. «فقد روي أنهم كانوا شبانا من أبناء أشراف الروم وعظمائهم مطوقين مسورين بالذهب»
#201 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The explicit noun rather than a pronoun highlights their youthful state; another explanation is that cave ownership results from their retreat and should not be presupposed before it is stated. «وإيثار الإظهار على الإضمار لتحقيق ما كانوا عليه في أنفسهم من حال الفتوة»
