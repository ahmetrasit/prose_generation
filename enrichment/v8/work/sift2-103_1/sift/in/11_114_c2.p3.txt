#121 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · الزمخشري · reports · Al-Zamakhshari allows coordination with الصلاة, meaning night prayers that draw worshippers near to God. «قال الزمخشري: بعد أن ذكر القراءات المتقدمة " وهو ما يقرب من آخر النهار ومن الليل»
#122 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn ʿAbbas, Hasan, and ʿAbd al-Rahman ibn Zayd identify the two ends of day as morning and sunset prayers. «قال علي بن أبي طلحة، عن ابن عباس: {وأقم الصلاة طرفي النهار} قال: يعني الصبح والمغرب»
#123 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · الحسن · reports · Hasan in another report, Qatada, al-Dahhak, and others identify the prayers as dawn and afternoon. «هي الصبح والعصر»
#124 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · مجاهد · reports · Mujahid, Muhammad ibn Kaʿb, and al-Dahhak in another report identify them as dawn, noon, and afternoon prayers. «وقال مجاهد: هي الصبح في أول النهار، والظهر والعصر من آخره»
#125 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn ʿAbbas, Mujahid, Hasan, and others take the night approaches to mean the night prayer. «يعني صلاة العشاء»
#126 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · الحسن · reports · Hasan in another report, along with Mujahid, Muhammad ibn Kaʿb, Qatada, and al-Dahhak, identifies them as sunset and night prayers; a prophetic report calls these the night's two approaches. «هما زلفتا (1) الليل: المغرب والعشاء»
#127 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · holds · The verse may predate the five daily prayers prescribed on the Night Journey: initially two daily prayers and night vigil were required, then night vigil was abrogated for the community, remained for the Prophet, and, in one view, was later abrogated for him too. «وقد يحتمل أن تكون هذه الآية نزلت قبل فرض الصلوات الخمس ليلة الإسراء»
#128 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · holds · Good deeds erase earlier sins, as shown by reports that a sinner who performs ablution and prays two units is forgiven. «إن فعل الخيرات يكفر الذنوب السالفة»
#129 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · علي بن أبي طالب · reports · The Prophet says a Muslim who sins, performs ablution, and prays two rakʿas is forgiven; Abu Bakr affirmed that he heard this from the Prophet. «سمع رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم يقول: "ما من مسلم يذنب ذنبا، فيتوضأ ويصلي ركعتين، إلا غفر له»
#130 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · عثمان بن عفان · reports · In the two Sahih collections, Uthman demonstrates the Prophet's ablution and says that performing it and praying two units without distracting thoughts forgives prior sins. «وقال: "من توضأ نحو وضوئي هذا، ثم صلى ركعتين لا يحدث فيهما نفسه، غفر له ما تقدم من ذنبه»
#131 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · عثمان بن عفان · reports · Another report from Uthman says each prayer forgives sins committed since the previous prayer, and ablution and dawn prayer forgive the night; these are good deeds that erase sins. «وهن الحسنات يذهبن السيئات»
#132 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · أبو هريرة · reports · The Prophet compares five daily prayers to bathing five times in a river at one's door: just as dirt would not remain, the prayers erase sins. «وكذلك الصلوات الخمس، يمحو الله بهن الذنوب والخطايا»
#133 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · أبو هريرة · reports · The five prayers, one Friday to the next, and one Ramadan to the next expiate sins between them when major sins are avoided. «أن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم كان يقول: "الصلوات الخمس، والجمعة إلى الجمعة، ورمضان إلى رمضان، مكفرات ما بينهن إذا (3) اجتنبت الكبائر"»
#134 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · أبو أيوب الأنصاري · reports · The Prophet says each prayer removes the sins that preceded it. «إن كل صلاة تحط ما بين يديها من خطيئة»
#135 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · أبو مالك الأشعري · reports · The Prophet says prayers were made expiations for what lies between them, citing the verse that good deeds remove evil deeds. «عن أبي مالك الأشعري قال: قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم "جعلت الصلوات كفارات لما بينهن؛ فإن الله قال»
#136 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · عبد الله بن مسعود · reports · In one account a man confesses kissing a woman; the Prophet recites the verse and says its promise applies to all his community. «عن ابن مسعود؛ أن رجلا أصاب من امرأة قبلة»
#137 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · عبد الله بن مسعود · reports · In another account the man describes kissing and embracing a woman without intercourse; after the Prophet recalls him and recites the verse, ʿUmar or Muʿadh asks whether it is only for him, and the Prophet says it is for everyone. «جاء رجل إلى النبي (1) صلى الله عليه وسلم فقال: يا رسول الله»
#138 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم · reports · The Prophet says God distributes character as He distributes provision: He grants worldly life to those He loves and those He does not, but grants faith only to those He loves; faith requires a surrendered heart and tongue and a neighbor safe from harm. «قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم: "إن الله قسم بينكم أخلاقكم (3) كما قسم بينكم أرزاقكم»
#139 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم · reports · The same report says illicit wealth is not blessed or accepted as charity and becomes provision for Hell; God erases evil with good, since evil cannot erase evil. «إن الله لا يمحو السيئ بالسيئ، ولكنه يمحو السيئ بالحسن»
#140 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · إبراهيم · reports · Ibrahim reports an unnamed Ansari who had sexual contact with a woman short of intercourse; the Prophet could not answer until this verse was revealed, then recited it to him. «عن إبراهيم قال: كان فلان ابن معتب رجلا من الأنصار، فقال»
#141 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · ابن عباس · reports · Reports differ over the man's identity: Ibn ʿAbbas names ʿAmr ibn Ghaziya al-Ansari al-Tammar; Muqatil says Abu Nufayl ʿAmir ibn Qays; al-Khatib says Abu al-Yusr Kaʿb ibn ʿAmr. «وعن ابن عباس: أنه عمرو بن غزية الأنصاري التمار»
#142 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · ابن عباس · reports · A man who had done less than intercourse with a woman brought his case first to ʿUmar and Abu Bakr, then the Prophet; after the verse came, ʿUmar said its remedy was for everyone, and the Prophet affirmed him. «بل للناس عامة. فقال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم: "صدق عمر"»
#143 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · أبو اليسر كعب بن عمرو الأنصاري · reports · A woman came to buy dates; Abu al-Yusr invited her inside and kissed her, then confessed to ʿUmar, Abu Bakr, and the Prophet, who reproached him for betraying a man away fighting; Gabriel brought the verse, which applies to all. «أتتني امرأة تبتاع مني بدرهم تمرا»
#144 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · معاذ بن جبل · reports · A man who touched an unlawful woman in every way short of intercourse was told by the Prophet to make a careful ablution and pray; the verse came, and the Prophet said it applied to all Muslims. «فقال له النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم: "توضأ وضوءا حسنا، ثم قم فصل"»
#145 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · يحيى بن جعدة · reports · A mursal report tells of a Companion who found a woman at a pool, touched her short of intercourse, regretted it, and confessed; the Prophet told him to seek forgiveness and pray four units, then recited the verse. «فأخبره بما صنع، فقال له: "استغفر ربك، وصل أربع ركعات"»
#146 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · أبو أمامة · reports · A man asking to receive God's prescribed penalty was ignored until after prayer; the Prophet asked whether he had performed ablution and prayed with them, then said he was cleansed like a newborn and must not repeat the sin. «فإنك من خطيئتك كما ولدتك أمك، ولا تعد»
#147 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · سلمان الفارسي · reports · The Prophet shook a dry branch so its leaves fell and likened this to a Muslim whose careful ablution and five prayers shed sins; he recited the verse. «عن أبي عثمان قال: كنت مع سلمان الفارسي تحت شجرة، فأخذ منها غصنا يابسا»
#148 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · معاذ بن جبل · reports · The Prophet tells Muʿadh to follow a bad deed with a good one that erases it, and to treat people with good character. «عن معاذ، رضي الله عنه؛ أن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم قال له: "يا معاذ، أتبع السيئة الحسنة تمحها»
#149 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · أبو ذر · reports · The Prophet gives Abu Dharr the same counsel: fear God everywhere, follow a bad deed with a good one, and treat people well. «وقال الإمام أحمد، رضي الله عنه: حدثنا وكيع، حدثنا سفيان، عن حبيب، عن ميمون بن أبي شبيب، عن أبي ذر»
#150 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · أبو ذر · reports · The Prophet advises Abu Dharr to follow every sin with a good deed to erase it and says that proclaiming there is no god but God is the best of good deeds. «هي أفضل الحسنات»
#151 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · أنس بن مالك · reports · A report says whoever says there is no god but God at any hour has the sins in the record erased until good deeds balance them; Ibn Kathir notes that Uthman al-Waqqasi is weak. «عن أنس بن مالك قال: قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم: "ما قال عبد: لا إله إلا الله، في ساعة من ليل»
#152 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · أنس · reports · A man says he has left no need or small matter undone; the Prophet asks whether he testifies that there is no god but God and that Muhammad is His Messenger, and says this testimony covers it all; this route is unique through Mastur. «قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم: "تشهد أن لا إله إلا الله وأني رسول الله؟»
#153 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · Zulaf are groups, stages, or periods of night; they may mean the early obligatory night vigil, or Maghrib and Isha with witr or night vigil. «يمكن أن يكون المراد به التهجد , فقد كان مفروضا في أول الإسلام»
#154 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · All acts of obedience grounded in faith, including prayer, erase minor sins; major sins require repentance, while merely avoiding them expiates only with a sound intention. «الطاعات كلها الصلاة وغيرها المبنية على أساس الإيمان»
#155 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · Prayer is the highest expiation for the weakness and lassitude that cause minor sins, and after faith it is the best act of worship and the broadest aid to uprightness. «وذلك يدل على أنها بعد الإيمان أفضل العبادات»
#156 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · The two ends of the day mean morning and afternoon prayers, as prescribed in Mecca before the Night Journey; noon may also be intended as the second end. «بالصبح والعصر كما كان مفروضا بمكة في أول الأمر قبل الإسراء»
