#50 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · Alternatively, min is origination and the omitted object is Islam or confirmation of recompense, rooted in the heart; it may also indicate proving sincerity to other believers. «وقد يجعل مفعول تثبيتا محذوفا أي تثبيتا للإسلام وتحقيقا للجزاء من أصل أنفسهم وقلوبهم»
#51 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · مجاهد · reports · Mujāhid’s reading tabyīnan, ‘making clear,’ supports interpreting the phrase as showing others one’s sincere faith. «ويعضده قراءة مجاهد وتبيينا من أنفسهم»
#52 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · أبو علي الجبائي · reports · Abū ʿAlī al-Jubbāʾī takes min to mean li, so the phrase describes steadying oneself for obedience to God; the author finds this plausible. «بمعنى اللام والمعنى توطينا لأنفسهم على طاعة الله تعالى»
#53 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · Spending purifies the donor from miserliness and love of wealth, described as a grave disease and root of every sin. «وفيه تنبيه على أن حكمة الإنفاق للمنفق تزكية للنفس عن البخل وحب المال»
#54 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · A garden on elevated ground has especially attractive trees and wholesome fruit because its air is mild and does not stagnate densely. «وأعتبر كونها في ربوة لأن أشجار الربى تكون أحسن منظرا وأزكى ثمرا للطافة هوائها»
#55 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · ابن عامر، عاصم · reports · Ibn ʿĀmir and ʿĀṣim read rabwa with fatḥ; most read ḍamm, Ibn ʿAbbās reads kasr, and another form is rabāwa. «وقرأ ابن عامر وعاصم بربوة بالفتح والباقون بالضم وابن عباس بالكسر وقرئ رباوة»
#56 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · A variant reads ḥabba, ‘grain,’ instead of janna, ‘garden.’ «وقرئ كمثل حبة بالحاء والباء»
#57 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · The doubled yield may mean repeated abundance, twice the ordinary crop after heavy rain, four times if ḍiʿf means double, or two harvests in one year. «فالتثنية للتكثير أو مثلي ما كانت تثمر»
#58 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · أبو عمرو، ابن كثير، نافع · reports · Abū ʿAmr, Ibn Kathīr, and Nāfiʿ read the kāf in akl with sukūn as a lightened pronunciation. «وقرأ أبو عمرو وابن كثير ونافع بسكون الكاف تخفيفا»
#59 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · Even light drizzle suffices for a good garden on high ground, so sincere spending never fails to yield reward, though sincerity, effort, love of wealth, and the recipient’s need affect its degree. «والمراد أن خيرها لا يخلف على حال لجودنها وكرم منبتها ولطافة هوائها»
#60 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · The analogy may be composite, matching pure growing expenditure with a pure fruitful garden under either rain, or differentiated, matching the donors and their small or great gifts with garden and rain. «فهناك تشبيه حال النفقة النامية»
#61 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · On the composite analysis the common feature is growth joined with purity; on the differentiated analysis, either the expenditure or the donor is the main comparison, and scholars prefer different constructions. «وهذا من التشبيه المركب العقلي ولك أن تعتبر تشبيه حال أولئك عند الله تعالى بالجنة على الربوة»
#62 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · God sees every deed and rewards the sincere while warning the ostentatious giver who seeks the gaze of those whose view cannot help, instead of God’s decisive sight. «فيجازي كلا من المخلص والمرائي بما هو أعلم به ففي الجملة ترغيب للأول وترهيب للثاني»
#63 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The sincere donor seeks God's pleasure and uses wealth to steady themselves, their deeds, and their companions against corrupt inclinations and instability, making good works a settled habit. «hayr ü hasenâtı kendilerine meleke-i sâbite kılmak ve ondan sonra her nevi fezâil ve ibâdâtı suhûletle yapmak»
#64 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · A garden on a high hill yields twice its usual fruit under a heavy rain; the earlier rain that stripped rock becomes mercy here. «O kayayı cascavlak bırakan yağmur, bu tepede ‘ayn-ı rahmet olur»
#65 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The garden's doubled yield does not reduce the earlier promise of seven hundredfold; it adds another multiplication. «Bu temsil bâlâdaki bire yedi yüz ve daha kat kat vaadini tenzil etmiş değil, belki bir daha katlamıştır»
#66 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Even without a heavy downpour, a light drizzle or slight moisture is enough for the garden to produce its yield. «hafif bir yağmur, az bir nem de yetişir, vereceğini yine verir»
#67 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Because God sees and knows deeds, donors must avoid showing off and remain sincere in private and public. «Sakın gösteriş yapmayın, gizlide âşikârda ihlâstan ayrılmayın»
#68 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The sincere spender's garden is set against the show-off spender's image to make the distance between the ranks vivid and renew praise; a more intricate picture beautifies its subject. «لزيادة بيان ما بين المرتبتين من البون»
#69 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The earlier seed image is simple for swiftly picturing multiplied reward; this garden image is more involved because several favorable features enhance the scene and its analogue. «ومثله فيما سلف تمثيلا غير كثير التركيب لتحصل السرعة بتخيل مضاعفة الثواب»
#70 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · ابتغاء مرضاة الله and تثبيتا are circumstantial accusatives, meaning seeking God's pleasure and strengthening themselves; تثبيت depicts restraining inner hesitation and stinginess. «على الحال بتأويل المصدر بالوصف»
#71 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Repeated practice of difficult virtues makes them firm habits; spending especially strengthens obedience because wealth is precious, and من marks a figurative partiality among states of the self. «فإن إراضة النفس على فعل ما يشق عليها لها أثر في رسوخ الأعمال حتى تعتاد الفضائل»
#72 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The dual ضعفين indicates repetition rather than exactly two, like لبيك: the garden yields multiplied produce in varying measure. «التثنية فيه لمجرد التكرير مثل لبيك»
#73 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Even without heavy rain, light rain yields a lesser crop; sincere spending brings great reward that varies with sincerity and self-strengthening but never disappoints its giver. «أي فإن لم يصبها مطر غزير كفاها مطر قليل»
#74 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The plural form 'helpers' hints that there are supporters in cases of wrongdoing; the excerpt begins mid-discussion, so its precise antecedent is not preserved here. «ومن هنا يظهر وجه إتيان الأنصار بصيغة الجمع فإن في مورد مطلق الظلم أنصارا» [filed under 2:261-274]
#75 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · Withholding spending is a grave wrong that is not expiated or waived as a minor lapse; repentance concerning people's rights requires returning the right to its owner. «وهو ترك الإنفاق لا يقبل التكفير ولو كان من الصغائر لقبله فهو من الكبائر» [filed under 2:261-274]
#76 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The author says this wrong receives no intercession at the Resurrection, citing the question to the people of Saqar and their failure to feed the poor, followed by the denial that intercession helps them. «فما تنفعهم شفاعة الشافعين» [filed under 2:261-274]
#77 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · Because such a wrongdoer is not approved by God and intercession is only for those whose religion He approves, the wording 'seeking God's pleasure' is significant. «إذ لا شفاعة إلا لمن ارتضى الله دينه» [filed under 2:261-274]
#78 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · Refusing all aid to present, needy poor is a destructive major sin; 41:7 describes withholding zakat as associationism and disbelief in the afterlife, although that Meccan verse predates the formal zakat system. «وويل للمشركين الذين لا يؤتون الزكاة وهم بالآخرة هم كافرون» [filed under 2:261-274]
#79 TABATABAI d.1402 · الصادق عليه السلام · reports · Al-Qummī glosses ṣafwān as a large rock, wābil as rain, tall as nighttime dew on plants, and iʿṣār as winds. «والصفوان هي الصخرة الكبيرة التي تكون في المفازة» [filed under 2:264, 2:265, 2:266]
#80 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The connected themes suggest this passage was revealed as one unit: spending grows seven hundredfold or more, sincere giving bears fruit, ostentation and reminders or harm ruin reward, good wealth should be given, the poor detained in God's path are recipients, and the reward is great. «اتحادها في بيان أمر الانفاق» [filed under 2:261, 2:262, 2:263, 2:264, 2:265, 2:266, 2:267, 2:268, 2:269, 2:270, 2:271, 2:272, 2:273, 2:274]
#81 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The passage orders its teaching around purpose (giving for God, not people), manner (without reproach or injury), quality (good rather than bad wealth), recipient (the needy constrained in God's path), and reward. «وهو أن يكون لله لا للناس» [filed under 2:261, 2:262, 2:263, 2:264, 2:265, 2:266, 2:267, 2:268, 2:269, 2:270, 2:271, 2:272, 2:273, 2:274]
#82 TABATABAI d.1402 · prefers · Tathbīt is the self's resolve to keep itself firm in the pure intention it formed; min marks its source, anfus is the agent, and the implied object is the self itself. «والتقدير تثبيتا من أنفسهم لأنفسهم، أو مفعول مطلق لفعل من مادته»
#83 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · Rabwa derives from growth and means good elevated land; akl is what is eaten, and tall is weak rain with little effect. «والربوة بالحركات الثلاث في الراء الأرض الجيدة التي تزيد وتعلو»
#84 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The garden parable says sincere spending unfailingly bears good fruit because divine care remains attached to it, though yield varies with the purity of intention and the quality of the rain. «فإن العناية الإلهية واقعة عليه متعلقة به لانحفاظ اتصاله بالله سبحانه»
#85 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · God's seeing means He distinguishes differing deeds and rewards without confusing one person's reward with another's. «ولا يختلط عليه ثواب الأعمال المختلفة فيعطي ثواب هذا لذاك»
#86 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · Seeking God's pleasure means intending His face: for the commanded servant, God's 'face' is His satisfaction with obedience after the command first addresses the servant. «فابتغاء مرضاة الله هو إرادة وجهه عز وجل»
#87 TABATABAI d.1402 · قيل · reports · One reported interpretation takes 'stabilizing themselves' to mean assent and certainty. «قيل إن المراد التصديق واليقين»
