#167 TABATABAI d.1402 · rejects · The author says interpreters wrongly treat the dream as a forecast of seven fertile then seven barren years; Joseph instead states the required policy for surviving the anticipated famine. «لم يبن كلامه على أساس إخبارهم بما سيستقبلهم من السنين السبع المخصبة ثم السنين السبع المجدبة» [filed under 12:47, 12:48, 12:49]
#168 TABATABAI d.1402 · تفسير القمي · reports · Al-Qummī reports the king’s dream of seven fat cows eaten by seven lean cows and seven green ears with dry ones; the cupbearer recalled it after a time, and Joseph interpreted seven plentiful years, seven famine years, then rain. «ثم إن الملك رأى رؤيا فقال لوزرائه إني رأيت في نومي سبع بقرات سمان» [filed under 12:45, 12:46, 12:47, 12:48, 12:49]
#169 TABATABAI d.1402 · أبو عبد الله عليه السلام · reports · Al-Qummī’s report has al-Ṣādiq explain that harvest should remain in its ears through seven years so it keeps, with only a little eaten. «قال يوسف تزرعون سبع سنين دأبا فما حصدتم فذروه في سنبله إلا قليلا مما تأكلون»
#170 TABATABAI d.1402 · أبو عبد الله عليه السلام · reports · Al-Ṣādiq’s reported wording is “what you bring near for them” rather than “what you laid up”; the commentary glosses this as approximating the harvest in revelation’s ordering. «وقوله عليه السلام إنما نزل ما قربتم لهن أي إن التقديم بحسب التنزيل بمعنى التقريب»
#171 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · Joseph interpreted Pharaoh's dream as seven productive years followed by famine, proposed storing surplus grain, and was appointed to Egypt's stores after the women and al-ʿAzīz's wife cleared him; prison thus led to kingship. «وفي هذه السنين انتصب يوسف لمقام عزة مصر» [filed under 12:42-57]

#173 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · Tazraʿūn is grammatically a declarative, but it functions as an imperative, a style suited to advice and guidance and paralleled elsewhere in the Qur'an. «occurs as an enunciative (khabar) but implies the imperative.»
#174 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · Joseph supplied a practical drought strategy in addition to interpreting the dream. «not only told them the interpretation of the dream but also guided them to a practical suggestion to combat the seven years of drought.»
#175 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · Leaving grain in its ears protects it from termites, insects, and other damage while it is stored. «as long as the corn is in the ears, they remain secure from termite, insects and other calamities.»
#176 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · holds · Joseph forecasts seven years of abundance and advises sowing grain annually, setting aside food and seed, and storing the remaining harvest in its ears. «her sene bolca hububat ekmelerini, kaldıracakları ürünlerden sadece yiyeceklerini ve tohumluklarını ayırıp kalanları başak halinde depolamalarını tavsiye etti»
#177 QIRAAT-ER · holds · The standard form fully vocalizes daʾab with hamza fatḥa and tanwīn. «Standard canonical form — hamza with fatḥa + tanwīn; full voweling of daʾab»
#178 QIRAAT-ER · holds · Sukūn on hamza removes its following vowel without changing meaning. «Variant with sukūn on hamza (daʾban) — elision of second vowel; same meaning»
#179 QIRAAT-ER · holds · Another variant drops hamza entirely as a phonological simplification. «Variant dropping hamza entirely → dāban; phonological simplification»
#180 QIRAAT-ER · holds · The pausal form is dābā, followed by daʾban on resuming recitation. «Pausal form dābā with subsequent recitation as daʾban — phonological pause variant»
