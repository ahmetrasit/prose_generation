#64 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The Qurʾān omits names, ancestry, birth, upbringing, occupations, social standing, dates, rulers, cities, persecutors, and the dog’s identity because guidance does not depend on them; historians and early exegetes filled these gaps, producing divergent accounts. «إلى غير ذلك من الأمور التي لا يتوقف غرض الهداية على العلم بشيء منها كما يتوقف عليه غرض البحث التاريخي» [filed under 18:9, 18:10, 18:11, 18:12, 18:13, 18:14, 18:16, 18:17, 18:18, 18:19, 18:21, 18:25, 18:26]
#65 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The narrative presents the youths sleeping in the cave with their dog for the stated long period, protected by the cave’s position and God’s care. «ثم دخلوا الكهف واستقروا على فجوة منه وكلبهم باسط ذراعيه بالوصيد» [filed under 18:10, 18:11, 18:17, 18:18, 18:25]
#66 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · After 309 years God raised the youths to show how He had rescued them; they awoke disoriented, and some guessed they had slept a day or part of one while others deferred to God’s knowledge. «بعثهم بعد هذا الدهر الطويل وهو ثلاثمائة وتسع سنين من يوم دخلوا الكهف» [filed under 18:12, 18:19, 18:25]
#67 TABATABAI d.1402 · أهل الرواية والتاريخ · reports · Most historians date the story between Jesus and Muḥammad; it is absent from the two Testaments, though some Muslim reports say Quraysh heard it from Jews, while Christians preserved it extensively. «معظم أهل الرواية والتاريخ على أن القصة وقعت في الفترة بين النبي صلى الله عليه وآله وسلم وبين المسيح» [filed under 18:9, 18:10, 18:11, 18:12, 18:13, 18:14, 18:16, 18:17, 18:18, 18:19, 18:21, 18:25, 18:26]
#68 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · Christian accounts resemble Ibn Isḥāq’s report from Ibn ʿAbbās in some respects but differ from Muslims on the number of sleepers, whether they had a dog, and the length of their sleep. «أن المصادر السريانية تذكر عدد أصحاب الكهف ثمانية في حين يذكره المسلمون» [filed under 18:9, 18:10, 18:11, 18:12, 18:13, 18:14, 18:16, 18:17, 18:18, 18:19, 18:21, 18:25, 18:26]
#69 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The author explains the Christian estimate of at most two centuries by identifying the persecutor as Decius and dating the awakening to 425, 437, or 439 CE; he says James of Sarūg first recorded this chronology. «أن الطاغية الذي كان يجبر الناس على عبادة الأصنام وقد هرب منه الفتية هو دقيوس الملك» [filed under 18:9, 18:25]
#70 TABATABAI d.1402 · prefers · After centuries as a ruined, forgotten site, Jordanian excavation uncovered Rujayb; the author considers its archaeological signs and some Muslim reports the strongest fit for the Qurʾānic cave. «وقامت عدة من الأمارات والشواهد الأثرية على كونه هو كهف أصحاب الكهف المذكورين في القرآن» [filed under 18:9, 18:10, 18:11, 18:12, 18:13, 18:14, 18:16, 18:17, 18:18, 18:19, 18:21, 18:25, 18:26]
#71 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · Amman stands on the site of Philadelphia, a major and beautiful city before Islam that remained under Roman control from the early second century until the Muslim conquest. «كانت هي وما والاها تحت استيلاء الروم منذ أوائل القرن الثاني الميلادي حتى فتح المسلمون الأرض المقدسة» [filed under 18:9, 18:10, 18:11, 18:12, 18:13, 18:14, 18:16, 18:17, 18:18, 18:19, 18:21, 18:25, 18:26]
#72 TABATABAI d.1402 · reports · Other proposed locations include a cave on Mount Qāsiyūn near Damascus, one at Petra, a Scandinavian cave with seven uncorrupted Roman-looking bodies, and a cave near Nakhchivan. «والكهف الثالث كهف بجبل قاسيون بالقرب من الصالحية بدمشق الشام» [filed under 18:9, 18:10, 18:11, 18:12, 18:13, 18:14, 18:16, 18:17, 18:18, 18:19, 18:21, 18:25, 18:26]
#73 TABATABAI d.1402 · prefers · No evidence identifies any proposed cave as the Qurʾānic one; the Scandinavian and Caucasus claims conflict with the story’s Roman setting and the limits of Roman power. «ولا شاهد يشهد على كون شيء من هذه الكهوف هو الكهف المذكور في القرآن الكريم» [filed under 18:9, 18:10, 18:11, 18:12, 18:13, 18:14, 18:16, 18:17, 18:18, 18:19, 18:21, 18:25, 18:26]
#74 ASAD-NOTES d.1412 · holds · Asad reads the three hundred years and added nine as a report of idle guesses, refuted by God's statements in verses 22 and 26. «mentioned in the first paragraph of verse 22 above - guesses refuted by the subsequent statements»

#77 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · The cave-dwellers passage answers an objection from the Prophet’s opponents and presents their real story without needless details. «their real life aside from needless details becomes evident» [filed under 18:9-26]
#78 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · The youths’ trials parallel the Prophet and his companions’ preaching phase; perseverance brings God’s easing of hardship and provision, and He does not let His persevering servants lose their way. «God will ease out their hardships the way He did so for them» [filed under 18:9-26]
#79 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · This overview is offered as sufficient to establish the sūrah’s coherence and central theme before its detailed exegesis. «This concise analysis of the meanings of the sūrah is sufficient to explain its coherence and central theme» [filed under 18:1-110]
#80 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · The cave-dwellers question was planted by the People of the Book and posed by Quraysh to harass and corner the Prophet, not to seek truth. «These irrelevant questions were obviously not meant to find out the real truth» [filed under 18:9-26]
#81 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · Opponents planned to exploit either an inability to answer or an answer conflicting with their own beliefs. «if the Prophet (sws) was able to answer them and the answer was against their beliefs» [filed under 18:9-26]
#82 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · The Qur’an generally counsels ignoring such questions, but answers when useful; the truthful account both answers and trains the Prophet and companions in perseverance during comparable trials. «if some question is beneficial or its answer can be beneficial for education» [filed under 18:9-26]
#83 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · The Qur’an preserves the useful, true part of the cave story, correcting false accretions and restoring a major chapter in truth-seekers’ history. «a very important chapter in the history of the seekers of the truth was cleansed from all sorts of wrong details» [filed under 18:9-26]
#84 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · The dwellers’ identities, date and ancestry cannot be known securely; beyond the Qur’anic account, claims are folk tales, and further inquiry is discouraged. «Who were the cave-dwellers, when did they inhabit the earth and whose ancestors were they are questions that cannot be answered with certainty» [filed under 18:9-26]
#85 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · Islahi’s opinion is that the youths were ancestors of Christians, not Quraysh or Jews, and belonged to the early Christian period under Roman persecution. «in my opinion, they belonged to the initial period of the Christians when they were persecuted by the Romans» [filed under 18:9-26]
#86 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · He says they boldly preached monotheism and accountability, faced imminent stoning, then took refuge in a cave where God supplied extraordinary protection. «They fearlessly invited people to monotheism and warned them of the accountability in the hereafter» [filed under 18:9-26]
#87 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · After their circumstances became known, groups built memorials and claimed them; Christian retellings gradually reduced their story to miracles and unsupported details. «Gradually, this tradition became confined to some miraculous feats and baseless details about them» [filed under 18:9-26]
#88 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · Debates over their number and dog obscured their message and sacrifices; Islahi says the true history was later lost as Christianity’s teachings were distorted. «no one knew what their real message was and the extent of sacrifices they gave» [filed under 18:9-26]
#89 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · Distorted reports about the youths circulated among Arab Christians, who prompted the question; the Qur’an answered to reassure the Prophet and companions in adversity. «he could receive great assurance and strength in the adverse circumstances he and his companions were passing through» [filed under 18:9-26]
#90 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · The youths’ persecution and cave refuge parallel the Prophet and companions’ trials and their coming migrations to Abyssinia, the Thawr cave and Madinah. «the Prophet (sws) and his companions too had to face tremendous trials at the hands of the Quraysh» [filed under 18:9-26]
#91 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · The parenthetical discussion in verses 22–24 ends here; this statement returns to the earlier estimates of the cave-dwellers' number. «The parenthetical sentence that began with verse 22 ended on verse 24» [filed under 18:25, 18:22]
#92 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · müfessirler · reports · In interpretation A, verse 25 reports what people said: some claimed 300 years in the cave and others 309, just as they differed over the youths’ number. «25. âyet, daha önce Ashâb-ı Kehf’in sayıları hakkında farklı şeyler söyleyenlerin sözüdür.» [filed under 18:25, 18:26]
#93 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · İbn Mes‘ûd · reports · Ibn Masud’s reading adds ‘and they said’ at the beginning of the verse, supporting interpretation A. «İbn Mes‘ûd’un, âyetin başına “ve kalû” (ve dediler ki) cümlesini ilâve ederek okuması»
