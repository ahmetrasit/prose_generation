[ELMALILI:103:1-3#4/r6] ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · A hadith compares prior peoples' work periods to morning and midday, and the Muslim community's work from asr to sunset, for which God grants twice the wage after the Jews and Christians object. «Sizden evvel geçmişlere nisbetle sizin vaktiniz, ikindi namazıyla gurûb-ı şems arası kadardır»
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#4/r7] ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · Some exegetes use this hadith to identify asr with the time of the Prophet and the community praised as the best brought forth for humanity. «Bir kısım müfessirîn bu hadis ile istidlâl ederek demişlerdir ki: Bu hadis»
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#4/r8] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · As Allah swore by the Prophet's location and life, this surah swears by his era. «diye mekânına ve لَعَمْرُكَ [el-Hicr 15/72] diye ömrüne kasem buyurduğu gibi burada da asrına kasem buyurmuştur»
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#4/r9] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The oath encompasses every meaning of asr, especially the final era as the afternoon of time and the age of Muhammad, before the declaration that humankind is in loss. «Her mânasıyla asra ve bâhusus pek büyük hâdisâta» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
[ELMALILI:103:1-3#6/r14] ELMALILI d.1361 · Ebû Medîne · reports · He reports that two of the Prophet's Companions would not part after meeting until they recited Sūrat al-ʿAṣr to each other and then exchanged greetings. «Ebû Medîne’den[^57]-ki sohbeti vardır- şöyle dediğini rivayet eylemişlerdir» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
[ELMALILI:103:1-3/r1] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The rendering says: By the age; humankind is in absolute loss, except those who believe, do righteous works, counsel one another to truth, and counsel patience. «Kasem olsun ki asra (1) insan mutlak bir hüsranda (2)» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
[ELMALILI:103:1-3/r10] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Prophetic reports call the delayed middle prayer the asr prayer and warn that missing asr is like losing one's family and property. «Hendek vak‘asında Aleyhissalâtü vesselâm شَغَلُونَا عَنِ الصَّلاَةِ الْوُسْطَى صَلاَةِ الْعَصْرِ buyurmuştu»
[ELMALILI:103:1-3/r11] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Afternoon prayer is especially attention-awakening because it interrupts the period when people are most absorbed in work and trade. «gündüzün âhiri olmak hasebiyle dünyada diğer mahlûkāt ve hayvânâtın yaratılışından»
[ELMALILI:103:1-3/r12] ELMALILI d.1361 · Katâde · reports · Qatada says Allah swears by afternoon time as a counterpart to duha, with signs of divine power. «Katâde demiştir ki: İkindi namazının vakti olmak ve “duhâ”ya mukabil birtakım delâil-i kudreti hâvî bulunmak»
[ELMALILI:103:1-3/r13] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Afternoon evokes humanity's creation late in the world's day, the closing period of human life, and the sorrow of returning without gain; this complements the previous surah's warning about rivalry. «dünyada bir gün mesabesinde demek olan insan ömrünün de son demlerini andırdığı» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
[ELMALILI:103:1-3/r14] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Dehr, in Raghib's definition, is the interval from the universe's beginning to its end; zaman can name that whole or any part, while dehr also means long duration, remaining lifetime, or lifespan. «“dehr”in asıl mânası Râgıb’ın dediği gibi»
[ELMALILI:103:1-3/r15] ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · Ibn Jarir transmits Ali's interpretive recitation: by time and its calamities, humankind remains in loss until time's end. «İbn Cerîr’in rivayet ettiği vechile Hazret-i Ali radıyallâhu anh’tan وَالْعَصْرِ وَنـَوَائِبِ الدَّهْرِ» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
[ELMALILI:103:1-3/r16] ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · This reading interprets asr as dehr, whose recurring calamities and changes warn people and whose loss continues to the end; squeezing or confinement may also reinforce the loss side. «İbn Cerîr’in rivayet ettiği vechile Hazret-i Ali radıyallâhu anh’tan» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
[ELMALILI:103:1-3/r17] ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · Another view says dehr contains every wonder that displays the Creator's power, where ordinary and extraordinary events, blessings, disasters, gains, and losses unfold. «Demek ki “asr”, dehr mânasınadır ve dehre kasemin hikmeti de onun insana» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
[ELMALILI:103:1-3/r18] ELMALILI d.1361 · Râzî · reports · Razi presents time as puzzling: it is divisible into past, present, and future, yet the present is indivisible and past and future are nonexistent, so time seems neither simply existent nor nonexistent. «Denilmiştir ki “dehr” Hâlık teâlâ’nın kudretine»
[ELMALILI:103:1-3/r19] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Philosophers and theologians have debated whether time exists, is nonexistent or imagined, and whether our knowledge of it is immediate or abstracted. «Râzî derki “akıl ona yok diye hükmedemez; çünkü devirler»
[ELMALILI:103:1-3/r2] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The opening waw marks an oath, as in Wad-Duha. «Vâv وَالضُّحٰى gibi kasem için»
[ELMALILI:103:1-3/r20] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · A cited Arab poet compares time to a ship carrying people toward death while its movement remains unseen. «Onun için felâsife ve mütekellimîn zaman mevcud mudur, ma‘dum mudur, mevhum mudur»
[ELMALILI:103:1-3/r3] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · As a noun, asr can mean the whole age, day and night, parts of the day, afternoon prayer or time, a group, and rain; as a verb it can mean restraining, preventing, taxing, or squeezing out juice. «Kāmûs’ta zikrolunduğu üzere “asr” kelimesi lügatta isim olarak dehr»
[ELMALILI:103:1-3/r4] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Vocalization variants asr, isr, and usr can mark a specific time; paired asran can refer to day and night or morning and evening. «Gündüzle geceye ve sabah ile akşama “asrân” denilir.»
[ELMALILI:103:1-3/r5] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · With the generic article, al-asr may include every sense; with the covenant article, it may identify one particular time. «Lâm-ı cins ile وَٱلْعَصْرِ makāmına göre bunların her birinin cinsine»
[ELMALILI:103:1-3/r6] ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · In Turkish usage asr commonly means time generally, the present era, or a period of eighty or a hundred years. «Ondan dolayı lisânımızda “asr” mutlak zaman ve bilhassa içinde bulunulan zaman»
[ELMALILI:103:1-3/r7] ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · Exegetes propose afternoon prayer, afternoon time, the age or time generally, Muhammad's era, and the end times. «müfessirîn başlıca ikindi namazı, ikindi vakti, dehr ve zaman, bâhusus asr-ı Muhammedî»
[ELMALILI:103:1-3/r8] ELMALILI d.1361 · İbn Cerîr · prefers · Ibn Jarir holds that asr is broad enough to include all its lexical senses, since the verse gives no reason to select one; the author endorses this inclusive reading. «Bu hususta savab olan kavil şudur: Rabbimiz teâlâ asra kasem etmiştir»
[ELMALILI:103:1-3/r9] ELMALILI d.1361 · Mukātil · reports · Muqatil says Allah swears by the afternoon prayer, whose special merit follows from the majority's identification of the middle prayer as asr. «Mukātil demiştir ki: Allah teâlâ ikindi namazına kasem buyurmuştur»

## QUTB-ZILAL: Sayyid Quṭb, d. 1386 AH
[QUTB-ZILAL:103:1/r1] QUTB-ZILAL d.1386 · holds · The surah is Meccan, has three verses, and compresses Islam's full way of life and the Muslim community's identity and role into a brief constitution. «سورة العصر مكية وآياتها ثلاث» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
[QUTB-ZILAL:103:1/r2] QUTB-ZILAL d.1386 · holds · The surah presents the Muslim community's essence and function in its final verse, a concise form of inimitable divine expression. «وتصف الأمة المسلمة : حقيقتها ووظيفتها . في آية واحدة هي الآية الثالثة من السورة» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
[QUTB-ZILAL:103:1/r3] QUTB-ZILAL d.1386 · holds · Across all ages there is one successful path: faith, righteous action, and mutual exhortation to truth and patience; every other way ends in loss. «ليس هنالك إلا منهج واحد رابح ، وطريق واحد ناج . هو ذلك المنهج الذي ترسم السورة حدوده» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
[QUTB-ZILAL:103:1/r46] QUTB-ZILAL d.1386 · الرجلان من أصحاب رسول الله · reports · Qutb reports that two Companions would not part after meeting until one recited Surat al-Asr to the other, renewing their commitment to its guidance. «كان الرجلان من أصحاب رسول الله [ صلى الله عليه وسلم ] إذا التقيا لم يتفرقا حتى يقرأ أحدهما على الآخر سورة» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
[QUTB-ZILAL:103:2/r1] QUTB-ZILAL d.1386 · holds · Qutb treats mutual exhortation to truth and patience as defining the Muslim community's distinct identity and bond. «أما التواصي بالحق والتواصي بالصبر فتبرز من خلالها صورة الأمة المسلمة - أو الجماعة المسلمة - ذات الكيان الخاص» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
[QUTB-ZILAL:103:2/r2] QUTB-ZILAL d.1386 · holds · Across all ages there is one successful path: faith, righteous action, and mutual exhortation to truth and patience; every other way ends in loss. «طريق واحد لا يتعدد . طريق الإيمان والعمل الصالح وقيام الجماعة المسلمة» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
[QUTB-ZILAL:103:2/r45] QUTB-ZILAL d.1386 · الرجلان من أصحاب رسول الله · reports · Qutb reports that two Companions would not part after meeting until one recited Surat al-Asr to the other, renewing their commitment to its guidance. «كان الرجلان من أصحاب رسول الله [ صلى الله عليه وسلم ] إذا التقيا لم يتفرقا حتى يقرأ أحدهما على الآخر سورة» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]

## IBNASHUR-FULL: Ibn ʿĀshūr, d. 1393 AH
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p528/r1] IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The oath by time confirms the statement, as Qur'anic oaths do, and its object is a sign of God's wondrous creation, power, and knowledge. «أقسم الله تعالى بالعصر قسما يراد به تأكيد الخبر»
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p528/r2] IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · “Time” has several possible referents; whichever is intended, its significance comes from being a period that manifests divine action, by contextual indication, addition, or shared reference. «وللعصر معانن يتعين أن يكون المراد منها»
