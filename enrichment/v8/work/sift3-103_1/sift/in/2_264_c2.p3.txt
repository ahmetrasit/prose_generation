#115 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · rejects · That answer means do not perform the act as an invalid gift, rather than canceling a valid accepted deed; the author admits this departs from the apparent wording, though the simile partly supports it. «حينئذ لا تأتوا بهذا العمل باطلا كذا قالوا»
#116 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The phrase comparing the donor to one who spends for show may modify an implied noun for nullifying or describe the acting donors’ state; the latter supports the proposed answer because ostentation is never accepted. «في محل نصب إما على أنه نعت لمصدر محذوف أي لا تبطلوها إبطالا كإبطال الذي الخ»
#117 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · Riyāʾ can be the purpose of spending or the donor’s state; its verbal form is reciprocal because the donor displays deeds and people return praise and honor. «إما على أنه علة لينفق أي لأجل ريائهم»
#118 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · الخزاعي، الشموني · reports · Al-Khuzāʿī and al-Shamūnī are among those who read the first hamza of riyāʾ lightly as a yāʾ to avoid the weight of a hamza after kasra. «ويجوز تخفيف الهمزة الأولى بأن تقلب ياءا فرارا من ثقل الهمزة بعد الكسرة وقد قرأ به الخزاعي والشموني»
#119 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · Some say the ostentatious donor may be a believer or unbeliever, but most commentators take him as a hypocrite because he does not believe in God and the Last Day. «والمراد من الموصول ما يشمل المؤمن والكافر كما قيل وغالب المفسرين على أن المراد به المنافق»
#120 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The ostentatious donor can attain no reward or benefit from what he spent; this either explains the comparison or answers an implied question about its outcome. «أي لا يجدون ثواب شيء مما أنفقوا رياءا»
#121 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · السمين · rejects · Al-Samīn’s suggestion that the clause is a circumstantial modifier of the relative antecedent is judged weak. «وجعلها حال من الذي كما قال : السمين مهزول من القول»
#122 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · The plural pronoun may refer to the singular-form relative by meaning; alternatives include collective usage or treating man and alladhī as interchangeable, while return to the believers is dismissed. «والضمير راجع إلى الموصول بإعتبار المعنى بعدما روعي لفظه إذ هو صفة لمفرد لفظا مجموع معنى»
#123 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · rejects · God’s statement that He does not guide the unbelieving people closes the argument and implies reproach, injury, and ostentation are traits believers must avoid. «وفيه تعريض بأن كلا من الرياء والمن والأذى على الإنفاق من صفات الكفار»
#124 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Believers are warned not to nullify charity by reminding recipients or hurting them, since either behavior removes its reward. «sadakalarınızı yüze vurmak, başa kakmakla ibtal etmeyiniz, bunlardan biriyle ecrini kesmeyiniz»
#125 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The insincere donor resembles a bare rock thinly covered with soil: a downpour strips it clean, leaving the apparent planting fruitless. «cascavlak bırakmış, bir toz bile kalmamış yalçın kayanın bu hâli gibidir»
#126 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The showoff who lacks belief in God and the Last Day gains nothing from the deed, just as charity poisoned by reminders or hurt earns nothing. «imansızlıkla infak, riyâ ile, menn ü ezâ ile sadaka verenler kesb ettikleri bu amelden hiçbir şey elde edemezler»
#127 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · God does not guide the disbelieving people to good; believers who make their charity like disbelievers' ostentatious spending also lose their reward. «sadakalarını menn ü ezâ ile kâfirlerin mürâiyâne sarfiyâtına benzeten mü’minler de onlar gibi ecirden mahrum kalırlar»
#128 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The discussion moves from spending in God's path to charity for needy individuals, where reminders and injury are more likely because donor and recipient know one another personally. «تخلص من غرض التنويه بالإنفاق في سبيل الله إلى التنويه بضرب آخر من الإنفاق» [filed under 2:263, 2:264]
#129 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The comparison phrase is a settled circumstantial expression describing the addressees: do not act like one who spends for people's eyes and disbelieves in God and the Last Day. «في إتباع صدقاتكم بالمن والأذى»
#130 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The relative expression denotes a whole class, not a particular person; the comparison vilifies the example and does not make the legal ruling identical, preserving other evidence. «مراد به جنس وليس مراد به معينا»
#131 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Riyāʾ is an intensive form from seeing, describing frequent display of good deeds; the hamza may be replaced by yāʾ after kasra, producing رياء. «وهو أن يكثر من إظهار أعماله الحسنة للناس»
#132 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Muslims who seek reward but follow charity with reminders and harm are compared with unbelievers who give only for show and praise, seeking no afterlife reward. «والمعنى تشبيه بعض المتصدقين المسلمين الذين يتصدقون طلبا للثواب ويعقبون صدقاتهم بالمن والأذى»
#133 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Neither group benefits beyond gratifying pride over the weak or the habit of harming them; the image of the rock and washed-away soil extends from unbelieving show-offs to harmful donors. «ووجه الشبه عدم الانتفاع مما أعطوا بأزيد من شفاء ما في صدورهم من حب التطاول»
#134 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The rock's thin soil looks fit for planting, but a downpour washes it away and leaves smooth bare stone, frustrating the farmer's hope; the image's pronoun can refer to the spender because the parable also depicts the analogue. «فحذفت صفة التراب إيجازا اعتمادا على أن التراب»
#135 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · لا يقدرون على شيء مما كسبوا is deliberately flexible: it may be a state of the spenders, of the figurative rock, an explanatory resumption, a summary, or a state with an omitted relative pronoun. «وصالحا لأن يجعل استينافا بيانيا»
#136 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · They cannot retrieve what they gave or benefit from its reward, so nothing remains for them. «لا يستطيعون أن يسترجعوه ولا انتفعوا بثوابه»
#137 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · reports · Another possible sense is that they fail to place their earnings where they belong, spending fruitlessly for the afterlife, as the next statement about disbelievers' guidance suggests. «ويجوز أن يكون المعنى لا يحسنون وضع شيء مما كسبوا موضعه»
#138 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The bare rock yields no crop, as in the description of a person turning his hands over what he spent; God's denial of guidance to disbelievers warns believers against adopting their reminders and harm. «مسوق لتحذير المؤمنين من تسرب أحوال الكافرين»
#139 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · prefers · The more precise image is a rock concealed beneath apparently fertile soil, which the rain strips away; this differs from simply picturing soil on rock being swept off, as in a separate context at 14:18. «وهذا أحسن وأدق من أن نجعل المعنى تمثيل إنفاق الكافر بحال تراب على صفوان»
#140 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · reports · Alternatively, the comparison particle can describe an implied noun for spending, with a possessive omitted before the relative phrase: spending like the one who spends for show. «صفة لمصدر محذوف دل عليه ما في لفظ صدقاتهم»
#141 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · This image reverses the earlier seed yielding seven hundred grains, pairing the two pictures of failed and fruitful spending. «وقد روعي في هذا التمثيل عكس التمثيل لمن ينفق ماله في سبيل الله بحبة»
#142 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The compound comparison maps a promising hope that yields nothing onto the donor's hope for reward, which heedlessness can ruin by prompting reminders and harm. «ووجه الشبه الأمل في حالة تغر بالنفع ثم لا تلبث ألا تأتي لآملها بما أمله»
#143 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The plural form 'helpers' hints that there are supporters in cases of wrongdoing; the excerpt begins mid-discussion, so its precise antecedent is not preserved here. «ومن هنا يظهر وجه إتيان الأنصار بصيغة الجمع فإن في مورد مطلق الظلم أنصارا» [filed under 2:261-274]
#144 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · Withholding spending is a grave wrong that is not expiated or waived as a minor lapse; repentance concerning people's rights requires returning the right to its owner. «وهو ترك الإنفاق لا يقبل التكفير ولو كان من الصغائر لقبله فهو من الكبائر» [filed under 2:261-274]
#145 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The author says this wrong receives no intercession at the Resurrection, citing the question to the people of Saqar and their failure to feed the poor, followed by the denial that intercession helps them. «فما تنفعهم شفاعة الشافعين» [filed under 2:261-274]
#146 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · Because such a wrongdoer is not approved by God and intercession is only for those whose religion He approves, the wording 'seeking God's pleasure' is significant. «إذ لا شفاعة إلا لمن ارتضى الله دينه» [filed under 2:261-274]
#147 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · Refusing all aid to present, needy poor is a destructive major sin; 41:7 describes withholding zakat as associationism and disbelief in the afterlife, although that Meccan verse predates the formal zakat system. «وويل للمشركين الذين لا يؤتون الزكاة وهم بالآخرة هم كافرون» [filed under 2:261-274]
#148 TABATABAI d.1402 · النبي صلى الله عليه وآله وسلم · reports · Al-Qummī transmits the Prophet’s statement that one who does a believer a favor and then hurts him verbally or reminds him of it has nullified the charity. «عن الصادق عليه السلام قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وآله وسلم» [filed under 2:262, 2:264]
#149 TABATABAI d.1402 · الصادق عليه السلام · reports · Al-Qummī glosses ṣafwān as a large rock, wābil as rain, tall as nighttime dew on plants, and iʿṣār as winds. «والصفوان هي الصخرة الكبيرة التي تكون في المفازة» [filed under 2:264, 2:265, 2:266]
