#170 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · The loss is grave because the servant sins against the Lord and against immense benefaction; the indefinite form may also mark a distinctive kind of loss beyond ordinary human usage. «والذنب يعظم إما لعظم من في حقه الذنب أو لأنه في مقابلة النعمة العظيمة» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#171 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · Humanity is in loss through its ventures, efforts, and lifetimes spent on aims that do not benefit in the afterlife and may harm them. «أي خسران في متاجرهم ومساعيهم وصرف أعمارهم في مباغيهم التي لاينتفعون بها في الآخرة»
#172 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The definite article in loss is comprehensive because of the exception that follows; the indefinite form either magnifies the loss or identifies a kind humans do not recognize. «والتعريف للاستغراق بقرينة الاستثناء والتنكير قيل للتعظيم»
#173 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn ʿAbbās is cited as mentioning ʿAlī and Salmān among those described as saved, and Abū Jahl as an example of the losing human; Alusi says neither example is exclusive. «اقتصار ابن عباس رضى الله تعالى عنهما في الذكر عليهما»
#174 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Time gathers every kind of change, benefit, harm, and wonder; the oath draws attention to how people fare in gain or loss within it. «Zaman böyle varlıkla yokluk arasında nîk ü bed, ni‘met ü nikmet, serra vü darrâ» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#175 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The oath warns that time consumes life and leaves people in loss unless they believe and do good works. «o geçen leyl ü nehar vücûdunu kemirmekte ve bu suretle o her dem hüsran içinde kalmaktadır» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#176 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · In an elegy for his teacher and the Balkan War dead, the author portrays life as deceiving people while death draws its nourishment and each breath weaves shrouds. «إِنَّ الْحَيَاةَ تَغُرُّنَا وَمَ مَاتُنَا مِنْ ضَرْعِهَا يَسْتَرْضِعُ الأَْلْبَانَ» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#177 ELMALILI d.1361 · rejects · People wrongly blame time for their misfortunes; a cited poem says time is not corrupt, but people themselves are. «Zaman bozulmadı, kendileri fesad» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#178 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The oath honors time as a valuable blessing and says those who fail to work toward reform are themselves in loss. «Haddizâtında asrın, zamanın aybı, kabahati, zararı yoktur, o değerli bir nimettir» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#179 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Human life is precious capital; each unprofitable instant wastes it, but a final righteous act may make up for years of waste and win eternal paradise. «İnsanın ömrü en kıymetli sermayesidir. Ne kazanacaksa onunla kazanacaktır» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#180 ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · Sufis say a person should be the child of the moment: value the present and use it to gain good for the afterlife. «sûfiyye, “sûfî ibnü’l-vakt olmalıdır; yani ömrünün ve bilhassa fiilen içinde bulunduğu vakt-i hâlin» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#181 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · People who deny the afterlife also urge seizing the present for worldly pleasure, though their aim differs from the Sufi's afterlife-oriented use of time. «gün bugündür, saat bu saattir, ne yapacaksak şimdi yapmalıyız” diyerek herçibâdâbad vaktine» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#182 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Time is the opportunity for action, whether productive or wasteful, profitable or harmful; the oath highlights the value and nature of life's moments. «İşte vakit böyle bir fırsat ve ömür bütün ânâtıyla böyle kâr ve zarara mâruz bir sermaye» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#183 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Time's senses of restraint and bestowal can support both the loss side and the exception of those who gain through faith and good works. «asrın dehr ü zaman mânası mülâhaza olunurken men‘ve atâ mânalarına da işaret edilmiş bulunduğu hafî değildir» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#184 ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · A cited couplet says each passing day takes away part of the speaker, since nothing is more precious than life. «“Her gün geçtikçe benim birazım gider, benim için ise» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#185 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · People fail to calculate what they gained against the life they spent and wrongly imagine that passing time means they are growing. «insan geçen cüz’ünün yerine ne kazandığını hesab etmez de gün geçtikçe büyüdüğünü zu‘m eder» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#186 ELMALILI d.1361 · reports · A poem says people rejoice at days passing although each one reduces their remaining lifespan. «Biz günleri geçiriyoruz diye ferahlanıyoruz, hâlbuki her geçen gün ecelden, ömürden bir eksikliktir» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#187 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The first Prophetic-era phase began with Muhammad's mission amid religious disorder, extinguished guidance, and widespread ignorance, disbelief, injustice, and corruption. «nübüvvet, fazilet, hakk u adalet, ma‘rifetullah» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#188 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The Qur'an began descending with the command iqra on a Night of Power, and prophetic light rose to call humanity toward reform, paradise, and divine approval. «bir leyle-i kadirde اِقْرَأْ بِاسْمِ رَبِّكَ emriyle Kur’an nâzil olarak nûr-ı nübüvvet tulû‘etmişti» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#189 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · As discussed in Surat al-Bayyina, spreading the upright religion called people away from total loss and began transformations that led from the middle ages to the last era. «لَمْ يَكُن Sûresi’nde geçtiği üzere dîn-i kayyimenin neşriyle insanlığı şerrü’l-beriyyenin istîlâsından» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#190 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The oath by this era before the declaration that humanity is in loss except believers and doers of good carries important meaning. «O vakit bu asra kasem ederek إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#191 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The generic al-insan, signaled by the following exception, means all humanity across eras, especially people of the last era, are in absolute loss except those named next. «insan lâm, istisnâ karînesiyle istiğrak içindir, yani her insan bütün nev‘-i beşer her asırda»
#192 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Husr means losing rather than gaining, wasting capital, and ending in bankruptcy, sorrow, and deprivation; the indefinite form magnifies or specifies the kind of loss. «“Hüsr” ü “hüsran”, kazanacak yerde ziyan etmek, sermayeyi»
#193 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Life is humanity's capital: it is spent with every breath and hour, while accountability for this blessing approaches. «Çünkü insanın sermayesi ömrüdür, o ise her nefes, her saat»
#194 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Life is a limited loan belonging to the Creator, entrusted to people to invest well; their benefit depends on the profit made by spending it. «insanın kendi milki değil, yaratan Hâlık teâlâ’nın milki olup O’nun nâmına güzel tasarruf ederek» [filed under 103:2, 103:3]
#195 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · A person receives only what they strive for and is held accountable for what they earn. «İnsanın bütün istifadesi onun sarf u mübâdelesinden hâsıl olacak kâra bağlıdır» [filed under 103:2, 103:3]
#196 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · After returning the loaned capital to its owner, people are saved and rewarded according to their profit or left liable, bankrupt, and punished according to their deficit; the scales express this reckoning. «hesap günü kendisine kalacak olan kâra göre kendisini kurtaracak ve o nisbette mütena‘‘im olacak» [filed under 103:2, 103:3]
#197 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Every breath spent idly is loss; a worldly or neutral act leaves no lasting gain, evil is certainly loss, and even obedience could have been done better. «Bir mubah ise bir eseri kalmamak itibariyle boşuna geçmiş gibidir. Bir şer ise muhakkak bir hüsrandır» [filed under 103:2, 103:3]
#198 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Because knowledge of Allah and His majesty can grow without limit, greater knowledge should produce deeper awe and love and more complete worship; settling for less entails some loss. «insanın Allah’a ilmi ne kadar çok olursa haşyet ve mahabbeti de o nisbette çok» [filed under 103:2, 103:3]
#199 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Obstacles and constricting events that assail people compound the danger and loss inherent in the passing of time. «asrın, zamanın cereyânı içinde her taraftan hücum etmekte bulunan mâni‘aları»
#200 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Even if people enjoyed constant comfort, their lives move toward death and each passing breath is itself a death. «her dem ömrü ölüme doğru gitmekte bulunan insanın bir hüsran içinde bulunduğunu inkâr etmek kābil olmaz»
#201 ELMALILI d.1361 · Râzî · reports · Razi says loss is humanity's basic condition: true happiness is loving the afterlife rather than attaching oneself to worldly pain and pleasure, yet most people pursue the world and are ruined. «Bundan dolayı Râzî der ki: “Bu âyet insanda hüsran ve haybetin asl»
#202 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Those with the four traits named in the next verse are exceptions to loss and instead gain. «Ancak o kimseler müstesnâ şu zikrolunacak dört vasıf ile muttasıf olanlar hüsranda değil, kârdadırlar» [filed under 103:2, 103:3]
#203 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The oath encompasses every meaning of asr, especially the final era as the afternoon of time and the age of Muhammad, before the declaration that humankind is in loss. «Her mânasıyla asra ve bâhusus pek büyük hâdisâta» [filed under 103:1, 103:2]
#204 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · True faith in the real and abiding God cannot fail; a life spent in beautiful works for God is never wasted, while faith in falsehood and its deeds vanish. «O’nun yolunda güzel amellere sarfedilmiş bulunan bir hayat, bir ömür hiçbir zaman boşa gitmez» [filed under 103:2, 103:3]
