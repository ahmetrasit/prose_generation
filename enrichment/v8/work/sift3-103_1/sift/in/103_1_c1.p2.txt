#101 RAZI-FULL d.606 · holds · A final moment of life can redeem a wasted thousand years through repentance and eternal salvation, so time is among the greatest blessings. «فلو ضيعت ألف سنة ، ثم تبث في اللمحة الأخيرة من العمر»
#102 RAZI-FULL d.606 · holds · Night and day are opportunities to remember and give thanks; the Qur'an also connects time and what occupies it to God's dominion. «فلذلك أقسم به ونبه على أن الليل والنهار فرصة يضيعها المكلف»
#103 RAZI-FULL d.606 · holds · Arabs blamed misfortune on time, but the oath teaches that time is a blessing and the human being who wastes it is at fault. «أنهم كانوا يضيفون الخسران إلى نوائت الدهر»
#104 RAZI-FULL d.606 · holds · As each age passes, life shrinks; unless a person gains something in return, the loss of lifespan is itself a loss. «أنه تعالى ذكر العصر الذي بمضيه ينتقص عمرك» [filed under 103:1-2]
#105 RAZI-FULL d.606 · مقاتل · reports · Muqatil says the oath is by the Asr prayer, citing the Hafsa recension of 2:238 and a reported reading of 5:106 as referring to Asr. «القول الثالث : وهو قول مقاتل : أراد صلاة العصر»
#106 RAZI-FULL d.606 · holds · Razi says the parable identifies the Prophet's era as the time assigned to him and his community; the oath can mean his era, alongside his place and lifespan (90:2; 15:72). «فهذا الخبر دل على أن العصر هو الزمان المختص به وبأمته»
#107 RAZI-FULL d.606 · holds · The Prophet and his community are like the setting in which events occur; since God honors that setting, their rejection of his call makes their loss and abandonment all the greater. «فإذا وجب تعظيم حال الظرف فقس حال المظروف ، ثم وجه القسم ، كأنه تعالى يقول : أنت يا محمد حضرتهم ودعوتهم ، وهم أعرضوا عنك»
#108 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · A Prophetic report likens missing the Asr prayer to losing one's family and wealth, supporting its special merit. «قوله عليه السلام : ( من فاتته صلاة العصر فكأنما وتر أهله وماله )»
#109 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · One argument for the Asr interpretation is that people find it harder to pray as trade and livelihood occupy them late in the day. «لتهافت الناس في تجاراتهم ومكاسبهم آخر النهار واشتغالهم بمعايشهم»
#110 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · An unnamed report tells of a woman confessing adultery, killing her child in a vessel of vinegar, and selling it; the Prophet named penalties and linked the matter to abandoning Asr. «أما الزنا فعليك الرجم ، أما قتل الولد فجزاؤه جهنم»
#111 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · Asr closes the day's acts of worship like repentance closes deeds; swearing by it magnifies the prayer and urges its performance, through which loss becomes profit. «أن صلاة العصر بها يحصل ختم طاعات النهار ، فهي كالتوبة بها يختم الأعمال ، فكما تجب الوصية بالتوبة كذا بصلاة العصر»
#112 RAZI-FULL d.606 · النبي · reports · The Prophet says God will neither look at nor speak to three people on Judgment Day, including a man who lies under oath after Asr. «قال النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم : ( ثلاثة لا ينظر الله إليهم يوم القيامة»
#113 RAZI-FULL d.606 · holds · Although prayer is a human act, God swears by it as a noble command He prescribed, not as our deed. «والجواب : أنه ليس قسما من حيث إنها فعلنا ، بل من حيث إنها أمر شريف تعبدنا الله تعالى بها»
#114 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · An unnamed fourth view takes the oath as referring to the era of the Messenger. «القول الرابع : أنه قسم بزمان الرسول عليه السلام»
#115 RAZI-FULL d.606 · النبي · reports · A Prophetic parable compares workers hired from dawn to noon, noon to afternoon, and afternoon to sunset; the last group receives double pay, prompting the others' complaint and God's reply that this is His grace. «قوله عليه السلام : ( إنما مثلكم ومثل من كان قبلكم»
#116 RAZI-FULL d.606 · أبو مسلم · reports · Abu Muslim reads العصر as one end of the day, the afternoon, paired with the morning's signs of resurrection, judgment, and the evening's signs of death and destruction. «وهو قول أبي مسلم : المراد بالعصر أحد طرفي النهار»
#117 RAZI-FULL d.606 · الحسن · reports · Al-Hasan says afternoon signals that markets are closing: whoever has failed to earn must face dependents and creditors, a picture of the approaching Judgment and unprepared worldly life. «قال الحسن رحمه الله : إنما أقسم بهذا الوقت»
#118 RAZI-FULL d.606 · النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم · reports · A prophetic report says that one who swears falsely after the afternoon prayer will face God's displeasure and lack of regard on Judgment Day. «قوله عليه السلام : ( من حلف بعد العصر كاذبا»
#119 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · As midmorning in the previous sura assures the righteous of rising fortunes, the afternoon oath warns the doomed of decline; its remaining daylight urges repentance. «فكما أقسم في حق الرابح بالضحى فكذا أقسم في حق الخاسر بالعصر»
#120 RAZI-FULL d.606 · بعض السلف · reports · A seller of ice taught a student the sura's meaning by crying, Pity the one whose capital melts: age passes without gain and leaves its owner at a loss. «وعن بعض السلف : تعلمت معنى السورة من بائع الثلج» [filed under 103:1-2]
#121 RAZI-FULL d.606 · سلام بن المنذر · reports · A report has Salām ibn al-Mundhir reading al-ʿaṣr with a kasra on ṣād; al-Rāzī attributes it to stopping for breath or another impediment, not to carrying pause into liaison. «ما يروى عن سلام بن المنذر أنه قرأ ، والعصر بكسر الصاد ولعله وقف لانقطاع نفس أو لعارض منعه من إدراج القراءة»
#122 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · ابن عباس وغيره · reports · Ibn Abbas and others say al-asr means time or age; its changing conditions point to the Creator. «أي الدهر قاله ابن عباس وغيره. فالعصر مثل الدهر»
#123 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · reports · The oath may also have an omitted object, meaning by the Lord of the age. «وقيل: معناه ورب العصر.»
#124 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · مالك · reports · Malik says a person who swears not to speak to someone for an asr has sworn off speech for a year, the most expansive interpretation and consistent with his stricter approach to oaths. «قال مالك: من حلف ألا يكلم رجلا عصرا: لم يكلمه سنة.»
#125 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · الشافعي · reports · Al-Shafi'i says the oath is fulfilled by refraining for an hour, unless the speaker intended another duration. «وقال الشافعي: يبر بساعة، إلا أن تكون له نية،»
#126 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · ابن العربي · reports · Ibn al-Arabi prefers al-Shafi'i's hour unless the person swearing is Arab, in which case the stated intended meaning is accepted if plausible, except the shortest reading. «وقال الشافعي: يبر بساعة، إلا أن تكون له نية، وبه أقول إلا أن يكون الحالف عربيا»
#127 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · الشاعر · reports · A poem uses the contrast of a day, month, and age to illustrate time's scale. «ويوم الهوى شهر وشهر الهوى دهر»
#128 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · حميد بن ثور · reports · Humayd ibn Thawr uses al-asran for day and night. «ولن يلبث العصران: يوم وليلة»
#129 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · reports · Al-asran can also mean morning and evening, as in a poet's promise to repay at day's end what was asked in its morning. «والعصران أيضا: الغداة والعشي.»
#130 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · الحسن وقتادة · reports · Al-Hasan and Qatada say al-asr is the afternoon interval from the sun's decline until sunset. «وقيل: إنه العشي، وهو ما بين زوال الشمس وغروبها قاله الحسن وقتادة.»
#131 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · قتادة · reports · Qatada also defines al-asr as the final hour of the daytime. «وعن قتادة أيضا: هو آخر ساعة من ساعات النهار.»
#132 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · مقاتل · reports · Muqatil says the oath invokes the afternoon prayer, the middle prayer because it is the most excellent prayer. «وقيل: هو قسم بصلاة العصر، وهي الوسطى لأنها أفضل الصلوات قاله مقاتل.»
#133 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم · reports · An authentic report identifies the middle prayer as the Asr prayer; Qurtubi says its explanation appeared under al-Baqara. «وفي الخبر الصحيح: " الصلاة الوسطى: صلاة العصر "»
#134 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · reports · Another interpretation takes the oath as invoking the Prophet's era, honored by the renewal of prophethood in it. «وقيل: هو قسم بعصر النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم، لفضله بتجديد النبوة فيه.»
#135 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · holds · The generic definite al-nafs parallels generic human statements qualified by exceptions; the verse forbids killing any protected life, Muslim or treaty-holder, except for a legally valid cause. «وهذه الآية نهي عن قتل النفس المحرمة، مؤمنة كانت أو معاهدة إلا بالحق» [filed under 6:151, 70:19, 70:22, 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#136 BAYDAWI-FULL d.685 · holds · العصر may mean the afternoon prayer for its merit, the prophetic era, or time itself, whose marvels also counter claims that time causes misfortune. «{ والعصر } أقسم سبحانه بصلاة العصر لفضلها، أو بعصر النبوة أو بالدهر»
#137 NASAFI-FULL d.710 · reports · The oath may be by the afternoon prayer, whose merit is shown by Hafsa's codex identifying it as the middle prayer; performing it is difficult amid late-day trade and work. «أقسم بصلاة العصر لفضلها بدليل قوله تعالى والصلاة الوسطى صلاة العصر فى مصحف حفصة ولأن التكليف فى أدائها شق»
#138 NASAFI-FULL d.710 · reports · Alternatively, the oath is by evening, as the surah swears by duha, because evening displays God's power. «أو أقسم بالعشى كما أقسم بالضحى لما فيها من دلائل القدرة»
#139 NASAFI-FULL d.710 · reports · Alternatively, the oath is by time, whose passage contains many kinds of wonders. «أو أقسم بالزمان لما فى مروره من أصناف العجائب»
#140 QASHANI d.736 · holds · Affliction falls first on prophets, then saints, then the best people in descending order of spiritual excellence. «البلاء موكل بالأنبياء ثم الأولياء ثم الأمثل فالأمثل» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#141 QASHANI d.736 · holds · Affliction is God’s whip, by which He drives His servants toward Himself. «البلاء سوط من سياط الله يسوق به عباده إليه» [filed under 103:1, 103:2, 103:3]
#142 QASHANI d.736 · holds · Time is the duration of the temporal world and its source; people wrongly treat it as the agent of change, while God is the true agent manifest through it. «أقسم بالعصر أي: بامتداد بقاء الزمان وما فيه وما يحدث معه بمبدعه وعلته الذي هو الدهر.»
