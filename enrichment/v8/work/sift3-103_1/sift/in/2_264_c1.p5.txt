#216 NASAFI-FULL d.710 · holds · God does not guide people who choose disbelief for as long as they remain committed to it. «ماداموا مختارين الكفر»
#217 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · holds · رئاء is a verbal noun of showing people one’s good deed so they praise and honor the giver, with no other intention. «الرئاء: فعال مصدر من راء من الرؤية،»
#218 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · reports · The source defines صفوان as a large smooth stone and records disagreements: some use it as a collective, al-Kisāʾī gives singular/plural analyses, and al-Mubarrad rejects his proposals; Abū Ḥayyān also rejects al-Kisāʾī’s account. «الصفوان: الحجر الكبير الأملس، وتحريك فائه بالفتح لغة،»
