#45 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · rejects · This is coordination of two genitive and accusative pairs, not coordination under two separate governors; he gives مررت بزيد أمس وعمرو اليوم as the parallel. «ليس ما في الآية من العطف على عاملين، وإنما هو من باب عطف اسمين» [filed under 92:1, 92:2, 92:3]
#46 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · سيبويه · reports · Sibawayh's poetic example coordinates a genitive and nominative with the same case pattern, supporting the author's construction. «وقد أنشد سيبويه في كتابه: فليس بمعروف لنا أن نردها» [filed under 92:1, 92:2, 92:3]
#47 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · الخليل · reports · Al-Khalīl says the last two wāws join nouns like ordinary coordination, while the first is like the prepositional bāʾ and tāʾ of oath. «قال الخليل: في قوله عز وجل: والليل إذا يغشى والنهار إذا تجلى» [filed under 92:1, 92:2, 92:3]
#48 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · rejects · The author says al-Khalīl's statement indicates prohibition, contrary to al-Zamakhsharī's claim that the construction is merely disliked. «وأما قوله على استكراه فليس كما ذكر، بل كلام الخليل يدل على المنع.» [filed under 92:1, 92:2, 92:3]
#49 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · reports · The surah is Meccan according to the author, Madinan according to ʿAlī ibn Abī Ṭalḥa, with another report that some of it is Madinan. «هذه السورة مكية. وقال علي بن أبي طلحة: مدنية. وقيل: فيها مدني.» [filed under 92:1, 92:2]
#50 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · holds · The surah explains which traits bring the success and failure just mentioned in the previous surah, then warns of the Fire and distinguishes those entering it from those spared. «ولما ذكر فيما قبلها قد أفلح من زكاها وقد خاب من دساها ، ذكر هنا» [filed under 92:1, 92:2, 92:4, 92:5, 92:6, 92:7, 92:8, 92:9, 92:10]
#51 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · holds · The object of يغشى is omitted and may be the day, the sun, or the earth and all it contains under darkness. «ومفعول يغشى محذوف، فاحتمل أن يكون النهار، كقوله: يغشي الليل النهار ،»
#52 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · holds · The oaths evoke the night when living creatures take shelter and the day when they spread out. «أقسم بالليل الذي فيه كل حيوان يأوي إلى مأواه، وبالنهار الذي تنتشر فيه.» [filed under 92:1, 92:2]
#53 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · holds · The night oath refers to darkness covering creation and the day oath to its light and brightness; the paired created male and female recalls other creation pairs. «أي: إذا غشي الخليقة بظلامه، {والنهار إذا تجلى} أي: بضيائه وإشراقه،» [filed under 92:1, 92:2]
#54 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas relates that a palm owner repeatedly snatched fruit from poor children in a neighbor's house; the Prophet offered him a palm in Paradise, but he refused, so another man exchanged forty palms for it and donated it to the family. «أن رجلا كان له نخل، ومنها نخلة فرعها إلى (7) دار رجل صالح فقير ذي عيال،» [filed under 92:1, 92:10]
#55 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas says the story of the palm and the charitable buyer occasioned the revelation of the surah's opening through its contrast between giving and withholding. «قال عكرمة: قال ابن عباس: فأنزل الله عز وجل: {والليل إذا يغشى} إلى قوله:» [filed under 92:1, 92:10]
#56 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · holds · Ibn Kathir calls the palm-story report transmitted by Ibn Abi Hatim very unusual. «هكذا رواه ابن أبي حاتم، وهو حديث غريب جدا.» [filed under 92:1, 92:10]
#57 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · After the preceding surah’s account of purified and corrupted souls and Thamud’s fate, people diverge between good and evil while God governs souls through piety and wickedness. «لما بين في الشمس حال من زكى نفسه وحال من دساها» [filed under 92:1, 92:2, 92:4]
#58 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · Night signifies confusion and obscurity, gradually covering what daylight made visible; it is suited to secrecy. «أي يغطي ما كان من الوجود مبصرا بضياء النهار على التدريج قليلا قليلا»
#59 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · Those seeking secrecy choose night and those seeking publicity choose day, whether righteous or wicked. «فمن كان يريد السر قصد الليل» [filed under 92:1, 92:2]
#60 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · The surah demonstrates God’s complete, freely chosen governance of souls through people’s divergent efforts despite sharing aims. «مقصودها الدلالة على مقصود الشمس ، وهو التصرف التام في النفوس بإثبات كمال القدرة»
#61 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · Its name, al-Layl, signals that night frustrates ordinary desire through darkness and sleep, the sibling of death. «ليل نفسه دال على ذلك لأنه على غير مراد النفس بما فيه من الظلام والنوم الذي هو أخو الموت»
#62 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · بعضهم · reports · Some interpreters say spiritual light from night prayer and other good deeds will bring sensory light after death. «وقال بعضهم : إن المراد بالتجلي بالنور المعنوي من إنارة ظلام الليل» [filed under 92:1, 92:17]
#63 DURR-FULL d.911 · النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم · reports · The Prophet says paired angels call people daily to seek their Lord and prefer enough over distracting abundance; at sunset they pray for replacement for spenders and loss for hoarders, linked to 10:25 and Surat al-Layl. «عن أبي الدرداء رضي الله عنه قال: قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم ما من يوم طلعت شمسه إلا وكل بجنبتيها ملكان» [filed under 10:25, 92:1-10]
#64 DURR-FULL d.911 · النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم · reports · The Prophet rebuked Muadh for overlong prayer and directed him to recite shorter chapters, including the chapters beginning with glorification, the sun, dawn, and night. «عن جابر قال: أفتان يا» [filed under 89:1, 87:1, 91:1, 92:1]
#65 DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن عباس · reports · The Prophet told him to recite both the Night and the Sun surahs in the dawn prayer. «وأخرج الطبراني عن ابن عباس أن النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم أمره» [filed under 91:1, 92:1]
#66 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The Night is unanimously counted as twenty-one verses; most say it is Meccan, while ʿAlī ibn Abī Ṭalḥa says Medinan and others divide it between the two settings. «لا خلاف في أنها إحدى وعشرون آية واختلف في مكيتها ومدنيتها فالجمهور على أنها مكية وقال علي بن أبي طلحة مدنية»
#67 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · reports · Most reports say the surah was revealed about Abū Bakr, with sound chains from Ibn Masʿūd, Ibn ʿAbbās, and others; al-Suddī instead links it to Abū al-Daḥdāḥ's charitable purchase of a palm for orphans, but that Ibn ʿAbbās report is weak according to al-Suyūṭī. «فالجمهور على أنها نزلت في شأن أبي بكر الصديق رضي الله تعالى عنه وروي ذلك بأسانيد صحيحة عن ابن مسعود وابن عباس وغيرهما» [filed under 92:1, 92:17]
#68 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The night may cover the sun, the day, or whatever it veils in general; on every reading, the sworn object is the whole night. «أي حين يغشى الشمس كقوله تعالى والليل إذا يغشاها»
#69 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The opening oaths summarize the preceding surah: that surah moved from light to darkness and toward knowledge of God and self, ending with Thamud's ruin; this surah moves from annihilation toward witnessed light, purification, growth, and abiding in God. «fenâdan nûr-ı şuhûda doğru tecellî, tezekkî ve nemâ ile bekā-billâha gidilecek»
#70 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The previous surah ended with a warning, while this one begins in warning and proceeds toward glad tidings; destruction of a people or world does not diminish the Creator, who can renew creation with day, male, and female. «Bir kavmin ve hatta bütün âlemin helâk ve fenasıyla Hâlık’a bir eksiklik, bir ziyan gelmez, O’nun kudretine bir tevakkuf ârız olmaz.»
#71 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Night signifies cosmic and inward darkness; its definite article may refer to the whole kind of night or to a particular terrifying night, literally or figuratively. «“leyl”in lâmı cinse masruf olarak gece cinsinin böyle bir lahzasına veya ahde mahmul olarak hakīkī ve mecâzî bütün mânasıyla»
#72 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Yaghshā can mean that darkness covers the sun, daylight, all the horizons, grief, or the eyes, hearts, and faculties through sleep and heedlessness. «karanlığıyla Güneş’i veya gündüzü veya örtebileceği her şeyi ile âfâkı ve hatta gam u kasveti»
#73 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The encompassing night can carry a person from differentiation to unity, multiplicity to solitude, and witnessing to annihilation in an overwhelming immersion. «insanı farktan cem‘e, kesretten halvete, şuhuddan fenâya götürmek üzere»
#74 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Omitting the object of covering broadens the meaning and heightens terror; the wording also points back to the night covering the sun in the previous surah. «Bununla evvelki sûredeki وَٱلَّيْلِ إِذَا يَغْشَىٰهَا’ya [eş-Şems 91/4] işaret edilmiş olmakla beraber mef‘ûlün hazfinde daha ziyade bir ta‘mîm ve ibham ile tehvil vardır.»
#75 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The conditional idhā asserts realization and, because the imperfect can indicate present or future, signals that the event may occur now or later. «اِذَا tahakkuk ifade etmek ve muzâri‘hâl ve istikbâle muhtemil olmak»
#76 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · The image can warn of a town or people being engulfed by punishment, the era of ignorance around the Prophet's mission, death agonies, or resurrection events. «bâhusus bi‘set-i seniyye sıralarında dünyayı sarmış bulunan devr-i cehalet hâline ve istikbâle ait olmak üzere de sekerât-ı mevt veya kıyamet ahvâline»
#77 ELMALILI d.1361 · holds · Night here is the accidental darkness of passing-away after existence, not primordial nonexistence; at this moment the verses declare that all perishes except God's face, and readers pass from annihilation to abiding witness. «كُلُّ مَنْ عَلَيْهَا فَانٍۢ وَيَبْقَىٰ وَجْهُ رَبِّكَ ذُو ٱلْجَلَٰلِ وَٱلْإِكْرَامِ [er-Rahmân 55/26-27] ve كُلُّ شَىْءٍ هَالِكٌ إِلَّا وَجْهَهُۥ [el-Kasas 28/88]»
#78 ELMALILI d.1361 · İbn Mes‘ûd · reports · Ibn Masʿud reports that Abu Bakr bought and freed Bilal from Umayya ibn Khalaf for a cloak and ten uqiyyas, after which the opening of this surah was revealed. «İbn Mes‘ûd’dan tahric etmişlerdir, demiş ki» [filed under 92:1, 92:4]
