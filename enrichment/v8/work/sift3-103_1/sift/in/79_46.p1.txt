#43 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · بعض بني عقيل · reports · A poem says they attacked at the evening of the new moon or its sirr, meaning the evening's final hidden phase; it supports the idiom. «عشية الهلال أو سرارها»
#44 BAYDAWI-FULL d.685 · holds · When they see it, worldly life or their grave-stay seems no longer than an evening or its morning, as in 46:35; evening and morning belong to one day. «{ كأنهم يوم يرونها لم يلبثوا } في الدنيا أو في القبور. { إلا عشية أو ضحها }»
#45 BAYDAWI-FULL d.685 · النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم · reports · A report promises that a reciter of Sūrat al-Nāziʿāt will be held at resurrection only as long as an obligatory prayer before entering Paradise; no grading is stated. «من قرأ سورة النازعات كان ممن حبسه الله في القيامة حتى يدخل الجنة قدر صلاة المكتوبة»
#46 NASAFI-FULL d.710 · holds · At the Hour, people will think they stayed in this world only an evening or its noon, because the terror makes the duration seem short; this means less than a full day. «استقلوا مدة لبثهم فى الدنيا لما عاينوا من الهول كقوله لم يلبثوا»
#47 NASAFI-FULL d.710 · holds · “The noon of the evening” is licensed by the two periods' relation within one day: their stay did not reach an entire day, only one end of it. «وإنما صحت اضافة الضحى إلى العشية للملابسة بينهما لاجتماعهما فى نهار واحد»
#48 QASHANI d.736 · reports · The evening or morning may mean the setting of truth’s light in bodies or its rising from the west; witnessing resurrection through unity makes worldly existence seem only imagined. «وقت غروب نور الحق في الأجساد أو وقت طلوعه من مغربه»
#49 QASHANI d.736 · reports · Alternatively, the two brief spans are bodily and spiritual worlds, concealed by sensation and intellect; passing both is described as two steps before arrival. «في عالم الأجسام والاحتجاب بالحس أو في عالم الأرواح والاحتجاب بالعقل»
#50 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · holds · The brief-life comparison brings the Hour close: they will think they lived only an afternoon or its morning, with day-end terms joined by expansive إضافة. «كأنهم يوم يرونها: تقريب وتقرير لقصر مقامهم في الدنيا.»
#51 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · holds · ‘Aw duha-ha’ means the bright part of its evening; it figuratively attaches one time expression to another, naming the two ends of the day, and the cadence favors the construction. «أي: ضحى العشية، أضاف الظرف إلى ضمير الظرف الآخر تجوزا واتساعا»
#52 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · holds · When raised from their graves, people will consider worldly life so brief it seemed like part of one afternoon or morning. «إذا قاموا من قبورهم إلى المحشر يستقصرون مدة الحياة الدنيا»
#53 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas defines evening as noon to sunset and morning as sunrise to noon. «قال جويبر، عن الضحاك، عن ابن عباس: {كأنهم يوم يرونها لم يلبثوا إلا عشية أو ضحاها} أما عشية: فما بين الظهر إلى غروب الشمس،»
#54 IBNKATHIR-FULL d.774 · قتادة · reports · Qatada says the whole duration of worldly life will seem brief when people behold the Hereafter. «وقال قتادة: وقت الدنيا في أعين القوم حين عاينوا الآخرة.»
#55 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · The surah’s purpose is to establish resurrection and the gathering, displaying humanity’s final fate after the preceding surahs’ full exposition; its oaths show the matter has reached its decisive climax. «مقصودها بيان أواخر أمر الإنسان» [filed under 79:1, 79:2, 79:3, 79:4, 79:5, 79:6, 79:7, 79:8, 79:9, 79:10, 79:11, 79:12, 79:13, 79:14, 79:15, 79:16, 79:17, 79:18, 79:19, 79:20, 79:21, 79:22, 79:23, 79:24, 79:25, 79:26, 79:27, 79:28, 79:29, 79:30, 79:31, 79:32, 79:33, 79:34, 79:35, 79:36, 79:37, 79:38, 79:39, 79:40, 79:41, 79:42, 79:43, 79:44, 79:45, 79:46]
#56 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · The name al-Nāziʿāt points to angels extracting souls and then the account of Pharaoh and Moses; al-Sāhirah and al-Ṭāmmah also fit the oath and the surah’s flow. «وصور ذلك بنزع الأرواح بأيدي الملائكة الكرام ، ثم أمر فرعون اللعين وموسى عليه السلام ، واسمها النازعات واضح في ذلك المرام» [filed under 79:1, 79:15, 79:42, 79:46]
#57 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · At the Hour, people will think their stay in the world and graves lasted only part of a day: from noon to sunset, or from morning until noon. «أي في الدنيا وفي القبور»
#58 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · The comparison belittles worldly time against the limitless afterlife; the Prophet likens this world’s share to a finger dipped in the sea and withdrawn. «وهذا تعبير لنا بما نحسه تقريبا لعقولنا»
#59 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · Disbelievers give differing estimates of their stay, but the truth is that their past seems like a brief hour beside the endless future they face. «على أن الكفار أيضا يستقصرون مدة لبثهم , فكأنهم أصناف : بعضهم يقول : إن لبثتم إلا عشرا»
#60 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · The comparison recalls the judgment scene where people say they stayed only an hour, or answer that they remained a day or part of one and defer to the counters. «كأن لم يلبثوا إلا ساعة من النهار يتعارفون بينهم»
#61 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · The surah closes by returning from resurrection to its opening, then connects to the next surah through fear and remembrance. «فقد رجع آخرها بالقيامة على أولها , والتف مفصلها بنزع الأنفس اللوامة على موصلها , واتصلت بأول ما بعدها من جهة الخشية والتذكر»
#62 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · The report that some answer “a day or part of a day” is linked to the question about years in al-Muʾminūn. «كم لبثتم في الأرض عدد سنين قالوا لبثنا يوما أو بعض يوم اسأل العادين»
#63 DURR-FULL d.911 · النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم · reports · Anas transmits a prayer for success that invokes God's mercy, forgiveness, safety, good deeds, Paradise, and deliverance from Hell, and includes the two verses about the briefness of worldly time. «عن أنس أن النبي صلى» [filed under 46:35, 79:46]
#64 DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن عباس · reports · A weakly transmitted report says Meccan polytheists mockingly asked when the Hour would come; its timing belongs to God, Muhammad warns those who fear it, and the world will seem only an evening or morning. «بسند ضعيف عن ابن عباس» [filed under 79:42-46]
#65 DURR-FULL d.911 · عائشة · reports · Aisha reports that when visiting Bedouins asked about the Hour, the Prophet pointed to the youngest person and said that if he lived a century, their own Hour would have come. «عن عائشة قالت: كانت الأعراب»
#66 DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن جرير · reports · Ibn Jurayj glosses the Hour's endpoint as God's knowledge and interprets the brief evening and morning as worldly time. «ابن المنذر عن ابن جرير في قوله» [filed under 79:44, 79:46]
#67 DURR-FULL d.911 · قتادة · reports · Qatada says worldly life will seem slight to people when they see the affair of the Hereafter. «عن قتادة في قوله: {كأنهم يوم يرونها}»
#68 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · The deniers, especially Meccan unbelievers, will think at the Hour that they had stayed only a single evening or morning after being warned. «أي المنكرين وبالفارسية كوييا كفار مكة»
#69 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · Duha is the period from sunrise until noon; the short span refers to part of one day, with evening and morning linked by their belonging to the same day. «الضحى اسم لما بين إشراق الشمس إلى استواء النهار ثم هي عشى إلى الغداة»
#70 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The likeness either confirms the warning that the Hour will arrive swiftly, or answers mockers who impatiently ask when it will come: after the warning they will have waited only briefly. «إما تقرير وتأكيد لما ينبيء عنه الإنذار من سرعة مجيء المنذر به»
#71 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · الزمخشري · reports · The compact wording replaces “only an hour of the day, its evening or forenoon”; possessive suffixes rule out imagining an interval from one day's evening until another day's forenoon. «على ما نقل عن الزمخشري له أصل وهو لم يلبثوا إلا ساعة من نهار عشيته أو ضحاه»
#72 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · الطيبي · reports · Al-Tibi allows evening or forenoon to stand metaphorically for the whole day; adding the pronoun reinforces this and excludes the broader possibility, like saying one saw something with one's own eyes. «وقال الطيبي إنه من المحتمل أن يراد بالعشية أو الضحى كل اليوم مجازا»
#73 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · prefers · Alusi prefers the less forced explanation of the added pronoun, while allowing it to reinforce the whole-day interpretation too; he takes the interval as worldly life for some and the grave for others. «ولكن السابق أبعد من التكلف ولا منع من الجمع وزاد الإضافة حسنا»
#74 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · prefers · The context calls for a short stay after the warning or threat, which both makes the warning real and rebuts their impatient challenge; the sentence is circumstantial on one reading and independent on the other. «اعتبار كونه بعد الإنذار أو بعد الوعيد تحقيقا للإنذار وردا لاستبطائهم»
#75 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · rejects · Connecting “what place are you in regarding its reminder?” as a substitute for their question, or as their quoted words, is weak; the direct answer is that the Hour's endpoint belongs to God. «ولا يخفى ضعف ذلك وأخراج البزار»
#76 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · عائشة · reports · Aisha reports that the Prophet was repeatedly asked about the Hour until 79:43–44 was revealed, after which he stopped asking about it; al-Hakim authenticated the report. «وصححه عن عائشة قالت ما زال رسول الله صلى الله تعالى عليه وسلم يسأل عن الساعة»
#77 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · طارق بن شهاب · reports · Tariq ibn Shihab reports that the Prophet often mentioned the Hour until 79:43–44 was revealed and then refrained from mentioning it. «عن طارق بن شهاب قال كان رسول الله صلى الله تعالى عليه وسلم يكثر ذكر الساعة»
#78 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · أبو جعفر وشيبة وخالد الحذاء وابن هرمز وعيسى وطلحة وابن محيصن وابن مقسم وأبو عمرو · reports · These readers, in a report from al-Mundhir, read mundhir with tanwīn and make it govern its object; the construction is original, while genitive attachment can also serve lightness. «وقرأ أبو جعفر وشيبة وخالد الحذاء وابن هرمز وعيسى وطلحة»
