#208 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم · reports · From Ibn Abbas, al-Tirmidhi transmits that a person who lies about a dream will be made to tie two barley grains together on Judgment Day, which he cannot do; al-Tirmidhi grades it hasan sahih. «من تحلم كاذبا كلف يوم القيامة أن يعقد بين شعيرتين»
