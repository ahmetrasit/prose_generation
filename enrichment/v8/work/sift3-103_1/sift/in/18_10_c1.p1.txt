#39 SULAMI d.412 · سهل · reports · The youths' request for mercy asks for remembrance and gratitude in every state, while asking for the easiest path of divine enabling. «قال سهل بن عبد الله رحمه الله : ارزقنا ذكرك وشكرك على جميع الأحوال فهو جل رحمة من عندك»
#40 SULAMI d.412 · جعفر · reports · Seeing the youths from one's own perspective brings flight, but seeing them from the Real's perspective reveals unity and lordship within them. «قال جعفر : لو اطلعت عليهم من حيث أنت لوليت منهم فرارا ، ولو اطلعت عليهم من حيث الحق لشاهدت فيهم معاني الوحدانية»
#41 SULAMI d.412 · ابن عطاء · reports · A view of all created things from one's own position would turn one's sight away from the youths; the Prophet is both a beholder of God and beheld from Him. «ابن عطاء»
#42 SULAMI d.412 · ابن عطاء · reports · The youths were surrounded by lights of the Real, exaltation, and awe, explaining why the Prophet would flee if he saw them. «وقال ابن عطاء في هذه الآية : إنه وردت عليهم أنوار الحق من فتون الخلع ، وأظلتهم سرادقات التعظيم»
#43 SULAMI d.412 · الحسين · reports · The Prophet would recoil from the overpowering spiritual states displayed in them, despite recognizing their lofty station of nearness and witnessing. «وقال الحسين في قوله : لو اطلعت عليهم لوليت منهم فرارا . قال : أنفة مما هم فيه من إظهار الأحوال»
#44 SULAMI d.412 · جعفر · reports · The awe of God's power, care, and guardianship over them would be unbearable; the flight would truly be from God, since what appears in them comes from Him. «وقال جعفر : لو اطلعت على ما بهم من آثار قدرتنا ورعايتنا لهم وتولية حياطتهم لوليت منهم فرارا»
#45 SULAMI d.412 · جعفر · reports · One flees when looking from the human self but stands firm when looking from God; the Prophet's flight from unbelievers reflects the former human perspective. «وقال أيضا : لو اطلعت عليهم من حيث أنت . لفررت ولو اطلعت عليهم من حيث أنا لوقفت»
#46 SULAMI d.412 · بعضهم · reports · Angelic lights are honors, while human lights are guidance, outward and inward; their greater awe explains the report that Satan fled from ʿUmar. «قال بعضهم : حين سئل عن الفرق بين أنوار هدايته وأنوار الملائكة»
#47 THALABI d.427 · ابن إسحاق · reports · Ibn Ishaq narrates that pagan Roman ruler Decius persecuted Christians in Ephesus; eight noble youths kept monotheism, prayed and fasted, then fled the forced choice between idol worship and death after believers were tortured and displayed on the city walls. «فقال محمد بن إسحاق بن يسار: مرج أهل الإنجيل وعظمت فيهم الخطايا وطغت فيهم الملوك» [filed under 18:10, 18:14]
#48 THALABI d.427 · holds · The youths' prayer declares God alone as Lord of heaven and earth and says invoking another deity would be an outrageous falsehood; they ask for the persecution to be lifted. «ربنا رب السماوات والأرض لن ندعوا من دونه إلها لقد قلنا إذا شططا» [filed under 18:10, 18:14]
#49 THALABI d.427 · holds · The youths' taking refuge means going and joining the cave, a cavern in Mount Yanjalus called Khayram. «إذ أوى أي صار وانضم الفتية إلى الكهف، وهو غار في جبل ينجلوس»
#50 THALABI d.427 · holds · Their prayer asks God to ease for them what they seek of His good pleasure. «أي يسر لنا ما نلتمس من رضاك»
#51 THALABI d.427 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas interprets right guidance as a safe way out of the cave. «وقال ابن عباس: رشدا أي مخرجا من الغار في سلامة»
#52 THALABI d.427 · reports · Another interpretation takes rushd to mean correctness. «وقيل: صوابا»
#53 THALABI d.427 · معاذ · reports · The Prophet says reciting the beginning and end of Surat al-Kahf gives light from head to foot, while reciting all of it gives light from earth to heaven. «من قرأ أول سورة الكهف وآخرها كانت له نورا من قرنه إلى قدمه» [filed under 18:10, 18:110]
#54 MAWARDI-FULL d.450 · الحسن · reports · One account says the youths fled to the cave to preserve their religion. «أنهم قوم هربوا بدينهم إلى الكهف , قاله الحسن»
#55 MAWARDI-FULL d.450 · مجاهد · reports · Another account says they were noble young men who met outside the city by chance; their eldest professed the Lord of heaven and earth, they agreed, then entered the cave and remained for three hundred years plus nine. «أنهم أبناء عظماء وأشراف خرجوا فاجتمعوا وراء المدينة على غير ميعاد , فقال أسنهم : إني أجد في نفسي شيئا ما أظن أحدا يجده , إن ربي رب السموات والأرض» [filed under 18:10, 18:14]
#56 MAWARDI-FULL d.450 · ابن قتيبة · reports · Ibn Qutaybah says they were Roman youths who entered the cave before Jesus; God put them to sleep, Jesus disclosed their story, and they were raised in the interval between Jesus and Muhammad. «قال ابن قتيبة : هم أبناء الروم دخلوا الكهف قبل عيسى , وضرب الله تعالى على آذنهم فيه , فلما بعث الله عيسى أخبر بخبرهم , ثم بعثهم الله تعالى بعد عيسى في الفترة التي بينه وبين النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم»
#57 QUSHAYRI d.465 · holds · The youths were sheltered outwardly in a cave and inwardly under divine care; God took charge of them and brought spiritual states upon them while they were absent from themselves. «آواهم إلى الكهف بظاهرهم ، وفي الباطن فهو مقيلهم»
#58 QUSHAYRI d.465 · holds · Their prayer disavows their own power and sincerely turns to God; He answers, relieves their need, and gives them a good refuge. «أخذوا في التبري من حولهم وقوتهم ، ورجعوا إلى الله بصدق»
#59 QUSHAYRI d.465 · holds · God effaced their self-awareness in the witnessing of unity and divine self-sufficiency, then returned them to sober discrimination after the state of union. «أخذناهم عن إحساسهم بأنفسهم ، واختطفناهم عن شواهدهم»
#60 WAHIDI-BASIT d.468 · holds · The opening idh cannot attach to the preceding question because the youths' flight was long before the Prophet; it attaches to an omitted command, remember when they fled. «لا يجوز أن يكون متعلقا بما قبله على تقدير: أم حسبت إذ أوى الفتية»
#61 WAHIDI-BASIT d.468 · holds · The youths went to the cave and made it their shelter; Ibn Abbas says they fled there. «ومعنى {أوى الفتية إلى الكهف}: صاروا إليه وجعلوه مأواهم»
#62 WAHIDI-BASIT d.468 · ابن عباس · reports · He glosses their going to the cave as fleeing to it. «قال ابن عباس: (يريد هربوا إلى الكهف)»
#63 WAHIDI-BASIT d.468 · holds · After fleeing those pursuing them, the youths prayed for mercy, understood here as forgiveness and provision. «لما هربوا عمن يطلبهم اشتغلوا بالدعاء والتضرع إلى الله تعالى»
#64 WAHIDI-BASIT d.468 · ابن عباس · reports · They ask for mercy from God alone so that it will make them independent of everyone else. «قال ابن عباس: (يريدون تغنينا بها عن جميع من سواك)»
#65 WAHIDI-BASIT d.468 · holds · Hayyiʾ means set right or prepare; rushd and its related forms are the opposite of error. «وقوله تعالى: {وهيئ لنا} أي: أصلح»
#66 WAHIDI-BASIT d.468 · أبو إسحاق · reports · He explains the prayer as asking for guidance to what brings one near to God and pleases Him; Ibn Abbas says to direct their deeds toward God's love. «قال أبو إسحاق: (أي أرشدنا إلى ما يقرب منك ويزلف عندك)»
#67 WAHIDI-BASIT d.468 · أهل المعاني · reports · They parse the request as having an omitted noun, a thing of right guidance; further omission yields the sense, prepare what will enable us to attain guidance. «وقال أهل المعاني: (تقدير الآية: هيئ لنا من أمرنا ذا رشد)»
#68 WAHIDI-WAJIZ d.468 · holds · The youths fled to the cave from their pursuers, devoted themselves to supplication, and asked for forgiveness, provision, and guidance toward what brings them near God. «الفتية إلى الكهف } هربوا إليه ممن يطلبهم»
#69 JISHUMI d.494 · holds · Awā means to return, and maʾwā is a place of return or refuge. «أوى أويا إذا رجع، ومنه: الآوية، والمأوى المرجع.»
#70 JISHUMI d.494 · holds · A kahf is a cave in a mountain; rushd is reaching the path that leads to one's aim, opposite error. «والكهف: الغار في الجبل. والرشد: إصابة الطريق المؤدي إلى البغية،» [filed under 18:9-12]
#71 JISHUMI d.494 · holds · عجبا is the predicate of كان; رشدا is the object of هيئ; سنين is an accusative specification or governed by ضربنا; عددا describes the years or is a verbal noun, as Abū ʿUbayda says. «و عجبا نصب لأنه خبر (كان).» [filed under 18:9-12]
#72 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · محمد بن إسحاق بن يسار · reports · Ibn Isḥāq recounts that Christian society fell into grave error and idolatry under King Daqyānūs, who persecuted believers; eight elite youths fled, prayed for rescue, were captured, and refused the king's demand to worship idols, with Miksalmina speaking for them. «فقال محمد بن إسحاق بن يسار: مرج أهل الإنجيل وعظمت فيهم الخطايا وطغت فيهم الملوك حتى عبدوا الأصنام»
#73 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · holds · Awā means they went to the cave and made it their dwelling; the cave was in Mount Banjulus and was called Khayram. «يقال: أوى فلان إلى موضع كذا أي: اتخذه منزلا»
#74 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · holds · The requested mercy means guidance in religion, or, by another view, provision. «ومعنى الرحمة: الهداية في الدين. وقيل: الرزق»
#75 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · holds · Hayyiʾ means make easy; rushd is what seeks God's approval and what brings right guidance. «{وهيئ لنا} يسر لنا {من أمرنا رشدا} أي: ما يلتمس من رضاك وما فيه رشدنا»
#76 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · ابن عباس · reports · He interprets rushd as a safe way out of the cave. «وقال ابن عباس: رشدا أي مخرجا من الغار في سلامة.»
#77 KASHSHAF-FULL d.538 · holds · The requested mercy is from God's stores: forgiveness, provision, and safety from enemies; preparing their affair means guiding them as they leave unbelievers or setting their whole affair right. «من خزائن رحمتك ، وهي المغفرة والرزق والأمن من الأعداء»
#78 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · reports · Reports describe the youths as sons of nobles in the city of the unbelieving king Daqiyus, also called Daqliyus or Daqinus; they were Romans and followers of Jesus, though another view places them before Jesus. «الفتية فيما روي، قوم من أبناء أشراف مدينة دقيوس»
#79 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · holds · The youths' foreign names have weak transmission; al-Tabari's list gives Makselminya, Majseliniya, Tamlikha, Martus, Kashutunus, Birunus, Dinamus, and Yatūnus. «وأما أسماؤهم فهي أعجمية،»
