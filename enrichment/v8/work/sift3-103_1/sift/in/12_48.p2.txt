#92 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · The exception is what people preserve as seed for sowing after the famine. «إلا قليلا مما تحصنون تحرزون وتدخرون للبذر»
#93 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The seven difficult years follow the seven named years; saying 'after that' rather than 'after them' magnifies the preceding period. 'Seven' is understood as years from the earlier wording. «من بعد السنين السبع المذكورات وإنما لم يقل من بعدهن قصدا إلى تفخيم شأنهن»
#94 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The famine years consume the grain stored for them; attributing eating to years is figurative for what people undergo, and لهن reinforces that personification or matches the dream's imagery. «وإسناد الأكل إليهن مع أنه حال الناس فيهن مجازي»
#95 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · بعضهم · reports · Some understand 'eating' as utterly exhausting or destroying, as in the idiom that a journey ate a camel's flesh. «أكل السير لحم الناقة أي أفناه وذهب به»
#96 ALUSI-FULL d.1270 · holds · The small amount they preserve is what they protect and store for the next planting; the verb derives from the sense of a fortress as protection and refuge. «أي تحرزونه وتخبئونه لبروز الزراعة مأخوذ من الحصن»
#97 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · In the dream's interpretation, cows represent agricultural years, fatness fertility, leanness drought, and ears of grain provisions. «فالبقرات لسنين الزراعة ، لأن البقرة تتخذ للإثمار . والسمن رمز للخصب» [filed under 12:47, 12:48, 12:49]
#98 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · Seven dry ears signify provisions stored across seven years; the lean cows consuming the fat ones represent drought consuming what prosperity produced. «والسنبلات اليابسات رمز لما يدخر ، وكونها سبعا رمز لادخارها في سبع سنين»
#99 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · شداد describes drought years through rational metaphor: the years are called severe because hardship occurs in them. «والشداد : وصف لسني الجدب ، لأن الجدب حاصل فيها ، فوصفها بالشدة على طريقة المجاز العقلي»
#100 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · يأكلن means “consume” or “use up,” as in Q 4:2; attributing the consumption to years is a rational metaphor because they are the time when loss occurs. «وأطلق الأكل في قوله : { يأكلن } على الإفناء ، كالذي في قوله : { ولا تأكلوا أموالهم»
#101 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · تحصنون means secure and store in granaries; the drought years exhaust the stores except for a little, so the wording urges ample stockpiling. «والإحصان : الإحراز والادخار ، أي الوضع في الحصن وهو المطمور»
#102 TABATABAI d.1402 · الرضا عليه السلام · reports · A report says Joseph stockpiled the harvests, sold food during famine first for money, then valuables, livestock, servants, property, and land, and finally for people’s freedom; he then freed the Egyptians and restored their possessions and kingship. «سمعت الرضا عليه السلام يقول وأقبل يوسف على جمع الطعام في السبع السنين المخصبة فكبسه في الخزائن» [filed under 12:47, 12:48, 12:49]
#103 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The author omits many similar reports about the famine economy because most do not directly serve the interpretation of these verses. «والروايات في هذا المقام كثيرة أغلبها غير مرتبطة بغرض تفسير الآيات ولذلك تركنا نقلها» [filed under 12:47, 12:48, 12:49]
#104 TABATABAI d.1402 · prefers · Shidad can mean severe because famine is hard on people, or things that attack; the latter better fits the following image of years devouring stored food. «الشداد جمع شديد من الشدة بمعنى الصعوبة لما في سني الجدب والمجاعة»
#105 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The wording personifies the harsh years as ravenous predators that pounce on people and consume their stored provisions. «وهذا أنسب لما بعده من توصيفها بقوله»
#106 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · Ihsan here means securing and storing a small remainder for later use. «والإحصان الإحراز والادخار»
#107 TABATABAI d.1402 · prefers · The dream depicts the practical measures the king must take for a coming famine: repeated planting, preserving grain in ears, and rationing reserves, rather than simply forecasting years. «أن الذي رآه الملك من الرؤيا إنما كان مثال ما يجب عليه من اتخاذ التدبير لإنجاء الناس من مصيبة الجدب» [filed under 12:43, 12:47, 12:48]
#108 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · Cows represent yearly provisions: fat cows picture plentiful harvests from sustained planting, lean cows picture shortages, and their eating the fat ones pictures famine consuming saved food. «فحكت السنين المخصبة والمجدبة أي الرزق الذي يرتزقون به فيها في صورة البقرة» [filed under 12:43, 12:47, 12:48]
#109 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The green ears represent stores to be saved and the dry ears indicate preserving grain unthreshed; the dream shows the work required to protect food supplies. «وحكت ما يجب عليهم في حفظ ذخائر الرزق بالسنبلات اليابسة قبال السنبلات الخضر» [filed under 12:43, 12:47, 12:48]
#110 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The small amount to be preserved is a reserve and seed for the recovery year; the dream says the lean cows eat the fat ones, not that they consume every last one. «وثانيها قوله { إلا قليلا مما تحصنون }»
#111 TABATABAI d.1402 · rejects · The author says interpreters wrongly treat the dream as a forecast of seven fertile then seven barren years; Joseph instead states the required policy for surviving the anticipated famine. «لم يبن كلامه على أساس إخبارهم بما سيستقبلهم من السنين السبع المخصبة ثم السنين السبع المجدبة» [filed under 12:47, 12:48, 12:49]

#113 TABATABAI d.1402 · أبو عبد الله عليه السلام · reports · Al-Ṣādiq says the revealed wording is “what you bring near for them” and interprets the following year as one in which people receive rain. «قال الصادق عليه السلام إنما نزل ما قربتم لهن ثم يأتي من بعد ذلك عام فيه يغاث الناس» [filed under 12:48, 12:49]
#114 TABATABAI d.1402 · أبو عبد الله عليه السلام · reports · Al-Ṣādiq reads “they are rained upon” passively and takes yughāth from rain rather than rescue; al-ʿAyyāshī transmits the same interpretation. «إنما نزلت وفيه يعصرون أي يمطرون، أي بالبناء للمفعول ومنه يعلم أنه عليه السلام يأخذ قوله يغاث من الغيث» [filed under 12:48, 12:49]
#115 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · Joseph interpreted Pharaoh's dream as seven productive years followed by famine, proposed storing surplus grain, and was appointed to Egypt's stores after the women and al-ʿAzīz's wife cleared him; prison thus led to kingship. «وفي هذه السنين انتصب يوسف لمقام عزة مصر» [filed under 12:42-57]
