#158 IBNASHUR-FULL d.1393 · holds · The account implies the prison had no reliable register, oversight, or periodic report to the king; Islamic law addressed this neglect by requiring a judge to review prisoners daily. «وهذا من الإهمال والتهاون بحقوق الناس وقد أبطله الإسلام»
#159 TABATABAI d.1402 · ابن عباس · reports · A report says Joseph stumbled three times: when he desired her and was imprisoned, when he asked the cupbearer to mention him and forgot his Lord, and when he said the brothers were thieves. «أخرج أبو الشيخ عن ابن عباس قال عثر يوسف عليه السلام ثلاث عثرات حين هم بها» [filed under 12:24, 12:42, 12:50]
#160 TABATABAI d.1402 · rejects · The report conflicts with verses describing God’s election and purification of Joseph and Satan’s lack of access; its alleged grave sins cannot fit his status as a truthful benefactor, and similar reports are unreliable. «والرواية تخالف صريح كلامه تعالى حيث يذكر أن الله اجتباه وأخلصه لنفسه» [filed under 12:24, 12:42, 12:50]
#161 TABATABAI d.1402 · rejects · The al-Qummi report is textually disordered: it makes the two men royal attendants assigned to guard Joseph, which conflicts with the Qur’an’s description of them as his prison companions. «أقول وفي الرواية اضطراب لفظي»
#162 TABATABAI d.1402 · سماعة · reports · A report in al-Ayyashi identifies Joseph’s lord in the request to remember him as the Aziz. «وفي تفسير العياشي عن سماعة عن قول الله»
#163 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The pronouns in the request to remember Joseph and in the statement that he remained in prison refer to Joseph; he asked the man he expected to survive to mention his case to the king. «راجعة إلى يوسف أي قال يوسف للذين ظن هو أنه سينجو منهما»
#164 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · Here zann can mean conviction rather than uncertain conjecture, as in the Qur’anic description of those who expect to meet their Lord. «إطلاق الظن على مطلق الاعتقاد وله نظائر في القرآن كقوله تعالى الذين يظنون أنهم ملاقوا ربهم»
#165 TABATABAI d.1402 · rejects · The view that Joseph interpreted the dreams only by conjecture conflicts with his declaration that the matter was settled and God’s statement that He taught him interpretation. «أنه صرح لهما بعلمه في قوله» [filed under 12:41, 12:42]
#166 TABATABAI d.1402 · reports · A possible alternative makes the saved prisoner, rather than Joseph, the subject of the pronoun in the phrase about expecting rescue; the author accepts it if context allows. «إلى الموصول أي قال يوسف لصاحبه الذي ظن ذلك الصاحب»
#167 TABATABAI d.1402 · prefers · The pronouns in the forgetting clause refer to the surviving cupbearer: Satan made him forget to mention Joseph to the king, so Joseph stayed imprisoned for several years. «أي فأنسى الشيطان صاحبه الناجي أن يذكره لربه»
#168 TABATABAI d.1402 · rejects · The alternative that Satan made Joseph forget God and that Joseph was punished for seeking help from a person contradicts the text’s protection of the sincere and wrongly treats ordinary use of means as reliance. «فمما يخالف نص الكتاب فإن الله سبحانه نص على كونه عليه السلام من المخلصين ونص على أن المخلصين لا سبيل للشيطان إليهم»
#169 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · The later statement that the cupbearer remembered Joseph after a lapse of years confirms that the cupbearer, not Joseph, was the one who forgot. «قرينة صالحة على أن الناسي هو الساقي دون يوسف» [filed under 12:42, 12:45]
#170 TABATABAI d.1402 · ابن عباس · reports · A report attributed to the Prophet says Joseph would not have remained imprisoned so long had he not uttered the request to remember him to the king. «لو لم يقل يوسف الكلمة التي قال ما لبث في السجن»
#171 TABATABAI d.1402 · أبو هريرة · reports · A parallel report attributed to the Prophet says, God have mercy on Joseph, had he not said remember me to your lord he would not have remained imprisoned so long. «عن ابن المنذر وابن أبى حاتم وابن مردويه عن أبي هريرة»
#172 TABATABAI d.1402 · عكرمة والحسن · reports · Similar blame of Joseph’s request is also transmitted from Ikrima, al-Hasan, and others; the author gives no grading for these reports. «وروى مثله عن عكرمة والحسن وغيرهما»
#173 TABATABAI d.1402 · أبو عبد الله عليه السلام · reports · A report says God reminded Joseph of favors, rebuked him for seeking a creature’s help, and ordered him to remain imprisoned for several years; the author classifies it with reports contradicting the Qur’an. «قال الله ليوسف ألست الذي حببتك إلى أبيك وفضلتك على الناس بالحسن»
#174 TABATABAI d.1402 · ابن عباس · reports · Another report counts Joseph’s request to the king among three alleged lapses, alongside saying the brothers were thieves and saying he had not betrayed them; it also has Gabriel rebuke him. «ومثلها ما في الدر المنثور عن ابن مردويه عن ابن عباس قال عثر يوسف عليه السلام ثلاث عثرات» [filed under 12:42, 12:79, 12:80]
#175 TABATABAI d.1402 · rejects · The author rejects these reports as imputing explicit lies to the truthful Joseph and says God’s text clears him of such accusations. «وفي الرواية نسبة الفرية والكذب الصريح إلى الصديق عليه السلام» [filed under 12:42, 12:79, 12:80]
#176 TABATABAI d.1402 · rejects · The reading that Joseph was punished for asking another person for help contradicts the text’s declaration that he was among God’s sincere servants, and using ordinary means does not imply reliance on them. «والله سبحانه يبرئه من هذه المفتريات بنص كتابه»
#177 TABATABAI d.1402 · holds · Joseph interpreted Pharaoh's dream as seven productive years followed by famine, proposed storing surplus grain, and was appointed to Egypt's stores after the women and al-ʿAzīz's wife cleared him; prison thus led to kingship. «وفي هذه السنين انتصب يوسف لمقام عزة مصر» [filed under 12:42-57]

#179 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · The annexation (iḍāfat) in the relevant expression follows the same grammatical pattern as another cited construction. «nature of annexure ( iḍāfat)»
#180 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · The freed prisoner forgot Joseph's request; the text attributes the forgetting to Satan because Satan makes people forget virtuous acts. «This forgetfulness is asrcibed to Satan. This because it is Satan who makes people forget acts of virtue.»
#181 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · The master Joseph asked the freed prisoner to mention him to was the king, whose wine-server was that prisoner. «The word “master” here refers to the king and this person was the wine-server of the king.»
#182 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · Mentioning Joseph means conveying his knowledge and wisdom, especially his skill in interpreting dreams, to the king. «The word “mention” here means to convey Joseph’s knowledge and wisdom that came to his observation, in particular, his dream interpretation skill.»
#183 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · Joseph saw the request as a possible way out after all routes to justice had closed. «a way out for him in a situation where all doors to justice had been closed.»
#184 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · Using a legitimate plan for a legitimate aim is compatible with trust in God; resorting to illegitimate means after frustration is not. «Planning a legitimate scheme to achieve a legitimate purpose is not against trust in God.»
#185 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · True reliance includes effort with available means while respecting God's limits, rather than doing nothing and merely trusting. «uses all means and resources but at every step respects the boundaries set by God.»
#186 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · This disciplined effort under hardship is the test and purpose for which God created the world. «This is the real test for which God has created this world and it is actually the very purpose of its creation.»
#187 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · People must plan and use means, but whether the plan succeeds depends on God's wisdom; accordingly Joseph remained imprisoned longer. «the materialization of this plan depends on God’s wisdom and scheme.»
#188 ISLAHI-TADABBUR · holds · Joseph's fellow prisoner forgot his request after release, fulfilling God's wise plan that Joseph stay in prison longer. «it was a requisite of God’s wisdom that Joseph (sws) remain in prison for some more years.»
#189 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · holds · Joseph asked the prisoner expected to be released to tell the king he was innocent and jailed unjustly; the man forgot, leaving Joseph in prison several more years. «Hz. Yûsuf, kurtulacağına inandığı gençten kendisinin suçsuz olduğunu ve haksız yere zindana atılmış bulunduğunu krala anlatmasını rica etti»
#190 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · some commentators · reports · One reading says Satan made Joseph forget God; he sought release through his fellow prisoner instead, so God kept him imprisoned longer as punishment. «Şeytan Hz. Yûsuf’a Allah’ı anmayı unutturdu»
