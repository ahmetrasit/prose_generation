#124 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · The opening’s hidden night recalls uncertainty about one’s path and destiny; remembering the signs and seeking refuge in God bring certainty. «برفع القسم في الأولى بقوله :» [filed under 93:1, 93:2]
#125 BIQAI-FULL d.885 · holds · The opening night evokes the beginning’s obscurity, while the manifest day evokes the clarity reached by people whose hearts God has strengthened. «فلابتداء الأمر وشدة الإبهام والإظلام أشار قوله سبحانه وتعالى : { والليل إذا يغشى }» [filed under 93:1, 93:2]
#126 DURR-FULL d.911 · عقبة بن عامر · reports · The Prophet instructed them to pray the two midmorning rakʿahs using the Sun and Midmorning surahs. «وأخرج البيهقي في شعب الإيمان عن عقبة بن عامر» [filed under 91:1, 93:1]
#127 DURR-FULL d.911 · holds · The surah is Meccan and has eleven verses. «مكية وآياتها إحدى عشرة»
#128 DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن عباس · reports · He said the surah was revealed in Mecca. «عن ابن عباس رضي الله عنهما قال:»
#129 DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن عباس · reports · A transmission from the Prophet instructs reciters to pronounce takbir at the end of each surah, beginning when reaching al-Duha. «أخبره مجاهد أن ابن عباس رضي الله عنهما أمره بذلك»
#130 DURR-FULL d.911 · جندب البجلي · reports · When the Prophet was ill and did not rise at night for two or three nights, a woman taunted him that his devil had abandoned him; the opening verses answered her. «والنسائي وابن جرير والطبراني والبيهقي وأبو نعيم معا في الدلائل عن جندب البجلي» [filed under 93:1, 93:2, 93:3]
#131 DURR-FULL d.911 · جندب · reports · When Gabriel was withheld, a cousin's daughter said the Prophet's companion had left him; the passage was revealed. «وأخرج الطبراني عن جندب» [filed under 93:1, 93:3]
#132 DURR-FULL d.911 · جندب · reports · After a stone injured his finger and the Prophet stopped night prayer for two or three nights, a woman blamed his devil's departure; al-Tirmidhi authenticated the report. «وأخرج الترمذي وصححه وابن أبي حاتم واللفظ له عن جندب» [filed under 93:1, 93:2, 93:3]
#133 DURR-FULL d.911 · زيد بن أرقم · reports · After Abu Lahab's surah, his wife confronted the Prophet; when revelation paused and she said his companion had abandoned and hated him, the opening verses were revealed. «وأخرج الحاكم عن زيد بن أرقم» [filed under 93:1, 93:2, 93:3]
#134 DURR-FULL d.911 · عبد الله بن شداد · reports · He reports Khadija saying that the Prophet's Lord seemed to have rejected him, after which the verses were revealed. «وأخرج ابن جرير عن عبد الله بن شداد» [filed under 93:1, 93:2, 93:3]
#135 DURR-FULL d.911 · عروة · reports · When Gabriel was delayed and the Prophet became distressed, Khadija inferred from his distress that God had rejected him; the surah followed. «وأخرج ابن جرير وابن المنذر عن عروة» [filed under 93:1, 93:2, 93:3]
#136 DURR-FULL d.911 · ابن عباس · reports · After revelation paused for days, polytheists taunted that God had abandoned Muhammad; the passage came down, and the night ‘when it settles’ means when it arrives. «وأخرج ابن جرير وابن مردويه من طريق العوفي عن ابن عباس» [filed under 93:1, 93:2, 93:3]
#137 DURR-FULL d.911 · قتادة والضحاك · reports · Their mursal reports give the same occasion: a pause in revelation prompted the claim that God had abandoned Muhammad, answered by this passage. «وأخرج ابن جرير نحوه من مرسل قتادة والضحاك» [filed under 93:1, 93:2, 93:3]
#138 DURR-FULL d.911 · قتادة · reports · He glossed the midmorning light as an hour of daylight and the night as quiet among people. «وأخرج عبد الرزاق وعبد بن حميد وابن جرير وابن المنذر عن قتادة» [filed under 93:1, 93:2]
#139 DURR-FULL d.911 · أم حفص · reports · A dead puppy hidden beneath the Prophet's bed delayed revelation for four days; after Khawla found it, the Prophet trembled and the passage through ‘you will be satisfied’ came down. «وأخرج ابن أبي شيبة في مسنده والطبراني وابن مردويه عن أم حفص عن أمها» [filed under 93:1, 93:2, 93:3, 93:5]
#140 ITQAN d.911 · unnamed · reports · Oath by al-Duha and the night when still: God swears to His favour to the Prophet, which confirms his prophethood and the afterlife. «أقسم تعالى على إنعامه على رسوله وإكرامه له وذلك متضمن لتصديقه له»
#141 SUYUTI-LUBAB d.911 · جندب · reports · Jundub reports that when the Prophet was ill and did not rise for one or two nights, a woman said his Satan had abandoned him, and the opening verses of al-Duha were revealed. «اشتكى النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم فلم يقم ليلة أو ليلتين فأتته امرأة فقالت يا محمد ما أرى شيطانك إلا قد تركك»
#142 SUYUTI-LUBAB d.911 · جندب · reports · In another report, Gabriel was delayed and the polytheists said Muhammad had been abandoned, prompting the revelation. «أبطأ جبريل على النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم فقال المشركون قد ودع محمد فنزلت»
#143 SUYUTI-LUBAB d.911 · زيد بن أرقم · reports · Zayd ibn Arqam reports that after Gabriel did not descend for days, Umm Jamil, Abu Lahab's wife, said the Prophet had been abandoned and rejected, and the verses were revealed. «مكث رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم أياما لا ينزل عليه جبريل فقالت أم جميل امرأة أبي لهب»
#144 SUYUTI-LUBAB d.911 · خولة · reports · Khawla's report says a dead puppy under the bed delayed revelation for four days; after it was removed Gabriel came, and verses through “until you are satisfied” were revealed. «أن جروا دخل بيت النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم فدخل تحت السرير فمات فمكث النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم أربعة أيام لا ينزل عليه الوحي»
#145 SUYUTI-LUBAB d.911 · ابن حجر · rejects · Ibn Hajar says the puppy story as a cause of revelation is unusual and anomalous, rejected because it conflicts with sound reports, although the delay due to a puppy is well known. «ولكن كونها سبب نزول الآية غريب بل شاذ مردود بما في الصحيح»
#146 SUYUTI-LUBAB d.911 · عبد الله بن شداد · reports · Abdullah ibn Shaddad reports that Khadija told the Prophet she thought his Lord had rejected him, and then the surah was revealed; this report is mursal. «أن خديجة قالت للنبي صلى الله عليه وسلم ما أرى ربك إلا قد قلاك فنزلت»
#147 SUYUTI-LUBAB d.911 · عروة · reports · Urwa's mursal report says delayed revelation distressed the Prophet and Khadija compassionately told him she thought his Lord had rejected him; Ibn Hajar says Umm Jamil said it spitefully while Khadija said it out of concern. «لكن أم جميل قالته شماتة وخديجة قالته توجعا»
#148 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · Duha is the time when the sun rises high, the beginning of the day; it is used metonymically for its time or with an omitted noun to match the reference to night. «هو وقت ارتفاع الشمس وصدر النهار»
#149 BURSEVI d.1127 · جعفر الصادق · reports · Ja‘far al-Sadiq identifies the duha with the morning when God spoke to Moses and the night with the Night Journey. «وعن جعفر الصادق رضيعنه أن المراد بالضحى هو الضحى الذي كلم الله فيه موسى وبالليل ليلة المعراج» [filed under 93:1, 93:2]
#150 BURSEVI d.1127 · صاحب كشف الأسرار · reports · Kashf al-Asrar reads day and night as disclosure and veiling, signs of the breeze of divine gentleness and the scorching wind of divine severity, and of beauty's lights and majesty's traces. «مراد از روز وشب كشف وحجا بست كه نشانه نسيم لطف وسموم قهر بود» [filed under 93:1, 93:2]
#151 BURSEVI d.1127 · reports · The oath alludes to the morning when God spoke to Moses and the magicians fell prostrate, and when people were gathered at morning; this gives the time special honor. «التي كلم الله فيها موسى عليه السلام وألقى فيها السحرة سجدا»
#152 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · The duha prayer is unanimously a sunnah; its time begins once the sun is high and ends shortly before noon. «وصلاة الضحى سنة بالاتفاق ووقتها إذا علت الشمس إلى قبيل وقت الزوال»
#153 BURSEVI d.1127 · أبو حنيفة · reports · Abu Hanifa sets the duha prayer at two or four units, with one salutation. «وهي عند أبي حنيفة ركعتان أو أربع بتسليمة»
#154 BURSEVI d.1127 · مالك · reports · Malik does not set a fixed upper limit for the number of duha prayer units. «وعند ملك لا تنحصر»
#155 BURSEVI d.1127 · الشافعي · reports · Al-Shafi‘i and Ahmad set the minimum at two units; al-Shafi‘i says the maximum is twelve, while Ahmad says eight, which most Shafi‘i authorities adopt and al-Nawawi validates in al-Tahqiq. «وعند الشافعي وأحمد أقلها ركعتان واختلف في أكثرها فقال الشافعي ثنتا عشرة وقال أحمد ثمان»
#156 BURSEVI d.1127 · رسول الله · reports · A sound report says the Prophet prayed eight units of duha in Umm Hani's house on the conquest of Mecca and had also prayed duha before then. «وقد صح إن النبي عليه اسلام صلى صلاة الضحى يوم فتح مكة ثماني ركعات وهو في بيت أم هانىء»
#157 BURSEVI d.1127 · الجنيد · reports · Al-Junayd calls duha the station of witnessing and the settled night the station of ghayn, the clouding over the heart mentioned in a Prophetic report. «كما قال الجنيد قدس سره والضحى مقام الشهود والليل إذا سجا مقام الغبن الذي قال عليه السلام فيه إنه ليغان على قلبي» [filed under 93:1, 93:2]
#158 BURSEVI d.1127 · reports · A Persian couplet treats duha as the brightness of the Prophet's face and night as the darkness of his hair. «والضحى رمزي زروي همو ماه مصطفى معنى والليل كيسوى سيه مصطفى» [filed under 93:1, 93:2]
#159 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · Night precedes in the preceding surah because darkness is the original state: day arises with the sun and the air returns to its first state when it sets; day leads here because of its intrinsic and incidental honor. «وتقديم الليل في السورة المتقدمة باعتبار الأصل لأن النهار إنما يحدث بطلوع النير» [filed under 93:1, 93:2]
#160 BURSEVI d.1127 · holds · Although duha is only one hour and night spans many, it equals the whole night as Muhammad equals all prophets; joy is brief while worldly sorrow and anxiety last longer. «بأنه وإن كان ساعة من النهار لكنه يوازي جميع الليل كما إن محمدا عليه السلام يوازي جميع الأنبياء» [filed under 93:1, 93:2]
#161 BURSEVI d.1127 · reports · A report says a black cloud over the Throne's left side rained grief for three hundred years, while a white cloud on the right rained joy for only an hour, explaining why sorrow is common and lasting and joy rare. «أظلت غمامة سوداء عن يساره ونادت ماذا أمطر» [filed under 93:1, 93:2]
