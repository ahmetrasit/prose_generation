#39 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · الحسن · reports · A report from Yunus and al-Hasan attributes the same statement to the Prophet; al-Hasan then laments that people complain to others in hardship. «وروى إسماعيل بن إبراهيم عن يونس عن الحسن قال: قال رسول الله»
#40 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · reports · The alternative says the cupbearer, not Joseph, forgot to mention him; some prefer it because a person is not accountable for forgetfulness. «وقيل: إن الهاء تعود على الناجي، فهو الناسي أي أنسى الشيطان الساقي أن يذكر يوسف»
#41 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · rejects · The author argues that 12:45 explicitly identifies the cupbearer as the one who remembered after a time; prophets are protected from forgetfulness only in delivering revelation, and may forget ordinary matters. «وقال الذي نجا منهما وادكر بعد أمة } فدل على أن الناسي هو الساقي لا يوسف»
#42 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم · reports · The Prophet is quoted saying Adam forgot and his descendants forgot, and that he himself forgets as other humans do. «قال صلى الله عليه وسلم:
" نسي آدم فنسيت ذريته "
وقال:
" إنما أنا بشر أنسى كما»
#43 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas says Joseph was punished with a prolonged imprisonment for telling the cupbearer to remember him; had he remembered God, he would have been freed. «وقال ابن عباس: عوقب يوسف بطول الحبس بضع سنين لما قال للذي نجا منهما { اذكرني»
#44 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · الحسن · reports · Yunus reports al-Hasan quoting the Prophet that Joseph's words kept him in prison; al-Hasan wept and said people complain to others when hardship arrives. «وروى إسماعيل بن إبراهيم عن يونس عن الحسن قال: قال رسول الله»
#45 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · reports · An alternative makes the pronoun refer to the cupbearer: Satan made him forget to mention Joseph to his master; some scholars prefer this because forgetfulness itself is not culpable. «وقيل: إن الهاء تعود على الناجي، فهو الناسي أي أنسى الشيطان الساقي أن يذكر يوسف»
#46 BAYDAWI-FULL d.685 · reports · If Joseph's expectation that one would be saved came from reasoning, it illustrates human effort; if from revelation, then 'thought' means certain knowledge. «الظان يوسف إن ذكر ذلك عن اجتهاد وإن ذكره عن وحي فهو الناجي»
#47 BAYDAWI-FULL d.685 · holds · Joseph asked the saved cupbearer to mention his situation to the king so that he could be released. «اذكر حالي عند الملك كي يخلصني»
#48 BAYDAWI-FULL d.685 · reports · The pronoun in 'Satan made him forget' may refer to the cupbearer, whom Satan made forget to mention Joseph; the source can be attributed through association or refer to mentioning the Lord's affairs. «فأنسى الشرابي أن يذكره لربه»
#49 BAYDAWI-FULL d.685 · reports · Alternatively, Satan made Joseph forget God by seeking help from someone else; a prophetic saying supports this by saying that without the request Joseph would not have remained seven years after the first five. «أو أنسي يوسف ذكر الله حتى استعان بغيره، ويؤيده قوله عليه الصلاة والسلام»
#50 BAYDAWI-FULL d.685 · رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم · reports · A prophetic report says, 'May God have mercy on my brother Joseph'; had he not asked the cupbearer to mention him, he would not have remained in prison seven years after the five already served. «رحم الله أخي يوسف لو لم يقل { اذكرني عند ربك } لما لبث في السجن»
#51 BAYDAWI-FULL d.685 · holds · Seeking people's help in hardship is generally acceptable but does not suit the station of prophets; bid' means a span from three to nine and comes from cutting. «والاستعانة بالعباد في كشف الشدائد وإن كانت محمودة في الجملة لكنها لا تليق»
#52 NASAFI-FULL d.710 · reports · If the interpretation came through reasoning, Joseph was the one who thought the cupbearer would be saved; if by revelation, the cupbearer was the one who thought so; alternatively, ẓanna means certainty. «الظان هو يوسف عليه السلام أن كان تأويله بطريق الاجتهاد»
#53 NASAFI-FULL d.710 · holds · Joseph asks the cupbearer to describe him and his case to the king in hope of release from his predicament. «صفنى عند المك بصفتى وقص عليه قصتى لعله يرحمني»
#54 NASAFI-FULL d.710 · reports · Satan made the cupbearer forget to mention Joseph to the king, or made Joseph forget God when he entrusted his affair to another. «فأنسى الشرابى ﴿ ذكر ربه ﴾ أن يذكره لربه أو عند ربه»
#55 NASAFI-FULL d.710 · reports · A prophetic report says God have mercy on Joseph: had he not asked the cupbearer to remember him, he would not have remained in prison seven years; most say the years were seven, while biḍʿ means three through nine. «وفي الحديث رحم الله اخى يوسف لو لم يقل اذكرنى عند ربك لما لبث فى السجن سبعا»
#56 QASHANI d.736 · holds · Asking the cupbearer to remember him means seeking stable spiritual existence through love; intoxicated love raises spirit to unity and heart to spirit, but both remain veiled in divine light rather than annihilated. «اطلب الوجود في مقام الروح بالمحبة والاستقرار فيه»
#57 QASHANI d.736 · holds · Remaining at that level can produce ego and transgression; Satanic illusion makes Joseph forget annihilation because he seeks the spirit's station and retains a remainder of self. «ومن الوقوف في هذا المقام ينشأ الطغيان والأنائية»
#58 QASHANI d.736 · عليه السلام · reports · A prophetic report says God have mercy on Joseph: had he not asked the cupbearer to remember him, he would not have remained imprisoned for years. «أشار النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم بقوله: " رحم الله أخي يوسف»
#59 QASHANI d.736 · holds · Alternatively, illusion forgets the love-messenger when love dominates in bewildered witnessing; the lover forgets creation and self in unity until annihilation ends and divine life restores differentiation. «أو أنسى شيطان الوهم المقهور الممنوع المحجوب عن جناب الحق»
#60 QASHANI d.736 · holds · The prison's duration is the spiritual journey; the later visions of cattle and grain symbolize changes in soul and bodily qualities during seclusion and return. «ثم لما انتهى فناؤه بالانغماس في بحر الهوية والانطماس»
#61 QASHANI d.736 · holds · When both faculties function through God rather than themselves, confinement ends and subsistence begins, though a trace of ego remains. «لكونهما بالله حينئذ لا بنفسهما وانتهى زمان الخلوة بابتداء زمان البقاء»
#62 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · قتادة · reports · Qatāda defines biḍʿ as a number from three through nine. «البضع: ما بين الثلاث إلى التسع قاله قتادة»
#63 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · مجاهد · reports · Mujāhid narrows biḍʿ to three through seven. «وقال مجاهد: من الثلاثة إلى السبعة»
#64 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · أبو عبيدة · reports · Abū ʿUbayda says biḍʿ does not reach a full unit or half of one and ranges from one to ten. «وقال أبو عبيدة: البضع لا يبلغ العقد ولا نصف العقد، وإنما هو من الواحد إلى العشرة»
#65 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · الفراء · reports · Al-Farrāʾ says biḍʿ is used with tens, not with a hundred or a thousand. «وقال الفراء: ولا يذكر البضع إلا مع العشرات، ولا يذكر مع مائة ولا ألف»
#66 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · قتادة · reports · Qatāda takes ẓann in its ordinary sense because dream interpretation is conjectural. «قال قتادة: الظن هنا على بابه، لأن عبارة الرؤيا ظن»
#67 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · الزمخشري · reports · Al-Zamakhsharī makes Joseph the one who thought the cupbearer would be released; Abū Ḥayyān says this is difficult if the interpretation came by conjecture because the decree is final, unless the phrase only means Joseph has finished speaking. «وقال الزمخشري: الظان هو يوسف عليه السلام إن كان تأويله بطريق الاجتهاد»
#68 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · reports · Joseph may have asked the cupbearer to tell the king about his knowledge and standing, or to report his unjust imprisonment. «فالمعنى أن يوسف عليه السلام قال لساقي الملك حين علم أنه سيعود»
#69 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · reports · Abū Ḥayyān sees Joseph's request as permissible practical help, with God making it a cause of relief; he compares it to Jesus seeking helpers and the Prophet seeking a guard. «وهذا من يوسف على سبيل الاستعانة والتعاون في تفريج كربه، وجعله بإذن الله»
#70 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · prefers · Abū Ḥayyān prefers that Joseph wanted to lead the cupbearer to faith, as he had sought the guidance of both prisoners. «والذي أختاره أن يوسف إنما قال لساقي الملك: اذكرني عند ربك ليتوصل»
#71 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · holds · The pronoun in فأنساه refers to the cupbearer; ذكر ربه means remembering Joseph for his master, and the loose relation permits the possessive. «والضمير في فأنساه عائد على الساقي، ومعنى ذكر ربه: ذكر يوسف لربه»
#72 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · holds · Satan's whisper distracted the cupbearer until he forgot Joseph's request; God prolonged Joseph's imprisonment to increase his reward. «وإنساء الشيطان له بما يوسوس إليه من اشتغاله حتى يذهل عما قال له يوسف»
#73 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · reports · A few years is left vague; reports give seven or twelve, or count seven after the prisoners left or two years, while the apparent sense is Joseph's full prison term. «وبضع سنين مجمل، فقيل: سبع، وقيل: اثنا عشر. والظاهر أن قوله: فلبث»
#74 ABUHAYYAN-FULL d.745 · rejects · Some make the pronoun in أنساه refer to Joseph and build stories on that reading that Abū Ḥayyān says should not be attributed to prophets. «وقيل: الضمير في أنساه عائد على يوسف. ورتبوا على ذلك أخبارا لا تليق نسبتها»
#75 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · holds · بضع سنين is a temporal adverb, and authorities give different ranges for بضع. «قوله: { بضع سنين } منصوب على الظرف الزماني وفيه خلاف»
#76 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · قتادة · reports · Qatada says بضع covers three through nine. «فقال قتادة: " هو بين الثلاث إلى التسع ".»
#77 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · أبو عبيد · reports · Abu Ubayd says بضع is below ten and below half a decade? He gives its range as one to ten. «وقال أبو عبيد: " البضع لا يبلغ العقد ولا نصف العقد، وإنما هو من الواحد إلى العشر ".»
#78 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · مجاهد · reports · Mujahid defines بضع as three through seven. «وقال مجاهد: " هو من الثلاثة إلى السبعة ".»
#79 SAMIN-DURR d.756 · الفراء · reports · Al-Farraʾ says بضع is used with tens, not with one hundred or one thousand. «وقال الفراء: " لا يذكر البضع إلا مع العشرات ولا يذكر مع مئة ولا ألف ".»
