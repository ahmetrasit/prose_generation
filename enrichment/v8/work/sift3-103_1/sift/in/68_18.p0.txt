# 100 notes filed under 68:18
verse 68:18: وَلَا يَسْتَثْنُونَ

#1 MUQATIL d.150 · holds · They did not make an exception by saying God willing; God heard this, then sent fire from heaven that night and burned the garden black. «{ ولا يستثنون } [آية: 18] فيقولون: إن شاء الله، فسمع الله تعالى قولهم فبعث نارا من السماء»
#2 FARRA d.207 · reports · After their father's death, the sons decided to stop giving because their dependents had grown and wealth shrunk; they planned to harvest in the late-night darkness so the poor would receive nothing. «فإنا ندع ذلك، ثم تآمروا أن يصرموا في سدف: في ظلمة- باقية من الليل» [filed under 68:17, 68:18]
#3 FARRA d.207 · holds · The justest brother's call to tasbih means to make an exception by saying إن شاء الله, as in the instruction to remember God after forgetting. «ألم أقل لكم لولا تسبحون؟ فالتسبيح هاهنا في معنى الاستثناء» [filed under 68:18, 68:24]
#4 ABDURRAZZAQ d.211 · قتادة · reports · The garden belonged to a charitable old man whose sons criticized his almsgiving; he kept a year's food and gave away the surplus, but after his death they planned to exclude the poor. «عن قتادة في قوله تعالى ﴿ إذ أقسموا ليصرمنها ﴾ قال كانت الجنة لشيخ وكان يتصدق» [filed under 68:17-18]
#5 IBNQUTAYBA-GHARIB d.276 · holds · They swore they would gather the garden's fruit in the morning and did not make an exception. «{إذ أقسموا ليصرمنها مصبحين * ولا يستثنون} أي حلفوا ليجذن ثمرها صباحا؛ ولم يستثنوا.» [filed under 68:17, 68:18]
#6 TAB-FULL d.310 · holds · God tested the Quraysh disbelievers as He tested the garden owners: they swore to harvest at dawn and did not say “if God wills.” «فأقسموا ليصرمنها مصبحين، ولا يستثنون» [filed under 68:17, 68:18]
#7 TAB-FULL d.310 · عكرمة · reports · ʿIkrima says the owners were Ethiopians whose father fed the poor from his garden; after he died, his sons called him foolish and vowed to exclude the poor. «هم ناس من الحبشة كانت لأبيهم جنة كان يطعم المساكين منها» [filed under 68:17, 68:18, 68:24]
#8 TAB-FULL d.310 · قتادة · reports · Qatāda says the garden belonged to a charitable old man; his sons opposed his charity and, after his death, set out to keep poor people away. «كانت الجنة لشيخ، وكان يتصدق، فكان بنوه ينهونه عن الصدقة» [filed under 68:17, 68:18, 68:24]
#9 ZAJJAJ d.311 · reports · The sons feared that continuing their father’s charity would leave too little for their group, so they swore to harvest at night and omitted “if God wills.” «فقال بنوه: نحن جماعة، وإن فعلنا بالمساكين» [filed under 68:17-18]
#10 NAHHAS d.338 · holds · They were censured for not saying 'if God wills': an intended action may be prevented, making the speaker a liar unless he qualifies it. «ولا يقولون إن شاء الله فذموا بهذا لأن الإنسان إذا قال لأفعلن كذا لم يأمن»
#11 THALABI d.427 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas identifies the garden as al-Qayrawan in Yemen, two farsakhs from Sanaa, planted by worshippers and inherited by their sons. «عن أبي صالح عن ابن عباس في قوله تعالى: إنا بلوناهم كما بلونا أصحاب الجنة قال: بستان باليمن» [filed under 68:17-18]
#12 THALABI d.427 · ابن عباس · reports · The father let the poor keep missed fruit, fallen fruit, and grain scattered during harvest and threshing. «عن ابن عباس في قوله تعالى: إنا بلوناهم كما بلونا أصحاب الجنة قال: بستان باليمن» [filed under 68:17-18]
#13 THALABI d.427 · ابن عباس · reports · After inheriting, the sons said their assets were small and dependents numerous, so they could no longer give the poor their customary share. «عن ابن عباس في قوله تعالى: إنا بلوناهم كما بلونا أصحاب الجنة قال: بستان باليمن» [filed under 68:17-18]
#14 THALABI d.427 · ابن عباس · reports · The brothers swore to harvest before people went out, without making an exception by saying if God wills. «عن ابن عباس في قوله تعالى: إنا بلوناهم كما بلونا أصحاب الجنة قال: بستان باليمن» [filed under 68:17-18]
#15 THALABI d.427 · ابن عباس · reports · They went to the garden in the dark and found it blackened after a punishment from heaven burned it during the night. «عن ابن عباس في قوله تعالى: إنا بلوناهم كما بلونا أصحاب الجنة قال: بستان باليمن» [filed under 68:17-20]
#16 THALABI d.427 · holds · Aqsumu means they swore, yasrim means to cut or harvest its fruit, and musbihin means at dawn before the poor knew. «قوله تعالى: إذ أقسموا حلفوا، ليصرمنها لتجديها ولتقطيع ثمرها، مصبحين إذ أصبحوا قبل أن يعلم المساكين»
#17 THALABI d.427 · holds · They did not say insha Allah when they planned their harvest. «ولا يستثنون لا يقولون إن شاء الله»
#18 MAWARDI-FULL d.450 · عكرمة · reports · Not making an exception means that they excluded no share for the poor. «أحدها : لا يستثنون من المساكين , قاله عكرمة .»
#19 MAWARDI-FULL d.450 · أبو صالح · reports · Not making an exception may mean that their exception would have been the words سبحان ربنا. «الثاني : استثناؤهم قول سبحان ربنا , قاله أبو صالح .»
#20 MAWARDI-FULL d.450 · reports · The omitted exception may mean saying إن شاء الله. «الثالث : قول إن شاء الله .»
#21 WAHIDI-BASIT d.468 · المفسرون · reports · The exegetes say they did not say if God wills. «قوله تعالى: {ولا يستثنون} يقولون: إن شاء الله. هذا قول جماعة # المفسرين (¬1). ويقال: حلف فلان يمينا ليس فيها ثنيا ولا ثنوى ولا»
#22 WAHIDI-BASIT d.468 · holds · Several forms describe an oath without exception; they derive from ثني, to restrain or turn back, because exception returns the oath to God's will. «الثني وهو الكف والرد (¬2)، وذلك أن الحالف إذا قال: والله لأفعلن كذا إلا أن يشاء الله غيره فقد رد ما قاله بمشيئة الله غيره.»
#23 WAHIDI-WAJIZ d.468 · holds · They did not make an exception by saying “if God wills.” «{ ولا يستثنون } ولا يقولون إن شاء الله»
#24 JISHUMI d.494 · holds · An exception removes a member from a general class; an exception by God's will instead lifts the ruling of the statement, such as saying unless God wills to prevent or enable me. «والاستثناء: إخراج بعض من كل، لولاه لدخل فيه»
#25 JISHUMI d.494 · holds · The owners are blamed for denying the poor their due, swearing to do so, and omitting an exception; this supports requiring an exception when speaking about the future, since one cannot be sure the statement will come true. «ذمهم على منع الحق، وذمهم على القسم عليه، وذمهم على ترك الاستثناء» [filed under 68:17, 68:18]
#26 JISHUMI d.494 · holds · Intending an evil act is itself blameworthy: they were punished for the resolve even before committing the wrong, and actually taking property and denying rights is worse. «أن العزم على القبيح قبيح؛ لأنهم عوقبوا على عزمهم من غير منع وظلم بالمنع» [filed under 68:17, 68:18]
#27 BAGHAWI-FULL d.516 · holds · They did not make an exception by saying ‘if God wills.’ «{ولا يستثنون} ولا يقولون إن شاء الله.»
#28 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · holds · ولا يستثنون can mean they did not pause, or turn back from their decision to exclude the poor. «وقوله تعالى: ولا يستثنون ولا يتوقفون في ذلك، أو ولا ينثنون عن رأي منع المساكين»
#29 IBNATIYYA-FULL d.542 · مجاهد · reports · Mujahid says they did not say إن شاء الله and resolved as if they controlled the outcome. «وقال مجاهد معناه: لا يقولون إن شاء الله، بل عزموا على ذلك عزم من يملك أمره»
#30 IBNJAWZI-ZAD d.597 · الأكثرون · reports · They did not say in shāʾ Allāh when making their vow. «أحدهما لا يقولون إن شاء الله قاله الأكثرون»
#31 IBNJAWZI-ZAD d.597 · عكرمة · reports · Alternatively, they did not set aside the poor people's due. «والثاني لا يستثنون حق المساكين قاله عكرمة»
#32 IBNJAWZI-ZAD d.597 · أهل التفسير · reports · Interpreters tell of a believing man in Yemen, after Jesus, who kept what he needed and gave the surplus and gleanings to the poor; after his death, his three sons planned to harvest before anyone came and deny the poor their share. «وكان مؤمنا وذلك بعد عيسى بن مريم عليهما السلام وكان يأخذ منه قدر قوته وكان يتصدق بالباقي» [filed under 68:17-18]
#33 RAZI-FULL d.606 · reports · Most commentators say they did not make an exception by saying 'if God wills' because they were overconfident they could harvest; another view says they refused to set aside the poor's usual share. «فالأكثرون أنهم إنما لم يستثنوا بمشيئة الله تعالى» [filed under 68:17, 68:18]
#34 RAZI-FULL d.606 · holds · Istithnāʾ derives from th-n-y, turning something back: adding 'unless God wills' suspends the oath's certainty. «وأصل هذا كله من الثني وهو الكف والرد»
#35 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · ابن عباس · reports · Ibn Abbas says a righteous owner had three sons and gave the poor all fallen grapes, missed fruit, stray grain, and spilled threshed produce. «كانت تلك الجنة دون صنعاء بفرسخين، غرسها رجل من أهل الصلاح» [filed under 68:17-18]
#36 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · عكرمة · reports · Ikrima interprets their failure to make an exception as failing to exempt the poor’s due from their plan. «أي لا يستثنون حق المساكين»
#37 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · reports · The father’s charity supported orphans, widows, and poor people; after he died, the sons complained of fewer assets and more dependents. «وكان يعيش في ذلك في حياة أبيهم اليتامى والأرامل والمساكين، فلما مات أبوهم» [filed under 68:17-18]
#38 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · reports · The sons agreed to go before the poor at dawn and secretly harvest the garden so the needy would not know. «فتحالفوا بينهم ليغدون غدوة قبل خروج الناس ثم ليصرمنها ولا تعرف المساكين» [filed under 68:17-18]
#39 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · holds · “They made no exception” means they did not say “if God wills.” «أي ولم يقولوا إن شاء الله»
#40 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · أبو صالح · reports · Abu Salih says their formula of exception was “Glory be to God, our Lord.” «كان استثناؤهم قولهم سبحان الله»
#41 QURTUBI-FULL d.671 · holds · Qurtubi says people can be held accountable for a firm intention: the garden owners were punished before carrying out their plan, as in the threat concerning wrongdoing in the Sacred Mosque. «ومن يرد فيه بإلحاد بظلم نذقه من عذاب أليم» [filed under 68:17-19, 22:25]
