# Section 1 Bible audit notes

The eight base paragraphs move from the worn road (1), the guide and destination (2), straightness (3), inherited communal route (4), signs and the surah's full prayer shape (5), the lost road and home plus cognates (6), competing routes and worship (7), to dispersion, judgment and opposed destinations (8).

Original-language searches checked Hebrew straight ways, paved paths, guideposts, service, Aramaic judgment, and Greek straight road and guiding forms. The additional paved-path witness is WLC:Prov.15.19. WLC:Ps.27.11 and the Matthew 6 prayer were also opened as additions from the local Corpus Coranicum leads. WLC citations use the ketiv stream; the differing qere in Jer.31.21 is not quoted as main text.

WLC:Isa.19.23 speaks of Egypt serving Assyria, so its proposed shared divine worship is not claimed.
