root	ع ص ر	עצר	shifted: In the ayah al-ʿaṣr means time or the late afternoon, while Biblical Hebrew עצר means to restrain or detain and also gives the noun for a solemn assembly.
verse	ع ص ر	Gen.16.2	עצרני means “has kept me from” bearing children, showing the Hebrew sense of restraint.
verse	ع ص ر	Gen.20.18	עצר עצר describes the LORD closing the wombs of Abimelech’s household, showing the Hebrew sense of holding back.
verse	ع ص ر	Lev.23.36	עצרת names a solemn assembly on the eighth day, a Hebrew sense distinct from the ayah’s “time.”
verse	ع ص ר	Deut.16.8	עצרת names the solemn assembly on the seventh day, rather than a time of day.
