- ¶28: The base hears the Lord's name as an identifying mark while keeping the ordinary praise command. Psalm 9:3 supplies the closest name/Most High hymn formula. Matthew 6:9, John 12:28, and 2 Timothy 2:19 provide Christian prayer and seal parallels. Exodus 3:15 shows Hebrew *shem* but does not by itself establish the proposed Arabic etymology.
- ¶29: The base connects fire, the stamped camel footprint, remnant, divine knowledge, and facial signs. Galatians 6:17 and 2 Corinthians 1:22; 3:2-3 provide bodily and inward Christian marks. 2 Corinthians 1:22 seals believers and gives the Spirit as a pledge in their hearts; it does not locate the seal on the hearts. Aramaic *nûr* in Daniel 3:6 is clear, but the verse alone cannot verify an Arabic cognacy claim or a branding link.
- ¶30: The base treats preference as leaving a recognizable trace, with the Lord's name and fire as opposite outcomes. Jeremiah 17:1/31:33 contrasts inner inscriptions; Deuteronomy 30:19 and Proverbs 20:11 join choice and recognizable conduct. Matthew 7:16-20 joins fruit, recognition, and fire; 1 John 2:17, Matthew 6:19-21, and Hebrews 11:25-26 join present value and lasting outcome. Revelation 13:16-17 and 14:1,9-10 distinguishes rival forehead signs and their result.

Exodus 24:7 and Deuteronomy 31:26 give Moses' book a witness function without identifying it with the Qur'anic sheets. 2 Peter 2:6 and Jude 1:7 make Sodom an example; Matthew 6:16-18 warns that deliberately altered facial appearance does not prove inner devotion.
