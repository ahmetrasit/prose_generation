## Confirmed problems in Sonnet

1. **Translation absence claim:** `S107-MEAL-004` (line 70), repeated in `S107-MEAL-009` (line 242), says no checked meal represents delaying prayer. The very [comparison page](https://www.kuranmeali.com/AyetKarsilastirma.php?sure=107&ayet=5) includes Suleyman Tevfik's explanatory parenthesis about letting prayer times pass, Ahmet Tekin's reference to observing prayer times, and Suat Yildirim's explanatory note. A narrower claim would need to specify its actual sample and treatment of notes/parentheses.
2. **Tabari's rationale:** `S107-TAF-014` (line 211) attributes his singular-reading preference to greater plurality/variety. [Tabari 9:103](https://quran.ksu.edu.sa/tafseer/tabary/sura9-aya103.html) reports that argument from others but explicitly rejects it as his own reason. His preference concerns the verse's purpose: the Prophet's supplication reassures these people. Sol/max `S107-QIR-003` accurately separates the numerical argument from his rationale.

The source list also says Tabersi where Tabari is meant (`S107-SRC-001`, line 12).

## Findings that distinguish the drafts

- **Sol/max:** `S107-CQ-002` and `S107-NOV-010` preserve Ibn Zayd's explicit water analogy. Sonnet read the same Tabari 51:11 passage, but its `S107-XQ-003` omits that part. This matters when testing whether the project's image has an older antecedent.
- **Astra/high:** `S107-SEM-006` and `S107-NOV-019` add actual Raghib antecedents for the prayer/fire image and the S107/Muddaththir comparison. Sonnet did not independently inspect the relevant entry; its bounded negative findings should be expanded when this evidence is incorporated.
- **Astra/max:** `S107-TAF-015/016` and `S107-NOV-016` establish that water, easy assistance and fulfilling rights are already connected in Wahidi, who also joins 23:50 and 67:30. Sonnet covers water meanings in S107 tafsir but lacks this stronger outside-surah antecedent.
- **Sonnet:** The name conflict for the an/fi remark is a useful corroborated source-critical finding, and its meal module opens a valuable editorial dimension. Translation edition ambiguity, summaries rather than full fetched text, and an incorrect absence claim mean the module needs further checking before its categorical judgements are adopted.

## Project use

Use Astra/max as the main reference among these files. Astra/high offers the strongest additional lexical antecedents; Sol/max offers strong local corrections and claim auditing. Sonnet is useful as a supplementary translation-review and classical-interpretation draft after correcting the two confirmed errors. Primary hadith access failed in Sonnet's run, so its weaker direct verification should be assessed as an artifact limitation rather than an intrinsic model characteristic.

The independent structural audit passed: all 63 base blocks and all 243 inline tags are preserved. Structural validity and substantive source accuracy are separate checks; the accompanying independent editorial review records that source corrections remain necessary. No generated output was modified during this comparison.
