# 96:19/q11 Who is told to draw near, and to what? [وَٱقْتَرِب · referent]
turns on: Whether the second imperative continues addressing Muhammad or turns to Abū Jahl.
- 96:19/q11/p1 The Prophet is told to draw near to God.
  reasons: Al-Māwardī gives this as his view; Ibn al-Jawzī ascribes it to the majority. Al-Jishumī prefers it because the sūra addresses the Prophet.

# 96:19/q12 How is nearness in 'draw near' attained or understood? [وَٱقْتَرِب · interpretation]
turns on: Whether nearness is sought rank, direct closeness, reward, supplication, or general worship.
- 96:19/q12/p1 Approach God through obedience, worship and prostration.
  reasons: Muqātil and al-Zajjāj specify obedience; Ibn ʿAṭiyya adds good deeds. Al-Rāzī speaks of nearness in rank and contrasts obedient closeness with Satan's distance (2:186). Al-Biqāʿī adds inward love through every worship. Ibn ʿĀshūr says prayer pleases God.
  holders (39 notes): MUQATIL; TAB; ZAJJAJ; THALABI; MAWARDI; MAWARDI-FULL; WAHIDI-BASIT(أبو إسحاق، مقاتل); WAHIDI-QT; WAHIDI-WAJIZ; JISHUMI; BAGHAWI; BAGHAWI-FULL; KASHSHAF; IBNATIYYA(مجاهد); IBNATIYYA-FULL(مجاهد); TABRISI(قيل); IBNJAWZI-ZAD; RAZI; RAZI-FULL; QURTUBI; QURTUBI-FULL; BAYDAWI; NASAFI; ABUHAYYAN; BIQAI; BIQAI-FULL; BURSEVI; ALUSI; ELMALILI; QUTB-ZILAL; IBNASHUR; IBNASHUR-FULL; ISLAHI-TADABBUR; STUDYQURAN
