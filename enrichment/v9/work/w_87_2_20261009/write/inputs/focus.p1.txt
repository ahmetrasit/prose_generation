# 87:2/q04 What is the sense of 'fashioned' (sawwā) as a word? [فَسَوَّىٰ · meaning]
turns on: The word's range includes equality, adjustment, straightening, improvement, and perfection; Iṣlāḥī says the context selects perfection.
- 87:2/q04/p1 It means making proportionate, adjusted, straight, or precise.
  reasons: Al-Ṭabarī calls it adjustment; Jishumī says equality in form, rank, status, or any respect. Al-Ālūsī calls proportion its original sense; Ibn ʿĀshūr glosses it as straightening and precise making, citing 91:7 and 75:38. Iṣlāḥī also notes senses of improving and flattening, while choosing perfection here.
  holders (7 notes): TAB; JISHUMI; ALUSI; IBNASHUR-FULL; ISLAHI-TADABBUR
  notes: TAB:87:2/r1 JISHUMI:v10p7372/r6 ALUSI:87:1/r39 ALUSI:87:2/r26 IBNASHUR-FULL:v29p340/r9 ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p274/r1 ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p274/r4
- 87:2/q04/p2 In this context it means completing or perfecting what is made.
  reasons: Iṣlāḥī contrasts possible senses of improving or flattening and says context selects perfecting; he also pairs an initial sketch with its later completion. Ibn ʿAṭiyyah and Qutb speak of perfection suited to the creature.
  holders (6 notes): IBNATIYYA; IBNATIYYA-FULL; QUTB-ZILAL; ISLAHI-TADABBUR+
  notes: ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p273/r6 ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p274/r1 ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p274/r4 IBNATIYYA:87:2/r1 IBNATIYYA-FULL:v5p468#3/r3 QUTB-ZILAL:87:2/r1

# 87:2/q05 What does the 'fa' between creating and fashioning express? [فَسَوَّىٰ · grammar]
turns on: Whether the ordered verbs mark a conceptual and causal sequence accompanying one act, or emphasize observable stages of human development.
- 87:2/q05/p1 It makes fashioning the consequence and chief point of creation, ordered in thought though simultaneous in occurrence.
  reasons: Ibn ʿĀshūr compares Ibn Ziyāba's poem, where a raid, plunder, and escape provide the ordered groundwork for grief; creation likewise precedes fashioning in the observer's understanding. Al-Biqāʿī says creation requires proportion for balance.
  holders (7 notes): IBNASHUR(ابن زيابة); IBNASHUR-FULL; BIQAI-FULL
  notes: IBNASHUR:87:2/r5 IBNASHUR:87:2/r6 IBNASHUR:87:2/r7 IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p275/r6 IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p275/r7 IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p276/r1 BIQAI-FULL:v8p395#2/r3
- 87:2/q05/p2 The sequence reflects a gradual development from initial making to completed form.
  reasons: Muqātil describes drop, clot, and morsel of flesh in the womb before proportionate formation; Iṣlāḥī says God's works unfold gradually in wisdom, and a human sketch passes through stages before completion.
  holders (4 notes): MUQATIL(مقاتل); ISLAHI-TADABBUR
  notes: MUQATIL:87:1-7/r2 ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p270/r3 ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p273/r5 ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p274/r1

# 87:2/q07 What divine claim does the clause make about human acts? [خَلَقَ · theology]
turns on: Whether universal creation includes human acts without qualification, or allows mediated creation by God's granting human capacities.
- 87:2/q07/p1 God's universal creation includes human acts and challenges the claim that humans create their own acts.
  reasons: Al-Ālūsī appeals to the verse's unrestricted generality against the Muʿtazilī claim.
  holders (3 notes): ALUSI-FULL+; ALUSI+
  notes: ALUSI:87:1/r41 ALUSI:87:2/r28 ALUSI-FULL:v30p103#2/r3
- 87:2/q07/p2 God creates human acts directly or through enabling people and granting their capacities.
  reasons: Al-Ālūsī suggests this as a possible way to understand al-Zamakhsharī's broad wording despite his Muʿtazilī commitments; he also allows that al-Zamakhsharī may avoid strict universality.
  holders (3 notes): ALUSI; ALUSI-FULL
  notes: ALUSI:87:1/r42 ALUSI:87:2/r29 ALUSI-FULL:v30p103#2/r3
