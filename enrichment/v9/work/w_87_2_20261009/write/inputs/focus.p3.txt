# 87:2/q16 How does the following determination develop the account of creation? [* · links]
- 87:2/q16/p1 The following verb can be read as 'measured' or as 'had power' or 'determined,' affirming God's direct power over what He created.
  reasons: Study Quran reports qaddara and qadara as variant readings and explains the latter by God's continuing direct power over creation.
