# 75:40: 12 questions

## 75:40/q01 What force does the question introduced by أَلَيْسَ have?  [أَلَيْسَ · rhetoric]
- 75:40/q01/p1 It rebukes denial and turns the negative form into firm affirmation of God's power. — Ibn ʿAṭiyya calls the ending a rebuke. Ibn al-Naḥḥās says the interrogative hamza reverses laysa's negation. Bint al-Shāṭiʾ says this pattern consistently yields definite affirmation; its bāʾ reinforces declarative force. [4 notes: IBNATIYYA-FULL; BURHAN(ابن النحاس); BINTSHATI-IJAZ]

## 75:40/q02 Whom does ذَٰلِكَ indicate?  [ذَٰلِكَ · referent]
- 75:40/q02/p1 God, the unspoken agent of the preceding creation. — Al-Wāḥidī points to the implied agent of 'He created and fashioned.' Al-Sāmarrāʾī notes that the demonstrative's referent is unstated, matching the omitted agents of the preceding acts. [2 notes: WAHIDI-BASIT; SAMARRAI-LAMASAT]

## 75:40/q03 What does the bāʾ in بِقَٰدِرٍ contribute?  [بِقَٰدِرٍ · rhetoric]
turns on: The notes discuss the bāʾ's syntactic augmentation and its rhetorical reinforcement.
- 75:40/q03/p1 It strengthens the affirmative force of the question. — Bint al-Shāṭiʾ notes its regular presence with these interrogatives over laysa clauses. Al-Sāmarrāʾī says it underscores resurrection, whose mention carries greater rhetorical weight than the earlier finger-shaping affirmation. [4 notes: BINTSHATI-IJAZ; SAMARRAI-LAMASAT]
- 75:40/q03/p2 It is grammatically extra in the predicate of laysa, an exceptional construction. — Al-Samīn identifies the genitive active participle after the extra bāʾ. Ibn ʿAṣfūr calls this augmentative use rare and nonanalogical, comparing 39:36 and 17:99 without bāʾ. Ibn al-Naḥḥās says the affirmative interrogative makes it exceptional in analogy, though not in actual usage. [3 notes: SAMIN-DURR(العامة); BURHAN(ابن النحاس,ابن عصفور)]

## 75:40/q04 How is بِقَٰدِرٍ read?  [بِقَٰدِرٍ · readings]
turns on: Whether the predicate is the common active participle or a finite verb.
- 75:40/q04/p1 The common reading has qādir, an active participle after bāʾ. — Al-Samīn describes it as genitive in the predicate of laysa. [1 notes: SAMIN-DURR(العامة)]
- 75:40/q04/p2 Abū Bakr, Abū Rajāʾ, ʿĀṣim al-Jaḥdarī, and Zayd read the finite imperfect yaqdir. — Ibn al-Jawzī names the first three readers; al-Samīn and al-Ālūsī name Zayd ibn ʿAlī and gloss the verb as 'is able.' [3 notes: IBNJAWZI-ZAD(أبو بكر الصديق وأبو رجاء وعاصم الجحدري); SAMIN-DURR(العامة); ALUSI-FULL(زيد)]

## 75:40/q05 How is the final yāʾ of يُحْۦِىَ vocalized?  [يُحْۦِىَ · readings]
turns on: Whether the accusative fatḥa is pronounced or the weak final yāʾ is still.
- 75:40/q05/p1 The majority give the final yāʾ a fatḥa, the accusative after an. — Ibn ʿAṭiyya reports the majority's fatḥa. Al-Samīn identifies the accusative governed by an. [2 notes: IBNATIYYA-FULL(جمهور القراء); SAMIN-DURR(العامة)]
- 75:40/q05/p2 Ṭalḥa, Sulaymān, and al-Fayyāḍ read the final yāʾ with sukūn. — Ibn ʿAṭiyya says it drops in pronunciation before the still lām of al-mawtā. Al-Samīn identifies either omitted case vowel or connected speech treated as a pause; al-Ālūsī notes a comparable poetic deletion. Names of Ṭalḥa and al-Fayyāḍ vary slightly. [3 notes: IBNATIYYA-FULL(طلحة بن مصرف، سليمان، الفياض بن غزوان); SAMIN-DURR(العامة); ALUSI-FULL(طلحة بن سليمان والفيض بن غزوان)]

## 75:40/q06 May the two yāʾs of يُحْۦِىَ be assimilated?  [يُحْۦِىَ · readings]
turns on: The dispute turns on whether a temporary case vowel licenses merger despite consecutive sukūns, and on al-Farrāʾ's poetic parallel.
- 75:40/q06/p1 They remain clearly pronounced without full assimilation. — Al-Akhfash says assimilation is not required without the necessary fatḥa. Al-Naḥḥās, following al-Khalīl and Sībawayh, rejects it because two still sounds meet and the accusative vowel is incidental. Al-Samīn reports the majority's same two-sukūn objection and the Basran objection to an inflectional vowel. Al-Ālūsī cites Ibn Khālawayh for the Basran view. [6 notes: AKHFASH; NAHHAS; JISHUMI(الفراء); SAMIN-DURR(جمهور الناس); ALUSI-FULL(ابن خالويه); SAMIN-DURR(مكي)-]
- 75:40/q06/p2 Assimilation is permitted after moving the first yāʾ's vowel to the ḥāʾ. — Al-Farrāʾ permits this, as al-Naḥḥās, al-Jishumī, and al-Samīn report; al-Jishumī says he cites a poetic line and al-Samīn gives the form fa-taʿī. Al-Samīn says al-Farrāʾ permits it in the accusative and rejects Makkī's claim of consensus against it in the nominative: the cited line is nominative. Al-Ālūsī calls the reading irregular. [6 notes: NAHHAS(الفراء); JISHUMI(الفراء); SAMIN-DURR(بعض الناس,مكي); ALUSI-FULL(الفراء); AKHFASH-; NAHHAS-; SAMIN-DURR(جمهور الناس)-; ALUSI-FULL(ابن خالويه)-]
- 75:40/q06/p3 An intermediate concealment between full assimilation and clear articulation is possible. — Al-Akhfash reports this pronunciation and says the final yāʾ's alternation in yaḥyā and loss in the jussive prevent full assimilation while allowing intermediate concealment. [2 notes: AKHFASH(some)]

## 75:40/q07 How does earlier human creation prove power to revive the dead?  [* · interpretation]
- 75:40/q07/p1 The Creator who first formed a living human can recreate and revive the dead. — Muqātil, al-Ṭabarī, al-Zajjāj, al-Naḥḥās, al-Wāḥidī, al-Jishumī, al-Baghawī, al-Zamakhsharī, Ibn ʿAṭiyya, al-Ṭabrisī, Ibn al-Jawzī, al-Rāzī, al-Qurṭubī, al-Nasafī, Ibn Kathīr, al-Biqāʿī, al-Bursevī, al-Ālūsī, Ibn ʿĀshūr, al-Ṭabāṭabāʾī, Kur'an Yolu, and al-Sāmarrāʾī's Ḥalaqāt all derive resurrection from first creation. Al-Ṭabarī stresses restoration as before from semen; al-Naḥḥās notes growth to offspring; al-Jishumī connects resurrection to judgment and recompense; al-Ṭabrisī names five senses, distinct organs, and the sexes and calls the inference valid rational analogy; al-Qurṭubī specifies bodily restoration after decay; Ibn Kathīr cites 30:27; al-Biqāʿī says the sexes from one drop are subtler than separating dust from earth and allows revival all at once or successively; Ibn ʿĀshūr cites 21:104; al-Wāḥidī al-Wajīz adds universal power. [25 notes: MUQATIL; TAB-FULL; ZAJJAJ; NAHHAS; WAHIDI-BASIT; WAHIDI-WAJIZ; JISHUMI; BAGHAWI-FULL; KASHSHAF-FULL; IBNATIYYA-FULL; TABRISI; IBNJAWZI-ZAD; RAZI-FULL; QURTUBI-FULL; NASAFI-FULL; IBNKATHIR-FULL; BIQAI-FULL; BURSEVI; ALUSI-FULL; IBNASHUR-FULL; TABATABAI; KURANYOLU-TEFSIR; SAMARRAI-LAMASAT-HALAQAT]

## 75:40/q08 How difficult is revival compared with first creation?  [أَن يُحْۦِىَ ٱلْمَوْتَىٰ · theology]
turns on: The notes distinguish rational comparison between first and second creation from equality of all acts for divine power.
- 75:40/q08/p1 Revival is easier by rational comparison with originating life. — Ibn Kathīr calls this the more famous of two views and relates the debate to 30:27. Al-Bursevī reasons that primordial matter and original elements remain. Al-Ālūsī and al-Ṭabāṭabāʾī argue from the completed first creation. Al-Biqāʿī compares forming two sexes from one drop with separating a dead person's dust from earth. [6 notes: BURSEVI; ALUSI-FULL; TABATABAI; BIQAI-FULL; IBNKATHIR-FULL+]
- 75:40/q08/p2 The two acts are equal in divine power, even if revival seems harder to people. — Ibn Kathīr reports equality as the other view in the argument about 30:27. Al-Sāmarrāʾī says reviving the dead can seem harder than reshaping fingertips to human reason, although divine acts are equal. [2 notes: IBNKATHIR-FULL; SAMARRAI-LAMASAT]

## 75:40/q09 Why is this final resurrection question so emphatic?  [أَلَيْسَ ذَٰلِكَ بِقَٰدِرٍ عَلَىٰٓ أَن يُحْۦِىَ ٱلْمَوْتَىٰ · rhetoric]
- 75:40/q09/p1 Its full interrogative and emphatic bāʾ give resurrection more rhetorical weight than the shorter affirmation of shaping fingertips. — Al-Sāmarrāʾī contrasts the explicit balā qādirīn about fingertips, where verb and circumstantial subject are omitted, with this complete question; human reason can see revival as harder. He says the two passages pair God's power over fingertips and over the dead, affirming the former directly and the latter interrogatively. [3 notes: SAMARRAI-LAMASAT]

## 75:40/q10 What is reported as the response when this verse is recited?  [* · reports]
turns on: The reports give distinct affirmative formulas, some adding glorification, testimony, divine might, or a direct declaration of ability.
- 75:40/q10/p1 Glorify or praise God and affirm yes; the transmitted invocations vary. — Ibn ʿAbbās, Qatāda, al-Barāʾ, Abū Hurayra, Abū Jaʿfar, Abū ʿAbd Allāh, al-Riḍā, and reports from the Prophet supply forms such as subḥānaka Allāhumma wa-balā, subḥānahu wa-balā, subḥāna rabbī wa-balā, and praise with assent. Some add 'by God' twice or 'Lord of the worlds'; al-Wāḥidī reports 'Our Lord, yes.' Rooftop and prayer reports attribute this response to the Prophet; Ibn Kathīr says Abū Dāwūd alone transmits the rooftop report and its unnamed Companion does not harm it. Ibn ʿAbbās says to answer even while leading prayer, comparing the response at 87:1. [31 notes: TAB(ابن عباس); TAB-FULL(قتادة); THALABI(ابن عباس,النبي); WAHIDI-BASIT(ابن عباس,النبي); JISHUMI(البراء بن عازب); BAGHAWI-FULL(رجل عن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم); KASHSHAF-FULL(رسول الله); IBNATIYYA-FULL(النبي); TABRISI(رسول الله); RAZI-FULL(النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم); QURTUBI-FULL(ابن عباس,النبي); NASAFI-FULL(النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم); IBNKATHIR-FULL(ابن عباس,قتادة,محمد صلى الله عليه وسلم); DURR-FULL(أبو هريرة,رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم,موسى بن أبي عائشة); BURSEVI(ابن عباس,النبي,النبي عليه السلام); ALUSI-FULL; TABATABAI(أبو جعفر وأبو عبد الله عليهما السلام,أبو هريرة,البراء بن عازب,الرضا عليه السلام)]
- 75:40/q10/p2 Answer simply 'yes' (balā). — The Prophet is reported to do this and to instruct reciters through Abū Hurayra and Jābir; Ibn ʿAbbās also teaches assent. The Abū Hurayra instruction pairs 75:40 with 95:8 and 77:50, giving the latter their own answers. Ibn Kathīr names Abū Dāwūd, Aḥmad, and al-Tirmidhī as transmitters; al-Ḥākim authenticates one version, while al-Haythamī notes two unknown men in Aḥmad's chain. Al-Suyūṭī also cites the instruction and separately notes that seven of twenty-two Qur'anic occurrences of balā cannot be paused on because following words are syntactically attached; his count concerns Qur'anic balā generally. [15 notes: TAB-FULL(رسول الله); BAGHAWI-FULL(أبو هريرة عن النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم); IBNATIYYA-FULL(النبي); IBNJAWZI-ZAD(ابن عباس); BIQAI-FULL(الحافظ نور الدين الهيثمي,رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم); DURR-FULL(أبو هريرة,جابر بن عبد الله); ITQAN; BURSEVI(النبي); ALUSI-FULL(أبو هريرة); ELMALILI(Ebû Hüreyre); IBNKATHIR-FULL(أبو هريرة); STUDYQURAN(Prophet)]
- 75:40/q10/p3 Answer 'yes, and I am among those who testify.' — Abū Umāma reports hearing the Prophet frequently recite the sūra and use this response. [1 notes: DURR-FULL(أبو أمامة)]
- 75:40/q10/p4 Answer 'yes, by the might of our Lord.' — Al-Biqāʿī attributes this version of the closing response to al-Tirmidhī. [1 notes: BIQAI-FULL(الترمذي)]
- 75:40/q10/p5 Answer 'O God, indeed You are able.' — Ibn al-Jawzī reports Ibn ʿAbbās prescribing this answer to whoever recites the verse. [1 notes: IBNJAWZI-ZAD(ابن عباس)]
- 75:40/q10/p6 Answer 'subḥānaka Allāhumma, qabli.' — Ibn Abī Ḥātim records this wording both from a reciter who heard the Prophet and from Ibn ʿAbbās himself. [2 notes: IBNABIHATIM(ابن عباس,رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم)]

## 75:40/q11 What promise is reported for a reciter who believes this verse?  [* · reports]
- 75:40/q11/p1 The Prophet and Gabriel will testify on Resurrection Day that the reciter believed in it. — Al-Zamakhsharī attributes this promise to the Prophet and gives no grading. [1 notes: KASHSHAF-FULL(رسول الله)]

## 75:40/q12 How does this ending connect to the sūra's opening and argument?  [* · links]
- 75:40/q12/p1 It returns to the opening doubt about resurrection and completes the argument from human creation. — Al-Biqāʿī joins the opening collection of bones to resurrection on the Day of Gathering. Iṣlāḥī links 75:3–4 and the question whether humanity is left unaccountable, which he says would violate divine justice and wisdom. Al-Sāmarrāʾī links the opening oath, gathered bones, record of deeds, threats in 75:34–35, omitted agents in the creation account, and the closing resurrection proof. Kur'an Yolu notes the warning after human creation; Ibn ʿĀshūr says 75:37–40 prepare the closing question and echo 21:104. [8 notes: BIQAI-FULL; ISLAHI-TADABBUR; KURANYOLU-TEFSIR; SAMARRAI-LAMASAT; IBNASHUR-FULL]
