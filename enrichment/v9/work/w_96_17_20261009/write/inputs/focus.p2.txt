# 96:17/q08 What did the reports say prompted this challenge? [* · reports]
turns on: The reports differ in the threat, the visual barrier or angelic intervention, the order of boast and recitation, and whether a scene or grading is preserved.
- 96:17/q08/p1 Abū Jahl barred the praying Prophet, boasted of the valley's largest gathering, and the verses answered his boast.
  reasons: Ibn ʿAbbās places the confrontation at the Maqām and says angels would seize him if he summoned his group; al-Tirmidhī's grading is given as ḥasan gharīb ṣaḥīḥ by al-Qurṭubī and ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ by Ibn Kathīr and al-Suyūṭī. Reports vary over whether the Prophet's rebuke is preserved, whether the boasts follow recitation, and whether his threat is explicit. Al-Tirmidhī and al-Nasāʾī are named by Ibn ʿĀshūr.

# 96:17/q09 When is the threatened seizure or defeat realized? [* · interpretation]
turns on: Whether the angels' seizure is the immediate counter to his call, a Badr fulfillment, or an afterlife punishment.
- 96:17/q09/p1 If he summoned his group or approached, the angels would seize him immediately, visibly.
  reasons: Ibn ʿAbbās repeatedly gives this conditional explanation, and the Prophet's warning in the trample reports is similar. Ibn Kathīr cites al-Tirmidhī's ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ grading of the assembly report; al-Qurṭubī gives ḥasan gharīb ṣaḥīḥ.
  holders (14 notes): TAB(ابن عباس); BAGHAWI-FULL(ابن عباس); RAZI(ابن عباس); RAZI-FULL(ابن عباس); QURTUBI(ابن عباس); IBNKATHIR(ابن عباس); IBNKATHIR-FULL(رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم); QUTB-ZILAL(ابن عباس)

# 96:17/q10 How does this challenge connect to the surrounding verses? [* · links]
turns on: The connection may stress the counter-summons of 96:18 or the broader self-sufficiency theme of 96:6–7 and denial in 96:19.
- 96:17/q10/p1 His call to a human council is met by God's call to the zabāniya, exposing the council's helplessness.
  reasons: Al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī and Ibn Kathīr relate 96:17 to 96:18. Muqātil describes the zabāniya as Hell's keepers
