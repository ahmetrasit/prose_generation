# 87:15/q03 What prayer does 'then prayed' denote? [فَصَلَّىٰ · interpretation]
turns on: Whether ṣallā names the later prescribed cycle, Eid, voluntary prayer, supplication, or earlier/broader worship.
- 87:15/q03/p1 The five prescribed daily prayers.
  reasons: Ibn ʿAbbās repeatedly supplies this gloss; the Jābir report adds keeping them at their appointed times. Al-Ṭabarī and al-Naḥḥās prefer ritual prayer, and al-Naḥḥās especially chooses Ibn ʿAbbās. The Kashshāf compares 2:177. Ibn Kathīr stresses seeking God's pleasure and following His law.
