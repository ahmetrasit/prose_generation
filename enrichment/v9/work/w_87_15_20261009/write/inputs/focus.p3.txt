# 87:15/q09 Can the verse apply specifically to fitr alms and Eid prayer despite the sūra's date? [* · interpretation]
turns on: How a Meccan date relates to practices instituted later, and whether word order is decisive.
- 87:15/q09/p1 Yes; general wording may anticipate a later rule or these verses may have been revealed in Medina.
  reasons: Al-Baghawī compares 90:2 and 54:45, fulfilled at the conquest and Badr; ʿUmar recognized the Badr prediction later. Al-Wāḥidī says God can praise foreknown acts. Ibn al-Ḥaṣṣār cites Meccan zakāt language at 6:141 and 73:20. Al-Ālūsī says the unusual order may urge payment before Eid; al-Ṭabāṭabāʾī locates the closing verses in Medina.
  holders (16 notes): BAGHAWI(عمر بن الخطاب); BAGHAWI-FULL; RAZI-FULL(الواحدي); BIQAI; BIQAI-FULL; ITQAN(ابن الحصار، البغوي); ALUSI; ALUSI-FULL; TABATABAI; STUDYQURAN(family of the Prophet)

# 87:15/q10 What reported conduct was associated with reciting these verses? [* · reports]
turns on: Which transmitted event or practice the verse was applied to.
- 87:15/q10/p1 The Prophet or early Muslims gave or directed fitr alms before going to Eid prayer.
  reasons: Reports name the Prophet, ʿAlī, Ibn ʿUmar, Abū Saʿīd, Abū al-ʿĀliya, Ibn Sīrīn, ʿUmar ibn ʿAbd al-ʿAzīz and al-Nakhaʿī. Ibn ʿUmar checked payment before departing; Abū al-ʿĀliya praised fitra and providing water.
