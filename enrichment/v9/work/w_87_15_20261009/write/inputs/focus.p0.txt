# 87:15/q02 What does 'the Lord's name' point to? [ٱسْمَ رَبِّهِۦ · referent]
turns on: Whether 'name' designates God, the uttered divine designation, or a particular attribute.
- 87:15/q02/p1 God Himself, whose name calls its bearer to mind.
  reasons: Al-Thaʿlabī records 'God Himself' versus actual pronunciation. Al-Biqāʿī says uttering the name inscribes its bearer in the heart; Ibn ʿĀshūr says 'name' may be inserted to indicate God's affairs and qualities.
  holders (6 notes): THALABI; QURTUBI; BIQAI; BIQAI-FULL; IBNASHUR; IBNASHUR-FULL
  notes: THALABI:v10p185/r2 QURTUBI:87:15/r1 BIQAI:87:15/r3 BIQAI-FULL:v8p400/r6 IBNASHUR:87:15/r2 IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p288/r1
- 87:15/q02/p2 God's spoken name, including His attributes of beneficence and perfection.
  reasons: Al-Biqāʿī says attributes
