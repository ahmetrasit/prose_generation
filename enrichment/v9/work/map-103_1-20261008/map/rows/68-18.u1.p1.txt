  68:18/q10/p1 The garden owners' conduct serves as a parable of the Quraysh's wealth and stinginess. — reasons: Al-Ṭabarī says God tested the Quraysh disbelievers as He tested the garden owners. Asad links their failure to qualify the plan with the question about wealth in 68:14-15. Islahi says the oath to leave nothing mirrors the Quraysh leaders' forbidding good, especially Abū Lahab and his associates.
  68:18/q10/p2 The omitted qualification illustrates the instruction to remember God's will when planning. — reasons: Al-Farrāʾ invokes 18:24 for the justest brother's call to tasbīḥ. Kur'an Yolu cites 18:23-24 and 31:34 for the limits of human knowledge and power.
