79:42/q13 What is rebuked in the reply to this question? [* · interpretation] (turns on: Whether the reply redirects the Prophet's attention from timing or rebukes the questioners for demanding timing despite sufficient warning.)
  79:42/q13/p1 The Prophet's occupation with recalling and disclosing the Hour's appointed time. — reasons: Razi reports this reading and invokes Aisha's account that the Prophet kept mentioning and being asked about the Hour until revelation. Ibn Atiyya reports Aisha saying he stopped after this verse descended.
  79:42/q13/p2 The questioners' demand for a date despite the warning and signs they have already received. — reasons: Razi reports this alternative: the final messenger has given signs of nearness, while God alone knows the time. Islahi says their teasing should not trouble the Prophet, whose duty is warning and preparation.
79:42/q14 What did the Prophet reportedly say when asked to date the Hour? [* · reports] (turns on: Whether the reported answer refrains from a date or appears to set one; Tabatabai rejects the latter on the basis of God's exclusive knowledge.)
  79:42/q14/p1 Asked by Gabriel, he said the one asked knew no more than the asker. — reasons: Ibn Kathir transmits the exchange to show that the Prophet did not know the Hour's appointed time.
  79:42/q14/p2 Asked by Bedouins, he pointed to the youngest and said their Hour would come if he lived a century. — reasons: Tabatabai records this report but rejects it as an unsuitable setting of the Hour's date, since revelation repeatedly says only God knows it.
79:42/q15 What is reported about the divine state when the Hour occurs? [* · reports]
  79:42/q15/p1 The Hour will occur only with an anger that God becomes angry. — reasons: Qurtubi reports the Prophetic statement from al-Hasan.
79:42/q16 What merit of reciting this sūra is reported? [* · sciences]
  79:42/q16/p1 Its reciter's stay in the grave and at Resurrection will last only as long as an obligatory prayer, then the reciter will enter Paradise. — reasons: Kashshaf transmits this as a Prophetic report.
