12:42/q40 What general principles of Quranic expression and interpretation accompany the prison-verse example? [* · rhetoric]
  12:42/q40/p1 Concise Quranic wording can carry a primary meaning and a supplementary secondary meaning when language and context permit. — reasons: Ibn Ashur says the Quran's legislative, educational and instructional purposes call for many apt meanings in few words; its eloquent constructions may intend familiar senses unless strong contrary evidence intervenes. He also notes the Quran's force on hostile rhetoricians and its shared authority for jurists despite differences over reports.
