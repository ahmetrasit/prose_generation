2:266/q33 How is the wider spending passage organized? [* · links]
  2:266/q33/p1 It forms a connected teaching on the purpose, manner, quality, recipient and reward of giving. — reasons: Al-Tabatabai says the connected themes suggest revelation as one unit: seven-hundredfold or greater growth, sincere fruitfulness, loss through ostentation or harm, giving good wealth, aid to the needy detained in God's path, and great reward.
