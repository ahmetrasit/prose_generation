IRAAT-ER · author | holds | The standard reading has tanwīn on miʾatin in an unusual construct, with sinīn as apposition or explanatory specification.
[QIRAAT-ER:18:25:5#2/r1] QIRAAT-ER · author | holds | Without tanwīn, miʾati is mudāf and sinīn its genitive complement, forming a tighter unit.
[QIRAAT-ER:18:25:5#3/r1] QIRAAT-ER · author | holds | The form miyatin drops hamza as a dialectal pronunciation without semantic change.
[STUDYQURAN:18:25/r1] STUDYQURAN · some commentators | reports | Some say the 309 years report the People of the Book’s reckoning, not necessarily the youths’ actual time in the cave; verse 26 then reserves knowledge to God.
[STUDYQURAN:18:25/r2] STUDYQURAN · others | reports | Others take 309 years as the youths’ actual sleep in the cave and understand God knows best to concern the interval after their death, shortly after discovery. | mentions 18:19, 18:20, 18:21
[STUDYQURAN:18:25/r3] STUDYQURAN · still others | reports | Still others say 309 is the actual duration and settles the dispute about how long they remained.
[STUDYQURAN:18:25/r4] STUDYQURAN · some commentators | reports | Some explain 300 years by the solar calendar plus nine years to express the same period in the lunar calendar used by the Prophet and Arabs.
[STUDYQURAN:18:intro/r11] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | The youths' centuries-long sleep represents an intermediate realm between life and death, analogous to the state between individual death and universal resurrection.
