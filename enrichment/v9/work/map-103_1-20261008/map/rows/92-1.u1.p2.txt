92:1/q24 What does the paired day's tajallī mean against this night's covering? [* · meaning]
  92:1/q24/p1 It is appearing, becoming uncovered, and making things clear. — reasons: Bint al-Shāṭiʾ illustrates the physical senses with a receding hairline, polish that clears sight, the display of a bride, and polishing a blade or mirror; this supplies the lexical opposite of the night’s covering.
