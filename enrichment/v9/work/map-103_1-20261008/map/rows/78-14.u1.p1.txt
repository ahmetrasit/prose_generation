78:14/q09 What does this rain sign establish in the surah? [* · interpretation] (turns on: The notes draw different lessons from rain: provision and resurrection, a reproductive image of creation, or a condition concerning the Hour.)
  78:14/q09/p1 Rain and its resulting growth show divine provision, power, and the certainty of resurrection and judgment. — reasons: Al-Biqai says cooling rain yields food and drink; Islahi says sky-earth harmony displays monotheism, providence and accountability, while the signs of mercy, wisdom and power require a fixed reckoning. Kur'an Yolu links the seven heavens, sun, rain clouds and growth to God's power to create the afterlife, citing 2:29 and 67:3. Ibn Ashur says the surah uses creation and humanity's first creation to prove resurrection, warn of punishment and contrast believers' reward; Muqatil says each sign is more astonishing than resurrection.
  78:14/q09/p2 Sky, earth, water, and vegetation form a reproductive analogy. — reasons: Al-Biqai likens sky to male, earth to female, water to semen, and plants to offspring; he says springs and pools are implied.
  78:14/q09/p3 The Hour cannot come while any water remains. — reasons: Muqatil adds this consequence while explaining the rain from the lowest heaven's sea of provisions and cites 51:22.
78:14/q10 How does this surah connect with al-Mursalāt? [* · links]
  78:14/q10/p1 Both use signs of providence and pointed questions to establish judgment and address deniers. — reasons: Islahi says the two surahs use providence across heaven and earth to establish a purposeful world's judgment, rewarding the God-fearing and punishing rebels. Their affirmative-question style reproaches deniers while glad tidings for believers are understood against the warning.
78:14/q11 What names of this surah are reported? [* · sciences]
  78:14/q11/p1 Five names: al-Nabaʾ, ʿAmma yatasaʾalūn, ʿAmma, al-Tasāʾul, and al-Muʿṣirāt. — reasons: Ibn Ashur lists these five and says al-Itqan lists four.
78:14/q12 Where and when was this surah revealed? [* · sciences]
  78:14/q12/p1 It is Meccan; Jabir ibn Zayd's order places it after al-Maʿārij and before al-Nāziʿāt. — reasons: Ibn Ashur reports the place and relative revelation order.
78:14/q13 How many verses are counted in this surah? [* · sciences] (turns on: Regional verse-counting traditions differ.)
  78:14/q13/p1 Forty verses in Medinan, Syrian, and Basran counts. — reasons: Ibn Ashur reports the regional counts.
  78:14/q13/p2 Forty-one verses in Meccan and Kufan counts. — reasons: Ibn Ashur reports the regional counts.
