103:2/q18 How does al-insān differ from other words for humankind? [ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ · meaning]
  103:2/q18/p1 Al-insān can denote human beings in contrast with wild creatures, while al-nās denotes humanity broadly. — reasons: Bint al-Shati compares Qur'anic use of al-nas with al-ins in contexts paired with jinn, citing 49:13, 13:17 and 59:21.
103:2/q19 What contextual reading of the opening oath is reported? [* · readings]
  103:2/q19/p1 Al-ʿaṣr is read with kasra on the ṣād. — reasons: Qurtubi attributes this vocalization to Salam.
103:2/q20 What claim did opponents make that this verse reverses? [* · reports]
  103:2/q20/p1 Opponents claimed Muhammad was in loss; the divine oath declares the opposite. — reasons: Razi transmits the report as an explanation of the oath's rebuttal.
