# 87:17/q07 What choice and conduct does the comparison call for? [* · interpretation]
turns on: The sources prescribe choosing the lasting reward, name its preparatory acts, or describe the highest aspiration as seeking God beyond both worlds.
- 87:17/q07/p1 Choose and seek the enduring Hereafter over worldly life.
  reasons: Al-Rāzī reasons that what is better and more enduring deserves preference; Ibn Kathīr calls such a choice rational. Al-Biqāʿī urges rejecting sensory attractions and imagined pleasures. Al-Bursawī says preferring mixed, temporary pleasure merits rebuke; Qutb calls the reverse foolish judgment. Mālik ibn Dīnār's illustration values even lasting pottery over passing gold. Al-Māwardī and al-Qushayrī specify faithful seekers; Kur'an Yolu warns unbelievers and believers, citing 7:169, 12:109 and 93:4.
  holders (9 notes): RAZI; RAZI-FULL; IBNKATHIR; BIQAI; BURSEVI; QUTB-ZILAL; KURANYOLU-TEFSIR; QURTUBI(مالك بن دينار); QURTUBI-FULL(مالك بن دينار)
  notes: RAZI:87:17/r1 RAZI-FULL:v31p135#2/r1 IBNKATHIR:87:17/r1 BIQAI:87:17/r3 BURSEVI:v10p318/r1 QUTB-ZILAL:87:17/r1 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:87:16/r1 QURTUBI:87:17/r4 QURTUBI-FULL:87:17/r3
- 87:17/q07/p2 Work for purification, charity, remembrance and prayer in preparation to meet God.
  reasons: Elmalılı says worldly preference ends in loss, while afterlife seekers can bear hardship without despair, seek escape from pain and God's pleasure rather than mere survival, and give obligatory charity, remember the Lord and pray.
  holders (3 notes): ELMALILI
  notes: ELMALILI:87:16-17/r5 ELMALILI:87:16-17/r6 ELMALILI:87:16-17/r7
- 87:17/q07/p3 Seek the Lord above both worldly life and the afterlife.
  reasons: Abū al-ʿAbbās al-Dīnawarī distinguishes noble states and sound realities from the merely elevated ambition that chooses the afterlife.
  holders (1 notes): SULAMI(أبو العباس الدينوري)
  notes: SULAMI:v2p390#4/r1

# 87:17/q09 What reported counsel addresses love of the two lives? [* · reports]
turns on: The reports differ in whether they give reciprocal counsel about the two lives or a caveat about testimony and worldly attachment.
- 87:17/q09/p1 Love of either life harms one's share in the other, so choose what endures.
  reasons: Al-Ṭabrisī transmits the reciprocal warning; Ibn Kathīr reports Abū Mūsā's Prophetic version and notes Aḥmad alone transmits it, also through another route; al-Durr repeats the counsel.

# 87:17/q11 Which teaching here belongs to the earlier scriptures? [* · links]
turns on: Whether 'this' in the next verses points to the nearby passage as a whole or specifically to 87:17; Ṭabarī appeals to the nearest referent.
- 87:17/q11/p1 The nearby passage from purification and prayer through the afterlife comparison belongs to them.
  reasons: Al-Ṭabarī prefers 87:14–17 as the nearest referent of 'this' and says ṣuḥuf means the books or scrolls of Abraham and Moses. Al-Kalbī takes it from 'successful is one who purifies' through the sūra's end; al-Bursawī includes reward, soul purification, knowledge, obedience and warning against worldly distraction. A report says the Prophet recited this passage when asked what he received from the scrolls.
  holders (5 notes): THALABI(الكلبي); BURSEVI; DURR-FULL(النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم); TAB-FULL+; TAB+
  notes: TAB:87:19/r6 TAB-FULL:v24p323/r6 THALABI:v10p185/r6 BURSEVI:v10p318/r5 DURR-FULL:v8p488/r6
- 87:17/q11/p2 The specific teaching is that the afterlife is better and more enduring than worldly life.
  reasons: Ibn Zayd limits 'this' to the comparison; Qatāda says God's books repeatedly or successively teach it, including the earlier scrolls. Iṣlāḥī says the Prophet's message continues earlier prophetic teaching.
