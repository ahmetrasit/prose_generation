- 96:18/q07/p1 It derives from zabn, forceful pushing or repelling.
  reasons: Ibn Qutayba, Jishumī, Ibn ʿAṭiyya, al-Qurṭubī and others say they drive sinners into Hell. Evidence includes a camel kicking its milker, war pushing people in poems and ʿUtba's saying, muzābana sales, and Bint al-Shāṭiʾ's fierce zābin.
