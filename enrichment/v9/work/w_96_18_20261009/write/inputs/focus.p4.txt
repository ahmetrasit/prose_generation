# 96:18/q13 How does God's summons answer the man's assembly? [* · links]
turns on: The balance between Abū Jahl's supporters and the divine force summoned against them.
- 96:18/q13/p1 His earthly clan cannot prevail against God's powerful guards; the challenge exposes the outcome.
  reasons: Al-Farrāʾ contrasts the stronger zabāniya with the men summoned; Ibn Kathīr describes a contest between God's party and Abū Jahl's. Bint al-Shāṭiʾ and Corpus Coranicum stress human helplessness; Quṭb says the result is already known.
  holders (12 notes): FARRA; TAB; RAZI; RAZI-FULL; IBNKATHIR; IBNKATHIR-FULL; QUTB-ZILAL; TABATABAI; BINTSHATI; CORPUSCORANICUM; ISLAHI-TADABBUR; MUQATIL

# 96:18/q15 What happened to Abū Jahl at Badr, according to the extended account? [* · reports]
- 96:18/q15/p1 Ibn Masʿūd killed the wounded Abū Jahl and brought evidence of his death to the Prophet.
  reasons: Bursawī first recounts Abū Jahl bloodying Ibn Masʿūd's ear after his Sūrat al-Raḥmān recitation. Gabriel foretells an outcome; at Badr Ibn Masʿūd spears, overcomes, and decapitates him, dragging the head by its ear. Abū Jahl remains defiant, and the Prophet compares him unfavorably with Pharaoh.
