# 103:3/q22 What overall life does the verse commend? [* · interpretation]
- 103:3/q22/p1 Faith and personal good joined to communal care for truth, justice and endurance.
  reasons: Iṣlāḥī stresses individual and collective duties; Bint al-Shāṭiʾ social responsibility; Kur'an Yolu God's and others' rights. Quṭb invokes al-Nadwī's model of revealed, accountable, egalitarian leadership. Quṭb laments nationalist replacement of Islam's banner. Al-Nadwī praises early Muslim leadership: revealed law (6:122, 5:8), moral training, office as a trust (28:83, 4:58, 6:165), liberation from human servitude, equal dignity (49:13), ʿUmar's redress for an Egyptian, shared learning across peoples, and balanced spiritual and material civilization. Rabīʿ b. ʿĀmir's address to Yazdegerd illustrates its mission; Quṭb regards the Rightly Guided Caliphate as embodying the verse.
  holders (58 notes): BIQAI-FULL(الغزالي); ELMALILI; QUTB-ZILAL(أبو الحسن الندوي، ربعي بن عامر، عمر بن الخطاب); BINTSHATI; KURANYOLU-TEFSIR(Mehmet Âkif Ersoy)
  notes: BIQAI-FULL:v8p521#s103/r4 BIQAI-FULL:v8p521#s103/r6 BIQAI-FULL:v8p521#s103/r7 BIQAI-FULL:v8p521/r4 BIQAI-FULL:v8p521/r6 BIQAI-FULL:v8p521/r7 ELMALILI:103:1-3#3/r1 ELMALILI:103:1-3#3/r2 ELMALILI:103:1-3#3/r3 ELMALILI:103:1-3#6/r15 QUTB-ZILAL:103:1/r1 QUTB-ZILAL:103:1/r2 QUTB-ZILAL:103:1/r3 QUTB-ZILAL:103:1/r30 QUTB-ZILAL:103:1/r31 QUTB-ZILAL:103:1/r32 QUTB-ZILAL:103:1/r33 QUTB-ZILAL:103:1/r34 QUTB-ZILAL:103:1/r35 QUTB-ZILAL:103:1/r36 QUTB-ZILAL:103:1/r37 QUTB-ZILAL:103:1/r38 QUTB-ZILAL:103:1/r39 QUTB-ZILAL:103:1/r40 QUTB-ZILAL:103:1/r41 QUTB-ZILAL:103:1/r42 QUTB-ZILAL:103:1/r43 QUTB-ZILAL:103:2/r1 QUTB-ZILAL:103:2/r2 QUTB-ZILAL:103:2/r29 QUTB-ZILAL:103:2/r30 QUTB-ZILAL:103:2/r31 QUTB-ZILAL:103:2/r32 QUTB-ZILAL:103:2/r33 QUTB-ZILAL:103:2/r34 QUTB-ZILAL:103:2/r35 QUTB-ZILAL:103:2/r36 QUTB-ZILAL:103:2/r37 QUTB-ZILAL:103:2/r38 QUTB-ZILAL:103:2/r39 QUTB-ZILAL:103:2/r40 QUTB-ZILAL:103:2/r41 QUTB-ZILAL:103:2/r42 QUTB-ZILAL:103:3/r22 QUTB-ZILAL:103:3/r23 QUTB-ZILAL:103:3/r24 QUTB-ZILAL:103:3/r25 QUTB-ZILAL:103:3/r26 QUTB-ZILAL:103:3/r27 QUTB-ZILAL:103:3/r28 QUTB-ZILAL:103:3/r29 BINTSHATI:v2p86/r4 BINTSHATI:v2p93/r1 BINTSHATI:v2p93/r2 BINTSHATI:v2p93/r3 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:103:1/r16 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:103:1/r17 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:103:1/r19
