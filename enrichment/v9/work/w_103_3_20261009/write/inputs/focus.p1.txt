# 103:3/q07 How are faith and deeds related? [ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ · theology]
turns on: Whether coordination separates concepts or highlights a manifestation.
- 103:3/q07/p1 Deeds are outside faith's definition, or the coordination repeats faith.
  reasons: Rāzī's first group rejects analogy with 33:7 and 2:98; repetition is possible but not the default. Bint al-Shāṭiʾ also cites 3:81.
  holders (7 notes): RAZI(الأولون، من قال : العمل غير داخل في مسمى الإيمان); RAZI-FULL(الأولون، من قال إن العمل غير داخل في مسمى الإيمان); BINTSHATI

- 103:3/q07/p2 Deeds express or overlap faith; mentioning them emphasizes action.
  reasons: Al-Ḥalīmī allows repetition because deeds include faith. Bint al-Shāṭiʾ calls it specification; Iṣlāḥī says faith is foundation. Quṭb cites 14:18 and 24:39 for fruitless works without faith.

# 103:3/q10 What does tawāṣaw imply? [وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ · meaning]
- 103:3/q10/p1 Reciprocal, regular advice and support, practiced by those offering it.
  reasons: Thaʿlabī and Baghawī say urge; Qurṭubī adds love; Bint al-Shāṭiʾ traces binding and bequest. Ibn ʿĀshūr recalls 2:44 and 89:18; Biqāʿī emphasizes gentleness.
