# 103:3/q02 Who is the person in loss from whom believers are excepted? [إِلَّا · referent]
turns on: Whether the generic warning is applied to one opponent or several.
- 103:3/q02/p1 The warning concerns humanity generally, including named opponents.
  reasons: Wāḥidī treats individual unbelievers as included under the general wording, since believers are excepted.
  holders (1 notes): WAHIDI-BASIT
  notes: WAHIDI-BASIT:v24p297#2/r3
- 103:3/q02/p2 Al-Walīd ibn al-Mughīra, al-ʿĀṣ ibn Wāʾil and al-Aswad ibn ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib are named.
  reasons: ʿAṭāʾ transmits this from Ibn ʿAbbās.
  holders (1 notes): WAHIDI-BASIT(عطاء عن ابن عباس)
  notes: WAHIDI-BASIT:v24p297#2/r1
- 103:3/q02/p3 Abū Lahab is named.
  reasons: Wāḥidī transmits this from Muqātil.
  holders (1 notes): WAHIDI-BASIT(مقاتل)
  notes: WAHIDI-BASIT:v24p297#2/r2
- 103:3/q02/p4 Abū Jahl is named.
  reasons: A report attributed to the Prophet names him; the four-Companion interpretation likewise uses him.
  holders (3 notes): WAHIDI-BASIT; THALABI(النبي); WAHIDI-QT

# 103:3/q03 What human subject does this exception address? [إِلَّا · interpretation]
- 103:3/q03/p1 A morally responsible human, individually and socially accountable.
  reasons: Bint al-Shāṭiʾ surveys Qurʾānic al-insān as tested, articulate and entrusted with duty; she accepts al-Rāghib's sociability derivation but stresses obligations beyond mere membership of humankind.
  holders (4 notes): BINTSHATI(الراغب الأصفهاني)
  notes: BINTSHATI:v2p82/r1 BINTSHATI:v2p82/r2 BINTSHATI:v2p82/r3 BINTSHATI:v2p82/r4
sources: BINTSHATI = ʿĀʾisha ʿAbd al-Raḥmān (Bint al-Shāṭiʾ), d. 1419

# 103:3/q05 What loss remains for a believer lacking some qualities? [إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ · theology]

- 103:3/q05/p1 Full exemption requires all four; omission entails some loss.
  reasons: Muḥammad b. Kaʿb says faith alone is insufficient; Rāzī and Elmalılı require both personal and communal duties. Elmalılı rejects the Murjiʾite denial of loss from inaction.
  holders (6 notes): MUQATIL; IBNABIHATIM(محمد بن كعب القرظي); ELMALILI; SAMARRAI-ASRAR; ELMALILI(Mürcienin)+
  notes: MUQATIL:103:1-3/r7 IBNABIHATIM:v10p3462/r3 ELMALILI:103:1-3#6/r9 ELMALILI:103:1-3#6/r11 ELMALILI:103:1-3#6/r12 SAMARRAI-ASRAR:r17#2/r16
