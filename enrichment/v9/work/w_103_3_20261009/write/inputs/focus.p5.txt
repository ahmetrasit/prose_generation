# 103:3/q24 What is said about setting and verse division? [* · sciences]
turns on: The evidence concerns chronology and textual division.
- 103:3/q24/p1 The sura is generally Meccan, though Madinan attribution exists.
  reasons: Abū Ḥayyān names Ibn ʿAbbās, Ibn al-Zubayr and most authorities for Meccan; Mujāhid, Qatāda and Muqātil for Madinan. Ṭabāṭabāʾī favors Meccan; Asad and Study Quran place it after Sura 94.

# 103:3/q25 How is loss outside the exception understood? [* · interpretation]
turns on: How literally to apply the commercial metaphor and the force of exemption.
- 103:3/q25/p1 Finite life expires without afterlife profit, culminating in reckoning.
  reasons: Ibn Qutayba glosses deficiency; Elmalılı likens life to loaned capital; Quṭb stresses Paradise and divine pleasure (78:40); Ṭabāṭabāʾī cites 13:26, 21:35, 53:39–41.
  holders (5 notes): IBNQUTAYBA-MUSHKIL; QUTB-ZILAL; STUDYQURAN; BINTSHATI-
  notes: IBNQUTAYBA-MUSHKIL:v1p204#2/r5 QUTB-ZILAL:103:1/r44 QUTB-ZILAL:103:2/r43 QUTB-ZILAL:103:3/r30 STUDYQURAN:103:1/r4 | against: BINTSHATI:v2p85/r2 BINTSHATI:v2p85/r3
- 103:3/q25/p2 The explicit exception means qualified believers are never in loss, limiting universal trade claims.
  reasons: Bint al-Shāṭiʾ rejects Zamakhsharī's trade metaphor and Rāzī's claim that permissible acts or imperfect worship always lose. She traces commercial and religious khusr (83:3, 55:9, 26:181) and rejects al-Rāghib's exclusively religious gloss.
