# 103:3/q15 Why repeat patience after truth? [وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ · rhetoric]
turns on: Whether the second clause emphasizes an included virtue or distinguishes work.
- 103:3/q15/p1 Patience is part of truth but singled out because defending truth is hard.

# 103:3/q18 Why state profit's cause but leave loss's cause vague? [* · rhetoric]
- 103:3/q18/p1 Loss may arise through sin or omitted obedience, while profit needs action; the focus suits divine generosity.
  reasons: Rāzī gives both explanations; Bayḍāwī says other pursuits lose or have reduced shares; Bursawī stresses eternal success.
