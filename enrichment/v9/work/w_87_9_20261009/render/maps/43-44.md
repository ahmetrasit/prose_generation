# 43:44: 19 questions

## 43:44/q01 What does “it” refer to?  [وَإِنَّهُۥ · referent]
turns on: The scope of the revelation under discussion.
- 43:44/q01/p1 The Qur’an or revelation to Muhammad. — Qatāda and Ibn ʿAbbās name the Qur’an. Al-Qurṭubī prefers this because the discourse rests on and returns to it; the Study Quran cites its name al-Dhikr at 15:9 and 36:10–11. [5 notes: TAB-FULL(قتادة); DURR-FULL(ابن عباس,قتادة); STUDYQURAN; QURTUBI-FULL+]
- 43:44/q01/p2 Revelation to Muhammad and earlier prophets. — Al-Naḥḥās reports this as a reminder that teaches religion. [1 notes: NAHHAS]

## 43:44/q02 What does “dhikr” mean here?  [لَذِكْرٌۭ · meaning]
turns on: Whether the word denotes honor, instruction, authority, divine remembrance, or an inward attainment.
- 43:44/q02/p1 Honor, renown, and enduring good reputation. — Ibn Qutayba says honorable people are remembered; compare 21:10 and 23:71. Mujāhid illustrates renown through Arab, Qurashī and Hāshimī lineage. Ibn Zayd points to prophethood and revelation. Ibn ʿĀshūr cites reports from ʿAlī and Ibn ʿAbbās. Asad renders 'source of eminence.' Al-Ṭabāṭabāʾ reports the majority reading but prefers another. [69 notes: MUQATIL; ABDURRAZZAQ(مجاهد); IBNQUTAYBA-GHARIB; IBNQUTAYBA-MUSHKIL; TAB-FULL(ابن زيد,ابن عباس,السدي,مجاهد); ZAJJAJ; IBNABIHATIM(ابن عباس,مجاهد); NAHHAS(علي بن أبي طلحة عن ابن عباس,مجاهد); BAQILLANI; SULAMI(ابن عطاء,مالك بن أنس); THALABI(مالك بن أنس); QUSHAYRI; WAHIDI-BASIT(المفسرون,مجاهد); WAHIDI-WAJIZ; DAMGHANI; JISHUMI; BAGHAWI-FULL(مجاهد); IBNATIYYA-FULL(ابن عباس وقتادة ومجاهد والسدي وابن زيد); TABRISI(ابن عباس والسدي); IBNJAWZI-ZAD(ابن قتيبة); QURTUBI-FULL(مالك); BAYDAWI-FULL; NASAFI-FULL; ABUHAYYAN-FULL(ابن عباس); IBNKATHIR-FULL(ابن عباس ومجاهد وقتادة والسدي وابن زيد,قيل); BIQAI-FULL; DURR-FULL(ابن عباس,عدي بن حاتم,مجاهد); ITQAN; BURSEVI(ابن عطاء,الكواشي,بعضهم); ALUSI-FULL(بعضهم); IBNASHUR-FULL(القرطبي,علي وابن عباس); TABATABAI(أكثر المفسرين); ASAD-NOTES; STUDYQURAN(IK, JJ,IK, JJ, Ṭ); ALUSI-FULL(ابن عباس)+; TABATABAI-]
- 43:44/q02/p2 An admonition to remember religion and act. — Al-Ḥasan couples this with the whole community. Ibn Kathīr cites 21:10 and 26:214 to show named addressees do not exclude others. Ibn ʿĀshūr likens guidance to recollection of something forgotten. [8 notes: NAHHAS; IBNATIYYA-FULL(الحسن بن أبي الحسن); QURTUBI-FULL; ABUHAYYAN-FULL(الحسن); IBNKATHIR-FULL(قيل); BIQAI-FULL; IBNASHUR-FULL; ISLAHI-TADABBUR]
- 43:44/q02/p3 Clarification of what the Prophet and community need. — Al-Qurṭubī reports the view; Biqāʿī includes explanation. [2 notes: QURTUBI-FULL; BIQAI-FULL]
- 43:44/q02/p4 Remembrance of God and its obligations. — Al-Ṭabāṭabāʾ prefers this apparent sense, citing other uses in the sūra, the lām marking duties, and subsequent questioning on Judgment Day. [1 notes: TABATABAI+]
- 43:44/q02/p5 The Qurayshi caliphate or leadership. — Al-Qurṭubī cites a report that believers and unbelievers follow Quraysh in this affair. [1 notes: QURTUBI-FULL]
- 43:44/q02/p6 Inherited ancestral transmission. — Mālik illustrates with 'my father, from my grandfather'; Ibn al-ʿArabī says Baghdad's Banū Tamīm preserved this chain and cites descendants recounting ʿAlī on al-ḥannān and al-mannān. [4 notes: SULAMI(مالك بن أنس); THALABI(مالك بن أنس); QURTUBI-FULL(ابن العربي,مالك)]
- 43:44/q02/p7 Inward honor through reaching God. — Ibn ʿAṭāʾ links the Prophet to God and his people to him; the Najmī reading asks whether this honor was fulfilled or spent on worldly and egoistic aims. [3 notes: SULAMI(ابن عطاء); BURSEVI(ابن عطاء,التأويلات النجمية)]

## 43:44/q03 Who are “your people”?  [وَلِقَوْمِكَ · referent]
turns on: Whether qawm denotes kin, Arabic speakers, followers, or all addressees.
- 43:44/q03/p1 Quraysh, with nearest clans foremost. — Mujāhid's lineage exchange moves from Arab to Qurashī to Banū Hāshim. Al-Kawāshī adds Banū al-Muṭṭalib. Al-Ālūsī favors Quraysh; al-Qurṭubī says acting on the Qur’an extends honor beyond lineage. [24 notes: TAB-FULL(مجاهد); IBNABIHATIM(ابن عباس,مجاهد); NAHHAS(علي بن أبي طلحة عن ابن عباس,مجاهد); THALABI; WAHIDI-BASIT(مجاهد); BAGHAWI-FULL; IBNATIYYA-FULL(ابن عباس وقتادة ومجاهد والسدي وابن زيد); TABRISI(ابن عباس والسدي); IBNJAWZI-ZAD; QURTUBI-FULL(مجاهد); ABUHAYYAN-FULL(ابن عباس); IBNKATHIR-FULL(ابن عباس ومجاهد وقتادة والسدي وابن زيد); BURSEVI(الكواشي,بعضهم); ALUSI-FULL(علي وابن عباس); IBNASHUR-FULL(القرطبي); ALUSI-FULL(ابن عباس)+; QURTUBI-FULL-]
- 43:44/q03/p2 All Arabs, especially Quraysh and Banū Hāshim. — Mujāhid and others cite the Arabic language of revelation. Al-Wāḥidī extends honor outward from Muhammad's nearest clan; Ibn ʿĀshūr says the Qur’an secured the Arabs' lasting renown among nations. [15 notes: ABDURRAZZAQ(مجاهد); WAHIDI-BASIT; BAGHAWI-FULL(مجاهد); IBNATIYYA-FULL(ابن عباس وقتادة ومجاهد والسدي وابن زيد); TABRISI; IBNJAWZI-ZAD(مجاهد); ABUHAYYAN-FULL(ابن عباس); IBNKATHIR-FULL(قيل); BIQAI-FULL; ALUSI-FULL(بعضهم); IBNASHUR-FULL; TABATABAI(أكثر المفسرين); STUDYQURAN(IK,IK, JJ)]
- 43:44/q03/p3 Believers and followers among his people. — Muqātil and Ibn ʿAbbās restrict the honor to believers; al-Baghawī links it with recognizing truth. Qatāda and al-Ḥasan are reported for the followers or Muslim community. [12 notes: MUQATIL; NAHHAS; WAHIDI-BASIT(ابن عباس ومقاتل); BAGHAWI-FULL; IBNJAWZI-ZAD; QURTUBI-FULL(قتادة والحسن); DURR-FULL(قتادة); BURSEVI(مجاهد); ALUSI-FULL(الحسن,قتادة)]
- 43:44/q03/p4 The whole community or everyone to whom he was sent. — Al-Ḥasan combines this with admonition; al-Wāḥidī allows all addressees. Ibn Kathīr cites 21:10 and 26:214 for nonexclusive naming. Al-Qurṭubī extends the honor to anyone who acts on revelation. [8 notes: WAHIDI-BASIT; IBNATIYYA-FULL(الحسن بن أبي الحسن); ABUHAYYAN-FULL(الحسن); IBNKATHIR-FULL(قيل); NASAFI-FULL; BIQAI-FULL; ISLAHI-TADABBUR; QURTUBI-FULL+]

## 43:44/q04 How does the Qur’an confer this honor?  [لَذِكْرٌۭ لَّكَ وَلِقَوْمِكَ · interpretation]
turns on: The channels through which the proclaimed honor is received or realized.
- 43:44/q04/p1 Through one of their own in their language, giving distinction and access. — Al-Wāḥidī, al-Qurṭubī, Abū Ḥayyān and Ibn Kathīr cite language and kinship. Ibn Kathīr says they understand best and should follow best, as early emigrants did. Others need Arabic; Biqāʿī adds knowledge and leadership. [12 notes: WAHIDI-WAJIZ; WAHIDI-BASIT; BAGHAWI-FULL(مجاهد); TABRISI; QURTUBI-FULL; ABUHAYYAN-FULL; IBNKATHIR-FULL(قيل); BIQAI-FULL; BURSEVI(بعضهم); IBNASHUR-FULL(القرطبي); STUDYQURAN(IK,IK, JJ)]
- 43:44/q04/p2 Through wisdom for the Prophet and truth and guidance for believers. — Al-Wāḥidī and al-Baghawī distinguish these gifts; Ibn ʿĀshūr emphasizes followers' benefit. [3 notes: WAHIDI-BASIT; BAGHAWI-FULL; IBNASHUR-FULL]
- 43:44/q04/p3 Through Muhammad's affiliation with God and his people's affiliation with him. — Ibn ʿAṭāʾ's explanation comes through al-Sulamī and Bursawī. [2 notes: SULAMI(ابن عطاء); BURSEVI(ابن عطاء)]
- 43:44/q04/p4 Through remembering and practicing its law, regardless of lineage. — Biqāʿī makes observance the cause of honor. Al-Qurṭubī prefers anyone who acts on it; Asad says conduct, including spiritual and social use, determines eminence. [3 notes: BIQAI-FULL; ASAD-NOTES; QURTUBI-FULL+]
- 43:44/q04/p5 Through lasting praise, knowledge, and standing. — Qushayrī mentions reputation and rank. Biqāʿī says a formerly weak community becomes a source of religious and worldly knowledge. Bursawī cites a report that the community's distinction is the Qur’an. [5 notes: QUSHAYRI; BIQAI-FULL; BURSEVI; IBNASHUR-FULL]
- 43:44/q04/p6 Through inherited ancestral transmission. — Mālik's formula is 'my father, from my grandfather'; Ibn al-ʿArabī illustrates it among Baghdad's Banū Tamīm. [4 notes: SULAMI(مالك بن أنس); THALABI(مالك بن أنس); QURTUBI-FULL(ابن العربي,مالك)]

## 43:44/q05 What does the verse imply about Qurayshi rule?  [وَلِقَوْمِكَ · interpretation]
turns on: Whether Qurayshi authority is stated simply or with religious and ethical conditions.
- 43:44/q05/p1 Quraysh inherit Muslim authority through this honor. — Al-Wāḥidī, Ibn al-Jawzī and Biqāʿī infer this from the tribes report; al-Qurṭubī reports dhikr itself read as caliphate. [4 notes: WAHIDI-BASIT; IBNJAWZI-ZAD; BIQAI-FULL; QURTUBI-FULL]
- 43:44/q05/p2 Authority remains among Quraysh while two people remain. — Ibn ʿUmar reports this; Biqāʿī adds that they uphold religion. [4 notes: THALABI(النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم); BAGHAWI-FULL(النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم); IBNATIYYA-FULL(ابن عمر); BIQAI-FULL(النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم)]
- 43:44/q05/p3 Authority remains while Quraysh uphold religion; opposition is cast down. — Muʿāwiya's report gives the condition and punishment; Ibn Kathīr says al-Bukhārī records it. Al-Qurṭubī cites a report of all people following Quraysh. [6 notes: THALABI(النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم); BAGHAWI-FULL(النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم); IBNATIYYA-FULL(معاوية); IBNKATHIR-FULL(النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم); BIQAI-FULL(النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم); QURTUBI-FULL]
- 43:44/q05/p4 Rulers must judge justly, show mercy, and keep covenants. — Abū Mūsā and Abū Burda add ethical conditions and curses for failure; the latter distinguishes rulers' and subjects' rights, while the former includes a sister's son among Quraysh. [3 notes: THALABI(النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم); IBNATIYYA-FULL(أبو موسى الأشعري)]

## 43:44/q06 What happened when tribes asked who would rule after the Prophet?  [وَلِقَوْمِكَ · reports]
- 43:44/q06/p1 He waited for this verse, then answered Quraysh. — Reports from Ibn ʿAbbās and ʿAlī vary: he presented himself to Meccan tribes and promised victory; some say the tribes rejected Quraysh until the Anṣār accepted it. Al-Ālūsī states no grading. [10 notes: THALABI(ابن عباس); WAHIDI-BASIT(ابن عباس (رواية الضحاك)); BAGHAWI-FULL(ابن عباس); IBNATIYYA-FULL(ابن عباس); IBNJAWZI-ZAD(ابن عباس); ABUHAYYAN-FULL; BIQAI-FULL(ابن عباس); DURR-FULL(علي وابن عباس); ALUSI-FULL(علي وابن عباس); IBNASHUR-FULL(ابن عباس)]

## 43:44/q07 What other report links the verse with Quraysh's honor?  [وَلِقَوْمِكَ · reports]
- 43:44/q07/p1 ʿAdī ibn Ḥātim says God honored Quraysh and the Prophet recited the verse gladly. — Al-Durr connects it to warning close kin, gentleness to believers, the blessed tree of noble ancestry and Islam, and Sūrat Quraysh, 106:1–4. Al-Ālūsī adds glad tidings, a truthful one and a martyr; no grading is stated. [5 notes: DURR-FULL(عدي بن حاتم); ALUSI-FULL(عدي بن حاتم)]

## 43:44/q08 What will they be asked about?  [وَسَوْفَ تُسْـَٔلُونَ · interpretation]
turns on: Whether the inquiry assesses obedience, gratitude, rejection, prophetic delivery, or inward fidelity.
- 43:44/q08/p1 Their fulfillment of the Qur’an's rights, commands, prohibitions, and call. — Al-Ṭabarī specifies commands and prohibitions. Al-Qurṭubī says related interpretations nearly coincide. Ibn ʿĀshūr weighs believers' obedience; Asad includes spiritual and social use; Biqāʿī includes response to revelation. [19 notes: TAB-FULL; WAHIDI-BASIT(غيره); BAGHAWI-FULL; IBNATIYYA-FULL(ابن عباس وغيره); TABRISI(قيل); IBNJAWZI-ZAD; QURTUBI-FULL(ابن جريج); BAYDAWI-FULL; NASAFI-FULL; IBNKATHIR-FULL; BIQAI-FULL; BURSEVI; ALUSI-FULL; IBNASHUR-FULL; ASAD-NOTES; ISLAHI-TADABBUR; STUDYQURAN(JJ)]
- 43:44/q08/p2 Whether they gave thanks for the Qur’an and its honor. — Al-Kalbī, al-Zajjāj and al-Ḥasan give this reading. Al-Ḥasan allows both gratitude and duty; al-Qurṭubī says they nearly coincide. Bursawī says the gift surpasses that of other peoples. [16 notes: IBNQUTAYBA-GHARIB; ZAJJAJ; THALABI; WAHIDI-BASIT(أبو إسحاق,الكلبي); WAHIDI-WAJIZ; BAGHAWI-FULL; IBNATIYYA-FULL(الحسن بن أبي الحسن); TABRISI(الكلبي والزجاج وغيرهما); IBNJAWZI-ZAD; QURTUBI-FULL(مقاتل والفراء); NASAFI-FULL; ABUHAYYAN-FULL(الحسن); BURSEVI; ALUSI-FULL(الحسن والكلبي والزجاج)]
- 43:44/q08/p3 Rejecters face rebuke for denying revelation. — Muqātil asks 'why did you reject it?' Ibn ʿĀshūr contrasts polytheists' rebuke with believers' account, citing 43:19 and 67:8,11. [5 notes: MUQATIL; WAHIDI-BASIT(مقاتل); ABUHAYYAN-FULL(مقاتل); IBNASHUR-FULL]
- 43:44/q08/p4 Messengers answer for delivery and response; nations for conduct. — Asad calls prophetic questioning metaphorical, citing 5:109. Iṣlāḥī cites 7:6–7 and says final accounting rewards messengers and companions and punishes rejecters. [3 notes: ASAD-NOTES; ISLAHI-TADABBUR]
- 43:44/q08/p5 Whether they fulfilled Godward honor or misused it for ego and world. — The Najmī inward reading reported by Bursawī. [1 notes: BURSEVI(التأويلات النجمية)]

## 43:44/q09 When will the questioning occur?  [وَسَوْفَ تُسْـَٔلُونَ · interpretation]
- 43:44/q09/p1 At Resurrection or in the Hereafter. — Muqātil says Hereafter; al-Nasafī and Bursawī say Resurrection or Judgment Day. Al-Ṭabāṭabāʾ takes this as support for accountability; the Study Quran likewise specifies Hereafter. [6 notes: MUQATIL; NASAFI-FULL; BURSEVI; TABATABAI; STUDYQURAN(JJ); IBNASHUR-FULL]

## 43:44/q10 What force does “sawfa” have?  [وَسَوْفَ تُسْـَٔلُونَ · grammar]
- 43:44/q10/p1 It signals longer delay than sīn. — Al-Burhān reports al-Zamakhsharī and several grammarians, contrasting nearby sayaqūl with sawfa tusʾalūn. [1 notes: BURHAN(الزمخشري وابن الخشاب وابن يعيش وابن أبان وابن بابشاذ وابن عصفور)]

## 43:44/q11 Why can “dhikr” express honor?  [لَذِكْرٌۭ · rhetoric]
- 43:44/q11/p1 Honored people are remembered and publicly mentioned. — Ibn Qutayba gives the lexical rationale; Ibn al-Jawzī repeats it. Ibn ʿĀshūr calls the Qur’an a cause of remembrance. Al-Bāqillānī uses it as Qur’anic metaphor; Biqāʿī ties remembrance to observing law. [7 notes: IBNQUTAYBA-MUSHKIL; IBNJAWZI-ZAD(ابن قتيبة); BAQILLANI; BIQAI-FULL; IBNASHUR-FULL]

## 43:44/q12 What does the verse teach about lasting good renown?  [لَذِكْرٌۭ · interpretation]
- 43:44/q12/p1 Worthy praise is desirable and outlives its bearer. — Abū Ḥayyān cites God's favor here, Abraham's prayer in 26:84, Ibn Durayd and another poet; a good name reaches every place and age. Hulagu notes Muhammad's name on minarets five times daily over six centuries after death. Al-Ālūsī likens renown to continued life. [10 notes: ABUHAYYAN-FULL(ابن دريد); ALUSI-FULL(ابن دريد,هلاكو)]

## 43:44/q13 Does ancestry itself secure honor or salvation?  [وَلِقَوْمِكَ · theology]
- 43:44/q13/p1 Standing before God depends on piety and conduct. — Al-Qurṭubī cites the Prophet warning Fāṭima, wives and kin he cannot save them, and rejecting special standing for Banū Hāshim, Quraysh, Anṣār and freedmen. Abū Hurayra rejects ancestral boasting; all descend from Adam. Al-Qurṭubī points to al-Ḥujurāt and favors honor for all who act on the Qur’an. [5 notes: QURTUBI-FULL(أبو هريرة,النبي); ASAD-NOTES; QURTUBI-FULL+]

## 43:44/q14 How does the verse fit its passage and sūra?  [* · links]
- 43:44/q14/p1 It commends the Qur’an while urging persistence and warning opponents of deferred judgment. — Ibn ʿĀshūr calls it the seventh Qur’anic commendation. Iṣlāḥī connects rejection with affluence, late removal of Satan's blindfold, Quraysh's rebellion and the prophets' path. Kur'an Yolu describes hardened hearers, patience and God's certain repayment. [6 notes: IBNASHUR-FULL; ISLAHI-TADABBUR; KURANYOLU-TEFSIR]
- 43:44/q14/p2 The whole sūra forms a unified argument around its central theme. — Iṣlāḥī says all parts connect, and final accounting assures Muhammad and companions while threatening opponents. [2 notes: ISLAHI-TADABBUR]
- 43:44/q14/p3 Other passages use remembrance as scriptural honor. — Al-Ṭabrisī points to a related verse; several compare 21:10. Al-Qushayrī's reading of Adam's vicegerency likewise uses remembrance for rank and honor. [8 notes: TABRISI; IBNQUTAYBA-MUSHKIL; WAHIDI-BASIT(المفسرون); THALABI; BAGHAWI-FULL; QURTUBI-FULL(القشيري); ASAD-NOTES]

## 43:44/q15 What other qualities of scripture and this community are cited?  [* · interpretation]
- 43:44/q15/p1 This community memorizes scripture in its hearts. — Bursawī says the Torah describes its scriptures as being in their hearts. [1 notes: BURSEVI]
- 43:44/q15/p2 The Qur’an has a hidden spirit beyond visible markings. — Bursawī quotes Mathnawī couplets comparing the Qur’an to a human; the devil saw Adam only as clay. [1 notes: BURSEVI(المثنوي)]
- 43:44/q15/p3 A report promises Paradise after confession of God and Muhammad despite immense sins. — Bursawī compares sins to sea foam, raindrops, stars and the world's days. [1 notes: BURSEVI]

## 43:44/q16 What inward contrast is drawn from this honor?  [لَذِكْرٌۭ · inward]
- 43:44/q16/p1 Godward knowledge rests on unveiling and vision and ends in God's presence. — Bursawī contrasts it with intellectual learning, worldly office and transient goods; the Najmī reading warns against worldly or egoistic use of Qur’anic honor. [2 notes: BURSEVI(التأويلات النجمية,بعضهم)]

## 43:44/q17 What does the instruction to ask convey?  [* · rhetoric]
- 43:44/q17/p1 It conveys a report's wide recognition and certainty. — Ibn ʿĀshūr cites similar poetic challenges by al-Samawʾal, alternatively al-Ḥārithī, and Zayd al-Khayl. [1 notes: IBNASHUR-FULL]

## 43:44/q18 What can the name “al-Dhikr” denote elsewhere?  [لَذِكْرٌۭ · meaning]
- 43:44/q18/p1 The Qur’an, scripture generally, or the Torah. — The Study Quran cites 15:9 and 36:10–11 for the Qur’anic name, and 16:43, 21:8 and 41:41–42 for wider scriptural usage and the People of the Reminder. [2 notes: STUDYQURAN]

## 43:44/q19 How much meaning is compressed into this verse?  [* · rhetoric]
- 43:44/q19/p1 It gathers many meanings into very little wording. — Ibn ʿĀshūr counts ten meanings in a line's space, compared with six in Imruʾ al-Qays's celebrated concise line, and points to emphasis, allusion, and verbal ornament. [1 notes: IBNASHUR-FULL]
