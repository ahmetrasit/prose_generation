# 87:9/q06 Who benefits from the reminder, and how? [نَّفَعَتِ ٱلذِّكْرَىٰ · interpretation]
turns on: Whether benefit means acceptance, establishment of proof, or an opportunity offered despite resistance.
- 87:9/q06/p1 The God-fearing, believers, God's allies, and prepared hearers heed.
  reasons: Sulamī and Thaʿlabī refer to 87:10; Qushayrī says God enables the receptive; Ibn ʿAbbās distinguishes allies from enemies; Qāshānī frames readiness inwardly; Ṭabāṭabāʾī says it inclines the fearful to truth; Study Quran cites 50:45.
