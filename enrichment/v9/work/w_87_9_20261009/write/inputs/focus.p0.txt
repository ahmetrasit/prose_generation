# 87:9/q01 What is conveyed in the reminder? [فَذَكِّرْ ٱلذِّكْرَىٰ · interpretation]
turns on: Whether dhikrā denotes the Qur'an, the testimony, recollection through explanation, or admonition about God.
- 87:9/q01/p1 Convey and admonish with the Qur'an or revelation.
  reasons: Mujāhid names the Qur'an (Māwardī). Wāḥidī calls dhikrā admonition. Jishumī includes promises, warnings and rulings; Ālūsī and Elmalılı include revelation's legal rules and knowledge; Ibn ʿĀshūr says the imperative calls for continued proclamation.

  reasons: Ibn Shajara names hope and fear (Māwardī); Ṭabarī names God's greatness, admonition and punishment.

# 87:9/q02 Whom is the Prophet to remind? [فَذَكِّرْ · referent]
turns on: Whether the audience is universal, immediately Meccan, or restricted to receptive hearers.
- 87:9/q02/p1 Direct attention to receptive people likely to benefit.
  reasons: Yaḥyā ibn Sallām explains benefit as acceptance; Abū Muslim mentions hopeful receptivity; Ibn Kathīr says to address suitable hearers; Islahi says to leave the obdurate.
  holders (9 notes): MAWARDI(يحيى بن سلام); MAWARDI-FULL(يحيى بن سلام); JISHUMI(أبو مسلم); IBNJAWZI-ZAD(يحيى بن سلام); IBNKATHIR; IBNKATHIR-FULL; BURSEVI; ISLAHI-TADABBUR

# 87:9/q03 How does فَذَكِّرْ follow the earlier verses? [فَذَكِّرْ · links]
turns on: How preserved recitation and ease prepare the public call.
- 87:9/q03/p1 The earlier promises of preserved recitation and ease prepare the Prophet for reminding.
  reasons: Rāzī reads 87:8 as completing the Prophet so he may guide others; Abū Ḥayyān says recitation should benefit him and his audience; Qutb emphasizes the mission's weight; Ṭabāṭabāʾī includes purification from shirk; Ibn ʿĀshūr says fāʾ makes the command a result of what preceded it.

# 87:9/q05 What does the condition mean for the duty to remind? [فَذَكِّرْ إِن نَّفَعَتِ ٱلذِّكْرَىٰ · interpretation]
turns on: Whether in is a real condition, an ellipsis, a rebuke, or an affirmative or temporal use; and whether initial delivery differs from repetition.
- 87:9/q05/p1 Remind whether or not the hearers benefit; an unspoken alternative is understood.
  reasons: Al-Farrāʾ, al-Naḥḥās, al-Zahrawī and al-Jurjānī explain the omitted opposite. Wāḥidī, Baghawī, Ṭabrisī, Rāzī and others compare 16:81: heat protection implies cold. Rāzī cites 24:33, 2:172, 4:101, 2:283, 2:229 to show a condition need not imply its converse. Abū ʿAlī says the universal call establishes proof; al-Ḥasan distinguishes admonition for believers and proof for unbelievers. Ibn ʿĀshūr rejects only the need to supply explicit omitted words.
