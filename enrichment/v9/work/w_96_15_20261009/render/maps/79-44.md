# 79:44: 8 questions

## 79:44/q01 What is the Hour's مُنتَهَىٰهَآ?  [مُنتَهَىٰهَآ · meaning]
turns on: Whether 'end' denotes the limit of knowledge, its lexical endpoint, the reach of its news, or the end of the created order; some explain a shift from spatial endpoint to knowledge.
- 79:44/q01/p1 Its end is the limit of knowledge of the Hour, held by God alone, especially knowledge of its appointed time. — Al-Zajjaj, al-Wahidi's Abu Ishaq and exegetes, Ibn Abbas, al-Hasan, and Ibn Jurayj gloss the end as the limit of knowledge or knowledge with God. Al-Nahhas says it is the endpoint of knowledge. Al-Kashshaf says no created being has received it; al-Alusi's first interpretation says no one shares any part of it. Al-Alusi's preceding interpretation, Elmalili, and Tabatabai extend the scope to the Hour's essence, reality, properties, and details, as well as its time; Asad calls it the beginning and end of all that can be known. Ibn Ashur explains that an omitted noun is 'knowledge of its time'; he cites 53:42. Al-Qurtubi cites 7:187 and 31:34; Ibn Kathir cites 7:187, and the Prophet's answer to Gabriel that the questioned knows no more than the questioner, also reported by Study Quran. Mawardi says God concealed five matters even from a near angel or sent prophet and cites the end of 31:34; Kuran Yolu also cites 31:34. Study Quran says God will manifest the Hour at its appointed time and cites 7:187 and 30:34. Tabatabai says its sudden arrival in 7:187 supports divine exclusivity, while observing that reconciliation with other verses needs reflection. Al-Qashani adds that knowing it requires annihilation of one's knowledge in divine knowledge and one's self in God. Al-Durr's Ibn Jurayj also reads the following evening and morning as worldly time. [33 notes: TAB-FULL; ZAJJAJ; NAHHAS; MAWARDI-FULL; WAHIDI-BASIT(أبو إسحاق والمفسرون,ابن عباس); JISHUMI; BAGHAWI-FULL; KASHSHAF-FULL; IBNATIYYA-FULL; TABRISI(الحسن); IBNJAWZI-ZAD; QURTUBI-FULL(الزهري عن عروة بن الزبير); BAYDAWI-FULL; NASAFI-FULL; QASHANI; IBNKATHIR-FULL(رسول الله); BIQAI-FULL; DURR-FULL(ابن جرير); ALUSI-FULL; ELMALILI; IBNASHUR-FULL; TABATABAI; ASAD-NOTES; KURANYOLU-TEFSIR; STUDYQURAN(Prophet); IBNASHUR-FULL+; TABATABAI+]
- 79:44/q01/p2 Muntahā literally denotes the place or point where something reaches its end, later extending to destiny and knowledge. — Al-Jishumi gives the terminal-place sense. Ibn Ashur traces its shift from a journey's endpoint to destiny and then knowledge. [2 notes: JISHUMI; IBNASHUR-FULL]
- 79:44/q01/p3 Its end may be the point at which news of the Hour reaches someone. — Ibn Ashur reports this as an alternative to the interpretation through knowledge of its time. [1 notes: IBNASHUR-FULL]
- 79:44/q01/p4 The Hour's end may be the extinction of things and causes, when God's sole dominion becomes manifest. — Tabatabai reports this alternative and says even time cannot truly delimit the Hour. [1 notes: TABATABAI]

## 79:44/q02 How is مُنتَهَىٰهَآ construed grammatically?  [مُنتَهَىٰهَآ · grammar]
- 79:44/q02/p1 It is a nominative subject, with 'knowledge of its time' understood in the expression. — Al-Nahhas calls muntahāhā nominative as subject. Ibn Ashur supplies the omitted noun 'knowledge of its time,' referring back to the question in 79:42. [2 notes: NAHHAS; IBNASHUR-FULL]

## 79:44/q03 How does this statement answer the preceding question about the Hour?  [* · links]
turns on: Whether the statement directly explains the Prophet's inability to answer the timing question or follows a pause after a question already rejected by his mission as the last prophet.
- 79:44/q03/p1 It redirects the question about the Hour's time to God, because the Prophet has not been given that knowledge. — Al-Tabari and Ibn Atiyya say the Prophet has no role in fixing its time; Ibn Ashur says the statement explains that lack of involvement. Al-Qurtubi's report from al-Zuhri through Urwa presents it as an answer to the Prophet's frequent questioning and an instruction not to seek the time. Kuran Yolu says God's question establishes that the Prophet cannot obtain this knowledge. Al-Alusi says, on one interpretation, the Prophet was only to announce its approach and, on another, the question to him is misplaced because all knowledge of it belongs to God. Nasafi's first interpretation confines knowledge of its timing to God. [8 notes: TAB-FULL; IBNATIYYA-FULL; QURTUBI-FULL(الزهري عن عروة بن الزبير); NASAFI-FULL; ALUSI-FULL; IBNASHUR-FULL; KURANYOLU-TEFSIR]
- 79:44/q03/p2 The question is rejected because the last Prophet's mission itself signals the Hour; a pause after 'in what' leaves this verse as an independent statement. — Nasafi reports this alternative and the possible pause after the preceding phrase. [1 notes: NASAFI-FULL]

## 79:44/q04 What happened when people asked about the Hour, according to the reports?  [* · reports]
turns on: Whether the repeated questions were asked by the Prophet or addressed to him; Tabatabai objects to the first reading from the context.
- 79:44/q04/p1 The Prophet himself questioned repeatedly, then ceased when the verse placed the Hour's knowledge with God. — Al-Qurtubi transmits al-Zuhri through Urwa that the Prophet's frequent questioning was answered by this verse and he was told not to seek the time. Mawardi says he stopped asking and cited God's concealment of five matters from even an angel or prophet, with the end of 31:34. [2 notes: QURTUBI-FULL(الزهري عن عروة بن الزبير); MAWARDI-FULL; TABATABAI(عائشة)-]
- 79:44/q04/p2 The Prophet was repeatedly asked about the Hour until these verses were revealed; one report says the questions then stopped. — Urwa is reported by al-Qurtubi to say the Prophet kept being asked. Tabatabai transmits Aisha's report, graded sound by al-Hakim, adding that he was no longer asked afterward; Tabatabai says the context does not fit a reply to the Prophet's own question. [2 notes: QURTUBI-FULL(عروة بن الزبير); TABATABAI(عائشة)+]

## 79:44/q05 What task concerning the Hour is assigned to the Prophet?  [* · interpretation]
- 79:44/q05/p1 He is to warn those who fear the Hour or announce its approach, without determining, investigating, or disclosing its exact time. — Al-Tabari and Ibn Atiyya deny him any role in fixing its timing; Ibn Atiyya and Bint al-Shati specify warning those who fear it, and Bint al-Shati cites Abu Hayyan that such people benefit from warning. Al-Alusi says his role was to announce its approach; al-Baghawi says he has no knowledge of the Hour. Al-Biqa'i says he was not commissioned to determine its exact time. Elmalili says he is not charged to reveal the Hour's full reality and details. Tabatabai says people should not ask him because he cannot answer. [8 notes: TAB-FULL; IBNATIYYA-FULL; BAGHAWI-FULL; BIQAI-FULL; ALUSI-FULL; ELMALILI; TABATABAI; BINTSHATI]

## 79:44/q06 What temporal limits or descriptions of the Hour bear on this verse?  [* · links]
turns on: The notes address different aspects of temporal description: the limit supplied by cosmic upheaval and the suddenness of arrival.
- 79:44/q06/p1 Its only direct Qur'anic temporal limit is the world's extinction and cosmic upheaval; descriptions of a brief interval are figurative. — Tabatabai cites 39:68 for the upheaval and 46:35 and 30:55–56 for the brief interval. [1 notes: TABATABAI]
- 79:44/q06/p2 The Hour arrives suddenly, supporting God's exclusive knowledge of it. — Tabatabai cites 7:187 but says the argument needs further reflection to reconcile other verses. [1 notes: TABATABAI]

## 79:44/q07 What inward meaning is given to knowledge of the Hour ending with God?  [* · inward]
- 79:44/q07/p1 Knowing the Hour requires one's knowledge to be annihilated in divine knowledge and one's self in God. — Al-Qashani draws this from the claim that knowledge of the Hour ends with God. [1 notes: QASHANI]

## 79:44/q08 How does the verse fit the surah's overall argument?  [* · links]
- 79:44/q08/p1 It belongs to the surah's establishment of resurrection and gathering and its display of humanity's final fate. — Al-Biqa'i says the preceding surahs fully expounded this theme and this surah's oaths mark its decisive climax. [1 notes: BIQAI-FULL]
