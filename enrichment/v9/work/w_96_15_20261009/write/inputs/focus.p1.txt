# 96:15/q07 What event does the forelock threat foretell? [لَنَسْفَعًۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ · interpretation]
turns on: The notes dispute whether the scene belongs to Judgment, worldly defeat at Badr, or an unspecified act, and whether seizure or scorching is foregrounded.
- 96:15/q07/p1 Abū Jahl will be seized by the forelock and dragged to Hell, especially at the Resurrection.
  reasons: Al-Farrāʾ, al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī and others compare 55:41, where sinners are seized by forelocks and feet. Al-Qurṭubī says he may be folded with his feet and thrown into Hell. Muqātil and Bursevī specify angels dragging him face-first; Ibn Kathīr alternatively allows the forelock to be blackened on Judgment Day. Bint al-Shāṭiʾ hears in the wording both dragging and scorching.
