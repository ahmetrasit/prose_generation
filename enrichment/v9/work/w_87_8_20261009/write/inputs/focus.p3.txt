# 87:8/q13 How is an agent made ready to perform an act? [نُيَسِّرُكَ · theology]
- 87:8/q13/p1 An act occurs when divine facilitation tips a possible action toward performance.
  reasons: Rāzī says possibility leaves doing and refraining equal until the active side is preferred; the preponderance is facilitation. He cites the Prophetic saying that each person is facilitated for what they were created for and Q 92:7. Jishumī says facilitation can come through ability or preparation and notes yasar may be named optimistically or because right-hand work is easy.

# 87:8/q14 Why is the promised way already called easy? [الْيُسْرَىٰ · rhetoric]
- 87:8/q14/p1 Its ease is already established; God protects the Prophet from obstacles to receiving and following it.
  reasons: Ibn ʿĀshūr says the description points to an intrinsically easy charge and to the Prophet's divinely given capacity to fulfill it.
