# 87:8/q07 How does this clause connect syntactically with the preceding verses? [وَنُيَسِّرُكَ · grammar]
- 87:8/q07/p1 It coordinates with 'We shall make you recite' in 87:6; the knowledge statement in 87:7 is parenthetical.

# 87:8/q08 How does the promise lead into the Prophet's mission and the next command? [* · links]
- 87:8/q08/p1 Divine preparation for revelation and religion enables the Prophet to remind and guide others.
  reasons: Ālūsī says this broad reading makes the fāʾ of 87:9 especially apt; Ibn ʿĀshūr says God equips the anxious Messenger to bear revelation and the hard work of guiding the community. Baghawī's contextual reading moves from recitation to memory and teaching. Biqāʿī includes reflection and every gentle course.
