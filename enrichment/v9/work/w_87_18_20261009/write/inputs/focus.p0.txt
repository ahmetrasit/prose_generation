# 87:18/q01 What does “this” refer to? [هَٰذَا · referent]
turns on: Which earlier material the demonstrative reaches: the nearest statement, the adjacent passage, the whole sūra, or the Qurʾān; proximity and the reported sayings are offered as tests.
- 87:18/q01/p1 The teaching in verses 14–17: purification, remembrance, prayer, worldly preference, and the better, lasting afterlife.
  reasons: Al-Ṭabarī, Abū Ḥayyān, and Ibn ʿAṭiyya favor proximity; Ibn Kathīr calls this a strong choice. Rāzī reads the sequence as purification of belief and morals, knowledge of God, bodily obedience, and release from worldly attachment. Abū Dharr's report is cited for the span; Ibn ʿĀshūr says the opening through verse 13 is not confirmed here and cannot grade that report.

# 87:18/q02 In what sense is this teaching “in” the earlier scriptures? [لَفِى ٱلصُّحُفِ · interpretation]
turns on: Whether being written earlier entails shared teaching or actual transcription and verbal correspondence.
- 87:18/q02/p1 Its meaning or counterpart is there, though the wording and language may differ.
  reasons: Ibn Qutayba excludes an exact verbal identity; al-Kashshāf, Nasafī, al-Ālūsī, and Ibn ʿĀshūr specify shared meaning. Ibn ʿĀshūr takes scrolls as a container metonymy, comparing 38:16.
  holders (9 notes): IBNQUTAYBA-GHARIB; KASHSHAF; KASHSHAF-FULL; IBNJAWZI-ZAD(ابن قتيبة); NASAFI; NASAFI-FULL; IBNASHUR; IBNASHUR-FULL; ALUSI+
