# 87:18/q03 What does ṣuḥuf mean? [ٱلصُّحُفِ · meaning]
- 87:18/q03/p1 Written sheets or scrolls, plural of ṣaḥīfa; by extension, books of revelation.
  reasons: Al-Ṭabarī and al-Thaʿlabī give the plural. Bursawī distinguishes a muṣḥaf as a collection of sheets. Ibn ʿĀshūr calls ṣuḥuf an eloquent irregular plural, like sufun from safīna; Kuran Yolu says it became synonymous with book. Corpus Coranicum suggests Old South Arabian borrowing and notes the early Qurʾānic revelatory sense, later largely replaced by kitāb, alongside zubur.

# 87:18/q05 Which scriptures are meant by the earlier scrolls? [ٱلصُّحُفِ ٱلْأُولَىٰ · referent]
turns on: Whether the named prophets possessed scrolls, exemplify all revelation, or evoke biblical writings.
- 87:18/q05/p1 Abraham's scrolls and Moses's books, especially the Torah.
  reasons: Al-Ṭabarī names the two; Muqātil says Abraham's scrolls have been taken away. Ibn ʿĀshūr says Moses had many scrolls comprising Torah books. Kuran Yolu treats them as examples of prophetic revelation, not its limits.
  holders (10 notes): MUQATIL; TAB; TAB-FULL; THALABI(الضحاك); BURSEVI; IBNASHUR; IBNASHUR-FULL; KURANYOLU-TEFSIR; STUDYQURAN
  notes: MUQATIL:87:14-19/r4 TAB:87:18/r7 TAB:87:19/r6 TAB-FULL:v24p323/r6 THALABI:v10p185/r8 BURSEVI:v10p318/r6 IBNASHUR:87:18/r6 IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p290/r7 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:87:18/r1 STUDYQURAN:87:18/r4
- 87:18/q05/p2 All of God's earlier revealed books.
  reasons: Al-Ḥasan says the teaching occurs in all God's books; al-Māwardī gives this as a third interpretation. The claim is about the reach of the plural, beyond the two named exemplars.
  holders (7 notes): IBNABIHATIM(الحسن); MAWARDI; MAWARDI-FULL; QURTUBI(الحسن); QURTUBI-FULL(الحسن); DURR(الحسن); DURR-FULL(الحسن)

# 87:18/q06 How many scrolls or revealed books were given, and to whom? [ٱلصُّحُفِ · reports]
turns on: The reports differ in their totals and in whether Adam or Moses receives ten preliminary scrolls, and whether Abraham receives ten or twenty.
- 87:18/q06/p1 104 books: ten sheets to Adam, fifty to Seth, thirty to Idrīs, ten to Abraham, plus four named books.
  reasons: Abū Dharr's account in al-Thaʿlabī and al-Kashshāf gives this allocation; al-Thaʿlabī adds that Idrīs first wrote with a pen. Kuran Yolu repeats the ten, fifty, thirty, ten distribution.
  holders (3 notes): THALABI(أبو ذر); KASHSHAF-FULL(أبو ذر); KURANYOLU-TEFSIR
  notes: THALABI:v10p186/r1 KASHSHAF-FULL:v4p742#2/r2 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:87:18/r2
- 87:18/q06/p2 104 books: fifty to Seth, thirty to Idrīs, ten to Abraham, ten to Moses before the Torah, then four named books.
  reasons: Abū Dharr's prophetic account gives this distribution in al-Durr; Study Quran reports it and notes Shiite variants.
  holders (3 notes): DURR(أبو ذر); DURR-FULL(النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم); STUDYQURAN(Prophet)
  notes: DURR:87:18/r9 DURR-FULL:v8p488/r2 STUDYQURAN:87:18/r5
- 87:18/q06/p3 105 scrolls and four books: thirty-five to Seth, fifty to Idrīs, twenty to Abraham.
  reasons: Wahb ibn Munabbih names the four later books for Moses, David, Jesus, and Muhammad; Study Quran says some reports assign Abraham twenty scrolls.
  holders (4 notes): MAWARDI(وهب بن منبه); MAWARDI-FULL(وهب بن منبه); STUDYQURAN(Shiite sources)

# 87:18/q07 When were these and other scriptures revealed? [ٱلصُّحُفِ · reports]
- 87:18/q07/p1 Abraham's scrolls on Ramadan's first night; Torah on the sixth, Psalms on the twelfth, Gospel on the eighteenth, and Furqān on the twenty-fourth.
  reasons: Abū al-Khald gives this sequence in both Ṭabarī versions.

# 87:18/q08 What do reports say Abraham's scrolls taught? [ٱلصُّحُفِ · reports]
- 87:18/q08/p1 Practical maxims for wise people and rulers.
  reasons: The sayings counsel guarding tongue and time, attending to one's affairs
