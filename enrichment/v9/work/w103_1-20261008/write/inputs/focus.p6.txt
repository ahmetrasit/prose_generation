# 103:1/q23 What does the general day-night pattern of revelation say about this sūra's timing? [* · sciences]
- 103:1/q23/p1 Most Qur'anic revelation occurred by day; this report gives no specific time of day for al-ʿAṣr.
  reasons: Al-Suyuti relays Ibn Habib's general statement and notes that examples of night revelation were collected separately.
  holders (1 notes): ITQAN(ابن حبيب)
  notes: ITQAN:v1p65/r1
sources: ITQAN = al-Suyūṭī, d. 911
