# 96:5/q05 How does this clause relate to 'who taught by the pen'? [عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ · links]

# 96:5/q09 What does divine teaching show about God and humanity? [* · theology]
- 96:5/q09/p1 It shows generosity, lordship and wisdom, raising humanity from ignorance to knowledge.
  reasons: Zamakhsharī and Nasafī contrast darkness and light. Ibn Kathīr calls these first verses mercy and blessing. Bayḍāwī and Biqāʿī describe a rise from lowest to highest, rational proof then transmitted or sensory knowledge. Al-Malawī argues knowledge is noblest since it follows 'most generous'. Ṭabrisī says knowledge comes from a knower. Ibn ʿĀshūr says 'most generous' intensifies generosity rather than comparing God with another; divine gifts begin with creation and sustenance.
