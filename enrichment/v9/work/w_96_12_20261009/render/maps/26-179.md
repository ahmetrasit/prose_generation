# 26:179: 6 questions

## 26:179/q01 What does the call to fear God ask of Shuʿayb’s people, and why?  [فَٱتَّقُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ · interpretation]
- 26:179/q01/p1 They must fear God and practice piety in response to Shuʿayb’s call. — MUQATIL says Shuʿayb asks whether they fear God. BIQAI grounds the call in God’s gifts to them, including the grove, and in the messenger’s established sincerity. DURR describes the people of al-Aykah as following Madyan’s polytheistic pattern. IBNASHUR says the later appeal to fear the Creator adds His creation of them and earlier peoples as proof that He deserves fear. [4 notes: MUQATIL; BIQAI-FULL; DURR-FULL; IBNASHUR-FULL]

## 26:179/q02 How does the later command to fear the Creator relate to this call?  [فَٱتَّقُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ · links]
- 26:179/q02/p1 It reinforces the opening command and adds creation as a reason to fear God. — IBNASHUR says creation of the hearers and earlier peoples proves that God deserves fear, and the conjunction signals the added basis. [1 notes: IBNASHUR-FULL]

## 26:179/q03 Whom must they obey in وَأَطِيعُونِ, and why?  [وَأَطِيعُونِ · referent]
- 26:179/q03/p1 They must obey Shuʿayb as God’s trustworthy messenger; because his commands come from God, this is obedience to God in reality. — MUQATIL places the appeal for obedience after Shuʿayb’s claim to be a trustworthy messenger. BIQAI bases obedience on his established sincerity. DURR also connects the call with his trustworthy messengership, while describing it as obedience to God. BURSEVI explicitly says his command comes from God, so obeying him is really obedience to God. QIRAAT-ER says the fuller pronoun stresses Shuʿayb’s personal authority, ‘obey me,’ in the Aykah context. [5 notes: MUQATIL; BIQAI-FULL; DURR-FULL; BURSEVI; QIRAAT-ER]

## 26:179/q04 How is the final yāʾ of وَأَطِيعُونِ read, and what does it convey?  [وَأَطِيعُونِ · readings]
- 26:179/q04/p1 The final yāʾ is affirmed, restoring the fuller first person pronoun and emphasizing Shuʿayb’s ‘obey me.’ — NASHR reports Yaʿqūb affirming the yāʾ in both connected reading and pause; أطيعون occurs among eight such places in his list of sixteen extra yāʾs. QIRAAT-ER says the restored pronoun underscores Shuʿayb’s personal authority in the Aykah setting. [2 notes: NASHR(يعقوب); QIRAAT-ER]

## 26:179/q05 How does this call fit the repeated prophetic message in the sūra?  [* · links]
- 26:179/q05/p1 It repeats the prophets’ shared call to piety and obedience, joined with sincere worship and refusal of payment for preaching. — WAHIDI-BASIT reports the commentators that the repeated formula unites piety, obedience, exclusive worship, and no payment; WAHIDI-BASIT also cites 26:106–109 for refusing payment for the call and message. BAGHAWI says the prophets share this form because they agree on those demands. MUQATIL says Shuʿayb asks no payment for belief and awaits reward only from the Lord of the worlds. ALUSI places the call in the same recurring pattern, followed by the wage disclaimer and full measure. ISLAHI says the opening verses repeat material from the preceding prophetic account and have already been explained. [6 notes: MUQATIL; WAHIDI-BASIT(المفسرون); BAGHAWI-FULL; ALUSI-FULL; ISLAHI-TADABBUR]

## 26:179/q06 How do Shuʿayb’s reported commands and prohibitions compare with Ṣāliḥ’s?  [* · links]
- 26:179/q06/p1 Shuʿayb’s reported commands and prohibitions are more numerous than Ṣāliḥ’s. — SAMARRAI compares their quoted speeches, citing 26:150, 26:153, 26:179, and 26:185. [1 notes: SAMARRAI-ASRAR]
