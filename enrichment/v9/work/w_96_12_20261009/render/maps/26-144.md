# 26:144: 6 questions

## 26:144/q01 What does Ṣāliḥ mean by telling them to fear God?  [فَٱتَّقُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ · interpretation]
turns on: Whether the command is explained through worship and avoidance of disobedience or through fear of imminent punishment.
- 26:144/q01/p1 They are to fear God by worshipping Him and avoiding disobedience. — Muqātil specifies worship of God; Jishumī specifies avoiding disobedience; Kur'an Yolu renders the command as avoiding disobedience to God. Elmalılı states the command without further gloss. [4 notes: MUQATIL; JISHUMI; ELMALILI; KURANYOLU-TEFSIR]
- 26:144/q01/p2 They must fear punishment and heed Ṣāliḥ's warning while there is time. — Al-Ṭabarī explains fear as fear of punishment. Iṣlāḥī says the horrific day is near, warns against following deviant leaders, and says Ṣāliḥ later repeats the warning while time remains to heed it. [3 notes: TAB-FULL; ISLAHI-TADABBUR]

## 26:144/q02 What does obedience to Ṣāliḥ entail?  [وَأَطِيعُونِ · interpretation]
- 26:144/q02/p1 They are to obey Ṣāliḥ's instructions and warning as the message he conveys from God. — Muqātil mentions his instructions. Al-Ṭabarī specifies his warning and his command to follow God's obedience, citing 3:50. Jishumī says he is trustworthy in conveying the message and asks obedience to what was delivered. Iṣlāḥī warns against following deviant leaders. Elmalılı and Kur'an Yolu state obedience to Ṣāliḥ without further detail. [6 notes: MUQATIL; TAB-FULL; JISHUMI; ELMALILI; ISLAHI-TADABBUR; KURANYOLU-TEFSIR]

## 26:144/q03 How is the final yāʾ of أطيعون read, and what does it add?  [وَأَطِيعُونِ · readings]
- 26:144/q03/p1 The final yāʾ is affirmed, yielding a fuller first-person pronoun form and a longer ending. — Al-Nashr reports that Yaʿqūb affirms sixteen listed extra yāʾs in both connected recitation and pause, including أطيعون in eight places. Qiraat-Er explains the fuller pronoun form and lengthened ending. [2 notes: NASHR(يعقوب); QIRAAT-ER]

## 26:144/q04 Why are fear of God and obedience to Ṣāliḥ paired?  [فَٱتَّقُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُونِ · rhetoric]
- 26:144/q04/p1 Ṣāliḥ's established commission grounds the call to fear God, and his trustworthiness grounds the call to obey him. — Al-Biqāʿī connects each command to the corresponding claim Ṣāliḥ has already established about his mission and reliability. [1 notes: BIQAI-FULL]

## 26:144/q05 Where does this command recur in the sūra's prophetic accounts?  [* · links]
- 26:144/q05/p1 It repeats the appeal used in other prophetic accounts, including the preceding account of Hūd. — Al-Ālūsī refers back to the preceding explanation of this recurring formula. Ibn ʿĀshūr says it follows the preceding rebuke as in Hūd's story and cites 26:126. Iṣlāḥī points to the formulation at the end of the preceding account, citing 26:124 and 26:126. The Study Quran says Ṣāliḥ's opening repeats the pattern discussed for Noah and cites 26:105–109, 123–127, 160–164, and 176–180. [4 notes: ALUSI-FULL; IBNASHUR-FULL; ISLAHI-TADABBUR; STUDYQURAN]
- 26:144/q05/p2 Ṣāliḥ repeats this warning later in his own appeal. — Iṣlāḥī says he recalls the warning of 26:144 to tell his people that they still have time to fear God and obey him. [1 notes: ISLAHI-TADABBUR]

## 26:144/q06 How does Ṣāliḥ reinforce this appeal in the surrounding verses?  [* · links]
- 26:144/q06/p1 He seeks no payment, looks to God for reward, and reminds them of divine favors and comforts. — Yaḥyā ibn Sallām says his reward is recompense from the Lord of the worlds. Ibn Kathīr says he seeks no wage from them and only God's reward, then reminds them of God's favors. Bursevī says payment for counsel and invitation would cast suspicion on people of integrity. Al-Bayḍāwī says the gardens and springs specify the comforts just mentioned. [4 notes: YAHYA-SALLAM; IBNKATHIR-FULL; BURSEVI; BAYDAWI-FULL]
