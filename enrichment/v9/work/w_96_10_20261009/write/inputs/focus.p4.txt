# 96:10/q20 How do the later assembly and guards relate to the forbidding episode? [* · links]
- 96:10/q20/p1 Abu Jahl's assembly is his clan, while the summoned guards are angels.
  reasons: Mujahid makes both identifications; Qatada identifies the assembly as Abu Jahl's people and gives zabaniya the Arabic sense of police. Repeated Durr extracts preserve these details across the sura.
