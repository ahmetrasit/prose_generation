MAWARDI-FULL (author): A just scribe must neither add a term to harm the debtor nor omit one to harm the creditor. WAHIDI-BASIT (مجاهد والربيع): Mujāhid and al-Rabīʿ say the scribe must write when asked because God ordered him not to refuse; al-Farrāʾ says there were few literate people in the Prophet’s time. WAHIDI-BASIT (الحسن): Al-Ḥasan limits the duty to cases where no other scribe is available, because refusal would harm the creditor. JISHUMI (الشعبي وجماعة من المفسرين): Al-Shaʿbī and other exegetes say writing is a communal obligation, like jihad; Jishumi says this is the view followed in practice. JISHUMI (السدي): Al-Suddī says the duty applies to the scribe when he is free to write. JISHUMI (عطاء وطاووس والحسن وقتادة وابن زيد): The writer must not be harmed by being forbidden to write or made to add or subtract from what was dictated; the witness must not be made to refuse or testify to what he does not know. JISHUMI (author): If the verse concerns bearing testimony, that duty is variously described as communal obligation, recommendation, or permission; if giving testimony, it is communal when others can do it and individual when no substitute exists. JISHUMI (author): The scribe must have enough expertise to distinguish fair documentation from unfair terms. JISHUMI (قيل): Another view makes writing a collective duty when a group is present, and an individual duty when only one writer is available. BAGHAWI-FULL (مجاهد): A scribe must write when asked; al-Hasan requires it only when no other scribe is available, while others regard it as recommended. KASHSHAF-FULL (author): If the phrase attaches to ‘write,’ the scribe must use that taught form; if it attaches to ‘let him write,’ refusal is prohibited generally before writing is ordered with qualifications. KASHSHAF-FULL (الشعبي): Al-Shaʿbī regards writing as a communal obligation. IBNATIYYA-FULL (عطاء): ʿAṭāʾ and others say “let a scribe write” makes writing obligatory on the scribe; al-Shaʿbī and ʿAṭāʾ also specify that this applies when he is the only available scribe, while al-Suddī says it applies when he is free. IBNATIYYA-FULL (القاضي أبو محمد): No particular scribe must write if another is available, and he may decline unless hired; if none is available, an exhortatory duty falls on the present person. IBNJAWZI-ZAD (سعيد بن جبير): Saʿid ibn Jubayr says the person owing the debt must dictate what he owes the claimant to the scribe and must not reduce any part of it. IBNJAWZI-ZAD (ابن عباس): Ibn ʿAbbas, Mujahid, ʿIkrima, al-Suddi, al-Rabiʿ, al-Farra, and Muqatil say a scribe or witness must not be burdened with a summons while busy; al-Rabiʿ describes a requester insisting despite an available alternative.
