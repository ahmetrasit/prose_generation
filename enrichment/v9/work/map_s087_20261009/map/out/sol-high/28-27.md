# 28:27: 43 questions

## 28:27/q01 Who was the father making the offer?  [قَالَ ٱبْنَتَىَّ · referent]
turns on: Reported kinship and names differ; al-Tabari questions their proof.
- 28:27/q01/p1 Shuayb, the women's father. — Muqatil and Ibn Atiyya's majority identify him; Biqai names Torah equivalents Reuel and Yithro. [7 notes: MUQATIL; IBNATIYYA-FULL(الجمهور); BIQAI-FULL; BURSEVI; TABATABAI; IBNKATHIR-FULL; DURR-FULL(ابن جريج)]
- 28:27/q01/p2 Shuayb's nephew, named Tharwan, Athrun or Yathrun. — Al-Hasan, Abu Ubayda and Abu Ubayda ibn Abd Allah ibn Masud give variant forms. [2 notes: IBNATIYYA-FULL(الحسن وأبو عبيدة); IBNKATHIR-FULL(أبو عبيدة بن عبد الله بن مسعود)]
- 28:27/q01/p3 A righteous man unrelated to Shuayb, or an uncle called father because he acted as one. — Ibn Atiyya reports both. [1 notes: IBNATIYYA-FULL(قيل)]
- 28:27/q01/p4 Yathra, Yathrib or Yathribi, lord of Midian. — Ibn Abbas reports vary in Ibn Kathir and al-Durr. [4 notes: IBNKATHIR-FULL(ابن عباس); DURR-FULL(ابن عباس)]
- 28:27/q01/p5 His identity cannot be established from the available reports. — Al-Tabari requires compelling transmission. [1 notes: IBNKATHIR-FULL(ابن جرير)]

## 28:27/q02 Why did the father offer Moses marriage?  [إِنِّىٓ أُرِيدُ أَنْ أُنكِحَكَ · interpretation]
- 28:27/q02/p1 He valued Moses's strength, trust and character after the daughter's report. — Wahidi mentions her account; Abu Hayyan adds detachment, reliance on God and flight from unbelievers; Ibn Jawzi says he was drawn to Moses. [4 notes: WAHIDI-BASIT; IBNJAWZI-ZAD; ABUHAYYAN-FULL; KURANYOLU-TEFSIR]
- 28:27/q02/p2 The emphatic words reassure and welcome a stranger. — Biqai says a newcomer rarely expects welcome, especially from a leader; Alusi treats the speech as an emphatic explanatory resumption. [2 notes: BIQAI-FULL; ALUSI-FULL]

## 28:27/q03 Was this offer itself the marriage contract?  [أُرِيدُ أَنْ أُنكِحَكَ · law]
turns on: The wording expresses intent and leaves the bride unnamed.
- 28:27/q03/p1 It was a proposal; a later contract needed an identified bride and assent. — Kashshaf contrasts an intention with 'I have married you'; Qurtubi says no uncertain spouse or option is allowed; Razi, Ibn Atiyya and Ibn Ashur infer later specification. [13 notes: KASHSHAF-FULL; RAZI-FULL; IBNATIYYA-FULL(القاضي أبو محمد); QURTUBI-FULL(أبو الحسن الكرخي,ابن القاسم,مكي); BAYDAWI-FULL; NASAFI-FULL; ABUHAYYAN-FULL; IBNASHUR-FULL; TABATABAI]
- 28:27/q03/p2 The account states an intended pact without establishing its legal form. — Bursevi reports this possibility. [1 notes: BURSEVI]

## 28:27/q04 What does unkiḥaka mean?  [أُنكِحَكَ · meaning]
turns on: Lexical use and legal literal sense are distinguished.
- 28:27/q04/p1 I will give one daughter to you in marriage. — Zajjaj, Wahidi and Nasafi gloss it; Biqai compares the joined bond to jaws; Ibn Atiyya rejects an objection to the object construction. [5 notes: ZAJJAJ; WAHIDI-BASIT; NASAFI-FULL; BIQAI-FULL; IBNATIYYA-FULL]
- 28:27/q04/p2 Nikah can mean intercourse or contract; the contract is the sounder legal literal sense. — Jishumi reports both usages. [1 notes: JISHUMI]

## 28:27/q05 What are the daughters' reported names?  [ٱبْنَتَىَّ هَٰتَيْنِ · reports]
turns on: The reports give differing names and age order.
- 28:27/q05/p1 Safura or Safuriyya and a sister called Layya, Leya or Labani. — Shuayb al-Jubbai and later transmitters vary in spelling; Ibn Abi Hatim identifies them as the women who held back their flock. [8 notes: THALABI(شعيب الجبائي); IBNABIHATIM(محمد بن اسحاق); MAWARDI-FULL; BAGHAWI-FULL(شعيب الجبائي); IBNKATHIR-FULL(شعيب الجبائي,محمد بن إسحاق); IBNATIYYA-FULL(الرواة); BURSEVI]
- 28:27/q05/p2 Safura or Safra and a sister called Sharafa, Sharfa or Sharqa. — Ibn Ishaq is quoted in different transcriptions. [6 notes: THALABI(ابن إسحاق); IBNABIHATIM(محمد بن اسحاق); MAWARDI-FULL; BAGHAWI-FULL(ابن إسحاق); IBNKATHIR-FULL(محمد بن إسحاق); IBNATIYYA-FULL(الرواة)]
- 28:27/q05/p3 Elder Safra and younger Safira or Sufayra. — Other authorities in Thalabi and Baghawi. [3 notes: THALABI(غيرهم); BAGHAWI-FULL(غيرهما); KASHSHAF-FULL]
- 28:27/q05/p4 Moses's wife was Balaqis. — Al-Husayn ibn Ali in Mujahid. [1 notes: MUJAHID(الحسين بن علي)]
- 28:27/q05/p5 Moses's wife was Safira, daughter of Yathrun. — Ibn Abbas in al-Durr. [1 notes: DURR-FULL(ابن عباس)]

## 28:27/q06 Which daughter did Moses marry?  [إِحْدَى ٱبْنَتَىَّ · reports]
turns on: Reports differ on the bride's age and identification.
- 28:27/q06/p1 The younger, often identified as the inviter and Safura or Safuriyya. — Gabriel, Prophetic reports and Baghawi's majority say younger; Abu Dharr mentions her modest conduct. Qurtubi suggests Moses favored her after their encounter. [14 notes: MUJAHID(جبريل); IBNABIHATIM(عبد الرحمن بن زيد بن اسلم); MAWARDI-FULL; BAGHAWI-FULL(أكثرهم); IBNATIYYA-FULL(النبي عليه السلام); QURTUBI-FULL(أبو ذر,ابن خويزمنداد); IBNKATHIR-FULL(أبو ذر); DURR-FULL(أبو ذر,أبو هريرة); BURSEVI]
- 28:27/q06/p2 The elder daughter. — Wahb, al-Qushayri and Ibn Jurayj report elder; Ibn Jurayj calls her Safura and the inviter. [4 notes: IBNATIYYA-FULL(وهب); QURTUBI-FULL(القشيري); BAGHAWI-FULL(أكثرهم); DURR-FULL(ابن جريج)]
- 28:27/q06/p3 The daughter who had invited him, without a settled age. — Abd al-Rahman ibn Zayd describes Moses checking the father's preference and hearing of her chastity; Ibn Abbas and Ja'far al-Sadiq agree on inviter. [4 notes: IBNABIHATIM(عبد الرحمن بن زيد بن اسلم); MAWARDI-FULL(عبد الرحمن بن زيد); IBNATIYYA-FULL(ابن عباس); TABATABAI(أبو عبد الله عليه السلام)]

## 28:27/q07 Does hātayn imply other daughters?  [هَٰتَيْنِ · referent]
turns on: Whether pointing to these two entails additional daughters.
- 28:27/q07/p1 It points to the two women Moses met, who were present. — Biqai says Moses could consider them; Tabatabai notes their presence; Abu Hayyan denies further daughters follow from it. [4 notes: BIQAI-FULL; TABATABAI; ABUHAYYAN-FULL; ALUSI-FULL]
- 28:27/q07/p2 It suggests the father had other daughters. — Kashshaf and Nasafi infer this; Alusi reports four younger sisters or seven daughters, but calls the demonstrative inconclusive. [3 notes: KASHSHAF-FULL; NASAFI-FULL; ALUSI-FULL]

## 28:27/q08 How is the conditional phrase construed?  [عَلَىٰٓ أَن تَأْجُرَنِى · grammar]
- 28:27/q08/p1 ʿAlā introduces a condition; the infinitive clause can be circumstantial to either party. — Damghani and Ibn Ashur note contractual use; Abu Hayyan, Samin and Alusi attach it to father or Moses. [6 notes: DAMGHANI; IBNASHUR-FULL; ABUHAYYAN-FULL; SAMIN-DURR; ALUSI-FULL; IBNATIYYA-FULL]
- 28:27/q08/p2 The agreement requires Moses's hired labor for eight years. — Yahya, Muqatil, Zajjaj, Wahidi, Baghawi and others give the practical sense. [13 notes: YAHYA-SALLAM; MUQATIL; ZAJJAJ; WAHIDI-BASIT(مقاتل); WAHIDI-WAJIZ(الشيخ); BAGHAWI-FULL; NASAFI-FULL; BURSEVI; TABATABAI; BURHAN; IBNJAWZI-ZAD(الزجاج)]

## 28:27/q09 What does taʾjuranī mean here?  [تَأْجُرَنِى · meaning]
turns on: The verb is viewed from either the worker's or recipient's side.
- 28:27/q09/p1 Moses will work for the father as an employee. — Thalabi, Makki, Kashshaf, Razi, Baydawi, Alusi and Ibn Ashur give this sense. [8 notes: THALABI; MAWARDI-FULL; IBNATIYYA-FULL(مكي); KASHSHAF-FULL; RAZI-FULL; BAYDAWI-FULL; ALUSI-FULL; IBNASHUR-FULL]
- 28:27/q09/p2 Moses will reward or compensate the father with service. — Ibn Qutayba and Tabari compare God's ajr; Farra makes herding the compensation; Kashshaf cites a Prophetic condolence formula. [13 notes: MAJAZ; IBNQUTAYBA-GHARIB; TAB-FULL; WAHIDI-BASIT(ابن قتيبة,الفراء); BAGHAWI-FULL(الفراء); KASHSHAF-FULL(رسول الله); RAZI-FULL; BAYDAWI-FULL; BIQAI-FULL; ALUSI-FULL]

## 28:27/q10 What forms or vocalizations of the hiring verb occur?  [تَأْجُرَنِى · readings]
turns on: Vocalization and derivational forms differ.
- 28:27/q10/p1 The jīm can take ḍamma or kasra. — Ibn Jawzi attributes both Arabic forms to al-Farra. [1 notes: IBNJAWZI-ZAD(الفراء)]
- 28:27/q10/p2 Ajar(a) gives maʾjūr or muʾjir; ājara gives muʾājir. — Akhfash and Basran grammarians list the forms; al-Mubarrad favors the short verb and distinguishes ijāra from ijār; al-Raghib views the verbs from one or both parties. [10 notes: AKHFASH(بعضهم); TAB-FULL(بعض أهل العربية من أهل البصرة,بعضهم); WAHIDI-BASIT(الأخفش,المبرد); BIQAI-FULL; ALUSI-FULL(الراغب,المبرد,تحفة المحتاج)]

## 28:27/q11 How are the eight ḥijaj parsed?  [ثَمَٰنِىَ حِجَجٍۢ · grammar]
turns on: The parse tracks the sense assigned to taʾjuranī.
- 28:27/q11/p1 As an adverbial duration of eight years. — Tabrisi, Nasafi and others allow or adopt this; Samin says it fits hired-work sense. [7 notes: KASHSHAF-FULL; TABRISI; BAYDAWI-FULL; NASAFI-FULL; ABUHAYYAN-FULL; SAMIN-DURR]
- 28:27/q11/p2 As an object meaning eight years of work or herding. — Razi adopts it; Kashshaf and Abu Hayyan allow it; Samin insists it belongs to the compensation sense. [8 notes: KASHSHAF-FULL; RAZI-FULL; BAYDAWI-FULL; ABUHAYYAN-FULL; SAMIN-DURR(الزمخشري); ALUSI-FULL]

## 28:27/q12 What does ḥijaj mean and why use that word?  [حِجَجٍۢ · meaning]
turns on: The sources distinguish lexical sense, etymology and implied blessing.
- 28:27/q12/p1 It is the plural of ḥijja, meaning years. — Tabari, Thalabi, Jishumi, Baghawi, Nasafi, Biqai, Alusi and Ibn Ashur agree; Jishumi glosses ajal as appointed time. [10 notes: TAB-FULL; THALABI; JISHUMI; BAGHAWI-FULL; NASAFI-FULL; BIQAI-FULL; ALUSI-FULL(تحفة المحتاج); IBNASHUR-FULL; DAMGHANI]
- 28:27/q12/p2 The year takes its name from the recurring pilgrimage. — Ibn Ashur notes its place near the Arabic year's end; Tabatabai infers Abrahamic pilgrimage among Shuayb's people; Ja'far al-Sadiq cites ḥijaj rather than sinīn. [4 notes: IBNASHUR-FULL; TABATABAI(أبو عبد الله عليه السلام)]
- 28:27/q12/p3 The term auspiciously suggests blessing, provision or yearly pilgrimage. — Biqai offers these associations. [1 notes: BIQAI-FULL]

## 28:27/q13 What work did Moses perform?  [أَن تَأْجُرَنِى ثَمَٰنِىَ حِجَجٍۢ · interpretation]
turns on: Whether narrative reports specify work left unstated in the verse.
- 28:27/q13/p1 He tended the father's flock, possibly with other rural work. — Tabari, Farra, Thalabi, Ibn Ishaq and others specify herding; Biqai adds other tasks. Qurtubi's al-Muhallab invokes custom, but Ibn al-Arabi says no sound transmission proves herding. [15 notes: FARRA; TAB-FULL; THALABI(الأئمة); IBNABIHATIM(محمد بن اسحاق); MAWARDI-FULL; WAHIDI-BASIT(الفراء); BAGHAWI-FULL(الفراء); KASHSHAF-FULL; RAZI-FULL; QURTUBI-FULL(ابن العربي,المهلب); BIQAI-FULL; IBNKATHIR-FULL]
- 28:27/q13/p2 The Qur'an leaves the work unspecified for its narrative purpose. — Ibn Ashur notes Exodus names Jethro's sheep. [1 notes: IBNASHUR-FULL]

## 28:27/q14 What was the status of the eight years?  [ثَمَٰنِىَ حِجَجٍۢ · interpretation]
- 28:27/q14/p1 They were the binding minimum. — Ibn Abbas explicitly says obligatory and reasons a prophet would fulfill it; Razi says the contract used the shorter term. [6 notes: IBNABIHATIM(ابن عباس); MAWARDI-FULL(ابن عباس); RAZI-FULL; BIQAI-FULL; DURR-FULL(عمر بن الخطاب)]

## 28:27/q15 Was the service dower or a separate condition?  [عَلَىٰٓ أَن تَأْجُرَنِى ثَمَٰنِىَ حِجَجٍۢ · interpretation]
turns on: Who receives the work and whether the narrative abbreviates a separate dower.
- 28:27/q15/p1 It was the bride's marriage payment or dower. — Tabari, Thalabi and others take the direct exchange; Tabrisi prefers it from surface wording; Ibn Ashur suggests the daughter consented silently or her father could receive her dower. [11 notes: TAB-FULL; THALABI(الأئمة); MAWARDI-FULL; TABRISI; RAZI-FULL(الفقهاء); QURTUBI-FULL; ABUHAYYAN-FULL; IBNASHUR-FULL]
- 28:27/q15/p2 It was a condition benefiting the father, with separate or legally presumed dower. — Mawardi reports this; Bursevi points to 'hire me' rather than 'her'; Kashshaf, Baydawi and Ibn Ashur list separate wages, separate dower or flock ownership. [8 notes: MAWARDI-FULL; KASHSHAF-FULL; BAYDAWI-FULL; NASAFI-FULL; BURSEVI; IBNASHUR-FULL]
- 28:27/q15/p3 The pact is reported without settling its legal form. — Bursevi reports this; Ibn Ashur calls the verse too ambiguous to prove a current dower rule alone. [2 notes: BURSEVI; IBNASHUR-FULL]

## 28:27/q16 May marriage and hire be combined, or service count as dower?  [أُنكِحَكَ تَأْجُرَنِى · law]
turns on: Views turn on what qualifies as dower and on the authority of earlier law.
- 28:27/q16/p1 Yes, specified service may be linked to marriage or count as dower. — Jishumi, Shafii, Ibn Habib, Ashhab and Asbagh allow it; Tabrisi cites surface wording; Ibn Atiyya and Qurtubi cite Qur'an teaching as dower; Ibn Ashur prefers permission and honoring marriage conditions. [15 notes: JISHUMI; KASHSHAF-FULL(الشافعي); IBNATIYYA-FULL(مالك وابن حبيب وغيره); TABRISI; QURTUBI-FULL(ابن العربي,ابن حبيب,ابن خويزمنداد,المزني,رسول الله); IBNASHUR-FULL(أشهب وأصبغ,النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم); TABATABAI(أبو عبد الله عليه السلام)]
- 28:27/q16/p2 A free husband's service is not property and cannot be dower; property or a slave's labor can. — Abu Hanifa says the husband is not property; Bursevi says a valid marriage for free labor still owes the normal dower, citing 4:4 and 4:24. [5 notes: KASHSHAF-FULL(أبو حنيفة); QURTUBI-FULL(أبو حنيفة); BURSEVI]
- 28:27/q16/p3 The combination is void or rescinded because hire and marriage differ. — Malik and Ibn al-Qasim are quoted; Ibn Habib allows it if the wife is paid first; Ibn Ashur distinguishes effects before and after consummation. [5 notes: IBNATIYYA-FULL(مالك وابن حبيب وغيره); QURTUBI-FULL(ابن القاسم,مالك); IBNASHUR-FULL(مالك)]
- 28:27/q16/p4 It is disliked initially, but an existing pact may stand. — Abu Zayd and another Malik report say so; Ibn Khuwayz Mindad allows service but prefers money. [3 notes: QURTUBI-FULL(أبو زيد,ابن خويزمنداد); IBNASHUR-FULL(مالك)]
- 28:27/q16/p5 The earlier law is not binding proof for Muslims. — Razi rejects direct inference, listing guardian benefit, no separate woman's consideration and extra conditions as earlier-law possibilities; Bursevi invokes abrogation by 4:4. [7 notes: RAZI-FULL; BURSEVI]
- 28:27/q16/p6 Valuable services can be dower in principle, though this ambiguous verse alone cannot establish it. — Ibn Ashur calls for legal-principle analysis. [1 notes: IBNASHUR-FULL]

## 28:27/q17 Must hired work, term and flock be specified?  [تَأْجُرَنِى ثَمَٰنِىَ حِجَجٍۢ · law]
turns on: Whether custom removes uncertainty.
- 28:27/q17/p1 Custom can specify ordinary work; the start runs from contract if unstated. — Malik, Bukhari and al-Muhallab cite the general verse; Ibn Atiyya and Qurtubi say negotiations may have set the start. Known months, wages and counted flock are agreed valid. [8 notes: IBNATIYYA-FULL(القاضي أبو محمد,مكي); QURTUBI-FULL(أبو بكر الرازي,البخاري,المهلب,مالك)]
- 28:27/q17/p2 Unnamed tasks or flock are indeterminate and invalid. — Abu Hanifa and Shafii require tasks; Ibn al-Qasim requires replacement terms for an identified flock, which Qurtubi calls very weak. [3 notes: QURTUBI-FULL(أبو حنيفة,ابن القاسم)]
- 28:27/q17/p3 Custom and known shepherd capacity can determine an unspecified flock. — Qurtubi approves knowing capacity, recalling Moses lifting the stone. [2 notes: QURTUBI-FULL]

## 28:27/q18 What wage or flock did Moses receive?  [تَأْجُرَنِى · reports]
turns on: Reports differ in the criterion and miracle details; the text leaves wages unstated.
- 28:27/q18/p1 Lambs unlike their mothers in color; a staff miracle produced them. — Yahya, Mawardi, Tabrisi, Qurtubi and Anas vary over placing or striking the staff, dream instruction, ropes over water and one exception; Thalabi says Shuayb recognized divine provision. [12 notes: YAHYA-SALLAM; MAWARDI-FULL(يحيى بن سلام); TABRISI(يحيى بن سلام); QURTUBI-FULL(القشيري,الهروي,يحيى بن سلام); IBNKATHIR-FULL(أنس بن مالك); THALABI; BURSEVI]
- 28:27/q18/p2 Every spotted or piebald lamb; all were spotted. — Yahya, Mawardi, Qurtubi and Thalabi report this variant. [4 notes: YAHYA-SALLAM(بعضهم); MAWARDI-FULL; QURTUBI-FULL(غير يحيى); THALABI]
- 28:27/q18/p3 The verse leaves the wages unstated; marriage or customary wages are possible. — Ibn Ashur recalls shirts, balagha shoes and other customary shepherd wages; Qurtubi says the wage is unspecified. [3 notes: QURTUBI-FULL; IBNASHUR-FULL]
- 28:27/q18/p4 At departure a miraculous colored flock multiplied for Moses. — Utba's report mentions twins, triplets and later Samaritan sheep in Syria; al-Durr reports colored offspring. [3 notes: IBNKATHIR-FULL(عتبة بن الندر); DURR-FULL(عقبة بن المنذر السلمي)]

## 28:27/q19 How are reported sheep traits explained?  [تَأْجُرَنِى · reports]
turns on: Report glosses differ for two animal terms.
- 28:27/q19/p1 The lamb reward excluded sheep with several deficient milking traits. — Harawi lists the exclusion; Ibn Kathir prefers wording that says deficient types were absent. [2 notes: QURTUBI-FULL(الهروي); IBNKATHIR-FULL]
- 28:27/q19/p2 ʿAzūz has little or narrow milk flow. — Harawi relates it to hard ground; Ibn Lahiʿa says narrow flow. [2 notes: QURTUBI-FULL(الهروي); IBNKATHIR-FULL(ابن لهيعة)]
- 28:27/q19/p3 Fashūsh leaks milk freely through a broad passage. — Harawi adds idioms and a hadith image; Ibn Lahiʿa agrees on a large flow. [2 notes: QURTUBI-FULL(الهروي); IBNKATHIR-FULL(ابن لهيعة)]
- 28:27/q19/p4 Kumush or kumaysha has a small contracted udder. — Harawi mentions kushud and kamish al-izar; Ibn Lahiʿa says too small to grasp. [2 notes: QURTUBI-FULL(الهروي); IBNKATHIR-FULL(ابن لهيعة)]
- 28:27/q19/p5 Dabūb has a narrow milk opening or a long dragging udder. — Harawi says dab means forceful milking; Ibn Lahiʿa gives the divergent long-udder sense. [2 notes: QURTUBI-FULL(الهروي); IBNKATHIR-FULL(ابن لهيعة)]
- 28:27/q19/p6 Thaʿūl has an extra teat, or an extremely reduced udder. — Harawi also explains extra tooth and narrow passage; Ibn Lahiʿa describes something like two teats. [2 notes: QURTUBI-FULL(الهروي); IBNKATHIR-FULL(ابن لهيعة)]

## 28:27/q20 Is payment in future lambs a valid hire?  [تَأْجُرَنِى · law]
turns on: Whether uncertain yield is acceptable compensation.
- 28:27/q20/p1 No: births and survival are uncertain wages. — Qurtubi cites prohibition of uncertain sale and unborn offspring in wombs or loins. [2 notes: QURTUBI-FULL]
- 28:27/q20/p2 A share of flock offspring or of woven cloth can compensate labor. — Rashid permits one-third or one-quarter of offspring; Ibn Sirin, Ata and Ahmad allow a weaver's share. [2 notes: QURTUBI-FULL(ابن سيرين,راشد بن معمر)]

## 28:27/q21 What does the optional ten-year completion mean?  [فَإِنْ أَتْمَمْتَ عَشْرًۭا فَمِنْ عِندِكَ · interpretation]
turns on: Whether the offer is framed as fixed eight plus gift or a stated choice.
- 28:27/q21/p1 The extra two years are Moses's voluntary favor beyond the required eight. — The commentaries repeatedly stress this; Ibn Ashur says Moses could decide after eight; Biqai connects the years to maturation and observing a grandson. [26 notes: MUQATIL; YAHYA-SALLAM; FARRA; TAB-FULL; ZAJJAJ; IBNABIHATIM(ابن عباس); THALABI; MAWARDI-FULL(ابن عباس); WAHIDI-BASIT; WAHIDI-WAJIZ(الشيخ); BAGHAWI-FULL; KASHSHAF-FULL; IBNATIYYA-FULL; TABRISI; IBNJAWZI-ZAD; RAZI-FULL; BAYDAWI-FULL; NASAFI-FULL; ABUHAYYAN-FULL; IBNKATHIR-FULL; BIQAI-FULL; BURSEVI; ALUSI-FULL; IBNASHUR-FULL; TABATABAI]
- 28:27/q21/p2 The offer states a choice of eight or ten years. — Tabatabai uses this formulation but denies the father could require ten. [2 notes: TABATABAI]

## 28:27/q22 How is 'from you' analyzed?  [فَمِنْ عِندِكَ · grammar]
turns on: The ellipsis may be nominal or verbal.
- 28:27/q22/p1 It means from Moses's favor, outside the employer's claim. — Damghani and Burhan gloss generosity; Ibn Ashur calls min partitive, ʿinda figurative self, with omitted predicate. [3 notes: DAMGHANI; BURHAN; IBNASHUR-FULL]
- 28:27/q22/p2 It may predicate omitted 'it is' or imply the verb 'you added it'. — Samin gives nominal and accusative analyses. [1 notes: SAMIN-DURR]

## 28:27/q23 Which term did Moses actually complete?  [أَتْمَمْتَ عَشْرًۭا · reports]
turns on: Reports differ on whether he stayed another decade.
- 28:27/q23/p1 The longer, better ten-year term. — Gabriel, Prophetic reports, Ibn Abbas and Ja'far al-Sadiq say this; Ibn Kathir says mursal routes reinforce each other, though al-Bazzar finds the Abu Dharr chain singular. Ibn Abbas says a prophet fulfills the better term. [24 notes: MUJAHID(الحسين بن علي,جبريل); IBNABIHATIM(ابن عباس); MAWARDI-FULL(ابن عباس,النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم); QURTUBI-FULL; IBNKATHIR-FULL(أبو ذر,البزار,مجاهد,محمد بن كعب القرظي,يوسف بن تيرح); DURR-FULL(أبو ذر,أبو هريرة,ابن عباس,الحسن بن علي,جابر,محمد بن كعب القرظي); TABATABAI(أبو عبد الله عليه السلام,رسول الله صلى الله عليه وآله وسلم)]
- 28:27/q23/p2 Ten years of service followed by ten more years' stay. — Mujahid reports this additional period. [1 notes: MUJAHID(مجاهد)]

## 28:27/q24 Why did Moses hire himself out?  [تَأْجُرَنِى · reports]
- 28:27/q24/p1 To preserve chastity and obtain food. — Reports in Ibn Abi Hatim, Mawardi, Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir and al-Durr say this; Ibn Kathir calls one Utba route weak due to Maslama ibn Ali and questions another; he doubts Ibn Lahiʿa's raised report. [9 notes: IBNABIHATIM(رسول الله); MAWARDI-FULL(النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم); QURTUBI-FULL(عيينة بن حصن); IBNKATHIR-FULL(النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم,عتبة بن الندر); DURR-FULL(عقبة بن المنذر السلمي)]

## 28:27/q25 What hardship does the father disclaim?  [وَمَآ أُرِيدُ أَنْ أَشُقَّ عَلَيْكَ · interpretation]
turns on: The assurance may concern the extra years, the work, or both.
- 28:27/q25/p1 He will not demand the full ten years. — Muqatil, Tabari, Wahidi, Baghawi and others say so. [15 notes: MUQATIL; TAB-FULL; WAHIDI-BASIT; WAHIDI-WAJIZ(الشيخ); BAGHAWI-FULL; KASHSHAF-FULL; TABRISI; IBNJAWZI-ZAD; RAZI-FULL; BAYDAWI-FULL; NASAFI-FULL; ABUHAYYAN-FULL; BURSEVI; ALUSI-FULL]
- 28:27/q25/p2 He will be lenient about daily work, scrutiny and extra duties. — Kashshaf compares strict employers; Razi mentions excessive care; Tabatabai says he presents as a good employer; Ibn Ashur cites gracious trade. [16 notes: YAHYA-SALLAM; KASHSHAF-FULL; TABRISI; RAZI-FULL(رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم); BAYDAWI-FULL; ABUHAYYAN-FULL; IBNKATHIR-FULL; BIQAI-FULL; BURSEVI; ALUSI-FULL; TABATABAI; STUDYQURAN(R, Ṭ); IBNASHUR-FULL]
- 28:27/q25/p3 No part of the agreement, required or optional, is meant to be onerous. — Biqai gives this wider reading. [1 notes: BIQAI-FULL]

## 28:27/q26 What is the image behind 'ashuq'?  [أَشُقَّ · meaning]
- 28:27/q26/p1 A hard task seems to split one's judgment over ability to bear it. — Kashshaf, Razi, Baydawi, Nasafi, Samin, Bursevi and Alusi give the root image; Ibn Ashur says the employer would cause the burden. [8 notes: KASHSHAF-FULL; RAZI-FULL; BAYDAWI-FULL; NASAFI-FULL; SAMIN-DURR; BURSEVI; ALUSI-FULL; IBNASHUR-FULL]

## 28:27/q27 What is promised by being among the righteous?  [مِنَ ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ · interpretation]
turns on: Whether the adjective is contextual or general.
- 28:27/q27/p1 Good companionship, gentleness, fair treatment and keeping the covenant. — Muqatil cites Moses's counsel to Aaron at 7:142; Ibn Ishaq, Umar and later commentators stress fidelity and kind dealing; Ibn Ashur calls it character disclosure. [27 notes: MUQATIL; TAB-FULL(ابن إسحاق); IBNABIHATIM(محمد بن اسحاق); THALABI; MAWARDI-FULL(ابن إسحاق); WAHIDI-BASIT(عمر,مقاتل); WAHIDI-WAJIZ(الشيخ); BAGHAWI-FULL(عمر); KASHSHAF-FULL(رسول الله); IBNJAWZI-NUZHA(أهل التفسير); IBNJAWZI-ZAD; RAZI-FULL(رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم); BAYDAWI-FULL; NASAFI-FULL; ABUHAYYAN-FULL; DURR-FULL; BURSEVI; ALUSI-FULL; IBNASHUR-FULL; STUDYQURAN(R, Ṭ)]
- 28:27/q27/p2 General righteousness, including good dealings. — Kashshaf, Razi and Nasafi allow this broader sense; Asad notes the literal 'one of the righteous'. [4 notes: KASHSHAF-FULL; RAZI-FULL; NASAFI-FULL; ASAD-NOTES]

## 28:27/q28 Why is the promise qualified by 'if God wills'?  [إِن شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ · theology]
turns on: The sources differ over what is conditioned.
- 28:27/q28/p1 For blessing and reliance on divine help, without doubting his intention. — Kashshaf, Razi, Nasafi, Bursevi and Alusi say this; Alusi compares al-Shafii's 'I am a believer, God willing'. [7 notes: KASHSHAF-FULL; RAZI-FULL; NASAFI-FULL; BIQAI-FULL; BURSEVI; ALUSI-FULL(بعضهم)]
- 28:27/q28/p2 The speaker might die before fulfilling his promise. — Tabrisi makes uncertain survival the point. [1 notes: TABRISI]
- 28:27/q28/p3 The qualification concerns Moses finding him righteous, not whether he is righteous. — Tabatabai assigns this grammatical scope. [1 notes: TABATABAI]

## 28:27/q29 How are the opening hamzas read?  [أُنكِحَكَ إِحْدَى · readings]
turns on: The hamza at the word boundary differs.
- 28:27/q29/p1 The standard reading retains the iḥdā hamza, with ordinary waṣl elision and nūn ḍamma. — The Qiraat-ER segments give standard Hafs forms. [3 notes: QIRAAT-ER(hmz)]
- 28:27/q29/p2 Warsh and Ahmad ibn Musa from Abu Amr omit the hamza across unkiḥaka iḥdā. — Abu Hayyan, Samin and Alusi report it; Samin compares Ibn Muhaysin at 28:25. [3 notes: ABUHAYYAN-FULL; SAMIN-DURR(أبو عمرو); ALUSI-FULL(ورش وأحمد بن موسى عن أبي عمرو)]

## 28:27/q30 How is the nūn of hātayn read?  [هَٰتَيْنِ · readings]
turns on: The nūn is light or doubled.
- 28:27/q30/p1 A single nūn is standard. — Qiraat-ER gives the standard form. [1 notes: QIRAAT-ER(Gm)]
- 28:27/q30/p2 A variant geminates the nūn. — Qiraat-ER and al-Nashr report it; Ibn Kathir geminates five analogous forms, with Abu Amr and Ruways agreeing in one. [2 notes: QIRAAT-ER(Gm); NASHR(ابن كثير)]

## 28:27/q31 How are the possessive yāʾs read?  [إِنِّىٓ سَتَجِدُنِىٓ · readings]
turns on: Transmission and morphology govern the vocalization.
- 28:27/q31/p1 The two Medinan readers open both yāʾs. — Ibn Mujahid says Nafi opens nearly all twelve listed cases; al-Nashr specifies both phrases. [2 notes: IBNMUJAHID(نافع); NASHR(المدنيان)]
- 28:27/q31/p2 Ibn Kathir and Abu Amr leave these two yāʾs quiescent. — Ibn Mujahid lists them among three exceptions to nine opened cases. [1 notes: IBNMUJAHID(ابن كثير وأبو عمرو)]
- 28:27/q31/p3 Opening is obligatory in certain noun forms, otherwise reading transmission permits opening or quiescence. — Samarrai states the general rule. [1 notes: SAMARRAI-LAMASAT-HALAQAT]

## 28:27/q32 Does the father act as marriage guardian, and may he offer a daughter?  [أَنْ أُنكِحَكَ إِحْدَى ٱبْنَتَىَّ · law]
turns on: Whether the narrative establishes a mandatory guardian.
- 28:27/q32/p1 A guardian contracts marriage and may offer his daughter to a worthy man. — Qurtubi compares Umar offering Hafsa to Uthman and a woman offering herself to the Prophet; major jurists and Abu Hayyan read a guardian role. [5 notes: QURTUBI-FULL(ابن عمر); ABUHAYYAN-FULL]
- 28:27/q32/p2 An adult competent woman may contract her own marriage in some cases. — Abu Hanifa disputes an absolute guardian requirement. [2 notes: QURTUBI-FULL(أبو حنيفة); ABUHAYYAN-FULL]

## 28:27/q33 May a father marry an adult virgin without asking her?  [ٱبْنَتَىَّ · law]
turns on: Whether narrative silence proves absence of consent.
- 28:27/q33/p1 Yes, without prior consultation. — Malik and Shafii use the offer; Qurtubi calls Malik's argument strong but notes reliance on Israelite reports. [5 notes: QURTUBI-FULL(الشافعي,مالك); ABUHAYYAN-FULL; IBNASHUR-FULL(مالك)]
- 28:27/q33/p2 An adult woman's consent is required; silence here does not show it was absent. — Abu Hanifa distinguishes minors; Ibn Ashur records opponents' reply that permission may have occurred unmentioned. [3 notes: QURTUBI-FULL(أبو حنيفة); ABUHAYYAN-FULL; IBNASHUR-FULL(من يمنع ذلك)]

## 28:27/q34 Which formula can validly contract marriage?  [أُنكِحَكَ · law]
turns on: Whether specific Quranic marriage verbs are necessary.
- 28:27/q34/p1 Only nikah or tazwij wording. — Shafii's followers, Rabiʿa and others argue so; Abu Umar requires explicit speech before witnesses, rejecting divorce analogy. Qurtubi says the verse as earlier law does not prove it. [6 notes: QURTUBI-FULL(أبو عمر,الشافعي,ربيعة); ABUHAYYAN-FULL]
- 28:27/q34/p2 Clear permanent-transfer or gift wording can suffice. — The well-known Maliki position is broad; Abu Hanifa's circle allows gift before witnesses; Ibn al-Qasim accepts an intended gift as marriage. [3 notes: QURTUBI-FULL(أبو حنيفة,ابن القاسم)]

## 28:27/q35 What other contract rulings are drawn from the episode?  [عَلَىٰٓ أَن تَأْجُرَنِى عَشْرًۭا · law]
turns on: These are distinct analogies and adjacent legal deductions.
- 28:27/q35/p1 An addition to agreed consideration can be valid. — Jurists cite the two optional years; Razi cautions that an earlier law does not bind. [2 notes: RAZI-FULL(الفقهاء)]
- 28:27/q35/p2 A sale of one of two slaves for one price can be valid. — Hanafi jurists use the unnamed daughter analogy. [1 notes: IBNKATHIR-FULL(أصحاب أبي حنيفة)]
- 28:27/q35/p3 Alternative cash and deferred prices can be valid. — Al-Awzai invokes the verse and two-sales hadith; Ibn Kathir questions the inference. [2 notes: IBNKATHIR-FULL(الأوزاعي)]
- 28:27/q35/p4 Hiring for food and clothing is valid. — Ahmad's followers infer it from Moses's employment. [1 notes: IBNKATHIR-FULL(أصحاب الإمام أحمد)]
- 28:27/q35/p5 Permissible poetry teaching may be dowry; obscene poetry may not. — Al-Muzani cites a pious poem; Qurtubi compares obscene content to wine or pork. [2 notes: QURTUBI-FULL(المزني)]
- 28:27/q35/p6 Marriage compatibility is legally relevant, though its criteria differ. — Qurtubi lists religion, wealth and lineage disputes and permits non-Arab clients to marry Arab or Qurayshi women. [2 notes: QURTUBI-FULL]

## 28:27/q36 How is Moses's staff said to have come to him and acted?  [* · reports]
turns on: Reports differ on staff origin and miracles.
- 28:27/q36/p1 Gabriel preserved Adam's paradisal staff and handed it to Moses. — Ikrima reports the heavenly origin. [1 notes: THALABI(عكرمة)]
- 28:27/q36/p2 Prophets inherited the staff until Shuayb gave it to Moses. — Other authorities and al-Qushayri connect it to Adam. [2 notes: THALABI(آخرون); QURTUBI-FULL(القشيري)]
- 28:27/q36/p3 An angel entrusted it to Shuayb; Moses repeatedly selected and alone lifted it. — Al-Suddi describes the daughter fetching only this staff and an angel arbitrating ownership. [2 notes: THALABI(السدي)]
- 28:27/q36/p4 Yathrun's thirteen staffs killed eleven sons sent to retrieve one, but Moses took his. — Ibn Abbas says Yathrun recognized Moses as a prophet. [1 notes: THALABI(ابن عباس)]
- 28:27/q36/p5 The staff killed a dragon and returned bloody. — Thalabi says Moses saw a divine sign and Shuayb found the sheep healthier; al-Qushayri relates the dragon too. [3 notes: THALABI; QURTUBI-FULL(القشيري)]
- 28:27/q36/p6 God gave Shuayb Moses as servant after Shuayb wept blind from love of God. — Bursevi transmits the account. [1 notes: BURSEVI]

## 28:27/q37 How does this episode connect to the larger narrative?  [* · links]
turns on: Sources relate the verse to prayer, repetition, ethics and sura setting.
- 28:27/q37/p1 It answers Moses's prayers for safety, guidance and provision. — Tabatabai links the Midian road, work, wife and ten years' livelihood to 28:21. [1 notes: TABATABAI]
- 28:27/q37/p2 Repeated Moses stories vary in wording and order to convey distinct nuances. — Burhan contrasts the dispersed Moses account with Joseph's collected story. [1 notes: BURHAN]
- 28:27/q37/p3 It displays virtues preparing Moses for revelation and models character. — Ibn Ashur names generosity, aid, modesty, gratitude, chastity and fidelity; Shuayb shows hospitality and gentleness; Meccans could compare Muhammad. [2 notes: IBNASHUR-FULL]
- 28:27/q37/p4 The offer illustrates a natural wish for increase. — Qurtubi compares Sa'd, Joseph's brothers at 12:65 and Job. [1 notes: QURTUBI-FULL]
- 28:27/q37/p5 It follows the daughter's suggestion in 28:25. — Kuranyolu connects the agreement to her advice. [1 notes: KURANYOLU-TEFSIR]
- 28:27/q37/p6 Al-Qasas is wholly Meccan. — Ibn Jawzi transmits al-Hasan, Ata and Ikrima on the sura's revelation. [1 notes: IBNJAWZI-ZAD(الحسن وعطاء وعكرمة)]

## 28:27/q38 What inward reading is given to marriage and years of service?  [* · inward]
turns on: People and years are mapped onto spiritual faculties and stages.
- 28:27/q38/p1 Marriage brings the daughter under the heart's command for holy light and unveiling. — Qashani gives this inward mapping. [1 notes: QASHANI]
- 28:27/q38/p2 Eight years symbolize eight stages of annihilation and witnessing; ten add annihilation in Essence and subsistence. — Qashani cites 7:143 and Abraham's ten perfections at 2:124; Bursevi reports completion at eight and fuller perfection at ten, linking Moses's age thirty and later call. [4 notes: QASHANI; BURSEVI(بعض العرفاء)]
- 28:27/q38/p3 Serving Shuayb images the seeker leaving the commanding soul for the heart and serving a guide. — Bursevi cites Ibrahim ibn Adham's seventeen years of wood-carrying and a Hafiz couplet on devoted service. [4 notes: BURSEVI]
- 28:27/q38/p4 Fidelity in service follows one beyond death. — Bursevi cites the Mathnawi. [2 notes: BURSEVI(أبو الليث)]

## 28:27/q39 What incidental grammatical and scholarly examples appear with these notes?  [* · grammar]
turns on: These retrieved passages are contextual rather than explanations of the verse.
- 28:27/q39/p1 Thamānī is among examples of final-yāʾ treatment before a nisba suffix. — Sibawayh compares nājī, adlī and ṣaḥārī; Khalil and Yunus discuss optional fatḥ and poetic ḥānawī. [4 notes: SIBAWAYH(الخليل)]
- 28:27/q39/p2 Exclamations can take accusative specification and emphatic min. — Sibawayh discusses wayḥahu rajulan, Abbas ibn Mirdas and al-Asha. [3 notes: SIBAWAYH]
- 28:27/q39/p3 Bint al-Shati's preface records scholarly collaborators and gratitude. — It names al-Kannuni, al-Madghari, al-Rawandi and Suhayr's two theses and quotes 35:10. [6 notes: BINTSHATI]
- 28:27/q39/p4 Her iʿjāz study compares Itqan questions with earlier works and discusses poetry as Arabic evidence. — She finds parallels without proving dependence; Abd al-Baqi compiles Ibn Abbas answers and later writers follow Anbari. ## 28:27/q40 What grammatical role does 'an ashuq' have?  [أُرِيدُ أَنْ أَشُقَّ · grammar]
- 28:27/q40/p1 The infinitive clause is the object governed by 'I intend'. — Samin al-Halabi states this iʿrāb. [1 notes: SAMIN-DURR]

## 28:27/q41 Why is the father's statement of his own righteousness acceptable?  [سَتَجِدُنِىٓ إِن شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ · rhetoric]
- 28:27/q41/p1 Self-description for a practical or religious purpose is permissible; only boasting is blameworthy. — Ibn Ashur compares Joseph's request at 12:55 and says Shuayb was showing Moses his character. [1 notes: IBNASHUR-FULL]

## 28:27/q43 What is reported of Moses's child from this marriage?  [* · reports]
