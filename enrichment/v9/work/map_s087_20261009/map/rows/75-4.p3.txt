[TABATABAI:75:1-4/r1] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | holds | The surah announces the Great Resurrection, describes its signs and human fate, says the journey toward it begins at death, and closes by arguing for resurrection from God's power to originate life; its context marks it Meccan.
[TABATABAI:75:1-4/r10] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | holds | Bala means that God will gather the bones; qadirin is a circumstantial accusative attached to the implied subject of bala.
[TABATABAI:75:1-4/r11] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | holds | Banan means the fingertips; another reported definition is the fingers themselves.
[TABATABAI:75:1-4/r12] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | holds | To make the fingertips even means to recreate their original form, including their remarkable images and structure.
[TABATABAI:75:1-4/r13] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | holds | The fingertips are singled out for their intricate forms, structure, and number, which enable countless precise grasping, movements, crafts, and human skills; their markings also reveal new secrets.
[TABATABAI:75:1-4/r14] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · قيل | reports | An alternative says God could make fingers and toes undivided, like a camel's sole or donkey's hoof, depriving people of the many acts enabled by separate digits.
[TABATABAI:75:1-4/r15] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | prefers | He prefers the preceding interpretation of recreating the original, distinct fingertips over making the fingers an undivided surface.
[BINTSHATI-IJAZ:p565/r1] BINTSHATI-IJAZ, d. 1419 · ابن عباس | reports | Ibn Abbas glosses banan as the fingertips and cites Antara's line about warriors holding reins by their fingers.
[BINTSHATI-IJAZ:p565/r2] BINTSHATI-IJAZ, d. 1419 · author | holds | Banan is the plural or collective form related to banana and denotes fingers or their tips; the lexicon notes it can mean a single finger.

[BINTSHATI-IJAZ:p566/r2] BINTSHATI-IJAZ, d. 1419 · الزمخشري | reports | Al-Zamakhshari says banān are the fingers as the body’s extremities and last parts to form, or that God can restore their finely detailed joints exactly as before.
[BINTSHATI-IJAZ:p566/r3] BINTSHATI-IJAZ, d. 1419 · author | reports | Another reported reading is that God can gather human bones yet make fingers and toes into a single hoof-like piece, preventing ordinary dexterity.
[BINTSHATI-IJAZ:p566/r4] BINTSHATI-IJAZ, d. 1419 · أبو حيان | rejects | Abu Hayyan calls al-Zamakhshari’s wording affected; he reads the verse as restoring the fingers, among the most finely divided bones, at resurrection.
[BINTSHATI-IJAZ:p566/r5] BINTSHATI-IJAZ, d. 1419 · ابن عباس | reports | Ibn Abbas and most exegetes say the fingers could be made into one lump or bone during life, diminishing their usefulness; the author says the resurrection reading is the clear intent.
