[RAZI-FULL:v15p84#2/r4] RAZI-FULL, d. 606 · author | prefers | Rāzī's fifth view addresses disbelievers at the start of revelation; applying the verse to followers behind an imam would sever its link to the preceding discussion of demands for signs and the Qur'an's sufficiency.

[RAZI-FULL:v15p84/r1] RAZI-FULL, d. 606 · author | holds | Since idhā does not entail repetition, one occasion of Qur'an recitation fulfills the command; the sermon reading suffices, leaving no further indication in the wording.
[RAZI-FULL:v15p84/r2] RAZI-FULL, d. 606 · author | reports | On al-Shāfiʿī's position the imam pauses so the follower may recite al-Fātiḥa; Abū Salama says the imam has two pauses in which one may seize an opportunity to recite.
