[NAHHAS:v1p457/r3] NAHHAS, d. 338 · author | holds | Junuban is coordinated to the syntactic position, meaning do not approach prayer while in major ritual impurity; ʿābirī sabīl is circumstantial accusative, like “do not come except riding.”
