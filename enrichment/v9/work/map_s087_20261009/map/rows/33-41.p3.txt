[STUDYQURAN:33:41/r2] STUDYQURAN · the Prophet | reports | The Prophet counseled a man to keep his tongue always moist with God's remembrance.
[STUDYQURAN:33:41/r3] STUDYQURAN · the Prophet | reports | A report says the Prophet urged remembrance until people call one mad.
[STUDYQURAN:33:41/r4] STUDYQURAN · Ibn ʿAbbās | reports | Ibn ʿAbbās says duties have known limits and excuses except remembrance, which has no limits or excuse unless someone is oppressed and forced to neglect it.
[STUDYQURAN:33:41/r5] STUDYQURAN · Q | reports | Frequent remembrance is said to arise from sincerity of heart, while little remembrance is likened to hypocritical remembrance on the tongue alone.
[STUDYQURAN:33:41/r6] STUDYQURAN · Ibn ʿAjībah | reports | Ibn ʿAjībah says the verse means remembrance of the heart, which alone does not cease.
[STUDYQURAN:33:41/r7] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | Dhikr is considered the quintessential prayer and the core of every act of worship.
[STUDYQURAN:33:41/r8] STUDYQURAN · the Prophet | reports | A report says those who remember God most have the greatest reward among strivers, the righteous, and worshippers in prayer, alms, pilgrimage, and charity; Abū Bakr tells ʿUmar they acquired every good, and the Prophet agrees.
