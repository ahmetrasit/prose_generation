[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v2p626/r2] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · author | rejects | Abu Hayyan rejects the proposed objections to Abraham’s kingship: spiritual authority as prophethood can coexist with Nimrod’s worldly power, wealth, and coercive rule.

[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v2p626/r5] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · author | holds | Abraham invokes life and death because they are among God’s most original, renowned signs of power; the “when” clause grammatically attaches to “disputed.”
