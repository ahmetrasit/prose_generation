[QASHANI:18:21-22#2/r6] QASHANI, d. 736 · author | holds | If sound, the report maps the king to the commanding self; the right-hand advisers to the two intellects and thought; the left-hand advisers to imagination, estimation, and memory; and the shepherd to common sense over the senses.
[QASHANI:18:21-22#2/r7] QASHANI, d. 736 · author | holds | On this reading, the three-count includes the heart and two intellects; the five-count adds thought and estimation but omits common sense and memory as passive repositories.
