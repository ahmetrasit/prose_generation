[ASAD-NOTES:23:39/r2] ASAD-NOTES, d. 1412 · author | holds | A just and unavoidable blast overtakes the evildoers, leaving them like flotsam and scum on a torrent.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:23:31/r1] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · Eski müfessirlerin bir kısmı | reports | Some early commentators identify the unnamed generation as Thamūd and its prophet as Ṣāliḥ.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:23:31/r2] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · çoğunluk | reports | Most commentators identify the unnamed generation as ʿĀd and its prophet as Hūd.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:23:31/r3] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · Muhammed Esed | reports | Esed reads the passage as a general reference to recurring patterns in the lives and experiences of all God’s prophets, without naming a particular prophet or people.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:23:31/r4] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | prefers | The author agrees with Esed that the passage describes a common prophetic experience rather than one specific prophet and people.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:23:31/r7] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | Since the Qur’an answers these denials of the prophet’s person and of the afterlife elsewhere, this passage only warns of the rejecters’ painful fate.
[SAMARRAI-ASRAR:r7#4/r8] SAMARRAI-ASRAR · author | holds | The definite “the people” in 23:41 identifies a particular people, Ṣāliḥ’s nation, confirmed by their destruction; the indefinite “a people” in 23:44 is general, since successive generations and messengers are described. | mentions 23:42, 23:43
[STUDYQURAN:23:41/r1] STUDYQURAN · R | reports | One interpretation identifies the Cry as Gabriel’s literal shout, which causes death itself.
[STUDYQURAN:23:41/r2] STUDYQURAN · R | reports | Another interpretation takes the Cry to be a great quaking.
[STUDYQURAN:23:41/r3] STUDYQURAN · R | reports | A further interpretation takes the Cry to mean death and punishment themselves.
[STUDYQURAN:23:41/r4] STUDYQURAN · R | reports | “Rightly” indicates that the punishment was just.
[STUDYQURAN:23:41/r5] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | Dross is usually understood as leaves or grass borne on a torrent of water; see 87:5. | mentions 87:5
[STUDYQURAN:23:intro/r4] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | After recalling worldly blessings, the surah tells of Noah's struggle against his people's denial and rebuke and his journey in the ark.
[STUDYQURAN:23:intro/r7] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | The opposition of affluent and powerful people to prophets recurs throughout this surah, as elsewhere in the Quran. | mentions 7:59, 7:60, 7:61, 7:62, 7:136
