[SAMIN-DURR:2:106#4/r1] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | reports | Unhamzated forms may derive from forgetting and mean not remembering or leaving; alternatively, they derive from نسء with hamza replaced by alif, uniting the readings.

[SAMIN-DURR:2:106#4/r5] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · الزجاج | rejects | Al-Zajjāj rejects abandonment and forgetting because the Prophet did not forget Qurʾān; he cites the promise not to take away revelation. | mentions 17:86

[SAMIN-DURR:2:106#4/r9] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | On the comparative analysis, مثلها matches its predecessor in duty and reward.
[SAMIN-DURR:2:106#5/r1] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | reports | On the alternative, خير is a good or blessing, with من marking source and qualifying a good from the āya, as in the preceding verse. | mentions 2:105
[SAMIN-DURR:2:106#5/r2] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | rejects | Al-Samīn calls the alternative remote because مثلها does not naturally coordinate with بخير on that meaning; attachment to منها without repeating the preposition is Kufan-only.
[SAMIN-DURR:2:106#5/r3] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | ما ننسخ shifts from third-person reference to first-person speech after والله يختص and والله ذو الفضل. | mentions 2:105
[SAMIN-DURR:2:106#5/r4] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | نسخ may mean removal without a succeeding replacement, as wind removes a trace, or transfer without removal, as copying a book.
[SAMIN-DURR:2:106#5/r5] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · unspecified | reports | A further account divides removal into removal with replacement, like sun and shade, or without substitute, like wind and trace; it cites removing what Satan casts. | mentions 22:52
[SAMIN-DURR:2:106#5/r6] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | النسيئة means postponement and can also mean carrying through or confirming; the poem uses نسأتها.

[SAMIN-DURR:2:106/r10] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · ابن عطية | reports | Ibn ʿAṭiyya further derives the reading from copying a book without removing it, meaning write and send from the Preserved Tablet or delay and leave an āya from revelation.
