[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:69:13/r8] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · Müfessirler | reports | The Qurʾan does not explain the eight who carry the Throne; commentators infer from context that they are eight angels or carriers called the Throne-bearers, while others suggest eight persons, eight ranks, or eighty angels.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:69:13/r9] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | prefers | The author’s translation prefers the interpretation that the eight are angels; al-Razi instead prefers eight persons.
[QIRAAT-ER:69:18:4#1/r1] QIRAAT-ER · Imp | reports | The Imp form yakhfī is masculine singular instead of feminine takhfā; it can make Allāh the implicit subject: He will hide no secret from you.
[STUDYQURAN:69:18/r1] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | Tuʿraḍūn can mean either that people are exposed or brought forth, as in the verse about being brought before their Lord. | mentions 11:18
[STUDYQURAN:69:18/r2] STUDYQURAN · IK, Q, R, Ṭ | reports | A hadith describes three arraignments on Resurrection Day: people argue and make excuses at the first two; at the third, scrolls are handed out, with some receiving them in the right hand and others in the left.
[STUDYQURAN:69:18/r3] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | The phrase may mean nothing is hidden from God, as another verse says of the Day people come forth. | mentions 40:16
[STUDYQURAN:69:18/r4] STUDYQURAN · R | reports | R says the inner states and thoughts of people will be exposed, making believers rejoice and disbelievers grieve.
[STUDYQURAN:69:18/r5] STUDYQURAN · Q | reports | Q offers the translation “None among you shall be hidden,” meaning that no person can escape God's Judgment.
