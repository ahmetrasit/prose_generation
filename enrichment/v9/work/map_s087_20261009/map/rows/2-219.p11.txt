[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v1p293/r16] IBNATIYYA-FULL, d. 542 · author | holds | Arabs used this practice in winter scarcity and severe cold: participants guaranteed the camel’s price, slaughtered it, and divided it into ten portions; al-Aṣmaʿī’s claim of twenty-eight portions is mistaken.
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v1p293/r17] IBNATIYYA-FULL, d. 542 · author | holds | A winning arrow took its allotted shares for the poor; sometimes participants gambled for themselves, and those who lost paid the price. The practice sustained the neighborhood’s poor.
