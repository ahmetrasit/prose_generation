[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT:p148/r2] SAMARRAI-LAMASAT · السدي | reports | Al-Suddi identifies all rights of the religious law as trusts that believers accept and undertake to fulfill through faith.
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT:p148/r3] SAMARRAI-LAMASAT · قيل | reports | Another view treats the body and other gifts God gives a person as trusts, betrayed when used outside their intended and permitted purpose.
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT:p148/r4] SAMARRAI-LAMASAT · author | holds | Trusts are plural because they are numerous and varied, while the covenant is singular; trusts precede it because of their importance and breadth.
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT:p148/r5] SAMARRAI-LAMASAT · رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | The Prophet is quoted saying faith is a trust and that a person without trust has no religion or faith.
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT:p148/r6] SAMARRAI-LAMASAT · فتح القدير | reports | Al-Fath al-Qadir states that trust is broader than covenant and every covenant is a trust.
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT:p148/r7] SAMARRAI-LAMASAT · author | holds | رَاعُونَ is chosen over حفظ because رعى originally means tending livestock and includes keeping, managing, and checking on what is entrusted.
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT:p148/r8] SAMARRAI-LAMASAT · الكشاف | reports | Al-Kashshaf defines a guardian as one who cares for and improves what is entrusted, such as a shepherd or governor.
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT:p150/r1] SAMARRAI-LAMASAT · author | holds | Putting trusts and covenants before their guardian emphasizes their importance and that they are especially worthy of care.
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT:p150/r2] SAMARRAI-LAMASAT · author | holds | The added lām emphasizes exclusive association and lets راعون mean that they are the owners and stewards of the trusts and covenants.
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT:p150/r3] SAMARRAI-LAMASAT · author | holds | Since راعي can mean an owner or person managing something, the phrasing means they are responsible holders of trusts and covenants; يرعون would not convey that nuance.
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT:p166/r5] SAMARRAI-LAMASAT · author | holds | He notes that both sūrahs repeat the rules on guarding chastity, its stated exceptions, and faithfully keeping trusts and covenants.
