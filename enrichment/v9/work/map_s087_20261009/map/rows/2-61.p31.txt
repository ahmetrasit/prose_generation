[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:2:61/r15] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · İslâm bilginleri | reports | Scholars identify five defining prophetic traits: truthfulness, trustworthiness, sinlessness, keen judgment, and complete delivery of the divine message.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:2:61/r16] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | Prophets faithfully convey the truth; rare minor slips, called zalla, are corrected by revelation, and their intelligence enables them to understand and explain divine teaching accurately.
