[QURTUBI-FULL:20:14/r3] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · أبو هريرة | reports | Al-Dāraquṭnī reports from Abū Hurayra that the Prophet said a forgotten prayer’s time is when it is remembered.
[QURTUBI-FULL:20:14/r4] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · author | holds | The report makes makeup obligatory for a sleeper or forgetful person, whether one prayer or many; this is the majority view, while an anomalous view excuses makeup beyond five prayers, contrary to the hadith.
[QURTUBI-FULL:20:14/r5] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · author | holds | God commands prayer at specified times; doing at night what was commanded for day, or vice versa, fails to fulfill the command and earns no reward, so makeup rests on a renewed Prophetic command rather than the original one. | mentions 17:78
