[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-5-english:p243/r1] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · author | holds | A person's success before God depends on faith rooted deeply in the heart; outward profession alone carries no weight.
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-5-english:p243/r11] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · author | holds | The next sūrah, al-Nūr, supplements this one: faith illuminating the inner self brightens individual and collective life, while its absence darkens both.

[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:23:1/r1] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | The first eleven verses summarize essential worship and moral principles and set out the life expected of anyone professing faith or Islam.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:23:1/r2] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | The success promised here primarily means salvation and well-being in the afterlife, given the sūra’s later rejection of afterlife-deniers.

[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:23:1/r4] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | Right belief and good conduct also provide a recipe for happiness and well-being in this world, as transmitted evidence and human experience show.
