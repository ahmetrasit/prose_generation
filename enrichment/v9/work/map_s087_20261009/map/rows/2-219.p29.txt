[ELMALILI:2:219-220/r3] ELMALILI, d. 1361 · author | holds | Hamr originally means covering; specifically it names fermented grape juice after its foam subsides because it covers the intellect or clouds the head.
[ELMALILI:2:219-220/r4] ELMALILI, d. 1361 · author | holds | By extension, hamr can mean any substance that intoxicates, so all intoxicants fall under the word in its broad sense.
