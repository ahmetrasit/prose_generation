[IBNASHUR-FULL:v29p357/r7] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · author | holds | “The journey is to your Lord” explains the warning and brings near a refutation of the deniers’ question about when Resurrection will come (75:6). | mentions 75:6

[IBNASHUR-FULL:v29p358/r1] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · author | holds | Tarāqī is the plural of tarquwa, the hollow at the base of the neck; every person has two, one on each side.
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v29p358/r2] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · author | holds | The plural is used for the dual to lighten the expression and avoid the heaviness of the dual form; 66:4 similarly uses “your hearts.” | mentions 66:4
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v29p358/r3] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · author | holds | The soul has reached the throat, where its final breaths emerge: the last stage of dying, as in 56:83. | mentions 56:83
