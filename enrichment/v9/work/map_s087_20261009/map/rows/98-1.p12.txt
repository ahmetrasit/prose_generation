[IBNASHUR:98:1/r34] IBNASHUR, d. 1393 · author | holds | A close parallel is 9:64: hypocrites were described as fearing exposure, although their apparent fear was pretense, and then told to mock because God would reveal what they hid. | mentions 9:64

[IBNASHUR:98:1/r38] IBNASHUR, d. 1393 · author | holds | Another parallel is 2:89: the People of the Book had sought victory over the disbelievers before the confirming Book came, but rejected what they recognized when it arrived. | mentions 2:89
[IBNASHUR:98:1/r39] IBNASHUR, d. 1393 · author | holds | The intended force is: you used to claim you would not abandon your religion until the promised sign came.
