[RAZI:89:23/r1] RAZI, d. 606 · جماعة من المفسرين | reports | A group says Hell will be brought at Resurrection bound with seventy thousand reins, each pulled by seventy thousand angels; it will strain as if to escape and would burn the gathering if released.
[RAZI:89:23/r10] RAZI, d. 606 · author | holds | His argument is that people recognize their worldly choices were wrong and regret them, thereby repenting, yet the verse denies that this repentance benefits them.
[RAZI:89:23/r11] RAZI, d. 606 · author | rejects | An objection says their regret was only fear of punishment, not regret for wrongdoing, so it was not valid repentance; the author replies that useful regret must be for the wrongness itself, making it valid repentance despite nonacceptance.
