[STUDYQURAN:80:1/r2] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | The Prophet could not make people accept religion; his role was to convey God's message and guide those who accepted it. | mentions 28:56
[STUDYQURAN:80:1/r3] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | Other verses instruct the Prophet to be patient and not turn away from people seeking religion. | mentions 6:52, 18:28
[STUDYQURAN:80:intro/r1] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | This is an early Makkan surah, also called al-Ṣākhkhah after verse 33 and al-Safarah after verse 15.
[STUDYQURAN:80:intro/r2] STUDYQURAN · some | reports | Some connect its opening to 79:45: the Prophet should not focus on indifferent hearers while seekers await guidance. | mentions 79:45
[STUDYQURAN:80:intro/r3] STUDYQURAN · ʿĀʾishah | reports | ʿĀʾishah reports that the passage concerns blind ʿAbd Allāh ibn Umm Maktūm, who repeatedly sought guidance while the Prophet addressed Makkan leaders; the Prophet turned away, and the verses rebuked him.
[STUDYQURAN:80:intro/r4] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | Ibn Umm Maktūm was among Islam's first converts and devoted to the Prophet and Quran; afterward the Prophet welcomed him as the one for whom his Lord rebuked him.
[STUDYQURAN:80:intro/r5] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | Ibn Umm Maktūm was among the first two migrants to Madinah, taught Quran, and shared the call to prayer with Bilāl.
[STUDYQURAN:80:intro/r6] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | The Prophet sometimes left Madinah in Ibn Umm Maktūm's care; 4:95 is also said to concern his blindness and exemption from campaigns. | mentions 4:95
[STUDYQURAN:80:intro/r7] STUDYQURAN · author | reports | Though blind and excused from expeditions, Ibn Umm Maktūm insisted on a battlefield role; after Qādisiyyah he was found dead holding the Muslim standard.
