[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p399/r2] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · author | holds | Even if the opponents witnessed the greatest signs and miracles, they would invent excuses to conceal their haughtiness.

[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p402/r4] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · author | holds | Min is partitive: the verse targets only the hostile subset, since both groups included Muslims, impartial people, and balanced non-Muslims whom the Qur’an praised.
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p402/r5] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · author | holds | Bayyinah means a manifest sign, specifically an undeniable miracle demanded by stubborn opponents, a demand the Qur’an mentions elsewhere.
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p403/r1] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · author | holds | The People of the Book demanded a heavenly book and had earlier demanded that Moses show them God; the idolaters likewise wanted individual open pages, as cited in 4:153 and 74:52. | mentions 4:153, 74:52
