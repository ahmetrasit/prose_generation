[ALUSI-FULL:v29p105#2/r2] ALUSI-FULL, d. 1270 · author | holds | The verse gives the external motive for night prayer, following the account of its internal motive.

[ALUSI-FULL:v29p105#2/r6] ALUSI-FULL, d. 1270 · author | reports | On the free-time reading, the verse either completes the rationale for night worship because daytime allows rest, or urges preserving the vigil since it must be made up by day.
