<<9:102 notes, part 7 of 0..7>>
 Reports differ in details but agree that the verse concerns people who could have joined Tabuk, stayed behind, then sincerely regretted it and confessed without inventing excuses.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:9:102/r5] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | reports | After hearing verses about those who stayed behind, the repentant men felt such remorse that they tied themselves to the mosque pillars and vowed not to leave until the Prophet released them.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:9:102/r6] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | reports | When the Prophet returned, he first went to the mosque; after hearing why they were bound, he vowed to await revelation before freeing or pardoning them, then the verse came and he released and accepted their apology.
[STUDYQURAN:9:102/r1] STUDYQURAN · the most common account | reports | The verse concerns men who stayed behind from Tabūk, repented, tied themselves to mosque walls, and vowed to abstain from food and drink until pardoned.
[STUDYQURAN:9:102/r2] STUDYQURAN · some | reports | Some take the righteous deeds mixed with sins to include the men's earlier participation in battles alongside the Prophet.
[STUDYQURAN:9:102/r3] STUDYQURAN · some | reports | Some understand God's expression may He relent as conveying necessity because it comes from God.
