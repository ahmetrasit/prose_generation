[RAZI-FULL:v23p108#2/r5] RAZI-FULL, d. 606 · author | holds | People seek knowledge rather than ignorance; since even unbelievers intend knowledge but may become ignorant, God creates motives toward good or evil, producing felicity or wretchedness.
