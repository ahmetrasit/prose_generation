<<91:9 notes, part 6 of 0..6>>
ome early commentators make God the subject: the soul God purifies prospers, and the soul He obscures fails.
[STUDYQURAN:91:9/r5] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | The subject's ambiguity can express cooperation between God and people: people purify themselves for their own souls while willing only as God wills; God purifies whom He will, yet changes a people when they change themselves. | mentions 35:18, 76:30, 4:49, 24:21, 13:11
[STUDYQURAN:91:9/r6] STUDYQURAN · Sh, Z | reports | The literal meanings are to grow for purify and to bury for obscure; the verses can be rendered whoever grows the soul prospers, whoever buries it fails.
