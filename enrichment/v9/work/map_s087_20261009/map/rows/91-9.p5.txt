[CORPUSCORANICUM:91:9:anmerkung/r2] CORPUSCORANICUM · author | holds | Arabic zakā originally means ‘grow, flourish’; the Quranic moral sense of purity most likely reflects semantic convergence with Aramaic dḵā, unlike the Arabic root ṭ-h-r for ritual purity.
[CORPUSCORANICUM:91:9:anmerkung/r3] CORPUSCORANICUM · Torrey | reports | Torrey attributes the semantic convergence between Arabic z-k-w/y and its Aramaic homonym to Jewish usage; Andrae adduces Syriac literary parallels.
