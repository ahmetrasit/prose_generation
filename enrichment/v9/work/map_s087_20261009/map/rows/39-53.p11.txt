[STUDYQURAN:39:53/r11] STUDYQURAN · the Prophet | reports | The Prophet says that if people did not sin and seek forgiveness, God would replace them with people who sin, seek forgiveness, and are forgiven.
[STUDYQURAN:39:53/r2] STUDYQURAN · many scholars | reports | Many scholars count this among the Quran's most hopeful verses; the commentary also lists other hopeful verses and says some call these the most joyous. | mentions 4:48, 4:116, 24:22, 33:47, 42:19, 42:22, 93:5
[STUDYQURAN:39:53/r3] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | The address is understood to encompass all humanity.
[STUDYQURAN:39:53/r4] STUDYQURAN · many commentators | reports | Some understand the verse as first addressing Makkans who feared they could not repent and follow the Prophet after idolatry, invoking deities, unlawful killing, or fornication.
[STUDYQURAN:39:53/r5] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | Their prodigality covers every kind of sin, including killing innocent people in the pre-Islamic Age of Ignorance. | mentions 3:154, 5:50, 33:33, 48:26, 10:12
[STUDYQURAN:39:53/r6] STUDYQURAN · other commentators | reports | Another view says it was revealed about Muslims whom the Makkans barred from migrating to Madinah, persecuted into renouncing Islam, and who feared they could not repent.
[STUDYQURAN:39:53/r7] STUDYQURAN · the Prophet | reports | Thawbān reports that the Prophet valued this verse above the whole world and everything in it.
[STUDYQURAN:39:53/r8] STUDYQURAN · the Prophet | reports | When asked whether forgiveness could include someone who associates partners with God, the Prophet answered three times, “And why not?” | mentions 4:48, 4:116
[STUDYQURAN:39:53/r9] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | Forgiveness of idolatry depends on renouncing it and repenting, as 4:48 and 4:116 state that God does not forgive association of partners. | mentions 4:48, 4:116
[STUDYQURAN:39:intro/r11] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | The death passage shows disbelievers’ desperate end; verse 53 then emphasizes that God’s mercy and forgiveness remain available even to those who squandered their souls.
[STUDYQURAN:39:intro/r2] STUDYQURAN · Many commentators | reports | Many commentators hold verses 53–55 to be Madinan and say they were revealed concerning Waḥshī, who won freedom by killing Ḥamzah at Uḥud.
[STUDYQURAN:39:intro/r3] STUDYQURAN · Some commentators | reports | One reported view limits the Waḥshī occasion of revelation to verse 53 alone.
[STUDYQURAN:39:intro/r4] STUDYQURAN · Other commentators | reports | A different reported view says verses 53–59 were all revealed concerning Waḥshī.
[TIRMIDHI:3237/r1] TIRMIDHI · النبي | reports | Asma bint Yazid reports that the Prophet recited the verse, adding “and He does not mind”; Tirmidhi grades it ḥasan gharīb, knows it only through Thabit–Shahr, and identifies Umm Salama al-Ansariyya as Asma.
