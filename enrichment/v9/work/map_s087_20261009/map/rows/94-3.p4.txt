[BINTSHATI:v1p65/r6] BINTSHATI, d. 1419 · أبو حيان | reports | Abu Hayyan interprets the burden figuratively as protection from sins and purification from defilement.
[BINTSHATI:v1p65/r7] BINTSHATI, d. 1419 · الطبري | reports | Al-Tabari interprets the burden as past sins forgiven and the weight of the Prophet's earlier days lifted.
[BINTSHATI:v1p66/r1] BINTSHATI, d. 1419 · قتادة | reports | Qatada says the Prophet had sins that weighed on him, which God forgave.
