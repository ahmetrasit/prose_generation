[MUQATIL:80:1-7/r1] MUQATIL, d. 150 · author | reports | Muqatil identifies the blind man as ʿAbd Allāh ibn Abī Musraḥ al-Aʿmar, also called Umm Maktūm’s son ʿAmr ibn Qays, and gives their lineages. While the blind man sat alone in the Sacred Mosque, two angels descended to pray; he heard one remark that Abū Ṭālib called people to Islam without seeing them. He then sought repentance from Muhammad, who turned back to al-ʿAbbās and Umayya ibn Khalaf; after the man repeated his plea and left despairing, these verses were revealed.
