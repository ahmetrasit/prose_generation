[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p208/r13] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · الكسائي | reports | Al-Kisāʾī derives the shortened form from بيئس: easing the hamza brings two yāʾs together, so one is deleted and the first vowel is kasra.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p208/r14] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · غيره | reports | Other grammarians analyze the word on the pattern fiʿl, with an assimilated initial kasra and later hamza reduction.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p208/r15] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · الحسن ونافع وأبو بكر | reports | Al-Ḥasan reads بئيس with and without hamza; Nāfiʿ and Abū Bakr have the comparable unhamzated form.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p208/r16] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · أبو حاتم | rejects | Abū Ḥātim rejects the unhamzated reading because بيس needs a following noun or predicate, such as بيس الرجل.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p208/r17] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · النحاس | rejects | Al-Naḥḥās answers Abū Ḥātim by citing the established ellipsis in فبها ونعمت, where the omitted noun is understood; here the estimate is بيس العذاب.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p208/r18] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · يعقوب القارئ | reports | Yaʿqūb al-Qāriʾ reports a reading بئس on the pattern of شهد; al-Rāzī attributes it to ʿĪsā ibn ʿUmar.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p208/r6] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · author | holds | The pronoun in نسوا refers to those warned; neglect is called forgetting for emphasis, since leaving something can reach the extreme of forgetting, and ما is relative.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p208/r7] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · ابن عطية | rejects | Ibn ʿAṭiyya proposes that ما may mean the reminder itself or its contents; Abū Ḥayyān says neither possibility is apparent.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p208/r8] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · author | holds | السوء generally means acts of disobedience, but in this story it specifically means fishing for fish on the Sabbath.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v5p208/r9] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · author | holds | Those who were punished are the Sabbath violators; calling them wrongdoers identifies the cause of their seizure.
