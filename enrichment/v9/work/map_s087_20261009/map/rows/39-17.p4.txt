[IBNASHUR-FULL:v23p364/r4] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · author | holds | In Quran and Sunna, ṭāghūt denotes a powerful force in unbelief or oppression: an idol, a group of idols, or a leader of unbelievers such as Kaʿb ibn al-Ashraf; here it means idols collectively.
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v23p364/r5] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · author | holds | The plural ṭawāghīt reflects predominance of its nominal use, though a single idol is an uncommon referent; feminine ‘worship them’ treats it as a nonrational plural.
