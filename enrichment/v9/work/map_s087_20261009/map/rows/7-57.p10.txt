[SAMIN-DURR:7:57#2/r3] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · الأخوان | reports | The two Ḥamzas read نشرا with fatḥa on nūn and a vowelless shīn.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57#2/r4] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | reports | For this reading, نشرا may be a verbal noun used as a circumstantial adjective, meaning spreading, spread, or possessing spreading.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57#2/r5] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | reports | Alternatively, نشرا can be an emphatic verbal noun because sending and reviving are close in meaning, or a shortened form of إنشارا used circumstantially.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57#2/r6] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · عاصم | reports | ʿĀṣim reads بشرا with bāʾ, ḍamma, and a vowelless shīn; it may be plural of بشيرة or of فعيل, from the sense of announcing rain.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57#2/r7] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | The بشرا reading is related to the description مبشرات, meaning that the winds announce rain; the ḍamma was lightened, and Ibn ʿAbbās, al-Sulamī, Ibn Abī ʿAbla, and a transmission from ʿĀṣim retain it.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57#2/r8] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · مسروق | reports | Masrūq reads نشرا with fatḥa on both letters; Abū al-Fatḥ calls it a collective noun like غيب and نشأ, while another analysis makes it a passive verbal noun like قبض.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57#2/r9] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · أبو عبد الرحمن | reports | Abū ʿAbd al-Raḥmān reads بشرا with fatḥa on bāʾ and a vowelless shīn, as a verbal noun of the triliteral verb بشر; this is also reported from ʿĀṣim.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57#3/r1] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | Ibn Kathīr’s singular الريح with plural نشرا treats الريح as a collective noun plural in meaning, like ʿAntara’s line where حلوبة is described by سودًا.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57#3/r2] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | In some readings the circumstantial phrase may describe the subject of يرسل or its object; بين is governed by يرسل or by بشرا in that reading.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57#3/r3] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | حتى إذا أقلت marks the endpoint of يرسل; أقلت means carried, with the sense of bearing something easily.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57#3/r4] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | The word قلة also names a vessel; the jars of Hajar are called قلال because a camel bears them.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57#3/r5] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | السحاب can be masculine or feminine; the masculine pronoun in سقناه follows its grammatical form, while feminine agreement would yield سقناها.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57#3/r6] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · الزمخشري | reports | Al-Zamakhsharī takes the lām in لبلد as causal, meaning for the sake of its land.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57#3/r7] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · الشيخ | reports | The Shaykh distinguishes سقت له مالا, which delivers property to its recipient, from سقت لأجله مالا, which need not deliver it to that person.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57#3/r8] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | The reading ميت versus ميت with different vowel length and doubling was discussed earlier in Āl ʿImrān.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57#3/r9] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · الكرماني | reports | Al-Kirmānī explains that present tense يرسل in al-Aʿrāf and al-Rūm matches surrounding present clauses, while past أرسل in al-Furqān and Fāṭir matches surrounding past clauses. | mentions 30:48, 25:48, 35:9
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57#4/r1] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | In فأنزلنا به, the pronoun most naturally refers to the nearest antecedent, a dead land, so باء is locative: water was sent down in that land.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57#4/r2] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · الشيخ | reports | The Shaykh considers the land as antecedent and the locative reading of باء apparent.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57#4/r3] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | reports | Alternatively, به may refer to the clouds, with باء meaning either مِن, water from the clouds, or causation, water because of the clouds.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57#4/r4] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | rejects | A further proposal refers به to the understood act of driving the clouds, with causal باء; this is weak because a mentioned antecedent is available.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57#4/r5] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | For فأخرجنا به the antecedent debate recurs, but the author adds water as the best antecedent and says it should not be displaced.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57#4/r6] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | من in من كل الثمرات is either partitive or marks the point of origin.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57#4/r7] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | كذلك modifies an omitted verbal noun: the dead are brought out as fruit is brought out.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57/r1] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | reports | Earlier discussion in al-Baqara covered the readers’ differing singular and plural forms of الريح across sūras.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57/r2] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · نافع وأبو عمرو وابن كثير | reports | Nāfiʿ, Abū ʿAmr, and Ibn Kathīr read نشرا with ḍamma on nūn and shīn; several early authorities also read it this way.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57/r3] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | One analysis makes نشرا a rare plural of ناشر, meaning one who spreads or has spreading, or one associated with resurrection, by analogy to forms such as لابن and تامر. | mentions 67:15
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57/r4] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | reports | The first interpretation of ناشر derives it by association with spreading, opposite to folding, or with resurrection; the latter sense is illustrated by 67:15. | mentions 67:15
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57/r5] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | reports | Another interpretation makes ناشر a participle from نشر, the intransitive result of أنشر: God revives the dead and they come alive.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57/r6] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | reports | A third interpretation gives ناشر the meaning منشر, one who revives; the author calls this derivation weak.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57/r7] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | reports | A second analysis makes نشرا the plural of نشور; the author prefers the active sense, since فعول meaning فاعل regularly forms a plural on فعل.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57/r8] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | reports | Alternatively, نشور may be passive, since wind can be described as dead or life-giving; the author says this فعول pattern and its plural are not productive.
[SAMIN-DURR:7:57/r9] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | The author enumerates six possible meanings for the ḍamma reading: four senses of plural ناشر and two senses of plural نشور.
