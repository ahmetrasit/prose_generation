FULL:v2p345#2/r1] IBNATIYYA-FULL, d. 542 · الفراء | reports | Al-Farrāʾ says illā means ‘other than’ and the exception is added punishment God wills; al-Zajjāj inclines to this. Al-Ṭabarī takes it as the interval before entry into Fire, which Ibn ʿAṭiyya says the wording permits.

[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v2p345#2/r3] IBNATIYYA-FULL, d. 542 · author | prefers | Ibn ʿAṭiyya prefers the exception as an address to the Prophet and community, exempting unbelievers whom God knows will later believe; mā can denote rational persons, and Wise/Knowing fits this knowledge and just punishment.
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v2p345/r1] IBNATIYYA-FULL, d. 542 · author | holds | Yawm is governed by implied udhkur or possibly the preceding wali, coordinated with the syntactic position of 6:127's ‘because of what they did.’
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v2p345/r2] IBNATIYYA-FULL, d. 542 · author | holds | The pronoun includes both jinn and human groups, unbelievers under defilement and those granted the Abode of Peace; ‘all together’ confirms the scope.
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v2p345/r3] IBNATIYYA-FULL, d. 542 · حفص عن عاصم | reports | Ḥafṣ from ʿĀṣim reads with yāʾ, others with nūn; both are valid.
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v2p345/r4] IBNATIYYA-FULL, d. 542 · author | holds | An implied verb before ‘O company of jinn’ is estimated as ‘We say.’ Al-ʿAbbās, Mujāhid, and Qatāda say the jinn excessively misled humans.
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v2p345/r5] IBNATIYYA-FULL, d. 542 · author | holds | Human allies speak in excuse, saying both sides benefited. Al-Ṭabarī recounts Arabs seeking a valley jinn's protection; they credited safety to the jinn. Other worldly forms of benefit are possible.
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v2p345/r6] IBNATIYYA-FULL, d. 542 · السدي | reports | Al-Suddī says the appointed term is death; other views say Resurrection or the limit of mutual enjoyment, acknowledging God's decree.
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v2p345/r7] IBNATIYYA-FULL, d. 542 · author | holds | God's report follows their plea; past tense reports certain future action, common and eloquent Qurʾānic usage.
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v2p345/r8] IBNATIYYA-FULL, d. 542 · الزجاج وأبو علي | reports | Al-Zajjāj takes mathwā as place of recompense or abode. Abu ʿAlī takes it as verbal noun because it governs khalidīn, estimating ‘the Fire is the possessor of your abode.’
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v2p345/r9] IBNATIYYA-FULL, d. 542 · فرقة | reports | One group takes mā as man, exempting those who believe in worldly life after unbelief.

[TABRISI:6:128-129#2/r2] TABRISI, d. 548 · محمد بن كعب | reports | Muḥammad ibn Kaʿb says the mutual enjoyment means that each side obeyed and agreed with the other.

[TABRISI:6:128-129#2/r5] TABRISI, d. 548 · قول غير مسمى | reports | Another view takes the appointed term as resurrection and gathering: resurrection is the term for requital, just as death is the term for making up what passed.
[TABRISI:6:128-129#2/r6] TABRISI, d. 548 · الجبائي | reports | Al-Jubbāʾī argues that the verse implies one lifespan: if there were two terms, someone killed before dying naturally would not have reached his term, yet they say they reached it.
