[STUDYQURAN:54:47/r15] STUDYQURAN · most commentators | prefers | Most commentators and all recitation schools read kull shayʾ in the accusative, the translation’s form, though it is grammatically problematic.
[STUDYQURAN:54:47/r16] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | Reading kull shayʾ in the nominative makes the phrase mean that everything God created is according to a measure.
[STUDYQURAN:54:47/r17] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | The nominative reading can leave open whether God created everything, supporting a free-will reading associated with Qadarites and Muʿtazilites.

[STUDYQURAN:54:47/r19] STUDYQURAN · Muʿtazilites | reports | Muʿtazilites hold humans responsible for creating their evil deeds, absolving a just God from creating acts for which He punishes them.
