[IBNASHUR-FULL:v13p240#2/r5] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · author | holds | The wording parallels 39:28, ‘a Qur’an without crookedness’: the negated possessive construction means the thing is not subject to the stated quality. | mentions 39:28
