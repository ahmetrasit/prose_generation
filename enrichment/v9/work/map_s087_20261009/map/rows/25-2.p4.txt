[STUDYQURAN:25:2/r3] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | Measured it out translates qaddara followed by its cognate verbal noun taqdīr.
[STUDYQURAN:25:2/r4] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | Taqdīr includes both knowing a thing's measure and bestowing a measure, with senses of estimation and ordainment.
[STUDYQURAN:25:2/r5] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | The term bears on created order, God's knowledge and power, and thus divine omniscience and omnipotence.
[STUDYQURAN:25:2/r6] STUDYQURAN · al-Rāzī | reports | Al-Rāzī distinguishes human taqdīr as estimation from God's eternal, unchanging knowledge of each creature's whole existence from beginning to end.
[STUDYQURAN:25:2/r7] STUDYQURAN · IK and Q | reports | Other commentators stress taqdīr as ordainment or predestination of all that a creature was, is, or will be; it resembles qadar, one's allotted measure and the doctrine of predestination. | mentions 54:47, 54:48, 54:49, 6:60, 17:13, 17:14, 17:30, 65:3, 68:1, 91:8
