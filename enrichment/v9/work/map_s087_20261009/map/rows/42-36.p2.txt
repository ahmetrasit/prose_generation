[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:42:36/r1] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | The Qur'an aims to transform the Jahiliyya social order and form an exemplary generation by turning faith into action, with principles for duties to God and relations with others.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:42:36/r2] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | People should regard worldly means as gifts rather than products of their own skill, and remember that they are temporary.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:42:36/r3] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | What is with God endures; faith and reliance on God are the path to deserving lasting blessings.
