[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p210#2/r5] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · author | holds | Mā is a temporal adverbial verbal noun, meaning for the duration that the heavens and earth endure.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v6p210#2/r6] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · author | prefers | The duration signifies perpetuity, like Arabic expressions “as long as Thabīr stands” or “as long as a star shines,” without regard to whether either lasts forever.
