[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:20:115/r3] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · Şevkânî | reports | Unlike earlier accounts, this passage says Adam’s repentance was accepted and that he was chosen, meaning commissioned as a prophet.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:20:115/r4] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | The account rejects inherited original sin: despite Adam’s error, people are not born sinful as Christian belief holds, since Adam repented and was forgiven.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:20:115/r5] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | Each person is tested in an environment prone to sin and with capacities usable for good or evil, and can seek forgiveness directly from God without an intermediary.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:20:115/r6] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | This principle undercuts the Christian doctrine that God sacrificed Jesus to cleanse humanity of original sin.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:20:115/r7] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | The placement after Sūrat Maryam, which explains Jesus’s birth and condemns mistaken assumptions about it, creates a thematic connection with this account of Adam.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:20:115/r8] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | The authors render the phrase as “We had taken a covenant from Adam.”
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:20:115/r9] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · bazıları | reports | Some understand the phrase as “We had conveyed Our command to Adam,” specifically the warning not to obey Satan, his and his wife’s enemy, explained in 20:117. | mentions 20:117
