[ALUSI-FULL:v2p114/r13] ALUSI-FULL, d. 1270 · author | holds | ʿAfw is the opposite of exertion: what remains beyond need or does not burden the giver; smooth, easy-to-tread ground is also called ʿafw.

[ALUSI-FULL:v2p114/r16] ALUSI-FULL, d. 1270 · أبو هريرة | reports | The Prophet says the best charity is given from sufficiency and that one should begin with those one supports; this is reported by al-Bukhārī, Muslim, Abū Dāwūd, and al-Nasāʾī.

[ALUSI-FULL:v2p114/r2] ALUSI-FULL, d. 1270 · author | holds | Wine distracts from remembrance and prayer, commonly produces hostility and fights, deepens into addiction, and can cause illnesses and death; loss of reason and uprightness alone suffices to make it the source of vices.

[ALUSI-FULL:v2p114/r4] ALUSI-FULL, d. 1270 · author | holds | Gambling takes wealth wrongfully and leads many gamblers to theft, loss of life, neglect of dependents, shameful conduct, and hidden or open enmity.

[ALUSI-FULL:v2p115/r4] ALUSI-FULL, d. 1270 · author | holds | The kāf is accusative as an adjective of an omitted noun, while the definite article in “the signs” is generic: God clarifies rulings by clear revelation, another verse, or the Prophet’s explanation.

[ALUSI-FULL:v2p115/r7] ALUSI-FULL, d. 1270 · author | holds | Contemplating the signs means deriving rulings and understanding the interests and benefits attached to them; this makes reflection a fitting aim of their clarification.
