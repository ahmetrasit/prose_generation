# 96:7/q19 What virtue is reported for reciting this sura? [* · sciences]
- 96:7/q19/p1 A reciter receives a reward as though reciting all the shorter suras.
  reasons: Baydawi Full reports this promise from the Prophet without a grading in the note.
  holders (1 notes): BAYDAWI-FULL(النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم)
  notes: BAYDAWI-FULL:96:18-19/r4
sources: BAYDAWI-FULL = al-Bayḍāwī, d. 685

# 96:7/q20 Why is wealth set before children in a cited comparison? [* · links]
- 96:7/q20/p1 Wealth is the more persistent temptation and can prompt transgression.
  reasons: Ibn al-Hajib, reported in Itqan, says spouses precede children because spouses are more often enemies, and wealth precedes children because its temptation is more constant.
  holders (1 notes): ITQAN(ابن الحاجب)
  notes: ITQAN:v3p38/r8
sources: ITQAN = al-Suyūṭī, d. 911
