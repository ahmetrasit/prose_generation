# 87:11/q01 What does وَيَتَجَنَّبُهَا describe the person doing? [وَيَتَجَنَّبُهَا · meaning]
- 87:11/q01/p1 He deliberately turns away from and refuses to receive the reminder.
  reasons: Al-Farrāʾ says he does not remember; al-Zajjāj supplies al-dhikrā as the understood object. Jishumī explains tajannub as turning to one side from what conflicts with one. Ibn ʿĀshūr explains its form as taking a position beside something, a picture of deliberate distance. Naṣafī adds nonacceptance; Bursevī adds refusal to listen receptively. Biqāʿī says he forces his originally upright nature away from a reminder delivered by the noblest messenger. Muqātil describes slighting or neglecting monotheism. Qutb says he will neither hear nor benefit.

# 87:11/q02 What does the pronoun ـهَا refer to? [وَيَتَجَنَّبُهَا · referent]
- 87:11/q02/p1 It refers to the reminder or admonition.
  reasons: Al-Wāḥidī attributes this to al-Farrāʾ and al-Zajjāj; Corpus Coranicum points back specifically to verse 9 and notes Paret's comparison with Q 92:17, where subject and object are reversed and the object is Hellfire.

# 87:11/q03 Who is ٱلْأَشْقَى, and how strong is the description? [ٱلْأَشْقَى · referent]
turns on: Whether the elative marks all unbelievers, a distinct extreme of hostility, the contrast between response groups, or a specific occasion; the notes also differ on whether it functions as a true superlative.
- 87:11/q03/p1 The unbeliever as a class, more wretched than a sinful believer.
  reasons: Māwardī ties the condition to unbelief; Kashshāf, Bayḍāwī and Nasafī state the comparison with the sinner. Ṭabrisī specifies sinners who disbelieve in God and worship another. Bursevī includes all unbelievers as an alternative. Ālūsī reports this but prefers the persistent denier. Ibn ʿĀshūr says the definite article is generic and includes every polytheist; he prefers this over restriction to named men, because unbelief leads to eternal Hell. Kur'an Yolu calls them rejecters of God. The Study Quran describes them as deniers of return to God.
  holders (18 notes): MAWARDI; MAWARDI-FULL; KASHSHAF; KASHSHAF-FULL; TABRISI; BAYDAWI; BAYDAWI-FULL; NASAFI; NASAFI-FULL; BURSEVI; ALUSI(قيل); ALUSI-FULL(قيل); IBNASHUR; IBNASHUR-FULL; KURANYOLU-TEFSIR; STUDYQURAN; IBNASHUR+
  notes: MAWARDI:87:11/r1 MAWARDI-FULL:v6p253/r18 KASHSHAF:87:11/r2 KASHSHAF-FULL:v4p741/r6 TABRISI:87:10-17/r2 BAYDAWI:87:11/r1 BAYDAWI-FULL:87:9-12/r4 NASAFI:87:11/r2 NASAFI-FULL:v4p332#11/r2 BURSEVI:v10p316/r4 ALUSI:87:11/r4 ALUSI-FULL:v30p108#2/r3 IBNASHUR:87:11/r2 IBNASHUR:87:11/r4 IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p285/r8 IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p285/r9 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:87:9/r5 STUDYQURAN:87:10/r1
- 87:11/q03/p2 The particularly hostile or obstinate unbeliever, most wretched among unbelievers.
  reasons: Kashshāf, Nasafī and Ālūsī cite deep enmity toward the Messenger; Bayḍāwī says deep immersion in unbelief. Abū Ḥayyān and Biqāʿī explain the extreme degree by rejection of Muhammad, the noblest messenger, while believers in him surpass believers in earlier messengers. Rāzī contrasts knowledgeable, hesitant and defiant hearers: the obstinate denier refuses the call and benefits from no warning. Ālūsī prefers the persistent denier of resurrection and truths that should awaken fear.

- 87:11/q03/p3 The wretched one as God already knows or has decreed him to be.
  reasons: Thaʿlabī and Wāḥidī appeal to God's foreknowledge; Baghawī and Qurṭubī say he is wretched in God's knowledge. Ibn ʿAṭiyya calls his misery decreed and says he rejects the reminder through disbelief; his fuller account contrasts those fearing God and the Hereafter, who heed it.

- 87:11/q03/p4 The most wretched of the two response groups: those who avoid the reminder.
  reasons: Ṭabarī says they are the party whom the reminder did not benefit and who enter the great Fire. Abū Muslim explicitly compares the fearers and avoiders. Ibn ʿĀshūr treats the definite article as marking a general class, while Qutb calls the word the absolute endpoint of wretchedness. Rāzī says an Arabic superlative need not presuppose a separate class of merely wretched people, comparing Q 25:24.
