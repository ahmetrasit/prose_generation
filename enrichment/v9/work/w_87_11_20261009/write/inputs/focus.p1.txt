# 87:11/q04 What does wretchedness mean here? [ٱلْأَشْقَى · meaning]
- 87:11/q04/p1 It is the condition of severe punishment and afterlife misery caused by unbelief.
  reasons: Jishumī calls shaqwa a condition leading to severe punishment, opposite felicity. Ibn ʿĀshūr defines its religious sense as humiliation and torment in the afterlife from unbelief and says one who dies a believer is not wretched. Corpus Coranicum notes the eschatological sense in Q 92:15 and possible threat in Q 91:12.

# 87:11/q05 Why does the person avoid the reminder, and how responsible is he? [* · theology]
turns on: The notes variously stress personal choice, contempt, lack of fear, or divine foreknowledge; only Jishumī explicitly disputes determinism.
- 87:11/q05/p1 His refusal is his own blameworthy, avoidable act.
  reasons: Jishumī says he has the capacity to do otherwise, explicitly arguing against determinist created acts and capacity. Biqāʿī says he forces his upright nature against the reminder, a sign of aversion; Islāḥī says rejection reflects the receiver's condition rather than a flaw in the message or preacher.
  holders (6 notes): JISHUMI; BIQAI; BIQAI-FULL; ISLAHI-TADABBUR
  notes: JISHUMI:v10p7380/r13 BIQAI:87:11/r1 BIQAI:87:11/r2 BIQAI-FULL:v8p399/r1 BIQAI-FULL:v8p399/r2 ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p278/r6
- 87:11/q05/p2 He lacks fear of God or the Hereafter and clings to disbelief or worldly delusion.
  reasons: Ibn ʿĀshūr says delusion and pleasure leave him seeking no escape; Ṭabāṭabāʾ contrasts him with the fearful. Islāḥī says evaders treat this life as all there is, and that loss of nature's God-given light leaves some unresponsive until the Judgement trumpet. The Study Quran contrasts deniers of return with both certain and uncertain believers who fear God. Ālūsī specifies persistent denial of resurrection or other fear-producing truths; Qutb describes a dead, dull soul anxiously preoccupied with trifles.
  holders (9 notes): ALUSI-FULL; QUTB-ZILAL; IBNASHUR; IBNASHUR-FULL; TABATABAI; ISLAHI-TADABBUR; STUDYQURAN; ALUSI+

- 87:11/q05/p3 He shuns it out of contempt for the reminder and hatred of its people.
  reasons: Qatāda contrasts God-fearing people who remember with those who disdain the reminder and its people; Ibn Abī Ḥātim includes hostility as well as disdain.

# 87:11/q06 What reports identify people or events behind the verse? [* · reports]
turns on: The transmitted occasion or identification differs in number and identity of opponents, and one account instead concerns a hypocrite and ʿUthmān.
- 87:11/q06/p1 It was revealed concerning al-Walīd ibn al-Mughīra and ʿUtba ibn Rabīʿa.
  reasons: The two names occur as an unnamed report in Kashshāf, Qurṭubī, Nasafī and Ālūsī; Ibn ʿĀshūr says some restrict the subject to either one. Rāzī says the wording remains general despite occasion reports.

# 87:11/q07 What fate follows the avoidance? [* · interpretation]
turns on: The notes describe both the following verses' afterlife punishment and, in Qutb, a present worldly misery.
- 87:11/q07/p1 He enters the great Fire and endures continuing punishment without restful death or beneficial life.
  reasons: Ibn Jawzī calls it greater and more dreadful than worldly fire. Ḥasan, cited by Rāzī, identifies Hell in contrast with this world's lesser fire; other accounts distinguish levels of fire or make it the lowest level for unbelievers, with Q 4:145 on hypocrites. Ibn Kathīr relates the neither-dying-nor-living state to Q 43:77 and 35:36. Qutb says the punishment has no known end; Islāḥī says evaders wish for death in vain. Ibn ʿĀshūr says verse 12 explains the initially ambiguous description 'most wretched.'
  holders (13 notes): TAB; IBNATIYYA-FULL; IBNJAWZI-ZAD; RAZI; RAZI-FULL(الحسن); IBNKATHIR; QUTB-ZILAL; IBNASHUR; ISLAHI-TADABBUR; KURANYOLU-TEFSIR
  notes: TAB:87:11/r1 IBNATIYYA-FULL:v5p470/r4 IBNJAWZI-ZAD:v9p91/r1 RAZI:87:11/r1 RAZI-FULL:v31p132#2/r2 RAZI-FULL:v31p132#2/r3 RAZI-FULL:v31p132#2/r4 IBNKATHIR:87:11/r1 IBNKATHIR:87:13/r1 QUTB-ZILAL:87:11/r3 IBNASHUR:87:11/r6 ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p279/r1 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:87:9/r5
- 87:11/q07/p2 His earthly life is already spiritually dead, anxious, and confined to trivial concerns.
  reasons: Qutb says the dull soul cannot perceive existence's truths or respond to its signs, before the unending afterlife punishment described in verses 12–13.
