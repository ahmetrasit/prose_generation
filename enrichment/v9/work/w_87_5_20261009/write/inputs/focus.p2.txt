# 87:5/q07 What transition does فَجَعَلَهُۥ convey? [فَجَعَلَهُۥ · rhetoric]
turns on: Whether the particle conveys rapid succession or bare coordination.
- 87:5/q07/p1 The pasture's flourishing rapidly gives way to dry darkness.
  reasons: Al-Ālūsī says the green state seems barely complete before debris follows, though that ordering is contrary to apparent syntax. Al-Bursawī and Ibn ʿĀshūr see the brief span of worldly pleasure, comparing 10:24. Al-Ālūsī also observes a gradual nurturing through successive stages.

  reasons: Al-Burhān gives the coordination analysis for making vegetation dry and dark.

# 87:5/q08 What has God made of the pasture? [فَجَعَلَهُۥ غُثَآءً أَحْوَىٰ · interpretation]
turns on: Whether the dark description applies before drying, after drying, or to vigorous vegetation itself; one extension reads dark remains as fuel.
- 87:5/q08/p1 He turns fresh growth into withered, dark, discolored plant remains.
  reasons: Muqātil mentions winter herbage; Qatādah describes yellow, green and white phases. Ibn ʿAbbās calls it brittle and changed; Ibn Zayd adds wind and flood dispersal. One account says old debris darkens and rots after rain. Qutb says creatures can feed on plants in both green and dry states.
