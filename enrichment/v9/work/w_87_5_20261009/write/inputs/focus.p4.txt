# 87:5/q14 What background and recitation reports are given for this sūrah? [* · sciences]
- 87:5/q14/p1 It is Meccan, has nineteen verses, and was loved and recited by the Prophet.
  reasons: Qutb cites ʿAlī for the Prophet's love of the sūrah and Muslim for his pairing it with al-Ghāshiyah at Eid and Friday prayers, sometimes both on one day.
