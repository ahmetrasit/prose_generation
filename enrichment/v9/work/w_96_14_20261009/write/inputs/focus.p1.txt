# 96:14/q04 Why is this knowledge framed as an interrogative rebuke? [أَلَمْ يَعْلَم · rhetoric]
turns on: The notes give complementary explanations of the question's rebuking and astonishment effects.
- 96:14/q04/p1 The question confirms what the opponents themselves admit and exposes the contradiction of defying the Prophet under God's sight.
  reasons: Al-Biqaʿi says the denier's conduct is irrational despite divine monitoring and control; Ibn Ashur calls the interrogative a rebuke for failing to protect himself.
  holders (7 notes): BIQAI; BIQAI-FULL; QURTUBI-FULL; IBNASHUR; IBNASHUR-FULL

  reasons: Al-Alusi says the unjoined close signals the real occurrence of denial after a broadened hypothetical; three distinct reproaches concern forbidding prayer, claiming piety, then denying truth.

# 96:14/q06 What does divine seeing mean? [ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ · theology]
turns on: Whether 'seeing' is an irreducible divine perception or interpreted through knowledge; the notes also discuss its unlimited extent.
- 96:14/q06/p1 God truly sees things; sight is not reduced to knowledge.
  reasons: Al-Jishumi accepts literal divine seeing and rejects a Baghdadi claim that 'sees' only means 'knows'.
  holders (1 notes): JISHUMI+
  notes: JISHUMI:v10p7482/r7
- 96:14/q06/p2 God's seeing is His perception or knowledge of deeds and perceptible things, without bodily organs or likeness to creatures.
  reasons: Ibn Atiyya explicitly denies corporeal implication. Ibn Ashur defines it as knowledge relating to perceptible things. The Baghdadi 'means knows' view is reported by al-Jishumi, who rejects it.
  holders (4 notes): IBNATIYYA; IBNATIYYA-FULL; IBNASHUR; IBNASHUR-FULL; JISHUMI-
  notes: IBNATIYYA:96:13/r4 IBNATIYYA-FULL:v5p502#2/r6 IBNASHUR:96:14/r7 IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p449/r6 | against: JISHUMI:v10p7482/r7
- 96:14/q06/p3 God's sight and knowledge are unrestricted, grounding His power to punish wrongdoers and support the obedient.
  reasons: Al-Biqaʿi says He knows every knowable and sees every visible thing. Al-Tabataba'i infers this from God's creating everything, a premise polytheists themselves acknowledge.
  holders (4 notes): BIQAI; BIQAI-FULL; TABATABAI
  notes: BIQAI:96:14/r2 BIQAI-FULL:v8p485/r1 BIQAI-FULL:v8p485/r2 TABAT
