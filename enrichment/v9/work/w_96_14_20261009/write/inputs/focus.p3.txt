# 96:14/q11 What other textual and transmission notes bear on this verse or its sura? [* · sciences]
turns on: Different Mi'raj chronologies are reported; the other notes concern a sura virtue and an editorial cross-reference.
- 96:14/q11/p1 The Mi'raj and obligation of prayer are dated late enough to challenge the Abu Talib story.
  reasons: Al-Alusi cites Ibn Hazm's one year, Ibn Faris's one year and three months, and al-Suddi's one year and five months before Hijra, all after Abu Talib's death.
  holders (1 notes): ALUSI

  reasons: Al-Alusi reports Qadi Iyad's report from al-Zuhri, favored by Nawawi and Qurtubi, while noting objections.
