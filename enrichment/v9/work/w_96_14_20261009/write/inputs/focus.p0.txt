# 96:14/q01 Who is the person asked whether he knows God sees? [أَلَمْ يَعْلَم · referent]
turns on: Whether the pronoun and changing addresses are tied to the Abu Jahl incident, the Prophet and opponent in turn, or the general audience.
- 96:14/q01/p1 The forbidding man is Abu Jahl.
  reasons: Al-Tabari and others identify the opponent who forbade Muhammad's prayer; al-Farra explicitly makes this question about Abu Jahl. Al-Alusi reports Ibn Atiyya's claim of agreement that the servant is the Messenger and the forbidder Abu Jahl. Ibn Ashur says the prior prohibition supplies the omitted referent.

# 96:14/q02 How does 'know' take the following bāʾ phrase? [يَعْلَم بِأَنَّ · grammar]
turns on: Whether the preposition follows a certainty sense of yaʿlam or is an added bāʾ before its object.
- 96:14/q02/p1 Know means be certain, accounting for the bāʾ.
  reasons: Ibn Ashur expressly treats yaʿlam as carrying certainty.
  holders (2 notes): IBNASHUR; IBNASHUR-FULL
  notes: IBNASHUR:96:14/r3 IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p449/r2
- 96:14/q02/p2 The bāʾ is an added preposition on an object that the verb can take directly.
  reasons: Al-Zarkashi's Burhan compares 2:195 and 19:25, and cites 22:15, 38:33, and 21:31 among parallels.
  holders (1 notes): BURHAN
  notes: BURHAN:v3p83/r2
sources: BURHAN = al-Zarkashī, d. 794; IBNASHUR = Ibn ʿĀshūr, d. 1393; IBNASHUR-FULL = Ibn ʿĀshūr, d. 1393

# 96:14/q03 How does the final question complete the preceding arāʾayta clauses and conditions? [أَلَمْ يَعْلَم بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ · grammar]
turns on: The object requirements of arāʾayta, whether the closing interrogative is an apodosis or a fresh sentence, and whether an interrogative apodosis needs fāʾ.
- 96:14/q03/p1 It is the expressed answer to the later conditional, with the earlier answer omitted or supplied by it.
  reasons: Al-Zamakhshari allows a question to answer a conditional without fāʾ, using 6:47 and other interrogative examples; al-Radi approves. The forbidden person and conditional supply arāʾayta's objects, with the second arāʾayta emphatic. Al-Baydawi and al-Nasafi likewise supply the earlier answer from the later condition.

- 96:14/q03/p2 The final question is an independent rebuke; conditional answers are omitted and indicated by it or by arāʾayta.
