# 87:7/q12 How does God's knowledge explain the promise and exception? [إِنَّهُۥ يَعْلَمُ ٱلْجَهْرَ وَمَا يَخْفَىٰ · links]
turns on: Whether the knowledge clause chiefly explains selective revelation, relieves the Prophet's fear, warns against sins, or reinforces a wider sequence.
- 87:7/q12/p1 God knows what to preserve, erase, or abrogate for people's benefit.
  reasons: Al-Kashshāf and al-Rāzī say comprehensive knowledge includes what benefits or harms religion; al-Bayḍāwī mentions the benefit of preserving or causing forgetting. Al-Biqāʿī says one who determines what can be forgotten must know all, invoking 16:40 for the efficacy of God's will. Al-Ālūsī and Qutb relate preservation and exception to wise decisions grounded in complete knowledge.
  holders (14 notes): KASHSHAF; KASHSHAF-FULL; RAZI; RAZI-FULL; BAYDAWI; BAYDAWI-FULL; BIQAI; BIQAI-FULL; BURSEVI; ALUSI; ALUSI-FULL; QUTB-ZILAL; ELMALILI

# 87:7/q13 Is the knowledge clause parenthetical or attached to another clause? [إِنَّهُۥ يَعْلَمُ ٱلْجَهْرَ وَمَا يَخْفَىٰ · grammar]
turns on: Its discourse attachment: a parenthesis explaining preservation, a broader reinforcement, or an explanation of glorification.
- 87:7/q13/p1 It is parenthetical and explains the promise of preserved recitation.
  reasons: Ibn ʿĀshūr describes an interruption: God knows spoken recitation and even the verse hidden by an omission. Elmalılı says the clause is parenthetical while also grammatically attached to the promise to make the Prophet recite.
  holders (3 notes): IBNASHUR; IBNASHUR-FULL; ELMALILI
  notes: IBNASHUR:87:7/r10 IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p281/r4 ELMALILI:87:7-13/r3
- 87:7/q13/p2 It can reinforce what precedes and what follows, or particularly the recitation assurance.
  reasons: Al-Ālūsī reports both broad and narrow connections; the latter rests on God's knowledge of the Prophet's aloud recitation and fear. Al-Kashshāf's full note similarly gives both contextual reassurance and general public and hidden states.
  holders (4 notes): ALUSI(قيل); ALUSI-FULL; KASHSHAF-FULL
  notes: ALUSI:87:7/r28 ALUSI:87:7/r29 ALUSI-FULL:v30p106/r5 KASHSHAF-FULL:v4p740#2/r5
- 87:7/q13/p3 It should not be attached as an explanation of the command to glorify.
  reasons: Al-Ālūsī explicitly rejects that attachment to 87:1.
