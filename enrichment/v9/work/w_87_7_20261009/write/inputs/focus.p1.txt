# 87:7/q03 What does the promise of not forgetting guarantee? [إِلَّا مَا شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ · theology]
turns on: Whether the assurance excludes all lapses, lasting loss of conveyed teaching, or only loss outside God's chosen purposes.
- 87:7/q03/p1 The Prophet is protected from losing revelation that he must convey; any later lapse is corrected or serves a stated purpose.
  reasons: Ibn ʿAṭiyya says protection applies to what he was ordered to transmit; after learning and conveying it, he may temporarily forget, establish Sunnah, or experience abrogation. Abū Ḥayyān agrees. Al-Rāzī says a lapse in obligatory religion would damage the law. Al-Ālūsī says the verse bars lasting, uncorrected forgetting of essentials, while lesser etiquette or Sunnah can be forgotten, citing al-Rāzī; correction may come by revelation or a listener. Elmalılı says continuing non-forgetfulness is miraculous.
