# 29:45: 33 questions

## 29:45/q01 Who is commanded to recite, and to whom?  [ٱتْلُ · referent]
turns on: The address is to Muhammad, but its audience and extension vary.
- 29:45/q01/p1 Muhammad is addressed, and his community also receives the command. — Muqātil specifies People of the Book; al-Māwardī says the community; Ibn ʿĀshūr cites the plural ending and 11:112. [12 notes: MUQATIL; MAWARDI-FULL; TABRISI; QURTUBI-FULL; IBNKATHIR-FULL; BIQAI-FULL; BURSEVI; ALUSI-FULL; IBNASHUR-FULL]
- 29:45/q01/p2 Muhammad is told to recite for his own consolation, without a named audience. — Al-Rāzī likens his case to earlier rejected messengers; Elmalılı reads the absence of 'to them' as a turn from provocation. [4 notes: RAZI-FULL; ELMALILI; IBNASHUR-FULL]
- 29:45/q01/p3 The plural forms allow an address to believers generally. — Asad includes 29:46 and takes revelation as what has become clear to the reader. [1 notes: ASAD-NOTES]

## 29:45/q02 What is the revealed Book?  [مِنَ ٱلْكِتَٰبِ · referent]
turns on: Whether this is the Qurʾān or an earlier book.
- 29:45/q02/p1 It is the Qurʾān revealed to Muhammad. — Kur'an Yolu argues from 'revealed to you'; al-kitāb varies by context and Ateş's own readings at 16:64, 16:89, 18:1, 13:39 and 18:49. [12 notes: TAB-FULL; IBNABIHATIM(الحسن); MAWARDI-FULL; WAHIDI-BASIT(ابن عباس); BAGHAWI-FULL; IBNATIYYA-FULL; QURTUBI-FULL; KURANYOLU-TEFSIR; KURANYOLU-TEFSIR+]
- 29:45/q02/p2 It is the Torah and its appendices. — Kur'an Yolu reports Süleyman Ateş's proposal but rejects it on the wording. [1 notes: KURANYOLU-TEFSIR(Süleyman Ateş); KURANYOLU-TEFSIR-]

## 29:45/q03 What does recitation achieve and require?  [ٱتْلُ مَآ أُوحِىَ إِلَيْكَ · interpretation]
- 29:45/q03/p1 Continued, attentive Qurʾān recitation preserves, discloses and conveys its guidance. — Al-Rāzī says universal law needs repeated transmission and exhortation softens hearts; al-Bayḍāwī and Bursevī say new readings yield new insight; al-Ṭabāṭabāʾī says proofs, stories and warnings deter reader and listener. [14 notes: RAZI-FULL; BAYDAWI-FULL; NASAFI-FULL; BIQAI-FULL; QURTUBI-FULL; BURSEVI(عمر); ALUSI-FULL; ELMALILI; TABATABAI]
- 29:45/q03/p2 Recitation concerns whatever of divine writ becomes clear to the reader. — Asad links this to the verse's plural forms. [1 notes: ASAD-NOTES]

## 29:45/q04 What is the lexical force of tilāwa and waḥy?  [ٱتْلُ أُوحِىَ · meaning]
- 29:45/q04/p1 Tilāwa is reading in sequence, while waḥy is secret divine communication. — Bursevī allows the word revelation for speech conveyed to prophets and saints; al-Biqāʿī sees exclusive delivery as prophetic honor. [2 notes: BURSEVI; BIQAI-FULL]

## 29:45/q05 What practice and merit of recitation are reported?  [ٱتْلُ · law]
- 29:45/q05/p1 Recitation merits reward in differing settings and calls for humble etiquette. — Bursevī reports ʿAlī's scale of 100, 50, 25 and 10 deeds per letter according to posture and ablution; al-Ḥasan and jurists favor extensive recitation and long standing; Bursevī recommends ablution and facing qibla. [3 notes: BURSEVI(الحسن البصري,علي)]

## 29:45/q06 Why are recitation and prayer singled out together?  [ٱتْلُ وَأَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ · rhetoric]
- 29:45/q06/p1 They combine repeatable verbal remembrance and bodily action in the Prophet's mission. — Al-Rāzī says the Prophet's belief was already established; al-Biqāʿī says prayer focuses attention on the Qurʾān. [3 notes: RAZI-FULL; BIQAI-FULL]

## 29:45/q07 What does prayer denote here?  [ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ · referent]
turns on: Legal prayer, Qurʾānic recitation, and supplication are reported.
- 29:45/q07/p1 It is the Qurʾān recited in prayer or mosques. — Ibn ʿUmar cites 17:110; al-Rāzī objects that the preceding command already names recitation. [13 notes: TAB-FULL(ابن عمر); MAWARDI-FULL(ابن عمر); JISHUMI(قيل); BAGHAWI-FULL; IBNATIYYA-FULL(ابن عمر); QURTUBI-FULL(ابن عمر); IBNJAWZI-ZAD(ابن عمر); RAZI-FULL(بعض المفسرين); ALUSI-FULL(ابن عمر); DURR-FULL(ابن عمر); STUDYQURAN(IJ, Ṭ); RAZI-FULL-; ALUSI-FULL-; STUDYQURAN(al-Rāzī)-]
- 29:45/q07/p2 It is the prescribed ritual prayer. — Al-Jishumī argues for the legal sense of a revealed term; al-Rāzī and al-Ālūsī say Qurʾānic recitation is already commanded. [9 notes: MAWARDI-FULL(ابن عباس); IBNJAWZI-ZAD(الأكثرون); ABUHAYYAN-FULL; BURSEVI; STUDYQURAN(IJ, Q, R,al-Rāzī); RAZI-FULL; ALUSI-FULL+; JISHUMI+]
- 29:45/q07/p3 It is supplication or calling people to God. — Ibn Baḥr gives this sense; al-Ṭabāṭabāʾī objects to departing from the apparent sense. [4 notes: MAWARDI-FULL(ابن بحر); ABUHAYYAN-FULL(ابن بحر); ALUSI-FULL(ابن بحر); JISHUMI(قيل); ALUSI-FULL-; TABATABAI(بعضهم)-]

## 29:45/q08 What is required by establishing prayer?  [وَأَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ · interpretation]
- 29:45/q08/p1 Maintain prescribed prayer fully, regularly and at its times. — Al-Ṭabarī cites 11:114; al-Qurṭubī lists recitation, bowing, prostration, sitting and tashahhud; al-Biqāʿī says repeated practice makes sound habit. [11 notes: MUQATIL; TAB-FULL; WAHIDI-BASIT(ابن عباس); IBNATIYYA-FULL; TABRISI; QURTUBI-FULL; NASAFI-FULL; ALUSI-FULL; ELMALILI; BIQAI-FULL; IBNASHUR-FULL]

- 29:45/q09/p3 Prayer may reform a continuing sinner gradually after the prayer ends. — The reported Anṣārī youth and thief are offered as evidence; al-Wāḥidī says later repentance shows delayed benefit. - 29:45/q09/p4 Inward remembrance and watchfulness, prayer's chief purpose, provide its restraint. — Ibn ʿAṭiyya says movements alone cannot deter; Bursevī calls forgetfulness the root illness; al-Ṭabāṭabāʾī makes inward awareness the intended end of speech. - 29:45/q09/p6 Prayer ought to restrain, as a moral instruction rather than guaranteed compulsion. — Al-Jishumī and al-Ṭabrisī compare sanctuary safety in 3:97; al-Qurṭubī reads the clause as an imperative in sense and cites 45:29 and 30:35. - 29:45/q09/p8 Only some kinds or instances of prayer restrain wrongdoing. — Al-Ṭabāṭabāʾī reports this proposal but rejects it because the context concerns prayer's inherent effect. [1 notes: ALUSI-FULL; TABATABAI(آخرون)-]
- 29:45/q09/p9 Prayer's verbal remembrance of God alone prevents or at least reduces vice. — Al-Qutb links 20:14 to the thought that a praying sinner would sin still more without prayer; al-Ṭabāṭabāʾī rejects verbal remembrance alone as the intended mechanism. [1 notes: ALUSI-FULL(القطب); TABATABAI(بعضهم)-]

## 29:45/q10 What does 'indecency' mean?  [ٱلْفَحْشَآءِ · meaning]
- 29:45/q10/p1 A gravely ugly or shameless act or speech, especially fornication or adultery. — Al-Biqāʿī stresses extreme ugliness; Ibn ʿĀshūr explains exceeding legal limits and cites 2:169; Kur'an Yolu includes violations of modesty and chastity. [13 notes: MUQATIL; TAB-FULL(ابن عون); IBNABIHATIM(ابن عباس، عكرمة، الحسن); MAWARDI-FULL(ابن عباس); WAHIDI-BASIT(الكلبي); NASAFI-FULL; BIQAI-FULL; BURSEVI; ELMALILI; IBNASHUR-FULL; KURANYOLU-TEFSIR; STUDYQURAN(Ṭ); IBNJAWZI-ZAD]

## 29:45/q11 What does 'wrongdoing' mean?  [ٱلْمُنكَرِ · meaning]
turns on: Reports differ between broad wrong and association with God.
- 29:45/q11/p1 What law, reason or virtuous custom rejects; sin and disobedience. — Al-Wāḥidī and al-Wasīṭ say no revealed law or sunna recognizes it; Kur'an Yolu cites 7:157 and contrasts maʿrūf. [14 notes: MUQATIL; TAB-FULL(ابن عون); WAHIDI-BASIT(الكلبي); JISHUMI(قيل); BAGHAWI-FULL; NASAFI-FULL; BIQAI-FULL; BURSEVI(الوسيط); ELMALILI; IBNASHUR-FULL; KURANYOLU-TEFSIR; STUDYQURAN(Ṭ); IBNJAWZI-ZAD]
- 29:45/q11/p2 It means associating partners with God. — Ibn ʿAbbās pairs this with adultery as indecency; ʿIkrima and al-Ḥasan transmit it. [2 notes: IBNABIHATIM(ابن عباس، عكرمة، الحسن); MAWARDI-FULL(ابن عباس)]

## 29:45/q12 Why are indecency and wrongdoing paired?  [ٱلْفَحْشَآءِ وَٱلْمُنكَرِ · rhetoric]
- 29:45/q12/p1 They express distinct grounds or degrees of condemnation. — Ibn ʿĀshūr says each term supplies a separate reason; al-Biqāʿī contrasts extreme ugliness with even slight wrong. [2 notes: IBNASHUR-FULL; BIQAI-FULL]

## 29:45/q14 What is reported of sinners who nevertheless prayed?  [ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ تَنْهَىٰ · reports]
turns on: The Anṣārī youth and the praying thief are distinct accounts; details within each vary.
- 29:45/q14/p1 An Anṣārī youth prayed with the Prophet yet sinned, then repented after the Prophet foretold prayer would restrain him. — Some versions specify five prayers, indecency and theft; al-Ālūsī says Ibn Ḥajar could not locate the report in hadith books. [14 notes: WAHIDI-BASIT(النبي); BAGHAWI-FULL(أنس); KASHSHAF-FULL(النبي); IBNATIYYA-FULL(النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم); TABRISI(النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم); QURTUBI-FULL(أنس بن مالك); BAYDAWI-FULL(النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم); NASAFI-FULL(النبي ﷺ); ABUHAYYAN-FULL(النبي); BURSEVI; ALUSI-FULL(النبي); TABATABAI(النبي صلى الله عليه وآله وسلم); STUDYQURAN(Prophet)]
- 29:45/q14/p2 A man prayed and stole afterward; the Prophet said prayer or his recitation would restrain him. — Versions vary between night prayer with morning theft and day prayer with night theft. Ibn Kathīr reports al-Aʿmash's doubt and al-Bazzār's divergent chains through Abū Hurayra, Jābir and others. [11 notes: BAGHAWI-FULL(جابر); KASHSHAF-FULL(النبي); TABRISI(النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم); NASAFI-FULL(النبي ﷺ); IBNKATHIR-FULL(البزار,رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم); DURR-FULL(النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم); ALUSI-FULL(النبي); IBNASHUR-FULL(أبو هريرة)]

## 29:45/q15 What reading of the statement about prayer is reported?  [إِنَّ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ تَنْهَىٰ · readings]
- 29:45/q15/p1 An expanded reading says prayer enjoins right conduct and forbids indecency and wrong. — Al-Rabīʿ ibn Anas gives the wording; Sufyān connects prayer's commanding and forbidding with 11:87. [5 notes: IBNATIYYA-FULL(الربيع بن أنس); DURR-FULL(الربيع بن أنس); ALUSI-FULL(الربيع بن أنس); TAB-FULL(سفيان); IBNKATHIR-FULL(سفيان)]

## 29:45/q16 What does prayer teach in its approach to God and return to the community?  [وَأَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ · inward]
- 29:45/q16/p1 Its words and postures enact nearness, humility, dependence, gratitude to the Prophet and peace to people. — Al-Rāzī likens standing to royal service, prostration to nearness under 96:19, and salām to a returning traveler; he cites 19:72. [8 notes: RAZI-FULL]

## 29:45/q17 What other reported benefit do the five prayers bring?  [ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ · reports]
- 29:45/q17/p1 Five daily prayers wash away intervening sins like five river baths wash away dirt. — Al-Qurṭubī cites Abū Hurayra and notes al-Tirmidhī graded the report ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ. [3 notes: QURTUBI-FULL(أبو هريرة); STUDYQURAN(Prophet)]

## 29:45/q18 What construction explains prayer's apparent prohibition of vice?  [إِنَّ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ تَنْهَىٰ · rhetoric]
- 29:45/q18/p1 Inna gives the reason for prayer, and attributing prohibition to prayer depicts moral admonition. — Ibn ʿĀshūr compares inna with causal faʾ and denies forceful compulsion; al-Kashshāf says the generic claim need not hold of every worshipper. [8 notes: IBNASHUR-FULL; KASHSHAF-FULL; ALUSI-FULL]

## 29:45/q19 How is prayer's acceptance recognized?  [ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ تَنْهَىٰ · interpretation]
- 29:45/q19/p1 Its acceptance is proportional to how much it restrains indecency and wrong. — Abū ʿAbd Allāh's report is transmitted by al-Ṭabrisī, al-Ālūsī and al-Ṭabāṭabāʾī. [3 notes: TABRISI(أبو عبد الله); ALUSI-FULL(أبو عبد الله); TABATABAI(أبو عبد الله عليه السلام)]

## 29:45/q20 What recitational pronunciation rule applies to the cited words?  [ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ ٱللَّهِ · readings]
- 29:45/q20/p1 A heavy lām accompanies open alif from yāʾ, while imāla requires light lām; Warsh's light rāʾ does not lighten Allah after fatḥa or ḍamma. — Ibn al-Jazarī gives the articulation rule and Warsh's treatment. [2 notes: NASHR(ورش من طريق الأزرق)]

## 29:45/q21 What does the verse teach about divine grace and human responsibility?  [ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ تَنْهَىٰ · theology]
- 29:45/q21/p1 Prayer is divine grace aiding restraint; failure to respond belongs to the worshipper. — Al-Jishumī calls the grace obligatory; al-Ṭabrisī distinguishes God's enabling from personal failure. [3 notes: JISHUMI(قيل); TABRISI]

## 29:45/q22 What inward grades of prayer and remembrance are described?  [ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَلَذِكْرُ · inward]
- 29:45/q22/p1 Prayer has bodily, self, heart, secret, spirit, hidden and essential levels, each restraining a corresponding fault. — Al-Qāshānī pairs these with grades of knowledge and prayer; the seventh is annihilation and returns in subsistence. Outward prayer ends at death, real prayer at annihilation; he cites 15:99. [11 notes: QASHANI(عليه السلام); BURSEVI(بعض الكبار)]
- 29:45/q22/p2 The highest remembrance is annihilation of selfhood, in which divine remembrance surpasses human remembrance. — Al-Qāshānī identifies remembrance of the Essence in annihilation; Study Quran joins divine and human senses beyond time and place. [2 notes: QASHANI; STUDYQURAN]
- 29:45/q22/p3 Accepted inward prayer averts self-admiration, demands for reward and worldly gain. — Jaʿfar and Ibn ʿAṭāʾ name these faults; Bursevī recounts the dream of a man who failed to value prayer with Ḥabīb al-ʿAjamī. [5 notes: SULAMI(ابن عطاء,جعفر); BURSEVI]

## 29:45/q23 What does remembrance mean as a word?  [وَلَذِكْرُ · meaning]
- 29:45/q23/p1 It can name the ritual prayer itself. — Al-Itqān states this usage; several exegetes cite 62:9. [12 notes: ITQAN; KASHSHAF-FULL; BAYDAWI-FULL; NASAFI-FULL; ABUHAYYAN-FULL(الزمخشري); BURSEVI; ALUSI-FULL; IBNASHUR-FULL; TABATABAI; KURANYOLU-TEFSIR]
- 29:45/q23/p2 Primarily inward recollection or presence, with speech named remembrance through its inward content. — Al-Rāghib distinguishes retention from bringing knowledge to mind, and remembrance after forgetting from sustained awareness; Ibn ʿAṭiyya requires knowledge and attention. [7 notes: TABATABAI(الراغب); IBNATIYYA-FULL; QURTUBI-FULL(ابن عطية); SULAMI(القاسم); BURSEVI]

## 29:45/q25 How does the remembrance phrase work grammatically?  [وَلَذِكْرُ ٱللَّهِ أَكْبَرُ · grammar]
turns on: The genitive and comparative form support different readings.
- 29:45/q25/p1 Akbar is absolute or means 'great', without a comparative complement. — Al-Zajjāj says kabīr; al-Zamakhsharī makes it unrestricted superiority; Ibn ʿĀshūr and al-Ṭabāṭabāʾī give noncomparative readings, the latter citing 62:11. [8 notes: ZAJJAJ; BURHAN(الزمخشري); QURTUBI-FULL(قيل); ALUSI-FULL; IBNASHUR-FULL; TABATABAI(قيل); RAZI-FULL]
- 29:45/q25/p2 The genitive makes God the object of human remembrance. — Al-Wāḥidī contrasts this with the agentive reading; al-Ṭabāṭabāʾī applies it to alternatives about human remembrance. [3 notes: WAHIDI-BASIT; ALUSI-FULL; TABATABAI]
- 29:45/q25/p3 The genitive makes God the one remembering, with the servant understood as object. — Al-Wāḥidī states the agentive reading and al-Ālūsī notes the omitted object. [2 notes: WAHIDI-BASIT; ALUSI-FULL(أبو مالك)]
- 29:45/q25/p4 Akbar takes an omitted comparator, such as 'than everything'. — Al-Burhān and al-Ālūsī supply the ellipsis. [2 notes: BURHAN; ALUSI-FULL]

## 29:45/q26 How does the remembrance clause connect with its context?  [وَلَذِكْرُ ٱللَّهِ أَكْبَرُ · grammar]
turns on: It may develop prayer's benefit or stand with the recitation command.
- 29:45/q26/p1 It develops prayer's benefit and explains its superiority or restraint. — Al-Zamakhsharī and al-Bayḍāwī name remembrance as prayer's reason; Ibn ʿĀshūr coordinates it with the prayer clause. [7 notes: KASHSHAF-FULL; BAYDAWI-FULL; NASAFI-FULL; IBNASHUR-FULL; TABATABAI; KURANYOLU-TEFSIR]
- 29:45/q26/p2 It is independent of prayer's preceding statement or links back to recitation. — Al-Wāḥidī explicitly separates the second view; Ibn ʿĀshūr connects it to the recitation command. [2 notes: WAHIDI-BASIT; IBNASHUR-FULL]

## 29:45/q27 How does al-Ṭabāṭabāʾī assess the listed remembrance interpretations?  [وَلَذِكْرُ ٱللَّهِ أَكْبَرُ · interpretation]
- 29:45/q27/p1 Some alternative readings are arbitrary or insufficiently grounded. — Al-Ṭabāṭabāʾī leaves the enumerated alternatives unexamined while observing the arbitrary character of some. [1 notes: TABATABAI]

## 29:45/q28 What reports illustrate God's reciprocal remembrance?  [وَلَذِكْرُ ٱللَّهِ · reports]
- 29:45/q28/p1 God remembers individuals and gatherings that remember Him. — Al-Thāʿlabī reports the sacred saying of reciprocity and angels surrounding gatherings; Ibn ʿAṭiyya and Bursevī cite private and public remembrance. [6 notes: THALABI(أبو هريرة,أبو هريرة وأبو سعيد); BAGHAWI-FULL(أبو هريرة، أبو سعيد، رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم); IBNATIYYA-FULL(النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم); QURTUBI-FULL; BURSEVI(النبي)]

## 29:45/q29 What is prayer's chief intended benefit?  [ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَلَذِكْرُ ٱللَّهِ · interpretation]
- 29:45/q29/p1 It establishes inward awareness of God, a greater good than moral restraint alone. — Ibn Kathīr calls remembrance its greatest intended element; Elmalılı cites 20:14 and 2:152; al-Ṭabāṭabāʾī calls inward awareness the highest human work. [9 notes: IBNKATHIR-FULL; BIQAI-FULL; ELMALILI; TABATABAI; KURANYOLU-TEFSIR]

## 29:45/q30 What does the closing statement about God's knowledge convey?  [وَٱللَّهُ يَعْلَمُ مَا تَصْنَعُونَ · interpretation]
- 29:45/q30/p1 God knows prayer and all good and evil deeds, rewarding or requiting them; the clause warns and exhorts watchfulness. — Muqātil restricts the immediate scope to prayer; al-Jishumī calls it a grave threat; Ibn ʿĀshūr says it sums up the commands. [24 notes: MUQATIL; YAHYA-SALLAM; WAHIDI-BASIT(ابن عباس); JISHUMI(قيل); BAGHAWI-FULL(عطاء); KASHSHAF-FULL; IBNATIYYA-FULL; TABRISI; RAZI-FULL; QURTUBI-FULL; BAYDAWI-FULL; NASAFI-FULL; QASHANI; IBNKATHIR-FULL; BURSEVI(بعض الكبار); ALUSI-FULL(أبو حيان); ELMALILI; IBNASHUR-FULL; TABATABAI]

## 29:45/q31 What does 'what you do' mean?  [مَا تَصْنَعُونَ · meaning]
- 29:45/q31/p1 It means deeds or sustained work. — Ibn ʿĀshūr glosses ṣanʿ as deed; al-Biqāʿī hears repeated work and training in the wording. [2 notes: IBNASHUR-FULL; BIQAI-FULL]

## 29:45/q32 How does the verse connect with its sūra and surrounding passage?  [* · links]
- 29:45/q32/p1 After earlier peoples and shirk, it turns to revelation and prayer to strengthen Muhammad and the believers. — Al-Rāzī sees consolation; Ibn ʿĀshūr sees strengthened preaching; al-Ṭabāṭabāʾī calls revelation a deterrent after shirk's fate. [8 notes: RAZI-FULL; IBNASHUR-FULL; TABATABAI]
- 29:45/q32/p2 It begins an argument for the Qurʾān's miraculousness continuing through 29:46–51. — Bint al-Shāṭiʾ disputes limiting such arguments to sūra openings and cites the testing of believers and defense of revelation. [4 notes: BINTSHATI-IJAZ]

## 29:45/q33 What further watchfulness does the verse require?  [* · interpretation]
- 29:45/q33/p1 Recitation, prayer, remembrance and God's knowledge call for lasting obedience and moral care. — Al-Jishumī urges persistence; Kur'an Yolu warns those who violate others' rights; Bursevī says consciousness of divine knowledge should empty the heart of distractions. [5 notes: JISHUMI; KURANYOLU-TEFSIR; BURSEVI(بعض الكبار); IBNASHUR-FULL]
