# 96:9/q04 What does 'forbids' mean and what does its tense convey? [يَنْهَىٰ · meaning]
turns on: Whether the imperfect emphasizes repeated conduct or vividly represents the past incident.
- 96:9/q04/p1 It means preventing or stopping an act.
  reasons: Jishumī glosses ending as refraining. Al-Ālūsī says the word may indicate an attempted obstruction that failed; Ibn ʿAbbās's authenticated report also describes an explicit spoken prohibition. Ibn ʿĀshūr says the omitted prohibited act is understood from prayer.

# 96:9/q06 What happened in the incident that occasioned this warning? [ٱلَّذِى يَنْهَىٰ · reports]
turns on: The reports differ about threat, direct confrontation, stone and camel entrails.
- 96:9/q06/p1 Abū Jahl threatened to trample Muhammad's neck or soil his face, approached him praying, recoiled from a fiery trench, terror or wings, and would have been seized by angels.
  reasons: Abū Hurayra's reports sometimes give an oath by al-Lāt and al-ʿUzzā, the Maqām or Kaʿba, and a huge camel as a barrier. Al-Tirmidhī calls one account ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ gharīb.
