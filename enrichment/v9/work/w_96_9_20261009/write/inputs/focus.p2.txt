# 96:9/q09 Why use the indefinite 'a servant'? [* · rhetoric]

- 96:9/q09/p1 It magnifies Muhammad's perfect servanthood and the audacity of barring his worship.
  reasons: Rāzī invokes ʿAlī's answer to a Jewish visitor contrasting little worldly goods with the Prophet's great character at 68:4. Bursevī says 'a servant' stresses worship of one's Master rather than saying 'you'. Al-Ālūsī prefers this reading.
