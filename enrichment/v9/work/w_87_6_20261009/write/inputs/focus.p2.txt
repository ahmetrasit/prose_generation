# 87:6/q11 What does the exception for what God wills allow? [فَلَا تَنسَىٰٓ · interpretation]
turns on: Whether the exception identifies an actual removal, ordinary lapse, deferred revelation, limited practice, or a rhetorical assertion of divine will.
- 87:6/q11/p1 God can remove abrogated recitation, often with its ruling, so it is forgotten or no longer practiced.
  reasons: Ṭabarī explicitly prefers abrogation and withdrawal. Baghawī, Kashshāf, Rāzī and Ibn ʿĀshūr cite 2:106. Rāzī says it ceases to be recited in prayer then leaves memory. Qurṭubī prefers lifting recitation for a benefit, appealing to the normal force of an exception and the written yāʾ. Iṣlāḥī calls the removed directives temporary and says God signals their end.
