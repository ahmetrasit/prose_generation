# 87:6/q12 What prompted the reassurance about forgetting? [فَلَا تَنسَىٰٓ · reports]

- 87:6/q12/p1 The Prophet rehearsed revelation inwardly or repeatedly out of fear of forgetting.

# 87:6/q14 How does this promise relate to the passages against hurrying revelation? [* · links]
turns on: Whether the passages address the same moment of haste or different duties of preserving and reviewing revelation.
- 87:6/q14/p1 It belongs with 75:16–19 and 20:114: the Prophet need not hurry his tongue because God gathers and preserves revelation.
  reasons: Ibn ʿĀshūr says al-Qiyāmah came later, so 87:6 is the earlier pledge; Ṭabāṭabāʾī prefers the order 87:6, 75:16–19, then 20:114. Iṣlāḥī says revelation supplied support against opposition and answers to objections, explaining the eagerness.
