# 87:6/q15 What sign or miracle does this promise provide? [* · interpretation]
turns on: Whether the sign lies in the extraordinary memory, the fulfilled advance promise, or both.
- 87:6/q15/p1 An unlettered Prophet retains an extensive revelation accurately without normal study, writing, or repetition.
  reasons: Rāzī calls this beyond ordinary capacity. Ālūsī places it beside Qur’anic inimitability and reports of unseen events. Bursevī says he received knowledge without writing and became a source of knowledge about writing, rules and crafts. Kuran Yolu says the miracle continues in communal reading and memorization.

# 87:6/q16 How does this verse advance the sūra’s argument and sequence? [* · links]
turns on: How divine guidance, the opening command, and the preceding sūra prepare the pledge.
- 87:6/q16/p1 After general creation and guidance, God now gives the Messenger special guidance through revelation and his mission.
  reasons: Biqāʿī says divine attributes and acts prepare for prophethood and an exemplary Messenger, while revelation revives spirits by knowledge as God revives bodies. Ibn ʿĀshūr links God’s proportioning and guidance with equipping the Prophet to retain revelation and receive an easy law. Iṣlāḥī sees the same gradual development in vegetation and prophecy.
  holders (15 notes): BIQAI; BIQAI-FULL; BURSEVI; ALUSI; ALUSI-FULL; IBNASHUR-FULL; ISLAHI-TADABBUR
  notes: BIQAI:87:6/r1 BIQAI:87:6/r2 BIQAI:87:6/r3 BIQAI:87:6/r4 BIQAI-FULL:v8p393#2/r5 BIQAI-FULL:v8p396/r7 BIQAI-FULL:v8p396/r8 BIQAI-FULL:v8p396/r9 BURSEVI:v10p314#2/r3 BURSEVI:v10p314#2/r4 ALUSI:87:6/r1 ALUSI:87:6/r2 ALUSI-FULL:v30p105/r1 IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p277/r3 ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p276/r2
- 87:6/q16/p2 The promise follows the call to glorify because complete glorification involves reciting revelation and eases the Prophet’s fear.
  reasons: Ibn ʿĀshūr calls the opening command implicit glad tidings now made explicit for full delivery.
  holders (5 notes): RAZI; RAZI-FULL; ABUHAYYAN-FULL; IBNASHUR; IBNASHUR-FULL

# 87:6/q18 What bearing does the promise have on collecting the Qur’an? [* · sciences]
turns on: Why a codex was unnecessary for the Prophet yet needed for the community.
- 87:6/q18/p1 The Prophet did not need a collected codex because God protected his memory; later collectors faced possible loss.
  reasons: Burhān adds the exception for revelation God willed to abrogate.
  holders (1 notes): BURHAN
  notes: BURHAN:v1p237/r4
