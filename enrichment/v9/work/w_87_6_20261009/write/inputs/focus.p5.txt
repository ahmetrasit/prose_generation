# 87:6/q19 What abrogation relation is claimed for 87:6? [* · sciences]
- 87:6/q19/p1 The passage beginning “We sent before you” was abrogated by this verse.
  reasons: Suyūṭī also describes the sūra’s opening as travel-revealed and other abrogation examples concerning fighting, a judgment verse and the sword verse.
  holders (1 notes): ITQAN
  notes: ITQAN:v4p430/r10
sources: ITQAN = al-Suyūṭī, d. 911

# 87:6/q20 How is the report about the Prophet repeating Gabriel’s revelation assessed? [* · reports]
turns on: The distinct transmissions and the grading each cited source provides.
- 87:6/q20/p1 One Ibn ʿAbbās chain is very weak because it contains Juwaybir.
  reasons: Suyūṭī gives this assessment of al-Ṭabarānī’s report that the Prophet began repeating before Gabriel finished.
  holders (1 notes): SUYUTI-LUBAB
  notes: SUYUTI-LUBAB:v1p228#3/r2
- 87:6/q20/p2 An Ibn ʿAbbās report about lip movement is authentic.
  reasons: Ibn ʿĀshūr cites the report together with 75:16–18.
  holders (2 notes): IBNASHUR(ابن عباس); IBNASHUR-FULL(ابن عباس)
  notes: IBNASHUR:87:6/r2 IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p279/r4
sources: IBNASHUR = Ibn ʿĀshūr, d. 1393; IBNASHUR-FULL = Ibn ʿĀshūr, d. 1393; SUYUTI-LUBAB = al-Suyūṭī, d. 911

# 87:6/q21 What other interpretive details accompany the verse? [* · interpretation]
- 87:6/q21/p1 A phrase about pasture not becoming debris when humans or animals use it is reported as an alternative nearby explanation.
  reasons: Qurṭubī places this beside the deferred-revelation explanation.
  holders (1 notes): QURTUBI-FULL
  notes: QURTUBI-FULL:87:7-8/r2
- 87:6/q21/p2 An unrelated example distinguishes kinds of causal conditional answers.
  reasons: Burhān reports grammarians who distinguish a causally following answer from one replacing an unstated consequence.
  holders (1 notes): BURHAN(بعضهم)
  notes: BURHAN:v4p297/r6
- 87:6/q21/p3 The Prophet loved al-Aʿlā and recited it at Eid and Friday prayers, sometimes with al-Ghāshiya on the same day.
  reasons: Qutb says the sūra is Meccan and has nineteen verses, citing 88:1.
  holders (1 notes): QUTB-ZILAL
  notes: QUTB-ZILAL:87:1/r1
sources: BURHAN = al-Zarkashī, d. 794; QURTUBI-FULL = al-Qurṭubī, d. 671; QUTB-ZILAL = Sayyid Quṭb, d. 1386
