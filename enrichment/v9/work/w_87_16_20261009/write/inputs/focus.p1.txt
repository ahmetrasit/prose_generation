# 87:16/q05 What does تُؤْثِرُونَ mean? [تُؤْثِرُونَ · meaning]
- 87:16/q05/p1 It means choosing or giving priority to one thing over alternatives.
  reasons: Al-Biqāʿī glosses it as choosing and privileging; Ibn ʿĀshūr defines īthār as selection from several things and here as giving the world attention and concern.
  holders (2 notes): BIQAI; IBNASHUR
  notes: BIQAI:87:16/r2 IBNASHUR:87:16/r10
sources: BIQAI = al-Biqāʿī, d. 885; IBNASHUR = Ibn ʿĀshūr, d. 1393

# 87:16/q06 Whom does the rebuke address? [تُؤْثِرُونَ · referent]
turns on: Whether the second-person speech is directed narrowly at disbelievers, more widely at human beings or Muslims, or takes its subject from the preceding 'most wretched'; some supply an implicit 'say'.
- 87:16/q06/p1 Unbelievers, especially the polytheists or Meccan disbelievers in context.
  reasons: Al-Jishumī reads it after commands to obey; Ibn ʿAṭiyya says disbelievers deny an afterlife. Al-Ālūsī cites 10:7 for their satisfaction with this world. Elmalılı cites 10:7–8, 2:200 and 87:12–13. Ibn ʿĀshūr says they refuse reminders about the hereafter. Al-Nasafī and Abū Ḥayyān identify unbelievers, and Iṣlāḥī singles out Quraysh's worldly interests.

# 87:16/q07 What is preferred over what in this verse? [تُؤْثِرُونَ ٱلْحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنْيَا · interpretation]
turns on: The omitted comparison can be stated as the Hereafter, its deeds and reward, God's rights, or spiritual purification; the sources develop different aspects of the same worldly priority.
- 87:16/q07/p1 People favor this present life over the Hereafter.
  reasons: Al-Ṭabarī and Qatāda pair this with 87:17: the Hereafter is better in goodness and longer-lasting. Al-Qurṭubī's Abū Mūsā says seeing it would end wavering. Kur'an Yolu cites 7:169, 12:109 and 93:4. Ibn ʿĀshūr says the comparison is unstated because the world implies its alternative.
  holders (13 notes): MUQATIL; TAB(قتادة); TAB-FULL(قتادة); WAHIDI-QT; WAHIDI-WAJIZ; IBNKATHIR; DURR(قتادة); DURR-FULL(قتادة); IBNASHUR; ISLAHI-TADABBUR; KURANYOLU-TEFSIR
  notes: MUQATIL:87:14-19/r2 TAB:87:16/r1 TAB:87:16/r2 TAB-FULL:v24p322#2/r1 WAHIDI-QT:87:16/r1 WAHIDI-WAJIZ:87:7-16/r12 IBNKATHIR:87:16/r1 DURR:87:16/r4 DURR:87:17/r4 DURR-FULL:v8p487/r5 IBNASHUR:87:16/r11 ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p271/r3 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:87:16/r1
- 87:16/q07/p2 People favor worldly actions and pursuits over deeds for the Hereafter and the means of success.
  reasons: Al-Wāḥidī transmits al-Kalbī's contrast of actions; al-Rāzī contrasts worldly and afterlife work. Al-Kashshāf, al-Bayḍāwī and al-Nasafī say this neglects what brings success, happiness, or salvation. Al-Ṭabrisī says people work and build for this world. Elmalılı contrasts amusement and spending with purification, charity, remembrance and prayer.
