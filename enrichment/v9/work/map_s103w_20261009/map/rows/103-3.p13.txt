[SAMARRAI-ASRAR:r17#2/r16] SAMARRAI-ASRAR · author | holds | The exception here requires faith and good works, whereas al-ʿAṣr adds mutual exhortation to truth and patience; that added practice prevents further loss of reward. | mentions 103:1, 103:2, 103:3

[SAMARRAI-ASRAR:r17#2/r18] SAMARRAI-ASRAR · author | reports | Al-Tibyān distinguishes duties one performs personally from the additional duty or merit of enjoining right and forbidding wrong, which can be individual, communal, or recommended. | mentions 103:3
[SAMARRAI-ASRAR:r17#2/r19] SAMARRAI-ASRAR · author | reports | Exhortation covers obligatory and recommended truth and patience; those who do it gain extra reward, while those who only meet personal duties lose that extra gain without being utterly ruined. | mentions 103:3
