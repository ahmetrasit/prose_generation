[ELMALILI:95:4/r1] ELMALILI, d. 1361 · author | holds | God created the human species in the best form of bodily and spiritual shaping, ordering, and development; the phrase is a stable adverbial state of the human being.
[ELMALILI:95:4/r10] ELMALILI, d. 1361 · author | holds | Human beauty is not merely outward form: it lies in feeling and recognizing beauty, knowing God as the absolutely beautiful, and acquiring divine character; this is humanity's intended perfection.
[ELMALILI:95:4/r11] ELMALILI, d. 1361 · author | holds | Humans are created with the capacity to rise toward this perfection, not born already complete; otherwise no mortality or lowliness would be observable.
[ELMALILI:95:4/r12] ELMALILI, d. 1361 · author | holds | People begin with burdens and imperfections to shed: some purify themselves and rise, while many lose their share of beauty and sink toward the lowest ranks.

[ELMALILI:95:4/r2] ELMALILI, d. 1361 · author | holds | Taqwīm means straightening, ordering, strengthening, or valuing; the indefinite wording intensifies the phrase to encompass every kind of excellence.

[ELMALILI:95:4/r4] ELMALILI, d. 1361 · author | reports | Another reported gloss is the straightness of the human stature.

[ELMALILI:95:4/r9] ELMALILI, d. 1361 · علي | reports | A saying attributed to ʿAlī says that a person's remedy and disease are within, and that the greater world is folded inside the supposedly small human body.
[ELMALILI:95:6-8/r13] ELMALILI, d. 1361 · author | holds | The surah establishes human creation and religion, summarizes the preceding surahs, and prepares for the next; beginning the next surah with Read in your Lord's name is fitting because religion is true and God is the best judge.

[IBNASHUR:95:4/r12] IBNASHUR, d. 1393 · author | holds | The intended excellence is sound human perception and rational judgment, since bodily actions issue from the intellect and the body serves it; intellect is humanity's noblest distinguishing gift.

[IBNASHUR:95:4/r14] IBNASHUR, d. 1393 · author | holds | The exception for believers supports an inward reading: faith follows the intellect's sound constitution, which guides upright conduct and good dealings with people who do not oppose it. | mentions 95:6
[IBNASHUR:95:4/r15] IBNASHUR, d. 1393 · author | holds | The original constitution can be impaired before birth by parental illness or injury during pregnancy, and after birth by severe disease, deranged temperament, intoxicants, or drugs that weaken reason and resolve.
[IBNASHUR:95:4/r16] IBNASHUR, d. 1393 · author | holds | Humans are created with complete human fitrah: sound senses deliver truthful perceptions, and a sound mind analyzes and combines them correctly, yielding upright conduct if not diverted or if false influences are resisted.
[IBNASHUR:95:4/r17] IBNASHUR, d. 1393 · author | holds | People can be led astray by vanity and desire or by misleaders using fear and inducement; repeated imitation becomes habitual until they forget what is true and right.
