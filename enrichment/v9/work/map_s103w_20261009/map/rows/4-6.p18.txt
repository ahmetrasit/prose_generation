[IBNASHUR-FULL:v4p239/r5] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · author | holds | The wording makes puberty the default endpoint of guardianship only when sound judgment is found, preventing guardians from keeping wards' property for their own benefit.
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v4p239/r6] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · author | prefers | Both puberty and sound judgment must occur before property is delivered; their order of occurrence does not matter, the position of the Malikis and Imam al-Haramayn, which the author endorses.
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v4p239/r7] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · ثعلب | reports | As transmitted by al-Zajjaji, Tha'lab held that the second condition in wording is first in occurrence; Ibn Malik and al-Baghawi among the Shafi'is also chose this view.

[IBNASHUR-FULL:v4p239/r9] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · إمام الحرمين | prefers | Imam al-Haramayn rejects any significance to ordering the conditions; the author agrees, noting possible effects in conditional oaths and emancipation or divorce, not in reports or rulings here.
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v4p242/r1] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · author | holds | Choosing anistum rather than alimtum suggests that property should be handed over as soon as the first sign of sound judgment is known, without delay.
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v4p242/r2] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · author | holds | Rushd has two synonymous vocalizations and means ordered rational conduct; here it specifically means protecting and managing property well, as in the earlier testing.
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v4p242/r3] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · الفقهاء | reports | Jurists assign delivery to the guardian, who is responsible for establishing the ward's sound management by entrusting the property.
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v4p242/r4] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · اللخمي | reports | A guardian appointed by a father or judge should not be accepted as having certified the ward's maturity without investigation, given possible collusion and corruption.
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v4p242/r5] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · ابن عطية | reports | Ibn Atiyya likewise calls for court confirmation of a ward's sound judgment because guardians may collude with an immature ward to secure their own release.
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v4p242/r6] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · author | holds | Although this caution did not become general practice, notaries commend documenting maturity for a father's appointee; the sound view for a judge's appointee requires proof before the judge, as practiced.
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v4p242/r7] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · author | holds | The address is to the community generally, implemented by the officials responsible for wards and guardians; that official is the judge.
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v4p242/r8] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · author | holds | The verse's hatta indicates that testing and perceiving judgment precede puberty, the established school position; another view forbids giving any property for testing before puberty.
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v4p243/r1] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · author | holds | Property is withheld unless both maturity and sound judgment are present; the general Muslim scholarly consensus requires both.
