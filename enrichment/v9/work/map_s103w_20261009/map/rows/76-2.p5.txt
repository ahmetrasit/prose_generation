[BURSEVI:v10p200#3/r3] BURSEVI, d. 1127 · جعفر الصادق | reports | Ja‘far al-Sadiq glosses the question as asking whether there was ever a time when God was not remembering the human.
[BURSEVI:v10p200#3/r4] BURSEVI, d. 1127 · author | holds | ‘We created the human’ means the body; explicit naming reinforces the statement. The nutfa develops into a clot at forty days, a morsel at eighty, and receives the spirit at one hundred twenty.
[BURSEVI:v10p200#3/r5] BURSEVI, d. 1127 · الضحاك عن ابن عباس | reports | A report from al-Dahhak through Ibn Abbas says Adam’s creation proceeded through forty years each in clay, dark mud, and baked clay before the spirit was breathed into him; what took Adam years took descendants days.
[BURSEVI:v10p200#3/r6] BURSEVI, d. 1127 · author | prefers | Some take the first human as Adam and the second as his descendants, with ‘time’ an unknown long duration; the author prefers ‘human’ to mean the species in both places, recalling its origin from nothing and humble fluid to rebut denial of resurrection.
[BURSEVI:v10p200#3/r7] BURSEVI, d. 1127 · author | holds | Amshaj means mixed elements; it is a plural-form adjective for the single nutfa because the intended matter combines male and female fluids, whose colors, consistencies, and properties differ.
[BURSEVI:v10p200#3/r8] BURSEVI, d. 1127 · author | holds | The author says the male fluid is white and thick and the female fluid yellow and thin; the child resembles whichever prevails, with nerves, bones, and strength from the male and flesh, blood, and hair from the female, according to a Prophetic report.
[BURSEVI:v10p200#3/r9] BURSEVI, d. 1127 · رسول الله | reports | A report says each newborn has some of the soil of its burial place mixed into its nutfa.
[BURSEVI:v10p201/r1] BURSEVI, d. 1127 · قتادة | reports | Qatada says amshaj means colors and stages.
[BURSEVI:v10p201/r2] BURSEVI, d. 1127 · التأويلات النجمية | reports | Inwardly, the mixed nutfa is the interweaving of receptive potential and active power, represented by the most holy and holy effusions, respectively likened to male and female fluids.
[BURSEVI:v10p201/r3] BURSEVI, d. 1127 · author | holds | ‘Testing him’ is a prospective state: God intends to test through future obligations, making people’s acceptance, rejection, felicity, and misery manifest in detail after being known generally.
[BURSEVI:v10p201/r4] BURSEVI, d. 1127 · author | holds | Hearing and sight equip a person to hear revealed signs and witness created signs, enabling the intended test; intellect is omitted because the passage names its instruments and usual route to completion.
[BURSEVI:v10p201/r5] BURSEVI, d. 1127 · author | holds | The intensive forms signal the completeness of God’s favor; ‘seeing’ is the second object after the first object of ‘made.’
[BURSEVI:v10p201/r6] BURSEVI, d. 1127 · التأويلات النجمية | reports | Inwardly, God becomes the servant’s hearing and sight: by Him the servant hears and sees every audible and visible thing, with nothing escaping perception.
[BURSEVI:v10p201/r7] BURSEVI, d. 1127 · أبو عثمان المغربي | reports | Abu Uthman al-Maghribi describes nine inner mixtures: hearing, sight, and tongue tempt; ego, desire, and Satan oppose faith; intellect, spirit, and heart believe.
[BURSEVI:v10p201/r8] BURSEVI, d. 1127 · أبو عثمان المغربي | reports | With divine aid, intellect masters the heart, captures ego and desire, and makes the self kin to spirit and desire kin to intellect, so God’s word prevails; he cites the command to fight until there is no fitna. | mentions 8:39
