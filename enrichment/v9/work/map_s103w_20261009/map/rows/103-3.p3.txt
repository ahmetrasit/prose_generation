[RAZI-FULL:v32p84#3/r6] RAZI-FULL, d. 606 · author | holds | Alternatively, loss is stated broadly while profit is detailed and clarified, a presentation befitting divine generosity.
[RAZI-FULL:v32p84#3/r7] RAZI-FULL, d. 606 · author | holds | Believers urge others to their way because their strong love of obedience makes them seek others' obedience too; exhortation to truth covers all religion, knowledge and practice. | mentions 66:6
