[SAMIN-DURR:2:282#9/r4] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · ابن عطية | reports | Ibn Atiyya makes the conditional and answer a nominative adjective of the two women; the Sheikh objects to the awkward order and possible intervening phrase.

[SAMIN-DURR:2:282/r1] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | prefers | إلى أجل attaches to تداينتم; attaching it to an omitted adjective of دين is possible but weak because it fronts an interpreted adjective before an explicit one.

[SAMIN-DURR:2:282/r3] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | دنت الرجل means selling to him on credit, while أدنته means borrowing from him; the two patterns differentiate the directions.
[SAMIN-DURR:2:282/r4] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | reports | The pronoun in فاكتبوه returns to دين; that noun is repeated to provide an antecedent, disambiguate reciprocal تداينوا, or indicate every amount of debt.
