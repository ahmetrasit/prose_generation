[IBNKHALAWAYH-HUJJA:v1p104/r2] IBNKHALAWAYH-HUJJA, d. 370 · author | holds | فتذكر may be doubled or light and jussive, indicative, or subjunctive: it can continue the conditional, begin a clause after fa, or be governed by the open أن.
[FARISI-HUJJA:v2p418/r1] FARISI-HUJJA, d. 377 · author | reports | Hamza alone reads إن تضل with kasra and فتذكر geminated in the nominative; the others open أن and use accusative فتذكر, with Ibn Kathir and Abu Amr lightening its kāf.
[FARISI-HUJJA:v2p418/r2] FARISI-HUJJA, d. 377 · author | rejects | أن تضل cannot attach directly to استشهدوا شهيدين; the following alternative of one man and two women signals an omitted command to call those witnesses.
[FARISI-HUJJA:v2p418/r3] FARISI-HUJJA, d. 377 · أبو الحسن | prefers | Abu al-Hasan supplies فليكن رجل وامرأتان; Abu Ali calls this a good analysis because it gives أن تضل an implied verb that raises the indefinite and governs the clause.
[FARISI-HUJJA:v2p418/r4] FARISI-HUJJA, d. 377 · author | holds | Both an auxiliary كان with a predicate and a complete كان meaning occurrence can be supplied; the complete verb requires less ellipsis.
[FARISI-HUJJA:v2p420/r1] FARISI-HUJJA, d. 377 · author | holds | A predicate-taking كان may be omitted because its mention is recoverable from earlier wording; an existential كان is also possible and is simpler because it requires only one omitted element.
[FARISI-HUJJA:v2p420/r2] FARISI-HUJJA, d. 377 · author | holds | On either analysis, the phrase means that testimony by one man and two women should occur, not that the man and women themselves should occur; the noun testimony is omitted and its complement stands for it.
[FARISI-HUJJA:v2p420/r3] FARISI-HUJJA, d. 377 · author | holds | A third attachment is possible: أن تضل may depend on the predicate of an implied nominal clause, in addition to the implied command to witness or the verb فليشهد.
