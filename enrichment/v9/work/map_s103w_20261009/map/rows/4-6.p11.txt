[QURTUBI-FULL:4:6/r6] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · author | holds | Speaking kindly means gentle address and reassuring promises; interpretations include prayers for the child and promising delivery of the estate once he is prudent.

[BAYDAWI-FULL:4:6/r7] BAYDAWI-FULL, d. 685 · author | holds | Guardians must not consume orphan wealth wastefully or hastily before the children grow; this may describe the guardian's own extravagance and haste.
