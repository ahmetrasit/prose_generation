[RAZI-FULL:v12p194#2/r1] RAZI-FULL, d. 606 · ابن عباس | reports | Ibn Abbas, Hasan, and Mujahid take calling upon God at dawn and evening to mean the prescribed dawn and afternoon prayers; another explanation treats the two ends of the day as shorthand for keeping all five prayers.

[RAZI-FULL:v12p194/r2] RAZI-FULL, d. 606 · author | holds | Calling their dismissal wrongful means the poor deserved honor; expelling them from that gathering wronged them by leaving the more fitting and excellent course, not by violating a duty.
