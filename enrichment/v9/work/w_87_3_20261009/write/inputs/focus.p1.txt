# 87:3/q03 What is God said to guide creatures toward? [فَهَدَىٰ · interpretation]
turns on: Whether the phrase is unrestricted or names a particular kind of guidance, and whether religious guidance is read as its principal sense.
- 87:3/q03/p1 Every creature is guided toward its allotted function, course, or benefit; particular examples do not limit the verse.
  reasons: Al-Ṭabarī, Ibn ʿAṭiyya, Abū Ḥayyān, Ālūsī, and Elmalılı expressly keep the scope general. Al-Ṭabarī cites lack of evidence for restriction; Elmalılı cites 20:50. Al-Bayḍāwī, al-Biqāʿī, and Bursawī name inclination, inspiration, signs, and revelation. Ibn ʿĀshūr gives speech, craft, grazing, nursing, and bee architecture; al-Biqāʿī says ordainment needs guidance for completeness. Elmalılı includes planetary orbits, attraction, gases, stones, crystals, salt, coal, and cells. Qutb says provision accompanies creatures throughout life; al-Ṭabāṭabāʾī cites 15:21, 80:20, and 2:148.
