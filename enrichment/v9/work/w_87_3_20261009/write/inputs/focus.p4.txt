# 87:3/q13 Does 'Great' alongside 'Highest' imply more than one deity? [* · theology]
- 87:3/q13/p1 No; God's oneness is established, and the wording does not say He is higher than another lord.
  reasons: Al-Rāzī rejects the objection to the opening description of God.
  holders (1 notes): RAZI-FULL
  notes: RAZI-FULL:v31p125#2/r1
sources: RAZI-FULL = Fakhr al-Dīn al-Rāzī, d. 606

# 87:3/q17 How does varying related Qur'anic expressions convey meaning? [* · rhetoric]
turns on: Which purpose of expressive variation is being discussed.
- 87:3/q17/p1 Taṣrīf expresses one meaning through varied, connected meanings and forms, clarifying it from several sides.
  reasons: Al-Rummānī illustrates derivatives of ملك, from owner to king and dominion, and عرض, from appearance and turning away to poetic meter; the linked senses illuminate a shared core.
  holders (4 notes): RUMMANI
  notes: RUMMANI:v1p101/r1 RUMMANI:v1p101/r2 RUMMANI:v1p101/r3 RUMMANI:v1p101/r4
- 87:3/q17/p2 The varied telling of Moses' story sustains eloquence, strengthens admonition, and resolves doubts about the miracle.
  reasons: Al-Rummānī names al-Aʿrāf, Ṭā Hā, and al-Shuʿarāʾ and gives these three purposes.
  holders (4 notes): RUMMANI
  notes: RUMMANI:v1p101/r5 RUMMANI:v1p101/r6 RUMMANI:v1p101/r7 RUMMANI:v1p101/r8

# 87:3/q18 How extensive and beneficial is divine guidance? [* · interpretation]
- 87:3/q18/p1 It extends through religious and worldly human needs and through the beneficial instincts of other creatures.
  reasons: Al-Zamakhsharī says its range in humans, beasts, birds, and other creatures exceeds description. Al-Naḥḥās reports that God guides creatures to their interests.
