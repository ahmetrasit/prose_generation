# 87:3/q04 Does فَهَدَىٰ also imply 'led astray'? [فَهَدَىٰ · rhetoric]
turns on: Whether the single named act conventionally carries its opposite by ellipsis.
- 87:3/q04/p1 Yes; 'led astray' is left unspoken because guidance evokes its opposite.
  reasons: Al-Zajjāj's grammarians cite 14:4; al-Rāzī and al-Qurṭubī compare 16:81, which names protection from heat without naming cold; al-Nasafī cites 16:93 and 35:8. Al-Farrāʾ is repeatedly named as proponent.
