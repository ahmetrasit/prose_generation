# 87:3/q01 How is قَدَّرَ read? [قَدَّرَ · readings]
turns on: Whether the light form merely shares the doubled form's sense or also conveys ability or ownership.
- 87:3/q01/p1 The majority double the dāl, reading qaddara.
  reasons: Al-Farrāʾ and al-Ṭabarī prefer this by the readers' agreement or consensus. Ibn ʿAṭiyya allows decree or measured proportion; al-Qurṭubī relates it to estimation (25:2). Al-Samīn and Rāzī compare the forms in 77:23.

# 87:3/q02 What does قَدَّرَ mean? [قَدَّرَ · meaning]
turns on: Whether the determination names the universal assignment of measure or a particular allotted feature; the light reading also permits power or ownership.
- 87:3/q02/p1 God assigned each created thing its particular measure, properties, capacities, acts, circumstances, and destiny.
  reasons: Al-Rāzī cites 15:21 and lists essence, size, duration, place, beauty, guidance and misguidance. Elmalılı cites 65:3 and 15:21, stressing divine knowledge and will and the unknown secret of destiny; al-Ṭabāṭabāʾī describes particular existence arising from an undetermined plenitude and cites 15:21, 54:49, 25:2, 57:22, and 64:11. Ibn ʿĀshūr says measure starts with length, capacity, weight, or count and here means ordered organs and powers. Al-Biqāʿī gives hand, leg, ear, and eye examples. Al-Bayḍāwī, Bursawī, and Ālūsī include kinds, species, individuals, qualities, acts, and lifespans.
  holders (25 notes): TAB; NAHHAS; JISHUMI; RAZI(الجمهور); RAZI-FULL; BAYDAWI; BAYDAWI-FULL; BIQAI; BIQAI-FULL; BURSEVI; ALUSI; ALUSI-FULL; ELMALILI; IBNASHUR; IBNASHUR-FULL; TABATABAI; KURANYOLU-TEFSIR(Şevkânî)
  notes: TAB:87:3/r1 NAHHAS:v5p203/r14 JISHUMI:v10p7372/r7 RAZI:87:3/r1 RAZI:87:3/r4 RAZI-FULL:v31p126/r6 RAZI-FULL:v31p126/r8 BAYDAWI:87:3/r1 BAYDAWI-FULL:87:1-3/r5 BIQAI:87:3/r1 BIQAI-FULL:v8p395#2/r1 BURSEVI:v10p313/r7 ALUSI:87:3/r1 ALUSI-FULL:v30p104/r1 ELMALILI:87:3/r1 ELMALILI:87:3/r2 ELMALILI:87:3/r10 ELMALILI:87:3/r11 ELMALILI:87:3/r12 IBNASHUR:87:2/r11 IBNASHUR:87:2/r9 IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p276/r2 IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p276/r4 TABATABAI:2:21-25#19/r4 KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:87:1/r4
- 87:3/q02/p2 It means forming or shaping a creature, especially in the womb.
  reasons: Al-Dāmghānī calls it shaping well in the womb; al-Māwardī describes formation there and emergence fully formed. Study Quran reports the human-specific account of forming the first human after breathing in the Spirit and providing nourishment.
  holders (4 notes): DAMGHANI; MAWARDI(unattributed); MAWARDI-FULL; STUDYQURAN

- 87:3/q02/p3 It means assigning a particular provision, sustenance, or useful benefit.
  reasons: ʿAṭāʾ says every animal receives what suits it; al-Wāḥidī's additional report has God determine the offspring He wills. The accounts of livelihood distinguish assigned food from God's placement of useful properties in things.
