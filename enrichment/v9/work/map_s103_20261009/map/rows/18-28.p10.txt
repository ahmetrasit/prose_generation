[ALUSI-FULL:v15p307/r7] ALUSI-FULL, d. 1270 · author | reports | Another narration urges loving and sitting with the poor; their company teaches gratitude and contentment and avoids flattery, though it is hard on the self and thus greatly rewarded.

[ALUSI-FULL:v15p308#2/r1] ALUSI-FULL, d. 1270 · author | holds | The warning against obeying the heedless originally addressed people seeking to expel the poor, but its wording applies generally to all matters driven by the lower self.

[ALUSI-FULL:v15p308/r4] ALUSI-FULL, d. 1270 · author | holds | The wealthy may burden poor companions with reproach, even through the mere act of keeping them company; al-Zamakhsharī quips that favors turn bitter with reproach.
