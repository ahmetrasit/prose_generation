22:12/q18 What governs the order of meanings and words in composition? [* · rhetoric] (turns on: Whether composition is explained from the speaker's prior grasp of meanings or from isolated words and the listener's sequence of reception.)
  22:12/q18/p1 The composing speaker arranges understood meanings first, and verbal signs follow that thought. — reasons: Al-Jurjani rejects isolated-word thinking: without meaning there is nothing to think about, and thinking of words apart from meaning is implausible. 22:12/q21 What makes the Qur'an miraculous? [* · theology]
  22:12/q21/p1 The whole Qur'an, including its shortest surah, is miraculous through its form and contents. — reasons: Al-Alusi names order, eloquence, unseen reports, rational accord and subtle meaning; some aspects can be hidden while others suffice. On the no-contradiction account, ordinary fine sermons or poetry and short surahs do not negate the miracle of the complete Qur'an. Other scholars cite its awe, growing love on repetition, lasting presence and God's promise of preservation.
