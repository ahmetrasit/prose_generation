23:103/q17 How should rhetorical merit in an expression be identified? [* · rhetoric]
  23:103/q17/p1 It requires close thought about meaning and composition, including hints, allusion, metonymy and indirection. — reasons: Al-Jurjani says rhetorical scholars use indirect expression requiring the reader to uncover the point.
