  69:3/q10/p2 God's promise and threat become true at the Resurrection, so it is the Day of truth. — reasons: Al-Jishumī reports Abū Muslim on the fulfillment of promise and threat and al-Kisāʾī's 'Day of truth.'
  69:3/q10/p3 The realities of deeds are disclosed and their recompense becomes due there. — reasons: Al-Jishumī reports Ibn ʿAbbās on deeds becoming clear and Ibn al-Anbārī on each person's necessary recompense; Bursawī reports al-Rāghib's figurative attribution of the name to the day on which recompense becomes due; Qatādah says workers realize their deeds.
  69:3/q10/p4 The Day overpowers the unbelievers. — reasons: Al-Jishumī lists this among explanations of the name alongside the Day of truth and an undeniable true event.
  69:3/q10/p5 In its inward sense, it is true because the singular manifestation is established in itself. — reasons: Al-Taʾwīlāt al-Najmiyyah, transmitted by Bursawī, applies the name to the One's manifestation and cites 40:16.
69:3/q11 Why repeat al-Ḥāqqah in the question instead of using a pronoun? [ٱلْحَآقَّةُ · rhetoric]
  69:3/q11/p1 The explicit repetition magnifies the event and evokes grandeur, wonder, or terror. — reasons: Al-Naḥḥās says it adds grandeur and emphasis; al-Māwardī, al-Qushayrī, and al-Wāḥidī emphasize exaltation. Al-Wāḥidī reports Ṣāḥib al-Naẓm's comparison with 101:1–2 and cites Imruʾ al-Qays's poetry for awe conveyed by an interrogative. Al-Thaʿlabī describes wonder, compares asking 'What is Zayd?', and mentions 101:1 and 56:27. Bursawī says explicit noun for pronoun underscores majesty and terror and that the question asks its state and character. Study Quran compares 101:2–3 and says repetition exceeds rational comprehension.
69:3/q12 How does this question join the preceding clause? [* · grammar]
  69:3/q12/p1 The question as a whole coordinates with the preceding short clause, 'What is al-Ḥāqqah?' — reasons: Ālūsī explicitly gives coordination; Ibn ʿĀshūr says the sentence may coordinate with the prior question.
69:3/q13 How does the opening connect to the preceding surah? [* · links]
  69:3/q13/p1 It develops the preceding warning by describing Resurrection, its rewards and punishments, and destroyed peoples as warnings. — reasons: Al-Jishumī links it to al-Qalam's Resurrection warning and command to endure abuse. Abū Ḥayyān links it to the previous surah's fortunate, wretched, and deniers, cites 68:44, and says past peoples warn the Meccans.
69:3/q14 What calamity did Thamūd and ʿĀd deny in the following verses? [* · links] (turns on: Whether their denial concerns the messengers' threatened historical calamity or the Resurrection.)
  69:3/q14/p1 The calamity promised through their messengers, which struck them. — reasons: Al-Thaʿlabī gives this account before reporting the alternative identification with the Resurrection.
  69:3/q14/p2 The Resurrection, named the Striker in the following passage. — reasons: Al-Thaʿlabī reports Ibn ʿAbbās and Qatādah for the identification; Ibn ʿĀshūr proposes it if the clauses are connected and says 'the Striker' replaces a pronoun to disclose the event's terrifying force.
69:3/q15 What is the Striker in the following verse, and why name it? [* · links]
  69:3/q15/p1 The Striker is the Hour, synonymous with al-Ḥāqqah; naming it exposes its terrifying force. — reasons: Al-Durr transmits Qatādah's identification of al-Qāriʿah with the Hour. Ibn ʿĀshūr says the explicit synonym replaces a pronoun to explain its terror when the clauses are connected.
69:3/q16 How do deniers react to the reported event? [* · interpretation]
  69:3/q16/p1 Because reason cannot grasp it, they dismiss divine report as superstition, fable, poetry, invention, or deception. — reasons: Elmalılı describes these dismissals by those who reject the transmitted report of Resurrection.
69:3/q17 When was this surah revealed? [* · sciences]
  69:3/q17/p1 It is Meccan, specifically dated by some to the late Meccan period shortly after al-Mulk. — reasons: Abū Ḥayyān calls it Meccan; Study Quran describes the late Meccan dating and placement shortly after al-Mulk.
69:3/q18 What merit is reported for reciting Sūrat al-Ḥāqqah? [* · sciences]
  69:3/q18/p1 The reciter will receive an easy reckoning from God. — reasons: Al-Jishumī transmits the report from Ubayy ibn Kaʿb from the Prophet.
