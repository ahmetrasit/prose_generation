[TABATABAI:70:19-35/r3] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | holds | There is no contradiction in saying people are created with haluʿ while condemning it: the blame concerns human misuse, as with other created blessings.
[TABATABAI:70:19-35/r4] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · الزمخشري | reports | Al-Zamakhshari avoids attributing a blameworthy act to God by treating the wording as a metaphor: people favor panic and withholding so strongly that they seem innately stamped with them.
[TABATABAI:70:19-35/r5] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · الزمخشري | reports | Al-Zamakhshari argues the believers' exception shows the trait is not literally created in everyone.
[TABATABAI:70:19-35/r6] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | rejects | The author rejects the metaphorical escape: the trait is a created blessing, while blame falls on people who mismanage it; believers preserve its good form rather than lack it.
[TABATABAI:70:19-35/r7] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · الزمخشري | reports | Al-Zamakhshari cites believers who struggle against themselves and escape panic and withholding as evidence for his metaphorical reading.
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-8-english:p463/r8] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · author | holds | People generally become frustrated and complain when God punishes them even a little for their deeds.
