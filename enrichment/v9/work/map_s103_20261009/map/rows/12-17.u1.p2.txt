12:17/q23 Do the brothers' tears establish that their story is true? [قَالُوا۟ · interpretation]
  12:17/q23/p1 No; tears can be produced deliberately and so cannot prove a claim. — reasons: Qurtubi says some can manufacture tears and others cannot; he reports a sage's saying that tears mingled on the cheeks distinguish real weeping from pretense.

  12:17/q24/p1 The dialogue resumes after an implied question, 'What did they say?', which their address to Jacob answers. — reasons: Burhan treats the apparent sequence as a resumption with the intervening question omitted.
