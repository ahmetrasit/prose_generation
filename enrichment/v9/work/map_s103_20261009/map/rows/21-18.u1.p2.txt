  21:18/q22/p1 An anecdote suggests angels recite al-Fatiha alone. — reasons: A man saw Umar pray at night with only al-Fatiha, then glorify, bow and prostrate; Umar called it the angels' prayer. Suyuti infers that angels were permitted to recite al-Fatiha alone.

  21:18/q23/p1 Hearing its opening or completion earns merit likened to witnessing battle or distribution of spoils. — reasons: Abu Qilaba transmits this from the Prophet.
  21:18/q23/p2 Reciting it with another sura before sleep brings an angel companion on waking. — reasons: Suyuti reports this Prophetic saying.
21:18/q24 What is reported about al-Fatiha in prayer? [* · law]
  21:18/q24/p1 Prayer without al-Fatiha is invalid or deficient; al-Fatiha can substitute for other recitation but nothing substitutes for it. — reasons: Suyuti reports the Prophet's 'no prayer' ruling, the Mother of the Quran's unique sufficiency, Abu Hurayra's deficient/deceptive prayer report, and a threefold repetition that prayer without the Mother of the Book is deficient.
