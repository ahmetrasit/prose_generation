[RAZI-FULL:v2p62#2/r5] RAZI-FULL, d. 606 · author | reports | If “their devils” means senior hypocrites, junior hypocrites tell believers they believe but reassure those leaders that they remain with them; if it means unbelievers, the claim may come from all hypocrites.
