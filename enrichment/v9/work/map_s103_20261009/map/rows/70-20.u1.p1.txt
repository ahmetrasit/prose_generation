70:20/q09 How does the passage fit between the hoarder and the worshippers? [* · links]
  70:20/q09/p1 It interrupts the sequence to summarize the human disposition that drives greed and retention, while the worshippers are excepted from its blame. — reasons: Ibn ʿĀshūr places verses 19–21 between the hoarder of 70:17 and the exception at 70:22; he says fear of losing benefits explains the trait but does not excuse hoarding. The Study Quran says those with the eight qualities listed in 70:22–35 escape this blameworthy state. Al-Nasafī says the exception supports taking 'human' as the species.
70:20/q10 What other Qur'anic passages are compared with this description? [* · links]
  70:20/q10/p1 It echoes 41:49–51: people ask for good, despair when harmed, and withdraw after mercy. — reasons: The Study Quran cites the sequence in 41:49–51.
  70:20/q10/p2 Its account of believers is strongly parallel to al-Muʾminūn 23:1–11, with differences in wording and order. — reasons: Al-Sāmarrāʾī observes both the parallel and the differences across the two passages.
70:20/q11 What does the report about a sick person's groaning say? [جَزُوعًۭا · reports]
  70:20/q11/p1 Groaning is recorded: a patient sick person gains good deeds, but one who despairs is recorded as halūʿ and receives no reward. — reasons: Al-Durr reports this as attributed to ʿAlī.
