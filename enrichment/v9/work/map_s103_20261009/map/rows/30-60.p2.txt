خف means making light of something and is intensified; here it means not letting disbelievers provoke him into abandoning patience, with lightness figuratively expressing panic or visible anger.
[TABATABAI:30:56-60/r8] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | holds | Muhammad is told to endure their taunts because God's promise of victory is true, as also stated in 30:47; the surah begins and ends with the promise of help. | mentions 30:47
[TABATABAI:30:56-60/r9] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | rejects | Al-Ṭabāṭabāʾī rejects the alternative that patience is urged because disbelievers deny the clear signs and distress the Prophet; the promise at both ends of the surah is victory.
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-6-2:p60/r5] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · author | holds | Verses 46–60 cite historical proof of divine help; only people of intellect receive the message, so the Prophet must persevere and await God’s promise despite stubborn rejection.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:30:59/r3] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | The surah closes by urging patience and trust in God against deniers; the Prophet and believers are comforted and told to remain steadfast despite their pressure.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:30:59/r4] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | The final command means to continue calling people to faith without letting them unsettle, belittle, or fill the caller with doubt and anxiety.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:30:59/r5] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · Derveze | reports | Given the verses' timing as the Prophet prepared intellectually for migration to Medina, they may warn against acting before conditions are sufficiently mature.
