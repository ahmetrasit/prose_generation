[QURTUBI-FULL:102:1-2#3/r3] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · أبو هريرة | reports | A report in al-Tirmidhi says the Prophet cursed women who frequently visit graves; the chapter also cites Ibn Abbas and Hassan ibn Thabit.
[QURTUBI-FULL:102:1-2#3/r4] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · أبو عيسى | reports | Abu Isa grades the report about women who frequently visit graves Hasan Sahih.
[QURTUBI-FULL:102:1-2#3/r5] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · بعض أهل العلم | reports | Some scholars say the curse came before the Prophet later permitted grave visits, after which the permission included women as well as men.
[QURTUBI-FULL:102:1-2#3/r6] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · بعضهم | reports | Others restrict the disapproval to women because they are thought to have less patience and greater grief.
[QURTUBI-FULL:102:1-2#3/r7] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · author | holds | Men's grave visits are agreed upon, while women's visits are disputed; Qurtubi permits older women and allows women to go when separated from men.
[QURTUBI-FULL:102:1-2#3/r8] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · author | holds | Where a mixed gathering risks temptation, visits are unlawful: either sex may be distracted by the other's presence and leave sinful rather than rewarded.
[QURTUBI-FULL:102:1-2#4/r2] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · author | reports | The report transmitted by Ibn Abbas says direct experience is not like hearing about an event.
[QURTUBI-FULL:102:1-2#4/r3] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · author | holds | A visitor should attend with a present heart, seeking God, repairing the heart, or benefiting the dead with Qur'an and prayer; merely touring graves is an animal-like activity.
[QURTUBI-FULL:102:1-2#4/r4] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · author | holds | Visitors should avoid walking or sitting on graves, greet the cemetery and the known deceased, and face the deceased as they would a living person they address.
