51:53/q14 What is a letter and how do its kinds behave? [* · meaning]
  51:53/q14/p1 A letter is a perceptible distinguishing quality of sound; vowels are long and soft, while consonants vary in their capacity to continue. — reasons: Avicenna, reported by RAZI-FULL, defines the letter as distinguishing equally light or heavy sounds.
