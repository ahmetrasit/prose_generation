# 87:1/q03 Do sabbih isma and sabbih bi-ismi mean the same thing? [سَبِّحِ ٱسْمَ · grammar]
turns on: Whether bi- is freely alternating, semantically distinct, or understood as omitted in an interpretation of prayer.
- 87:1/q03/p1 Both Arabic constructions convey the same glorification.
  reasons: Al-Farra affirms both forms; Thalabi restricts the equivalence to contexts of remembrance and glorification. Razi reports the comparison with 56:74. Samarrai lists Quranic transitive and prepositional patterns.

# 87:1/q04 Is God's name identical with the one named? [ٱسْمَ · theology]
turns on: Whether ism denotes a spoken designation or the divine referent, and whether the customary formula proves identity.
- 87:1/q04/p1 The name is identical with the named God in this context.
  reasons: The author of al-Nazm says only God, not a distinct name, can be glorified. Baghowi and Qurtubi cite 'Glory to God/my Lord,' never 'Glory to God's name'; Sa'di al-Mufti uses the hadith formula likewise.
