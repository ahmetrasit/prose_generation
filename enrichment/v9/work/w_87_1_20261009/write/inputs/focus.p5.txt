# 87:1/q17 What are the sura's names and verse count? [* · sciences]
- 87:1/q17/p1 It is called al-Aʿla and also Sabbih or Subh, and has nineteen verses.
  reasons: Ibn Ashur says al-A'la comes from a word unique to this sura and is favored by commentators and mushaf compilers, while the Prophet's Muadh report and Aisha's witr report use its opening; Baydawi and Ibn Kathir also call it Sabbih. Alusi and Ibn Ashur say the count of nineteen is unanimous.

# 87:1/q18 What merit or reward is reported for the sura or its glorification? [* · reports]
turns on: Distinct transmitted promises and appraisals, with no adjudication in the notes.
- 87:1/q18/p1 The Prophet loved this sura, and it was called the best of the glorification suras.
  reasons: Ali transmits the affection; Ibn Kathir says Ahmad alone transmits one route. Abu Tamim reports that Ubayy supplied its title after the Prophet forgot which glorification sura was best; al-Malawi attributes the love to abundant knowledge and blessings.
