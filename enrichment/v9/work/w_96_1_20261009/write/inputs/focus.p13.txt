turns on: The accounts' details about dreams, repeated embraces, cloth, chest opening, and recitation.
- 96:1/q23/p1 Gabriel commanded reading three times, pressed Muhammad, then recited the opening verses.
  reasons: ʿĀʾisha's report has Gabriel pressing Muhammad three times after each reply that he could not read, then giving the verses.
