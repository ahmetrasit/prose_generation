# 96:1/q38 What wider effect is attributed to this first revelation? [* · interpretation]
- 96:1/q38/p1 It opened a direct connection with heaven and redirected human values and law to revelation.
  reasons: QUTB-ZILAL: For twenty-three years believers experienced an immediate, direct connection with the heavenly realm, awaiting revelations that resolved their concerns and guided their steps; its distance from earthly sources was immense.
