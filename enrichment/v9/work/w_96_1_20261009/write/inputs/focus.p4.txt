# 96:1/q09 What practice does the opening command imply for the basmala? [بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ · law]
turns on: Whether it requires or supports saying the basmala at each sūra opening.
- 96:1/q09/p1 Begin every sūra or Qurʾān recitation with the basmala.
  reasons: Al-Wāḥidī explicitly prefers this inference against omitting the basmala; Rāzī argues the opening requires it at every sūra, and al-Qurṭubī and Ibn ʿAṭiyya report the same practice. BIQAI: The commentators say this indicates that recitation is not complete without invoking God's name; honoring the name honors it
