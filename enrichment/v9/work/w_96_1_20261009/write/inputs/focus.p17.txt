:1/q29 How many verses does the sūra have? [* · sciences]
turns on: Counting traditions differ.
- 96:1/q29/p1 Nineteen in Iraqi or common counts.
  reasons: Al-Baghawī, al-Kashshāf, al-Qurṭubī, al-Bayḍāwī, and al-Nasafī count nineteen. DURR-FULL: The author classifies the chapter as Meccan and counts nineteen verses. ALUSI: Sūrat al-ʿAlaq, also called Sūrat Iqraʾ, is Meccan by consensus; verse counts differ: twenty in the Ḥijāzī count, nineteen Iraqi, and eighteen Syrian. ALUSI-FULL: Surat Iqra is Meccan without dispute, but its verse count differs: twenty in the Hijazi count, nineteen Iraqi, and eighteen Syrian. IBNASHUR: The verse counts are twenty in the Medinan and Meccan counts, eighteen in the Syrian count, and nineteen in the Kufan and Basran counts. IBNASHUR-FULL: Verse-count traditions give twenty verses in Medina and Mecca, eighteen in Syria, and nineteen in Kufa and Basra.
