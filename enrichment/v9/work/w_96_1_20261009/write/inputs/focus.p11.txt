# 96:1/q21 How much of this sūra descended in the first revelation? [* · sciences]
turns on: Whether only the opening five verses or the whole sūra came first.
- 96:1/q21/p1 The first five verses came at Ḥirāʾ; the rest came later.
  reasons: Most exegetes, as al-Thaʿlabī, al-Wāḥidī, and al-Baghawī state, place the first five verses at Ḥirāʾ and the rest later; al-Burhān resolves Bukhārī's shorter wording as abridgment of Muslim's account. BIQAI: While the Prophet was devoted to solitude at Ḥirāʾ, Gabriel came with the first five verses of this sūra, ending with the phrase about what he did not know. DURR (ابن عباس): Ibn ʿAbbās says the first revelation consisted of five verses, ending with “what he did not know.” DURR (الزهري وعمرو بن دينار): Al-Zuhrī and ʿAmr b. Dīnār report that, at Ḥirāʾ, an angel brought the Prophet brocade bearing the opening five verses in writing. DURR (عمرو بن جابر): ʿAmr b. Jābir likewise reports that an angel came to the Prophet at Ḥirāʾ with brocade inscribed through “what he did not know.” DURR (عبد الله بن شداد): ʿAbd Allāh b. Shaddād recounts Gabriel’s repeated command to recite and squeezing of Muhammad, who then received the opening passage through 96:5. DURR (عائشة): A second ʿĀʾisha account describes Gabriel’s vast appearance, washing the Prophet’s opened chest, teaching the opening five verses, and weighing him against men; Michael says his community will follow. DURR (بعض علمائهم): Scholars heard by Muḥammad b. ʿAbbād said the opening through 96:5 was revealed at Ḥirāʾ; the sūra’s ending came later as God willed.
