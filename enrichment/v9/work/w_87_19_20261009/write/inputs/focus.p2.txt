# 87:19/q11 What admonitions are attributed to Moses' scrolls? [صُحُفِ وَمُوسَىٰ · reports]
- 87:19/q11/p1 They challenge those who rejoice despite death, trust a changing world, chafe at destiny, or fail to act despite reckoning.
  reasons: Al-Qurṭubī includes weariness despite belief in decree; al-Ālūsī includes laughter despite certainty of Hell. Al-Durr and its full version present the items together as admonitions or lessons.

# 87:19/q12 When were the named scriptures revealed? [صُحُفِ إِبْرَٰهِيمَ وَمُوسَىٰ · reports]
turns on: The reported days for Psalms, Gospel and especially Qur'an differ; all reports place Abraham's scrolls on Ramadan's first night.
- 87:19/q12/p1 Abraham's scrolls came on Ramadan night one, Torah on six, Psalms on twelve, Gospel on eighteen, Qur'an on twenty-four.
  reasons: Qatāda and a report in Ibn ʿAṭiyya give this sequence, except Ibn ʿAṭiyya reports the Qur'an on fourteen; al-Ṭabrisī reports more generally that all scriptures came during Ramadan.
  holders (4 notes): TAB(قتادة); IBNATIYYA; IBNATIYYA-FULL; TABRISI
  notes: TAB:87:19/r8 IBNATIYYA:87:19/r5 IBNATIYYA-FULL:v5p470#2/r21 TABRISI:87:18-19/r6
- 87:19/q12/p2 Abraham's scrolls came on Ramadan night one, Torah on six, Gospel on thirteen, Psalms on eighteen, Qur'an on twenty-four.
  reasons: Wāthila transmits this sequence from the Prophet.
  holders (1 notes): WAHIDI-ASBAB(واثلة)

# 87:19/q14 Which preceding teaching is said to be in the earlier scriptures? [* · referent]
turns on: The scope of the preceding demonstrative: nearest verses, the whole sūra, its afterlife statement, or the Qur'an.
- 87:19/q14/p1 The teaching of 87:14-17: purification, remembrance and prayer, worldly preference, and the superior enduring afterlife.
  reasons: Al-Ṭabarī prefers the nearest antecedent; Ibn Kathīr calls that a strong choice and reports Qatāda and Ibn Zayd likewise. Al-Baghawī lists the four verses. The Prophet reportedly recited this passage when asked what remained from the scrolls; Ibn ʿĀshūr excludes the opening verses and could not establish that report's grade. Al-Biqāʿī includes glorification in the admonition and says its aim pervades every revealed law and cannot be abrogated.

# 87:19/q15 Is the earlier scripture's wording identical to this passage? [* · interpretation]
turns on: Whether the assertion concerns shared meaning or the same revealed wording.
- 87:19/q15/p1 The same teaching or meaning occurs there, without requiring identical wording.
  reasons: Al-Qurṭubī explicitly separates meaning from exact words. Ibn ʿĀshūr dignifies the enduring teaching through its older attestation, and al-Ṭabarī names its proximate content.
  holders (4 notes): QURTUBI; QURTUBI-FULL; IBNASHUR-FULL; TAB
  notes: QURTUBI:87:19/r1 QURTUBI-FULL:87:18-19/r6 IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p290/r3 TAB:87:19/r6
- 87:19/q15/p2 This sūra occurred in those scrolls as revealed to Muhammad.
  reasons: Al-Suddī explicitly says as revealed; Ibn ʿAbbās says it was transcribed or copied. Other reports use broader whole-sūra wording without asserting verbatim identity.
  holders (6 notes): IBNABIHATIM(ابن عباس، السدي); DURR(ابن عباس، السدي); DURR-FULL(ابن عباس، السدي)

# 87:19/q16 What broader religious teaching does this shared scripture establish? [* · theology]
- 87:19/q16/p1 Revelation has one divine source and a continuous core of faith, worship, morality and afterlife accountability.
  reasons: Qutb locates the sūra's oneness, revelation and recompense in earlier scriptures: one creator perfects, apportions and guides, while details vary with eras.
