79:19/q14 How does Moses' offer fulfill the instruction to speak gently? [* · links]
  79:19/q14/p1 The offer is a tactful invitation that draws Pharaoh toward faith, elaborating the command to speak gently in 20:44. — reasons: Baydawi, Nasafi, Bursevi, Elmalili, Ibn Ashur, and Bint al-Shati connect the offer with 20:44; Bint al-Shati also cites 20:42–43 and the hope Pharaoh might remember or fear. Nasafi compares the interrogative opening to a host inviting a guest and says gentle speech coaxes Pharaoh from arrogance. Bursevi notes the wording avoids blunt accusations of polytheism, ignorance, and unbelief while detailing purification and guidance. Alusi says the question draws him down from arrogance. Ibn Ashur says it offers and encourages acceptance. Study Quran says Moses was instructed to invite Pharaoh peacefully to Revelation first, as the Prophet invited Quraysh. Ibn Ashur and Bint al-Shati explain the gentle force of 'your Lord.'

79:19/q18 What does sending guidance before punishment teach about divine justice? [* · theology]
  79:19/q18/p1 People must receive the guidance needed for moral discernment before God punishes their wrongdoing. — reasons: Asad says full awareness of God is necessary to distinguish right from wrong, and God's justice withholds punishment until moral discernment, linking this to 6:31.
