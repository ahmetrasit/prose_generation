[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:42:27/r1] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | The verse addresses the difficult question of how worldly means are distributed and clarifies the preceding statement that God gives believers who do good more of His grace. | mentions 42:26
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:42:27/r2] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | If everyone received abundant and equal intelligence, health, ability, and wealth, social order, self-development, systematic work, and civilization would fail, leaving chaos.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:42:27/r3] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | Allocating livelihood according to who is good or bad would remove life's test, which is tied to humanity's purpose and to the purpose of life and death.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:42:27/r4] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | The verse explicitly answers that God, who fully knows and sees His servants' condition, distributes provision according to the measure He wills.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:42:27/r5] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | People cannot grasp this measure from their own perspective.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:42:27/r6] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | A person's duty is to strive fully for means that bring well-being in this life and the next, and to use what is given as well as possible.
