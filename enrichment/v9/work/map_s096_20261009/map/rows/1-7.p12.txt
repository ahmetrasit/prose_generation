[IBNATIYYA:1:7/r17] IBNATIYYA, d. 542 · نافع | reports | Reports give Nāfiʿ a choice between mīm with ḍamma and sukūn; since he did not object to ḍamma, the author infers his own reading used sukūn.
[IBNATIYYA:1:7/r18] IBNATIYYA, d. 542 · عبد الله بن كثير | reports | Ibn Kathīr links the mīm with a wāw when it is pronounced with ḍamma, whether the preceding hāʾ has ḍamma or kasra.
