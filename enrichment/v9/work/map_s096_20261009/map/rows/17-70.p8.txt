[TABATABAI:17:70#4/r2] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | holds | The author reiterates that this verse concerns cosmic existence, not afterlife reward, and that its partitive many means creatures other than angels, including humans, animals, and jinn.

[TABATABAI:17:70#4/r4] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · الأشاعرة | reports | One argument says a human who freely obeys despite equal capacity for sin has greater merit than an angel naturally disposed to obedience.
