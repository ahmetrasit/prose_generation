[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:2:186/r11] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | reports | The author poses the apparent problem that if destiny determines all events and requests absent from one’s destiny will not be granted, supplication seems useless.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:2:186/r12] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | God orders events in the material world through laws and causes, using intermediaries such as parents for birth, clouds for rain, causes for death, and organs for hearing and sight.
