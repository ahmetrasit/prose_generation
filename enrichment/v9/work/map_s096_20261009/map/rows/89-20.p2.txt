[BINTSHATI-IJAZ:p573/r3] BINTSHATI-IJAZ, d. 1419 · author | holds | The form and root occur only here in the Qur'an; Arabic usage gives jamm the sense of gathered abundance, as in a full measure, abundant collected water, and a mass of hair.

[BINTSHATI-IJAZ:p574/r2] BINTSHATI-IJAZ, d. 1419 · الراغب | reports | Al-Rāghib links abundance to a pool’s collected, full body of water.
[BINTSHATI-IJAZ:p574/r3] BINTSHATI-IJAZ, d. 1419 · ابن الأثير | reports | Ibn al-Athīr derives jamm ghafīr from gathering and abundance, and ghafīr from covering, yielding a sense of comprehensiveness.
[BINTSHATI-IJAZ:p574/r4] BINTSHATI-IJAZ, d. 1419 · author | holds | The adjective jammāʾ occurs only with a qualifier and is accusative as a verbal noun, like qāṭibatan and ṭurran.
