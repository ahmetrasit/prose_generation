[SIBAWAYH:(هذابابالتضعيففيبناتالواو)#797/r13] SIBAWAYH, d. 180 · author | holds | Medial doubled letters resist the treatment of final geminates because their position is stronger, so اقتتلوا does not assimilate like رددت.

[SIBAWAYH:(هذابابالتضعيففيبناتالواو)#797/r16] SIBAWAYH, d. 180 · author | holds | When the two waws are medial in احوييت, they remain because medial doubling is stronger, like the internal sequence in اقتتلنا.
[SIBAWAYH:(هذابابالتضعيففيبناتالواو)#797/r17] SIBAWAYH, d. 180 · author | holds | The medial form احوويت follows اقتتلت; its verbal noun is احوواء, parallel to قتال, while the other form yields حواء.
[SIBAWAYH:(هذابابالتضعيففيبناتالواو)#797/r18] SIBAWAYH, d. 180 · author | holds | The form of شويت is شي: waw becomes yāʾ when quiescent before yāʾ, and the preceding consonant takes kasra to avoid ḍamma with yāʾ.
