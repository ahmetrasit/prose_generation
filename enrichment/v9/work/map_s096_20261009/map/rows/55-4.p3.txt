[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT-HALAQAT:p590/r2] SAMARRAI-LAMASAT-HALAQAT · author | holds | The author says the human is singled out because he received the revealed book and explains it to both groups; البيان is the ability to express what is within oneself.
[STUDYQURAN:55:4/r1] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | Human beings are distinguished from other creatures by speech, which lets them articulate and comprehend, as well as by knowing revelation.
[STUDYQURAN:55:4/r2] STUDYQURAN · Q | reports | Verses 2–4 can allude to God's honoring humanity by enabling people to learn and recite the Quran, His Word, as in 44:58 and 54:17, 22, 32, and 40. | mentions 44:58, 54:17, 54:22, 54:32, 54:40
[STUDYQURAN:55:4/r3] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | Bayān means speech and also something clear or evident.
[STUDYQURAN:55:4/r4] STUDYQURAN · some | reports | Some understand bayān as alluding to God's teaching Adam all the names, as in 2:31. | mentions 2:31
[STUDYQURAN:55:4/r5] STUDYQURAN · Q, Sh | reports | Another proposed sense is God's teaching people to distinguish good from evil, permitted from forbidden, and truth from falsehood through revelation.
[STUDYQURAN:55:4/r6] STUDYQURAN · Q, R | reports | Bayān is also regarded as a name of the Quran, as in 3:138, which calls it an exposition for humankind. | mentions 3:138
