87:6/q18 What bearing does the promise have on collecting the Qur’an? [* · sciences] (turns on: Why a codex was unnecessary for the Prophet yet needed for the community.)
  87:6/q18/p1 The Prophet did not need a collected codex because God protected his memory; later collectors faced possible loss. — reasons: Burhān adds the exception for revelation God willed to abrogate.
  87:6/q18/p2 Collection proceeded in stages using written fragments checked against reciters. — reasons: Al-Ḥākim distinguishes collection with the Prophet, under Abū Bakr, and ordering sūras under ʿUthmān. Al-Muḥāsibī says the Prophet ordered writing on separate materials; Abū Bakr bound copies. Burhān says the community knew its miraculous composition through twenty years’ recitation and Zayd verified fragments against memorizers, fearing loss rather than additions.

  87:6/q19/p1 The passage beginning “We sent before you” was abrogated by this verse. — reasons: Suyūṭī also describes the sūra’s opening as travel-revealed and other abrogation examples concerning fighting, a judgment verse and the sword verse.
