[RUMMANI:v1p102/r1] RUMMANI, d. 384 · author | holds | Tadmin is a meaning conveyed without explicitly naming it by a noun or attribute; it may be communicated by direct reporting or by inference.
[RUMMANI:v1p102/r2] RUMMANI, d. 384 · author | holds | One kind of implication is built into morphology: calling something known entails a knower, just as calling someone honored entails one who honors.
[RUMMANI:v1p102/r3] RUMMANI, d. 384 · author | holds | Some implications are required by a word's meaning: قاتل entails someone killed, since the sense of killer cannot stand without a victim.
[RUMMANI:v1p102/r4] RUMMANI, d. 384 · author | holds | An implication may also come from customary usage: الكر بستين leaves out dinars because convention supplies the understood unit.
[RUMMANI:v1p102/r5] RUMMANI, d. 384 · author | holds | All implication is concise expression that dispenses with elaboration when the meaning can be inferred from the wording.
[RUMMANI:v1p102/r6] RUMMANI, d. 384 · author | holds | Inferential implication is a distinctive feature of God's speech: God misses no valid implication, so His choice of wording indicates every meaning the wording can support; human speakers may intend only its lexical sense.
