74:26/q09 What does a report say about the inhabitant of Saqar? [سَقَرَ · reports]
  74:26/q09/p1 God told Moses that the inhabitant of Saqar is the poorest servant. — reasons: Al-Qurṭubī transmits Abū Hurayra's account of Moses asking God which servant is poorest and God's reply; al-Thaʿlabī supplies no grading.
74:26/q10 What does the verse's punishment teach in the sūra? [* · interpretation] (turns on: The notes draw distinct lessons from the threat: inescapability, personal moral causation, and the response of truth-seekers versus mockers.)
  74:26/q10/p1 The disbelievers cannot escape the horrors of Hell's punishment. — reasons: Ibn ʿĀshūr describes the sūra's terrors and says the passage makes disbelievers despair of escape.
  74:26/q10/p2 Sin and deliberate blindness bring afterlife suffering on the person himself; its descriptions are allegorical. — reasons: Asad calls Saqar one of seven metaphorical names for this suffering, points to 15:33, and reads this passage and the descriptions from 74:28 onward allegorically.
  74:26/q10/p3 The description rests on divine wisdom: truth-seekers benefit, while arrogant mockers ruin their fate. — reasons: Iṣlāḥī says disbelievers mock the description of Hell because they assume they know everything; the differing effects on sincere and arrogant readers express divine wisdom.
74:26/q11 Which other infernal terms are discussed alongside Saqar? [* · interpretation] (turns on: The notes distinguish a list of rarer synonymous words from a list of specifically named valleys.)
  74:26/q11/p1 Some rare Qurʾānic terms are treated as synonyms in a discussion including Saqar. — reasons: Al-Suyūṭī lists ḍīzā, al-ḥuṭama, Saqar, Laẓā, and Hāwiya as rarer synonymous choices, often serving the sūra's rhyme.
  74:26/q11/p2 Several other named terms are classified as valleys in Hell. — reasons: Al-Suyūṭī lists Ghayy, Atham, Mwbq, Saʿīr, Wayl, Saʾil, and Suhaq as valleys. He reports Saʿīd ibn Jubayr's more specific description of Saʿīr as a valley of pus and Suhaq as a valley in Hell. These notes do not classify Saqar as a valley.
