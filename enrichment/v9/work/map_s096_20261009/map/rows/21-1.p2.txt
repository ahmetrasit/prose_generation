[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v7p405/r4] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · author | holds | Sūrat al-Anbiyāʾ is Meccan without disagreement.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v7p405/r5] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · عبد الله | reports | ʿAbd Allāh counts al-Kahf, Maryam, Ṭā Hā, and al-Anbiyāʾ among the earliest suras he learned, an old, accumulated portion of the Qur’an.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v7p405/r6] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · author | holds | The sura answers Quraysh’s reaction to the preceding warning of reckoning: they said Muhammad’s promised resurrection and judgment were false or far off, so God revealed that their reckoning had drawn near.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v7p405/r7] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · author | holds | اقترب has the basic sense of قرب, though some consider it more emphatic because of its augmented form; الناس may mean Meccan polytheists or all deniers of resurrection.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v7p405/r8] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · author | holds | The approaching reckoning means the Hour’s time; حساب can mean measuring an amount or the amount counted. What is coming is near despite a long wait, near to God, or near relative to the shorter future than past.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v7p406/r1] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · النبي | reports | The Prophet says he and the Hour were sent like these two fingers, indicating nearness; a poet similarly says the hoped-for one is nearer than tomorrow and the feared one farther than yesterday.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v7p406/r2] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · الزمخشري | reports | Al-Zamakhsharī says للناس either attaches directly to اقترب or emphasizes that the reckoning belongs to them; the author rejects the second as unattested and syntactically problematic.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v7p406/r3] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · author | rejects | He objects that the possession-emphasis analysis has no recognized parallel here, lacks a governor if attached to the genitive source, and should follow the emphasized phrase; لَأبا لك is exceptional and not a model.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v7p406/r4] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · author | holds | The wāw in وهم is circumstantial. Their heedlessness and turning away are compatible: they fail to consider consequences, then ignore reminders when awakened from inattention.

[SAMIN-DURR:21:1#2/r3] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | The whole clause وهم في غفلة معرضون is a circumstantial clause describing للناس.

[SAMIN-DURR:21:1/r2] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · الزمخشري | reports | Al-Zamakhshari offers the lām either as a complement of اقترب or as emphasis of the حسابهم possessive relation, comparing أزف للحي رحيلهم and related constructions; he calls the latter more unusual.
[SAMIN-DURR:21:1/r3] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · الشيخ | rejects | The Shaykh rejects the possessive-emphasis analysis: it lacks a known precedent, leaves the lām without a governor, conflicts with the order of an emphasized element, and differs from the cited examples; لا أبا لك is disputed and anomalous.
[SAMIN-DURR:21:1/r4] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | prefers | The author says al-Zamakhshari's account resembles لا أبا لك and preserves its correct meaning; its difficulty follows the majority's account of that construction, so its established analysis can support the analogy.
