  1:2/q09/p4 Rabb is read nominative with an implied subject. — reasons: IBNJAWZI-ZAD, d. 597 (أبو رزين العقيلي والربيع بن خيثم وأبو عمران الجوني): These readers pronounce the bāʾ of rabb in the nominative. Al-Zajjāj explains nominative rabb al-ʿālamīn as ‘He is the Lord of the worlds’; his unnamed poetic example illustrates how nominative epithets may state ‘they are the ones,’ among case combinations accepted by grammarians.
