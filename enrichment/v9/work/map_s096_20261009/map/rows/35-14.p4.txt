[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:35:14/r1] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | Following the previous verse's statement that the beings worshipped besides God possess or control nothing, this verse concretely shows the absurdity of seeking help from them. | mentions 35:13
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:35:14/r2] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | The beings worshipped besides God do not hear their callers, cannot answer even if assumed to hear, and will disavow the attribution of divinity on Judgment Day with an ability God gives them.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:35:14/r3] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · müfessirler | reports | Exegetes generally identify the beings worshipped here as idols.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:35:14/r4] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | prefers | Because the wording is general, it is better to include all beings treated as gods, such as humans, animals, the moon, and the sun.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:35:14/r5] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | The point is not that a human fails to hear or answer as an ordinary person, but that these supposed gods cannot hear or answer worshippers' pleas from anywhere, whether secret or open, near or far.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:35:14/r6] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | The false gods' future disavowal is an unseen matter known only because God reports it, so no one can inform the Prophet as the all-aware God can.
