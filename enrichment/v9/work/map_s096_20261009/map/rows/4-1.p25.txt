[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:1/r1] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | The Qur’anic address “O people” targets all humanity, not believers alone.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:1/r10] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · Şah Veliyyullah | reports | Under the second definition, God creates an individual nafs for each person as well as a body; Shah Waliullah calls this nafs nasama, whose spiritual constitution and traits vary with a person’s deeds.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:1/r11] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | Spirit is universal rather than personal, like electricity from one source lighting bulbs; it is divine, belongs to God and the command-world, not the created world.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:1/r12] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | Spirit illuminates or draws the nafs toward the path that leads to God’s approval.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:1/r13] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | Human nature also contains emotions, drives, and needs that pull against God’s approval, while Satan works to divert people from God’s path.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:1/r14] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | Through education and will, the human nafs struggles between these attractions and seeks to fulfill servitude and spiritual development in worldly life through trial.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:1/r15] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | The nafs strives to rise from ammara, which commands evil, through self-reproaching, divinely inspired, tranquil, content, and God-approved stages.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:1/r16] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | Because every person has a mother and father and arises through reproduction, “from one nafs” and “from it” mean from its essence and a like kindred root, not a body part such as a rib.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:1/r17] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | Rūm 30:21 uses nafs for one’s own kind: God made spouses of your kind and placed love and compassion between you. | mentions 30:21
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:1/r18] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | Nahl 16:72 and Shura 42:11 use similar expressions for spouses and creation from one’s kind. | mentions 16:72, 42:11
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:1/r19] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | These verses do not mean that a woman was made from a bodily part, so they cannot establish the widespread claim that Hawwa came from Adam’s rib.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:1/r2] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | The verse says to be mindful of your Lord rather than to avoid disrespecting God because Rabb fits the relation of human creation and development, while Allah or ilah fits worship and servitude.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:1/r20] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | Hadiths about woman being created from a crooked rib figuratively describe innate, enduring differences between women and men; some reports explicitly say woman is like a rib.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:1/r21] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | Trying to make women resemble men and erase their natural characteristics is like trying to straighten a crooked rib: its curve makes it fitting, strong, complete, and able to protect the lung, while straightening it would spoil and break it.
