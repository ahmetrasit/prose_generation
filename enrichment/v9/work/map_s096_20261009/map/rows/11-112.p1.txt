[TABRISI:11:112/r1] TABRISI, d. 548 · author | holds | Mirya means doubt despite clear evidence; a share is an allotted portion; disagreement can be incompatible opposites or different valid judgments such as facing the qibla; istiqama is staying straight, and transgression exceeds the proper measure in corruption.
[TABRISI:11:112/r10] TABRISI, d. 548 · author | rejects | For the experts, doubling the m of لما alongside either doubled or light إن remains problematic: it yields none of the known senses of لم, حين, or إلا.
[TABRISI:11:112/r11] TABRISI, d. 548 · author | holds | The related poem uses إلا as an extra particle, meaning the era makes its people confused and leaves petitioners frustrated.
[TABRISI:11:112/r8] TABRISI, d. 548 · author | rejects | When إن is light and كل is accusative after a negative sense, the analysis remains difficult; proposals inserting وإن هم إلا or an explanatory كلا are disfavored.
[TABRISI:11:112/r9] TABRISI, d. 548 · author | holds | The reading وإن كل إلا ليوفينهم can mean God will certainly repay each one, with إلا treated as an extra particle if إن is lightened.
