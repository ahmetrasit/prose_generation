38:7/q21 What theological and editorial commitments does al-Nasafī state for his tafsīr? [* · theology]
  38:7/q21/p1 God is unique, transcendent, eternal, sovereign and wise; the tafsīr presents Sunni explanation with grammar, readings, rhetoric and allusion. — reasons: Al-Nasafī's preface denies bodily, spatial and human likeness, praises God's knowledge and the Qur'an's cure for souls and bodies, and says he wrote a moderate tafsīr excluding innovators' doctrines. He notes initial hesitation over human limits, speedy completion with divine aid, and the title Madārik al-tanzīl wa-ḥaqāʾiq al-taʾwīl.
