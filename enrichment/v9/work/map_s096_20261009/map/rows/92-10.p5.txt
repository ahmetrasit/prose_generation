ANICUM · author | holds | The sura develops two antitheses: behavior and recompense for the saved and damned, first in verses 5–13 and then in verses 14–21.

[CORPUSCORANICUM:92:5-12:kommentar/r4] CORPUSCORANICUM · author | holds | The first stanza is symmetrical, with matching behavior-to-consequence sequences and a cross-rhyme produced by vowel variation, as Neuwirth observes.
[CORPUSCORANICUM:92:5-12:kommentar/r5] CORPUSCORANICUM · author | holds | The two halves are linked by paired terms: giving versus stinginess, piety versus self-sufficiency, affirmation versus denial, and ease versus hardship.
