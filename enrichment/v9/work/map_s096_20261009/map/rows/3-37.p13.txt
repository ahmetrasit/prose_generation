[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v2p35/r5] IBNKATHIR-FULL, d. 774 · author | holds | God assigned Zakariyya as Mary’s guardian for her happiness, so she could gain abundant useful knowledge and righteous practice from him.

[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v2p35/r7] IBNKATHIR-FULL, d. 774 · author | holds | The Prophet ruled that Imara daughter of Hamza be placed in her maternal aunt Jaʿfar’s wife’s custody, saying the maternal aunt is like a mother.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v2p35/r8] IBNKATHIR-FULL, d. 774 · مجاهد، وعكرمة، وسعيد بن جبير، وأبو الشعثاء، وإبراهيم النخعي، والضحاك، وقتادة، والربيع بن أنس، وعطية العوفي، والسدي، والشعبي | reports | These early authorities say Zakariyya found summer fruit with Mary in winter and winter fruit in summer.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v2p35/r9] IBNKATHIR-FULL, d. 774 · مجاهد | reports | Mujahid also glosses Mary’s provision as knowledge, or scrolls containing knowledge; Ibn Kathir prefers the seasonal-fruit explanation as sounder.
