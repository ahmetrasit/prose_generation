[QURTUBI-FULL:111:1#2/r5] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · author | holds | The hands are singled out because most work is done with them: they lost, and Abu Lahab himself lost.
[QURTUBI-FULL:111:1#2/r6] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · author | reports | Alternatively, 'hands' stands for the whole person, as in 22:10, and Arabic uses a part for the whole; a poem illustrates the idiom. | mentions 22:10
[QURTUBI-FULL:111:1#2/r7] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · الأصمعي عن أبي عمرو بن العلاء | reports | Al-Asma'i reports that a caller after Uthman's killing recited a lament using tabban for the calamity of what people had done.
[QURTUBI-FULL:111:1#3/r1] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · الفراء | reports | Al-Farra says the first tabbat is a curse and the second a factual report, as in 'May God destroy him; he has perished'; Ubay's and Abdullah's reading adds 'qad'.
[QURTUBI-FULL:111:1#3/r10] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · الحسن | reports | Hasan says Abu Lahab could not escape the Fire, since his fate was written in the Preserved Book before he and his parents were created.
[QURTUBI-FULL:111:1#3/r11] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · author | reports | The Adam-and-Moses exchange is cited as a precedent for God's prior decree; the Prophet says Adam prevailed over Moses.
[QURTUBI-FULL:111:1#3/r2] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · author | holds | Abu Lahab's personal name was Abd al-Uzza, and he was the Prophet's paternal uncle; his wife Umm Jamil was Abu Sufyan's sister, and both strongly opposed the Prophet.
[QURTUBI-FULL:111:1#3/r3] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · طارق بن عبد الله المحاربي | reports | Tariq ibn Abdullah recounts seeing the Prophet preach the testimony of faith at Dhu al-Majaz while Abu Lahab followed him, bloodied his legs with stones, and called him a liar.
[QURTUBI-FULL:111:1#3/r4] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · عطاء عن ابن عباس | reports | Ata transmits Ibn Abbas's account that Abu Lahab accused Muhammad of magic, while marveling that Muhammad's food and drink satisfied people.
[QURTUBI-FULL:111:1#3/r5] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · author | holds | Al-Qurtubi rejects using this verse to prove that an unbeliever may receive a respectful kunya; he gives four reasons for God's use of Abu Lahab.
[QURTUBI-FULL:111:1#3/r6] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · author | holds | The four reasons are that his given name invoked an idol, his kunya was better known, a kunya is lower than a proper name, and the name foreshadows his abode in Hell.
[QURTUBI-FULL:111:1#3/r7] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · قيل | reports | Another account says his family called him Abu Lahab for his glowing, handsome face; God turned them from more favorable names such as Abu al-Nur toward the ill-omened fire-name.
[QURTUBI-FULL:111:1#3/r8] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · مجاهد، حميد، ابن كثير، ابن محيصن | reports | These readers pronounce the hāʾ in 'Abi Lahab' with sukūn, while preserving it open in 'dhāt lahab' to fit the verse endings.
[QURTUBI-FULL:111:1#3/r9] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · ابن عباس | reports | Ibn Abbas says that when God created the Pen He ordered it to record what would occur, including the doom of Abu Lahab.
[QURTUBI-FULL:111:1#4/r1] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · أبو هريرة | reports | In the report from Abu Hurayra through Hammam, Adam asks how long before his creation God wrote the Torah; Moses answers two thousand years, including the statement that Adam disobeyed, so Adam asks why he is blamed for what was decreed.
[QURTUBI-FULL:111:1#4/r2] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · طاوس، ابن هرمز، الأعرج عن أبي هريرة | reports | A separate transmission gives forty years, rather than two thousand, for the interval before Adam's creation.
[QURTUBI-FULL:111:1/r1] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · ابن عباس | reports | In the two Ṣaḥīḥ collections, Ibn Abbas says the sura followed the Prophet's call from Mount Safa after 26:214: Quraysh affirmed he never lied, then Abu Lahab cursed him for warning them. | mentions 26:214
[QURTUBI-FULL:111:1/r2] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · الحميدي وغيره | reports | An added report says Abu Lahab's wife came to the mosque carrying a stone, but God concealed the Prophet from her sight; she threatened him in verse, then left seeing only Abu Bakr.
[QURTUBI-FULL:111:1/r3] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · author | reports | Quraysh called the Prophet 'Mudhammam' to abuse him; he said God diverted their harm because they reviled Mudhammam while he was Muhammad.
[QURTUBI-FULL:111:1/r4] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · عبد الرحمن بن زيد | reports | Abd al-Rahman ibn Zayd gives another occasion: Abu Lahab asked what advantage he would receive by becoming Muslim, rejected equality with other Muslims, and the sura was revealed.
