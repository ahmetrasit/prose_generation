[SAMIN-DURR:9:103#2/r3] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | Sakan means tranquility; an unnamed poetic line uses سكنا for a source of comfort, and al-Samīn concludes that here it means something calming for them, as indicated by لهم.
[SAMIN-DURR:9:103#2/r4] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · أبو البقاء | reports | Abū al-Baqāʾ treats سكن as a passive-form noun, explaining why it is masculine; al-Samīn prefers an active sense, since that would mean a thing calming them, whereas the passive would require سكن إليها.
[SAMIN-DURR:9:103/r1] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | من أموالهم either attaches to خذ as a partitive preposition or modifies صدقة, originally as its adjective but advanced and made circumstantial.
[SAMIN-DURR:9:103/r2] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | The tāʾ of تطهرهم can address the Prophet, making the clause circumstantial to the subject of خذ or an adjective of صدقة with an omitted بها; or it can be third-person with صدقة as subject, in which case it only modifies صدقة.
[SAMIN-DURR:9:103/r3] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | In تزكيهم the tāʾ must address the Prophet, since بها refers to صدقة and the clause can be circumstantial or an adjective, with an omitted return pronoun.
[SAMIN-DURR:9:103/r4] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · مكي | reports | Makkī allows تطهرهم to modify صدقة with a third-person tāʾ and تزكيهم to be circumstantial with an address tāʾ; the objection is that coordination normally requires the same syntactic role.
[SAMIN-DURR:9:103/r5] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | rejects | Al-Samīn rejects Makkī's mixed parse under ordinary coordination, which would require both clauses to be adjectives or both circumstantial clauses; a circumstantial waw with an omitted-subject predicate for تزكيهم is possible but weak.
[SAMIN-DURR:9:103/r6] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | Al-Samīn summarizes the available combinations: both clauses circumstantial with address, both adjectives with third-person and an omitted return, Makkī's mixed circumstantial option, or a weak predicate-of-an-omitted-subject parse for تزكيهم.
