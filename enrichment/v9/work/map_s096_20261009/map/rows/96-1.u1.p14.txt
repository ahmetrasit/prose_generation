  96:1/q37/p1 Its whole descent to the lowest heaven preceded prophethood. — reasons: ITQAN (أبو شامة): Abu Shāma says the Qur’an’s descent all at once to the lowest heaven apparently preceded prophethood, though it may have occurred afterward.
  96:1/q37/p2 Its whole descent followed prophethood. — reasons: ITQAN (أبو شامة): Abu Shāma says the Qur’an’s descent all at once to the lowest heaven apparently preceded prophethood, though it may have occurred afterward. ITQAN: Al-Suyuti favors the second possibility, descent after prophethood, because the preceding reports from Ibn Abbas state this explicitly.
  96:1/q37/p3 Earthly revelation came gradually to strengthen and guide Muhammad. — reasons: ITQAN: Fresh revelation with each event strengthens the Prophet and renews divine care and angelic contact, bringing joy; this is why he was most generous in Ramadan, when he often met Gabriel. ITQAN: Answering why the Qur’an was not sent all at once, al-Suyuti says God sent it gradually to strengthen the Prophet’s heart, as 25:32 states.; cites 25:32
  96:1/q37/p4 Reports specify Ramadan dates for the Torah, Gospel, Psalms, and Qurʾān. — reasons: ITQAN (النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم): Wathila reports the Prophet said the Torah descended on 6 Ramadan, the Gospel on 13, the Psalms on 18, and the Qur’an on 24; another wording places Abraham’s scrolls on the first night. ITQAN (ابن حجر): Ibn Hajar links the 24 Ramadan report to verses about Ramadan and Laylat al-Qadr, proposing a whole descent to the lowest heaven then and the first earthly revelation on the 24th.; cites 2:185, 97:1 ITQAN (البيهقي وغيره): To reconcile the famous claim that the Prophet was sent in Rabi, a report says he first received prophetic dreams in his birth month for six months, then revelation while awake. ITQAN (أبو قلابة): Abu Qilaba’s report that all scriptures descended complete on the night of 24 Ramadan complicates the preceding date account.

  96:1/q38/p1 It opened a direct connection with heaven and redirected human values and law to revelation. — reasons: QUTB-ZILAL: For twenty-three years believers experienced an immediate, direct connection with the heavenly realm, awaiting revelations that resolved their concerns and guided their steps; its distance from earthly sources was immense. QUTB-ZILAL: Revelation's effects endure: people derive values from heaven and law from revelation rather than earth or desire, and the event decisively changed human history.
