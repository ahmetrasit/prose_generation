Abu Bakr asks whether deeds follow a settled decree or a new decision, the Prophet replies that each person is made ready for what he was created to do.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p418/r1] IBNKATHIR-FULL, d. 774 · رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | The Prophet says each persons place in Paradise or Hell and status as fortunate or wretched have been written, prompting the question whether people should then rely on fate.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p418/r2] IBNKATHIR-FULL, d. 774 · رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | In a fuller version, the Prophet says the fortunate are enabled toward fortunate deeds and the wretched toward wretched deeds, then recites the two paths.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p418/r3] IBNKATHIR-FULL, d. 774 · رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | Umar asks whether human deeds are already decreed; the Prophet says each person is facilitated, with the fortunate working toward happiness and the wretched toward misery. Tirmidhi grades the report hasan sahih.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p418/r4] IBNKATHIR-FULL, d. 774 · رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | Jabir asks whether deeds concern a settled matter or a new one; the Prophet says the matter is settled, and tells Suraqah that every worker is facilitated for his work.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p419/r1] IBNKATHIR-FULL, d. 774 · رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | Muslim also transmits the report in which the Prophet says every worker is facilitated for the work for which he was created.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p419/r2] IBNKATHIR-FULL, d. 774 · رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | Two young men ask whether deeds follow what is written; the Prophet says to act, since everyone is facilitated for the work he was created to do.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p419/r3] IBNKATHIR-FULL, d. 774 · رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | The Prophet tells Abu al-Darda that each person is prepared for what he was created for; Ibn Kathir says Ahmad alone transmits this route.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v8p420/r2] IBNKATHIR-FULL, d. 774 · author | reports | A report cited by Ibn Jarir says the verses concern Abu Bakr, who freed vulnerable women and elderly converts in Mecca to seek God's reward despite his father's advice to free stronger men for protection.

[BIQAI:92:7/r1] BIQAI, d. 885 · author | holds | The majestic first-person plural marks the difficulty obedience poses to the self, even though obedience is intrinsically easy.
[BIQAI:92:7/r2] BIQAI, d. 885 · author | holds | God, by a promise that cannot fail, prepares the person for the easy, restful way through mercy and action pleasing to Him, leading to a good life and entry into Paradise.
[BIQAI-FULL:v8p447/r3] BIQAI-FULL, d. 885 · author | holds | God prepares the truthful giver for the easy path through mercy, enabling pleasing conduct, a good life, and entry into Paradise; obedience itself can feel burdensome to the self.
