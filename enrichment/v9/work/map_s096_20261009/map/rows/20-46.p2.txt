[TABATABAI:20:46-47/r3] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · بعضهم | reports | Some infer that hearing and seeing are attributes additional to knowledge: “I am with you” already signifies knowledge, so taking these verbs likewise would repeat it.
[TABATABAI:20:46-47/r4] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | rejects | The author calls this a very weak proof: “with you” means presence and witnessing, not knowledge, and certain proofs establish that God's essential attributes are identical with one another and the essence.
[TABATABAI:20:46-47/r5] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | rejects | A speculative verbal appearance cannot override certainty; foundational doctrines must rest on knowledge, not an appeal to the default avoidance of repetition.
[SAMARRAI-ASRAR:r4#2/r7] SAMARRAI-ASRAR · author | holds | Another possible reason for hearing before sight is its shorter range: hearing ordinarily suggests nearness, so God's promise to hear and see Moses and Aaron conveys closeness.
[SAMARRAI-ASRAR:r8#2/r2] SAMARRAI-ASRAR · author | holds | Ta-Ha presents a joint mission: both are sent, answer in fear, receive reassurance, deliver the message, and are addressed by Pharaoh together, making Aaron Moses's partner in conveying the message. | mentions 20:29, 20:42, 20:63, 20:90, 20:92
