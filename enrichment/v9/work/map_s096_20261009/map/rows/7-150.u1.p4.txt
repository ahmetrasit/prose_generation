  7:150/q07/p1 Biʾsa has a concealed subject explained by mā; the condemned succession is understood. — reasons: KASHSHAF-FULL (author): In the grammar of biʾs, the subject is implicit and explained by mā khalafتمūnī; the omitted object of blame is their bad succession. BAYDAWI-FULL (author): The indefinite mā is descriptive and explains the concealed subject of biʾsa; the omitted object of blame is their bad succession in his place. NASAFI-FULL (author): The blame is for worshipping the calf in place of God, or for failing to stop worship of anything besides God; the omitted object of blame is their bad succession.   7:150/q07/p2 Biʾsamā is written as a joined form. — reasons: BURHAN (author): The formula بئسما is written joined in three Qur’anic occurrences, including al-Aʿraf, because ما denotes one undivided blameworthy thing.
