[RAZI-FULL:v4p105/r1] RAZI-FULL, d. 606 · author | reports | An argument against requiring exact Kaaba alignment says that the Kaaba is only about twenty cubits wide, so almost no eastern or western worshippers can line up with it, and even those few cannot know that they do.
