2p68/r1] BINTSHATI, d. 1419 · ابن مسعود | reports | A report transmitted by al-Ṭabarī from Ibn Masʿūd through Abū al-Zaʿrāʾ says God appears and asks whom people worship; believers recognize and prostrate, while hypocrites’ backs become rigid and joined.
[BINTSHATI:v2p68/r2] BINTSHATI, d. 1419 · ابن مسعود | reports | Al-Zamakhsharī also cites an Ibn Masʿūd report saying the Merciful uncovers His shin; believers prostrate and hypocrites’ backs become one rigid surface.
[BINTSHATI:v2p68/r9] BINTSHATI, d. 1419 · author | holds | The Day of Judgment is an appalling hardship for disbelievers: they are called to prostrate as a belated rebuke after wasting their worldly chance, when they were safe and able.
[BINTSHATI:v2p69/r1] BINTSHATI, d. 1419 · author | rejects | Physical explanations say the backbones lose their joints, the vertebrae fuse into one, or the body becomes rigid after a forty-year wait and a divine manifestation; the author rejects such elaborations as incompatible with the text and context.
[BINTSHATI:v2p69/r2] BINTSHATI, d. 1419 · author | prefers | The inability to prostrate means the time for worship and the period of religious obligation have passed, rather than a bodily disability.
[BINTSHATI:v2p69/r3] BINTSHATI, d. 1419 · author | holds | The missed opportunity parallels al-Fajr’s scene of Hell brought forth and the sinner’s belated wish that he had prepared for his true life, after rejecting responsibility and loving wealth. | mentions 89:23, 89:24, 89:25
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-8-english:p438/r4] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · author | holds | The Day will be cataclysmic for people dreaming of worldly luxuries: those who arrogantly refuse prostration now will then be called to bow but unable.
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-8-english:p439/r1] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · author | holds | On Judgment Day their backs will be too rigid to prostrate even if they wish; downcast eyes and encompassing ignominy will display their helplessness.
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-8-english:p439/r2] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · author | holds | The call to prostrate humiliates them and shows their denial had no excuse, with their own beings testifying to arrogance and deprivation.
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-8-english:p439/r3] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · author | holds | Al-Maʿārij 70:43–44 parallels the scene; its emergence from graves with downcast eyes and ignominy clarifies that ‘the Day the shin is exposed’ means a day of severe upheaval. | mentions 70:43, 70:44
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:68:42/r5] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · Râzî | reports | Razi says unbelievers will be summoned to prostrate as a means of reproach and rebuke because they did not prostrate in worldly life.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:68:42/r6] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | reports | A hadith says men and women prostrate; those who prostrated ostentatiously in life will wish to prostrate then but will be unable to bend.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:68:42/r7] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · Şevkânî | reports | A further narration says that even unbelievers will wish to prostrate but will lack the ability to do so.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:68:42/r8] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | They will be fearful-eyed, humiliated, and in a wretched state; since they refused when able to prostrate in life, their ability to do so will be taken away in the afterlife.
[STUDYQURAN:68:43/r1] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | Although some Arabs considered the bodily position of prostration humiliating, the disbelievers will face greater humiliation in the Hereafter and be unable to prostrate. | mentions 68:42, 70:44
[STUDYQURAN:68:43/r2] STUDYQURAN · author | reports | One interpretation takes the prostration in both verses as prostration for prayer in worldly life.
