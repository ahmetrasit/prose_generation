IBNKATHIR, d. 774 (زيد بن أسلم وأبو عمرو بن العلاء): Zayd ibn Aslam and Abu Amr ibn al-Ala define worlds as everything with a spirit that receives provision. ASAD-NOTES, d. 1412 (author): “Worlds” covers every category of physical and spiritual existence. KURANYOLU-TEFSIR (author): All created things in the material and spiritual, visible and unseen worlds, in this life and the hereafter, are included in the worlds.
