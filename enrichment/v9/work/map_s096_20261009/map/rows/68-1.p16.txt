<<68:1 notes, part 16 of 0..16>>

[STUDYQURAN:68:1/r10] STUDYQURAN · Prophet | reports | Some attribute to the Prophet the saying that Nūn is a tablet of light; no grading is stated.
[STUDYQURAN:68:1/r11] STUDYQURAN · Bg, IK, JJ, Q | reports | The pronoun they in that which they inscribe may refer to angels who record all human deeds; related passages include 43:80, 50:17–18, 80:15–16, 82:10–12, and 86:4. | mentions 43:80, 50:17, 50:18, 80:15, 80:16, 82:10, 82:11, 82:12, 86:4
[STUDYQURAN:68:1/r12] STUDYQURAN · al-Qurṭubī | reports | Al-Qurṭubī divides pens into those recording the decree until Resurrection, angels' pens recording human deeds, and human writing pens; he cites 18:49 for the second kind. | mentions 18:49
[STUDYQURAN:68:1/r2] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | This is the earliest sūrah in revelation chronology in which separated letters appear.
[STUDYQURAN:68:1/r3] STUDYQURAN · some commentators | reports | Nūn also means fish; some commentators connect it with an old Arab myth that Earth rests on a mythical fish's back.
[STUDYQURAN:68:1/r4] STUDYQURAN · others | reports | Others take Nūn's cup-shaped Arabic form as an allusion to the inkwell from which the pen draws ink.
[STUDYQURAN:68:1/r5] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | In Islamic metaphysics, the Pen symbolizes the Divine Intellect or Logos through which God brings all things into being.
[STUDYQURAN:68:1/r6] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | What the Pen writes signifies the existence of beings in creation, while Nūn represents the reservoir of possibilities manifested there.
[STUDYQURAN:68:1/r7] STUDYQURAN · ḥadīth qudsī | reports | A famous hadith qudsi says God created the Pen first and commanded it to write the decree of all that would occur until the Hour; no grading is stated.
[STUDYQURAN:68:1/r8] STUDYQURAN · author | reports | Nūn may allude to Divine Names containing nūn: al-Raḥmān, al-Nūr, or al-Nāṣir.
[STUDYQURAN:68:1/r9] STUDYQURAN · Bg, Q | reports | Others connect Nūn with God's helping the believers, naṣr al-muʾminīn, a phrase appearing in 30:47. | mentions 30:47
[STUDYQURAN:68:intro/r3] STUDYQURAN · author | holds | The sūrah takes its name from the pen mentioned in its first verse.

<<end of notes>>
