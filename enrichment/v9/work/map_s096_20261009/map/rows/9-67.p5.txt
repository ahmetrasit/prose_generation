[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:9:71/r2] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · Râzî | reports | Razi's comparison says hypocrites promote evil and restrain good, whereas sincere believers encourage good and try to turn people away from evil.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:9:71/r3] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | Hypocrites are tight-fisted in God's cause, while believers pay zakat, give generously beyond it, and regret being unable to give more.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:9:71/r4] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | Hypocrites approach prayer reluctantly, while believers pray sincerely, with an inner desire and sense of duty.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:9:71/r5] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | When called to fight for God, hypocrites evade with excuses, but believers give their lives and wealth and answer without faltering.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:9:71/r6] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | Hypocrites disregard God and His message and mock believers, while believers obey God and His Messenger fully.
