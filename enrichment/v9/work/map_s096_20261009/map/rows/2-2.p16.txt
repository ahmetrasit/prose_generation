[SAMIN-DURR:2:1-2#8/r5] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | Muttaqin derives from muttaqiyin; the first of two yāʾs is deleted under the weight of the kasra, then one of the adjacent sukūns is removed; the word is an iftiʿāl form from protection.
[SAMIN-DURR:2:1-2#8/r6] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | In iftiʿāl, an initial wāw or yāʾ changes to tāʾ when followed by the infix tāʾ, as in ittaʿada and ittasara; analogous hamza changes are rare.
[SAMIN-DURR:2:1-2#8/r7] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | Iftiʿāl has twelve uses, including adoption, causation, reflexive action, selection, snatching, passive counterparts, reciprocal or intensive equivalents, equivalence to a simple verb, and replacing an unattested simple form.
[SAMIN-DURR:2:1-2#8/r8] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | Wiqaya means intense protection and caution against harm, illustrated by a horse that protects its hoof; another view says its original sense is little speech.
