[IBNASHUR-FULL:v1p121/r15] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · الزمخشري | reports | Al-Zamakhshari says the question is not an explanation of the discussion: it quotes the questioners and the criminals’ answer that they did not pray. | mentions 74:42, 74:43
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v29p293/r6] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · author | holds | It contrasts their condition with believers characterized by prayer, almsgiving, and belief in the Day of Recompense.
[TABATABAI:74:36-43/r10] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · المجرمون | reports | The criminals say they were not among those who prayed; prayer means worshipful turning toward God, regardless of differences in the form of prayer across true revealed religions.
[ASAD-NOTES:74:43#n26/r1] ASAD-NOTES, d. 1412 · author | holds | Because canonical salah was not yet obligatory when this early sūra was revealed, Asad takes prayer here broadly as conscious belief in God.
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p32/r4] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · author | holds | Nature’s manifestations argue for the Hereafter and expose heedlessness; the Qur’an warns so people can prepare, while evaders are left with stubbornness as their excuse.
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p32/r5] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · author | holds | Each soul is held for its own deeds, and only its deeds can free it or lead it to Hell; neither another’s deeds nor intercession benefits someone else.
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p32/r6] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · author | holds | The people of the right hand are the victors in Paradise and ask those in Hell what brought them there; the latter openly confess their crimes.
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p56/r3] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · author | holds | Hell's inhabitants first confess that they did not pray; prayer is the foremost deed and the practice that distinguishes believers from disbelievers, as all prophets taught.
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p57/r5] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · author | holds | The sinners' confession confirms that each soul is pledged to its deeds: ancestry, creed, deities, and their intercession cannot benefit anyone before God.
[STUDYQURAN:74:43/r2] STUDYQURAN · one Shiite interpretation | reports | A Shiite reading takes verse 43 to mean they were not followers of the Imams, alluding to failure to follow Shiite leaders.
