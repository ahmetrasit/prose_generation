[QURTUBI-FULL:9:103#3/r10] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · author | holds | An oath to give all one’s wealth as charity covers every kind of property, zakatable or not, unless the person intended a particular kind; another view limits it to zakatable property.
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:103#3/r11] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · author | holds | The command is unrestricted as to what is collected, from whom, and how much; Sunnah and consensus specify details. Zakat is due on livestock, crops, gold, and silver, with disagreement over horses and other trade goods.
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:103#3/r12] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · author | holds | The commentary on horses and honey will come in the Bee sūra; crop zakat was treated under al-Anʿam, mines under al-Baqara, and jewelry in this sūra. | mentions 16:68, 6:141, 2:267
[QURTUBI-FULL:9:103#3/r13] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · أبو سعيد | reports | A Prophetic report from Abu Saʿid sets minimum thresholds of five wasqs of dates, five awaq of silver, and five camels.

[QURTUBI-FULL:9:103#4/r1] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · العلماء | reports | Scholars agree an uqiyya equals forty dirhams; a free Muslim holding 200 minted silver dirhams for a full year owes five dirhams, one-fortieth.
