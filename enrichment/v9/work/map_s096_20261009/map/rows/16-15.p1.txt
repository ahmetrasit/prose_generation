[RAZI-FULL:v20p7#2/r3] RAZI-FULL, d. 606 · الجمهور | reports | The majority compare the earth on water to a boat: it wavered until God set heavy mountains on it like anchors, making it stable.
[RAZI-FULL:v20p7#2/r4] RAZI-FULL, d. 606 · author | rejects | Rāzī objects that if earth is naturally heavier than water it would sink rather than float and wobble like a hollow wooden boat; if natures do not cause motion, God's creation of movement and rest makes the mountain explanation fail.
[RAZI-FULL:v20p8/r1] RAZI-FULL, d. 606 · author | rejects | A further objection asks what keeps the supporting water in its place: if its nature does so, the same account could explain the earth; if God does, He could directly keep the earth still.
[RAZI-FULL:v20p8/r2] RAZI-FULL, d. 606 · author | rejects | A third objection is that movement of the entire enormous earth over the sea would be imperceptible; proponents compare it to a passenger not sensing a moving ship, while Rāzī replies that visible quakes move only a small part.
