[ALUSI-FULL:v29p149/r1] ALUSI-FULL, d. 1270 · author | holds | Awla is originally a comparative from proximity, but became a threat of approaching destruction: “destruction is nearer to you,” like سحقا in ruin.
[ALUSI-FULL:v29p149/r2] ALUSI-FULL, d. 1270 · الأصمعي | reports | Al-Asma‘i glosses the threat as nearing what will destroy a person and cites poetry where أولى means “nearer.”
[ALUSI-FULL:v29p149/r3] ALUSI-FULL, d. 1270 · author | reports | On the al-Asma‘i analysis, أولى is a verb with an implicit subject, destruction; the lām is said by some to be extra.
[ALUSI-FULL:v29p149/r4] ALUSI-FULL, d. 1270 · author | reports | Another analysis makes أولى a past-tense imprecation, with God as subject: may He bring what you hate near or bring destruction close to you.
[ALUSI-FULL:v29p149/r5] ALUSI-FULL, d. 1270 · أبو علي | reports | Abu Ali proposes that أولى is a proper name for ruin, patterned on أفعل by metathesis from أويل, and indeclinable for definiteness and pattern.
[ALUSI-FULL:v29p149/r6] ALUSI-FULL, d. 1270 · author | rejects | Al-Alusi objects that الويل is already indeclinable, that the analogy with يوم أيوم is nonstandard, and that metathesis or a generic proper name lacks clear grounds.
[ALUSI-FULL:v29p149/r7] ALUSI-FULL, d. 1270 · قائلون | reports | A favored alternative treats أولى as a comparative meaning “more fitting”: the implied subject is fire, and the addressee deserves it.
