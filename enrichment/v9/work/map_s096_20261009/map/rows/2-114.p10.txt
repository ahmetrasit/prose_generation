[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v1p573/r1] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · author | holds | The continuation lists parses for the clause as a second object, a purpose phrase with an omitted noun such as دخول, an inclusive apposition to mosques, or a complement with من elided; grammarians differ on its case.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v1p573/r2] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · author | holds | Mentioning God's name stands for all prayer and devotion in mosques, inward and bodily; alternatively, it highlights that the preventers barred even the easiest act, so barring other worship is still worse.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v1p573/r3] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · author | holds | The passive omits the doer for brevity because the perpetrators are too numerous; the prepositional phrase is fronted before the passive subject to echo the earlier mention of mosques.
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v1p573/r4] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · author | holds | The possessive construction honors the mosques, as in the declaration that mosques belong to God; the word masjid highlights prostration as the clearest posture of submission. | mentions 72:18
[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v1p573/r5] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | The Prophet's saying that a servant is nearest to his Lord while prostrating supports the choice of masjid: the worshipper submits fully and places the face, the body's noblest part, on dust.
