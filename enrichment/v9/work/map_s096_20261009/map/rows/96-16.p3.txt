[TABATABAI:96:11-19/r5] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | holds | Calling the forehead deceitful and sinful is figurative: those qualities belong to its owner.
[BINTSHATI:v2p31/r4] BINTSHATI, d. 1419 · author | holds | The forelock is the hair at the front of the head and can stand for the face, anything foremost, or the leading people of a community.
[BINTSHATI:v2p31/r7] BINTSHATI, d. 1419 · author | holds | Calling the forelock lying and sinful invokes the Quran’s predominant religious sense of disbelief and misguidance.
[CORPUSCORANICUM:96:15-16:anmerkung/r2] CORPUSCORANICUM · Ibn Masʿūd | reports | Ibn Masʿūd transmits fāǧira as a variant in place of ḫāṭiʾa.
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p390/r4] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · author | holds | The second ‘forelock’ is grammatically a بدل of the preceding definite forelock; it can count as definite through its adjective despite lacking the article.
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-9-english:p390/r5] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · author | holds | The harsh wording conveys anger; the forehead represents a person’s honor, and the prostration mark is its greatest honor, so one who refuses prostration and prevents others deserves Hell.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:96:15/r1] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · Müfessirler | reports | Seizing the forelock is figurative; explanations include taking the offender to Hell, disgracing him, stamping his forehead, or humiliating him.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:96:15/r2] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | A self-satisfied tyrant who blocks worship may be left free for a time, but will ultimately be caught and punished in this world, the Hereafter, or both; Abu Jahl and others suffered defeat here and will face the promised afterlife punishment.
[QIRAAT-ER:96:16:3#1/r1] QIRAAT-ER · author | holds | The ten-reader variant drops hamza and substitutes yāʾ, a recognized dialectal hamza treatment that is semantically identical.
[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT-HALAQAT:p931/r3] SAMARRAI-LAMASAT-HALAQAT · author | holds | Badal may serve praise or blame; ناصية كاذبة خاطئة is blame, while وهذا البلد الأمين exemplifies praise.
