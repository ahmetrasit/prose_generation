[SAMIN-DURR:82:8/r1] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | reports | One analysis attaches في أي صورة to ركبك, treats ما as extra, and makes شاء an adjective of صورة; ركبك explains فعدلك rather than joining it with a فاء.
[SAMIN-DURR:82:8/r10] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | The passage yields three analyses of ما: extra, conditional with an omitted answer, or accusative as a verbal noun.
[SAMIN-DURR:82:8/r2] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | holds | On that analysis, God placed the person in the form his will specified, across such dimensions as beauty, ugliness, height, shortness, and sex.
[SAMIN-DURR:82:8/r3] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | reports | A second analysis treats the prepositional phrase as a circumstantial phrase attached to an omitted element: he assembled you present in one of the forms.
[SAMIN-DURR:82:8/r4] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | rejects | A third reported analysis attaches في أي صورة to عدلك, but the author objects that interrogative أي must lead its clause and cannot have a preceding governor.
[SAMIN-DURR:82:8/r5] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | rejects | Al-Zamakhshari senses wonder in أي, glossing the phrase as God proportioned the person in a wondrous form; the author says this still does not license a preceding governor.
[SAMIN-DURR:82:8/r6] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | prefers | The correct grammatical position is that even interrogative forms used for confirmation cannot have their governor precede them; the same restriction applies to the resembling exclamatory كيف and أنى and to كم الخبرية.
[SAMIN-DURR:82:8/r7] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · الزمخشري | reports | If في أي صورة attaches to عدلك, ما is accusative with شاء, apparently as a verbal noun: God assembled you as he willed, in a fine arrangement.
[SAMIN-DURR:82:8/r8] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · أبو البقاء | reports | Abu al-Baqa allows ما to be extra or conditional; either way the clause modifies صورة with an omitted return pronoun (ركبك عليها), and في attaches to ركبك.
[SAMIN-DURR:82:8/r9] SAMIN-DURR, d. 756 · author | reports | Another view gives the clause no syntactic position because في attaches to one of the two verbs, شاء or ركبك; the interrogative precedes ما, its proper position.
