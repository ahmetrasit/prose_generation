[IBNATIYYA:1:1/r84] IBNATIYYA, d. 542 · author | rejects | Ibn Atiyya knows no one who transmitted al-Kisa'i's form as a recognized reading; it matches Yahya b. Ziyad's grammatical analysis of Alif Lam Mim Allah.
