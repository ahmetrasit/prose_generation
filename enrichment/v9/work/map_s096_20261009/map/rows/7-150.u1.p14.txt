TAB-FULL (author): Full brothers invoke mother to appeal to their bond. NAHHAS (author): Maternal address appeals to kinship, poetic evidence. MAWARDI-FULL (الشاعر): A poetic address, son of my mother and twin of my soul, illustrates the appeal to kinship in Aarons plea.
