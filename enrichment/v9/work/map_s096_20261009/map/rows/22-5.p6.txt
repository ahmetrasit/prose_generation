[TABRISI:22:4-5/r18] TABRISI, d. 548 · author | holds | Some die before maturity, in childhood or youth.
[TABRISI:22:4-5/r19] TABRISI, d. 548 · author | reports | أرذل العمر means the worst or ugliest period of life in the eyes of one’s family; alternatively, the lowest and most contemptible age.

[TABRISI:22:4-5/r21] TABRISI, d. 548 · author | reports | One explanation is that the elderly no longer acquire knowledge and forget what they once knew.
[TABRISI:22:4-5/r22] TABRISI, d. 548 · author | reports | Alternatively, old age may extinguish reason or erase knowledge; losing most of one’s knowledge can be described as losing all of it.
