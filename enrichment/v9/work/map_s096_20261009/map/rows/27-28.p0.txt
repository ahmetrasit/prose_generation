[NAHHAS:v3p208/r2] NAHHAS, d. 338 · علي بن سليمان | rejects | The grammarians only permit the quiescent-hāʾ reading by an artificial assumption of pausing; ʿAlī ibn Sulaymān says to disregard it, since the same logic would permit dropping case endings from nouns in connected speech.
