[BINTSHATI:v2p31/r2] BINTSHATI, d. 1419 · author | holds | The word is also common for scorching by poisonous heat or flame, as when a blaze sears a face.
[BINTSHATI:v2p31/r3] BINTSHATI, d. 1419 · author | holds | Metaphorically, seizing someone by the forelock means dragging him violently to humiliate and punish him, while signaling the seizer’s power.
[BINTSHATI:v2p31/r4] BINTSHATI, d. 1419 · author | holds | The forelock is the hair at the front of the head and can stand for the face, anything foremost, or the leading people of a community.
[BINTSHATI:v2p31/r5] BINTSHATI, d. 1419 · author | holds | سفع occurs only here in the Quran; the forelock elsewhere signifies God’s control in Hud and the humiliation of sinners in al-Rahman. | mentions 11:56, 55:41
