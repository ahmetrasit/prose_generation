[RAZI-FULL:v30p71/r7] RAZI-FULL, d. 606 · author | holds | Character is a settled psychic disposition that makes beautiful actions easy; performing such actions and the ease of performing them are distinct.
[RAZI-FULL:v30p71/r8] RAZI-FULL, d. 606 · author | holds | Good character includes avoiding miserliness, stinginess, and anger; kindness in dealings and speech; avoiding estrangement; lenience in contracts; and generosity toward relatives and in-laws.
