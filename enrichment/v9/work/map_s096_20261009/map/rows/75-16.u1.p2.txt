  75:16/q14/p3 The verses continue the resurrection record scene rather than introducing a prophetic aside. — reasons: Al-Balkhī and al-Qaffāl invoke the surrounding self-witness and record passage; al-Jishumī finds this contextual fit stronger while conceding the prophetic reading is more apparent.   75:16/q14/p4 The seeming break prompted an allegation of missing or altered text. — reasons: Al-Khaṭṭābī records the objection that 75:16–19 separates 75:14–15 from 75:20–21. Al-Rāzī attributes alteration allegations to early Rāfiḍīs; al-Suyūṭī records a Shiʿite allegation of dropped material. Abū Ḥayyān repeats al-Rāzī's report. Al-Khaṭṭābī, al-Rāzī, and Abū Ḥayyān answer with contextual connections.
