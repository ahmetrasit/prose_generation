[MUJAHID:v1p108/r1] MUJAHID, d. 104 · مجاهد | reports | Mujahid interprets the three qurūʾ as three menstrual periods.
[MUJAHID:v1p108/r2] MUJAHID, d. 104 · مجاهد | reports | The concealed matter is pregnancy: a woman must not claim she is not pregnant when she is, or pregnant when she is not.
[MUJAHID:v1p108/r3] MUJAHID, d. 104 · مجاهد | reports | Mujahid takes “during that” to mean during the waiting period.
[MUQATIL:2:228/r1] MUQATIL, d. 150 · author | holds | A divorced woman who menstruates waits for three menstrual periods; Muqatil takes qurūʾ to mean menstruations.
[MUQATIL:2:228/r2] MUQATIL, d. 150 · author | holds | What God created in their wombs means a child; belief in God’s oneness and the resurrection entails not concealing it.
[MUQATIL:2:228/r3] MUQATIL, d. 150 · author | reports | The husband has the stronger right to take his wife back while she is pregnant; the verse concerns Ismaʿil al-Ghifari and his wife, who did not know she was pregnant.
[MUQATIL:2:228/r4] MUQATIL, d. 150 · author | holds | For reconciliation, Ismaʿil revoked the divorce while his wife was pregnant; she gave birth, then she and the child died.
[MUQATIL:2:228/r5] MUQATIL, d. 150 · author | holds | Women have rights from their husbands corresponding to the rights their husbands have over them, in fairness.
[MUQATIL:2:228/r6] MUQATIL, d. 150 · author | holds | Men have an advantage in rights over women; God is mighty and wise in His merciful ruling concerning pregnancy.
