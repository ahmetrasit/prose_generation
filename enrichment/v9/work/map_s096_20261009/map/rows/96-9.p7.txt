[ALUSI:96:9/r38] ALUSI, d. 1270 · علي | reports | A report says ʿAlī saw people praying before the Eid prayer and said he had not seen the Prophet do that; asked why he did not stop them, he feared the verse’s threat. [ALUSI:96:9/r4] ALUSI, d. 1270 · أبو حيان | reports | Abū Ḥayyān says the prayer was ẓuhr; another account says it was Islam’s first congregational prayer, with the Prophet, Abū Bakr, and ʿAlī, and that Abū Ṭālib urged Jaʿfar to join his cousin and left happily, composing verses in their support.
[ALUSI:96:9/r40] ALUSI, d. 1270 · author | holds | The caution about forbidding prayer extends to forbidding other forms of worship; verbal commands and circumstantial interference alike count, including distracting someone from worship, a common failing.
