[QURTUBI-FULL:2:285-286/r11] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · author | reports | The account glosses “if we forgot” as ignorance and “or erred” as deliberate action, while also offering the reading of acting through forgetfulness and error.
[QURTUBI-FULL:2:285-286/r12] QURTUBI-FULL, d. 671 · author | reports | The Miʿraj account glosses iṣr as a burden: earlier peoples had wholesome things forbidden for wrongdoing, sins written on their doors, and fifty prayers imposed before relief.

[BAYDAWI-FULL:2:286#2/r4] BAYDAWI-FULL, d. 685 · author | holds | The earlier people’s burdens may be Israelite duties—killing one another, cutting away impurity, fifty daily prayers, and giving a quarter of wealth as alms—or their hardships and ordeals.
