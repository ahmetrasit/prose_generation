[NASAFI-FULL:v2p6#3/r1] NASAFI-FULL, d. 710 · author | holds | Waswasa is repeated, quiet, unhurried speech; «waswasa lahu» means whispering for someone, whereas «waswasa ilayhi» means casting it to him.
[NASAFI-FULL:v2p6#3/r2] NASAFI-FULL, d. 710 · author | holds | The form for a man is «musawwis» with kasra, not «musawwas» with fatḥa; one says «musawwas lahu» or «musawwas ilayhi» of the recipient.
[NASAFI-FULL:v2p6#3/r3] NASAFI-FULL, d. 710 · author | holds | Satan aimed to expose what was concealed of their nakedness; uncovering private parts has always been repugnant to nature and reason and is a grave matter.
[NASAFI-FULL:v2p6#3/r4] NASAFI-FULL, d. 710 · author | holds | The second wāw is a long vowel, so it is not hamzated; two moving wāws would be heavy, unlike a wāw followed by a silent one.
