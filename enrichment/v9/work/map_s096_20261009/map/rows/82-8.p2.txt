[ELMALILI:82:8-19/r2] ELMALILI, d. 1361 · author | reports | A second parsing makes ayyi conditional and rakabaka future: God may form a person in any shape or remove him from human beauty into the ugliest forms, warning against delusion.
[ELMALILI:82:8-19/r3] ELMALILI, d. 1361 · author | reports | A third parsing attaches adalaka to fi ayyi sura and treats ma sha’a rakabaka as a separate relative, indefinite, or conditional clause; it can mean balancing or shaping at will, or reshaping if God wills.
