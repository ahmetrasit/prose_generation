[TABATABAI:11:112-113/r1] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | holds | The verse commands steadfastness in monotheism and servitude, requires believers to stand firm, and warns them not to cross God's limits because He sees their deeds.
[TABATABAI:11:112-113/r2] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | holds | The severe address singles out the Prophet and follows accounts of earlier nations' destruction, intensifying the warning. | mentions 17:74, 17:75
[TABATABAI:11:112/r1] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | holds | Standing signifies a person's stable, empowered state; the image extends to anything established so that it can produce its full effects, including justice, law, prayer, testimony, and religion.
[TABATABAI:11:112/r2] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | holds | Iqama sets something upright so all intended effects remain available; istiqama seeks and sustains that complete functioning, as with a straight road or a person perfecting an undertaking.
[TABATABAI:11:112/r3] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | holds | The command means steadfastly fulfilling religion and its right, maintaining monotheism in inward and outward life, as explained by earlier commands to face religion sincerely. | mentions 41:6, 41:30, 30:30
[TABATABAI:11:112/r4] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · صاحب روح المعاني | reports | Alusi suggests as commanded may refer to another, unrecited revelation and calls istiqama a comprehensive middle path between excess and neglect.
[TABATABAI:11:112/r5] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | rejects | He rejects an appeal to an unmentioned revelation: the Qur'an had already commanded the Prophet to establish himself upon religion, so no hidden instruction is needed.
[ASAD-NOTES:11:111#n141/r1] ASAD-NOTES, d. 1412 · Ibn Kathir | reports | Ibn Kathir takes the plural injunction as addressed to all believers and governing their conduct toward everyone, believer or unbeliever, following Ibn Abbas.
[ASAD-NOTES:11:111#n141/r2] ASAD-NOTES, d. 1412 · Ibn 'Abbas | reports | Ibn Abbas explains the injunction as humility before God and avoiding false pride toward anyone.
[ASAD-NOTES:11:111#n141/r3] ASAD-NOTES, d. 1412 · Tabari, Zamakhshari, Baghawi, Razi | reports | Later commentators broaden the command to mean staying within God's limits and not exceeding equity.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:11:112/r1] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | Istiqama in the Qur’an means comprehensive, continuous, consistent religious devotion and life; the verse combines uprightness as commanded with not exceeding God’s limits.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:11:112/r2] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · Resûlullah | reports | The Prophet said Hud and its sister surahs made him old, explaining that the command to remain upright as commanded was what aged him because it was especially difficult to fulfill.
