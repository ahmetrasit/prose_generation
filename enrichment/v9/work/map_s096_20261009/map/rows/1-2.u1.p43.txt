RAZI-FULL, d. 606 (author): The seven Fatiha verses remove seven vices: praise dispels desire, Lord of the worlds greed, Judgment Day anger, worship and aid pride and vanity, guidance caprice, the favored path unbelief and doubt, and the final phrase innovation. RAZI-FULL, d. 606 (author): Raising the hands signals leaving both worlds and directing all inner faculties toward God; “God is greatest” means He exceeds all beings, knowledge, comparison, and even the claim that He is simply greater.
