[ABUHAYYAN-FULL:v10p292/r4] ABUHAYYAN-FULL, d. 745 · author | holds | The passive construction means that the hearing of a group of jinn occurred; the common account links it to 46:29 as one event, while another makes the Meccan and Nakhlah encounters two events. | mentions 46:29
