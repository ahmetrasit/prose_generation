  18:34/q20/p1 Moses, Aaron and the Night Journey are described in distinct reports. — reasons: Al-Itqān reports Moses as tall, dark and curly-haired and al-Thaʿlabī gives his lifespan as 120. Aaron's kinship is disputed between full, maternal or paternal brother; he is described as taller and eloquent, born a year earlier and dying first. A Night Journey report places him in the fifth heaven with half-white beard; Ibn Miskawayh glosses his Hebrew name as 'beloved.'
  18:34/q20/p2 David's lineage, appearance and reign are reported. — reasons: Al-Itqān lists his ancestry through Jesse, Judah and Jacob; al-Tirmidhī/Kaʿb describe his devotion, appearance, voice and prophethood with kingship; al-Nawawī reports 100 years of life, 40 of rule and twelve sons.
  18:34/q20/p3 Solomon's qualities and reign are reported. — reasons: Kaʿb describes his appearance, devotion, humility and early consultation by his father. Ibn ʿAbbās names him and Dhū al-Qarnayn among believing world kings, against Nimrod and Nebuchadnezzar. Historians give accession at thirteen, Temple building four years later and death at fifty-three.
  18:34/q20/p4 Job's lineage is uncertain, with different reports. — reasons: Ibn Isḥāq says only his father Abyad is secure; Ibn Jarīr traces him through Esau to Isaac; Ibn ʿAsākir makes his mother Lot's daughter and his father Abraham's believer, placing him before Moses.
  18:34/q20/p5 Accounts of early conversions and teaching in Yathrib are excerpted. — reasons: Bint al-Shāṭiʾ reports ʿUmar hearing Ṭā Hā after learning his sister and her husband converted, then accepting Islam. After al-ʿAqaba the Prophet sent Muṣʿab to Yathrib to teach with Asʿad, and Usayd arrived armed to confront them at Saʿd's request.
