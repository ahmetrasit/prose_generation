[SAMARRAI-LAMASAT-HALAQAT:p371/r2] SAMARRAI-LAMASAT-HALAQAT · author | holds | The prefixed س marks an assured future, a view attributed to al-Zamakhshari and many grammarians; the Prophet's ongoing ease needs no such marker, while others receive ease after the stated deeds. | mentions 87:8
