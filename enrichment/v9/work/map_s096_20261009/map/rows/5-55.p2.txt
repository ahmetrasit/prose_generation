[TABRISI:5:55-56#3/r2] TABRISI, d. 548 · أبو ذر | reports | The report says Gabriel told Muḥammad to recite the revelation beginning innamā waliyyukum Allāh wa-rasūluhu wa-lladhīna āmanū.

[TABRISI:5:55-56#4/r1] TABRISI, d. 548 · ابن عباس عن أبي صالح | reports | In the continuation of the Ibn ʿAbbās account, the beggar says the standing man gave him a silver ring while bowing; the Prophet exclaims and recites the verse about God's party being victorious.

[TABRISI:5:55-56#4/r4] TABRISI, d. 548 · author | holds | God, His Messenger acting by God's command, and the believers described next are the ones who manage people's interests and whose authority over them requires obedience.
[TABRISI:5:55-56#4/r5] TABRISI, d. 548 · author | holds | The believers in view are described as those who establish prayer with its conditions and give zakāt while bowing.
[TABRISI:5:55-56#4/r6] TABRISI, d. 548 · author | holds | He identifies the believers here as ʿAlī, making this one of the clearest proofs of ʿAlī's immediate imamate after the Prophet.
[TABRISI:5:55-56#4/r7] TABRISI, d. 548 · author | holds | His argument is that walī means the one more entitled to manage people's affairs and whose obedience is obligatory; once the believers are identified as ʿAlī, the verse explicitly appoints him as imam.
[TABRISI:5:55-56#5/r1] TABRISI, d. 548 · author | holds | Innamā is restrictive: it confines the stated guardianship to those named and negates it for others.
