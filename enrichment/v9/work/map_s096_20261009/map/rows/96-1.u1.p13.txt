  96:1/q34/p1 Gabriel taught refuge before the basmala and first command. — reasons: Rāzī reports Ibn ʿAbbās that Gabriel taught refuge, then basmala, then Iqraʾ; Ibn al-Jazarī calls one chain weak and disconnected and cites al-Dānī's alternative.
