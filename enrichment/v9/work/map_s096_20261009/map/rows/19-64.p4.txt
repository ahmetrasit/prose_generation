[QASHANI:19:64-65/r2] QASHANI, d. 736 · author | holds | What lies behind comprises lower, earthly stages of the World of Dominion.
[QASHANI:19:64-65/r3] QASHANI, d. 736 · author | holds | What lies between is the dominion's stages where we dwell; all remain under divine mastery, command, and knowledge.
