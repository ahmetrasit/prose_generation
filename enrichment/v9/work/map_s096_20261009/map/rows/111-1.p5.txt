[RAZI:111:1/r37] RAZI, d. 606 · author | holds | Kinship would normally demand compassion, but Abū Lahab’s extreme hostility merited an equally severe rebuke.

[RAZI:111:1/r39] RAZI, d. 606 · author | holds | In al-Kāfirūn the unbelievers attacked God, so God told Muḥammad to answer; here they attacked Muḥammad, so God answers Abū Lahab directly. | mentions 109:1
