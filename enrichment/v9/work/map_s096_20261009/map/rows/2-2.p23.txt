[IBNASHUR-FULL:v1p221/r3] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · author | holds | The genus reading means the Quran gathers the perfect qualities of books, while other scriptures lack some such perfection; grammarians call this definite article a marker of perfection.
