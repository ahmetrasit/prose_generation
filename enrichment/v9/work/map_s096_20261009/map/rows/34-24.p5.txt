[TABATABAI:34:24/r5] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | holds | After decisive proof, the Prophet frames the disagreement fairly: one side must be guided and the other clearly astray, so the hearers should judge the evidence and distinguish truth from falsehood.
[TABATABAI:34:24/r6] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | holds | The contrast between being on guidance and being in error pictures the guided as elevated at a beacon overlooking the path, while the astray are immersed in darkness without knowing their direction.
[ASAD-NOTES:34:24#n35/r1] ASAD-NOTES, d. 1412 · author | holds | Asad refers readers to note 49 on 10:31 for the opening question about who provides sustenance. | mentions 10:31
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:34:22/r7] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · müfessirlerin çoğunluğu | reports | Most commentators say the verse refutes opponents’ claims through an elevated expression that follows the rules of disputation.
[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:34:22/r8] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · Râzî | reports | Razi presents the verse as a method for productive debate: admitting either side may be wrong avoids prejudice and lets both reconsider sincerely.
[SAMARRAI-ASRAR:r7#7/r3] SAMARRAI-ASRAR · author | holds | In 34:24, على presents the guided as elevated and firmly established, whereas في portrays the misguided as fallen or immersed; other verses consistently use these prepositions this way. | mentions 2:5, 27:79, 23:54, 9:45, 7:186, 19:75
[SAMARRAI-ASRAR:r7#7/r4] SAMARRAI-ASRAR · الكشاف | reports | Al-Kashshaf pictures the person with truth riding a fine horse at will, while the misguided person is submerged and lost in darkness.
[SAMARRAI-ASRAR:r7#7/r6] SAMARRAI-ASRAR · التفسير القيم | reports | By contrast, في for error, doubt, and darkness depicts a person immersed and entangled within them. | mentions 9:45, 6:39, 23:54, 11:110
[SAMARRAI-ASRAR:r7#7/r7] SAMARRAI-ASRAR · author | holds | The path of truth rises with its traveler toward God Most High, whereas the path of error descends with its follower toward the lowest depths.
[STUDYQURAN:34:24/r1] STUDYQURAN · IJ | reports | The question implies that there is no creator besides God who provides; provision from heaven alludes to rain and from earth to plants. | mentions 35:3, 10:31, 27:64

[STUDYQURAN:34:24/r4] STUDYQURAN · IJ, Ṭ | reports | Others retain aw's disjunctive force: either group may be astray or guided, according to the wording.
[STUDYQURAN:34:24/r5] STUDYQURAN · JJ | reports | The slight ambiguity that disbelievers might perceive in the wording is understood by some as a gentle invitation to embrace faith.
