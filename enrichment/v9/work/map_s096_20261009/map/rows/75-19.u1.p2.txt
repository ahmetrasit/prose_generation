  75:19/q15/p2 It means “indeed,” introducing the following assertion without rebuke. — reasons: The alternative allows pausing before kallā and beginning with it. Al-Kisāʾī gives this sense; Makkī treats kallā as a noun here, citing a tanwīn reading and possible roots denoting weariness or burden. Abū Ḥayyān rejects a noun analysis in his answer to the tanwīn analogy.
  75:19/q15/p3 It serves as the opening particle alā, calling attention to what follows. — reasons: Abū Ḥātim proposes this; Abū Ḥayyān says none preceded him and al-Zajjāj and others followed.
  75:19/q15/p4 It answers like “yes.” — reasons: Al-Naḍr ibn Shumayl cites “By the moon” for this response use.
  75:19/q15/p5 It carries the future sense “soon.” — reasons: Al-Farrāʾ and Ibn Saʿdān offer this; Abū Ḥayyān records it.
  75:19/q15/p6 Rebuke need not be present in every kallā occurrence, especially after this distant clause. — reasons: Ibn Hishām says the proposed rebuke is strained here, as at verses concerning human fashioning and standing. Other scholars therefore add a second, non-rebuking sense and permit beginning with kallā.
