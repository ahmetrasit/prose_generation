[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v1p505#3/r4] IBNKATHIR-FULL, d. 774 · أبو موسى الأشعري | reports | Abū Mūsā reports that during a campaign the Prophet told the Companions to lower their voices: they call one who hears and sees, not one deaf or absent, and is nearer than a rider’s mount’s neck; he then taught them that there is no might or power except through God, a treasure of Paradise. The report is in the two Ṣaḥīḥs and other collections.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v1p505#3/r5] IBNKATHIR-FULL, d. 774 · النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم | reports | Anas reports that the Prophet quoted God as saying, ‘I am as My servant thinks of Me, and I am with him when he calls upon Me.’
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v1p506/r1] IBNKATHIR-FULL, d. 774 · أبو هريرة | reports | Abū Hurayra reports God’s promise to be with His servant while the servant remembers Him and moves his lips in remembrance.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v1p506/r2] IBNKATHIR-FULL, d. 774 · author | holds | Ibn Kathīr links this assurance of divine company to God’s being with the pious and doers of good, and His promise to Moses and Aaron to hear and see them. | mentions 16:128, 20:46
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v1p506/r3] IBNKATHIR-FULL, d. 774 · author | holds | God does not disappoint a supplicant or become distracted from the prayer; He hears supplication. The verse encourages prayer, which is never lost with Him.
[IBNKATHIR-FULL:v1p506/r4] IBNKATHIR-FULL, d. 774 · سلمان الفارسي | reports | Salman reports the Prophet’s statement that God is too generous to return a servant’s raised hands empty after the servant asks Him for good. Al-Tirmidhī grades it ḥasan gharīb; some transmitters did not raise it to the Prophet, and other collections transmit it.
