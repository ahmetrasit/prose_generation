[TABATABAI:31:26-27/r2] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | holds | As source of creation and giver of every perfection, God must possess all that beings need; otherwise he could not originate or give it, so he is absolutely independent.
[TABATABAI:31:26-27/r3] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | holds | Absolute independence entails that God owns everything and may dispose of it freely; no other entity can independently own or govern creation.
[TABATABAI:31:26-27/r4] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | holds | Hamd is praise for chosen beauty; because every beauty in the world is God's, praise returns to him and he is absolutely praiseworthy.
[TABATABAI:31:26-27/r5] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | holds | If any beautiful, ordered governance came from another independently, praise would belong to that other, contradicting God's absolute praiseworthiness.
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-6-2:p122/r1] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · author | holds | Everything in the heavens and earth is God's creation and subject to Him; all depend on Him, while He alone is self-sufficient and worthy of praise.
[ISLAHI-TADABBUR:vol-6-2:p122/r2] ISLAHI-TADABBUR · author | holds | Because God alone is self-sufficient and possesses every praiseworthy attribute, no one can share His claim to gratitude.
