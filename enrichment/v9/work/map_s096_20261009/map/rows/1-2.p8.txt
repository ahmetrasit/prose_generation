[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v1p66/r1] IBNATIYYA-FULL, d. 542 · author | holds | Al-hamd is complete praise, with the definite article covering the whole class of praise; it is broader than thanks, which responds to a favor, while praise may simply describe qualities.

[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v1p66/r3] IBNATIYYA-FULL, d. 542 · بعض الناس | reports | Some distinguish thanks as praise for God's acts and favors, and praise as praise of his attributes; the author finds this more accurate than synonymy.
[IBNATIYYA-FULL:v1p66/r4] IBNATIYYA-FULL, d. 542 · author | rejects | Al-Tabari's evidence “praise to God, in thanks” actually limits praise to praise for a favor and therefore argues against synonymy.
