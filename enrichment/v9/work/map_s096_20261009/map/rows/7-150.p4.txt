[KHATTABI:v1p65/r2] KHATTABI, d. 388 · author | holds | Literary comparison can rank poets within a subject rather than require direct imitation: each may excel in a genre, and the stronger is the one who captures its details and fine meanings most fully.
[KHATTABI:v1p65/r3] KHATTABI, d. 388 · author | holds | Once the conditions and forms of rhetorical contest are understood, it is clear that the Quraysh produced no genuine answer to the Qur’an’s challenge.
