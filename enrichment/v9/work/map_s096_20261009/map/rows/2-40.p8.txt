[RAZI-FULL:v3p37/r13] RAZI-FULL, d. 606 · author | holds | The passage indicates that the Messenger was sent to Israelites as well as Arabs.

[RAZI-FULL:v3p37/r15] RAZI-FULL, d. 606 · author | holds | If a servant acted independently, one would have to fear the servant as one fears God, and indeed fear oneself because control of reward and punishment would be in one's own hands; this would defeat the verse's exclusive command.
[RAZI-FULL:v3p37/r16] RAZI-FULL, d. 606 · author | holds | The verse also indicates that a morally responsible person must perform obedience through fear and hope, which are necessary to its validity.
[RAZI-FULL:v3p37/r2] RAZI-FULL, d. 606 · أصحابنا | reports | Razi's Ash'ari school denies that anything is owed by a servant upon God; since prior blessings ground the servant's duty, obedience does not generate a further duty of reward, contrary to the Mu'tazilites.
[RAZI-FULL:v3p37/r3] RAZI-FULL, d. 606 · author | prefers | Razi says the proper account is that God's promised reward must occur: otherwise His truthful report would become a lie, which is impossible, making the promise more certain than an oath or vow.

[RAZI-FULL:v3p37/r8] RAZI-FULL, d. 606 · author | holds | When someone is unsure whether they obeyed fully or avoided prohibitions, fear arises from uncertainty about deserving reward.
