[ELMALILI:68:1-6/r4] ELMALILI, d. 1361 · author | holds | Nūn can point from the ordered components of creation to God, just as words and sentences are assembled from letters; as a specific letter it can also indicate the alphabet generally.
[ELMALILI:68:1-6/r5] ELMALILI, d. 1361 · author | holds | Other possible allusions are the human groan and cosmic resonance, the earth-and-sky world viewed as a hemisphere, or the letter’s resemblance to an inkwell and ink.

[ELMALILI:68:1-6/r7] ELMALILI, d. 1361 · author | holds | Nūn takes the mind from one point into the depths, inviting reflection on the letters of the revealed Book, the Book of creation, and the origin of existence.
