[WAHIDI-BASIT:v1p530/r2] WAHIDI-BASIT, d. 468 · author | holds | Relative pronouns such as alladhī and alladhīna are incomplete until their clause supplies a predicate, verb, circumstance, or conditional structure; that clause must contain a pronoun referring back.
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v1p530/r3] WAHIDI-BASIT, d. 468 · author | holds | Alladhīna is indeclinable across nominative, accusative, and genitive because it remains incomplete without its relative clause and thus resembles a particle.
[WAHIDI-BASIT:v1p530/r4] WAHIDI-BASIT, d. 468 · author | holds | A singular indeclinable relative gains case marking in the dual because particles are not dualized, but the plural is again a single form like hāʾulāʾ and remains indeclinable.
