[DURR:1:1/r150] DURR, d. 911 · عمر بن عبد العزيز | reports | Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz struck a scribe for writing the mim before the sin and dismissed another who omitted the sin.

[DURR:1:1/r153] DURR, d. 911 · الليث | reports | Layth objected when a scribe elongated the basmala's ba, saying it changes the meaning by making it resemble a lam.
[DURR:1:1/r154] DURR, d. 911 · النبي | reports | A mursal report says the Prophet forbade putting the basmala on the ground and said it belongs only in its proper place.
[DURR:1:1/r155] DURR, d. 911 · النبي | reports | A report from Anas promises that respectfully lifting paper bearing the basmala earns the rank of the truthful and eases his parents' burden, even if unbelievers.

[DURR:1:1/r157] DURR, d. 911 · ابن عباس | reports | A report from Ibn Abbas says Quraysh cursed the Prophet when he said the basmala in Mecca.
[DURR:1:1/r158] DURR, d. 911 · سعيد بن جبير | reports | Sa'id ibn Jubayr says the Prophet first recited the basmala aloud in Mecca, then concealed it after Meccans linked al-Rahman to Musaylima.
[DURR:1:1/r159] DURR, d. 911 · ابن عباس | reports | Another report says Meccan polytheists mocked the basmala because Musaylima called himself al-Rahman, after which the Prophet was ordered not to recite it aloud.
[DURR:1:1/r16] DURR, d. 911 · الشعبي | reports | Al-Sha'bi prescribed al-Fatiha, the foundation of the Qur'an, to a man with flank pain.
[DURR:1:1/r160] DURR, d. 911 · أنس | reports | Anas says the Prophet, Abu Bakr, and Umar recited the basmala quietly.
[DURR:1:1/r161] DURR, d. 911 · عبد الله بن مغفل | reports | Ibn Abd Allah ibn Maghfal says his father objected to his reciting aloud, having heard none of the first caliphs do so behind the Prophet.
[DURR:1:1/r162] DURR, d. 911 · ابن عباس | reports | Ibn Abbas calls loud recitation of the basmala Bedouin-style recitation.
[DURR:1:1/r163] DURR, d. 911 · إبراهيم | reports | Ibrahim calls an imam's loud recitation of the basmala an innovation.
[DURR:1:1/r164] DURR, d. 911 · الحسن | reports | Yahya ibn Atiq says al-Hasan directed scribes to place the basmala at the imam's opening and draw a line between pairs of suras.
[DURR:1:1/r17] DURR, d. 911 · علي | reports | Ali identifies al-Hamd as the seven oft-repeated verses and counts the basmala as the seventh ayah.
[DURR:1:1/r18] DURR, d. 911 · النبي | reports | The Prophet says al-Fatiha has seven verses, one of them the basmala, and is the seven oft-repeated verses and Great Qur'an.
[DURR:1:1/r19] DURR, d. 911 · أم سلمة | reports | Umm Salama reports the Prophet recited the full seven-verse al-Fatiha beginning with the basmala.
[DURR:1:1/r2] DURR, d. 911 · إبراهيم | reports | Ibrahim asked al-Aswad whether al-Fatiha belongs to the Qur'an; al-Aswad affirmed that it does.
[DURR:1:1/r20] DURR, d. 911 · النبي | reports | The Prophet told Abu Sa'id ibn al-Mualla that al-Hamd is the greatest sura, the seven oft-repeated verses, and Great Qur'an given to him.
[DURR:1:1/r21] DURR, d. 911 · النبي | reports | The Prophet told Ubayy that no counterpart to al-Fatiha was revealed in the Torah, Gospel, Psalms, or Qur'an.
[DURR:1:1/r22] DURR, d. 911 · النبي | reports | The Prophet describes al-Fatiha as divided between God and the servant; the servant receives what he asks.

[DURR:1:1/r26] DURR, d. 911 · النبي | reports | The Prophet says scripture is the most rightful basis for taking a fee after companions cured a bitten man with al-Fatiha.
[DURR:1:1/r27] DURR, d. 911 · عبد الله بن جابر | reports | A good-chain report calls al-Fatiha the best sura revealed and says it contains healing for every illness.
[DURR:1:1/r28] DURR, d. 911 · السائب بن يزيد | reports | A weak report says the Prophet treated al-Sa'ib ibn Yazid with al-Fatiha and a light spitting.
[DURR:1:1/r29] DURR, d. 911 · أبو سعيد الخدري | reports | Al-Fatiha is reported from the Prophet as a cure for poison; another route attributes the saying to Abu Sa'id and Abu Hurayra.
[DURR:1:1/r3] DURR, d. 911 · محمد بن سيرين | reports | Ubayy wrote al-Fatiha, the two refuge suras, and two supplicatory phrases; Ibn Masud wrote none; Uthman wrote al-Fatiha and the refuge suras.
