[SIBAWAYH:(هذابابآخرأنفيهمخففة)#512/r4] SIBAWAYH, d. 180 · author | holds | The أن that governs subjunctive verbs does not occur in a context of certainty and assertion.

[SIBAWAYH:(هذابابآخرأنفيهمخففة)#512/r8] SIBAWAYH, d. 180 · author | holds | If خشيت concerns an event already established as certain, indicative is possible, though it is not the usual construction.

[SIBAWAYH:(هذابابالإضافةإلىكلاسمكانآخرهألفاوكانعلىخمسةأحرف)#565/r5] SIBAWAYH, d. 180 · author | holds | Mamdūd nouns retain the final hamza in nisba regardless of length or declension because its case-vowel mobility makes it strong.
[SIBAWAYH:(هذابابالإضافةإلىكلاسمكانآخرهألفاوكانعلىخمسةأحرف)#565/r6] SIBAWAYH, d. 180 · author | holds | The maqṣūr alif is weak because it takes no case endings, so it is deleted; a quiescent yāʾ can likewise drop, unlike a moving one.

[SIBAWAYH:(هذابابالإضافةإلىكلاسمكانآخرهألفاوكانعلىخمسةأحرف)#565/r8] SIBAWAYH, d. 180 · author | holds | Nisba on عثير or حثيل follows the pattern of حميري.

[SIBAWAYH:(هذابابالوقففيms754آخرالكلمالمتحركةفيالوصلالتيلاتلحقهازيادة#733/r9] SIBAWAYH, d. 180 · author | holds | In accusative and genitive forms speakers use rawm, doubling, or plain silence, but not ishmām, which depends on the nominative ḍamma.
