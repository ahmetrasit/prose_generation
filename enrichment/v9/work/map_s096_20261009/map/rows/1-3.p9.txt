[IBNASHUR-FULL:v1p171/r4] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · author | holds | الرحمن is restricted to God, while الرحيم describes mercy reaching many recipients and can also describe the Messenger; therefore the second adjective adds meaning instead of repeating the first. | mentions 9:128
