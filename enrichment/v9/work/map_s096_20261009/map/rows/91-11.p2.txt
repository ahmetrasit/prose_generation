[BIQAI:91:11/r3] BIQAI, d. 885 · author | holds | Thamud are singled out because their sign was exceptionally clear and especially indicative of the Hour; Quraysh and other Arabs knew them from ruins and transmitted reports.
[BIQAI:91:11/r4] BIQAI, d. 885 · author | holds | One explanation is that their own disposition to exceed bounds—in disbelief, sins, and oppression—caused them to deny their messenger and all he brought from God.
