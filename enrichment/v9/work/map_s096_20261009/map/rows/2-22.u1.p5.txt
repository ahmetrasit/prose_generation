  2:22/q18/p1 Some favor heaven for its angels, purity, scriptural praise, and higher position. — reasons: RAZI-FULL [RAZI-FULL:v2p97/r1]: Some prefer heaven because it is where angels worship and no place in it has seen anyone disobey God. RAZI-FULL [RAZI-FULL:v2p97/r3]: A third argument favors heaven because scripture calls it a protected roof and says it has constellations, with no comparable description of earth. RAZI-FULL [RAZI-FULL:v2p97/r4]: A fourth argument is that scripture most often mentions heaven before earth.   2:22/q18/p2 Others favor earth for its blessed places, prophets, and use for prayer. — reasons: RAZI-FULL [RAZI-FULL:v2p97/r5]: Others prefer earth because God singled out several earthly places as blessed: Mecca, the blessed spot at the tree, Jerusalem's surroundings, and the lands of Syria.
