[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:105/r1] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | The ten-verse passage explains how the Prophet and, through him, the Muslim community should act in disputes; specific occasions introduce general rules for Muslims through the end of time.

[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:105/r14] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | Advocacy retains legitimacy when lawyers defend rightful claims, seek settlement in unjust cases, and help ensure fairness in penalties and compensation.

[KURANYOLU-TEFSIR:4:105/r5] KURANYOLU-TEFSIR · author | holds | The Prophet cannot err in understanding the Qur’an or applying its universal principles to particular matters, since these tasks belong to conveying the message; other people can err in ijtihad.
