[IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p207/r15] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · author | holds | The clarification identifies Tasnim as a spring from which the brought-near drink.
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p207/r16] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · author | holds | The brought-near are the righteous; those who drink from this water are themselves brought near.
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p208/r1] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · author | holds | The bāʾ may indicate cause, with yashrabu carrying the sense “mix”; the drinkers mix the nectar with Tasnim.
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p208/r2] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · author | holds | On this parse, the brought-near mix the nectar with Tasnim, as the preceding verse says its blend comes from Tasnim. | mentions 83:27
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p208/r3] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · author | holds | Alternatively, the bāʾ indicates accompaniment; an omitted object is the nectar, which they drink while beside and gathered around the spring.
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p208/r4] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · الأصمعي والفارسي وابن قتيبة وابن مالك | reports | Al-Aṣmaʿī, al-Fārisī, Ibn Qutayba, and Ibn Mālik list a partitive bāʾ meaning “from,” an interpretation attributed to Kufan grammarians.
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p208/r5] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · author | rejects | Ibn ʿĀshūr finds the partitive reading unclear: Arabic commonly constructs “drink” with bāʾ rather than min, so the grammarians may mean accompaniment.
[IBNASHUR-FULL:v30p208/r6] IBNASHUR-FULL, d. 1393 · author | holds | Another possibility is an extra bāʾ, as in Abū Dhūʾayb’s line about clouds drinking sea water and rising above it.

[TABATABAI:83:28-36/r2] TABATABAI, d. 1402 · author | holds | The close ones drink Tasnim pure, whereas the sealed nectar of the righteous is mixed with it; this shows Tasnim's superiority and the close ones' higher rank. | mentions 83:27
