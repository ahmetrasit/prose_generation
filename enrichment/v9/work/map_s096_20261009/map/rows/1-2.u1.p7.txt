BIQAI-FULL, d. 885 (الحرالي): Because praise is the seventh inclusive letter, the Fatiha begins with it and gathers the seven universals diffused through the Qur'an, as the Qur'an gathers earlier scriptures. BIQAI-FULL, d. 885 (الحرالي): The first verse contains praise's seventh letter; the second contains the lawful and forbidden letters that Rahmaniyyah establishes for worldly life, allowing enjoyment and removing either prohibition's burden or sin. BIQAI-FULL, d. 885 (الحرالي): The fifth verse's request returns to praise, the sixth to the grace of lawful and forbidden provisions, and the seventh to the sovereign's command; together these complete all seven letters.
