[TAB:1:7/r12] TAB, d. 310 · author | holds | The reciters unanimously read ghayr with a genitive ending.
[TAB:1:7/r13] TAB, d. 310 · author | holds | On one parsing, ghayr is an adjective of alladhīna: the relative phrase is not a specific proper-name-like definite, so an indefinite adjective can describe it.
[TAB:1:7/r14] TAB, d. 310 · author | holds | Alternatively, ghayr is genitive by an understood repetition of ṣirāṭ: “the path of those You favored, the path of those not under wrath.”
[TAB:1:7/r15] TAB, d. 310 · author | holds | The two genitive parses are close in meaning: those favored with true guidance are not under God's wrath or astray; the adjectival parse states this directly.
[TAB:1:7/r16] TAB, d. 310 · author | rejects | Although a grammatical route for reading ghayr in the accusative exists, al-Ṭabarī rejects it as anomalous against the reciters' transmitted consensus.
[TAB:1:7/r17] TAB, d. 310 · author | holds | The possible accusative parse treats ghayr as a detached adjective of the pronoun in ʿalayhim, which is syntactically governed by anʿamta.
