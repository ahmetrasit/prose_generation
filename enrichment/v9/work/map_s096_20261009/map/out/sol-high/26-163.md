# 26:163: 5 questions

## 26:163/q01 What does Lot urge by commanding his people to fear God?  [فَٱتَّقُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ · interpretation]
- 26:163/q01/p1 Worship God and avoid disobeying Him, fearing His punishment and power to destroy. — Muqatil says Lot calls them to worship God. Al-Tabari says they should fear punishment for denying God's messenger. Ibn Kathir adds worship of God alone and avoidance of disobedience. Al-Biqa'i grounds the fear in God's ability to destroy whom He wills. Elmalılı states the command without further explanation. [5 notes: MUQATIL; TAB-FULL; IBNKATHIR-FULL; BIQAI-FULL; ELMALILI]

## 26:163/q02 What does obeying Lot mean, and why should his people obey him?  [وَأَطِيعُونِ · interpretation]
- 26:163/q02/p1 Follow Lot's counsel and call as God's messenger; his guidance and commands are trustworthy. — Muqatil describes following Lot's counsel. Al-Tabari says Lot's call guides to the right path and cites 3:50. Ata, reported by Ibn Abi Hatim, explains obedience to the messenger as following the Book and the Sunna. Ibn Kathir identifies Lot as the messenger sent to them. Al-Biqa'i says obedience brings safety and Lot commands only what God approves. Bursevi says a trusted person's word is reliable. Elmalılı states Lot's call to obey him without further explanation. [7 notes: MUQATIL; TAB-FULL; IBNABIHATIM(عطاء); IBNKATHIR-FULL; BIQAI-FULL; BURSEVI; ELMALILI]

## 26:163/q03 Is the final yāʾ of وَأَطِيعُونِ retained or shortened?  [وَأَطِيعُونِ · readings]
turns on: Whether the fuller final yāʾ is affirmed in recitation and spelling or shortened to match the verse endings.
- 26:163/q03/p1 The final yāʾ is retained, giving the fuller -nī form. — Ibn al-Jazari reports that Yaqub affirms the extra yāʾ in both connected reading and pause; أطيعون occurs in eight of his sixteen listed instances. Qiraat-ER says adding the yāʾ restores the fuller -nī pronoun and lengthens the ending. [2 notes: NASHR(يعقوب); QIRAAT-ER]
- 26:163/q03/p2 The yāʾ is shortened in spelling and pronunciation. — Ibn Atiyya says shortening matches the verse endings. [1 notes: IBNATIYYA-FULL]

## 26:163/q04 Does Lot seek payment for this call?  [* · interpretation]
- 26:163/q04/p1 No; his reward is recompense from the Lord of the worlds. — Yahya ibn Sallam says Lot asks no payment. [1 notes: YAHYA-SALLAM]

## 26:163/q05 How does Lot's appeal connect with the surrounding prophetic discourse?  [* · links]
- 26:163/q05/p1 The opening messenger appeal and command recur in earlier prophetic anecdotes. — Islahi says the earlier anecdotes already explained this recurring appeal and command. [1 notes: ISLAHI-TADABBUR]
- 26:163/q05/p2 Lot's preceding argument rests on a demonstrative proof also reflected in parallel wording in al-ʿAnkabut. — Al-Tabatabai points to the preceding discussion and cites 29:29 as parallel wording. [1 notes: TABATABAI]
