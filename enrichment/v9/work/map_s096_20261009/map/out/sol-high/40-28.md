## 40:28/q01 How does the believer's intervention follow Pharaoh's threat?  [وَقَالَ · links]
- 40:28/q01/p1 God raised him when Pharaoh threatened Moses — IBNJAWZI-ZAD, d. 597: As Pharaoh sought to kill Moses, the believing man from Pharaoh's people spoke up.
