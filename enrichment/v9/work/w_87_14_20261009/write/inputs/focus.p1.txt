- 87:14/q05/p6 Giving charitable alms from one's wealth, including general charity.
  reasons: Abū al-Aḥwaṣ urges even a small gift before prayer and sometimes two units afterward; Ibn Masʿūd applies the sequence to giving before setting out for prayer. Ibn ʿAbbās, asked about fiṭra, says he heard no such restriction and means all zakāt and then all charities. Qatāda says wealth-giving pleases the Creator; Nasafī compares the verb to tasaddaqa.

- 87:14/q05/p8 Paying the charity of breaking the fast before Eid prayer.
  reasons: Saʿīd b. al-Musayyib, Abū Saʿīd, Ibn ʿUmar, Abū al-ʿĀliya, Ibn Sīrīn and others apply the verse to fiṭra; variants place payment before setting out, at the prayer ground, or early Eid morning. Abū al-ʿĀliya says Medinans valued it with providing water. A prophetic report through Kathīr b. ʿAbd Allāh is marked weak by some, while Tirmidhī called its transmitter ḥasan; other reports receive no grade. The Imams' reports also support this.
