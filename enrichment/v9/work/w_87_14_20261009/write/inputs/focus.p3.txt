# 87:14/q10 How does this promise relate to the sūra's warning and opening? [* · links]
turns on: Which part of the sūra or other verse is taken as the key to this promise: the immediate warning, the opening command, or parallel accounts of purification and success.
- 87:14/q10/p1 It balances the warning about the most wretched with the reward of those who fear, heed the reminder and purify themselves.
  reasons: Muqātil contrasts Meccan unbelievers who neither purify nor pray. Rāzī supplies unbelief as purification's object from the warning. Ibn ʿĀshūr calls the verse a resumption after punishment and identifies the purified with those who fear and remember. Corpus Coranicum describes a balance, and a chiasm between responses in 10–11 and outcomes in 12–15, comparing 92:5–11 and 14–21; it says the reminder's implied content includes fear, purification, invocation and prayer.
  holders (12 notes): MUQATIL; RAZI; BIQAI; IBNASHUR; IBNASHUR-FULL; QUTB-ZILAL; CORPUSCORANICUM; ISLAHI-TADABBUR

# 87:14/q11 Does 'this' in the closing scripture statement include verse 14? [* · links]
- 87:14/q11/p1 Yes; it refers to the nearby passage on purification, prayer, preference for this life and the lasting afterlife.
  reasons: Ṭabarī prefers 87:14–17 as the nearest antecedent of the demonstrative and says ṣuḥuf means Abraham's and Moses' books. Abū Dharr's report has the Prophet recite these verses when asked about earlier scriptures; a further report says the Prophet recited the passage when asked what he received from those scrolls. Ṭabāṭabāʾī says that report supports the broader referent.
