## C. Scoring

1. **Commitments:** each row of A matched to the expected question (or a justified alternative recorded in the
   ledger), with the expected outcome.
2. **Positions:** every "must show" position present with its holders; minor positions counted.
3. **Required voices:** Biqāʿī and Bint al-Shāṭiʾ cited wherever the used question holds them (at least 103:1/q02,
   68:18/q02), or a ledger reason.
4. **Attribution:** ten named holders sampled from the blocks, each found in the cited notes.
5. **No page judgement:** no block grades, confirms or corrects the page.
6. **Cost:** recorded against the estimate ($3–5).
